## Executive Summary

Comprehensive compilation of actual CRE distress transactions showing loss severities of **80-97%** on office properties — far exceeding typical bank loss assumptions of 20-40%. This data validates the "extend-and-pretend" thesis: when banks can no longer extend, actual outcomes are catastrophic.

## Implications for Bank Analysis

### Loss Severity Gap

| Assumption | Rate | Source |
|------------|------|--------|
| Bank internal models | 20-40% | Typical CRE loss assumption |
| Actual office transactions | **70-97%** | FCNightingale data |
| **Gap** | **30-57pp** | Capital impact if marked to market |

### Capital Impact Scenario

If a bank with $1B office CRE exposure had to mark to actual transaction values:
- Bank assumption (30% loss): $300M loss
- Actual outcome (85% loss): $850M loss
- **Unrecognized loss:** $550M

This is why extend-and-pretend continues — recognition would be catastrophic.
