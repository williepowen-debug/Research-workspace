## Executive Summary

**Key Finding:** Defense/military metros have **different DOGE exposure** than civilian agency metros. Atlantic Union Bank explicitly stated defense contractor lending is "unlikely to be impacted significantly by Trump cutbacks."

**Thesis:** Defense metros are **INSULATED for now**, with NASA-dependent metros (Huntsville) as the exception.

## Thesis Evaluation

### Evidence for INSULATION:
- ✅ AUB: defense contractors "unlikely to be impacted significantly"
- ✅ DOGE cuts focused on civilian agencies (USAID, HHS, SSA, Education)
- ✅ Space Force and Navy shipbuilding appear protected
- ✅ Pentagon cuts only 0.07% — "performative"
- ✅ Hampton Roads military = 40% of regional GDP

### Evidence for DELAYED RISK:
- ⚠️ NASA Marshall civil servant cuts (279 senior staffers)
- ⚠️ Some defense advisory contracts cut ($1B Air/Space Force)
- ⚠️ Long-term budget pressure could eventually reach defense

### Conclusion:
**Defense metros INSULATED for now. NASA-dependent metros (Huntsville) = exception.** The key differentiator is civilian vs. uniformed/defense contractor composition.
