## Assessment: Is the Temp Employment Thesis On Track?

### The Thesis:
Temp employment leads NFP by 3-6 months. If temp is still declining/bottoming now, NFP should weaken through mid-2026.

### Current Status: **THESIS INTACT, BUT WITH NUANCE**

**Supportive Evidence:**
1. Temp penetration still at cycle lows (1.54%)
2. Temp jobs still shedding (-5,700 in December)
3. YoY revenue declines across agencies (-4% to -14%)
4. Permanent placement weak (no hiring confidence)
5. Agencies still cutting own headcount

**Complicating Evidence:**
1. Sequential growth returning (first in 3 years for RHI)
2. January momentum cited by multiple CEOs
3. MAN's light industrial brand actually growing
4. IT staffing decline rate narrowing
5. Management tone shifted from "challenging" to "stabilizing"

### Interpretation:
The temp sector appears to be **forming a bottom** (Q4 2025/Q1 2026) rather than re-accelerating downward. However, the **leading indicator signal remains negative for NFP** because:

- A temp bottom ≠ immediate NFP strength
- Historical lag is 3-6 months
- Even if temp troughs now, NFP should continue softening into Q2-Q3 2026
- **Key:** Watch for temp to show POSITIVE YoY growth before calling all-clear on employment
