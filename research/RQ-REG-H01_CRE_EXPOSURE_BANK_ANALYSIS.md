## Executive Summary

Analysis of the Chicago bank failure pattern (Pulaski Jan 2025, Metropolitan Jan 2026) led to discovery of a much larger systemic issue: **1,788 US banks have CRE exposure >300% of equity**, with the number increasing each quarter. The FAU screener reveals EGBN is #3 most exposed nationally, confirming our DOGE employment thesis is layered on top of severe CRE concentration risk.

## Part 1: Chicago Bank Failure Pattern

### Metropolitan Capital Bank & Trust (Failed Jan 30, 2026)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Assets | $261M |
| Equity Ratio | **1.62%** (critically thin) |

---

### Pulaski Savings Bank (Failed Jan 17, 2025)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Assets | $49.5M |
| DIF Cost | **$28.5M (62% loss rate)** — highest since 2019 |
| Cause | **FRAUD** — $20.7M deposits unrecorded in core system |
| Acquired By | Millennium Bank (Des Plaines, IL) |

**The Fraud:** Certificates of deposit were never posted to the core banking system. When examiners discovered this in December 2024, the bank was instantly "critically undercapitalized."

**Regulatory Failure:** Pulaski was under MOU with FDIC/IDFPR since **2017** — 8 years of known problems before failure.

**FDIC OIG called for in-depth review** given the 62% loss rate.

## Part 4: Individual Bank Deep Dives

### Live Oak Bank (LOB) — #1 at 605%

**Why the ratio is misleading:**
- Primarily SBA 7(a) lender (#1 in US by dollar volume)
- SBA guarantees 75-85% of most loans
- "CRE" is owner-occupied small business (vet clinics, dental offices, etc.)
- Not rent-regulated NYC multifamily exposure

**Q4 2025 Performance:**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| EPS | $0.95 (+73% YoY) |
| Loan Production | $6.21B (record) |
| Provision | $96.3M (flat YoY) |
| Stock Reaction | +6.2% |

**Risk Assessment:** 🟡 YELLOW — High ratio but different risk profile due to SBA guarantees.

### Dime Community Bank (DCOM) — #2 at 550%

**The NYCB comparison:** Same NYC rent-regulated multifamily exposure that broke NYCB.

**But different execution:**

**Q4 2025 Performance:**

### EagleBank (EGBN) — #3 at 547%

**The most concerning bank in our analysis:**

1. **#3 highest CRE exposure nationally**
2. **Pure DC metro geography** — maximum DOGE exposure
3. **Already stressed:**
   - Q3 2025: $67.5M loss
   - CEO retiring 2026
   - Dividend cut to $0.01
   - Stock plunged in 2025
4. **Q4 2025:** Small profit ($7.6M) but deposits -4% QoQ (flight signal)
5. **No visible de-risking actions** (unlike Dime, MTB, AUB)
6. **Explicit government contractor lending division:** government.eaglebankcorp.com

**Risk Assessment:** 🔴 **RED** — Double exposure (CRE + DOGE), already stressed, no defensive actions, deposit flight.

## Part 6: Key Findings

### 1. EGBN Double Whammy Confirmed
EagleBank has #3 CRE exposure nationally (547%) AND pure DC DOGE employment exposure. This is worse than we initially assessed.

### 2. De-Risking Separates Winners from Losers
Banks that recognized CRE risk early (MTB, AUB, Dime) are being rewarded. Banks that didn't (EGBN, BHRB, TOWN) are exposed.

### 3. The 1,788 Bank Problem
Over 1/3 of US banks have CRE exposure above the 300% danger threshold, and the count is increasing. This is systemic, not idiosyncratic.

### 4. Fraud Underlies Recent Failures
2 of 3 recent bank failures (Pulaski, Santa Anna) were fraud-related. Combined with the October 2025 Stupin wave ($2.9B+), fraud may be more pervasive than official data suggests.

### 5. Maturity Wall is Real
NY Fed's warning + CRE extend-and-pretend = stress recognition delayed but inevitable. Peak: Late 2025-2027.
