# BlackRock 13F Verification: UST Selling Claim
**Research Date:** 2026-03-25  
**Claim Source:** Felix Prehn (FinTwit)  
**Claim:** BlackRock sold USTs for 3 consecutive quarters, rotating into commodities (GDX, FCX, CCJ) — adding voluntary institutional demand destruction on top of sovereign selling.

---

## Verdict: **CLAIM IS FALSE / NOT SUPPORTED BY DATA**

The 13F data contradicts the narrative at multiple levels.

---

## Data Source

BlackRock files a **combination 13F-HR** aggregating holdings across hundreds of institutional managed accounts. CIK changed: old CIK 0001364742 (through Q2 2024), new CIK 0002012383 (Q3 2024 onward). All values pulled directly from SEC EDGAR XML info tables.

## Analysis

### Why the Claim Fails

1. **IEF (intermediate Treasuries) is *growing* substantially** — +44% over the study period. Even if some long-duration (TLT) was trimmed, intermediate-duration UST exposure expanded dramatically. Net UST exposure in aggregate is not decreasing.

2. **TLT is volatile, not in a clean downtrend.** You can cherry-pick Q4 2024 + Q1 2025 to show "selling" but then it rebounds sharply. It's noise around a lower level, not a deliberate multi-quarter reduction.

3. **CCJ was actively SOLD** — the opposite of the rotation claim. CCJ shares fell from 7.25M to 3.91M in three quarters.

4. **GDX build is statistically irrelevant** relative to portfolio size (~0.0003% of AUM).

5. **FCX is flat.** A stable passive-like holding in the aggregate equity book, not a tactical rotation.

### The Structural Caveat

BlackRock's 13F is a **combination filing** — it aggregates holdings from >30 included managers and hundreds of institutional accounts. Individual mandates within BlackRock (e.g., a specific total return fund, a commodity-focused SMA) could behave differently. Prehn may be looking at a specific BlackRock fund, not the aggregate.

**However**, if the claim is meant to characterize BlackRock as an institution making a strategic rotation, the aggregate data — which is the only legitimate representation of "BlackRock" — does not support it.

---

## Conclusion

**Do NOT weight Felix Prehn's BlackRock rotation claim as a demand-destruction signal.** 

- UST selling claim: **False at aggregate level** (IEF growing, TLT volatile not trending down)
- Commodity rotation claim: **False or rounding error** (CCJ sold down, FCX flat, GDX tiny)
- This particular FinTwit narrative appears to be cherry-picked or based on incomplete data

**What IS real:** There's a modest TLT reduction visible in Q4 2024 / Q1 2025 that could generate a narrative, but context kills it — IEF tells the opposite story, and it's the bigger position.
