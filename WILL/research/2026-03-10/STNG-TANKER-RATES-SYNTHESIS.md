# STNG / Tanker Rates vs Oil Price — 4-Source Synthesis

**Date:** Mar 10, 2026
**Sources:** Perplexity, KIMI, Gemini (Deep Research), ChatGPT (Deep Research)
**Prompt:** What happens to tanker rates and shipping stocks when crude oil price crashes but physical supply disruption continues?

---

## Core Finding (Unanimous)

**Tanker rates decouple from oil price when physical disruption persists.** Rates are driven by ton-miles and fleet utilization, not crude price level. Oil price correlation is weak (~4.72% of VLCC rate variance per academic study).

Driver hierarchy (all 4 sources agree):
1. Fleet utilization / vessel availability (dominant short-term)
2. Ton-miles / route disruptions (dominant medium-term)
3. Oil price level (weakest, regime-dependent)
