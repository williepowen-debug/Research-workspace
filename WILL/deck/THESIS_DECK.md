## SLIDE 6 — The Bank Problem (Plain English)

They're taking loans that are clearly real estate (CRE) and reclassifying them as business loans (C&I) in their regulatory filings. This makes their balance sheets look healthier than they are.

## SLIDE 13 — Why the Fed Can't Cut

Everyone expects rate cuts. Here's why they won't come:

1. **Inflation is sticky.** Core PCE at 3.1%, well above the 2% target.
2. **GDP is weak.** Last print: 0.7%. The economy is slowing.
3. **This is stagflation.** Slow growth + persistent inflation = the Fed's nightmare. Cutting rates helps growth but makes inflation worse. Raising rates kills inflation but crashes the economy.

**They're frozen.** And every month they don't cut, the pressure on banks and borrowers increases.

TLT (the long-term Treasury bond ETF) goes down when rates stay high or go higher. Our puts are betting rates don't come down.

## SLIDE 17 — The Demand Destruction Framework (Hamilton)

This is the intellectual backbone of the portfolio timing.

Economist James Hamilton studied every oil shock since the 1970s. The pattern:

1. **Oil prices spike** → measured by NOPI (Net Oil Price Increase)
2. **Consumer behavior changes** → $4/gallon gas is the behavioral breakpoint where people cut spending
3. **GDP drag follows with a lag** → peak damage hits about 4 quarters after the oil spike
4. **Credit markets crack BEFORE equities** → ~3 months lead time
5. **Equity trough comes 7-11 months after the oil peak**

**Current NOPI: 47 log points → implies GDP drag of -3.0% to -4.9%**

This framework tells us: June options capture about 1/3 of the damage. December options capture the peak. That's why we're rolling from June to December.

## SLIDE 20 — High Yield: The Transmission Mechanism

HYG tracks high-yield corporate bonds — the debt of weaker companies.

When stress builds:
1. First, spreads widen (investors demand more compensation for risk)
2. Then, weaker companies can't refinance
3. Then, defaults rise
4. Then, banks that lent to these companies take losses
5. Then, bank stocks fall

**HY OAS (the spread) at ~317 bps. Our threshold: 320 bps = transmission confirmed.**

We're 3 basis points from the trigger. Eight contracts, positioned for December.
