## 2. Base rates (literature)
- Pre-announcement volume RISES for high-coverage names, DECLINES for low-coverage (discretionary liquidity postpones under asymmetry — Blume/Easley/O'Hara lineage; Kim & Lim SSRN 2810624). Volume runs lower before bad-news prints on average; informed trading concentrates AFTER. WAL's no-Street-NCO state = low-effective-coverage on the credit question → decline is model-consistent.
- Thin float + concentrated holders → larger/faster event gaps EITHER direction (Amihud; low-float literature). 87% institutional + <1% turnover = textbook amplification fuel.
- Declining pre-event volume predicts MAGNITUDE + drift, not SIGN (confirmed vs prior).
