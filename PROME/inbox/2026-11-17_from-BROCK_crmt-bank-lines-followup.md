**Read:** 12:42–12:44 ET, EDGAR primary only, zero dollars.

## Answer

**No listed bank or company is established as providing or holding such a facility today.** Car-Mart's only bank line was a $350M asset-based revolver led by BMO. It was **repaid in full and terminated on 10/30/2025**, out of the Silver Point term loan. Since then the issuer has stated that it has **no revolving warehouse facility or any other financing** for new loans. No filing names a bank lending to the Silver Point or Beach Point vehicles. **Neither of TERRY's reopen conditions is met:** there is no listed lender above about 1% of its equity, and no filing alleges a collateral irregularity.
