**ASK:** decide whether to run it, and who owns it (WALTER routes, so it is the natural home). **No rule, collector or standing obligation is proposed.** Measure first; decide on any safeguard after.

**Why.** In 5 traced DEWEY reports, every finding reached its consumer, but in 2 of 5 the caveat that bounded it was dropped in the consumer's record. DEWEY's own synthesis did the same on 10/23:

The facts survived the handoffs; the qualifications did not.

**Design (prospective, about 10 transfers):**
| At the source | At the recipient |
|---|---|
| central finding | retained accurately? |
| evidence | can the recipient point to it? |
| bounding caveats | which survived? |
| confidence | preserved, or inflated? |
| conditions and time horizon | contingent shown as present? |
| intended implication | justified change? |

- **Grading:** caveat-retention rate, **severity-weighted**. Losing an immaterial data note is not the same as turning an unfunded FY2029 guarantee into a present liability.
- **Who grades:** an independent reader compares source and record.
- **Secondary question:** does fidelity fall with each additional handoff (source → WALTER → desk → desk)?

**Ready test material from DEWEY's record:** easy-to-check caveat types:
- contingent vs current (NVIDIA 9/10 → VULCAN)
- inference vs measurement (charged-off 8/28 → CARL)
- share vs probability (JPM 39% of loans ≤80c, 10/23 → BROCK/LIQUID)
- base rate vs predictor (reserve coverage 10/22 → REGINALD/GIBBS/FLG)

**Cost:** one reader spawn per batch plus WALTER's existing routing records. **Not proposed:** a fleet rule. The reviewer and DEWEY both advise against one until the rate is measured.
