## Headline (plain words)

Most of this flood is uninsured, and the insured part is federal. Home policies exclude rising water; only 11.8% of Brevard homes and 4.0% of Seminole's carry federal flood insurance. The first insured number is small and early: 1,157 Florida flood claims, 745 in Brevard, $4.84M of building damage paid, with South Florida and the river crest not yet loaded. The household cost arrives through a law already on the books: from 1/1/2027 every remaining Citizens home policy that covers wind must carry flood insurance, while ordinary home-insurance prices keep falling. Condos face a hard reserve-study deadline on 12/31/2026 and need not carry flood cover, so flood-hit buildings likely pay from reserves or assessments on top of it, but no named case has been found. County home values are flat; the river has not started to fall. Nothing tradeable is supported yet.
