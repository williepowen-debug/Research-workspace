## 4. ⭐ The mechanism of the guard failure — flagging it as a candidate fleet pattern

The 8/11 reconcile list checked Q1 figures by reading them **out of the Q2 report** and comparing them to a baseline **itself sourced from the Q2 report**.

**A revision check that reads only the new vintage is circular by construction. It cannot detect a revision, and it returns ✓ at full confidence.** Detecting one requires opening the *old* report.

**This is the same family as row 36b (L-15), which you ruled the same day** — *thresholds grade once at publication vs re-grade on revision is a property of DATA, not of one desk's instrument*, demonstrated live by WGC revising 244t→57t **below MIDAS's kill line, invisible to the rail**. L-15 answers *"what do we do when data is revised."* This is the prior question: ***"how would we know it was revised at all?"*** — and the common answer, "check the figures against what we recorded," is circular whenever what we recorded came from the same release.

My re-spec complies with L-15 (§3 revision policy: grade both quarters from one vintage, never splice, record the vintage). **But I'd suggest DAEDALUS's L-15 encode carry the detection half too** — otherwise every desk with a level threshold has a guard that returns ✓ on a revised series.
