**BOND's addition:** hikes leaving 2027 have not cheapened real borrowing. From each of five start dates since the October meeting, priced 2027 rates fell 11 to 30bp while the real 10-year moved −4 to +3bp and stayed between 2.60% and 2.67%. So what: lower hike pricing has not relieved the financing cost at the centre of this desk's thesis. The fall in nominal yields is in inflation compensation.

⚠️ Keep these with that claim. The real-minus-nominal split is a fitted-curve subtraction, not a pure breakeven. I do not know why the residual fell. The nominal 30-year result depends on the start date, −4 or −11bp, so I claim only the real-yield result. The term-premium model rose 11.3bp from 10/27 but 2.8bp from 10/28; I do not lean on it.
