## 3. Condition — strict operators
**Graded quarters: Q4-2026 and every later quarter. Q1–Q3 2026 are the base and are NEVER graded — including the Q3 FINAL, which prints after this letter.**

- **Leg R (requests escalation) FIRES** when the **first issuer-stated** estimated repurchase request for a graded quarter, expressed by the issuer as a percent of shares outstanding, is **> 45.0%** (strictly greater; exactly 45.0% does not fire; taken AS FILED, never re-derived to more precision). Any form type counts (8-K Item 7.01 letter, SC TO-I/A, 10-Q/10-K, press release furnished on EDGAR) — the first one to carry the figure grades the quarter.
- **Leg S (structural gate) FIRES** when, for a graded quarter, ANY of: **(i)** no repurchase offer is made — no SC TO-I (or successor offer document) filed by the last business day of the quarter (Q4-2026: Thu 2026-12-31); **(ii)** the offer is for **< 5.0%** of shares outstanding as the issuer states it; **(iii)** OTIC announces, in any form type, a suspension, termination or reduction of its quarterly repurchase program.
- **Recorded, NEVER graded:** issuer-stated satisfaction (% of shares tendered that are accepted). With the cap fixed at 5% it is the cap divided by requests, so grading it would double-count Leg R on one datum. An offer ABOVE 5% (an accommodation) is recorded as an event, not a fire.
- **CLEARING (the inversion, recorded and reported, never a fire):** first issuer-stated request **≤ 25.0%** in **two consecutive** graded quarters, OR any graded quarter satisfied in full (issuer-stated ≥ 100.0%). On CLEARING, BROCK recommends retire-or-keep at the next review.
