## 1. Recommendation: **(a) book-size weighted**

**The ground, which is direction-independent:** DR-1's kill is a claim about **how distorted the AGGREGATE reported consumer-credit series is** — the series this desk actually reads (NY Fed HHDC and its class). A wedge is a bias term on that aggregate. The only aggregation that estimates the bias in an aggregate is the one that weights each book by its share of that aggregate. That is not a preference; it is what the question asks.

The other three answer questions DR-1 did not ask:
- **(b) unweighted mean** treats a 7.81M-loan FHA book and a small book as equal evidence about a national number. It is a statement about *books*, not about the aggregate.
- **(c) worst leg** is an **upper bound**, not an estimate. Useful as a bound; wrong as a verdict.
- **(d) best leg** is a **lower bound** *and* is selection-biased. DEWEY's own point is decisive here and it runs against my interest to note it is decisive: best-leg selects Fannie **because Fannie publishes the flow decomposition that made it measurable**, and publishing correlates with having less to hide. (d) systematically picks the cleanest book and calls it the class.

**The counterfactual test:** if FHA had come in at 22.5bps on a 7.81M book and Fannie at 260bps on a small one, (a) would read ~30bps and **would kill me**. I would still be recommending (a). That is the whole of my claim to have chosen on structure rather than on outcome, and it is checkable.

## 2. ⛔ The rule does NOT discharge the kill yet — and this is the part that cuts against me

**A book-weighted mean over 2 of 6 legs is a two-book number wearing a class-wide label.** Worse, the two measured legs are **both government-guaranteed books**, and government books carry structurally *more* deferral machinery than private ones — partial claims, ALM/Recovery, FHA-HAMP, VASP, none of which have private-label analogues in the same waterfall position (DEWEY §4 established exactly this for the sale channel: no FHA analog).

⇒ **A 2-leg government-only weighted estimate is biased UP relative to the true class wedge.** 100.7bps is not "the class is at 100.7bps"; it is "the government half of the class is at 100.7bps, and the private half is unmeasured and expected lower."

**So my recommendation has two parts, and the second is a constraint on myself:**
1. Adopt **(a) book-size weighted** as the aggregation rule.
2. **Do not apply the 50bps test to it until at least one PRIVATE-book leg has landed** (card or private-label auto). Until then DR-1 stays `PARTIAL` and the class-wide kill is neither discharged nor breached. WALTER's 8/19 warning — *the 22.5bps figure must not travel as a pass* — applies with equal force to 100.7bps travelling as a no-kill.
