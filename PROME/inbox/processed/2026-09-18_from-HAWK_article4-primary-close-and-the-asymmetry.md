## 1. THE WHOLE STORY FIRST

Will asked whether the chances of NATO/EU–Russia conflict are rising. Here is the honest shape of it.

**Two different questions are being merged in the headlines, and they have different answers.**

The chance of an **armed incident** between NATO and Russia is genuinely rising — and a large part of that rise is **NATO's own doing**. On 1 August, NATO's Baltic mission stopped being an air-*policing* mission and became an active air-*defence* mission, with the authority to open fire moved from national capitals up to its supreme commander. Estonia's defence minister described it as removing "restrictions preventing the pilot from pressing the button." That loosened trigger is now meeting a rising volume of drone traffic drifting out of the Ukraine war. More shooting follows almost mechanically. That is a real and underweighted risk, because accidents between armed forces are how wars start that nobody chose.

The chance of a **deliberate Russia–NATO war** is not visibly rising on the evidence I hold. The political and legal machinery that would register genuine alliance alarm has not moved at all: no Article 4 consultation in 2026, no Article 5 debate, Germany's mobilisation law built but its switch not thrown, and the EU's counter-hybrid response announced in a speech with no legal base, no figure and no date attached.

**So: the military rungs have stepped, the political-legal rungs have not.** That gap is the finding, and it has held all year.

## 3. TASK 2 — THE ASYMMETRY: NOT RESOLVED, AND THE DISCRIMINATOR REFUTES MY OWN FRAMING

The question was: *either the bar for invoking Article 4 has silently risen — meaning incident headlines carry less signal than in 2025 — or the next invocation arrives as a genuine surprise.*

**The discriminator:** is the silence caused by a **raised threshold applied to comparable events**, or by the **absence of a qualifying event**? Those look identical in a headline count and point opposite ways.

Applying it produced corrections to my own work.

| | Poland 2025-09-10 | Estonia 2025-09-23 | Every 2026 event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Character | **Mass** — ~19–21 drones in one night | **Manned** Russian state aircraft, 3 MiG-31s, 12 min | Single unmanned objects; one fast object; one ground sabotage device |
| Whose deliberate act | Unambiguous | Unambiguous *by construction* — a manned jet cannot be drifting debris | **Mostly not established** |
| Article 4 | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

⛔ **Correction 1 — withdrawn: my claim this morning that "the 2025 bar was cleared twice on events no larger."** It conflated *how bad* with *whose act it was*. The 2026 events are comparable or worse in consequence; they are categorically weaker in agency. Both 2025 triggers had a property no 2026 event has.

⛔ **Correction 2 — withdrawn: "declined ≥4× in four months."** A denominator error. Most of those events are not *trials* of the threshold at all. The honest trial count:

1. **Romania, 2026-05-29** — a drone into an apartment block, two injured. A trial **because the requester said so**: Foreign Minister **Oana Toiu** said the incident "falls into the category of events that justify the use of instruments" such as Article 4 [Il Sole 24 Ore, 2026-05-30]. Romania did not invoke. ⚠️ Her judgement concerns harm on Romanian soil, not who launched it.
2. **Leipzig/Halle, 2026-08-04, attributed 2026-09-01** — the only event where a NATO government asserts **Russian state agency** on NATO soil with no Ukraine leg and no drift explanation available. Germany chose prosecutors and expulsions **instead of** the alliance.
3. ⛔ **Poland, 2026-07-30 — DOWNGRADED tonight, and this was my strongest counter-fact.** I had it as a Russian cruise missile tracked 100 km inside Poland and not engaged. OSPREY went to the Polish operational command's account: radar contact **03:40 → 03:46 — six minutes — lost "before the object could be clearly identified."** The **crash site** was 100 km inside Poland; **the track was not 100 km of custody.** Tusk's identification of it as a Kh-101 is "all indications" — **preliminary, never upgraded to a forensic finding.** ⚠️ **Russian agency here is not established.** The no-Article-4 fact survives; the "unambiguous Russian cruise missile" framing does not.

**Record: 2025 — two trials, two invocations. 2026 — two solid trials plus one downgraded, no invocations.**

⚠️ **With two or three trials you cannot tell a raised bar from ordinary small-sample noise. The asymmetry is NOT resolved, and I will not manufacture a verdict from a sample of two.** That said, the silence is not pure event-absence either: one trial was a **deliberate decline stated on the record** (Toiu), and one was a **channel substitution** (Berlin).

### ★ The mechanism that explains both readings — and it is the most underweighted item here

**Article 4 has been functionally superseded.** In September 2025 it was the instrument that *unlocked* a collective response: Poland invoked on the 10th, **Operation Eastern Sentry launched on the 12th.** In 2026 that response is already standing and expanding with nobody invoking anything — a Turkish drone carrier deployed toward the Latvian coast on 02-20, NATO's largest allied airpower deployment of the year across the eastern flank on 03-04/05, alliance counter-drone training over Lithuania and Latvia on 05-27, and the mission conversion on 08-01.

**A country invoking Article 4 today gets nothing it does not already have.** It stopped being a thermometer and became a ratchet already pulled.

⇒ **Rung 3's quiet state is weak evidence in *both* directions and must never be read as reassurance.** A quiet indicator that has been superseded is not a quiet situation. This is a stronger reason not to score a gate on it than the sourcing gap in §2.

### ⚖️ The forward test I registered instead — watch this, not the headline count

> **Trigger:** the next event on NATO territory carrying **(i)** unambiguous Russian state agency at the object level **and (ii)** any one of — a **manned** platform, a **mass event** (≥5 objects in one night), or a **fatality** on NATO soil.
> - **Article 4 invoked within 7 days** ⇒ the bar never rose; 2026's silence was event-absence; the ladder is intact — but headlines carry signal only for that narrow class, never for the drone count.
> - **Not invoked** ⇒ the bar has demonstrably risen, **Article 4 is dead as an indicator**, and rung 3 should be retired rather than watched.

NATO firing more often at objects whose origin it cannot establish, under delegated authority, is a *higher* accident risk than the raw count implies, not a lower one. **The contamination kills the inference about Russian intent. It does not kill the risk.**
