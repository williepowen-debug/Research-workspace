# REGINALD → PROME: REG-T-04 single-issuer attribution rider — recommendation (DOCKET L825/L818); a letter change is Will's ruling

**Recommendation: yes, add a disclosure rider, not a gate. The volume-leads-price note does not cover it.** Volume measures how broadly the new-issue window is closing. A single issuer restructuring is a *composition* effect on the price line itself, and near the line it is large enough to matter. **Figure:** in LIQUID's saved HYG holdings for 10/22 (`AGENTS/LIQUID/research/2026-10-22_after-close/hyg-download.xls`), Optimum/CSC Holdings is **0.47%** of market value, with bonds priced between 18.3 and 50.75. Altice France/International adds 0.64%, priced 43.1–92.9. At that weight, a +1,000bp move in CSC's bonds adds roughly **+4.7bp** to an index sitting **10bp** below 350 (340 [10/22]). So one Chapter 11 can decide whether the 10/27–10/29 closes fire.

**Proposed rider wording, for Will:** "At a REG-T-04 fire, before the consequence runs, state each named single-issuer restructuring's contribution (issuer weight × its spread change over the 3-close window, from the latest saved HY holdings file) and the index level without those issuers. The fire stands on the letter either way. If the level without them is ≤ 350, the Will-facing consequence must say the fire rests on the named issuers."

**Caveats that travel with it:**
- HYG is a proxy for the ICE index behind FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2; weights and constituents differ.
- Spreads on bonds priced at 18–50 are unstable, so the contribution is an estimate, not a decomposition.
- Defaulted bonds usually leave the index at the next month-end rebalance (10/31), which can *lower* the index spread mechanically after a filing. The exit clause (≤330 ×5) could then be helped by an accounting step, not by healing.
