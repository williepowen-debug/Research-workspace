## What today's NFP grade found
That memory's taxonomy is **binary**:
- **FROZEN** — *"the number IS the threshold … Never move it; that is the whole discipline."*
- **TRACKED** — the number measures something that moves; carrying it forward is a stale instrument.

**My 9/4 instance fits neither, and it is the dangerous one because it looks like textbook FROZEN.**

My frozen NFP card pre-computed freeze-thaw LEG A as **"August ≥ +303K"**, derived 36h early from `(20 − 23 + X)/3 ≥ 100` on the then-current June (+20K) and July (−23K). **BLS revised both months in the very release that carried August** — to **+31K** and **+21K** — so the true bar was `(31 + 21 + X)/3 ≥ 100` ⇒ **+248K**.

⇒ **The frozen number was wrong by 55K before the print was even read.** The freeze was correct; the *inputs* were restated retroactively by the issuer. Nothing moved, nothing drifted, no anchor migrated — **the past changed.**

It graded correctly only because the card carried an explicit instruction beside the value: *"recompute with the revised numbers FIRST, per L-02, before reading August."* **A card that had frozen only "+303K" would have graded the wrong bar with complete confidence and left no trace of the error** — no tell, no alarm, and a clean-looking grade.

## Proposed third kind
> **FROZEN-ON-REVISABLE** — the threshold is genuinely frozen, but its VALUE was computed from a series the issuer **restates** (payrolls, GDP, JOLTS, QCEW benchmarks, CPI seasonals, any benchmarked or revised official series). The freeze is correct; the number silently decays anyway.
> **Rule: freeze the FORMULA as the threshold object and carry the number only as a dated illustration of it.** Write `LEG A: (m₋₂ + m₋₁ + X)/3 ≥ 100 ⇒ X ≥ +303K [on the 2026-09-02 vintage — RECOMPUTE on the revised vintage before grading]`, never the bare `+303K`.
> **Test:** *"If my inputs are restated between registration and grading, does my threshold still say the right thing?"* If not, it is FROZEN-ON-REVISABLE and needs its recompute instruction shipped with it.

**This is not a variant of TRACKED.** A TRACKED anchor migrates and you re-measure the present. Here the present is fine and the **history** moved under a correctly-frozen number.
