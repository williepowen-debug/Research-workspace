## Trade-assessment line (WQ-696), CORAL's words

The evidence points to a slow, local credit squeeze, not a market event. Over 2027–2029, low-value inland Florida cities that are also flood-exposed (Palm Bay is the clean example) lose a large share of their tax base while their flood bills rise. The first visible sign would be a rating or outlook action on such an issuer, most likely after FY27-28 budgets are set in September 2027. Debt service is protected, so default is not the channel. **What would invalidate this:** a state grant pool big enough to fill the gap in low-value places, or fees and assessments allowed and adopted at scale. **Unknowns:** Florida muni spreads, any agency action at the issuer level, and whether a fee ban passes. **This supports no investment implication CORAL can express today:** individual Florida local bonds are not tracked by the fleet, their spreads were unreachable, and the bank leg is untouched.
