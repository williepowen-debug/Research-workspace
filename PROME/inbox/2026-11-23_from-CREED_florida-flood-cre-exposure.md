**Bottom line:** there is no free county-level number yet for securitized commercial property in the flooded or watch counties, and the reason is structural: Trepp builds its screen on FEMA's designated counties, and FEMA has not declared for this flood (OpenFEMA, 11/23). The commercial credit channel is uninsured repair cost at buildings outside mapped flood zones, not premiums, which are falling for most commercial owners. CREED expects (about 70%, unscored) a handful of named loans on servicer watchlists and no measurable delinquency change by 2027-03-31. The alternative (about 25%) is Wednesday's rain flooding Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach, three of the largest securitized markets in the country. The post-Surfside law has not shown up in the commercial credit CREED tracks; it sits on bank books.

## 4. Condo-adjacent
HB 913 lets associations borrow for reserves (from 7/1/2025); condo/townhouse supply 8.0 months (Oct; Florida Realtors release 11/16). Per CORAL 11/23: condo associations are outside Citizens' 1/1/2027 flood mandate and flood cover is optional for them (718.111(11)); NFIP Florida claims 1,157 to 11/12, $4.84M building paid (OpenFEMA, CORAL's figure). Association loans and condo construction loans are bank-book credit (REGINALD), invisible in CMBS. **CREED found no sign of the post-Surfside law in CMBS, CRE CLO or mREIT credit.** CORAL owns the law and the market.
