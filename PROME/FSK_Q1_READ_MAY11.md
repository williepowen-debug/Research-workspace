## Strategic Support Package

FSK announced a four-part value support package:

1. **$150M cumulative convertible perpetual preferred** purchased by KKR affiliate.
   - 5% cash dividend or 7% PIK at FSK option.
   - Senior to common, junior to debt.
   - Initial conversion price equals Q1 NAV: **$18.83**.
2. **$150M tender offer** by KKR affiliate at **$11.00/share**.
3. **$300M share repurchase authorization** through June 1, 2027.
4. **50% subordinated income incentive fee waiver** for four quarters.

Interpretation: support package is equity-bullish optics / sponsor backstop, but credit-bearish evidence. Healthy BDCs do not usually need preferred capital, fee waivers, and repurchases after a ~10% NAV drop.

## Revolver / Funding Tell

This is an important funding-quality flag: lender support continues, but on tighter terms and with a lowered covenant floor.
