> News Release 2026-104 | November 24, 2026 OCC Announces Enforcement Actions for November 2026 Share This Page: WASHINGTON—The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) today released enforcement actions for November 2026. The OCC uses enforcement actions against banks to require the board of directors and management to take timely actions to correct the deficient practices or violations identified. Actions taken against banks are: Civil Money Penalty against Flagstar Bank, N.A., Hicksville, New York, for a pattern or practice of violating the Flood Disaster Protection Act. The assessed civil monetary penalty is $112,000. (Docket No. AA-ENF-2026-45) The OCC terminates a bank enforcement action once the bank has achieved substantial compliance with an enforcement action; or when the OCC determines that articles deemed “not in compliance” have become outdated or irrelevant to the bank’s current circumstances; or when the OCC incorporates the articles deemed “not in compliance” into a new action. The termination actions are: Order Terminating the Cease and Desist Order against USAA Federal Savings Bank , Phoenix, Arizona, dated December 18, 2024 (Docket No. AA-ENF-2024-96 ). (Docket No. AA-ENF-2026-49). To receive alerts for news releases announcing public OCC enforcement actions, subscribe to OCC Email Updates .
