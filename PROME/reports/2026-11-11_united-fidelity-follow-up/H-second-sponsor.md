Birge and Held are **not** listed among the directors or officers of United Fidelity Bank or Fidelity Federal Bancorp on their 2023 Form D filings (VERIFIED for those lists only).
3. **United Fidelity is a major lender to Birge & Held, and the bank's holding company holds a third lien on one property.** Florida UCC filings show United Fidelity first and second liens on New Lakeridge LLC (Aug 2022) and New City Place LLC (Gainesville, Jun 2022). **Fidelity Federal Bancorp (the holding company) holds the "THIRD MORTGAGE" on New City Place** (UCC 202201982110, filed 2022-06-16, prepared by a Pedcor employee; VERIFIED). 4. **I found no outside party sharing the loss on a United Fidelity-financed Birge & Held property.** No Form D was found for any "New ___" entity (SEARCH-NOT-FOUND). The Form Ds found cover 2013–2017 syndications of older Indianapolis properties ($48.2M sold to 453 investor slots across 12 filings). No other lender, agency loan, CMBS loan or rating is documented on any United Fidelity-financed property. The one securitization mention (D2-Natixis 2026-M1) names Birge & Held only as **third-party property manager** for another sponsor's loan.
5. **No 2024–2026 distress news was found for Birge & Held.** It was still buying in 2026: New Signature at Carmel LLC took title on 2026-07-02 (parcel AV $60.4M; trade press reports a $79.7M purchase), and The Reserve at William's Glen sold in February 2026 for a reported $61.6M (search snippets). ## Answer to question 4 (from documents only)

**No document shows an outside party sharing the loss on a United Fidelity-financed Birge & Held property.** The second group does not make the problem less in-house; if anything it adds layers inside the same group.
