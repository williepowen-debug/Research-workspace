Coverage gaps: the OCC applications search and the Federal Reserve parent filings (FFIEC NIC, CAPTCHA-blocked) were not checked. This is SEARCH-NOT-FOUND, not proof of absence.
2. **Through Sep 30 the parent put in nothing material.** In the Sep 30 Call Report's equity roll-forward, capital stock sold is $0 and "other transactions with stockholders" is $10.6M for the year to date. Only $1.5M of that came in Q3. Equity fell from $648.8M to $389.7M: a $249.6M loss and $20.1M of other comprehensive loss, offset by that $10.6M. The arithmetic ties exactly.
3. **Q3 funding shrank and cash was drawn down.** Domestic deposits fell $251M (-4.7%). Brokered deposits fell $147M to $1.007B, and reciprocal deposits fell $277M to $739M. Cash dropped $283M to $106M (-73%), while pledged securities rose $294M to $1.229B. There were no Federal Home Loan Bank (FHLB) advances due within a year, no fed funds or repo borrowing, and other borrowed money was $50.1M, fully secured.
4. **Rule comparison (not a regulator finding).** Total capital of 7.26% meets the ratio test for "undercapitalized" in 12 CFR 6.4(b)(3) (below 8.0%). Under 12 CFR 337.6(b)(3), an undercapitalized bank "may not accept, renew, or roll over any brokered deposit". $510.5M of the brokered deposits mature within one year. The consent order with specific capital minimums already ruled out "well capitalized" under 6.4(b)(1)(i)(E). Whether the OCC has assigned or notified a category, and whether the bank had an FDIC waiver, is UNKNOWN.
5. **No press coverage and no statement from the bank or Pedcor about the Q3 loss found.** The bank's website shows no news, depositor notice or results. It advertises CDs at 4.20% to 4.45% APY (as of 11/06/2026). The FDIC national rate for a 12-month CD is 1.76%, and the national rate cap is 6.20% (Oct 19, 2026). No 2026 management change found; the CEO was appointed in July 2025.
