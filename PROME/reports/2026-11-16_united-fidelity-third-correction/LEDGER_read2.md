## Counterexamples of my own

1. **The $1.75B misread (row 9, BLOCKING).** A reader acting on Addendum 3 sizes the funding the bank must replace at up to $1.75B, believing about $740M of reciprocal deposits sit "above the cap". On the sources, all $739.1M counts as brokered only while the bank is over the cap. It is $11.25M over the $727.85M cap, or $112.2M over the $626.9M cap, and it requalifies the whole reciprocal book by shrinking that much (337.6(e)(3)(ii), REGINALD). The true range of funding at risk from reciprocal deposits runs from about $11M (shrink to requalify) to $739M (shrink nothing). The relay's label names the top end as though it were the excess. The label has already been copied into TERRY's re-look row.
2. **"Never issued" (row 16, BLOCKING).** A reader concludes that no OCC prompt-corrective-action directive or written notice exists, and so that 10/30 is firm and supervision has not yet moved. The source says only that none has been published, and REGINALD A1-e says such a document "would not be public". The rewritten bullet says the opposite ("UNKNOWN") two sections earlier.
