## The story in a paragraph

Florida is running at two speeds. The household and condo gauges are all pointing the same way and several got worse this month: personal bankruptcies are rising at nearly twice the national rate and still accelerating, the state-backed insurer is shedding policies faster than any takeout round explains, the condo market is carrying a year and more of supply in Miami-Dade with sellers paying special assessments the size of a down payment, and net migration into the state has fallen to a trickle. The channels that would turn that strain into losses for banks, insurers or the state budget are quiet: every Florida bank on the frozen frame holds, no insurer has failed, reinsurance got cheaper, state reserves are full, and the hurricane season is on track to be one of the quietest in sixty years. The thesis desk scores this honestly as "household and condo stress confirmed; bank-loss transmission not confirmed", with its own falsification grade due 11/15 at a baseline of 1.5 of 6. Nothing on the record today is a Florida trade: CORAL's trade file is declared flat and AEOLUS's reinsurance vehicle is not armed.
