**Brazil: an observed market disruption, with a financing distinction.** Reuters reports a stock-exchange circuit-breaker halt after Lula's reelection. A separate Reuters dispatch says Treasury canceled this week's fixed-rate and inflation-linked auctions while retaining Tuesday's Selic-linked auction. This is actual market/issuance disruption rather than merely a widening-spread story. Interpretation: domestic fiscal repricing warrants attention; broader emerging-market contagion is not established. Do not call all sovereign funding closed, or use volatile social-media loss figures as verified prices.

**Saudi energy: a new named-infrastructure claim, not confirmed physical loss.** Reuters reports the Houthis claimed attacks on East–West oil pumping stations and Riyadh airport on Monday; the earlier Reuters market dispatch explicitly said no immediate Saudi confirmation. This differs from BRENT's morning routine description of weekend attacks with no oil facility named. Neither that difference nor the claim proves damage, interruption or lost barrels. Interpretation: confirmation of disruption to the route bypassing Hormuz would matter more than the claim alone. Diplomatic contacts and an early oil pullback coexist with escalation headlines; no peace or supply-restoration conclusion.
Sources: https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-pumping-stations-riyadh-airport-as-fighting-intensifies-ce785cdfd08df327 ; https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Stocks%2C%2Bbonds%2Bedge%2Bup%2Bahead%2Bof%2Bformidable%2Bweek%2Bfor%2Brisk/27229470.html (October26 event datelines).

**Texas manufacturing: continued expansion, slower production growth.** Dallas Fed's October26 release shows general business activity 13.4 versus September 9.8, while production falls from 29.5 to 15.8. Its text says price pressure edged higher and wage pressure eased. These are survey diffusion indices, not output growth percentages. Interpretation: a mixed growth/inflation read, counterevidence to an already generalized industrial contraction; one regional survey does not establish the national cycle. ISHM retains grading responsibility.
Source: https://www.dallasfed.org/research/surveys/tmos/2026/2610 (primary release).

**EQT/Range: consolidation aimed at gas demand.** The issuer announced an all-stock acquisition, 0.987 EQT shares per Range share, with combined pro forma enterprise value above $50 billion. That is the combined company's value, not Range's purchase price. Expected Q1 2027 closing remains subject to approvals. Interpretation: relevant to the gas/power/AI investment chain, but a merger does not itself establish new gas supply or realized demand. Its October28 earnings calls are canceled; results still expected after close October27 per the issuer.
Source: https://ir.eqt.com/newsroom/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/EQT-Announces-Strategic-Acquisition-of-Range-Resources-Creating-Americas-Undisputed-Natural-Gas-Champion/default.aspx (October26 primary).

**Verizon: operating resilience and an AI infrastructure contract.** Issuer release reports 406,000 postpaid phone net additions, adjusted EPS $1.31, raised annual guidance for the third consecutive quarter and an AWS agreement valued above $2 billion. Interpretation: useful counterevidence to treating telecom weakness as universal; this does not resolve distressed cable issuers' separate refinancing problems. The AWS agreement is contracted business, not already recognized revenue or proof of sector-wide AI returns.
Source: https://www.verizon.com/about/news/verizon-delivers-another-record-breaking-quarter (October26 primary).
