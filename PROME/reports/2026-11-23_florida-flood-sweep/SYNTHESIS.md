## Where the four desks agree

1. The loss is mostly uninsured and falls first on owners outside mapped flood zones.
2. A federal declaration with Individual Assistance is the switch for borrower relief, for exposure data and for the desks' odds.
3. The 11/24 to 11/26 rain on the southeast coast is the event that could turn a contained river flood into a large one.
4. No desk supports an investment implication on 11/23 evidence; each sent TERRY a WQ-696 handoff saying so.
