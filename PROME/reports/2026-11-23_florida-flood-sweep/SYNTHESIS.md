## Corrections (2026-11-23 17:22 ET, PROME, after CATO's review `AGENTS/CATO/runs/2026-11-23_1631_florida-flood-sweep-review.md`; PROME's own checks are in `FOLLOWUP_2026-11-23.md` beside this file)

The rows below are corrected in place; the text as first written is commit 2473773603.

1. **Insurance.** "The loss is mostly uninsured" was stated as a finding and as four-desk agreement. It is a HYPOTHESIS. The observed figures stand (countywide NFIP residential take-up; 1,157 early claims), but take-up across all homes in a county is not coverage among the flooded homes and not a share of loss dollars, and private flood cover is unmeasured.
2. **Housing finance.** "Fannie Mae's disaster forbearance is OFF" was too broad. Fannie's Servicing Guide D2-3.2-01 (04/08/2026) ties the FEMA Individual Assistance condition to one concession, an initial forbearance of up to three months without reaching the borrower; contact-based forbearance and the damage-assessment and workout duties (D1-3-01) do not depend on a declaration. FHA's moratorium is HOMER's reading and was not re-checked.
3. **Banks.** Seacoast is the most concentrated listed bank in the flooded basin by share of its deposits, not the only one present (SouthState and Truist have branches there). USCB's association loan book is UNSIZED; 66.252M is a table category and 0.33% a bank-wide ratio (neither figure was in this file).
4. **"A declaration is the switch for exposure data"** overstated: CREED itself names the SEC loan tapes as a deferred free route.
5. **Home values.** The outside-the-map figure (no discount to about minus 5%) is one meta-analysis (Beltrán and others 2018), and the mapped-zone fade is three of four tracked cases; "11 studies" is HOMER's whole literature check. These are analogues, not an observed November price change.
6. **Watch times CONFIRMED, not changed.** The review read the times as a wrong conversion from UTC. The NWS alert stamps (PROME pull 17:17 ET) carry a -05:00 offset: 19:00 ET Tue 11/24 to 01:00 ET Thu 11/26, and 01:00 ET Wed 11/25 for the Brevard to Martin watch. CORAL's file labels those local times "Z"; that label is the defect.

## Where the four desks agree

1. HYPOTHESIS, not established (correction 1): a large part of the loss is uninsured and falls first on owners outside mapped flood zones.
2. A federal declaration with Individual Assistance switches on FHA's moratorium and Fannie's no-contact forbearance, is what Trepp's exposure screen waits for, and moves each desk's odds (corrections 2 and 4).
3. The 11/24 to 11/26 rain on the southeast coast is the event that could turn a contained river flood into a large one.
4. No desk supports an investment implication on 11/23 evidence; each sent TERRY a WQ-696 handoff saying so.
