# Feedback on the prediction-rule draft — September 14, 2026

**Assessment at the initial review:** retain the four approved principles, but revise the encoding draft before inserting it verbatim. This report records PROME's feedback before Will authorized the draft revision; it is not the prescribed blind plan read. The initial review changed neither the draft nor the live canon. Subsequent draft edits and review receipts belong in the ruling record cited below.

Draft: `PROME/proposals/2026-09-10_wq161-prediction-canon-RULED.md`, bullets at lines 10–13.

## 1. Restore the actual amendment check — highest priority

**VERIFIED:** the fourth bullet says that after an amendment the falsifier is graded pass/fail for discrimination. It does not name the encoder, require the construction checks to run against the final amended text, or put the evidence before use. The originating NEXUS proposal does all three: `AGENTS/NEXUS/proposals/2026-09-02_self-audit-improvement-slate.md` §3, especially its SELF/WILL legs. The original incident distinguished “graded cleanly” from “discriminated”; successful grading did not prove a useful test.

Suggested core wording: **Before encoding an amendment to a frozen specification, the encoder reruns its applicable construction checks against the final amended text and records the results. Cover every affected branch, including reachability and discrimination; approval of the amendment does not carry forward the original certificate.** Refer to the existing check set rather than inventing a universal new checklist. A failed check must not become a valid instrument merely because the row is re-registered.

## 2. Scope the mass-moving prohibition to specification repairs

**VERIFIED:** draft bullet 2 broadly prohibits a mass-moving patch on any existing row. Existing `FORGE/PREDICTION_DISCIPLINE.md` §Grading & re-marking explicitly permits dated pre-resolution confidence re-marks under WQ-112 and preserves original confidence separately.

**INFERRED risk:** an encoder could read the new sentence as banning those legitimate updates. Suggested clarification: **This restriction governs retrospective repairs to outcome/status definitions; it does not repeal separately authorized, dated confidence re-marking on an unchanged forecast question.** Preserve the old mark and the existing scoring-vintage rule.

## 3. Make the neutrality proof about outcomes as well as probabilities

**VERIFIED:** bullet 3 requires the mass-bearing branches before/after and an unchanged scored set, but does not explicitly require unchanged branch definitions. ZHAO's own source explanation is stronger: no probability, threshold or outcome changes in any world where the data publishes (`AGENTS/ZHAO/workbook/PREDICTIONS.tsv`, ZHA-11/12).

**Counterexample:** a 70% YES / 30% NO distribution can retain those numbers and labels while moving YES's threshold. It can then score differently on the same observation. Suggested clarification: the proof records unchanged probabilities **and** branch-to-outcome mapping and grading conditions for every previously resolvable case. A probability total of 1.00 is insufficient evidence of neutrality.

## 4. Disposition the renormalization example explicitly

**VERIFIED:** bullet 1 explains that removing MIDAS-08's 0.02 non-resolution branch would renormalize the other branches from 0.98 to 1.00; bullet 2 prohibits mass-moving retrofits. The intended distinction is recoverable but the example does not finish with its disposition.

Suggested sentence: **That renormalization is not permitted as a mass-neutral repair to the already registered row; a corrected successor must follow the existing amendment/registration rules and preserve the original record.** This prevents “bring an old row into compliance” from becoming permission to rewrite its scored probabilities.

## Smaller corrections

- The draft's I1 invariant says every bullet names its raiser and record and no bullet restates a figure. The proposed bullets do not satisfy that literally. Fix the invariant or provenance fields; do not waive a check after insertion because the draft itself violates it.
- September 16 is the registered resolution date in ZHA-11/12, while their explicit non-publication/STUCK deadline is September 30. Keep those dates distinct in the consumer brief; September 16 is not an automatic STUCK instruction. This review did not validate Treasury's external release schedule or grade either prediction.
- Keep the separately proposed SL-5 clause outside this four-bullet encoding unless its authority is established separately.

Revise the wording within the existing ruling record, preserving the approved principles and other live rules. Then use the already prescribed plan read, one canon insertion and result read. No new tool or wider process redesign is needed for these drafting corrections.
