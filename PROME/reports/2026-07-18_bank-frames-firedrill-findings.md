## C. Cross-frame themes (for the Tuesday runbook + future frame-building)
1. **Two-stage grading is structural, not incidental** — both names have call-only discriminators (WAL Wed noon, OZK Wed 8:30a + OZK-08's F8). Tuesday night grades what the release resolves; everything else logs PENDING-CALL, never guessed. (Runbook §5 carries this.)
2. **Basis freezing beats basis naming** — "supplement basis" is not enough when the supplement itself offers multiple summable line-sets (Z2) or the anchored basis doesn't print until the 10-Q (W1). The fix is the same both times: freeze the exact line-set by *reproducing the Q1 anchor* before the print.
3. **Threshold edges need half-open convention fleet-wide** (W7, Z4, Z6) — candidate auto-memory after Tuesday validates the pattern.
4. **Realization deflates the stock-variable** (W4) — charge-offs shrink classified exactly when the thesis confirms; any classified-direction ladder needs a plus-realized basis. Likely generalizes to every CRE-migration frame in the fleet.
