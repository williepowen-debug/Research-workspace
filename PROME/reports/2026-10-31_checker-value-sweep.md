## Short answer (the reviewer's)

There are 61 automated checks: 47 lines in PROME's boot/closeout gate, the boot wrapper, 5 git hooks plus the push script, 6 Claude Code hooks, and 2 extra steps in the desks' closeout list. About half earn their keep.
