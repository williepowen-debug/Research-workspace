## What the market saw that the headline hid
- **Effect size shrank vs Phase 2b.** Leaked HR ~0.58 (42%) vs 0.51 (49%) at 5 yr and 0.56 at primary. Not a failure, but a regression toward the mean in a 1,137-pt trial vs 157-pt Ph2b, and the leaked number is no better than the original noisy estimate.
- **Priced for more.** MRNA +526% YTD; Aug 19 topline added ~$45B in a day. Citi had downgraded on valuation. Avg PT $118 vs ~$185 (TipRanks, 20 analysts).
- **Interim analysis**, no OS yet. Durability and OS are unknown.
- **Subgroup underperformance: CANNOT ANSWER.** No source reports any subgroup. Honest unknown. Watch at ESMO: stage IIB/C (low event rates), PD-L1-negative, BRAF status, CI width crossing 1 in subgroups.
- Unknown whether leaked 42% is RFS HR 0.58 or a different cut; only one summary gives 0.58, the Yahoo/Bloomberg-linked report gives 42% only.
