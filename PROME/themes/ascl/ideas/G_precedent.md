## Retracer vs continuer features
General mechanism, not derived from a verified sample (hypotheses):
- Likely RETRACERS: move driven by a non-fundamental trigger (leak, early look, positioning, index/short-covering), data clean and unchanged, no safety or subgroup caveat, a near-term hard catalyst still ahead (filing, approval).
- Likely CONTINUERS: a flaw surfacing in the detail (safety imbalance, weak secondary or OS endpoint, subgroup wart), a bar set above the result, or a financing or guidance miss alongside, so the print resets the thesis rather than just the price.
