## Verdict on bear case: **MODERATE**

The clinical readout is a hit, not a miss, by the Citi rubric — so the pure drug-value bear case is WEAK. But the stock was priced for platform validation and a "clear win" HR, and got a within-range HR with a Ph2b-to-Ph3 regression that leaves open the SWOG-equivalence question. Combined with 22/24 hold-or-worse coverage and a $55 consensus PT, a 25% unwind is defensible positioning math on an unchanged fundamental story — not a vindication of a bear thesis.
