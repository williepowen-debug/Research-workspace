Priming (putting new secured debt ahead of existing unsecured holders) widens legacy unsecured CDS mechanically, whoever the equity holders are. INFERRED, but this is the textbook mechanism.
- Supply: the new 9.125% 2036 notes were **issued at 100 on 9/30 and quoted ~91 on 10/13**.
