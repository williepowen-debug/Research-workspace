## 1. Plain-language description for Will

Five groups are propping each other up, and most of the support runs through two names: OpenAI and Larry Ellison. OpenAI has promised to buy about $300 billion of computing from Oracle (reported, 2025) and about $22 billion from CoreWeave (filed by CoreWeave). To pay for that, OpenAI raised $122 billion in March 2026 from Amazon, Nvidia and SoftBank, of which Nvidia put in $30 billion - and Nvidia is also the company that sells OpenAI's suppliers the chips. Oracle and CoreWeave borrow heavily to build the capacity (Oracle about $124 billion of debt plus roughly $48 billion of project loans from JPMorgan, MUFG and others; CoreWeave notes at 9.6-9.75%). SoftBank borrowed to fund its OpenAI share and lenders pushed back on using OpenAI stock as collateral. Separately, Larry Ellison personally guaranteed more than $40 billion of equity for his son's Paramount purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery (closed about October 6, 2026 per secondary reports), and his ability to stand behind that rests largely on Oracle stock that is pledged for loans - the same stock whose value depends on OpenAI paying Oracle. Elon Musk is tied in by an old $1 billion Ellison investment in X (2022), by xAI having rented chips from Oracle, by Nvidia funding xAI, and by a September 2026 report that Paramount has discussed asking Musk to invest (nothing agreed, no amount). **Caveat that could change a decision: the Musk-to-Paramount money is a discussion, not a deal; and most OpenAI-Oracle numbers are press-reported, not filed.** The web has several dependency loops but few that are contractually closed circles; the largest one is a *value* loop (OpenAI revenue -> Oracle stock -> Ellison collateral -> Paramount equity) rather than cash going round.
