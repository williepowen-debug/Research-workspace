## Lead: what the 10-Q adds to the 11/9 letter

The 11/9 letter gave targets and no balances. The 10-Q gives five things the letter did not:

1. **The debt stack, by instrument (FILED, Note 8).** $16.29B face at 9/30, plus $72.1B issued or assumed at the 10/6 close. That puts post-close face debt near **$86.6B** (INFERRED: 16.29 − 1.75 repaid after 9/30 + 72.1). Expected annual interest is **about $6B** (FILED, MD&A Liquidity). Covenants are **5.50x total leverage and 3.25x first-lien net leverage**.
2. **Who paid for the equity (FILED, Notes 2, 6 and 11).** The $47.01B PIPE (3,918M shares at $12.00) was **syndicated**. The Ellison Family put in **$14.85B for 1.24B shares**, not the up-to-$46.7B the Trust had committed. RedBird put in $5.2B. The named outside buyers are **PIF, L'Imad (Abu Dhabi), QIA and LionTree**. The Ellisons hold **35.0%** of the combined equity after close.
3. **No Musk entity is named anywhere.** The list of buyers is non-exhaustive ("include"), so this does not refute the reported stake.
4. **No Ellison pledge, margin loan, guarantee or backstop is disclosed.** The 10/6 8-K's lock-up on PIPE shares **exempts "pledging transactions"**, so PIPE shares can be pledged inside the 180-day lock-up.
5. **The $49B bridge was never drawn and was terminated at close.** Its fees of $384M hit Q4 interest expense.

## Bearing on the thesis and falsifiers (INFERRED, for PROME to weigh)

- **Claim 4 (personal guarantee)**: the guaranteed equity was funded at close and syndicated. The Ellison cash in is **$14.85B, not ~$46.7B**: about $32B of the commitment went to sovereign and institutional buyers. That shrinks the Ellison-specific exposure to Skydance relative to the thesis's framing. Whether the 3/2 guarantee is extinguished is unread.
- **Claim 6 (Musk)**: unchanged. The issuer is silent and structurally may stay silent. The resale prospectus (about 12/22) is the next primary test. The Musk falsifier ("filed terms show a market or below-market price") cannot be graded yet. Every disclosed PIPE share went in at $12.00, the collar floor, so any Musk stake bought in the PIPE was at the same $12.00.
- **Confirmer "Skydance stock added as collateral"**: the filings permit it (the lock-up exempts pledges) but disclose none.
- **Transmission channel**: Skydance has about $86.6B of total face debt (inferred in §1, before the receivables securitization payoff), with a 5.50x/3.25x covenant set and about $6B of yearly interest. A measurable channel exists on the Skydance side; this read does not test whether Oracle stress transmits into it.
