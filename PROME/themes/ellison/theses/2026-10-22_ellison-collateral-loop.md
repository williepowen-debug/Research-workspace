## Falsifiers (any one weakens the loop; two or more and the thesis is DOWNGRADED)

- Musk's filed terms show a market or below-market price, or a strategic contract attached (Starlink/xAI) → not support capital.
- Oracle's December print tightens Oracle CDS materially (say below 250 bp) on positive FCF guidance or an OpenAI prepayment → the hub heals.
- An Ellison Form 4 / proxy showing the pledge count FALLING, or a disclosed loan size implying LTV well under 50% → the collateral is not stretched.
- Legacy Paramount CDS shows no reaction to Oracle-specific news beyond HY (H's regression) → no transmission.
- OpenAI closes a large raise or discloses revenue covering the Oracle contract → the common payer is funded.
