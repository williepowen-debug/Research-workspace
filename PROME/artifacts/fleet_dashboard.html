"Gated" vs. a heat color — two different things

A gate is a specific, pre-registered tripwire: a measured threshold + a fixed action + a state. Example — GATE-RESHAPE-BC: condition "HY OAS >280 sustained OR WAL prints 7/21 AMC" → action "the bank-put reshape proposal comes to you." It's written down before the fact so it can't be rationalized after.

To call a risk "gated" means we've wired that plumbing — a defined trigger and a defined response — so a live event produces a clean proposal at your desk instead of a scramble. (Kharg was an open hole on Fri 7/18; gated by Sat — same risk, now with a tripwire and a fire path.)

A heat color (green/yellow/orange/red) answers a different question: how hot is this right now. A risk can be red-hot with no gate, or calm-yellow with a gate armed underneath it. Gates watch; colors describe. And a gate firing never executes a trade — it hands you a proposal.
