How to read this page in 20 seconds

Pending Will = the action is on you. Needs attention = the action is on the fleet (worst first). Fire ledger = the tripwires armed on your behalf — every one ends at your desk if it trips. Catalyst runway = what the calendar forces. Fleet grid = who's healthy, who's drifting. Everything else is context.

"Gated" vs. a heat color — two different things

A gate is a specific, pre-registered tripwire: a measured threshold + a fixed action + a state. Example — GATE-RESHAPE-BC: condition "HY OAS >280 sustained OR WAL prints 7/21 AMC" → action "the bank-put reshape proposal comes to you." It's written down before the fact so it can't be rationalized after.

To call a risk "gated" means we've wired that plumbing — a defined trigger and a defined response — so a live event produces a clean proposal at your desk instead of a scramble. (Kharg was an open hole on Fri 7/18; gated by Sat — same risk, now with a tripwire and a fire path.)

A heat color (green/yellow/orange/red) answers a different question: how hot is this right now. A risk can be red-hot with no gate, or calm-yellow with a gate armed underneath it. Gates watch; colors describe. And a gate firing never executes a trade — it hands you a proposal.

owner: PROME/GATES.tsv (the fire-ledger canon)

The fire ledger & gate states

A central register of every pre-registered "if X happens, someone must DO something" rule — not predictions that merely get graded, only rules that owe an action (build a packet, arm a trade card, bring you a proposal). PROME scans it at every boot.

Born 2026-07-09 from a real failure: two VIOLET triggers fired on 7/2 into a frozen session and the owed hedge-packet sat orphaned for seven days before an audit found it. The ledger makes that structurally impossible — the watching is centralized even though the trigger logic stays with the domain agents.

LIVEarmed tripwire — condition watched, not yet met
FIRED-UNEXECUTEDcondition came TRUE and the owed action hasn't happened — blocks all new PROME work until cleared or escalated to you. Should never survive a session.
RESOLVEDcondition tested, verdict landed, consequence executed (or explicitly not owed)
LAPSEDfired but the action window passed — your call, recorded, not retro-built

A card's lifecycle: ARMED → FIRED → FILLED

Three distinct stages — the words are not interchangeable, and only the last one moves capital.

ARMEDsetup conditions met + the trade fully built (strikes, ladder, $500 cap) — loaded, safety on. Never means "traded."
FIREDthe trigger condition crossed — the card now owes you a proposal (TERRY live-re-marks the strikes against the current book)
FILLEDyou gave [Approve] against a live broker book and it executed. You are the trigger, not the tape — nothing reaches here on its own.

Worked example (July 2026, historical): TRY-FIRE-004's arm fired 7/13 → card ARMED. Will first ruled NO-ADD (7/16 — the book already owned the grind), then approved a $330 re-fire on a clean red-day entry (7/20). Armed ≠ auto-fill in either direction — the fill returns as a TERRY proposal, and the current card state lives in GATES/FORGE, never in this example.

Break / Grind / Unresolved

NEXUS's regime probability split over the next 2–6 weeks — the one numeric regime call in the system, deliberately owned by exactly one agent (PROME runs no competing split, so you always know whose judgment you're reading).

Break — the accumulated stress (rates-vol, credit fuel, record equity complacency) cracks into an actual market repricing. Grind — the calm holds and the stress dissipates or stays latent. Unresolved — the honest bucket: the window ends and the tape still hasn't told us. A high Unresolved number means the decisive tests are still ahead — watch the catalyst runway, not the noise.
