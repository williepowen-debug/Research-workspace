The hinge

June CPI — Tue 14 Jul. Everything upstream is prologue; everything downstream reads off it.

The first inflation print with June's Brent-$71 energy collapse in it — and it lands the same morning as the big-bank earnings kickoff, on a razor-thin gamma cushion.

▶ PROMOTION TRIGGER — a hot core CPI promotes this whole node from background to active bandwidth. A soft print and it likely passes benign, like the X1 gate did.

The two-outcome fork

FIREHot CPI → duration cracks (10Y → 4.8, gamma flips negative) → the 21st & 28th credit prints show substance → transmission finally arrives, via the consumer/regional path — not the HY/wrapper path X1 was watching.
PASSSoft CPI → the energy washout shows, duration catches a bid, banks fine, marks maskable on shrinking bases → node passes; the credit-bear stays armed but unfired.

Convergence discipline — the credit-recognition prints (banks 14th → OZK/ALLY/SYF/COF 21st → BDC/ARCC/First-Brands 28th) are one correlated axis with three at-bats, not a dozen independent votes. And the overlays are fewer than they look: the rates/long-end demand-hole is ONE term-premium root — China/Korea flows, the JGB gauntlet, and the US-30Y are correlated expressions, not separate votes (7/6 x-domain synthesis graded the "4-way convergence" a pre-armed watch, not a thread). So the genuinely independent axes are just four: the rates demand-hole, the credit-recognition cluster, the labor freeze, and the AI-capex correction. Both a rates break and a credit break = real two-root convergence; one alone = size down. (7/6: the AI-credit canary NOT-FIRED — it reversed — so the credit axis has de-escalated at the front.)
