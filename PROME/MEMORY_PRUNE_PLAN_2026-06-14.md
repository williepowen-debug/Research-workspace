```md

```

Length budget:
- Normal entry: 1–2 lines.
- Critical safety/process entry: up to 3 lines.
- No stale `Next:` items.
- No stale old event dates unless historically structural.
- No exact stat clusters unless thesis-defining and not easily sourced elsewhere.
