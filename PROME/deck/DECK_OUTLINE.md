## STRUCTURAL NOTES

**Total slides:** ~27-30 (6 content sections × 4-6 slides + title + trade section)

**Pacing:** Sections 1-2 are emotional/narrative (the "oh shit"). Sections 3-4 are factual/mechanical (the "why"). Section 5 is intellectual (the "when"). Section 6 is structural (the "how it connects"). Section 7 is actionable (the "so what").

**Key design decisions:**
- LIQUID's rescue mechanism table (4.4) should be a full slide with the 7-row table. Most important single slide in the deck.
- BRENT's "Why can't they just fix it?" (3.5) is the second most important standalone slide. Preempts every Q&A objection.
- The full loop diagram (6.3) needs a visual — a circle showing the transmission chain. Words alone won't land it.
- Every section should open with one of the 7 final-cut slide sentences as the headline.

**What got cut (and why):**
- Florida migration (#9 in Batch 3) — vivid but narrow; doesn't carry a section
- Canadian tourism — same
- Belgium/Euroclear — fascinating but takes too long to explain in a slide
- Bear steepening — too technical for this audience
- LGFV recurring crunch — supporting, not driving
- Data deletion — great closer for MARCO section but not essential for the arc
- VIX coiled spring — positioning detail, moves to trade section if needed

- Border city fiscal stress → cut (too granular)
- OZK specifics → compressed into Sec 2.2 (insider behavior)
