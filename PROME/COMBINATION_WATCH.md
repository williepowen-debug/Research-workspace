# Combination watch: how a desk sets it up

**Will, 2026-11-05 15:22 ET, verbatim:** *"Even if any singular piece of news doesnt move the needle by itself - we do want to account for and document things that might matter eventually, or in combination, etc."* **15:43 ET:** *"Lets make sure this is something that is updated as new data is encountered"*. **16:11 ET (WQ-622 A):** *"622 A, but we need to give good guidance to the agents on how to set this up properly for themselves"*.

A threshold answers whether one item acts now. It says nothing about what several small items add up to, and an item dropped today cannot be added up next month. Too small to act on is not too small to record.

## Set it up once, at your next session

1. **Pick your own themes.** Choose 3 to 9 places in your domain where small items could add up to something a single print would not show. Name each theme as a question you could answer. Good: LABOR's `health-systems`, CREED's `outside-cohort-lenders`. Bad: a topic with no way to matter, such as `macro` or `general-weakness`.
2. **Use the ledger you have.** A dated row in your existing `KB.tsv`, or your desk's equivalent, with `combo:<theme>` first in Notes. No new file, schema, script or checker.
3. **Add one line to your own charter**, in the step where you read cards, sweeps or prints. This moves no authority, route or threshold, so it is class C4; PROME verifies. LABOR's line is at `AGENTS/LABOR/CLAUDE.md` and CREED's is step 6a of `AGENTS/CREED/CLAUDE.md`.
4. **Tell PROME your theme list** in your next delivery packet, one line each.

## What each theme row must carry

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Members | Each item, dated, with its source and read state: primary, secondary, or headline only |
| Counterevidence | What points the other way. Required, or the row is a pile of confirmation |
| What would make it matter | The specific next fact that would turn the theme into something: a print, filing, count or date. If you cannot name one, it is not a theme |
| Review date | When you will next look, in your ledger's existing column |

## Keep it running

Update the theme in the session the item or print arrives, counterevidence included. A theme only moves when a session runs, so never imply continuous coverage. Drop a theme when its question is answered, or when it has gone a full review with nothing new, by marking the row; never delete it.

## What protects the record

- **Logging is not scoring.** A tag moves no score, vector, band or trigger, and counts toward no gate.
- **Never add up unlike things.** Announced, voluntary, global and realized items stay apart; a theme is a list, not a total.
- **Count a shared root once.** Five headlines about one event are one member.
- **Do not mistake volume for signal.** A long theme is not a signal; only the fact you named under "what would make it matter" is.
- **Routing stays as it is.** Signals go through WALTER. A theme crossing another desk's domain goes by packet to that owner, who keeps the figure; overlapping themes reconcile to one figure.

## When a theme starts to matter

Use the normal paths. Register a prediction under the [prediction brief](reports/2026-11-05_prediction-registration-brief.md), send TERRY the read, or propose a threshold to Will. A theme never promotes itself into a score, gate or trade.
