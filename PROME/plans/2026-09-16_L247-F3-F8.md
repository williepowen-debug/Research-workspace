## Proposed text — new §1c

**1c. Source masses and normalization (F3).** Each metadata entry must carry `source_masses`, a branch-keyed object of the pinned letter's mass literals transcribed as finite base-10 Decimal strings, and `normalization`, an object with exactly `method` and `source_sum`. `source_sum` is the exact sum of `source_masses`; it is not assumed to equal one. Branch keys must equal the unique `outcome_map` branch set. Each source mass is finite and in [0,1]. `mapping_note` preserves approximation marks and the source's stated precision; the reviewer checks the literals against the native pin at ruling. These fields do not make mass authenticity machine-verifiable.

Two methods are registrable. `NONE` requires `source_sum == 1` and every outcome-map mass equal its source mass. `PROPORTIONAL_NONYES_EXACT` preserves the accepted scalar: the source masses mapped to YES must sum exactly to the accepted forecast probability p, and their output masses are unchanged. Let R be the sum of source masses mapped to other labels. If R > 0, each non-YES output is its source mass times (1−p)/R. If R == 0, the method is valid only when p == 1 and all non-YES outputs are zero. All arithmetic defining these relations is exact rational arithmetic over the decimal literals, independent of a Decimal context's precision. Each resulting rational must have a finite decimal expansion; otherwise the declaration is not registrable under this version. No automatic rounding, clipping, mass invention or scalar change is allowed. A nonrepresentable letter stays excluded pending a separately reviewed method; no fallback to the binary score.

MIDAS-06 carries `source_masses: {"a":"0.45","b":"0.20","c":"0.15","d":"0.20"}` and `normalization: {"method":"NONE","source_sum":"1.00"}`. Its vector and score remain unchanged. Normalization handles representable rounding discrepancies, not every approximate letter.
