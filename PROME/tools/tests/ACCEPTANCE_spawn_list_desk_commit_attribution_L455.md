## The defect, in its own terms
Attribution keys on a SUBJECT PREFIX with a two-form anchor. The fleet's subject forms are many, so the reader under-attributes (false DARK ⇒ a spurious spawn of a desk that graded the row hours ago; the preflight catches only a LIVE session, never a closed-out one). Loosening the anchor to a word boundary alone OVER-attributes in the other direction (measured 2026-09-25 over 3,866 commits since 9/1: 386 newly matched, of which 5 are another desk's commits — PROME's `BOND … packet -> processed` moves, PROME's `YURI registered on the shared routing surfaces`, MIDAS's `ZHAO refuted my …`) ⇒ false ACTIVE ⇒ a due row silently NOT spawned. The second direction is the worse one: it leaves no absence to detect.

⚠️ Not a restatement of the symptom: the defect is **authorship inferred from prose, where the fleet's git protocol makes authorship a property of PATH** (root `CLAUDE.md`: "`git add` ONLY files inside your own directory" + carve-outs ①–④).

## Acceptance conditions — the repair must hold ALL of these
1. **(ORDINARY)** a desk's own commit resolves regardless of what follows the name: `DESK:`, `DESK ->`, `DESK <date>`, `DESK closeout …`, `DESK (orch):`, `DESK memory:`, `DESK S46:` — when it touches `AGENTS/DESK/`.
2. **(OVERLAP — the neighbour most likely broken BY the fix)** `WALTER: …` is NEVER attributed to desk `WAL`; `WAL closeout …` touching `AGENTS/WAL/` IS attributed to `WAL`. Name-prefix collisions resolve at a word boundary AND at the path.
3. **(WRONG OWNER)** a subject that starts with, or merely mentions, a desk name but whose paths are another owner's — a PROME consume-move into `PROME/inbox/processed/`, a root/shared-doc edit (`AGENTS.md`, `AGENTS/_*.md`, `HEARTBEAT.md`, `FORGE/`), another desk's home (`AGENTS/OTHER/…` outside its `inbox/`) — is NOT attributed. The `--grep` body false-match (a PROME closeout body line `MIDAS: …`) stays un-attributed.
4. **(CARVE-OUTS ①③ + grants)** a desk commit that touches ONLY paths a desk may legitimately own outside its home — a top-level packet in another desk's `inbox/` (not `processed/`), `memory/auto/`, or a path under no desk's home (`scripts/`, DAEDALUS's grant) — IS attributed by subject. An `--allow-empty` marker commit (no paths) IS attributed by subject (documented choice: nothing on the path side refutes it).
5. **(MISSING INFORMATION)** the fail-closed git-error path survives: a failed `git log` still returns `!ERR`, never an empty result that reads as "no commits" (the 2026-09-12 acceptance file's conditions 1–4 stay green).
6. **(CONCURRENT ACTIVITY — justified N/A)** `last_self_commit` reads committed history and the caller re-runs per boot; a commit landing mid-read changes the answer without making it wrong. No test.
7. **(POPULATION)** validated against the measured 3,866-commit / 45-directory set since 2026-09-01 — the 5 wrong-owner instances found by the word-boundary-only measurement all resolve NOT ATTRIBUTED, the 381 others stay attributed, and no commit the old pattern attributed is lost (old-only = 0).
8. **(SIBLING)** `desk_activity.py:last_commit` uses the SAME helper, so the two readers cannot disagree on the same commit.
9. **(DIRECTION ON AMBIGUITY)** when the path evidence is ambiguous the reader fails toward DARK, never toward ACTIVE — a spurious spawn is visible (preflight + "ACTIVE ⇒ read the artifact first"); a missed spawn is not.
