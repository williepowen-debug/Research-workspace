## The defect, in its own terms (BRENT 9/23 20:47 ET, at the vendor)

After the CME Globex evening open (~18:00 ET) the vendor's daily series for a futures contract shows a bar LABELLED WITH TODAY'S DATE that holds the NEXT trade date's evening session, and the completed session's bar (the settle) is gone from the daily series until the vendor re-labels it later. `fetch.py` stamps that bar `asof = today`, the dashboard renders it `[today]` with no stale mark, and `change_pct` is computed against `regularMarketPreviousClose`, which at that moment is the settle BEFORE the overwritten one. Result on 9/23 (BRENT's packet L20, the source): PROME's 20:40 ET read of **$102.38** and `fetch.py`'s **+2.88%** measured the 9/24 trade-date evening session against the 9/22 settle — two sessions in one day-change — and the dashboard printed a 🟡→🔴 zone change on it. ⚠️ DOCKET L462's text carries `+3.13` (a dollar change, PROME's own dashboard read, implying a 99.25 denominator; BRENT's +2.88% implies ~99.51); the two are unreconciled and NEITHER is used by the fixtures, which carry their own numbers.

## The conditions — properties, not symptom restatements

**A — a futures bar labelled with a date whose last trade is at/after the day-session close is labelled as the NEXT session's evening bar, never as `[today]`.** `price_fetch` emits `session` ∈ {`day`, `evening-next-session`, `non-session-date`, `unverified`} on every FUTURES result (`is_future` True), with `trade_date` (the next weekday not in `US_MARKET_HOLIDAYS`; basis stated — the table is NYSE's, CME holidays may differ) and `session_basis` (the rule and the timestamps it was decided from). The renderers (`display_prices`, `cmd_prices`, `dashboard.py`) print an evening bar as `<asof> ⚠eve→<trade_date>` / `[M/D eve→M/D]` and never as a plain `[today]`.

**B — the day-change is settle-referenced or WITHHELD.** For `evening-next-session`, `non-session-date` and `unverified`, `change_pct` (and the dashboard `change`) is `None` with `change_basis` naming why (*"withheld: evening bar — regularMarketPreviousClose is the settle BEFORE the overwritten session"*). For `day`, `change_basis` names the denominator (`regularMarketPreviousClose`, `prev_asof`). The PRICE is never nulled (L409's fail-safe direction: a value survives a failed stamp; only the change is withheld because the change IS the defect).

**C — equity / index / FX rows are unchanged**, byte-for-byte in output shape: no `session` key, same `change_pct`, same stamps. An equity's after-hours `regularMarketTime` (`TLT` at 20:47 ET) triggers nothing.

**D — the L409 marks still pass:** `test_dashboard_marks_L409.py` (fill-forward ⚠Δ≈0, ⚠stale, two-leg spread dating) and `test_fetch_contract_repairs.py` (contract identity, 22 tests) run green before and after; the new `session` fields are ADDITIVE and the rendered table stays a parser contract (PAT-069 — the As-of stamp text changes only for the evening case, which previously rendered a wrong plain date).

**E — a pre-fix cache entry (no `session` key on a FUTURES row) is marked LOUD, never rendered as a verified day bar and never given a false evening mark.** The renderers print `⚠pre-L462 cache, re-pull --no-cache` on such a row (the `contract_basis` absent-key convention: a notice, not silence); its price and change print as cached because the tool cannot re-derive them, and the price cache TTL is 120 s (`CACHE_TTL_STANDARD`), so the exposure window after deploy is at most two minutes. Equity rows without the key are untouched (C).

**F — the detector's own failure is loud, not silent.** `regularMarketTime` missing / unparseable / a timezone the code cannot resolve ⇒ `session = "unverified"` with the reason, change withheld (B), price kept. Never a silent default to `day`.
