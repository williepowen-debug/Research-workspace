## What "the owed set" is (so the comparison covers the explanatory text)
Every row `decision_deck.parse_open` returns from § OPEN — open cards AND answered/hands-owed cards (the page renders both) — as its raw
cells (n · item · kind · by_raw · since · rec · notes), PLUS for each such n the whole `WQ_EXPLAINERS.tsv` row (every column, header-driven).
NOT in the set: the build stamp, git sha, build id, the hosted-reference note, the Docket/Decided/In-flight panels (CATO: generated
timestamps are not a trigger). Per-row fingerprint = sha256 over a canonical serialization of that row + its explainer row; the digest =
sha256 over the sorted `n:fingerprint` pairs — derived from the per-row map, never computed alone, so +/−/~ rows are nameable.
