Everything suppressed is INVISIBLE — no count, no list — so the miss is silent (`finding_instrument_reports_clean_against_the_wrong_reference`, n=36: a work selector that reads prose as a state token).

## Properties (in the defect's terms)
- **A1 token form.** Only the annotation form suppresses: uppercase `COVERED` as a whole word, immediately followed by `:` or `(`. Lowercase *covered*, *recovered*, *uncovered* and `COVERED` without the colon/paren never suppress.
- **A2 negation.** A `COVERED` preceded (within the same clause) by `NOT`/`Not`/`never`, or the word `UNCOVERED`, never suppresses.
- **A3 self-slate — PROME as the SUBJECT of the slate (amended after the independent read, 2026-10-30 09:3x ET).** The clause (the text from `COVERED` to the next ` · ` or `|`) names PROME as the agent that spawns, wakes, slates or dispatches the row, or reads it at its own boot — in any grammatical form (active *PROME spawns/wakes/slated*, auxiliary *PROME will/to wake*, passive *spawned/woken/slated by PROME*, *PROME('s) next boot*, *next PROME boots*), or the row sits on PROME's or Will's slate (*on Will's slate*, *boot slate*, *L0 spawn(ed)*, *WQ-184 wake/driver*, *slated for/at/tonight*). **Why the first letter ("names PROME and any slate word, either order") was wrong:** every annotation stamp says "by PROME" and prose like "calendar spawn suppressed" (L198) carries a slate word, so the literal rule un-hid legitimate coverage on L140 · L198 · L406 · L793 at the first fixture run; the subject rule keeps those hidden. The reader's ❌1 then showed the first SUBJECT regex too narrow (passive and auxiliary forms; real rows L467 · L555 at the 10/27 vintage ef7b600c2, now pinned in the fixture). The 9/11 fixtures (L319 · L323 at ffe54ea19) stay present; L317 today is present.
- **A4 lapsed promise.** A clause carrying a promise date — `sitting-YYYY-MM-DD`, or `by|to|until|through|before|at|on/after <YYYY-MM-DD | M/D>` — whose latest promise date is BEFORE today never suppresses. Dates inside a parenthetical that carries `ET` (an annotation stamp) are not promise dates. **A month/day date takes the year that puts it nearest today (±183 d), so "by 12/31" read on Jan 2 is the past December and "by 1/5" read on Dec 30 is the coming January (reader ❌2).** L440/L445 at today=2026-10-30 are present; at today=2026-10-24 they are suppressed.
