# Intel Packet — Macro Exogenous Shocks Chain

**Scope:** energy/geopolitics, migration/labor supply, Japan/carry, China/capital flows.
**Prepared:** 2026-05-14

## Current claim

The macro-exogenous chain is still a live stress amplifier, but unevenly priced. The strongest confirmed pressure is **physical energy + pump/gasoline pass-through**, with **Brent still red at $103.87 and AAA gas $4.50** in the May 14 dashboard (`HEARTBEAT.md`). BRENT’s latest status argues Phase 1 is **deepening, not unwinding**: Project Freedom is a narrow US-flagged corridor, >1,500 vessels remain queued/trapped, UAE was struck two consecutive days, both Petroline and Fujairah bypass routes have been targeted, and SPR/IEA releases failed to dent the tape (`AGENTS/BRENT/STATUS.md`).

Second layer: **Japan/carry risk is coiled, not yet detonated.** SAM has a hawkish BOJ hold with 3 dissents, 74% June-hike pricing, USD/JPY near intervention/stress territory, and CFTC yen shorts building rather than covering (`AGENTS/SAM/STATUS.md`). That makes carry unwind a credible independent trigger into June, especially if oil re-accelerates.

Third layer: **migration/labor is already transmitting through ag, construction, Florida housing/tourism, and food CPI**, but with longer lags than oil or FX. MARCO’s dashboard has 10 breached indicators, a DHS/ICE funding fast-track, H-2A bottlenecks, permanent ag labor data gaps, FL condo inventory breach, and Canadian travel habit formation (`AGENTS/MARCO/STATUS.md`).

Fourth layer: **China/capital-flow risk is directionally bearish for Treasury demand but stale/partly contradicted by newer auction data.** ZHAO’s structural demand-hole frame remains relevant, but May 12/13 Treasury auctions did not confirm acute demand stress (`AGENTS/ZHAO/STATUS.md`; `HEARTBEAT.md`).

## Confirmed evidence

- **Energy shock remains active:** Brent $103.87 red; gas $4.50 red in May 14 dashboard (`HEARTBEAT.md`). BRENT’s May 5 packet had Brent $116.55, WTI ~$105, gas $4.457, war-risk premium repricing, and a confirmed bypass-pair targeting pattern: Saudi Petroline Apr 9 + Fujairah May 4 (`AGENTS/BRENT/STATUS.md`).
- **Project Freedom is not a full reopening:** only 2 US-flagged vessels transited under escort on May 4; multiple commercial/non-US vessels were hit; CENTCOM reported >1,500 vessels waiting (`AGENTS/BRENT/STATUS.md`).
- **Admin narrative diverges from kinetic reality:** Hegseth/Trump maintained “ceasefire is not over” language while UAE suffered consecutive-day strikes and Brent repriced; BRENT explicitly flags this as War Powers/legal framing rather than energy-market reality (`AGENTS/BRENT/STATUS.md`).
- **Reserve releases are insufficient:** 172M US SPR exchange / 400M IEA coordinated release running since Mar 11; BRENT notes this has failed to bend the tape versus a 9.1M bpd Hormuz outage (`AGENTS/BRENT/STATUS.md`).
- **Japan setup confirmed:** BOJ held 0.75% but delivered 3 hawkish dissents for 1.0%, GDP forecast cut, inflation upgrade, and 74% swap-implied June hike; USD/JPY 157.03 in SAM, 157.89 near red in HEARTBEAT (`AGENTS/SAM/STATUS.md`; `HEARTBEAT.md`).
- **Yen short fuel loaded:** CFTC JPY net short grew to -102,059, highest of cycle / 56.7% of Jul 2024 peak; speculators pressed shorts through the BOJ event (`AGENTS/SAM/STATUS.md`).
- **Migration/labor supply shock is not just narrative:** H-2A certified FY25 corrected to 398,059; applications +9.3% YoY with 4.1% backlog; State Dept interview delays into July hit planting windows; permanent NASS/NAWS ag labor data blind spot (`AGENTS/MARCO/STATUS.md`).
- **Food/labor pass-through visible:** MARCO flags CPI fresh fruit/vegetables +4.0% YoY / +1.0% MoM in March and upgraded planting-season raid → produce spike confidence (`AGENTS/MARCO/STATUS.md`).
- **Florida transmission confirmed:** FL condo inventory breached 9.1 months; Miami domestic migration -2.0%; Canadian US travel down 15 consecutive months with 2-year auto stack -34.9%; Air Transat exiting three Quebec-FL routes (`AGENTS/MARCO/STATUS.md`).
