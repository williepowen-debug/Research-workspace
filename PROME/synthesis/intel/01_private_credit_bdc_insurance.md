# 01 — Private Credit / BDC / Insurance Chain Intel Packet

## Current claim
BDC/private-credit stress has moved from “redemption-gate / valuation-risk thesis” to **confirmed mark, income, and funding stress in the weak public-BDC tail**, with FSK as the cleanest confirmation. The insurance leg is structurally load-bearing but still mostly **transmission risk**, not confirmed break: Apollo/Athene and PE-insurance balance sheets provide permanent capital and potential bad-bank capacity, but public-credit contagion remains unconfirmed while HY OAS and VIX stay benign (`HEARTBEAT.md`; `PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`; `AGENTS/SHADE/STATUS.md`).

## Confirmed evidence
- **FSK Q1 was Strong Bear / near Max Bear.** NAV fell to **$18.83**, down **9.9% QoQ** from $20.89; adjusted NII fell to **$0.41**; Q2 distribution reset to **$0.42**; realized + unrealized loss/share reached **$2.00**; non-accruals rose to **8.1% cost / 4.2% FV**; net debt/equity rose to **1.31x** (`PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/inbox/PROME-20260511-fsk-q1-first-pass-read.md`).
- **FSK funding terms tightened.** May 8 revolver amendment cut commitments to **~$4.052B from $4.700B**, raised margins for extending lenders, and lowered the shareholder-equity floor to **$3.750B from ~$5.049B**. Read: lender support continues, but on worse terms (`PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/inbox/PROME-20260511-fsk-q1-first-pass-read.md`).
- **FSK required sponsor optics/support.** KKR affiliate bought **$150M convertible preferred**, launched **$150M tender at $11**, FSK authorized **$300M repurchases**, and manager waived 50% of subordinated incentive fees for four quarters. Supportive mechanically, but itself evidence of stress after a ~10% NAV hit (`PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`).
- **OBDC was not a collapse print, but it set up the contrast.** BROCK pre-build framed OBDC as a vehicle-level test; later posture classifies it as **Mixed / earnings-quality bear — grind, not cascade** (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/FSK_PREBUILD_MAY11.md`; `PROME/HANDOFF.md`).
- **ARCC Q1 was contained but not clean.** NAV down **1.76% QoQ**, core EPS **$0.47** vs $0.48 dividend, non-accrual cost **2.1%** (+30bps), but FV non-accrual flat and spillover **$988M** gives ~3 quarters cushion (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).
- **Redemption gates are broad and persistent.** BROCK tracks **13+ funds / 12 weeks**; BCRED Q1 gross requests **$3.2B / 7.9% NAV**, Apollo Debt Solutions met only ~45% of asks, Blue Owl OCIC/OTIC gated with severe requests, and Carlyle/Barings broadened the pattern beyond one manager (`AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/PRIVATE_CREDIT_CONTAGION_TRACKER.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_ARCHIVE_APR10.md`).
- **Blue Owl shows fee/lending split.** OWL Q1 AUM/FRE beat drove a manager rally, but direct lending strategy return was **-1.1%** and DL net deployment was **-$0.5B**. That is the core warning: manager earnings can rally while vehicle economics deteriorate (`AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).
- **Bank-PC exposure has been quantified.** Top four US banks disclosed roughly **$128B** PC exposure; WFC software collateral is **~$6.2B**, creating a direct portfolio-company → bank loss bridge (`AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).

## Suggestive evidence
- **Public-BDC weak tail is breaking before broad credit.** BIZD is red while HY OAS sits near **282bps** and VIX near **17.85**, implying localized stress not yet recognized by public spreads/vol (`HEARTBEAT.md`).
- **PIK/non-accrual structure points to cash-flow stress.** FSK entered Q1 from an already stressed base: Q4 PIK **$55M / 15.8% of investment income**, non-accruals **5.5% cost / 3.4% FV**, and 1.22x net debt/equity; Q1 worsened on NAV, non-accruals, income, and leverage (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/FSK_PREBUILD_MAY11.md`; `PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`).
- **Insurance balance sheets are the suspected pressure-release valve.** Blue Owl → Kuvare asset sale at ~99.7¢, Apollo → Athene, Ares → IHAM, KKR → Global Atlantic form the “sell to affiliated insurer / captive” playbook (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/PRIVATE_CREDIT_CONTAGION_TRACKER.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_ARCHIVE_APR10.md`).
- **Athene is central to APO equity value.** SHADE estimates SRE was **$3.361B / 57%** of Apollo core profitability, plus embedded Athene-related fee value; Athene-driven earnings may represent >60% of APO equity value (`AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/06_APOLLO_ATHENE_FEE_DEPENDENCY.md`).
- **Regulatory pressure is rising.** SEC/Treasury/Fed formal probe focuses on private-credit valuations; NAIC adopted SVO/guardrail moves and AG 55 filings are live; Egan Jones faces SEC review and BMA revocation (`AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/SHADE/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/08_EGAN_JONES_TIME_BOMB.md`).

## Unconfirmed / risks
- **No broad cascade confirmation yet.** HY OAS remains <300 and VIX <20, so the market is not pricing systemic credit/vol stress (`HEARTBEAT.md`; `PROME/HANDOFF.md`).
- **Sponsor support can delay recognition.** BCRED absorbed stress via cap upsize + $400M sponsor/exec capital; FSK’s KKR support may stabilize common/sentiment short-term (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`; `PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`).
- **Top-tier vs second-tier bifurcation may be the story, not universal collapse.** BX/OWL/APO may retain access to balance sheet and inflows while weaker BDCs leak NAV (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).
- **Insurance trigger remains theoretical.** Athene has claimed a **$69B liquidity buffer** and FABN wall is described as manageable absent spread blowout; the maturity wall is a hurdle, not current failure (`AGENTS/SHADE/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/07_FABN_MATURITY_WALL_2026_2027.md`).
- **Some source files are stale.** SHADE STATUS is Mar 26; BROCK STATUS is May 1, superseded in parts by HEARTBEAT/FSK reads (`AGENTS/SHADE/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`; `HEARTBEAT.md`).

## Transmission links
1. **BDC marks/income → BDC equity/ETF pressure:** FSK NAV/NII/non-accrual shock validates the weak-tail BDC problem; BIZD already red (`PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`; `HEARTBEAT.md`).
2. **BDC funding → banks:** FSK revolver amendment and BROCK’s bank-BDC matrix bridge to REGINALD; top-bank PC disclosure and WFC software collateral are live bank transmission paths (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/FSK_PREBUILD_MAY11.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).
3. **Gates → forced sales / affiliated transfers → hard marks:** Blue Owl / Kuvare and BCRED support show stress is being absorbed, not erased (`AGENTS/BROCK/domain/PRIVATE_CREDIT_CONTAGION_TRACKER.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).
4. **Insurance → funding run / fire-sale loop:** Institutional funding agreements/GICs/FABNs can become runnable if rating/regulatory confidence breaks, forcing liquid asset sales then private-credit marks (`AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/04_LIQUIDITY_CRISIS_TRANSMISSION_CHAIN.md`).
5. **Regulatory/rating trigger → RBC/capital cliff:** Egan-Jones/BMA/NAIC path can re-rate private assets and create capital needs at insurers (`AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/08_EGAN_JONES_TIME_BOMB.md`).

## Contradictions
- **Managers rally while vehicles deteriorate:** OWL/APO/ARES/BX rallied into May 1 even as gates, DL loss, and probes persisted (`AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`; `AGENTS/BROCK/domain/sources/STATUS_NARRATIVE_MAY01_EOD.md`).
- **FSK says stress; HY/VIX say calm:** BDC/vehicle deterioration is confirmed, but public credit/vol have not confirmed (`PROME/FSK_Q1_READ_MAY11.md`; `HEARTBEAT.md`).
- **Athene is both risk and stabilizer:** It is core to APO fragility, but its liquidity buffer and liability-management tools can delay or absorb shocks (`AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/06_APOLLO_ATHENE_FEE_DEPENDENCY.md`; `AGENTS/SHADE/domain/sources/07_FABN_MATURITY_WALL_2026_2027.md`).
