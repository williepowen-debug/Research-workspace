## The reconciliation: it was a ledger-line artifact

The apparent contradiction — front-half "reserve-build can DEFER / rolls forward" vs back-half "reserve-build IS Q2 transmission" — **dissolves** once you separate two ledger lines of the *same* loss (CARL_FH):

| Ledger line | What it is | Timing | Whose claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Provision / ACL build** | forward-loss recognized NOW → hits P&L + CET1 this quarter | **Q2-visible, does NOT defer** | terminus "Q2 transmission" |
| **Realized NCO / charge-off** | the cash loss crystallizing | **defers to 2H-26/Q1-27** (vintage seasoning) | front-half "roll-forward" |

A reserve build is the **first visible footprint** of a loss whose *realization* defers. So both are true: the **NCO defers; the reserve is the part that does not.** Q2 reserve-build is genuinely Q2-able — *conditional on management choosing to build* (CECL discretion + ~5pp/2yr runway means a release re-defers even the provision).

**→ The single Q2 grade both clusters point at:** on the prints, does ACL **BUILD or RELEASE** — and is the build **cohort-specific or macro-overlay-driven**?
- **Build + macro-overlay** = transmission live across *both* consumer (Jul 21 monolines) and CRE (Jul 23-30 regionals) — they reinforce.
- **Release** = mask/defer holds → both clusters' Q2 paths slip to Q3/Q1-27.
