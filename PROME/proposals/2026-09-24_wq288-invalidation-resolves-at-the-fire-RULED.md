## The clause (exact text to insert — one bullet, verbatim)

> - **A registered Invalidation that is MET INSIDE THE ROW'S LIVE WINDOW RESOLVES THE ROW ON THE DATE IT WAS MET — RATIFIED Will 2026-09-24 (WQ-288; raiser DAEDALUS on CARL CRL-08; record `PROME/proposals/2026-09-24_wq288-invalidation-resolves-at-the-fire-RULED.md`):** the Invalidation column is the letter's own kill clause; when its condition is met at the primary the row names, the row is dead as written, whatever the author does next. An Invalidation that names no series has an unnamed basis and is graded NO-VERDICT (the WQ-162 rule above) — never on a series chosen after the data; its owner names the series PROSPECTIVELY at the next touch, and until then the kill cannot fire. (i) **The row resolves MISS** (the Class 3 outcome token; a met kill clause is a forecast OUTCOME, never a failed premise — VOID is not available for it) **and WQ-112(i)'s pre-resolution anchor is the fire: the latest dated mark standing BEFORE the fire scores.** (ii) **Marks and window changes made after the fire do not attach to the row; they are recorded as history** (Class 3, `AGENTS/DAEDALUS/BLUEPRINTS/STATE_VOCABULARY.md`: historical rows are never rewritten; the Notes cell carries the ruling by pointer). (iii) **A window extension written after the fire extends nothing; a forecast the author still wants to hold is a NEW registration with its own Date_Made.** The grade record carries the date it is written (recognition may lag the fire; it is never backdated); the RESOLUTION date is the fire. A fire AFTER the window close is not this rule — the row already resolved at its window close on its own letter.

## Why (DAEDALUS's reasoning, adopted; PROME's ADDITIONS to DAEDALUS's sentence, as the clause now stands: the in-window scope · an unnamed-series Invalidation is NO-VERDICT until its owner names the series PROSPECTIVELY, per WQ-162 (the plan read's owner-declared fallback was REMOVED by the result read's ❌6) · the explicit `MISS`/not-VOID outcome · (iii) as a non-extension with a new-registration option · the grade-record-vs-resolution-date sentence — five additions; six plan-read changes + one result-read edit = seven changes in all)

- **Failure direction:** without the clause, a row's Invalidation is decorative — a desk can outlive its own kill by extension. With it, the kill clause binds the author as it binds the reader.
