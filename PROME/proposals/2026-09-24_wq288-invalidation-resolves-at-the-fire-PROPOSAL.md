## The clause (exact text to insert — one bullet, verbatim)

> - **A registered Invalidation that is MET at its named primary RESOLVES THE ROW ON THAT DATE — RATIFIED Will <date> (WQ-288; raiser DAEDALUS on CARL CRL-08; record `PROME/proposals/2026-09-24_wq288-invalidation-resolves-at-the-fire-RULED.md`):** the Invalidation column is the letter's own kill clause; when its condition is met at the primary the row is dead as written, whatever the author does next. (i) **WQ-112(i)'s pre-resolution anchor for an invalidated row is the EARLIER of the window close and the fire** — the latest dated mark standing BEFORE the fire scores; (ii) **marks and window changes made after the fire do not score and are recorded as history** (Class 3: historical rows are never rewritten; the Notes cell carries the ruling by pointer); (iii) **a window extension on a row whose kill clause has fired is a NEW registration, not an extension** — it changes the letter, not the timeline, and is registered as a new row with its own Date_Made. The grade is still written on the row's registered grade date; only the resolution date and the scoring mark move to the fire.

## Why (DAEDALUS's reasoning, adopted; PROME adds nothing to the mechanism)

- **The case.** CRL-08's registered Invalidation read *"Brent drops below $80 sustained 2 weeks"*. FRED `DCOILBRENTEU` printed below $80 on every trading day 6/22–7/10 (15 sessions; low $68.53; CARL's own 9/24 pull) ⇒ the kill clause was MET by ~7/06, inside the live window, with the standing mark at **28%**. CARL did not apply it; it extended the window on 7/24 ("re-arming, not failing") and walked the mark to 7% on 9/11. DAEDALUS RULED: the row resolved ~7/06; **28% scores** (MISSED, Brier 0.0784); the 7% and the extension are post-resolution acts.
- **Why not "latest dated mark regardless":** it lets a desk keep re-marking a row its own letter has already killed — the exact walk WQ-112 exists to stop, made legal by ignoring the letter.
- **Why not first-call:** first-call applies only when the scoring mark is undated (WQ-112 ii); 28% was dated and pre-resolution.
- **Failure direction:** without the clause, a row's Invalidation is decorative — a desk can outlive its own kill by extension. With it, the kill clause binds the author as it binds the reader. This is the letter-over-author principle the canon already applies to grade dates (WQ-161 ②: the retrofit test is scored mass, never the calendar).
