## 1 · The burden it removes (measured today, not pitched)
The checks themselves cost PROME nothing: `prome_gate.py boot` prints ~45 verdict lines. What consumed PROME's context on 2026-10-10 was the coordination AROUND the checks: listing nine desks' inboxes (`inbox_census.py`), reading ROSTER rows, running the same-minute preflight (`ps` + ListAgents) eight times, reading WALTER's doctor backlog, choosing the four wakes against the cap, drafting eight spawn briefs, writing eight ORCH_LOG rows, then reading nine desk memos (~60 KB), writing DOCKET/WILL_QUEUE/ORCH_LOG records, sending eight closeout asks and recording eight receipts. Roughly 300 KB of reads and ~40 write operations to reach decisions that fit on one page. That page is what the clerk returns.

## 2 · Recommendation — build ONE thing, narrowly: a boot TRIAGE clerk. Do not build a desk.
- **Scope (computed, never chosen by PROME):** every desk the boot gate names as a candidate — the `desk catalyst summons` advisory (due/past-due rows; EMPTY-FORWARD), the `aged waits` check, the §3.5 exempt-desk BOARD gap, and, once built under WQ-401, NEXUS's S1 owner-unconsumed line (🔴 and 🟡 tiers) — plus every PENDING DOCKET row at or past its date naming a desk owner (`spawn_list.py`, the WQ-184 class). A scope script (`PROME/tools/triage_scope.py`, DAEDALUS-built or PROME-built) writes the candidate list from the saved gate logs; the clerk opens nothing outside it.
- **Inputs it gathers, per candidate:** the desk's inbox census (files only, lanes separate — `inbox_census.py`); its last own commit and days dark (`spawn_list.Liveness`); `ps` on this host; the saved `ORCH_INFLIGHT.md`; the authority class the candidate falls in (WQ-184 due row · WQ-206 aged ACTION · WQ-221 aged wait · C6 catalyst day · none → Will) and the cap position; the owner's own next-session notes (STATUS/SCRATCH "next" lines) and the DOCKET row text; a **draft spawn brief** in the standing desk-spawn form (`.claude/agents/desk.md` carries the preamble).
- **Output (the ONE artifact):** `PROME/state/TRIAGE_SLATE_<date>_<session>.md`, one page: a ranked table — desk · trigger · authority class · cap position · liveness evidence (with its observation time and coverage, UNKNOWN where unreachable) · the draft brief's path · a one-line recommendation (SPAWN NOW / SLATE FOR WILL / NO ACTION, with the reason). Written to `PROME/state/`, read by PROME in the same session; never an inbox item.
- **What it may NEVER do:** launch anything; message any desk; write to DOCKET, WILL_QUEUE, GATES, ORCH_LOG or any owner surface; grade on an owner's behalf; treat ListAgents, `ps`, a clean tree or its own slate as proof of absence (UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN; the same-minute discovery before any launch remains PROME's act at launch time, per `PROME/CLAUDE.md` § Desk-spawn preflight); carry authority — PROME decides, launches and records, exactly as ANVIL cannot approve a reconcile and ARGUS cannot apply a fix.
- **Phase 2 (a separate ruling, not this one):** a CONSUMPTION clerk that pre-digests desk memos into DRAFT record edits (DOCKET annotations, ORCH_LOG delivered cells) for PROME to verify at the artifact before applying — the ANVIL pattern. Held until the triage clerk has four graded boots.
- **Memory:** ONE file, capped at the 32,550 B read cap from day one, checked by `read_cap_check.py`; a PROME-owned CALIBRATION section (which recommendations PROME declined and why) so the clerk stops re-slating accepted no-actions.
- **Cadence:** every PROME boot at which the gate prints at least one candidate; one Opus spawn (fleet feedback: every subagent on Opus); skipped when the candidate list is empty. It runs INSIDE the boot sequence between the gate and the report-then-continue step, so the slate is in hand when PROME reports to Will.
- **Name:** JANUS — the doorkeeper who faces both ways (the inbox behind, the calendar ahead); free at ROSTER, `_NETWORK.md`, `AGENTS.md` and the directory tree (checked 2026-10-10). Identity name per `finding_subagent_naming_identity_over_functional`. Agent definition `.claude/agents/janus.md` at root AND `PROME/.claude/agents/` (parity is a blocking gate check).
- **Trial:** FOUR boots that print candidates, then graded at the next spine audit on three numbers against today's baseline — (i) PROME context bytes spent on triage reads (the slate's size vs the 2026-10-10 reads, measured by `measure.py` on the slate and by the tool log on the reads); (ii) candidates the clerk missed that PROME or WALTER's doctor later found; (iii) the named missed-obligations inventory — whether obligations reach their stated date without an owner disposition at a lower rate across the four boots (coverage stated each time). Launches stay PROME's throughout; the clerk earns nothing.
- **Rule changes:** none to authority. `PROME/BOOT.md` step 5 gains one line (run the clerk when the gate prints a candidate; read its slate) — a process change, ONE session's slot, after Will's word; `PROME/CLAUDE.md` is untouched (the preflight and the spawn tiers stay as written).

## 4 · Why not the alternatives
- **A persistent desk (an `AGENTS/JANUS/`):** would itself go dark and recreate the owner-attention failure one level up; rejected.
- **More script, no agent:** the instruments (gate · census · spawn_list · S1 line) already exist as scripts; what they cannot do is read nine charters' next-session notes, draft briefs in the standing form and rank against the cap with reasons. That judgment is what the context window is for.
- **Folding it into WALTER:** WALTER routes signals and must never spawn (root canon: WALTER recommends and logs; never spawns); triage is the consumer's act. Rejected.
