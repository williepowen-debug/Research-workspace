## The class rule (what the word approves)

When a registered row's criteria cell names a metric from a **lagged-publication series** (e.g. FRED H.15 daily-yield series, T+1 business days) **"on \<date\>"**, then:

1. **The observation DATED \<date\> governs.** No substitute observation (an earlier print "available on" the date) instantiates the cell.
2. **The grade waits for that observation's publication.** The strike time slips to the publication moment; the referent never moves. A publication lag is a transport artifact, not a spec term.
3. Non-lagged legs of the same row (e.g. a same-day-readable futures settle) are **read at their own time and held**, with the grade marked PROVISIONAL on its face until the lagged leg publishes.
4. This is a **reading rule for existing frozen letters, not an edit to any of them** — it resolves an ambiguity the letters do not, choosing the reading that adds no rider to the frozen terms.
