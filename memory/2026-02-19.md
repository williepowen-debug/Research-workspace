## ⚠️ CVNA Mistake — Real Money Lost

**What happened:** Will had CVNA $310/$290 put spread before earnings. I recommended closing based on:
- Break-even at $305 (needed -15.7% drop)
- Options-implied downside only $313 (-13.5%)
- "Even a big miss likely lands above your strike"
- Created urgency: "20 min to close"

**Result:** Will closed. CVNA dropped **20% after-hours** to ~$290. Would have been **near-max profit** on the spread.

**My errors:**
1. Treated implied move as a ceiling — actual move was -20% vs implied -13.5%
2. Confidently predicted "big miss won't hit strike" — magnitude prediction, not direction
3. Created false urgency that biased toward action
4. Talked him out of conviction when thesis was still valid

**Lesson:** Don't second-guess someone's directional conviction with probabilistic hedging. Implied vol ≠ max possible move. "Thesis not invalidated" means HOLD, not close.

Added to LESSONS.md with ⚠️ COSTLY flag.
