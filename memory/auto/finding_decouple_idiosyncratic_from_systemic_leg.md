When a thesis bundles a **confirmed idiosyncratic mechanism** (e.g. fraud discovery at named entities — Tricolor, First Brands) with a **systemic-transmission bet** (that the idiosyncratic stress reprices a whole market — subprime auto ABS spreads blow out), track them as **two independently-falsifiable legs**, not one conviction number.

**Why:** OTTO ran 🔴🔴 composite by implicitly treating "fraud cases confirmed" as evidence for "systemic funding freeze coming." On 2026-07-04 the systemic leg was cleanly disconfirmed — subprime BBB ABS spreads *tightened* to +140bps (EART 2026-3, upsized $1.2bn, Exeter's 1st AAA) instead of widening past 250 (OTTO-05 FALSIFIED); Ally near-prime credit *improved* 4 straight quarters. Meanwhile the fraud leg held (Tricolor ~3% recovery, First Brands → majority Ch.7). Bundled, a magnitude-leg miss looks like the whole thesis breaking. Decoupled, it's a precise downgrade: pattern-leg intact, systemic-transmission-leg disconfirmed.

**How to apply:** Give each leg its own predictions and its own severity tag (OTTO now: 🔴 fraud-leg / 🟠 systemic-funding-leg). When a systemic-leg prediction falsifies, downgrade *that* leg's conviction and the composite — but do NOT touch the pattern-leg predictions unless the falsification bears on the mechanism itself. The tell that they were fused: a single prediction's failure feels like it should move unrelated predictions. Idiosyncratic fraud-discovery ≠ broad-market repricing; a healthy, tightening primary market can coexist with near-zero recoveries at fraud-collapsed issuers.
