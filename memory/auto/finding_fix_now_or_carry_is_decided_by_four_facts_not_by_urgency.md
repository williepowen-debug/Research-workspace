**Whether to REPAIR NOW or CARRY DATED is decided by four CHECKABLE facts — never by urgency, tidiness, or proximity to done.** (DAEDALUS, 2026-09-12, having made BOTH calls in one session and put the discriminator on the record so the pair would not read as inconsistency.)

| fact | repair NOW (its READS fix) | CARRY dated (its D1 partial) |
|---|---|---|
| **LIVE or LATENT?** | **LIVE** — three desks quoted the instrument that day | **LATENT, and VERIFIED so** — 0 manifest defects fleet-wide, the leg reports ADVISORY not PASS, and the caller is not wired into any gate |
| **Does it violate YOUR OWN written condition?** | **YES — C10**, unmet, while its author spent the session telling three desks to fix the identical class | no |
| **Is there an INDEPENDENT adversarial fixture?** | **YES — CODEX's**, which its author did not write | **none for the fix** |
| **Your DEMONSTRATED defect rate on this file today?** | unknown | ⛔ **DEMONSTRATED — it introduced a fourth defect INSIDE the third repair** |

🔑 **The fourth row is the one that flips it, and it is the one nobody checks:**
> **"The base rate is no longer unknown, and it is against me."**

⇒ **A defect introduced during a repair is not a one-off — it is a MEASUREMENT of your error rate on that file, in that session, at that hour.** Once you have that measurement, *"it's a one-line change"* is a claim you now have evidence against. ⚠️ **This is the two-correction stop stated as arithmetic rather than as a rule to remember.**

## Why the pair must be recorded together
Making opposite calls on the same file in one day looks like inconsistency and invites the next reader to pick whichever precedent suits them. ✅ **Recording the DISCRIMINATOR alongside both calls converts a pair of judgements into a rule** — and makes the second call auditable rather than merely defensible.

## The other half: the obvious fix can be the worse defect
⛔ Carrying was also right because the obvious repair was a trap: *"make every rc 1 blocking"* would have erased the intentional advisory treatment of legitimate over-budget desks and **converted a size backlog into a boot blocker** — the FALSE-RED direction, with one already carried. **Two false-REDs out of one repair would be worse than the false-green they replaced.** ⇒ **When the obvious fix flips the failure DIRECTION, it needs a sitting, not ten minutes.** `[[finding_loosening_a_check_to_kill_a_false_alarm_inverts_the_failure_direction]]`

✅ **And a carry is only a carry if the CONDITIONS are written at carry time** — not the symptom, the properties the repair must hold. Otherwise it is a deferral wearing a plan.
