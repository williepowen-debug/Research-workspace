**A forward docket is built out of events, and "the market is closed" is not an event — so it never gets a row, and its absence reads as an ordinary quiet day.**

**Bought 2026-09-20, by SAM, on its own primary market.** Japan was shut Mon Sep-21 → Wed Sep-23 (Silver Week — Respect for the Aged Day, a Citizens' Holiday created automatically between two holidays, and the Autumnal Equinox; a five-day break, only the third ever). It was in **neither** `docket/CALENDAR.md` **nor** `docket/CATALYSTS.tsv`, so `catalyst_countdown.py` could not surface it and **no boot would have.** The desk had tracked every JGB auction, every CPI print and every BOJ release on that calendar for months, with a dedicated sub-agent maintaining it, and had **never once modelled whether the market was open.**

**Why the gap is structural and not carelessness.** Every row on that docket answers *"what is published / auctioned / decided on this date?"* Each one is generated by a **source that announces it** — a release calendar, an auction schedule, a policy meeting. A closure is generated by nothing: no institution publishes "we will not be publishing," and the days in question are precisely the days where every feed the desk watches goes quiet. **The very thing that should raise the alarm — silence across all sources — is indistinguishable from a weekend, a slow day, or a fetch that failed.**

**The second-order damage is worse than the missing row.** ① Data staleness gets misread: a source that has not updated because the market was shut looks identical to a source that has broken or gone stale, and SAM's authoritative JGB curve went dark for ~6 calendar days with no surface saying why. ② Trading-day arithmetic silently lies: the countdown script computes "Nd trd" from **weekdays**, knows no national holiday, and on this occasion was right only by coincidence. ③ **Liquidity conditions are a risk input and vanish entirely** — here an intervention tell had just fired with a historical follow-through window of *hours to about a day*, and that window elapsed into the thinnest market of the year. **The conjunction was the whole finding, and one half of it lived nowhere.**

**This is the mirror of `[[finding_a_carried_obligation_living_only_in_prose_has_no_instrument]]`.** There, an obligation existed in prose with no machine-readable row. Here there is no prose either: it is a **fact about the world's state** that no one thought to write down because it is not anybody's announcement. Ask of any docket: *what does this surface assume that nothing on it asserts?*

**Default:** a desk whose domain has a **primary market** carries that market's holiday calendar as **dated rows in the machine-readable feed**, not as background knowledge — and any instrument computing "trading days" is checked against it rather than against weekdays. Verify the closure at a source, add the rows to **both** the human and machine surfaces, and **run the countdown afterwards to confirm they surface.** ⛔ A closure row is not clutter: it is the row that tells the next session why everything else is silent.
