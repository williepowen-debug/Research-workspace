> **Promotion (Will-approved 2026-08-21, Batch A):** extending a COLD-tier memory with a new instance obligates a promotion flag to PROME — executed or declined at the next flow pass, symmetric with demotion; the row carries n=. **`[embedded→]` census rows are promotion-EXEMPT (Will "go ahead" 8/22):** their content lives at a canon embed home — that is why they are cited — and promoting them reverses ruled compaction; the census tag settles them, never re-adjudicated by hand.
> **Trim-on-demotion (Will-approved 2026-08-23):** a row being demoted gets its hook cut to the **≤80-char canon AS IT MOVES** — the moment someone is already looking at it. Without this the cold index inherits hot-era hooks (measured 2026-08-23: median **177 chars**, 164 rows over canon) and the sink grows ~2× faster than the row count implies. **`INDEX_COLD.md` has its own ceiling — see its header; it breached and truncated silently once.**
> **Flow rule (Will-approved 2026-08-12):** this hot index is a WORKING SET, not an archive — at any PROME closeout where the byte check reads **≥75% of cap**, PROME demotes (never deletes) settled/predictable-trigger rows to the cold index until **under 70%**, recorded in the commit. Tripping agents still only FLAG (7/28 ruling unchanged); only PROME executes; demotion only — a slug never leaves the two-index union.
