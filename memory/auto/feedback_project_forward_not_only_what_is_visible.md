Will, 2026-11-19 12:01 ET (prompt clock), in PROME's chat, verbatim: "I think we often restrict ourselves on not saying too much based on what is visible right now.  I do want us to begin to attempt to project what we think will happen or could happen.  With understanding that we cannot predict anything with any certainty - we still want to start to be more proactive in our understanding, context, and tracking."

**Why:** said after a morning of desk deliveries that each ended at "nothing moves, no implication" (Florida floods, credit at 347, the Crimean Bridge, Philadelphia Fed). The reports were accurate and stopped at the observed fact. He wants the forward view stated and then tracked, accepting it will often be wrong.

**How to apply (PROME's interpretation, 11/19):** every desk packet and every PROME brief ends its findings with a short forward view: the base case and the main alternative, each with a rough probability and a horizon, the dated tells that would confirm or refute it, and what it would mean for a position or a card. It is labelled a projection and kept apart from evidence; it moves no score, band, gate or trade by itself (USER.md: logging is not scoring; root rule #5 unchanged). A call worth tracking is registered as a prediction row under the desk's existing rules (WQ-596, WQ-715, `FORGE/PREDICTION_DISCIPLINE.md`), so it gets graded. Carried as one paragraph in launch letters; no new checker, report or gate (his 10/31 word).
