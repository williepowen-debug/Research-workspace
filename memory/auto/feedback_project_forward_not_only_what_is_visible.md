Will, 2026-11-19 12:01 ET (prompt clock), in PROME's chat, verbatim: "I think we often restrict ourselves on not saying too much based on what is visible right now.  I do want us to begin to attempt to project what we think will happen or could happen.  With understanding that we cannot predict anything with any certainty - we still want to start to be more proactive in our understanding, context, and tracking."

**Why:** said after a morning of desk deliveries that each ended at "nothing moves, no implication" (Florida floods, credit at 347, the Crimean Bridge, Philadelphia Fed). The reports were accurate and stopped at the observed fact. He wants the forward view stated and then tracked, accepting it will often be wrong.

**Will's follow-up direction, 2026-11-19 13:31 ET (prompt clock), in PROME's chat, verbatim:** "Keep the reporting proportionate. Ask for a forward view on material findings: what you expect, why, the meaningful alternative, the horizon and what would change your mind. Use probabilities where helpful. No daily prediction quota or mandatory odds table on routine packets." And: "Projections do not automatically move scores, gates or trades, but they can motivate preparation and TERRY's prospective, NOT-FIRED proposals. Register material, resolvable calls under existing rules; do not register every outlook or recommission existing research."

**How to apply (PROME's interpretation, revised 11/19 after that direction and the two reviews, CATO `AGENTS/CATO/runs/2026-11-19_1213_forward-view-review.md` FV19-1 to FV19-5; the first wording, "every desk packet and every PROME brief ... each with a rough probability", is in `git log -p` of this file and is withdrawn):** on MATERIAL findings a desk packet or a PROME brief gives a short forward view: what it expects, why, the meaningful alternative, the horizon and what would change its mind. Probabilities where they help; routine and administrative packets need none, and there is no quota. Each number is attached to its actual event and horizon. Branches that overlap, nest or run on different clocks are labelled as separate event probabilities, never laid out as one distribution. Two desks reading the same evidence are not independent confirmation. It is labelled a projection and kept apart from evidence. It does not automatically move a score, band, gate or trade (root rule #5 unchanged), and it can motivate preparation and TERRY's prospective NOT-FIRED proposals. Material, resolvable calls are registered under the desk's existing rules (WQ-596, WQ-715, `FORGE/PREDICTION_DISCIPLINE.md`); not every outlook is. A registered row's result is reported as the answer to the exact question it grades, never as validation of a broader scenario, and a material outlook with no row is named as unscored. PROME's own cross-desk forward judgment stays in its briefs. No new checker, report or gate (his 10/31 word).
