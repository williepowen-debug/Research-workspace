A pre-registered discriminator that fires against your prior lean is exactly when pre-registration earns its keep — you update on it, not on the discomfort. The harder case: it also fires against the **prevailing consensus narrative**. Do not let the narrative override a resolver you registered *before* the print; but do not ignore the divergence either.

**Reconcile the contradicting narrative before weighting it, on two axes:**
1. **Timeframe staleness.** Is the narrative built on an OLDER vintage than your discriminator? (e.g. a quarterly TIC stat cited in a story vs your freshest *weekly* flow print — different windows, not a contradiction.)
2. **Policy-push vs actual-flow.** Is the narrative describing an *aspirational* policy jawbone (a finance-ministry push, a rebalance "if it happens") rather than realized flow? A push that "draws skepticism" or that officials disclaim ("no plans to change allocation") is not flow data and cannot refute a flow print.

If the narrative survives both checks (fresh + flow-grade + same-question), it is genuine counter-evidence — down-weight. If it fails either (stale, or push-not-flow, or measuring a different question), hold the discriminator and **flag the tension prominently**, keeping the read at MEDIUM (not higher) and letting the **next scheduled print of the same series** arbitrate. Being contrarian-to-consensus on a pre-registered flow read is a feature, not a bug — but it raises the stakes of the next confirmation, so name that explicitly.
