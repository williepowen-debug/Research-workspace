LABOR's Jul-2-2026 NFP grid conditioned every branch on (payrolls × U-3-up). The tape printed the un-written cell: payrolls +57K (weak) with **U-3 FALLING** to 4.2% — the labor force shrank 720K (immigration supply shock), mechanically suppressing the rate. The trigger keyed to "U-3 jump" couldn't fire on a genuinely weak print; prediction LAB-02 died on gauge choice, not mechanism.

**DEWEY C2 instance (2026-07-24), a STOCK-share variant:** the CC 90+ delinquency *balance share* rose to 13.12% (0.62pp from the 13.74% GFC peak) — but **~62% of the Q1 rise came from a shrinking denominator** (CC balances fell $25B) and aging of an existing 90+ pipeline, while the *flow* into delinquency actually softened (30+ transition ticked down, bank charge-offs fell). Two extra traps beyond the base case: (a) the denominator shrink was partly **seasonal** (Q1 paydown → reverses Q2–Q4); (b) a threshold *breach* driven this way is a **measurement/stock event, not confirmation the hypothesized mechanism fired** (the "student-loan cascade" was NOT the driver). Reading the breach as fresh flow-stress would have been the error.

**Why:** Any threshold denominated in a ratio inherits its denominator's dynamics. When the denominator becomes the fastest-moving part (participation collapse, shrinking base, index rebasing), the ratio stops measuring what the threshold assumed — in either direction.

**How to apply:** (1) Grade ratio-gauge moves JOINTLY with their denominator (U-3 with LFPR; NCO rate with loan-book size; share-of-X with X). Denominator-driven moves = NO-SIGNAL. (2) Print-reaction grids get an explicit third-axis artifact branch, written BEFORE the release. (3) Pre-registering a discriminating signature (LABOR's: U-3↓ + wages↑ + LFPR↓ = supply shock, logged Jun 16) is what makes an ambiguous print gradable same-day. (4) **Stock-share extension:** for a *stock* share near a historic threshold, decompose the move into numerator vs denominator, check the *flow* direction separately (a stock can breach on pipeline-aging while flows soften), and check denominator seasonality before reading a breach as a live mechanism — breach ≠ mechanism-confirmed.
