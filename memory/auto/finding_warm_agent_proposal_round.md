2026-07-18 Sat-eve: after wave-1, Will asked the 4 warm agents (FALCON/REGINALD/LIQUID/BOND) to sweep their domains and propose the 3 most valuable tasks completable that evening. Prompt structure: (1) what, one sentence; (2) why it moves the ball — tied to named catalysts; (3) effort estimate; (4) deliverable file; ranked; **"honest bar: edge or real gaps, not hygiene theater; your deferred sweep counts ONLY if it beats the alternatives"**; proposal-only (no execution, no commits). Result: 12 proposals, zero padding — both agents with owed hygiene sweeps explicitly ranked them below their picks with reasons; two proposals converted the session's own fresh findings into follow-on instruments (FALCON's bypass gauge cloned the just-proven Kharg method; BOND's Fed-path map quantified the falsifier its own relabel had just created). Will picked 9; all 9 delivered same evening.

**Why:** warm context makes agent self-prioritization cheap and well-calibrated — they know their open loops better than the coordinator does; the honest-bar clause + requiring catalyst-ties + effort + deliverable filename is what kept proposals concrete and prevented hygiene-padding. Proposal-only framing (Will picks) preserved the decision rail.

**How to apply:** on orchestration days with warm agents and remaining budget, run a proposal round before assigning new work top-down: require rank + catalyst-tie + effort + deliverable path, include the honest-bar clause, deliver via SendMessage only (no files), operator picks the execution set, then execute with full ceremony.
