When WALTER spawns Phase 1.5 verify-research sub-agents on incoming signal claims, the most-common returned verdict across recent sessions has been CORRECTED-FRAMING (FL Scott "43k critical industries" / FHA "180% of 2009" / Deutsche Bank call/put numerics / FHLB Trump mortgage EO / others). The pattern: directional thesis is confirmed by primary data, but the headline-specific number or absolute-framing is imprecise — usually a count when the original is a rate, a rounded rhetorical figure, or a secondhand-compressed mechanism-assertion that doesn't survive the actual primary source.

**Why:** Headline framings on social media systematically cherry-pick the most striking number or framing of a directional truth. The directional truth survives verification; the specific framing rarely does. This is a reliable enough pattern (≥4 of WALTER's last 9 verify-spawns originally; 10 of 10 samples confirm in May 6 re-measurement) that calibration should be built into the FILTER_SPEC verdict handling.

**How to apply:** When CORRECTED-FRAMING verdict returns, **dispatch confidence is calibrated to the directional-thesis strength after correction, not as a haircut on raw confidence**. Live distribution measured 2026-05-06 across 10 BOARD CORRECTED-FRAMING dispatches: range **0.60–0.88, median ~0.77** (e.g., SIG-005-001 IEA crude 0.65; SIG-005-007 factory orders 0.75; SIG-026-008 UMich 0.88; SIG-028-004 Phoenix BTR 0.60). Earlier rule "drop to ~0.55" was empirically too pessimistic; WALTER's actual practice is correct — CORRECTED-FRAMING confidence reflects how strongly the directional thesis survives correction, not how skeptical to be. Always explicitly flag the specific imprecise figure in the dispatch_note + signal body Caveats; route under the directional thesis to the same recipient the original framing would have. Downstream agents (REGINALD, CARL, etc.) MUST avoid anchoring on the imprecise number — the dispatch_note's job is making that explicit. **Don't recalibrate-down a 0.85 CORRECTED-FRAMING signal to 0.55 just because it's CORRECTED-FRAMING; the verdict label and the confidence value carry different information.**

**Cost-benefit:** Verify-research spawn ~$0.05; downstream cost of routing an over-stated thesis to an action agent that takes a position is asymmetric and large. CORRECTED-FRAMING is the highest-value verdict category because it discriminates real-thesis-transmission from headline-cherry-pick — the kill verdicts (FALSE) and confirm verdicts (CONFIRMED) are easier to handle without verify; the in-between is where the verify earns its keep.
