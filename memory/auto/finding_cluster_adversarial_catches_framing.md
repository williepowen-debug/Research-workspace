On the 2026-06-25 front-half de-mask cluster, a 5-lens independent adversarial round (verdict HOLDS-WITH-ADDITIONS) left the positioning conclusion intact and REINFORCED, but caught real framing overreach the synthesizer (Prome) had committed: (1) a seductive "it's ONE mechanism" unification that overclaimed a structural identity across legs that actually de-mask on different clocks; (2) a "triangulated" U-3 number that was really one input run through two transforms agreeing by accounting identity (single-source); (3) a dated convergence window that over-communicated reveal-confidence vs the legs' own roll-forward risk; (4) two stale figures. Notably one overreach (migration = "compositional") contradicted a retraction in the source agent's OWN STATUS that had been read at boot.

**Why:** cross-domain synthesis is biased toward elegant unification and toward citing each leg's peak-bearish decode; the synthesizer can't see its own framing inflation; the trade/positioning conclusion rarely depends on the framing, so the inflation stays invisible until attacked.

**How to apply:** when a cluster produces a clean unifying "insight," run an independent adversarial+judge round BEFORE banking it. Good lenses: unification-overreach, roll-forward-on-the-conclusion (not just the masks), genuine-calm/unfalsifiability, independence/double-count, and the source-agents'-own-bottom-line. Separate what SURVIVES (usually the direction/positioning) from what must be SOFTENED (usually the framing/labels/dated windows), and re-verify any unification claim against the source agents' own STATUS.
