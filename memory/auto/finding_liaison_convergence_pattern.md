**How to apply:**

1. **Substrate prep doc before Turn 1 fires.** WALTER pre-staged `AGENTS/WALTER/handoff_BRENT/LIAISON_TURN_2_DRAFT.md` and `route_log_brent_slice.tsv` before BRENT's first turn. BRENT's Turn 1 had concrete data to interrogate (48-row slice) instead of asking abstract questions. This compresses Turn 1's question-list-only opener (CARL's pattern) into question-list + already-staged-data.

2. **Inherit prior LIAISON's locked decisions explicitly.** BRENT Turn 1 stated upfront: "I've inherited the conventions and the FORMAT_SPEC v0.8 / ROUTING_TABLE / NEXUS-precedence decisions" from CARL↔WALTER. This skips re-deriving consensus on items already locked elsewhere. Saves 2-3 turns of architectural recap.

3. **Open Turn 1 with substance, not just questions.** BRENT Turn 1 had: domain summary + current routing rules + **honest gap acknowledgment** (no BOARD_LOG yet) + plug-in points to inherited decisions + 7 questions. CARL Turn 1 was domain + routing + retrospective + 5 questions. The "plug-in points" section is the difference — flagging where the new agent has stake in already-locked decisions accelerates ratification.

4. **Turn 3 = retroactive substance pass, not architecture-question continuation.** BRENT Turn 3 answered WALTER Turn 2's questions AND included the 47-signal back-disposition pass result (5 Post_Hoc_Conf deltas surfaced). This converts what would normally be 2 separate turns (architecture-Q-A + later cycle-1 calibration data) into one turn. CARL's pattern had architecture-Q-A on Turns 3-5 then deferred calibration data to a future cycle (~mid-late May).

5. **Filename convention for stitched joint artifacts uses stitch-date, not Turn-1-date.** WALTER suggested `JOINT_PROPOSAL_2026-05-06_walter_carl_brent.md` for the 3-way stitch even though dialog started 5/5 — date matches stitch, agents named for searchability. Per-agent section files keep their authoring date.

**Anti-pattern to avoid:** Opening Turn 1 with question-list-only and waiting for the other side to draft answers. CARL's pattern (which led to 7 turns) had legitimately useful architecture but burned cycles on Turn 1 questions that didn't have substrate to interrogate.
