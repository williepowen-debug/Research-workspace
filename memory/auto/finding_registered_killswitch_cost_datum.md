When a pre-registered falsifier/kill-switch fires against a framework whose directional call is PAYING, the temptation is to relitigate the verdict ("the trade was right, the switch must be wrong"). Don't.

**Why:** The switch's whole value is that it outranks the tape at decision time — softening it post-hoc when it costs money is exactly the goalpost-move it was registered to prevent. But silently eating the cost wastes the calibration information.

**How to apply:** (1) Hold the mechanical verdict — the framework is falsified/stopped per registration. (2) Log the counterfactual cost explicitly as a datum ON the switch (e.g., "the fade's directional thesis paid in full; the credit tree killed it — first false-positive-cost observation"). (3) If the fire exposes a structural blind spot (VIOLET 7/1: a single mega-issuer's pre-negotiated bankruptcy mechanically fired index-level credit lines — no single-issuer mask), register it as a REFINEMENT CANDIDATE for the next calibration pass, backtested before re-registration — never retro-applied to the live adjudication. Instance: VIOLET KB-VIO-107 (first Bin-A of the KB-VIO-090 tree, 2026-07-01). Related: [[finding_documented_divergence_as_discipline]], [[finding_pre_registration_discipline_through_corroboration]].
