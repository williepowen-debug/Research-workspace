**When you name a hypothesis, name the instrument that measures the mechanism the name refers to — at registration time, before it starts earning weight.** Without one, the hypothesis is never wrong; it is only never checked.

## The instance

**RED, 2026-08-12.** *Full Stagflation Spiral* had been the modal or co-modal bucket at 37-40% since roughly May — four months, carried through weekly re-marks, defended in cross-agent challenges, cited by other desks.

A stagflation **spiral** is *defined* by inflation expectations coming unanchored. That is not a connotation; it is the mechanism the word names, and it is what separates "spiral" from "an inflation number that is currently high."

**No instrument for it had ever been registered.** Not stale, not disputed, not awaiting a print — never pulled once.

Measured for the first time: **5y5y forward inflation compensation 2.31%**, three-week range **2.26–2.33**; 10Y breakeven 2.27%, range 2.22–2.29. **These had not moved ten basis points through the largest supply shock in the series** — a first-ever $100 crude settle, a formal chokepoint closure, energy +14.7% YoY, a central bank locked hawkish.

The mechanism the hypothesis requires had **never engaged on any instrument**, and the book did not know, because the book had never looked. The weight came down 6 points the day it was measured.

## Why this is not just "check your data"

The series was **publishing continuously and freely the entire time.** Nothing was blocked, delayed, revised, or contested. This is not a coverage failure, an access failure, or a staleness failure — every existing hygiene check would have passed, because **there was no row to be stale.**

That is what makes the class dangerous: **hygiene tooling audits the instruments you registered.** It is structurally blind to the instrument you never registered, and the absence is invisible in exactly the surfaces where a hypothesis lives — a weight table shows a number, not what measured it.

**A hypothesis with no instrument is unfalsifiable in practice while looking rigorous on the page**, because it sits in the same table, with the same decimal places, as buckets that do have one.

## Two tells

1. **The name contains a mechanism claim you have never measured.** "Spiral", "cascade", "contagion", "unanchoring", "capitulation", "squeeze" — each names a *process*, not a *level*. Ask: what instrument shows that process engaging, and when did I last read it? If the honest answer is "high inflation" for a spiral or "prices fell" for a cascade, you are measuring the **precondition**, not the mechanism.
2. **The hypothesis has survived several re-marks without its weight ever moving on its own evidence.** A bucket that only ever moves as the *residual* of other buckets' moves is one nobody is measuring directly.

## How to apply

- **At creation:** every hypothesis gets a registered instrument for its defining mechanism, with a threshold and a direction, before it is allowed to carry weight.
- **On existing books:** go bucket by bucket and ask the question for each. Expect to find more than one hole — they cluster, because the same authoring habit produced all of them.
- **When you do find one, say plainly that the series did not change — you did.** The honest sentence is *"the instrument has been flat all along and I am marking to a measurement I owed this thesis months ago,"* not *"new evidence emerged."* The first is a calibration finding about your process; the second is a false claim about the world, and it quietly protects the habit that caused it.
- **Register the re-arm at the same time**, so a downgrade taken on a first reading is reversible on evidence rather than on argument.

## The counter to keep

An un-fired instrument is a **detector**, not a guarantee. Market-priced instruments in particular embed liquidity premia, and expectation anchors historically break **late and nonlinearly** — so "the instrument says the mechanism is not engaging" is a live reading, not proof the mechanism cannot engage. Register it, read it, weight it; do not promote it to certainty.
