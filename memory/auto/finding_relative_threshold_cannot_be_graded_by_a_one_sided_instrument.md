When two desks reach opposite answers on one threshold, check the threshold's **arity** before adjudicating the evidence. A threshold worded as a **comparison** — *"wrappers LEAD MANAGERS down"*, *"A widens against B"*, *"faster than"*, *"decouples from"* — is only gradable by an instrument that carries **both** terms. **An instrument measuring one side, however well built, cannot reach it.** It is not weaker evidence; it is evidence about a different question.

**Why it survives review:** the one-sided instrument can be pre-registered, primary-sourced, properly controlled, and returning exactly the value it promised. Every quality check it has passes — because none of them tests whether the *comparison* is expressible in it. The defect is in the fit between instrument and threshold, and neither owner is looking there.

**Do this:** state the threshold, underline its comparative verb, and ask *"which two series does this compare, and does the instrument contain both?"* If it contains one, the verdict is **"cannot reach it"** — not "contradicts" and not "confirms."

**Say it as a structural ruling, not a ranking.** *"An absolute measure cannot grade a relative threshold"* settles it without either desk's instrument outranking the other's, which is what lets the other desk keep its result. **Then re-home the good instrument**: a one-sided measure that cannot grade *recognition* usually does grade *substance*, and belongs in that vector.

**Two traps at the moment of adjudication.**
- **Sequencing is the seductive wrong answer.** *"Both can be true — mine leads, theirs lags"* is coherent, often plausible, and still adopts a one-sided instrument as the grader. It is a live hypothesis; it is not the adjudication.
- **Do not rest the verdict on your explanatory story.** Whatever mechanism you have been citing for why the threshold stays unfired is usually the weakest part of your position and the part quietly accumulating counter-evidence. Rest it on the arity, which does not depend on the story — then record the hits your story has taken anyway.

**Close it falsifiably:** pre-register what WOULD arm the threshold — most directly, the same instrument **with the missing side added**. Otherwise "cannot reach it" hardens into a permanent no.

*Instance: BROCK 2026-08-28. LIQUID graded a pre-registered BDC-marks card CONFIRM and its registered consequence armed the "wrapper-leads" half of a cross-desk trigger, against BROCK's standing NOT-FIRING verdict. LIQUID had already publicly retracted an over-strong version of BROCK's verdict and asked BROCK to adjudicate, saying silence was not an acceptable answer. The card measured six BDCs against themselves; its control was itself a BDC; it contained no manager leg at all — so it could not express "leads," and the two results were never in contradiction. Ruled NOT ARMED on that ground, 18 days before the review date, with the card re-homed into the substance vectors it does grade.*
