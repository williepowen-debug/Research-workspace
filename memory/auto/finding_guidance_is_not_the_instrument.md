**BRENT, 2026-07-31.** I registered a base case of **LAPSE** for the Russian diesel/gasoil export ban at its stated 7/31 expiry, off Deputy PM Novak's on-record guidance (7/25-27): the gasoline ban is extended, *"the diesel ban is to be LIFTED as the market recovers,"* explicitly not extended alongside it [Bloomberg 7/25; Interfax/S&P 7/27]. **The base case FAILED.** The cabinet issued a signed decree on 7/30 extending the ban Aug 1 → Jan 31 2027, keeping diesel restricted a further month, and **permanently splitting the channel** — from Sept 1 the lift applies only to producers exporting directly; traders/resellers stay banned six months longer.

**Nothing about the guidance was misread or mis-sourced.** It was a named deputy prime minister, correctly quoted, at two independent institutional outlets. The failure was **treating a stated intention as a resolved outcome.** A government that says it will lift a ban retains every degree of freedom until the instrument publishes — including partial lifts, channel carve-outs, and timing changes that invert the trade-relevant direction.

**Why:** the guidance→instrument gap is where the *tradeable* content lives. A blanket lapse and a producer-only carve-out are opposite signals for the same headline, and only the instrument distinguishes them. This generalizes past decrees to any body that announces then formalizes: central-bank guidance vs the statement, agency signalling vs the rule, a court's stated leaning vs the opinion, sanctions "intent" vs the designation.

**How to apply:**
- When a policy outcome is your falsifier, **register the call as PENDING THE INSTRUMENT**, with the instrument named (decree/statement/rule/designation) — not "resolved by the guidance."
- Put the **instrument's publication venue** in the spec, not the event date (`[[finding_discovery_instrument_defines_the_claim]]`, `[[finding_pre_register_against_the_carrying_filing]]`).
- **Read the instrument for CHANNEL/SCOPE carve-outs, not just the headline direction.** Producer-vs-trader, entity-vs-sector, prospective-vs-retroactive — the carve-out is usually where the number changes.
- A weak counter-source that contradicts strong guidance is **still weak** — declining to carry a single unattributed claimant was correct here even though it was directionally right. The fix is not "trust weak sources"; it is "don't resolve on the strong one either." (`[[finding_single_source_liveevent_is_a_lead]]`)
- If you got it wrong, the second-order read registered *before* the event may still be untouched — check it separately rather than retracting the whole frame (`[[finding_claim_outlives_its_discredited_instrument]]`).
