A net-SELLING flow print is not evidence of abandonment if the seller is running a **roll-up rotation** (sell low-yield legacy holdings, realize losses, buy the same asset class at higher yields). The sell-leg prints first and looks like exit; the program is accumulation.

**Worked example (SAM-32, resolved FALSE in 48h, 2026-07-02):** Japanese lifers net-SOLD ~¥201B of super-long JGBs in May 2026 — SAM read it as structural abandonment ("no re-entry yield near current") and built a demand-vacuum thesis leg on it. Meiji Yasuda then DOUBLED its FY2026 super-long purchase plan to >¥2T calling ~4% 30Y a "perfect buying opportunity," funded *by selling low-coupon legacy JGBs* — the May selling was likely that program's sell-leg. Dai-ichi had stated the identical intent a year earlier (Jul-2025: "selling has dominated… we want to increase buying going forward"); the intent trail was on the record and unchecked.

**Why:** flow data carries a sign; a program carries a direction. During a rotation they point opposite ways for months. Sibling failure modes: stock-vs-flow conflation, seasonal-vs-regime, composition masks ([[finding_composition_mask_unmask_discriminator]]) — this adds the *program* layer: intent statements, plan announcements, and revision news are the cheap check that flips the reading.

**How to apply:** before citing a net-flow sign as regime evidence (insurer books, pension allocation, FX reserves, fund flows — LIQUID/CARL/BOND flow reads included), search for a stated program at the flowing entities: plan announcements, mid-year revisions, executive intent quotes going back ~12 months. If a roll-up/rotation program exists, gross and net selling prints are EXPECTED during accumulation — grade the flow as program-consistent, not directional. Pre-registered falsifiers on flow-based claims should include a plan-announcement leg (SAM-32's did — it's what caught the error in 48h instead of quarters).
