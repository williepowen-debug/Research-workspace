**The class:** a viral post's load-bearing stat is a REAL number lifted from the artifact or an adjacent source, but with its **unit or semantic role re-labeled**. The number survives a spot-check ("104 does appear on the chart"; "70M is a real arithmetic result"), which is what makes the class dangerous — the fact-check passes while the claim is inverted.

**Instances (n=2, both WALTER):**
- **2026-07-21 — buffer→floor (Z4 SPR "70M"):** "DOE says SPR operational minimum is 70M bbl" — no DOE primary supports it; the arithmetic that reproduces it exactly is *current level (316.5M) − analyst threshold (~250M) ≈ 66M ≈ "70M"* — the BUFFER re-labeled as the FLOOR. (§6241 statutory floor 252.4M stands.) SIG-W-20260721-005.
- **2026-07-23 — price-level→percent (Pictet "104%"):** "Every Brent surge >104% triggered a recession, 6/6" — the posted chart's threshold is **50% deviation-from-trend**; the "104" is the chart's own *"Oil Brent price > $104"* PRICE annotation misread as a percent surge. The chart was also stamped "Last Update: 02/03/22" — **both refutations were printed on the posted artifact itself.** SIG-W-20260723-007.

**Why:** virality selects for the strongest-sounding rendering of a number, and re-labeling (floor, threshold, %-surge, record) is the cheapest strengthening move. The poster usually isn't fabricating — they're misreading.

**How to apply:** (1) For any "the floor/limit/threshold is N" claim, check whether N ≈ (current level − a well-known threshold) — if yes, it's almost certainly the buffer re-labeled. (2) For any "%-move triggered X" claim over a chart, read the chart's own axis/annotation — dollar levels and percent deviations get swapped. (3) Read the artifact's own date stamp before anything else. (4) Disposition: **INOCULATION, not KILL**, when the claim is thesis-relevant-if-true + viral — it WILL recirculate, and a pre-registered refutation is cheaper than re-refuting per arrival.
