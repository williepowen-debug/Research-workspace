**Rule (verbatim, CATO's wording, approved by Will 2026-09-26 21:01 ET — "approve CATO text"):**

> Within authorized reviewer-owner work, deliver factual findings through a committed artifact and notify the owner directly. The owner handles routine corrections under existing authority. Bring Will policy choices, authorization requests, unresolved material disagreements, and consequential facts requiring his attention. Preserve independent findings and their disposition; routing through the owner does not give the owner veto over reporting them.

**Why:** on 9/26 Will carried CATO's corrections, receipts and completion clarifications between CATO and PROME across roughly ten relays (boot-brief corrections, the L511 candidates, acceptance replies). His policy judgments were needed; the relaying was not. PROME's first draft of this preference ("Will gets only the decisions") was too narrow. It would have cut Will off from material warnings and unresolved limits that ask no decision. Related: [[finding_relayed_recommendation_is_not_an_approval]] — a reviewer's recommendation reaching PROME directly is still a recommendation. Gated surfaces still need Will's own word.

**How to apply:**
- Take a reviewer's committed findings of fact (a false claim, a missing caveat, a runner contradiction) straight from its artifact. Correct the routine ones under existing authority.
- Don't return routine choices to Will, such as the timing of an already-agreed carry.
- Still surface policy choices, authorization requests, unresolved material disagreements and consequential facts, even when no decision is asked.
- Never suppress or re-grade the reviewer's independent finding. Report it with its disposition.
