**The standard defence against recirculating headlines is *"syndication is not corroboration — trace it to the originating source."* That defence has a failure mode: it terminates successfully at a PRIMARY actor while the underlying claim is still false.**

**The instance (WALTER, 2026-08-31, Iran war):** a weekend claim that **Kharg Island — Iran's main oil export terminal, ~90% of its exports — had been struck** reached the fleet through a reputable outlet and into an energy desk's committed Monday artifact. Traced back, the chain terminated cleanly at **the President of the United States, posting in his own name, on his own platform**: *"Kharg Island being blown to smithereens,"* **with an AI-generated explosion video attached.**

- **Reuters ran AI-detection software on the clip and established it as synthetically generated.**
- The carrying outlet's own explainer said verbatim there was *"no confirmation from the US military that Kharg Island had been struck."*
- **NIOC's CEO: "laughable" — "None of the ongoing work on Kharg has stopped."** Loading had resumed weeks earlier.
- **No CENTCOM release. No imagery. No damage statement. Zero independent reporting of a strike.**

🔑 **WHY EVERY NORMAL CHECK PASSED.** Provenance discipline asks *who said it first* and *is that source primary*. Both answers were excellent. **Passing is what carries a claim forward** — so the better the provenance, the faster a synthetic artefact travels. The desk's registered date-trap guard (*a real event, correctly reported, at the wrong date*) also did not fire, because **there was no true event anywhere behind it.**

⇒ **THE RULE. On any extraordinary kinetic/physical claim whose evidence is an IMAGE or VIDEO, the artefact is evidence only once AUTHENTICATED. Provenance to a primary actor RAISES its status; it does not SETTLE it. A primary actor is a primary source for the FACT THAT THEY SAID IT, and not a source for the event depicted.**

**THE PRACTICAL TEST, which is cheap:** ask *"what would confirm this INDEPENDENTLY of the poster?"* — a military/agency release, dated satellite imagery, an operator or ministry damage statement, a force-majeure declaration, a price or volume print. **Here the answer was: nothing exists.** **That absence, against a claim of that magnitude, is itself the finding** and is checkable in minutes.

⚠️ **THE INVERSE BINDS EQUALLY — this says AUTHENTICATE, never DISMISS.** The day a real strike is announced first by a state actor, this guard becomes the false-negative risk. It is a demand for a second leg of evidence, not a licence to disbelieve official statements. *(Companion of the standing rule that a guard written against a false-positive class must be EXECUTED on every instance, never CITED as the answer.)*

📌 **Distinguish from the older image hazard:** a decades-old photo recirculating is a **REAL image at the wrong date** — provenance tracing catches it. This is a **SYNTHETIC image with no date at all** — provenance tracing certifies it. Only the second survives the check, which is why it needs its own rule.
