A prediction-market "divergence" (ORACLE's core product) is a subtraction of two numbers: the live market price and the fleet thesis. The live side is easy to keep fresh (re-pull). The thesis side rots silently — and a stale or definition-mismatched baseline manufactures a divergence that isn't there.

ORACLE 2026-06-21 boot: reported a "−64pp recession gap, biggest divergence, ORACLE's whole job" (market 12% vs stored fleet ~76%). Audit of the live fleet state (RED/HENRY/LABOR) found **no agent holds 76%** — it was an April strawman. RED's current 57% net-bear is a regime-direction *blend* (stagflation+dislocation+war), NOT a GDP/NBER two-negative-quarters number (which is what the Polymarket market resolves on). HENRY had soft-killed the cyclical axis; LABOR right-sized to 48/80. The fleet had de-risked *toward* the crowd since April. Same pattern on banks: stored ~25–50% conflated "CRE-stress conviction" with "P(a bank fails by a date)" — on a like-for-like basis crowd and fleet agreed (low). Both "big divergences" evaporated under audit.

**Why:** divergence is the headline ORACLE sells; an inflated one wastes RED adjudication and mis-signals a contrarian edge that doesn't exist. The error is asymmetric — fresh live price × stale thesis always *over*states the gap, because the fleet typically moves toward the market over time.

**How to apply:** (1) Every divergence row carries the thesis side's **source file + date** (not just the live pull's). (2) Re-audit the thesis baseline against the domain agent's *current* files (not a central board — [[finding_board_lags_agents_not_vice_versa]]) before calling a gap load-bearing. (3) Match **definitions**: pull the market's resolution text and confirm the fleet number answers the *same* question (GDP/NBER recession ≠ regime-blend bear; named-bank-failure ≠ CRE-stress conviction). A regime/scenario blend is never a like-for-like for a single-event contract. (4) When the baseline is stale, retract honestly and re-source — don't carry the strawman.
