**The finding (2026-09-02, twelve-desk night — ZHAO KB-ZHAO-136/140, MIDAS L-48, NEXUS `35ebf7054`, OTTO `6eafc63c9`/`7673ca727`, BROCK `8acd2fa34`/`abe8de4a2`):** every fix was correct and every one was placed by asking *where does this belong?* instead of *which surface does the reader travel?* Six shapes in one session:

1. **A hot/cold split strands or DELETES obligations.** ZHAO moved an owed action verbatim to a non-boot-read archive and the fresh hot NEXT ACTIONS carried ten items forward without it; NEXUS's split deleted two owed actions from BOTH files and archived a live guard. `measure.py` and `read_cap_check.py` returned green on both — **a byte or line census is structurally blind to a deletion; audit a split by OBLIGATION** (list every open obligation at the pre-split HEAD and confirm each is on a surface the boot reads). OTTO's 3-pass version: hand list · machine-extracted identifiers · obligations *created* this session. Limit: an extractor skips an obligation phrased in pure prose.
2. **A correction made on the surface you are looking at does not travel.** OTTO amended letter bands in the letters file (not boot-read) and left the boot-read catalyst row with the old bands; BROCK corrected STATUS and four other surfaces — including the NEXUS brief that reaches Will — kept the superseded values, one of them a RETIRED trigger presented as a live de-escalation condition. Fix = the fleet's own self-consumer check: grep the superseded strings across your directory and **finish every hit**; then read every section whose HEADING implies currency (NEXT DECISION, CURRENT VIEW, "next 2–4 weeks"), because staleness by omission carries nothing to grep.
3. **A carried "unknown" never self-evaluates.** DOCKET's "summit date unannounced" range row aged into a false RECENTLY-FIRED at ZHAO's boot while the date had been announced for six weeks. A month-known/day-unknown row is a dated claim about ignorance — give it a `recheck_by`.
4. **A commitment living in recipients' inboxes but on no surface the author's boot reads** (ZHAO's Entity-List watch; MIDAS's registration; BOND's finding in its own outbox). Passes every local completeness check.
5. **The flag written to prevent 1–4, filed on a surface with no reader** (ZHAO's WQ-161 "not canon" flag in a KB whose `Stale_By` its own STATUS documents as unread).
6. **A mostly-closed item reads as closed** — three of four legs done, the one still-owned leg is exactly what strands; partial closure is evidence of attention, which is worse than neglect.

**Why:** each surface is well-formed on its own, so no per-file check fires; the failure lives between files, in the reader's path. The remedy is a question, not a place: *which surface does the reader consult, and is this on it?* Related: [[finding_transfer_completes_only_when_the_receiver_encodes]] · [[finding_dated_carry_item_has_no_expiry_check]] · [[finding_supersession_marker_suppresses_the_live_value_beside_it]] · [[finding_scan_keyed_on_naming_reads_local_form_as_absence]] (the fuzzy survival scan threw five false positives; hand-check separated relabels from drops).

**How to apply:** after any split, rotation or compaction — obligation diff against the pre-edit HEAD, boot-read total before/after (a split CHOOSES which cost to pay: on the reading path = no saving; off it = check for strands — MIDAS/ZHAO measured opposite signs on the same operation).
