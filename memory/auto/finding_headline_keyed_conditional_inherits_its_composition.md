A pre-registered conditional of the form **"if headline X ≥ T, move the prior to P"** is keyed on an **aggregate**. The aggregate cannot see the composition that produced it — so the rule can fire on a print whose components say the **opposite** of the branch's stated reading.

**The instance (HANS, 2026-09-05).** The ECB pre-registration's §3a pre-committed: *EA flash HICP ≥3.2% → "broadening beyond energy" → move H1 from 75% to ~88%.* August printed **3.3%** and the rule fired. But **energy went 10.3%→14.3% and did all the work, while services fell 3.3%→3.0% and core fell 2.5%→2.4%.** The print was **narrowing and energy-concentrated** — it cleared a bar written to detect broadening. Worse, the composition **strengthened** the desk's original below-consensus case, which the rule had just pushed *up* by 13 points.

**What to do, in order:**
1. **Apply the rule as written.** A conditional you revise after seeing the number is not a conditional — that is the whole value being spent. Move the prior.
2. **Record the disagreement in the same breath, in the appendix, before the outcome.** "The rule was right for the wrong reason; written fresh today the branch would read ~80%, not 88%." Both numbers stay on the record so the *row* and the *calibration* can be graded separately.
3. **Do NOT loosen or re-word the trigger to prevent recurrence** — that trades a loud, safe rule for a silent, certifying one `[[finding_loosening_a_check_to_kill_a_false_alarm_inverts_the_failure_direction]]`.

**The forward fix — write branches with two legs:** a **trigger** (the aggregate, mechanically checkable) **and a composition test** (what the branch claims is happening underneath). Disagreement between them is not noise to resolve — **it is the finding**, and it is the only part of the exercise that teaches you anything.

⚠️ **The trap that makes this hard to catch:** the rule firing *toward your registered position* feels like confirmation, so nothing prompts the composition check. It is the same trigger gap as `[[finding_confounds_align_with_the_prior_you_brought]]` — a result that agrees with you does not feel like it needs auditing.
