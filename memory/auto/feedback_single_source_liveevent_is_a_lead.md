A sub-agent's or news-secondary's report of a live/breaking event is where verification **starts**, not a fact you can elevate. AEOLUS (2026-07-22) took a research agent's "DOE Energy Emergency Alert + PJM forecast record peak 166,304 MW Thursday" and elevated it to a 🔴, told Will it was "the most time-sensitive item," and routed it — all on one secondary (ABC News). WATT (canonical for power) refuted it the same night on 4 primaries: no 7/22 DOE §202(c) order, PJM board quiet, EIA-930 running 36 GW *below* mid-July, the record event was 7/2-4 only. It was `fused_true_facts_false_premise` — real earlier-July facts welded to a false 7/22 date — and it even contradicted AEOLUS's own KB (the record had already broken 7/2, so "first to break it Thursday" is impossible).

**Why:** live-event claims arrive pre-framed as the day's alarm, which creates urgency-pressure to route *before* verifying. Research agents and news secondaries confabulate specific figures/dates and conflate adjacent events. Elevating one propagates the error to Will and downstream agents, then costs a multi-surface retraction.

**How to apply:** before elevating any live-event claim to 🔴 / "time-sensitive for Will" / a channel upgrade, run three gates — **(a) PRIMARY**: corroborate against the primary (DOE/PJM/NHC/CPC/BLS/EDGAR…), never a secondary alone; **(b) INTERNAL CONSISTENCY**: check it against your own logged KB — a claim that contradicts a recorded fact is a red flag; **(c) CANONICAL OWNER FIRST**: if an owner exists (WATT/power, BRENT/oil, CORAL/FL, REGINALD/credit), route for their primary-check *before* elevating, not after. The cross-agent reconciliation backstop works (WATT caught this fast) — but it should be the second line, not the first.
