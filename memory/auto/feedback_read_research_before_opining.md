Do not give position-level views (hold/sell, earnings outlook, catalyst predictions) without first reading the relevant subfolder research. REGINALD has deep research in WAL/, OZK/, etc. with KB.tsv, THESIS.md, EARNINGS_PREP.md, and SCENARIOS.md.

**Why:** REGINALD gave a cautious "I don't have strong conviction WAL earnings will be bad" based only on microstructure data, without reading WAL/EARNINGS_PREP.md which documents three independent vectors, quantified thresholds, and a full decision matrix. After reading the folder, the view changed to "more bullish on the puts." Will flagged this: "And this is with the context of all the research we have done in WAL's subfolder right?" — catching that the research wasn't informing the view.

**How to apply:** Before opining on any position, check if there's a subfolder with research (WAL/, OZK/, etc.). If there is, read STATUS.md and key files (THESIS.md, EARNINGS_PREP.md) before giving a view. State explicitly what informed your assessment.
