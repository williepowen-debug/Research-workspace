In the 2026-07-12 six-agent first-session wave (WATT/VULCAN/MIDAS/OSPREY/FALCON/HOMER), 3 of 6 agents falsified assertions from their own build sessions on the FIRST live data pull:

- **MIDAS:** the build session's M1 framing ("gold structurally bid despite rising real yields") was asserted without a spot pull and reversed on the first one (gold −18.6%/90d vs +36bp real yields) → thesis re-derived as v2.
- **OSPREY:** the inherited "swept-complete through 7/12" mark carried the right date but wrong content — 4 material strikes missing. *A swept-complete date is not proof of swept-complete content.*
- **FALCON (round 2):** the inherited "production spared for the entire war" decoupling rationale was cycle-scoped, not war-scoped — the founding-mandate ledger backfill showed production-class assets repeatedly hit in the pre-MOU phase.

**Why:** build sessions (DAEDALUS scaffolds, promotions, splits) write plausible domain framings without live pulls — they encode the builder's summary of parent material, not verified current state. Sibling of [[finding_daedalus_encode_existing_needs_live_read]] (that one is about encoding existing *handles*; this one is about founding *assertions*).

**How to apply:** when spawning a new/promoted/split agent's first real session, task an explicit falsification pass: verify seeded figures against primaries, treat thesis framings as hypotheses pending the first live pull, and treat inherited completeness marks (swept-through dates, "current as of" stamps) as claims to test. Expect and reward day-one self-falsification — it is the first session's highest-value output, not a defect in the build.
