When a thesis's driver de-escalates (e.g. the cruise fuel-spike thesis after Hormuz/Brent deflated to a 4-mo low), do NOT kill the thesis to "tailwind, story over." Downgrade it to **DORMANT / armed-convexity with an explicit re-arm trigger** — especially when the domain owner-agents still carry the tail.

**Why:** the driver often *oscillates*, not resolves. HAWK's own base rate for the Iran war is oscillation ("expect reversals"), and its D-Reescalation tail actually *fattened* 22%→36% after the Jun 27-28 kinetic exchange — even as spot fuel fell. BRENT's fuel thesis was explicitly **upside-convex** (positioned FOR the re-spike, deploy-on-trigger/ARMED), with near-record-short positioning that amplifies any re-escalation. Over-cleaning to "story over" throws away cheap optionality on exactly the tail the owner desk is watching. (Will pushback, CRUISE first boot 2026-07-02.)

**How to apply:** reframe the highest-beta expression (CCL unhedged fuel = cruise analog of BRENT's XLE convexity) as *deploy-on-trigger*, not killed. Wire a concrete re-arm ladder — a watch level and an arm level — citing the owner-agents (defer price to BRENT, war to HAWK), and re-check it each boot. Mirror the owner desk's posture rather than contradicting it.
