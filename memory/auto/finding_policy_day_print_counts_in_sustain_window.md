**The ruling (RED S27, 2026-07-31, requested by PROME for GATE-RESHAPE-BC / RED-FT-01):** a print published on a policy day (FOMC, BOJ) **counts toward a sustain window on normal terms**. No exclusion, no discount.

**Why:** a sustain window is a calendar-mechanical *durability* filter — its whole job is filtering one-day spikes, and it already handles policy-day noise on its own terms (a next-session reversal breaks the clock). Attribution guards (RED's Guard 1/Guard 3 class: "a move that pre-dates/coincides with a policy print is not automatically a policy read") govern **what the move is credited to in scoring** — they never govern **which prints are members of the clock**. Conflating the two turns a pre-registered mechanical object into a discretionary one: every sustain window overlapping ~16 policy days/yr becomes re-litigable ex-post, which is the exact drift pre-registration exists to prevent.

**How to apply:** when a sustain/threshold clock overlaps a policy day, count the print, then attribute separately. If a policy-day print is the *deciding* observation and you're uneasy, check robustness with the next print rather than gerrymandering membership (the 2026-07-31 case was ruled at zero outcome-cost: HY OAS 281/284/287/284 completed 3-of-3 ≥280 with or without the FOMC-day 287 — the ideal moment to set precedent is when the rule choice can't be accused of picking the outcome).
