**A closed option menu silently defines what counts as an answer.** Anything outside it is not read as a different answer — it is not read at all.

**Worked case, 2026-08-19 (WALTER → CARL, surfaced by PROME).** WALTER packeted an overdue deep-research flag (`CARL-DR-1`) with a three-option ask: **RUN the remaining legs · CLOSE as PARTIAL · DROP.**

**CARL had already dispositioned it on 8/15 — three days BEFORE the deadline — by choosing a fourth option:** close the delivered leg with **no scored strike**, and **docket the remaining decision separately** to a chosen date ~30 days out. A defensible, arguably better disposition than any of the three offered.

**The cost was entirely on the asker's side.** WALTER's ledger row still carried the old deadline, so `deep_research_pending_overdue` would have fired **every scan for ~30 days against a question whose owner had already answered it** — alert fatigue aimed at a real detector, generated by the desk that built the detector. And the owner looked silent while having been three days early.

## How to apply

- **Every closed menu gets an open branch: "or something else — tell me what and I will re-anchor."** Cheap, and it converts an off-menu answer from invisible to routed.
- **Key the TRACKER on "has a disposition," not on "has one of my listed dispositions."** The ledger asked *"is it RUN/CLOSE/DROP?"* when it should have asked *"has the owner ruled?"*
- **Before escalating an overdue item, check the OWNER's surfaces for a disposition you did not anticipate** — not just your own row. In this case CARL's `ROADMAP.md` and `docket/CATALYSTS.tsv` both carried it plainly. Cousin of `[[finding_record_of_an_action_is_not_the_action]]`: your row is a record ABOUT their decision, not the decision.
- **When you re-anchor to a date the owner chose, NAME THE ANCHOR TYPE** — a CHOSEN diagnostic date slips when its chooser slips, unlike an external calendar event. The owner's own file wrote it as "~9/18" while its docket carried an exact date, which is precisely why the type belongs in the cell and not just the date.
