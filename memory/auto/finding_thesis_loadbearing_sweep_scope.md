When running a news sweep over a gap window (multi-day offline, post-vacation, after a long focus session), scope it by what is LOAD-BEARING TO THESIS — specific mechanism tests, falsification anchors, counter-signal indicators — NOT by topical breadth of the agent's domain.

**Why:** Validated CARL Jun 5 2026 head-to-head. CARL ran a 6-query consumer-domain-coverage sweep (credit DQ / foreclosure / BNPL / subprime auto ABS / student loans / insurance + small biz). Prome reviewed and reframed into 4 thesis-load-bearing buckets: (1) Iran/Brent/gas pump CRL-08 re-trigger check, (2) bank/ABS/CMBS specific (EART/AMCAR/SDART subordinate tranches + Tricolor/Wilmington thread), (3) HY OAS / credit-spread market verdict on masking thesis, (4) Q1 stragglers / single-name credit events.

Prome's framing found the highest-value item of the entire session — HY OAS 272bps tightening as a counter-signal to the masking thesis — that CARL's consumer-domain framing missed entirely (HY OAS wasn't in any of the 6 topical buckets). The Carvana prime ABS find that both frames caught was equally surfaced. The energy/Brent/gas bucket was in scope under Prome's framing as a thesis check (CRL-08 re-arm verification) but excluded under CARL's "stay in consumer-domain" framing.

**How to apply:**
- Before kicking off a sweep, list the agent's load-bearing thesis components: active predictions awaiting data, vectors at downgrade-trigger thresholds, counter-signal indicators (HY OAS for credit-stress thesis; VIX for vol-thesis; positioning for momentum-thesis), and recent mechanism-extension tests
- Build sweep buckets around those, not around domain topical coverage
- Topical coverage feels comprehensive; load-bearing coverage is comprehensive on what matters
- A market-verdict indicator (HY OAS, IG OAS, breakeven spreads, vol surface, equity-credit divergence) is almost always load-bearing because it confronts the thesis directly even when it agrees — keep one in every sweep
- Document explicit "bucket N: no thesis-relevant news in window" findings, so gaps are checked-not-skipped
- Pair with [[feedback_single_month_subcomponent_skepticism]] applied at integration step

Cross-agent applicability: SAM (JGB / USDJPY / BOJ-policy thesis-tests), VIOLET (vol-regime tests + Z-score counter-signals), BRENT (oil-substance vs tape-divergence), REGINALD (bank stress + CMBS tests), HENRY (vol/positioning), BROCK (any). Generic pattern fits any agent maintaining a thesis with named falsification anchors.
