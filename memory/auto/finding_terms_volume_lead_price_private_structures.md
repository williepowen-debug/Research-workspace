**The pattern (named 2026-07-20, CREED×BROCK×SHADE cross-read):** this cycle's credit stress is deliberately housed in structures WITHOUT a public tape (gated evergreen funds, GP-led levered secondaries, insurance-wrapped 144A bonds, statutory-only L3 books, FABN programs). Consequence: price-triggered watches under-fire BY CONSTRUCTION — "no arms-length print exists" can be the design, not a data gap. Three domains found the same replacement observable independently:

- BROCK: CCLFX discretionary accommodation EXHAUSTED then WITHDRAWN while redemption demand rose 14%→17% (liquidity-TERMS, not price).
- SHADE: Athene FABN gross issuance $13.4B FY25 → $2.0B Q1'26 + a 43-48bp peer-relative spread penalty (funding-TERMS, no downgrade anywhere).
- CREED: CMBS defeasance at a decade-low $5.2B with only trophy assets transacting (VOLUME, while REIT equity rallied).

**Why:** honoring the tape is correct discipline but has a known blind side when stress migrates to tape-less wrappers; a calm/rallying price surface is NOT evidence of absence there.

**How to apply:** when building or grading a watch on private-credit / insurer-wrapper / CRE-recognition stress, ask "what would the TERMS/VOLUME tell be?" alongside any price line — accommodation/cap changes, issuance run-rates, transaction volumes, satisfaction ratios, top-up usage. Treat a price-only watch on a no-print asset class as mis-specified.
