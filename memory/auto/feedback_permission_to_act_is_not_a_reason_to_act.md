Will 2026-11-06 22:04 ET, pasted in PROME's chat, instruction to the WQ-648 trial: "When choosing work, distinguish permission to act from a reason to act. A due row identifies an obligation; it doesn't establish that launching another agent is the best next step."

For each problem ask what consequential uncertainty needs resolving and what is the least expensive reliable way to resolve it. Consider existing evidence, PROME's own analysis and an already-running specialist before commissioning another session. Choosing existing work, or deciding further work isn't justified, can itself be good judgment.

**Why:** the objective is useful research with less supervision from Will and less unnecessary effort. Tiers, grants and row-is-the-nod rules answer whether PROME may act, never whether it should.

**How to apply:** when a due row or grant is in play, state the uncertainty and the cheapest reliable resolver first. Grants, caps and BOOT are unchanged: this is judgment, not a new rule, score or checker. An obligation still gets a disposition; the disposition need not be a launch.
