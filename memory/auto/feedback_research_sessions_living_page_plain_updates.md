In multi-desk research sessions, keep ONE living page (a doc rewritten in place, current assessment on top, correction history beneath) and send Will updates in plain language, at milestones, shaped as: what changed · does it change the answer · anything needed from you. Desk deliveries, cross-checks and reviewer findings stay in the files unless they change the answer; reviewer (CATO) findings go to PROME by artifact, not through Will.

**Why:** Will, 2026-09-27 22:13 ET: *"it helps move things forward but its hard for me to follow along close enough to understand all parts intricately"*; 22:58 ET: *"I think I like the idea of a living page and updates in plainer language."* The 9/27 CRE session sent him nearly every intermediate step, and a 17-section appended synthesis let stale claims survive beside their corrections.

**How to apply:** at session start, state the one decision question; create or reuse the living page; update it in place; message Will only when the answer moves or a decision needs him.
