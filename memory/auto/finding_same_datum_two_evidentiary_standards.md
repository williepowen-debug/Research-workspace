When red-teaming a thesis, check whether the **load-bearing datum is held to a consistent evidentiary standard across the narrative AND the thesis's own trigger/falsification rails.** A thesis is over-confident when the same piece of evidence is treated as *decisive confirmation* in the prose but as *half-confirmation* in the structured trigger logic.

**Concrete instance (2026-06-26, RED red-team on BRENT energy adjudication):** BRENT marked sub-$75 Brent "STRUCTURAL, not coiled-spring" with the decisive evidence being a single first-true-post-MOU CFTC COT print (continued long-liquidation read as de-risking). But BRENT's *own* Path-B Trigger #3 graded that identical print "**1/2 — needs a 2nd weekly decline to re-fire.**" Same datum, two standards: decisive in the label, provisional in the trigger. RED's catch downgraded the regime claim from settled to "structural LEAN, unconfirmed pending the 2nd-week print." (The print also showed shorts *building* into record-low inventory — a two-sided squeeze setup, not clean structural de-risking — reinforcing that one print couldn't carry the regime label.)

**Why:** Narrative prose has no schema enforcing evidentiary discipline; trigger/falsification tables do (they pre-register "what counts as confirmed"). So when prose and the agent's own trigger rail disagree about whether a datum confirms, the **trigger rail is usually the honest one** and the prose is where confidence leaked in. This is a close cousin of [[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]] (mechanism-intact ≠ threshold-stuck) and [[finding_cluster_adversarial_catches_framing]] (synthesis over-claims an elegant unification) — but the specific tell here is the *single datum cited at mismatched standards*, not a mechanism/threshold conflation.

**How to apply:** When verifying a confident thesis label, locate the 1-2 load-bearing data points and cross-check them against the thesis's OWN pre-registered triggers/falsification tests. If the trigger logic grades a datum partial/pending while the narrative treats it as decisive, flag the label as outrunning the evidence and re-anchor confidence to the trigger's standard (the conservative one). Single-print confirmation of a regime claim is especially suspect — demand the 2nd print the trigger already asks for.
