**The "buying options is negative-EV" doctrine is regime-bounded AND instrument-split — do not apply it flat across a long-premium book.** Dug 2026-07-17 (Will-directed, from a tastytrade-video prompt → `AGENTS/TERRY/options/RESEARCH.md`).

- **Baseline (SPX):** Dew-Becker & Giglio (Chicago Fed / Yale, 2026-06-02, SPX 1987-2025) find a **structural break ~Aug-2012**: post-2012 index-option alphas/information ratios are **statistically indistinguishable from zero**. So the classic negative premium (Coval-Shumway 2001, Bakshi-Kapadia 2003; the whole tastytrade premium-selling edge) describes a window ~1987-2010 that **has closed for SPX**. ⚠️ alpha≈0 = **fairly priced, NOT profitable** — the edge still must come entirely from the thesis.
- **The split — this is the load-bearing part.** The break is **index-specific** (mechanism = retail gaining ability to *sell* SPX options + dealer gamma normalizing). It does **not** transfer uniformly:
  - **RATES (TLT crash-ladder) → pays the FULL vol tax (persists).** Bond VRP real+large (Mueller-Vedolin-Yen, Sharpe≈2, but data ends 2010/pre-break); persisted post-break (swaption VRP live 2015-2020; MOVE-implied > realized ~82nd pctile 2026); rates options are **institution-dominated**, so the retail-supply mechanism that killed equity VRP has no analog. **MEDIUM-confidence inference** — no direct post-2012 rates break-test found. Cuts toward expensive: rates VRP is most negative at **short-dated/OTM**, the ladder's exact end.
  - **SINGLE-NAME (HBAN tail) → structurally CHEAP.** The VRP lives at the **index** level as a **correlation-risk premium** (institutions over-buy *index* puts; SPX implied corr ~39.5 vs realized ~32.5, implied>realized ~70% of days). Single names don't carry it → ~fairly priced. **Local exception: single-name IV gets rich into EARNINGS** — HBAN Oct-16 spans the 7/23 print, so it likely paid event-vol premium at entry.

**Third axis — DEPTH (the deep-OTM skew/crash premium, dug 2026-07-17):** the skew premium was real and **monotonic in depth** (Bondarenko 1987-2000: ATM puts −39%/mo, deep-OTM −95%/mo — near-total monthly decay is the base rate for BUYING deep tail insurance). DBG's post-2012 collapse **reaches to 5% OTM and into jump risk** (further than "ATM only"), **BUT the deep 8-13% strip is untested** and is where a residual premium most plausibly survives — price-insensitive institutional hedging + near-pure-jump strikes retail can't easily supply. So the live tails stack their taxes: **crash-ladder (TLT, ~8-11% OTM) pays rates-VRP + deep-skew = DOUBLE**; **HBAN (single-name, ~13% OTM into 7/23 print) is structurally cheap but its deep strike + earnings-event is where its small premium concentrates.**

**How to apply:** when justifying/pricing a long-premium tail, name **both** axes — instrument class AND strike depth. Don't let SPX "options are fair now" bleed onto (a) a **rates** tail or (b) a **deep** strike; both still pay. Justify paying only via convexity — TT-02: the short-strangle's terminal-window CVaR explosion IS the long tail's payoff window; the research **prices** the tax, it doesn't veto the trade. A **single-name near-ATM** tail is the genuinely cheap case; a **deep single-name into earnings** is not. **Two acknowledged gaps (NOT actively tracked — recognize if you happen upon them, don't hunt; Will 2026-07-17 declined a standing watch as un-serviceable over a months+ horizon):** (a) no post-2012 deep-OTM (8-13%) put-return study exists — genuine literature gap; (b) the rates verdict wants a direct post-2012 break-test. If either crosses the desk during options/VRP/tail research, it resolves this finding's depth/rates axis — check the **Bollerslev/Todorov/Kelly-Jiang** orbit or DBG follow-ups. Deep premium *collapsed* → ladder skew tax small (upgrade); *persisted* → confirms the DOUBLE tax. Either way it prices the tax, doesn't veto.
