**Agreement among secondaries is evidence about the STORY they are all covering, not about the facts they all left out.** They select the same newsworthy angle, so their omissions CORRELATE. Adding a fourth agreeing source adds nothing, because the deficiency is CLASS, not COUNT.

ZHAO, 2026-09-19. A load-bearing claim (China's export-control suspension expires 2026-11-10) rested on **three strong, independent secondaries** — a China-policy consultancy, industry trade press, and CSIS. All agreed. The row was honestly graded B2 with "primary not fetched" stated unprompted, and the pull was registered as owed. Will directed it. **The primary took ~20 minutes and moved three separate claims, every one in the direction of understatement:**
- the "rare-earth package" was **six** announcements, two of them **lithium batteries and industrial diamond** — a whole supply chain nobody had been told about;
- a licensing clause reported as military-gated was a `或者` **disjunction**; the semiconductor limb was standalone, civilian, and reached production equipment, test gear and materials;
- ★ **the rules had been suspended BEFORE THEY EVER COMMENCED** — most by one day, the furthest-reaching by 24 — so what everyone was calling *restraint expiring* was a **first use**, with no licensing practice, no compliance base and no precedent.

⚠️ **SCOPED 2026-09-19, same day, after review — the original wording over-claimed twice and the claim is narrowed, not withdrawn:** it said *no secondary carried* the finding and treated the three sources as *independent*. **Three sources were inspected. Neither universal absence across all secondary coverage nor causal independence was established** — shared sourcing is plausible and unproven. **Correlated omission is the mechanism worth carrying; the count of what else exists is not evidence I have.**

**Why the third was invisible to all three sources, and this is the generalisable part:** *"a rule that never started"* **is not a story.** No outlet reports the commencement date of something that got suspended, because nothing happened on that date. ⇒ **certain fact-classes are SYSTEMATICALLY absent from secondary coverage, not randomly absent** — and no amount of cross-source agreement can surface them, because none of the sources are looking. **A suspended instrument's commencement date is one. Others: which clauses of a multi-clause rule were severed, effective-date staging, what an amendment did NOT touch, the exhaustive scope list.**

**CRUISE, 2026-09-20 (n+1 — the COUNT/INDEPENDENCE face of the same coin, KB-CRU-113).** Weighing whether an aggressive-discount signal was corroborated across houses, CRUISE first read Investing.com and Yahoo as **two** independent outlets; they are **ONE Sam Boughedda wire story** — n=1, not n=2. And the related trap it caught in its own reasoning: a source that **predates** the others rules out **copying**, NOT a **shared upstream channel check both drew on** — temporal precedence is not independence. Truist survived as a genuine third house, but *"the shared-source caveat is substantially answered"* was withdrawn as too strong. Same lesson as ZHAO's, arrived at from the other direction: ZHAO over-counted what agreeing secondaries OMIT; CRUISE over-counted how many secondaries there were.

**How to apply:**
- **Count sources, then ask what they all share.** Same beat, same trade press, same news cycle, same originating wire ⇒ **treat N agreeing secondaries as n=1 on everything outside the headline.** Confidence caps at B until a primary is read, whatever N is.
- **For any instrument — a rule, order, statute, filing — the primary is cheap and these five facts are the ones secondaries drop:** ① **the exhaustive scope list** (secondaries name the newsworthy item, never the full enumeration) · ② **commencement vs issuance vs suspension dates, per clause** · ③ **conjunctions vs disjunctions** in operative text (`或者`/"or" merged into one condition NARROWS the rule and reads natural) · ④ **what is explicitly NOT covered** · ⑤ **staged effective dates.**
- ⛔ **"I stated the caveat" is not "I did the work."** This desk graded B2, disclosed the gap, and registered the pull — all correct — and the claim still propagated to three desks in a form that was wrong three ways for a day. `[[finding_naming_a_caveat_can_substitute_for_fixing_it]]`
- **Precedence ≠ independence.** *"It was published first, so it isn't copying the others"* rules out copying ONLY — not a shared upstream source all of them drew on. And two outlets under one wire byline are n=1 whatever their mastheads say (CRUISE KB-CRU-113, 2026-09-20).
- ⚠️ **On correction, fix the PARENT row in place with a pointer**, don't just append new rows — the consumer re-reads the row that is already in their hands.
