**Finding (2026-07-09 eve):** after a week of regime changes (truce collapse, energy re-arm, BND-11 refutation, WAL/ZION date fixes), PROME ran a fleet-wide self-sweep across 11 warm agents, each seeded with (a) a **grep list of tonight's canonical facts** (WAL 7/21, X1 CLOSED, BND-11 NOT-FIRED, truce collapsed, QT ended…), (b) header/mtime staleness w/ fix-or-FROZEN, (c) dead-pointer check, (d) overdue-prediction grading. Result: **30+ real defects fixed in ~1 hour**, including **3 actively INVERTED forward-facing files** — ORACLE's TRADE.md Iran row still read "de-escalation = oil downside" while the escalation tail traded at 48%; SAM's THESIS still said "Iran deal SIGNED / oil-in-yen DORMANT"; HAWK's CALENDAR carried the *inverse* scenario ordering (C57/D35 vs live D46-co-dominant). Plus two ledger-drift catches (ZHAO's ZHA-09/10 never written to PREDICTIONS.tsv; BROCK's BRK-29 formally OPEN 5 days after its prose LAPSE grade) and a dead 5-week-old "shadow-adj 270k" overlay still in HEARTBEAT.

**The pattern:** rot concentrates in **secondary surfaces** — CALENDAR, TRADE.md, THESIS, NEXUS_BRIEF — because same-session refreshes touch STATUS and stop. Five agents independently found the identical class. A stale secondary surface is worse than a missing one: it looks authoritative and reads as anti-signal (the inversion cases). Sibling classes: [[finding-derived-surface-band-rot]] (threshold tables), [[finding_deep_research_stale_vintage_headline]] (vintage figures), [[finding_ledger_drift_behind_narrative]] (prose-vs-ledger). Tonight generalized the vintage lesson beyond deep-research: BRK-24's "new" analysis figures were FY2023-recycled per the owner's own primary; PortWatch/war-risk figures were pre-event vintage feeding a next-day capital gate — **a published number's vintage is a claim requiring verification, same as its value.**

**How to apply:** (1) after any multi-day regime shift, run the seeded self-sweep across warm agents — the canonical-facts grep list is what makes it cheap and high-yield (agents don't rediscover canon, they diff against it); (2) same-session refreshes must touch the agent's forward-facing secondary surfaces or explicitly banner them stale — STATUS-only refresh is the rot vector; (3) any figure feeding a gate/grade carries its vintage stamp, and pre-event-vintage baselines get written into the grading packet so "fresh" is decidable (the Friday fresh-legs baselines).

**STATE-TOKEN VARIANT (2026-07-16, BOND episode):** the same rot class operates *within a single session* when a gate state changes mid-session. BOND wrote "ARM2 FIRED-UNEXECUTED / consequence owed" across its surfaces in the morning (true at write time); Will's NO-ADD decision landed mid-session; BOND's closeout appended fresh material without refreshing the old token. It took THREE correction rounds because each sweep was scoped too narrow: round 1 fixed STATUS/SCRATCH/KB, missed the **live grading template** (an instrument another agent grades against) and the **VX ledger row**; round 2's "final grep = CLEAN" was scoped to the files it had touched. **Rules:** (a) a state-token change sweeps ledgers (TSV rows) + live templates/instruments, not just STATUS/SCRATCH; (b) verification = `grep -rn <token>` from REPO ROOT over the agent's whole dir, excluding point-in-time outbox memos (those get a correction banner, never a rewrite — don't falsify history); (c) coordinator verifies by disk-grep, never by the agent's confirmation.
