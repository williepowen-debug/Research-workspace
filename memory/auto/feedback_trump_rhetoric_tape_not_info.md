Trump "deal close" / "talks productive" rhetoric is a tape catalyst, NOT substantive information about resolution probability.

**Why:** Will calibration Jun 1 2026 PM, BRENT session: "Trump has declared a deal is close repeatedly with nothing to show for it." Concrete pattern through May 2026 on the Iran/Hormuz MOU: declared "deal close" at Witkoff round (late April), May 25 weekend, May 29 Situation Room, May 31 "orderly and constructive" — none of these moved the bilateral substance. The MOU itself went rumor → "mostly agreed" → walked in <7 days entirely on Iran's bilateral demands (uranium, $12B frozen assets, no-tolls, Lebanon ceasefire enforcement). Those grievances are not Trump-rhetoric-fixable. The kinetic clock (Kuwait missile intercepts, IRGC threats, drone strikes) ran independently of his statements throughout.

**How to apply:**
- When Trump rhetoric hits ("deal close," "productive call," "great progress"), DO update tactical positioning for short-dated P&L — one tweet can retrace $5-10/bbl (or analogous in other assets) in minutes regardless of substance. Tape impact is real.
- Do NOT update Bayesian on resolution probability from Trump rhetoric alone. The signal is uncorrelated with bilateral reality.
- Substantive watches (the things that DO update probability): counterparty walkback / re-engagement signals (Iran, in the Hormuz case), key sub-principals' positional moves (Rubio "Plan B" allies-only activation analog), kinetic escalation/de-escalation, operational confirms (P&I resumption, vessel traffic, etc.).
- Apply across any active Trump-touching channel: HAWK (Iran/Hormuz), SAM (Japan/trade), HENRY (Fed-pressure rhetoric), CARL (tariff threats), MARCO (tariffs). Same pattern: rhetoric for tape, substance for thesis.
- Calibration is current-cycle-specific (2026 second term, Hormuz crisis). Re-test if pattern breaks — if a future "deal close" is followed by material substance within 48-72hr, downgrade this rule's confidence.
