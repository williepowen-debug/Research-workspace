An agent's unprocessed inbound lane is not backlog hygiene — it is the **falsification channel** for any thesis that agent owns. Scan inbound *headlines* at boot for anything naming your canonical claim or its key metric, even when you are not spawned for inbox processing. That scan takes seconds; full processing can still be deferred.

**Why:** HAWK went dark 13 days (2026-07-12→25) while its STATUS *and* NEXUS_BRIEF carried "Brent decoupled ~$76-79" — the oil-decoupling thesis being HAWK's own canonical property (FLOW-HAWK-19). Brent broke ~$85 on 7/16 and printed its first-ever >$100 settle 7/23. On catch-up, the **oldest of 17 unread WALTER signals — dispatched 7/16 — led with "oil decoupling BROKE on RISK-PREMIUM, not one lost barrel"**, carrying the exact premium-vs-supply-loss discriminator HAWK then spent a whole session re-deriving from scratch. The agent's own CLAUDE.md rule ("do NOT process inbox on normal spawns unless pending") made deferral feel correct each time, so 17 signals accumulated. Two compounding harms: the wrong claim was **propagating** (NEXUS boot-reads NEXUS_BRIEF), and the correction was **already paid for** — a sibling had done the work and routed it.

**How to apply:**
- Treat "does any inbound headline name my own load-bearing claim?" as a boot step distinct from "process the inbox." Cheap, and it is the one that catches thesis death.
- **A dark window is not neutral for a thesis-owning surface.** Time preserves the text, not the claim. Any gap >1 week → explicitly re-check the canonical claim against external primaries *before* the surface is cited or consumed by anyone.
- The rot is worse on the **primary** lane than a dormant one, because nobody expects the live surface to be the stale one — see [[finding_passive_surface_rot_push_not_dashboard]] and [[finding_ledger_drift_behind_narrative]].
- Same session, same shape, second instance: regenerating a *derived* surface surfaced a sibling's 13-day-old analysis that partly falsified a prediction HAWK had registered an hour earlier — read the source artifact's header/analysis, not just its row count ([[finding_read_the_artifacts_own_header_first]]).
