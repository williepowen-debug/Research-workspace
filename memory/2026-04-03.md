## Session 1: News Sweep Build (8:51 AM - 11:15 AM ET)

### Built: News Sweep Tool (`FORGE/tools/news-sweep/`)
Full thesis-tagged news monitoring system. Designed, researched, built, tested, deployed in one session.

**Architecture (based on research — TDT/FSD, Salesforce EDR, Trilogy Knowledge Tree, Google Memory Bank, DeepMind multi-agent):**
- 15 Google News RSS queries (thesis-tagged per agent)
- 3 RSS feeds (FT, BBC Business, BBC World)  
- 1 web scrape (ZeroHedge)
- Entity index (~35 entities, manually curated)
- WATCH_FOR lists (9 agents, forward-looking gap detection)
- Source quality weighting (4 tiers)
- Noise title filters (CRE awards, FDIC conferences, etc.)
- Escalation qualifiers (KNOWN entity + "SEC"/"bankruptcy"/etc. = DEVELOPMENT)
- Classification: NEW_WATCH_HIT > NEW_ALERT > DEVELOPMENT > NEW_WATCH > NEW > KNOWN > NOISE
- Routes to agent inboxes. Suppresses KNOWN with confirmation density counts.
- Cron M-F 8:30 AM ET + Telegram push. Dashboard `/api/news`.

**Key design decisions:**
- Forward-looking (WATCH_FOR: what agents want to know) > backward-looking (what's already known)
- Centralized sweep (supervisor pattern from DeepMind research) — agents process, don't collect
- Entity index manually maintained by Prome (auto-generation is v2)
- Will only sees priority items (WATCH_FOR hits, ALERTs, DEVELOPMENTs) + confirmation density counts

**Test results (live run):**
- 282 raw → 271 after dedup → 162 actionable, 97 KNOWN suppressed, 12 noise filtered
- 12 WATCH_FOR hits (all legitimate after fixing 50%→100% word match threshold)
- 2 ALERTs (keyword matches)
- 9 agent inboxes received routed files

**Bugs found and fixed:**
- "SEC" matching "Second" (substring) → fixed with word-boundary regex
- "regional bank" query too broad → tightened to "regional bank stress"  
- WATCH_FOR 50% word threshold → 147 false hits. Changed to ALL words required → 12 hits
- "oil embargo" matching Cuba politics → tightened to "Iran oil embargo"
- "subprime auto DQ above 7%" matching all subprime auto news → tightened to "subprime auto delinquency rate"
- "healthcare layoffs" matching any layoff → tightened to "healthcare sector layoffs"
