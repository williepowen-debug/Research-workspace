# Burry Scion Analyst Template

*Extracted from "Final Stop GameStop: The Jig is Up" (Jan 26, 2026)*

---

## Overview

Michael Burry's valuation framework used at Scion Asset Management. Focus on tangible value, cash generation, and capital allocation - not narrative or momentum.

---

## Core Framework

### 1. Tangible Book Value Anchor
- Start with **tangible book value** as floor
- Buying at or below 1x TBV = margin of safety
- Intangibles (goodwill, brand) get zero credit until proven

### 2. Adjusted Free Cash Flow
- LTM FCF as primary earnings metric
- Adjust for:
  - Stock-based compensation (ADD BACK as cash, but account for dilution separately)
  - One-time items
  - Working capital distortions
  - Capex normalization

### 3. Capital Allocation Assessment
**Key questions:**
- What is management doing with excess cash?
- Buybacks: At what valuation? (Below book = good, above 2x book = destroying value)
- Dividends: Sustainable? Growing?
- M&A: Track record? Price discipline?
- Debt: Paying down or levering up?

### 4. Management Alignment
- Insider ownership (skin in game)
- Compensation structure (salary vs equity vs performance)
- Capital allocation track record over 5-10 years
- Do they buy stock with personal money?

### 5. Margin of Safety Calculation
```
Intrinsic Value = Tangible Book + PV(Normalized FCF)
Margin of Safety = (Intrinsic Value - Current Price) / Intrinsic Value
```
Target: 30-50% margin of safety for new positions

---

## Red Flags (Automatic Disqualification)

1. **Trading above 3x tangible book** with no clear FCF justification
2. **Persistent dilution** (share count growing >3% annually)
3. **Management selling** while company buying back
4. **Convertible debt** at terms favoring holders over company
5. **"Story stock"** - narrative driving price, not fundamentals

---

## Application Notes

### For Shorts (Inverse the Template)
- Trading at 5x+ book with declining FCF = short candidate
- Buybacks at elevated valuations = capital destruction
- Heavy SBC + buybacks = "buyback to nowhere" (see: NVDA)
- Retail/meme enthusiasm + weak fundamentals = fragile

### For Longs
- 1x tangible book or below
- Positive FCF yield (>5% preferred)
- Management buying personally
- Catalyst for value recognition (activist, buyout, turnaround)

## The SBC Adjustment (Cross-Reference)

See: `AGENTS/REGINALD/sub-agents/BROCK/sources/Burry_SBC_Dilution_Framework_2025-11-30.md`

**Key formula:**
```
PV = CF / (d - g + y)

Where:
- CF = Cash flow
- d = Discount rate
- g = Growth rate  
- y = Dilution rate (SBC as % of market cap)
```

**Impact:** 5% dilution rate can destroy 30-50% of stated value at typical discount rates.
