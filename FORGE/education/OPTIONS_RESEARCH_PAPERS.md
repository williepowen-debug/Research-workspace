# Options Research — Core Papers Library

**Compiled:** 2026-04-05
**Purpose:** Reference library for options mechanics, volatility risk premium, and tail hedging research

---

## TIER 1 — Immediate Access (PDF Direct Links)

### Pan & Poteshman (2006) — Information in Option Volume
*The foundational paper on option volume as a predictor of future stock prices.*
- **PDF:** https://www.mit.edu/~junpan/volume.pdf
- **Source:** MIT (Jun Pan's page)
- **Status:** Direct download

### Bakshi & Kapadia (2003) — Delta-Hedged Gains
*Empirical proof that options are systematically overpriced (negative volatility risk premium).*
- **PDF:** https://people.umass.edu/~nkapadia/docs/Bakshi_and_Kapadia_2003_RFS.pdf
- **Source:** UMass (Nikunj Kapadia's page)
- **Status:** Direct download

### Bollerslev, Tauchen & Zhou (2009) — Variance Risk Premia
*VRP predicts equity returns — theoretical foundation for why vol selling works and when it fails.*
- **PDF:** https://public.econ.duke.edu/~boller/Published_Papers/rfs_09.pdf
- **Source:** Duke (Tim Bollerslev's page)
- **Status:** Direct download

### Xing, Zhang & Zhao (2010) — Volatility Smirk
*Skew predicts future equity returns — practical signal for directional positioning.*
- **PDF (Tsinghua):** https://www.pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn/__local/A/24/EA/815CC33800CAA0F7D894E9292A1_6CC8B38B_27B25.pdf?e=.pdf
- **PDF (Cambridge):** https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/ECFD16BA9ACBDC8D577D1BD866FBEA72/S0022109010000220a.pdf/what-does-the-individual-option-volatility-smirk-tell-us-about-future-equity-returns.pdf
- **Status:** Direct download

### Gârleanu, Pedersen & Poteshman (2009) — Demand-Based Option Pricing
*Why the VRP exists: demand pressure from non-hedging investors drives systematic overpricing.*
- **PDF:** http://docs.lhpedersen.com/DBOP.pdf
- **Source:** Lasse Pedersen's site
- **Status:** Direct download

## TIER 2 — Requires Login / Navigation

### Israelov (2017) — Pathetic Protection
*The bear case against protective puts. Essential reading for sizing and monetization decisions.*
- **PDF:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/SSRN_ID2934538_code437885.pdf?abstractid=2934538&mirid=1
- **AQR Version:** https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/White-Papers/Pathetic-Protection-The-Elusive-Benefits-of-Protective-Puts
- **Status:** SSRN direct PDF or AQR white paper

### Israelov & Tummala (2018) — Being Right is Not Enough
*Shows you need top-decile vol changes to profit from long puts — addresses sizing and path dependency.*
- **SSRN:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3248500
- **AQR Version:** https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Being-Right-is-Not-Enough-Buying-Options-to-Bet-on-Higher-Realized-Volatility
- **Status:** Requires SSRN (free) login or AQR navigation

### Easley, O'Hara & Srinivas (1998) — Where Informed Traders Trade
*Foundational model on informed trading in options vs. equity markets.*
- **SSRN:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=98724
- **Wiley (paywall):** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.194060
- **Status:** SSRN usually requires free login; working paper widely available
