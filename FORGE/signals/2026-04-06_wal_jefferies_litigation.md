## Executive Summary

Western Alliance Bancorporation (WAL) sued Jefferies Financial Group on March 6, 2026 for $126.4 million tied to failed receivables-backed financing linked to First Brands Group's September 2025 bankruptcy. This represents a **fast-transmission credit loss** — losses bypass delinquency pipeline and hit P&L directly via litigation.

---

## Transaction Structure

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Western Alliance Bank** | Lender to Point Bonita-owned SPVs (non-recourse) |
| **LAM TFG I SPV LLC** | Borrower entity holding First Brands receivables |
| **Point Bonita master fund** | Owner of borrowing entities; Leucadia fund vehicle |
| **Jefferies / Leucadia Asset Mgmt** | Fund sponsor and indirect investor (~$45M exposure) |
| **First Brands Group** | Underlying servicer and receivables originator (bankrupt Sept 2025) |

**Exposure:** $715M First Brands-linked receivables (~25% of Point Bonita's $3B trade-finance portfolio)

## Timeline of Collapse

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2021 | WAL begins lending to LAM Trade Finance Group SPVs |
| Sept 2025 | First Brands bankruptcy amid fraud allegations |
| Sept 2025 | WAL learns UCC filings lapsed = technical default |
| Oct 2025 | Disputed forbearance agreement (alleged repayment by Mar 31, 2026) |
| Jan 15, 2026 | $42.1M payment made; Jefferies informs WAL no further payments |
| Mar 6, 2026 | WAL sues Jefferies for breach of contract and fraud ($126.4M) |
| Mar 8-9, 2026 | Jefferies disputes claims, argues loans were non-recourse |

---

## The Legal Dispute

**WAL's Position:**
- Jefferies agreed to complete repayment in Oct 2025 forbearance
- Breach of contract + fraud (sponsor assumed obligations beyond SPV docs)

**Jefferies' Defense:**
- "Meritless" — loans explicitly non-recourse to SPV collateral only
- WAL had diligence/audit rights throughout
- WAL requested guarantees pre-bankruptcy, both Point Bonita and Jefferies refused
