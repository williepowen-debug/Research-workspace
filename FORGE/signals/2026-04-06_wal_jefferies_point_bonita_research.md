## The Legal Dispute

**WAL's Position:**
- Jefferies agreed to complete repayment in Oct 2025 forbearance
- Breach of contract + fraud (sponsor assumed obligations beyond SPV docs)
- $42.1M paid, then stopped — evidence of acknowledgment

**Jefferies' Defense:**
- "Meritless" — loans explicitly non-recourse to SPV collateral only
- WAL had diligence/audit rights throughout
- WAL requested guarantees pre-bankruptcy, both Point Bonita and Jefferies refused

### 1. Legal Recourse (30% weight)

### 2. Collateral Perfection (25% weight)
- Were UCC filings current and perfected?
- Did liens lapse around default window?
- Were receivables double-pledged?

### 3. Cash Dominion (20% weight)
- Who controlled lockboxes in practice?
- Did First Brands retain collection control?
- Was "cash dominion" representation accurate?

### 4. Management Credibility (15% weight)
- Did WAL use its audit rights?
- Did Jefferies blur sponsor/fund boundaries?
- Were disclosures consistent?

### 5. Contagion Risk (10% weight)
- Similar structures elsewhere in WAL book?
- Other Point Bonita-style funds at Jefferies?
- Pattern or isolated case?
