## Core Thesis

Regional banks face a multi-channel stress convergence that markets are underpricing:

1. **CRE Exposure:** 8.16 percentage point gap between regional bank CRE concentration (28.7%) and large banks (6.5%). Extend-and-pretend is failing.

2. **BDC Transmission:** Private credit stress (Blue Owl gate, HRZN collapse) transmits to middle-market lending, which transmits to regional bank customers.

3. **Consumer Stress:** Wright data shows 609K borrowers went current→delinquent in one month. Employed people defaulting because underwater, not because unemployed.

4. **Japan Risk:** Independent catalyst via carry unwind. August 2024 showed KRE can drop 18% in 10 days on Japan stress alone.

5. **November Rally Unwind:** The 19% rally (Oct-Jan) was built on rate cut hopes. Fed delivered hawkish guidance. Rally should unwind.
