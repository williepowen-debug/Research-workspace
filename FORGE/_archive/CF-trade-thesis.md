## Core Mechanic: Price Inelasticity, Not Volume Growth

CF can't ramp (95-99% utilization). Nobody else can fill the gap. Demand is biologically inelastic (planting deadline). Price MUST spike to ration supply. CF is the price-taker beneficiary.

### Supply Side — Stranded
- 12.6M tons/year of Gulf urea exports stranded behind Hormuz (Qatar+Saudi+Bahrain+UAE)
- ~1M tons/month missing from global market
- 45% of globally traded urea transits Hormuz
- No strategic fertilizer reserve exists anywhere
- China restricting exports. Europe 25% curtailed on high gas costs.
- 3 layers of stranding: physical (ports), logistical (loaded vessels), commercial (insurance/finance)

### Demand Side — Inelastic
- Northern Hemisphere spring planting: corn V5-V6 window is weeks away
- Fertilizer markets operate just-in-time, "mostly made-to-order"
- Yield penalties: 7% at V10, 40% at V14 delay (Tennessee trial data)
- Farmers will pay ANY price for prompt tons rather than accept yield loss

### CF Specifics
- Production: ~17M tons ammonia capacity, ~19.1M product tons sold (2025)
- Utilization: 95-99% — cannot meaningfully ramp
- Feedstock: Henry Hub gas (~$4/MMBtu), 28% of total production costs
- Cost advantage: US gas insulated from global LNG spike. CF is low-cost producer.
- **EBITDA sensitivity: $50/ton urea = $800M EBITDA change**
- At urea $450/gas $3: EBITDA $3.6B
- At urea $500/gas $3: EBITDA $4.4B
- At urea $700/gas $3: EBITDA ~$5.6-6.0B (approaching 2022 levels)

## Bear Case
1. **US gas spike** — $3→$5 gas at fixed urea = -$600M EBITDA. But urea is rising faster than gas. Henry Hub insulated from global LNG.
2. **Quick Hormuz resolution** — scarcity premium collapses. But logistics restart takes weeks even after ceasefire. Spring window doesn't wait.
3. **Trade reshuffling** — non-Gulf exporters divert cargoes. Possible but limited: China export controls, Europe curtailed, system at capacity.
4. **Some Hormuz transits still occurring** — Fujairah staging. But intermittent ≠ reliable schedulable commerce. Insurance still prohibitive.
5. **CF operational risk** — Donaldsonville outage would be "catastrophic." Idiosyncratic, low probability.
