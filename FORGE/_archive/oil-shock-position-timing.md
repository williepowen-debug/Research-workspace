### Equity Lag: 10-27 Months After Oil Peak
- 1973-74: S&P bottomed Dec 1974, 11 months after oil peaked Jan 1974. -48% total.
- 1979-80: S&P bottomed Jul 1982, 26 months after oil peaked mid-1980. -25%.
- Our Jun/Jul/Aug put expirations cover the FIRST leg of equity decline, not the bottom.
- If oil peaks April-May 2026, equity bottom could be late 2026 to early 2028.

- 1980: Baa spreads peaked 5 months after recession began

### Airlines: Post-Deregulation = Catastrophe
- 1973-74 (regulated): Airlines passed costs through via fare increases. Survived.
- 1979-80 (deregulated): Industry went from +$877M profit to -$848M loss by 1982.
- We are in a deregulated environment. AAL CANNOT pass through costs.
- Pan Am bought National Airlines for $437M right as oil doubled. Revenue +62% but fuel +157%.

### Fertilizer: Front-Loaded, Not Lagged

- Food prices lagged 1-2 quarters (existing harvests buffer the transmission).
- **CF $115C May entry should be ASAP — fertilizer inputs spike first.**

### Bonds: Toxic in Stagflation

- 1974: Embargo lifted March. Yields kept rising through December.
- 1979-82: 10Y went 9% → 15%. Real returns -3% annually for a decade.
