## Catalyst Thesis

KRE doesn't need "new" stress to decline. The stress is already present in the data. What we're waiting for is **recognition events** — moments when markets acknowledge what's already visible.

Recognition events can be:
- Earnings misses / provision increases
- Credit fund gates / failures
- Data releases that force narrative change
- Sudden correlation (credit stress → equity repricing)
