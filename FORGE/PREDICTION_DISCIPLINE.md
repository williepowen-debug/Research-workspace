## Registration
- **finding_threshold_level_is_a_measurement_not_a_constant** ★HOT — "A threshold written as a NUMBER but defined against a MOVING quantity (a gamma flip, a fair-value line, a rolling percentile) silently decays into a stale reference — and comparing today's spot to yesterday's level manufactures a false sense of margin. Re-measure the anchor before grading distance-to-trigger, and state on the threshold whether the number is FROZEN or TRACKED." `[[finding_threshold_level_is_a_measurement_not_a_constant]]`
- **finding_threshold_spec_fails_before_world** ★HOT — A pre-registered threshold usually fails because of how it was SPECIFIED, not because the world moved — audit the spec (published? seasonal? conjunction still reachable?) before concluding anything about the thesis. `[[finding_threshold_spec_fails_before_world]]`
- **finding_compound_gate_jointly_unsatisfiable** ★HOT — A multi-leg gate can be individually satisfiable on every leg yet JOINTLY unsatisfiable in the only state that matters — base-rate gates jointly AND conditional on the trigger state, never marginally. `[[finding_compound_gate_jointly_unsatisfiable]]`
- **finding_count_the_connectives_in_versus_out** ★HOT — A state machine that enters disjunctively (any 1 of N) and exits conjunctively (all N) is a ratchet — pure arithmetic, no base rate needed `[[finding_count_the_connectives_in_versus_out]]`
- **finding_guard_scope_expires_at_the_fill** ★HOT `[[finding_guard_scope_expires_at_the_fill]]`
- **finding_pre_register_against_the_carrying_filing** — "Pre-register a threshold against the FILING/SOURCE that carries the metric, not the event date — a metric living in a 10-Q graded at the 8-K print date is unreachable by construction, and the failure tempts substituting the available-but-wrong series." `[[finding_pre_register_against_the_carrying_filing]]`
- **finding_anchor_prediction_to_surprise_not_priced** — "Anchor an event→reaction prediction to the SURPRISE-vs-pricing, not to a named outcome that's already priced; dovish/hawkish labels can invert" `[[finding_anchor_prediction_to_surprise_not_priced]]`
- **feedback_date_specificity_weakest_link** ★HOT — "On a low-confidence (<50%) event-driven prediction, a tightly-pinned Resolve_Date is the weakest link — it falsifies the call on timing even when direction/substance are right; widen the window or loosen the date, don't pin both tight" `[[feedback_date_specificity_weakest_link]]`
- **finding_widened_scope_needs_rescoped_instrument** — Widening a prediction's scope while keeping the old base-rate instrument can make it already-failed at registration — and you cannot see it from inside the derivation `[[finding_widened_scope_needs_rescoped_instrument]]`
- **finding_discovery_instrument_defines_the_claim** — "A forward-discovery prediction measured by press-sampling measures your own discovery latency, not the world — name the complete-scan instrument in the claim and pre-register the re-check, or the same known-unknown trap recurs (OTTO-30: Origin Bancorp 5/22, Triumph Financial 7/25, same prediction, twice)" `[[finding_discovery_instrument_defines_the_claim]]`
- **finding_redated_falsifier_inherits_premise** — "When you re-date a falsifier/prediction because a catalyst moved, it silently inherits the premise that forced the re-date — re-audit the CHANNEL and the premise, not just the calendar row; an inbound date correction is the trigger to re-audit the whole object." `[[finding_redated_falsifier_inherits_premise]]`
- **finding_pre_registration_discipline_through_corroboration** — "Hold a pre-registered trigger's mark through interim corroborating evidence — don't discretionary-fire early; cost = days of mark-lag, benefit = clean spec test. Validated SAM-21 Jun 3→9 2026" `[[finding_pre_registration_discipline_through_corroboration]]`
- **feedback_prediction_canonical_measure** — "When revising a prediction, pull the canonical measure of the prediction's variable — not company-level or proxy evidence. Cross-level conflation (company revenue → industry employment) silently overweights wrong-tier signals." `[[feedback_prediction_canonical_measure]]`
- **finding_threshold_vs_mechanism** — "Falsifiable predictions must separate \"mechanism intact\" propositions from \"threshold sticks/breaches\" propositions; threshold-only predictions can resolve TRUE-in-letter / FALSE-in-spirit when cross-currents or alternate mechanisms drive the same numeric outcome" `[[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]]`
- **finding_catalyst_vs_consequence_conflation** — Probability-shaped signals derived from catalyst probabilities silently inflate when transcribed as consequence probabilities — require the explicit P(consequence | catalyst fires) conditional `[[finding_catalyst_vs_consequence_conflation]]`

## Grading & re-marking
- **finding_confidence_priced_against_thesis_not_letter** ★HOT — "A prediction's confidence gets priced against the thesis you MEANT, but it resolves on the letter you WROTE — if the letter is weaker, it fires in worlds where the thesis is wrong and banks a meaningless CONFIRMED; test by asking what the letter does if the thesis is false" `[[finding_confidence_priced_against_thesis_not_letter]]`
- **finding_rebased_metric_check_made_date** ★HOT — "Before re-basing an open prediction onto a better metric, test whether the NEW metric was already satisfied at the original Made_Date — if it was, the prediction is retired with no credit, not re-based, or you launder a likely-miss into a hit (OTTO-04, 2026-07-25)" `[[finding_rebased_metric_check_made_date]]`
- **finding_resolvability_defect_is_status_not_confidence** ★HOT — "A prediction that can't resolve because its window or instrument is broken is a Status change (STUCK), never a Confidence cut — pricing resolvability into Confidence corrupts the Brier record in both directions" `[[finding_resolvability_defect_is_status_not_confidence]]`
- **feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast** ★HOT — "Don't log a not-yet-passed bill / unrealized forecast as a resolved structural fact — it can reverse" `[[feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast]]`
- **Negative resolutions require a dated search attempt** *(fleet convention RATIFIED Will 2026-08-17, war-triad batch — ruling record `PROME/proposals/2026-08-17_war-triad-spec-batch-RULED.md` row 52③; encoded by DAEDALUS on PROME commission)* — "A prediction that resolves on a NEGATIVE ('no print appeared', 'not refreshed by <date>', 'no event occurred') requires a DATED SEARCH ATTEMPT as a resolution precondition — CONFIRMED must mean 'I looked and found nothing,' never 'I didn't look.' Without it the prediction pays a CONFIRMED for neglect: if the owner never searches, the aggregates stay dark and the row auto-confirms (OSPREY OSP-04, slate #5 8/10 — the origin catch, applied to OSP-04 before its 8/31 resolution). Binds at registration for new rows: name the search instrument in the spec alongside the resolve date. This is the 'nobody published vs nobody looked' distinction applied at the prediction-resolution layer." `[[finding_count_what_published_before_reading_the_verdict]]`
- **finding_seasonal_trough_baseline_resolves_true_on_normal** — A falsifier baselined on the LATEST single period of a seasonal series can pick the trough — then ordinary seasonal recovery resolves it TRUE while carrying zero information about the event being tested `[[finding_seasonal_trough_baseline_resolves_true_on_normal]]`
- **finding_sample_size_vs_identification_defect** — "More data" fixes a sample-size defect and never fixes an identification defect — check which one you have before promising that another period, quarter, or print will settle it `[[finding_sample_size_vs_identification_defect]]`
- **finding_perturb_inputs_to_test_base_rate** — Reproducing a base rate only proves the arithmetic; the real test is correcting its INPUTS at source and seeing whether the conclusion survives — do it before the number carries a live position or prediction `[[finding_perturb_inputs_to_test_base_rate]]`
- **finding_gate_bias_is_placement_error_compare_to_margin** — A known bias in a threshold is a PLACEMENT error and only binds near the boundary — quantify it in the metric's own units and compare to the realized margin before discounting a verdict `[[finding_gate_bias_is_placement_error_compare_to_margin]]`
- **finding_sustain_count_role_discriminating_power** — "A registered trigger needs a stated ROLE, not just a derived number — test what the instrument can and cannot catch; the derivation may return \"this tool can't catch that failure class\"" `[[finding_sustain_count_role_discriminating_power]]`
- **finding_level_conditional_probability_remarking** — "A forward probability attached to a price level/range silently re-marks itself as spot moves; ask what spot was when it was computed, and split range-composites into per-level ladders" `[[finding_level_conditional_probability_remarking]]`
- **finding_delta_vs_own_prior_local_extreme** — "a Δ measured against your OWN last reading misleads if that prior was a local extreme — contextualize a short-window delta against the medium-term trajectory before calling a reversal/regime-change" `[[finding_delta_vs_own_prior_local_extreme]]`
- **feedback_corrected_framing_calibration** — WALTER verify-research's most-frequent verdict is CORRECTED-FRAMING; live distribution 0.60-0.88 confidence (median ~0.77), retain directional thesis, flag specifics as imprecise `[[feedback_corrected_framing_calibration]]`
- **finding_lessons_file_cannot_detect_own_contradictions** — A prose lessons/LEARNINGS file cannot detect its own contradictions, so when two entries disagree the one written FIRST wins by default in every spec drafted afterwards — give lessons machine-comparable asserts and check them automatically. `[[finding_lessons_file_cannot_detect_own_contradictions]]`

## Judgment & framing
- **finding_thin_liquidity_prediction_market_discipline** — "Thin-liquidity binary prediction-market single-print moves are not \"holds\"; require cross-source verification + ≥3-day re-check + earned-discount calibration before any mark update" `[[finding_thin_liquidity_prediction_market_discipline]]`
- **finding_calibration_discount_regime_conditional** — An earned calibration discount is conditional on the pricing regime it was earned in — re-derive before applying at a different market-confidence level `[[finding_calibration_discount_regime_conditional]]`
- **feedback_two_way_read_directional_clarity** — "When presenting a \"two-way read\" or scenario branches, grade each branch's near-term direction explicitly — symmetric-sounding framing (\"also bullish via different mechanism\") can hide directional asymmetry across time horizons" `[[feedback_two_way_read_directional_clarity]]`
- **feedback_single_month_subcomponent_skepticism** — "Single-month sub-component metric moves (ISM internals, CMBS by-property-type, single-trust ABS, single-print sentiment, etc) must be flagged \"needs 2nd-print confirmation\" before treating as load-bearing thesis evidence; rule applies regardless of magnitude" `[[feedback_single_month_subcomponent_skepticism]]`
- **finding_noise_filter_erases_signal_class** — "Before widening a noise filter to cut a detector's false positives, check whether the true positives live in the noise you're filtering — events often cluster on the very dates the filter excludes; prefer a persistence/duration rule, and score FP per-episode not per-day" `[[finding_noise_filter_erases_signal_class]]`
- **feedback_forward_discovery_prediction_spirit** — "When a prediction's literal text is satisfied by pre-existing public data the agent didn't know about at creation, resolve by the forward-discovery SPIRIT (was something NEW found in-window?) not the literal reading — log the data, keep the prediction OPEN, drop confidence" `[[feedback_forward_discovery_prediction_spirit]]`
- **feedback_litigation_allegation_weighting** — Plaintiff/litigation-allegation-only signals should be weighted ≤40% confidence pending corporate-side or independent corroboration; litigation rhetoric routinely over-claims the strategic implication of a real-but-narrow operational fact `[[feedback_litigation_allegation_weighting]]`
- **finding_base_rate_vs_mechanism_discriminator** — "In a signal stack mixing a base-rate/population layer with mechanism-specific discriminator layers, the discriminators are only as good as their enumerated mechanism set; a novel mechanism bypasses them silently, emitting their default 'inactive' reading as a confident wrong veto rather than abstaining" `[[finding_base_rate_vs_mechanism_discriminator]]`
- **finding_catalyst_path_decoupling** — "Level trigger ≠ path trigger — a pre-registered conjunction anchored on an assumed catalyst path can be hit via a different upstream path; that invalidates the path-dependency, not the level read. SAM-23, 2 windows Jun 2026" `[[finding_catalyst_path_decoupling]]`
- **feedback_dont_retire_dormant_thesis** — "de-escalated catalyst -> dormant/armed convexity + re-arm trigger, don't kill the thesis" `[[feedback_dont_retire_dormant_thesis]]`
- **finding_flow_discriminator_vs_narrative** — "When a pre-registered flow/data discriminator resolves against both your prior lean AND the prevailing street narrative, trust the discriminator — but first reconcile the contradicting narrative by checking timeframe-staleness and policy-push-vs-actual-flow, then let the next scheduled print arbitrate." `[[finding_flow_discriminator_vs_narrative]]`
