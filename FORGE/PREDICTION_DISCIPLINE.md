- **finding_corrective_inherits_the_anchor_it_corrects** — "**A correction is sized against the number it is correcting, so it inherits that anchor and fails in the SAME DIRECTION as the original.**" A reprice is framed as a *move from* the standing value, so the standing value sets the scale: *'65 is too high, cut it hard'* produces 35, and a 30-point cut feels large **precisely because it is measured against 65**. Measured case (LABOR LAB-08): 65% → 35% pre-print, unforced, symmetric, and explicitly citing the desk's own 0-for-4 record at ≥60% as the reason to cut — the event printed in a band pre-assigned **4%**. **35% was ~8.75× the honest number; the corrective made *because* this desk runs too hot was itself too hot, in the same direction.** ⚠️ **A decomposition does NOT rescue you** — if the band probabilities were set beside the standing prior, the 'independent' derivation inherits the anchor and returns a number that confirms it; *a decomposition anchored at its inputs looks like arithmetic and functions as a rationalisation.* **Fix, two cheap lines: (1) write the number TWICE — once as a move from the standing value, once COLD from base rates with the standing value not visible; if they disagree by more than ~2×, take the cold one and record both, because the disagreement IS the finding. (2) Score the corrective as its OWN row at resolution** — books that score only the as-made value are structurally blind to a mis-calibrated repricing step, so the desk can never learn its corrections are too small. ⛔ **This is the opposite case from the stale-row sweep: it fires on the row that was JUST revised** — the step that received the most attention, and therefore looks safest, is the one nothing re-grades. Complements `[[finding_a_correction_pass_is_unreviewed_work]]` (that one is defects *in* the correction; this one is the correction's **magnitude** being wrong predictably). `[[finding_corrective_inherits_the_anchor_it_corrects]]`

- **finding_calibration_discount_regime_conditional** — An earned calibration discount is conditional on the pricing regime it was earned in — re-derive before applying at a different market-confidence level `[[finding_calibration_discount_regime_conditional]]`
- **feedback_two_way_read_directional_clarity** — "When presenting a \"two-way read\" or scenario branches, grade each branch's near-term direction explicitly — symmetric-sounding framing (\"also bullish via different mechanism\") can hide directional asymmetry across time horizons" `[[feedback_two_way_read_directional_clarity]]`
- **feedback_single_month_subcomponent_skepticism** — "Single-month sub-component metric moves (ISM internals, CMBS by-property-type, single-trust ABS, single-print sentiment, etc) must be flagged \"needs 2nd-print confirmation\" before treating as load-bearing thesis evidence; rule applies regardless of magnitude" `[[feedback_single_month_subcomponent_skepticism]]`
- **finding_noise_filter_erases_signal_class** — "Before widening a noise filter to cut a detector's false positives, check whether the true positives live in the noise you're filtering — events often cluster on the very dates the filter excludes; prefer a persistence/duration rule, and score FP per-episode not per-day" `[[finding_noise_filter_erases_signal_class]]`
- **feedback_forward_discovery_prediction_spirit** — "When a prediction's literal text is satisfied by pre-existing public data the agent didn't know about at creation, resolve by the forward-discovery SPIRIT (was something NEW found in-window?) not the literal reading — log the data, keep the prediction OPEN, drop confidence" `[[feedback_forward_discovery_prediction_spirit]]`
- **feedback_litigation_allegation_weighting** — Plaintiff/litigation-allegation-only signals should be weighted ≤40% confidence pending corporate-side or independent corroboration; litigation rhetoric routinely over-claims the strategic implication of a real-but-narrow operational fact `[[feedback_litigation_allegation_weighting]]`
- **finding_base_rate_vs_mechanism_discriminator** — "In a signal stack mixing a base-rate/population layer with mechanism-specific discriminator layers, the discriminators are only as good as their enumerated mechanism set; a novel mechanism bypasses them silently, emitting their default 'inactive' reading as a confident wrong veto rather than abstaining" `[[finding_base_rate_vs_mechanism_discriminator]]`
- **finding_catalyst_path_decoupling** — "Level trigger ≠ path trigger — a pre-registered conjunction anchored on an assumed catalyst path can be hit via a different upstream path; that invalidates the path-dependency, not the level read. SAM-23, 2 windows Jun 2026" `[[finding_catalyst_path_decoupling]]`
- **feedback_dont_retire_dormant_thesis** — "de-escalated catalyst -> dormant/armed convexity + re-arm trigger, don't kill the thesis" `[[feedback_dont_retire_dormant_thesis]]`
- **finding_flow_discriminator_vs_narrative** — "When a pre-registered flow/data discriminator resolves against both your prior lean AND the prevailing street narrative, trust the discriminator — but first reconcile the contradicting narrative by checking timeframe-staleness and policy-push-vs-actual-flow, then let the next scheduled print arbitrate." `[[finding_flow_discriminator_vs_narrative]]`
