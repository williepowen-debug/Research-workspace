## Assessment
**Verdict: Consistent with thesis, not a catalyst accelerator.**

- But 23% EPS miss + 15.7% TBVPS erosion = real balance sheet stress under the hood
- $17M feels light given £103M MFS exposure + First Brands — may see additional reserves in future quarters
- Slow bleed is actually worse for WAL: if JEF had blown up, WAL would've front-run the disclosure
