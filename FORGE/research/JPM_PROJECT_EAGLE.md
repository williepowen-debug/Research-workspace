# JPMorgan "Project Eagle" — Research Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-29
**Source:** Bloomberg (3/28/26), Financial Post, Business Standard, Wikipedia

---

## What Is Project Eagle?

**Project Eagle is JPMorgan's internal codename for the debt financing package behind the $55 billion leveraged buyout of Electronic Arts (EA)** — the largest LBO in history.

The deal: A consortium of Saudi Arabia's **Public Investment Fund (PIF)**, **Silver Lake**, and **Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners** is acquiring EA. JPMorgan arranged ~$15-20 billion in debt financing, including:
- **$8.125 billion leveraged loan** (sold to investors over ~1 week)
- **$6.4 billion in bonds** (sold after the Trump post)

The "high-wire act" was executing this massive debt sale during extreme market volatility caused by trade war / tariff uncertainty and geopolitical tensions (West Asia conflict referenced in coverage).

## The Trump Post (March 23, 7:23 AM)

The exact content of the Trump post isn't fully detailed in available coverage, but context strongly suggests it was a **tariff-related signal that eased market sentiment** — likely indicating narrower or softer tariff implementation than feared. Bloomberg says this post was "the final piece" that gave JPMorgan the green light to proceed with the bond sale portion of the financing.

The timing: JPMorgan bankers had been "anxiously watching" geopolitical/trade events for weeks. The leveraged loan sale had already started ~1 week prior. Trump's post provided enough market confidence to launch the riskier bond tranche.

## Why Bloomberg Considers This Significant

1. **Largest LBO ever** — $55B deal, dwarfing all prior PE buyouts
2. **Bellwether for $100B+ in M&A debt** — Bloomberg explicitly notes failure could have "stalled not only the EA deal but the rest of the $100 billion in M&A debt financing Wall Street is working on this year"
3. **Wall Street navigating the trade war** — The article frames this as emblematic of how banks are threading the needle between deal execution and macro volatility
4. **Political connections** — Jared Kushner (Trump's son-in-law) is part of the buyer consortium, adding a political dimension

## Relevance to Our Positions

### Direct Relevance: **LOW to MODERATE**

This is **NOT**:
- ❌ A bank crisis response or backstop
- ❌ A private credit liquidity facility
- ❌ A Treasury market intervention
- ❌ An oil/energy market response

This **IS**:
- ✅ A massive leveraged debt issuance in a volatile market
- ✅ A signal about credit market appetite/health

### Indirect Relevance to Our Thesis:

**Private Credit (APO, ARES shorts):**
- The fact that JPMorgan could place ~$15B in leveraged debt suggests **credit markets remain open and functioning** — this is modestly *against* the thesis that private credit is facing stress
- However, this was a Herculean effort requiring perfect timing around a presidential tweet — fragility is visible beneath the surface
- If anything, this shows banks are still willing to take risk, which means the credit cycle hasn't turned yet

**Regional Banks (KRE, OZK, WAL shorts):**
- No direct connection. This is bulge-bracket investment banking, not regional bank lending

**Systemic Risk:**
- The $100B M&A debt pipeline number is worth tracking — if this pipeline starts backing up due to volatility, it could create broader credit stress
- The dependence on a single Trump post for market confidence to execute is itself a fragility signal

## Bottom Line

Project Eagle is a big deal for investment banking and credit markets, but it's **not directly relevant to our short thesis**. It's a leveraged buyout financing story, not a systemic risk event. The most useful signal is that credit markets are still functioning well enough to absorb $15B+ in leveraged debt — which means the credit cycle deterioration we're tracking hasn't reached the point of freezing deal flow yet. Worth monitoring the $100B M&A debt pipeline as a canary.
