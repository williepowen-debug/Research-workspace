## Framework

The Iran/Hormuz crisis is NOT just oil/financial — it's simultaneous physical depletion across 7 supply chains with **hard deadlines diplomacy can't extend.** These are physical constraints, not market sentiment. The calendar is the only actor that never lost a negotiation.

Key insight (Perera): Markets price this as a transit problem. Reopen the strait, restart the flow. **Wrong.** Production shut-ins, infrastructure damage, and insurance lag mean even a ceasefire doesn't reset these clocks — it just slows them.

## The Seven Clocks

### 1. 🌾 PLANTING WINDOW — mid-April (IRREVERSIBLE)
- **What:** Corn and soybean planting is nitrogen-dependent. Nitrogen fertilizer (urea, UAN) requires natural gas feedstock.
- **Chain:** Qatar LNG offline (3-5yr repair) + Hormuz closed → gas feedstock disrupted → fertilizer production curtailed → nitrogen unavailable for spring planting
- **Hard deadline:** Mid-April. If nitrogen isn't applied by then, corn/soy yields are permanently reduced for the Oct 2026 harvest. This is biological — no policy can extend it.
- **Scale:** US is world's largest corn producer. Corn belt fertilizer application window is ~4-6 weeks.
- **Agent:** FERT owns fertilizer supply chain. CF Industries ($115C Jun) is our direct exposure.
- **Fertiglobe (UAE):** 6.6M tonnes/yr nitrogen capacity trapped behind Hormuz. One of world's largest producers.

### 2. 📊 USDA PROSPECTIVE PLANTINGS — March 31
- **What:** USDA releases farmer planting intentions. This single report sets the 12-month food supply model for global commodity markets.
- **Chain:** Fertilizer uncertainty + diesel costs ($5.10/gal) + tariffs on equipment → farmers may shift acreage away from nitrogen-intensive crops → structural food supply reduction
- **Hard deadline:** March 31 release. Markets reprice immediately.
- **Why it matters:** If planting intentions show reduced corn/soy acreage, food inflation locks in through Q1 2027 regardless of what happens with oil.

### 3. 🍞 FAO FOOD PRICE INDEX — April 3
- **What:** UN FAO releases monthly food price index. 2022 peak was 159.7 (Russia-Ukraine shock).
- **Chain:** Oil $107+ → diesel/transport → fertilizer → food production costs all compounding simultaneously
- **Threshold:** If index exceeds 159.7 (2022 peak), this becomes a food emergency narrative. Political pressure intensifies — food riots historically topple governments faster than energy crises.
- **Context:** 2022 was a single-vector shock (Ukraine grain). This is multi-vector (energy + fertilizer + shipping + insurance). Could exceed 2022 peak.

### 4. 💊 PHARMACEUTICAL APIs — late May depletion
- **What:** India manufactures 47% of US generic drugs. Indian pharmaceutical production depends on Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and intermediates, many sourced from Hormuz-transit countries.
- **Chain:** 87.7% of global methanol transits Hormuz → methanol is key feedstock for pharmaceutical intermediates → India API production disrupted → US generic drug supply threatened
- **Buffer:** India holds 2-3 months of API/intermediate inventory from Feb 28 baseline = **late May depletion**
- **Scale:** 47% of US generics from India. Disruption affects hospitals, pharmacies, chronic disease management nationwide.
- **No agent currently owns this.** Cross-domain — touches CARL (consumer), HENRY (inflation), HAWK (geopolitical).

### 5. 🛢️ CHINA CRUDE RESERVES — mid-to-late April exhaustion
- **What:** China drawing commercial crude reserves at ~1M bpd to offset lost Hormuz imports.
- **Chain:** Iran shipments down 44% (2.16M→1.22M bpd). Russian pipeline maxed at 600-700K bpd. Commercial reserves buffer = 4-6 weeks from Feb 28.
- **Hard deadline:** Mid-to-late April. When commercial reserves deplete, China faces three options:
  1. Russian pipeline (already maxed)
  2. Premium reroute (Cape of Good Hope — adds cost, limited tanker availability)
  3. **Crack open SPR** — this reprices every commodity globally and signals panic
- **Total reserves:** 1.1-1.2B bbl (2.5-4 months at normal draw, but accelerating)
- **Agent:** ZHAO owns China capital flows and energy security. See `iran-war/05_CHINA_RESPONSE.md`
- **Escalation trigger:** If China cracks SPR, it's a global signal. Commodity markets reprice across the board.

### 6. 💨 HELIUM — late May to June (SEMICONDUCTOR FABRICATION)
- **What:** SK Hynix and Samsung hold 2-3 months of helium inventory. Qatar's Ras Laffan supplies 64.7% of global helium (now offline, 3-5yr repair).
- **Chain:** Ras Laffan offline → helium supply collapses → semiconductor fabrication requires helium for cooling and processing → chip production rationed
- **Hard deadline:** Late May to June. When fab inventories deplete, semiconductor production physically cannot continue at current rates.
- **Scale:** This is an AI hardware physical wall. TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix all affected. Every advanced chip requires helium.
- **AAPL risk:** Apple holds 100 shares (~45% of portfolio, ~$25,500). iPhone/Mac production depends on semiconductor supply chain. Helium rationing = production cuts = earnings risk.
- **Qatar detail (QatarEnergy official Mar 20):** Helium loss = 309.54 MCFA (14% of Qatar exports). Trains 4+6 damaged = the helium-producing trains.
- **No dedicated agent.** Cross-domain — most directly affects portfolio via AAPL position.

### 7. 🚢 INSURANCE / SHIPPING NORMALIZATION — 6-16 months post-ceasefire
- **What:** Solvency II regulations require 30-60 days of zero incidents before P&I clubs can reinstate war-risk coverage for Hormuz transit.
- **Chain:** Ceasefire announced → mine clearance (30-90 days, 5,000+ mines) → zero-incident period (30-60 days) → insurance reinstatement → shipping restart → production restart (ADNOC 2-4 months) → Fujairah repair (2-4 months)
- **Key insight:** "Military clear" ≠ "Insurance clear." The gap between these two is the alpha window. Market will misjudge normalization speed on any ceasefire headline.
- **Named P&I clubs withdrawn:** Gard AS, NorthStandard, Steamship Mutual, Skuld, American Club, Swedish Club, London P&I Club
- **War-risk premiums:** $250K→$1M/voyage. 3% of vessel value (vs 0.25% pre-conflict). Commercially unviable.
- **Current:** 3,200 ships idle (4% global tonnage). 70% decrease in Red Sea cargo volume.
- **Agent:** HAWK owns military/insurance timeline. LIQUID for financial market impact. BRENT for tanker rates.
- **This clock is the longest.** Even best-case ceasefire tomorrow = 6+ months to shipping normalization. This is why Hamilton demand destruction is sustained, not spike-then-revert.
