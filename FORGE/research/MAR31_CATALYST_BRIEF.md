## 1. USDA Planting Intentions (Mar 31, 12:00 ET)

**What:** Annual survey of farmer planting plans for 2026 crop year — corn, soy, wheat acreage. First hard data on how the fertilizer crisis translates to actual food supply.

**Why it matters:** Three fertilizer sources offline simultaneously — Gulf urea (strikes), China N-K (export halt), Russia ammonium nitrate (suspended). Urea FOB Egypt ~$700/mt (+40% from $490). This is depletion clock #1: if farmers can't afford inputs, they plant fewer acres or under-fertilize → yield drops → Q3-Q4 food CPI locked in regardless of what happens with oil.

**Confirms thesis if:**
- Corn acreage below 90M (vs ~91M expected) — input cost deterrence visible
- Any survey commentary on fertilizer availability/cost constraints
- Wheat acreage shifts (cheaper to plant, lower input needs) — classic stress rotation

**Challenges thesis if:**
- Acreage holds or increases — farmers planting through cost spike (margin compression, not supply reduction)
- USDA commentary suggests fertilizer supply improving

**Position impact:** CF $130C Jun. CF Industries is the largest US nitrogen producer. If planting data confirms input scarcity, CF pricing power extends through summer. Acreage reduction = bullish CF (scarce product, high prices maintained). Strong planting = mixed (volume up, but price competition may emerge if supply normalizes).

**Watch for:** Soybean/corn ratio shift. Survey timing note: data collected first 2 weeks of March — captures urea at $650-700 but NOT the Russia AN suspension (Mar 25). Reality may be worse than survey shows.

- If Yanbu struck before Mar 31 → Brent $145-165+ → Q1 avg jumps → NOPI exceeds 50 → unprecedented territory

**Position impact:** USO position expired/expiring (Mar 27 calls). Macro thesis: NOPI >40 has NEVER not produced recession within 2-4 quarters. This feeds Hamilton lag framework — demand destruction visible by Jul-Aug (lag2-3), peak GDP damage Q4 2026/Q1 2027 (lag4). Supports short positioning across consumer discretionary, airlines, regional banks.

**Confirm vs challenge:**
- Confirms: Oil stays >$100 through Mar 31 → Q1 average locks in severe NOPI
