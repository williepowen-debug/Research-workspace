# Iran's Win Conditions
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** HIGH (CSIS, BBC, Atlantic Council, ISW converge)

## Core Logic
Iran isn't playing to win. It's playing to survive. War of endurance, not conquest. Iran's "win" looks nothing like America's "win" — different games entirely.

## Win Conditions (Ranked by Priority)

### 1. Regime Survival (Non-Negotiable)
- Supreme leader Khamenei killed in opening strikes
- Mojtaba Khamenei installed within days (controversial but fast)
- Security forces remain loyal
- January protests crushed

### 2. Permanent Deterrence
- Hit 14 countries day one — "if you attack us again, region burns"
- Fighting THIS war partly to prevent the NEXT one
- Calibrated responses in June 2025 were read as weakness → went maximum this time

### 3. Hormuz as Economic Weapon
- Closed effective Mar 4. Brent surged past $120
- Hurts Iran too (own exports) but hurts everyone else more
- Attrition economics: interceptors cost orders of magnitude more than drones/missiles
- Insurance costs make strait "uninsurable" — don't even need to fire

### 4. Yuan-for-Hormuz Play
- Negotiating with 8 countries for selective passage in yuan
- Chinese vessels reportedly passing through (limited)
- Directly attacks petrodollar hegemony
- Gives China incentive to NOT pressure Iran
- See [09_YUAN_FOR_HORMUZ.md](09_YUAN_FOR_HORMUZ.md)

### 5. Fracture the Coalition
- GCC strikes pressure host governments to pressure Washington
- US-Israel goals already diverging (NYT Mar 20)
- Israel wants regime change; US wants off-ramp
- Iran's strategy depends on this fracture widening

### 6. Nuclear Ambiguity
- 440kg at 60% enrichment (~10 warheads)
- Breakout time still ~6 days (hidden centrifuges)
- Offered to downblend (FM Araghchi, CBS Mar 15) — negotiating card, not concession

## Theory of Victory
**Endure → Impose Costs → Wait for Political Fractures → Negotiate from Strength**

## Ceasefire Status
- FM Araghchi: "Iran never asked for a ceasefire"
- ISW Mar 14: neither side prepared for negotiations
- Oman/Egypt mediation rejected by BOTH sides
- Iran wants the war to END (durable settlement), not pause

## Where Iran Could Lose
- Command & control degrades → miscalculation
- Missile/drone stockpiles exhaust
- Kharg Island struck (already happened)
- Internal fracture (protests crushed but grievances remain)
- China doesn't actually help substantively

## Duration Implication
**WAR IS MONTHS NOT WEEKS.** Even ceasefire → mine clearance 30-90 days (5,000-6,000 mines) + P&I reinstatement 4-12 weeks.

## Trade Connection
Iran's endurance strategy = sustained high oil prices for quarters. Phase 1 extends into summer 2026 minimum. Hamilton demand destruction timeline confirmed.
