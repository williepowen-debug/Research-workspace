# Dual-Chokepoint Crisis — Hormuz + Bab el-Mandeb
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** HIGH on Hormuz, MEDIUM-HIGH on Houthi strategic reserve theory

## The Framework
Two of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints are under simultaneous threat:
- **Strait of Hormuz:** Closed by Iran since ~Mar 4. ~20% of global seaborne oil + LNG.
- **Bab el-Mandeb / Red Sea:** Under Houthi threat. ~12% of global trade.
- **Combined:** ~1/3 of global seaborne crude halted if both activate simultaneously.

This is unprecedented. No historical parallel exists.

## Hormuz — Current Status
- Effectively closed to non-Iranian/non-Chinese commercial shipping since Mar 4
- Daily transits collapsed from ~153 to ~13 vessels
- Iran has **5,000-6,000 naval mines** (pre-war estimate). Only small fraction neutralized.
- Insurance premiums at "uninsurable" levels since Mar 5
- Some Iranian/Chinese traffic trickling through (selective blockade, not total)
- Mine clearance: 30-90 days AFTER ceasefire. This is the binding constraint on reopening.

## Bab el-Mandeb — The Second Shoe That Hasn't Dropped

### Houthi "Strategic Reserve" Theory (Gemini — MAJOR insight)
- Houthis are **deliberately holding back** Red Sea interdiction
- NOT fighting because the THREAT is worth more than the ACTION
- Escalation card grows MORE powerful as war continues
- If ceasefire talks begin or Iran feels cornered → Houthis could activate
- This represents an escalation option the market has NOT priced

### Why Houthis Can Operate Independently
- Predate the Islamic Republic (not an Iranian creation)
- Manufacturing arms independently in Yemen
- Have their own revenue streams (Yemeni territory taxes, port fees, fuel)
- Demonstrated anti-shipping capability in 2023-2024 Red Sea campaign
- See [08_PROXY_RESILIENCE.md](08_PROXY_RESILIENCE.md) and pending [HOUTHI_CAPABILITY.md](pending/HOUTHI_CAPABILITY.md)

### Houthi Horizontal Coordination
- Operatives reported in Iraq and Lebanon
- Providing lateral support and "lessons learned" to other proxy members
- Horizontal coordination structure bypasses decapitated IRGC-QF leadership
- Proxy network is a "distributed system," not just top-down

## No Safe Alternative Routes
- **Yanbu (Saudi Red Sea port):** SAMREF refinery struck by IRGC. NOT safe.
- Saudi East-West Pipeline can bypass Hormuz for ~5M bpd max — but endpoint is under fire
- Regional pipelines: only ~2.6M bpd capacity vs ~20M bpd normally through Hormuz
- No combination of alternatives can replace Hormuz volume
