# China's Response to the Iran War
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** HIGH (4 models, 150+ sources cross-referenced)

## Executive Summary
China is strategically prepared, diplomatically cautious, and economically vulnerable in ways its coal-heavy energy mix partially masks. Pre-war stockpiling was deliberate. Response is pragmatic hedging: buffers + diplomatic calls for peace + selective yuan leverage, without overcommitment.

## Energy Security

### Reserves & Buffer
- **1.1-1.4B barrels** total (strategic + commercial). Gemini: 1.3-1.4B. Bloomberg commercial: 851M.
- ~120 days of import cover (exceeds US SPR)
- Deliberately stockpiled: Jan-Feb imports +15.8% YoY (96.93M tonnes, ~11.99M bpd)
- Iranian crude to China surged to **2.16M bpd in Feb** (highest since July 2018)
- Russian crude deliveries ~2.1M bpd via ESPO + seaborne
- **85% total energy self-sufficiency** (coal 51.4%, renewables 30.4%, oil only 18.2%)
- Hormuz-routed crude = only 6.6% of total energy consumption (Nomura)

### Since War Started
- Iranian shipments **down 44%** (2.16M → 1.22M bpd)
- 11.7-13.7M barrels transited Hormuz to China (Kpler, TankerTrackers)
- Only **11 China-linked vessels** transited Mar 1-15 (mostly bulk carriers, NOT tankers)
- **55 Chinese-flagged ships trapped** inside Persian Gulf
- **Cosco suspended all new ME bookings** after Chinese vessel hit by debris
- Russia pipeline **maxed at 600-700K bpd** — can't increase
- China banned diesel/gasoline/aviation fuel exports through end-March
- **17 VLCCs redirecting to Yanbu** (Saudi Red Sea port)
- Iran activating **Jask terminal** on Gulf of Oman to bypass Hormuz (limited capacity)
- No SPR drawdown announced yet (conservative hierarchy: halt exports → accelerate maintenance → cut runs → THEN tap SPR)

### LNG Crisis
- Qatar = ~27-30% of China's LNG imports. All offline (force majeure).
- 12.8 MTPA (17% Qatar capacity) damaged 3-5 years
- JKM Asian spot LNG surged ~130% (high-$10s to mid-$20s/MMBtu)
- **Helium crisis:** Ras Laffan = 30% global helium supply. Irreplaceable for semiconductor EUV lithography. Threatens Chinese chipmakers.

## Diplomatic Posture
- Condemns US-Israeli strikes as "unacceptable" and illegal
- Calls for immediate ceasefire and return to dialogue
- **Abstained** on UNSC Resolution 2817 (anti-Iran, 13 in favor) — didn't veto but didn't endorse
- Backed Russia's ceasefire draft (failed, 4 votes)
- Dispatched envoy Zhai Jun to Saudi, Bahrain, Oman, likely Iran
- **Privately pressured Iran** not to hit Qatari LNG infrastructure (Beijing views Qatar LNG as national security imperative)
- **Refused Trump's demand for warships** — "a war Washington started and can't finish"
- Humanitarian aid to Iran: $200K via Red Cross (tells you everything)

## Economic Impact

### Inflation Transmission
- Feb CPI: +1.3% YoY (highest since Jan 2023). Core CPI: +1.8% (highest since Mar 2019)
- PPI: -0.9% YoY but narrowing. **May flip positive March** — first time in years
- PMI input sub-index at 54.8 vs output 50.6 = margin compression
- "Bad inflation" — cost-push, not demand-pull. Worst possible mechanism.
- 10% oil rise → +0.4pp PPI, minimal CPI (companies absorb costs)
- 25% oil rise → -0.5pp GDP (Standard Chartered)
- **Capital Economics: growth could fall below 3%** if war continues months (vs 4.5% baseline)

### LGFV Cascade (NEW — Gemini)
Oil shock → manufacturer margin compression (25% already loss-making) → reduced corporate tax receipts → LGFV debt servicing crisis (¥66T hidden debt, ~50% GDP) → potential municipal defaults. This is the transmission from oil shock to Chinese financial system.

### Property Sector
- Sales -12-13% in 2025, another -10-14% expected 2026 (S&P)
- Investment -11.1% YoY Jan-Feb
- 5th year of decline. Compounds energy shock.

### Relative Resilience
- Better positioned than Japan/Korea/India due to reserves + coal + renewables
- Stock market showed surprising relative resilience (PetroChina, Sinopec, CNOOC hit 52-week highs initially)
- But reserves are depletable buffer, not solution. Clock runs out mid-May to late June.

## Trump Sanctions Reversal (MAJOR)
- Mar 20-21: **General License U** — authorized sale of ~140M barrels Iranian oil at sea
- Bessent: "using Iranian barrels against Tehran to keep price down"
- Effectively a regulatory bailout for Chinese teapot refineries
- Highlights structural limit: can't squeeze Iran AND protect Western consumers simultaneously

## Trade Connection
China has 2.5-4 months before real pain. LGFV cascade connects oil shock to Chinese financial stress → weaker global demand → second half of Hamilton. China is NOT the circuit breaker — it's another transmission mechanism.
