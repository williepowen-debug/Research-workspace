# Yuan-for-Hormuz Play
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** HIGH on infrastructure, MEDIUM on operational impact

## What's Happening
Iran is attempting to weaponize the Hormuz closure against dollar hegemony by offering selective passage for oil traded in yuan instead of USD.

## The Proposal
- Negotiating with **8 non-Middle East countries** (Chosun, Mar 18)
- CNN confirmed senior Iranian official considering "limited tanker passage if cargo traded in yuan"
- Known/suspected participants: China, Japan, India, Pakistan
- Chinese-flagged vessels have reportedly passed through (limited, inconsistent)

## Operational Reality (ALL models agree: thin)
- Only **11 China-linked vessels** transited Hormuz Mar 1-15
- Most were **bulk carriers, NOT tankers** (one exception: Skywave, already US-sanctioned)
- Chinese ships have also **turned back under fire** — not reliable
- **Cosco suspended all new ME bookings** after debris hit
- **55 Chinese-flagged ships trapped** in Persian Gulf
- Vessels broadcasting "CHINA OWNER" via AIS to claim passage — desperate signaling
- **~90% of Iranian crude to China already settled in yuan/barter pre-war** — formalizes existing practice

## Beijing's Position: Deliberate Ambiguity
- **No public endorsement.** MFA: "I'm not familiar with what you mentioned"
- Quietly enabling via ongoing imports and safe passage talks (Reuters, Mar 5)
- Chinese analysts urge caution — operational risks, US entanglement
- Not rejecting outright, not embracing publicly
- Strategy: maximize economic benefit while minimizing sanctions exposure

## Infrastructure (Already Built)
- **Shanghai INE** crude oil futures (launched 2018): 200-300K contracts daily (~2-3M barrels). Price limits widened to ±20% on Mar 9.
- **CIPS:** 180T yuan ($26.4T) annually, +43%. **Spiked to 928B RMB ($128B) daily on Mar 9** (war-driven clearing). New rules effective Feb 2026 enable multi-currency processing.
- **Bank of Kunlun:** Closed RMB Settlement System — Iranian oil proceeds converted to yuan credits, redeemed for Chinese industrial goods. "Yuan-Petroleum Barter" loop immune to Western interdiction.
- **mBridge multi-CBDC platform:** $55B+ cumulative transactions (China, HK, Thailand, UAE, Saudi).
- **Yuan-denominated LNG futures** in preparation (Shanghai Futures Exchange, reported Feb 2026).
- **PBOC gold buying:** 16 consecutive months, 74.22M troy ounces (~$388B). ~9.6% of reserves.

## De-Dollarization Assessment
- Crisis **accelerates pre-existing trends** but is NOT a step-change
- Yuan share of global payments: ~4.3% (2025, up from 1.95% in 2020)
- RMB trade financing: record 7.5% (Aug 2025)
- Dollar still ~80-85% of global oil settlement
- Saudi settles "overwhelming majority" in dollars
- Structural constraints: capital controls, limited yuan investment opportunities
- **Incremental, not revolutionary**

## Trade Connection
Feeds ZHAO's UST demand hole thesis. Any shift in Gulf sovereign recycling from USD to yuan weakens petrodollar demand for Treasuries. But timeline is years, not months. Near-term trade impact is marginal. Long-term structural significance is high.
