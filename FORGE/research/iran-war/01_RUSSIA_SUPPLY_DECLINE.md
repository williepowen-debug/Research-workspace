# Russia Oil Production — Structural Decline
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** HIGH (verified against 5+ independent sources)

## Key Findings
- Russia at **8.6M bpd** (IEA Feb 2026) — ~1M below OPEC+ quota
- Carnegie projects 8M bpd by 2030, <7M by 2035 at ~3%/year — but IEA Feb data shows they're **4 years ahead** of that projection
- Decline is **structural, not cyclical** — driven by state policy, not geology
- Russia **cannot swing produce** even with $100+ oil
- Iran war windfall helps revenue but can't increase volume

## Why Russia Can't Ramp
1. **State takes 58.4%** above $13.5/bbl → companies can't retain enough to invest
2. **20% real cost of capital** (CBR rate) → no debt-funded growth
3. **OPEC+ quotas killed the project pipeline** — no incentive to develop → no capacity even if needed
4. **Arctic/frontier projects mothballed** — require specialized imported equipment blocked by sanctions
5. **Labor shortages** from the Ukraine war
6. **Tight oil** (Bazhenov/Achimov) exists but costs $40-60/bbl, comparable to US shale, no fiscal regime to develop

## Brownfield Economics
- Full-cycle pre-tax: ~$16/bbl. Wells won't shut in. They just deplete at natural rate without replacement drilling.

## National Wealth Fund
- $130B pre-war → ~$50B by early 2025. Moscow Times warns possible depletion by 2026.

## OPEC+ Trap (Self-Reinforcing)
- Russia values OPEC+ as last multilateral forum → won't defect
- But membership caps production → kills development incentive → accelerates decline
- Self-reinforcing doom loop
