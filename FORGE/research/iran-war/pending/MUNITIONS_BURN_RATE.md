## Production Constraints

### Surge Capability Is Limited
- JDAM: Boeing ramping from 35,000 to 50,000/year — but takes 18-24 months
- THAAD: Lockheed Martin targeting 400/year but **"years away"** from achieving
- Tomahawk: RTX surge to 253/year by FY2028 (currently ~125)
- Arrow: Israeli production highly classified, likely 24-36/year
- **Lead times for new orders: 18-36 months for most precision systems**

### Critical Mineral Dependencies (POTENTIAL KILL SHOT)
- Rare earth elements essential for guidance systems, motors, sensors
- **China controls 70%+ of global rare earth processing**
- Pentagon holds only **2-3 months of rare earth reserves**
- If China tightened export permits: could theoretically force campaign cessation
- Samarium-cobalt magnets, neodymium — no substitutes for precision guidance
- This is a theoretical escalation card China hasn't played (yet)

## Iran's Counter-Strategy
- **Attrition by design.** Iran's strategy explicitly exploits the cost asymmetry.
- Cheap drones exhaust expensive interceptors
- Iran can produce missiles faster than the US can produce interceptors
- Even if every drone is shot down, the economics favor the attacker
- "If both sides fire everything, Iran runs out of targets before the US runs out of interceptors — but the US runs out of interceptors before Iran runs out of drones"

## Trade Connection
This is the most concrete, measurable constraint on war duration. PGM exhaustion forces either:
1. Ceasefire/off-ramp (oil drops, but mine clearance takes months)
2. Shift to less precise weapons (civilian casualties spike → political pressure → ceasefire)
3. Degraded campaign tempo (war continues but at reduced intensity → Hormuz stays closed longer)

All three paths keep oil elevated through at least summer 2026. None produces a quick resolution.
