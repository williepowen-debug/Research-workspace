# Ukraine Strikes on Russian Oil Infrastructure
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** HIGH (Reuters, Forbes, BBC, Bloomberg)

## Escalation Trajectory
- **Pre-Sept 2025:** Sporadic refinery strikes, mostly symbolic
- **Sept 2025+:** Systematic and effective (expanded long-range drone force)
- **Nov 29, 2025:** First shadow fleet tanker strikes (Kairos, Virat — Sea Baby naval drones)
- **Dec 2025:** Caspian Sea platforms hit — first offshore production strikes (Lukoil, 200K bpd lost)
- **Feb 2026:** Druzhba pipeline pumping station → 250K bpd intake cut. Black Sea export facility "barrage"
- **Mar 4, 2026:** Tanker SUNK in Mediterranean (Arctic Metagaz, 2,000+ miles from Ukraine)
- **Mar 21, 2026:** Bashneft/Saratov refinery strikes (Bloomberg)

## Impact by Category

### Refining
- **~500K bpd offline** (~15% of total capacity) — Forbes
- 1.1M bpd disrupted at peak (17%) — Reuters, Aug 2025
- Refining volume down 1.7% in 2025
- Estimated $12B+ total damage (United24 — Ukrainian source)

### Pipeline/Transport
- Druzhba pumping station hit Feb 23 → 250K bpd pipeline intake cut (Reuters/BBC)
- Black Sea export facilities under sustained barrage

### Tankers (NEW escalation vector)
- 6+ tankers attacked in Baltic since Nov 2025
- Tanker sunk in MEDITERRANEAN — 2,000+ miles from Ukraine. Geographic range expanding.

### Offshore
- Lukoil Caspian platforms — 200K bpd lost (Carnegie confirmed)

## Net Effect
- Capital diverted from new drilling into rebuilding → treadmill effect
- Russian crude/oil exports hit LOWEST since 2022 invasion in Feb 2026 (IEA via ISW) — BEFORE Iran war
- Three forces compounding: structural decline + fiscal squeeze + physical destruction

## Trade Connection
Ukraine strikes accelerate Russia's inability to act as supply buffer. Compounds Carnegie structural decline. Tightens global supply picture → keeps oil elevated → Hamilton runs.
