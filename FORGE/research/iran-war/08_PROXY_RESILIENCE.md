# Proxy Network Resilience — Life After Tehran
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH (Gemini strongest, AEI, MEI, Stimson)

## Key Finding
Common assumption that "Axis of Resistance" collapses without Iran is WRONG. The proxy network is a distributed system designed to survive exactly this scenario. Iranian collapse may remove RESTRAINT, making proxies MORE dangerous, not less.

## Hezbollah
- Leadership decapitated (Nasrallah killed in 2024 campaign)
- Syria supply lines severed
- Would "wither" without Iranian funding (AEI) but not disappear
- Has local revenue streams and entrenched political power in Lebanon
- Becomes partly self-financing, somewhat less disciplined actor
- Short-term: spasm of retaliatory activity. Medium-term: resource constraints.

## Houthis (MOST LIKELY TO SURVIVE)
- **Predate the Islamic Republic** — not an Iranian creation
- Already manufacturing arms independently in Yemen
- Revenue from controlled territory (taxes, ports, fuel)
- Demonstrated anti-shipping capability (2023-2024 Red Sea campaign)
- Currently holding back as "strategic reserve" (see [07_DUAL_CHOKEPOINT.md](07_DUAL_CHOKEPOINT.md))
- Operatives in Iraq and Lebanon — horizontal coordination
- Could seek new patrons (Russia, China) if Iran collapses
- May become "new center of gravity" in resistance

## Iraqi Militias (PMF)
- **80,000-100,000 fighters**
- **Controls government ministries** (oil, education, agriculture)
- Revenue streams independent of Tehran (government budget, domestic taxes)
- Integrated into Iraqi state structures
- Even total Iranian collapse doesn't disarm them
- Removing Iranian restraint → **uncontrolled attacks** on US bases and neighbors
- "The last layer of Iranian restraint" is what keeps them somewhat predictable

## Palestinian Groups (Hamas, PIJ)
- Severe funding/coordination cuts expected
- But have diversified funding sources
- Local legitimacy independent of Iran

## Key Insight: Collapse Removes Restraint
- Iran provided COORDINATION and RESTRAINT as well as funding
- Without centralized Iranian command, proxies become autonomous and unpredictable
- "Risks losing control over these networks entirely, creating fragmentation within proxy structures and generating unpredictable armed actors" — Stimson Center
- More autonomous = potentially more dangerous even if materially weaker

## Trade Connection
No "peace premium" even if Iran war ends. Proxy threats persist independently. Houthi Red Sea capability outlasts Iran. Iraqi militia attacks on Gulf energy could continue or escalate. Insurance/shipping costs remain elevated.

**Sources:** Gemini, AEI, MEI, Stimson Center, Sana'a Center, Times of Israel, ACLED
