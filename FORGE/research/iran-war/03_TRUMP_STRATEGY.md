# Trump Strategy — Net Assessment
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-22 | **Confidence:** MEDIUM (analytical framework, not verified claims)

## Methodology
Steelmanned Trump's energy strategy (net assessment), then tested against observable reality. Asked: "What if this is all going according to plan?"

## The Coherent Plan (If One Exists)
1. **Supply consolidation** — knock out Iran, Venezuela, pressure Russia. US + Saudi control remaining barrels.
2. **Break OPEC+** — Russia declining, Iran offline. OPEC+ becomes US-Saudi duopoly.
3. **Dollar hegemony** — if US + Saudi control marginal barrel, all oil stays in dollars.
4. **Russia leverage** — budget bleeding at $40 Urals → negotiate Ukraine on US terms.
5. **Domestic energy boom** — cheap gas + expensive global oil = manufacturing advantage + tariffs = reshoring.
6. **Relief valve** — SPR releases, Saudi coordination, managed Hormuz reopening before midterms.

## What's Working
- ✅ Supply consolidation executing
- ✅ Saudi cooperating (no flood like 2014-15)
- ✅ Dollar hasn't collapsed

## What's NOT Working
- ❌ Gas prices: no narrative cover, no relief valve deployed. $3.91 and rising.
- ❌ Credit/financial transmission: HY OAS 328, PC gating, regionals wobbling. None looks managed.
- ❌ Labor market: NFP -92K. Stagflationary, not expansionary.
- ❌ Russia leverage: Iran war gave Russia windfall (contradicts squeeze goal)
- ❌ Timeline: midterms Nov 2026 in Hamilton damage zone

## Key Contradictions
- Can't squeeze Russia AND wage Iran war (low oil vs high oil)
- Can't have manufacturing boom AND $100 oil AND 20yr-high real rates
- Can't let credit markets crack AND expect economy to power through
- A real plan would resolve these contradictions

## The Russia Question
- Ukraine strikes on Russian oil: green-lighted by Trump (except American commercial interests)
- This argues AGAINST "enriching Russia" — Trump is indifferent, not helpful
- Russia isn't in Trump's equation at all

## Assessment: "Indifference Rather Than Conspiracy"
- Directional strategy exists (energy dominance, maximum pressure)
- But no unified financial model underneath
- Multiple power centers (WH, Treasury, Fed, Pentagon) executing independently
- No one understands the second-order effects
- No one is going to pull the emergency brake at the right time
- **This is what we're trading.**

## Trump's "Venezuela Model" (Gemini)
- Preferred outcome: negotiate a pragmatic insider (Pezeshkian/Rouhani) into power
- Keep oil flowing, check regional aggression
- Not regime change, not status quo
- Problem: Iran won't accept this

## Trade Connection
Whether plan is genius or chaotic, Hamilton still runs. Financial transmission is mechanical, not political. The plan SUCCEEDING may require passing through the exact stress zone we're positioned for.
