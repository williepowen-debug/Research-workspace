# Prompt: Who Sells First? The 2007 Selling Sequence and 2026 Comparison

## Context

## What I Know About 2007

The Federal Reserve's Flow of Funds data (Z.1 release) shows quarterly changes in corporate bond holdings by sector. My understanding is the approximate sequence in 2007-2008 was:

1. Hedge funds / structured vehicles sold first (Q2 2007)
2. Mutual funds started redeeming (Q3 2007)  
3. Insurance companies began reducing exposure (Q3-Q4 2007)
4. Banks recognized losses and wrote down (Q4 2007)
5. Pension funds / endowments sold last (2008)
