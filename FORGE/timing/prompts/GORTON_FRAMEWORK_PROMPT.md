# Prompt: Applying the 2007 Subprime Cascade Framework to 2026 Private Credit

## Context You Need

In 2007, the credit crisis followed a specific transmission chain that Gary Gorton documented in his 2008 paper "The Panic of 2007":

**Subprime losses → ABX index decline → CDO mark-to-market → bank writedowns → interbank funding freeze → systemic crisis**

I'm trying to understand whether the same structural logic applies to the current (2026) private credit market. Here's what's happening:

- Private credit funds (BDCs like Blue Owl, Ares, Apollo) have started gating investor redemptions
- DBRS Morningstar reports 16% of their rated private credit universe is now CCC-C rated
- Default events increased 78% YoY in 2025, accelerating into 2026
- 94% of defaults are distressed debt exchanges (borrowers deferring interest via PIK), not hard defaults
- 2021-2022 vintage leveraged buyouts are cracking — these were done at low rates with high leverage
- CLO vehicles hold much of this debt; OC (overcollateralization) tests are beginning to trip
- PE-controlled insurance companies (Apollo/Athene, Blackstone/Evermore, KKR/Global Atlantic) hold significant private credit on their balance sheets
- The US Treasury convened meetings with insurance regulators about private credit markets on March 30, 2026
- CCC OAS is at 1,013bps while broad HY OAS is at 342bps — a ratio of 2.96, far exceeding anything seen in 2007

## Questions

1. **Map the 2007 transmission chain onto 2026.** What's the equivalent of each link?
   - Subprime mortgages → ?
   - ABX index → ?
   - CDOs → ?
   - Bank balance sheets → ?
   - Interbank funding → ?
   
2. **Gorton's key insight was that opacity created panic** — investors couldn't tell which CDOs held toxic subprime, so they treated ALL structured credit as suspect. Where is the opacity in private credit? What's the equivalent of "nobody knows what's in the CDO"?

3. **In 2007, the ABX index was the early warning signal** — it cracked months before HY OAS moved. It was a traded, transparent index on an opaque underlying market. Is there an equivalent transparent signal for private credit stress? (BDC stock prices? CLO tranche spreads? BCRED NAV?)

4. **The monoline insurers (AMBAC, MBIA) were a hidden amplification layer in 2007** — they guaranteed structured products, and their downgrade created a cascade. Are PE-controlled insurance companies (Athene, Evermore, Global Atlantic) playing the same structural role? How is their exposure different?

5. **What's different about 2026 that could make transmission faster or slower?**
   - Faster: no Fed rate-cutting room (PCE above target), oil shock adding simultaneous stress, private credit is larger ($1.7T vs subprime ~$1.3T)
   - Slower: private credit loans are floating rate (no maturity mismatch like subprime ARMs), less repo/wholesale funding dependency, regulators are engaging earlier

6. **Gorton identified "information insensitive" debt becoming "information sensitive" as the trigger for panic.** At what point does private credit cross that threshold? What specific event would cause investors to suddenly demand transparency on holdings they previously accepted at face value?
