# Prompt: OFR Brief 26-02 — Counterparty Exposures to Private Credit

## Context

The Office of Financial Research (OFR) published Brief 26-02 in March 2026, analyzing counterparty exposures to the private credit ecosystem. This is cited in multiple research analyses as a primary source for:

- **$410-540B in bank + nonbank lending** to private credit funds
- **$300B in uncalled capital commitments** (dry powder that may not materialize if LPs face liquidity stress)
- Counterparty network mapping between banks, insurers, pension funds, and private credit managers

I have not read the primary source. I need a comprehensive analysis of it.

## Questions

1. **Summarize the key findings of OFR Brief 26-02.** What data sources did OFR use? What's the scope (US only? global?)? What time period does it cover?

2. **The $410-540B range.** What's included?
   - Direct lending by banks to private credit funds (subscription lines, NAV loans, margin loans)?
   - Nonbank lending (insurance companies, pension funds)?
   - What's the breakdown by lender type?
   - How much is secured vs. unsecured?
   - What collateral backs the secured portion?

3. **The $300B in uncalled commitments.** 
   - Who are the LPs that have committed?
   - What happens if LPs can't or won't fund capital calls? (Secondaries market? LP default provisions?)
   - Has OFR identified concentration risk (a few large LPs providing disproportionate commitments)?
   - In a stress scenario, how much of the $300B actually gets called and funded?

4. **Counterparty network structure.**
   - Which banks have the largest direct exposure to PC funds?
   - Is exposure concentrated (top 5 banks hold X%) or distributed?
   - Are there hidden interconnections (Bank A lends to Fund B which invests in Company C which has a credit facility from Bank A)?
   - Does OFR map the PE-insurer channel (fund manager → captive insurer → FHLB)?

5. **What does OFR identify as the primary risk channels?**
   - Credit risk (direct losses on PC loans)?
   - Liquidity risk (fire-sale dynamics if multiple funds liquidate)?
   - Counterparty risk (cascading defaults through the network)?
   - Operational risk (valuation opacity, rating inflation)?

6. **How does OFR's assessment compare to other estimates?**
   - BIS: $2.1T global PC market
   - IMF GFSR: various systemic risk warnings
   - Morgan Stanley / Joyce Jiang: 8% default estimate
   - Goldman Sachs / Blostein: $45-70B outflow projection
   - Our own estimate: $3-4T total ecosystem including PE-insurer assets, CLOs, and bank commitments

7. **What recommendations does OFR make?** Enhanced reporting? Stress testing? Regulatory changes? And critically — has anyone acted on them?

8. **Data gaps OFR acknowledges.** What can't they see? Offshore (Bermuda/Cayman) vehicles? Insurer-to-reinsurer transfers? Inter-fund lending within the same PE platform?
