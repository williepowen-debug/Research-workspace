# How Fast Did the Monoline Cascade Play Out in 2007-2008, and How Does the PE-Insurer Version Compare?

## What I Need

A detailed timeline of the 2007-2008 monoline insurer (AMBAC, MBIA) crisis — from first warning to full cascade — so I can map it onto PE-controlled life insurers in 2026.

## Context

In 2007-2008, monoline insurers guaranteed ~$2.4T in structured products against ~$30B in capital. When they were downgraded, banks had to recognize losses on all guaranteed products. The transmission was: downgrade → guarantee worthless → bank writedowns → interbank freeze.

I believe PE-controlled insurers (Athene, Evermore, Global Atlantic) play a structurally similar amplification role in 2026. They hold ~$700B in assets, fund via FABNs and FHLB advances (~$160B), and invest heavily in illiquid private credit originated by their parent PE firms. Athene's surplus ratio is 2.4% vs 7.2% industry average. ~1/5 of investments at Athene and Global Atlantic are loans to affiliated funds (circular exposure).

Current stress: Egan-Jones ratings revoked (Jan 2026), NAIC found private letter ratings 3 notches inflated, SEC/DOJ investigating. Treasury meeting with insurance regulators April 1. 7+ PC funds gated. CCC OAS at 1,013bps.
