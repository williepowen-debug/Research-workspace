# Can Norinchukin's CLO Losses Trigger a Broader Japanese Carry Unwind?

## What I Need

Model the feedback loop: Norinchukin forced to sell US CLO holdings → repatriates to yen → USD/JPY drops → other Japanese institutions face unrealized FX losses → they also repatriate → self-reinforcing carry unwind. How realistic is this, and what's the tipping point?

## Context

**Norinchukin:** ¥8.2T ($54B) in CLOs. If US PC stress forces markdowns, they may need to sell and repatriate — exactly as they sold ¥10T in foreign bonds in 2023-2024.

**Broader Japanese institutional positioning:**
- Life insurers: hedge ratio collapsed 60% → 45.7% (lowest since 2011), hedged UST return now -0.34% vs JGB 2.27%. 7/10 major insurers announced reductions. $50-120B annual swing from buyer to neutral/seller.
- GPIF: world's largest pension fund, rebalances at FY-end (March 31 = today)
- Regional banks: many hold foreign bonds with FX exposure
- Current USD/JPY: ~159.5 with MOF intervention warnings at 160
- BOJ expected to hike May 1 (Shunto 5.26% confirmed, 3rd year >5%)

**1998 precedent:** LTCM crisis + Russia default → carry unwind took yen from 147 to 111 (~24%) in about 2 months. Triggered by loss of confidence in the carry trade, not a single institution.

**CLO secondary market:** Typical daily trading volume is thin relative to Norinchukin's $54B position. Even orderly unwinding over 6 months would be enormous relative to market depth.

## Questions

1. If Norinchukin sells $54B in CLOs, what's the price impact on the CLO secondary market? Who are the potential buyers and at what discount would they step in?

2. What's the transmission from Norinchukin repatriation to broader Japanese institutional behavior? Is there a "herd" dynamic where other institutions pre-emptively repatriate once they see Norinchukin moving? Or do they have different incentive structures?

3. At what USD/JPY level does yen strengthening become self-reinforcing? (Where unrealized FX losses force institutions to de-risk, causing more yen strengthening.) Is there a specific level — 155? 150? 145?

4. How does the BOJ May 1 hike interact? If the hike narrows the rate differential while Norinchukin is simultaneously selling US assets, do these reinforce each other or is the hike already priced?

5. Cross-contamination: if Norinchukin's CLO selling depresses CLO prices in the US, this hits US BDCs and PE-insurers (who also hold CLO tranches) → more PC stress → more CLO deterioration → Norinchukin's remaining holdings lose more value. Is this circular loop realistic or are the volumes too small to matter?

6. How does this compare to 1998? Scale of positions, speed of unwind, institutional actors involved. Is 2026 potentially larger or smaller than the 1998 carry unwind?

An assessment of whether this feedback loop is realistic or theoretical.
