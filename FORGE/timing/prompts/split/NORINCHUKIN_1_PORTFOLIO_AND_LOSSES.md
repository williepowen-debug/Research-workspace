## Context

Norinchukin is Japan's largest agricultural cooperative bank. After ¥1.5T+ in losses on foreign bonds in 2023-2024, it sold ~¥10T in foreign bonds and rotated into CLOs:

- **¥8.2 trillion ($54B) in CLOs** — ~18% of total portfolio
- One of the world's largest single CLO holders
- Structural need for yield above cooperative deposit costs
- Required ¥1.9T capital injection from cooperatives after 2008 GFC losses on CDOs/CLOs
