### Critical Corrections to Conventional Wisdom

1. **Banks were BUYERS in Q3-Q4 2007** (+$296-310B SAAR in Q3). They absorbed SIV portfolios onto balance sheets. Didn't flip to selling until Q2 2008 — a full 3-quarter lag from first sellers. This contradicts the popular narrative that banks were early victims.

2. **Mutual funds were net BUYERS through Q3 2008 in aggregate.** Manconi/Massa/Yasuda (2012 JFE) resolved this paradox: exposed funds sold aggressively while unexposed funds bought the dip. Z.1 aggregate data masked massive cross-sectional heterogeneity.

3. **Private pensions never sold** in some analyses — they maintained buying throughout. Longest-duration, most patient capital.
