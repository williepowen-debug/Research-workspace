# Treasury Safe-Haven Failures and Collateral Chain Exposure

> **HEADLINE:** Treasuries have failed as safe havens in at least five major episodes since 1994. The financial system's dependence on Treasury collateral has grown so large — roughly $5-7T pledged in repo alone — that any future breakdown could trigger a self-reinforcing margin spiral. March 2020 required two emergency interventions and ultimately unlimited QE to restore functioning.

### B) October 15, 2014 Flash Rally
- 37bps intraday range (4th largest since 1998; other 3 had major policy catalysts)
- 12-minute window: 9:33-9:45 AM ET
- Yield: ~2.23% → 1.86% → closed -6bps from open

### C) September 2019 Repo Crisis
- **Sept 16 PM:** First stress in DVP-brokered interdealer segment. Domestic bank-affiliated dealers ceased lending. SOFR: 2.43% (+13bps). EFFR: 2.25% (top of target).
- **Sept 17:** SOFR spiked to **5.25%** (median). 99th percentile: **9%**. Bilateral: up to **10%**.
- NY Fed announced repo ops ~9:00 AM Sept 17 (~18-20 hours after first signs). Delayed to 9:55 AM (tech issues). Only $53B of $75B taken (most trades already done).
- Reserves had fallen below $1.4T (lowest since 2011). $65B drained by tax payments + $54B Treasury settlement.
- **SOFR/EFFR back in range by Sept 19 (72 hours).**
- Emergency repo operations continued for **16 months** (through Jan 2021).
- T-bill purchases ($60B/month) began Oct 15, 2019.

### D) 1994 Bond Market Massacre
- 10Y bottom: ~5.17-5.24% (mid-Oct 1993) → peak ~8.0% (Nov 1994) = **~270-280bps over 13 months**
- 20Y+ segment: **-20.5% in price**
- Global bond losses: **$1.5T**

### E) The Positive Correlation Regime
- Morgan Stanley (2 centuries): positive stock-bond correlation in **78% of years since 1870**. Negative era (2000-2021) was the exception.
- **2022:** S&P -18.04%, 10Y -17.83%, Bloomberg Agg -13.01% (worst in ~5 decades). 60/40: -15-17%.
- Rolling 1Y correlation: turned persistently positive, averaging **0.41 (2022-24)** vs -0.37 prior decade (State Street).
- **April 2025 "Sell America":** After Liberation Day tariffs, 10Y spiked 3.96%→4.50% (Apr 4-9), 30Y topped 5%, S&P -20%, dollar -6%. Stocks+bonds+dollar all fell simultaneously. Bessent raised bond market concerns with Trump → 90-day pause Apr 9. Moody's downgraded US in May 2025.
- **Correlation flips positive when:** supply-driven inflation (countercyclical), high inflation uncertainty vs growth uncertainty, hawkish policy (Campbell/Sunderam/Viceira 2017; Pflueger JFE 2025). AQR: growth-vol-to-inflation-vol ratio explains ~70% of long-term variation. Threshold: **CPI above ~2.4%** (Morgan Stanley, 150yr data).

- 10-15Y: 6%
- 15+Y: 9%

### LCR Haircut Rules
- **Level 1 HQLA (Treasuries): permanent 0% haircut, no cap on share.** No market-price threshold for haircut.
- Key constraint: **encumbrance.** Treasuries pledged elsewhere (repo, discount window) CANNOT count as HQLA.
- Structural tension: using Treasuries for margin calls during stress simultaneously reduces LCR buffers.
- SRF partially addresses by allowing Treasury→reserves conversion on demand.

### 5% Price Drop Margin Estimate
- March 2020 (3-5% long-duration moves over 2-3 weeks): IM +$300B, peak VM $140B/day
- Basis trade ($800+B gross notional at ~6x leverage): **$40-80B margin calls** from 5% cash-leg drop
- CME Treasury futures OI ($4-5T notional): enormous VM exposure
- Sudden 5% move (75-100bps): plausibly **$50-100+B aggregate margin calls** across FICC + CME alone

- Earliest indicators: (1) BrokerTec bid-ask Mar 6-9, (2) order book depth collapse Mar 12-13, (3) MOVE spike ~164, (4) Treasury-OIS spread blowout, (5) yield curve fitting errors (BIS Bulletin 2, Graph 2)

### Lehman 2008: Bankruptcy → Guarantee = 4 Days; → TARP = 18 Days
- **Sept 15:** Lehman filed
- **Sept 16 (~24hrs):** Reserve Primary broke the buck (NAV $0.9944 by 11AM; later determined $0.97)
- **Sept 17:** $144B withdrawn from US MMFs
- **Sept 19 (4 days):** Treasury money market guarantee
- **Sept 20 (5 days):** TARP proposal submitted

- **Oct 3 (18 days):** TARP signed
- Earliest sustained indicator: **LIBOR-OIS spread** jumped 10→50bps in early Aug 2007 (**13+ months before Lehman**). BNP Paribas suspended 3 mortgage fund redemptions Aug 9, 2007. TED spread: 240bps on Aug 20, 2007.
- Lehman CDS paradoxically stable through summer 2008 (moral hazard, assumed rescue) — CDS was a POOR early warning.

## CONCLUSION

The collateral chain is the transmission mechanism. Treasury price declines → margin calls → forced Treasury sales → higher haircuts → more margin calls. Self-reinforcing.

**Vulnerability points that have GROWN since March 2020:**
- $5-7T Treasuries pledged in repo
- $800+B basis trade (was $598B pre-2020)
- 74% of bilateral repos at zero haircuts
- 3 of 6 GSIBs at zero remaining SLR capacity
- Collateral velocity stuck at 2.2-2.5x (vs 3.0 pre-crisis) = $4-5T lost effective collateral
- Positive stock-bond correlation now the regime (CPI >2.4% threshold breached)

**The shift to positive stock-bond correlation means the next Treasury stress may coincide with equity weakness — removing the natural buyer that typically stabilizes the market.**
