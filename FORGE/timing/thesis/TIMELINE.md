## What We're NOT Calling

- **Exact OAS levels at each phase.** The 2007 overlay provides guideposts, not predictions. The velocity matters more than the level.
- **Timing to the week.** "May-July" means May-July. Not "May 15th."
- **Equity levels.** Credit leads equity by ~100-120 days. We trade credit timing, not equity calls.
