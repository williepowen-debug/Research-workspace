## Key Pattern: The Denial-Recognition-Panic Sequence

**2007 pattern (9 months):**
1. **June-July**: Fund-level problems dismissed as "isolated" and "contained"
2. **August**: Liquidity shock (BNP) reveals systemic interconnection
3. **Sep-Oct**: Central bank cuts + brief relief rally → false calm
4. **Nov-Dec**: Writedown cascade destroys the "contained" narrative
5. **Jan-Mar 08**: Panic → emergency measures → institutional casualties

**2026 watch for the same sequence:**
1. ✅ Private credit "gates" begin (Blue Owl, Ares) — "idiosyncratic"
2. ⏳ A European or Asian CLO/credit fund freezes redemptions — systemic signal
3. ⏳ Fed cuts → brief relief
4. ⏳ Major manager NAV writedowns in Q3/Q4 reporting
5. ⏳ Institutional failure or forced merger
