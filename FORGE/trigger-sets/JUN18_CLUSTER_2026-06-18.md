## Operating Principle

If no trigger fires, do nothing → let expire mechanically. If a trigger fires, take the named action. Eliminates "should I roll?" judgment in the moment. Daily monitor checks regime triggers (R1-R4) + position-specific triggers. Hard backstop on 2026-06-16 16:00 ET clears the field.

**Vol-floor constraint (Will, 2026-05-22):** triggers require *regime break*, not drift. Bias toward let-expire when triggers are ambiguous. The motivating concern is that rolling equity puts at all-time-highs + suppressed vol pays rich premium for replacement strikes; a pullback gives both cheaper IV and directional confirmation. Cross-references `feedback_put_vs_duration_expression` memory entry.
