### 2d · The category-mismatch statement — VIOLET's, adopted as the sharper framing

> **The bloc's gates are, by construction, co-movement detectors.** VIX prices index variance, which is mechanically a function of constituent correlation. Credit OAS indices blend tranches. SKEW prices tail *demand*, which spikes on expectation of a *common* shock. **These instruments are built to see stress that is already correlated across names or assets. Migration into idiosyncratic channels — one AI borrower's DDTL repricing, one sovereign-credibility repricing in gold, one currency's RV/IV gap — is close to invisible to them while it is still idiosyncratic.** This is not an instrument-selection mistake fixable by adding more daily series; it is a mismatch between what correlation-sensitive gates can see and what migration looks like in its early phase. *(`02_VIOLET_cross-read` §2. BOND endorses it over HENRY's framing as the one that should survive: `04_BOND_cross-read` §5.)*

**The one exception, and it is a hybrid, not a clean counter-example:** **GATE-LIQ-069** is the only gate in the bloc with genuinely idiosyncratic legs — single-issuer CDS, single-deal new-issue pricing, a four-ticker cohort, one issuer's rating ladder. Four of its five legs are the right *shape* to see migration early. **But its consequential endpoint — index ejection — requires 2 of 3 rating agencies to independently cross to HY, which is itself an aggregation/co-movement construct.** LIQUID's own conclusion, adopted: **the quiet legs see it first; the loud leg still needs co-movement to fire.** *(`03_LIQUID_cross-read` §1.)*

**LIQUID's corollary belongs in the map:** the idiosyncratic instruments are **expensive and slow to trust** — the ORCL/CRWV picture required four inbox packets and three successive corrections to reach its current state, versus FRED's always-on daily series. **A bloc under time pressure defaults to cheap co-movement gates not only because they are cheap, but because the alternatives take four rounds of correction to believe.**
