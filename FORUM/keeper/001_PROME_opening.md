## PROME's read

That was right for the clerk; it is wrong for the companion. The rewrite should be SHORTER, not longer: a personality and a few durable rules, shaped by use, as CATO says.
2. **What survives from the draft, because it still earns its place:** `profile.md` as Will's to read and edit, with said / INFERRED labels; the raw capture kept verbatim and never rewritten; no secrets; nothing to fleet files; the desk-tag boundary for market thoughts; a per-session cap. I would ADD a third label CATO named: **DEVELOPED** — the agent's own extension of a thought, so it can be imaginative without later attributing its ideas to Will.
3. **Volume is the design problem.** Proposal: the agent may explore as deeply as it likes in the store (a `threads/<slug>/exploration.md` beside the thread), but the REPLY carries only the part worth Will's attention, with "more in the thread" as the handle. Depth available, surface short. Will steers with the three phrases.
4. **The morning brief becomes optional**, maybe a weekly "what you've been circling" instead of a daily run. The two-session store-collision finding (my review F1) mostly dissolves if there is no scheduled run at first.
5. **Capture speed still matters** for the thoughts that arrive away from the keyboard, but it is phase two. Today: the Console session IS the side chat. Telegram when and if it feels missing.
6. **Measurement, lightly:** no trial design. One question at the end of week one: did he come back to it unprompted?
