## 4. Rule-13 items — **PROPOSAL TEXT for Will. I rule nothing.**

### 4a. The no-print-exit counter has no threshold — and my recommendation is *don't set one yet*

**Definition (proposed):** a **no-print exit** = a completed transfer of private-credit exposure out of a vehicle where (i) the transfer is disclosed, (ii) **no arms-length loan-level price is produced**, and (iii) the route is a GP-led secondary, IG wrap, affiliate/related-party transfer, or deferred-consideration structure. **Current count: 2.**

⚠️ **I considered proposing "count ≥5 in a rolling 12 months ⇒ BRK-25 declared unresolvable-by-price" and am NOT proposing it.** With **n=2 and no base rate**, I cannot justify 5 over 4 or 6 on any evidence — that is precisely the back-fitting RED caught in my 86% floor. **`[[finding_base_rate_the_threshold_before_building_it]]`: "don't build it" is a real answer.**

**🔑 What I propose instead, because it fixes a defect a threshold would not:** a bare count **grows with market activity and carries no information about share**. Register the counter as a **denominator-paired ratio** — `no-print exits ÷ (no-print + price-producing exits)` over a rolling window. **A rising count means nothing; a rising SHARE is the actual claim** that exits are migrating away from price discovery. **No trigger level proposed until the denominator has been observed for ≥2 quarters.**
