## 1. Holding all three lanes: this is a gradient, not three separate findings

Read together, the three P0s do not describe three parallel instances of the same problem — they describe a **worsening gradient down the chain**, and the gradient itself is the answer to "health or deferral":

- **Collateral (mine):** the instruments exist and move (DQ, SS), but they measure *payment status*, and modification/extension is a lever built specifically to keep payment status clean regardless of collateral value. Deferral here is **visible if you know where to look** (the maturity-adjusted DQ, the SS-over-DQ spread) — it just isn't visible in the headline.
- **Vehicle (BROCK):** recognition has **already occurred**, in the one channel (cash — FSK's fee waiver) that cannot be deferred — but no registered trigger on BROCK's board watches cash. Deferral here is a **specification gap**, not an absence of signal.
- **Absorber (SHADE):** there is **no forcing clock at all**. The one time recognition surfaced (Delaware Life's $16.4B restatement), neither the examiner nor the rating agency produced it — a grand jury subpoena did. Deferral here is **structural absence of a mechanism**, not a gap in an existing one.

Each stage is *more* deferral-friendly than the one before it. That ordering matters for the bloc's verdict: if the chain runs collateral → vehicle → absorber and each hop makes recognition harder to force, then **the "quiet" gets more uninformative as it migrates up the chain, not less** — the absorber stage's silence tells you almost nothing, precisely because it has no clock, while the collateral stage's silence at least has a payment-status series moving underneath it that a maturity-wall date will eventually force.
