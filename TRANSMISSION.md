## Primary Chain: LABOR → CARL → REGINALD

### Link 1: LABOR → CARL
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Mechanism** | Job loss → income loss → missed payments → delinquencies |
| **Timing lag** | 3-6 months |
| **Trigger (LABOR)** | Claims >300K sustained OR U-3 >5.0% OR NFP <0 |
| **Watch (CARL)** | Auto 60+ DPD, CC charge-offs, subprime ABS spreads |
| **Owner** | LABOR signals; CARL confirms |

**Current status:** LABOR ORANGE (leading indicators), CARL YELLOW (lagging)

### Link 2: CARL → REGINALD
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Mechanism** | Consumer defaults → credit losses → bank earnings hit → capital pressure |
| **Timing lag** | 2-4 quarters |
| **Trigger (CARL)** | Auto losses >3%, CC charge-offs >5%, subprime stress |
| **Watch (REGINALD)** | NCO ratios, reserve builds, NIM compression |
| **Owner** | CARL signals; REGINALD confirms |

**Current status:** CARL YELLOW → REGINALD GREEN (not yet transmitting)

## Parallel Track: LIQUID

### Amplification Role
LIQUID doesn't cause the chain — it **amplifies** if any stage breaks.

| If this breaks... | LIQUID amplifies via... |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| LABOR shock | Flight to quality → Treasury demand → RRP spike |
| CARL stress | CP/ABS market stress → funding costs rise |
| REGINALD wobble | Interbank stress → SOFR-IORB blowout |
| BROCK redemptions | Fund finance scramble → dealer balance sheet pressure |

**Key levels:**
- RRP <$5B = RED (currently ~$10B, effectively zero cushion)
- SOFR-IORB >+5bps = YELLOW, >+15bps = ORANGE
- SRF usage >$50B sustained = ORANGE

**Current status:** LIQUID GREEN but fragile ("metastable")

## Market Positioning: HENRY

### Role
HENRY tells us **how the market is positioned** when stress hits — not a cause, but determines severity of reaction.

| Positioning | Impact on stress transmission |
|-------------|------------------------------|
| High GEX (positive gamma) | Dealers buy dips → dampens volatility |
| Low/negative GEX | Dealers sell into weakness → amplifies moves |
| VIX complacency (IV Rank <15) | No hedges in place → violent repricing |
| CTA long exposure | Trend break → systematic selling cascade |

**Key levels:**
- Put Wall: 6,920 (first support)
- CTA Flip: 6,494 (systematic selling begins)
- Volatility Trigger: 6,400 (gamma flip zone)

**Current status:** HENRY YELLOW (complacency high, positive gamma masking fragility)
