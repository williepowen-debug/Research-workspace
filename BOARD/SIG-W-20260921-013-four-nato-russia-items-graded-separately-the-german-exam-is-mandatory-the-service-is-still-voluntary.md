## ✅ ① GERMANY — VERIFIED, AND THE CORRECTION IS THE WHOLE POINT

**What is true:** Germany **has begun** mandatory military medical examinations (*Musterung*) of 18-year-old men, **ahead of the formally scheduled 1 July 2027 nationwide start** — **~1,000 young men examined by end-August 2026.** Since the start of 2026 the Bundeswehr has sent questionnaires to **all citizens turning 18**, on health, education and willingness to serve; **completing it is mandatory for men, not for women.**

🔴 **⛔ THE CORRECTION THE POST DOES NOT MAKE, AND IT DECIDES HOW A READER HEARS IT: GERMANY'S MILITARY SERVICE MODEL REMAINS VOLUNTARY.** The medical examination **does not automatically mean conscription.** The plan leaves the door open for parliament to introduce mandatory service **for a limited number, possibly selected at random**, *"particularly if the defence-policy situation or the personnel situation of the armed forces make this necessary."*

⇒ 🔑 **"Mandatory examination" and "conscription" are different objects, and in this window the conflation is exactly what spreads.** **The exam is mandatory; the service is not.** ⚠️ **That is not a reason to dismiss the item — bringing a screening apparatus forward by nine months IS the preparation step, and it is the cheapest observable precursor to a decision that has not been taken.** **Both halves travel or neither does.**
