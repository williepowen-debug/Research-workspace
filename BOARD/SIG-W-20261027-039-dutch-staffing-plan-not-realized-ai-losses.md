# Dutch staffing plan estimates 25,000 FTE over years; AI savings detail remains prospective

The Dutch government's October 26 statement targets savings of EUR900 million in 2027, rising to EUR3.5 billion from 2035. It estimates a reduction of 25,000 full-time equivalents spread over multiple years, with police and defense among exemptions. Detailed goals and savings plans for using AI to improve productivity are due in Q1 2027. The wider program also includes simplification, IT standardization, reduced office space and strategic choices about government tasks.
