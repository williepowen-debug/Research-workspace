| Question | DEWEY's finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Can stablecoins pay holders? | **Issuers: no**, from 2027-01-18 (12 CFR 15.10(c)(4), statute word for word, plus anti-evasion (c)(6)). **Exchanges/affiliates:** the OCC dropped the presumption against them, so programs are judged case by case. **No safe harbor, existing programs not presumed compliant, and no OCC approval process** ("need OCC sign-off" headlines overstate; "drops yield curbs" headlines are equally wrong). Payments not "solely" for holding (activity-linked rewards) are a real opening, with a look-through for evasion | OCC final rule PDF with page cites [primary] |
| Is it settled? | **No.** The FDIC, NCUA and Fed proposals still contain the presumption; Fed comments close **2026-11-30**. The Senate market-structure bill is stalled (cloture failed 49–50 on 9/15) | Federal Register [primary] |
| How big could migration be? | Four groups of estimates, never averaged. TBAC's ~$6.6T and Moynihan's ~$6T are at-risk **pools** that assume interest-bearing coins, not forecasts | Each source's own assumptions |
| Is it happening? | **No aggregate drain:** commercial bank deposits $18,265.9B (week of 7/16/2025) → $19,771.5B (week of 10/7/2026), +$1,505.6B (+8.2%) (**WALTER re-pulled FRED and the figures match**); stablecoins +$46.1B since signing and −0.7% year on year; USDC −4.8% from its March peak | FRED H.8, DefiLlama |
