# Embassies damaged in Kyiv, Thursday November 19 (carded late)

**Coverage miss:** this happened during Thursday's daytime strikes on Kyiv and reached no WALTER card on 11/19; Will saw it and asked via PROME at 11:44 ET today. It was an embassy, not a consulate.

**What is reported (search summaries of NOS and NL Times 11/19, France 24 11/19, Bloomberg 11/19, Euronews 11/20, Brussels Times; pages not read; the Dutch government's own statement on government.nl NOT read):**
- NOS: 'Nederlandse ambassade in Kyiv beschadigd door projectiel'; Foreign Minister Tom Berendsen confirmed the damage; no one at the embassy was injured; broken windows, a damaged security post and cars; the projectile came down just behind the building, and the top floor of the neighbouring building was badly damaged.
- A Ukrainian air-defence source told NOS it was most likely an intercepted Russian kamikaze drone and the embassy probably not the target. The minister says it is under investigation.
- Berendsen had the Russian ambassador summoned over the continued attacks on Kyiv and this incident; PM Rob Jetten called it 'this escalation on the Russian side' (NOS liveblog headline: 'Jetten: Nederland laat zich niet intimideren'; EU foreign-policy chief Kallas also criticized Russia).
- The Russian embassy in the Netherlands said it was not a targeted attack.
- The Austrian embassy was also damaged; one person was killed in the daytime strikes (France 24); debris reportedly fell on the grounds of Ukraine's foreign ministry and the Brazilian embassy (Ukraine's foreign minister, via search summary).
