## Signal

**Blue Owl Capital (OWL) Co-CEOs Doug Ostrover and Marc Lipschultz unwound >$1.1B of personal share-pledged margin loans**, releasing OWL Class A common units from collateral. Schedule 13D/A filed with SEC ~Apr 17 2026. Pledged-share ratio moved from 12.4% to 3.1% of their combined stake; <600K shares remain pledged between them.

**Critical caveat:** Michael Rees (third co-founder, Co-President) is NOT party to the amendment. Fongern's "co-founders" plural framing (implying all three) is inaccurate.

**Second critical caveat:** SEC filings confirm RELEASE of pledged shares. They do NOT disclose what replaced the collateral. Original loan agreements permitted "alternative collateral in lieu of the pledged common units" — but whether the loans were repaid, refinanced, or actually replaced with alternative collateral is NOT public.
