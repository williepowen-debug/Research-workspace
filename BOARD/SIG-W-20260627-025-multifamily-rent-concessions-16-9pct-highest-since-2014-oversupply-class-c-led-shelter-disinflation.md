# Multifamily rent concessions hit 16.9% — highest since 2014, oversupply-driven, Class-C-led; effective rents falling (CREED)

**One line:** Concession usage across stabilized US apartments rose to **16.9% in March 2026** (+0.2pp MoM, **+5.1pp YoY — highest since 2014**), average discount steady at 10.8% (~6 weeks free rent on a 12-mo lease). **Class divide:** Class C (workforce) 21.5% > Class B 15.0% > Class A 14.5%, but Class A saw the sharpest monthly uptick. **Region:** South 21.2% + West 16.4% lead (supply-heavy); Midwest 10.2% / Northeast 12.6% restrained. **Markets:** Austin/Denver/San Antonio ~⅓ of units offering concessions (Austin/Denver depth 14.8%), Texas metros dominate, rising in **Jacksonville + Tampa**. The takeaway: concessions are structural — a response to oversupply — until construction slows.

The supply correction is starting (permits −16% Q1, MF-led). **Macro/shelter read:** rising concessions + rents only +0.2% April = effective rents *falling* = continued CPI-shelter disinflation, the offset to the stagflation thesis. **Bifurcation:** Sunbelt oversupply vs Chicago/Miami/SF *tightening* (supply shrinking, demand climbing) = a regional split, not uniform weakness. **NOTE: the clipping's "Starwood $22B SREIT redemption halt" is NOT new — already routed 5/11 (SIG-511-015, BROCK has it); not re-dispatched.**
