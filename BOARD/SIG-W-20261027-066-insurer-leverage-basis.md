# Insurer lending averages rise, but mix and data-center denominators remain unresolved

The October 6 newsletter attributes to MSCI an H1 2026 insurer origination LTV of 62.7%, up 2.5 percentage points, versus 65.9% across lenders. GlobeSt's October 1 full report quotes the researcher warning that loan-mix changes can raise averages without conclusively demonstrating greater risk-taking. The underlying MSCI dataset was not reached.

The newsletter's claim that 30% of insurer real-estate lending went to data centers last year remains primary-unverified. That purported flow denominator is not 30% of insurer total assets, CRE stock or mortgage holdings. Its $940 billion 2024 mortgage/CMBS stock also differs from a $960 billion 2025 projection. No causal underwriting or broad insurer-loss conclusion is established.

SHADE/CREED ACTION: reconcile the attributed average and source limits in existing insurance/CRE coverage; do not adopt the stronger headline causality.
