# Michigan: weaker durable buying conditions and spending plans split by income and stock ownership

**New primary through Will’s transcript3 source list:** Michigan’s **October preliminary** survey reports sentiment **46.3**, current conditions **44.7**, expectations **47.3**; year-ahead inflation expectations **4.7%**, long-run **3.5%**. Lower-income/smaller-stock-portfolio sentiment fell; buying conditions for durables weakened. Final October release is October23.

A separate **October9 gasoline/spending study** covers **3,492 interviews June23–September21**, asking about plans for items with large price increases. **31%** expect usual spending, **54%** cutting back, **16%** stopping; rounding prevents exact sum. Usual-spending intentions: **20% lower income third vs42% upper third**, **18% non-stockholders vs50% upper stockholding third**. These cohort splits are new to the checked BOARD/consumer record.

**Limits:** intentions are not realized spending; cutting one item or substituting brands need not reduce total expenditure. Income thirds and stockholding thirds are separate populations. High-resource groups retain stronger buffers; this does not establish that high-income spending is collapsing, or that gasoline caused a measured aggregate decline.
