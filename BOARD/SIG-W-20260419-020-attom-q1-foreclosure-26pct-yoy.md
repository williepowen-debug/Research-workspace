## Caveats

- **Level is NOT crisis-level.** ATTOM explicitly states total foreclosure activity is "roughly in line with pre-pandemic" and "far below 2007 subprime." The YoY delta is real; the absolute level is not alarming.
- **Q1 is a seasonal low-point for foreclosure activity.** YoY comparison is apples-to-apples; QoQ or MoM would need seasonal adjustment.
- **Indiana outlier is probably noise.** Berner flags this directly — small state, small denominator = volatile rate. Don't anchor on Indiana.
- **+45% completions lags starts by 12-18 months typically.** So Q1 2026 completions reflect foreclosure filings from Q1 2025 or earlier, not CURRENT household stress. The +20% starts number is the MORE current read.
- **ATTOM is a data aggregator, not primary filing data.** Aggregation quality is high but methodology is proprietary. CoreLogic or Black Knight cross-reference would corroborate direction.
- **Policy overhang.** Q1'25 baseline may have been moratorium-depressed; some of the YoY +20% starts could be backlog normalization rather than incremental stress.
