## 2. 🔑 WHY THE **UNIT** IS THE SIGNAL, NOT THE FIRE

This morning HAWK answered WALTER's open refinery-vs-processing question with a natural experiment in OSPREY's theater (`KB-HAWK-234`, folded into `SIG-W-20260727-001` §8 and the anchor):

> **Russia 2026 had ~30% of national refining capacity destroyed — and its CRUDE EXPORTS hit 4.22M bpd, the highest since the 2022 invasion, while diesel/gasoil loadings COLLAPSED from ~817 kbpd (2025 avg) to 234 kbpd.**
> **Mechanism: a refinery outage does not remove barrels from the world — it re-routes them from the PRODUCT pool to the CRUDE pool. CRUDE-BEARISH, PRODUCT-BULLISH.**

**A deep diesel hydrotreater is the specific unit whose loss removes DIESEL, specifically.** It is not a generic "refinery on fire" — it is a strike on the diesel leg precisely.

**⇒ This is HAWK's aggregate mechanism appearing at the unit level.** The aggregate finding said *diesel loadings collapsed while crude exports rose*; this is one observation of **the physical means by which that happens.** It is a confirming instance, and it is the kind that is normally missing when a mechanism is inferred from two aggregate series.

**⚠️ AND THE PRECISION THAT CUTS AGAINST OVER-READING IT — the same caveat WALTER insisted on this morning applies here:**
- **151 kbpd is small** against ~30% of Russian refining capacity already offline. HAWK's own caveat on Jazan was *"direction well-evidenced, magnitude at 400 kbpd may be lost in noise"* — **this is a THIRD the size of Jazan.**
- **A hydrotreater hit is not the same as the unit being destroyed.** The fire was extinguished; **no outage duration, no confirmed throughput loss, and no Russian disclosure of damage.** *(The same standard applied to Jazan: a unit on fire and a unit offline are different facts.)*
- **This does NOT confirm the mechanism on today's tape.** Brent is −7.86% on the US–Iran pause (`SIG-W-20260727-015`). **Two effects pointing the same way is exactly the condition under which a desk banks a confirmation it has not earned.** **The clean instrument remains CRACKS AND DIESEL, not flat crude** — landing on the Goldman 7/20 long-diesel work (OECD diesel 4th percentile, `SIG-W-20260721-006`).
