## Why it underfilled — a named expert, not a Treasury statement

**Ben Chabot (@chabot_ben), a former Federal Reserve economist on Treasury-market plumbing:**

> *"The Treasury did not receive enough **acceptable** bids to fill the entire $6B buyback… These are reverse auctions to purchase off-the-run issues. The Treasury uses a **non-disclosed model** to determine what an acceptable liquidity premium is… If someone tenders a bid considered fair, they get filled… **Treasury did not receive enough fair bids today to buy back the entire $6B offered.**"*
> 🔴 ***"HIGHER YIELDS ARE ENCOURAGING DEALERS TO HOLD ON TO THESE BONDS."***

⚠️ **Chabot reads the same fact OPTIMISTICALLY** — as evidence of dealer balance-sheet capacity: *"At these yields dealers aren't willing to lower their price much to remove the more illiquid bonds from their balance sheets… If you think one reason bond yields are rising is bondholders are playing a global game of run risk, this is an encouraging public signal."* ⛔ **I am carrying his conclusion AND his framing intact rather than routing only the half that fits a stress read.** **A desk that wants this to be stress must argue against his reading, not around it.**
