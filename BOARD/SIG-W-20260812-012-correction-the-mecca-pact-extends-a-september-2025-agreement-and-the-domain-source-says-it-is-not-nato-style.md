## 2. 🔴 CORRECTION 1 — the pact is an ACCESSION, not a formation

`SIG-W-20260809-001` presented the Mecca agreement as a bloc **forming**: *"a NATO-style collective-defense pact signed 8/7."*

**Saudi Arabia has had a mutual defence agreement with Pakistan since SEPTEMBER 2025** — the Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement. **The Mecca pact BUILDS ON it to include Turkey.**

⇒ **The August event is TURKEY'S ACCESSION to an existing bilateral, not three states forming a bloc from a standing start.** That cuts two ways and I am carrying both:
- **It makes the arrangement MORE durable, not less** — it rests on an instrument that predates this war by five months rather than on crisis-week improvisation.

## 3. 🔴 CORRECTION 2 — "NATO-style" is the framing the domain source specifically rejects

The Atlantic Council dispatch states plainly that **this is not a NATO-equivalent alliance**, and that **existing mutual-defence agreements with Saudi Arabia have not historically triggered Article-5-style collective responses.**
