# Dallas Fed survey: about half of oil executives (48%) expect the diesel crack to stay above 2025 levels for more than a year.

**Primary: Dallas Fed Energy Survey, Q3 2026 (released 9/30), special questions, 125 firms.**
- Q: how many quarters until the **spread between fuel prices and crude** returns to 2025 levels?
  - **Diesel: 48% say more than four quarters**
  - Gasoline: 36% say more than four quarters
  - "More than four quarters" was the most common answer for both.
- A support-services respondent: *"Diesel is the mother's milk of the economy. We are just starting to see the impact on the wider economy."*

**So what:** the people who run refineries and oilfield firms expect expensive diesel relative to crude to last **into 2028**. It lands the same week India, Russia and China pulled product exports (`-0930-006`, `-008`).

## Caveats (corrections to the relay)
- The question is about the **crack spread**, not the pump price. "Diesel prices won't return to normal" overstates it.
- **n = 125**, not 100.
- The Chevron CEO "Great Fuel Crisis" quote in the same post is **not verified** here.
