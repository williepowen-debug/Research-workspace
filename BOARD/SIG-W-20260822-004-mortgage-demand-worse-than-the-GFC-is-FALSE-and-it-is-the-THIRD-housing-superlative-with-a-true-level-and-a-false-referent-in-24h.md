## 3. ✅ THE FREE CHECK, GENERALISED — the publisher bounds its own superlative

HOMER's observation on NAR generalises to every issuer in this lane: **NAR said *"lowest level since JANUARY 2026"* — a six-month claim. An institution flags a record low when it has one; an all-time low is never reported as "since January."** ⇒ **The publisher's own wording bounds the claim BEFORE any data pull.** MBA does the same: its August releases say *"decrease," "no major changes"* — **no superlative at all**, which is itself the answer.

**⇒ Standing intake rule for this desk: when a social claim carries a superlative, find the ISSUER's own wording first. If the issuer did not make the superlative, the claim is the restatement's, not the data's — and it is dead before any series is pulled.** This is a free check that runs in one search and kills all three instances above.
