## Caveats

- **Tax-related share is unquantified in the chart.** April 15 tax payments routinely cause MMF outflow spikes — historical April prints are systematically more negative than other months. Without a same-week-prior-year comparison, can't isolate "structural" from "tax-mechanical." BofA's framing acknowledges this; analysis should not.
- **EPFR data covers only EPFR-tracked funds.** Series captures retail-skewed MMF, not the full institutional MMF universe (which is much larger and slower-moving). The $-175bn print is from EPFR's coverage subset, not industry total.
- **"Risk-on" framing assumes outflow → equity inflow.** Cash leaving MMF could go to: equities, longer-duration bonds, bank deposits, real estate, or just spending. The "into equities" assumption is plausible but not data-shown in this chart.
- **Single weekly print.** Weekly MMF flows are noisy; need to see if the trend persists into late April. One spike could revert.
