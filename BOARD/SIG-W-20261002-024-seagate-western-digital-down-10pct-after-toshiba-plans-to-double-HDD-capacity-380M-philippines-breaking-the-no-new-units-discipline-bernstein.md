# Seagate and Western Digital fall about 10% after Toshiba plans to double hard-drive capacity, breaking the industry's "no new units" discipline.

**Short version:** **Toshiba** plans to **double its hard-disk-drive production capacity within fiscal 2027**, investing **about ¥60B (~$380M)** in the Philippines and aiming to lift its market share **from just over 10% to 30% over the medium term**. That breaks the supply discipline Bernstein described: *"None of STX, WDC, or Toshiba are adding drive unit capacity."* **Seagate (STX) $849.76, −10.1%, and Western Digital (WDC) $414.84, −10.3%, at 15:58 ET**, after gains of **+208% and +141% year to date** on AI data-center storage demand.

**So what:** The AI storage trade has run on **constrained supply.** A third supplier adding units is the first **capacity-side crack** in that story. Whether it bites depends on Toshiba getting **media and heads**, which it buys from others.
