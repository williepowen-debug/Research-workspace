**China (secondary):** the PBOC midpoint was reported at 6.7180 (FXStreet) and 6.7192 (Finimize), the strongest fix since early February 2023, with the yuan heading for a fourth straight weekly gain. The PBOC's standing line is an "appropriately loose" policy with the yuan "broadly stable at a reasonable and balanced level"; it has rejected claims that it holds the yuan weak for trade advantage. The fifth plenum runs 10/26-29 and the IEEPA tariff clock (11/10) is ZHAO's existing watch.

A Reuters poll cited by investingLive has 69% of economists expecting a hike to 1.50% this quarter, about 80% of those naming December over the meeting ending 10/30.
