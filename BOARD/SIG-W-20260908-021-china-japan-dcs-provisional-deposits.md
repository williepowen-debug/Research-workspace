# China imposes provisional deposits on Japanese dichlorosilane imports

## Signal and data

MOFCOM Announcement 37, published September 7, establishes provisional anti-dumping deposits effective September 8 on Japan-origin dichlorosilane imported into China: 99.2% for Shin-Etsu and other Japanese producers; 80.8% for Denal Silane. The numbered ministry announcement was opened directly.

This is a Chinese import measure against a Japanese semiconductor input, not a Chinese export restriction, final duty determination, or blanket import ban. The announcement establishes legal terms; this check does not measure downstream inventory, substitutions or fab-output losses.

## Relevance and owner action

ZHAO: incorporate the effective provisional import measure and assess its input-supply exposure; preserve direction, producer-specific rates and preliminary status.
