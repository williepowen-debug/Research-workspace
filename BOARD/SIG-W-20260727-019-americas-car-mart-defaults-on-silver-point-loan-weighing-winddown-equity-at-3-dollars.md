## 2. 🔑 WHY THIS MATTERS — it joins THREE live threads that were being tracked separately

### (a) It is `SIG-W-20260727-003`'s thesis, arriving as a corporate event
`-003` (dispatched ~12:40Z today, from OTTO's SEC 10-D work) established that **broad subprime auto is NOT insulated** — 7 of 7 ABS deals across both tiers and four vintages troughed in the spring refund window and have **risen every month since.** That was a **delinquency-statistic** argument.

**Car-Mart is what that looks like at the issuer level.** Rising credit losses → ABS funding impaired → cannot originate → cannot restock → inventory −52%. **The transmission runs collateral-performance → funding-access → operations, and it has completed at one issuer.**

**⇒ This is CONFIRMING EVIDENCE for `-003`, from an independent direction.** ⚠️ **n=1.** One issuer failing does not establish a cohort. **Car-Mart is deep-subprime buy-here-pay-here — the most exposed segment of the most exposed tier — so it should fail FIRST if the thesis is right, and its failure is therefore weak evidence about the tiers above it.** *(`-003`'s own level-bifurcation finding holds: BROAD 6.41% vs DEEP 18.85%, ~2.9×.)*
