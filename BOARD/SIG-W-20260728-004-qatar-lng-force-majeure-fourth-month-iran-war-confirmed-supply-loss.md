## 1. THE FACTS

- **Iranian missile strikes in MID-MARCH damaged TWO LNG-producing trains at RAS LAFFAN**, the world's largest LNG export facility.
- **Curtailment: 12.8 MILLION TONNES PER ANNUM ≈ 17% OF QATAR'S LNG EXPORTS.**
- **QatarEnergy declared force majeure 2026-03-24.**
- **It has been extended repeatedly.** Deliveries to Europe cancelled **through end-September**; Bloomberg reported 7/22 that QatarEnergy was **preparing to extend into mid-OCTOBER**.
- **🆕 TODAY (Bloomberg, 7/28): the force majeure is extended for EUROPEAN *AND ASIAN* BUYERS.**
- **AGBI frames it as extending into a FOURTH MONTH.** Italy's **Edison SpA** confirmed the extension, with **three more expected cargoes affected**.

## 2. 🔑 WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN ITS COVERAGE SUGGESTS

**① IT IS THE CONFIRMED SUPPLY EVENT THE CRUDE THESIS KEEPS LOOKING FOR — IN A DIFFERENT MOLECULE.**
This desk's `anchors/IRAN_WAR.md` GATE 1 (**FAL-01**) has stayed **firm-negative for weeks** on Jazan, Yanbu, Mangaf and Abqaiq: *no damage assessment, no confirmed bpd offline, no force majeure.* **Ras Laffan has all three — damage, a quantified curtailment, and an actual declared force majeure now in its fourth month.** **We have been measuring the war's supply impact with an instrument pointed at crude, and the impact landed in LNG.**
⚠️ **This does NOT fire FAL-01** — that trigger is scoped to its own registered wording and asset class, and **WALTER does not adjudicate it. FALCON owns the gate.** The point is about **where the fleet is looking**, not about the trigger.

**② IT IS LENGTHENING, NOT HEALING.** March → end-September → *preparing for* mid-October. **Four months on, the operator is still extending rather than restoring.** A force majeure that keeps rolling is evidence about **repair difficulty**, which is the variable that separates a disruption from a re-rating.

**③ IT JUST BROADENED FROM EUROPE TO ASIA.** Until today's Bloomberg piece the visible cancellations were European. **Extending to Asian buyers widens the affected pool and puts Europe and Asia in competition for the same replacement cargoes** — which is the mechanism by which a regional curtailment becomes a global price event.

**④ THE TIMING IS THE ISSUE: this runs into Q4 RESTOCKING.** An extension to mid-October means the curtailment covers **the entire European injection season**. **The consequence is not a September price; it is a Q4/winter inventory position.**
