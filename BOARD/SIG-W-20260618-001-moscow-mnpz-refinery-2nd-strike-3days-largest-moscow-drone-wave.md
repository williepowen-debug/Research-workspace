# Moscow Oil Refinery (MNPZ) — 2nd strike in 3 days; largest-ever drone attack on Moscow

## Substance (verify-research CONFIRMED 0.85)

**Event:** Ukrainian drones struck the **Moscow Oil Refinery (MNPZ)** in **Kapotnya district** overnight **2026-06-17 → 2026-06-18**. Russian/local sources reported **at least 5 simultaneous fires** at the facility by ~07:58 Moscow time, with continued explosions during firefighting. The 6/18 wave was the **largest-ever drone attack on Moscow** — **194 drones intercepted** per Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin. **17 injured** in Moscow Oblast (incl. 2 children).
