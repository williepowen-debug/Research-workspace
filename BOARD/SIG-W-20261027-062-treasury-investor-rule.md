# Treasury proposal is not a finalized blanket institutional-homebuyer ban

Treasury's October 22 primary release describes proposed implementing regulations under the ROAD to Housing Act. The statute's general purchase restriction begins January 7, 2027; the large-investor definition includes direct/indirect investment control over at least 350 single-family homes, not strictly more than 350.

The release identifies excepted purchases including build-to-rent, renovate-to-rent and homeownership programs. Its NPRM proposes standards and safe harbors; it is not a finalized blanket prohibition on all institutional purchases.
