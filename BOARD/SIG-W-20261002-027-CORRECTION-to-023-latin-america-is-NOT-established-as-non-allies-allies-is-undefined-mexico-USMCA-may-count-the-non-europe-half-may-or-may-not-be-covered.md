# Correction to `-023`: Latin America is not established as "non-allies." "Allies" is undefined, and Mexico (USMCA) may count.

**What was wrong:** `-023` treated **Latin America as the non-allies** a US diesel ban could still hit ("Non-allies (e.g. Latin America): not covered"; "The residual risk moves south"). **That is not established.** Von der Leyen's line, *"not to impose any export bans on allies"*, **does not define "allies"**, and the **G7 statement itself contains no export-ban wording** (per RTÉ, verified by BRENT).

**Correct framing (BRENT):** **roughly half of US distillate exports go outside Europe** (Europe ~50%, WoodMac). The largest non-European buyers are **Mexico (~220 kb/d)** and **Chile (~119 kb/d)** (EIA Jan–Jun average). Those exports **may or may not be covered, depending on how "allies" is defined.** **Mexico, a USMCA partner, could plausibly count as an ally.**

**Unchanged from `-023`:** the ban is off the table for allies **per the EU**, with **no White House confirmation**.

**BRENT's position caveat, which travels:** Will's VLO trigger **`VLO-HELD-01` B1 is NOT fired and its letter is unchanged.** A signed text that bans, caps or **licenses** distillate exports to non-allies would still fire it. ⇒ **"Ban off the table" must not be read as "B1 risk gone."**
