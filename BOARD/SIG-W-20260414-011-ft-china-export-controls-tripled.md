## Signal

EU Chamber of Commerce in China report: **China's use of export controls has nearly tripled** over the past 5 years — **30 announced restrictions 2021-2025 vs 11 in the prior five years**. On top of that, a new **"State Council Regulations on Industrial and Supply Chain Security"** regulation, signed by **Premier Li Qiang** (China's #2 leader), went into effect **March 31 2026** but was only publicly announced **~Apr 8 2026**. The regs criminalize supply-chain due diligence by foreign firms and authorize exit bans on individuals.

## The new regulation — specific articles

- **Article 13:** illegal to "carry out information-gathering activities related to industrial and supply chains in China"
- **Article 15:** sweeping authority to investigate + impose countermeasures against any foreign org/individual that "interrupts normal transactions" with Chinese counterparties
- **Article 16:** ministries can "prohibit or restrict them from leaving the country" (exit bans)

## Hard data

- **Tripling:** 30 export-control announcements 2021-2025 vs 11 in 2016-2020
- **Geoeconomic controls since 2020** (EU Chamber classification): 10 leveraging chokepoints (rare earths et al.), 10 coercive
- **2025 China goods trade surplus: $1.2 trillion** (refined from SIG-010's ">$1T")
- **Trump Beijing visit:** **mid-May 2026** — was **postponed from early April** (previously unknown to us)
- **Historical coercion precedents named:**
  - 2010: China cut rare-earth supplies to Japan during territorial dispute
  - 2020: Blocked Australian imports after Canberra called for COVID investigation
  - 2025: Forced Trump into trade-war truce using critical-minerals export controls

## What these regs are responding to (US actions China is counter-punching)

- **USTR Section 301 investigation** — Trump's new probe to allow reimposed tariffs after his earlier attempts blocked by US Supreme Court
- **Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act** — prevents Xinjiang-sourced products from US market
- **"One Big Beautiful Bill Act" (2025)** — contains supply-chain restrictions on Chinese products

## Lawyer reads

- **Zhan Kai (Yuanda, Shanghai):** rules are "building leverage in trade negotiations from both sides" ahead of postponed Trump visit
- **Dai Menghao (King & Wood):** Beijing wants "broad toolbox" to respond to foreign-gov moves
- **James Zimmerman (Loeb & Loeb, Beijing):** rules are "counter-productive" — prevent buyers from enforcing quality + international labor standards. "Messaging is that all buyers are at risk if they choose to enforce fundamental labour standards."
- **Jens Eskelund (EU Chamber):** European companies often need to comply with European audit directives. "There is a risk that information collected as part of this process could be interpreted as violating Chinese law." Regs "increase the risk of doing business in or with China."
