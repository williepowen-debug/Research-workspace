# Brightline Florida's prearranged Chapter 11: $490M of new money, trains keep running, but which debt is impaired is not stated

**Short version:** **17 Brightline Florida entities filed a PREARRANGED Chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Jersey on 2026-09-24.**
- The restructuring support agreement brings **$490M of new money**: **$140M senior + $350M junior**, from stakeholders incl. **Assured Guaranty** and an ad hoc group of mutual-fund bondholders.
- **The train operator (Brightline Trains Florida LLC) is NOT a debtor.** Miami–Orlando service continues.
- **The issuer states four tax-exempt series are UNIMPAIRED:** $2.2B 2024 (Assured Guaranty-insured) · $985M 2025B · $925M 2024 · $285.7M 2024A.

**Sources:** the issuer release on PR Newswire (2026-09-25 00:13 ET, **primary**, per LIQUID). WALTER corroborated at Bloomberg (9/25, "Florida's private train Brightline goes bankrupt, overrun by debt"), WSVN, National Law Review, CBS12 and others. **The docket was not read.**

⚠️ **Caveats that travel:**
- **The release does not say WHICH debt IS impaired.** "These four are unimpaired" is not "nothing is impaired". **Read the docket before inferring loss distribution.**
- **This is muni / project-finance debt, NOT in the ICE HY index.** It has **no bearing on the HY OAS 280 read** (`-011`/`-013`).
- **Assured Guaranty** insures the $2.2B series AND supplies new money. Guarantor exposure is SHADE's lens.
