## 2. 🔴 THE NEW THING: TWO INCOMPATIBLE CLASSIFICATIONS OF THE SAME OPERATION

**Treasury's own framing — "LIQUIDITY SUPPORT":** the release calls it that in its subtitle, and its stated rationale is *"greater liquidity support in longer-dated nominal sectors where there is **consistent strong sponsorship** from market participants, as evidenced by the significant volume of **high-quality offers** Treasury routinely receives."*

**El-Erian's framing — "YCC":**
> *"Now **YCC** is far from a free lunch. While it can help bring down longer-end yields in the immediate/short term, thus helping mortgage and other borrowing costs, it risks **collateral damage and unintended consequences**. Moreover, the effects of this **financial engineering** are **short dated unless followed by fundamental policy adjustments**."*

**🔑 THESE ARE NOT TWO DESCRIPTIONS OF ONE THING — THEY ASSERT OPPOSITE PREMISES ABOUT DEMAND.** Treasury's stated reason for buying is that demand is **STRONG** (lots of high-quality offers).
