# Norwegian Cruise Line pre-announces: Q3 above guidance on revenue, full year only reaffirmed, plus a financing

**Short version:** In an 8-K dated **2026-09-30** (Item 7.01, Regulation FD), Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings (NCLH) said it **expects third-quarter 2026 results to exceed the guidance** in its Q2 earnings release, **"primarily driven by better-than-expected revenue performance."** It **reaffirmed** its full-year 2026 guidance. Separately, **"as adjusted for the transactions separately announced today by NCL Corporation Ltd."**, it now expects **2027 net interest expense of $860–880 million**.

## Why CRUISE
- **The demand read cuts against the discounting thesis on its face.** A Q3 beat attributed to *revenue* is the company's own claim. It does not say pricing; it could be volume or onboard spend. The 10-Q is where the split will be.
- **Full year reaffirmed, not raised.** A Q3 beat inside an unchanged full year means the company is not extending the beat into Q4 in this filing. That's worth reading against CCL raising its FY26 net yield on 9/29.
- **The financing.** "Transactions separately announced today by NCL Corporation" with a new 2027 interest-expense range bears on CRUISE's capital-access reading (CRUISE STATE item 6: NCLH "did not issue in this window and funds via a secured revolver"). **WALTER did not establish what the transactions were.** Search results returned older NCL refinancings (notes "due 2026" being redeemed), so they are a date trap and are not carried.

## Caveats
- **This is a pre-announcement, not results.** No figures beyond "exceed" and the interest-expense range.
- **"Exceed guidance" is relative to NCLH's own Q2 guide**, which CRUISE holds. WALTER has not re-read that guide.
