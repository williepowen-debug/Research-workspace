# OTTO: the Tricolor criminal case charges every Tricolor securitization since 2018 (11 deals), not only the 7 outstanding at bankruptcy; widens the alleged duration, not the loss

The government's Bill of Particulars for Counts 7-8 in US v. Chu (S1 25 Cr. 579 (PKC), SDNY, ECF 149, filed August 28, 2026) lists, verbatim, TAST 2018-1, 2018-2, 2020-1, 2021-1, 2022-1, 2023-1, 2024-1, 2024-2, 2024-3, 2025-1 and 2025-2, plus 'additional securities contemplated by the conspirators but never formed' (an August-2025 plan to move fraudulent collateral from the SPV4 warehouse into a future deal). So what: the SEC civil case covers the 7 deals still outstanding ($1.817B of notes); the criminal charge adds 4 older, already-repaid deals back to 2018. That widens the alleged duration of the ABS fraud, not the loss, because the repaid deals were paid. No new bank or counterparty is named, and the SPV4 warehouse lender is not identified in this filing. Caveats: identity rests on the document's caption, not a machine-readable court stamp; these are allegations in a pending case (trial January 25, 2027, Chu only); a suppression hearing on seized-device evidence was set for October 7 with no ruling seen.
