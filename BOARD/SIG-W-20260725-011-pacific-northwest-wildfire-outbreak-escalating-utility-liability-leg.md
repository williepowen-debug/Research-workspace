## 🔑 Why WATT, and why this is a dispatch

**Western US wildfire is one of the few climate surfaces with a direct, named, historically enormous corporate-financial channel: UTILITY IGNITION LIABILITY.** The precedent is not speculative — **PG&E entered Chapter 11 in 2019 over wildfire liabilities**, and inverse-condemnation exposure has since driven credit ratings, equity multiples and rate design across every western IOU.

**The three legs WATT should hold, none of which a weather item conveys:**

1. **Ignition liability.** If any of these fires is later attributed to utility equipment, it becomes a balance-sheet event for the operator, not a weather story. **Attribution takes months; the exposure window opens now.**
2. **PSPS — Public Safety Power Shutoffs.** "Extreme fire weather conditions arriving" is the literal trigger language for de-energization. **PSPS is a deliberate load-shedding event: it suppresses delivered volumes, hits revenue, and is a reliability datum that looks like demand destruction in the data.** Anyone reading western utility volumes over this window without knowing a PSPS ran will mis-attribute it.
3. **Transmission risk.** Smoke and fire in the Northern Rockies/PNW corridor bears on **inter-regional transfer capability** — the PNW is a large hydro exporter, and wildfire near major transmission is a summer reliability constraint.
