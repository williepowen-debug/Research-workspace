**(b) THE CHART'S OWN HISTORY REFUTES THE IMPLIED SIGNAL.** Read the series as drawn: the ratio spent **2009 through ~2021 rising almost monotonically off the 0.07 trough**, and it crossed most of its own historical range without marking anything. **A gauge whose only two touches of a level are 26 years apart has n=1 as a sell signal.** *"Same level as the peak"* is a coincidence of scaling until someone shows the level has predictive content — and one prior instance cannot.
