## Signal

**Viva Energy Geelong Refinery (Corio, Victoria) — major fire 2026-04-15 ~11:15pm local.** Equipment/valve failure → gas plume ignition, flames reported to 60m. Fire burned ~13 hours, extinguished morning of Apr 16. MOGAS (motor gasoline) complex affected. No injuries. Viva Energy has entered trading halt; CEO reportedly stated "production no longer priority" pending damage assessment. Repair estimate: **"could take weeks"** (SBS / 1News / Viva guidance).

**Scale of the outage:**
- Up to **120,000 bpd capacity**
- **~50% of Victoria's fuel supply**
- **~10% of Australia's total fuel consumption**
- One of only **TWO operating refineries** in all of Australia (Lytton/Brisbane ~110k bpd is the other). Down from 8 refineries in 2003.
- Australia imports ~90% of refined product already (SK, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, Taiwan)
