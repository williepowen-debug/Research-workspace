## LEAD

**On the ballot:** **Amendment 3 / HJR 1F** ("Save Our Homes from Excessive Property Taxes") — a **legislatively-referred** constitutional amendment (impl. bill SB 4F). **Already passed** both chambers June 2 2026 (House 75-26 / Senate 30-9, DeSantis special session) and **filed with the SoS June 16 2026 → it is ON THE BALLOT, not pending, not a citizen initiative.** Multi-pronged bundle, effective Jan 1 2027 if ratified.

**Structure (4 prongs):** (a) non-school homestead exemption **$50K→$150K (2027)→$250K (2028)**, inflation-indexed 2029, **school levies CARVED OUT**; (b) **non-homestead assessment cap 10%→5%** Jan 1 2027 (rentals/vacation/apartments/commercial); (c) **new post-2026 residents capped $50K for 5 years** (explicitly designed to suppress an in-migration surge); (d) spending restrictions + full-elimination pathway. **The full-repeal measure (HJR 203) DIED in the Senate March 2026 — extreme scenario off the table.**

**P(pass):** 60% voter supermajority required. **Sachs Media poll (850 voters, June 22-24 2026): 64% support, ±3.8pp** → above the bar but **within MOE of failing** (64−3.8 = 60.2). Plus legislative supermajority + DeSantis backing + no organized statewide opposition → **elevated but tight** ("likely-not-safe").

**Net read on the FL thesis (medium):** genuinely two-sided, but **the housing tailwind is structurally MUTED** (5-yr residency gate suppresses migration; school levies + dominant FL insurance costs untouched; phases in 2027-28; savings tend to capitalize into prices) and **the larger identifiable tail is muni/county/special-district fiscal stress** (non-school revenue loss **$4.6B FY27-28 → $8.4B FY28-29 → ~$12B recurring by FY32** per EDR/REC; revenue-protection fund stripped → GrayRobinson county-insolvency flag; S&P 6/12 local-credit-pressure warning; **school districts SHIELDED**). Closer to **neutral-to-modestly-two-sided** than a clean FL-housing bull catalyst.
