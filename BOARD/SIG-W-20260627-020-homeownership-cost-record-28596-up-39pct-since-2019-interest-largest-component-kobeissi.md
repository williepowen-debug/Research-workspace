# Owning a home in America has never been more expensive — total annual cost +39% since 2019 to a record $28,596, interest now the largest single component (CARL)

**One line:** Per ICE/Angi/BLS via Kobeissi, the **average annual cost of US homeownership has surged +39% since 2019 to a record $28,596/year in 2025** — outpacing the +26% CPI rise over the same span. **Interest payments are now the largest single cost** (+35% since 2019 to >$10,500/yr); **property taxes +31% to $5,900** are the 3rd-largest component; insurance +72%; home maintenance +85%; emergency repairs +175%.
