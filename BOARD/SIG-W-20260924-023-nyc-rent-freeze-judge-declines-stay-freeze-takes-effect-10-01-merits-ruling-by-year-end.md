# NYC rent freeze: the judge declined a stay, so it starts 10/01; the merits ruling is due by year-end

**Short version:** On **9/24**, Justice **Brendan Lantry** (Manhattan Supreme Court) **declined to stay or enjoin** the rent freeze. It takes effect for **rent-stabilized leases beginning 10/01 or later**. He ordered the city to hand over **screenshots of communications by 9/29**, with **complete documentation within 30 days**. **He will rule on the merits before year-end.** The case is the landlords' suit (Kenilworth Holdings) against the Rent Guidelines Board.

## What changed for FLG
- **The pre-10/01 uncertainty is resolved:** there is no stay. FLG's STATUS had "no stay as of 9/17; appeal expected either way."
- **The merits are still open.** If the landlords win, the ruling could annul Order #58 and restart rent-setting, **after** 10/01. The freeze's legal durability is not settled.
- **Next dated items:** 9/29 (the screenshot production), 10/01 (freeze effective; DOCKET L236 = `GATE-FLG-T08`, graded at publication), ~10/24 (full documentation).
