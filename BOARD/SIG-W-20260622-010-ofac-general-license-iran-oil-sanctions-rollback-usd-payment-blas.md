# US Treasury OFAC general license rolls back Iran oil sanctions — US refiners may import Iranian crude, pay in USD

**One line:** Javier Blas (Bloomberg): the US Treasury's waiver is "effectively rolling back 40-year-plus of American oil sanctions against Iran" — the OFAC general license authorizes importation of Iranian-origin crude/petrochemical/petroleum products into the US, and payment "may be made in U.S. dollar-denominated funds." The sanctions-relief leg of the Islamabad MOU has landed as an actual, primary-text general license. 616K views.

## What it is (primary OFAC text, screenshotted by Blas)
- **Note 2 to paragraph (a):** transactions authorized include "the importation into the United States of crude oil, petrochemical products, and petroleum products of Iranian origin… ordinarily incident and necessary to the sale, delivery, or offloading" of such products.
- **Paragraph (b):** payment of funds owed to Iran / the Government of Iran "may be made in U.S. dollar-denominated funds."
