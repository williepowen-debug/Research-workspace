# Broadcom supports equipment residual value in $63 billion Anthropic financing structure

The partners' October 26, 16:15 ET joint release confirms a $63 billion SPV transaction with committed financing to buy Broadcom accelerators and networking equipment for lease to Anthropic, with deliveries expected from calendar 2027. Bank of America, Citi and Morgan Stanley arrange the first lien; Blackstone is sole provider of the committed second-lien portion. Broadcom supplies residual value support equivalent to the first-lien financing amount.

This is evidence that large financing commitments remain available, alongside a supplier-linked risk channel: the chip supplier supports equipment value in the customer-financing vehicle. It is not proof that $63 billion has been funded, retained by the named banks or guaranteed against every loss. Tranche sizes, distribution/retention, collateral valuation, support triggers and lease economics are not disclosed in the release. Previously announced compute capacity must not be counted again as wholly incremental capacity.
