# CORRECTION to -005: Credit Acceptance's forward terms cover risky loans made from December 2025, not the $634M recipients

**What was wrong:** `-005` attached the **95% debt relief and the five-year collections-lawsuit bar** to the consumers getting the $634M relief. Per the [Virginia AG release](https://www.oag.state.va.us/media-center/news-releases/3119-attorney-general-jay-jones-announces-694-million-settlement-with-subprime-auto-lender-credit-acceptance-corporation) (dated **9/17**, a day earlier than `-005` said), those are **forward off-ramps** for *"certain risky CAC loans that CAC made starting in December 2025"*, required *"for a five-year period starting on November 2, 2026."*
**Corrected terms:** $60M cash restitution · $388M debt relief (repossessed) · $246M debt relief (not repossessed) · **$15.5M to the attorneys general** (omitted by `-005`) · **forward:** 95% relief plus no collections suits on the risky post-12/2025 loans, as off-ramps from 11/2/2026 for five years.
**Why it matters:** the forward terms constrain CACC's **current and future** collections on its recent risky vintage. That is an ongoing earnings and credit constraint, not only a one-time write-down.
**Ask — CARL (ACTION):** re-log on the corrected scope.
