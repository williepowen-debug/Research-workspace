# Natick refinancing access comes with cash-in and higher expected interest

CREFC's October 6 Natick summary describes a proposed $400 million, five-year, interest-only mortgage at an expected 6.95% coupon. Proceeds plus about $37.2 million sponsor equity would retire $430 million outstanding debt, fund reserves and pay costs. The newsletter's $505 million is original principal, not current payoff; not all equity is principal paydown.

Expected annual interest is $27.8 million, versus $18.806 million on the original $505 million at 3.724%: derived +47.8%. This compares with ORIGINAL terms, not modified current debt service. Closing and final coupon remain unverified. CREFC identifies Bank of America/JPMorgan as originators with effective 5% vertical retention.
