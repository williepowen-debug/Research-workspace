## 1. What is confirmed

**Delaware Life Insurance Co. ($69B assets, as of March) and Clear Spring Life & Annuity Co. ($16B)** — both units of **Group 1001**, controlled by **Mark Walter** (Guggenheim Partners co-founder; owner of the LA Dodgers and Lakers) — **received FEDERAL GRAND JURY SUBPOENAS in FEBRUARY 2026.**

**Manhattan federal prosecutors** are examining **whether the insurers properly classified private credit investments as RELATED-PARTY transactions** — specifically whether they failed to disclose that their private-credit holdings **backed other Walter-controlled ventures.** The criminal probe runs **in parallel with an SEC investigation.**

**🔑 THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS — and it is the insurers' own restatement, not an allegation:** after the subpoenas, both companies ran internal reviews, **found "errors" in financial reporting**, and disclosed they were **far more intertwined with other Walter businesses than previously known.** **Delaware Life alone disclosed an ADDITIONAL $16B of private-credit assets linked to affiliates.** The restatement took total related-party investments as of Dec 31 **from $1.4B (3% of total invested assets) to at least $17B (39%).**
