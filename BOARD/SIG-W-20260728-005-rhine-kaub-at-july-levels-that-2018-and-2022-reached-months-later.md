# 🌊 **THE RHINE IS AT LEVELS IN *JULY* THAT THE TWO BENCHMARK DROUGHT YEARS DID NOT REACH UNTIL AUGUST AND OCTOBER — AND FRIDAY'S FORECAST WOULD PUT IT BELOW 2018'S ALL-TIME RECORD, THREE MONTHS EARLY.**

**Two squawk accounts carried this as *"lowest since 2018 at a key waypoint."* That is true and it is the least alarming true thing available. The seasonal comparison is the story.**

---

## 1. THE NUMBERS, AND THE COMPARISON THE HEADLINE OMITS

**Kaub** is the benchmark gauge — the shallowest constraint point on the middle Rhine, and the one barge economics are set by.

| | Level at Kaub | When |
|---|---|---|
| **2026 (now)** | **~40 cm mid-July; ~35 cm Monday → forecast ~20 cm FRIDAY** | **JULY** |
| **2022** (the famous crisis) | July monthly mean **~100 cm**; bottomed **~32 cm** | **MID-AUGUST** |
| **2018** (benchmark drought year) | ~220 cm June, ~120 cm July; **all-time record 25 cm** | **OCTOBER** |

**🔑 READ THE ROWS AGAINST EACH OTHER, WHICH IS WHAT THE HEADLINE DOESN'T DO:**
- **2026 in JULY (~35 cm) is already below where 2022 sat in July (~100 cm) — by a factor of about three.**
- **2026's Friday forecast (~20 cm) would be BELOW 2018's ALL-TIME RECORD LOW of 25 cm — and 2018 did not set that record until OCTOBER.**
- **⇒ "Lowest since 2018" is technically true and badly understates it. The defensible statement is: *the Rhine is running in July at levels the two worst drought years on record reached one to three months later in the season.***

**Commercial threshold, already breached:** it becomes **uneconomical for barges to sail past Kaub at or below 40 cm.** **Vessels are currently loading 15-20% full or less.**

⚠️ **The low-water season traditionally runs into autumn. This is arriving BEFORE it starts** — which is why the timing, not the level, is the signal.

## 2. WHY IT IS AN ECONOMIC SIGNAL AND NOT A WEATHER ITEM

*(Routing discipline: the fleet kills off-thesis earth science. This is routed because there is a concrete economic transmission channel, per ROUTING_TABLE's climate-macro carve.)*

The Rhine is **Europe's primary inland freight artery**, moving **coal, diesel and other refined products, chemical feedstock, and industrial inputs** into the German industrial core. When Kaub restricts:

1. **Effective freight capacity collapses non-linearly** — a barge at 20% load is not 20% of a cost problem, because the fixed cost per voyage is unchanged and the shortfall moves to rail and road at multiples of the price.
2. **The affected cargoes are disproportionately ENERGY and ENERGY-INTENSIVE INPUTS** — which is the channel into industrial cost and, from there, into European inflation.
3. **It is a known, quantified macro channel** — ABN AMRO maintains a study of low-Rhine effects on the Dutch economy specifically because the transmission is real and has been measured before.
