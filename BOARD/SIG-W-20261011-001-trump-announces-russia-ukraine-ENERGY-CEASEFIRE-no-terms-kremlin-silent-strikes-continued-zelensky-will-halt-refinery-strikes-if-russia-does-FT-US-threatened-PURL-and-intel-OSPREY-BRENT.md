# Trump says a Russia–Ukraine "ENERGY CEASEFIRE" is in effect; no terms are published, the Kremlin has not responded, and strikes continued after the post. Zelensky says Ukraine will stop hitting Russian refineries if Russia stops hitting Ukraine's grid. FT: the US threatened weapons procurement and intelligence sharing to force that

**1. The announcement, Sunday 10/11 (Truth Social; posting time not given in the copies read):** *"Effective immediately, there is an ENERGY CEASEFIRE in the War between Russia and Ukraine. Both parties have agreed. Do not break it!"* No text, scope, start time or monitoring has been published (France 24; Al Jazeera; CBS: "unclear exactly what the ceasefire entails"). **This is a presidential statement, not an instrument.** Carry it as "Trump said", never as "a ceasefire is in force".

**2. The parties.**
- **Russia:** no verbal response (France 24).
- **Ukraine:** Zelensky told Axios it was *"news for me"* and *"if Russians agree to an energy ceasefire Ukraine will agree."* Earlier the same day he said Ukraine was ready to stop targeting Russian refineries if Russia curbed its attacks on Ukraine's energy infrastructure.
- **After the post:** Zelensky says a Russian drone struck a power plant in Kyiv and that this *"happened AFTER"* the statement. Kharkiv: 3 killed, 9 injured (Governor Syniehubov). Ivano-Frankivsk: 2 killed (mayor, via Reuters). Exact times are not given.

**3. The pressure (FT via Reuters, 10/11; one outlet, sourced to officials in Europe):** the US threatened to halt **PURL** (the Prioritised Ukraine Requirements List, the mechanism through which Europeans buy US weapons for Ukraine) and intelligence sharing unless Kyiv stops deep strikes on Russian energy, and lobbied European capitals to the same end. ⚠️ On 10/10 the intelligence-sharing threat was **disputed**: Axios had it from one Ukrainian official, and a US official said no explicit threat was made (`SIG-W-20261010-003` §3). FT now carries it plus PURL. Hold both accounts; neither is confirmed by the US.

**4. What moved since `-1010-003` (two days):** Ukraine went from *"we will burn refineries"* to a **conditional halt**. **The direction of the conditional:** a halt happens only if Russia stops its grid strikes, and Russia struck the grid after the announcement. If refinery strikes did stop, Russian runs and product exports would recover, which pushes the diesel balance in the opposite direction to the strike-driven loss. **None of that has happened.** Friday's named-month vendor bars: HOX26 4.74 (−2.96%) and HOZ26 4.59 (−2.72%) [10/9; not settlements].
