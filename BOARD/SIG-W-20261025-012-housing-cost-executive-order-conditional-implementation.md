# October22 housing order directs conditional mortgage-cost measures; it does not reduce every borrower’s rate today

[White House executive order October22](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/decreasing-housing-costs-for-the-american-people/), operative sections1–6 read; attached images are illustrative. FHFA is directed, subject to statutory/safety conditions, to establish a proactive mortgage-insurance-cancellation pilot applying **only to mortgages originated after the pilot is established**. It also directs expansion of title-acceptance alternatives for eligible refinances, with various eligibility/fee/documentation changes to be considered and savings passed to borrowers.

A manufactured-housing task force has a180-day recommendation clock. USDA/VA are directed to reduce rates on their **direct** single-family loans, subject to law/appropriations. This does not mean all guaranteed VA loans or all conventional mortgages have immediately cheaper rates. Agency rollout, borrower eligibility and realized savings are not verified here.
