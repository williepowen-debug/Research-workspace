# Kazakhstan halts CPC shipments to Novorossiysk after tanker attacks — Ukraine drone campaign in Black Sea now producing state-level supply pullbacks

**One-line:** Kazakhstan (Russia's largest downstream via CPC, ~1.4 M bpd) has halted oil shipments to Novorossiysk following Ukrainian drone strikes on tankers loading at the CPC terminal — direct state-action follow-on to `SIG-W-20260720-004` (CPC drone strike / loading halted).

## Body

Bloomberg-sourced (via LIGA.net + Anadolu Ajansı + Voice of the Emirates 7/21): **Kazakhstan has halted oil shipments to the CPC terminal at Novorossiysk after Ukrainian "Mamai" drones struck tankers loading Russian crude at the terminal.** Ukrainian news outlet ukranews.com released strike video. Russia has publicly condemned the drone attacks (Anadolu). @zerohedge headline (7/21 6:59 AM): *"KAZAKHSTAN TO STOP PIPING OIL TO BLACK SEA AFTER TANKER ATTACKS"* — content-corroborated across 4 wires.

**Why this matters:**
- CPC (Caspian Pipeline Consortium) carries ~1.4-1.5 M bpd, of which ~80% is Kazakh crude bound for global markets via the Black Sea. A Kazakh-side pullback is a MUCH larger economic event than the terminal-side loading halt already dispatched yesterday.
- **State-action tier:** yesterday was operational (loading halted after strike). Today is state-action (a sovereign supplier withdrawing supply, not just a terminal reacting). This is the escalation rung.
- **Duration matters more than the halt itself:** the CPC has had prior brief suspensions in 2022-2024 that resumed within days. A sustained multi-day/week halt at this scale (~1M+ bpd) would be materially disruptive to global crude balances.
- **R/U theater is OSPREY's primary; oil-impact is BRENT's.** Both need the datum. HAWK for cross-war synthesis; SAM because China is a large buyer of CPC/Kazakh crude via Black Sea → Suez → Asia; if the halt persists, China refiners re-source.
