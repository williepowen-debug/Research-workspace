## 🔴 REFUTATION ① — TIMING. Nothing is "coming due."

The 10-Q states the guarantee terms, and they dissolve the claim:

- Each guarantee **becomes effective upon commencement of the applicable lease**.
- Amounts **increase as each of NINE PHASES of construction completes** — **the first expected in FISCAL YEAR 2029**.
- Payment obligations are **triggered only upon certain tenant defaults**.
- They **decrease over each phase's 20-year lease term**.

⇒ A **contingent, phased, default-triggered** exposure that **has not begun**, amortising over 20 years, is being compared to a point-in-time cash balance. **"Impossible to pay its bills when they come due" describes bills that do not come due for roughly three years, and then only if a tenant defaults.**
