# 🔴 **Canada SUSPENDED trade negotiations with the US on 8/21. US 50% tariffs on ~$28B of Canadian goods took effect AT MIDNIGHT. Canada will match "dollar for dollar" — but its retaliation is DATED 8 SEPTEMBER, and that gap is the part worth trading.**

## 1. What the primary says, verbatim

Fetched from `pm.gc.ca` 2026-08-22. Statement dated **2026-08-21**. Verbatim:

- *"At midnight tonight, the U.S. intends to impose a 50% tariff on roughly $28 billion of Canadian goods."*
- *"Canada will match those tariffs dollar for dollar to protect our workers and businesses."*
- *"I have decided to suspend trade negotiations with the U.S. and have directed Canada's negotiators to return to Ottawa."*
- Reason given: *"last-minute changes in the U.S. proposed terms were unfair, uneconomic, and called into question the reliability of any deal."*
- Support: additional measures coming, building on *"nearly $25 billion in support provided over the past 18 months."*
- Context Carney chose to state: *"nearly $500 billion in major infrastructure projects"*; preferential access to *"1.5 billion consumers,"* on track to **double** that market access **by end of this year**.

## 2. 🔑 THE PART THAT IS NOT IN WILL'S SCREENSHOTS, AND IT IS THE DECISION-RELEVANT PART

**The screenshots carry the announcement. They do not carry the SCHEDULE.** From Carney's Saturday (8/22) news conference, via CP24 and NBC:

- **Canada's retaliatory tariffs take effect 2026-09-08** — seventeen days after the US tariff went live.
- **Named target basket: steel · dairy · appliances · agricultural equipment · pulp and paper · electronics.**

⇒ **The two legs are NOT simultaneous.** The US 50% is a **realized** cost as of 8/22 00:00; the Canadian match is an **announced, dated, and still-revocable** one. **A dated retaliation is a negotiating instrument — it exists to be withdrawn if terms change, and there are seventeen days in which that can happen.**

⚠️ **DO NOT MODEL THIS AS A SYMMETRIC SIMULTANEOUS TRADE SHOCK.** One side is in effect and one side is a deadline. Anyone pricing both today is pricing a counterfactual.

## 3. What is genuinely established vs. what is not

**ESTABLISHED:** the suspension of negotiations · the 50% rate · the ~$28B base · the midnight effective time · the dollar-for-dollar commitment · the 9/8 date and the six named sectors.

❌ **NOT ESTABLISHED, and I am naming these because the gap is where the bad numbers will come from:**
- **The $28B basis is UNSTATED.** Annualized import value? A specific tariff-line schedule? **Nobody has published the line list**, and a "$28B of goods" figure without a base year and a customs basis cannot be turned into a price-level estimate. **Do not derive a CPI contribution from it.**
- **Whether the Canadian match is $28B of US goods or a dollar-equivalent duty yield.** *"Dollar for dollar"* is ambiguous between the two and they are different magnitudes.
- **Whether this survives to 9/8.** Reporting notes Trump had **paused these tariffs for three days**, posting that the two sides *"have a DEAL!"* — and then the terms changed. ⇒ **This lane has already reversed once inside a week. Treat any 9/8 assumption as revocable.**

## 4. Why CARL is on action, and the one trap in the transmission

**CARL owns tariff→goods-price.** The naive read is 50% × $28B ⇒ a mechanical CPI contribution. **That read is wrong in a way that matters:**

- **A 50% tariff on a narrow basket is a SUBSTITUTION event before it is a price event.** At 50%, a large share of affected volume does not pay the tariff — it re-sources, re-routes, or stops. **The realized price effect is bounded by the elasticity, not by the rate**, and the revenue base shrinks as the rate rises.
- **The named Canadian retaliation basket points at US EXPORTERS, not US consumers** — steel, dairy, appliances, ag equipment, pulp/paper, electronics. **That leg is a margin/volume story for US industrials and an input-cost story for Canadian buyers. It is NOT a US CPI story**, and merging the two directions into "the tariff is inflationary" loses the sign on half of it.

⇒ **The two legs transmit through DIFFERENT channels with DIFFERENT owners: US goods CPI (CARL) and US exporter volumes (HENRY/MARCO). Keep them separate.**
