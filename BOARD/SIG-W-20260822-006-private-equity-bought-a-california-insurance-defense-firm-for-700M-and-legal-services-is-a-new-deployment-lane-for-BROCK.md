## 2. 🔑 Why it is routed anyway — the structural question BROCK can answer and this desk cannot

**US law firms have historically been barred from outside ownership** under legal-profession rules in nearly every state (Arizona is the notable exception, having repealed its ban; Ohio and others have moved partially). ⇒ **A ~$700M PE acquisition of a law firm is either (a) structured around those rules via a managed-services or captive-MSO arrangement, or (b) evidence the rules have loosened further than this desk has tracked.** **Both readings are materially interesting to a private-credit desk and they have different implications:**

- **(a) MSO-structured** ⇒ this is the **healthcare-rollup playbook ported to legal services** — the same structure that produced the physician-practice roll-ups now sitting in private-credit books. **A known stress pattern arriving in a new vertical.**
- **(b) genuine ownership liberalization** ⇒ a **new addressable asset class** opening at a moment when BROCK's own coverage describes direct-lending funds SHRINKING and capital hunting for lanes.

⚠️ **This desk does not know which. BROCK does or can find out — that is the ask.**
