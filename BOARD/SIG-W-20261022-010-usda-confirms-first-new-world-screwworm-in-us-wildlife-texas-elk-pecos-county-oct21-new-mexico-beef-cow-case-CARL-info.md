# USDA confirmed the first New World screwworm case in US wildlife on October21: a hunter-shot elk in Pecos County, Texas, plus a New Mexico beef cow case; confirmed US cases now in two states

**Confirmed (USDA, announced Wed 10/21; CBS, The Hill, Axios, Bloomberg 10/22):** the first New World screwworm case in **US wildlife**: an elk shot on a game ranch in **Pecos County, Texas**, identified at USDA's Kerrville lab. Reuters (via MarketScreener) also reports a **New Mexico beef cow** case. Confirmed US cases are now in Texas and New Mexico. The current outbreak began with a calf in Zavala County in early June. The Texas Animal Health Commission is redrawing the infested zone, and USDA and Texas Parks & Wildlife are surveying the area. A Florida deer outbreak last year was eradicated.
