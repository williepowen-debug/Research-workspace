## Caveats

- **This is thesis-frame, not a data release.** MS/Piper Sandler is making an argument, not reporting a new number. The underlying numbers (gasoline share, margins, fiscal stance) are all publicly available; the VALUE is in the structured framing, not new information.
- **Table is internally consistent but selection is curated.** Six axes MS picked support the 2026-is-better conclusion. Omitted: equity valuations (much higher 2026), asset price levels (ATHs across many measures), fiscal deficit level (5-7% 2026 vs ~4% 1990), household debt composition. A different 6-row table could look different.
- **"Strong economy" based on stale-ish data.** ISM rising / jobless very low is conditional; both can move in 1-2 months. Signal strength decays as data drifts.
- **US-centric framing.** Global oil shock transmission via EM currencies, dollar-indebted sovereigns, and Asia-import sensitivity is not in this table. Japan/Korea/Germany are structurally 1990s-like.
- **'Piper Sandler' attribution on MSIM slide.** MS is republishing Piper Sandler's comparison, not generating it. Piper Sandler sell-side macro shop is credible but not top-tier — MSIM's endorsement is what upgrades this.
- **Pages 17 in 'The BEAT' Q2 2026** — a single slide in a MSIM sales deck. The institutional weight depends on whether this is a house thesis or a page in a pack. Not verifiable from the image alone.
