# Houthis push to seize Taiz, about 100km from Mokha; the government claims 20 air strikes on 10/02; Yemen's 2022 truce has broken into full-scale war

**Short version:** Yemen's ground war is moving inland from the coast the Houthis took last month. **The Houthis are pushing to seize Taiz**, the city that links the Houthi north with the government south and with the road to **Aden**, about **100km from Mokha** (seized 9/10). The government side reports **20 air strikes in three hours** on Houthi forces in Taiz province on **10/02**, after six on 10/01. Per The National, the conflict **"returned to full-scale fighting last month, having been largely frozen since a truce was agreed upon in 2022."** Neither the BOARD nor FALCON's STATUS carries Taiz.
