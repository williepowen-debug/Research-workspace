|---|---|---|
| Composite output | 48.4 (4-month low) | 51.1 |
| Services activity | 48.4 (2-month low) | 51.2 |

| Manufacturing PMI | 49.3 (11-month low) | 50.6 |

Demand for French goods and services fell at the sharpest rate since May; firms cited rising uncertainty and economic pressure. Twelve-month business expectations were the most pessimistic in six and a half years. Price indices eased in services, but supply-chain strain pushed manufacturing prices up again.
