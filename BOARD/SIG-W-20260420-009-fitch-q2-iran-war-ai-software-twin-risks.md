## Key data

**Private credit / BDC stress (Fitch's quantified datapoint):**
- **Non-traded perpetual BDC redemption requests +36% QoQ in Q1'26**, driven by (a) investor concerns about software exposure and (b) valuation uncertainty.
- Near-term default rates remain contained, but **refinancing risk building as debt maturities for leveraged borrowers concentrate in the 2028-2031 window**.
- Fitch: **"Stress transmission into BDCs and CLOs bears close monitoring, even if current cushions remain adequate."**
- AI infrastructure investment "continues to anchor private fixed investment and capital market activity, with strong issuance activity reflecting robust investor appetite" — the institutional bull case, also named.

**Adverse macro scenario (oil $100/bbl avg 2026):**
- US GDP 2026: **1.5% vs 1.8% baseline** (-0.7pp impact).
- Peak second-order impact at **4 quarters lag**: Q4'26 GDP **0.6% YoY** (vs 1.8% baseline).
- Higher inflation complicates Fed rate path → **delays expected rate cuts**.
- Sector impact: **most acute headwinds = consumer-facing sectors, housing, airlines**. Beneficiaries: upstream energy, aerospace & defense.

**Framing flag:** AI software disruption framed as "multi-year credit story" (not near-term default catalyst). Redemption pressure today, refi wall in 3-5 years. Not a threshold breach — a building drumbeat.
