The -39pt gap might be 80% composition (no Mag-7) and only 20% defensive-bid-absent. Cleaner test: BRK.A vs equal-weight SPY (RSP) would isolate composition from quality.

- **Window is short (~2 weeks).** -39pts is large for the timeframe but a 2-week sample isn't a regime call. Watch follow-through.
- **Direction of causality unclear.** BRK underperformance could be CAUSE (capital rotating out of defensive-quality into beta) or EFFECT (announcement-specific) — both can be true.
