# Russia's note to NATO threatens nuclear use if Kaliningrad is "isolated", plus strikes on alliance "decision-making centers". NATO calls it irresponsible.

- **The document (9/30):** a Russian **non-paper delivered to NATO**, confirmed on record by NATO spokesperson **Allison Hart**.
- **The wording:** Russia *"will be ready to use the entire arsenal of forces and capabilities at its disposal, **including nuclear weapons**"* if NATO countries try to isolate the Kaliningrad Region, and threatens *"strikes against **decision-making centers** in the alliance's member states right from the outset."*
- **Claimed trigger:** Moscow says NATO is preparing an **air and naval blockade of Kaliningrad**. **No evidence offered**; no NATO plan or transit restriction has been announced.
- **Response:** NATO *"strongly denounce[s]… irresponsible nuclear rhetoric"*; Rutte: stop the nuclear threats; Lithuania: baseless, "psychological pressure."

**So what:** an explicit nuclear threshold tied to a Baltic chokepoint, put in writing to NATO. It is rhetoric so far, but it raises the stakes of any Baltic shipping or transit incident.
