# Gartner survey: limited major AI value so far, alongside rising funding

Gartner's October 19 release reports that 13% of CIO respondents describe significant or transformational enterprise AI value so far. Its 2027 agenda survey covered 2,002 CIOs/technology executives in May-July 2026. The same release says AI funding is increasing; 58% face pressure for cost improvement and 51% expect AI to increase technology ownership cost. Forty percent expect IT headcount growth in 2027.

These are self-reported outcomes and expectations, not audited returns, realized layoffs or measured hyperscaler capex cuts.
