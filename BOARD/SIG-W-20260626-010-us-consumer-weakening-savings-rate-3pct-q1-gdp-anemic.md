# US consumer weakening: savings rate 3% (15-yr low) + Q1 GDP up only on imports, consumer anemic (CARL)

**One line:** Two BEA datapoints say the consumer is thinning out: (a) the **personal savings rate fell to 3% in April and May — one of the lowest levels in 15 years** (BEA via FRED; well below the ~5–7% pre-pandemic norm, back near the 2022 "revenge-spend" trough); (b) **Q1 GDP was revised UP to 2.1% — but the upward revision came from FEWER imports, not strength: consumer spending (the backbone of the economy) was anemic in Q1** (Heather Long, on the BEA third estimate). Growth flattered by the import math while the household engine sputters, and the savings cushion is nearly gone.
