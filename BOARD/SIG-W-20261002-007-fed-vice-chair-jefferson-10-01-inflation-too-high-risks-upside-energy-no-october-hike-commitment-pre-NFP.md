# Fed Vice Chair Jefferson (10/01): inflation "too high", risks to the upside on energy, but no October hike commitment; spoke before the payrolls miss

**Short version:** Fed Vice Chair **Philip Jefferson** spoke on 10/01 at UVA's Darden School ("The U.S. Economy and Monetary Policy"). He said inflation is **"too high"** and that he sees its risks **"tilted to the upside"**, with energy (gasoline, diesel) as the main driver. **He did not endorse an October hike:** *"Any future adjustments in policy should be determined by carefully examining trends in the data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks."* The BOARD had not carried it.
