# Amazon dropped its four-building data center plan for the former GWU campus in Ashburn under a Loudoun County deal (Board meeting Tuesday 10/20): a siting settlement after county opposition, not a capex cut

**The deal ("Project Potomac Promise", WTOP 10/21, read at source):** Amazon bought the 122-acre former George Washington University campus for $427 million in March 2026 and had filed a site plan for four data center buildings. Under the deal, presented at the Loudoun Board of Supervisors on Tuesday 10/20 and worked out with Chair Phyllis Randall since late September, Amazon donates 25 acres (about $65 million) for an arts center and indoor sports facility, where data centers are banned even by special exception; rezones the rest for mixed-use housing; and puts $5 million into the county Housing Fund. Amazon's Josh Weissman (VP, data center capacity delivery) said the two sides chose a shared plan over "costly litigation". The board only agreed to fast-track the rezoning, not approve it.

**What it is not:** the bookmarks frame it as "$AMZN abandons Virginia data center". No Amazon capex guide changed; the company dropped one contested site after the county said it would fight it (the plan was a by-right filing opposed by Supervisor Juli Briskman and the county economic-development director, summer 2026). Search also surfaced an earlier Amazon withdrawal near Maryland's Calvert Cliffs nuclear plant; its date was not checked here. Two local-opposition exits are a siting pattern worth watching, not a spending signal.
