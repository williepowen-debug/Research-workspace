# NIST: Surfside collapse began ~3 weeks early — design + construction deficiencies + 1990s deadweight + corrosion (reinforces FL condo-reserve thesis) (CORAL)

**One line:** NIST's forensic report (released June 22 2026, ~5 years on) concludes the 2021 Champlain Towers South collapse **began ~3 weeks before the final breakdown** — a structural failure at two garage column-to-slab connections beneath the pool deck in early June 2021 triggered a silent 21-day slow-motion domino, until the pool deck gave way June 24. Root causes: the **original design failed to meet the codes/standards of 40 years ago**; **construction deviated from that design**; **1990s additions** (heavy landscape planters + thick paving stone on the pool deck) added tons of **uncalculated deadweight**; and **long-term corrosion** degraded the deficient slab. Investigator: "margins against failure were too narrow from the start." Undiagnosed warning signs in the final weeks (sliding door popping out, jammed gates, garage-ceiling water leaks hours before).
