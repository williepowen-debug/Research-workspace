# US cities carry ~$1T in deferred-infrastructure liability (Ciccarone) — the "next pension-recognition wave" → tax hikes / muni-bond pressure (CARL)

**One line:** Municipal-finance researcher **Richard Ciccarone** estimates US cities carry roughly **$1.03T in deferred infrastructure wear-and-tear** (roads/bridges/buildings/utilities) across ~2,000 municipalities — **largely OFF balance sheets.** Unlike pensions, cities face *no accounting penalty* for delaying infra repairs, so maintenance gets pushed behind debt service. Ciccarone explicitly compares it to the **2010s pension-accounting reforms** that forced recognition → tax hikes, spending cuts, and muni-bond pressure: infrastructure could be the "next major fiscal challenge for local governments." Older cities worst (Philadelphia weighing $1.5B/6yr, Baltimore decade-long overhaul, Milwaukee); Sun Belt better (Austin/Charlotte/Phoenix newer; Jacksonville via local infra taxes + federal aid + hurricane reinvestment).
