# Fitch (October13, via two trade outlets): nontraded BDC redemption requests eased to 9.8% of shares in Q3 from 10.3% in Q2, still about twice the 5% cap; Blue Owl Technology Income Corp faces requests of 39% of its shares. Much of the excess is backlog from prorated earlier quarters

**What is reported:** Fitch Ratings, as reported October13 by InvestmentNews (Bruce Kelly) and Pensions & Investments (Jesse Pound), says Q3 2026 redemption requests at 13 of 15 perpetual-life nontraded BDCs averaged **9.8% of shares**, down from **10.3%** in Q2. Fitch: "Repurchase activity began to ease." Six BDCs had requests below 5% and nine above 5%. Fitch says that for the nine above the cap, "a substantial share reflected unfulfilled investor requests that were prorated in prior quarters," so much of the volume is backlog, not new selling. **Blue Owl Technology Income Corp** is "facing client redemptions requests of 39% of its shares," per the Fitch report. Funds cap repurchases at about 5% a quarter and prorate the rest.
