# San Carlos Reservoir fell **below 1% of capacity** — the economic channel is **real but unquantified**, and the **Globe Equity Decree** is the actual mechanism

> ⚠️ **Routed on the water-rights leg, not the fish.** A 100% fish kill is an ecological event and would fail the climate-routing discriminator on its own. **What clears the bar is the allocation mechanism underneath it** — a 1924 decree still delivering water downstream while the reservoir that feeds it empties. That is an institutional/economic object, and it is what the post is actually about.

## The event — confirmed, multi-source

**San Carlos Reservoir** (Coolidge Dam, **Gila River**, Pinal/Graham County, Arizona) — one of the state's largest.

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Reservoir level | **<1% of capacity** |
| Gila River watershed snowpack | **2% of the 1991-2020 March median** |
| Fish kill | **100%** — hypoxia (dissolved-oxygen collapse as the lake shrank) |
| Status | **Closed indefinitely, 2026-06-05** |
| Remaining storage by June | **under 400 acre-feet** (Insurance Journal 7/15) |

Corroborated across **NASA Earth Observatory, ScienceDaily, Insurance Journal, Gila Herald, Yahoo/AP** — this is well-sourced, not a viral claim.

## 🔑 The mechanism is more specific than the post says — and better

The post says water kept flowing to farms *"under agreements written decades ago."* **True, but vague. The actual instrument:**

- The **Globe Equity Decree** + the **San Carlos Irrigation Project** (authorized by Congress in **1924**) establish **senior rights** for the **Gila River Indian Community / Pima lands** and the **San Carlos Irrigation & Drainage District (SCIDD)** to store and draw reservoir water via the **Ashurst-Hayden Diversion Dam → Florence-Casa Grande Canal**.
- **Mandatory downstream releases continued as the reservoir collapsed**, leaving it under 400 acre-feet.

**That is the routable content: a century-old priority structure executing as written into a hydrology it never contemplated.** The post's own line — *"the rules were built for a world that no longer exists"* — is, unusually for a viral post, **the correct frame.** It is an institutional-mismatch story, not a weather story.

## The economic channel — real, documented, **and not sized**

**The verify was asked point-blank whether there was any channel or whether this was purely ecological. Its answer: do NOT say "no channel."**

- **Agriculture:** one named farmer (**Nancy Caywood**, ~250-acre alfalfa/cotton near Casa Grande) pays a **$21,000/year water-district fee despite receiving zero river water since March**, and has shifted to leasing land with groundwater access. **Agriculture is ~34% of Gila River Basin water consumption** vs ~24% for municipal/commercial/industrial combined.
- **Land / water-rights reallocation is actively underway:** housing developers and **solar companies are buying agricultural land near Phoenix**; farmers are being approached to lease for solar; **a Phoenix-suburb official is on record discussing buying groundwater rights from farmers** to move water from farming to municipal use.
- **Power: NO linkage found.** Coolidge Dam is minor/non-major hydro; **no source connects this to generation.** *(Explicitly checked — this is a negative finding, not an omission. WATT has no leg here.)*

**⚠️ THE HONEST LIMIT — why this is ROUTINE and 0.75, not higher:** **there is no dollar-quantified regional agricultural loss, no crop-yield estimate, and no water-rights pricing effect anywhere in the press.** The evidence is **one farmer and a rancher with tripled hay prices** — anecdote, not aggregate. **The correct statement is: a live economic transmission channel exists (ag output loss + land-use reallocation + water-rights market activity) whose magnitude is unmeasured.** That is a **data gap, not an absence of channel** — and the distinction is exactly what AEOLUS should decide what to do with.
