# ASML–TSMC larger-mask roadmap targets 2031 pilots and 2033 readiness

## Signal and data

TSMC’s September 8 release announces an ASML–TSMC initiative formed September 7 to develop 12-inch photomasks for High NA EUV. Pilot capability targets 2031 and system readiness for advanced-node production targets 2033.

The companies expect larger masks to improve productivity and address stitching constraints. These are development targets and expected benefits, not demonstrated near-term wafer-output gains. Current adoption begins with six-inch masks. The new roadmap belongs on the technology horizon; it does not relieve 2026 memory or packaging bottlenecks by itself.
