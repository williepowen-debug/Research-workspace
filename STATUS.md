## Key Thesis Points

### 1. Extend-and-Pretend is Masking CRE Stress
- 1,788 banks have CRE >300% of equity (and rising)
- Actual office sale losses: 70-97%
- Bank models assume: 20-40%
- FDIC stopped disclosing problem bank total assets
- Loan modifications keep NPLs artificially low

### 2. Employment is the Transmission Trigger
- ~580K announced layoffs (307K federal DOGE + ~275K private)
- Multi-sector: Tech, Auto, Logistics, Services, Consumer
- Not sector rotation — broad demand destruction
- Temp employment down 12%, re-accelerating decline
- When employment breaks, borrowers can't pay, extend-and-pretend fails

### 3. Geographic Concentration Creates Pockets of Risk
- DC/NoVA: Federal employment 14-25% of workforce
- UCFE claims: DC +543%, VA +215% YoY
- Banks with DC exposure: EGBN 🔴, BHRB 🔴, AUB 🟡
- Defense metros (Norfolk, Huntsville) may be insulated

### 4. Fraud is a Wildcard
- Stupin/Cantor ring: $270M+ across WAL, ZION, others
- 2 of 3 recent bank failures were fraud-related
- "Cockroach theory" — more likely hidden
- 10-K season (March 2026) may reveal more
