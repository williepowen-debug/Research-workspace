- **Date:** 2026-05-13T14:00:00+00:00

- **Summary:** In our most recent Short-Term Energy Outlook (STEO), we forecast that annual electric power generation from utility-scale solar will surpass that from coal for the first time in 2026 within the electricity grid that covers most of Texas.
