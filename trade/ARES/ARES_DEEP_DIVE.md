**Focus:** The MANAGER, not ARCC the BDC

## THESIS SUMMARY

Ares is the third-largest pure-play alt manager by AUM ($622.5B). Its "quality" reputation and FRE growth narrative have insulated it relative to APO/OWL, but the stock is -48% from peak — more than APO (-41%). The key vulnerability: **$66B in semi-liquid wealth products + $97B total retail channel AUM + unreported Q1 tender results + $27B+ Aspida insurance exposure.** The town hall signals stress the market hasn't priced.

**Overall Grade: B+** (strong event-driven case, less catastrophic tail than APO, but significant redemption + fee vulnerability)

## 3. FEE STRUCTURE VULNERABILITY

### CONFIRMED (Q4 2025 Earnings)
| Revenue Line | FY2025 | FY2024 | YoY | Notes |
|-------------|--------|--------|-----|-------|
| **Management Fees** | $3.68B | $2.94B | +25% | Recurring, based on fee-paying AUM |
| **Fee-Related Performance Rev (FRPR)** | ~$540M | ~$415M | +30% | From HWM products (REITs, etc.) |
| **Total FRE** | ~$1.88B | ~$1.44B | +30% | FRE margin 41.7% |
| **Net Realized Perf Income** | $169M | ~$120M | +41% | Volatile, European-style funds |
| Q4 Mgmt Fees (record) | $993.7M | $783M | +27% | |

### FEE SENSITIVITY MODEL
**Management fees** are the backbone (~76% of fee revenue). They're calculated on fee-paying AUM (FPAUM), not total AUM.

**Scenario: 10% AUM decline ($622B → $560B)**
- FPAUM declines proportionally (est. FPAUM ~$484B → ~$435B)
- Management fee rate ~0.76% avg → fee loss = **~$370M** (10% of $3.68B)
- FRE impact: ~$370M × 42% margin = **~$155M FRE loss** (8.2% of FRE)

**Scenario: 20% AUM decline ($622B → $498B)**
- Fee loss = **~$740M** (20%)
- FRE impact: **~$310M** (16.5% of FRE)
- **But it's worse:** FRPR ($540M) requires HWM breaches. If NAVs decline, FRPR goes to ZERO until marks recover.
- Worst case: 20% AUM decline + FRPR wipeout = **~$1.28B fee loss** = ~26% of total fee revenue

**Critical detail:** Q4 earnings revealed REITs had $79M in "suppressed" FRPR due to high-water marks. These products just barely recovered HWMs. Any pullback re-triggers this trap. FRPR from non-traded REITs could stay at zero for multiple quarters.

### REALIZED PERFORMANCE INCOME
- $169M in FY2025, guided $350M for FY2026 (more than 2x)
- This is the most volatile line. A credit downturn could defer realizations entirely.
- $984M of net accrued performance = 89% in European-style funds = long-dated, hard to pull forward.

**Confidence: 🟢 HIGH** (fee structure from filings) / 🟡 MEDIUM (sensitivity estimates modeled)

| Dividend | $1.35/quarter ($5.40/yr annualized) | 20% increase, ex-div Mar 17 |

### WAREHOUSE / LEVERAGE ANALYSIS
- **Ares the manager carries moderate corporate leverage** (~0.92 D/E) — not dangerous at the holding company level.
- The real leverage sits **inside the funds** (CLO warehouses, fund-level credit facilities, BDC leverage).
- ARCC alone has ~1.2x debt-to-equity leverage at the BDC level.
- CLO warehouses are the hidden risk — Ares manages one of the largest CLO platforms. Warehouse facilities are typically provided by banks (WFC, JPM, etc.) and can be pulled.
- Aspida ($27.4B) adds insurance leverage — annuity reserves leveraged against invested assets.

### KEY RISK
- Ares itself is not over-leveraged at HoldCo level.
- But **fund-level leverage + Aspida + CLO warehouses** create significant embedded leverage that doesn't appear on the manager's balance sheet.
- The $5.40/yr dividend on $33B market cap = $2.2B annual payout. At current FRE levels (~$1.88B), this is covered but with limited margin if fees decline.
- **Dividend yield at current price: ~5.4%** — elevated for a growth stock, reflects market skepticism.

**Confidence: 🟢 HIGH** (HoldCo balance sheet from filings) / 🟠 LOW (fund-level warehouse exposure not publicly broken out)

### BULL CASE (Why this could fail)
1. **FRE is genuinely resilient** — 25% management fee growth, institutional capital is locked, $156B dry powder
2. Aspida is 1/16th of Athene — no systemic insurance risk today
3. Ares has $949M liquidity — can absorb short-term stress
4. Arougheti is considered one of the best operators in alts
5. $66B semi-liquid has gates — max outflows capped at ~5%/quarter = manageable
6. Weinstein buying alt managers (name mentioned in X sentiment)

### BEAR CASE (Why this works)
1. **Town hall + unreported tenders = they're hiding something.** When the number drops, the stock drops.
2. $66B semi-liquid grew 69% in one year — panic money that came in fast will leave fast.
3. FRPR ($540M) is a ticking time bomb — any NAV decline below HWM wipes it out.
4. 12% DL software exposure + ARCC analog suggests true exposure is higher.
5. -48% drawdown with no specific Ares bad news yet = market is front-running.
6. Dividend payout ($2.2B/yr) leaves little cushion if fees compress.
7. Aspida growing 44% YoY with unknown statutory leverage = mini-Athene building in real time.

### POSITIONING
- **Vehicle:** June/Sep 2026 puts, $90-95 strike range
- **Catalyst timeline:** Q1 tender disclosure (late Mar/Apr) + Q1 earnings May 5
- **Stop:** ARES reclaims $120 sustained (3+ sessions)
- **Payoff:** Event-driven — binary on tender disclosure. If tenders show >5% net redemptions, $80-85 likely. If tenders are benign, stock bounces to $110-115.
