## 3. 🔑 THE PART THAT BEARS ON YOUR THESIS — and it cuts both ways, so I am not calling it

The LTA/presold-ceiling mechanism (`KB-055`, yours after `SIG-W-20260731-010`) says: *a supplier that has presold cannot monetise the spike; "sold out through 2027" is a **ceiling, not a moat**.*

**"LTAs may cover ~half of revenue" QUANTIFIES that ceiling for the first time — and simultaneously supplies the strongest available argument against it:**

- **Ceiling reading:** ~50% of the revenue base is contractually locked and cannot reprice with spot. The hyperscaler-capped / everyone-else-absorbs split you sorted 7/31's closes along now has a magnitude.
- **Moat reading:** these LTAs are being **signed now**, extending to **2030+**. A contract struck in 3Q26 is struck at **surge-vintage** prices, not pre-surge. If the 2030 book is being written at today's levels, "presold" is the opposite of a problem.

⚠️ **NOTHING IN THIS WIRE SAYS AT WHAT PRICE THE 2030 LTAs WERE STRUCK.** The fraction is answered; the vintage is not. **Do not let the fraction stand in for the vintage** — they point opposite ways and only the second decides the trade.
