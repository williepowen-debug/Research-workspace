# Oracle is trucking natural gas to AI data centres while pipelines lag (Bloomberg 10/8; ORCL ~−5%); the "OpenAI cancelled a multi-GW Lordstown data centre" post is a framing stretch of SoftBank saying it was never building a large one there

**1. Oracle — power, not financing (VULCAN action; WATT, BROCK info).** Bloomberg 10/8 (via three relays; original not read):
- Oracle has **trucked natural gas to a data centre near Salt Lake City for over a year** while awaiting pipeline hookup.
- It uses the same method at the **OpenAI-linked Shackelford County, TX campus**, and is considering it for the delayed **New Mexico "Project Jupiter"** site.
- Trucked gas reportedly costs **~4× hub prices**.
- **Market reaction (vendor intraday, 10/8):** ORCL fell ~5–6% intraday, Bloom Energy (on-site fuel cells) >7.5%.
- Context, also relayed: Energy Transfer's 17-mile Green Chile line, which supplies Jupiter, slipped from **Aug 2026 → Feb 2027**. Oracle sent a force-majeure notice in September (`-0925-007`) and says Jupiter "remains on our planned schedule".

**So what:** gas is reaching sites by truck at about 4× the hub price. That is a cost and timing tell on the "power is the binding constraint" thesis, and WATT owns the gas-to-power leg.

**2. Lordstown — CORRECTED-FRAMING (VULCAN, info-grade).**
- **The bookmarked claim:** "OpenAI tells county officials it's cancelled a multi-gigawatt data centre" at the Foxconn site. It rests on SoftBank's written reply to council members: *"No, we are not building a large-scale data center at the Lordstown facility,"* describing a small proof-of-concept on existing infrastructure. The letter is **quoted by the tweet; WALTER has not read it.**
- **Why that reading is a stretch:** 2025 coverage already had SoftBank saying the data centre would be a **"small part"** of the site, which was being retooled to **build modular data-centre equipment** for Milam County, TX. DCD separately reports that **Lordstown banned data centres** (article 403; date unverified).
- ⛔ **Do not carry "OpenAI cancelled a multi-GW site"**: no multi-GW plan at Lordstown is established.
- **What may still matter:** a Stargate site named in 2025 is now confirmed as not-a-data-centre. That bears on site-count claims, not on capex.
