PJM has issued a Capacity Advisory for Monday, Oct. 19, ahead of expected tight system conditions due to equipment outages needed for maintenance and construction.
The PJM load forecast is estimated at about 94,573 MW against expected generation outages of about 72,000 MW as well as planned transmission outages.
A
Capacity Advisory
is a new Emergency Procedure endorsed by PJM Members in June. It was created specifically for circumstances when projected grid operating conditions indicate an approaching capacity shortfall.
The advisory was issued to provide advance notice that capacity is tightening and additional actions may be required, such as recalling generation outages, rescheduling generation outages, calling generation out of market, recalling transmission outages to maximize generation deliverability, and/or notifying neighbors to anticipate less reliance on imports from PJM.
The Oct. 19 Capacity Advisory follows a week of unseasonably warm fall conditions, during which PJM proactively implemented procedures to reliably serve increased electricity demand amid planned seasonal equipment outages required for maintenance and construction.
Managing Planned Shoulder Season Outages
PJM is currently in planned maintenance season. Typically, both transmission and generation facilities are taken out of service to perform maintenance and construction from mid-September through early December, so they are fully available to serve loads during peak winter periods.
