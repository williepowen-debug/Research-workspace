10.16.2026 Docket No. ER27-197-000 PDF
On October 16, 2026, in Docket No. ER27-197-000, PJM submitted for filing proposed revisions to the PJM Operating Agreement, Schedule 12, and the RAA, Schedule 17, to reflect new members, removals or withdrawals, current signatories to the RAA, and corporate name changes through October 1, 2026. 10.15.2026 Docket No. ER27-167-000 PDF
On October 15, 2026, in Docket No. ER27-167-000, PJM submitted a proposed Tariff revisions under section 205 of the Federal Power Act to establish a new pro forma Reliability Must-Run Agreement and related capacity market rules. PJM requested that the Tariff revisions become effective as of December 15, 2026.
