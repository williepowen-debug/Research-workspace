**Last Updated:** 2026-01-26

## PRE-DOCUMENTED SIGNAL TYPES

These signals are expected based on domain analysis. When observed, log to ML.tsv and assess for escalation.

---

### SIGNAL: Office REIT Capitulation
**Trigger:** Major office REIT NAV discount exceeds 50%
**Interpretation:** Market pricing permanent impairment
**Action:** URGENT to REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** 2009 REIT crisis

### SIGNAL: mREIT Dividend Cut
**Trigger:** Major mREIT (NLY, AGNC, STWD) cuts dividend
**Interpretation:** Spread compression unsustainable
**Action:** ELEVATED to LIQUID
**Historical Precedent:** 2020 cuts, 2022 pressure

### SIGNAL: REIT Sector Rotation
**Trigger:** VNQ underperforms SPY by >10% over 3 months
**Interpretation:** Rate sensitivity or sector stress
**Action:** ELEVATED to LIQUID, HENRY
**Historical Precedent:** 2022 rate shock

### SIGNAL: Retail REIT Stress
**Trigger:** Major retail REIT reports >10% same-store NOI decline
**Interpretation:** Consumer spending deterioration
**Action:** URGENT to CARL
**Historical Precedent:** 2020 lockdowns

### SIGNAL: REIT Debt Maturity Wall
**Trigger:** Major REIT unable to refinance at reasonable rates
**Interpretation:** Credit market stress
**Action:** ELEVATED to LIQUID, REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** 2009 credit crisis

### SIGNAL: Cap Rate Expansion
**Trigger:** Transaction cap rates rise >100bps in sector
**Interpretation:** Repricing of real estate values
**Action:** ELEVATED to REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** Post-GFC normalization

### SIGNAL: Residential REIT Weakness
**Trigger:** Major residential REIT guides rent growth <2%
**Interpretation:** Housing market cooling
**Action:** ELEVATED to CARL, MARCO
**Historical Precedent:** Limited (new cycle)
