* **Strategic Intent:** In interviews, leadership has cited the desire to be "closer to their largest clients".6 This statement confirms that the client base—the ultra-wealthy—has already moved to Florida, and the bank is merely following the money.

#### **3.1.2 The Broader Financial Cluster**

* **Millionaire Growth:** According to a 2025 report by Henley & Partners, the millionaire population of West Palm Beach grew by **112%** between 2014 and 2024\.5 This growth rate is among the highest in the world, signaling a rapid gentrification of the tax base.  
* **The "Centi-Millionaire" Rise:** The city is now home to **78 centi-millionaires**—individuals with investable assets exceeding $100 million.5 This specific demographic is the primary target for the wealth management firms moving into One Flagler. * **Tax Incentives:** The catalyst remains Florida’s 0% state income tax, which creates a massive arbitrage opportunity for high earners relocating from high-tax jurisdictions like New York (top rate \~10.9%) and California (top rate \~13.3%).5
