## What a hike does and does not do to stocks

19 of 25bp is priced. A hike adds about 6bp at the front. A hold removes about 19bp. The sign for stocks depends on why.

| Case | Front end | Stocks, my judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Hike on firm data, dots show one more or fewer | Small rise | Absorbed; the 12/9 SEP matters more than the 25bp |
| Hike with dots above the strip | Rises through the path | Worst delivered outcome, long-duration tech first |
| Hold on soft inflation | Falls about 19bp | Good |
| Hold because stocks or credit broke | Falls about 19bp | Not a relief, it is a symptom |

Do not assume the Fed stops for a drawdown. It hiked into two (vendor ^GSPC): 12/16/2015 with SPX −1.7% from its early-November high, then −11.8% to the 2/11/2016 low; and 12/19/2018 with SPX −14.5% from its 9/20 high, then −6.2% to the 12/24 low. n=2, and in both stocks fell further after the hike. Today SPX is −5.4% from the 7,818.93 record.
