## CEASEFIRE NARRATIVE FADE CRITERIA
*How to distinguish real deal from headline bait*

### RED FLAGS = It's Real (Reduce Exposure)
- ✅ **Iran-side confirmation:** IRNA, PressTV, or Khamenei/Raisi office specifically confirms negotiations OR ceasefire
- ✅ **IAEA involvement:** Inspector re-access announced or scheduled (Natanz, Fordow)
- ✅ **Qatar/Oman as verified intermediary:** Named channel with specific timeline + terms
- ✅ **Hormuz reopening:** IRTV (state TV) or IRSL (Iranian Shipping Lines) confirms transit resumption
- ✅ **Infrastructure repair timeline:** Specific timeline for Ras Laffan, Gulf terminals mentioned
- ✅ **Military stand-down:** 82nd Airborne withdrawal order or CENTCOM press conference confirming drawdown

### GREEN FLAGS = It's Narrative Bait (Hold or Add)
- 🟢 **US-only statement:** Trump/Rubio/Pentagon without Iran confirmation
- 🟢 **"Productive talks" language:** Exact same language as Mar 23 (already faded)
- 🟢 **Twitter/X breaking news only:** No Reuters, AP, Bloomberg wire confirmation in 15 min
- 🟢 **Iran denial or silence within 2 hours:** If Tehran doesn't confirm within 2 hours, it's not real
- 🟢 **"Framework" without terms:** Vague framework headlines with no specifics = positioning leak, not deal
- 🟢 **Brent fails to hold below $95 after initial spike down:** Oil market is smarter than equity market

**RULE:** If SPX spikes +3%+ on Iran headline, give it 90 minutes. If Brent doesn't stay below $95 AND Tehran is silent, it's a fade. Start adding back.
