## 5. CORE vs. HEADLINE DIVERGENCE RISK

### The Key Dynamic
- **Headline PCE** = includes food and energy → war-exposed → elevated
- **Core PCE** = strips food/energy → more persistent → what Fed focuses on
- Core PCE is already at **2.7% YoY** — above target

### Divergence Scenarios

**Scenario A: Headline > Core (Energy Leading)**
- Headline: 2.9–3.0% | Core: 2.7%
- Market reads: "it's just oil — transitory"
- Fed reads: "monitoring energy pass-through"
- Risk: **Market under-prices persistence.** ULC at 4.4% means cost-push is already embedded. Energy pass-through to services (transportation, food delivery) has 3–6 month lag. The "transitory" narrative returns — and then gets destroyed in April/May.

**Scenario B: Core ≥ Headline (Broad-Based)**
- Core: 2.8–2.9% | Headline: 2.7–2.8%
- Market reads: "broad inflation — Fed must hold longer"
- Fed reads: "both mandates at risk"
- Risk: **Rates spike, equities sell, recession probability reprices sharply.** This is the most dangerous scenario for the portfolio.

**Scenario C: Both Hot (Convergence)**
- Headline: 3.0%+ | Core: 2.8%+
- Full stagflationary confirmation
- VIX rip, SPX down 2–3%, gold up

**Most likely:** Scenario A (headline slightly above core, energy-driven). **Most dangerous:** Scenario B. **Market is pricing Scenario A.**
