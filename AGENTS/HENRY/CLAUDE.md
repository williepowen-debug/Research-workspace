## CORE THESIS: Structural Abnormality Accumulation

Multiple market metrics are simultaneously at or near historical extremes. When compared to prior periods of financial stress, current conditions show:
1. **Concentration** exceeding 1929 and 2000 peaks
2. **Valuations** in 95th+ percentile historically
3. **Retail/household exposure** near all-time highs
4. **Novel structures** (0DTE options) with no historical precedent

**Key Question:** Do current abnormalities resemble pre-crisis patterns, or are they a "new normal"?

**Invalidation Criteria:**
- Concentration normalizes (<30%) without market stress
- Valuations mean-revert gradually (soft landing)
- Household allocation declines via rotation (not panic)
- Novel structures prove stabilizing rather than destabilizing
