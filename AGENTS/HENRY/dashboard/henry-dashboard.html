Dealer gamma: the amplifier

HENRY-owned · free-tier estimate from the Cboe chain
Above the flip, market makers' hedging leans against moves; below it, their hedging adds to them. Before the open the S&P sat about 0.7% under its flip, in amplifying territory. It opened at 7,567, bounced, and crossed above the flip at 11:08 ET. It did not hold: the S&P closed at 7,592, about 40 points under the flip, so hedging adds to moves in either direction into Friday's 08:30 PPI. The put wall at 7,500 is a clean single strike again. On this model it is where hedging pressure is near its heaviest, not a cushion. Walls sometimes hold, as the Nasdaq's did at today's open, but that is a tendency, not a computed floor.
