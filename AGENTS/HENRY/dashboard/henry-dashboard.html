Dealer gamma: the amplifier

HENRY-owned · free-tier estimate from the Cboe chain
Above the flip, market makers' hedging leans against moves; below it, their hedging adds to them. On 10/21 the S&P closed 8–13 points above the flip. It opened below it on 10/22 and closed 78–85 points below. The put wall, the strike with the most put-side dealer gamma, moved down with the market from 7,750 to 7,650; a close under it would be a break.
