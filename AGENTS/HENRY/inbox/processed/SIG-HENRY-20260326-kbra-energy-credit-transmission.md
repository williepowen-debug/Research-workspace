KBRA published a full framework (Mar 25) for how oil shock transmits to corporate credit. Key points:

- **Physical disruption, not just risk premium** — supply/logistics actually broken
- **Brent briefly >$120**, Hormuz flows "fallen materially"
- **Ras Laffan: 5-year rebuild** on two liquefaction trains

- **Most exposed:** chemicals, industrials, transportation, fuel/feedstock-intensive with limited pass-through
- **"Petroleum derivatives in nearly every end market... pressure on consumer wallets extends beyond gas pump"**
- **Pure upside for US upstream producers** (high prices, no operational disruption)
