## 2 · THE THREE LEGS — each two-sided, each graded independently

### **LEG A — ENERGY PASSTHROUGH (the mechanism)**
**August gasoline CPI `CUSR0000SETB01` MoM SA prints INSIDE `+2.0%` to `+4.5%`.**
- Point estimate **+3.2%** (the NSA pump monthly-average delta), band widened to absorb the August seasonal factor, whose sign I do not pre-judge.

- **FAILS HIGH (> +4.5%)** ⇒ passthrough stronger than the pump; the products/refining-side channel is doing work the crude headline does not carry — **FALCON's 8/6 crude-vs-refined split made visible.**

### **LEG B — HEADLINE (the aggregate)**
**August headline CPI MoM prints INSIDE `+0.25%` to `+0.45%`.**
- Arithmetic: core at **+0.20% to +0.30%**, plus a motor-fuel contribution of **≈+0.10pp** (a +3.2% component move at a ~3.0% relative importance) ⇒ **+0.30% to +0.40%**, band widened ±0.05pp.
- **FAILS LOW (< +0.25%)** ⇒ energy rose and the index did not; something else disinflated against it.
- **FAILS HIGH (> +0.45%)** ⇒ the move is bigger than motor fuel can explain, and the excess is by construction non-energy.

### **LEG C — THE DISCRIMINATOR: DOES IT REACH CORE?** *(this is the leg the letter is for)*

- **DENY** = core MoM **≥ +0.35%** **OR** core YoY **≥ 2.60%** ⇒ second-round effects are in train.
