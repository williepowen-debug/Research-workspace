**Pros:**

- Can't lose more than premium

**Cons:**
- **Expensive:** VIX at 21.77 already pricing some stress — calls expensive
- **Theta decay:** Time decay accelerates as expiration nears
- **Timing critical:** Feb 19-20 is 3-4 days away — must spike SOON

**Verdict:** 3/10 Conviction — Too expensive, too binary, timing too tight.

---

### 2. UVXY (Leveraged VIX Futures ETF)
**Structure:** 1.5x daily leverage to short-term VIX futures (1-2 month)

**Pros:**
- Leveraged exposure to VIX spike
- Liquid, tradable
- Amplifies gains if VIX spikes

**Cons:**
- **Extreme decay:** Loses ~5-8% monthly in contango (normal market)
- **Leveraged losses:** Works both ways — small VIX decline = large UVXY decline
- **Rebalancing risk:** Daily rebalancing = path-dependent returns
- **Timing ultra-critical:** Holding >few days = hemorrhage
- **Not for multi-day holds:** Designed for intraday/1-2 day trades

**Verdict:** 2/10 Conviction — Toxic structure. Only works if catalyst hits IMMEDIATELY.
