## The Case Against Palantir

### Not a Software Company
- FDE (Forward Deployed Engineers) = expensive consultants
- Costs hidden as "R&D" to inflate gross margins
- Foundry built in 8 weeks, AIP in a few weeks — not real R&D
- Karp admits: "We don't have the bandwidth outside America" = consultancy admission
- International commercial grew only 2% (proves not SaaS)

### Financial History
- Lost $4B over 20 years as private company
- Nearly bankrupt twice (SoftBank rescue 2019)
- First profitable quarter: Q4 2022 (19 years after founding)
- 6.5% annual dilution rate

### "First B/S Ratio >1 in History"
- 5 billionaires from stock ownership
- $4.5B annual revenue
- "Such B/S speaks to egregious stock-based compensation"

### Marketing Stolen Valor
- Claimed credit for GhostNet (Citizen Lab: "wrong tool for us")
- Claimed credit for Bin Laden kill (CIA agents: PLTR played no part)
- Karp: "I am very proud of what people think"
- Internal policy: "Make claims verbally, never in writing"

### Industrial Espionage Allegation
- Accused of stealing IP from i2 to reverse-engineer Analyst's Notebook
- Settled for $10M

### SPAC Circular Revenue
- $450M invested in SPACs (Lilium, Sarcos, Wejo)
- SPACs required to buy Palantir software
- All now bankrupt
- "Almost not forgivable"

### NDR Collapse Before AI
- 131% → 107% in 9 quarters
- "The escalator was broken"
- AI bootcamps rescued with low-base effect inflation
- Bootcamps = Acquire phase rebranded, hiding churn

### Customer Churn History
- NYPD, LAPD, every US police department dropped Palantir
- NSA ended relationship
- Karp: "just too complicated" and "hadn't been much of a payoff"
- Home Depot, Kimberly Clark, Coca Cola all dropped Palantir
