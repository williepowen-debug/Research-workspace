## Core Thesis

> "Inflation doesn't negotiate. GDP can disappoint and the Fed shrugs. Employment can soften and they wait for confirmation. But if inflation stays elevated, the Fed stays restrictive. There is no discretion here."

**Transmission Chain:**
```
Prices → Fed Policy → Real Rates → Financial Conditions → Asset Prices → Growth → Employment
```

Get the inflation call right = triangulate the Fed's reaction function.

## The Conceptual Unlock: Composition Over Level

Inflation splits into three fundamentally different economies:

### 1. Goods
- Globally traded, supply-chain responsive, volatile
- Fast to inflate, fast to deflate
- **Shock absorber** of the CPI basket

### 2. Services  
- Locally produced, wage-driven, structurally sticky
- Can't offshore a haircut
- Labor costs = 60-70% of service sector expenses
- Wages don't deflate without unemployment rising substantially
- **Structural floor**

### 3. Shelter
- 34% of CPI basket
- Measured via lease renewals and OER
- **12-18 month lag** relative to market conditions
- **Lagging anchor** - distorts headline in both directions

**Key Insight:** A headline 2.7% tells you nothing. A uniform 2.7% is different from +1.4% goods + 3.0% services averaging to 2.7%.

---

## Historical Pattern (Since 1980)
- Goods = shock absorber
- Services = structural floor  
- Shelter = lagging confirmation

**2008:** Goods deflated violently, services stayed positive until unemployment hit 10%
**2021-2023:** Goods peaked 13 months before services

## What to Watch

### Leading Indicators
- **PPI** - signals manufacturer costs before consumer prices
- **Market rents** - signal where CPI shelter will be 12 months ahead
- **Dollar** - signals where goods prices heading

### Persistence Indicators
- **Sticky CPI** (Atlanta Fed) - separates infrequent vs rapid change components
- **Trimmed Mean PCE** (Dallas Fed) - strips extreme price changes

### Confirming Indicators
- **Core PCE** - Fed's actual target
- **Services ex-shelter (supercore)** - wage-driven core Fed watches most closely

### Transmission Speed Hierarchy
- Goods: months
- Services: quarters  
- Shelter: 12+ months

## Key Indicators Deep Dive

### CPI vs Core PCE
- Fed targets Core PCE, not CPI
- PCE uses chain-weighted spending (accounts for substitution)
- Shelter: 34% of CPI but only 18% of PCE
- PCE tends to run 0.3-0.5pp below CPI

**Track Core PCE if you want to know what the Fed is thinking.**

### Goods vs Services Divergence
- Goods self-correct via global markets with elastic supply
- Services persist due to local markets with inelastic (labor) supply
- 2021-2023 confirmed: supply normalized mid-2022, inflation persisted through 2023
- **Demand was doing the heavy lifting**

### Shelter Lag Trap
- 12-18 month lag from market to CPI (tested average: 12.7 months)
- **The trap:** Even after shelter normalizes, services ex-shelter remains sticky
- Shelter was masking the problem, not causing it

### Sticky vs Flexible CPI
- Sticky CPI = best predictor of future core inflation (2x predictive power)
- Flexible → Sticky with ~12 month lag
- **Sticky CPI has never broken below 3.0% without a recession**

### PPI Pipeline
- PPI leads CPI by 3-6 months
- PPI-CPI spread = directional signal
- PPI < CPI = disinflation building in pipeline
- PPI > CPI = inflationary pressure coming

### Inflation Expectations
- **5Y5Y Forward:** Bond market's 5-10 year view
  - Near 2.0% = trust Fed
  - Above 2.5% = trust eroding
  - Above 3.0% = Fed lost anchor
- **UMich 1Y:** Consumer expectations (they watch grocery bills, not PCE)

### Trimmed Mean PCE
- Strips most extreme price changes each month
- Meaningfully lower forecast errors than headline CPI
- When trimmed mean and sticky CPI agree = high-confidence signal

### Wages & Unit Labor Costs
- **ECI (Employment Cost Index):** Gold standard for labor cost pressure (controls for mix shift)
- **ULC = Compensation growth - Productivity growth**
- At 3.5% comp + 1.5% productivity = 2.0% ULC → services inflation 2.5-3.0%
- Phillips curve reasserted: 4.4% unemployment suggests services inflation 3-4%
- **For services at 2%, unemployment likely needs 4.5-5.0%**

### Dollar Channel
- Trade-weighted dollar flows into goods prices with 9-18 month lag
- 10% appreciation → 1.0-1.5pp reduction in goods CPI
- **Two-way implication:** If dollar weakens in cutting cycle, goods re-inflate
- Tariff channel: 10% tariff → +0.3-0.5pp headline CPI over 6-12 months

## Prices Composite Index (PCI)

Six components vs Fed-consistent targets:

1. **Core PCE momentum** (3-month annualized) - highest weight
2. **Services inflation trend**
3. **Shelter trajectory**
4. **Sticky price persistence**
5. **Expectations anchoring** (5Y5Y forward)
6. **Goods price level**

**Regime Bands:**
- PCI > +0.5 = Fed constrained
- PCI > +1.0 = No cuts possible

## How to Track This Pillar

| Indicator | Purpose | Frequency | Source |
|-----------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Core PCE YoY | Fed's actual target | Monthly | BEA, FRED: PCEPILFE |
| Trimmed Mean PCE 12M | Cleanest trend signal | Monthly | Dallas Fed, FRED: PCETRIM12M159SFRBDAL |
| Sticky CPI | Persistence gauge | Monthly | Atlanta Fed, FRED: CORESTICKM159SFRBATL |
| Shelter CPI | Lagging anchor (34%) | Monthly | BLS, FRED: CUSR0000SAH1 |
| PPI Final Demand | Pipeline signal | Monthly | BLS, FRED: PPIFIS |
| 5Y5Y Forward | Expectations anchor | Daily | FRED: T5YIFR |
| ECI Total Compensation | Gold standard wages | Quarterly | BLS, FRED: ECIALLCIV |

**Release Schedule:**
- CPI: mid-month (~13th)
- PCE: end-of-month (~30th)
- PPI: mid-month (typically one day before CPI)

Use CPI for real-time signals, PCE for Fed's reaction function.

## Invalidation Criteria

### Bull Case (Elevated Regime Ends)
Three of the following for three consecutive months:
- [ ] Core PCE 3M annualized < 2.5%
- [ ] Services ex-shelter < 3.0%
- [ ] Sticky CPI < 3.0%
- [ ] 5Y5Y forward < 2.3%
- [ ] PCI < +0.5

### Bear Case (Inflation Reaccelerating)
Any two of the following:
- [ ] Core PCE 3M annualized > 4.0%
- [ ] Goods CPI > 3.0%
- [ ] 5Y5Y forward > 2.75%
- [ ] Services ex-shelter > 4.5%

## Structural Forces Suggesting Higher Floor (2.5-3.0%)

Five tailwinds reversed from pre-pandemic:
1. Deglobalization
2. Energy transition
3. Demographics
4. Housing underbuilding
5. Fiscal deficits

**Pre-pandemic 2.0% equilibrium required:**
- Globalization tailwinds ✗
- Subdued wages ✗
- Fiscal austerity ✗

---

## Key Takeaways for Trading

1. **Watch composition, not headline**
2. **Track PCI regime bands**
3. **PCI > +0.5 = Fed constrained**
4. **Sticky CPI ≥ 3.0% = persistence problem unsolved**
5. **Services ex-shelter > 3.0% = last mile incomplete**

The headline may approach 2% eventually. The question is whether through genuine services moderation or through demand destruction.
