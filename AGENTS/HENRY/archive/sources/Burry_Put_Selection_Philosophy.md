# Burry Put Selection Philosophy

*Extracted from "The Blessed Fraud Recurrence" (Jan 9, 2026)*

---

## Core Principle

> "The reality is **no one knows when a speculative bubble in stocks will collapse**. No one will ring a bell, and no one on CNBC or in the pages of the WSJ will tell you, 'The top has arrived, it is time to sell.' Or, if someone does proclaim such a thing, it will not be accurate. I say that from experience."

---

## The Goal

> "The goal is not to time a top in stocks perfectly, but to **have the exposure on the right side when the music stops**."

> "I've been there a handful of times – 2000, 2007, 2018, 2020, 2021, and 2025. **Only in 2007 did I know approximately when it was coming.**"

---

## Expiry ≠ Thesis Duration

> "The actual put expiries are not necessarily indicative of the duration of my thesis."

> "I can buy an April 2026 put as part of my thesis if it is the cheapest one, and still have the expectation it may take a year or two or three to play out."

> "If I buy an April 2028 put... it does not mean that I expect it to work in April 2028. It simply means **it was the cheapest put, all things considered, for me at the time**."

---

## Selection Criteria

> "What makes for a cheap put? There is no one way – I am looking for **maximum asymmetry and lowest implied volatility** in the price."

> "There are **no formulas or spreadsheets** – I eyeball the options tables on Bloomberg, looking across expiries and strikes."

---

## Why NVDA Over Others

**Purity of exposure:**
> "Nvidia is simply the purest play... entirely dependent on hyperscaler spending."

**Cheapness of puts:**
> "Nvidia also is the most loved, and least doubted. So **shorting it is cheap**, and its puts are cheaper than some of the other big shorts out there that are more doubted."

---

## Speculative vs Analyzable

> "Predicting 2007-2008 was different because it was not a stock bubble. It was an **analyzable part of the economy, housing lending**."

> "This is not like the housing crisis, which involved real-world lending dynamics that could be timed."

**Implication:** For speculative bubbles (AI, memes), you can't time the top. For credit/lending (regional banks, CRE), you CAN analyze the mechanics.
