## Context for Research Assistant

HENRY tracks market conditions that are at historical extremes, but extremes can persist for extended periods. The critical question is: what actually triggers the transition from "extended" to "correcting"? This research analyzes the specific catalysts that triggered major corrections when valuations were elevated, looking for patterns that might provide early warning.

Current HENRY findings for context:
- CAPE: 40.65 (only 1999-2000 was higher)
- Buffett Indicator: 230% (all-time high)
- Household equity: 47.08% (all-time high)
- Top 10 concentration: 38.22% (all-time high)
- VIX: 16.07 (complacent)

## Research Questions

### 1. THE 1929 CRASH
Timeline: August 1929 - November 1929 (then continued to 1932)

**Pre-Crash Conditions:**
- What were CAPE-equivalent valuations?
- Margin debt levels and trends
- Retail participation characteristics
- Market concentration (Radio stocks, etc.)

**The Catalyst Question:**
- What was the specific proximate trigger for the September-October 1929 decline?
- Was there a specific news event or was it internal market dynamics?
- What was the sequence of events in the final 30 days before the crash?
- Role of margin calls in acceleration

**Leading Indicators:**
- Did any indicators deteriorate before the crash?
- Breadth? Credit conditions? Insider selling?
- How long were valuations elevated before the break?

---

### 2. THE 1973-74 BEAR MARKET
Timeline: January 1973 - October 1974 (-48%)

**Pre-Crisis Conditions:**
- "Nifty Fifty" concentration - which 50 stocks? What % of market?
- Valuation levels at the peak
- Inflation readings and trajectory

**The Catalyst Question:**
- What role did the oil shock play vs pre-existing overvaluation?
- Did the catalyst CAUSE the decline or REVEAL existing fragility?
- Sequence: Oil embargo was October 1973, but market peaked January 1973 - why?
- Fed policy error timeline

**Leading Indicators:**
- What broke first?
- Was there a "canary in the coal mine"?

---

### 3. THE 1987 CRASH
Timeline: August 1987 - October 1987 (-34%, 22% in one day)

**Pre-Crash Conditions:**
- Valuation levels (CAPE ~18, not extreme)
- Portfolio insurance AUM and mechanics
- Trade deficit concerns
- Interest rate environment

**The Catalyst Question:**
- Specific sequence of events October 14-19, 1987
- What happened on each day?
- Role of portfolio insurance in the acceleration
- International linkages (London, Tokyo)
- What news triggered the initial decline?

**Leading Indicators:**
- Market had risen significantly in 1987 before crash
- Any breadth deterioration?
- VIX-equivalent readings?

**The Speed Question:**
- Why was 1987 so fast compared to other corrections?
- What made it a "crash" vs a "bear market"?

---

### 4. THE 2000 DOT-COM BUST
Timeline: March 2000 - October 2002 (-49%, Nasdaq -78%)

**Pre-Crisis Conditions:**
- CAPE at 44.19 (all-time high)
- Tech concentration (MSFT, CSCO, INTC weights)
- Retail day trading mania
- "New paradigm" narrative

**The Catalyst Question:**
- What caused the March 2000 top specifically?
- Was there a specific catalyst or did it just run out of buyers?
- Role of Fed rate hikes (Feb 2000 hike?)
- Earnings disappointments - which companies, when?

**The Shape Question:**
- How did the decline begin? Slow then accelerating? Or sharp immediately?
- Timeline of denial: How long did "buy the dip" persist?
- When did sentiment shift from "correction" to "bear market"?

**Leading Indicators:**
- Breadth deterioration timeline (when did it start diverging?)
- IPO market signals
- Insider selling patterns

---

### 5. THE 2007-08 FINANCIAL CRISIS
Timeline: October 2007 - March 2009 (-57%)

**Pre-Crisis Conditions:**
- CAPE at 27.5 (elevated but not extreme)
- Housing market conditions
- Credit spreads
- VIX levels (very low)

**The Catalyst Question:**
- Timeline from first subprime warnings (2007) to Lehman (September 2008)
- Key dates and events in sequence
- What was the "denial period" length?
- When did sentiment shift from "contained" to "systemic"?

**The Acceleration:**
- What event specifically shifted the narrative?
- Bear Stearns (March 2008) vs Lehman (September 2008)
- Role of money market fund breaking the buck

**Leading Indicators:**
- Credit spreads began widening when?
- Housing data deterioration timeline
- Bank stock performance as leading indicator

**Types of Catalysts:**

**Common Patterns:**
