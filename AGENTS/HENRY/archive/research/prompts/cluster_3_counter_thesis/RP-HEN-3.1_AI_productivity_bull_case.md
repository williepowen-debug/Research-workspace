## Context for Research Assistant

HENRY has identified multiple valuation metrics at historical extremes (CAPE 40.65, Buffett Indicator 230%, negative ERP). However, rigorous analysis requires steelmanning the counter-thesis. The primary bull case is that AI will deliver transformational productivity gains that justify current valuations - similar to arguments made about electricity in the 1920s or the internet in the 1990s.

This research should make the STRONGEST POSSIBLE case for why current valuations might be justified, then assess its plausibility.

---

## Research Questions

### 1. VALUATION MATH: WHAT WOULD JUSTIFY CAPE OF 40?

**Earnings Growth Required:**
- At CAPE of 40, what forward earnings growth would justify the multiple?
- If we assume 10-year Treasury at 4.25% and equity risk premium of 2%, what earnings CAGR is implied?
- Show the math explicitly

**Historical Comparison:**
- What earnings growth did 2000's CAPE of 44 imply?
- What actually happened to earnings 2000-2010?
- What would need to be different this time?

### 2. AI PRODUCTIVITY EVIDENCE

**Current AI Impact:**
- What is the measurable productivity impact of AI so far?
- Corporate earnings mentions of AI - quantified impact vs "potential"
- McKinsey, Goldman, other estimates of AI economic impact
- Which companies are actually showing AI in their earnings?

**Historical Technology Parallels:**
- Electricity adoption (1880s-1920s): Timeline from invention to productivity impact
- Internet adoption (1990s-2000s): Same analysis
- How long did it take for transformational tech to show up in aggregate productivity?

**Productivity Data:**
- Current US productivity growth rate
- Has AI shown up in productivity statistics yet?
- What productivity growth rate would AI need to deliver?

### 3. MAG 7 SPECIFIC ANALYSIS

**Current AI Revenue/Earnings:**
- NVDA: What % of revenue is AI-specific? Growth rate?
- MSFT: Azure AI revenue? Copilot revenue?
- GOOGL: AI contribution to advertising? Cloud?
- AMZN: AWS AI services?
- META: AI impact on engagement/advertising?
- AAPL: AI features monetization?
- TSLA: FSD revenue? AI narrative vs reality?

**Valuation Assessment:**
- Are Mag 7 valuations reasonable IF AI delivers on promises?
- What growth rates are currently priced in?
- Scenario: If AI growth continues for 5 years, are current prices justified?

### 4. MARGIN EXPANSION ARGUMENT

**AI and Margins:**
- Could AI drive margin expansion across S&P 500?
- Which industries might see AI-driven cost reduction?
- Quantify: If AI cuts costs by X%, what happens to S&P 500 EPS?

**Labor Cost Reduction:**
- Labor as % of S&P 500 costs
- AI automation potential by industry
- Counter: Will AI savings flow to profits or competition?

### 5. "THIS TIME IS DIFFERENT" ANALYSIS

**What Would Make It Different:**
- Arguments for why mean reversion might not apply
- Fed put / policy support argument
- Passive flows structural bid argument
- Globalization of investor base argument
- Scarcity of quality assets argument

**Historical "This Time Is Different" Claims:**
- 1929: "Permanent plateau"
- 2000: "New paradigm" / "eyeballs matter"
- 2007: "Great moderation"
- What made those wrong? Could current claims be right?

### 6. INTERNATIONAL COMPARISON

**US Premium Justification:**
- Why might US deserve higher valuations than historical average?
- US tech dominance argument
- Rule of law / property rights premium
- Growth differential vs Europe/Japan

**Counter:**
- Is the US premium excessive even accounting for these factors?
- Historical US vs international valuation spread

### 7. EXPLICIT BULL CASE SCENARIO

**Model the Scenario Where Current Prices Are Correct:**
- Required assumptions:
  - Earnings growth rate
  - Margin trajectory
  - Discount rate / risk premium
  - Terminal multiple
- How plausible are these assumptions?
- What could go wrong with each assumption?
