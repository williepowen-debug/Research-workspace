### 7. SOFR-IORB >+15bps as Funding Stress Indicator
**Question:** REGINALD uses "SOFR-IORB >+15bps = funding stress" — what's current spread and how close are we?

**OTTO's understanding:**
- SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) = what banks pay to borrow cash in repo market
- IORB (Interest on Reserve Balances) = what Fed pays banks on reserves
- Normal: SOFR ≈ IORB (tight spread = healthy funding market)
- Stress: SOFR > IORB by significant margin = banks need cash, liquidity premium

**Why it matters:**
- If funding stress is building (SOFR-IORB widening), banks pull warehouse lines BEFORE fraud is discovered
- Timing: Does funding stress CAUSE fraud discovery (lenders audit when nervous) or vice versa?

**Request:**
- Current SOFR-IORB spread?
- Historical context (what was it in Aug 2007 pre-crisis? March 2023 SVB?)
- Leading vs lagging indicator?
