## 1. WHAT HAPPENS MAR 28?

**Mar 28 = SECOND full missed paycheck** for DHS employees working without pay since Feb 14.

**Agencies affected (working without pay):**
| Agency | Employees | Status |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| TSA | ~60,000 | Working, unpaid. 5x callout rate. |
| CBP | ~60,000 | **Partially funded via OBBBA** — some back-pay covered |
| FEMA | ~20,000 | Furloughed or essential-only |

| Secret Service | ~7,500 | Working, unpaid (essential) |
| ICE | ~20,000 | **FUNDED via OBBBA** — not affected |
| CISA | ~3,500 | Skeleton crew |

**Key distinction:** ICE is getting paid (OBBBA backstop). TSA, Coast Guard, Secret Service are NOT.
- Total unpaid essential workers: ~130,000–150,000
- House Appropriations cited: 100,000+ missed FULL paychecks as of Mar 20

**2018-2019 Historical Comp:**
- 35-day shutdown ended Jan 25, 2019 just before the 2nd paycheck miss on ~Jan 28
- TSA no-show rate hit 10% peak but no widespread sickout — ended before 2nd miss
- Coast Guard was only agency to miss a full paycheck in that shutdown
- **Key diff 2026:** TSA already missed FIRST paycheck + callout rate is 5x normal (vs 10% peak in 2018). This is worse. The 2018 shutdown ended precisely because the political cost of a 2nd missed check was too high. That same pressure is building now — but Congress is in recess.

- **Not a clean UI claims signal** — effect is indirect and diffuse
