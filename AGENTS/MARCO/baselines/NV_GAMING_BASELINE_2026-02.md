## Key Observations

### 1. Las Vegas Strip Deterioration
- **October:** +8.21% (strong)
- **November:** -0.56% (first negative)
- **December:** **-6.07%** (significant decline)

**Trend:** Strip went from +8% to -6% in just 2 months.

### 2. December = Snowbird Season Impact
December is the START of Canadian snowbird season. The -6.07% Strip decline aligns with:
- Canadian airline capacity cuts (-33% Air Canada, -31% WestJet)
- Canadian visitor decline (-14.7% to FL, similar expected for NV)
- Google Trends showing depressed Canadian search intent
