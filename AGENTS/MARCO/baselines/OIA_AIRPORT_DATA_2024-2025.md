## Analysis: The Deceleration Pattern

### OIA is 12 Months Behind MIA

**MIA Timeline:**
- 2024: Domestic weak, LatAm buffer held → +6.85%
- 2025: Buffer cracked → -1.09%

**OIA Timeline:**
- 2025: Domestic ALREADY negative (-0.36%), international buffer holding → +0.81%
- 2026: Buffer expected to crack → FLIP TO NEGATIVE

### Theme Park Buffer (OIA-Specific)

OIA serves Disney World, Universal, SeaWorld — major drivers of domestic traffic.

**Why the buffer will break:**
1. Domestic is 85% of traffic — already negative
2. Theme park visits are discretionary spending
3. Consumer stress (CARL thesis) impacts family vacations first
4. Canadian/European visitors face USD strength headwind

### Prediction
**OIA flips negative by Q2-Q3 2026**
- Domestic decline accelerates as consumer stress hits
- International buffer erodes (Canadian boycott, USD strength)
- Theme parks can't offset macro headwinds
