## Key Insight

**FLL is the canary in the coal mine.**

Fort Lauderdale has higher Canadian concentration than Miami. The -15.9% international decline and -22.9% February collapse directly correlate with:
- Canadian airline capacity cuts (-33% Air Canada, -31% WestJet)
- Google Trends showing Canadian search intent -15% to -24%
- Visit Florida showing Canadian visitors -14.7%

**The transmission chain:**
1. Canadian boycott/sentiment shift (political)
2. Airline capacity cuts (structural)
3. Search intent decline (behavioral)
4. Actual passenger decline (realized)
5. → Hotel occupancy impact (next)
6. → Tourism tax revenue impact (fiscal)
