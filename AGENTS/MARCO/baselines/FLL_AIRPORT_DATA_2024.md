## Key Insights for MARCO Thesis

### 1. International Collapse = Canadian Boycott Signal
- H2 2024 international decline (-15% to -24%) aligns with:
  - Canadian tourism boycott (CTI 0.72)
  - Google Trends collapse in flight searches
  - Visit Florida data showing -14.7% Canadian visitors

### 2. Domestic Deceleration
- Domestic turned negative in Sep-Nov
- Could indicate broader economic softening OR
- Substitution effect (Floridians flying less)

### 3. Recovery Not Complete
- 2024 total still 4.2% below 2019
- International down 17.2% vs 2019 (7.32M vs 8.84M)
- Domestic down 0.1% vs 2019 (near full recovery)

### 4. Seasonality Disrupted
- Typically Q4 strengthens into winter season
- 2024 Q4 was weakest in 4 years (YoY basis)
