## Key Insights for MARCO Thesis

### 1. MCO is NOT Showing Canadian Collapse — WHY?

**The Disney/Universal Effect:**
- Theme parks are "must-go" family destinations
- Less price-sensitive than beach vacations
- Canadian families still coming for Disney (YYZ +5.7%)
- BUT: Budget routes collapsing (Hamilton -75.7%, Winnipeg -14.2%)

**Implication:** Canadian boycott is hitting DISCRETIONARY travel (FLL beach, MIA cruise), not ANCHOR destinations (MCO theme parks).

### 2. The Bifurcation

| Segment | Status | Example |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Premium Canadian | Holding | Toronto +5.7%, Calgary +7.8% |
| Budget Canadian | Collapsing | Hamilton -75.7%, Winnipeg -14.2% |
| UK/Europe | Strong | Gatwick +22.9%, Shannon +66.8% |
| LatAm Mixed | Mixed | Cancun -10.5%, San Jose +17.5% |
| Caribbean | Strong | Punta Cana +44.2%, Nassau +36.7% |

### 3. Domestic is the Canary at MCO

- Domestic: -0.4% (first negative signal)
- International: +8.2% (UK/Caribbean offsetting Canada)
- **If domestic continues negative, MCO will flip like MIA did**

The December 2025 traffic summary shows:

**Transmission Order:**
1. FLL cracked first (H2 2024) — highest Canadian exposure
2. MIA followed (2025) — medium Canadian, LatAm buffer fading
3. MCO holding — theme park anchor, UK/Caribbean growth

**Prediction:** If domestic weakness at MCO continues (-0.4%) and Canadian budget routes keep collapsing, MCO will join FLL/MIA in 2026.
