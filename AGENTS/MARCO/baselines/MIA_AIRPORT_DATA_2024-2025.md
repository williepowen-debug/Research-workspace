## Key Insights for MARCO Thesis

### 1. The Flip Confirms FLL Was Canary
- FLL showed H2 2024 collapse (-15% to -24% international)
- MIA was still positive in 2024 (+8.41% international)
- MIA flipped negative in 2025 (-1.25% international)
- **Lag suggests stress spreading from higher-Canadian-exposure airports (FLL) to broader market (MIA)**

### 2. Feb 2026 Is Weak — Peak Season
- Feb is peak snowbird month
- Daily traffic running -6.24% YoY
- Flights down -8.26% (capacity cuts)
- **This is the worst possible time for weakness**

### 3. Cargo Divergence
- Passenger traffic: -1.09%
- Cargo traffic: +13.61%
- **Business activity strong, tourism weak = consumption shift**

### 4. FLL vs MIA Comparison

| Airport | 2024 Total | YoY | Intl YoY | Notes |
|---------|------------|-----|----------|-------|
| FLL | 35.2M | +0.3% | -7.2% | Canadian canary |
| MIA | 55.9M | +6.85% | +8.41% | LatAm buffered |
| **FLL** | -- | -- | **H2: -15% to -24%** | Collapse |
| **MIA 2025** | 55.3M | **-1.09%** | **-1.25%** | **Caught up** |

### 5. Implications
- FLL leading indicator (higher Canadian share)
- MIA lagging but now negative
- Both airports now negative = Florida tourism thesis strengthening
- Feb 2026 daily data suggests Q1 2026 will be worse
