## Key Insights for MARCO Thesis

### 1. Canadian Collapse is WORSE at LAS than Florida

| Airport | Canadian YoY | Total Intl YoY |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **LAS** | **-24.5%** | -7.4% |
| FLL | -14.7% (Visit FL) | -7.2% |
| MCO | Mixed (-15% to +6%) | +8.2% |

Vegas has HIGHER Canadian exposure than Florida and is seeing LARGER declines.

### 2. Budget Carrier Collapse Pattern Confirmed

| Carrier Type | LAS YoY |
|--------------|---------|
| Flair (budget) | **-65.9%** |
| Lynx (budget) | **-100%** (ceased) |
| WestJet (mid) | -27.8% |
| Air Canada (premium) | -21.9% |

Same pattern as Florida: Budget travelers cutting first.

### 3. Domestic Weakness Beyond Spirit

Even excluding Spirit's bankruptcy:
- Domestic ex-Spirit: ~-3% to -4%
- This suggests broader Vegas tourism softness, not just Spirit capacity loss

### 4. December Acceleration

| Month | Total YoY |
|-------|-----------|
| October | -8.2% |
| November | -9.6% |
| December | **-10.3%** |

**Q4 2025 got progressively worse** — not stabilizing.
