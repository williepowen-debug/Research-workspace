# EUROPEAN INBOUND TO US — SECOND WAVE CHECK (2026-04-20)

## HEADLINE
**Yes — a second wave is forming, but it is structurally different from the Canadian collapse.** European inbound to the US is in sustained decline (9+ consecutive months through January 2026, country-level drops of -4% to -7% in Dec 2025, deeper regional -17% figure reported for "Western Europe" in a March snapshot), but the trajectory is shallower and more uneven than Canada's -28% sustained collapse. The more aggressive forward signal is in **flight bookings** (Europe→US July 2026 bookings down **-15.34% YoY** as of mid-March 2026 per Cirium, with Frankfurt -35.7%, Amsterdam -22.9%, Paris CDG -18.5%). Confidence: **HIGH on direction, MEDIUM on magnitude** (country-level NTTO data lags, and one widely circulated "-93%" figure appears to be a misreport).

**Note on the "93% drop" claims:** Several Travel And Tour World articles (e.g., Mar 2026) claim January 2026 arrivals from UK / Germany / Italy / Switzerland were each down 90%+. **This conflicts directly with NTTO aggregate data** (overall overseas -4.2% in Jan, Europe -5%+). The 93% figures appear to be either (a) reporting the share-of-decline contribution, (b) confused with "93% of pre-pandemic baseline," or (c) outright clickbait. **Do not use in the thesis without primary-source verification.** Use the Skift / NTTO / US Department of Commerce figures instead.

## KEY FINDINGS

1. **9+ consecutive months of US inbound decline through Jan 2026.** Skift (19 Feb 2026) reports the slump extended through January — down 4.2% YoY on overseas arrivals. February 2026 broke the streak marginally (+0.8% overseas per NTTO Feb report) but YTD 2026 remained -1.9% below same-period 2025. Sources: [Skift 2026-02-19](https://skift.com/2026/02/19/u-s-inbound-tourism-slump-deepens/), [Skift 2026-03-18](https://skift.com/2026/03/18/u-s-travel-breaks-9-month-losing-streak-but-just-barely/), [NTTO via IITA](https://www.inboundtravel.org/news/ntto-february-2026-international-air-travel-report).

2. **Germany is the clearest European canary.** Dec 2025 NTTO: -7.3% (deepest of the big-3). Frankfurt Europe→US summer bookings **-35.74%** per Cirium mid-Mar 2026 — the worst of any major European city and on par with the worst Canadian route collapses. Two-country layering: worst NTTO stock data + worst forward booking flow. Source: [Travel And Tour World 2026-03](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/u-s-europe-flight-bookings-plunge-by-over-11-in-2026-major-european-cities-see-drastic-drops-in-air-travel/).

3. **Forward bookings deteriorated throughout Q1 2026.** Cirium Jan 31 snapshot: Europe→US July bookings -14.2%. Cirium Mar 14 snapshot: **-15.34%**. The trend is getting worse, not stabilizing — distinct from a one-time sentiment shock. [Airways Mag](https://www.airwaysmag.com/new-post/cirium-flags-softer-transatlantic-bookings).

4. **TUI (Europe's largest tour operator) explicitly flagged the US weakness on its earnings call.** CEO Sebastian Ebel cited "significantly lower demand" and redirected inventory to the Emirates, Asia, and Caribbean. TUI named two causes: "strained diplomatic tensions" and "strict entry requirements." Timing: reported mid-2025 and reaffirmed into 2026. [Travel Research Online](https://www.travelresearchonline.com/blog/index.php/2025/08/tui-ceo-blames-trump-immigration-policies-for-us-tourism-decline/).

5. **French SETO tour operators: -14.6% package-holiday decline** France→US for period Nov 2024–Oct 2025 vs prior year. This is a concrete operator-level data point that confirms the NTTO macro figure for France is not a statistical fluke.

6. **Germany issued a formal travel advisory update on 29 January 2026** post the Minneapolis immigration-agent shootings. Federal Foreign Office urged citizens to avoid large gatherings and noted "demonstrations that can escalate into violent clashes with immigration and security authorities." Cities named: Minneapolis, Chicago, parts of Texas. This is the most pointed European-government travel warning to the US since Rebecca Burke triggered the UK FCDO update in Mar 2025. [VisaHQ 2026-01-30](https://www.visahq.com/news/2026-01-30/de/berlin-warns-german-travellers-to-remain-vigilant-in-the-united-states-amid-protest-violence/); [eTurboNews](https://eturbonews.com/germany-updates-us-travel-advisory-protests-security/).

7. **Multiple 2026 European ICE detention incidents have kept the story live:**
   - **Jan 2026:** Dmitrii Georgiev (Bulgarian national, flying home to Germany) — detained ~2.5 months after accidental border approach near Vermont.
   - **Jan 2026:** Unnamed German tourist detained at Tijuana crossing, held 6+ weeks incl. solitary.
   - **Feb 2026:** Karen Newton (UK, age 65) — 6 weeks ICE detention despite valid tourist visa.
   These extend the Rebecca Burke narrative through 2026 — the story has not faded. [NPR 2026-02-04](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/04/nx-s1-5696428/american-dream-denied-a-frenchmans-ice-nightmare); [NHPR 2026-01-20](https://www.nhpr.org/new-england-news/2026-01-20/immigration-ice-detention-mistaken-border-crossing-vermont-newhampshire); [Snopes 2026-02-23](https://www.snopes.com/news/2026/02/23/british-grandma-detained-ice/).

8. **European opinion of the US at record low.** YouGov (published 4 Mar 2026): no European country has >50% positive view of the US. Unfavorable views: France 62%, Denmark 84%. Swedish boycott polling: 70% of Swedes have or are considering refraining from US products. [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/european-opinion-of-america-hits-record-low-11519527).

## COMPARATIVE VS CANADIAN TRAJECTORY

| Dimension | Canada (2025-26) | Europe (2025-26) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger event | Trump tariff/annexation rhetoric (Feb 2025) | Rebecca Burke / ICE detentions (Mar 2025); compounded by Germany Jan 2026 |
| Depth of decline | **-28% sustained YoY** | **-4% to -7% country-level; -5%+ aggregate** |
| Duration at peak | 15+ consecutive months | 9+ consecutive months (through Jan 2026) |
| Carrier response | Air Transat, WestJet exiting routes | Virgin Atlantic specific cuts; Lufthansa broader fleet retirement |
| Forward booking signal | Collapsed early | Cirium Mar 2026: **-15.34%** — deeper than current arrivals data, suggesting acceleration |
| Government advisory | Canadian gov didn't need to issue — grassroots | **Germany DID issue formal update Jan 29, 2026** |
| Social movement | Organic Canadian boycott | Scandinavian / Danish / German Facebook boycott groups (100K+ members each) |

**Conclusion:** Europe is following Canada on a ~**6-9 month lag**, but with **shallower magnitude** so far. The forward booking signal (-15% summer 2026) is the strongest leading indicator and suggests the European arrivals data will deteriorate further into summer 2026. **Germany specifically is tracking closest to the Canadian profile** — official advisory + deepest country-level YoY decline + worst forward booking flow (FRA -35.7%).

## CONFIDENCE + CAVEATS

**HIGH CONFIDENCE:**
- Europe is in a multi-month arrivals decline (9+ months, corroborated by Skift/NTTO/Dept of Commerce).
- Germany is the European country closest to Canadian-style collapse trajectory.
- Forward bookings are deteriorating faster than current arrivals — leading indicator is negative.
- TUI tour-operator data and French SETO package data provide independent confirmation.

**MEDIUM CONFIDENCE:**
- Magnitude comparison to Canada (-28% vs European mid-single-digit). The NTTO December 2025 numbers are real but lag; we don't have clean Feb-Mar 2026 European country-level arrivals.
- Whether fuel-price capacity cuts are being confused with demand-driven cuts. Virgin Atlantic cleanest; Lufthansa muddied.

**LOW CONFIDENCE / DO NOT USE:**
- Travel And Tour World's "93%+ drop" headline figures for Jan 2026. These contradict aggregate NTTO data (overall overseas -4.2% in Jan). Almost certainly a misreport or clickbait reframe.
- Italy, Spain country-level specifics. Search results found no credible country-specific Italy/Spain numbers from primary sources.
- Speculation about European boycott sustainability. Polling shows strong negative sentiment, but sentiment-to-action gap in European travel is historically variable.

**KEY UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS:**
- What is the actual country-level NTTO data for UK/Germany/France in Q1 2026? (Not yet released with granularity in the sources surveyed.)
- Does the Q2 2026 "World Cup lift" materialize or does it fail to offset the structural weakness?
- Will the Lufthansa / Virgin Atlantic cuts be followed by British Airways, Air France-KLM formal route reductions? So far their pullbacks have been widebody redeployment rather than headline US-cuts.
