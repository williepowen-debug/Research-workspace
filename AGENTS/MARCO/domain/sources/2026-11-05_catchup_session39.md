## 1. Amendment 3 / CS/HJR 1-F

**Count (secondary, election-night):** NBC6 results box, updated 2026-11-04 00:02 ET, 99% expected vote in: Yes 62.01% / 4,894,305; No 37.99% / 2,998,348; 60% bar. WUSF 11/3 23:30 ET: "nearly 62%". The official endpoints did not give a figure (results.elections.myflorida.com has no general-election entry readable; floridaelectionwatch.gov needs JavaScript; WALTER got a 403). **Certification not read.**

**Text (primary):** enrolled CS/HJR 1-F, flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026F/1F/BillText/er/PDF (pdfminer extraction; line numbers are the resolution's).
- Lines 209–213: for non-school levies, up to $150,000 from 1/1/2027 and $250,000 from 1/1/2028.
- Lines 214–230: a person with a homestead on or after 1/1/2027 "who had not maintained a permanent residence in this state as of December 31, 2026" is exempt up to $25,000 for school levies and $50,000 for other levies. "beginning with the fifth year of exemption" that person gets the full amount.
- Lines 258–262: the (1)b.2 amount (the $50,000) indexes to CPI from 1/1/2028.
- Lines 286–290: from 1/1/2030 a county or municipality, by two-thirds vote of the governing body, may reduce the five-year requirement for a critical local need.
- Non-homestead assessment cap falls from 10% to 5% from 1/1/2027.
- Ballot summary says residency "after January 1, 2027"; operative text says "as of December 31, 2026". The operative text governs.

**Reading.** The docket letter said PASSES = structural anti-migration, reinforces the collapse thesis. The condition is met, so the letter is scored MET. The mechanism is untested and I do not carry "reinforces" past the letter:
- A new resident keeps about today's exemption; an existing resident gets up to $250,000. It is a relative disadvantage of up to $200,000 of non-school assessed value for four years, not a new tax on movers. Dollar size depends on local millage, not looked up.
- INFERRED, pull-forward: a mover already planning to come has a reason to establish permanent residence by 12/31/2026. That would inflate Q4-2026 inflow proxies (FLHSMV exchanges, voter registration) and then show a payback in 2027. No evidence yet.
- INFERRED, retention: an existing homesteader gives up a larger exemption by leaving, which lowers outflow. Net migration is inflow minus outflow, so the sign on the net is not identified.
- Unread: implementing general law, DeSantis's requested special session, any court challenge to a residency-date benefit.

**Poll lesson.** KB-FLI-05 and STATUS framed UNF 50/30/14 as short of the 60% bar. A ballot bar is a share of votes cast. Decided share: UNF 50/(50+30) = 62.5%; St. Pete 45/(45+30) = 60.0%. Result 62.01%. The polls were at or over the bar all along on the correct denominator.
