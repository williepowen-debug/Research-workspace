# S23 BAND AUDIT — all 37 live VX vectors, 2026-08-21

**What this is:** the census behind the GRADING CONVENTION block in `workbook/VX.tsv`'s banner.
**Method:** every live row's four band cells read via `tsvutil.read_tsv` (decoded), then hand-scored
against the real line. **Every defect claim below carries a WITNESS — a specific value that falls in
zero bands or in two-or-more bands.** Three witnesses are LIVE current values, not hypotheticals.

**Why it was run:** `SCRATCH` carried *"unscorable-band family is now n=3 (`VX-1.03`, SDL-01 count
tell, `VX-2.03`)"* and flagged it as a candidate for a sweep **as a set**, on the 8/11 lesson that a
single instrument always looks reasonable alone. The sweep was authorised 8/21.

## 1. THE HEADLINE: n=3 WAS WRONG BY AN ORDER OF MAGNITUDE

**35 of 37 live rows carry at least one band defect.** Only `VX-1.03` and `VX-H2A-02` were
disjoint-and-exhaustive as written — and both had been re-spec'd by hand within the previous ten days.

The n=3 figure was not a measurement. It was **a list of the rows I had happened to notice while
working on something else**, and it was recorded in SCRATCH in the same grammar as a finding. The
8/11 lesson said *sweep instruments as a SET because a single instrument looks reasonable alone*; the
failure mode one level up is that **a hand-curated list of defective instruments is itself a sample,
not a census** — and it is biased toward whatever was recently touched.

## 3. WHAT WAS FIXED, AND WHERE

### 3a. Ledger-wide — five rules in the `VX.tsv` banner
Classes **C**, **D** and **E** are *conventions*, not per-row facts. Re-writing 35 rows to fix them
would have been a large fix pass on a ledger whose last fix pass carried a higher defect rate than the
original work. They are now resolved **in one place**:

1. **Highest satisfied band wins** — kills C.
2. **A boundary belongs to the more severe band** — kills D.
3. **A value in no band is UNSCORED; never round it into the nearest band** — makes B and F *visible*
   instead of silently graded. This one does not fix anything; it stops the concealment.
4. **`>-N%` means a decline deeper than N%** — kills E for the legacy cells.
5. **A band must name its basis and observation window** — makes A a stated requirement. Until a row
   complies, its basis is whatever its Current Value explicitly names; a row naming none is UNSCORED.

⚠️ **Rule 1 changed no status.** Every mark checked was already the highest satisfied band — evidence
the convention codifies existing intent rather than re-grading the ledger.

## 5. WHAT THIS AUDIT DOES **NOT** ESTABLISH

- It is a **hand** audit. No parser was built: the defect base rate is ~95%, so a detector would flag
  nearly every row and ship pure alert fatigue. The witnesses are the evidence; re-measure before
  building anything. *(→ MARCO MEMORY, "Don't build it is a real answer.")*
- It audits **band arithmetic and stated basis**. It does **not** verify that any row's current value
  is correct, fresh, or drawn from the right series — that is a different sweep, and `2.01`'s BREACHED
  still rests on an unrefreshed Jun-15 arrest rate.
- Classes **A** and **G** are judgement calls in the residue rows. I fixed the eight where a *live
  value* demonstrated the defect, and left the rest documented rather than re-spec'ing on inspection.
