## 1. THESIS

**Statement:** Population movement disruptions create localized economic stress that compounds in regions with multiple exposures—and traditional indicators miss or lag these effects.

**Confidence:** 76% | **Status:** STRENGTHENING | **Phase:** 2→3 (Stress Emergence → Economic Transmission)
*↑ from 74% (S4) based on geographic expansion validating multi-state pattern*

**Invalidation Criteria:**
| Condition | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Canadian tourism rebounds to within 10% of 2024 by mid-2026 | -25% |
| Labor shortages don't materialize or quickly filled domestically | -20% |
| Florida real estate stabilizes and outperforms Snowbelt by end 2026 | -15% |
| Net US immigration returns to positive 500K+ annually | -20% |

**Competing Hypotheses:**
- Temporary Disruption (**10-12%**, ↓ from 20%) — Markets normalize in 12-18 months. *Weakened: 12+ months sustained decline; 47% of Canadians say boycott lasts beyond Trump term; habit formation in alternative destinations.*
- Policy Reversal (20%) — Enforcement moderates under pressure
- Domestic Substitution (15%) — Domestic tourism/labor substitutes for international

## 3. FLOWS

### FLOW-REG-01: Florida Triple Exposure Cascade (PRIMARY)
```
Tourism Decline → Hospitality stress
        ↓
Workforce Displacement → Labor shortages in hospitality/construction
        ↓
Insurance/Climate Push → Housing demand decline, price drops
        ↓
Tax Revenue Decline → Reduced services
        ↓
Accelerated Out-Migration → Population/economic contraction
```
**Breakpoint:** Florida net domestic migration turns negative
**Status:** ACTIVE — Leading indicators fired, lagging catching up
**Confidence:** 75%

### FLOW-IVF-01: Canadian Tourism → Regional Economy
Canadian decline → spending loss → hotel occupancy → retail (border) → closures → tax revenue
**Status:** ACTIVE | **Confidence:** 85%

### FLOW-WFD-01: Workforce Displacement → Sector Disruption

**Status:** ACTIVE | **Confidence:** 80%

Climate risk → insurance costs → affordability decline → out-migration → price drops → wealth effect
**Status:** EARLY STAGE | **Confidence:** 60% | **Timeline:** 5-15 years

## 4. LOG SYSTEM

### Decision Tree
```
New information arrives:
├─ About CURRENT/PAST state? → ML (Master Log)
│   "Q3 Canadian visitors -15.1%"
├─ Has a FUTURE DATE to watch? → FL (Future Log)
│   "BLS Jan 2026 release Feb 7"
│   ⚠️ MUST RETIRE when date passes
└─ Structural relationship (if X → Y)? → FLOW
    "Insurance costs → out-migration"
```

### Entry Essentials
Every entry needs:
1. **ID:** ML-[DOMAIN]-## or FL-[DOMAIN]-##
2. **Timestamp**
3. **Source** with date
4. **Interpretation** (what it means, not just what it is)
5. **Cross-references**
6. **Diagnostic value:** HIGH (discriminates hypotheses) / MEDIUM / LOW

### FL Discipline
- FL entries RETIRE when date passes, regardless of outcome
- Document outcome in new ML entry
- Never delete — mark RETIRED with pointer to ML

## 9. KEY CONCEPTS (Reference)

**Leading vs. Lagging:** Insurance/prices are LEADING (fire first). Migration counts are LAGGING (12-24 month delay). Don't wait for lagging confirmation to act on leading signals.

**Triple Exposure:** Region facing tourism + workforce + migration stress simultaneously. Florida is primary case.

**Diagnostic Value:** Evidence that discriminates between hypotheses > evidence consistent with all hypotheses. Prioritize HIGH diagnostic value.

**Canadian Decline Driver Attribution (Session 4):**
| Driver | Attribution | Reversibility |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Political sentiment/boycott | 55-65% | LOW — requires political change |
| Exchange rate (CAD weakness) | 15-20% | MEDIUM — economic conditions |
| Alternative destination habits | 10-15% | LOW — habits sticky once formed |
| Canadian economic conditions | 5-10% | MEDIUM — tied to macro |
| Travel friction (border) | 5% | MEDIUM — policy dependent |

*Key insight: Canadians ARE traveling (Mexico +11.8%, France +49%, Portugal +40%). This is SUBSTITUTION driven by politics, not budget constraints. Reversal requires political change, not economic recovery.*

**Invalidation Probability Update:** "Canadian tourism rebounds to within 10% of 2024 by mid-2026" has **~10-15% probability** of firing (only 28% of Canadians expect boycott to end during Trump term).

**Domain Transfer — Food Prices (Session 6):**

Food price tracking (VX-MARCO-2.07, ML-WFD-09, ML-WFD-11, FL-WFD-09, FL-WFD-12) **TRANSFERRED TO CARL** effective 2026-01-22.

**Rationale:** Food prices are a consumer cost-of-living burden, not a population movement indicator. National food CPI is influenced primarily by factors outside MARCO's domain (Mexican import substitution, retailer behavior, tariffs, weather). MARCO retains ag **WORKFORCE** tracking (VX-2.02, VX-2.05) as population movement issue.

**Key finding preserved:** Session 6 research identified three buffers (Mexican imports 63%, retailer absorption, volatility smoothing) absorbing ag labor shortage pressure. Latent stress exists but not transmitting to consumers. Full analysis transferred to CARL ML-MARCO-FOOD-01.

**Cross-agent coordination:** If CARL detects food price stress emerging, notify MARCO for workforce causation context.
