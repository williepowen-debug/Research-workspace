## Prediction Details

### Prediction #1: Nogales Residential Floor
**Thesis:** Pure cross-border retail dependency (60% of sales tax from Mexican shoppers) + residential crash (-43.2% YoY) = continued decline until stabilization.
**Trigger:** Q2 2026 Florida Realtors / Redfin data
**Invalidation:** Prices stabilize above -40% for 2+ months

### Prediction #2: El Paso Credit Event
**Thesis:** Structural pension underfunding (60%) + $55-62M deficit + sales tax collapse (88% decline) = rating pressure.
**Trigger:** S&P/Moody's rating action or negative outlook
**Invalidation:** Deficit closed to <$20M; pension funded >70%

### Prediction #8: FL Condo Distress
**Thesis:** Insurance push (4.5x national) + Canadian snowbird collapse (-28%) + domestic migration collapse (93%) = condo oversupply.
**Current:** 8.8 months supply
**Trigger:** >9 months = BREACHED (distress territory)
**Invalidation:** Inventory declines below 7 months

### Prediction #11: H-2A Surge Continues
**Thesis:** Domestic labor scarcity + enforcement pressure = continued H-2A demand growth.
**Baseline:** FY2025 = 415K certifications (confirmed)
**Trigger:** FY2026 DOL OFLC data shows >425K
**Invalidation:** H-2A growth <5% YoY

### Prediction #12: Central America Remittance Reversal
**Thesis:** Current +20-26% surge is precautionary liquidation (migrants sending savings before deportation). Unsustainable — once savings exhausted, flows collapse.
**Trigger:** H2 2026 IDB/remittance data shows YoY decline
**Invalidation:** Surge continues through Dec 2026

### Prediction #14: CA Produce Price Spike
**Thesis:** Fear-withdrawal (25-45% attendance drops) + H-2A gap (year-round excluded) = harvest disruption → price transmission.
**Trigger:** USDA/supermarket data shows CA produce +15%
**Invalidation:** Prices stable; import substitution (Mexico 63%) absorbs

### Prediction #17: FL Citizens Exposure Growth
**Thesis:** Private insurer exit + hurricane risk = continued growth of state insurer of last resort.
**Current:** $678.8B (+23% YoY)
**Trigger:** Quarterly Citizens reports show >$750B
**Invalidation:** Private insurers return; exposure declines

### Prediction #20: Thompson Ag Labor Bill
**Thesis:** Economic pressure from labor shortage forces bipartisan action on year-round H-2A.
**Key Provisions:** 20K year-round visas, 3.25% AEWR cap, CAW legalization pathway
**Risk:** Judiciary Committee jurisdiction on legalization provisions
**Trigger:** Bill passage with year-round provisions intact
**Invalidation:** Bill fails or provisions stripped

### Prediction #21: Early Produce Spike (Upgraded Feb 23)
**Thesis:** If ICE raids surge during planting season (Mar-May), produce price spike could hit earlier than H2 2026.
**Upgrade Reason:** 15% tariff announcement (Feb 21) increases demand for domestic produce as Mexican imports get more expensive — compounds fear-withdrawal effect.
**Trigger:** CA/national produce prices spike Mar-May 2026
**Invalidation:** No planting-season enforcement surge; prices stable
