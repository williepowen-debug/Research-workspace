## THESIS

Canadian travel boycott is **structural, not cyclical**. Four confirming patterns:

1. **Asymmetric:** Canada→US down -22%, US→Canada flat (-0.3%) = political boycott, not mutual cooling
2. **Persistent:** 13 consecutive months of YoY decline
3. **Airline response:** WestJet/Air Canada cutting 450K seats, shifting to Mexico/Europe
4. **First since 1972:** Overseas returns exceeded US automobile returns

**Key insight:** This is a demand destruction event for US tourism-dependent regions (FL, NV, AZ, NY). The seat cuts make it irreversible in the near term.
