## Key Finding: No "January 2026" Remittance Data Exists Yet — Not Overdue

**Remittances are part of the quarterly Balance of Payments (BOP), not a separate monthly release.** The relevant releases:

| Release | Date | Reference Period |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| BOP Q4 2025 | **Feb 26, 2026** (published) | Oct-Dec 2025 |
| BOP Q1 2026 | **May 28, 2026** (scheduled) | Jan-Mar 2026 |

**January 2026 remittance data will not be available until the Q1 2026 BOP release on May 28, 2026.** There is no separate monthly remittance series from StatCan. The data is not overdue — it simply doesn't exist on a monthly cadence.

Source: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260226/dq260226a-eng.htm

## Q4 2025 BOP Highlights (Latest Available)

- Current account deficit narrowed to **-$0.7B** (from -$5.3B in Q3)
- Trade in goods deficit narrowed by $5.0B to $4.5B
- Education-related travel services exports **declined** (lower international students)
- Record foreign investment in federal govt bonds ($33.6B)
- Record Canadian divestment of US Treasuries ($20.5B)

**No country-level remittance breakdown (to Mexico/Latin America) is published in the BOP summary.** Granular remittance flows by destination country would require CANSIM table drill-downs or special tabulations.
