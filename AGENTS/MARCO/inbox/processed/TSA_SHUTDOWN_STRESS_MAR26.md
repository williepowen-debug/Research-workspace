# TSA & DHS Shutdown Stress Signals — Mar 26, 2026

**Status:** Day 40 of DHS partial shutdown (began Feb 14). Congress recessing until Apr 13. No deal.

---

## 1. TSA Paycheck Status — ⏰ TIME-SENSITIVE

- TSA workers are about to **miss their second full paycheck** (~Mar 28). The only paycheck received was **half pay** near the start of the shutdown.
- Musk offered to cover TSA salaries (~$250M). **White House rejected it** citing legal barriers from his government contracts. ([CBS News, Mar 26](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/white-house-elon-musks-offer-pay-tsa-workers-dhs-shutdown/))
- ICE and CBP continue getting paid via the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" passed last year — TSA does not benefit from this. ([Houston Chronicle, Mar 25](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/news/houston-texas/trending/article/houston-tsa-officer-unpaid-ice-shutdown-22095987.php))

## 2. Airport Disruption — ACTIVE AND WORSENING

- **National callout rate: 11%**, but concentrated at major hubs:
  - **Houston Bush Intercontinental: ~40%** callout rate
  - **Houston Hobby: 43%**
  - **Atlanta: ~30%** callout
  - **New Orleans: ~30%** callout
  - ([Fortune, Mar 26](https://fortune.com/2026/03/26/houston-george-bush-airport-face-of-travel-shutdown-unpaid-tsa-workers/); [AP News, Mar 25](https://apnews.com/live/tsa-government-shutdown-ice-03-25-2026))
- **400+ TSA officers have quit** since shutdown began. Thousands calling out. ([CNBC, Mar 23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/23/airport-lines-government-shutdown-tsa.html))
- **Acting TSA head warned airports may close.** Transportation Sec. Duffy: "You're going to see small airports shut down... air travel is going to almost come to a [halt]." ([Reuters, Mar 19](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-official-warns-small-airports-could-soon-shut-down-over-tsa-absences-2026-03-19/); [Politico, Mar 19](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/03/19/like-a-snowball-going-down-the-hill-shutdown-standoff-risks-nationwide-travel-chaos-00836555))
- **ICE agents deployed to airports** (Atlanta, others) to assist with non-screening duties like guarding exit doors. ([CNBC, Mar 23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/23/airport-lines-government-shutdown-tsa.html))
- **Delta suspended specialty services for members of Congress** — political pressure move. ([CNBC, Mar 23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/23/airport-lines-government-shutdown-tsa.html))
- JFK travelers reporting 2+ hour security waits for morning flights. Spring break travel colliding with staffing crisis.
- House Appropriations: "TSA agents calling out sick are surging to **five times their normal rate**." ([House Appropriations, Mar 24](https://appropriations.house.gov/news/press-releases/pay-our-homeland-defenders-ciscomani-pushes-end-democrat-dhs-shutdown-chaos))

## 3. DHS Broader Impacts

- **ICE/CBP:** Funded separately via last year's legislation — continuing operations normally.
- **FEMA, Coast Guard, Secret Service:** Affected by shutdown but less visible. TSA is the public-facing pain point.
- Shutdown is **longest DHS-specific funding lapse ever** at Day 40. Previous record was 43 days (last fall). Will break that record before Congress returns.

## 4. Congressional Action — STALLED

- **Emerging proposal:** Fund most of DHS while tackling ICE enforcement funding separately. ([NPR, Mar 25](https://www.npr.org/2026/03/25/g-s1-115106/dhs-funding-tsa-congress-stalled))
- **Both sides rejected it.** Democrats want ICE reforms included. Trump and conservatives raised doubts. ([Politico, Mar 24](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/03/24/dhs-shutdown-proposal-doubts-00842576))
- Thune suggested canceling recess if no deal — **recess proceeded anyway.**
- **Congress recessed until week of April 13.** If no deal before then, shutdown hits **Day 60.** ([Politico, Mar 23](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/03/23/dhs-shutdown-deal-pressure-00839528))
- Democrats unified on demanding ICE enforcement reforms — no signs of breaking. ([CBS News, Mar 25](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/dhs-shutdown-2026-senate-funding-deal/))

## 5. Travel Industry

- Transportation Secretary explicitly warned of small airport closures.
- Airlines publicly blaming Congress. Delta's move against Congressional members is unprecedented political signaling.
- NPR/travel experts: "Disruptions and delays could get even worse." ([NPR, Mar 21](https://www.npr.org/2026/03/21/nx-s1-5755796/airport-security-tsa-lines-travel-tips))
- Spring break peak travel + 40% callout at major hubs = visible consumer disruption.

---

## 🔴 Key Signals for MARCO

1. **Mar 28 paycheck miss (2nd full) is the inflection point.** After this, quits accelerate and callout rates likely exceed 15% nationally. Small airport closures become real.
2. **No resolution possible before Apr 13.** Congress is gone. 18 more days minimum of this. The trajectory is worsening — not stabilizing.
3. **Consumer confidence transmission is live.** Spring break travelers experiencing 2-3 hour security waits at major hubs. This hits consumer sentiment surveys in April.
4. **Political dynamics are frozen.** Both sides dug in. White House rejected the only creative workaround (Musk). No off-ramp visible.
5. **If small airports actually close,** that's a major economic disruption signal — regional connectivity, business travel, tourism all impacted.

**Bottom line:** This is no longer a political standoff story — it's an active travel infrastructure degradation with no resolution mechanism for 18+ days. The stress is compounding, not plateauing.
