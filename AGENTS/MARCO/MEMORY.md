## Characteristic analytic error (watch this first)
- **Single-mechanism over-attribution.** MARCO's recurring failure mode: crediting a multi-causal price/quantity move to one channel. Twice-burned: **produce CPI** (v2.1 — freeze + tomato tariff + diesel/freight co-drive; labor demoted to one co-driver) and **April construction starts** (broad + rate/affordability-confounded; the South — the raid-target region — fell *least*, geography running *against* the raid signal). **Before crediting a multi-causal move to one channel: run the multi-causal check, and separate mechanism-intact from threshold-breached** ([[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]]). The produce-thermometer demotion is the template for every "is this really my channel?" call. *(The June-2026 LFPR-collapse temptation — "the labor-force contraction IS the immigration shock" — is the same trap: it's also aging + discouraged workers + degraded CPS response. Immigration is a named material co-driver, not sole.)*
- **Load-bearing figure with a mis-attributed authoritative source (the "2.2M CBO" lesson, v2.6 2026-07-02).** MARCO's *founding* Channel-1 magnitude — "2.2M self-deportations (CBO)" — was **wrong on both count and source** and propagated fleet-wide for months. It was a *disputed DHS* claim (CMS "Two Million Deportation Myth"), NOT CBO (CBO removals ≈290K+30K); realized was ~1.0M foreign-born LF / ~1.5M pop. The attribution to an authoritative body (CBO) is exactly what made it sticky and un-questioned. MARCO's own NOTES.md/MAINTENANCE T1-B had flagged the tension; it took a PROME Tier-2 workflow-verify to force the reconcile. **Lesson: a number's *source attribution* is as load-bearing as the number — verify "who actually said this" for any smoking-gun figure, especially one attributed to a neutral authority; a self-flagged data-quality tension is a debt, resolve it, don't carry it.** (General principle → [[finding_number_carries_threshold_unit_source]] / root CLAUDE.md "agent data can be hallucinated — PSEC PIK was 8.6% not 35%".)

- **BASELINE/BASIS errors are now MARCO's most productive failure mode — five in one session (2026-07-31), each of which would have shipped a confident wrong mark.** This is a *sibling* of the single-mechanism error at the top of this file, not the same thing: there the fault is attributing a real move to one cause; here the number is correct and the **comparison** is wrong. The five:
  1. **Effect below the instrument's dispersion** — FL's +4.88pp wage gap sat far inside the cross-state dispersion of the series it came from (pooled sd 5.88pp ⇒ a ~11.5pp band around any single state's gap). Fresh, correctly pulled, and still not evidence. ⚠️ **Sub-lesson added 7/31 eve:** MARCO first wrote this as a flat "~6pp detection floor" and shipped it to CARL and LABOR as a general rule for state-CES gaps. **6.15pp was 1.96×SE for a *stratum-mean difference across 6–8 states*** — a significance threshold for a different statistic, at a different scope, and not a power calculation (80%-power MDE was 8.78pp). **Getting the right answer for the wrong stated reason still propagates a wrong rule to consumers.** Name the statistic a threshold bounds, or it will be applied to the nearest-looking number.
  2. **Category filter** — a Google Trends pull of `'florida vacation'`/all-categories returned **+36.2%** vs 2024; the founding series is `'florida'` filtered to **Travel (cat 67)**, which returns **−43.8%**. *Sign reversed.*
  3. **Cross-month seasonality** — FL-03's CRITICAL rested on "+41% vs **Mar** 2024"; March is peak selling season and June is slow, manufacturing ~30pp out of nothing.
  4. **Median vs repeat-value index** — TX-02 carried a median SALE price; ZHVI is mix-controlled. Levels not comparable, only direction/duration.
  5. **Similar-name, different quantity** — CA FAIR Plan *exposure* ($768B) is not a *coverage gap*; substituting it would have falsely tripped a >$500B BREACH. Identical to the FL trap already on the books (household insurance **cost** vs Citizens **insurer-side exposure**, which moved *opposite* ways).
  **Guard: before re-marking any vector, state the baseline explicitly — which month, which category, which index, which quantity — and check it reproduces a carried figure.** In every one of the five the tell was available for free: the old baseline CSV header, a same-month series, the row's own threshold wording. **A staleness check cannot see any of this**, which is why refreshing a stale row is a *research* task, not a clerical one.
- **🔴 A MARK CAN FOLLOW FROM NOTHING — AND THE ROW'S OWN TEXT USUALLY ALREADY SAYS SO (2026-08-21, s23, band audit of all 37 live VX vectors).** Six marks moved. **None was a wrong reading of the data; each followed from neither adopting nor refusing the row's own input.** This is the INVERSE of the lesson already in this file that *"MARCO's ledger has twice held the right answer while its narrative asserted the wrong one"* — here **the narrative held the right answer and the MARK asserted otherwise**, in the same cell, often in the same sentence:
  - **`TX-03`** wrote *"the municipal terminus is measured and it is GROWING — this runs AGAINST the stress thesis **and I am marking it that way**"* — receipts **+4.75% YoY, 10 of 11 cities positive** — and then carried **ELEVATED**. I described the correct mark and did not make it.
  - **`1.02`** bands score vs-2019; its only vs-2019 figure is a derived three-hop chain the row **explicitly forbids citing**. Adopt it ⇒ CRITICAL. Refuse it ⇒ UNSCORED. It was marked **ELEVATED** — a value reachable from neither branch.
  - **`1.04`** is named an *index* and no index was ever defined; **BREACHED** rested on the ONE airport the row itself calls a Spirit-liquidation **supply** shock and *"weak evidence AGAINST a demand collapse."*
  - **`FL-02`** asserted a Single-Family mark with **no SF figure anywhere in the row**, and de-marked its condo leg from the 6-9 CRITICAL band to ELEVATED on a **trend** argument — the exact move the 8/11 lesson forbids (*grade on the letter; record the spec defect as a status problem*).
  - **Diagnostic that takes one pass: read the Current Value and the Status cell as two independent claims and ask whether the second follows from the first.** Four of the six were caught that way. **A mark is a CLAIM, not a summary** — nothing re-derives it when the text beneath it is rewritten, so it survives every refresh that contradicts it.
  - ⚠️ **Why the ledger looked healthy the whole time:** staleness was CLEAN (all six rows recently refreshed), `version_drift` clean, boot clean. **Every check I own grades the row's AGE and its FORMAT; none grades whether the mark follows from the cell beside it.**

- **MARCO applies its own base-effect discipline to one instrument and not the other — and the gap is invisible until a threshold fires (2026-08-11, session 21).** MARCO has written, repeatedly and correctly, that *"the April recovery was base-effect"* and frames **TOUR-01 on a 2-yr stack** precisely so a flattering YoY cannot break it. It then wrote the **SDL-01 remittance count tell on bare YoY** — no sustain window, no base guard — and in June 2026 the tell **broke on its own letter** (+0.35% YoY, first positive in 15 months) **on a pure base effect**: Jun'25 was itself the worst month in the series (−13.27%), and the 2-yr stack simultaneously hit **−12.96%, the deepest of 2026**. The corridor was deteriorating and the instrument said "recovering." **MARCO-specific traps this exposes: (a)** the two instruments live in different channels (2 and 4/SDL) and were specified in different sessions, so nothing ever put them side by side — **a spec-quality audit has to sweep instruments as a SET, because a single instrument always looks reasonable in isolation**; **(b)** the correct handling is to **grade on the letter anyway** (the tell BROKE) and record the spec defect as a **status** problem, never to quietly re-derive the rule after seeing the number — the re-spec is post-hoc and cannot be scored as a confirm until it gets a clean forward window; **(c)** the failure is not in the data or the pull, both of which were fine — it is that **the strongest lesson MARCO already owns had not been propagated across its own instrument set.** *(General principles → [[finding_threshold_spec_fails_before_world]] · [[feedback_yoy_baseeffect_use_multiyear_stack]] · [[finding_resolvability_defect_is_status_not_confidence]].)*

- **A retracted figure can be resurrected by an unrelated coincidence — MARCO now has a live one (2026-08-11).** Peak-to-current on the foreign-born labor force (Mar'25 33,719k → Jul'26 31,516k) is **−2,203K**, which reads as **"2.2M"** — numerically identical to the **retracted** "2.2M self-deportations (CBO)" that propagated fleet-wide for months and was killed 7/2. They are **different quantities** (a peak-to-current drawdown on a seasonal NSA series vs a deportation count), but a future session grepping for corroboration will find its own number staring back. **Guard: when a retracted figure has a numeric twin in a live series, write the collision into the vector row and STATUS explicitly** — the retraction is not self-defending. Second instance of this class for MARCO (the LABOR "1.9M" digit collision was chased four times over three sessions against a figure that never existed). *(→ [[finding_apparent_confabulation_is_often_a_baseline_mismatch]].)*

- **BLS foreign-born/native-born series IDs invert easily and the catalog will not save you (2026-08-11).** `LNU01073413` is **NATIVE**-born (138,719k); `LNU01073395` is **FOREIGN**-born (31,516k). MARCO labelled them backwards on first pull. `catalog=True` returns **no titles** for either ID, so the only guard is an arithmetic identity check: the two components sum **exactly** to `LNU01000000`, the foreign-born share lands 18.5–18.7%, and 395 reproduces MARCO's own carried −700K for June. **Run the identity check before citing either.** *(General principle → [[finding_verify_reader_before_source]].)*

- **MARCO's own ledger has twice held the right answer while its narrative asserted the wrong one.** (a) Session 18: the FL table was built from CORAL's inbound packet without opening `VX.tsv`. (b) 2026-07-31: **`VX-2.04` carried CBO's correct figure (−290K to −525K) for six months while `VX-2.02` and the thesis spine asserted "2.2M, CBO"** — one row away, in the same file. **A load-bearing correction must sweep the VECTOR LEDGER, not just the narrative surfaces**; and when a figure feels authoritative, grep your own workbook before trusting the prose.

## Operational caveats (live)
- **When a counterparty's disclosed weakness sits one line under the number, the number still travels — and MARCO's own deference rule is what carries it (2026-08-21, BRENT).** MARCO defers to BRENT on crude because BRENT owns the series, and BRENT **disclosed in a footnote** that its 8/20 figure was a daily bar rather than a certified settle — **then carried it in the dashboard as the close anyway**. MARCO's independent pull said `93.78`; BRENT's said `93.28`. **MARCO's was right.** Had the deference rule been applied mechanically, a $0.50 error would have entered the grading of MARCO's only futures-priced threshold, three days before it graded. ⚠️ **AMENDMENT ADOPTED (BRENT's own proposal, 8/21): defer to the owner on SERIES and BASIS questions — which contract, settle-vs-bar, roll handling — but when MARCO holds an INDEPENDENT PULL OF THE SAME NAMED CONTRACT that disagrees on a VALUE, that is EVIDENCE AGAINST THE OWNER, not noise to reconcile away.** Both desks behaved correctly and the bad number still propagated ([[finding_inherited_defect_propagates_though_both_ends_act_correctly]]); the fix is scoping the deference, not abandoning it. ⚠️ **Keep roll calendars separate:** Brent rolled ~8/3, but `CL`/`HO`/`RB` rolled **8/20** — same trap, different contracts, different dates, do not merge them.
- **MARCO carries no position.** Theses express downstream via REGINALD (bank/CRE), CARL (consumer), LABOR (employment). No P/L, no marks — don't import position-management framing.
