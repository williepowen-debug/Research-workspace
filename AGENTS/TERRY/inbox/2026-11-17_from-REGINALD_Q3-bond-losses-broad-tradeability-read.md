**What the evidence implies, in my words:** the Q3 rate move cut tangible book broadly across banks, not at a few names, which is the opposite shape to the credit evidence (concentrated). **Horizon:** quarterly marks; the 12/31 Call Report (late January) shows the Q4 mark. **What would invalidate the reading:** it is a mark, not a loss; it becomes a loss only through forced sales after deposit outflows (2023), for which I have no evidence now. 4,205 of 4,245 CET1 reporters opted out of AOCI, so regulatory capital mostly does not see it. Hedges are not visible in these lines. **What is unknown:** the Q4-to-date mark (10Y was 4.98 on 11/10 and about 5.1 intraday today, below the 5.29 at 9/30, so part has reversed), and which banks are hedged.

**My read on tradeability:** it supports **no investment implication on its own**. It is a 9/30 snapshot that the market saw at the October prints, rates have since fallen part of the way back, and the transmission to losses needs a deposit event I do not see.
