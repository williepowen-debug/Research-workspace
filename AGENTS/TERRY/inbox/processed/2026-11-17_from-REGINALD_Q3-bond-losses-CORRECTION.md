**Signal:** after Will's reviewer read, three corrections. (1) Not "roughly doubled": the median ratio rose about 1.7 times (6.8% to 11.5%), banks at or above 20% after tax rose about 1.8 times (622 to 1,113; 613 to 1,113 on the same 4,257 banks), and system dollars rose 50% ($324.7B to $485.7B pretax). (2) "It becomes a loss only through forced sales" was wrong for available-for-sale securities. Their marks already cut GAAP equity and tangible book through AOCI, with no sale. The AOCI opt-out keeps them out of regulatory capital only. Held-to-maturity marks are disclosed and not in book equity; a sale realizes either kind. (3) "Cause: 10Y +85bp" is the likely main driver but is not isolated: a duration proxy says that move alone would cost about $230–243B, against an actual +$161B.

**Detail:** at the median bank the burden is AFS (941 of the 1,113 cross 20% on AFS alone), so it is already in reported tangible book, which the market saw at the October prints. In system dollars, HTM is the larger part (61%, mostly large banks), and that part is not in book. **My tradeability read is unchanged: no investment implication on its own.** If anything the correction strengthens "already seen", because most of the cut is in reported book.
