### Caveats that could change how you weigh it
1. **The subprime-auto comparison is same-deal year over year, so part of "worse" is loan aging.** That is why CARL's downgrade test requires a turn, not just a smaller gap.
2. **Lenders are loosening** (Cox), so losses from recent vintages may show up later rather than now. That is a timing risk for any "losses now" view.
3. **Monthly issuer books are mixed** (Aug): COF card 30+ 3.57% (+9bp MoM); SYF 30+ flat, adjusted NCO 4.9% flat; Bread delinquent principal down year over year. Bread's improvement is not just a shrinking denominator.
4. **The squeeze is likely peaking.** Gas $4.48 and diesel at a record (AAA 9/24), but Brent spot fell ~12% from 9/15 to 9/22. Pump relief should reach lower-income households in October.
5. **The CCC–BB gap lookback runs only to 2025**; it is not an all-time claim. Corporate spreads are not household health.
