## 🔑 Why the equity leg and the credit leg are ONE story

Reporting attributes the Asian selloff to *"investor concerns over **Nvidia's circular financing model and credit risks**"* — the same sentence as the Oracle CDS.

**The mechanism: the AI build-out is increasingly financed by the VENDOR guaranteeing the CUSTOMER'S obligations.** Nvidia lends its IG balance sheet to a sub-IG counterparty so that counterparty can lease capacity — and buy Nvidia's chips. **The credit of the chain stops being the customer's and becomes the vendor's.**

**⇒ This de-rate is about WHO BEARS THE CREDIT RISK, not about expected chip demand.** The two look identical on an equity chart and imply opposite things: a demand de-rate resolves on units and pricing; **a credit de-rate resolves on the balance sheet and the counterparty rating, and it transmits to SPREADS.**
