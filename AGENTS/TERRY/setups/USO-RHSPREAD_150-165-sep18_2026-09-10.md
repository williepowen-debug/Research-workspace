## 4. ★ TWO FACTS THAT DECIDE THIS, AND THE SECOND ONE IS NOT DIRECTIONAL

### FACT 1 — Closing early is NEGATIVE CARRY on this structure, and the amount is knowable

An ITM debit call spread near expiry has **more extrinsic in the short leg than in the long leg**, because the long leg is deeper in the money. Closing early means selling your own cheap extrinsic and **buying back their expensive extrinsic.**

- Value at expiry **if USO simply stays at $156.80**: `min(156.80 − 150, 15) = $6.80` ⇒ **$680.**
- Value if closed **now**: **$585.**
- ⇒ **Closing today gives up `$95` — 16.2% of the current realizable value — purely to the clock, for nothing.**

⇒ **On carry alone, the correct action is to WAIT.** That is the argument for HOLD, and it is a real one.
