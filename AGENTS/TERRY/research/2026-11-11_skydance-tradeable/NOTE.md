## Addendum 11:5x ET — the filed credit agreement (PROME packet 289d00b490; report `PROME/themes/ellison/reports/2026-11-11_skydance-credit-agreement-addbacks.md`)

**What the filing settles (FILED, Ex 10.8 Annex A to the 10/6 8-K):** deal synergies are uncapped in covenant EBITDA (clause (f): no percentage or dollar cap, no time window, no "factually supportable" test); the 25% cap covers other acquisitions only (clause (g)); the borrower's good-faith figure is "conclusive absent manifest error".
