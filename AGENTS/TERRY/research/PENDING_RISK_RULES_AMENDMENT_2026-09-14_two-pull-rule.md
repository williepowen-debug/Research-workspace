## The proposal, in the form it would take

> **① TWO-PULL RULE.** ⛔ **Never quote a figure to TWO SIGNIFICANT FIGURES off a single pull.** Pull it twice. **If the digits move, the QUALITATIVE claim is the finding and the number is decoration** — state the comparison, not the decimal.
>
> **② FLATTERING-DIRECTION AUDIT.** **A figure that flatters your own finding has NO NATURAL AUDITOR.** A number overstating a COUNTERPARTY's position gets contested by them; one overstating YOUR OWN gets adopted — nobody downstream is motivated to shave it and everybody upstream already agrees. ⇒ **Audit hardest the figures that make your case strongest, and re-check the claims you have already WON the argument on. Winning is exactly when checking stops.**
