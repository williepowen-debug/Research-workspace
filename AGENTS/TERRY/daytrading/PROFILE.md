## The 5 Rules (enforceable at order entry)

1. **Flat by the close.** No overnight option hold unless *pre-registered* as a swing with written invalidation + time-stop.
2. **No new long premium after a big move** (>5% single-name / >2% index) — you may only *sell* or *exit*.
3. **Short credit is a liability,** marked at intrinsic until expiry/buyback — never booked as "income."
4. **Per-idea max-loss cap,** sized *below* a typical scalp stack. Cap the size, cap the chase.
5. **Loss-side time-stop** that mirrors the winner-cut reflex — same fast exit on reds as on greens.
