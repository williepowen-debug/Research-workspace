## Assessment
1. **The gap was the entire May escalation + early June** — the single most intense stretch of the whole campaign, and it was absent from HAWK state. Now logged (KB-185, KB-186) + this file.
2. **Track-divergence confirmed and deepened:** Iran cluster de-escalated into a signed MOU (Jun 17) the same week the Russia cluster hit its all-time campaign intensity. The geopolitical-energy risk regime is **rotating**, not subsiding.
3. **Channel = product/crack, not crude export (mostly).** With ~1/3 of *refining* offline, the squeeze is on Russian *domestic fuel* + *product exports* (diesel/gasoline cracks), not crude flows — Russia can still export crude even as it can't refine it. Crude-export terminal hits (Primorsk/Ust-Luga in March) were a *separate, earlier* channel. **BRENT owns the price read; flagging the scale.**
4. **Open verify items:** Volgograd Lukoil May date (LOW-CONF); precise May per-facility damage durations; whether any *crude-export* terminal (vs refinery) was hit in May-June (sweep found refineries + one Rostov depot, no new Baltic-terminal strike in the window).
