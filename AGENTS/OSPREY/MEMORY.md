And when the desk owns no instrument for a series (this one is BRENT's), the correct DARK entry is a POINTER to the owner, not a frozen copy with a label on it.** `[[finding_plausible_stale_value_evades_review]]` · `[[finding_supersession_marker_suppresses_the_live_value_beside_it]]`
- **[2026-09-02] WHEN I WRITE A MULTI-OBJECT PREDICTION, THE CONFIRM AND FAIL LIMBS MUST HAVE THE SAME LOGICAL SHAPE.** OSP-04's headline claim was plural — *"the TWO dark aggregates stay un-refreshed"* — but its resolution text confirmed on **EITHER** staying dark while requiring **BOTH** to revive to fail. One aggregate revived inside the window and the row still booked CONFIRMED. **The verdict is right on the letter and the credit is not earned**, so it is recorded as CONFIRMED-DISCOUNTED rather than quietly banked. ⇒ **Practice: before registering, write out the confirm limb and the fail limb side by side and check they are mirror images (AND/AND or OR/OR).
