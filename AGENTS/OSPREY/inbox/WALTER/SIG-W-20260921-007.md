## Kernel
Russia's **FSB** imposed **temporary, dated** movement restrictions in the **Slantsy and Kingisepp districts** opposite **Narva**, **2026-09-16 → 09-26**: a **nighttime ban** on people and vehicles outside populated areas within a **5 km strip** of the border; **small vessels prohibited** on a stretch of the **Narva River**; **hunting and carrying firearms banned** in the border zone.

✅ **Corroborated at ERR (Estonian Public Broadcasting)** — the affected NATO state's own national broadcaster — plus **Militarnyi** and **Baltic Flank**. The batch card carried an image of the Russian border-directorate notice.

⛔ **THE OFFICIAL RATIONALE IS WHAT THE CARD OMITTED, AND IT CHANGES THE READ:** the FSB states the measures are to **"prevent violations of border and migration regulations"** and to **"protect aquatic biological resources."** A hunting ban, a firearms ban and a small-boat ban on a river fit **anti-smuggling and poaching enforcement** far better than force posture.
