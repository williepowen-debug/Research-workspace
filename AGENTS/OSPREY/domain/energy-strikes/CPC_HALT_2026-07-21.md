## 2. Scale of the asset
- CPC pipeline: ~**1.5–1.58M bpd**, ~**1,510 km** Tengiz→Novorossiysk, ~**80% of Kazakhstan's** crude exports, ~**1–1.5% of global** oil supply. [CONF Bloomberg/BOE Report 7/19]
- Already running soft: **June CPC supplies 1.699M bpd, −7% vs May** (late-May Tengiz accident + lower Russian crude volumes). [CONF marinelink/bairdmaritime, June-2026]
- Ownership of the crude: majority **Kazakh** (Tengiz/Kashagan/Karachaganak), minority Russian (~10–20%). **This is not a Russian-export disruption.**
