## 4 · What it closes, and what it does not

**CLOSES:** STATUS carried, verbatim, *"No independent capacity-offline figure for any of them"* across the seven 9/1–9/8 strikes. That is now closed **for one — the largest one.** The other six (Perm 9/7, the Tatarstan plant 9/7, Saratov 9/8, Omskiy-107 9/1, Baryon 9/2, SIREN 9/3) remain without a capacity figure.

**DOES NOT MOVE THE BAND** (~30%, 25–35% [EST], KB-OSPREY-029). Two independent reasons, either sufficient:
1. **Different objects.** The band is a **national monthly-runs** proxy (August 3.8 M bpd vs a 5.3–5.5 norm, EA Analytics via Bloomberg). This is a **per-facility unit-nameplate** figure. Adding one to the other mixes bases — precisely the error HAWK's 9/8 basis-pair audit was convened over, and the reason that audit's 32.1–35.7% (on July's 3.6) and this desk's 28.3–30.9% (on August's 3.8) were not averaged.
2. **Vintage.** The August print **predates the 9/6 strike entirely.** These barrels are in no print yet; the earliest instrument that could see them is a **September runs print, which does not exist.**

**DOES NOT MOVE THE CHANNEL-1 MARK (4)** — by the trigger's own letter, not merely by session constraint. C1 upgrades on sustained **national** runs <3 M bpd **OR** an independently-sourced **>40% national** offline aggregate. 160 kb/d at one plant is neither, and a per-facility figure is not this trigger's unit.

## 5 · The open argument — OWED-36, written not executed

The ruled WQ-216 **E1** mapping says, of a refinery/processing strike: *"and only on B=destroyed capacity does it touch the offline band."* `RU-20260906-RYAZAN` is graded **B = destroyed capacity**, on the E1 letter's own test (*"when units are named"* — CDU-6 and CDU-4 are named and quantified). **So the taxonomy's letter points at the band while §4 above says the band cannot take this input.** That is a real conflict and it is registered rather than resolved by whichever reading was convenient.

**FOR touching the band:** ~240 kb/d against a 5.3–5.5 M bpd norm is ~4.4 percentage points — not noise, and it is the first facility-level figure the campaign has produced since Orsk.

**AGAINST:** §4's two reasons, plus a third that is the one that actually decides it —

> **The baseline is unauthenticated.** This desk has **never authenticated a Ryazan restart** after 7/29, and CDU-3 was down for maintenance throughout, so the plant was **not** at ~340 kb/d nameplate on 9/5. *An outage measured against an unauthenticated baseline adds tempo, not barrels* — the standing Saratov discipline (board_log 2026-09-08). The sources imply the plant was operating ("**ceased** operations", and it had resumed at some point after the 7/29 shutdown), but "implied by a verb" is not an authenticated running rate.

**THE DECIDING SUB-QUESTION for the next full session: are these ~240 kb/d incremental?** It is answerable — a pre-9/6 Ryazan throughput or restart report would settle it — and it has not been searched, because this session was drain-only.

**A SECOND QUESTION THIS RAISED, owed to the taxonomy rather than the band:** should the E1 letter's `B = destroyed capacity` value carry a **duration qualifier**? As written, a unit that caught fire with a several-week repair and a precautionary halt grade the same, and only the first should reach a capacity instrument. Amending an axis is HAWK's and Will's business, not a unilateral re-grade — routed as an observation, applied as written in the meantime (`finding_headline_keyed_conditional_inherits_its_composition`: apply the pre-committed rule, then record the disagreement).

⚠️ **A band change is Will-gated in the up direction. Do not let a ruled taxonomy's letter auto-apply one.** `finding_a_ruling_governs_the_next_write_not_the_existing_state`.
