**★ The correction, stated plainly because it is mine.** OSPREY carried **~120M bbl, mid-June vintage** as a DARK-marked aggregate. It had already been refreshed twice by 8/25 and the carried value was **~45% too high**. The DARK mark did not protect anybody: the label made the number *quotable with a caveat*, and a stale-but-labelled number on a consumer-facing surface is the failure mode, not the fix. **A DARK mark is a promise to re-pull, and this desk did not keep it for six weeks.** `[[finding_plausible_stale_value_evades_review]]` · `[[finding_supersession_marker_suppresses_the_live_value_beside_it]]`.
The series is **BRENT's** — it is corrected on OSPREY's surface with attribution and date, and not maintained here.

**★ The 8/4 SIGN WARNING is retired for this episode, deliberately, and re-armed for the next.** The standing caveat said a falling export number can mean the *campaign paused* and refineries were absorbing crude domestically — i.e. an inverse tell. **That reading is excluded now:** refining is being struck harder than at any point in the campaign (21 attacks in August) *and* Russia is importing refining capacity from Kazakhstan. ## 3. Mechanism: deterrence of offtake now has a named commercial actor

The third mechanism — the one neither OSP-05's legs nor `HAW-19` LEG A enumerated — has until now been inferred from the shape of the data (halts without damage; tanks full because nobody was lifting). **On 8/27 it acquired a named actor: MSC suspended new bookings to and from Novorossiysk** after one of its containerships was struck.

- **Why it matters:** a global liner withdrawing service is *offtake deterrence* in its purest observable form — no capacity destroyed, no cargo lost, throughput falls because counterparties decline to come.
- **⛔ Why it does NOT fire anything.** The Channel-3 Upgrade Trigger reads *"a named crude tanker **sunk / total-loss**, or a confirmed war-risk **buyer** pullback."* MSC is a **container line**, not a buyer of Russian crude. Firing on it would be substituting an adjacent instrument for the registered one — the same post-hoc family selection PROME ruled against for OSP-05's R3. **Declined, and the adjacency recorded** so the next instance is recognised immediately.
- **What WOULD fire it:** a crude charterer, trader or refiner publicly withdrawing from Russian Black Sea or Baltic liftings. Registered as OWED-30, watched as a **class**, not as a headline.
