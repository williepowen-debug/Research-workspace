**Reading (ASSESSMENT, not fact):** the October collapse is best explained as **target substitution under US leverage**, not target exhaustion or a negotiated pause. Two observations support this: (a) the deep-strike tempo continued against other industrial targets, so capability was not the constraint; (b) no order or agreement exists, so the brake is reversible by one Kyiv decision. **What would falsify it:** a refinery strike within days with no change in US posture, which would make the gap noise; or a public US–Ukraine understanding, which would make it policy rather than leverage.

**Channel-model consequence:** THESIS v1.0 models the campaign's supply-side mechanisms (damage, offtake deterrence). **It has no term for an external political brake on the attacker.** That is a known hole to carry into a THESIS revision. Until then, C1's mark is held on CAPACITY evidence (upgraded 9/29 on end-August S&P), which a lull does not reverse. The §1b exits are the honest path: 5→4 needs an independent aggregate <40% twice (blackout day 56/60), and 4→3 needs runs ≥4.5 M bpd for two months.
