## 2. THE PUBLISHED MACRO ATTRIBUTION FOR 8/4 IS INSUFFICIENT — MEASURED, NOT ARGUED

**That is a beta claim, and beta is exactly what the table above already prices.** Testing it:

- **The real-rate leg moved 3bp.** DFII10 **2.43 [8/3] → 2.40 [8/4]**. At my registered rolled beta (**−0.0514 %/bp**, L-19) that is **+0.154% of gold** ⇒ **10.1% of gold's +1.53%** — and via β=1.1485, **+0.177% of platinum = 2.2% of the +7.99%.**
- ⚠️ The article's own **"10-year yield fell over 1% to ~4.62%"** is the **nominal** leg (DGS10 4.70 → 4.63 = −7bp); the **real** leg fell 3bp. **The nominal move was more than twice the real move**, so quoting the nominal overstates the monetary channel by ~2×.
- **INVERSE FRAMING — the load-bearing number.** For β to explain Pt's +7.99%, **gold would have had to rise +6.98% that session.** The **largest single gold session in the entire 666-day sample is +6.08%.** ⇒ **No gold move that has actually occurred in this sample would have been sufficient.** The "amplification" story requires ~4.6× amplification; the measured amplification is **1.15×**.

**⇒ Macro explains ~2% of the platinum move. REJECTED as sufficient. Not repaired by a bigger macro story — the residual is the finding.**
