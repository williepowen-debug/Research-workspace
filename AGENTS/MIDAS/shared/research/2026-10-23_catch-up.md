## 1. What happened — one paragraph

Every metal fell on **Thursday October 22**, at the same time as US stocks fell (S&P 500 −1.2%, Nasdaq −2.6%, VIX 16.7 → 19.0) and Brent crude jumped to about $113. Media wraps blame oil-driven inflation fears and rising odds of a Fed hike. **The primary Treasury data contradict the rate half of that story:** on October 22 the 10-year real yield fell 12bp (2.76% → 2.64%), the 10-year nominal fell 17bp (5.13% → 4.96%) and the 2-year fell 16bp (4.81% → 4.65%). The dollar index was roughly flat (101.78 → 101.61). Gold fell 2.2% on a day when real yields, nominal yields and the dollar all fell. **That is a liquidation pattern — metals sold with equities while bonds rallied — not a rate-driven repricing.** The issuer data back this up: GLD's trust shed **11.98 tonnes** of gold on October 22, its largest one-day outflow of the month, on 17.5M shares of volume (about 2.5× the prior days).
