The issuer's Q3 rebound reflects maintenance scheduling and processing material accumulated during the annual navigation interruption between the Norilsk and Kola sites. It is partly a timing/catch-up mechanism. The nine-month decline is attributed to the prior year's high comparison base. No current outage, sanction or force majeure is identified in this release. **I2 supply-disruption trigger NOT-FIRED on this evidence; I2 remains 2.** The existing price-band scoring floor remains separate.

The [July 27 H1 issuer release](https://nornickel.com/news-and-media/press-releases-and-news/nornickel-announces-production-results-for-1h2026/) closes a source gap in the setup: H1 palladium is **1,199 koz**, rather than the setup's approximate 1,198 koz sum; H1 platinum is 281 koz. Subtracting these rounded H1 totals from the nine-month totals gives Q3 **735/191 koz**, versus directly reported **736/192 koz**. Both releases warn that totals may differ from component sums because of rounding. The October release does not publish a restated H1 total. Retain both calculations without inventing a restatement or modifying the setup: **both methods exceed the same fixed 634/178 koz boundaries**, so the interpretation is unchanged.

**Later supply risk is a separate evidence tier.** [Reuters, republished by Mining.com.au](https://mining.com.au/nornickel-says-furnace-overhaul-may-cut-2027-28-metals-output/), reports a planned nine-month furnace overhaul starting in 2027 that may affect 2027 and possibly 2028 output, and a company expectation of palladium at the upper end of 2026 guidance. Those details are absent from the held full issuer release; the underlying company briefing/transcript is not held. Carry them as MEDIA, unquantified future risk, not an immediate outage or a reason to score the current supply trigger. This is the principal offset to the stronger Q3 read.

For copper, the PRIMARY table reports group total 311,777 tonnes including Zabaykalsky's 55,097 tonnes. Do not add the component again to reproduce a larger media total. No separate I1 reassessment is made here.

**Five implications:** Q3 Pd recovery weakens immediate shortage-only reasoning; Pt also beat its pace test; unchanged guidance contains no new 2026 cut; catch-up production and remaining YoY declines prevent extrapolating Q3 indefinitely; reported 2027–28 maintenance is a future offset with unheld primary detail.
