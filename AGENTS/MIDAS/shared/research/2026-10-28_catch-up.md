## Copper: WALTER's deferred figures checked against ICSG primary data

KB-212 held WALTER's figures for ICSG's forecast as owner-sourced. Both ICSG releases were retrieved from icsg.org (hashes in `access.json` and `SHA256SUMS`).

- **[ICSG forecast, issued October 14](../../copper/sources/2026-10-28_ICSG-check/icsg_2026-10-14_forecast.pdf): refined surplus about 104 kt in 2026 (April: 96 kt) and about 368 kt in 2027.** Refined production +0.4%/+3.4% and usage +1.5%/+2.5%; mine production growth for 2026 was cut to 1.3% from 1.6%. **WALTER's 104/368 kt figures are verified.**
- **[ICSG monthly, issued October 23](../../copper/sources/2026-10-28_ICSG-check/icsg_2026-10-23_monthly.pdf):** January–August 2026 apparent refined **surplus 218 kt**, or 181 kt adjusted for estimated Chinese bonded-stock changes. Chinese apparent demand +2.8%; **Chinese net refined imports −12%**. Mine output −0.9% (Grasberg concentrate −29%, Chile −7%).
- **First Quantum's same-quarter acid sales (~36 kt):** still owner-sourced. The company's Q3 2026 results were not found; Q1 commentary of about 10 kt per month is a different period. This half stays PROVISIONAL until the issuer publishes.
- **Reconciliation:** the ICSG data run to August and show a surplus year with weaker Chinese imports. The late-October Yangshan premium and backwardation are later and point the other way. These are different clocks, not a contradiction. A premium spike says buyers want metal *now*; it does not revise the annual balance.
- **I1 stays 1:** the bands are downside-only and nothing fired. This is supportive evidence for the China-demand read, not a score.
