NORNICKEL ANNOUNCES CONSOLIDATED PRODUCTION RESULTS FOR 9M 2026  

Moscow, 26 October 2026 – Nornickel, the world’s largest producer of palladium and a major 
producer of nickel, platinum and copper, announces preliminary consolidated production results 
for the third quarter and nine months of 2026. 

First Vice-President — Chief Operating Officer, Evgeniy Fedorov commented on the 
operating results, 

«In  9M  2026  we  achieved  higher  nickel  output  compared  to  the  same  period  of  last  year  by 
increasing  raw  material  processing  volumes.  Meanwhile,  production  of  platinum-group  metals 
saw  a  slight  year-on-year  decline,  primarily  due to  a  high  base  effect  from  the  corresponding 
period of last year.  

Copper output remained almost unchanged, even though the Zabaykalsky Division increased its 
metal production as a result of higher ore extraction volumes and a higher metal content in the 
ore. 

To ensure production continuity, we are upgrading our fleet of mining equipment by acquiring 
machinery from new suppliers. We are collaborating with BELAZ to develop and mass-produce 
underground  mining  equipment.  Factory  tests  are  currently  underway  for  the  newest 
underground complex of machines for rock loading and transporting.   

A mechanized tunnelling complex Norda has begun excavation at the Taimyrsky Mine in Norilsk. 
The technology eliminates the need for drill-and-blast excavation. This reduces vibration impacts 
on the rock mass and prevents blast-related damage to the surrounding rock. 

In  addition,  the  large-scale  environmental  Sulphur  Program  continues  its  ramp-up  to  design 
capacity. Since its launch in late 2023, more than 1.5 million tones of sulpfur dioxide emissions 
have been captured at Nadezhda Plant, including 640 thousand tones in the first nine months of 
this year. Under the federal Clean Air project, total captured emissions are expected to reach at 
least 735 thousand tones in 2026. 

As part  of our production chain reconfiguration aimed  at  consolidating precious metals output 
within  the  chemical-metallurgical  facility  at  Kola  site,  commissioning  has  been  successfully 
completed. 

The company reiterates its previously announced production guidance for FY2026».

Nickel 

In 3Q 2026, consolidated nickel output increased by 41% quarter-on-quarter (q-o-q) to 61 kt. 
The increase was driven by the processing of higher volumes of raw materials accumulated during 
the inter-navigation period  at  Norilsk  site1,  as well as an  increase in the production of market 
sulfide nickel concentrate at Kola site. 

In 9M 2026, consolidated nickel output increased by 4% year-on-year (y-o-y) to 146 kt due to 
higher ore mining volumes. 

Copper 

In 3Q 2026, consolidated copper output (including Zabaykalsky Division) increased by 4% q-o-
q to 109 kt due to increase in the production of copper concentrate for sale, as well as through 
an increase in ore extraction at Zabaykalsky Division. 

In 9M 2026, total copper output amounted to 312 kt and remained virtually unchanged y-o-y. 

Zabaykalsky Division increased the production of copper in concentrate by 5% q-o-q to 19 kt, 
due  to  higher  processed  volumes  of  concentrate.  In  9M  2026,  production  of  copper  in 
concentrate at Zabaykalsky Division increased by 6% y-o-y to 55 kt due to higher ore production, 
increased metal grades and improved copper recovery. 

Platinum Group Metals 

In 3Q 2026, palladium production increased by 25% q-o-q to 736 koz and platinum production 
increased  by  32%  q-o-q  to  192  koz  due  to  the  optimization  of  maintenance  schedules  at 
Nadezhda Plant, as well as the processing of matte2 at Kola site accumulated during the inter-
navigation pause. 

In 9M 2026, palladium and platinum output amounted to 1,934 koz (down 4% y-o-y) and 472 
koz (down 2% y-o-y), respectively. The decrease was attributed to a high base effect from the 
same period last year, as a result of optimizing the modernization timeframe for the flash smelting 
furnace №2 and implementing a production efficiency improvement program.

ABOUT THE COMPANY  

MMC Norilsk Nickel is a diversified mining and metallurgical company, the world’s largest producer of  

palladium and high-grade nickel and a major producer of platinum and copper. The company also produces  

cobalt, rhodium, silver, gold, iridium, ruthenium, selenium and other products. 

The main production units of Norilsk Nickel Group are located at the Norilsk Industrial District, on the Kola  

Peninsula and Zabaykalsky Krai in Russia. 

MMC Norilsk Nickel shares are listed on the Moscow Stock Exchange.
