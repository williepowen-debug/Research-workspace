NORNICKEL ANNOUNCES PRODUCTION RESULTS FOR 1H 2026 

Moscow,  27  July  2026  –  Nornickel,  the  world’s  largest  producer  of  palladium  and  a  major 
producer of nickel, platinum and copper, announces preliminary consolidated production results 
for the first half of 2026.   

First Vice-President — Chief Operating Officer, Evgeniy Fedorov commented on the 
operating results: 

«In 1H 2026 the output of all key metals decreased, as expected, compared to the same period 
of last year. The decrease in production was driven by the scheduled equipment repairs, as well 
as of high base effect of previous period. 

In  the  2Q  2026  we  continue  to  upgrade  the  mining  equipment  fleet  as  a  part  of  the  import 
substitution  program  and  conduct  annual  schedule  capital  maintenance.  As  example,  after  a 
major overhaul, depletion furnace No. 1 at the Nadezhda Metallurgical Plant was restarted ahead 
of  schedule.  For  the  first  time,  all  key  furnace  components  were  entirely  designed  and 
manufactured  by  the  company’s  engineers,  whereas  previously  they  were  imported.  This 
demonstrates the potential for import substitution and creation of an internal competence center 
capable to replicate this experience across other process stages. 

Another significant event was the commissioning of the Norda tunneling system at the Taimyrsky 
mine at the Norilsk site. The company became a pioneer in the Russian mining industry by using 
a tunneling system for underground polymetallic ore mining. Its technology will enable mining at 
great depths without additional ventilation costs and will also improve mining safety. 

In Kola we begun the active expansion phase of the Severny mine, reaching a new 730-meter 
level  horizon.  The  launch  of  the  first  complex  is  scheduled  for  2028,  which  will  increase  the 
production by more than 1 million tons of ore to 7 million tons by 2030. 

As part of our production chain reconfiguration, the Company plans to consolidate precious metal 
concentrate production at the Kola site. This will increase metal recovery, speed up the production 
process, and reduce costs, as the precious metal concentrate production cycle in Monchegorsk is 
significantly shorter. The project is being implemented using modern solutions and considers all 
potential environmental impacts. We remain committed to high environmental standards across 
all our production assets. 

The  company  continues  to  implement  its  production  efficiency  improvement  program  and 
reiterates its previously announced production guidance for FY2026».

Nickel 

In 2Q 2026, consolidated nickel output increased 4% quarter-on-quarter (q-o-q) to 43 kt. The 
increase was driven by the earlier end of the inter-navigation pause in the second quarter, as 
well as the optimization of repair schedules, which allowed to produce the additional volumes of 
convert matte. 

In 1H 2026,  consolidated nickel output decreased  2%  year-on-year (y-o-y)  to 85 kt, due to 
equipment repairs, including a major overhaul of the depletion furnace No. 1 at the Nadezhda 
Metallurgical Plant (from February to April) and the high base effect in the first half of last year. 

Copper 

In 2Q 2026, consolidated copper output (including Zabaykalsky Division) increased 6% q-o-q to 
104 kt. The increase was attributed to the lower base of 1Q 2026 due to the redistribution of 
production volumes of commercial products between periods. 

In 1H 2026, total copper output decreased 5% y-o-y to 203 kt, due to the need of restocking 
the volumes of technological work-in-progress material at Copper Plant and high base of the same 
period of 2025.  

In 2Q 2026, copper output in concentrate at Zabaykalsky Division decreased 4% q-o-q to 18 
kt due to scheduled maintenance on the autogenous grinding mill. In 1H 2026, production of 
copper  in  concentrate  at  Zabaykalsky  Division  increased  2%  y-o-y  to  36  kt  due  to  increased 
copper recovery due to higher metal content in the ore. 

Platinum Group Metals 

In 2Q 2026, platinum production increased 7% q-o-q to 145 koz, while palladium production 
decreased 3% q-o-q to 590 koz. The multidirectional dynamics was attributed to a change in 
the  platinum-to-palladium  ratio  in  mined  ore  amid  a  shift  in  the  composition  of  processed 
feedstock. 

In 1H 2026,  platinum and palladium  output  amounted to 281 koz  (-16%  y-o-y) and 1,199 
koz (-14% y-o-y), respectively. The decline was due to factors similar to those described above 
for copper, as well as a change in the metal composition of processed feedstock.

ABOUT THE COMPANY  

MMC  Norilsk  Nickel  is  a  diversified  mining  and  metallurgical  company,  the  world’s  largest  producer  of 
palladium  and  a  major  producer  of  nickel,  platinum,  cobalt,  copper  and  rhodium.  The  company  also 
produces silver, gold, iridium, ruthenium, selenium and other products. 

The main production units of Norilsk Nickel Group are located at the Norilsk Industrial District, on the Kola 
Peninsula and Zabaykalsky Krai in Russia. 

MMC Norilsk Nickel shares are listed on the Moscow Stock Exchange.
