Commodities, Currencies & Treasuries
Gold futures eased slightly overnight then extended losses into regular trading. Rising yields and a higher US Dollar pressured futures and a rally never materialized. Concerns over higher oil prices, inflation and rising rates also continue to pressure precious metals as Middle East hostilities persist. December gold settled -$52.50/oz, or -1.29%, at $4,007.90 while December Silver settled -$1.06/oz, or -1.92%, at $53.95.
WTI crude futures gained overnight and extended gains into the regular session as concerns grew over escalation with Iran and throughout the Middle East. Comments from Trump did nothing to de-escalate as he said we could end the war in Iran very quickly and take all of the oil but was non-committal on an actual path forward before noting the US would not attack prior to mid-term elections. Also supporting today’s rise was another tanker strike by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz near Oman. December WTI crude settled +$3.80/bbl, or +3.82%, at $103.27. Brent Crude futures settle at $116.72/bbl, up $3.55, or 3.14% (for the week Brent rose 6.2% and WTI crude 4.3%).
Treasury prices declined this week as a sharp pullback in Tech stocks led to a rally in safe haven assets. The benchmark 10 year yield rose 3.5 bps today to settle at 4.987%, but was down 11 bps on the week, falling for a third straight week (down 29 bps over that stretch). The 2 year yield fell 5.4 bps on the week to 4.68%, down 4 straight weeks into the FOMC meeting next week, and down 18bps over that stretch.
