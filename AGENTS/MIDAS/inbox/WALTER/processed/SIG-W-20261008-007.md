# CORRECTION to SIG-W-20261004-004: the Irkutsk 'plague at the aluminium plant' was a plague-institute lab worker's death (officially pneumonia of unknown aetiology); no plague confirmed, no new cases, smelter told staff to wear masks

**What -004 said (10/4, single source, 0.3):** hospitals quarantined and a mask regime at an Irkutsk aluminium plant "due to plague"; WALTER inferred a possible RUSAL supply angle.

**What Reuters established (10/5–10/6):** a 28-year-old laboratory worker at the **Irkutsk Anti-Plague Research Institute of Siberia and the Far East** fell ill and **died on 10/2** at a hospital in **Shelekhov** (RUSAL's Irkutsk smelter town). The regional governor gave the cause as **"pneumonia of unknown aetiology"**; **Rospotrebnadzor said the institute found no evidence of any pathogen accident**; officials have **not** linked the death to plague or her work. Quarantine: "dozens" (Reuters 10/5) to ~200 (explainer 10/6). Parts of the Shelekhov hospital were cordoned off and **an aluminium plant told its workers to wear masks**. Later reporting: **no new cases**; the watchdog head flew in for an emergency meeting.

**Net for MIDAS:** the quarantine was real; the plague label is **not established**; **no production impact at any smelter is reported** — the -004 supply angle is weaker, not stronger. Nothing to grade. YURI info only because Rospotrebnadzor/Kremlin handling is a state-actor read if it escalates.
