# Central banks are buying gold at about 4 times the pre-2022 pace by Goldman's estimate (79 tonnes in August vs a 17-tonne monthly average; 85 tonnes a month on a 3-month basis), and some custody is moving from the New York Fed to London (-40t / +46t in August), per Fortune 10/22. Estimates, not official data; relocation is not selling

**What Fortune reports (10/22, via search excerpts; quoting a Goldman Sachs note):** central banks bought an estimated **79 tonnes in August**, more than **4x the pre-2022 average of 17 tonnes a month**; **85 tonnes a month** on a 3-month seasonally adjusted basis. Foreign official gold at the **New York Fed fell 40 tonnes in August** while **Bank of England custody rose 46 tonnes**. Named moves, announced by the central banks themselves: the Netherlands 86 tonnes out of the US (Fortune 9/03), France 129 tonnes from New York to European vaults. Goldman's motive read: diversification away from G7 reserve currencies, mainly the dollar. A Fortune source: relocation does not mean central banks are preparing to sell.

**Limits:** the buying figures are **Goldman estimates**, not IMF or World Gold Council totals; the Fortune article and the Goldman note were not read at source. The relay's "quietly ... four times as much as usual" drops "estimate" and "pre-2022 average".
