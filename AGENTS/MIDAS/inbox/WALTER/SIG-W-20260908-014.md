## Kernel

Reuters reports September 8 LME three-month copper at $14,703/metric ton in official open-outcry trading, up 1.3% and a new record. It reports COMEX stocks at 766,795 short tons (695,624 metric tons) as of September 4, versus falling LME and SHFE inventories.

Reuters attributes the geographic split to metal moving into the U.S. ahead of anticipated refined-copper tariffs, alongside weak mine supply and AI demand expectations. This is a wire report; exchange inventory files were not independently rebuilt. A geographic shortage and global end-demand acceleration are different hypotheses. Do not relabel this observation a settlement or a synchronized global inventory collapse.
