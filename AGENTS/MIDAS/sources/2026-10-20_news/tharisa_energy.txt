THARISA SECURES EARLY WHEELED RENEWABLE ENERGY SUPPLY FROM ETANA ENERGY 

Tharisa,  the  mining,  metals  and  innovation  company  dual-listed  on  the  Johannesburg  and  London 
stock exchanges, is pleased to announce that its operating subsidiary, Tharisa Minerals, received its 
first wheeled renewable energy from Etana Energy in September 2026. The initial supply is expected 
to  meet  approximately  10%  of  the  Tharisa  Mine’s  current  monthly  electricity  demand,  marking  a 
material step in the Group’s strategy to reduce energy costs and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions. 

Highlights 

•  First  wheeled renewable energy  received  by  Tharisa Minerals from Etana Energy  in  September 

2026, commencing delivery under the Group’s renewable energy programme 

•  Initial supply from an early wind-energy tranche generated in Mpumalanga is expected to meet 

approximately 10% of the Tharisa Mine’s current monthly electricity demand 

•  Demonstrates  the  successful  use  of  wheeled  renewable  energy  to  decrease  energy  costs  and 

reduce Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions 

•  Under  the  long-term  power  purchase  agreement  (‘PPA’)  signed  in  July  2024,  Etana  Energy  is 
forecast to supply approximately 70% of Tharisa Minerals’ current electricity demand, including 
wheeled solar from Q1 2027 and further wheeled wind later in 2027 
•  Supports the Group’s target of a 30% reduction in emissions by 2030 

Early wheeled renewable energy supply 

Tharisa  has  committed  to  reducing  its  carbon  emissions  by  30%  by  2030.  To  support  this  target, 
management has progressed several decarbonisation projects and opportunities over the past few 
years, including the procurement of wheeled renewable energy for the Tharisa Mine. 

In September 2026, Tharisa Minerals received its first wheeled renewable energy from Etana Energy, 
representing a material step in the Group’s decarbonisation programme. This initial delivery forms 
part of an early energy tranche generated by a wind farm in Mpumalanga and precedes the larger 
renewable energy supply scheduled under the long-term PPA. 

The  initial  supply  is  expected  to  meet  approximately  10%  of  the  Tharisa  Mine’s  current  monthly 
electricity demand. By sourcing renewable electricity through the grid, Tharisa Minerals can reduce its 
reliance on conventional grid electricity, supporting lower energy costs and a reduction in Scope 2 
greenhouse gas emissions.

Long-term power purchase agreement 

Under the long-term PPA signed in July 2024, Etana Energy is forecast to supply approximately 70% of 
Tharisa Minerals’ current electricity demand from its broader generation portfolio. Following the early 
wind-energy tranche received in September 2026, wheeled solar supply is expected to commence in 
Q1 CY2027, with additional wheeled wind energy scheduled for later in 2027. 

Decarbonisation strategy 

The early renewable energy supply converts the Group’s decarbonisation strategy into an operational 
initiative  at  the  Tharisa  Mine.  Together  with  the  larger  volumes  scheduled  under  the  PPA,  it  is 
expected to reduce Scope 2 emissions and support the Group’s target of a 30% reduction in carbon 
emissions by 2030. 

Tharisa underground project 

The PPA was negotiated and signed prior to the commencement of the underground project and the 
70%  coverage  relates  to  the  open  pit  operation.  The  Tharisa  underground  project  is  expected  to 
double the operation’s electricity demand as mining activities extend below surface, which moves a 
lot  of  the  energy  requirement  from  diesel  to  electricity.  Tharisa  is  therefore  preparing  to  procure 
additional renewable energy supply to support the complete mine's future requirements and maintain 
progress towards the Group’s decarbonisation targets, according to Cosmas Chiteme, Group Energy 
Manager at Tharisa.

About Tharisa – funded growth, a second operating asset, commercialising technology 

Tharisa  plc  is  an  integrated  resource  group  producing  platinum  group  metals  (PGMs)  and  chrome 
concentrates, commodities central to the global energy transition and the decarbonisation of industry. 
The  Group  operates  across  the  full  value  chain  –  exploration,  mining,  processing,  beneficiation, 
marketing,  sales,  and  logistics  –  while  pursuing  initiatives  to  reduce  the  emissions  intensity  of  its 
operations. 

The  Tharisa Mine, on  the south-western  limb  of  the  Bushveld  Complex  in  South  Africa,  is  a  multi-
generational operation co-producing PGMs and chrome from a single orebody – a combination that 
underpins  the  Group’s  cost  position  through  the  commodity  cycle.  Downstream  beneficiation, 
including the Vulcan ultra-fine chrome recovery plant and proven chrome and PGM alloy production, 
extends the Group’s reach along the value chain. 

The Karo Platinum Project on Zimbabwe’s Great Dyke is the Group’s second major mining asset and is 
under development. Phase 1 is  designed to produce approximately 226 koz of PGMs a year at full 
capacity, more than doubling Group PGM production, with first ore in mill expected by the end of 
2027. PGM concentrate offtake has been secured with Valterra Platinum for an initial five-year term. 

Through  Redox  One,  Tharisa  is  commercialising  proprietary  iron-chromium  redox  flow  battery 
technology for long-duration energy storage, using the chrome and iron the Group already produces. 

Tharisa targets a 30% reduction in carbon emissions by 2030 and net carbon neutrality by 2050. 
Tharisa plc is incorporated in Cyprus. Its ordinary shares are primary listed on the Main Board of the 
Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE: THA), with listings on the Equity Shares (Commercial Companies) 
category of the London Stock Exchange (LSE: THS) and A2X Markets (A2X: THA). 

About Etana Energy 
Etana Energy is a licenced electricity trader that supplies electricity generated by renewable energy 
projects to businesses in South Africa, using the national electricity grid. Etana’s customers benefit 
from certified low-carbon electricity at tariffs competitive with Eskom or municipal supply, forward 
price certainty, flexible contracting terms, and qualifying B-BEEE expenditure.

Tharisa plc is incorporated in Cyprus, and its ordinary shares are primary listed on the Main Board of 
the JSE. The shares are an approved inward listing, denominated in Rand and settled through Strate. 
In terms of South African Reserve Bank Exchange Control Circular No. 9/2022, approved inward listed 
shares  are  classified  as  domestic  assets  for  exchange  control  purposes.  On  that  basis,  holdings  of 
Tharisa ordinary shares acquired on the JSE are not marked off against the prudential foreign exposure 
limit  applicable  to  South  African  institutional  investors,  and  do  not  consume  the  foreign  asset 
allowance under Regulation 28 of the Pension Funds Act. This treatment differs from that applied to 
inward listed debt instruments, derivatives, and exchange traded funds.
