# Gold and conflict — method fixed before event-return calculations

Will requested historical gold performance during conflict/war, October 25, 2026. This is descriptive research, not a war forecast, new trigger, allocation, trading strategy or causal event study.

1. PRIMARY issuer GLD history from the prior study supplies daily nominal USD fund closes since November 2004, plus its labelled October 23, 2026 MIRROR extension. Reuse the hashed unchanged input. Expenses embedded, no inflation adjustment; not spot bullion or certified futures settlements.
2. Fixed modern event list, selected for identifiable start/escalation dates before measuring returns: Russia–Georgia August 8, 2008; Crimea armed seizure February 27, 2014; full-scale Russian invasion of Ukraine February 24, 2022; Hamas attacks/Israel–Hamas conflict October 7, 2023; Iran's direct attack on Israel April 13, 2024; Israel–Iran war June 13, 2025; US/Israeli attacks on Iran February 28, 2026. Verify dates with primary chronology sources; record any unresolved date and withhold that row rather than refit. Some are escalations in longer conflicts; this is not a census of all wars or independent random trials.
3. Baseline = last observed GLD close strictly before event calendar date. First response = first observed close on/after event date versus baseline. Then show 5/21/63/126/252 event-session closes versus the same baseline, with exact dates. For weekends, first market session follows the event. Pre-event 21-session return ends at baseline. First-response means close-to-close, not intraday spike. Any after-US-close date ambiguity is recorded; no choice made to improve a result.
4. Report missing future endpoints as right-censored. Preserve all selected rows regardless of signs. Show paths normalized to the pre-event close through 126 event sessions and the 21-session lead-in. Peak-selection and future-trough timing cannot establish a trading edge. Later prices reflect all concurrent shocks, not exclusively war.
5. Older conflicts use PRIMARY World Bank Pink Sheet monthly gold averages, if retrieved: October 1973 Arab–Israeli war; December 1979 Soviet invasion of Afghanistan; August 1990 Iraq invasion of Kuwait; January 1991 coalition Gulf War campaign; March 2003 Iraq invasion. Baseline = preceding calendar month's average; report event month and +3/+6/+12 calendar months' averages versus baseline. Monthly averages cannot identify the outbreak-day response and are never spliced into the daily GLD event table or pooled with it. December 1979 began late in month; January 1991 is a second stage of the same conflict, not an independent war.
6. Keep the older monthly series' full named gold definition/units and vintage. Exclude pre-1971 fixed/exchange-controlled USD gold from an apples-to-apples modern-war return claim. No claim that WWI/WWII USD gold behaved like today's freely traded price.
7. External primary research: WGC March 2, 2026 geopolitical-risk spike analysis (14 weeks, 1985–February 2026), BIS October 2026 working paper on financial conditions and geopolitical gold response, and contemporary LBMA analysis of the 1991/2003 Gulf wars. Record their samples/definitions and disagreements; do not turn a small-sample frequency into odds for the next war. Author research is not an institutional policy view; the industry council is not a neutral regulator.
8. Save chronology/source URLs, raw public captures, source hashes, exact endpoint tables, calculations and charts. Separate initial fear bid, anticipated/built-in risk, war-stage resolution and the subsequent monetary/liquidity environment. Claim directions only as interpretations; war alone is not measured as the cause of subsequent multi-month returns.
