### 3b. 🔴 The one finding I did not expect: **the denominator moved with the numerator, and it damped the metric.**

In **contracts**, this week is not marginal at all. Net NC long fell **15,210 contracts (−6.25%)**, and NC long fell **16,674 (−6.02%)** — a *substantial* long liquidation, close in percentage terms to the price move itself. But **open interest fell too (−12,761, −2.98%)**, so the **ratio** moved only −1.92pp. **A metric that divides by OI understates a liquidation in which OI is itself liquidating** — precisely this week's shape.

⛔ **This does NOT change the grade and must not be read as one.** MIDAS-08 registered **net/OI** and grades **net/OI**; re-grading on absolute contracts after seeing the print is choosing the metric at 15:31, which is the failure this desk pre-registers computations to prevent. **The alternative metric is recorded as a limit of the chosen one, not as a second verdict.**
⇒ **Prospective, for the successor row only (never retrofitted here):** a positioning test in a shock week should register **both** the ratio and the absolute net, with the ratio binding — otherwise a co-moving denominator can convert a real unwind into an "indeterminate."
