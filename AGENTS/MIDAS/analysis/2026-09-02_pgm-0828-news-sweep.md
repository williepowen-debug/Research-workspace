The question this sweep answers is **NOT** "did palladium move" — it is **"was there a dated, public, PGM-specific event on or immediately before 2026-08-28 capable of explaining a Pd-led move of that size?"**

**A HIT is any ONE of:**
1. **Supply** — SA or Russian PGM mine outage, strike, load-shedding/power curtailment, smelter failure, force majeure, or export restriction, dated 2026-08-25 → 2026-08-28.
2. **Sanction / policy** — new or threatened sanctions, tariffs, or export controls naming Russian PGMs or Nornickel, same window.
3. **Demand** — a dated auto/catalytic-converter or industrial demand shock (major OEM production change, emissions-rule change, substitution announcement).
4. **Market structure** — a reported squeeze, lease-rate spike, exchange-stock drawdown, large ETF creation/redemption, or a named large position, **specifically reported** in that window.
5. **Corporate** — a producer's guidance cut/suspension (Sibanye, Impala, Amplats, Northam, Nornickel, Valterra) dated in the window.

**A NON-HIT (explicitly, so I cannot count these as news):**
- Commentary *about* the price move itself with no named cause ("palladium surged on supply concerns") — that is the move restated, not a cause.
- Undated or vintage-ambiguous articles.
- Generic macro (dollar, rates, risk sentiment) — those are gold-channel, and the residual is already **vs gold**, so a common macro root is regressed out by construction.
- WPIC/Johnson Matthey structural deficit material with no 8/25–8/28 date.

**Pre-declared expectation:** I expect **NO HIT**, because the desk searched once on 8/28 and found none. ⚠️ **That expectation is exactly why this must be multi-query and why the non-hit list is frozen above** — a confirmatory sweep that finds nothing is worthless if it never could have found anything.
