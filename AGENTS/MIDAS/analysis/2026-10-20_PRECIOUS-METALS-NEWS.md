## Silver shortage claims require a date and an available-metal measure

**PRIMARY LBMA latest vault page:** September silver28985t,+1.95%MoM; recheck confirms the existing level rather than a new October inventory print. Includes allocated metal and does not measure metal immediately available to lend or sell. LBMA's October14 Q3 review describes solar manufacturers substituting copper for silver; that is a retrospective development, not a newly quantified October demand shock. April annual forecasts remain forecasts. **Inference:** structural deficit, replenished total stocks and local tightness can coexist; none alone establishes a current squeeze. [LBMA vaults](https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/london-vault-data), [October14 review](https://www.lbma.org.uk/articles/lbma-precious-metals-market-report-q3-2026)

The October19–20 claim that silver is gaining central-bank reserve status was encountered in secondary/social coverage. No primary, dated silver-specific central-bank purchase or quantity was established. A multi-metal government stockpile budget is not a central-bank silver reserve allocation. Do not use this as demand evidence. Likewise, the April2026 survey's October squeeze narrative refers to2025, not October2026. London leases/freefloat and Indian silver import quantities remain unresolved.
