## Context

Our latent vulnerability research documented conversion rates for individual triggers (job loss, medical event, auto accident, disaster). However, triggers may not occur in isolation. Job loss can lead to loss of health insurance, which increases medical event probability. Stress from financial hardship can cause health problems. Disasters can cause job loss.

If triggers cluster or cascade, the compound conversion rate may be significantly higher than individual rates suggest — potentially non-linear (multiplicative rather than additive).

---

## Prompt

I'm researching how financial trigger events interact, cluster, and compound. Specifically, I need to understand whether experiencing one trigger increases the probability of experiencing others, and what happens to conversion rates when multiple triggers hit.
