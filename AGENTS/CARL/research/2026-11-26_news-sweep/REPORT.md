**Judgment: the split held and sharpened.** Prime card credit kept improving year on year while stress concentrated in lower-tier borrowers: more people behind on smaller balances, personal-loan delinquency at a Q3 high since 2010, households' own expected chance of missing a payment up, subprime auto losses rising without a delinquency rise, and a deep-subprime lender in Chapter 11. Spending is the counterweight: value-store traffic held, lower-income card spending grew fastest at BofA, and Walmart's share gains were led by upper-income households. This is the concentrated-stress picture of the 10/12 scope review, not broad collapse.
