**Prepared:** 2026-10-14 (late ET), read-only research helper for CARL. Covers the issuer universe and dated events only. It does not run the test.

**Evidence labels**
- **OPENED**: I fetched and read the page or document myself.
- **SNIPPET**: the figure comes from a search-result summary that quotes or paraphrases the named source. I did not open that page. Most rows are SNIPPET because of the call budget.
- **SECONDARY**: a non-agency, non-company aggregator such as investmentgrade.com, TradingView, bondblox or kavout. Low reliability.
- **STALE**: the latest action found is more than about 12 months old.
- **NOT FOUND**: no current rating was found in this pass.

> **Caveat on index buckets.** ICE BofA quality buckets use **bond-level** ratings, not issuer ratings (assumed from general knowledge; not checked against ICE methodology this session). Several names below have bonds rated below the issuer. Examples: Hertz unsecured notes at CCC under a B- issuer, Michaels second-lien notes at CCC+ under a B issuer, and NCLH unsecured notes at Moody's B3 under a B1 issuer. Such a name can feed the CCC bucket while its issuer rating reads "B". For each name I give the bond-level rating where I found one.
