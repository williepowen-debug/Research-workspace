Credit Card Delinquencies
Credit card delinquencies increased modestly in September, but remained in line with typical
seasonal patterns and historical trends. The delinquency rate rose to 2.63%, up 9 basis points
(3.5%) year over year. Delinquency rates remained higher among younger generations, led by
Gen Z at 4.4% and Millennials at 3.4%, compared with 2.4% for Gen X and 1.8% for Boomers.
