- [2026-08-15] **A REVISION CHECK THAT READS ONLY THE NEW VINTAGE IS CIRCULAR BY CONSTRUCTION — it cannot detect a revision and it returns a confident tick.** My frozen HHDC card's guard #4 said *"If Q1 is revised, grade against the revised Q1, and say so."* I graded it **✓ Q1 unrevised** — and Q1 **had** been revised (Q2 report's own footnote; CC balances $1.2520T → $1.2420T, −$10B). The mechanism: every figure in my supporting reconcile list was read **out of the Q2 report** and checked against a baseline **itself sourced from the Q2 report**. Same-source comparison can only ever agree. **Detecting a revision requires opening the OLD report** — which took one curl. Two second-order lessons that are the actually-useful part: **(1)** the grade survived anyway because the SHARE and the FLOW were unrevised and I had pulled both quarters from one vintage — so the *substantive* half of the guard was honoured and only the *reporting* half ("and say so") failed, which is a reminder that a wrong guard verdict can sit on top of correct work and still do damage downstream; **(2)** the load-bearing figure *"delinquent dollars ROSE $0.23B"* is **derived** (share × balance) and therefore **vintage-dependent — same-vintage +$0.23B ROSE, cross-vintage −$1.08B FELL, the sign reverses.** **Any derived figure spanning a revision boundary must carry its basis or it is not a number.** Pairs with `[[finding_derived_metric_across_vintages_biases_toward_stale_leg]]` and `[[finding_diff_the_vintages_not_just_refresh]]`. Promotion candidate — this is not CARL-specific.

Neither asked whether the THRESHOLD discriminates.** A throwaway line in DEWEY's C3 — *"flat at 6.93-7.18% for eight quarters"* — sent me to base-rate it: **two consecutive QoQ declines fires in 43.5% of 94 quarters and is not silent through the GFC build.** I had registered a *full-thesis kill* on a coin flip. The median absolute QoQ move (20bp) is nearly the width of the whole band the series has occupied for two years. **Rule: "does the instrument exist / is it clean" and "does the threshold separate" are two independent audits, and passing the first feels like passing both.** Also: the fix (a ≥100bp materiality floor — 5.4%, GFC-silent, fires once in 23 years on the genuine healing episode) **reset the count in my favour, 1-of-2 → 0-of-2** — so I routed it to Will as an explicit A/B/C choice and pre-committed to accepting the ratchet-proof option without argument. **When the honest fix happens to benefit you, the sequence (measure first, compute the consequence after) is worth stating — but it is a claim about your process and must not be the only thing holding the rule up.** Direct application of `[[finding_base_rate_the_threshold_before_building_it]]`, which I had already read at boot.
