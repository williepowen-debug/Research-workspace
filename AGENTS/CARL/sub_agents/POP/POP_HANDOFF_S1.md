## KEY INSIGHT

**The Optimism-Operations Divergence:** Current data shows a textbook early-stress pattern. Small business owners report high optimism (NFIB 99.5) while operational metrics deteriorate (revenue declines, closures, bankruptcies). This is NOT contradictory—it's the Owner Squeeze Cascade in early phase:

1. Revenue stress appears first
2. Owners absorb stress personally (cut own pay, use savings)
3. Owners maintain optimistic outlook (denial, hope for recovery)
4. Only after prolonged stress does sentiment deteriorate
5. Employment cuts follow sentiment shift by 3-6 months

**Implication for CARL:** The consumer stress from small business transmission is ALREADY occurring through owner personal finances—it just won't show in headline data until Q2-Q3 2026 when employment effects materialize.
