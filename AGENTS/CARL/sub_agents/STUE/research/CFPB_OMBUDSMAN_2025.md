## CRITICAL NOTE: TWO SEPARATE REPORTS

The CFPB maintains two distinct annual student loan ombudsman functions. Both released reports for 2024-2025:

| Report | Coverage Period | Published | Focus |
|--------|----------------|-----------|-------|
| Annual Report of the CFPB **Student Loan Ombudsman** (2024) | AY 2023-2024 (Jul 1, 2023 – Jun 30, 2024) | November 2024 | Federal student loans — primary servicer analysis |
| Annual Report of the CFPB **Private Education Loan Ombudsman** (2025) | AY 2024-2025 (Jul 1, 2024 – Jun 30, 2025) | January 2026 | Private + federal loans, fraud/scam focus |

**The 2024 Student Loan Ombudsman report (Nov 2024) is the primary source for federal servicer data including MOHELA.**
**The 2025 Private Education Loan Ombudsman report (Jan 2026) is the most recent overall report but focuses on private loans and provides aggregate federal data only — no servicer-level breakdown published.**

| Category | Relief Rate |
|----------|------------|
| Private student loan complaints warranting relief | **3.1%** |
| Federal student loan complaints warranting relief | **0.8%** |

**Context:** Both rates are extremely low. 99.2% of federal student loan complaints receive no monetary or non-monetary relief.

### Top Complaint Categories — Private Loans

Per AY 2024-2025, consumers may select from six categories:
1. Dealing with your lender or servicer (dominant category)
2. Struggling to repay your loan
3. Getting a loan
4. Problem with your credit report or score
5. Issue with income share agreement
6. Issue where my lender is my school

Specific percentage breakdown by category not published in this report (figures are in chart form, not text-extractable).

### Notable Findings
- Frauds and scams rising; report attributes this partly to servicer unresponsiveness creating information vacuums exploited by bad actors
- 46 million consumers have student loans; $1.85 trillion outstanding ($1.67T federal, $181B private)
- 4.7 million borrowers in default no longer appearing on credit records (loans >7 years in default)
- No Ombudsman in office for a portion of 2025 — new Ombudsman Geof Gradler designated January 6, 2026
- CFPB leadership issued directive to "deprioritize" student loan enforcement under Trump administration (active as of May 2025)

### MOHELA-Specific Data in This Report
**None.** The 2025 PELO report does not break out servicer-level complaint data. It is aggregate-only. The report acknowledges the institutional limitation that CFPB complaint data is not a statistical sample and cannot be used to make prevalence claims about the full market.

### MOHELA Data — AY 2023-2024 (Report's Primary Dataset)

**Complaint ranking:** MOHELA received the most federal student loan complaints of any servicer in both the CFPB and FSA Ombudsman reports for AY 2023-2024.

**Estimated MOHELA complaint count (FY2024):** ~5,701 [Source: third-party analysis of CFPB complaint database, corroborated by multiple secondary sources]

**Servicer complaint rankings (FY2024):**

| Servicer | Estimated Complaints | Notes |
|---------|---------------------|-------|
| MOHELA | ~5,701 | #1 — highest of any servicer |
| Nelnet | ~3,034 | #2 |
| Navient | ~2,478 | #3 (banned from federal servicing Sep 2024) |
| Aidvantage (Maximus) | ~1,629 | #4 |
| EdFinancial | ~1,602 | #5 |

**Complaint-to-market-share ratio (from LendEDU 2026 analysis of CFPB data):**

| Servicer | Share of Accounts | Share of Complaints | Ratio |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|-------|
| MOHELA | ~20% | ~41% | **>2 complaints per borrower serviced** |
| EdFinancial | ~18% | ~13% | Near 1:1 |
| Nelnet | ~38% | ~22% | ~0.5:1 |
| Aidvantage | ~25% | ~12% | <0.5:1 (best) |

**Bottom line:** MOHELA generates ~2x its proportional share of complaints. Its complaint share (41%) is more than double its account share (20%).

### MOHELA-Specific Failures Documented in 2024 Report

The report contains direct narrative examples naming MOHELA (from the 73-complaint borrower narrative sample):

**PSLF Payment Count Error (Oct 2023):**
- Borrower paid before due date, payment marked past due
- Called MOHELA 3 times over 3 days in October 2023
- Hold time: "more than two hours each time" — could not reach anyone
- MOHELA corrected error and adjusted payment count **4 months later in February 2024**
- Delay: ~4 months to resolve a payment processing error for a PSLF borrower

**Broader MOHELA Performance Failures (AY 2023-2024):**
- Failed to send billing statements to **2.5 million borrowers** in October 2023
- Result: **800,000 delinquent borrowers** due to missing/late bills
- DOE finding: "MOHELA failed to meet its basic obligation" (official DOE language)
- DOE financial penalty: MOHELA fined $7.2 million (2023)
- **>40% of MOHELA borrowers** experienced a servicing failure since loan payments resumed September 2023
- "Call deflection" scheme documented: company funneled borrowers to bad information to avoid resolving errors (per AFT lawsuit, July 2024)

### Top Complaint Issue Categories (AY 2023-2024, federal loans)

Based on the 4,278-complaint manual sample:

1. **Payment processing errors** — Payments marked as past due incorrectly; auto-pay problems; duplicate debits; payments not applied to accounts
2. **Incorrect repayment information** — Inaccurate billing statements; wrong guidance on IDR plans; wrong recertification dates; incomplete payment histories
3. **Customer service doom loops** — Unable to reach servicer; transferred between agents and webpages without resolution; could not enroll in IDR plans
4. **IDR/SAVE processing delays** — IDR backlog of 1.25 million pending applications by end of October 2023
5. **Refund delays** — CARES Act refunds and overpayment refunds taking months
6. **PSLF processing errors** — Lost account histories, miscalculated payment counts

**Quantified harm (73-complaint sample only):**
- 38 borrowers reported **>$500,000 in unauthorized withdrawals, overpayments, and related harms**
- 48 borrowers reported processing delays totaling **401 months (33 years) of collective delay**

## OVERSIGHT ACTIONS AND REGULATORY STATUS (2025)

### Active Investigations as of April 2025

| Entity | Action | Date | Status |
|--------|--------|------|--------|
| 9 state attorneys general + state regulators | Investigations into MOHELA | Active as of April 28, 2025 | At risk due to Trump admin interference |
| AFT | Consumer protection lawsuit (DC Superior Court) | Filed July 2024 | Ongoing |
| OIG (ED + Federal Reserve) | Petitioned by AFT/SBPC to probe ED and CFPB oversight failures | May 7, 2025 | Pending |
| Senate investigation | MOHELA credit reporting errors (Warren et al.) | December 2024 | ~2 million duplicate credit reporting errors identified |
| Congress members (50+) | Called for MOHELA contract termination | September 2024 | No action taken |

### CFPB Oversight Rollback (2025)

- CFPB Student Loan Ombudsman Julia Barnard terminated by Trump administration — February 2025
- Court reinstated Barnard following federal judge order
- CFPB leadership issued directive to **"deprioritize"** student loan enforcement and supervisory activities
- Directive confirmed active as of May 2025
- FSA Vendor Oversight Division and Vendor Performance Division eliminated through mass layoffs — April 2025
- Result: Student loan servicers including MOHELA now face reduced federal oversight

### FSA Account Transfer (2025)

- DOE announced plan to transfer "unspecified number" of MOHELA-serviced accounts to other contractors by end of 2025
- Also: MOHELA assumed servicing of all former Navient accounts (federal + private) in early 2025
