## WHAT FULL SETTLEMENT RELIEF MEANS (per settlement terms)

For each non-Exhibit C post-class applicant who did not receive a decision by April 15:
1. **Complete loan discharge** — outstanding federal student loan balance from the school in question goes to zero
2. **Payment refunds** — every dollar paid toward those loans gets refunded (may arrive in multiple Treasury payments)
3. **Credit report deletion** — the credit tradeline associated with those loans is removed from all credit reports
4. **Federal aid eligibility restored**

## CARL THESIS IMPLICATION

**NOT a thesis invalidation.** CARL's partial invalidation criterion is "Government intervention — student loan forgiveness, mortgage forbearance 2.0, stimulus, UI extension." This discharge is:
- Court-compelled (not policy-driven forgiveness)
- Bounded (a defined post-class cohort, ~170K borrowers)
- The result of DOE inaction/non-compliance, not a relief policy shift
- Narrow relative to 9.2M in default and 7.5M in SAVE transition

The discharge is relief for ~170K borrowers out of 40.9M total ($1.7T portfolio). The structural stress thesis for the remaining cohort is unchanged. This is a government compliance failure, not a government intervention that addresses the underlying stress.

**However:** Removal of ~$X billion in loan tradelines from credit reports for ~170K borrowers will modestly improve FICO scores for this cohort — a localized positive effect that does NOT offset the -62pt average loss across 7M+ other borrowers.
