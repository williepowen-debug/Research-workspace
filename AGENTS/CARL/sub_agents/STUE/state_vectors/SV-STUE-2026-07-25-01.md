### Findings

**1. Default wall re-anchored to primary — and the stock/flow question is CLOSED.**
FSA EA **GENERAL-26-38** (posted **Jun 23 2026**), data **as of Mar 31 2026**: **~9M borrowers / $220B in default = >13%** of the **$1.64T** federally-managed portfolio; **+1.3M borrowers QoQ**. Forbearance **8.4M / $485B**. In repayment-or-delinquency **17.2M (42%) / $633B**.

The long-open **stock-vs-flow** question resolves cleanly: Dec 2025 **7.7M/$180B** → Mar 2026 **~9.0M/$220B** = **+1.3M net**, while NY Fed reports **2.6M gross Q1 DRG transfers**. The two series never conflicted — one is gross inflow, the other net stock. The residual is the finding: **~1.3M borrowers exited default in the quarter** (rehab / consolidation / discharge / cure).

> **⚠️ This cuts against our own projection.** A live ~1.3M/qtr cure channel was unmodelled. At **+1.3M/qtr** (not the +1.5M we assumed) the EOY-2026 run-rate is **~11.6M**, so the **13M projection now requires acceleration and is no longer "conservative."** Recommend CARL stop citing 13M as a floor.
> **Also:** parent's "**9.2M / $180B Mar 2026**" conflated a March borrower count with a **December** dollar figure. And press dated **Jul 21** (Fox) cites **9.5M/$233.3B** attributed to FSA — **above** the Mar-31 primary, unreconciled. **Primary wins; do not cite 9.5M.**

**2. AFT v. MOHELA is STAYED — CRL-14's litigation leg cannot fire in its own window. [decision-relevant]**
The case was **removed to federal court**: **D.D.C. 1:24-cv-02460**, Judge **Tanya Chutkan** (from D.C. Superior 2024-CAB-004575). A **Minute Order of 10/27/2025 stayed both defendant's response deadline and discovery**, pending SCOTUS in *NJ Transit v. Colt* (24-1113) + *Galette v. NJ Transit* (24-1021).

**SCOTUS decided those on Mar 4 2026 — unanimous (Sotomayor): NJ Transit is NOT an arm of the state**, with the test now turning on formal structure and fiscal independence rather than public mission or state control. That is **directionally adverse to MOHELA's "creature of Missouri" defense.**

Yet the docket shows: Mar 16 status report (#51) → **Mar 20 2026 Order Staying Case** → Mar 24 deadlines → May 18 status report (#52) → May 19 order (#53) → **Jul 17 2026 status report (#54, most recent)**. **No class-certification motion or ruling appears anywhere on the docket.**

> **Implication:** CRL-14's Q3-Q4 2026 window **cannot** produce a class-cert or damages event. Combined with involuntary collections still paused indefinitely, **both the litigation and garnishment legs are gated** — CRL-14 now rests on **operational failure alone**. That is a narrower base than 65% was priced against. *Recommend CARL either re-state CRL-14 explicitly as an operational-failure-only claim, or re-mark it.*
