# POLLY REPORT — UNH Q1 2026 + ELV Q1 2026 Earnings Watch
**Prepared:** 2026-04-29 | **For:** CARL | **Task:** Earnings Watch — Health Insurer MLR + MA Cost Trend

---

## VERDICT

No MLR breach at either carrier — both UNH and ELV printed better than feared. UNH consolidated MCR came in at **83.9%** (Q1 2026), well below the 89% thesis threshold and a 90 basis point improvement from Q1 2025 (84.8%). The UnitedHealthcare segment MCR is not separately disclosed. The improvement reflects deliberate membership shedding (-965K MA members in Q1), repricing, and favorable reserve development — not underlying utilization relief. ELV printed **86.8%** BCR (+40 bps YoY from 86.4% Q1 2025; sequential seasonal improvement from 93.5% Q4 2025). ELV also took a **$935M CMS risk-adjustment accrual** (one-time, historical dispute). Neither carrier breached a crisis threshold. The MA cost-trend re-acceleration thesis is **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**: both carriers price 2026 MA around ~10% cost trend (vs. historical 3-5%), V28 RAF recalibration is an unresolved forward risk, and Medicaid stress persists at ELV with elevated utilization. CARL's Vector #12 (stagflation / healthcare burden) is not weakened — these beats reflect insurer discipline in shedding risk, not consumer relief.

## CARL IMPLICATION

- **Vector #12 (Stagflation / Healthcare Cost Burden) — NOT WEAKENED.** Both carriers beat via shedding unprofitable MA members and repricing, not because utilization softened. MA cost trend ~10% is embedded in 2026 pricing. Managed stress, not cleared stress.

- **MA cost-trend re-acceleration thesis — PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** Both carriers confirm ~10% structural MA cost trend (vs. historical 3-5%). V28 RAF recalibration is unresolved forward risk — if cuts land harder than priced, Q3-Q4 MLR re-accelerates. UNH management declined to quantify.

- **ELV $935M CMS accrual = systemic signal.** Ongoing regulatory scrutiny of MA risk-adjustment mechanics. If CMS tightens industry-wide, 2027+ MA margins compress, forcing premium increases or benefit cuts onto consumers.

- **Medicaid stress at ELV persists — OBBBA runway matters.** Medicaid running at elevated trend with -1.75% operating margin pre-OBBBA. December 2026 redetermination wave will accelerate Medicaid MCO revenue loss while adverse selection concentrates sicker members in the pool. Watch ELV Q2-Q3.

- **ELV ACA bronze plan shift = CARL high-deductible trap in motion.** "Pronounced" member migration to lowest-premium, highest-deductible bronze plans. These consumers are technically insured but functionally exposed to full OOP — medical debt amplifier remains armed.
