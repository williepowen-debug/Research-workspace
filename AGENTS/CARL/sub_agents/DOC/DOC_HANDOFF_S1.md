## S — SYNTHESIS

### Key Mental Model

**Core Thesis:** Healthcare costs are uniquely threatening because they are:
1. **UNAVOIDABLE** — Medical needs must eventually be addressed
2. **UNPREDICTABLE** — A diagnosis can create $100K debt overnight
3. **CATASTROPHIC** — Single events can destroy financial stability

**What Matters Most:**
The Healthcare Cost Crisis thesis is VALIDATED. The data tells a consistent story:
- 36% defer care → 18% health worsens → acute events → medical debt
- 23% underinsured → even with insurance, one event can devastate
- $88B medical debt in collections → 20% of Americans affected
- 20% medication non-adherence → 100K preventable deaths annually

The transmission mechanisms (FLOW-DOC-02, 03, 04) are now ACTIVATING.

**New Finding — Record Anxiety:**
47% of Americans are worried about affording healthcare in 2026 — the highest level since tracking began in 2021. This is a leading indicator of behavioral changes (more deferral, more non-adherence) and suggests the stress is not yet priced into current data.

**Biggest Uncertainty:**
1. Will ACA subsidies be extended? Expiration would spike premiums and coverage losses.
2. Is medical CPI moderation sustainable, or will employer-reported 9% increases flow through?
3. Will CFPB medical debt protections be restored legislatively?

**Transmission Channels to CARL:**
- **DIRECT (immediate):** Healthcare spending crowds out discretionary spending
- **MEDIUM-TERM (6-18mo):** Medical debt accumulates → credit damage
- **LATENT (event-triggered):** Deferred care → acute event → catastrophic cost
