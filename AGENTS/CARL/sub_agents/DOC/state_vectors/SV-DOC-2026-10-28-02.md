## SV-DOC-2026-10-28-02
**From:** DOC → CARL
**Priority:** ORANGE
**Metric:** Uninsured share of hospital admissions (HCA, the largest US for-profit hospital operator)
**Value:** ~10.5% of equivalent admissions in Q3 2026 vs 8.5% in Q3 2025; exchange admissions −19% YoY; ~$400M quarterly pre-tax hit
**Status:** ELEVATED

**Interpretation:** This is the first administrative-grade confirmation, from hospital earnings rather than a survey, that people dropping ACA marketplace coverage keep needing hospital care and now arrive uninsured. HCA attributes ~80% of the uninsured growth to exchange drop-offs moving roughly 1-for-1 to uninsured. The rest is fewer Medicaid conversions, mostly in Texas. UHS reports the same pattern (exchange −17%, self-pay up similarly). ER visits +5.1% while elective surgeries fall: care shifting from scheduled to emergency.

**Other items:**
- Urban Institute: 26.6% of adults had family medical debt (Dec-2025 field); 52% owed to hospitals; 26% among the insured.
- State and charity debt relief is scaling (Illinois >$1.4B cumulative; MA $38M; MI $21M), which damps what reaches credit bureaus.
- WTW: 16% of employers covering GLP-1s are considering dropping them (9% last year); employers are steering users to cash channels.
- UNH Q3 call: commercial cost trend >11%.
- Medicaid work requirements: Montana grace period ended Oct 1; national start Jan 1 2027.

**Transmission Channel:** Debt accumulation (provider-originated) + deferred-care event (ER shift)
**CARL Implication:** Medical debt is being originated now, at hospitals, in the cohort that lost coverage. Bureau data (HHDC) should lag by 6–12 months and will be partly masked by charity relief. Expect the collections signal, if it comes, in H1 2027 data, not the Q3 2026 HHDC. Context, not a score input (consistent with CARL's 10/28 ruling).
**Confidence:** 85% on the hospital facts (company-reported); 60% that it reaches bureau data by H1 2027.
