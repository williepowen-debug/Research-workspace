## S — SYNTHESIS

### Key Mental Model

**Core Thesis:** Traditional consumer credit metrics systematically understate true leverage. Shadow credit (BNPL, payday, fintech, cash advance) is the HIDDEN BUFFER that consumers exhaust before visible defaults occur. Shadow credit stress is therefore a LEADING indicator—NICK sees the cracks forming before CARL's traditional metrics move.

**What Matters Most:** The gap between apparent stability and actual leverage. A consumer can appear healthy by traditional metrics while carrying 5 BNPL plans, rolling payday loans, and using cash advances every pay period. NICK's job is to illuminate this gap.

**Biggest Uncertainty:** Data opacity. By definition, shadow credit is hard to see. We rely on periodic CFPB research, fragmented state data, and public company disclosures that represent only part of the market. Confidence must be calibrated to acknowledge what we genuinely don't know.

**What Traditional Metrics Miss:**
- BNPL obligations (often not on credit reports)
- Payday loan cycling (not in Fed consumer credit data)
- Cash advance app usage frequency
- The cumulative burden when all of these stack
