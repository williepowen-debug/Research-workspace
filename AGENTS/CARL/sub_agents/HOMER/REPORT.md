# HOMER EARNINGS WATCH — DHI Q2 FY2026 + PHM Q1 2026
**Date:** 2026-04-29 | **Analyst:** HOMER sub-agent

---

## VERDICT

DHI Q2 gross margin BEAT the 19.0-19.5% guide, printing 20.1% reported (19.7% normalized for a 40bps litigation/warranty benefit) — above-guide on volume gains and cost control, NOT price recovery. Orders surged +11% YoY to 24,992 units but ASP fell -3% to $361,600 and incentives held at ~10% of revenue, confirming demand is rate-sensitive and fragile, not organically recovering. PHM Q1 CONTRADICTS a simple containment thesis but does NOT invalidate K-shape: margins compressed 310bps to 24.4% (incentives +290bps to 10.9%), cancellations rose to 13% (from 11%), and management explicitly named the K-shape — "lower- and middle-income families struggling much more." Move-up and active-adult showed relative order strength (+3% and +14% YoY respectively); first-time flat. Tariff pass-through: both builders deflected direct tariff cost quantification. DHI management said material cost announcements "don't always stick" and price impacts would hit FY2027, not 2026 — the $10,900/home NAHB tariff estimate is NOT yet fully in earnings. Housing demand is bifurcated and rate-hostage, not recovering.

## CARL IMPLICATIONS

- **K-shape thesis REINFORCED, not broken.** PHM management explicitly: "the K-shaped economy — lower- and middle-income families struggling much more than those in upper incomes." First-time buyer orders flat; active adult +14%. DHI 65% first-time buyer share (their volume segment) at ASP $361,600 = rate-hostage entry-level demand. Both builders are keeping volume alive through incentives (~10% of revenue/price), not organic demand recovery.

- **Tariff cost NOT yet in margins — $10,900/home headwind is forward, not current.** DHI explicitly: material cost increases "don't always stick" and would flow into FY2027 closings. NAHB's $10,900/home (60% of builders, +6.3% avg cost) is a Q3-Q4 2026 margin torpedo, not reflected in Q2 actuals. DHI Q3 guidance 19.7-20.2% implies management expects to absorb; if tariffs accelerate, guide breaks.

- **CARL Vector #10 (foreclosure acceleration via new-home channel) receives INDIRECT support.** Existing inventory at 4.1mo + new home inventory 9.7mo + falling ASPs (-3% DHI, -5% PHM) creates price competition that squeezes existing sellers into distress. DHI margin beat signals builders trading price/margin for volume to clear spec inventory — not a healthy demand environment.

- **Cancellation rates rising, not falling.** PHM: 13% (from 11%); DHI: 16% (stable but elevated). DHI specifically: "vast majority of cancellations are buyers who cannot qualify for mortgage once they get into full documentation." Credit access is the binding constraint — direct K-shape signal flowing into the housing channel.

- **Demand fragility confirmed for Q2 entry:** NAHB HMI 34 + buyer traffic 22 + spring season not materializing + incentives locked at 10%+ of price + rates 6.30%+ = no organic recovery. PHM Q2 margin guide 24.1-24.4% = sequential compression continuing. Tariff cost shock ($10,900/home) materializing Q3+ hits builders who have already exhausted pricing power. Pass-through to consumer: deflected for now, unavoidable by Q3-Q4.
