## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR CARL

1. **CMBS MF DQ new ATH 7.15%** — This is the most actionable new data point. The multifamily CMBS market just broke through its October high. Combined with Fannie MF at 0.74% (6bps from GFC), the maturity wall is starting to produce casualties.

2. **New home sales collapse (587K, 9.7mo supply)** — Builder demand destruction is accelerating. This is the worst reading since 2022. Combined with NAHB HMI at 38 and 64% offering incentives, builders are under severe pressure.

3. **FL condo inventory 13.2 months** — This is a massive upgrade from the 8.8mo previously tracked. FL condo market is in outright collapse with -6.1% YoY prices and 92% of markets declining. The triple squeeze thesis is validated and worsening.

4. **Case-Shiller deceleration to +0.9% YoY** — National price growth approaching zero. Tampa already negative (-2.5%). West South Central (FHFA) already negative (-0.8%). Price support is eroding.

5. **MBA refi collapse (-40% from 1mo ago)** — Rate sensitivity is extreme. The 6.57% MBA contract rate (highest since Aug) is killing refinancing activity. This feeds back into the cure rate problem — homeowners in trouble can't refi out.

6. **Foreclosure YoY trend now at 12 consecutive months** — The normalization thesis is getting stretched. REO completions +35% YoY means actual home seizures are accelerating, not just filings.
