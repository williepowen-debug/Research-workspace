## CALIBRATION (Spawn 5 → Spawn 6 postmortem)

**Error:** Spawn 5 cited "Trepp CMBS MF May 2026 6.57% (-46bps from Apr 7.15% ATH)" as load-bearing two-source confirmation of an MF reversal. The article (Multifamily Dive, dated **Jun 16 2025**) reported May **2025** data; relative phrasing "in May" + repeating the year in surrounding context masked the year mismatch. Reality: Trepp Apr 2026 = 7.71% NEW ATH (+56bps from Mar 7.15%) — the OPPOSITE direction. Built a downgrade SV (SV-01) off the bad anchor. Round 2 (Will Part A) flushed the error via fresh queries that surfaced the actual Apr 2026 number.

**Root cause:** Searched a web-aggregator (Multifamily Dive) without confirming publication date OR the year on the underlying datapoint; relied on context-inferred year. Cluster of correct-year 2026 results around it gave false confidence.

**Discipline rule (HOMER going forward):** Before citing any web-pulled metric as **load-bearing** (i.e. capable of changing a CRL-prediction confidence, a STATUS color, or transmission-chain status), **confirm the year explicitly from the primary source** — either article publication date OR explicit year-stamp on the cited number itself. Relative phrasing ("May print", "latest data", "fell to X%") is INSUFFICIENT. If the primary source isn't fetchable, the metric stays in OPEN QUESTIONS until verified, not in the dashboard.

**Secondary discipline:** When two series are claimed to "cross-confirm" a regime change, verify each independently from its own primary source before treating as multi-source.
