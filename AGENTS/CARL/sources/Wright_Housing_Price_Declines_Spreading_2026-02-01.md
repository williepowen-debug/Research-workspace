# Wright: Housing Price Declines Spreading Nationally

*Source: Melody Wright "Read My Lips" (Feb 1, 2026)*

---

## Core Finding

**Price declines are no longer confined to FL/TX — they're spreading nationally.**

| Metric | Current | Prior | Change |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|
| Cities with negative YoY prices | **41 of 85** | 15 of 85 | **+173%** |
| Median list price | **$399,950** | >$400K | First time below $400K since 2022 |
| Freddie Mac HPI YoY | **+0.70%** | Higher | Lowest since housing correction |
| NAR YoY | **+0.42%** | Higher | Near-zero growth |

---

## Executive Selling Signal

> "Almost 1000 executives at roughly 6,000 US-listed firms have unloaded shares this month, compared with 207 who added, resulting in the **highest sell-to-buy ratio in five years**"

**Interpretation:** Smart money exiting. Insiders see something retail doesn't.

---

## Regional Spread

Previously: "Trouble confined to FL and TX" narrative

**Now:** Wright tracks 85 cities — 41 showing YoY declines including:
- Northeast joining
- Midwest joining
- California showing "signs of price weakness"

**Key insight:** FL and TX are two of largest markets. California is THE largest. All three weakening = not regional, it's national.

---

## Affordability Math

Using Tampa as example:
- Median household income: **$74,475**
- Median home price: **~$400K**
- Price-to-income ratio: **5.4x**
- Sustainable ratio: **3.0-3.5x**
- Implied fair value: **~$250K** (37% below current)

This math applies broadly — incomes don't support prices.

---

## Migration Narrative Breaking

> "New Census estimates have just been released showing that net migration to Florida between 2024 and 2025 has slowed significantly. In fact, Florida in total showed **22,517 in net migration gains** during that time period."

**Context:** FL was adding 300K-400K/year at peak. 22K is a >90% collapse in migration.

## Implications for CARL

1. **Price declines spreading** — Not FL/TX idiosyncratic, national phenomenon

3. **Smart money exiting** — Highest insider sell ratio in 5 years
4. **Migration exhausted** — FL demand source dried up
5. **Inventory building** — More sellers, fewer buyers
