## Summary

The 10-K contains **no granular MENA / Gulf disclosure** for insurance underwriting or policyholder exposure. Zero mentions of: UAE, Dubai, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Abu Dhabi, Oman, MENA. "Middle East" appears 3 times — twice as a generic geopolitical risk citation, once as a consolidated AFS investment region bucket. No reinsurance counterparty is identified as Gulf-domiciled.

## What IS Disclosed

### 1. Investment Portfolio — "Africa & Middle East" Bucket
The 10-K provides a geographic breakdown of international AFS securities (invested assets, not insurance liabilities):

| Region | Dec 31 2025 Amortized Cost | Fair Value | % of Total |
|--------|---------------------------|------------|------------|
| Europe (total) | $32,409M | $32,427M | 40.2% |
| Non-US North America | $38,776M | $38,476M | 47.8% |
| Australia & New Zealand | $3,161M | $2,941M | 3.6% |
| Asia/Pacific | $3,109M | $2,639M | 3.3% |
| Central & South America | $1,646M | $1,531M | 1.9% |
| **Africa & Middle East** | **$2,843M** | **$2,580M** | **3.2%** |
| **Total International AFS** | **$81,944M** | **$80,594M** | **100%** |

**Key finding:** "Africa & Middle East" is a combined, undifferentiated bucket. No country-level breakdown within this bucket is provided. The $2.8B amortized cost ($2.6B fair value) represents 3.2% of international AFS — up sharply from $1.4B / 1.4% in 2024. This jump is notable but not explained in the filing. No disclosure of which countries within Africa & Middle East or what instrument types.

### 2. Geographic Breakdown — Insurance Operations / Premiums
**Not disclosed.** The 10-K provides no geographic breakdown of:
- Insurance premiums by policyholder jurisdiction
- Annuity reserves by policyholder geography
- Distribution channel geography (retail, flow reinsurance, institutional) by country/region

Athene's core business is US retirement (FIAs, fixed annuities, pension risk transfer). The 10-K treats insurance operations as essentially a US domestic business.

### 3. Reinsurance Counterparty Disclosure
No reinsurance counterparty is identified as Gulf-domiciled or MENA-based. The filing discusses:
- **Global Atlantic Financial Group** — ceded block of universal life policies (admin dispute with NYSDFS)
- **ACRA (Athene Co-Invest Reinsurance Affiliate)** — internal reinsurance structure, Bermuda-domiciled
- Generic references to third-party reinsurers with no names or geographies

Flow reinsurance is described as a growth channel (US-focused — "bank and broker-dealer networks"). No Gulf/MENA reinsurance partners named.

### 4. Bermuda Subsidiary (ACRA)
Athene's primary non-US reinsurance operations run through Bermuda. 123 Bermuda references. This is the vehicle for capital efficiency and international reinsurance — not MENA-specific. ACRA is fully consolidated.

### 5. Geographic Business Segments
Athene reports as a **single segment**. No geographic revenue breakdown by country. US/non-US distinction exists only in tax footnotes (Bermuda corporate tax mentioned) and investment portfolio tables.

### 6. Risk Factor Mentions — "Middle East"
Middle East mentioned twice in risk factors as generic geopolitical risk (Russia-Ukraine + Middle East armed conflicts as co-cited macro risks), with no insurance-specific exposure statement. Standard boilerplate language, not entity-specific.

### 7. Iowa / Domicile Regulation (18 Iowa references)
Primary US insurance subsidiaries:
- **AAIA** — domiciled in Iowa
- **AANY** — domiciled in New York
Regulated under Iowa Insurance Holding Company Act (Iowa HCA). Statutory filings (Annual Statements) submitted to Iowa Insurance Division.

## What's Missing / Where to Find It

| Question | 10-K Answer | Where to Look |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Country breakdown within "Africa & Middle East" AFS bucket | Not disclosed | Contact IR or check Schedule D in statutory filing |
| Reinsurance counterparties by geography | Not disclosed | Iowa Insurance Division Annual Statement (Schedule F) |
| Policyholder geography for annuities / PRT | Not disclosed | Iowa statutory Annual Statement, Exhibit of Premiums |
| MENA insurance underwriting exposure | Not disclosed | Iowa statutory filing or Bermuda BMA filings |
| Gulf-domiciled reinsurance cedants/assumers | Not disclosed | Iowa Schedule F-Part 3 (assumed), F-Part 4 (ceded) |

**The Iowa Insurance Division** is the primary regulatory body for AAIA. Statutory annual statements are publicly available via NAIC iSite+ or direct IDOI request. These would contain Schedule F (reinsurance detail by counterparty name and domicile) and geographic premium exhibits unavailable in the 10-K.

**The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)** receives filings for ACRA and other Bermuda reinsurance subs. BMA filings are not public but can be requested via formal channels.

---

## Notable Flag: Africa & Middle East AFS Jump

The "Africa & Middle East" investment bucket grew from $1,050M fair value (Dec 2024) to $2,580M (Dec 2025) — a **+146% increase in one year**. This is the fastest-growing geographic bucket in the international AFS portfolio. No explanation provided in the filing. Given Apollo's known infrastructure/credit activities in the Gulf (Abu Dhabi sovereign wealth, UAE infrastructure deals), this likely reflects Apollo-originated credit assets flowing into Athene's investment portfolio — but this is an investment exposure, not insurance underwriting exposure.

---

## Conclusion

The Athene 10-K (FY2025) **does not contain granular MENA geographic disclosure** for insurance underwriting. The only MENA-relevant data point is the combined "Africa & Middle East" AFS investment bucket at $2.6B fair value (3.2% of international AFS), which jumped +146% YoY with no explanation. 

For true Gulf insurance underwriting exposure and reinsurance counterparty geography:
1. **Iowa Insurance Division** — Request AAIA's statutory Annual Statement (Schedule F for reinsurance, Exhibit of Premiums by state/country)
2. **NAIC iSite+** — Public access to certain statutory filing schedules
3. **Bermuda BMA** — ACRA/Bermuda reinsurance subs (non-public, formal request)
