## The one discriminator that matters
The BDC read-through is **not the headline NCO** — it's **NCO direction × ACL-coverage direction**. A monoline/near-prime lender can post a *clean* headline loss rate while **releasing reserves into a shrinking, de-risked book** — that is the exact optical-clean mechanism CARL's masking framework (CRL-20/21) tracks, and it is what a BDC holding the *same* consumer paper cannot do (mark-to-model lags, but the underlying cash flows are shared). **So the bearish BDC tell is NCO ↑ AND ACL coverage ↓ in the SAME print.** NCO ↑ with ACL ↑ (honest build) is *less* bearish for the BDC mark, not more — the lender is pre-provisioning.
