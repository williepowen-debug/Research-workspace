## Context

California's insurance market is in crisis:
- Private insurers are retreating from wildfire-prone areas
- FAIR Plan (insurer of last resort) has grown from 1.6% to 3%+ of market
- 150,000+ households in high-risk zones are explicitly uninsured
- $1.35-2.0 TRILLION coverage gap (McKinsey estimate)
- January 2025 LA fires (Eaton, Palisades) destroyed 18,000+ structures

We need to understand the TRAJECTORY of this retreat and what happens next. Is this linear growth or exponential? When does it become systemic?
