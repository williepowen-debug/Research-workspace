## Key Findings

### Market Scale
| Metric | 2025 Value | Context |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Total BNPL Transaction Value | **$70B** | ~20% annual growth since 2021 |
| Share of Credit Card Spending | **1.1%** | Still small but growing |

| Average Loan Size | $131 | Short-term, pay-in-four |

### Charge-Off / Default Rates
| Year | BNPL Charge-Off | Credit Card Charge-Off |
|------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 2022 | 2.63% | — |
| 2023 | 1.83% | 4.19% (Q4) |

**BNPL default rates lower than credit cards** — but this may be misleading due to underreporting.

### 🔴 STRESS SIGNAL: Late Payment Spike
| Year | BNPL Users Making Late Payment |
|------|-------------------------------|
| 2024 | 34% |
| 2025 | **41%** (+7pp YoY) |

**41% of BNPL users made at least one late payment in the past year** — significant deterioration.

### User Profile (2021-2022 Originations)
| Credit Tier | FICO Range | Share of Originations |
|-------------|------------|----------------------|
| **Deep Subprime** | 300-579 | **45%** |
| **Subprime** | 580-619 | **16%** |
| Near-Prime+ | 620+ | 39% |

**61% of BNPL originations go to subprime or worse borrowers.**

### Repayment Confidence
- **Only 59% of BNPL users are "very confident"** they can repay on time
- **41% have lower confidence** — financial planning/liquidity stress

### Credit Bureau Reporting (June 2025)
- **Affirm:** Started reporting to credit bureaus
- **Klarna, Afterpay:** Resisting — argue models may penalize short-term usage
- **Sezzle:** Optional reporting

## CARL Implications

### Why BNPL Matters for Consumer Stress Thesis

1. **Hidden Leverage:** 63% stacking rate + no bureau reporting = phantom debt
2. **Subprime Concentration:** 61% of originations to subprime/deep subprime
3. **Late Payment Spike:** 34% → 41% YoY is meaningful deterioration
4. **Liquidity Signal:** 41% not confident in repayment = stretched

### Why Richmond Fed May Be Understating Risk

1. **"No clear evidence of elevated stress"** — but late payments +7pp YoY
2. **Small scale argument** — $70B is small vs credit cards, but it's ADDITIONAL phantom debt
3. **User overlap:** BNPL users "carry higher balances on other unsecured credit" — they're already stressed
4. **Subprime concentration:** 61% to subprime means BNPL IS the stress population

### Transmission Path
```
Employment shock (LABOR)
    ↓
BNPL users (61% subprime) miss payments first
    ↓
Late payments spike (already 41%, rising)
    ↓
Credit card/auto stress follows (BNPL users have higher balances)
    ↓
CARL vectors trigger
```
