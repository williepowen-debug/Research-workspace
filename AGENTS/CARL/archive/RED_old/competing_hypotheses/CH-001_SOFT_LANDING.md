# CH-001: Soft Landing Hypothesis

**Status:** STRONGLY CONTRADICTED
**Confidence:** <10%
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-21 (CARL 005)

---

## Hypothesis Statement

Consumer stress is **transitory**; Federal Reserve rate cuts will **normalize conditions**. The economy will achieve a "soft landing" where inflation is tamed without recession or significant consumer distress.

---

## Key Claims

1. **Transitory Stress:** Current consumer stress metrics are temporary, driven by post-pandemic adjustment and rate shock
2. **Fed Effectiveness:** Rate cuts will ease debt service burden sufficiently to stabilize delinquencies
3. **Labor Market Anchor:** Strong employment will prevent consumer stress from cascading
4. **Wealth Effect:** Asset prices (housing, equities) support consumer balance sheets
5. **Aggregate Resilience:** Headline metrics (GDP, spending) will remain positive

## Invalidation Criteria (for Soft Landing)

The Soft Landing hypothesis would be **VALIDATED** if:
- [ ] Credit card DQ falls below 10% for 2 quarters
- [ ] Real wage growth exceeds inflation for 3+ months
- [ ] Savings rate recovers to >7%
- [ ] Minimum payment rate declines from 12-year high
- [ ] Fed cuts 150+ bps AND delinquencies stabilize

**Current Status:**
- Fed cuts: YES (partial)
- DQ stabilization: NO
- Other criteria: NOT MET

---

## Assessment

### Why Soft Landing Fails

1. **Rate Cuts Insufficient:** Fed has cut but consumer delinquencies continue rising. Rate transmission to consumer credit (22%+ APR cards) is slow and incomplete.

2. **Structural Costs:** Healthcare deductibles up 43% over a decade is structural, not cyclical. Rate cuts don't fix this.

3. **Buffer Exhaustion:** Pandemic savings depleted. 401k hardship withdrawals at ATH. Gig economy saturated. There are no buffers left to absorb stress.

4. **Synchronized Stress:** All consumer credit verticals showing stress simultaneously. This is not isolated to one segment.

5. **Leading Indicators Breached:** Minimum payment rate, BNPL stacking, student loan DQ — all at extreme levels. These LEAD headline delinquencies.

### Probability Assessment

**Soft Landing Probability: <10%**

For Soft Landing to occur, we would need:
- Rapid, significant rate cuts (150+ bps more)
- Immediate wage growth exceeding inflation
- Consumer behavior change (reduced spending, increased saving)
- No employment shock
- Banks continuing to extend credit despite rising NCOs

This combination is highly improbable given current trajectory.

---

## What Would Change This Assessment

### Toward Higher Probability
- DQ rates flatten AND decline for 2+ quarters
- Minimum payment rate reverses
- Real wage growth exceeds 4% for multiple months
- Major fiscal stimulus to consumers ($1T+)
- Savings rate recovers above 6%

### Toward Lower Probability
- Credit card DQ exceeds ATH (13.7%)
- Major bank warns on consumer NCOs
- Unemployment rises above 5%
- Additional lender failures
- Fed pauses cuts due to inflation concerns
