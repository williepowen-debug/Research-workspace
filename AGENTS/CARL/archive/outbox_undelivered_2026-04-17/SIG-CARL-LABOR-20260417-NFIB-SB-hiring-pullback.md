## Why this routes to LABOR

POP's FLOW-POP-02 (Employment Transmission) cascade: business stress → hiring freeze → layoffs with 3-6 month lag. Compensation plans falling to Jul 2025 lows + profit trend worst since COVID = owners preparing for either (a) shallower raises into 2026, (b) headcount cuts in 6 months, or (c) both. Owner comp cuts are immediate and invisible to your UI data; employee impact is lagged.
