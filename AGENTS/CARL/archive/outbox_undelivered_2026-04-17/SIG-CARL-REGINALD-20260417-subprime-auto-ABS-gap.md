## Why the gap exists — composition, not macro

ALLY has been actively selecting AWAY from subprime for 18 months (S-tier 40→37%, but still letting nonprime creep 9.7→10.1%). Santander/Bridgecrest/Exeter write the loans ALLY now rejects — the ones gig workers actually get. The stress hasn't disappeared; it moved to a different balance sheet.
