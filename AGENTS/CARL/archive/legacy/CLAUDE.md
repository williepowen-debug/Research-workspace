## KEY CONCEPTS

**Zombie Borrower:** Making minimum payments only. Technically current, functionally insolvent. Transitions to default 6-12 months after liquidity exhaustion.

**K-Shape Bifurcation:** Top 10% = 49.2% of spending. Aggregate metrics MASK lower-income deterioration.

**Super-Lien:** HOA lien with priority OVER first mortgage. Consumer HOA stress → bank mortgage losses.

**Credit Lockout:** Converging factors systematically denying credit access. Self-reinforcing spiral.

**Cockroach Thesis:** One visible lender failure = more hidden. Tricolor + PrimaLend validated this.

**Phantom Debt:** Shadow credit (cash advances, BNPL, earned wage access, medical payment plans, utility arrears) invisible to credit bureaus. **Quantified at $150-200B** (ML-CR-18), representing 3-4% additional consumer leverage. Traditional metrics UNDERCOUNT true consumer debt. GIG workers especially affected (58% quarterly emergency loans).

**Buffer Exhaustion:** Gig economy can no longer absorb displaced workers. 58% utilization = oversupplied. The "side hustle safety net" is failing.

**Deductible Gap:** The dollar difference between insurance deductibles and household liquid savings. 60%+ of Americans cannot cover their deductible, meaning they are "insured" but cannot afford to access coverage. A single medical event converts them from "stable" to "crisis." This metric is MORE diagnostic than the 37% "$400 emergency" measure. (ML-CARL-06)

**Conversion Velocity:** Speed at which a trigger event becomes visible delinquency. For low-liquidity households (<$1k savings), conversion is 0-3 weeks. Standard credit models assume 12-month Loss Emergence Period; reality for 37% of population is <3 months. (ML-CARL-06)

**Double Trigger:** Default requires BOTH negative equity/high leverage (Trigger 1) AND liquidity shock (Trigger 2). Neither alone causes default. When both present, conversion approaches 100%. Interaction effects are multiplicative, not additive. (ML-CARL-06)

**Latent Vulnerability:** Population that is not currently delinquent but is ONE INCIDENT away from crisis. Includes: 23% underinsured (medical event triggers debt spiral), 36% deferring care (condition becoming acute triggers catastrophic cost), 33.4% inadequately insured drivers (accident triggers financial ruin), 150K CA uninsured homeowners (fire triggers total loss). Traditional metrics miss this population until incident converts latent to active. Estimated to add 25-35% to at-risk population beyond visible delinquency. (ML-CARL-01)
