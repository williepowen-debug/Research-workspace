## SPILLOVER TO OTHER CREDIT

**JPMorgan Research:**
- Collections on defaulted loans may reduce disposable income **$3.1-8.5B/month**
- Part of the reason people are adding CC debt = student loan payments consuming income
- Student loan delinquency → CC delinquency → Auto delinquency transmission

**NY Fed Finding:**
- "Potential spillovers" to other debts as borrowers juggle payments
- Student loan stress competes with CC/auto for limited income
