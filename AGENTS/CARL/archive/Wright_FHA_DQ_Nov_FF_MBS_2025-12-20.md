# Wright: FHA Delinquency Nov + Fannie/Freddie MBS Intervention

*Source: Melody Wright "Make it Happen" (Dec 20, 2025)*

---

## FHA Delinquency Trajectory

| Month | Headline DQ | Serious DQ |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| October 2025 | 10.81% | 3.69% |
| **November 2025** | **12.11%** | **4.20%** |
| December 2025 | — | 4.68% (per later Wright article) |

**Nov change:** +130 bps headline, +51 bps serious in ONE month.

---

## Fannie/Freddie Back in MBS Market

> "Fannie and Freddie have plunged back into the mortgage-backed securities (MBS) market, **increasing their retained portfolios by 25% since May**."

**Historical context:**
> "By 2008, their combined holdings swelled to more than $1.5 trillion... when the housing market buckled during the financial crisis, those positions — and the leverage behind them — **delivered heavy losses that helped push Fannie and Freddie into conservatorship**."

**Runway:** Bloomberg reports they are $200B below their cap — "plenty of runway."

**Wright's assessment:** "For those of us molded in the fires of the GFC it sends shivers down your spine."

---

## November Existing Home Sales

> "Existing home sales non-seasonally adjusted came in at 293,000 in November, which is the **second worst November since 1999. The worst? November of 2008.**"

- Sales: -6.98% YoY, -18.38% MoM
- Median price: $409,200 (+1.20% YoY, -1.37% MoM)

---

## Regional Price Deceleration

| Region | Prior Month YoY | November YoY |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Northeast** | **6.5%** | **1.1%** |
| West | Positive | **-0.9%** |
| Midwest | — | +5.8% (holding) |

> "What region's home price appreciation screeched to a crawl? Last month the median sales price in the NE was up 6.5% YOY. This month? 1.1%."

**Westchester County** (wealthy NY suburb) went negative -1.60% YoY.

---

## Median List Price Went Negative

> "After months of shenanigans and monkeying with the data, the median list price as reported by FRED/Realtor just went **negative YOY**. While only down -.39% YOY and -2.17% MOM this in my opinion signals a **capitulation of sorts** by the industry."

---

## Builder Stress

**Lennar incentives: 14%** — "whopping" level to move inventory.

> "Lennar results disappointed... Each passing day that these homes sit empty and don't sell, potential losses mount. In Florida, mold alone will be a massive problem."

---

## AI Skepticism Spreading

> "More and more articles are also hitting mainstream about **AI hype versus reality**."

> "Without AI mania we would have seen housing continue its correction in 2023. The madness and stock market highs gave credence to soft landing hopium for just a bit longer."

## Implications

1. **FHA stress accelerating** — 130 bps headline increase in one month
2. **F&F back in MBS** — Systemic risk building, GFC echoes
3. **Nov 2025 = Nov 2008** — Sales volume at crisis levels
4. **Northeast cracking** — Last holdout region showing stress
5. **List prices capitulating** — Industry narrative shifting

**Timeline update:**
- May 2025: F&F begin increasing MBS portfolios
- Oct 2025: HUD guardrails implemented
- Nov 2025: FHA DQ spikes, sales = 2nd worst since 1999
- Dec 2025: FHA serious DQ → 4.68%, 609K new delinquencies
