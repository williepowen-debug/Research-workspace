## SIGNAL

CARL research (Apr 13) identified material deterioration in non-bank mortgage servicer health since Jul 2025. The FHA foreclosure pipeline (878K in 90+/FC, +25% in 4 months, FHA = 80% of increase) is now converting to servicer liquidity stress via the Ginnie Mae advance obligation asymmetry.

**Key findings REGINALD should assess for bank exposure:**

### 1. Warehouse Line Exposure
- Non-banks hold 76% of origination, 66% of servicing. They depend on bank warehouse lines.
- NYCB exited mortgage warehouse lending (sold ~$5B to JPMorgan). JPM has ~20.8% market share.
- Texas Capital, Western Alliance (WAL), First Horizon also active in warehouse lending.
- **MFS UK collapsed Feb 2026** — Barclays lost ~$669M through warehouse line exposure. "Cockroaches" narrative. This is the template for US contagion.
- **Question for REGINALD:** Which regionals (WAL, FHN, TCBI) have material warehouse line exposure to FHA-heavy non-bank servicers? Is this in their 10-K risk factors?

### 2. Servicer-Specific Stress
- **PennyMac (PFSI):** FHA DQ spiked 5.9% → 7.5% in Q4 (+160bps). Servicing expenses +14% QoQ from advance balances. Acquiring Cenlar ($740B UPB, total >$1T). Debt-to-equity 3.6x, total indebtedness $15.6B.
- **loanDepot (LDI):** $107.5M net loss FY2025. 28% Ginnie Mae exposure. Pledging GNMA MSR income via $200M term notes. Most vulnerable public name.
- **Lakeview:** 18% DQ (Jul 2025, stale). Private, no updated data.
- **Freedom:** 15.5% DQ (Jul 2025, stale). Private. Freedom exec "speculated more M&A could be driven by greater delinquency in 2026."
- **Rithm/NewRez:** 18% Ginnie MSRs. Acknowledged Q4 DQ spike. Q1 earnings Apr 28.

### 3. GAO Structural Findings (GAO-26-107436, Feb 2026)
- 35% of 550+ non-banks have "high levels of debt"
- Only 30% were profitable during 2022-2023 downturn
- Ginnie Mae has NO stagflation stress test scenario (our environment is the untested one)
- Credit reviews "frequently omitted" warehouse line covenant violations

### 4. Transmission Pathway
```
FHA DQ 11.52% (4x conventional)
  → Non-bank advance obligations grow (must advance until FINAL resolution)
  → Cure rates -40% = advances not recovered, cash drain cumulative
  → Non-bank liquidity strain
  → Warehouse lines (bank-held) become counterparty exposure
  → If non-bank fails: Ginnie Mae backstops loans, but warehouse lenders eat losses
```

### REGINALD ACTION REQUESTED
1. Check WAL, FHN, TCBI 10-Ks for mortgage warehouse line exposure disclosures
2. Assess whether JPM's 20.8% warehouse market share appears in JPM Q1 earnings (Apr 14) or risk commentary
3. Flag any regional bank with combined CRE + warehouse line exposure — double vulnerability
4. Track RITM Q1 earnings (Apr 28) — "DQ will reverse in Q1" is a testable claim
