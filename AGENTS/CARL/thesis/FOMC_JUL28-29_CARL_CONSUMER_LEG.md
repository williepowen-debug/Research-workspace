## 1. THE LEG — how oil enters the language

RED pre-registers a **binary**: *"upside risk to inflation / pass-through"* = S1/S4b confirm (Fed-locked); *"headwind to growth / tax on households"* = S3-lean. That binary is right as far as it goes. **CARL adds a third cell it currently collapses, and a finer listen inside it.**

### The third cell RED's binary misses: **oil as a supply shock to be LOOKED THROUGH**

On 6/17 Warsh delivered exactly this — *"look through energy"*, oil and eggs having "no first-order consequence," the job being to stop second- and third-round effects. **That is neither "upside inflation risk" nor "growth tax."** It is a deliberate *exclusion* of the energy channel from the reaction function.

**Why it needs its own cell:** it is **V12-confirming but not hawkish-escalating.** A Fed that looks through a $4.10 pump and a $100 Brent is a Fed that will not ease on energy pain *and* will not tighten on energy prices — locked in both directions. RED's tree would score it S1; the market would likely read it as less hawkish than S1 implies. **That gap is where the mispricing lives.**

**The live question, and it is genuinely open:** the 6/17 "look through" line was delivered into a **falling** pump (gas $3.93 and dropping five straight weeks, Brent $77). It has never been tested against a rising one. Repeating it at $4.10 with a $100 print behind him is a much harder sentence to say.
