## 2 · Books and terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scored book** | WQ-112 (i): the latest **dated** pre-resolution mark governs. (ii): an undated re-mark is not a mark. `FORGE/PREDICTION_DISCIPLINE.md` §Grading |
| **First-call book** | the as-made (Date_Made) confidence. Reprices and as-made restorations are NEUTRAL here by construction |
| `*` | ex-ante only: the move changed the odds but realised nothing (e.g. an extension on a row that missed anyway) |
| REPRICE / WINDOW / RESPEC / DISPOSITION / RECORD-CORRECTION | confidence move · window move · threshold, leg or **kill-clause** change · discretionary grading call · re-mark of the record itself |
| Initiator CARL | includes CARL's sub-agents (POLLY, PHAN, STUE), HOMER-supplied data where CARL made the call, and Will-approved or Will-directed CARL calls. OUTSIDE = a mechanical restoration run by another desk |
| PROV | row still OPEN. Outcome basis = CARL's own current confidence (≤25 M, ≥75 H). **Circular by construction:** a cut on a walked-down row scores HELPS. Threshold sensitivity: 24/29 at ≤25/≥75, 20/25 at ≤20/≥80, 18/23 at ≤10 |

## 7 · Limits
1. PROV outcomes are circular (§2). The ratio is robust to the threshold; the count is not.
2. Only calls visible in the TSV, plus the 8 MANUAL rows found by the challenge pass, are counted. Confidence moves made only in STATUS are dated at the TSV commit, and two edits inside one commit collapse into one.
3. The TSV's IDs are claim-consistent at `91c301279` and `9e30a573c`; the early ID reassignment affected STATUS only. STATUS-only predictions dropped at the 3/09 renumbering carried no confidence and are out of scope.
4. The census reads Status, Confidence, Timeframe, Prediction and **Invalidation**. It cannot see as-made values written after an unchanged leading % (those are added as MANUAL rows).
5. Brier stakes compare against a named alternative per row. Where the alternative is "unscored", no sum across rows is meaningful, so none is given.
6. Sub-agent ledgers (PHAN, POP, DOC, POLLY, GIG) are out of scope: RED asked for CRL rows.
