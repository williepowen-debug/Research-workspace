## Hypothesis

## Assessment

Soft landing requires rate cuts + employment stability + DQ stabilization. None are occurring. Fed is trapped (3.1% PCE prevents cuts), employment is structurally weakening (JOLTS inverted, duration 25.7wk), and DQs are accelerating across every vertical. Gas $4+, food CPI loading, and UI exhaustion provide three independent cost-squeeze vectors that rate cuts couldn't fix even if they came.

Downgraded from <10% to <5% since January based on stagflation confirmation and SPR failure eliminating the last policy relief valve.

**What would change this:** Ceasefire in Gulf + Brent <$80 + Congress passes stimulus + DQ rates plateau for 2 quarters. All needed simultaneously.
