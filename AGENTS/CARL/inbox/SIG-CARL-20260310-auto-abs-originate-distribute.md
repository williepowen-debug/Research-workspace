### Auto ABS: Originate-to-Distribute Fraud Patterns Re-Emerging

**Source:** Analysis article (Will)

- Originate-to-distribute problem: party with most info on loan quality has least long-term exposure; party bearing credit risk has least visibility into inputs
- Historical pattern: elevated default rates revealed pool quality only after originating dealers were "long gone, fee in pocket"
