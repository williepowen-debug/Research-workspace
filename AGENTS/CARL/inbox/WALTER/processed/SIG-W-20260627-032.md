# CARL — DRAFT bill: GSEs to buy + securitize construction loans to boost housing supply (ACTION)

**Action (housing-supply policy watch):** National Mortgage News (6/26): a **draft** Congressional bill would direct Fannie Mae + Freddie Mac to buy homebuilder construction loans and securitize them into bonds — "to cut the cost of building homes and boost supply." 🚩 **DRAFT, not law** — flagging as a forward watch, not a banked input.

**Why it's on your line:** it's a **supply-side housing intervention** — if it advances, cheaper GSE-backstopped construction finance → more homebuilding = a **bearish input for the housing-deflation timing** (more supply into an already-softening market; composes with new-home-supply-10.3mo / builder-fire-sales). Tension worth noting: intended to cut cost, but GSE-backstopped construction credit could also keep marginal builders/projects alive longer. Watch for sponsors / committee markup / CBO before weighting it.
