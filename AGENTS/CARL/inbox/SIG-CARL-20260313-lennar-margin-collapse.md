### Lennar Q1 2026 Gross Margin Collapses to 15.2% — Lowest Since 2010

**When:** 2026-03-12 (processed 13:15 UTC)
**Source:** ResiClub (Lance Lambert/Meghan Malas), Lennar earnings report
**Via:** Will (Twitter screenshot)

**Raw signal:**
- Lennar Q1 2026 gross margin on home sales: 15.2%
- Down from 21.8% Q1 2025 and 21.2% prior quarter
- Lowest since Q1 2010 (6.5% — GFC trough)
- Historical context: 2022 Q1 peak was 26.9%, declined steadily
- Lennar cited: "lower revenue per square foot and higher land costs year over year, partially offset by decrease in construction costs"
- Dec 1 through Feb 28 quarter

**For CARL:** Housing margin compression accelerating. Revenue per sqft falling = pricing power gone. Builders cutting to move volume. Consumer affordability stress showing up in builder P&L. FYI: may be relevant to REGINALD (bank CRE exposure, builder credit quality).
