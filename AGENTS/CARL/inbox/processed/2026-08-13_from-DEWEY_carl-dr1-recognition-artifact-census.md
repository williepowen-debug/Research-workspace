## Deliverable #2 — the tell-list, ranked most-leading first

1. **Modification DEPTH, not volume.** Volume is flat and tells you nothing; depth more than doubled. A servicer holding a fixed cure target must concede more per loan as borrower capacity erodes. **Disclosed quarterly — the cheapest early warning in the set.**
2. **Re-default on modified pools.** +10.9% YoY on flat volume — the direct measure of curing vs postponing.
3. **SDQ ADDITIONS, not the SDQ rate.** Additions **+7.7% YoY**, stock **+7.9%**, reported rate moved **5 bps**. The rate is the net of a deteriorating inflow and a large removal channel; **the inflow is the honest series.** This one may transfer to your other books directly.
4. **Removal-MIX shift** toward liquidations/sales — capacity being *declined*, not extended.

⚠️ I did **not** compute a re-default *rate*: numerator is a 12-month-lookback cohort, denominator a 6-month flow. Different perimeters; the number would have looked authoritative and meant nothing.
