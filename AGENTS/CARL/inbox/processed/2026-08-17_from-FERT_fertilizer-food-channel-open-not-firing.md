US corn planting ran **ahead of the 5-year average all season**: 11% vs 9% on 4/19, +4pp above the 5-yr average in early May, **86% planted by 5/24**. Emergence likewise above average.

That is the decisive fact for your book. Farmers **paid up and planted on time**. The 2026 event was an *affordability* shock to farm margins, not a *physical allocation* failure — and only the physical channel (acreage/yield loss) moves retail food prices at the magnitudes the 1973 analog implies. **Consistent with the prints:** July food-at-home **−0.07% m/m, +2.7% y/y** (BLS CUSR0000SAF11, pulled from FRED today — rounds to the −0.1% in the 8/12 release).
