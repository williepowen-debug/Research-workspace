# CARL — S-FL distress deep-research (INFO) — consumer-led + energy→core NOT yet realized

Deep-research deliverable (Will-commissioned, verify-first, primary-sourced) on S-FL distress. CORAL is action; you're info on the consumer/stagflation leg. Full packet in canonical BOARD file.

**Two CARL-relevant findings:**

1. **The FL bankruptcy surge is consumer-led, not corporate.** AOUSC 12-mo end 3/31/26: national filings +11.9% to 591,850; **Ch-7 (liquidation) rising fastest (~+15.3%)**; FL nonbusiness +22.2%. Business cases are only ~5–6% of FL district volume. This is a **household-insolvency** story (credit-card debt toward record per UCF/Snaith), per-capita elevated-but-not-extreme (FL ~190/100k vs national ~168–173).

2. **⚠️ Iran-energy → core-CPI spillover is forecast/narrative, NOT yet in the data — counter-evidence to near-term core acceleration.** May 2026 CPI +4.2% YoY (highest since Apr-2023) but **energy drove >60% of the monthly gain**; **core only +0.2% MoM / +2.9% YoY; core goods −0.1%;** transport services −0.6%. Multiple economists (Rupkey/Fwdbonds: "core goods inflation risks in retreat") note energy is "not bleeding into core." Treat the spillover as a **live forward risk, not a realized fact** — relevant to how you weight the energy-disinflation-reversing channel vs realized core. Snaith/UCF's own read is conditional: GDP slowing 2.1→1.7%, explicitly **no recession**, FL housing "flattened."

Net for your K-shape: the consumer-distress side corroborates (Ch-7 acceleration + CC debt + "full impact not yet felt"), but the energy→core transmission you've been watching is **not yet printing in core** — a calibration check against over-banking the spillover.
