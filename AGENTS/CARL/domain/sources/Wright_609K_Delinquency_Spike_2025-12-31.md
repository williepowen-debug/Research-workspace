# Wright: 609K Delinquency Spike — Largest Since May 2020

*Source: Melody Wright "Grifter Nation" (Dec 31, 2025)*

---

## The Headline Number

> "**609,000 borrowers who were current on payments in October became delinquent in November**, marking the **largest single-month inflow since May 2020**. Rolls from 30- to 60-day and 60- to 90-day delinquency bands also increased sharply."

**Source:** ICE/Black Knight

---

## Why Now? HUD Guardrails

> "When HUD put the brakes on the repeat partial claim solution, those that were able to keep deferring payments **can no longer do so as of October 1st**."

**Translation:** COVID-era loss mitigation runway ended. Borrowers who were deferring via partial claims have run out of options.

**Plus:** Insurance + property tax increases creating new distress cohort independent of employment.

---

## Wright's Assessment

ICE/Black Knight attributed the spike to "the month ending on a Sunday." Wright is skeptical:

> "Based on my own experience in servicing operations I find 'the calendar' explanation questionable as the main driver for this increase in delinquency."

> "There comes a point where there are no more options. This time around as well the institutionals will not be who steps in at these prices to 'save' the market and home prices as they are also being crushed."
