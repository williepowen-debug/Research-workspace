*Source: Melody Wright "Under the Covers" (Jan 20, 2026)*

## FHA Serious Delinquency Rising

| Month | Serious DQ (GNMA II) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|--------|
| November 2025 | 4.20% | — |
| December 2025 | **4.68%** | +48 bps |

**Wright's assessment:**
> "The guardrails put in place in October mean that our seriously delinquent in this portfolio will soon completely run out of options. **Foreclosures will rise from here and will be materially higher by Q2 2026**, putting downward pressure on home prices."

---

## Historical Parallel: October 2007

> "There were **57,000 new home sales in October 2025 which matches October 2007 exactly**."

**Context:** October 2007 was ~12 months before Lehman collapse. Sales had already cratered but official recognition of crisis was still ahead.

## 401(K) Hardship Withdrawals

| Year | Hardship Withdrawal Rate |
|------|-------------------------|
| 2018 | 2.7% |
| 2024 | **~6.0%** |

> "As of 2024, nearly 6% of employees have taken a hardship withdrawal from their retirement account compared to roughly 2.7% in 2018, and retirement plan loans have been on the rise since 2021, rising from 6.5% to 9.2%."

**Implication:** Middle class already tapping retirement savings to survive. No cushion left.

## 2025 = Worst Sales Year of Cycle

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| 2025 Combined Sales (est) | **4.7M** | New + Existing |
| Average since 1999 | 5.9M | Baseline |
| 2008-2011 Average | 4.6M | Crisis years |

> "2025, from a combined sales perspective, will likely be lower than any year besides 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2011."

## Regional Price Declines Spreading

| Region | % of Sales | YoY Price Trend |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| South | 45% | **Negative** |
| West | 20% | **Negative** |
| Midwest | 22% | **Just turned negative** (first time) |
| Northeast | 13% | Slowing (5.1% → 3.7%) |

> "Combined, the South, Midwest and West make up **87% of home sales** in the United States."

**Midwest turning negative is significant:** "I expected further slowing, but I did not expect such a sharp reversal in trend."

## Builder Price Cuts

| Price Tier | Aug 2025 | Sep-Oct 2025 |
|------------|----------|--------------|
| Under $300K | 18% | **24-25%** |
| $400K-$1M | Higher | Lower |

> "Sales in the middle tiers $400K-$1M were lower signaling that **builders are under pressure to reduce prices** while the existing home sales market grabs market share in the middle."
