# Wright: 41% Builders Cutting Prices + Credit Tightening

*Source: Melody Wright "I, American" (Dec 4, 2025)*

---

## Builder Price Cuts at Record

> "**41% of builders reported cutting prices in November**, a record high in the post-Covid period and the first time this measure has passed 40%."

**Source:** NAHB November Housing Market Index

**Wright's note on framing:** "By stating this is a 'record high in the post-Covid period' one might think that maybe we are just returning to norms. The NAHB has been doing this survey since 1985."

---

## Credit Door Slamming Shut

**NY Fed Survey of Consumer Expectations (SCE) Credit Access Survey:**

> "That mortgage refinance rejection rate is **even worse than what we saw in February (a series high)**."

**Implication:**
> "The door to credit is being slammed shut meaning that **many of our investors will have to list those properties** just as delinquency increases due to layoffs and limitations being placed on overly generous FHA loss mitigation programs."

---

## Layoff Data (Through November 2025)

> "U.S.-based employers announced 71,321 job cuts in November... Through November, employers have announced **1,170,821 job cuts, an increase of 54%** from the 761,358 announced in the first eleven months of last year."

> "Announced hiring plans tracked by Challenger in November are the **lowest since they began tracking hiring plans in 2012**."

> "Job cuts in November have risen above 70,000 only twice since 2008: in 2022 and in 2008."

## Silver Tsunami

**Harvard data:**
- **15.6M Americans will die** between 2025-2035
- **Another 23M** between 2035-2045
- Total: **38.6M deaths** in 20 years

**Florida:** 2nd highest share of older Americans (retirees)
**Northeast:** VT, NH, ME very high; NY, PA above 17.7% national avg

> "This silver tsunami will have material impacts on supply and housing market dynamics."

## DC Area Stress (DOGE Proxy)

**Bethesda, MD:**
- Prices: -7.1% YoY, **-20.4% MoM** (motivated selling)
- Inventory: +52.7% YoY

**Government employment:** -3.7% YoY (August data)

> "Federal grants account for about 9% of Montgomery County's revenue... County officials warn that cuts to those grants could strain critical services."

## Implications

1. **41% cutting prices** — Builders in distress; can't move inventory
2. **Refi rejection series high** — Investors forced to sell; can't refinance out
3. **Hiring plans lowest since 2012** — "Hotel California" confirmed
4. **62% of sales in distressed regions** — No safe haven left
5. **Silver tsunami** — Long-term supply wave, concentrated in FL and NE
