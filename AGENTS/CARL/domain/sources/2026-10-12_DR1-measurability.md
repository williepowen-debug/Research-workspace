## What must be measured

The commission asks how much reported delinquency improved because of deferral/classification rather than borrower health. A modification balance, re-aging share, workout action count or loan sale establishes activity, not that causal difference. Accounting loss recognition is a separate outcome from delinquency classification.

For a specified book/date and fixed denominator, the delinquency effect is the difference between the observed reported delinquency share and the share that would have prevailed without the deferral intervention. A mechanical shadow-status estimate additionally needs original payment schedule, actual payments, intervention dates/terms and status before/after. Holding actual payments fixed yields a classification comparison, **not** the full behavioral no-treatment counterfactual: relief can itself change repayment.

A credible causal comparison needs untreated comparable borrowers or an exogenous assignment rule, common pre-treatment risk/seasoning and pre-trends, follow-up long enough to distinguish cure from postponed default, plus complete exits/charge-offs/sales. Random assignment, a justified eligibility discontinuity or a defensible comparison design could supply that identification. Aggregate modified/current tables do not.

## Aggregation and correction reconciliation

WQ104 fixes Fannie16.95M /FHA7.81M weights at each numerator's measurement vintage. It requires a compatible private-book leg before the kill is applied and permanent best-leg shadow display. A private disclosure has now landed, but that does not make its scale ratio a compatible causal wedge. Preserve the weights; do not add dollar-weighted cards to count-weighted mortgage proxies, re-pick weights after seeing results, or omit uncovered legs to make a “class”.

DEWEY's committed October12 erratum is consumed. Recomputed examples:

- `(16.95×22.5+7.81×260)/24.76 =97.4142bp`, beside22.5bp best-leg.
- If FHA's action-scale proxy were multiplied by20%, the same illustration is31.8051bp, not~50bp; the two-book50bp break-even share is42.1858%. FHA-only `50/260 =19.23%` is a **different**, unweighted calculation.
- CARL KB401 also carried a false counterexample: swapping the two wedge values under these same weights gives185.0858bp, **not~30bp**. That example is withdrawn as a below-threshold justification. The ruled weights and historical97.4/22.5pair are unchanged.

No illustrative share is a measured treatment effect. Gross/repeated actions cannot carry a scored no-kill; absent causal evidence cannot carry a scored strike. Modification relief can improve reported status while lifetime losses are already in an allowance or fair-value markdown.

## Recommendation / review boundary

Keep the class-wide test **NOT COMPUTABLE**, with a named observability defect. Existing Q3 tables remain useful for the original tell-list: concession depth, cohort performance/defaults, program exits, stock/flow decomposition and recognized losses. They do not alone provide the missing counterfactual or common aggregation basis.

A possible **separate**, descriptive successor would track compatible issuer-cohort performance after relief, or measure a mechanical shadow delinquency status where loan-level schedules/payments permit it. Neither is proposed as a registered replacement here; thresholds, eligible population, window, overlap and revision policy would need a concrete draft and Will/PROME review. No50bp rule change, early grade or replacement confidence is encoded under this methods assessment.
