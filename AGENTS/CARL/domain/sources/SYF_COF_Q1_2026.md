# SYF + COF Q1 2026 — CARL Earnings Catch-Up
**Date:** 2026-04-29 | **Covers:** SYF Q1 2026 (released Apr 21 premarket) + COF Q1 2026 (released Apr 21 AMC)

---

## VERDICT

**SYF: GUIDANCE CEILING CLEARED — CRL-12 STRESS REDUCED.** Q1 NCO 5.42% (down 96bps YoY from 6.38%). SYF revised FY2026 NCO guidance DOWN to "less than 5.5%" from prior range of ~5.5–6.0%. This undercuts CRL-12 (SYF FY2026 NCO >6.0%) in the near term. HOWEVER, composition signals are mixed: super-prime shift is deliberate and real, but active accounts are declining (-0.7% YoY) and new account growth (15%) is front-weighted to higher-FICO cohorts whose loss curves are deferred, not absent. ACL ratio INCREASED 36bps QoQ to 10.42% despite improving NCO — management building reserves while headline improves (ALLY framework Axis 3: ambiguous, not a clear release). No segment NCO breakdowns disclosed. No CFPB late fee commentary. 30+ DQ 4.54% (up 2bps YoY) — flat but NOT improving.

**COF: COMPOSITION-MASKING PATTERNS CONFIRMED — ALLY ANALOG PRESENT.** Domestic card NCO 5.1% (down 109bps YoY, up 17bps QoQ — seasonal). Card 30+ DQ 3.7% (improving). HOWEVER: auto originations +21% YoY with management explicitly disclosing "slightly higher subprime mix" drove Consumer Banking ACL BUILD of $155M. Portfolio coverage 5.28% (+12bps). Total ACL built $230M in quarter. This is textbook Axis 2 (origination composition shift) + Axis 3 (ACL build = management sees forward deterioration). Auto book growing into tariff/oil-shock headwinds with admitted subprime expansion. Domestic card "resilient" reads as survivorship bias (worse borrowers already exited via charge-off or Discover credit tightening). Discover "brownout" = prior vintage was cleaned; new COF-platform originations (+8% of Discover book, full transition end Q3) will reflect COF's nonprime skew going forward.

**K-SHAPE IMPLICATION:** SYF shift toward super-prime is K-shape WIDENING behavior — the private-label / subprime end of SYF's book (Home & Auto -3.7% receivables YoY, Lifestyle -1.3%) is contracting as those borrowers self-select out or lose access. The healthier headline is a survivor-pool story, not credit improvement in the bottom 60%. COF's 21% auto origination growth into "higher subprime mix" is exactly the payment-hierarchy next-domino signal — the near-prime auto segment is absorbing the borrowers that subprime ABS trusts are now shedding.

## CARL IMPLICATIONS

- **CRL-12 (SYF FY2026 NCO >6.0%) confidence DROP:** From 77% to ~55%. SYF has guided to <5.5% for FY2026 and Q1 actuals of 5.42% are tracking below that. The prediction was a ceiling breach; SYF is now guiding to stay well below. Retain at reduced confidence pending Q2 — if gas at $4.50+ passes through to consumer stress by Q3, SYF lower-income cohorts (CareCredit, Home & Auto) could pressure back up. But barring a macro shock materializing in Q2-Q3 P&L, the prediction is likely to resolve MISS.
- **COF auto book = ALLY analog, forward stress window 2H 2026 / Q1 2027:** +21% origination growth + admitted subprime mix increase + $155M ACL build = payment hierarchy thesis intact, timeline Q3 2026 / Q1 2027. This is the same delay pattern as ALLY — headline OK now, cohort deteriorates on 12-18 month lag.
- **K-shape WIDENING at SYF, not closing:** Home & Auto receivables -3.7% YoY = bottom-of-K borrowers losing access to private-label credit (furniture, home improvement, auto parts). These are the borrowers CARL thesis needs to be deteriorating — and they are exiting the SYF book, which is why the headline looks cleaner. They do not disappear; they migrate to BNPL/cash advance or default. Strengthens phantom debt thesis.
- **K-shape MIGRATION confirmed at COF:** Near-prime auto borrowers absorbing subprime origination as COF grows 21% into that segment. This is the payment hierarchy transition: subprime auto ABS (EART Class E CE breached) → now near-prime auto lenders (COF) expanding into the same credit tier.
- **Gas/tariff stress NOT yet visible in Q1 P&L:** Both companies flagged "no adverse effects yet" on portfolio. Brent at $110-$115 as of Apr 29 will not appear in consumer credit P&L until Q2-Q3 2026 (2-3 week pump lag + 30-90 day delinquency formation lag). Q2 2026 earnings (July) will be the first read. Danger Window Q2-Q3 2026 CONFIRMED as the test quarter.
- **ACL builds at COF = management not complacent:** $230M build citing "potential downside economic scenarios" and specifically auto subprime + vehicle values. Management sees forward risk even on clean trailing numbers. This is the opposite of ALLY's complacency ("consumer behavior resilient, disconnect with sentiment"). COF is hedging; ALLY was not.
