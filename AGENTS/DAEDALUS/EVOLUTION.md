### 2026-09-12 (u) — the read-cap instrument stops guessing: `READS.tsv` replaces the charter heuristic where a desk has attested

**DOCKET L209, R7-stage-2.** `scripts/read_cap_check.py` now consumes `PROME/registry/READS.tsv` (PROME's declaration half, born 8/31). **Precedence: a desk's own ATTESTED manifest beats a scan of its charter.** Three states, deliberately three: **DECLARED+ATTESTED** → graded on the declaration · **DECLARED, NOT attested** → **rc 2 UNKNOWN, failing closed rather than back to the heuristic** (the manifest's own ⛔: a check over a partial perimeter that reports clean is the exact defect the file exists to retire) · **UNDECLARED** → heuristic unchanged, with an explicit caveat on the clean line.
