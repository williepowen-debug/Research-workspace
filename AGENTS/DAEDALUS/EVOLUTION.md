### 2026-08-20 (f) — STRICT_TEXT rule 7 gains a rounding clause (FLG build, measured live)

**Change:** rule 7 (threshold registration) now states that a level must not be typed from the **printed/rounded form of the observation it describes**.

**Why, measured not reasoned:** REGINALD's 168-bank-quarter cohort base rate published `CO/prov ≥ 14.6× occurs 1/166 = 0.6%`. The observation under test computes to **14.5959×**, and **14.6 was its own one-decimal display value** — so the unrounded datum fell just outside a threshold derived from its own printed form. **Prescription:** carry the unrounded figure on the row, or set the cut from the DISTRIBUTION (a percentile, a median multiple) rather than from the datum — **a level derived from an observation is not an independent test of that observation.**

**Provenance worth preserving (the part that is about review behaviour, not arithmetic):** DAEDALUS spotted the 1-vs-2 count discrepancy **and then supplied a charitable explanation for it** — *"I assume you excluded the observation under test, which is the right method"* — which was **wrong**. The general form is REGINALD's and is now fleet memory `finding_a_charitable_reading_of_your_work_is_the_one_to_check`: **an accusation gets checked because being wrong is costly; a charitable explanation gets accepted because it costs nothing and confirms competence — so the reading most likely to enter the record unverified is the flattering one.** Directly binding on this desk, which reviews other agents' work for a living: **supplying the generous explanation closes the discrepancy without testing it.** Grandfathering follows rule 7's existing next-write-only convention.
