**(1) `READ_CAP.md` rule 16 — the scoped/whole discriminator is ADDRESSABILITY, not the boot verb.** Rule 14 (9/1) said the declaration follows the boot VERB and left *"literally scopes it"* to the reader. New: *a read is `scoped` only if the scope is addressable WITHOUT reading the whole — a named section, a marked block, a bounded head/tail, a sorted or indexed region; a predicate over unindexed rows ("scan for items where X") is a WHOLE read with a scoped OUTPUT.* **WHY, measured:** adjudicating `AGENTS/NEXUS/PREDICTIONS_MONITOR.md` (WQ-163 item 1, Will-routed because NEXUS is the interested party).
