### 2026-09-12 (u) — the read-cap instrument stops guessing: `READS.tsv` replaces the charter heuristic where a desk has attested

**DOCKET L209, R7-stage-2.** `scripts/read_cap_check.py` now consumes `PROME/registry/READS.tsv` (PROME's declaration half, born 8/31). **Precedence: a desk's own ATTESTED manifest beats a scan of its charter.** Three states, deliberately three: **DECLARED+ATTESTED** → graded on the declaration · **DECLARED, NOT attested** → **rc 2 UNKNOWN, failing closed rather than back to the heuristic** (the manifest's own ⛔: a check over a partial perimeter that reports clean is the exact defect the file exists to retire) · **UNDECLARED** → heuristic unchanged, with an explicit caveat on the clean line.

**The measurement that justified it, and it went the opposite way from the brief.** PROME's verdict read `✅ 1 boot-mandated read` against a 29-row declared perimeter. The heuristic is not empty; it is *systematically narrow, in the silent direction*.

**The design decision worth recording: the rollout is the gating work, not the code.** `--require-manifest` (strict) exists and is **opt-in**, because flipping the default turns ~35 desks rc 2 at once and ~14 desks invoke this tool in a closeout step. A desk must declare and attest **itself** — no desk may commit inside `PROME/`, and `PROME(from-charter)` can never attest. **The honest end state is strict-by-default; the dated flip is Will's call and is asked, not taken.**

**Mode handling keeps the manifest's two defect axes separate:** `whole`/`programmatic` are CAP-BEARING and measured; `scoped`/`grep`/`summary` are printed and **never counted**. Collapsing them would re-create the false breach the `mode` column exists to prevent. **Three states the heuristic structurally could not produce** now exist: a declared path **absent from disk** (a scan finds only what exists, so a manifest pointing at a deleted file reads as silence), a mode outside the vocabulary, and an attestation signed by someone other than the reader.
