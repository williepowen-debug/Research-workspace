## 2. Why standalone rather than a REGINALD sub-agent

Every prior per-bank agent (OZK Apr, WAL Jul) was a **promotion** of an existing sub-tree. FLG had none, so this is the fleet's **first greenfield per-bank build**.

Seed-then-promote (the WAL path) was rejected deliberately: it places the new corpus inside the desk that measurably cannot maintain it (REGINALD: 15 unprocessed inbox packets, VX.tsv +126d, THESIS trigger expired). **WAL's own promotion was justified on exactly that finding** — *"the WAL fossil surfaces rotted while REGINALD's hub surfaces stayed current."* Repeating it knowingly would re-run a diagnosed failure. Full option analysis: proposal §3.
