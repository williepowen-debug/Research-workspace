## 5. THE DAY-ONE FALSIFIABLE INSTRUMENT (condition ⓷ — the crux)

The stated #1 risk is that an actor desk with no falsifier **degrades into commentary** — concretely, the **TWN-01 defect HAWK designed against tonight**: months of evidence accreting against a judgement-scored "aggression" band that can never be graded. ⛔ **The instrument is therefore NOT a band on "Russian aggression"** — it is a set of dated questions each of which *can be wrong on a date*, and each of which **can return UNDETERMINED** (we hold no imagery/SIGINT/paid OSINT; FIRMS unavailable, WQ-238 — *an instrument that cannot say "unknown" will manufacture answers*). Two-part instrument, both satisfying blueprint §4 (positively-measured, dated, non-conjunctive):

### 5a. `workbook/INTENT_LEDGER.tsv` — the thesis-level falsifier (dated, resolves in ≤2 weeks)
Formalises the exact row shape PROME **already wrote** in DOCKET L432/L433/L434. Each row = one Russian-state-action hypothesis with:
- a **dated resolver** and a **primary-source-only close rule** (a press-cycle claim is NOT a close — L432);
- **positive measurement** (PAT-060): the row names what DECIDED / NOT-DECIDED / **UNDETERMINED** looks like, so "no news" (absence) ≠ "not yet decided" (signal-of-absence). *UNDETERMINED is a legitimate, often the LIKELY, close* (L433) — recording it is the point;
- **kill-on-sight interested-party guards** (L432 belligerent-claim fence; L433/L434 kill-strings);
- an **if-confirmed ROUTING action** naming which pricing desk it feeds (the feeder contract).

**Falsifiable on Day One** — three live rows already resolve: **9/21 mobilisation** (L432), **9/25 Suwałki equipment-return** (L433), **10/02 Nestlé/Auchan seizure→sale** (L434). ⚠️ **These three are currently HAWK-owned (PROME registered them before the desk was named).** Moving or co-owning them into YURI's ledger is a Will+DAEDALUS decision folded into the §3 boundary ruling — not PROME's to reassign. Home: standalone dated TSV so the **Falsification Freshness Sweep can date it** (blueprint §4 ★); two-clock header (PAT-044); token enum in header (STATE_VOCABULARY).

### 5b. The DESK-level falsifier — the anti-commentary test (this is what actually protects against rot)
The desk's whole premise is that holding-the-actor-whole yields a view no channel desk can. So the desk's OWN existence is falsifiable:

> **By 2026-10-18 (30 days), YURI has produced ≥3 actor calls each citing ≥2 channels held by DIFFERENT desks and NOT present on any single channel desk's surface at the time of the call. If 0 such calls, the desk's premise is falsified → retire or re-merge into HAWK.**

This directly targets the failure mode PROME rates most likely, is dated, and is not gameable by narrating (a call must be cross-channel AND absent from the channel desks to count). Recorded on the charter as the desk's standing self-falsifier.
