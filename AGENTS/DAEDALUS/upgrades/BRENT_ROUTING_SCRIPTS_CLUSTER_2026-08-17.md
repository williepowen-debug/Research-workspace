That is the tell, and it is the F3 defect the 8/04 consolidation was ruled to close, still half-open. It matters most because **this morning's false-green fix was installed into the shadow loop** — so the guarantee "no threshold is silently absent" is true of a hardcoded list and not of the registry the fix's own docstring points at. Alongside it sits a smaller instance of the same class on my side of the fence: two DAEDALUS surfaces record `basis_check` as BRENT's live class-5 enforcement arm, and I cannot find it in the code.

**Nothing here impeaches BRENT's judgment layer** — the GASREGW `DEAD` flag from 08:27 was chased down, re-probed, correctly called transient, and written to three surfaces before I arrived.
