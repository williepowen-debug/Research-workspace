## The finding in one line

**A channel can be alive, correctly scored, and accumulating strong filing-primary evidence while its upgrade trigger is structurally incapable of registering any of that evidence — not because the metric surface is missing, but because the band is keyed to a *different mechanism* than the one the channel is actually evolving through.**

## Why this is a new form, not another instance

The registered finding covers **no metric vector ⇒ untrippable threshold**. This desk has hit that twice and fixed both: `mag7.py` v1 measured concentration *level* with no breadth leg, so S1's red conjunction was untrippable; `tsmc_watch.py` v1 could not parse accounting-parenthesis negatives, so S4's red band (*decline*) was untrippable. **Both were absent-instrument cases.**

**This is the opposite.** The instrument is present, current, issuer-primary and working. What is missing is any **band pointed at it**.

## The evidence — two independent channels, same morning

**S5 — AI-infra financing.** All three registered upgrade triggers are **credit-market pricing events**:

|---|---|
| cleared new issue **+150bp flex or PULLED** | new-issue execution |

What S5 actually did this quarter, per NVDA's 10-Q filed 2026-08-26 (acc `0001045810-26-000075`): the guarantee book went **$3.5B → $108.5B (31×)**; a **~$36B take-or-pay backstop** on customers' capacity appeared; **$500B** of financing platforms were announced at MOU stage; and the issuer **stated the rating-level mechanism itself** (*"customers lack the ability to secure… investment-grade financing capacity"*; IG enterprises self-finance, sub-IG get NVDA's balance sheet). **Not one of those is a priced new issue, a second jurisdiction, or a failure to post.** Score correctly held at 3, and a reader scanning only the band states would see a quiet channel.

**S2 — Memory cycle.** Upgrade trigger is **contract price −25% QoQ**, a *cycle-roll* test. This quarter's S2 evidence arrived as **supply lock-up** (NVDA supply-and-capacity commitments $119B → $279B in one quarter, *"primarily related to the procurement of memory"*) and **cost-push consumer destruction** (NVDA Edge: *"slower consumer PC sales… tempered by elevated memory and systems prices"* — the first issuer-primary corroboration of a leg that had rested on a modelled BOM). **The band can only register a roll. It cannot register either of these, in either direction.**
