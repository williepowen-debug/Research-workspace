## Verdict

WAL is a strong research desk with a weak front-of-house. Its core discipline is the fleet's reference standard: frames pre-registered before the filing, graded verbatim, with dated notes only. The Q3 10-Q frame was frozen today with pre-filing anchors pinned, so the frame is ready. The problems are around that core:

- **Stale analysis surfaces.** The post-Q3 thesis judgment is deferred with no date and no pre-registered mapping. THESIS, WEAKNESSES and the charter's identity block still describe the pre-print world.
- **No forward predictions.** After the 10-Q the ledger will hold no OPEN rows, and none are registered for Q4.
- **Unreadable live surfaces.** STATUS, MEMORY, POSITIONS, INDEX, CHANGELOG and REGINALD_CHANNEL have glued words from the 10/14–10/19 Codex sessions. STATUS stacks "Prior" blocks and has no BOTTOM LINE.
- **Two instruments misreport.**
  - The fleet `ledger_staleness.py` reads MI3_SERIES.tsv, a live ledger, as FROZEN. This silences the alert on the V1a falsifier series just before the Q3 Call Report lands.
  - The desk's `predictions_due_check.py` counts the resolved WAL-02 as OPEN.
- **Closeout gaps.** The closeout checklist omits root steps 1b–1e. The REGINALD pair channel is a write-only log that REGINALD never reads.

None of this threatens the L4 grade by itself, but several items are L3/L4 legs (dated falsification surface, predictions resolving). On the next adjudication they should be checked, not assumed.
