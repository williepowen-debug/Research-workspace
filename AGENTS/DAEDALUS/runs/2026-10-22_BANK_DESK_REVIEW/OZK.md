It graded in two stages, sent its work to consumers, and REGINALD's tree shows those consumer receipts. Its 10/21 self-sweep (36 rows) was honest, and most of its 🔴/🟠 items were fixed within a day.

The weaknesses sit where a print-driven desk is weakest: the surfaces between prints. Three problems stand out.

1. **The thesis apparatus did not move with the evidence.** The kill table, SCENARIOS (EV $38.97, Bear 55%, unchanged since 4/23), WEAKNESSES (no mention of the RaDD extension or of Q3) and the subdomain "pillar" home are all Q2 vintage or older. The KB/prediction layer is large but under-maintained: 156 of 249 ACTIVE KB rows are past their own Stale_By date, and no prediction has been registered since 7/04.
