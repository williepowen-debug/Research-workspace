# STRICT TEXT — ten rules for cost-bearing text (fleet build standard)

**Owner:** DAEDALUS · **Created:** 2026-07-31 (Will-approved; provenance: STE/ASD-STE100 adaptation assessment, `design/2026-07-31_STE_WRITING_STANDARD_ASSESSMENT.md` §3B — the "10-line ruleset" option, not the 900-word dictionary).

**Why:** our cost-bearing text is read by LLM sessions with limited context — the modern version of STE's stressed non-native mechanic. A misread packet ACTION line is misexecuted; a hedge-stacked claim can't be graded; a vague error message converts a warning into a post-mortem. Banked incident classes: paraphrase drift corrupting a redelivered instruction ([[finding_backstop_redelivery_is_a_paraphrase_not_a_copy]]), summary compression inverting a meaning ([[finding_triage_summary_compression_inversion]]), PASS lines that mean nothing (PAT-074).

## Scope — STRICT applies ONLY where a wrong reading has a cost

| Surface class | Mode |
|---|---|
| Packet **ACTION/ASK lines** · script output & error messages · gate/threshold/kill specs · the **letter** of a prediction · checklist steps | **STRICT (these 10 rules)** |
| Thesis docs, research prose, audit narratives, STATUS bodies | Output Canon as-is (root `CLAUDE.md §Output Canon`) — dense is fine, line caps force it |
| Will-facing synthesis (Telegram, dashboards) | **VOICE — exempt.** Never lint the operator's reading experience |

## The ten rules

1. **One instruction per sentence** in ACTION blocks; target ≤20 words. Four asks = four sentences (or a numbered list).
2. **Active voice with a named actor.** "REGINALD re-points row 7" — not "row 7 should be re-pointed." An instruction without an actor is a wish.
3. **Verbs, not nominalizations.** "Re-grade X" — not "perform a re-grading of X."
4. **At most one hedge per claim carrying a number** — and prefer a stated probability/confidence tier over any hedge word. "May potentially help to reduce" is zero gradable content.
5. **One name per thing per document.** Entity, metric, file, agent: pick the name, keep it. State tokens come from `BLUEPRINTS/STATE_VOCABULARY.md`.
6. **Every number carries unit + source + as-of date** (root Output Canon, restated here as the per-sentence checkable form — no naked numbers). **On a load-bearing figure, the source must be the ISSUER-PRIMARY where reachable** (Freddie for PMMS, Treasury for DGS, USBR for Powell, WGC for CB gold, etc.); an aggregator (FRED, Yahoo, vendor cache) is a MIRROR and must be cited as one — carry the token `PRIMARY`, `MIRROR`, or `MIRROR-WALLED` (STATE_VOCABULARY Class 6). A `MIRROR` citation on a load-bearing figure with a reachable primary is a defect. Next-write-only: existing citations are grandfathered. Ruling: `PROME/proposals/2026-08-14_rows-49-50-RULED.md` §Row 49; worked example: `AGENTS/HOMER/workbook/RATES.tsv` from 2026-08-13. **A quantitative TSV column (numbers a reader will aggregate) carries a unit/basis field, and the `0`/blank cells carry a STATE_VOCABULARY Class 5 token** — bare `0` in a quantitative column is not a claim. Ruling: `PROME/proposals/2026-08-12_audit-convention-RULED.md` §Ruling 1. **Where two vintages of ONE figure are both legitimately current — an as-made scoring value and a live belief, a headline and a restated figure, a frozen-card value and a working value — the LIVE value takes the most-read position and the scoring/archival value rides beside it as a labelled companion, never the reverse** (`live 35% · scores as-made 65%`). Ordering is the whole mechanism: a reader who does not know the desk's convention picks up whatever sits in the headline position, and no supersession marker exists to catch it because nothing is superseded (LABOR/RED 2026-08-27 — RED registered a counter-forecast against a confidence LABOR had abandoned 21 days earlier; `consumer_check` is blind by construction). Convention note, not a tool.
7. **Every threshold carries LEVEL + INSTRUMENT + WINDOW + REVISION POLICY** ([[finding_guard_scope_expires_at_the_fill]], [[finding_number_carries_threshold_unit_source]]). "HY OAS ≥300bp (ICE BofA, 3 consecutive sessions, GRADE-ON-REVISION)" — not "spreads blow out." **INSTRUMENT names the ticker/series that grades it** — a registry may name a CONCEPT, but the tool resolves an INSTRUMENT, and the two disagree silently (BRENT F-2 futures-vs-spot; RED S30 registry-basis n=4; `finding_registry_names_a_concept_tool_resolves_an_instrument`). **A continuous rolling series is not a contract** — if the threshold is keyed to one (`BZ=F`, `CL=F`, `GC=F`), pin it to a named contract with a documented roll rule OR record the roll adjustment on the row; a bare continuous series lets the roll itself move a threshold ($2.2 = 2.6% of the $85 line, BRENT F-3, 2026-08-03 roll). **REVISION POLICY** is one of {`GRADE-AT-PUBLICATION`, `GRADE-ON-REVISION`, `REGRADE-ON-REVISION`}: does the threshold grade once at the first print, does a revision that would newly cross the line COUNT AS A FIRE (WGC 244t → 57t revision crossed the 100t kill line, invisible to the rail; MIDAS, 2026-08-07), or does an already-graded row re-grade from scratch when the underlying series is revised? A threshold with no policy stated defaults to `GRADE-AT-PUBLICATION` (backward-compatible with pre-convention rows) AND fails the STRICT_TEXT check on next-write. **Post-fire treatment when a revision lands (Will-ratified 2026-08-14):** an already-fired threshold defaults to `STAND-WITH-ANNOTATION` — the fire remains as historical record with the revision annotated beside it, honoring `finding_record_of_an_action_is_not_the_action` and the H-1 non-latching precedent. `REGRADE-ON-REVISION` rows may un-fire; `GRADE-AT-PUBLICATION` rows never re-open. Ruling: `PROME/proposals/2026-08-12_rule-batch-RULED.md` row 36b. **Fleet needed before 2026-08-28 (MIDAS-06 grades).** **⚠️ AND THE LEVEL MUST NOT BE TYPED FROM THE PRINTED FORM OF THE OBSERVATION IT DESCRIBES** *(added 2026-08-20, measured live)*: a cut copied from a rounded display value will misclassify the very datum it was derived from. REGINALD's cohort base rate reported `CO/prov ≥ 14.6× occurs 1/166`; the observation computes to **14.5959×** and 14.6 was its own one-decimal display, so the datum fell just outside a threshold taken from its own printed form. Self-corrected `8b5a65231`; the published count had been **right by accident**, and an accident is not repeatable. **Carry the unrounded figure on the row, or set the cut from the DISTRIBUTION (a percentile, a median multiple) rather than from the datum** — a level derived from the observation is not an independent test of it. Pairs with `finding_threshold_level_is_a_measurement_not_a_constant` (state FROZEN vs TRACKED) and with the base-rating rule that a KILL is base-rated against the same cohort as its SIGNAL (PAT-119). **FORWARD REGISTRATION BUNDLE (Will-ruled 2026-08-27, verbatim *"Approve A class-wide plus the forward bundle - go ahead"*, record `PROME/proposals/2026-08-27_moving-referent-class-RULED.md`; MIDAS moving-referent class n=3; encoded here 2026-08-28 at the wiring sweep — FORWARD-ONLY, no live registered row is touched):** **(a)** the INSTRUMENT names the **exact contract**, or the continuous ticker **plus its declared roll handling** — a bare vendor continuous ticker is **no longer a registrable referent form** (this hardens the roll sentence above from "pin it OR record the adjustment" to a registration requirement). **(b)** a tracked/derived baseline enters a criteria cell as a **frozen value + as-of date pair with the anchor TYPE named** (VULCAN's `anchor_type` convention, 2026-08-21); the frozen pair is the graded referent, never the live series. **(c)** a row keyed to a **lagged-publication series** carries **observation date and read date as two separate fields** (companion to `PROME/proposals/2026-08-27_lagged-series-grade-date-RULED.md`). **(d) — same family, CREED 2026-08-27 (`CREED-T-03`: a real, correct, current document that does not contain the cited 3.40%):** a baseline cited to an external document names the **exact locator inside it — table, column, row-label** — so a reader dereferences in one minute; a document name alone is where that defect lived. The mechanical roll check (roll = a TICKER property ⇒ grep every registered spec for the ticker, never only the row about to fire) sits at fleet memory `registered_gate` limb 4; this bundle is its registration-time complement. Whether the existing STOCK of futures-/baseline-keyed rows gets a census is a separate, Will-priced question (`finding_a_ruling_governs_the_next_write_not_the_existing_state`). **REGISTRATION JOIN RULE (WQ-86, Will 2026-09-01; canonical text in the `PROME/GATES.tsv` header — cite, don't restate), two symmetric lines: ① a gate/threshold ratified at a DESK is routed to `PROME/GATES.tsv` at fire-time — the owner's one-line duty; ② a ledger row naming a NON-SELF owner is UNDELIVERED until that owner has a read-path — the registrar's duty. A threshold row is not registered until both halves of the join exist; the weekly spine audit reconciles ACROSS the join by whoever owns neither side (`design/2026-09-01_SPINE_AUDIT_JOIN_AXIS.md`).
8. **No unresolved pronoun across a sentence boundary in instructions.** Repeat the noun; "it/this/that" pointing at the previous sentence is where misexecution starts.
9. **A status/error message states what it searched and what a null result means** (PAT-074 — audit a check by what its PASS means). "0 ledgers found in workbook/*.tsv (3 TSVs exist outside the glob)" — never a bare benign line.
10. **Redelivery is a copy, never a paraphrase.** Restating another agent's instruction/figure = quote it verbatim with its source stamp.

## What this is not

- Not a style gate — no linter enforces it (ruled DO-NOT-BUILD, assessment §3C: a mechanized style screen false-positives against the best-documented agents; deliberate density and sloppy density are byte-identical from the condition alone).
- Not a prose standard — STATUS/thesis density is deliberate (line caps) and stays.
- Not a vocabulary whitelist — finance vocabulary is the content, not the noise.

**Applied at:** packet templates, new-build script messages, gate/threshold registration (DAEDALUS checks at build — REGISTRATION_CHECKLIST row 15). Blueprint variants cite this file — they do not restate the rules.
