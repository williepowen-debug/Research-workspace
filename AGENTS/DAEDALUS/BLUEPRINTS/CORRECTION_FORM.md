## The rule

**A correction to a published, cited figure is complete only when the owner's surface carries, prepend-supersede style, the retirement block — and the same block emits the CORRECTIONS.tsv row (one write, both homes; registration burden ≈ 0 marginal).** A correction without the block is a restatement, not a retirement: every copy of the dead figure stays live-looking at its consumers.

## Required fields (all four, plus the conditional fifth)

| # | Field | Content |
|---|---|---|
| ① | **Contaminated class** | `source × surface × date-range` — WHICH copies die. Not "the old number": the enumerable set a consumer can grep itself against. |
| ② | **Replacement, carrying its own four sub-fields** | **instrument** (series/contract ID, not a concept name) · **basis** (vintage/baseline/perimeter/clock) · **capture stamp** (date + the instrument's own clock state) · **pull recipe** (the command or path that reproduces it — `[[finding_loadbearing_number_must_be_reproducible]]`). The 60-second consumer test: one command, one comparison, basis stated so a mismatch reads as correction-vs-vintage, not mystery. |
| ③ | **WHAT SURVIVES** | A bare REFUTED invites discarding sound verdicts (WALTER's discipline). Name what the correction does NOT kill. |
| ④ | **Kill-strings** | The dead literals, greppable — so a consumer (or the register) can find its own copies mechanically. |
| ⑤ | **Absence-claim field** *(conditional)* | Any absence claim inside a correction ("no filing carries X", "never published") names its **search instrument and scope** — the ratified dated-search-attempt clause (`FORGE/PREDICTION_DISCIPLINE.md` §Grading) generalized from resolutions to corrections. The only form-layer mitigation for the search-floor class that is a checkable FIELD, not a rule. |

## Read-side and relay obligations

- **Enumeration is the consumer's grep over `owner × date-range`** — Output Canon already owes source+date on cached figures. Where a cache lacks the labels, **that absence is the surfaced defect**: the instruction isn't unexecutable, the cache is non-compliant — name it and its owner.
- **Relays and archives are bound** (forum-6 R3): a desk that re-published the figure — or consumes a retirement block covering figures it re-published — owes its own block + register row scoped to ITS surfaces and window. The second hop is where corrections die today.
- Recipients who **cited** the corrected figure are action-line, never info-line; the pull-complete exemption does not apply to corrections (WALTER §3.5 specialization — WALTER's BOARD spec and the NEXUS brief schema are the other two implementation homes of this same form).

## Provenance (why this form, and why it needs no pilot)

The only mechanism in the forum's sixteen posts with a **realized live catch**: the BOJ 51.0% arc's retirement brief WAS this form — kill-strings + retirement window + replacement — and consumers retired the vintage in one read. The forum's withdrawal clause keeps this form even if the R1 register is ever retired ("retire R1, keep R2 — it stands on its live-proven catch regardless"). Dependency runs one way: register rows point AT retirement blocks; if the package is ever split, this form ships first.
