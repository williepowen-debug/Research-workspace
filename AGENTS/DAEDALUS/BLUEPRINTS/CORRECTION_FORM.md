## The rule

**A correction to a published, cited figure is complete only when the owner's surface carries, prepend-supersede style, the retirement block — and the same block emits the CORRECTIONS.tsv row (one write, both homes; registration burden ≈ 0 marginal).** A correction without the block is a restatement, not a retirement: every copy of the dead figure stays live-looking at its consumers.

### C1 — THE PASSENGER RULE (correction-class validation, D1 APPROVED)
**Separate the KILL from the REPLACEMENT.** A retraction packet carries only: the dead figure · the cause · the holders. Any NEW analytical claim that rides inside a retraction ships tagged **`UNVALIDATED`** (or as its own packet) — it arrives as a fresh claim at the start of its life, never as a passenger on the fleet's highest-authority document class. *Instance: LIQUID KB-LIQ-106 ×2 — a retraction carried a new wrong "~6bp" claim that died only because PROME said it was going to Will.* Kin: C2 (`market-agent.md` §4) applies the one-alternative pass to correction-born findings BEFORE they leave the desk.

## Read-side and relay obligations

- **Enumeration is the consumer's grep over `owner × date-range`** — Output Canon already owes source+date on cached figures. Where a cache lacks the labels, **that absence is the surfaced defect**: the instruction isn't unexecutable, the cache is non-compliant — name it and its owner.
- **Relays and archives are bound** (forum-6 R3): a desk that re-published the figure — or consumes a retirement block covering figures it re-published — owes its own block + register row scoped to ITS surfaces and window. The second hop is where corrections die today.
- Recipients who **cited** the corrected figure are action-line, never info-line; the pull-complete exemption does not apply to corrections (WALTER §3.5 specialization — WALTER's BOARD spec and the NEXUS brief schema are the other two implementation homes of this same form).
- **C3 — ADJACENT-CELL RULE (D1 APPROVED, RED's check verbatim in substance):** on applying any INBOUND correction, before you fix it, **re-pull every OTHER dated quantity on the same row from its primary**, and log any stale one as a SEPARATE defect with its own provenance — never fold it into the flagger's record (the flagger gets credit for a catch it did not make and the desk's own miss disappears). The flagger's scope is structurally narrow; the receiving desk supplies the width. *Instance: RED KB-RED-067 — the same row wrong on three consecutive passes; the second fire found two defects beyond the flag.* **HANS corollary (2026-09-18, ML-HANS-451): a DERIVED row is recomputed from its LEGS at their own primaries, never patched with a supplied difference** — a reviewer's arithmetic is correct as of the reviewer's read, and the legs move between review and remediation (the Fed hiked 25bp in the gap; the "wrong" 137.5 was accidentally right and the supplied 112.5 would have manufactured the defect).
- **RECEIPT ACTIONS — terminal vs nonterminal (D2 item 2):** `APPLIED` and `NO-OP` are terminal; `DEFERRED` / `CONTESTED` / missing are nonterminal. A post-word `APPLIED` carries `artifact=<path#stable-key>`; a post-word `NO-OP` carries `scope=<what was checked>`. **EVIDENCE-SYNTAX CUTOFF (D3 (a), forward-only from the word, 2026-09-24 14:59 ET):** receipts dated after the word carry those keys; receipts dated before count as terminal-by-action for the `RECEIPTED` rung, and a row holding any such receipt reaches `RETIRED` only if the target RE-RECEIPTS with evidence — voluntary, never swept. Pre-word receipts are listed `PRE-WORD`, never as defects. **Register-status ladder** (D2 item 1) → `STATE_VOCABULARY.md` Class 11 (the mapping lives there, not here).
- **DEAD-AT-CAP never clears a NAMED target (D4 (a), A3):** a passed `date_cap` changes the ROW's status (WALTER's prune) and never the named target's obligation — `corrections_boot_check.py` keeps blocking a named target with no receipt, labelled DEAD-AT-CAP, until a receipt of any action exists. `date_cap` governs `ALL`-rows (broadcast expiry) and row status only.
- **Two deterministic closure legs, adopted not built (D8 = WQ-185 (b)/(c)):** *filing ≠ application* — an `APPLIED` receipt whose `artifact=` path has NO commit between the correction date and the receipt date reads `UNAPPLIED` (`git log --since --until -- <path>`; line-number keys ⇒ CANNOT-EVALUATE); *corrected premise invalidates dependents* — `python3 scripts/consumer_check.py --agent <corrector> --old <kill-string>` is the instrument; HOLD/WEAKEN/FLIP per dependent stays a human call. The `--closure <COR-id>` mode (D7) is built AFTER D3+D4 land, ceiling `RECEIPTED-WITH-POINTER`, never `CLOSED-VERIFIED`.

## Provenance (why this form, and why it needs no pilot)

The only mechanism in the forum's sixteen posts with a **realized live catch**: the BOJ 51.0% arc's retirement brief WAS this form — kill-strings + retirement window + replacement — and consumers retired the vintage in one read. The forum's withdrawal clause keeps this form even if the R1 register is ever retired ("retire R1, keep R2 — it stands on its live-proven catch regardless"). Dependency runs one way: register rows point AT retirement blocks; if the package is ever split, this form ships first.
