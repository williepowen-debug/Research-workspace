# HAWK Phase 3.5 — US / Israel Military Posture Check

**Date:** 2026-05-22 12:50 EDT  
**Scope:** Narrow verification of Will's concern: US tanker / cargo / strike-support posture in Israel before Phase 4. No Phase 4-7, no KB update.

## Verdict

**Classification: PRESERVED STRIKE OPTION / elevated forward sustainment — not routine, but not enough for “active buildup” or “imminent strike prep” on public evidence.**

**Confidence: 0.72.**

The strongest verified posture evidence is Israeli reporting that **dozens of US refueling aircraft are stationed at Ben Gurion and Ramon**, creating enough civilian-airport disruption that Israel’s Civil Aviation Authority chief warned Ben Gurion is operating “like a military base.” The primary N12/Mako report says the American message is that the refuelers are expected to remain **at least until the end of the calendar year** — i.e. through end-2026 / into 2027 language. English rewrites, including JPost/ArmyRecognition, inflate or ambiguously render this as **“until the end of 2027.”** Treat the long-extension claim as **confirmed for end-2026 / at least into 2027 ambiguity**, not cleanly confirmed through end-2027.

The cargo/ammunition-flight claim is more escalatory but lower quality: Anadolu/MEM/ANI/Tribune cite **Channel 13 / Al Jazeera** saying **dozens of cargo planes carrying ammunition from US bases in Germany landed in Tel Aviv on May 18** and were part of preparations to resume war on Iran. Misbar independently monitored **8 outbound C-17-style flights toward the region and 6 returns** on May 17 via Flightradar24, mostly from Germany, but does **not** prove cargo contents, final delivery to Israel for every aircraft, or imminent strike timing.

Bottom line: the posture materially validates Will’s concern that the situation is **not closed**. It fits the Phase 3 “armed pause / controlled grind” frame: Trump deferred a prepared strike, while the enablers for rapid restart — tankers, logistics, carriers/MEU/MCM/blockade architecture — remain forward. But public evidence does not yet show the distinct signatures of imminent strike execution: public evacuation orders, NOTAM/airspace closures, bomber package launch, tanker bridge surge beyond the standing posture, US/Israeli official deadline language, or fresh CENTCOM strike orders.

## Specific checks requested

### KC-46 / KC-135 tankers at Ben Gurion, Ramon, or other Israel bases

**Confirmed in substance; exact current count and mix not fully confirmed.**

- Air & Space Forces confirms **KC-46 and KC-135** tankers flew to **Ben Gurion** during the Epic Fury buildup; F-22s went to **Ovda**.
- JNS/TPS gives a credible Israeli lower bound: **at least 14 American refueling aircraft at Ben Gurion**, plus additional US military aircraft at **Ovda**.
- N12/Mako says **dozens** of US refueling aircraft are positioned at **Ben Gurion and Ramon**.
- ArmyRecognition names KC-46A/KC-135R and Ramon but should be treated as lower-confidence because it expands N12 into technical specifics without transparent sourcing.

**Read:** this is too large and persistent to call routine. It is a forward refueling hub preserving the Iran-strike option.

### Cargo / ammunition flights from Germany or other US bases

**Partially verified: logistics airbridge likely; ammunition contents and strike-prep purpose lower confidence.**

- The Channel 13 / Al Jazeera / Anadolu claim says **dozens** of cargo planes carrying ammunition moved from US bases in Germany to Tel Aviv in a 24-hour period around May 18.
- Misbar flight-tracking observed **8 outbound and 6 return** C-17-style movements involving Ramstein/Spangdahlem/Trier and the Middle East / Eilat, supporting a logistics pulse.
- No cargo manifests, official statements, tail-by-tail AirNav table, or Reuters/AP confirmation found in this pass.

**Read:** treat as a yellow/orange posture indicator, not a confirmed imminent-strike trigger by itself.

### Extension through end-2026 / 2027 claims

**Confirmed as long-duration presence, but wording must be disciplined.**

- Hebrew N12/Mako: “at least until the end of the calendar year” — that means through end-2026, effectively “until 2027” in headline shorthand.
- JPost and ArmyRecognition render it as “until the end of 2027.” That stronger version is not supported by the fetched Hebrew primary.

**Correct wording:** “US refuelers expected to remain at least through end-2026 / into 2027; end-2027 claim is lower-confidence English-source drift unless N12 or US/Israeli officials clarify.”

### Carrier / MEU / MCM / air defense / other strike-support movements

**Confirmed as standing forward architecture, not a new May 22 strike surge.**

- Atlantic Council May 15: two available US carriers supporting Epic Fury; MCM assets moving/in theater; USS Canberra supporting blockade; Tripoli ARG/MEU committed, Boxer reportedly en route.
- HAWK/WALTER anchor: 31st MEU boarded Celestial Sea May 20; blockade tally 91 redirected vessels.

## What would move this from preserved option to active/imminent strike prep

### Moves to active buildup

1. Reuters/AP/Stars & Stripes/Air & Space Forces/official confirmation of **new** tanker/cargo arrivals after May 18, not just standing posture.
2. Flight-tail evidence showing a sustained **C-17/C-5 ammunition shuttle** Germany/CONUS → Israel for 2-3+ days with consistent callsigns and landing points.
3. Tanker count at Ben Gurion/Ramon/Ovda rising materially above the known **14+ / dozens** baseline.
4. Additional F-22/F-35/B-2/B-52/B-1 or EA/ISR/E-3/E-11 deployments into Israel/nearby bases tied to Iran planning.
5. THAAD/Patriot/Aegis ashore reloads or additional air-defense batteries entering Israel/Gulf specifically ahead of expected Iranian retaliation.
6. USS Boxer ARG/MEU arrival confirmed as reinforcement rather than replacement, or MCM assets visibly entering Hormuz operating areas.

### Moves to imminent strike prep

1. Israeli/US airspace closures, NOTAMs, airport civilian-flight halt, or large-scale diversions beyond existing disruption.
2. US/Israeli noncombatant evacuation / embassy drawdown / ordered departure.
3. Trump/DoD/Israeli official language shifts from “no hurry / few days” to deadline, ultimatum, “tonight,” or operational-security silence after NSC meeting.
4. Bomber package launch from CONUS/Diego Garcia/UK with tanker bridge aligned toward CENTCOM.
5. Multiple tankers airborne in racetrack/orbit patterns east Med / Jordan / Iraq corridors coincident with fighter launches.
6. Iranian preemptive dispersal, air-defense activation, or official warning of imminent attack from Tehran/IRGC.
