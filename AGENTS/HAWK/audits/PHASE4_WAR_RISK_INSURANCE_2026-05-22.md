# HAWK Phase 4 — Hormuz War-Risk / Insurance / Legal Mechanism

**Date:** 2026-05-22 13:15 EDT  
**Scope:** Hormuz maritime risk mechanism only: insurance/P&I availability, war-risk pricing, crew safety / owner appetite, sanctions/payment risk, PGSA/IRGC toll legality.  
**Context:** Phase 3 = selective Iran-controlled carve-outs / trickle flow (~10/day vs 125-140/day normal), not closed and not normalizing. Phase 3.5 = US/Israel armed pause, preserved strike option.

---

## Verdict

**Verdict:** The old HAWK shorthand “Hormuz is uninsurable / P&I refusing” should be **superseded as current mechanism language**. The best current mechanism is:

> **War-risk and P&I cover may exist, but normal commercial transit is constrained by (1) very high / case-specific war-risk pricing, (2) crew and vessel safety refusal by masters/owners, (3) mine/electronic-warfare/congestion hazards, and now (4) sanctions/legal risk around Iranian/IRGC/PGSA toll or checkpoint payments.**

**Confidence: 0.78.**  
High confidence that insurance availability is not the sole blocker (LMA/Lloyd's evidence); high confidence that safety / navigational risk is a binding blocker (LMA, Lloyd's chair, industry guidance, IMO); high confidence that direct/indirect Iran/IRGC safe-passage payments create OFAC sanctions exposure (OFAC FAQ 1249). Lower confidence on exact current premium prints and exact toll levels because fresh quote data are broker/reporter-derived and ship-specific.

**Implication for HAWK frame:** reinforces **C / Grind-Partial-Thaw**. Selective carve-outs can continue, but B/Deal-Reopen needs lawful, safe, broadly insurable, non-coercive transit — not just a few ships clearing Iranian waypoints.

## Triggers Through May 29

### Move toward B / normalization

1. **US-side sanctions waiver or explicit OFAC general license** covering safe-passage-related payments/arrangements, with non-US bank/insurer comfort. Iranian-media-only waiver claims are not enough.
2. **IMO/BIMCO/ICS/INTERTANKO/OCIMF guidance softens** from defer-if-risk to safe-corridor operating guidance.
3. **Independent AIS/Kpler/SynMax traffic sustains >30-40/day** with crude/LNG schedules, not one-off bilateral carve-outs.
4. **War-risk quotes fall toward low single digits or sub-1% vessel value** for normal flags/cargoes, with multi-day availability rather than last-minute 48-hour windows.
5. **Mine/EW/salvage confidence improves:** verified clearance, stable GNSS/AIS, no attack/seizure threats, credible port-of-refuge/salvage plan.
6. **Iran/PGSA drops toll/permission regime** or accepts a lawful IMO/Oman traffic-separation framework without IRGC payment/control.

### Move toward freeze / re-ratchet / D

1. **OFAC enforcement action** against a shipowner, bank, insurer, broker, charterer, or intermediary tied to Hormuz safe-passage/toll payments.
2. **Iran attacks/seizes/threatens a vessel for non-payment or non-compliance** with PGSA/IRGC permission regime.
3. **US or allies target checkpoint/PGSA/IRGC assets** enforcing Hormuz tolls.
4. **Fresh industry advisory hardens** to no-transit / avoid area / no safe passage.
5. **Insurance market materially withdraws appetite** below LMA's March appetite levels, or P&I clubs issue new broad exclusions/cancellations.
6. **Traffic falls toward 0-3/day** or crude/LNG carve-outs stall after current diplomatic hold expires.
