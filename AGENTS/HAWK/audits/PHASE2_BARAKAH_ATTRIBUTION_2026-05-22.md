## Trigger list

### Moves to direct-Iran attribution / D-up

1. UAE MoD/MoFA, US, Saudi, Iraq, or IAEA-adjacent investigation explicitly says **Iran directed, launched, ordered, supplied targeting data, or controlled the Barakah drones**.
2. IRGC/Iran official or state media claims responsibility or praises the Barakah strike as Iranian operation.
3. A named Iraqi militia claims responsibility and explicitly says it acted under Iranian/IRGC command.
4. Technical evidence: drone wreckage/flight-control/communications/logistics chain publicly tied to IRGC units, not merely Iranian-origin hardware.
5. Second nuclear-infrastructure strike or follow-on UAE energy/bypass strike from same Iraq route.
6. UAE retaliation or formal diplomatic move naming Iran as responsible.

### De-escalates / C-stable or D-down

1. UAE keeps attribution at “Iraq-origin” and avoids direct Iran naming after investigation completion.
2. Iraq announces arrests/interdiction and UAE accepts Iraq-origin militia framing without naming Tehran.
3. No second nuclear-infrastructure or UAE energy-infrastructure strike through May 29.
4. IAEA/FANR continue to report normal safety status and no reactor impact.
5. Diplomatic framework language appears while Barakah attribution remains proxy/ambiguous.
6. Credible group denial plus forensic evidence pointing to non-Iran-directed local militia or false-flag actor.
