**Current frame: armed pause / controlled grind.**

The Iran/Hormuz situation is **not closed** and not a clean de-escalation. ### 2. Barakah attribution
- Phase 2 verdict: **Iraq-origin / proxy-route confirmed**, confidence **0.78**.
- Direct Iran state attribution is **not public/confirmed**.
- Strike was real and escalation-significant: **3 drones**, **2 intercepted**, **1 hit an outer-perimeter generator**, no injuries/radiological release.

### 3. Hormuz traffic regime
- Phase 3 verdict: **selective carve-outs**, confidence **0.84**.
- Not closed / not zero-crossings: Qatari LNG, Chinese VLCCs, South Korean VLCC, Indian **13 cleared / 13 stranded**, and other bilateral examples moved.
- Not normalizing: roughly **10 vessels/day** vs **125-140/day** normal; hundreds of vessels / ~20,000 seafarers still affected.
- Correct phrase: **Iran-controlled trickle flow / selective carve-outs**, not reopening.

### 4. US / Israel posture
- Phase 3.5 verdict: **preserved strike option / elevated forward sustainment**, confidence **0.72**.
- Evidence supports “dozens” of US tankers at Ben Gurion/Ramon, at least **14** refuelers at Ben Gurion, plus lower-confidence cargo-airbridge claims (**8 outbound / 6 return** C-17-style movements observed).
- This is not enough for imminent strike prep, but it validates the “armed pause” frame.

### 5. Insurance / legal mechanism
- Phase 4 verdict: old **“uninsurable / P&I refusing”** shorthand is superseded.
- Better mechanism: coverage may exist, but normal traffic is constrained by:
  1. uneconomic / case-specific war-risk pricing,
  2. crew-safety and owner-appetite refusal,
  3. mine / EW / congestion hazards,
  4. OFAC / IRGC / PGSA toll-payment legal risk.
- OFAC safe-passage payment risk is a real blocker for shipowners, banks, insurers, and intermediaries.
