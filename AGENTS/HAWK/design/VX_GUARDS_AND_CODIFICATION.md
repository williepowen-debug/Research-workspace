# VX Guard and cross-domain codification contract

Executed September 8, 2026 under Will's August 17 row 54②③ ruling and the current approved next steps.
Reader: HAWK boot 6b; canonical rows remain `workbook/VX.tsv`.

`Guard` is `CLOCK`, `EVENT`, or `CLOCK+EVENT` when both apply. `Watch_Source` names the publishers to inspect; it is not a declaration that a live feed exists. Every active row retains the 45-day maximum re-sweep cadence. An EVENT trigger can require an earlier review. The SUPERSEDED ceasefire row is historical and excluded from scheduling. `Last_Updated` dates the row's substantive review; dated source observations and unresolved subseries remain explicit in Current_Value/Source. A failed pull never refreshes an unknown measurement.

CODIF-01 now covers durable trade restrictions and cross-war transit institutions. TRADE-02 owns tariff scope, rates, affected goods and economic exposure. CODIF owns the instrument's legal stage and reversibility. The same instrument may appear in both views; it is one event, never two additive shocks. HAW-20 retains its frozen four-instrument set and original resolution test.

The existing stage distinction is extended to trade instruments: DRAFTED proposals or signed pacts without machinery are YELLOW; enacted restrictions or operational institutions are ORANGE; evidenced collection of a tariff/toll from commercial traffic is RED. GREEN requires the relevant instruments to be repealed, lapsed or withdrawn. Record each instrument separately in the Current_Value cell; the highest evidenced stage sets the axis mark. A reversal of one instrument does not erase other operative instruments. Rhetoric, appointments, an effective date alone and a payment instruction alone cannot establish actual collection. No inference that law is permanent: repeal remains possible.

First expanded-scope reading: ORANGE on Canada's effective September 8 order and CBSA implementation notice. Collection receipts UNKNOWN. Gulf instruments retain their dated owner evidence; this is a scope addition, not fresh Gulf escalation. The legacy YELLOW row is preserved in its Notes. New scope does not make TRADE-02 RED or resolve HAW-20.

Revision policy: GRADE-AT-PUBLICATION for new stage evidence; annotate later corrections beside the original observation. No revision to historical prediction scores.
