## Raw Fact
Iraqi oil production from southern oilfields fell to 1.3 million bpd from around 4.3 million bpd — a ~3M bpd shut-in (~70% loss). Three sources cited by Walter Bloomberg. Bloomberg editorial says "collapsed by about 60% as the Iran war constrains the availability of tankers to load the nation's crude." Rory Johnston contextualizes: "The volume of crude production that Iraq has *already* shut-in is larger than the peak of *feared* [but never realized] Russian supply loss in early 2022 that spiked crude prices above $120 per barrel."
