## Raw Fact
Four Coface research charts on Gulf fertilizer supply chain vulnerability:

**1. Fertilizer Trade Structure — Gulf Countries:**
- Nitrogen (Urea/Ammonia): Gulf exports to India (Oman = 62% of India's imports), Brazil (Qatar/SA = 24%), USA (SA/Qatar = 21%), Australia (SA = 52%)
- Phosphate (DAP): SA = 33% of US imports, 22% of India's imports
- UAE, Qatar, Iran all significant exporters

**2. Global Fertilizer Market:**
- Nitrogen = 36% of global fertilizer imports (~$90B+ market total)
- APAC = 31% of global demand. India = 29% of APAC. "India is particularly vulnerable."
- US = 19%, Brazil = 18%, Europe = 18%

**3. Global Export Rankings:**
- Russia #1 (18%), China #2 (11%), Morocco (7%), Canada (8%), Gulf countries collectively 13%, US 6%
- KSA = 6th largest exporter. Iran = significant nitrogen exporter
- Nitrogen = two-thirds of Gulf region's fertilizer exports

**4. Supply Chain Map:**
- ALL Gulf fertilizer production/export routes are Hormuz-dependent
- KSA: Waad Al Shamal (nitrogen) + Hasa Al Jalamid (phosphate) → rail to Ras Al Khair Port
- Iran: 4 nitrogen plants (Assaluyeh, Bandar Imam, Khorramshahr, Shiraz) → Shahid Rajaee Port (Bandar Abbas)
- Even Oman ports "not located in the Persian Gulf" would "remain exposed to tensions in the Gulf of Oman"
- "Highly vulnerable to a blockade of the Strait of Hormuz" label on map
