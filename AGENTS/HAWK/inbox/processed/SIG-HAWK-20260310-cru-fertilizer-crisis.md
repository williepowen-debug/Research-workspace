### CRU: 47% of Global Sulphur, 43% of Urea at Risk — "Industry in Paralysis"

**When:** 2026-03-10 05:21 AM (processed 22:30 UTC)
**Source:** CRU Group / @FertilizerWeek1 (X)
**Via:** Will (screenshot)

**Raw signal:**
- 47% of global sulphur traded supply at risk
- 43% of global urea traded supply at risk
- 27% of global ammonia traded supply at risk
- 24% of global phosphate traded supply at risk
- "The industry is in paralysis, and the full implications are not yet known"
- CRU charts show sulphur price index going vertical (~1,400 vs ~250 baseline, Jan 2020 = 100)
- Urea index spiking to ~275 (from ~175 base)
- DAP and MOP (potash) relatively flat so far

**For HAWK:** CRU is the authoritative commodity data source for fertilizer. These percentages are higher than UNCTAD's (1/3 of seaborne). Sulphur at 47% is the biggest risk — sulphur is essential for phosphate fertilizer production globally.
