# SIG-HAWK-20260331 — Airgas Helium Force Majeure

**Source:** Kobeissi Letter / WSJ, 3/31/26
**Priority:** 🟡

Airgas declared force majeure on helium — will only meet 50% of normal monthly demand. Hormuz closure cut ~30% of global helium supply. $13.50/ccf surcharge above contract price. Hundreds of $1M cryogenic containers stuck in Middle East.

Helium critical for: semiconductor fab, MRI machines, fiber optics, AI infrastructure. This is a new depletion clock beyond the original seven.
