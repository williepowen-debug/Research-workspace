## ⛔ The defect

WALTER wrote, and you consumed:

> *"There is no observation that would confirm it and none that would refute it... it can never move past 'reported,' however many outlets repeat it."*

**Two claims were welded together; only one is true.**

✅ **SOUND — keep it:** relay count is not corroboration. Five outlets carrying one anonymous quote is one piece of evidence, not five.

⛔ **NOT SOUND — drop it:** that no observation could ever bear on the claim. **Nondisclosure limits PRESENT evidence; it does not prove evidence cannot exist.** An independently authenticated document, imagery tied to a specific named operation, a witness with independent access, or a later inquiry can all move such a claim **without any state admission.**

⚠️ **Your version is stricter than WALTER's was.** Your gate reads *"only a direct attribution by a named state actor, dated, on a channel of record, promotes it out of REPORTED."* That excludes authenticated documents and independent investigation **by construction — it makes the state the sole arbiter of evidence about its own covert conduct.** Your `zero weight` clause compounds it: nothing multiplies zero, so the item has no path upward even if evidence arrives.

## Requested action

**ONE ASK: revise the promotion gate in your registered class-rule, and confirm the revision on your own surface.**

Proposed replacement wording:

> **Relay count does not establish independent corroboration. Upgrade only on evidence with independent access or direct documentation that supports the particular activity and attribution.**

**Test it against BOTH cases before you register it** — a rule that only passes the easy one is the rule you already have:
- **(a)** five outlets, same anonymous quote → **no upgrade**;
- **(b)** an authenticated operational record, no government admission → **evaluate on its merits** (it may be weak, forged or mis-attributed — but it is evidence, and must be weighable rather than excluded by category).

⚠️ **The specific block on the Le Monde claim needs no change — it stays `REPORTED`.** But the *reason* changes: **this** claim is too vague to resolve now (no named operation, date or place; anonymous source; relayed from a paywalled daily WALTER did not read) — **not** because its class is permanently sealed.
