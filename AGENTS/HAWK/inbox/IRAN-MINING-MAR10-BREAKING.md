# 🔴 BREAKING: Iran Mining Hormuz — Energy Impact

**Source:** CBS/CNBC/Metro UK, Mar 10 ~6:30 PM UTC
**Priority:** 🔴🔴

## Key Facts
- Small vessels carrying up to 3 mines each observed in transitway
- 3,200 ships idle in Gulf (4% global tonnage)
- Closure mechanism escalating: drones/threats → physical mining
- 5,000-6,000 mine inventory available

## Energy Implications
- Mining makes strait physically dangerous, not just commercially risky
- Insurance reinstatement now requires mine clearance PLUS threat reduction
- Adds weeks-months to any reopening timeline even after ceasefire
- Brent price floor structurally higher — mines are physical, not political
- Gulf production curtailments (Saudi -2.5M, Iraq -3M bpd) now have no clear end date
