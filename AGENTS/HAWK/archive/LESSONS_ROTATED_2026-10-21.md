## [2026-06-12] Prediction text and promotion-threshold text must be written identically
**What happened:** HAW-10 was registered in PREDICTIONS.tsv as "Houthi commercial-vessel kinetic attack (mechanism) **on Bab al-Mandab** (threshold)" while the same session's STATUS emerging-vector row said "first Houthi **kinetic** commercial-vessel hit = promote to core + fires HAW-10" — no locus restriction. When the Houthis struck two vessels in the **Gulf of Aden** (6/8-9), the two texts gave different answers: vector promotion criterion satisfied, prediction locus unmet.
**Why it bites:** the wedge only surfaces when the event arrives, exactly when you're under pressure to resolve fast — inviting post-hoc rubric drift toward the convenient reading.
**Rule:** when a prediction and a vector threshold describe the same event, write one canonical sentence and reference it from both places. On conflict, the registered PREDICTIONS.tsv text is canonical (pre-registration discipline: fire only on the registered condition). Same-class second instance same session: GULFSTATE-01's Red line ("Gulf-state infra damage") vs its promotion criterion ("energy infra / US base hit") diverge — airport hit fired one, not the other.

## [2026-07-12] A prediction's literal wording narrows your evidence sweep — search the MECHANISM, not just the named targets
**What happened:** re-checking HAW-15 ("Ukraine does NOT strike Russian crude-export INFRASTRUCTURE — Primorsk/Ust-Luga/Novorossiysk/Druzhba"), I searched exactly those named terminals, got April-vintage hits, and wrote "no in-window crude-export strike." I entirely missed a week-long, industrial-scale Ukrainian campaign against Russian shadow-fleet **tankers** (Sea of Azov + Black Sea, 7/6-12, 21-42 vessels claimed) — a DIFFERENT channel for the same underlying mechanism (disrupt Russian crude to market). Will surfaced it. Shadow fleet is explicitly in my domain scope.
**Why it bites:** the row's named targets (fixed terminals) became the search scope, so a real, decision-relevant escalation arriving via an UN-named channel (the vessels) was invisible. Same wording-wedge family as HAW-10 (locus) and HAW-14 (catalyst) — but here it corrupted *evidence-gathering*, not just resolution. A "clean CONFIRMED" on the letter would have read as a good call while the mechanism fired next door.
**Rule:** when re-checking a prediction at boot, sweep the underlying **mechanism** broadly (here: "Ukraine attacking Russian oil" — refineries AND terminals AND tankers AND pipeline), not just the prediction's named literal targets. Then map what you find back to letter-vs-spirit. Corollary 1: write prediction labels that name the mechanism, not one channel of it (`[[feedback_forward_discovery_prediction_spirit]]`, `[[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]]`).
**Corollary 2 (added 7/12 same day — Will pushed a SECOND time and I was STILL wrong):** my "tighter confirmation" *also* concluded "terminals unstruck in-window" — wrong again. Two compounding causes: (a) a **single-search-per-terminal-name sweep returned only the 'famous' Mar/Apr headlines**, masking the June/July re-strikes (Primorsk ~6/25, NOVATEK-Ust-Luga 7/10, Vysotsk 7/6, Kavkaz 6/20); (b) my **own "canonical" ledger was stale (last updated 6/20) AND had silently missed the 5/23 + 6/8 Novorossiysk crude-terminal re-strikes** — so the internal baseline I leaned on *under-stated the very channel the prediction tracked*. **Rule:** don't trust your own ledger as the baseline without checking its Last-updated + running a completeness pass; and when a first sweep says "nothing," escalate to a date-careful multi-angle sweep (per-facility June/July queries + a neutral dated timeline like Wikipedia + operational-impact queries) BEFORE banking a "no." A first-pass "no results" is a search-coverage statement, not a fact. HAW-15 resolved FAILED on the corrected evidence (KB-222/223).
