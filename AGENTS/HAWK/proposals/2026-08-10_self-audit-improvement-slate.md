### 3 · WILL · **Split the dormant book into CLOCK-guarded and EVENT-guarded rows — the 45-day cadence is the wrong instrument for half of it**

**Evidence.** `VX-HAWK-TRADE-02` fired on **2026-07-20/07-23** and this desk recorded it on **2026-08-10** — and the re-sweep clock (8/4) was itself **6 days overdue** when it ran. **By the time I caught it, Section 301 had been legally in effect for 17 days.** The cadence was the *only* guard, and a clock can only ever catch a scheduled event late.

**The distinction the book does not currently make:**
- **EVENT-guarded classes** — tariff proclamations, legislative acts, treaty signings, designation lists. These have **knowable publication venues and often pre-announced dates** (Federal Register, USTR dockets, proclamation texts). Rows: **`TRADE-01`, `TRADE-02`, `CODIF-01`** (and any future sanctions-regime row).
- **CLOCK-guarded classes** — slow-drift state where nothing announces a change. Rows: **`VEN-01`, `SULPHUR-01`, `IRAQ-01`, `TWN-01`, `TWNMIL-01`, `FININFRA-01`**. For these a 45-day sweep is genuinely appropriate.

**Proposal:** add a `Guard` column to `workbook/VX.tsv` (`CLOCK` / `EVENT`), and for every `EVENT` row require a **named watch source** in the row itself. **What it catches:** the TRADE-02 class — a vector that fires on a published instrument while its guard sleeps. **Cost:** ~1 session. **Requires Will** because it changes the dormant book's contract, which other agents consume.
