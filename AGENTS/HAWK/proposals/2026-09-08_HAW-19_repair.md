**Proposed successor letter:** During October 1–31, no newly damaged crude-export terminal in either theater meets all of the following: confirmed physical damage; the terminal's only operable loading path disabled, or all identified loading paths unavailable; terminal crude throughput at least 200 kb/d below its frozen pre-event baseline for 45 consecutive days; damage-attributed force majeure or a restoration estimate of at least 90 days from the operator, sovereign or a named technical assessor. Both Kpler and Vortexa must measure the same terminal, crude population and dates, agree within 1.5×, and have independent imagery corroboration. Freeze the baseline as the mean of the 28 completed days immediately before the event, from both trackers, at first evaluation. A baseline or path inventory that cannot be established makes that candidate UNKNOWN. Do not select one tracker after seeing its verdict. Security closures, loading avoidance, refinery damage, gas losses and hull losses do not qualify.
