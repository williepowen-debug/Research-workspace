# TRADE-02 RED definition — DOCKET L778 draft

**Date:** 2026-10-25. **State: DRAFT; NOT REGISTERED.** Authority: PROME/DOCKET.tsv L778. HAWK drafts; PROME registers options; Will rules. Current ORANGE and every existing band remain unchanged.

## Recommended option A: measure breadth at the existing 50% rate

**Proposed letter:**

> HAWK grades VX-HAWK-TRADE-02 RED when ALL conditions below hold.
> 1. A final US legal instrument imposes an additional ad valorem tariff of at least 50% on Canadian-origin goods.
> 2. The operative scope covers every ordinary goods heading in HTSUS chapters 1–97, except the exclusions listed below.
> 3. The tariff applies in addition to Section 232 duties wherever Section 232 applies to those goods.
> 4. The instrument has taken effect and is neither suspended nor enjoined at the observation time.
> Allowed exclusions: civil aircraft under the WTO agreement; Chapter 98 treatment; diplomatic and personal-use administrative exemptions.
> A product, sector, USMCA-origin or end-use exemption outside that list defeats condition 2.
> HAWK requires the operative legal text and its complete annex, plus CBP implementation guidance or the amended HTSUS.
> Missing scope or implementation evidence produces UNDETERMINED, never a satisfied condition.

**Level/operator/instrument:** additional duty **≥50%**, **ALL four conditions**, Federal Register/GovInfo final instrument + complete annex + CBP/USITC implementation. The 50% is the existing Canada Section 338 level, not a fitted economic tipping point. No trade-weighted coverage estimate or revenue claim is implied. This grades a legal tariff wall; actual duty collection remains CODIF-01's separate ENFORCED test.

**Why this definition:** the existing ORANGE band measures targeted country/auto duties. A countrywide extension at the same rate is a clear next breadth step and distinguishes it from adding a few tariff lines. It also prevents the proclamation's motor-vehicle title from substituting for its actual product scope. Section 338's statutory ceiling of 50% is stated in Proclamation 11065 paragraph 8; the rate alone already exists and cannot distinguish ORANGE from RED.

**Tradeoff:** this is deliberately strict. A severe sector-only escalation can remain ORANGE. Adding another partner on a narrow annex also remains ORANGE. That is a stated limitation, preferable to an undefined “major partner bloc.” The earlier unregistered sketch's bloc branch is withdrawn from this draft.

## Option B: retain an escalation watch, leave RED undefined

Will can decline A and require a measured import-value coverage threshold before defining RED. HAWK then reports UNDEFINED for RED and cannot certify an ORANGE→RED transition. A coverage threshold would require a fixed trade year, valuation basis, import denominator and reproducible annex mapping; none is established here. Do not invent a percentage from the number of HTS lines.
