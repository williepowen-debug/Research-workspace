# FLOW-HAWK-21 falsifier check: Q3-2026 owner TCE prints compared with the freight index

Retrieved 2026-10-29 (Thu), from 13:36 to about 13:40 EDT (`date`). This was a read-only check; no repository file was edited.

## 1. Bottom line

The falsifier can be tested only in part. Of the six registered names, **one has issued a final Q3 print (STNG, 2026-10-28)** and **two have published issuer Q3 estimates in SEC filings (DHT, 6-K dated 2026-10-12; INSW, preliminary 8-K filed 2026-10-16)**. FRO, Dorian LPG and BW LPG have not printed. Each of the three measurable reads moved **AWAY** from the index: realized TCE was flat or down while the Baltic assessments rose sharply through Q3. DHT VLCC spot realized was 38.5% of the TD3C average in Q2 and 25.2% in Q3. **On the Q3 evidence the joint holds and the row is not falsified.** One caveat could reverse this at the Q4 prints. Q4-to-date bookings have jumped: DHT $509,100/day on about 50% of spot days, STNG LR2 $181,000/day on 44%, INSW blended $180,000/day on 37%. That pattern fits a fixture lag that is closing, not a joint that is permanently broken. The Q4 prints (Jan–Feb 2027) are the stronger test.

## 3. Access failures and caveats

1. **The benchmark is a mean of Friday prints from a reprint.** It is not an official Baltic quarterly average, and 3 Q3 weeks are missing (31 Jul and 7 Aug returned 404; 14 Aug had no TD3C TCE). Direction and magnitude are robust: every Q3 TD3C print from 21 Aug on ($585k+) exceeds every Q2 print. The exact ratios are approximate.
2. **Units do not match on the two sides.** Realized figures are per revenue, operating or available day, net of voyage costs and including ballast. Baltic TCE is a round-trip assessment for a standard ship on one route. Some gap is structural, so the falsifier test is the *change* in the gap, not its level.
3. **DHT and INSW are issuer estimates, not final prints.** Both carry preliminary language. The final DHT (11-09) and INSW (11-05) numbers should be re-checked.
4. **Timing lag weakens a "broken joint" reading.** Q3 realized reflects voyages fixed partly in Q2. The index surge came late in Q3; STNG cites a "late-quarter increase". Q4-to-date bookings of $181k–$509k/day suggest realized TCE will move sharply *toward* the index in Q4. **If the Q4 prints confirm that, the row's "breaks at the joint" claim reduces to "lags at the joint".** That change in reading could alter how the row is cited.
5. **Not measurable:** STNG MR/Handymax (no matched Baltic route TCE); INSW (blended, no segment split); TC1 quarterly average.
6. **Unmeasured (access):** dorianlpg.com returned 403. ir.teekay.com did not connect. Dorian's and TNK's Q3 dates are unverified, which is a coverage statement, not a negative. The BWLP Q2 figures and Q3 guidance, Ardmore and Hafnia figures come from search summaries and were not re-fetched at the primary.
7. **Possible baseline discrepancy:** the FLOW row cites a VLGC spot peak of "~$170,000/day". The BLPG1 Friday prints for Q2 average $183,432, with a high of $233,265 (12 Jun). The Dorian baseline gap is therefore likely understated in the row. Not re-verified at source.
8. Quartr was not used. cmegroup.com was not accessed.
