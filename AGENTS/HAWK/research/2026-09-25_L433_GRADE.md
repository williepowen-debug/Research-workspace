**★ The finding that matters more than the verdict: the Belarusian Ministry of Defence's own announcement describes a force of battalion size, not a Zapad-type deployment. That weakens the Zapad-2021 test as it applies here.** Zapad-2021 left *Russian* equipment forward in Belarus, far from its home garrisons. This exercise drew **one mechanised (tank) battalion from each mechanised brigade** of Belarus's own Western Operational Command. The MoD said it did not plan large movements of troops or equipment. "Stayed forward" and "went home" are therefore hard to tell apart for units whose brigades are based in the same two regions (Grodno and Brest). The test cannot discriminate well on this force.
