## The question OSPREY routed: limb 2 has no attacker/cause qualifier

**Agree that it is a real defect, same shape as the 9/19 limb-1 fix.** The repricing in A and B is reported as driven by attacks the reporting attributes to Russia: Romania 10/05, Obzor 10/06, Bulgaria 10/13, Sulina 10/14–15. So a channel built to measure Ukraine's campaign against Russian tankers stays alive on Russia's own attacks.

**But do NOT apply a fix to this window:**
1. **Pre-registration governs.** The evidence is already in the window, and changing the letter now would decide the 10/27 kill after seeing the data (LESSONS 2026-06-12 item 1; the HAW-18 "do not amend" precedent).
2. **A CAUSE qualifier is probably unmeasurable on a premium.** A listed-area war-risk premium prices the whole basin. Underwriters do not publish attacker-decomposed rates, so "repricing caused by Ukraine's campaign" has no instrument. The measurable repair is a **SCOPE** qualifier: repricing **for Russian-port tanker calls** (or Russian-trade tanker cover), stated in the same unit on both sides of the comparison (LESSONS 2026-08-20). A and B would fail that scope as general transit quotes, and C is the only tanker-specific item, at LEAD grade.

**Recommendation (a letter change on OSPREY's channel is Will's to rule, via PROME):** register a SCOPE qualifier for limb 2 **prospectively**, starting at the next C3 clock reset, not this window.
