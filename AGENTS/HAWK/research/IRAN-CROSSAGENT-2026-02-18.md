## Situation Summary

The US-Iran crisis is the dominant geopolitical risk vector right now. Two carrier strike groups in theater, US prepping for "weeks-long operations," and Iran closed Hormuz during nuclear talks. Brent at ~$67-69 — arguably underpriced given the risk.

Diplomacy is active (Geneva Round 2 productive) but fragile. No deal is close. This is maximum-pressure-meets-negotiation: the US is deploying force while talking.

### 🔴 → SAM (Japan/Asia Macro)

**What SAM needs to know:**

Japan imports ~90% of its oil. Any Hormuz disruption = Japan energy crisis. This feeds directly into:
- Inflation → BOJ policy pressure
- USD/JPY (energy import shock = yen weakness)
- Japanese corporate margins (energy-intensive exporters and manufacturers)

**Hormuz closure (partial, like Feb 17):** Already happened. Markets skittish.
**Hormuz full closure > 72hrs:** Japan emergency oil reserve drawdown. LNG spot spikes. BoJ faces inflation + growth dilemma simultaneously.

**Action for SAM:** Iran strike = yen weakness + Japan energy stock selloff. What's the BOJ's capacity to respond if growth slows AND inflation surges? Monitor carefully.

### 🟠 → ZHAO (China Analyst)

**What ZHAO needs to know:**

Iran → China more nuanced:
- China buys Iranian oil (heavily discounted, sanctions workaround)
- US strike on Iran = China loses cheap oil supply
- But: China benefits from oil spike hurting US competitors
- Taiwan angle: US carriers in Middle East = reduced Pacific presence (theoretically)

China is in an interesting position: doesn't want Iran nuked (loses cheap oil, gains chaos) but also using the crisis opportunistically for Taiwan pressure timing.

**Action for ZHAO:** Watch for Chinese diplomatic statements on Iran. If China suddenly calls for restraint loudly, they're worried about their oil supply. If they're quiet, they're watching for Pacific opportunities.

### 🔴 → LIQUID (Macro/Flows)

**What LIQUID needs to know:**

Iran strike = classic risk-off flight to safety:
- UST rally (safe haven buying)
- Gold spike (already elevated — watch $2,500+ on strike)
- USD strength (short-term safe haven)
- EM selloff (risk-off)

**Action for LIQUID:** This is the event-driven flows setup. Pre-positioned gold/UST or hedged against risk-off? The binary nature (deal vs. strike) argues for being balanced but having the playbook ready. Watch gold as the leading indicator — gold is signaling the risk premium, and it's already elevated.

## Tail Risk Scenarios (for all agents)

### Scenario A: Israel Unilateral Strike (20-25% probability, 30-day window)
- US doesn't need to do anything — Israel has struck Iran before (2025)
- Israel opposes any deal seen as weak
- If Geneva Round 3 looks like it's producing a soft deal → Israeli strike probability rises
- Market reaction: Oil +$20-30 immediate, VIX +25+, gold spike, UST rally
- Duration: 1-2 weeks of elevated volatility minimum

### Scenario B: Iran Misreads US and Attacks First (<10% probability)
- Iran attacks US assets (bases, ships) in response to perceived imminent strike
- US responds with full scale operation
- Most disruptive outcome: sustained conflict, Hormuz closed
- Market reaction: Oil +$40-50 (black swan), VIX +40+, global growth shock

### Scenario C: Deal (20-30% probability, 30-60 day window)
- "Guiding principles" become framework; Iran accepts intrusive inspections
- Iran drops proxy demand but gets sanctions relief
- Market reaction: Oil -$10-15 (Brent $52-59), VIX flush, risk-on
- Note: Even a deal doesn't remove ALL Iran risk (implementation, Israel reaction)

### Scenario D: Stalemate (40-50% probability)
- Talks drag on without deal OR strike
- Markets grind in uncertainty range
- Oil stays $65-75
- Background tension but no resolution
