### ★ THE ASYMMETRY — resolved as far as the evidence allows, and it partly refutes my own framing

The open question was: *either the bar has silently risen (incident headlines carry less signal than in 2025), or the next invocation arrives as a genuine surprise.*

**The discriminator: is the silence caused by a raised threshold applied to comparable stimuli, or by the absence of a qualifying stimulus?** Those look identical in a headline count and point opposite ways.

**Apply it to the two 2025 triggers and the 2026 set:**

| | 2025-09-10 Poland | 2025-09-23 Estonia | Every 2026 event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Character | **Mass saturation** (~19–21 drones, one night) | **MANNED** state aircraft (3 MiG-31s, 12 min) | Single unmanned objects; one cruise missile; one ground sabotage device |
| Russian agency at the object | Unambiguous | Unambiguous *by definition* — a manned state aircraft cannot be EW-drifted debris | **Mostly not established**; largely Ukrainian-origin or EW-diverted |
| First-of-kind | Yes (first shoot-down over allied soil) | Yes | No |
| Article 4 | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

⛔ **My own earlier claim — *"the 2025 bar was cleared twice on events no larger"* — is WITHDRAWN. It conflated magnitude with agency.** The 2026 events are comparable or worse in *consequence*; they are categorically weaker in *whose deliberate act they were*. Both 2025 triggers had a property **no 2026 event has**: unambiguous Russian agency directed at NATO territory, in a mass or manned form.

**⚠️ BASE RATE — and this is the crux.** "Declined ≥4×" was a **denominator error**: most of those events are not trials of the Article 4 threshold, because they are not qualifying stimuli. The honest 2026 trial count is **n ≈ 3**:
1. **Romania 2026-05-29** (Geran-2 into an apartment block, 2 injured) — a trial **because the requester said so**: FM **Oana Toiu** stated the incident *"falls into the category of events that justify the use of instruments"* such as Article 4 [Il Sole 24 Ore, 2026-05-30]. Romania did not invoke. ⚠️ Her assessment is about **harm on Romanian soil**, not about agency — the object's provenance remains OSPREY's open cell.
2. **Poland 2026-07-30** — a fast object, crash site ~100 km inside Poland, unengaged, no invocation. ⛔ **DOWNGRADED 2026-09-18 by OSPREY: this was my strongest counter-fact and it was OVERSTATED.** DORSZ's account is radar contact **03:40 → 03:46 — six minutes — lost *"before the object could be clearly identified."*** The **crash site** was 100 km inside Poland; **the track was not 100 km of custody.** Tusk's Kh-101 identification is *"all indications"* — **preliminary, and never upgraded to a forensic finding.** ⚠️ **So Russian agency here is NOT established.** The no-Article-4 fact survives intact and is still strong; the "unambiguous Russian cruise missile" framing does not.
3. **Leipzig/Halle 2026-08-04, attributed 2026-09-01** — the only event with Russian **state agency asserted by a NATO government**, on NATO soil, with no Ukraine leg and no EW-drift explanation available. Germany chose criminal and diplomatic channels **instead of** alliance channels.

Conditional record: **2025 = 2 trials, 2 invocations. 2026 = 2 solid trials + 1 downgraded (the Kh-101), 0 invocations.** ⚠️ **n≈3 cannot separate a risen bar from small-sample noise. The asymmetry is NOT resolvable today, and saying otherwise would be manufacturing a verdict from a sample.** What *is* now established is that at least one trial was a **deliberate, articulated decline** (Toiu) and one was a **channel substitution** (Berlin) — so the silence is not purely stimulus-absence either.

**★ THE MECHANISM THAT EXPLAINS BOTH, and it is the most underweighted thing here.** In September 2025, Article 4 was the instrument that **unlocked a collective response**: Poland invoked 09-10, **Operation Eastern Sentry launched 09-12**. In 2026 that response is **already standing and expanding without any invocation** — Turkish drone carrier TCG Anadolu deployed to Latvian waters 2026-02-20; NATO's largest allied airpower deployment of the year across the eastern flank 03-04/05; AIRCOM counter-drone training over Lithuania and Latvia 05-27; and the Ankara ROE change 07-07/08 moved engagement authority to SACEUR. **A requester in 2026 gets nothing new from invoking.** Article 4 has been functionally superseded — it stopped being a thermometer and became a ratchet already pulled. ⇒ **Its silence is weak evidence about alliance alarm, in both directions.**

> **⚖️ FORWARD DISCRIMINATOR — registrable, dated, falsifiable. This is what to watch instead of the headline count.**
> **Trigger:** the next event on NATO territory carrying **(i)** unambiguous Russian state agency at the object level **AND (ii)** any one of — a **manned** platform, a **mass-saturation** event (≥5 objects in one night), or a **fatality** on NATO soil.
> - **Article 4 invoked within 7 days ⇒** the bar never rose; 2026's silence was stimulus absence; the ladder is intact — but incident headlines carry signal **only** for that narrow class, never for the drone count.
> - **Not invoked ⇒** the bar has demonstrably risen; **Article 4 is dead as an indicator and rung 3 should be retired rather than watched**, with the RED trigger (a) rewritten.
>
> ⚠️ Until that trigger fires, **rung 3's state is uninformative and must not be cited as reassurance.** A quiet indicator that has been functionally superseded is not the same as a quiet situation.
