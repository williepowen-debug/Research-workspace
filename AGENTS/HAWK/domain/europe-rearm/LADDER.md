## RUNG DETAIL

### 1 — Rules of engagement 🔴 MOVED (2026-07-07/08, NATO Ankara summit)
Baltic Air Policing converted from an air-**policing** to an active air-**defence** mission; engagement authority transferred **from national capitals to SACEUR** via CAOC Uedem. Estonia's defence minister: the change removed *"restrictions preventing the pilot from pressing the button."*
⇒ Structural, hard to reverse, decided **months before** the shoot-downs that followed. The most underweighted item in the theme.

### 2 — Kinetic engagement 🔴 ACTIVE
**Four engagements in Baltic Air Policing's 22-year history; all four in 2026** — 05-19 Estonia · 06-08 Latvia · 08-14 Latvia · 09-15 Lithuania. Separately Romania (Torrejón CAOC) logged ≥23 airspace violations in 2026 against 18 across 2022–2025, with four July shoot-downs, a Spanish F-18 kill 08-16, and a sea drone destroyed near the **Neptun Deep gas works** 08-20. Detail: `INCURSIONS.tsv`.

> ⚠️ **THE COUNT IS A COMPOSITE — carry this caveat wherever the count goes.** At least two of the four Baltic engagements were **Ukrainian or EW-diverted** objects, not deliberate Russian probes (05-19 *suspected Ukrainian*; 08-20 Latvia identified Ukrainian, EW-diverted; the 09-15 Lithuanian object assessed "most likely" Russian but diverted by *Ukrainian* jamming). Combined with rung 1, **the series partly measures NATO's own loosened trigger and Ukraine-war EW spillover, not rising Russian intent.** An instrument that changed its own threshold mid-series cannot be read as a clean measure of the thing it is cited for.
> Independent of this caveat: Romania's 23-vs-18 violation count, Leipzig/Halle, and the Neptun Deep sea drone. **Build the case on those.**

### 3 — Article 4 🟢 NOT INVOKED in 2026
NATO's record lists nine invocations ever; the two most recent are **Poland 2025-09-10** and **Estonia 2025-09-23**. Since then, *without* an Article 4 request: four NATO kinetic shoot-downs; an explosive drone on the apron at **Leipzig/Halle, a NATO heavy-airlift hub**, attributed by Berlin 2026-09-01 to Russian state entities with the Bonn consulate-general ordered shut by 09-18; ≥23 Romanian violations. The 2025 bar was cleared twice on events **no larger**.

> ⚠️ **VERIFICATION LIMIT — stated, not hidden.** NATO's Article 4 page was **last updated 2025-09-23, the same date as its newest entry.** Its silence on 2026 carries **no information** — a reference that predates the period cannot fail to report it. This finding rests on (a) an incursion running record updated 2026-09-18 asserting it explicitly, (b) two independent search sweeps surfacing no 2026 invocation. **Strongly supported; NOT primary-closed.** Close it at a 2026-vintage NATO primary before any gate is scored on it.

> **THE ASYMMETRY — the highest-value open question in this theme.** The ladder has been declined repeatedly under conditions that previously triggered it. **Either the bar has silently risen — in which case incident headlines carry less signal than in 2025 — or the next invocation arrives as a genuine surprise.** Both are decision-relevant and they point opposite ways. Resolving which comes first.

### 4 — Article 5 🟢 NONE
No ally has formally raised collective defence. No evidence of a debate.

### 5 — German mobilisation 🟢 NOT TRIGGERED
Military Service Modernisation Act in force **2026-01-01**. **Service is voluntary**; mandatory elements are registration and medical screening only, men born 2008+. Conscription exists as a **"compulsory on demand" fallback** requiring a further Bundestag law. Targets **>260,000 active + 200,000 reservists by 2035**; 30,000 volunteers/yr.
⇒ **Machinery built, switch not thrown** — the same shape as rungs 3 and 6.
⏳ Unverified, would change the "voluntary" characterisation if true: reporting (April 2026) that German men now need approval for foreign travel under the law. Not checked at a primary.

### 6 — EU counter-hybrid instrument 🟡 ANNOUNCED, NO INSTRUMENT (2026-09-16)
Von der Leyen's State of the Union proposed a **European Security Council**, a **"counter-hybrid playbook"** and an EU-level Article-4-style emergency protocol. Verbatim: *"Europe needs its own counter-hybrid playbook. We urgently need a consensus on how to respond to certain incidents"* — for acts that *"fall below armed aggression but clearly threaten our national security"*; and *"hybrid threats facing Europe are less and less hybrid and less and less threats."* Named Denmark, Lithuania, Poland.
⚠️ Reported to carry **no legal base, no figure and no date**. A named institution without a legal base is an announcement, not an instrument. Compare the EU "drone wall," still non-existent ten months after announcement (July 2026).

## NOT ON THIS LADDER, DELIBERATELY

**Spending levels, budget shares and disbursement schedules are NOT rungs** and must not be added.

Money belongs to the fiscal leg (HANS — see `README.md` §4) and it is largely discounted. Putting it in the bands would repeat the **TWN-01 defect** exactly: months of evidence accreting against a scale that cannot score it, and a mark earned by one leg laundered onto another (LESSONS 2026-07-28). If a candidate rung cannot be written as *"this moves the ladder from state X to state Y,"* it does not belong here.

The slow-clock context that is real but is **not** a rung: NATO Hague 3.5%+1.5%; EU Readiness 2030 €800bn to 2029; SAFE €150bn disbursing to end-2030 and repaying over 45 years; and **European NATO equipment stocks still below 2021 levels** at McKinsey's Feb-2026 dashboard — ⏳ 7 months old, the only inventory datum held.
