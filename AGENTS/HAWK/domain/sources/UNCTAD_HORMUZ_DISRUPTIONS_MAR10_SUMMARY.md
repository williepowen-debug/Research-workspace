# UNCTAD: Strait of Hormuz Disruptions — Implications for Global Trade
**Source:** UN Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD), 10 March 2026
**Data providers:** Clarksons Research, Kpler, LSEG, EIA, Baltic Exchange, World Bank
**Priority:** 🔴 PRIMARY SOURCE — institutional-grade data for thesis

## Key Data Points

### Strait Traffic
- Pre-conflict average: **141 daily transits**
- Post-conflict (Mar 5-6): **3-6 daily transits**
- Drop: **97%**

### What Flows Through Hormuz (% of global seaborne trade)

| LPG | 29% |
| LNG | 19% |

- **84% of Hormuz crude → Asia, 83% of Hormuz LNG → Asia**

### Fertilizer
- **1/3 of global seaborne fertilizer** transits Hormuz
- 16M tonnes from Persian Gulf in 2024
- **67% is urea**, 20% DAP, 9% MAP
- Most vulnerable importers: Sudan (54%), Sri Lanka (36%), Australia (32%), Tanzania (31%)
