### 4.2 Strait of Hormuz — 2026 Crisis (CURRENT CRITICAL EVENT)

| Date | Event | Impact | Source |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| **Feb 15–20, 2026** | Iran rushed to pre-position crude exports at 3x normal rate ahead of anticipated US strikes; reduced oil storage to minimize infrastructure vulnerability | ~Temporary surge in Iranian crude exports before blackout | OilPrice.com |
| **Feb 28, 2026** | US-Israel "Operation Epic Fury" strikes Iran; IRGC transmits VHF warnings to all vessels: **"No ship is allowed to pass the Strait of Hormuz"** | Tanker traffic dropped **~70%**; 150+ ships anchored outside strait | Reuters, Guardian, Wikipedia (2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis) |
| Feb 28 – Mar 2, 2026 | At least 3 tankers struck near strait; 1 seafarer killed | Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd, Japan shippers suspend Hormuz transits | Al Jazeera, CNA, Reuters |
| Mar 1–2, 2026 | Effective partial closure continues; 17 tankers still transiting as of Mar 1 | ~20% of global daily oil supply (~20M bbl/day) at risk; ~15% of global LNG transit through Hormuz | Wikipedia (2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis), NYT |

**Key facts:**
- Strait is 21 miles wide at narrowest point; ~20M bbl/day normally transit
- Iran did **not** formally declare a blockade (would be illegal under international law), but de facto closure via IRGC threats
- Brent crude rose 10–13% to ~$82/bbl; analysts flagging risk of $100/bbl if sustained
- OPEC+ pledged additional 206,000 bbl/day output increase as mitigation
- 84% of Hormuz crude flows to Asia; China, India, Japan, South Korea most exposed
- Insurance premiums spiked from 0.125% to 0.2–0.4% of vessel value per transit (additional ~$250K per VLCC)

**Alternative infrastructure notes:** Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline (Petroline) can handle ~5M bbl/day bypass to Red Sea. UAE's Abu Dhabi Crude Oil Pipeline (ADCOP) handles ~1.5M bbl/day bypass to Fujairah. Net effective loss if full closure: 8–10M bbl/day with no bypass alternatives. Source: Reuters market analyst reactions.

### 6.2 Scale of Sanctions Pressure on Russian Fleet

- **623 vessels added to sanctions lists in 2025** (vs. 225 in 2024) — ~3x acceleration
- **~40% of tankers carrying Russian oil now sanctioned** by at least one government
- **1,337 ships** on Ukraine's shadow fleet catalog as of Feb 2026
- Shadow fleet has **tripled in size** since 2022 invasion
- 510 vessels carried sanctioned Russian oil in 2025 (Kpler data)
- Russia formally began **convoying shadow tankers** with naval warships (Jun 2025) — unprecedented escalation of state protection for sanctions evasion
