## THREAD 1: VENEZUELA

### US Posture — Escalation Ladder

**Sanctions / Economic Pressure:**
- Jan 2025: EO 14157 — Cartel de los Soles designated FTO
- Mar 2025: EO 14245 — 25% tariff on any country importing Venezuelan oil (secondary tariffs)
- Aug 2025: DOJ reward for Maduro raised to $50M
- Nov 2025: State Dept designates Cartel de los Soles as foreign terrorist organization
- Dec 2025: Expanded sanctions on Venezuelan oil shipping companies; 30+ ships in Venezuelan waters under US sanctions

**Military Escalation:**
- Sep 2025: First lethal strikes on drug trafficking vessels in Caribbean (22 total by Dec 10, **87 killed**)
- Nov 13, 2025: Operation Southern Spear formally launched — "largest US naval deployment in Caribbean since Cuban Missile Crisis" (12,000 troops, ~12 Navy ships)
- Dec 10, 2025: Seizure of tanker Skipper
- Dec 17, 2025: Trump declares naval blockade of all sanctioned tankers
- Dec 26, 2025: First US land strike in Venezuela
- Jan 3, 2026: Capture of Maduro. Trump states US will "run" Venezuela until "safe, proper, judicious transition"

**Post-Maduro:**
- Trump/officials signal intention to "develop vast oil reserves with US energy companies"
- Chevron operating 150,000 bpd under extended license (exempt from blockade)
- Opposition leader María Corina Machado calls for Edmundo González Urrutia (election winner) to be installed
- CFR: opposition is unarmed, armed groups (military/colectivos/FARC-aligned) remain in control territorially

## THREAD 4: SYNTHESIS — COORDINATED ENERGY DOMINANCE?

### The Thesis
A coordinated multi-theater strategy to **suppress sanctioned-country oil supply** (Iran, Russia, Venezuela) while positioning compliant Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE) to replace that supply — at higher prices and under US/dollar-system control.

### Evidence For Coordination

**Structural alignment:**
1. Venezuela sanctions tightened → blockade → Maduro captured → US controls oil export policy  
2. Shadow fleet crackdown accelerated → reduces discount oil reaching China/India  
3. Iran struck militarily → removes ~1.5M bpd from market at minimum  
4. Saudi Arabia pre-positioned (+500k bpd) before Iran strikes → supply replacement ready  
5. OPEC+ raised output 2.9M bpd through 2025 (offsetting some sanctions) → now the only legitimate replacement supply  
6. Treasury partnership with Saudi Arabia signed Nov 2025 (covers World Bank/IMF/G20 alignment)  

**Financial entrenchment:**
- PIF → $2B to Kushner (2022); EA deal ($52.5B, Sep 2025)  
- UAE → $500M to Trump family crypto (Jan 2025)  
- Saudi → $600B investment commitment to US (May 2025)  
- Trump family crypto: $800M+ in H1 2025, heavily foreign-sourced  

### Total Supply Impact (Rough Estimate vs. Pre-2025 Baseline)

| Theater | Volume At Risk/Removed | Status (Mar 2026) |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Venezuela to non-US buyers | ~680,000 bpd (total exports minus Chevron) | **Effectively removed** — blockade + regime change |
| Iran (pre-strike) | ~1.5M bpd (exports to China via shadow fleet) | **Major disruption** — US/Israel strikes ongoing |
| Russia shadow fleet (enforced) | ~500,000–1M bpd at risk | **Partial** — workarounds persist; re-flagging ongoing |
| **Total at-risk / removed** | **~2.7–3.2M bpd** | vs. ~102M bpd global demand (~3% of global supply) |
| Saudi/UAE replacement | +2.9M bpd quota increase (2025) + 206k bpd (Apr 2026) + 500k bpd already ramped | **Partially offsetting** |

**Net result:** Supply is not collapsing (Saudi/UAE absorbing slack), but **supply routes and buyers are being forcibly restructured** — away from China/India at discounts toward Western/US-priced market.

### Winners

| Party | Why |
|-------|-----|
| US energy producers | Venezuela oil eventually flows to Gulf Coast refiners; higher global prices benefit US shale |
| Saudi Arabia / UAE | Filling supply void at market prices (vs. deep discounts Russia/Iran/Venezuela offered China); closer US security umbrella |
| Chevron | Positioned as primary Western operator in Venezuela post-Maduro |
| Trump family | Financial ties to Saudi/UAE/Gulf (crypto, Kushner PIF) |
| Trump administration | "Energy dominance" narrative domestically; geopolitical leverage globally |

### Losers

| Party | Why |
|-------|-----|
| China | Loses ~389k bpd Venezuelan discount oil + ~1.5M bpd Iranian oil (Feb 2026 disruption); forced into more expensive replacement barrels |
| India | Loses access to discounted Venezuelan and (potentially) Russian crude |
| Turkey | Shadow fleet business model under pressure |
| Venezuela (Maduro faction) | Removed; US now controls oil governance |
| Iran | Production and export infrastructure damaged; revenue cut |
| Global inflation | Oil price spike risk embedded in Strait of Hormuz uncertainty |

### What Breaks the Strategy?

**China countermoves:**
- China holds $10–12B in Venezuela debt claims — leverage in post-Maduro oil governance
- Chinese refiners can absorb Venezuelan loss from other sources (Russia, Middle East) short-term
- China could flood shadow fleet with new tonnage / new flags faster than US can sanction
- Military response to shadow fleet seizures (hasn't happened; risk non-zero)

**OPEC defection:**
- If Saudi/UAE decide US strikes threaten Strait of Hormuz and their own production, they could cut output (currently Iran is targeting Saudi Arabia with missiles)
- Saudi-UAE tensions over Sudan (noted pre-strike) could fragment GCC coordination

**Strait of Hormuz closure:**
- Iran threatening / attacking tanker traffic through Hormuz would cut ~20M bpd (20% of global demand)
- Saudi/UAE have partial alternative routes (East-West pipeline, Fujairah) but not full replacement
- Would collapse OPEC+ production advantage

**Domestic opposition (US):**
- Venezuela occupation costs, legality questions under international law
- Oil price spike hits US consumers — political liability
- Congressional pushback on undeclared war in Venezuela (no AUMF)

**Russia escalation:**
- Russia reflagging shadow fleet to Russian registry at accelerating pace (17 ships in one month vs 15 in prior 5 months) — creating legal gray zone for seizures
- Russia-Venezuela military cooperation (Seahorse naphtha deliveries) continues
