## CLAIM C — IRGC said Sunday 8/30 it struck US bases in Jordan and the UAE

**VERDICT: CORRECTED-FRAMING.** The Jordan half is real; **the UAE half was DENIED by the UAE**; and
**the date is Monday 2026-08-31 (overnight), not Sunday 8/30.**

**DATE ESTABLISHED: strikes early Monday 2026-08-31; IRGC statement published via Iranian state media
2026-08-31.** Established from Stars & Stripes (dated 8/31), Wikipedia *2026 Iranian strikes on Jordan*,
The Hill, Euronews. "Iran fired back early Monday" is the consistent phrasing across outlets.

- **JORDAN — confirmed by the Jordanian government.** IRGC claim: ballistic missiles at *"technical
  infrastructure, maintenance facilities, and enemy fighter jet positions at the US-operated King
  Hussein and Al-Azraq air bases"*, claiming *"heavy damage."* **Jordan's armed forces said they
  intercepted EIGHT missiles that entered Jordanian airspace, "destroyed before posing a threat to
  people or property"** — Jordan did not name the source or the intended targets.
- **UAE — DENIED.** Iran's ARMY (not IRGC) said it used attack drones against US personnel and
  helicopters at **Al Minhad Air Base**. **The Emirati defence ministry DENIED Al Minhad was attacked**,
  acknowledging only that the UAE air force responded to a **single drone detected over territorial
  waters approaching from Iran.**
- **US:** a US official said there were **"no impacts at U.S. bases."** No CENTCOM damage confirmation.

⚠️ Note the attribution split: **Jordan = IRGC / ballistic missiles; UAE = Iranian ARMY / drones.**
Collapsing both into "the IRGC said it struck bases in Jordan and the UAE" is wrong on the actor, the
weapon, and the outcome.

**CONFIDENCE: HIGH** on Jordan (government statement both sides); **HIGH** on the UAE denial.
Would change on: UAE MoD reversing, or CENTCOM confirming damage.
