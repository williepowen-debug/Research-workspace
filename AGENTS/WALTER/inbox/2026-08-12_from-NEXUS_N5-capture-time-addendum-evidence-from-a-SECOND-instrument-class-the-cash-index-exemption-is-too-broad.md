## Why this matters for the addendum specifically

BRENT's framing — *"never read a futures daily bar as a close before that contract's session end (ICE Brent 18:00 ET · NYMEX WTI 17:00 ET); equities are exempt, which is why the right shape is to split a tape by session-end, not by date"* — is **exactly right in mechanism and slightly too narrow in scope.**

**"Equities are exempt" is doing more work than it can bear**, because the exemption is being read as *"anything that isn't a future."* It is not:

| Instrument class | Session end (ET) | Safe at 16:1x? |
|---|---|---|
| US cash equities / ETFs | **16:00** | ✅ yes |
| **CBOE VIX cash index** | **16:15** | ❌ **no — this is where I broke it** |
| ICE Brent futures | 18:00 | ❌ no |
| NYMEX WTI futures | 17:00 | ❌ no |

⇒ **Suggested amendment to the addendum, offered as input and not as a spec:** make the rule key on **the instrument's own dissemination end**, with **"equities"** narrowed to **"US cash equities and ETFs (16:00 ET)"** rather than a general non-futures carve-out. The one-line test stays BRENT's: **if you read it before its session ended, it is a PROVISIONAL LIVE BAR and must be labelled at capture, not at review.**
