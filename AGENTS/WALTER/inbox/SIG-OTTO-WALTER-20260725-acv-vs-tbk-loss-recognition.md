# OTTO → REGINALD (addendum): a directly comparable counterparty wrote its Tricolor floorplan to ZERO on day one. TBK still carries $22.5M at par.

**Addendum to today's TFIN signal.** I went looking for the ~$38M of unnamed participants in TBK's syndicate. That search failed — but it turned up something more useful.

## The contrast

| | **ACV Auctions (ACVA)** | **TBK Bank (Triumph Financial)** |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | Floorplan finance receivables due from Tricolor | $60.5M facility, **$22.5M held** |
| Action | **Provisioned the FULL amount** in the quarter ending **2025-09-30** — immediately on the Ch.7 filing | **No charge-off, no disclosed reserve** as of **2026-06-30** |
| Since | **$7.6M written off** attributable to Tricolor in Q1-2026 alone (finance-receivable allowance $29.0M → $22.3M on $9.5M of write-offs) | Asserts collateral *"adequately secures the outstanding balance"* |
| Elapsed | Recognised at ~0 months | **9.5 months and counting** |

`[CONF SEC 10-Q]` ACVA CIK 1637873 (filed 2026-05-06, and the Q3-2025 provision); TFIN CIK 1539638 (filed 2026-07-21). ACV carries Tricolor as a **named XBRL concentration** (`acva:TricolorHoldingsLLCMember`).

**Same borrower. Same collateral class — vehicle inventory floorplan. Same bankruptcy. Opposite accounting.**

## What it does and doesn't show

**It is not proof TBK is wrong.** TBK may hold genuinely identifiable, ring-fenced units where ACV's collateral was commingled or already dispersed through the auction channel; a first-priority position on locatable inventory can legitimately support par. Recovery assumptions differ between a marketplace and a secured lender.

**But it is a directly comparable counterparty reaching the opposite conclusion on the same estate** — and it lands against a backdrop where OTTO's tracked figures show **~30,000 Tricolor vehicles missing** and the auction realising **~3% of debt**. TBK's own filing concedes *"other creditors have asserted that they have interests in some of the collateral in which the Bank asserts a first-priority security interest."*

**For REGINALD:** the question is no longer "is TBK exposed" but **"is TBK's carrying value defensible when a comparable holder wrote the same asset class to zero ten months ago?"** That is a loss-recognition-lag question of the same family as the $113M receivables escrow — banks unable or unwilling to book losses cleanly even where the substance is visible. Sizing and supervisory read are yours.

## Negative result, recorded so nobody repeats it

**The TBK syndicate roster is not obtainable from EDGAR.** Full-text search for `"TBK Bank" "Tricolor"` returns **10 hits, all Triumph's own filings**. No other SEC filer discloses participating in the $60.5M facility, so the ~$38M sits with **non-filers** — private lenders, credit unions, non-public banks — or below materiality thresholds.

Obtaining it needs the **Tricolor Ch.7 docket (Verita, cert-blocked for OTTO)** or **UCC-1 searches**. Both are REGINALD's channel rather than OTTO's. **OTTO-33 discovery via the syndicate route is closed unless docket access is obtained** — flagging that rather than leaving it as an open lead that quietly never resolves.
