# First Brands: Ch.7 Conversion Promoted PENDING → IN MOTION

## Headline

US Trustee Kevin Epstein (S.D. Tex.) filed motion **May 13 2026** to dismiss-or-convert all First Brands Group Ch.11 cases to Ch.7. This is regulator-driven, not just creditor pressure — the strongest possible institutional posture short of an actual order.

**OTTO signal trigger "First Brands converts to Ch.7" promoted from PENDING → IN MOTION.** Per CLAUDE.md routing matrix, this triggers 🔴 URGENT to CARL/REGINALD/BROCK/LIQUID.

## Three Material Filings (Apr-May 2026)

1. **Apr 28 2026 — Premier Marketing Group Ch.11 Plan + Disclosure Statement.** Architecture: PMG (one of ~112 FBG debtors) becomes the *only* Ch.11 vehicle; funded by Global Settlement with ad hoc group + UCC; **all other 111 FBG debtors convert to Ch.7**. Settlement transfers ALL estate litigation claims into the PMG-only litigation trust, which prosecutes/monetizes for creditors.

2. **May 13 2026 — US Trustee dismiss-or-convert motion.** Attacks the PMG-plan structure specifically. Arguments:
   - "No realistic prospect for reorganization"
   - $245M+ already paid in advisory fees
   - $223M outstanding administrative fees that FBG cannot pay (administratively insolvent)
   - Calls the Global Settlement a **"sleight of hand"** that strips 111 FBG debtor entities of remaining assets (litigation claims) while leaving liabilities behind

3. **May 20 2026 — Disclosure Statement conditional-approval hearing.** Held; outcome not yet sourced through public press as of EOD May 22. May 25 omnibus hearing follows.
