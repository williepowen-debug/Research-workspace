Two stronger industry/size comparisons are available in the **secondary** shortlist: MGM versus Wynn, and McDonald’s versus Starbucks. They pass the original return, conditional-loss and formation-size rules, but the weak names fail some separate index/sector repetition checks. MGM trailed Wynn by 17.68 / 16.07 percentage points over 20 / 60 sessions; McDonald’s trailed Starbucks by 5.23 / 3.03 points. Geography and business mix differ, particularly burger franchising versus coffee shops. These are qualified comparisons, not matched financing experiments. The important exclusions are explicit: SKYD has unresolved corporate-action history; VYLR lacks a complete grid. DELL and SMCI have positive 60-session returns and few recent BOTH-up losses, so their sharp recent declines do not establish the same persistent pattern. No peer’s missing size or security-class evidence is filled with an EPS average, current market capitalization or a filing published after formation.

These exclusions are descriptive sensitivities: removing good-news days can worsen remaining performance. They do not estimate what prices would have done without the events. Broad event halos leave too few 60-session qualifying days even for Mosaic and ADM. Missing announcement clocks, incomplete news capture and unmatched primary cases prevent a strong causal conclusion.

Equal-weight SPY exposure through RSP gained 0.10% / 0.34% / 0.30% over 5 / 20 / 60 sessions. HII, ADM and Copart also have positive five-session returns. This is inconsistent with a claim of uninterrupted market narrowing or uniform deterioration.
