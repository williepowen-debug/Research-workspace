## Limits that must travel

- Revenue is neither profit, operating cash flow nor invested-capital return. A revenue hurdle conditioned on a 15% return is not a market price for compute.
- ACV, current run-rate, modeled future revenue and potential token revenue have different recognition periods and assumptions. They are not one realized annual series.
- A powered GW does not itself normalize GPU generation, utilization, IT versus gross load, workload, useful life, networking or software service content.
- Cross-provider contract duration, cancellation clauses and denominators are not reconciled. No directly comparable executable quotes were obtained.
- Gross rent cannot establish a cash payback period without operating costs, maintenance/replacement capex and financing. The article’s collapse/compression thesis is an inference, not an observed cancellation or rate decline.
- Keep Brookings’s separately routed $10.3T buildout scenario separate: aggregate capital spending is a different quantity and time horizon.
