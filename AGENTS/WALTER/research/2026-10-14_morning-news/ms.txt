Morgan Stanley Third Quarter 2026 Earnings Results 

Morgan Stanley Reports Net Revenues of $20.3 Billion, EPS of $3.03 and ROTCE of 22.6%

NEW YORK, October 14, 2026 – Morgan Stanley (NYSE: MS) today reported net revenues of $20.3 billion for the 
third quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared with $18.2 billion a year ago. Net income applicable to Morgan 
Stanley was $4.9 billion, or $3.03 per diluted share, compared with $4.6 billion, or $2.80 per diluted share, for the 
same period a year ago.1 

Ted Pick, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, said, “Morgan Stanley reported another strong quarter with net 
revenues of $20.3 billion, EPS of $3.03 and a ROTCE of 22.6%. The Integrated Firm is connecting the full breadth of 
Morgan Stanley to deliver differentiated solutions and advice for our clients. Institutional Securities benefited from 
global strength, led by our Equity franchise, and Wealth Management reported record revenues and added $232 
billion in net new assets and $80 billion in fee-based flows. Across Wealth and Investment Management, total client 
assets grew to $10.5 trillion, demonstrating the scale of our platform and the strength of our franchise.  We continue 
to invest for growth while delivering strong returns for shareholders.”
