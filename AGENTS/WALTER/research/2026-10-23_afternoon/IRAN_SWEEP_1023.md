## 6. Istanbul ministerial, Fri 10/23: CONFIRMED

- **Eight FMs** attended: UK, France, Germany, **Italy**, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Egypt. That makes it "4+4" (Tajani's term), **not R4+E3**. Iran did not attend.
- They called for an **immediate reopening of Hormuz** and an end to Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, warned against military action, and agreed to meet at regular intervals.
- Quotes:
  - Barrot: escalation "would create nothing but losers".
  - Tajani: "I do not believe the problem can be solved from a purely military standpoint."
- Sources: CBS 1:22 PM and 10:15 AM (AT SOURCE); The National, Al-Monitor, Daily Sabah, 24NewsHD and AOL, all 10/23 (SNIPPET).
- No joint communiqué text found.
- "First in format" remains contested: an Istanbul meeting with 7 of these 8 was expected **8/30** (SNIPPET).
