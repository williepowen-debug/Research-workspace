## 1. Summary

UKMTO issued 7 products after 173-26 by the stamp time: 174-26 through 180-26 (six ATTACK warnings, one ADVISORY, 178-26). No 181-26 was found. **6 of 7 are mapped to a named hull with an IMO number; 1 (178-26, the Duqm advisory) remains unnamed.** None of the names come from UKMTO's own text. UKMTO printed no hull name on any of 174–180. Every name comes from a secondary source, though four of them rest on official non-UKMTO instruments: the IMO highlighted-incidents board (as at 21 Oct, 119 incidents, 24 deaths) and NAVAREA IX. **Reachability:** ukmto.org returned **403** to every request: the home page, the recent-incidents page, the warnings page and the per-number PDF URLs (curl with a browser UA, and WebFetch). So UKMTO's text below comes from (a) search-engine-indexed PDF text (179-26), (b) wire reprints, and (c) the Regulas Shipping blog, which quotes the PDFs at length. **Regulas is of unknown provenance**, looks automated (posts at fixed ~03:33 and ~11:33 UTC times) and is treated as a lead only. Each of its UKMTO quotations used here is corroborated by Clearwater Dynamics alerts (200) and/or wires. The IMO board (200), the NAVAREA IX mirror at sealagom.com (200) and the Clearwater alerts (200) were read directly. JMIC Update 106 (ukmto.org PDF) and MARAD MSCI returned 403, so the JMIC namings are second-hand, via Regulas.

**Most decision-relevant finding: FALCON's "AL DASMA struck 10/20" lead and its "10/21 tanker off Bukha, fire out" lead are very probably ONE event (UKMTO 179-26), not two.** Do not count them twice.

## 4. Gaps

- **UKMTO primary not read directly for any number.** All sources were 403: the site, the recent-incidents board and the per-number PDFs. Text for 174–178 and 180 comes from Regulas plus wires and Clearwater; text for 179 comes from search-indexed PDF text plus wires. A reader with UKMTO access should spot-check the 176-26 bearing (SW vs SE) and the 179-26 date (21 Oct 0145Z vs the IMO/NAVAREA 20 Oct).
- **178-26 (Duqm advisory): hull unknown.** No source names the tanker or the military force.
- **176-26 (AL KHARSAAH): single-source name** (Clearwater only). Neither IMO nor JMIC names it.
- **No UKMTO number has an explicit number-to-name link from an official body except 174-26** (JMIC 106, second-hand). Loyalty, Jif Barracuda, Al Dasma and Soyia are linked by matching date and geometry, plus the commercial Clearwater alerts.
- **181-26 onward:** none found as of 2026-10-22T00:43Z. The sequence may continue overnight.
- **JMIC Update 106** (ICOD 181300 UTC 18 Oct): the PDF returned 403. Its namings (ST3 on 174-26; 175, 176 and 177 "Name withheld UFN") are known only via Regulas. No Update 107 was seen.
- **"≥10 vessels in 7 days to 10/17":** source not located.
- The Basrah STS four-projectile event and the IRGC mine claims of 20–21 Oct have no UKMTO number and no named hull.
