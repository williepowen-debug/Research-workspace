**CLAIM CHECKED:** OddStats reports 55 QQQ instances of two 2.5% declines within five sessions also containing two 1% advances.
**EVIDENCE LOCATOR:** [Original October 23 post](https://x.com/OddStats/status/2113601040926785698); [Yahoo daily-price endpoint](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/QQQ?period1=915148800&period2=1792800000&interval=1d), retrieved October 25.
**SUPPORTING OBSERVATION:** Computed close-to-close returns from QQQ inception. Count a rolling five-session window when it contains at least two returns ≤−2.5%, at least two ≥+1%, **and its final session is ≤−2.5%**. This reproduces all 55 and every posted annual count: 2000:21, 2001:10, 2002:8, 2003:1, 2008:4, 2009:1, 2016:1, 2018:2, 2020:4, 2022:2, 2026:1. Raw and dividend-adjusted closes both match, through October 22 or 23. Without the final-down-day condition, there are 155 qualifying windows. October 16 is marginally through the threshold at −2.5000017%; premature rounding matters.
**BOUNDED CONCLUSION:** The 55-count is reproducible under those semantics. Overlapping windows and clustered years are not independent events; no predictive return or crash probability has been validated. Reproduction: `/tmp/walter-qqq-check.py`; own results `/tmp/walter-qqq-results.json`; raw `/tmp/walter-qqq-raw.json`.
