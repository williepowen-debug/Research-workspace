Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley Announce Landmark $63 Billion Financing Structure to Enable AI Compute Capacity in Partnership with Broadcom

NEW YORK, New YorkN.Y., CHARLOTTE, North CarolinaN.C., and PALO ALTO, CaliforniaC.A. — Bank of America (N Y S ENYSE: B A CBAC), Blackstone (N Y S ENYSE: B XBX), Citi (N Y S ENYSE: C) and Morgan Stanley (N Y S ENYSE: M SMS) (the “Lead Financial Partners”) today announced a sixty three billion dollar$63 billion transaction to enable the deployment of A IAI compute capacity, leveraging Broadcom Inc.’s (NASDAQ: A V G OAVGO) technology, for Anthropic. This transaction is part of the strategic compute capacity agreement previously announced by Broadcom, with expected deliveries starting in calendar 2027.

The transaction will be executed through a newly formed special purpose vehicle (“S P VSPV”) with committed financing from the Lead Financial Partners to support the purchase of Broadcom X P UXPU A IAI accelerators and networking solutions to be leased to Anthropic. The first lien portion of the financing is being arranged by Bank of America, Citi and Morgan Stanley, with Blackstone as the sole provider of the committed portion of the second lien financing. Broadcom is providing residual value support to the S P VSPV for the amount equivalent to the first lien portion of the financing. This transaction highlights the deep collaboration across the A IAI technology and financial ecosystems.

Jon Gray, President and C O OCOO, Blackstone, said, “The rapidly growing demand for A IAI is creating an enormous opportunity to finance compute capacity at scale. We’re proud to help advance this buildout with Broadcom and Anthropic.”

Dan Simkowitz, Co-President of Morgan Stanley, said, “This transaction represents an important milestone in enabling the next buildout of A IAI compute capacity. Morgan Stanley has been at the forefront of innovation in designing scalable capital solutions for A IAI infrastructure, and we are proud to continue to advance new financing structures through this transaction.”

Vis Raghavan, Head of Banking and Executive Vice Chair at Citi, said, “Financing A IAI demands capital solutions at unprecedented scale. Citi is proud to pioneer innovative structures and leverage our global capital markets structuring and distribution expertise to help accelerate the buildout of critical A IAI infrastructure that will power the future of technology for years to come.”

Jim DeMare, Co-President of Bank of America, said, “Advancing A IAI responsibly requires turning ambitious technology plans into tangible compute infrastructure. Bank of America’s infrastructure financing expertise, advisory capabilities and broad capital solutions platform enable us to deliver innovative structures and mobilize institutional capital at scale. This transaction brings together Broadcom’s leading technology and significant bank capital to accelerate the buildout of compute capacity that will power the next wave of A IAI innovation.”

Hock Tan, President and C E OCEO of Broadcom Inc., said, “Today’s transaction underscores the power of our advanced technology and our strong conviction in the A IAI ecosystem. Fundamentally, the need for A IAI compute continues to grow at an extraordinary pace. This validates that there is capital available to finance such growth and that Broadcom is uniquely positioned to enable it.”

Kirkland & Ellis L L PLLP is serving as legal counsel to Blackstone. Latham & Watkins L L PLLP is serving as legal counsel to Bank of America, Citi and Morgan Stanley. Sullivan & Cromwell L L PLLP is serving as legal counsel to Broadcom, with Evercore serving as financial advisor.

Additional information about the transaction is available on Broadcom’s investor relations website.
