            Overview
          

        

        
           

        

        

        Trepp and Commercial Real Estate Direct released the Q3 2026 edition of the Quarterly Data Review magazine. As rising long-term rates put the commercial real estate (CRE) recovery to the test, the team recaps the quarter’s data, trends, and activity in CRE finance and the commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) market.

The magazine features stories including:

- Increasing Treasury Yields Threaten to Stall CRE Credit Recovery- Office Loans Bring Different Risks to the CMBS Hard Maturity Calendar- CMBS Delinquency Volume Increases 11.64% Since Start of Year- Latest Property Pricing Indices Show Stabilization- And more...
