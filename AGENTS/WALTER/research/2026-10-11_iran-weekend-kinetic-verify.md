## (a) Bottom line

UKMTO's own products confirm **two** tanker strikes this weekend, and both are **projectile** strikes. Neither says "mine", and neither names the attacker.
- **162-26:** an outbound tanker was struck on its port side at **10 Oct 1920Z** in the Strait of Hormuz, with a fire on board and the crew safe.
- **164-26:** an outbound tanker was struck on its port side at **11 Oct 1200Z**, with damage, the crew safe and no fire stated. The map circle sits just east of the Musandam tip, the Omani side.

Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy (the IRGC Navy) separately claimed **two mine hits**, and both are **CLAIM-ONLY**:
- **Saturday claim:** on the wire by **17:19Z Sat** (Walter Bloomberg post). That is about two hours **before** the 162-26 strike, so the two cannot be the same event on the stated times. The ZeroHedge, Al Jazeera and Mehr items I read do not tie the two together. A search-engine summary of the weekend did state that the Saturday tanker "hit a naval mine", which runs the two together. Treat that merge as an error.
- **Sunday claim:** "afternoon", southern strait, ship exiting, engine-room fire. It surfaced about 17:30–17:45Z. It **may or may not** be the 164-26 ship: same day, same direction and same broad area, but a different mechanism and no vessel name. **INDETERMINATE.**

The "third tanker this weekend" count is the **IRGC's own** ("third tanker to hit mines since the previous day"). No independent source establishes three distinct tanker incidents. Independently established is 2 UKMTO strikes, plus 1–2 IRGC mine claims that may overlap them.

Two more items fall in the window, and **neither is an Iranian weekend strike**:
- The UAE coastguard (part of its National Guard) rescued the 22 crew of the VLCC **GEM NO. 2** on 10/10 after a fire. GEM NO. 2 was the 10/9 UKMTO 160-26 projectile hit, 13 nm west of Al Jazeera, UAE.
- CENTCOM's own jet disabled the Panama-flagged cargo ship **Ocean Molica** in the Gulf of Oman on 10/10.

**No mine detonation on a hull is established.** CENTCOM's X timeline (read at ~20:35Z 10/11; latest post 15:42Z) carries **no comment** on either IRGC mine claim, neither confirming nor denying it.

In Saudi Arabia:
- **Riyadh's King Khalid airport was hit again on Sun 10/11.** This is CONFIRMED by the coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki and the civil aviation authority GACA: a projectile struck Terminal 4 / Passenger Hall 4, causing a fire, injuries were reported, and a cruise missile was intercepted. It is distinct from the 10/10 strike that killed 12–13.
- **Dammam's King Fahd airport was "attacked" on Sun evening.** CONFIRMED by GACA; damage UNKNOWN.
- **Aramco's Shedgum gas plant ("Shagig") and Tanajib facility:** Houthi claim only. No Saudi or Aramco confirmation found.

**Bushehr:** Rosatom chief Likhachev put the Russian staff at 135 on Sun 10/11 (TASS via WANA). The claim's "up from ~20" baseline is wrong: the prior published figure was **63** on 9/22.

**Image:** the Paul M Smith image is a space-weather (solar-wind) screenshot, not Gulf content.

## (c) The mine question

**Is a mine detonation on a hull established? NO.**
- **UKMTO:** both weekend attack warnings (162-26, 164-26) say *projectile*.
- **CENTCOM:** no statement on the 10/10 or 10/11 claims. Its X timeline, read via fxtwitter at ~20:35Z 10/11, has posts on 10/11 at 00:06Z (Ocean Molica) and 15:42Z (F/A-18 maintenance), with no denial or confirmation. On 10/8 17:02Z it posted that CENTCOM forces "have successfully cleared sea mines from primary transit lanes".
- **Operator or flag state:** none found. Both weekend vessels are unnamed.
- **Independent wire:** none states a mine on its own sourcing. Reuters, The National, Al Jazeera and gCaptain all carry the mine only as an IRGC claim, and The National says it "could not be independently verified".
- **Pattern:** the T3/T4 IRGC mine claims extend the registered claim-and-deny pattern (July, ~8/31–9/1, 9/14 El Gaia, 10/8 Fars). No CENTCOM denial was found **this time**. That is an absence of comment, not a confirmation.
- **Date-trap:** search results surfaced "El Gaia" (IMO 9325336) mine stories. Those are the **mid-September** event, not this weekend.
