The observation month is August, publication date September 8. This is worldwide goods trade, not the bilateral US surplus. AP text was available through search; direct opening failed.

[Caixin September 8](https://economy.caixin.com/m/2026-09-08/102482752.html) separately reports USD exports **$401.44B**, imports **$282.36B**, and surplus **$119.09B**, confirming the rounded headline. It expressly identifies the currency basis as dollars. These are nominal trade values; neither headline growth rate is a shipment-volume rate. Caixin describes semiconductor export value/volume divergence, so avoid translating export-value strength into equivalent physical chip-demand growth.

## Japanese dichlorosilane: operative primary reached

[MOFCOM Announcement 37 of 2026](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/blgg/gg/2026/art/2026/art_89051f27ba814b4a97271861d47b39b5.html), dated **September 7**, was opened through the ministry's 2026 announcement index. Its preliminary determination requires provisional anti-dumping deposits beginning **September 8, 2026**, for **Japan-origin dichlorosilane imported into China**.

| Producer | Deposit ratio |
|---|---:|
| Shin-Etsu Chemical | 99.2% |
| Denal Silane | 80.8% |
| Other Japanese companies | 99.2% |

The covered substance is DCS, SiH2Cl2, purity above 99%, within tariff heading 28539090; other products under that heading are excluded. Its formula applies the ratio to customs taxable value and multiplies by one plus import VAT. It is a provisional deposit following a preliminary determination, not a final duty ruling, import ban, or blanket measure on semiconductor materials. The January 7 investigation is historical; September's preliminary determination and deposit start are new.

The [AP Japanese-material report](https://apnews.com/article/ea58c95c47481c5c822a3a544e6b68cc) is therefore directionally accurate: Japanese exports face Chinese import measures. **This is not China restricting its own exports to Japan.** AP search text was accessible; direct opening failed. MOFCOM original HTML supplies the operative evidence; the linked investigation PDF was not read, so the underlying dumping/injury calculations were not independently audited.
