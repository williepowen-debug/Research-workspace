I am looking for data for a specific city or county of the state. Where do I find this?

VISIT FLORIDA tracks information for the entire state and by eight geographic vacation regions. We do not keep data on specific cities or counties. This information can best be obtained from the local Convention and Visitors Bureau (CVB). The Florida Association of Destination Marketing Organizations maintains a list of local destination marketing organizations.

How many visitors came to Florida last year?

Estimates indicate that in 2025, Florida received 143.3 million visitors (not including residents), an increase of 0.2% from 2024. For current figures, please check the research page on VISITFLORIDA.org.

Do most visitors come to Florida by car or plane?

The air/non-air split for all visitors (not including residents) in 2025 is estimated to have been 40.7% by air and 59.3% by non-air means.

How many international visitors come to Florida?

Estimates indicate that in 2025, 12.5 million visitors came to Florida from Canadian and overseas markets. Overseas visitation is estimated at 9.3 million in 2025, a 4.0% increase from 2024 (and a decrease of 5.1% from 2019), with overseas travelers accounting for 6.5% of total visitors. Overseas visitation came closer to full recovery of pre-pandemic 2019 levels in 2025 than in any year since the beginning of the pandemic. Canadian visitation is estimated at 3.2 million, a decrease of 6.8% from 2024 (and a decrease of 22.4% from 2019), with Canadians making up 2.2% of total visitors.

How many domestic visitors choose Florida as a destination?

Estimates indicate that domestic travel to Florida was up by 0.2% in 2025 compared to 2024, with 130.9 million domestic visits to Florida. Domestic visitation accounted for 91.3% of total visitation, down slightly from 91.4% in 2024. 

What states do domestic visitors to Florida come from?

The top origin states for domestic visitors to Florida in 2025 were Georgia (8.1%), Texas (8.1%), New York (7.7%), Pennsylvania (5.2%), and North Carolina (4.9%).

What countries do international travelers to Florida come from?

The top origin countries to Florida in 2025 were Canada (3.2 million), Brazil (1.3 million), the United Kingdom (1.2 million), Colombia (655k), and Mexico (613k).

What activities do Florida visitors participate in the most?

The most popular activities for domestic visitors in 2025 were beach or waterfront activities (36%), culinary/dining (23%), and shopping (21%). Overseas visitors in 2024 cited shopping (88%), sightseeing (73%), and amusement and theme parks (49%) as the most popular activities in Florida.

How many people are employed in tourism in Florida?

In 2024, visitor spending directly and indirectly supported 1.8 million jobs in the state.

What is the hotel occupancy in Florida?

In 2025, the statewide average hotel/motel occupancy rate was 68.1%. This was a decrease of 1.7% from 2024. Source: STR

What is the average daily room rate in Florida?

In 2025, the average daily room rate for hotels/motels in Florida was $193.69. This was a 2.7% increase from 2024. Source: STR

How many hotel rooms are in Florida?

As of December 2025, there were 507,282 hotel and motel rooms in Florida spread over 4,738 properties. For more information contact the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation who track these statistics.

What is a bed tax?

Counties may levy a tourist development tax or "bed tax" as a percent of the total charge (rate varies depending on county) for the lease or rental of living accommodations in any hotel, apartment hotel, motel, resort motel, apartment, apartment motel, rooming house, recreational vehicle park, mobile home park or condominium for a term of six months or less. The Florida Department of Revenue, Office of Tax Research collects and tracks bed tax collections by Florida county. For more information, visit the Florida Department of Revenue website at www.floridarevenue.com.

What is Florida's population?

The U.S. Census estimated Florida’s population in 2025 at 23.5 million people, an 8.9% increase from 2020. For more information visit the U.S. Census Bureau's website at www.census.gov.

How many people move to Florida each year?

VISIT FLORIDA does not track how many people relocate to the state each year, but the U.S. Census Bureau does and refers to it as "Domestic Migration" (www.census.gov).

How many "snowbirds" does Florida have?

Since many seasonal residents ("snowbirds") own homes and are part-time residents of the state, they are not tracked by VISIT FLORIDA.

How many nature parks are in Florida?

There are 175 state parks and 35 state forests (www.floridastateparks.org). There are also 11 national parks, preserves, seashores or monuments, 3 national forests, and 1 national scenic trail (www.us-national-parks.net). For additional information check the websites listed above.
