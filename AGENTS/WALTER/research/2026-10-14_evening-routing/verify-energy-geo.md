## 7. UN warning on Hormuz fertilizer: CORRECTED-FRAMING (a conditional warning; no deal "failed")

- **Source read:** Reuters, Oct 14, via ThePrint: https://theprint.in/world/failure-to-activate-hormuz-fertilizer-plan-could-trigger-food-crisis-un-official-says/3071771/ (also on USNews).
- **Speaker:** Jorge Moreira da Silva, **UN Under-Secretary-General** (Reuters' title). He oversees the UN Hormuz fertilizer taskforce and heads UNOPS. He spoke in Bangkok on the sidelines of the IMF/World Bank annual meetings. This is **not** FAO, WFP or UNCTAD.
- **Exact claim:** failure by the US and Iran to agree **within weeks** on the UN plan **could** trigger a food-security crisis in parts of Asia and Africa. "It's absolutely crucial to get the deal in the next few weeks. Otherwise, we will miss the planting season."
- **The plan:**
  - Taskforce set up in March; mechanism drafted but **not activated**.
  - It can be activated **7–10 days after** a US–Iran agreement.
  - An Oman coordination centre would register vessels, UN monitors would verify loading at Gulf ports, and ships would be tracked in transit.
  - **Five ships a day** would keep fertilizer moving to countries in critical need.
- **Status:** no agreement. There was **no discrete fertilizer negotiation that "failed."** Broader US–Iran proposal exchanges through intermediaries have stalled since the June interim ceasefire collapsed, over Tehran's conditions and Washington's demand that the nuclear programme be addressed first. The White House, State Department and Iran's embassy did not comment.
- **Figures (each with its basis):**
  - Pre-war Hormuz share: about one-third of globally traded urea and nearly half of seaborne sulphur.
  - IFPRI: Gulf fertilizer exports remain "largely" halted. Fertilizer was a "very negligible" share of shipping during the mid-June to mid-July ceasefire.
  - ICC August report: cereals up to +81%, oil crops +66%, animal products +30% **if the disruption continues**.
- **Tweet vs UN:** the tweet ("as the U.S. & Iran fail to reach a deal") turns a **forward-looking conditional** into an accomplished failure and a present crisis.
- **Domain note:** the product is urea and sulphur, not potash.

- **Sources read:** NYT (not read directly) as relayed by EFE/Infobae on Oct 14 (https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/10/14/eeuu-inicia-despliegue-de-600-soldados-en-panama-por-campana-antidrogas-segun-el-nyt/) and by Anadolu.
- **Facts:**

  - Purpose: expansion of **counternarcotics** operations. The unit would train with Panamanian, Colombian and Honduran forces and **may support US special-operations advisers in partner-force raids** on suspected traffickers.

- **Not the canal, not migration:** the location is not named and nothing in the reports links it to canal security.
- **Status:** **reported via anonymous officials.** No Pentagon or SOUTHCOM announcement was found (USA Herald says the same). No Panamanian government reaction found.
- **Background:** the jungle-training programme was revived in August 2025 under a bilateral arrangement. WOLA's Adam Isacson called it the largest such regional deployment outside an invasion or disaster response.
