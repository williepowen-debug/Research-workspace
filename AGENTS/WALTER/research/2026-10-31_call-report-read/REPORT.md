## What matters for the existing bank exposure

The 14-bank sample shows rate-related book-equity pressure, but does not establish the predicted broad October 30 capital shock. No selected bank lost 20% of reported bank equity quarter-to-quarter; the largest decline is Amerant at 8.83%. AOCI deterioration relative to June equity ranges from 1.58% to 9.75%. These are two distinct measured objects. This nonrandom cohort cannot disprove the separate nationwide claim about 95 of 4,295 banks.

For the existing OZK/WAL downside thesis, the releases contain meaningful contrary evidence: fewer nonaccrual loans and lower quarterly net charge-offs. This does not close unresolved collateral/OREO questions, but adds no 20% capital-cliff evidence. FLG has the more concerning combination of thin earnings, persistent problem loans and lower capital; much of that was already in the October 23 release. Its newly retrieved M3 composition step is an incremental owner-review item. AMTB/SBCF demonstrate the duration channel most clearly but are not the core single-name puts in the October 30 FORGE mirror. No current option quotes, consensus revision or unpriced-catalyst claim is made.
