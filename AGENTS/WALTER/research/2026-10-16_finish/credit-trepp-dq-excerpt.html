CMBS Delinquency Rate Rose 17 Basis Points in September 2026

  • Share This Article

October 2nd, 2026 | 3 min. read

Subscribe

Trepp Research

Print/Save as PDF

Disclaimer: This text is an excerpt from September 2026 CMBS Delinquency Report. To access the full analysis of the report, click here.

The Trepp commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) delinquency rate rose 17 basis points to 8.02% in September 2026, its highest level since November 2020. Five large single-asset, single-borrower loans drove much of the increase.

The five largest newly delinquent loans spanned mixed-use, office, and lodging collateral: a $1.10 billion eight-property studio-and-office portfolio loan in Los Angeles, a $470.0 million two-tower office complex in Houston, a $280.0 million beachfront hotel loan in Santa Monica, a $230.1 million office loan in Denver, and a $208.9 million single-tenant office loan in Silicon Valley.

Delinquency rates increased or remained flat across four of the five major property types, while retail fell sharply. Multifamily rose 35 basis points to 8.04% behind a broad group of loans moving to 30-day delinquent status across several states. Lodging rose 34 basis points to 6.18%, and office rose 16 basis points to 12.16%, both led by the new delinquencies described above. Industrial was unchanged at 1.14% with limited newly delinquent volume. Retail fell 62 basis points to 6.58% on the mall cures.  

Download the September 2026 Delinquency Report to see readings for each major property type and CMBS 1.0 and 2.0+.
