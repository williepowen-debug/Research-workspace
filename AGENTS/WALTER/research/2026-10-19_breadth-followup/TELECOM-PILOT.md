June 30, 2026 balances, $ billions. Filings all predate September18, the 20-session return-window start. Verification helper read the primary filings; issuer accounting perimeters differ.

**Verizon:** current debt includes $15.640B receivable/ABS financing. Unused revolving capacity $11.976B. Debt presentation also includes finance leases; there is a $2.5B current residual-interest repo. About76% of debt principal fixed after designated swaps. These facts prevent equating current debt with unsecured bonds coming due. Frontier acquisition affects debt and operating comparisons. [10-Q, balance sheet, Notes3/5 and Market Risk](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/732712/000073271226000046/vz-20260630.htm).

**AT&T:** current debt is long-term debt due within a year. Cash was elevated ahead of an approximately$23B EchoStar spectrum transaction; $17.5B delayed-draw financing was undrawn at June30. Additional facilities have separate purposes/conditions and cannot all be pooled as unrestricted liquidity. Vendor finance outside debt and receivable sales further complicate comparison. Net floating exposure unmeasured. June30 cash is not September available liquidity. [10-Q, balance sheet, Notes7/10 and liquidity discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/732717/000073271726000297/t-20260630.htm).

**T-Mobile:** $84.621B debt excludes separately presented leases and tower obligations. ABS debt includes $0.580B current/$1.415B noncurrent. A separate $1B SOFR-linked receivables facility expires initially February5,2027; not all debt floats. General $10B revolver undrawn, but exact unused capacity after other uses not independently reconciled. Full net floating exposure unmeasured. [10-Q, balance sheet and Notes2/8/9](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1283699/000128369926000101/tmus-20260630.htm).

## What is actually unfinished

Subsequent-event and acquisition financing updates; reconciled 12/24-month unsecured maturities; cash restrictions and committed availability; comparable interest coverage and floating exposure after hedges; broader within-industry/size-matched cohort with pre-window fundamentals. Standard SEC CompanyFacts tags alone are insufficient: some familiar exact debt/interest tags ended years ago for these issuers. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces). No missing figure is filled with a stale tag. The pilot began the fundamental investigation; it did not complete the proposed market-wide test.
