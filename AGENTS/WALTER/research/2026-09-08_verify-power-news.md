### ERCOT: September 3 provisional notices, with conditions remaining

**[ERCOT market notice M-A080326-04](https://www.ercot.com/services/comm/mkt_notices/M-A080326-04), September 3, 2026, 15:55**, opened and read directly.

ERCOT notified interconnecting DSPs/TSPs of provisional classifications. Conditions can include applicant verification, curing dynamic-model deficiencies, or PUCT good-cause exceptions. Failure to satisfy applicable conditions may disqualify a load. Base-load submissions reclassified as studied load have a dispute/reconciliation process. The notice explicitly discusses some submissions originally described as energized not qualifying under that eligibility provision. It does **not** list IREN by name or independently certify IREN's project-specific classification.

Novelty: real September 3 process development after the August pause. This is not a blanket end to all constraints or proof of actual consumption.

### PJM: proposals already dated July 31 and August 13

**[PJM RBP announcement](https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-reliability-backstop-proposal-outlines-steps-to-secure-new-supply-and-maintain-reliability/), July 31, 2026**, opened and read directly. The page's masthead displays today's date; the article date is July 31.

The proposal seeks a September 30 procurement start against the 6,831 MW 2028/29 auction shortfall, for terms up to 15 years, with maximum willingness to pay expressed as a MW-weighted average $555/MW-day. Qualifying bilateral new supply reduces the initial target to avoid double procurement. State action remains necessary for retail cost allocation. This is a procurement proposal, not 6 GW awarded or built on September 8.

**[PJM filed IRAS proposal, ER26-3515-000](https://www.pjm.com/pjmfiles/directory/etariff/FercDockets/9827/20260813-er26-3515-000.pdf), August 13, 2026**, direct PJM-hosted 435-page filing opened; cover, introductory text, definition, and requested-effective-date passages read (PDF pages 1, 7, 11, 13–14, 37, 127–128; one-indexed).

The filing explicitly requests **October 12, 2026** effectiveness; IRAS would become available from **June 1, 2027**, the 2027/28 delivery year. The large-load definition uses cumulative peak **50 MW or greater**, with affiliated-facility aggregation provisions. The construct is **Bring Your Own New Capacity (BYONC)**. It proposes load-reduction priority where new large loads lack new capacity backing. No FERC approval order was retrieved; the filing proves proposal terms, not enactment. This directly upgrades WATT's IRAS docket/date citation from secondary to primary. Do not merge RBP generation cost allocation with compensation for interrupted loads.

Novelty: July/August policy content already in WATT KB070 and KB102, not new September 8 or August 27 enactment. New to this check is retrieval of the actual IRAS filing, not a new policy event.
