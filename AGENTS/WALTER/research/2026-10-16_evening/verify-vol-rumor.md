**Readings (inference, labelled as such):**
1. **Oil does not explain the steep VIX leg in time.** The pipeline headline came at 12:08. Brent rose 107.66 → 109.46 by 12:35, and VIX gained only about +0.4 over that stretch. The main VIX leg (12:47 → 13:20, +4.47 pts) came while Brent was flat at ~109.0–109.6. Oil was the backdrop, not the trigger of that 33-minute leg.
2. **The steep leg was vol-market-specific.** VIX rose 25% while the S&P fell only 0.45%. The AI-infra names that overlap the VARA 13F (CRWV, NBIS, BE, LITE, STX, WDC, COHR) fell just −0.1% to −1.1% in that window, and HYG fell −0.16%. A forced *equity* liquidation of an AI-infra book is **not visible in that window**; most of those names' declines came later (13:30 → 15:59). This fits VIX-call demand or a short-vol squeeze (SpotGamma's "monetizing October VIX positions and rolling"; SVIX −8.8% in the window). It is also consistent with a rumor-driven hedge bid. **The data cannot tell these apart**, and a "short-vol participant blown out" reading is equally unproven.
3. **Rumor vs path:** the earliest rumor publication found is SA at 14:47, *after* the 13:20 peak. Renick's "30-minute window" matches the 12:47–13:20 leg, but the time of his remark is unknown. The direction of causation (did the rumor drive the spike, or the spike breed the rumor?) is **not established**.
4. Daily: the VARA 13F top names closed −2.9% to −12.2% (NBIS −12.2%, STX −7.5%, COHR −7.1%, WDC −6.9%, BE −6.3%, LITE −5.2%) vs QQQ −2.5%. Caveat: I picked these names from the rumor post (selection bias), and they are exactly the AI-debt names that would fall on the CoreWeave/AI-credit story anyway. Their underperformance does not identify a seller.
5. Untested alternative: on monthly-opex Friday the VIX *calculation* can be inflated by wider SPX option quotes. However, the VX futures (V6 +2.48), VVIX and the ETP moves show real trading in the vol complex, so a pure calculation artifact is unlikely to explain the whole move.
