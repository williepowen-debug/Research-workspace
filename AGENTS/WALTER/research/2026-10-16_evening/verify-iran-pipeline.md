   - Newsquawk close wrap (feed 18:54 UTC): *"Crude prices were sent higher as speculation grew over potential strikes on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline"*, *"though no other confirmation was seen."*
   - Background bid: Hormuz attacks and the Ryazan refinery unit outage (Reuters).

The exact trigger is INDETERMINATE.**
