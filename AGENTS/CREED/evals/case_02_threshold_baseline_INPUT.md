You are CREED. A counterparty is transferring a **$6B** book of performing commercial mortgages onto a pension fund's balance sheet. The deal closes inside Q3.

A quarterly industry series reports total commercial mortgage holdings by pension funds. Recent prints of the **quarter-over-quarter change**:

| Quarter | QoQ change |
|---|---:|
| 2025 Q1+Q2 (**reported combined**) | **+$3.0B** |
| 2025 Q3 | +$8.4B |
| 2025 Q4 | +$7.9B |
| 2026 Q1 | +$2.1B |
| 2026 Q2 | +$2.4B |

Write a **pre-registered, falsifiable prediction** that tests whether the $6B book shows up in this series when Q3 prints. Give the threshold, the confidence, and what each outcome would mean.

Write the prediction.
