# 4. What this does and does not establish

**Establishes**
1. OZK's realized CRE losses are **mostly named and mostly in this ledger**. The 10 listed credits explain ~74% of OZK's gross charge-offs over four quarters (86% with The Jack), 87% of its nonperforming assets and 98% of its foreclosed property, all reconciled to the OZK desk at MATCH.
2. At OZK the named losses are **earnings-sized**: $179M over four quarters = 3.4% of CET1, ~0.7 of one quarter's pre-provision revenue. CET1 rose through the period.
3. CASE-CREED-128 duplicates CASE-CREED-027 (Sullivan). The Sullivan name now rests on press that ties to the filing's $72.4M charge-off, not only on the desk's mapping.
4. Arbor's named Texas pair is immaterial and closed at **no** realized loss. Arbor's **aggregate** REO + NPL (~$1.07B ≈ 37% of total equity) is material, and Q2 credit costs exceeded pre-credit earnings ~1.8×.

**Does NOT establish**
1. **A named loss is not a trade.** CREED maps holders. Trade construction belongs to TERRY; OZK and multifamily judgment belong to the OZK desk and HOMER; Will approves.
2. **Bank disclosures are aggregate and unnamed.** Every property name at OZK is a desk or press mapping. Sullivan and Chapter have lender-plus-amount links; Brickworks has lender, uniqueness and date; **San Carlos has only an inference**. None has a recorded-deed crosswalk (Will v4's standard).
3. **Carrying value is not recovery.** $288M of OZK foreclosed property sits at 86–100% of appraisals with **no 2026 sale tested**. $257.8M of OZK nonaccrual carries **$0** reserve. Future loss can exceed today's charge-offs, and none of it appears in §2's loss numerator.
4. **Origination is not holding.** The 777 Industrial note was originated by Square Mile, and The Jack by Claros (per Atrium). OZK holds collateral-assigned positions, so the loss sits partly with other lenders.
5. **"Paid off" and "recapitalized" are not "repaid in full"** (San Carlos: payoff after a 23% charge-off; Sullivan: new loan size undisclosed). **Foreclose-and-resell is not exit** (Arbor re-lent $41.9M of $42.5M in proceeds to the buyers, at 81–88% loan-to-capitalization). **A CLO par redemption is not asset repayment** (CLO 17's ~$1.21B of loans moved to JPMorgan repo at higher leverage).
6. The Sullivan ~$232M assumed mortgage vs OZK's $156.4M book is a **possible** future recovery, not a booked one. Its basis (legal balance vs new loan) is unstated.
7. A gain on foreclosure (+$0.5M, Arbor Texas) is an accounting result on a carrying value that already reflected reserves. It is not proof of full economic recovery (Will's workbook says the same).
