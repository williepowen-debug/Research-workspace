## DELIVERABLE 4 — What this does and does not establish
1. **At-issuance ≠ at-loss.** Prospectus buyers are "expected" buyers at closing. For 2018+ deals the 10-D cover prints the current directing holder, so COR3 (LoanCore), B17 (KKR), B18 (Eightfold), C9 (BSPRT/FBRT), C5 (Barings), CD2 (Och-Ziff) are CONFIRMED as the named directing/controlling party at or just before the loss month. For C29, C30, JPMDB 2016-C2, CD3, P7 the holder at loss is NOT printed → unknown; the B-piece may have traded.
2. **The named directing holder is the controlling-class representative; it owns at least part of the controlling class, and usually the classes below it.** It does not prove the same entity still owned every class that was written down (e.g., F-RR/G-RR/H-RR could sit in different vehicles of the same manager). Risk-retention (-RR / HRR) classes were transfer-restricted for ≥5 years from closing (B17/B18/C9 closed 2020 → restricted to 2025; COR3/C5/CD3 restriction lapsed 2022–23), which makes the at-issuance buyer more likely, not certain, to be the holder at loss.
3. **A fund is not its listed manager.** KKR (NYSE: KKR) funds, BlackRock "managed account", OZ/Sculptor funds (now Rithm's asset-management arm), SEI mutual funds: losses fall on LPs/clients; the listed parent bears at most fee and co-investment effects. **The one balance-sheet link found is FBRT**: BSPRT 2020-C9 Owner LLC is a subsidiary in FBRT's own Ex-21. Even here the 3000 Post Oak loss to C9 is NOT yet reported, and FBRT may hold these bonds in a consolidated or fair-value bucket — size the exposure from FBRT's 10-Q, not from this file. (C9's Class H balance was $19,444,612.43 in Aug-2026; a pro-rata C9 Post Oak loss ≈ $12.1M, DERIVED, would hit H first — subject to the VRR share.)
4. **Servicers are not loss-bearers.** LNR Partners (STWD), Midland (PNC), KeyBank (KEY), CWCapital, K-Star (KKR), Green Loan Services (SLG) earn fees. Do not read a servicer's parent as an exposure.
5. **Vertical risk retention = small bank balance-sheet exposure.** Post-2016 deals (B17, B18, C9, CD3, C5? [C5 used horizontal HRR], P7 V-classes) put ~2.9–5% of each loss on the sponsor banks (JPMCB, DB, Citi/CREFI, GS Bank). B17 VRR took $705,105.67 on Post Oak; CD3 VRR $1,916,917.96; P7 V-classes $679,579.72. These are real but immaterial to those banks.
6. **Originator ≠ holder** — confirmed in every deal here: JPMCB/DB/Citi originated, sold the notes into the trusts, and kept only the vertical slice where required.
7. **Senior-holder lists are a sample.** N-PORT shows only registered funds that reported a recognisable title; insurers (the likely largest AAA holders), banks, and private accounts are invisible in free sources. The four fund holdings found are dated 2021–2024 and may have been sold before the loss.
