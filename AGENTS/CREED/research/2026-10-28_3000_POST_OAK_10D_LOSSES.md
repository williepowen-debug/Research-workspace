**The $64.8M figure is REPRODUCED.** The three trusts' September 2026 10-D trustee reports (Ex. 99.1) together print realized losses of **$64,782,869.38**. All three liquidated the loan on the **09/17/2026** distribution. CRE News's $64.8M (2026-09-25, SECONDARY) is that sum rounded. CREED's pro-rata ESTIMATE of ~$64.50M ran **$0.28M low**. The gap is B18's higher fees and advances: B18 shows $1,622,324.84 of fees, advances and expenses against a pro-rata B17-equivalent of ~$0.56M.

⚠️ Basis: the $64.8M is the realized loss to the **three senior CMBS notes**, measured against the **balance at the loss of $74,579,200.28**. It is **not** an $80M whole-loan loss, and it **excludes the $20M mezz**, which is not in any trust.

## Anomaly, flagged and not resolved

**B18's gross proceeds are $6,115,883.16.** That is **$1,150,344 more than pro rata**: B17's $4,256,176.15 × 43.75/37.5 = $4,965,539. Two other numbers in the B18 line don't follow the pattern of the other two notes:
- Its "net proceeds received on liquidation" ($5,750,394.42) is $365,488.74 below its gross. On B17 and C9, gross and net are equal.
- Its fees and advances are 3.4× the pro-rata level.

C9's gross is exactly half of B17's ($2,128,084.73 vs $2,128,088.08 pro rata), so B17 and C9 are internally consistent.

**Hypothesis, not read:** B18's note A-1 is the lead or controlling note. If so, its gross figure carries reimbursements of servicing advances and expenses passed through from the whole loan. That would be consistent with the $364,889.56 of other-expense advances already noted as B18-only.

**What it means for the loss:** whatever the reason, B18's realized loss and severity (87.35%) are still printed figures. The anomaly changes how proceeds split across the trusts, not the total loss. **Total gross proceeds to the three trusts ($12.50M) should NOT be quoted as the sale price.** The price is still undisclosed. Trepp's "$11.4M" is closer to the pro-rata ~$11.35M implied by B17 and C9.
