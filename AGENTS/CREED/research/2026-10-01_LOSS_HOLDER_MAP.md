## Bottom line

**The losses are real and deep at the property level, but they land mostly on private funds. Where they reach a listed balance sheet, they are small, with one exception: Arbor.**
- **Arbor Realty (ABR) is the one listed lender where the problem book is large relative to capital.** Foreclosed property plus problem loans ≈ **$1.07B ≈ 37% of total equity ($2,881.0M, VERIFIED, 10-Q 6/30/26)**. Q2 credit costs ≈ 1.8× earnings before credit costs (agent calculation). Arbor resold its Texas foreclosures with its own financing, so the exposure was re-lent, not exited.
- **Bank OZK's named losses are earnings-sized, not capital-sized:** $179.2M over four quarters ≈ **3.4% of CET1** ($5,300.5M, 11.80%) and **39% of its reserve** ($461.5M) (FDIC call report 6/30/26, VERIFIED). They are also most of what OZK has disclosed: 74% of four-quarter gross charge-offs, 98% of foreclosed property. **The open risk is what is not yet booked:** $257.8M of nonaccrual loans with no reserve, including 10 Prospect at $169.3M; $288M of foreclosed property (VERIFIED: ORE $288.1M) untested by any 2026 sale; $616M of special-mention loans (agent, OZK filings).
- **CMBS losses fall on first-loss funds**, mostly private or fund vehicles: Eightfold, KKR funds, LoanCore, BlackRock and Torchlight accounts, OZ/Sculptor funds, Barings, Rialto. **One confirmed listed balance-sheet link: Franklin BSP Realty (FBRT)** holds the first-loss class of DBJPM 2020-C9, which carries a piece of 3000 Post Oak (FBRT 10-K Exhibit 21, filed 2026-02-25, VERIFIED). Its maximum exposure there is the $19.4M Class H balance (agent, 10-D): **≈1.4% of FBRT equity** ($1,372.6M, VERIFIED). Small.
- **Ashford (AHT): handing back the KEYS hotels produced accounting GAINS**: $53.4M (2023), $167.2M (2024), $39.1M (2025) (AHT 10-K, VERIFIED). The debt exceeded the hotels' book value. AHT is under severe stress for other reasons: equity **−$556.5M** (VERIFIED, 6/30/26), a going-concern warning and suspended preferred dividends (agent, 10-Q).
- **Safehold (SAFE) is the ground landlord at 135 W. 50th, not a lender.** Structurally senior: it advanced $8.3M of property taxes and is in court with the building's owner. It is SAFE's #2 asset (5.0% of gross book value; agent), with no impairment found. Equity $2,439.1M (VERIFIED).

## What this does and does not establish

- **A named loss is not a trade.** Size against capital, what is already priced, and position context belong to TERRY and Will.
- **At-issuance holders may have sold.** For 2018+ deals the 10-D names the current controlling holder; for 2015 deals (C29, C30) the at-loss holder is not printed.
- **A fund is not its listed manager.** Losses in KKR, BlackRock, OZ/Sculptor or SEI funds fall on investors; the listed parent bears fees and any co-investment at most.
- **Bank losses stay invisible outside OZK.** OZK is mapped only because it discloses by credit. No other regional bank appears, which is the `CREED-T-03` scope limit again, not evidence of no exposure.
- **Senior holders (insurers, banks) are not visible** in free sources. The SEI, Clarion and Catholic Responsible Investments fund positions are 2021–2024 samples, not at-loss holdings.
