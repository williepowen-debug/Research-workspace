## 5. When to re-check

1. **Doc 93 contents: this resolves the question.** Buy it on PACER (about $0.10–$0.30), or re-check CourtListener in 1–3 days in case a RECAP user uploads it. The notice's caption and signature block will name the filer. If it reads "FDIC as Receiver for Nano Banc", the lien was RETAINED. If it names Sunwest Bank, it was SOLD. If it is someone else (for example Zarnighian's client), neither.
2. **Minute entry or order for the 9/29 hearing:** usually posted 1–3 days later, so re-check about **9/30–10/2**. Also watch for an order on Doc 82 (receiver turnover and adequate protection). The order may name who receives adequate protection payments on the Nano lien.
3. **Continued-hearing calendar:** if the matters were continued, the next MH calendar PDF on ecf-ciao.cacb.uscourts.gov will list new appearances. Look for counsel appearing "for FDIC as Receiver for Nano Banc" or for Sunwest.
4. **Claim transfer or substitution filing** (Rule 3001(e) notice, substitution of creditor): only needed if the lien was SOLD, since a receiver steps in by operation of law. Could come days to weeks out.
5. **FDIC P&A agreement for Nano Banc:** expected on the FDIC failed-bank page around **10/05–10/09** (the brief's estimate). It usually does not list individual loans, so on its own it may not settle this lien.

Caveat on the calendar evidence: the posted calendar lists only telephone appearances registered before it was printed at 7:27 am 9/29. The missing Nano, FDIC or Sunwest name does not show that nobody appeared for the Nano lien. **Do not read the missing appearance as proof of either RETAINED or SOLD.**
