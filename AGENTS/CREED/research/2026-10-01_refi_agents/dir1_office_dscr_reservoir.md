# Direction 1: the office "current but not covering" reservoir, outside Trepp

*Commissioned by CREED, Will-approved 2026-09-30. Research agent run 2026-10-01 (stamp from `date`: Thu Oct 1 13:04 EDT 2026). Anchor held by CREED and NOT re-researched here: Trepp 2026-08-28 reports $12.1B / 162 performing urban+suburban office CMBS loans with DSCR <1.00x, out of $97.2B (median 0.67x). Trepp 2026-08-20 adds that $5.17B of that sits on buildings that are ≥80% occupied.*

## Bottom line (5 lines)

1. **No publisher other than Trepp was found to print a like-for-like aggregate for performing office loans with DSCR <1.00x.** Trepp's $12.1B has no independent replication. Checked: KBRA, CRED iQ, Morningstar DBRS, Fitch, S&P, Moody's, Fed FSR (May 2026 + Nov 2025), OCC SRP (Spring 2026), CREFC, and FDIC. The KBRA full reports are paywalled and S&P returned 403, so this is a finding about what was reachable, not a claim that no such figure exists.
2. **The best independent read on the same reservoir is CRED iQ (2026-09-24, PRIMARY-READ).** It measures what happens to the reservoir, not how big it is. In the last 12 months, 51% of office loans transferred to special servicing were still current at transfer (up from 42% in the prior 12 months), at a median of about 11 months before maturity. Of 93 office loans that transferred while current between Aug-2024 and Aug-2025, 72% later went 60+ days delinquent or matured unpaid. Separately, $13.9B of the $39B of office CMBS maturing in the next 12 months shows warning signs. That is a wider net (DSCR <1.25x, OR occupancy down 10+ points, OR a recent watchlist addition), so it is **not comparable** to Trepp's $12.1B.
3. **Interest reserves: no aggregate figure exists in anything reached.** There are only named-entity disclosures (Eagle Bancorp: 28% of its $861.2M construction (ADC) book carries loan-funded interest reserves, as of 6/30/2026) and qualitative signals. Fitch says CRE CLO borrowers rely on extension options and performance-test waivers. In KBRA's sample of 54 modified SASB loans, 48% included "liquidity management".
4. **Watchlist: no current non-Trepp office watchlist rate was found.** The last CRED iQ watchlist share found is for CRE CLOs (36.5%, May 2024), so it is stale. A search snippet attributing "18% loans of concern" to Fitch traces to 2009-era Fitch figures (17% of the Fitch-rated portfolio, NMN 2009-09-03). **Do not cite it.**
5. **Most usable repeatable series: the FDIC BankFind API field `RSNRES`.** It covers bank loans secured by nonfarm nonresidential property that were modified for borrowers in financial difficulty and are performing: **$10.66B at 2026-Q2**. It is free, quarterly and machine-readable, but has no office split and uses a 12-month window. Separately, SEC **ABS-EE** loan-level files (free, monthly) would let CREED build its *own* "current + DSCR <1.0x + office" figure for public conduits, at some engineering cost.
