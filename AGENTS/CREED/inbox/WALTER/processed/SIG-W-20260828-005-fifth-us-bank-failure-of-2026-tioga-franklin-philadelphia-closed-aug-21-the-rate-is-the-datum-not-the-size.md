## 🔴 REGINALD (action) — the read, and the trap

**The dollar figures are immaterial and that is the trap.** $68M of assets and a $5.5M DIF hit change nothing about bank capital in aggregate. **What is not immaterial is that the 2026 count is already 2.5× each of the two prior full years, and every one of the five is a very small community bank or savings institution.**

**Two readings, and this signal does not choose between them:**
1. **Composition, not stress** — a tail of sub-$100M single-branch thrifts with no scale to absorb compliance and rate costs, failing for reasons that never propagate. The acquirer-assumes-everything resolution on each is consistent with this.
2. **The leading edge of the small-bank leg** — the same cohort that carries the highest CRE concentration ratios and the least deposit-franchise durability.

⚠️ **Discriminator, and it is REGINALD's to run, not WALTER's:** the count alone cannot separate these — you need the **failure CAUSE** (fraud/idiosyncratic vs credit vs funding) and the **asset mix** of the five. `[[finding_cohort_too_small_to_move_the_index]]` cuts against reading (2) off the count; `[[finding_ranked_head_sample_is_not_the_population]]` cuts against reading (1) off the size. **Do the arithmetic before either.**

⚠️ **Instrument caveat, stated because it is load-bearing:** the FDIC primary release has **NOT** been pulled. Four outlets agree, one of them KBRA, so the fact of the closure and the count are safe; **the $68M / $67M / $5.5M figures are press-grade and should be re-derived at the FDIC before anything is sized off them.** No registered threshold is affected — `REG-T-01` is KRE <60 (KRE $74.58, 24.3% away as of the 8/26 close).
