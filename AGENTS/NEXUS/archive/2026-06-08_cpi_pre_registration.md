## Pre-registered anti-anchoring rules

1. **Don't retro-rationalize an IN-LINE print as confirming whichever direction the tape moved.** Tape moves can lag the print by hours; intra-day fade ≠ CPI verdict.
2. **Don't elevate energy-comp marginally hot to "BRT-16 confirmed."** Need +0.20 m/m or +0.4 YoY swing in energy CPI to count.
3. **Don't claim "credit confirmed" on a 5-10bps OAS move.** Need HY breach 290 to count as confirmation; 274 → 280 is noise.
4. **If 6/11 auction tails after a clean CPI**, that's a R1-not-CPI verdict — don't backfill the CPI read.
