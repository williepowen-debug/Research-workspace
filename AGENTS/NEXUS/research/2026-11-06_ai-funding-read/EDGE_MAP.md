**Marks.** FILING: an issuer document a desk read. RELAY: press or secondary, including issuer documents seen only through someone else. INFERENCE: reasoning, no document says it.
**Verdicts.** REAL: the link exists on a filing or a named report. UNSIZED: real, but nothing says how big. NARRATIVE: no source read shows it.

## What would make it a loop

A loop needs a way back from Oracle to SoftBank. The only candidate is OpenAI's value: Oracle stress hurts OpenAI, its mark falls, SoftBank's leverage rises, its checks shrink. That is inference, and **today the sign runs the other way:** booked at $1.25T, the holding would cut SoftBank's leverage about 2.7 points (SAM estimate; round unclosed, terms anonymous).
