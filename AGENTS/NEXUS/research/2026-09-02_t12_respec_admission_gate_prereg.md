## 2. THE ADMISSION GATE (frozen)

A candidate T-12 successor is **ADMISSIBLE** only if, base-rated over **≥250 sessions of its own instrument's history**, at the **exact resolution window length it will actually run**:

- **(a) BOTH operative branches fire in ≥10% of windows.**
- **(b) The ratio between the two branch rates is ≤3×.**
- **(c) The NO-VERDICT share is ≤60%.**
- **(d) (a)–(c) are computed BOTH unconditionally AND regime-conditionally** (here: sessions starting with CCC ≥1000), **and both figures are published in the registration.** A candidate that passes unconditionally and fails conditionally is **NOT admissible** — it is admissible only in a regime that is not the one it will run in.
- **(e) ⛔ NO CONJUNCTIONS in a branch unless BOTH legs are separately base-rated and BOTH clear (a).** *Reason, measured on three separate desks' rows this window: the one-sided conjunction — easy leg near, hard leg receding — is the fleet's dominant unreachability shape. My branch A had it. **REGINALD's re-arm has it right now** (leg (a) CCC ≥1050 is **1bp** away at 1049 [9/1]; leg (b) HY ≥272 ×2 is **7bp out of reach and receding**). **BROCK's X1 had it** and BROCK closed the gate itself.*

**Failing the gate is not a veto on the idea — it is a veto on FREEZING it.** A candidate that fails must be re-specified (different window, different magnitude, different instrument) and re-base-rated until it passes, or explicitly registered as **NOT A FALSIFIER** and used as an observable.
