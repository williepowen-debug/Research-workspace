**Why it's distinct:**
- Insurance failure doesn't trigger via credit spreads or bank runs first — it triggers via **regulatory action** (rehabilitation order, AG 55 disclosure, NRSRO revocation)
- Timeline is SLOWER (regulatory process takes months) but the event is BINARY (solvent → insolvent overnight)
- Downstream is CARL (annuity income shock) + REGINALD (bank counterparty) + LIQUID (spread contagion) simultaneously
- It's INVISIBLE to market until the regulatory action happens (no publicly traded stress indicator for captive reserve quality)

**Priority recommendation:** SHADE's most valuable output is an **early warning calendar**: AG 55 filing dates, FABN maturity dates, DOJ indictment probability. These are known dates, unlike macro data releases. The network should treat SHADE dates as hard catalysts.
