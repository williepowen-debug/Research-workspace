FAQs
What is the Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU)?
SAFU is an emergency reserve fund established by Binance in July 2018 to protect user assets in extreme cases, such as security breaches or platform-level incidents. Binance allocated a percentage of trading fees to grow the fund to a substantial level over time.
The SAFU fund is held and managed by Nest Clearing and Custody Limited, a Recognized Clearing House regulated by the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) Financial Services Regulatory Authority. A portion of the funds serves as capital reserves as part of Binance regulatory obligations in the ADGM.&nbsp;
Will Binance replenish the SAFU fund?
Binance is committing to replenishing the SAFU to return the fund size to approximately US$1 billion. We are replenishing SAFU to ensure it remains fully resourced and ready to protect users, consistent with its purpose as an emergency user-protection reserve.&nbsp;
If BTC is already 100% covered (as shown in the latest PoR snapshot), why are you still deploying the SAFU fund?
We are utilizing part of the SAFU fund to acquire the remaining 10% of outstanding assets in order to reach 100% coverage for all tokens.

What are Futures Insurance Funds?
Futures Insurance Funds are safety nets provided by Binance that limit the impact of liquidations. Their primary purpose is to reduce the occurrence of counterparty liquidations (commonly referred to as ADL), protect futures traders from deficit losses resulting from bankrupt positions, and help ensure profitable positions are paid out in full. Futures Insurance Funds are funded by Binance, including Liquidation Clearance Fees collected by the platform, and are not part of user assets.
Binance has rebalanced its Futures Insurance Funds to move a portion of the balance to cover the affected assets.
Is there a higher risk of Auto-Deleveraging (ADL)?
The Futures Insurance Funds remain above the coverage level required under Binance’s established risk-management framework, helping ensure the fund remains available to support users during extreme market conditions. We monitor fund size continuously against historical stress scenarios reflecting extreme but plausible market conditions, and top it up when required. Due to volatility, no exchange can completely avoid auto-deleveraging, but we always strive to keep occurrences to a minimum.
Will Binance replenish the Futures Insurance Funds?
The funds are maintained above their required size. When the balance falls below the level our framework requires, Binance tops them up from company funds, as we have done previously. What was released was excess above that level, which is why it could be reallocated without affecting coverage.
