How Invoice Factoring Works
1. Submit Your Invoices
Send us eligible invoices for completed work or delivered products, and our team will review them for funding.
2. Receive Your Advance
Once approved, you can receive up to 98% of the invoice value, often within 24 hours.
3. Your Customer Pays
Your customer pays the invoice according to their normal terms. Once payment is received, we release the remaining balance to you, minus the agreed factoring fee.
