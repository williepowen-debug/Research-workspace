26th October – Threat Intelligence Report
October 26, 2026
https://research.checkpoint.com/2026/26th-october-threat-intelligence-report/
For the latest discoveries in cyber research for the week of 26th October, please download our
Threat Intelligence Bulletin.
TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES
Japanese cloud hosting provider Onamae Desktop Cloud has
confirmed
a cyberattack in which intruders compromised its infrastructure and installed malware on customer servers. More than 26,000 servers were potentially affected. The provider began shutting down customer environments on October 21 to contain the intrusion and prevent further unauthorized activity.
India’s state-run State Bank of India, serving more than 500 million customers,
disclosed
a data breach after 3,897 customer records appeared online. The exposed information reportedly included contact details, residential addresses, birth dates, bank profiles, loan records, and identity-verification documents. The bank reported no disruption to core services.
WordPress backup developer TeamUpdraft has
suffered
a cyberattack after intruders accessed its website and potentially exposed customer information and management-service connection keys. Attackers subsequently abused compromised UpdraftCentral keys to install backdoors on WordPress websites during an 18-hour campaign on October 18 and 19.
LiteLLM, a widely used open-source AI gateway,
confirmed
an October 21 supply-chain attack in which attackers used stolen developer tokens to publish malicious packages and Docker images. The compromised version 1.107.0 was designed to steal cloud credentials and AI service secrets, with attackers subsequently testing stolen AWS credentials.
AI THREATS
Check Point Research
uncovered
how AI-enabled antidetect browsers allow operators to manage thousands of deceptive accounts while evading platform safeguards. Tools including Kameleo, AdsPower, and MultiLogin facilitate account takeovers, financial fraud, and automated distribution of AI-generated content, supporting large-scale influence operations across social media platforms.
Researchers
observed
affiliates of INC and The Gentlemen ransomware groups using AI agents to conduct human-approved cyberattacks. The operation targeted 62 organizations by exploiting remote-access systems and compromised credentials. Victims were concentrated in the manufacturing, energy, healthcare, and pharmaceutical sectors, primarily in the United States.
Researchers
analyzed
a cybercriminal operation that used multiple AI assistants to plan and execute different types of malicious activity. The attacker relied on AI-generated scripts and reconnaissance reports to develop attack capabilities. Exposed operational records revealed how AI tools supported campaign planning, technical execution, and adaptation across different targets.
VULNERABILITIES AND PATCHES
Critical PHP vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 have been
used
in widespread exploitation attempts that could enable remote code execution. Attack traffic reportedly increased from thousands to millions of attempts following disclosure. As of October 23, approximately 97.5% of publicly visible PHP instances did not indicate patched versions.
Check Point IPS provides protection against this threat
(PHP Remote Code Execution (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619, CVE-2026-115560((
Oracle has
released
its October 2026 Critical Patch Update, addressing 1,209 unique vulnerabilities through 1,295 security patches across 33 product families. The update includes 95 critical CVEs and affects widely deployed enterprise products, including Fusion Middleware, E-Business Suite, MySQL, and Java SE.
GitLab has
fixed
CVE-2026-110264, a critical vulnerability with a CVSS score of 10.0 affecting Community and Enterprise Editions. The integer overflow flaw could allow unauthenticated attackers to execute arbitrary code remotely on affected servers. The vulnerability affects versions before 18.11.13 and several 19.x releases.
A critical privilege-escalation vulnerability, CVE-2026-114625,
affects
on-premises Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator deployments. The flaw carries a CVSS score of 9.4 and allows authenticated users, including those with read-only access, to obtain operator-level permissions. Successful exploitation can expose restricted management functions, and the vulnerability has been exploited in attacks.
THREAT INTELLIGENCE REPORTS
Check Point Research has
profiled
Aokigahara, a newly identified infostealer targeting credentials and session data associated with AI applications, developer tools, and cloud platforms. Targeted applications include Claude, ChatGPT Desktop, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot. Stolen information may include authentication tokens, account credentials, and other sensitive application data.
Researchers
revealed
that U.S. federal investigators suspect an executive at a ransomware negotiation company assisted the ShinyHunters extortion group in operating its victim disclosure website. The site reportedly listed nearly 100 organizations between May and October 2026.
Researchers
mapped
Lazarus’s Operation FlashHole, a cyberespionage campaign targeting organizations in South Korea’s software, construction, and telecommunications equipment sectors. Attackers used tailored phishing messages and fraudulent portfolio websites to deliver malware. The campaign exploited trusted financial-security software to execute malicious components.
Researchers have
uncovered
a large-scale campaign by threat actor k0t targeting Fortinet FortiGate, FortiWeb, and Palo Alto Networks GlobalProtect devices. Attackers exploited vulnerabilities and weak credentials to establish persistent access and steal information. The operation exposed more than 70,000 credentials and showed possible connections to ransomware groups.
