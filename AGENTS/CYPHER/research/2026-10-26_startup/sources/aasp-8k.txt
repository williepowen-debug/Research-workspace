Clive

G. Brookes has more than 25 years of experience in Canadian capital markets, public company management and the mineral exploration sector.

a private company. His prior positions include Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer of Juggernaut Exploration Ltd.; Chief
Financial Officer, Secretary and a director of EnerGulf Resources Inc.; and Chief Financial Officer and a director of Candelaria Mining
Corp. Mr. Brookes holds a Bachelor of Commerce degree from the University of British Columbia.

(e)
Compensatory Arrangements
On
or around October 23, 2026, the Company entered into indemnification agreements, in substantially the form attached as
Exhibit
10.1
to this Current Report on Form 8-K (the “
Indemnification
Agreements
”), with each director serving on the Company’s Board of Directors and each officer of the Company
(each, an “
Indemnitee
”).
Each
Indemnification Agreement provides that the Company will indemnify the applicable Indemnitee, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable
law and subject to the agreement’s conditions and exclusions, against expenses, including reasonable attorneys’ fees, judgments,
fines and amounts paid in settlement actually and reasonably incurred in connection with covered proceedings arising by reason of the
Indemnitee’s service to the Company or service at the Company’s request to another enterprise. Covered proceedings include
threatened, pending or completed civil, criminal, administrative, legislative or investigative proceedings, including proceedings brought
by or in the right of the Company, subject to the limitations applicable to such proceedings. The agreements also provide for indemnification
of reasonable expenses incurred as a witness or other nonparty participant in a covered proceeding and for contribution in certain circumstances.
The
Company must advance reasonable expenses incurred in connection with covered proceedings, generally within 30 days after receipt of a
written request reasonably evidencing those expenses, subject to the agreements’ conditions and exclusions. Each Indemnitee undertakes
to repay amounts advanced to the extent ultimately determined by a court of competent jurisdiction, after exhaustion of any appeals taken,
not to be indemnifiable. The agreements establish procedures for determining entitlement to indemnification, address the defense and
settlement of covered proceedings, and provide for judicial enforcement of indemnification and advancement rights. Reasonable enforcement
expenses are subject to advancement, with ultimate indemnification generally available to the extent the Indemnitee is successful.
The
agreements exclude, subject to their terms and applicable law, duplicate payments, disgorgement of short-swing profits under Section
16(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, certain compensation reimbursements and clawbacks, certain proceedings initiated by the
Indemnitee without prior board authorization, and payments prohibited by applicable law. An exclusion from ultimate indemnification does
not, by itself, preclude lawful advancement of defense expenses, subject to the agreements’ repayment requirements.
The
rights provided by the agreements are in addition to other indemnification and advancement rights available to the Indemnitees. The Company’s
obligations extend to service beginning when each Indemnitee first held a covered position and continue after that service ends for so
long as the Indemnitee may be subject to a covered proceeding or related enforcement action. The agreements bind the Company’s
successors and require a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets expressly to assume its obligations. The Company
also agrees to use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain and maintain directors’ and officers’ liability insurance. The
agreements are governed by Nevada law and provide for enforcement in specified Nevada courts.
The
foregoing description is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the form of Indemnification Agreement filed as
Exhibit
10.1
to this report, which is incorporated herein by reference.
On
October 22, 2026, the Board of Directors approved the adoption of a Policy for the Recovery of Erroneously Awarded Incentive Based Compensation
(the “
Clawback Policy
”), with an effective date of October 22, 2026, in order to comply with the final clawback rules
adopted by the SEC under Section 10D and Rule 10D-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (“
Rule 10D-1
”),
and the listing standards, as set forth in the Nasdaq Listing Rule 5608 (the “
Final Clawback Rules
”).
The
Clawback Policy provides for the mandatory recovery of erroneously awarded incentive-based compensation from current and former executive
officers as defined in Rule 10D-1 (“
Covered Officers
”) of the Company in the event that the Company is required to
prepare an accounting restatement, in accordance with the Final Clawback Rules. The recovery of such compensation applies regardless
of whether a Covered Officer engaged in misconduct or otherwise caused or contributed to the requirement of an accounting restatement.
Under the Clawback Policy, the Board of Directors will recoup from the Covered Officers erroneously awarded incentive compensation received
within a lookback period of the three completed fiscal years preceding the date on which the Company is required to prepare an accounting
restatement.
The
foregoing summary of the Clawback Policy does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text
of the Clawback Policy, a copy of which is attached hereto as
