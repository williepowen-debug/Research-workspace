# CYPHER / banking — bank cyber, software and vendor risk

**Opened 2026-10-29 13:50 EDT on Will's word** (13:49 ET: *"I think maybe we should have a sub-folder focused purely on the banking cyber/software/whatever risk"*).

**What this folder is for:** the thread about how cyber and technology failures reach US banks and credit unions. That means through the software and service companies they share: core processors, digital-banking platforms, credit-union vendors, IT providers (MSPs) and their remote-management tools, statement print and mail vendors, mortgage platforms, and payment messaging. It covers attacks and accidents alike.

**What it is not:** a second ledger. Facts live in the workbook; this folder holds the reading of them (theses, syntheses, dated thread notes) and points to the rows. Consequences stay with their owners: REGINALD (community and regional banks, CUs), GIBBS (large banks), LIQUID (payments and funding), BOND (Treasury plumbing). ⛔ Charter guardrail 2: no list of exposed banks leaves the desk.
