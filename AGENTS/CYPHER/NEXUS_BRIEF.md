## VIEW

- Binance (10/29, off-wave) shows that the stablecoin issuers worked as a kill switch: Tether and Circle froze the attacker's Ethereum balances 37–41 minutes after the first drain. That is the control working, not a chokepoint failing. The US-finance risk sits at the issuer itself (its mint and freeze keys, its reserve custodian); it is now map row CHK-40, with nothing current touching it. Do not relay "$5B hack" or "$6.3B stolen": Binance says "impact", and about half of it is frozen.

- The MSP route into banks has carried real traffic: an August 2026 N-able N-central flaw (NYDFS 8/11 alert: compromised MSPs used to move into customers' networks; impact on "a limited number" of regulated firms per a NYDFS spokesperson, not the letter) (CYB-INC-051). Bank-supplier incidents run at about one a month in 2026 (KB-CYP-076). None is wave-rooted.

- DEWEY's 10/29 rerun agrees row by row: still Log4Shell-like on outcomes, one step toward MOVEit on a single unnamed vendor claim; a KEV listing does not separate the shapes (KB-CYP-071/072). Fortinet's -20.9% is earnings, not the wave.

- The wave has moved from "exploited somewhere" (KEV) to "compromises verified by the product's vendor, victims unnamed": Palo Alto's Unit 42 confirmed Dream's firewall results and says about .02% of its installed base was impacted. Still no named organisation, so the R1 flip is not met.
- No bank or vendor is named as a victim of this wave. Off the wave the vendor channel is live: Finzly and Abrigo each confirmed unauthorized access, and Crédit Mutuel Arkéa says a provider's platform was the way to its own account. That last case is a new class, stolen bank money rather than stolen data. Whether the provider serves other banks is the open question; nothing shows a second victim or an attacked rail, and nothing rules them out.
- The perimeter-appliance chokepoint (CHK-32) is now a CONFIRMED wave touch. The entry door every bank runs is being attacked. That is not evidence any bank's door was opened.

- New 11/19: the day's events are all vendor-side and small (JPMorgan's marketing vendors, TruStage's extortion deal, N-able's email-security cloud, an Irish CU vendor). The bank-side hit list is still empty; the vendor channel keeps producing events at about the 2026 rate.
- New 11/17: a confirmed vendor poisoning (Optimizely) reached named financial firms' customer pages, a channel the chokepoint map lacked (CHK-42 added). It is customer-side and short; no bank's own systems are reported breached. The week's outage cluster has no common cause on the evidence.
