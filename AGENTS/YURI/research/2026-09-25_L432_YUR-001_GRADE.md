## 4. WHY THE PERIMETER STAYS PARTIAL, AND WHY IT CANNOT CLOSE AS DEFINED

1. **mil.ru is permanently unreachable from this box.** I varied host and scheme four ways. Per Federal Law 31-FZ, mobilisation is **declared by presidential decree**, so the defence ministry is an *implementation* leg, not the *decision* leg. ⚠️ That is an argument for dropping mil.ru from the definition of a COMPLETE perimeter. **I am not applying it locally**: loosening a check to kill a false alarm turns a loud-and-safe failure into a silent one, and this is a change to the rules, so the ruling belongs to PROME (FOLLOW-UP).
2. **The numbering gaps (№ 671/673/675/676/677) are unreadable by design.** HAWK's 9/21 argument still holds: the September-2022 partial-mobilisation decree (№ 647) was **published openly the same day** because it imposed public obligations, even though one paragraph was restricted. The restricted-use band therefore does not match the kind of act this row asks about. That is a strong prior, not proof.
3. ⇒ Under the current definition the row stays `OPEN` indefinitely. By the charter's own words ("never leave OPEN-but-stale"), a re-scope ruling is owed, not another read.
