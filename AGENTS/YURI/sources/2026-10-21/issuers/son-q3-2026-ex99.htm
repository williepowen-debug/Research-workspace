Sonoco Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Hartsville, S.C., U.S. - Sonoco Products Company (“Sonoco” or the “Company”) (NYSE: SON), a Mid-cap Value global packaging company, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 27, 2026.
Summary:
•Net sales in the third quarter were $2.0 billion. Excluding divestitures, net sales were essentially flat
•Reported GAAP net loss of $(732) million, or $(7.36) diluted earnings per share, reflecting a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $(829) million attributable to the Consumer EMEA/APAC and Industrial APAC businesses, compared to GAAP net income of $123 million, or $1.23 diluted earnings per share, in the same period in 2025
•Third quarter adjusted net income was $167 million or $1.67 per adjusted diluted share, compared to $191 million or $1.92 per adjusted diluted share in the same period in 2025
•Generated operating cash flow of $285 million in the third quarter, and provided $218 million of operating cash flow year-to-date, which was net of approximately $103 million in one-time taxes paid in 2026 on gains from the sales of the divested TFP and ThermoSafe businesses in 2025

•Third quarter 2026 net sales of $2.0 billion were down (4.2)% compared to the corresponding prior-year quarter, driven primarily by the November 3, 2025 divestiture of the ThermoSafe business. Net sales benefited from higher selling prices implemented to offset the effects of inflation and tariffs, which was partially offset by lower volume/mix.

•GAAP operating loss for the third quarter was $(638) million, which includes non-cash goodwill impairment charges of $(802) million and $(27) million, attributable to the Consumer Packaging segment’s EMEA/APAC businesses and the Industrial Paper Packaging segment’s APAC business, respectively, for a total non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $(829) million. In addition to this charge, solid productivity savings stemming from fixed cost reduction initiatives and procurement savings were more than offset by significant price/cost headwinds and lower volume/mix.

•Effective tax rates on GAAP income from continuing operations before income taxes and adjusted income from continuing operations before income taxes, were (6.4)% and 24.6%, respectively, in the third quarter, compared to 6.0% and 22.9%, respectively, in the same period in 2025.

“During the third quarter, our businesses faced a combination of external challenges. Drought conditions across parts of Europe and the U.S. impacted crop yields and pressured food can demand, while the impact of inflation on raw materials, freight, energy, and other operating costs exceeded our expectations. Through strong productivity initiatives and rigorous cost control, our teams were able to mitigate a significant portion of this impact and deliver resilient operating results,” said Howard Coker, President and Chief Executive Officer.

“Our Industrial Paper Packaging segment delivered another strong quarter, with results essentially in line with the second quarter despite a challenging operating environment. Productivity improvements remained a key driver of performance, while North America URB trade ton sales increased 10% year over year and mill utilization remained strong at 94%. Segment volume/mix was modestly positive, and additional URB pricing realized in September positions us to further offset inflationary pressures.”

“Results in Consumer Packaging fell short of expectations, as volume/mix declined 3% year over year due primarily to adverse weather conditions and unfavorable price/cost dynamics. Encouragingly, international paper can operations performed well, with double-digit volume growth year over year driven by sustained demand for snack products across emerging markets.”

Paul Joachimczyk, Sonoco’s Chief Financial Officer, added, “Our businesses once again generated strong operating cash flow in the third quarter, delivering $285 million despite seasonal working capital headwinds. Year-to-date operating cash flow includes $103 million of one-time tax payments related to gains on our 2025 divestitures. We continued to strengthen our balance sheet during the quarter, reducing total debt and net debt by $162 million and $176 million, respectively, while successfully refinancing an $800 million term loan. Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Highlights

•The Company maintained strong liquidity of $1.3 billion at September 27, 2026, consisting of $1.1 billion of available borrowing capacity under its revolving credit facility and cash on hand.
•Cash and cash equivalents were $183 million as of September 27, 2026, compared to $378 million, as of December 31, 2025.
•Total debt and net debt were $4.3 billion and $4.1 billion, respectively, as of September 27, 2026, primarily reflecting seasonal working capital requirements within the Company's metal packaging business.
•Year-to-date cash flow from operating activities for the period ended September 27, 2026 provided $218 million, compared to $277 million in the same period of 2025. The main drivers of the year-over-year change in operating cash flow were a one-time payment of taxes in 2026 on the gains from the 2025 divestitures of the TFP and ThermoSafe businesses and the seasonal need for working capital for the Company’s metal packaging business.
•Capital expenditures, net of proceeds from sales of fixed assets, for 2026 were $175 million, compared to $248 million last year.
•Free Cash Flow for the period ended September 27, 2026 improved to $43 million compared to $29 million in the same period in 2025, reflecting the factors impacting operating cash flow discussed above.
•The Company returned $159 million to shareholders through dividends during the first nine months of 2026, compared to $156 million in the prior year period.
•On September 25, 2026, Sonoco amended a credit agreement, resulting in the addition of $800 million in new term loan facilities. The Company drew $500 million from the new facilities to refinance an existing $500 million term loan which was to mature in 2028. The Company retained $300 million in delayed-draw term loan capacity, which it can use within 12 months.

About Sonoco
Sonoco (NYSE: SON) is a Mid-cap Value global packaging company. With sales of $7.5 billion from continuing operations in 2025, the Company has approximately 22,000 employees working in 265 operations in 37 countries, serving some of the world’s best-known brands. Guided by our purpose of Better Packaging. Better Life., we strive to foster a culture of innovation, collaboration and excellence to provide solutions that better serve all our stakeholders and support a more sustainable future. A Fortune 500 company, Sonoco was proudly named one of the World’s Most Admired Companies by Fortune in 2026 as well as one of America’s Most Trustworthy and Responsible Companies by Newsweek and USA Today’s Climate Leaders in 2025. For more information on the Company, visit our website at www.sonoco.com.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

The Company’s results, determined in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles, are referred to as “as reported” or “GAAP” results. The Company uses certain financial performance measures, both internally and externally, that are not in conformity with GAAP (referred to as “non-GAAP financial measures”) to assess and communicate the financial performance of the Company. These non-GAAP financial measures, which are identified using the term “Adjusted” (for example, “Adjusted Operating Profit,” “Adjusted Net Income Attributable to Sonoco,” and “Adjusted Diluted EPS”), reflect adjustments to the Company’s GAAP operating results to exclude amounts, including the associated tax effects where applicable, relating to:
•restructuring/asset impairment charges1;
•acquisition, integration and divestiture-related costs;
•gains or losses from the divestiture of businesses;
•losses from the early extinguishment of debt;
•non-operating pension costs;
•amortization expense on acquisition intangibles;
•changes in last-in, first-out (“LIFO”) inventory reserves;
•certain income tax events and adjustments;
•derivative gains/losses;
