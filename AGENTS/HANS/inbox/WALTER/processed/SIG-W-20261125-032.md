# Ofgem price cap, 1 January to 31 March 2027 (issuer letter, 25 November 2026)

| Line | Oct to Dec 2026 | Jan to Mar 2027 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Direct debit, typical use (2026 TDCV) | GBP 1,723 | **GBP 2,166** | **+26%** |
| Standard credit | 1,861 | 2,328 | +25% |
| Prepayment | 1,678 | 2,108 | +26% |
| Wholesale allowance | 47% of cap | 56% of cap | +50% (gas +59%, electricity +39%) |

- **Ofgem's stated driver:** the Middle East conflict over the three-month averaging window, with historically low European gas storage; prices peaked at multiyear highs at end October and reversed in early November. VAT is off electricity until end June 2027 and applies to gas.
- **Not a surprise on its face:** Cornwall Insight had forecast GBP 2,166 (secondary page, not read here); the 26% was the consensus figure in that forecast.
- **Why it matters:** it enters UK CPI from the January print. It is a fuel-passthrough test for HANS-T-17 (UK core CPI 2.8, watch 3.0): the cap itself is energy, so it moves headline, and core only by second-round effects. WALTER did not estimate a CPI contribution.
- **Also:** it is a lagged read on TTF, which has since moved (TTFZ26 88.10, last trade about 10:38 ET 11/25; 91.669 close [11/24], vendor).
