### Iran's Attrition Strategy
- 5,000-6,000 mines (deploy in hours, clear in weeks)
- Mobile missile launchers survive initial strikes (disguised as civilian trucks)
- 3,000 fast attack craft for swarm tactics
- Can re-mine faster than US can clear
- Strategy: "manufacturing sustained friction" — NOT seeking decisive battle
- Goal: stretch US logistics, missile defenses, and political tolerance

### Assessment: Genuine + Posturing Simultaneously
- Two carrier groups + 150+ cargo flights = REAL preparation
- "War is complete" rhetoric + DFC insurance scheme = POSTURING
- Verdict: preparing for limited objectives (lane security) while pressuring Iran to negotiate

### Duration Implications
- Even with US seizure, commercial restart takes weeks-months (insurance)
- Iran retains asymmetric capability post-seizure (re-mining, submarines, mobile missiles)
- 4-6 month closure base case is NOT aggressive — may be conservative
- Insurance is the structural lock, not military control
