## The three things worth knowing

**1. EU gas storage is at a five-year low for the date — 59.32% full** (670.4 of 1,130.2 TWh, gas day 2026-08-11), **13.0pp below 2025 and 14.3pp below 2022**, the Russian-cutoff year. Demand-normalised it is **19.05% of annual consumption vs 21.71% in 2022** — also the lowest in the series. This is the **second consecutive undershoot**: 2025 peaked at just 83.2% vs 95–99% in 2022-24. [PRIMARY: GIE AGSI+]

**2. Europe will not refill to target.** Measuring injection pace from Aug-11 to each year's actual peak and applying it, 2026 lands at **77–80%**. **90% requires 1.49× the best rate of the last four years** — out of reach. Even the **80% deviation floor is a dead heat with the 2022 crisis-response maximum** (2.687 needed vs 2.684 best), sustained for 87 straight days. The binding rule is 90%, flexible 1 Oct – 1 Dec, with up to 10% deviation [Council of the EU, 2025-07-18].

**3. The impairment is cargoes, not capacity.** EU LNG send-out is **−20.4% YoY** (3,091 vs 3,885 GWh/d, identical window) **at only 38.3% utilisation** — 4,896 GWh/d of send-out capability sitting idle. The YoY gap alone is ~69 TWh ≈ **6.1pp of storage.**

⚠️ **And the framing premise this commission was built on is inverted.** DR-4 was scoped to explain "impaired channels with **TTF at €59 falling**." The level was exactly right (€59.07 on 7/31) but TTF rose **+38.1% across July** and is **+83.3% YoY**, closing **€60.49 on 8/12** in the top 5% of its 52-week range. "Falling" was a one-week pullback off a 52-week high. **If you are carrying "European gas prices are falling" anywhere, it is wrong.**
