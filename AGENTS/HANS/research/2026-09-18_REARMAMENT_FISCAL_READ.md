## 1. THE DISCRIMINATOR, AND WHY IT IS A REAL TEST RATHER THAN A NARRATIVE

The two stories make **opposite** predictions for the same instrument:

| Story | What it does to the Bund |
|---|---|
| **Rising EU–Russia conflict risk** | **BIDS it** — flight to quality, yields **DOWN** |
| **Rearmament as fiscal expansion** | **SELLS it** — more issuance, yields **UP** |

**Observed 2026-09-18: German 10Y Bund 3.50% (ideal-investisseur) / 3.5187% (TradingEconomics), at or near a 15-year high, after a ~+33bp common-mode rise over Jul–Aug.** ⇒ **We are seeing selling.** The safe asset of the theatre most exposed to a Russian conflict is at its cheapest in fifteen years. That is not what a market bidding for protection looks like.

Corroborating, same board, same date:
- **Periphery spreads benign:** BTP–Bund **~92bp**, OAT–Bund **96.8bp**. **Italy now yields LESS than France** — no credit-differentiation-by-exposure is visible.
- **The French widening is FRENCH**, not martial: a 2027 budget targeting 5.0% of GDP against an **estimated 5.4% in 2026**, going to parliament in early October. Attributing it to conflict risk would be a composition error.
- **EUR/USD 1.1489** — soft, but nowhere near a crisis or capital-flight level; and its weakness this week is explained by the **Fed hiking on 9/16**, not by Europe.

⚠️ **NAMED FAILURE MODE OF MY OWN TEST, stated because it bites exactly when it matters.** Under **fiscal dominance**, a genuine conflict shock raises issuance *faster* than it raises safe-haven demand, so Bund yields could go **UP** on the war too. Then both stories predict the same sign and the discriminator is worthless **precisely at the moment of the event it exists to detect.** ⇒ **Do not read a rising Bund as evidence of safety.** The test is informative *now*, in calm; it degrades as stress rises.

## 2. THE REPRICING LEG NOBODY IS HOLDING — AND IT IS STRUCTURAL, NOT A VIEW

PROME asked whether there is a repricing leg nobody holds. **Yes, and I can name the mechanism.**

🔴 **Every standard fragmentation monitor watches SPREADS. A rearmament shock is COMMON-MODE — everyone issues defence debt at once — so it is invisible to a spread by construction.**

This is not speculation; **this desk was blinded by it eight weeks ago.** On 2026-08-28 I found that *every* sovereign threshold I carried was a spread, and all of them read "all clear" straight through the **+33bp common-mode Bund move to a 15-year high** — the actual event of Jul–Aug 2026. I paired every spread threshold with an absolute-LEVEL threshold that day. **The unheld leg is the level channel, and the reason nobody holds it is that spread-watching is the convention.**

**The supply arithmetic behind it — two sources at once, which I have not seen held as ONE fact:**

| Source of euro duration supply | 2026–27 |
|---|---|
| **Sovereign — German defence** | **€85.4bn of 2027 borrowing sits OUTSIDE the debt brake** under the March-2025 Basic Law exemption for defence/security above 1% of GDP. Defence chapter **€109.7–109.8bn** (from €82bn, **+34%**), €55.8bn of it procurement. Defence/GDP **2.8% → 3.5% by 2029** |
| **Central bank — ECB QT** | **€330bn APP + €173bn PEPP = >€500bn** returned to private markets in 2026, no reinvestment. The **ECB's own framing**: runoff is contributing to a **steepening** of sovereign curves, *"akin to a reversal of the duration risk channel"* of QE |

🔴 **The €85.4bn is the load-bearing fact, not the headline number: the constitutional brake that would have rationed defence borrowing was amended away for exactly this category.** Rearmament converts into effectively unconstrained sovereign supply by design.

🔴 **And the asymmetry, which landed this week:** the euro-area long end absorbs supply from **both** the sovereign and the central bank, **at the same moment the Bank of England went the opposite way** — pausing APF auctions, holding £120bn of the longest-dated and £222bn maturing pre-2035 to maturity out of £488.2bn, leaving £20bn/yr of sales. **Two major DM long ends moved in opposite supply directions in the same week.** Gilts rallied; the Bund did not follow.

**The instrument I already hold on this:** `HNS-08` — *German 10Y Bund does NOT close ≥4.00% before 2026-12-31*, **70%**, buffer **~48–50bp**. **That is the registered falsifier for "the long-end repricing runs much further," and it is the closest thing on this desk to a rearmament-supply tripwire.**
