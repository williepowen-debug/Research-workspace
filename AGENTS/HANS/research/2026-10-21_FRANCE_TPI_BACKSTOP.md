# Would the ECB backstop France? — the TPI read, 2026-10-21

**HANS · hans-1021 · written the night before Moody's France review (Fri 10/23). Feeds `HANS-T-10`, `KB-HANS-126`/`127`, predictions `HNS-10`/`HNS-11`.**

## Answer first

**Not at today's spread, and probably not for a fundamentals-driven move at any spread.**

- **The legal test:** France passes the letter of the first eligibility test, but only on a June 2026 "effective action" finding that I read in secondary reports, not at the primary.
- **The design test:** the instrument is built to counter *unwarranted, disorderly* dynamics. Its own exit clause ends purchases when tensions are *"due to country fundamentals."*
- **The ECB's public line:** Lagarde says *"the primary and first solution lies at home in France."*
- **⇒ There is no backstop under the France–Germany spread at 157.5bp.** The level at which "unwarranted" could become the ECB's view is not published. One commentator's guess is ~200bp.

## 1. What the TPI is (primary: ECB press release, 21 July 2022)

| Element | ECB text (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| Purpose | "can be activated to counter unwarranted, disorderly market dynamics" |
| Criteria | "a cumulative list of criteria"; "These criteria will be an input into the Governing Council's decision-making" and "will be dynamically adjusted" |
| Decision | "based on a comprehensive assessment" by the Governing Council |
| Size | "Purchases are not restricted ex ante"; scale "would depend on the severity of the risks facing monetary policy transmission" |
| Scope | public-sector securities, "remaining maturity of between one and ten years" |
| Stance | purchases "cause no persistent impact" on the monetary policy stance |
| **Exit** | **"terminated either upon a durable improvement in transmission" or "based on an assessment that persistent tensions are due to country fundamentals"** |

**The four criteria:**
1. **EU fiscal framework:** not under an excessive deficit procedure (EDP), *or* not assessed as having failed to take effective action.
2. **No excessive imbalance procedure (EIP).**
3. **Debt sustainability:** the ECB takes into account the Commission's, ESM's and IMF's debt sustainability analyses.
4. **Sound macro policies:** recovery-and-resilience (RRF) commitments plus the fiscal parts of the country-specific recommendations.

## 2. France against each criterion

| Criterion | France, 2026-10-21 | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|
| 1. EU fiscal framework | **Under an EDP since 2024-07-26** (Council 126(6)). Council recommendation of 2025-01-21 sets correction by **2029**. Commission found France **had taken effective action** (Spring package, early June 2026). ⇒ **Passes the letter** of criterion 1. Its 2026 deficit is now guided at −5.4% vs a −5.1% target, so the next assessment could flip it. | EDP steps: **PRIMARY** (Commission EDP page). "Effective action": **SECONDARY** (Eunews 2026-06-03, ETAF 2026-06-08); the France-specific text was **not found**. |
| 2. EIP | No excessive imbalance procedure reported. | Not checked at primary |
| 3. Debt sustainability | Debt ~120% of GDP (AFP 10/21); Q2 €3.6tn / 119% (Reuters via Internazionale). The ECB *takes into account* DSAs; it does not mechanically apply them. | Secondary |
| 4. Macro policies | Not assessed here | — |
| **The real gate** | Whether the sell-off is **"unwarranted, disorderly."** AFP's analysts: it "can only be activated when a sell-off is judged unjustified… this does not describe indebted France." Lagarde (CNN, Bangkok, October): **"the primary and first solution lies at home in France."** | Lagarde quote via AFP (secondary) |

**⇒ On the criteria France is formally in. On the trigger it is out.** The exit clause shows the design intent: an instrument that must stop when tensions are fundamental will not start on a budget-driven spread.

## 3. What could change the ECB's read

| Condition | Why it matters | Status 10/21 |
|---|---|---|
| **Contagion** — Italy, Spain or Belgium widen with France | Turns a French story into a transmission problem for the euro area, the TPI's actual mandate | **Not present:** BTP–Bund ~116bp, flat vs ~114 on 10/02 |
| **Disorder** — liquidity breakdown, failed auctions, fire-sale moves | "Disorderly" is the other half of the trigger | Not observed. The 10/01 auction drew ~2× cover |
| **Spread level** | No ECB number exists. Continuum Economics: above ~200bp, "no" may turn into "monitoring the situation very closely". **A forecast, not an ECB commitment.** | 157.5bp [i-i 10/21] |
| **A credible French budget** | Satisfies the "home" condition, removes the need | Budget debate resumes 10/27; Assembly full vote reported for 11/17–18 |

**Alternatives and constraints:**
- **OMT (2012)** exists, but it needs an ESM programme with conditionality, a political step of a different order.
- **Legal risk:** OMFIF (David Marsh, 10/20) says TPI use "would almost certainly be contested by lawsuits at the German constitutional court."
- ⚠️ **NOT CARRIED:** OMFIF also reports an "early departure" of Lagarde. That is a single source, citing OMFIF's own September piece, and is unverified. It is not used anywhere here.

## 4. What this means for the US lens

- **No ECB put under French spreads ⇒ the French leg can run on fundamentals.** The catalysts in front of it are Moody's (10/23), the budget (10/27, 11/17–18) and S&P (11/27).
- **Transmission to the US** runs through two routes: French and European bank equity (−11% to −15% for the French majors in a month; `KB-HANS-121`), and dollar funding. LIQUID read no US-funding transmission on 10/01, and was re-asked 10/21.
- **The euro-area contagion test is Italy.** While BTP–Bund is flat, the TPI case stays weak and the stress stays French.

## 5. Limits

- The "effective action" finding is secondary. A Commission reassessment at the autumn package (the Draft Budgetary Plan opinion, ~late November) could reverse criterion 1.
- The ECB's "unwarranted" judgement is unobservable until it is made. Every spread level quoted for it is commentary.
- This is a read of the instrument, not a forecast of ECB behaviour. The forecast lives in `HNS-10`/`HNS-11`.
