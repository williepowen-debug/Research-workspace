# France — sovereign stress memo, 2026-10-21

**HANS · hans-1021 · the night before Moody's (Fri 10/23).** Companion to `research/2026-10-21_FRANCE_TPI_BACKSTOP.md`. Feeds `HANS-T-10`, `HNS-10`/`HNS-11`, KB-HANS-128–132.
**Evidence grades:** PRIMARY = read at the issuer. SECONDARY = a named publication. Three primaries were UNREACHABLE on 10/21 and are marked: Banque de France (site in maintenance), Agence France Trésor ratings page (403), OFR money-fund monitor (404).

## Answer first

1. **This is a fiscal-politics problem with no backstop and a heavy calendar.**
   - France borrows a record **€340bn in 2027** (AFT). Debt is **~119% of GDP**. The government is a minority trying to pass **€54bn** of savings.
   - The ECB has said the fix "lies at home". Four dated catalysts land before Christmas.
2. **The market has already re-priced France as a single-A credit.**
   - The OAT is above Greece's 10-year and the spread is the widest since 2012 (Apollo, 10/8).
   - **A Moody's cut to A1 on Friday would *converge* Moody's with Fitch and S&P (both A+), not open a new tier.** That limits forced selling. The risk is the *message*, not the mechanics.
3. **The bigger cliff is 2027.**
   - The presidential first round is **2027-04-18**, and Le Pen leads first-round polling (~35%).
   - Her eligibility hangs on a Cassation ruling due **by early January 2027**.
   - Spread pressure from here is as likely to be **election-premium** as budget-premium.
4. **Into the US, the channels are banks and funding, not French debt held by Americans.**
   - French bank shares are down 11–15% in a month, and the European selloff is broad (Deutsche −14.5%).
   - OATs back ~10% of European repo collateral.
   - French banks have historically been major dollar-repo counterparties for US money funds. **The current size is unmeasured on this desk.**

⚠️ **Basis note:** the Banque de France's own Q1-2026 release (summarised by search; the site was down 10/21) gives **55.9%** non-resident for *general government debt securities* (54.6% end-2025). That is a different perimeter from the 57.5% *negotiable debt at market value*. **Two bases, named, not averaged.**

**Read:**
- **Eurosystem QT is the swing factor.** The central-bank share fell **6.6pp in two years**, and foreigners and French banks absorbed it.
- The marginal buyer is a **price-sensitive foreigner**, not a captive domestic one. That is why spread moves can run.
- French banks' share is *rising* (8.3% → 10.5%), so the sovereign–bank link is **tightening**, not loosening.
- ⛔ The "Japanese fund dumped all OATs" story (10/03) is still **NOT CARRIED**: no primary.

**Read:**
- **Aa3 → A1 aligns Moody's with the other two.** It is a *convergence* cut, not a new low. It crosses no investment-grade or ECB-collateral line.
- UBS (via IndexBox): France would have to fall to **Baa3 / BBB** before it threatened EU-level ratings.
- ⇒ **The mechanical forced-selling channel is small. The signalling channel is not**, and a "hold" would itself be read as a relief rally (cf. `HNS-10`).
- **Market pricing is already below the letters:** France's spread sits with BBB-to-A-rated peers, and its 10-year is above Greece's (Apollo, 10/8).

## 4. Politics — the 2026 budget fight is the near cliff, 2027 is the far one

| Date | Event | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **2026-10-27** | Budget debate resumes (after 2,000+ amendments) | Article 49.3 / special-law routes; censure risk |
| **~2026-11-17/18** | Full Assembly budget vote (reported; one source says the revenue half is 11/03) | Censure threshold **289 of 577** |
| **2026-11-27** | S&P review | Second rating event |
| **by early Jan 2027** | Court of Cassation ruling on Le Pen | Her eligibility |
| **2027-04-18 / 05-02** | Presidential rounds 1 and 2 | The fiscal regime question |

**Le Pen:**
- The Paris Court of Appeal (**2026-07-07**) upheld the conviction but cut the ineligibility to 45 months, 30 of them suspended. That cleared her to run, pending Cassation.
- Polls: **~35% first round** (Ifop 10/20 via an aggregator); Mélenchon ~15%; Glucksmann (won the PS primary 10/11) and Philippe ~12%. **Aggregator, not the pollster's release — SECONDARY.**

**Street pressure:**
- 10/17 cost-of-living protests: **550k (Interior) / 800k (CGT)**, ~1,800 arrests.
- The government's response includes a **10M-barrel reserve diesel release and a pump-price cut from 11/1** (`KB-HANS-111`): relief that costs money.

**Read:**
- The 2026 budget will probably pass by **49.3 or a special law**. It did in February 2026, after two censure motions failed.
- **Passing is not the same as consolidating.** Markets will grade the *deficit number that survives*, against Fitch's doubt about −5.0%.
- After January, the Le Pen ruling turns the spread into an **election-premium** trade. That is a different driver from a budget premium, and it does not fade when a budget passes.

**Read:**
- **Exposure is moderate (2.4% of assets), but bank equity is pricing it as material**, through revenue concentration and the rising bank share of OAT ownership (§2).
- Amortised-cost accounting blunts the capital hit. It does not blunt the funding or equity channels.
- **This is not 2011.** Bank CDS sit below the sovereign, so no bailout loop has started. But the loop's *direction* is visible in equity.
