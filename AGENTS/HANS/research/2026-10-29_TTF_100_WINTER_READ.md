# TTF €100 — what is behind it and what it means for the US (HANS, 2026-10-29)

**Asked by:** Will, 14:04–14:06 ET: *"How should I interpret it? What else do we need to know?"* then *"can you start investigating what we can now?"*
**Status:** working model, not truth. Primary sources marked ✓; everything else is secondary and labelled.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Europe has a winter supply problem. It does not yet have a financial one.**
- **The weak point is Germany, not the EU average.** Germany is at 61.7% full against an EU 75.0%. On German storage alone (no import response), a winter with 2024/25's drawdown would run it dry.
- **There is no relief from Qatar before 2027.** The relief that exists is the seasonal weather lean, which is mild.
- **No 2022-style cash stress is visible** in utility shares.
- **US gas prices are insulated.** The US is hit through European inflation, the ECB, and France.

## 3. Where winter could end — past drawdowns applied to today (AGSI+ ✓)

**Method:** take each past winter's drawdown, from 10/27 to its spring low (sampled 3/15, 3/31 and 4/10), and subtract it from today's stock.

⚠️ **This is crude. It is not a model.**
- Drawdown depends on weather, LNG inflow and price, all of which differ this year.
- Qatar is out, so the same weather likely means a BIGGER draw. Lower demand (−18% vs 2022) pulls the other way.
- DEWEY's DR-4 is the real model.

| Winter used as template | EU drawdown (TWh) | EU end (of 1,133) | DE drawdown | DE end (of 247) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022/23 (mild, crisis demand cuts) | 422 | 427 = **37.7%** | 81 | 72 = **29%** |
| 2023/24 (mild) | 459 | 390 = **34.4%** | 92 | 61 = **25%** |
| 2021/22 | 571 | 278 = **24.5%** | 119 | 34 = **14%** |
| 2025/26 (last winter) | 632 | 217 = **19.2%** | 134 | 19 = **8%** |
| 2024/25 (cold, dark-calm spells) | 706 | 143 = **12.6%** | 174 | **−21: would run DRY** |

**Cross-check:** Berlin says current storage "exceeds all gas withdrawn last winter" (via energynews.pro, 10/23). Today's 153 TWh against last winter's 134 TWh draw from the same date fits that claim, but the margin is only ~19 TWh.

⚠️ **The risk before "empty" is deliverability.** Withdrawal rates fall as storage empties, so a cold spell in February against low storage is the dangerous combination. This is general engineering knowledge, not quantified here.

## 5. Transmission to the US — ranked

1. **European inflation → ECB hike in December.** EA HICP 3.8%, energy +18.9% (Sept). The ECB said today that financing conditions "have tightened". The ECB is also changing leaders (Lagarde leaves Feb 2027).
2. **France.** The cost-of-living riots and the 2027 budget fight are the political form of this energy shock. Spread 153bp, `T-10` OPEN.
3. **German industry.** The factory PMI holds at 54.2 (flash) on defence spending. Energy cost is the squeeze S&P names. Storage is the weak point.
4. **Liquidity at utilities and traders.** The 2022 route to a credit event. **Not visible now**, and not instrumented on this desk.
- **NOT a channel:** US natural gas prices.
- ⚠️ **Not a second confirmation:** oil and diesel. Same Hormuz cause, one witness.
