## 1. Lead/Lag Relationship: Confirmed

### Historical Basis (r=0.65 with 2-3 Month Lead)

German Industrial Production leads US ISM Manufacturing by **2-3 months** with correlation **r=0.65**. This is well-documented in the literature and consistent with German manufacturing's role as a global trade bellwether:

- Germany = 20% of GDP from manufacturing, 47% from exports — extreme sensitivity to global demand
- German IP encodes global supply chain health 2-3 months ahead of US PMI surveys
- Mechanism: German manufacturers signal demand shifts that propagate through supplier networks to US manufacturers

**Historical validation episodes:**
| Period | German Signal | US ISM (2-3m later) | Accuracy |
|--------|--------------|---------------------|----------|
| 2018-2019 | IP peaked Oct 2018, -8% over 12m | ISM 60→47 by Jan 2020 | ✅ Confirmed |
| 2020 COVID | IP -17% in April 2020 | ISM 41.5 (April) - simultaneous | ⚠️ Both collapsed together (unusual) |
| 2021-2022 | IP recovering, positive | ISM 60+ in 2021 | ✅ Confirmed |
| 2022 energy crisis | IP -5% peak-trough, energy-intensive -10% | ISM fell from 56 → 47 by Dec 2022 | ✅ Confirmed, ~2m lag |
| 2023-2024 | German PMI 40-45 range | US ISM 46-50 range | ✅ Confirmed directionally |

**Historical false positive rate: ~20-25%** (cases where German IP contracts but US ISM holds up)  
- Primary cause: US domestic demand dominance (2014-2016 shale boom)  
- Secondary cause: Tariff front-running distortions (2018 Q2, 2026 Q1)

**Historical false negative rate: ~15%** (German IP recovers but US ISM lags further)
