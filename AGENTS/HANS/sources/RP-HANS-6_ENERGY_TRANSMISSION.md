## Executive Summary

European energy markets transmit to US markets through multiple channels:

1. **LNG Competition** — Europe and Asia compete for US LNG exports → Henry Hub price floor
2. **Industrial Demand Destruction** — European manufacturing shutdowns → global supply chain disruption
3. **Commodity Repricing** — TTF/NBP price spikes → global energy inflation
4. **German Manufacturing Bellwether** — German IP correlates with global trade volumes → US export demand
5. **Risk Sentiment** — European energy stress → flight-to-safety UST bid

**Key Insight:** European energy is NOT just a European problem. US natural gas markets, equity earnings (energy, industrials), and inflation expectations all have European energy exposure.

**Current Status (Feb 2026):** European gas storage at **48%** (vs. 63% 5-year average) — YELLOW ZONE. TTF at $12.40/MMBtu (weekly avg Jan 15-21) vs. $14.57 same week 2025. Elevated but not crisis levels.

## 1. The 2022 Energy Crisis: What Happened & US Market Impact

### Timeline of Crisis

**Feb 2022:** Russia invades Ukraine  
**Jun-Aug 2022:** Nord Stream pipeline flows cut to 20% of capacity  
**Sep 2022:** Nord Stream 1 & 2 pipelines sabotaged/shut down permanently  
**Peak Crisis:** Aug 2022 — TTF hit €345/MWh (~$103/MMBtu equivalent)

### Transmission to US Markets

| Channel | Mechanism | US Impact (2022) |
|---------|-----------|------------------|
| **Natural Gas Prices** | Europe bidding for US LNG → Henry Hub floor support | Henry Hub average $6.45/MMBtu (2022) vs. $3.89 (2021) — **+66%** |
| **Equity Earnings** | US energy companies (XLE) margin expansion | XLE +59% (2022) vs. S&P 500 -18% |
| **Inflation** | Energy component of CPI elevated | Energy CPI +29.3% YoY (June 2022 peak) |
| **Industrial Costs** | US manufacturers face higher input costs | Industrial PPI +8.1% YoY (2022 avg) |
| **USD Strength** | Flight from EUR → DXY rally | EUR/USD fell 1.04 → 0.96 (parity break) |
| **UST Flows** | Risk-off → flight to safety | 10Y UST rallied from 3.5% → 3.0% (Oct-Nov 2022) |

**Key Data Point:** US LNG exports to Europe increased from **34 bcm (2021)** to **56 bcm (2022)** — a **+65% surge**. Europe replaced Russia with US as primary gas supplier.

### European Response (IEA Analysis)

From IEA Report "How to Avoid Gas Shortages in the EU in 2023":
- **Demand Destruction:** EU gas consumption fell ~15% (2022 vs. 2021)
- **Supply Diversification:** LNG imports replaced Russian pipeline gas
- **Storage Targets:** EU mandated 90% storage fill by Nov 1 (achieved via rationing)
- **Risk Assessment:** IEA projected potential **27 bcm supply gap** in 2023 if Russia cut to zero + China LNG demand rebounded

**US Market Lesson:** European energy stress creates persistent bid for US LNG → natural gas price floor even during mild US winters.

## 3. LNG Competition: Europe vs. Asia & US Natural Gas Prices

### The Global LNG Arbitrage

**Price Hierarchy (Normal Market):**
- **Asia (JKM):** Highest ($/MMBtu) — premium for spot cargoes
- **Europe (TTF):** Middle — balances pipeline + LNG
- **US (Henry Hub):** Lowest — domestic production surplus

**Crisis Inversion (2022):**
- **TTF peak:** €345/MWh (~$103/MMBtu equivalent) — Aug 2022
- **JKM peak:** $70/MMBtu — Aug 2022
- **Henry Hub peak:** $9.88/MMBtu — Aug 2022

**Arbitrage Economics:**
- US LNG export breakeven: ~$5-7/MMBtu (liquefaction + transport + regas)
- When **TTF - Henry Hub > $7**, US LNG exports max out
- **Henry Hub floor:** When Europe/Asia desperate, bids up to $10-12/MMBtu delivered, implies Henry Hub $3-5 minimum

### Current State (Jan 2026 Data — EIA Weekly)

**Prices (Week Ending Jan 21, 2026):**
- **Henry Hub:** $4.98/MMBtu (up from $3.12 prior week — cold snap)
- **TTF (weekly avg):** $12.40/MMBtu (Jan 15-21)
- **JKM (weekly avg):** $10.73/MMBtu (Jan 15-21)

**European Storage (Jan 20, 2026 — AGSI data):**
- **48% full** (vs. 63% 5-year average) — **YELLOW ZONE**
- Withdrawal season on track, but below normal

**US LNG Exports (Jan 15-21, 2026):**
- **37 vessels departed** (~139 bcf carrying capacity)
- Terminals: Sabine Pass (9), Plaquemines (8), Corpus Christi (6), Cameron (4), Calcasieu Pass (4), Cove Point (3), Freeport (3)
- Annualized rate: ~14 bcf/d (~130 bcm/year)

### Europe-Asia Competition Impact on Henry Hub

**Key Mechanism:**
1. Asian LNG demand surges (China restocking, cold winter, coal shortages)
2. Asia outbids Europe for spot LNG cargoes
3. Europe must draw storage + bid higher for US LNG
4. US producers hold back domestic supply to export
5. Henry Hub rises to balance domestic vs. export demand

**2022 Example:**
- China COVID lockdowns → low LNG demand → Europe won bidding war
- 2023: China reopened → LNG demand surged → TTF/JKM converged → Henry Hub rose

**Current Risk (Feb 2026):**
- European storage low (48%) → strong LNG demand through March
- Asian demand normal (mild winter in China/Japan)
- **If** Asian cold snap hits → bidding war → Henry Hub $6-8/MMBtu potential

**Henry Hub Basis Spread:**
- **Gulf Coast LNG hubs** (e.g., Houston Ship Channel) trade at **premium to Henry Hub** when export demand strong
- Current: Houston Ship Channel $4.55 vs. Henry Hub $4.98 (week ending Jan 21) — modest discount
- Crisis mode: HSC can trade $1-2/MMBtu ABOVE Henry Hub (export pull)

## Appendix: 2022 Energy Crisis Case Study Detail

### Chronology

**February 24, 2022:** Russia invades Ukraine  
- Immediate impact: Brent crude +$10/bbl, European gas futures +30%

**March-May 2022:** Sanctions escalate  
- EU bans Russian coal (phase-in through Aug 2022)
- Russia demands "unfriendly countries" pay for gas in rubles (most refuse)

**June 2022:** Nord Stream 1 maintenance  
- Reduced to 40% capacity (excuse: turbine stuck in Canada due to sanctions)
- TTF rises to €120/MWh

**July 2022:** Further Nord Stream cuts  
- Down to 20% capacity
- TTF €170/MWh

**August 2022:** PEAK CRISIS  
- Nord Stream 1 shut completely for "maintenance"
- TTF hits €345/MWh (~$103/MMBtu equivalent)
- European leaders warn of "rationing" and "blackouts"

**September 2022:** Nord Stream sabotage  
- Underwater explosions damage NS1 and NS2 pipelines
- Permanent destruction of primary Russia-Germany gas link

**October-December 2022:** Demand destruction + mild weather saves Europe
