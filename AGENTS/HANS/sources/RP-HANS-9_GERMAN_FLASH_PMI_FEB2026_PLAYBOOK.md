- January German recovery to 49.1 is "fragile" — employment still contracting, inputs at 37-month cost high

### Why IWM is the Right Vehicle

Small caps (Russell 2000 = IWM) are most exposed to:
1. **US domestic manufacturing cycle** — small caps more manufacturing-intensive than large caps
2. **Credit conditions** — small caps access regional banks and high-yield market, which tighten in slowdowns
3. **Earnings sensitivity** — thin margins, limited pricing power, can't absorb tariff costs like megacaps
4. **Historical pattern:** In 2019 manufacturing mini-recession, IWM underperformed SPY by 15%
