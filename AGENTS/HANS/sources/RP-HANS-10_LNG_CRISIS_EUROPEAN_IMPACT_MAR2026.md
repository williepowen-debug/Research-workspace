## Q1: LNG CRISIS — WHAT IT MEANS FOR EUROPE

### Immediate Supply Math
- EU storage: ~22% average (Germany 20.5%, France 21%)
- Qatar offline: ~7-8 bcm/month of LNG gone
- No insurance after Mar 5 → no ships can transit Hormuz
- Other LNG sources: US (Gulf Coast max utilization), Australia (Asia-locked), Norway (pipeline, limited spare)
- **Net result: 20-25% supply shortfall entering refill season**

### Price Trajectory
- TTF: €46/MWh now (+45% today alone)
- Goldman scenario: €74/MWh (+130% from pre-crisis levels)
- **HANS estimate: €60-80/MWh sustainable if Qatar offline >3 weeks**
- At €74 TTF: German industrial gas costs ~3x pre-Ukraine crisis levels
- Comparison: 2022 peak was ~€350/MWh (panic spike); this is not that — but it's sustained

### Industrial Production Cascade
Priority curtailment order (German market logic):
1. **Ammonia/fertilizer** (first to shut at TTF >€40/MWh) → BASF Ludwigshafen
2. **Chemicals** (polyethylene, methanol) — curtail at TTF >€50/MWh
3. **Glass, ceramics, cement** — continuous process; shutdowns = equipment damage
4. **Steel (EAF)** — can throttle, but massive cost increase
5. **Automotive** — downstream from above

**Expected German IP impact:** -3 to -5% MoM within 4-6 weeks if Qatar outage sustained
**Annual German GDP impact:** -0.8 to -1.5% if crisis lasts through Q2

### Inflation
- Energy component in EU HICP: ~15-20% weight
- TTF +100% from pre-crisis → CPI adds ~2-3pp directly
- Secondary effects (transport, chemicals, food via fertilizer) add another 1-2pp
- **European inflation could spike to 5-7% by April/May**

### ECB Policy — STAGFLATION TRAP
This is the key US transmission:
- ECB cut 8x (Jun 2024–Dec 2025) to reach 2.00%
- ECB was DONE cutting; market pricing modest hold
- Now: inflation shooting up + recession accelerating = STAGFLATION
- **ECB CANNOT CUT** to support growth without inflaming inflation
- **ECB CANNOT HIKE** without crushing already-contracting economy
- Most likely path: FORCED HOLD at 2.00%, emergency liquidity measures
- But if inflation >5%: hawks demand hike → political crisis within ECB
- **Implication for US:** ECB hold → EUR/USD support near-term, but energy capital flight to US overwhelms

### Russia Alternative
- TurkStream capacity: ~31.5 bcm/year (partial EU delivery)
- Some residual pipeline capacity via Ukraine corridor (if ceasefire happens)
- Russia CAN supply gas — but at political price
- See Q4 (Putin leverage)
