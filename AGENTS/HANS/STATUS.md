## Key Thesis

European dynamics affect US markets through several transmission channels:
1. **UST Holdings** — UK, Ireland, Luxembourg, Belgium are major holders
2. **Currency** — EUR/USD and GBP/USD affect trade, earnings, capital flows
3. **ECB Policy** — Global liquidity, rate differentials, USD funding
4. **Banking** — European bank stress can transmit to US (counterparty, sentiment)
5. **Energy** — European energy crisis affects global commodity prices, demand
6. **Risk Sentiment** — European stress = risk-off globally

## Transmission Channels to US

### 1. UST Demand/Supply
- UK, Ireland, Luxembourg, Belgium = ~$2T+ combined holdings
- European selling = UST yield pressure (like ZHAO thesis)
- Watch: Pension fund de-risking (UK gilt crisis 2022 model)

### 2. Currency Effects
- Strong USD = US multinational earnings headwind
- Weak EUR = European goods cheaper, US manufacturing pressure
- EUR/USD parity = significant psychological/flow threshold

### 3. ECB → Fed Divergence
- ECB cutting faster than Fed → EUR weakness
- Rate differential drives carry trades
- USD funding stress can emerge in European banks

### 4. Risk Transmission
- European bank stress → US bank CDS widen
- European sovereign crisis → flight to UST (paradoxically supportive)
- BUT: US money market fund exposure to European banks

### 5. Energy → Inflation
- European energy crisis → global commodity repricing
- German industry shutdown → supply chain effects
- LNG competition → US natural gas prices
