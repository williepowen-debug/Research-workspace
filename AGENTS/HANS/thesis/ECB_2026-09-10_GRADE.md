### 5a. ⚠️ Marker 1 fired, and its own mechanism refutes the reading it was written to capture — **the SECOND instance of this exact failure on THIS document in nine days**

§3b row 1 says an upward 2027 revision = regime, i.e. *second-round entrenchment*. **The ECB revised 2027 and 2028 up and attributed it to something else, in the same paragraph:**

> *"Higher energy prices are expected to **feed through gradually to core and food price inflation**. The **improved economic outlook** should also contribute to slightly higher core inflation."*

**That is energy pass-through plus stronger growth — the tactical mechanism — producing the regime observable.** And marker 3 independently kills the entrenchment reading: wages show *no material response*, ULC **decelerated** 3.5% → 2.6%, while **unit PROFITS rose 0.3% → 2.2%**. **The margin channel is carrying the pass-through, not the wage channel.** A wage-price spiral is the thing "regime" means, and it is measurably absent at the primary.

🔴 **This is `[[finding_headline_keyed_conditional_inherits_its_composition]]` for the second time on this pre-registration — n=2, nine days apart, same document, both times firing the hawkish branch on dovish composition.** Appendix A: the ≥3.2% HICP branch was written to detect *"broadening beyond energy"* and fired on a print where energy did all the work and core/services both decelerated. Today: the 2027-revision branch was written to detect *entrenchment* and fired on a revision the issuer attributes to energy pass-through and better growth. **Once is an unlucky branch. Twice on the same document is a design defect in how I write discriminators.**

**⇒ REGISTERED FIX, forward-only (does not touch this grade):** *every discriminator row states a **trigger** AND a **composition test**, and disagreement between them is the finding, not a tie to be broken.* Concretely, §3b row 1 should have read: *"projection revised UP for 2027 **AND the ECB attributes it to something other than energy pass-through.**"* Written that way, marker 1 scores TACTICAL today and §3b is **4–0**, not 3–1. **I am NOT retro-scoring it 4–0** — the row is graded as written, at 3–1 — but the corrected instrument and the grade it would have given are both on the record. *(The fix is a TIGHTENING, not a loosening — `[[finding_loosening_a_check_to_kill_a_false_alarm_inverts_the_failure_direction]]` does not apply.)*
