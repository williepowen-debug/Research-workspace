2. **The services leg is a red herring.** I am leaning on German Services 48.5 as the hold case; the ECB may weight it far less than I do.
3. **I have no OIS-implied probability.** I did not pull market pricing — my 75% is a HANS prior against *reported* consensus, not against a measured number. ⚠️ **That is a real weakness and it is the first thing to fix if this desk gets a rates feed.**
4. **Anchor risk.** The Sept-10 date is fixed, but an inter-meeting move would resolve this early and I have pre-committed to grading on the first decision, not the date.
