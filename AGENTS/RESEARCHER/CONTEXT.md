# Research Context (Thesis Mode Only)

This file provides context for connecting research findings to active investigation areas.
This is NOT a directional view. We are STUDYING these dynamics, not advocating a position.

## Research Domains

**Banking / CRE:**
We are studying regional bank exposure to commercial real estate, including exposure that may be classified under different loan categories (C&I vs CRE). Key regulatory data: FFIEC Call Reports, Schedule RC-C, Memo Item 3. Key names under study: WAL, OZK, EGBN, ZION, KRE (ETF).

**Private Credit / BDCs:**
We are studying interconnections between private equity firms, their insurance subsidiaries, and publicly traded BDCs. Key entity: Apollo Global (APO) and its relationship to Athene, ATLAS SP, MFIC. Also monitoring: FSK, BXSL, PSEC, OBDC.

**Labor Market:**
We are studying leading indicators of labor market deterioration including: WARN Act filings, staffing company revenues (RHI, KFRC, MAN), job posting withdrawal rates, insider selling patterns around layoff announcements, and the divergence between official employment data and alternative data signals. Also tracking DOGE government workforce reductions.

**Credit Markets:**
We are studying credit spread dynamics as transmission mechanisms for financial stress. Key series: HY OAS (BAMLH0A0HYM2), IG OAS (BAMLC0A0CM), CLO AAA spreads. Studying both absolute levels and rate-of-change as signals.

**Consumer:**
We are studying consumer financial stress including credit card delinquencies, mortgage delinquency transmission (Wright data), and the impact of energy price shocks on discretionary spending.

**Japan / Carry Trade:**
We are studying BOJ policy normalization and its potential to trigger carry trade unwinds that transmit stress to US markets. Key variables: USDJPY, JGB yields, Shunto wage negotiations.

**Geopolitical / Energy:**
We are studying the impact of geopolitical disruption (particularly Middle East conflict) on energy supply, and the second-order effects on consumer spending and corporate margins.
