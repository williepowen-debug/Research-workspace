**Short answer.** Q1 — what is common to large and regional banks this quarter is **rate-cycle deposit repricing and the working-off of already-reserved CRE losses**; what would mark *stress* (deposit flight, fresh reserve builds) is **not** showing up at the large banks, with one exception (PNC CRE). Q2 — **no transmission measured** where the large lenders disclose their non-bank book; the one unexplained large-bank credit item is Goldman's $368M provision on unattributed wholesale-loan impairments.
