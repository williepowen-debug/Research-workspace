## Kernel
A **Redfin/MLS** chart (*"Number of Sellers Jumps to 6-Year High"*, Datawrapper, series 2013→2026) puts active US home **SELLERS at 1,534,918** — a 6-year high — against active **BUYERS at 972,300**, at or near the series low. **Ratio ≈ 1.58 sellers per buyer.** The lines **crossed in ~2023**; before 2020 buyers ran above sellers for most of the series.

## Why you got this
Housing demand is yours.

## Requested action
**Low urgency. The ask: decide whether Redfin's active-buyer/seller estimator belongs on a HOMER surface at all** — and if so, **pull it at the Redfin release with its as-of date and methodology**, not off this chart. ⚠️ **If it goes on a surface, the BUYERS leg needs the "modelled" caveat attached permanently**, because a modelled series sitting beside counted ones in a table is what gets read as measured later.

## Confidence / caveats
- ⛔ **NO AS-OF DATE.** The axis ends at "2026" with no month on the final observation. **The level cannot be dated, so it cannot be sequenced against anything.** A "6-year high" without a date is a shape claim, not a print.
- **NO ARTICLE LINK** in the capture — WALTER has the chart, not the methodology note or the release.
- 🔴 **"ESTIMATED" IS REDFIN'S OWN WORD AND IT IS LOAD-BEARING.** Active buyers are not observable in MLS data the way listings are; the buyers line is **modelled**, the sellers line is much closer to a count. **They are not the same kind of quantity, and a ratio across them inherits the weaker leg.** ⛔ Do not quote 1.58 as a measured ratio.
- **Both figures are quoted to the unit** — precision a modelled estimate does not earn. `[[finding_exact_level_authenticates_a_wrong_direction]]`: the digits authenticate the number, not the method.
- ⛔ **Nothing fires.** No band on this board keys on Redfin active buyers or sellers. **$0.**
