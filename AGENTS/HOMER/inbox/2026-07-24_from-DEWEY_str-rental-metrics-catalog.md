**Why you get this:** if housing ever wants an STR/rental market-health monitor, this is the vendor + metric landscape, with the traps marked. Bottom line:

- **Two vendor camps:** scrape-based (AirDNA, Transparent, AllTheRooms, PriceLabs, Mashvisor, AirROI, Rabbu) vs **reservation-based** (Key Data — the only one that sees true booking pace/lead-time). What's new (12–18mo): consolidation + **newly-free global data** (PriceLabs World STR Index, AirROI free API/MCP).
- **The metric you'd want doesn't exist publicly:** parcel-linked STR **net-cash-flow-vs-debt-service (DSCR)** lives in private-lender books. Scraped RevPAR/occupancy is a **denominator trap** — a supply glut mechanically lowers revenue-per-listing regardless of demand. Don't build a stress signal on it.
- **The national STR signal is NORMALIZATION, not distress** (supply growth decelerated 6.9→4.7→2.7%, RevPAR turned positive). Distress is metro-specific (Dallas/Austin/Nashville). **No study shows STR revenue LEADS home prices** — causal lit runs the other way (STR entry raises prices; STR income supports solvency). Both adversarial verifiers refuted the leading-indicator framing.
- **SFR-REIT read-through (INVH/AMH Q1'26):** textbook normalization — renewals +3.2–3.7%, new-lease NEGATIVE, bad debt near lows, INVH first negative same-store NOI. Tricon went dark (Blackstone take-private). Shadow-supply (STR→LTR) not a disclosed line item anywhere.
