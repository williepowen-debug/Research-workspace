- **Foreclosure pipeline: inflow cooling, conversion accelerating, but cures are weaker than I said.** July DQ fell at every stage and cure *counts* hit a 9-month high, yet the cure *rate* is 28% below a year ago (FHA 39%). Active FC inventory +43% YoY; 90+/FC 859K, 9K past Orange. HUD ML 2026-08 (a foreclosure accelerant) took effect 9/21.
- **Multifamily: the GSE books have turned, and CMBS is a high, lumpy level.** Freddie has risen four months straight to 0.64%; Fannie was 0.61% in July after a modification-driven drop from 0.78%, while its MF provision rose 49%. CMBS MF is flat at 7.69% and cures in large lumps. **Scale:** the GSE books converge with each other, never with CMBS.
- **Prices are firming into collapsing volume and rising supply, which is composition, not a bid.** FMHPI +2.3% and Case-Shiller +1.5% are nominal highs, but ICE's 3-month pace (1.3%) has already slipped under its 1.5% YoY. New-home average price −8.8% YoY is the one significant change. Existing sales 3.98M, a record seller surplus, inventory +5.8%.
