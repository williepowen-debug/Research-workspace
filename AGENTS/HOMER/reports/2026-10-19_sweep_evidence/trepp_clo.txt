However, these loans account for 80.2% of the delinquent balance. Those loans are delinquent at 20.21%, against 0.69% for CRE CLO loans securitized in every other year. The delinquency rate alone does not capture the full extent of financial pressure on these loans. Every loan in the cohort carries a floating rate indexed to SOFR, exposing borrowers to higher debt service costs as benchmark rates have risen. The median net cash flow (NCF) debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) for the cohort fell from 1.60x at securitization to 0.64x in the most recent reporting period. A DSCR below 1.0x means property cash flow does not cover the loan payment, and 93.8% of the cohort’s performing balance with a reported most recent NCF DSCR is below that level. The sections below compare the cohort by state, explain how its floating coupons have moved, and show the change in coverage. The Cohort Accounts for Most Delinquent Balance in Every State The cohort is delinquent at a higher rate than other vintages in all five states. Georgia shows the widest gap, with a 59.65% cohort rate, and 10 of the state’s 11 delinquent CRE CLO multifamily loans come from the cohort. In New York, one of the state’s three delinquent CRE CLO multifamily loans comes from the cohort, so New York’s 89.2x multiple rests on a single loan. Floating Coupons Have Reset Higher CRE CLO loans are short-term loans, typically used to buy or reposition properties. All 93 loans in the cohort, representing $3.95 billion in outstanding balance, carry a floating rate. Their coupon equals a benchmark rate, the Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR), plus a spread set when the loan is made, so the coupon moves with SOFR. SOFR averaged 0.04% in 2021 and 3.66% in September 2026, an increase of 3.62 percentage points. For a loan originated when SOFR was near its 2021 average, this would translate to roughly 3.6 percentage points of additional interest, assuming an unchanged spread and no effective interest rate cap. The cohort’s median coupon is now 7.16%. Coverage Has Fallen for Delinquent and Performing Loans Alike A higher coupon raises the loan payment, so NCF DSCR can fall even when cash flow holds steady. Median NCF DSCR fell for delinquent loans, to 0.50x from 1.72x at securitization, and for performing loans, to 0.65x from 1.57x. Performing loans are not delinquent on their payments, yet 93.8% of their balance with a reported most recent NCF DSCR has coverage below 1.0x, close to the 100.0% for delinquent loans. Bottom Line CRE CLO multifamily loans securitized in 2021 and 2022 hold 12.2% of CRE CLO multifamily balance in the five largest securitized multifamily states and 80.2% of its delinquent balance. They are floating-rate loans with a median coupon of 7.16%, and their median NCF DSCR has fallen to 0.64x from 1.60x at securitization. The delinquency rate captures loans classified as delinquent but does not reflect the full extent of debt service pressure among performing loans. The coverage data show that most of the cohort’s performing balance with reported NCF DSCR data has cash flow insufficient to cover debt service, which makes those performing loans the ones to watch.
