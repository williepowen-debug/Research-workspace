Builders are holding more unstarted lots and fewer finished homes: less forced-sale pressure than the headline supply suggests.

## 5. Florida DR-4956: relief perimeter settled at HUD's text

- HUD Mortgagee Letter 2025-12 (replacing 2025-06; effective 2025-10-01): the procedures apply to "each designated area that is eligible for federal disaster assistance, designated for **public assistance, individual assistance, or both**". **Hardee IS covered**, so all seven counties. A 2026 change was searched once, none found.
- Moratorium: 90 days "beginning on the date of the disaster declaration": ends 2027-02-21 or 02-22 by counting convention. It covers starts AND foreclosures in process.
- **New: an automatic further 90 days** for servicers to commence or recommence, to about 2027-05-22. Local starts can stay low into May for policy reasons.
- Size (ACS 2020–24, Census Reporter): 1.377M housing units, **13.4%** of Florida's; 453,604 mortgaged homes, 13.6% (all mortgages, not FHA). Palm Beach is half of it. Foreclosure counts by county were not found free; the statewide count is CORAL's (address: FHA Annual Report Table A-21, FL row).
