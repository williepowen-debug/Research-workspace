## 6. Why the old >50K / >60K / >70K ladder pinned

| Old rung | Recent Q1-23..Q2-26 | Post-moratorium Q1-22..Q2-26 | All starts-basis ex-moratorium (51) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| >50K | 14/14 | 18/18 | **51/51** | **PINNED everywhere.** Has never failed to fire outside the moratorium |
| >60K | 14/14 | 17/18 (only Q1-22 missed) | 50/51 | **PINNED** since Q2-2022 |
| >70K | 5/14 (Q2-25 → Q2-26, 5 straight) | 5/18 | 38/51 (**every quarter 2012-Q1-2020**) | pinned since Q2-2025. Would have read **Red through the entire 2017-2019 normal** |

**Mechanism:** the old levels bracket the **2022-2024 post-moratorium trough (50,759-69,719)**. That was a regime still depressed by forbearance and loss-mitigation hangover, not a normal one. The 2017-19 floor (Q3-19 78,394) sits **above the old Red**. As starts normalized back toward 2019 levels, the ladder had to pin. This is the same failure as the disarmed band: **"a band calibrated on a regime the metric has left"**, only here the regime was the abnormally low one. ⚠ The source of the 50/60/70K levels was not found. The claim that they were calibrated to 2022-24 is an inference from the fit, not a documented origin.
