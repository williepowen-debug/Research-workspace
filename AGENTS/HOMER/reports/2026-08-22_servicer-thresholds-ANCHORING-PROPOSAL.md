## 1. ≥10% of market cap (Class E size gate) — **RE-AFFIRM. This one is genuinely earned.**

**Five of twelve actions clear it:** ONIT 2021 notes+warrants **115%** · UWMC 2026 preferred **55.9%** · RKT/Mr. Cooper **~34%** · ONIT 2024 Series B **22.6%** · UWMC rights **13.6%**.

★ **The reason to keep it is not the hit count — it is the SEPARATION.** The smallest firing action is **13.6%**. The largest *non*-firing action in the panel is **Oaktree's Dec-2020 placement into Ocwen at 4.3% of market cap.**

> **Nothing in the observed record sits between 4.3% and 13.6%. The gate lives in an empty region.**

**That is the strongest property a threshold can have** — it discriminates, and no observation sits near enough to make the exact level load-bearing. Moving it to 8% or 12% would change **nothing** in 32.3 company-years. ⇒ **Re-affirming is not laziness here; the data says the precise level does not matter, which is itself the finding.**

⚠️ **The caveat that keeps it honest:** an empty region on n=12 may be a **sample** artifact rather than a structural gap. Firms plausibly *do* raise 5–10% of market cap routinely; this panel simply did not in this window. **The separation is real in the observed record and must not be reported as a law.**

### ⛔ NEW DEFECT SURFACED BY THIS PASS — and it is a definition question, not a level

**LDI's contribution to Class F is an ATM shelf at 22.4% of market cap that was UNDRAWN at 6/30/26 — 0 executed.**

**As written, F is ambiguous about whether that fires.** If **capacity** counts, **F fires on a facility nobody drew** — a company that registers an ATM and never uses it scores identically to one that dilutes 22%. If it does not count, F's evidence base is **n=1, not n=2**, and everything in §2 above weakens accordingly.

**My recommendation: capacity is NOT an event. F fires on EXECUTED dilution only** — drawn amounts, issued shares, delivered warrants, converted preferred. **A registered shelf is an option, not a transfer of claim.** *(Consistent with the rest of the spec: A–D are all discrete events, and Class E's own size gate reads "gross proceeds," which capacity is not.)*

⚠️ **If ruled my way, F's base-rate drops to ONE observed event in 32.3 company-years, and I would rather report that honestly than keep an inflated n by counting a facility.**

**Two events, and here is the problem: both were 100% — outright suspensions.** UWMC 2026-08-06 (first in company history) and LDI 2022-05-10 ($0.08 → $0.00).

> ⛔ **A threshold at 50% has never discriminated anything in this panel. Any level from >0% to 100% catches both observed events identically. THE NUMBER HAS ZERO EVIDENCE BEHIND IT — not weak evidence, none.**

**No partial dividend cut has occurred in the observed record**, so the entire question the 50% line exists to answer — *how big a cut counts as distress?* — **has never been posed by the data.**

**I still recommend re-affirming**, for one reason: **there is nothing to re-anchor to.** A number invented off zero observations is worse than a number held off zero observations, because re-anchoring would imply the level had been informed. ⇒ **Hold at 50%, and write UNTESTED on the row so nobody reads "base-rated" and infers "validated."** *(`finding_adoption_is_not_validation` — consumed + confident + consistent means nobody tested it.)*
