## 1. ≥10% of market cap (Class E size gate) — **RE-AFFIRM. This one is genuinely earned.**

**Five of twelve actions clear it:** ONIT 2021 notes+warrants **115%** · UWMC 2026 preferred **55.9%** · RKT/Mr. Cooper **~34%** · ONIT 2024 Series B **22.6%** · UWMC rights **13.6%**.

★ **The reason to keep it is not the hit count — it is the SEPARATION.** The smallest firing action is **13.6%**. The largest *non*-firing action in the panel is **Oaktree's Dec-2020 placement into Ocwen at 4.3% of market cap.**

> **Nothing in the observed record sits between 4.3% and 13.6%. The gate lives in an empty region.**

**That is the strongest property a threshold can have** — it discriminates, and no observation sits near enough to make the exact level load-bearing. Moving it to 8% or 12% would change **nothing** in 32.3 company-years. ⇒ **Re-affirming is not laziness here; the data says the precise level does not matter, which is itself the finding.**

⚠️ **The caveat that keeps it honest:** an empty region on n=12 may be a **sample** artifact rather than a structural gap. Firms plausibly *do* raise 5–10% of market cap routinely; this panel simply did not in this window. **The separation is real in the observed record and must not be reported as a law.**
