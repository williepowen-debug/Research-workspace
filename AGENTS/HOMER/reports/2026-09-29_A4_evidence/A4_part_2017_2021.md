## 4. Regime notes (CARES Act moratorium, 2020–2021)

- Q1-2020 (81,251) is the last pre-moratorium quarter (the CARES Act was enacted 2020-03-27; federal-backed-loan foreclosure moratorium from 2020-03-18, extended repeatedly through 2021-07-31; CFPB servicing-rule protections to end-2021) — ⚠️ these policy dates are general knowledge, NOT re-sourced in this pass.
- Q2-2020 → Q2-2021 (15–19K/qtr) are **moratorium-suppressed** — ATTOM: "The government's moratoria have effectively stopped foreclosure activity on everything but vacant and abandoned properties" (YE-2020, 2021-01-14); "The government's foreclosure moratorium, and the CARES Act mortgage forbearance program have extended foreclosure timelines for owner-occupied homes by a full year" (Q1-2021, 2021-04-15).
- Q3-2021 (25,209) and Q4-2021 (30,395) = post-moratorium restart ("first double-digit quarterly percent increase since 2014", Q3-2021). Still ~70% below pre-pandemic per ATTOM (Sept 2021 vs Sept 2019).
- ⇒ For any band calibration, **Q2-2020 through Q2-2021 are a policy regime, not a credit regime**; Q3/Q4-2021 are a restart transient. 2017–2019 + Q1-2020 are the clean "normal" window (~78K–103K/qtr).
