# A4 (part) — U.S. national quarterly foreclosure starts / default notices, Q1-2006 → Q4-2011

Compiled 2026-09-29. Read-only research. Primary = RealtyTrac press-release page read directly (Wayback Machine `id_` raw capture of realtytrac.com, capture timestamp in URL) or ATTOM/RealtyTrac page read directly. Every release's dateline year was checked against its content (YEAR TRAP): one dateline anomaly noted (May-2006 data page carries a "May 16, 2006" dateline).

## 0. Bottom line (read this first)

1. **There is no single consistent RealtyTrac-era "foreclosure starts" quarterly series for 2006-2011.** What exists is **"default notices (NOD + LIS)"**, published on **three different bases** over the period, plus one later-restated annual **"foreclosure starts"** figure for 2009 on a fourth basis:
   | Period | What the release counts | Unit |
   |---|---|---|
   | Jan-2006 → Dec-2007 (monthly tables) | NOD and LIS **filings** (documents). Quarterly releases give only an all-type total, **no type split** | filings |
   | Q1-2008 → Q4-2010 (quarterly tables) | **Properties** by type of the **most recent** filing in the quarter (a property that got an NOD and then an NTS in the same quarter is counted as NTS, not as a default) | properties |
   | 2010-2011 (method text) | Same, plus "does not count the property if the same type of document was filed previously within the state's estimated foreclosure timeframe"; 2011 releases say default notices "filed **for the first time**" | properties, first-time |
   | Apr-2012 onward (and restated history) | **"Foreclosure starts" = default notices OR scheduled auction notices (NTS/NFS) "depending on the state's foreclosure process"** | properties |
2. **ATTOM / RealtyTrac restated 2009 annual starts = 2,139,005** (primary: RealtyTrac YE-2014, 2015-01-15; ATTOM YE-2016, 2017-01-10; ATTOM YE-2025, 2026-01-15). The RealtyTrac-era 2009 sum of quarterly default notices is **≈1,288K** → the restated figure is **≈1.66×** the contemporaneous one. **Never splice the two.**
3. **No restated annual "starts" figure was found for 2006, 2007, 2008, 2010 or 2011** (NOT FOUND). No restated quarterly starts for any 2006-2011 quarter was found.
4. Primary quarterly NOD+LIS counts found for **11 of 24 quarters** (Q1-08 → Q3-08, Q1-09 → Q3-09, Q1-10 → Q4-10, Q1-11, Q3-11). Q4-09, Q2-11, Q4-11 are **DERIVED** from published % changes (tight ranges). Q4-08 is **DERIVED** from monthly sums (wider range). **All 2006 and 2007 quarters are DERIVED** (sum of three monthly NOD+LIS filing counts; filings basis).

## 3. Basis changes, definition notes and revisions

**Basis / definition changes**
1. **2006 filings labelled as properties.** 2006 releases say "X properties entered some stage of foreclosure" (e.g. Q1-06 323,102; S4), but the YE-2006 total 1,259,118 is called "foreclosure filings" (S15) and was later restated as **717,522 properties** (S57). ⇒ 2006-07 monthly NOD/LIS counts are document counts.
2. **Switch to properties: January-2008 report** (2008-02-26, S60): "reported on 233,001 properties during the month". December 2007 (215,749) was still "filings" (S33).
3. **Quarterly type tables begin Q1-2008** (S34). The 2006-07 quarterly releases (Q1-06 S4, Q2-06 S7, Q3-06 S11, Q1-07 S21, H1-07 S25, Q3-07 S29, YE-07 S33) publish all-type totals only.
4. **Most-recent-filing rule** stated in the Q3-09 methodology (S43): "If more than one foreclosure document is filed against a property during the month or quarter, only the most recent filing is counted". Effect on 2008-2010 quarters: quarterly NOD+LIS = 0.867-0.973 × the sum of the three monthly counts.
5. **Prior-filing de-dup** in the Q1-10 methodology (S44): the property is not counted if the same document type was filed earlier within the state's estimated foreclosure timeframe. **"For the first time"** wording from the Q1-2011 release onward (S52).
6. **Starts definition widened, April 2012** (Q1-2012 release, 2012-04-12, S56): "First-time foreclosure starts, either default notices or scheduled foreclosure auctions depending on the state's foreclosure process". Size of the gap: under the new basis March-2012 starts "exceeded 100,000 for the first time since November 2011", while old-basis November-2011 default notices were **71,730** (S61). ⇒ roughly 40%+ higher on the new basis. RealtyTrac YE-2014 repeats it: "Scheduled auctions — which in some states are the foreclosure start" (S57). **This is why 2,139,005 ≫ ≈1.29M for 2009.** The claim in the brief that the restated series "includes NTS where NTS is the first notice" is **confirmed** by S56/S57. ATTOM YE-2025 (S59) itself does not restate the definition.
7. **State-law and collection breaks in the NOD series** (footnotes in the 2009 tables, S41/S42/S45): "Collection of some records previously classified as NOD in this state was discontinued starting in September 2008" / "…starting in January 2009"; "Collection of records classified as NOD began in August 2009 because of change in state law". California **SB 1137** (effective Sept 2008) cut CA NODs from 44,278 (Aug-08) to 21,665 (Sep-08) (S40); national NOD falls from 61,903 to 39,828. **Nevada AB 284** (Oct 2011) cut NV default notices by 70% QoQ in Q4-11 (S55).
8. **June-2007 NOD/LIS swing.** National NOD fell from 55,615 (May-07) to 36,048 (Jun-07) while LIS rose from 25,733 to 38,247 (S23, S24). Likely a classification or collection change. Not explained in the release; treat the NOD/LIS split as unreliable around mid-2007, and the sum as more robust.
