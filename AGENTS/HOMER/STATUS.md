## MARQUEE OPEN QUESTION — Multifamily: the frame changed 7/31

**★ REFRAMED this session on the Q2-2026 GSE prints. The old frame ("GSE improving vs CMBS deteriorating") is retired — it was never two books moving apart; it was two RECOGNITION REGIMES.** The Q2 prints break the GSE leg in half:

**The finding: the two GSEs moved in OPPOSITE directions in the same quarter, in the same asset class.** The agency with a large one-off modification event improved; the agency without one deteriorated to its band edge. That asymmetry is the evidence — Fannie's decline is a recognition artifact, not multifamily credit healing.

**Fannie says so itself — and the 10-Q goes further than the earnings release.** Release verbatim: the MF SDQ decline is *"primarily as a result of the modification of a loan portfolio previously in forbearance and foreclosure activity, partially offset by additional loans that became seriously delinquent due to sustained market challenges in recent periods."* **The 10-Q (filed 7/29) then adds the forward warning:** *"Although this loan modification and foreclosure activity resulted in a decrease in our multifamily serious delinquency rate, **ongoing market challenges are expected to result in additional delinquencies**."* **Fannie is telling holders the improvement is mechanical and does not expect it to persist.** (Also from the 10-Q: MF 180+ day DQ 0.43% from 0.56%; criticized loans 6% → 5%.) Both named channels — modification **and** foreclosure — remove loans from the numerator **without a single borrower curing**, while the inflow of newly-serious loans continued. And because footnote 11 defines the rate on **unpaid principal balance, not loan count**, one large modified portfolio moves it hard.

**Then the loss accounting contradicts the headline:** provision $174M → $259M QoQ, attributed to *"weaker property valuations and slower net operating income growth… and provision for loans that became seriously delinquent."* **A book that was genuinely healing would not be reserving 49% harder against it.** When anyone calls the GSE MF book "improving," this is the number to cite.

**Consequence for the record:** CRL-03 (resolved MISSED at CARL 7/02 on Fannie's decline) **stays resolved** — its pre-registered trigger fired and `LESSONS.md` forbids retro-fitting a survival argument. But the mechanism note that accompanied that resolution has now been **corroborated by the issuer's own disclosure and by Freddie moving the other way** — it is no longer HOMER's inference. Recorded, not reopened.
