## 4. Mechanism vs threshold — pre-committed, per `[[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]]`

**A ❌ on LAB-17 does NOT falsify the WARN→claims framework** (r=0.78 at ~6wk lag). It falsifies the **sizing** of *this* cohort against a ~200K weekly base.

Committed arithmetic for the post-mortem: the whole dated big-tech cluster inside the window (LinkedIn 606 + Meta 4,665 + Intuit 910 ≈ **6,181**) is **~3% of a single week's claims** and is spread across three separate weeks. **It was never large enough to move a 4-wk MA by 28K** (187.5K → 235K). That is a **specification** defect in LAB-17, identified *before* the resolution — the threshold was set on the aggregate series while the mechanism being tested was a cohort ~1/30th its size.

**Detectability rule to carry forward (new, from this card):** a WARN cohort needs to be **≥~10% of the weekly claims base (~20K+ separations in one week)** to be visible in the national SA series. Cohorts below that are testable only in **state-level** data. Log to `LESSONS.md` at C5 regardless of outcome — this lesson is earned now, not on Aug 6.

This is LABOR's known failure mode: raw as-made Brier **0.277**, dragged by high-confidence *threshold* misses (LAB-01 85%, LAB-02 65%) while every *mechanism* call landed. LAB-17 is the same shape, caught earlier — it was walked 35→5% on the 7/23 evidence rather than defended.
