## 3. MECHANISM vs THRESHOLD (stated before the print)

- **All three tests are THRESHOLD/COUNTER mechanics.** None of them measures displacement; they measure what employers **say** in announcements.
- **The AI share is a LABELLING statistic.** A low share can mean fewer AI-driven cuts or fewer cuts *attributed* to AI. **I will not read a share move as a change in displacement in either direction.**
- **LAB-11 (AI narrative shield breaks, 50%) is NOT graded on this card.** Any re-mark happens at a C2 against LAB-11's own letter, with the date written in the Confidence field (WQ-112 forward-only).
