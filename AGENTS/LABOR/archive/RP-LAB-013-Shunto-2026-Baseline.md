### 3. "Shock" Threshold Framework

#### BASELINE (GREEN) - Continuation

- **Demands:** 5.0-5.5% range
- **Settlements:** 4.8-5.5%
- **Market Impact:** Priced in, no BOJ surprise
- **Implication:** Gradual normalization continues

#### YELLOW - Upside Surprise

- **Demands:** >5.5% but <6.5%
- **Settlements:** >5.5% but <6.0%
- **Market Impact:** BOJ hawkish tilt, April hike probability rises
- **Implication:** Yen strength, vol spike, but manageable

#### ORANGE - Material Shock

- **Demands:** ≥6.5% 
- **Settlements:** ≥6.0%
- **Market Impact:** BOJ emergency inter-meeting hike possible
- **Implication:** Yen carry unwind begins, risk-off cascade
- **Transmission:** 2-4 week lag to US hiring freezes (CFO panic mode)

#### RED - Crisis Trigger

- **Demands:** ≥7.5%
- **Settlements:** ≥7.0%
- **Market Impact:** Multi-hike BOJ cycle priced immediately
- **Implication:** Full yen carry unwind, coordinated CB intervention
- **Transmission:** Immediate US hiring freezes, capex cuts

### 4. Transmission Mechanism (BOJ→US Employment)

```
Shunto Shock (≥6.0% settlement)
     ↓
BOJ Emergency Hike (0.50%+ possible)
     ↓
Yen Carry Unwind (USD/JPY: 145→135 in weeks)
     ↓
Global Risk-Off (VIX >30, credit spreads widen)
     ↓
US Corporate CFO Panic (liquidity preservation mode)
     ↓
Hiring Freezes + Capex Cuts (2-4 week lag)
     ↓
LABOR Metrics: 
  - Initial claims spike >250K
  - JOLTS openings collapse
  - Temp employment accelerates decline
```

**Historical Precedent:** Aug 2024 yen carry unwind saw USD/JPY move 155→141 in 3 weeks, VIX spiked to 65, US NFP printed -12K the following month (revised).

### 5. Why Electronics Matter

- **First mover:** Sets wage benchmark for manufacturing→services rounds
- **Supply chain signaling:** Electronics = global export sector, forward-looking
- **Government pressure:** PM Takaichi personally urged "above-inflation" hikes to business lobby
- **BOJ dependency:** Electronics wages feed directly into BOJ wage growth models

### 6. What Constitutes a "Shock"?

**Quantitative:**
- Settlement >1.0pp above consensus (5.0% → 6.0%+)
- Acceleration from prior year (5.25% → 6.0%+ = +0.75pp)

**Qualitative:**
- Emergency BOJ statement referencing "wage-price spiral"  
- Government intervention to cool demands
- Major company CEOs citing labor cost crisis
