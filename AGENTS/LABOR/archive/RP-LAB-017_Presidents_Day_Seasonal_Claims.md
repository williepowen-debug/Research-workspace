## SECTION 1: WHICH WEEK DOES THURSDAY'S PRINT COVER?

**Thursday Feb 20, 2026 = Data for week ending Saturday, Feb 14, 2026**

The DOL releases data on a one-week lag:
- Claim filers: Mon Feb 9 – Fri Feb 13
- Presidents' Day (Feb 16) falls **outside this window**

→ **Thursday's print is pre-holiday.** No Presidents' Day seasonal distortion applies to the Feb 20 release.

The holiday distortion affects the **Feb 27 release** (week ending Feb 21), which captures Mon Feb 16 (Presidents' Day) through Fri Feb 20.

### Pattern Analysis

**No consistent directional bias** across 4 years:
- 2025: Sharp spike (+19K) — DHS/DOGE backdrop in 2025 added complexity
- 2024: Moderate spike (+11K) — most "clean" holiday signal
- 2023: Slight decline (-8K) — labor market strength absorbed

**2024 is the best analog year** for current conditions (moderately elevated, no structural break). Under 2024 analog: expect +11K on the Feb 27 print vs. the Feb 20 baseline.

**Why can holiday weeks sometimes spike?** DOL processes claims Monday-Friday. A Monday holiday means some workers delay filing to Tuesday; state offices may also delay processing, creating a one-day shift that can show up in the next week's count. The seasonal adjustment factors have imperfectly captured this pattern in recent years, leading to residual volatility.

## SECTION 3: DHS SHUTDOWN — IMPACT ON INITIAL CLAIMS

### Worker Classification
| Category | Count | UI Eligibility | Impact on Claims |
|----------|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Essential (excepted)** | ~234,000 | ❌ **Cannot file** — still employed | **Zero claims filed** |
| **Non-excepted (furloughed)** | ~26,000 | ✅ Can file (state-dependent) | Historically DON'T (expect retro pay) |
| **Federal contractors** | Unknown (DHS-adjacent) | ✅ Can file | Some may file |

### Legal Framework
- **Public Law 116-1:** All furloughed federal employees will receive retroactive pay when funding resumes
- **Essential workers:** Cannot file UI because they are technically employed (just not paid immediately)
- **Historical precedent:** Oct 2023 shutdown — very few essential workers filed UI despite working without pay
- **State variability:** MD, VA, WA have explicit guidance allowing furloughed feds to file; most other states do not

### Net Impact on Feb 20 Claims Print
**→ MINIMAL to ZERO direct suppression effect on Thursday's headline number.**

Essential workers (~234K) never show up in claims regardless of pay status. Non-excepted workers (~26K) almost universally decline to file knowing retroactive pay is coming. The "suppression" narrative applies primarily to *spending behavior* (CARL's domain), not UI filings.

### Secondary Effects (NOT in Claims, But Real)
1. **Spending suppression:** 234K households cut discretionary spending immediately (lag 2-4 weeks into CARL data)
2. **Geographic concentration:** DHS workers concentrated in border states (TX, FL, CA, AZ) and major metros (DC, NY) — overlaps our WARN hotspot geographies
3. **Resolution spike risk:** When shutdown ends, retroactive pay will create a one-time spending bump that may temporarily mask underlying deterioration in retail/restaurant data
