## S - SYNTHESIS

### Architecture Rationale

Employment deserves top-level agent status because:
1. It's the TRANSMISSION MECHANISM across all domains
2. It's currently fragmented (each agent tracks pieces)
3. It breaks in a SEQUENCE that can be detected early
4. DOGE creates direct employment shock that's UNTRACKED
5. Leading indicators exist but aren't consolidated

### The Core Framework

```
LEADING (3-6 months ahead)
├── Job postings decline
├── Temp employment decline
├── NFIB hiring plans drop
└── Small business revenue stress (POP)

EARLY WARNING (weeks ahead)
├── Initial claims uptick
├── Hours worked decline
└── Hiring freezes announced

COINCIDENT (real-time confirmation)
├── NFP misses/negative
├── Unemployment rate rises
└── Layoff announcements

LAGGING (confirms trend)
├── Continuing claims plateau
├── Long-term unemployment
└── Labor force participation drop
```

**LABOR's job:** Track the LEADING indicators to provide warning before COINCIDENT confirms.
