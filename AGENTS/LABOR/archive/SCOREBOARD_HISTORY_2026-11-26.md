## § method-scope-20261120 — §A Method and Scoring-convention paragraphs as they stood

**Method:** Brier score = (confidence − outcome)², outcome = 1 if CONFIRMED, 0 if FALSIFIED. Lower is better. Reference points: **0.00** = perfect · **<0.20** = skilled · **0.1875** = base-rate (always predict the confirm rate; at **3/12 = 25.0%** that is `3(0.75²) + 9(0.25²) = 2.25 / 12 = 0.1875`) · **0.25** = no skill (always say "50%") · **1.00** = confidently, exactly wrong. 🔧 *(Corrected 2026-09-07, DAEDALUS F12: this line read **0.234** on a **3/8 = 37.5%** confirm rate — a stale n from when the book held 8 resolutions. The bar moved DOWN, so the "loses to its own base rate" verdict survives and widens.)*

**Scoring convention — AS-MADE, not final (this matters).** Each prediction is scored at the confidence it was **registered** with at `Date_Made`, NOT its walked-down final value. Grading the walked-down number credits a forecaster for conceding *after* the evidence turned — marking your own homework with the answer key visible, and the exact self-flattery a calibration artifact exists to catch (pre-registration discipline: `[[feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast]]`, `[[finding_delta_vs_own_prior_local_extreme]]`). Walk-downs are a *separate positive signal* (update discipline) noted below, not folded into the Brier. Scope: 12 LABOR-owned predictions resolved by LABOR (LAB-04 excluded — REHOMED to CORAL). Sorted worst-calibration-first.
