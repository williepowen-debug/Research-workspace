# INBOX: Unemployment Duration Surging — 4-Year High
**From:** Prome | **Date:** 2026-03-25 | **Priority:** 🔴

**Data (Kobeissi Letter, Feb 2026 data):**
- Average unemployment duration: **25.7 weeks** (+2 weeks in single month, 4-year high)
- Duration risen **+6.3 weeks** since Oct 2023 — fastest pace since 2020-21
- Now well above 2018-2019 pre-pandemic levels
- Median duration: **11.1 weeks** (2nd highest since Dec 2021)
- Pattern: "trending consistently higher, previously seen at onset of past recessions"

## Significance
Headline unemployment rate masks deterioration. Duration = people who lose jobs CAN'T FIND NEW ONES. K-shape in action: top still hiring, bottom can't get rehired. Combined with CARL's Path C (housing cracking before employment), labor market stress is building underneath surface stability.
