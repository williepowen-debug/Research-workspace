# SIG-LABOR-20260331 — Hiring Rate Lowest Since 2011

**Source:** Guy Berger / Yahoo Finance (JOLTS), 3/31/26
**Priority:** 🟡

February hiring rate 3.1% — lowest since early pandemic, and before that since beginning of 2011 (when unemployment was ~10%). "Great Resignation" → "Great Stay."

**Implications:**
- Pre-layoff phase: companies frozen, not yet firing
- When it breaks, acceleration is rapid (no hiring pipeline to restart)
- K-shape labor deterioration pattern confirming
- Feeds CARL (consumer stress) and HENRY (stagflation)
