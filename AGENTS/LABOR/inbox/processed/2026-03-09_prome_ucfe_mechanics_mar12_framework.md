**2. SF-50/SF-8 Administrative Bottleneck**
- UCFE claims require SF-50 (separation verification) and SF-8 (payroll routing) from employing agency.
- DOGE simultaneously gutted OPM and agency HR departments — the people who ISSUE these forms were fired.
- Without SF-50, state agencies set monetary determination to **$0** — claim is filed but financially inactive and likely excluded from compensated claims tallies.
- Backup: State sends Form 931 to federal agency, 12-day mandatory response. But agency HR staff gone → 931s go unanswered.
- NFC (USDA National Finance Center) processes payroll for 640K employees. Any disruption there = system-wide freeze.
- Some states allow ES-935 (claimant self-affidavit) as bypass, but fear of overpayment liability causes both claimants and agencies to wait for official docs.
- Additional delay: severance packages disqualify from UI in many states until severance period expires.
