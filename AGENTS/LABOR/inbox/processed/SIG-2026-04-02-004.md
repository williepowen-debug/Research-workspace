Compounds with Fed breakeven research: zero labor force growth + lowest hiring rate since 2011 = labor market frozen. People aren't being fired en masse yet, but nobody is hiring. Pre-recessionary pattern — hiring freezes precede layoffs by 2-4 quarters historically.

Combined picture: breakeven near zero + hiring frozen + shadow adjustment +65K = labor market far weaker than headline data suggests. Apr 10 claims print is the test.
