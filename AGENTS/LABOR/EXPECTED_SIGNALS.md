## Weekly Signals (Every Thursday)

### Initial Claims Release
- **Time:** 8:30am ET Thursday
- **Source:** Department of Labor
- **Key Metrics:** Initial claims, continuing claims, 4-week moving average

| Reading | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| <230K | Normal, healthy | Note, no action |
| 230-250K | Slightly elevated | Flag in VX, watch trend |
| 250-280K | Elevated | Update VX YELLOW, prepare cross-signal |
| 280-300K | Concerning | Signal CARL, REGINALD — ORANGE |
| >300K | Crisis level | Signal ALL agents — RED |

**Key Questions Each Week:**
- Is the trend rising, falling, or stable?
- Any unusual seasonal factors?
- Which states driving changes?

## Monthly Signals

### NFP / Employment Situation (1st Friday)
- **Time:** 8:30am ET, first Friday of month
- **Source:** Bureau of Labor Statistics

| Reading | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| >200K | Strong | Note, no action |
| 150-200K | Solid | Note trend |
| 100-150K | Cooling | Update VX YELLOW |
| 50-100K | Weak | Signal CARL — ORANGE |
| 0-50K | Very weak | Signal ALL — approaching RED |
| <0 | Negative | Signal ALL — CRITICAL (recession) |

**Also Watch:**
- Unemployment rate (4.1% current)
- Labor force participation
- Average hourly earnings
- Average weekly hours
- Revisions to prior months

### JOLTS (~40 days after reference month)
- **Key Metrics:** Openings, quits rate, layoffs rate

| Metric | Current | Watch For |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Openings | 7.5M | <7M = cooling, <6M = weak |
| Quits Rate | 2.0% | <1.8% = workers scared |
| Layoffs Rate | 1.0% | >1.2% = cuts accelerating |

### Challenger Job Cuts (1st Thursday)
- Announced layoffs by sector
- High visibility for tech layoffs
- YoY comparison matters (seasonality)

### ADP (2 days before NFP)
- Private sector preview
- Noisy but directional
- Compare to consensus

### NFIB (2nd Tuesday)
- Small business hiring intentions
- Leading indicator
- Net positive = planning to hire
