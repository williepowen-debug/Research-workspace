# STATE UI EXHAUSTION FRAMEWORK
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-11

---

## THESIS

**UI exhaustion is the transmission mechanism from employment stress to consumer stress.**

The lag structure:
1. **WARN filing** → Public notice of layoff (60 days advance)
2. **Actual layoff** → Initial claim filed
3. **UI benefit period** → State-specific (12-26 weeks)
4. **Exhaustion window** → When UI ends, consumer stress transmits to CARL's domain

Florida's 12-week duration makes it the **canary state** for exhaustion-driven stress.

## EXHAUSTION RATE CALCULATION

**Formula:**
```
Exhaustion Rate = (UI exhaustions in month) / (Initial claims 12 weeks prior)
```

**FL Example (April 2026 projected):**
- Initial claims week of Jan 13, 2026: ~5,000/week (FL share of national 231K)
- 12 weeks later = April 6, 2026
- Exhaustion rate: Track via FL unemployment data (monthly release)

**National baseline exhaustion rate (pre-pandemic):** ~35% of initial claimants exhaust benefits  
**FL exhaustion rate (12 weeks):** Likely ~50-60% due to shorter duration
