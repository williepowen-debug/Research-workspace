**Method:** Brier score = (confidence − outcome)², outcome = 1 if CONFIRMED, 0 if FALSIFIED. Lower is better. Reference points: **0.00** = perfect · **<0.20** = skilled · **0.222** = base-rate (always predict the confirm rate; at **5/15 = 33.3%** that is `(5/15)(10/15) = 50/225 = 0.222`) · **0.25** = no skill (always say "50%") · **1.00** = confidently, exactly wrong. *(Earlier base-rate figures 0.234 and 0.1875 at smaller n → `archive/SCOREBOARD_HISTORY_2026-11-26.md` § method-scope-20261120.)*

**Scoring convention — AS-MADE, not final (this matters).** Each prediction is scored at the confidence it was **registered** with at `Date_Made`, NOT its walked-down final value. Grading the walked-down number credits a forecaster for conceding *after* the evidence turned — marking your own homework with the answer key visible, and the exact self-flattery a calibration artifact exists to catch (pre-registration discipline: `[[feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast]]`, `[[finding_delta_vs_own_prior_local_extreme]]`). Walk-downs are a *separate positive signal* (update discipline) noted below, not folded into the Brier. 5. **🔬 (2026-08-07) ARITHMETIC RECONCILIATION — raised by the Will-directed 8/6 commit review, item 2. 1. **MEASURE — canonical, not proxy.** Denominated in the canonical *industry* series (BLS CES / JOLTS / official release), NOT a company proxy (staffing-firm revenue, single-firm earnings)? — killed LAB-01 & LAB-05. (→ **L-01**, `[[feedback_prediction_canonical_measure]]`)
2. **MEASURE — defeatable-gauge check.** If the threshold is a *ratio* (U-3, penetration rate): can its denominator defeat it without the mechanism moving? U-3 graded JOINTLY with LFPR — a participation-driven move = NO-SIGNAL. — killed LAB-02. (→ **L-06**)
3. **THRESHOLD vs MECHANISM — which am I predicting?** A *mechanism-in-motion* call earns conviction; a *specific-level* call does not (record at 2026-11-20: 4-for-4 on ≥60% mechanisms, **0-for-6** on ≥60% thresholds — 🔧 this gate read "0/3" until 2026-09-07, DAEDALUS F12). If it's a level call, cap confidence AND state the defeat condition. (→ `[[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]]`)
4. **SIZING — cohort-to-base ratio (compute it, write it down).** If the prediction is driven by a *cohort* (WARN filings, a named layoff wave, a sector event) but the threshold is denominated on an **aggregate series**: what fraction of the aggregate's per-period base is that cohort? **Detectability floor: ≥~10% of the weekly claims base (~20K+ separations landing in ONE week).** Below that floor the cohort cannot move the aggregate *regardless of how well the mechanism works* — **register the test against state-level data instead, or do not register a threshold at all.** Killed LAB-17: cohort ~6,181 spread across three weeks vs a ~200K weekly base = ~3% of a single week, against a threshold needing **+28K on a 4-wk MA**. **Write the computed ratio into the prediction's Notes at registration** — this gate is only real if the arithmetic is on the record *before* the print. (→ **L-08**, `[[finding_threshold_spec_fails_before_world]]`)
5. **>80% STOP.** Confidence above 80%? STOP — LABOR's only 85% call (LAB-01) was its single worst miss. 80%+ is earned ONLY by an intact mechanism on a non-defeatable measure. (→ §A)
6. **DATA VINTAGE — revised series.** If NFP/revisable: scoring on the REVISED series, and are Kill/exit rules evaluated on revised (not first-print) data? (→ **L-02**)
7. **BASE-EFFECT — gov/DOGE.** If gov/DOGE/federal: using level + MoM, not a base-effect-poisoned YoY comp? (→ **L-03**)
8. **CROSS-DOMAIN — sign check.** If the driver is cross-domain (ICE/immigration, oil, climate): which way does it move LABOR's headline metric (claims/U-3)? Same-direction = convergence; opposite/sideways = CONTEXT, don't score as employment-bearish. (→ **L-05**)
9. **ANNOUNCEMENT — TYPE + filing existence.** If layoff-announcement-based: tagged the TYPE (VR-offer ≈ 0 claims / involuntary RIF / closure / contract-churn) and verified the WARN filing exists before attributing a surge? Effective date (not notice date) sets the claims week (WARN 60-day). (→ **L-07**, `docket/WARN_COHORT.tsv`)
10. **ANCHOR — surprise, not priced.** Anchored to the SURPRISE vs consensus, not a level the market has already priced? (→ `[[finding_anchor_prediction_to_surprise_not_priced]]`)
11. **PRE-REGISTRATION — score as-made.** Registering a clean confidence at Date_Made that will be scored AS-MADE (§A convention)? Walking it down later is good *process* but earns no calibration credit — don't plan to concede-and-credit. (→ `[[feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast]]`)
12. **🆕 MULTI-DRAW — decompose "holds through DATE" into PER-DRAW survival.** If the prediction is *"X stays above/below L through DATE"*, it is not one call — it is **N draws where any single bad draw kills it permanently**. Count N (**and freeze which prints qualify — publication lag decides, not the data month**), then compute **conf^(1/N) = the per-draw survival you are implicitly asserting**, and ask whether *that* number is defensible against the series' own monthly volatility and current distance-to-line. Rule of thumb: **if the move that kills it is smaller than the series' recent average monthly move, per-draw survival is nowhere near 90%, so no multi-draw call belongs above ~50%.** Caught LAB-06: 80% through 2026 = **95.6% survival per print**, asserted from 0.3 away while the series was climbing +1.1/month — repriced to 20% pre-print. (→ **L-10**)
13. **🆕 LABEL != REPRICE.** Does any open row carry a qualitative risk marker (**"AT RISK"**, "watch", "fragile", "⚠️") while its **confidence field is unchanged**? That is an unpriced update — the marker discharges the *feeling* of having updated without moving the number that scores. **Either move the number or delete the label.** LAB-06 carried "AT RISK" for a month at an untouched 80%. **Run this as a sweep over the whole open book at C2, not just on the row you're touching.** (→ **L-10**, `[[feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast]]`)
14. **🆕 GRADING-CARD BANDS MUST REPRODUCE THE HEADLINE — and a PRE-PRINT REPRICE is a re-registration, not a concession.** Two halves, both from the 7/31 Band-B defect. **(a) Coherence:** when a card assigns post-print confidences to outcome bands, run the **law of total probability** before freezing it — `Σ P(band) × V(band)` must reproduce the current headline confidence, and the `V(band)` must be **monotone** in the direction of the evidence. The original Aug-3 card failed both: Band B said "survives → no change" (impossible — surviving *removes a draw* from a survive-N structure, so the number must rise), and a survival *further* from the line scored *lower* than one nearer it. **For a survive-N call, the post-survival floor is the remaining-path product** (here 0.68×0.75×0.78×0.80 = 31.8%), never "no change." This is gate #12's arithmetic applied to the *card* rather than the registration — and it was missed in the very first card written after #12 shipped. **(b) Scoring:** §A's as-made convention was written for a **walk-down after the evidence turned** and is *silent* on a reprice made **pre-print and unforced** (no new data, defect-driven). Do not cite it as if it settles that case. Treatment: **headline stays as-made** (it is what the fleet was told and could have acted on), with the pre-print value recorded as a **separate labeled diagnostic**, never folded into the mean. It is legitimate only because it is **symmetric** — a pre-print reprice that is wrong in the other direction is penalised by exactly as much as it would have gained. **The loophole is closed by timing:** a re-registration counts only if **declared before the print, with the git commit as receipt**; it can never be claimed retroactively. (→ **L-10**, `[[finding_confidence_priced_against_thesis_not_letter]]`, `[[feedback_dont_bank_unpassed_forecast]]`)
15. **🆕 RESOLUTION PROCEDURE — name the SOURCE and the ARITHMETIC before you write the confidence.** In one sentence: *which document, which figures, which comparison, which number settles it?* **If you cannot state that, you do not have a prediction — you have a sentiment, and no confidence value belongs on it.** Concretely the row must name: **(a)** the population/cohort by enumeration or by a rule that yields one; **(b)** the measured quantity and its basis (level vs growth *rate*, GAAP vs adjusted, which fiscal period); **(c)** the pass/fail bar **as an integer or an exact number**, not a percentage that will need rounding; **(d)** the tie-break, written to resolve **against** the prediction. 🔴 **Bought by LAB-10, which sat OPEN at 75% for five months with NO defined cohort and NO definition of "decel", was flagged "owed grading" three consecutive sessions, and was never graded because it could not be.** Every other gate here measures confidence, timing or staleness — **none of them asks whether the claim names what would settle it**, so an ungradable row is indistinguishable from a merely-overdue one and survives indefinitely under a label that reads as diligence. ⚠️ **The tell, missed three times: repeated deferral of a supposedly easy grade is evidence of a blocked PATH, not of low priority** (`[[finding_audit_resolution_path_before_reattempt]]`). **Runs at C2-0 as well as at registration:** for every OPEN row, can I state its resolution procedure right now? If not, mark it **STUCK** immediately — STUCK is a *status*, not a confidence cut (`[[finding_resolvability_defect_is_status_not_confidence]]`), and it stops the row masquerading as a live forecast. **Salvage rules if a row is already STUCK → §A finding 8.**

## §D — MAINTENANCE + WIRE-IN

**Freshness model:** this is a *derived* artifact — source of truth stays `PREDICTIONS.tsv` (ledger) + `LESSONS.md` (patterns). It refreshes on prediction *events* (resolutions), not a data cadence, so it carries **no boot mtime-alert** (unlike KB/CATALYSTS/WARN_COHORT). Its freshness is gated by C2 discipline below.

**Wire-in (where the loop closes — both ends are live in `CLAUDE.md`):**
- **boot B4** → *run §C (pre-write checklist) before registering any new prediction.*
- **closeout C2** → *when a prediction resolves, update §A (score as-made) + recompute.*

**Update procedure (at C2, per resolved prediction):**
1. Add a §A row scored at the **AS-MADE confidence** (registered at `Date_Made` in PREDICTIONS.tsv — NOT any walked-down final value). If the confidence was walked, note the walk as *update-discipline* in the verdict, but Brier uses as-made.
2. Recompute the stats block: mean Brier = mean of (conf − outcome)²; benchmarks = coin-flip **0.25** and base-rate (= running CONFIRMED share); refresh directional-lean and confidence-bucket tallies.
3. If a new success/failure pattern emerges (or an existing one shifts), update §B — state hit-rates plainly, don't inflate.
4. If the resolution produced a NEW durable lesson, write it to `LESSONS.md` (Lxx) and add a §C gate pointing to it — §C stays an index, never a restatement.
5. Keep `PREDICTIONS.tsv` Status vocab **terminal** (`OPEN` / `RESOLVED`) — `predictions_due.py` exact-matches it; no intermediate states.

**Do NOT:** score walked-down confidences (self-flattery — the bias this board exists to catch); drop the worst outlier from the headline number; restate lesson text here (drift). *(All three were caught in this board's own first draft — DAEDALUS adversarial-verify, 2026-07-10.)*

🔴 **AND THE FIRST RULE ABOVE FAILED ANYWAY, FOR TWO MONTHS, ON FOUR ROWS — read this before trusting a future as-made.** The rule was written, understood, and cited by name in this file; what defeated it was not forgetting it but **verifying it against `PREDICTIONS.tsv`**, whose earliest blob postdates registration by up to 30 days and whose `Date_Made` is a bulk-seed placeholder (§A audit). **A stated convention plus a verification against the wrong artifact reads exactly like compliance.** ⇒ **Mechanical rule, forward: as-made comes from the earliest `STATUS.md` blob carrying the row's confidence cell — never from the TSV for any row with `Date_Made` ≤ 2026-03-04, and never from a *later* commit of the TSV for any row at all.** Partner: `[[finding_instrument_reports_clean_against_the_wrong_reference]]`, `[[finding_adoption_is_not_validation]]`.
