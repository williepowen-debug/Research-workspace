## Undercount caveat (don't over-read magnitude)

WARN captures only US sites at/above threshold. Non-US, remote, and sub-threshold cuts are invisible (e.g. Cloudflare's stated 20% reduction >> its 224 WARN; MSFT's non-US studios Arkane FR / Ninja Theory UK / Compulsion Montreal never appear). So the ~7,725 UNDER-states the true white-collar cut; but it's also **CA/WA-concentrated high-comp workers with severance runways** → national claims impact is modest and lagged.
