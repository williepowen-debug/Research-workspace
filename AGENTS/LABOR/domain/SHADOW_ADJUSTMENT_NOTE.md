# Shadow Claims Adjustment — Increase to +65K

**Date:** 2026-04-02 | **Prior:** +55K | **New:** +65K

## Rationale

The shadow adjustment captures workers who lose employment but do not appear in initial claims due to DHS suppression (Mullin-era policy), immigration enforcement chilling effects, gig/contractor misclassification, and state-level processing bottlenecks.

### Evidence for Increase

**1. 202K print (wk ending Mar 28) — FL Wave 1 invisible.**
Florida fired Wave 1 UI exhaustees on Mar 24. The wk ending Mar 28 was the FIRST data window to capture these separations. Instead, claims FELL to 202K — a 2-year low. Either FL is not processing these claims (state bottleneck) or DHS suppression is actively filtering them. Either way, the gap between reality and reported data widened.

**2. Oracle 10-30K layoffs — zero claims signal.**
Oracle executed its largest restructuring ever (~$2.1B charge) in March. 10K-30K workers globally, significant US footprint. Severance packages delay claims filing, but the scale means thousands should be entering the pipeline. Claims show nothing.

**3. Unilever global hiring freeze — demand destruction not measured.**
Hiring freezes don't generate claims but destroy the denominator — fewer jobs available for the already-displaced. Shadow adjustment must account for this demand-side suppression.

**4. Tech layoffs: 85K+ YTD (TrueUp tracker).**
TrueUp reports 85,156 workers across 208 events — significantly higher than SkillSyncer's 51,686/102. Many tech workers on severance packages (3-6 months typical) = delayed claims filing. The severance pipeline is building toward a Q3 cliff.

**5. WA documented-worker detentions (NEW).**
ICE now detaining workers WITH legal documentation. WA arrests <100/mo → 400+/mo. This expands the chilling effect beyond undocumented workers — H-2A holders and green card holders now avoiding systems including UI filing.

### Why +65K, not +70K

+70K is defensible but risks overfitting to a single anomalous week. The FL Wave 1 invisibility could reflect 1-2 week state processing lag rather than permanent suppression. If the Apr 10 print (wk ending Apr 4) also shows no FL spike, upgrade to +70K.

**Decision rule:** If Apr 10 claims < 215K → upgrade to +70K (FL Wave 1 confirmed suppressed, not lagged).

This is above the 250K level that triggers CARL cross-agent alerts and well into recession-consistent territory.
