# Staffing Pre-Signal: Deep Dive Findings
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research + follow-up verification, Feb 28 2026

## FINDING 1: Staffing Is Bottoming (Counter-Thesis Signal)
- RHI and KFRC both returned to positive sequential growth after 12 consecutive quarters of YoY decline
- MAN US revenue nearly flat (-1.5% YoY)
- Historical pattern: when RHI flips to sequential growth, unemployment plateaus within 3-4 months
- **Implication:** If this holds, unemployment peaks ~Q2 2026 and stabilizes. Weakens LABOR → CARL chain.

## FINDING 2: "Bottoming" Is Misleading — It's a Freeze
- Low quits + low hiring = "suspended animation," not recovery
- Workers afraid to leave (quits below pre-COVID). Employers afraid to hire.
- Claims at 206K look healthy but are DECOUPLED from hiring — no longer signals strength
- **Implication:** The surface looks stable because nothing is moving. Vulnerable to sudden break if shocked.

## FINDING 3: Volume Collapsing While Price Holds
- KFRC bill rate stable at $90/hr for 3 years — but volume down significantly
- RHI Talent Solutions (staffing) down 9% YoY vs Protiviti (consulting) down only 6%
- Demand for specialized labor (cyber, cloud) stable. Generalist/middle-market temp demand still declining.
- **Implication:** Top of labor market is fine. Middle and bottom are where stress lives. K-shaped, not V-shaped.

## FINDING 4: DOGE Is an Unpriced Shock
- 312K-327K federal positions eliminated in the past year
- Financial activities down 49K from May 2025 peak
- Government contractor cascades not yet captured in staffing firm revenues
- **Implication:** A demand shock that won't show in RHI/KFRC numbers because those firms don't serve government. Separate transmission path.

## FINDING 5: Consumer Credit Exhaustion Confirming
- Credit card balance growth: 2.3% projected for 2026 — lowest non-pandemic rate since 2013
- 90+ day delinquencies inching up, projected to worsen at 4.5% unemployment
- CFPB compliance adding $160-253/borrower in costs
- **Implication:** Consumer is tapped out. Spending resilience is borrowed time.

## FINDING 6: Florida = Live Stress Test
- Florida #2 nationwide in foreclosure pressure (Feb 22, 2026)
- BayFirst National Bank: cut 17% of workforce, shuttered SBA lending platform
- FPI Management (6th largest US property manager): 100+ cuts, pivoting to AI
- Saks, Naples Grande, Legoland all filing WARN notices
- 50% tariffs on steel/aluminum already inflating FL construction costs
- **Implication:** CRE → regional bank → local employment chain is active in Florida RIGHT NOW.

## FINDING 7: Proposed WARN Act Overhaul Could Degrade the Signal
- H.R. 5761 (Fair Warning Act) would lower thresholds and extend notice to 90 days
- If passed, firms may delay or restructure how they report layoffs
- Could lag the WARN signal in 2026+ data
- **Implication:** Monitor legislative progress. If passed, our Texas API data gets noisier.

## KEY TENSION FOR LABOR
The staffing canaries say private sector employment is stabilizing. This is probably true for now. But our thesis transmission was never purely about private sector layoffs:
- Hidden CRE → bank stress → credit tightening (REGINALD path)
- DOGE → government workforce destruction (unpriced, separate path)  
- Consumer exhaustion → spending pullback (CARL path)

**Staffing bottom can be real AND thesis still plays out.** Watch for re-acceleration of staffing declines in Q1 2026 earnings (April). If sequential growth reverses → false bottom confirmed. If it holds → transmission path shifts away from labor as the lead domino.
