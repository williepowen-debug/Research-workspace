# Insider Selling Before Layoff Announcements (2024-2026)
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 28 2026

## Core Finding
C-suite executives at companies about to announce major layoffs systematically extracted liquidity in the 3-6 months prior. The behavior is CLUSTERED (multiple execs selling in same 30-day window) and statistically anomalous vs peers who weren't laying off.

## The Numbers
- Tech sector Sell-to-Buy ratio during peak layoff window (Dec 2025 - Jan 2026): **14.08x** ($14 sold for every $1 bought)
- Control group (Alphabet/Meta, growing headcount): **2.5-3.0x**
- Broader market baseline: **1.17-1.21x**
- **The layoff cohort was selling at 5-6x the rate of peers in the same industry**

## Case Studies

### Amazon — Regulatory Arbitrage Perfected
- **Aug 2025:** CEO Jassy ($4.4M), SVP Zapolsky ($4.6M), AWS CEO Garman ($3.9M), CFO Olsavsky ($4.0M) — all sold in same window, 2 months before Oct layoffs
- **Nov 10-11, 2025:** Four C-suite execs adopted new 10b5-1 plans within 48 hours (weeks after Oct layoffs, while planning Jan 2026 layoffs)
- **90-day cooling-off expires Feb 8-9.** Jan 28 layoff announcement. Feb 12-24: all plans execute. $12.7M extracted.
- **100% of sales were via 10b5-1 plans** — legally shielded

### Microsoft — AI Hype as Cover
- **Sep 2025:** CEO Nadella sold 149,205 shares for **$75.3M** while stock was at $4T market cap on AI euphoria
- Plan adopted March 2025 — precisely in the pre-WARN corridor before May/July layoffs
- Brad Smith: $19.9M. Hogan: $9.75M. Althoff: $7.3M. Coleman: $6M.
- **Hundreds of millions** extracted collectively during peak retail AI enthusiasm

### UPS — Most Suspicious
- Chief Legal Officer Norman Brothers converted 25,014 shares **24 hours BEFORE** the 30K layoff announcement
- Liquidated entire position the day AFTER announcement for $2.66M
- NOT via 10b5-1 plan — discretionary open-market sale
- A compliance officer timing trades around his own company's layoff announcement

### Intel — The Counter-Example
- Former CEO Gelsinger was a consistent BUYER ($20-43/share)
- No clustered selling despite massive layoffs
- Intel insiders reflected genuine distress + attempted confidence signaling, NOT extraction

## The Mansi Effect (Key Concept)
Academic research (Virginia Tech, 2025) found insiders time their sales to coincide with retail attention spikes. AI hype creates massive retail buying volume → insiders sell into that liquidity → retail absorbs shares without price impact. **Legally gray area** — not MNPI-based, just behavioral arbitrage.

## Regulatory Landscape
- SEC "Back to Basics" under Atkins — focused on 10b5-1 abuse, NOT novel theories
- Peizer conviction (June 2025): first criminal conviction for 10b5-1 plan manipulation — 42 months prison
- Result: execs now STRICTLY comply with 90-day cooling-off but still extract via precise timing
- Shadow trading theory effectively abandoned — cross-company trades won't be prosecuted
