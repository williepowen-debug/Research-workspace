# Wright: Education & Health Services Fraud Concern

*Source: Melody Wright "Grifter Nation" (Dec 31, 2025)*

---

## The Data Anomaly

| Category | Growth Since Feb 2020 |
|----------|----------------------|
| Total Nonfarm | +4.77% |
| **Education & Health Services** | **+12.41%** |
| Transportation & Warehousing | Peaked Feb 2025, now -1.16% |

> "If you exclude Education and Health Services, the average for the remaining categories combined is a **-.49% decline in payrolls YOY**."

---

## Wright's Hypothesis

> "What if many of the jobs that we think exist out there rolling up under Education and Health Services are actually fraudulent? What does that mean for the state of our economy?"

> "That would mean that the category that was one of the only categories to see a positive gain year-over-year (YOY), and the only category to see a gain of more than 1%, might not represent what we think."

---

## Supporting Evidence

**Minnesota Medicaid Fraud:**
- $18B potential fraud uncovered
- Not limited to Minnesota — Wright has "seen evidence of this fraud in my research and travels all over the country in both blue and red states"

**American Rescue Plan:**
- $350B sent to state, local, tribal, territorial governments
- "A good deal... funded jobs that rolled up into this category"
- "Public-private partnerships where the government gives money to an organization to provide some type of social service... are breeding grounds for at best ineffective ministrations or at worst fraudulent activities"

---

## Implication

If Ed & Health job gains are overstated due to fraud/waste:
- **True labor market is negative YoY** (-0.49% ex-EdHealth)
- GDP resilience is illusory
- When fraud is exposed, job losses accelerate

---

## AI Masking Reality

> "I believe **AI mania is being used to mask what is truly happening in the labor market**."

**Aligns with:**
- Burry: AI productivity = 1 bp
- Wright (Jan 9): AI layoff narrative = IR cover
- Real driver = demand weakness + offshoring

## Labor Arbitrage Thesis

Wright cites Adam Butler's article "The Trade Deficit is a Profit Margin":

> "The Current Account Deficit is not the root cause of our malaise. It is the accounting identity of a business model that **views the U.S. consumer as a revenue source and the U.S. worker as a cost liability to be arbitraged**."

> "Until companies are incentivized to value revenue and cashflow over total shareholder return (TSR), building factories in the United States is not going to fix our problems."
