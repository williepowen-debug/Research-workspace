# Analyst Revision Cycle After Layoff Announcements
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 28 2026

## The Mechanical Cycle (Highly Predictable)

### Phase 1: Days 1-30 — The Sugar High
- **75-85% of analysts RAISE NTM EPS estimates** within 30 days
- Purely mechanical: subtract salary/SBC/overhead from OpEx lines
- Non-GAAP excludes severance charges → savings flow straight to bottom line
- Consensus: "fiscal discipline," "margin expansion," "enhanced FCF"

### Phase 2: Day ~90 — First Earnings = Validation
- Company posts EPS beat (severance excluded, payroll savings immediate)
- "Efficiency" narrative holds, analysts reiterate Buy
- This is peak confidence and likely peak stock price

### Phase 3: Days 120-150 — The Reality Check
- Second post-layoff earnings call
- Revenue guidance softens (fewer sales reps, lower morale, fewer engineers shipping)
- **55-65% of analysts who initially raised estimates are forced to CUT within 2-3 quarters**
- Management admits top-line decelerating → analysts abandon margin expansion thesis
- Synchronized downward revisions begin

## The Scissor Effect (Near-Term vs Outer-Year)
| Timeframe | Direction | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Months 1-12 | EPS/margin UP ↑ | Cost cuts flow immediately to bottom line |
| Months 13-36 | Revenue/EPS DOWN ↓ | Can't shrink your way to growth. Innovation capacity impaired. |

Analysts reward near-term profitability but penalize terminal growth rate and outer-year revenue CAGR. The "jaws" between near-term and outer-year estimates widen after layoffs.

## AI vs Distress: Completely Different Treatment

### "AI Reallocation" (Meta, MSFT, Salesforce, Block)
- Analysts treat as **permanent structural margin upgrade**
- Sustain upward EPS revisions WITHOUT cutting outer-year revenue
- Buy the narrative that AI replaces lost human output
- Multiple expansion, not compression

### "Distress" (Intel, UPS, Peloton, Cisco)
- Analysts treat as **reactive capitulation to secular decline**
- Immediately slash outer-year revenue AND EPS
- Multiple compression
- "Shrinking to survive" framing

**The distinction is narrative, not math.** Same 10% headcount cut gets opposite treatment depending on whether management says "AI" or "macro headwinds."

## Key Timing: 90-120 Days Is the Window
- Efficiency consensus holds for exactly 1-1.5 earnings cycles
- The SECOND post-layoff earnings call is when it breaks
- This is why the event study shows pops fading by Day 90 — it maps perfectly to the analyst revision cycle

## Application for LABOR
1. **The 90-120 day window is tradeable.** After a layoff pop, the consensus holds through the first earnings beat, then cracks at the second. For our positions, this means timing puts to expire AFTER the second post-layoff earnings call.
2. **Watch for the Scissor Effect on bank watchlist names.** If WAL/OZK announce cuts and analysts raise near-term EPS but cut outer-year, that's the signal the market is starting to price in structural decline.
3. **The AI narrative shield will break eventually.** Right now "AI reallocation" gets a free pass from analysts. When AI capex doesn't translate to revenue (the "show me" moment), this cohort gets the distress treatment retroactively.
4. **55-65% reversal rate is the key number.** More than half of initial upgrades get walked back. The layoff pop is literally borrowed from future downgrades.
