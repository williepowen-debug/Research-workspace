# Job Posting Withdrawals as Leading Indicator
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 28 2026

## Core Finding
Job posting withdrawals lead layoff announcements by **4-12 weeks** (1-3 months). The sequence is consistent: hiring freeze → posting withdrawal → layoff announcement.

## The Mechanism
- Layoffs require weeks of internal prep (legal, severance, WARN compliance)
- First action: pull open requisitions from ATS/job boards to stop accidental new hires
- Sudden drop in non-essential postings = earliest external signal of coming cuts
- For structural reorgs, lag can stretch to 6 months (waiting for natural attrition first)

## Confirmed Case Studies
| Company | Posting Drop | Layoff Announcement | Lag |
|---------|-------------|--------------------|----|
| Meta | Postings -80% (Sep 2022) | 11K layoffs (Nov 2022) | 8-10 weeks |
| Amazon | Hiring paused (Nov 2022) | 18K layoffs (Jan 2023) | 2-3 months |
| Microsoft | Slowed hiring Q3 2022 | 10K layoffs (Jan 2023) | ~3 months |

Pattern held through 2024-2025 cuts as well — companies routinely pulled legacy roles weeks before public announcements.

## Macro Confirmation
- Tech job postings peaked April 2022, crashed **>60%** before heaviest layoff wave (Q1 2023)
- Indeed JPI tracks daily — shows postings fall well before unemployment rises
- Lightcast data used by the Fed to track real-time labor demand
- **Quant funds actively trade this:** Short companies with sudden posting withdrawals, long sustained hirers. Not novel edge but validated mechanism.
