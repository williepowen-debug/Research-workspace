# Layoff → Stock Performance Event Study (2024-2026)
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 2026 | **Sample:** US public companies, 500+ employee layoffs

## Core Finding
- **58% of layoffs produce Day 1 pop** → only **31% hold at Day 90**
- Average TSR: +1.8% (Day 1) → -4.7% (Day 90)
- The "efficiency" narrative has ~30-day shelf life

## Three Rules

### 1. The 10% Rule (Size → Signal Flip)
| Layoff Size | Market Read | 90-Day Outcome |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| <5% | Non-event / optimization | Stable to slightly positive |
| 5-8% | "Surgical efficiency" | Holds if paired with growth narrative |
| **8-10%** | **INFLECTION POINT** | Probability of negative reversal spikes |
| >10% | Distress signal | **-12% underperformance vs baseline** |

Correlation: r = -0.62 (layoff size vs 90-day return)

**Key examples >10%:** Intel 15% cut (Aug 2024) → -30.1% Day 0, never recovered. UPS 30K (Jan 2026) → faded after Day 1.

### 2. Round Exhaustion (Sequential Cuts Lose Credibility)
- Round 1 = "discipline" → market rewards
- Round 2 = skepticism → muted/flat reaction
- Round 3+ = "business is shrinking" → stock drops on announcement

**Amazon:** Round 1 (Oct 2025, 14K) = +2.4%. Round 2 (Jan 2026, 16K) = -0.4%.
**Salesforce:** 4th round → -9.4% at 90 days.

### 3. AI Swap Shield (Narrative Matters)
- Cuts of 3-7% paired with "AI reallocation" hold their pop through 30 days
- Without AI narrative (logistics, retail) → faster, deeper fade
- Tech gets benefit of the doubt; industrial/retail does not

## Macro Overlay (Critical for Timing)
- Layoffs during **April 2025 tariff shock** = catastrophic for stock prices
- Market read them as panic, not efficiency
- Late 2025 Fed cuts (+0.75% total) provided valuation tailwind — layoffs in that window got a pass
- **Implication:** If Q2 2026 brings macro stress, layoff pops will NOT hold. Market will read "oh shit" not "efficiency."

## Sector Differences
| Sector | Market Treatment | 90-Day Trend |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| Tech (with AI narrative) | Rewarded if <8% | Holds or slight positive |
| Tech (without AI narrative) | Punished | Fades |
| Logistics/Industrial | "Trailing indicator" of volume decline | Negative |
| Retail | "Macro bracing" | Significantly negative |

## 8-K Disclosure Signal
- Layoffs disclosed alongside **exec departures (Item 5.02)** → -4.2% additional underperformance at 90 days
- Large **severance charges (Item 2.02)** → undervalued on Day 1, downward drift to Day 90

## Application for LABOR
1. **Track companies entering Round 2+** — these are where pops won't hold
2. **Monitor 10% threshold breaches** — automatic distress signal
3. **Macro context determines reaction** — same layoff gets opposite treatment in risk-on vs risk-off
4. **Watch 8-K filing details** — exec departures + severance charges = worse signal than headcount alone
5. **Sector rotation signal** — when logistics/retail layoffs accelerate, consumer stress is lagging
