# Geographic Layoff Concentration → Local Economy Transmission
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 28 2026

## Core Finding
The Moretti local multiplier (1 tech job = 4.3-5 service jobs) is DEGRADED in 2024-2026. Actual realized ratio: **2.5-3.0x** due to remote work dispersion, K-shaped spending resilience, and AI automation of service sector.

| **Austin** | 3.5% | **-0.2%** | BEST — Samsung/Tesla manufacturing + infrastructure projects |

## Why the Multiplier Shrank
1. **Remote work dispersion** — "Seattle layoffs" include people living in suburbs of other states. Spending impact is scattered, not concentrated downtown.
2. **K-shaped resilience** — High-income tech workers have severance + stock gains. No immediate spending cliff for luxury services.
3. **AI replacing service jobs too** — The same tech driving layoffs is being deployed to maintain service operations with fewer staff.

## Key Insight: Data Center Jobs ≠ Tech Worker Jobs
A data center investment doesn't generate the same local multiplier as 10,000 software engineers. Engineers eat at restaurants, use dry cleaners, buy houses. A data center needs 50 technicians. The AI capex pivot is a LOCAL ECONOMIC downgrade even if it's a corporate efficiency upgrade.

## Application for LABOR
1. **Seattle is the canary metro** — highest excess unemployment, most dependent on AMZN/MSFT
2. **The multiplier degradation means layoff impact is SLOWER but WIDER** — distributed across more geographies at lower intensity
3. **Don't expect concentrated local credit events from tech layoffs** — the transmission is diffuse now. Instead watch aggregate national metrics (claims, delinquency rates)
4. **Austin's resilience = manufacturing diversification works** — relevant for thesis: metros without diversification (Seattle, and potentially bank-heavy metros) are more vulnerable
