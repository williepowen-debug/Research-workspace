# Layoffs Paired with Buybacks (2020-2026)
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 28 2026

## Core Finding
~20-30% of large-cap companies (>$10B) announcing 500+ layoffs also authorized or expanded buybacks within 90 days. Rate is higher within 6 months. Among S&P 500, ~387 of 500 companies were actively buying back shares in Q3 2025, so many layoff companies are simultaneously repurchasing without needing a new authorization.

## The Salesforce $50B Number
Salesforce authorized **$50B** in buybacks on Feb 26, 2026 — the same day as ongoing cuts. For context: if 4,000 employees averaged $150K/year, annual salary savings ≈ $600M. The buyback authorization is **83x the annual salary savings.** This isn't "reinvesting savings" — it's a completely separate capital allocation decision dressed up alongside the efficiency narrative.

## Stock Performance: Paired vs Unpaired
No systematic study exists, but case evidence shows paired companies outperform at +30 days because the buyback provides a price floor and signals management confidence. By +90 days, the buyback effect fades and fundamentals reassert — same fade pattern as unpaired layoffs.

## Why It Matters
1. **The "efficiency" narrative is financial engineering.** Layoffs cut costs, buybacks shrink share count — both inflate EPS without growing the business.
2. **Buyback provides cover for insider selling.** Company buys shares on open market while insiders sell via 10b5-1 plans. Net effect: wealth transfer from company treasury to executives.
3. **387 of 500 S&P companies are always buying back** — so the "pairing" is often coincidental timing rather than coordinated strategy. The notable cases are same-day announcements (Freshworks, Salesforce) where the optics are deliberate.

## The "Downsize-and-Distribute" Framework
Academic term coined by Lazonick (Brookings): companies systematically lay off workers while distributing cash to shareholders. Stock-based exec comp creates the incentive — layoffs boost EPS, buybacks boost EPS further, both trigger bonus thresholds.

**SEC Commissioner Jackson (2019):** Insiders are **2x more likely to sell personal shares within 8 days of a buyback announcement.** 38% of firms had no insider trading in the 30 days prior to a buyback, then selling surged afterward. Buyback → insider sells into the price support.

**Key stat:** In 2025, US companies announced 1.2M job cuts (highest since pandemic) while authorizing $1.36T in buybacks. Two-thirds of S&P 500 actively repurchasing in any given quarter.

## Application for LABOR
1. **Track same-day or same-week layoff+buyback announcements** — these are the deliberate pairings that signal "shrinking the denominator"
2. **Compare buyback $ to estimated salary savings** — when buyback is 10x+ the savings, the efficiency story is performative
3. **The 2x insider selling surge after buyback announcements** connects directly to the insider selling research — buyback provides the price floor insiders sell into
4. **For bank watchlist:** Regional banks doing buybacks while under CRE stress = the OZK pattern ($460K of $200M auth = 0.2%). Absence of buyback execution despite authorization is bearish signal.
5. **SEC tried to require daily buyback disclosure in 2023 — 5th Circuit vacated it.** This data gap is deliberate.
