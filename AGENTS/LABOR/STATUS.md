## THESIS

**"Hotel California" labor market: easy to keep your job, hard to find a new one.**

The U.S. labor market has entered a structural break masked by surface-level stability. Claims (209K) and U-3 (4.4%) appear healthy, but beneath the surface:
- **~580K announced layoffs** (2024-2026): 307K federal (DOGE) + 275K private
- **Temp employment RED** (-12% YoY, 400K lost, cycle bottom, re-accelerating)
- **Challenger Q4 2025**: Highest since 2008 (+71% YoY), lowest hiring since 2010
- **Google "Severance Package"**: ALL-TIME HIGH (above GFC + pandemic)

This is not synchronized recession — it's a **"barbell" economy**:
1. **Large corps cutting aggressively** (tech, logistics, auto, services)
2. **Manufacturing in "jobless expansion"** (output up, jobs down 28 consecutive months)
3. **Small business trying to hire but supply-constrained** (NFIB 33% hard to fill)

**WARN pipeline has locked in pain through H1 2026.** Q4 filings become Q1-Q2 actual layoffs.

**Employment is THE transmission trigger.** Per REGINALD: claims >300K or U-3 >5.0% → all ORANGE banks escalate simultaneously.
