## THESIS

**"Hotel California" labor market: easy to keep your job, hard to find a new one.**

The U.S. labor market has entered a structural break masked by surface-level stability:
- **~580K announced layoffs** (2024-2026): 307K federal (DOGE) + 275K private
- **Temp employment RED** (-12% YoY, 400K lost, re-accelerating)
- **Challenger Q4 2025**: Highest since 2008 (+71% YoY), lowest hiring since 2010
- **Google "Severance Package"**: ALL-TIME HIGH (above GFC + pandemic)
- **2025 job growth: +181K total** (revised from +584K) — **1M phantom jobs exposed**

**"Barbell" economy:**
1. Large corps cutting aggressively (tech, logistics, auto, services)
2. Manufacturing in "jobless expansion" (output up, jobs down 28 months)
3. Small business supply-constrained (NFIB 33% hard to fill) — BUT smallest firms (1-9 emp) now BREAKING

**Employment is THE transmission trigger.** Per REGINALD: claims >300K or U-3 >5.0% → all ORANGE banks escalate simultaneously.
