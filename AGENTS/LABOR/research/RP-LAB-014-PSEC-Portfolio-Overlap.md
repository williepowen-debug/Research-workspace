## PSEC as Leading Indicator

### Why PSEC Leads Aggregate Data

1. **Size Class Blind Spot:**  
   - BLS/Challenger data dominated by large corps (>1,000 employees)  
   - PSEC portfolio = 50-500 employee range (46% of US employment, invisible in headlines)

2. **Private Company Early Stress:**  
   - Public companies delay layoffs (optics, severance accruals)  
   - Private PE-backed firms cut faster (no quarterly earnings calls, board pressure for cash preservation)

3. **Financial Stress → Employment Lag:**  
   - PIK election = **Quarter 0** (cash stress)  
   - Layoffs begin = **Quarter +1** (cost reduction)  
   - Aggregate data captures = **Quarter +2** (statistical lag)

### PSEC Stress Signals to Watch

| **Signal** | **Threshold** | **Interpretation** | **LABOR Transmission Lag** |
|------------|---------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
