## Critical Findings

### 1. HELOC Crisis Signal

**HELOC 90+ transition rate: +121% YoY (0.56% → 1.24%)**

**Interpretation:**  
- Homeowners tapping HELOCs for liquidity (balance +$38B YoY)  
- Now unable to service even revolving credit  
- **This is a "last resort" failure** - people who exhausted savings, maxed credit cards, then tapped home equity, now defaulting

**Employment Link:**  
- HELOC stress = formerly middle-class households (homeowners) under acute pressure  
- Likely correlation: **UI exhaustion + inability to find new job** = HELOC default  
- Timeline: Job loss (Q3 2025) → UI runs out (Q4 2025) → HELOC default (Q4 2025-Q1 2026)

**Geographic Overlap:**  
- Report notes delinquencies concentrated in **"areas with declining home prices"**  
- LABOR's WARN hotspots: **FL (+76% YoY), CA (+16%)**  
- Overlap confirmed: FL home prices declining + WARN spike + HELOC stress

**Forward Signal:**  
- HELOC default → mortgage default (6-12 month lag)  
- Foreclosure wave Q2-Q3 2026 in FL/CA

### 2. Mortgage Delinquency Acceleration

**Mortgage 90+ transition: +27% YoY (1.09% → 1.38%)**

**NY Fed Commentary:**  
> "Delinquency rates for mortgages are near historically normal levels, but the **deterioration is concentrated in lower-income areas and in areas with declining home prices**."

**Translation:**  
- Aggregate data masks geographic concentration  
- Lower-income = layoff-sensitive cohorts  
- Declining home price areas = **FL sunbelt, CA inland** (LABOR WARN hotspots)

**Employment→Mortgage Transmission Confirmed:**

```
Q3 2025 WARN Filings (FL +76%, CA +16%)
         ↓
Q4 2025 Layoffs Materialize
         ↓
Q4 2025 UI Claims Elevated (continuing claims 1.86M)
         ↓
Q4 2025 Mortgage Delinquencies Rise (+27%)
         ↓
Q1-Q2 2026 Foreclosures Accelerate (predicted)
```

**Lag Time:** 60-90 days (2-3 missed payments to 90+ DPD)

### 3. Credit Card / Auto "Stable But Elevated"

**Credit Card:** 7.13% (down -0.05pp but still near cycle highs)  
**Auto:** 2.95% (flat, elevated vs pre-pandemic 2.3%)

**Interpretation:**  
- **Not accelerating** = cohort already selected out (defaults happened 2023-2024)  
- **High plateau** = new normals, survivors paying  
- **Risk:** If employment deteriorates further (claims >250K), this cohort stress resumes

**Auto Loan Note:**  
- Flat despite Carvana/subprime stress (OTTO watch)  
- Indicates: Prime auto borrowers still paying; subprime already defaulted  
- Forward risk: Prime cohort starts slipping if layoffs accelerate

### 4. Student Loan Distortion

**16.19% transition rate (up from 0.70%)**

**Context:**  
- Distorted by pandemic forbearance ending (5-year pause)  
- ~1M borrowers transferred to Default Resolution Group  
- **Not a labor market signal** - administrative catch-up

**Ignore for LABOR analysis.**
