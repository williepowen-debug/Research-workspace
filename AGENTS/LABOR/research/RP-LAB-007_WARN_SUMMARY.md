# RP-LAB-007: WARN Act Analysis Q4 2025 / January 2026

## Key Findings

### National Totals
- Q4 2025: 259,948 cuts (+71% YoY)
- Full Year 2025: 1,206,374 cuts (+58% YoY)
- October 2025: 153,000 cuts - highest October since 2003
- January 2026: ~600,000 global cuts (+500% vs Jan 2025)

### State-by-State (through Oct 2025)
| State | Losses | YoY Change | Key Drivers |
|-------|--------|------------|-------------|
| California | 158,734 | +16% | Tech/AI pivot (14% of US total) |
| New York | 81,701 | +20% | Finance, telecom (Verizon 13K, Citi) |
| Texas | 46,352 | -31% | Industrial shocks but more resilient |
| Florida | 22,771 | +76% | Logistics, hospitality (Spirit, Kroger) |
| Illinois | 20,678 | +3% | Manufacturing, insurance |

### Largest Individual Filings
1. Tyson Foods (TX): 1,761
2. Amazon (CA): 1,001-2,500 (Jan-April 2026)
3. Aramark (FL): 949

5. FedEx Supply Chain (TX): 856

### The 60-Day Pipeline
Q4 2025 filings = Q1-Q2 2026 actual layoffs:
- Feb 2026: Kroger, FedEx effective
- March-April 2026: Amazon, Pinterest, Meta effective
- This 'locks in' difficult labor market through H1 2026

### Key Correlations
- Florida: 76% WARN surge + 57% foreclosure increase
- Employment → Housing transmission active
- Cass Freight Index: -7.5% YoY (validates logistics thesis)

### AI as Driver
- 54,836 cuts cited AI in 2025
- NY now requires AI disclosure on WARN filings
- Tech sector 'swapping' human labor for AI

Source: State WARN databases, Challenger, ATTOM
