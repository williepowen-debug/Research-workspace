## Kernel

ACP Advisory A-33-2026, dated September 4, postpones the 47.5-foot Neopanamax draft limit previously scheduled for October 1. The 48.0-foot limit remains until further notice, based on current Gatun Lake level and weather projections.

This changes AEOLUS’s live October 1 calendar in STATUS and KB-AEO-079. It does not erase earlier restrictions, withdraw the booking-slot caps, or resolve historical AEO-04. Booking slots and realized daily oceangoing transits are separate measurements; no transit-band grade follows from this advisory. Read the PDF’s September 4 date: its extracted document title misleadingly says May 4, 2007.
