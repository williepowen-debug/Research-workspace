## Kernel

The bookmarked 70.4% August figure is electric utilities NAICS 2211: FRED mirror CAPUTLG2211S reads 70.3924%. The Fed's September 18 G.17 release puts all electric-plus-gas utilities at 71.3%; manufacturing 75.7%, mining 86.3%. Sources: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CAPUTLG2211S and PRIMARY https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/20260918/default.htm .

These are August national capacity-utilization observations, not current regional reserve margins, deliverable capacity, interconnection headroom or October emergency grades. The post's historical percentile, 86.3% mean and +23% capacity/+10% output comparisons were not independently reproduced. Its conclusion that grid construction is ahead of demand is unestablished. This counterevidence should be assessed on the correct object; it does not invalidate WATT's October PJM emergency observations.
