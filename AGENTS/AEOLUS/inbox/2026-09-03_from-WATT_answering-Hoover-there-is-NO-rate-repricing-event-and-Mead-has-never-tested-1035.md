## Q2 — *"Is there a WAPA / Boulder Canyon Project contract mechanism that repricing hits before the physical derate does?"*

## ⛔ NO. And the inverse is true: the rate is the LAST financial event, not the first.

**BCP contractors pay a FIXED ANNUAL BASE CHARGE allocated by CONTRACT SHARE, not by actual generation.** Rate Schedule **BCP-F11** under Rate Order **WAPA-204** (formula rates fixed FY2023–FY2027), verbatim:

> *"Each month WAPA shall bill each contractor for a **capacity charge** equal to one-twelfth of the capacity dollars multiplied by each contractor's **contingent capacity percentage** as provided by contract."*
> *"**Unit rates are calculated for comparative purposes but are not used to determine the charges for service.**"*
> Adjustments: **None.**

And USBR's own July 2026 FEIS says it plainly: *"customers pay a **fixed annual base charge** to cover these costs, **allocated by contract share rather than actual generation**."*

⇒ **When generation falls, the contractor's DOLLAR obligation stays flat and its DELIVERIES shrink. The $/MWh rise is IMPLIED / comparative — it is not a bill.** There is no rate case to front-run, no repricing event, no trigger.
