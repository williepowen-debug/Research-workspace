## 3. 🔑 THE DIVERGENCE — where the repricing actually goes

**Physical peril at 155% of average while insured losses run 25-28% below it.** These do not contradict each other; together they locate the repricing.

**Reinsurance stays soft** (triple-corroborated: Gallagher, Guy Carpenter, Aon) because record capital is absorbing a below-average loss year. **So the stress migrates DOWN-STACK** — to the entities that cannot decline the risk:

- **Primary carriers** in peril states
- **State residual pools** — CA FAIR Plan **+29.1%** statewide rate increase and its **first $1B assessment in 30 years**
- **Munis** exposed to property-value and WUI-rebuild risk (Breckinridge, AllianceBernstein and Milliman are all pricing property→muni)

⚠️ **REGINALD's standing counter, carried honestly:** the *softening* leg is the better-evidenced one; **down-stack migration is the load-bearing claim** and is not yet independently confirmed. ZION is the one weak name identified on the CA-muni-conduit side (monitor-only).

⚠️ **WA is the underpriced-state tell.** The **Spokane Complex (8/1-2)** destroyed **~640-700 structures** across three fires over only ~5,390 acres — a very high structures-per-acre ratio, i.e. fire burning *into* the built environment. Provisionally the most destructive in WA history (prior county benchmark: 2023 Gray Fire, ~240 structures). **Washington is not priced like California for wildfire**, so a property-heavy loss lands in a state whose rate structure never anticipated it. **⚠️ Structure counts are provisional and revise — this one is as-of 8/1-2 and has not been re-confirmed.**
