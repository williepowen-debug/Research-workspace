# Helper B: who is exposed by name if the Lower Basin agreement is unsigned on 2027-01-01

Compiled 2026-11-19 (ET) from free public sources only. PRIMARY means a statute, agency, issuer, court filing or rating-agency release. SECONDARY means press or a blog. Lines marked **INFERRED** are my arithmetic or reading, not a source's statement.

## Headline (sourced answer, with one INFERRED step)

1. **The guideline text.** Guidelines §5.3.A.2 sets Arizona at 2.04 maf (−760 kaf), California at 3.96 maf (−440 kaf) and Nevada at 250 kaf (−50 kaf), "in accordance with a Lower Basin implementing agreement(s)". §5.3.A.3 reads: "If the Lower Division States have not fully executed the applicable or necessary implementing agreements, the Secretary shall determine the quantities and apportionment thereof as identified in Section 5.3.A.1, in accordance with applicable law." The 1.25 maf total (6.25 maf apportioned) stays the same. Only the split changes. [PRIMARY: 2027–2028 Operating Guidelines, Aug 2026, p.6–7]
2. **Interior's own model of "applicable law".** Interior modeled "applicable law (i.e., the priority system)" in FEIS Appendix C, Table C-3. It is computed at exactly a 1.5 maf shortage including Mexico, which is the 1.25 maf Lower Basin case. The result: **Arizona 1,161,018 af, all on Arizona 4th priority (CAP + on-river P4) · Nevada 88,982 af, all on Nevada 8th priority (SNWA & Big Bend) · California 0 af at every priority (PVID, Yuma Project Reservation Division, IID/CVWD/PVID, MWD) · Mexico 250,000 af.** [PRIMARY: FEIS App. C Table C-3, Jul 2026]
3. **INFERRED delta against the agreement.** Without the agreement, Arizona takes **+401 kaf** (1,161 vs 760), Nevada **+39 kaf** (89 vs 50) and California **−440 kaf** (0 vs 440). California is the only state better off with no agreement, on Interior's model.
4. **Caveat that changes the reading.** The ROD says the FEIS "assumptions … shall not preclude or predetermine such apportionments" (ROD §10.6). Arizona disputes the priority interpretation, and Nevada is suing over it. The Table C-3 numbers are Interior's modeling of the law, not a determination.
