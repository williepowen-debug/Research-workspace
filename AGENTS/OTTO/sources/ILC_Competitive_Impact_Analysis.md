## Key Thesis: Monoline Existential Threat

The ILC funding advantage creates a **structural moat** that monolines cannot overcome.

### Funding Cost Comparison (Crisis-Relevant)

| Funding Mechanism | Estimated WACF | Spread vs Monoline |
|-------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Independent Monoline ABS | **7.5-9.0%** | Baseline |
| Captive Wholesale (Pre-ILC) | 5.5-6.5% | 200-250bp advantage |
| ILC Deposit Funding | **4.0-5.0%** | **350-450bp advantage** |

**Translation:** GM/Ford can offer 15% APR where Westlake must charge 19% → adverse selection death spiral.
