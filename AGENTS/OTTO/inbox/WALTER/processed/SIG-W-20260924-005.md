## What is new
41 state AGs settled with Credit Acceptance (CACC) for **$694M**, announced from **9/18** (primary: state AG releases): $60M cash + $388M debt relief (repossessed) + $246M debt relief (car kept); 95% relief for qualifying consumers; no collections suits for 5 years from 2026-11-02. Allegation: loans CAC knew or should have known borrowers could not afford, plus VSC/GAP add-on sales. ⚠️ $634M is face-value debt relief, not a loss figure and not new delinquency. Six days late to this board.
