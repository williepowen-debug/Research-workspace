## PRE-DOCUMENTED SIGNAL TYPES

These signals are expected based on domain analysis. When observed, log to ML.tsv and assess for escalation.

### SIGNAL: Additional Fraud Discovery
**Trigger:** 4th subprime lender revealed with double-pledging or similar scheme
**Interpretation:** Pattern is systemic, not isolated
**Action:** URGENT to CARL, REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** 2007 subprime mortgage — started with few, then cascade

### SIGNAL: Major Bank Loss Announcement
**Trigger:** Bank announces >$500M loss from auto warehouse/fraud exposure
**Interpretation:** Losses larger than contained; provisioning cycle begins
**Action:** URGENT to REGINALD, EARNINGS
**Historical Precedent:** 2008 bank write-downs

### SIGNAL: Warehouse Line Freeze
**Trigger:** Multiple warehouse lenders announce suspension of new subprime commitments
**Interpretation:** Funding market seizing; origination will collapse
**Action:** URGENT to CARL, LIQUID
**Historical Precedent:** 2008 mortgage warehouse freeze

### SIGNAL: DOJ Industry-Wide Probe
**Trigger:** DOJ announces investigation expanding beyond named companies
**Interpretation:** Systemic fraud suspected; all lenders under scrutiny
**Action:** ELEVATED to ALL
**Historical Precedent:** 2010 robo-signing investigation

### SIGNAL: ABS Downgrade Wave
**Trigger:** 5+ subprime auto ABS deals downgraded in single month
**Interpretation:** Collateral performance deteriorating broadly
**Action:** ELEVATED to REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** 2007-2008 subprime MBS downgrades

### SIGNAL: Cooperating Witness Revelation
**Trigger:** Kollar/Seibold testimony reveals additional fraud or participants
**Interpretation:** Investigation expanding based on insider evidence
**Action:** ELEVATED to CARL, REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** Enron cooperators

### SIGNAL: Subprime Origination Collapse
**Trigger:** Subprime auto origination volume -30%+ YoY
**Interpretation:** Credit access severely impaired; consumer stress accelerating
**Action:** URGENT to CARL
**Historical Precedent:** 2008-2009 credit crunch
