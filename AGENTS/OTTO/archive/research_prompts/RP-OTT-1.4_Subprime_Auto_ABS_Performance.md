# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.4
## Subprime Auto ABS Performance Analysis

### Context

The subprime auto fraud cases raise questions about the broader performance of subprime auto asset-backed securities (ABS). If collateral quality was systematically misrepresented, ABS deal performance may deteriorate beyond normal expectations.

Auto delinquencies are already at 2009 levels (per CARL: 6.65% 60+ DPD, BREACHED). This prompt seeks to understand ABS-specific performance.

### Research Questions

1. **Current subprime auto ABS performance metrics:**
   - Delinquency rates (30+, 60+, 90+ days) by vintage (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024)
   - Cumulative net loss rates vs. original expectations
   - Recovery rates on repossessions
   - Prepayment speeds

2. **Performance by originator:**
   - Are deals from certain originators underperforming?
   - Any deals from Tricolor, First Brands, or related entities in ABS market?
   - Originator concentration in outstanding subprime auto ABS

3. **Rating agency actions:**
   - Recent downgrades of subprime auto ABS tranches
   - Negative watch placements
   - Rating agency commentary on sector outlook

4. **Spread and pricing trends:**
   - Subprime auto ABS spreads (AAA, BBB, BB tranches)
   - New issue pricing vs. 6 months ago
   - Secondary market liquidity

5. **Structural protections:**
   - Credit enhancement levels
   - Subordination
   - Excess spread
   - Are protections being tested?

### Deliverables

1. **Performance dashboard** — Key metrics by vintage with trends
2. **Originator risk table** — Performance rankings by originator
3. **Rating action log** — Recent downgrades/watches in subprime auto ABS
4. **Spread chart** — Current spreads vs. historical (if data available)
5. **Risk assessment** — Which vintages/originators are most at risk?

### Sources to Consider

- Fitch, Moody's, S&P ABS performance reports
- Intex or Bloomberg ABS data (if accessible)
- Auto ABS new issue prospectuses
- Kroll Bond Rating Agency reports
- Bank of America, JP Morgan securitization research

### Output Format

Data-driven report with tables and trend analysis. Identify any ABS deals or originators that warrant concern. Flag if a "downgrade wave" scenario is becoming more likely.
