# RP-OTT-2.1: Demographic Composition of Deep Subprime Auto

## Objective
Map the demographic composition of deep subprime auto borrowers (<500 FICO / no-FICO / thin-file) and identify geographic concentrations that overlap with immigrant populations.

## Research Questions

1. **Demographic Breakdown of Deep Subprime**
   - What % of <500 FICO auto borrowers are Hispanic/Latino?
   - What % are no-FICO or thin-file (credit invisible)?
   - How does this compare to prime auto?
   - Source: Experian/Equifax demographic overlays, CFPB reports on credit invisibles

2. **BHPH Dealer Geographic Concentration**
   - Where are Buy Here Pay Here dealers most concentrated?
   - Map overlap: High immigrant population + high BHPH density
   - Key corridors: TX Rio Grande Valley, CA Central Valley/Imperial, AZ Phoenix, FL agricultural belt
   - Source: State dealer license databases, NIADA (National Independent Auto Dealers Association), BHPH industry reports

3. **Tricolor's Customer Profile**
   - Tricolor explicitly marketed "no credit, no problem" to Hispanic immigrants
   - What was their geographic footprint? (CA, TX, AZ primarily)
   - What demographic data exists from bankruptcy filings?
   - Source: Tricolor bankruptcy docs, marketing materials, trustee reports

4. **Credit Invisible Population**
   - CFPB estimates 26M Americans are "credit invisible" (no credit file)
   - Additional 19M have "unscorable" thin files
   - What % of subprime auto originations go to this population?
   - How does this map to immigrant-heavy MSAs?
   - Source: CFPB "Credit Invisibles" reports, Urban Institute research

5. **Lender-Specific Demographic Exposure**
   - Which subprime lenders have highest concentration in immigrant corridors?
   - Tricolor (defunct), Flagship, Westlake, CPS — geographic origination data
   - Are any publicly traded lenders disclosing demographic risk?
   - Source: ABS prospectuses (geographic distribution), company filings, state licensing data

## Output Requested
- Demographic composition estimates for deep subprime
- Heat map data: BHPH concentration × immigrant population × subprime originations
- List of lenders with highest exposure to immigrant corridors
- Any available Tricolor customer demographic data from bankruptcy

## Cross-References
- MARCO: Immigration corridor geography, Latino employment data
- CARL: Credit invisible consumer stress
- REGINALD: Bank exposure to immigrant-corridor lenders

## Why This Matters
If immigrant populations are leaving, going underground, or facing employment collapse, loans to this demographic won't just default — they'll **abandon**. Recovery rates approach zero on skip loans. This could explain why subprime auto losses are exceeding model expectations.
