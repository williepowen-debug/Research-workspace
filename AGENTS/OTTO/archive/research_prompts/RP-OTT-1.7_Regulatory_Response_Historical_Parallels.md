# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.7
## Regulatory Response and Historical Parallels

### Context

The subprime auto fraud pattern (double-pledging, data manipulation) has historical parallels to the 2007-2008 subprime mortgage crisis and the 2010 robo-signing scandal. Understanding regulatory response and historical patterns can inform expectations for how this situation unfolds.

### Research Questions

**Part A: Current Regulatory Response**

1. **Federal regulatory actions:**
   - OCC guidance or examination focus changes
   - Federal Reserve statements on auto lending
   - CFPB enforcement or rulemaking
   - SEC actions on ABS disclosure

2. **State regulatory actions:**
   - State AG investigations or settlements
   - State banking regulator actions
   - Consumer protection lawsuits

3. **Industry self-regulation:**
   - Trade association responses
   - Warehouse lender collateral verification changes
   - Electronic chattel paper control improvements
   - Shared collateral registry proposals

**Part B: Historical Parallels**

4. **2007-2008 mortgage warehouse collapse:**
   - How did mortgage warehouse lines freeze?
   - Timeline from first failures to systemic freeze
   - Which lenders failed? Which survived?
   - Government intervention (if any)

5. **2010 robo-signing scandal:**
   - How did fraud discovery expand from first case to industry-wide?
   - Regulatory response timeline
   - Settlement structure
   - Long-term consequences

6. **Other relevant precedents:**
   - Subprime auto lender failures in 2017-2019 (Summit, Honor Finance)
   - Lessons learned (or not learned)

### Deliverables

1. **Regulatory action tracker** — Federal and state actions taken or announced
2. **Industry response summary** — Self-regulatory measures being implemented
3. **Historical comparison table** — 2007-2008 mortgage vs. 2025-2026 auto fraud
4. **Timeline projection** — Based on historical precedent, what happens next?
5. **Intervention probability** — Will regulators intervene? How?

### Sources to Consider

- OCC, Fed, CFPB press releases and guidance
- State AG press releases
- Congressional testimony or hearings
- Academic papers on 2008 crisis and robo-signing
- FCIC (Financial Crisis Inquiry Commission) report
- News archives (2007-2010 period)

### Output Format

Two-part report: (A) Current regulatory landscape and (B) Historical pattern matching with implications for expected timeline and outcomes.
