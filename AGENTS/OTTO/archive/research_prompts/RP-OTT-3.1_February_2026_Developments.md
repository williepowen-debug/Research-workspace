# Research Prompt: Subprime Auto Lending — February 2026 Developments

**Goal:** Comprehensive update on subprime auto lending fraud and bankruptcy cases since early February 2026.

---

## Background Context

The subprime auto lending sector has experienced several high-profile fraud cases and bankruptcies:

1. **Tricolor Holdings** — Chapter 7 bankruptcy (Sept 2025); executives charged with fraud including double-pledging schemes and data manipulation; ~$2B debt
2. **First Brands Group** — Bankruptcy with $9.3B debt; fraud allegations involving double-pledging in supply chain/inventory finance; examiner report due Feb 25, 2026
3. **PrimaLend Capital Partners** — Chapter 11 (Oct 2025); $286M debt; auction held Jan 23, 2026

---

## Research Questions

### 1. PrimaLend Resolution
- What was the outcome of the Jan 23, 2026 auction?
- Who acquired the assets and at what price?
- Did the creditor plan vote pass (deadline was Feb 9)?
- What recovery rate did creditors receive?
- Were any fraud allegations uncovered during the bankruptcy process?

- Has any preliminary information been released before the Feb 25 report?
- What is the status of Ford and GM bridge financing?

### 3. Flagship Credit Acceptance
- What is the current status of the InterVest acquisition?
- Have any forensic audit findings been released?
- Are mass layoffs continuing?
- Are there any signs of collateral discrepancies similar to Tricolor's $800M gap?

### 4. Fed Stress Test Scenarios (Feb 15, 2026)
- What unemployment and equity decline scenarios were published?
- Are there any specific auto lending stress scenarios included?
- How do these compare to prior years?

### 5. Other Subprime Auto Lender Developments
- Any new bankruptcy filings from subprime auto lenders?
- Any new fraud allegations or investigations disclosed?
- Any rating agency actions (S&P, Moody's) on subprime auto ABS?

## Requested Output Format

For each development found, provide:
1. **Date** of announcement/filing
