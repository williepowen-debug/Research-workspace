# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.2
## Warehouse Lender Exposure to Subprime Auto

### Context

JPMorgan Chase ($170M) and Fifth Third Bank ($200M) have disclosed losses from Tricolor Holdings. Jefferies and UBS have also been reported as exposed. The total industry exposure to subprime auto warehouse lending is unknown, but warehouse lenders are now reassessing all relationships.

### Research Questions

1. **Who provides warehouse lines to subprime auto lenders?**
   - Major money center banks (JPM, Citi, BofA, Wells)
   - Regional banks with specialty lending
   - Investment banks (Jefferies, UBS, Credit Suisse successor)
   - Alternative lenders / private credit
   - Captive finance companies

2. **What is the total exposure?**
   - Aggregate warehouse commitment to subprime auto sector
   - Utilization rates
   - Trend in commitments (growing, stable, shrinking)

3. **Which banks have concentrated subprime auto exposure?**
   - Regional banks with outsized positions
   - Banks where auto warehouse is material to earnings
   - Banks with multiple subprime relationships

4. **What are warehouse lenders doing in response to fraud discovery?**
   - Line terminations or reductions
   - Covenant tightening
   - Enhanced due diligence requirements
   - Collateral verification changes

5. **What is the structure of a typical warehouse facility?**
   - Advance rates
   - Eligible collateral criteria
   - Concentration limits
   - Mark-to-market provisions
   - Cross-default provisions

### Deliverables

1. **Warehouse lender matrix** — Bank name, known subprime auto relationships, estimated exposure, disclosed losses, risk tier
2. **Regional bank exposure list** — Focus on banks where subprime auto is >5% of loan book
3. **Industry response summary** — What warehouse lenders are doing post-Tricolor
4. **Structural vulnerabilities** — How warehouse facilities can be exploited

### Sources to Consider

- Bank 10-K/10-Q filings (search for "auto" + "warehouse")
- Credit agreements (SEC filings, court documents)
- Auto Finance News coverage
- Rating agency reports on warehouse lending
- LCG Advisors and other restructuring coverage
- Tricolor bankruptcy documents (creditor lists)

### Output Format

Structured report with exposure tables. Identify which banks have the most risk from additional subprime auto fraud discovery.
