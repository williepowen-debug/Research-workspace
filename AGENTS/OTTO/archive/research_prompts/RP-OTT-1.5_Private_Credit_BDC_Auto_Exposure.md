# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.5
## Private Credit and BDC Exposure to Subprime Auto

### Context

Private credit (including BDCs) has expanded into specialty finance, including auto lending. The subprime auto fraud cases may have exposure beyond traditional bank warehouse lines. This prompt investigates private credit involvement.

### Research Questions

1. **Which BDCs have auto finance exposure?**
   - Direct loans to subprime auto originators
   - Warehouse line participation
   - Residual interest in auto ABS
   - Consumer auto platform investments

2. **Which private credit funds are active in auto?**
   - Dedicated auto credit funds
   - Multi-strategy funds with auto exposure
   - Insurance company investments in auto ABS

3. **Exposure to known fraud cases:**
   - Did any private credit funds have exposure to Tricolor, First Brands, or PrimaLend?
   - Any disclosed losses or write-downs?

4. **Structure of private credit auto investments:**
   - First-lien warehouse vs. mezzanine
   - Residual/equity positions
   - Recovery expectations in fraud scenarios

5. **Contagion risk:**
   - If warehouse lines tighten, can private credit fill the gap?
   - Or will private credit also pull back?

### Deliverables

1. **BDC auto exposure table** — BDC name, known auto investments, estimated exposure, disclosed issues
2. **Private credit fund list** — Major private credit players in auto space
3. **Tricolor/First Brands/PrimaLend exposure check** — Any private credit involvement in known cases
4. **Risk assessment** — How does private credit change the transmission dynamics?

### Sources to Consider

- BDC 10-K/10-Q filings (schedule of investments)
- Private credit fund marketing materials
- Preqin, PitchBook for private credit auto funds
- Court filings (creditor lists in bankruptcies)
- Rating agency BDC reports

### Output Format

Structured report identifying private credit players in subprime auto and assessing their exposure to the fraud pattern. Cross-reference for potential linkage to BROCK (BDC monitoring agent).
