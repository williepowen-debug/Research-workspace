## PART 1: Ally Financial — Carvana Exposure & Systemic Role

### Context
Ally Financial is systemically important to subprime auto funding:
- $6B forward flow arrangement with Carvana (upsized Oct 2025)
- Major warehouse line provider across industry
- 2% workforce cut announced early 2026
- Q4 2025 earnings: Jan 21, 2026 (EPS $1.09, beat by 6.86%)

### Research Questions

**1.1 Carvana Forward Flow Mechanics**
```
Research the Ally-Carvana forward flow arrangement ($6B as of Oct 2025):

1. Structure: When Carvana sells loans to Ally under forward flow, who bears credit risk?
   - Does Ally own the loans outright?
   - Are there performance triggers (DQ thresholds, loss caps) that allow renegotiation?
   - What recourse does Ally have if loan quality deteriorates?

2. Volume: What percentage of Carvana's originations flow to Ally vs ABS vs other buyers?

3. Contagion path: If Gotham/Hindenburg thesis validates (Carvana earnings overstated via related-party manipulation):
   - Does Ally face direct credit losses?
   - Would they pull the forward flow?
   - What's the disclosure risk (Ally earnings impact)?

4. Historical: How has Ally described Carvana exposure in recent 10-Ks and earnings calls?

Sources: Ally 10-K, Carvana 10-K, earnings call transcripts, Auto Finance News
```

## PART 2: GM/Ford Industrial Bank Charters

### Context
FDIC approved industrial bank (ILC) charters for GM and Ford in January 2026. This allows their captive finance arms (GM Financial, Ford Credit) to accept FDIC-insured deposits — a structural shift that changes the competitive landscape.

### Research Questions

**2.1 FDIC Approval Details**
```
Research the GM and Ford ILC approvals (January 2026):

1. Approval specifics:
   - Exact approval date(s)
   - FDIC order numbers / docket references
   - Any conditions or restrictions imposed?
   - Capital requirements?
   - Lending restrictions (geographic, product type)?

2. Entity structure:
   - What are the new ILC entities called?
   - How do they relate to GM Financial / Ford Credit?
   - Regulatory supervision (FDIC vs OCC vs state)?

3. Deposit insurance:
   - What's the deposit insurance limit?
   - Any restrictions on deposit types (brokered, retail, etc.)?

Sources: FDIC press releases, approval orders, Snell & Wilmer analysis, banking trade press
```

**2.2 Operational Timeline & Strategy**
```
Research GM Financial and Ford Credit's ILC implementation plans:

1. Timeline:
   - When do they plan to start accepting deposits?
   - What's the ramp-up schedule (Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 targets)?
   - Current status of implementation?

2. Deposit strategy:
   - Online savings accounts? (Like Goldman Marcus)
   - Retail branch presence?
   - Brokered deposits?
   - Target deposit base size ($B)?

3. Strategic rationale:
   - What have GM/Ford executives said about why they pursued ILCs?
   - Explicit mention of reducing warehouse/ABS dependency?
   - Mention of serving subprime borrowers banks won't?
   - Cost of funds advantage quantified?

4. Competitive positioning:
   - Are they explicitly targeting segments monolines are exiting?
   - Pricing strategy vs bank/monoline competitors?

Sources: GM Financial investor presentations, Ford Credit investor presentations, earnings call transcripts, Auto Finance News
```

**2.3 Competitive Impact on Monolines**
```
Analyze how GM/Ford ILCs change the competitive landscape:

1. Funding cost advantage:
   - Current captive funding cost (ABS/warehouse rates)?
   - Projected deposit funding cost?
   - Spread advantage vs monolines?

2. Impact on monolines:
   - If captives have 100-200bps funding advantage, can monolines compete?
   - Which monolines are most vulnerable (Exeter, CPS, Westlake, etc.)?
   - Does this accelerate monoline consolidation/failure?

3. Credit box implications:
   - Will captives expand into deeper subprime (FICO <580)?
   - "Lender of last resort" role — helping or hurting consumers?
   - Regulatory view on captives serving subprime?

4. Market share projections:
   - What's captive market share today vs 2019?
   - Projections for 2027-2028 with ILC funding?

Sources: S&P, KBRA, Janus Henderson, Auto Finance News, academic research
```
