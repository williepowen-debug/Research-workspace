# RP-OTT-2.1: Demographic Composition of Deep Subprime Auto

## Research Output - February 4, 2026

*Source: file_53 - Strategic Risk Assessment of Deep Subprime Auto Finance*

---

## Executive Summary

The deep subprime auto market is structurally dependent on credit invisible and immigrant populations concentrated in specific geographic corridors. 45 million Americans (20% of adults) lack scorable credit files, with Hispanic consumers disproportionately represented. The collapse of Tricolor Holdings validates the thesis that demographic concentration creates existential portfolio risk.

---

## Key Findings

### Credit Invisible Population
- **26 million** Americans are "credit invisible" (no credit file)
- **19 million** additional are "unscorable" (thin/stale files)
- **45 million total** = 20% of U.S. adult population
- This population is the primary market for BHPH and deep subprime lenders

### Racial/Ethnic Disparities in Credit Visibility

| Ethnicity | Credit Invisible | Unscorable | Total Scorable |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|----------------|
| Hispanic/Latino | 15% | 12% | 73% |
| Black | 15% | 13% | 72% |
| White | 9% | 7% | 84% |

**Critical finding:** Hispanic consumers aged 25-29 are **2x more likely** to remain credit invisible (15.5%) vs White counterparts (6%)

### West Palm Beach Case Study

- **27.1%** foreign-born (vs 13.8% national)
- **24.6%** Hispanic/Latino
- **12.8%** non-citizens
- Called "belly of the beast" for immigration enforcement
- 287(g) agreements = catastrophic event risk for BHPH portfolios

### Deep Subprime Growth

- Deep subprime auto debt grew **+8.7%** (fastest segment)
- Credit market "polarizing" — Super Prime deleveraging, Deep Subprime stretching
- Middle range (600-749 FICO) shrinking

## Implications

1. **Demographic ceiling reached** — Industry cannot expand without hitting enforcement risk
2. **Skip risk > default risk** — Traditional recovery models break when borrowers disappear
3. **Geographic concentration = portfolio concentration** — Single enforcement action can destroy entire dealer network
4. **Tricolor collapse is template** — Double-pledging + demographic concentration = catastrophic failure mode
