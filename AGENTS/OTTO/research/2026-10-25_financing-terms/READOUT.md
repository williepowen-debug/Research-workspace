### What changed in cost and conditions

- **Bridgecrest:** BBB spread +30bp and yield +89.6bp. Algebraically, the yield-minus-stated-spread residual rose59.6bp; it is not an independently observed benchmark series or proof of a causal decomposition. AAA spread +5bp while yield +46.3bp. The difference between spread and all-in yield matters: higher interest cost is not all a higher credit premium.
- **Exeter:** BBB spread unchanged; AAA spread7bp tighter, despite higher yields. The June print is therefore refreshed with an August comparable rather than silently replaced by Bridgecrest's different risk. June16 is the pricing-document date; June24 was expected settlement. These examples contradict a uniform spread-tightening or uniform spread-widening claim.
- **CPS:** October notes total$767.85M versus July$716.88M; issuer reports both transactions completed. Initial overcollateralization rose **2.40→3.70%** of original pool; reserve remains **1.00%**. Target original-pool OC cap rises7.70→8.00%, and current-pool target19.10→19.40%, each subject to the contractual lesser-of/floor mechanism. This is more initial protection and different structure; the sources do not establish whether investors demanded it or different collateral drove it. A signed/funded securitization is not proof of current unused warehouse capacity.
