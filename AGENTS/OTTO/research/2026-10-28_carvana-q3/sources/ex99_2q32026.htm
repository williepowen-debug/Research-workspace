Carvana Announces Record Third Quarter 2026 Results

Record 217k retail units drive industry-leading 39% YoY growth
Record $8.04 Billion Revenue, up 42% YoY
Industry-leading Net Income of $483 Million, up $220 Million YoY
Record Adjusted EBITDA of $800 Million, up $163 Million YoY
Expects a sequential increase in retail units sold in Q4 and Adjusted EBITDA1 in the higher end of previously communicated $2.7 - $3.0 Billion range for full year 2026


PHOENIX – (October 28, 2026) – Carvana (NYSE: CVNA), the leading e-commerce platform for buying and selling cars, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Carvana’s complete third quarter 2026 financial results and management commentary are available in the company’s shareholder letter on the quarterly results page of its Investor Relations website.
"In the last two years, we have doubled the number of customers we serve while continuing to lead the industry in unit economics and giving value back to our customers by offering twice the selection, faster deliveries, and lower interest rates on their loans," said Ernie Garcia, Carvana founder and CEO. "We are just beginning to realize the potential of the machine we are building."

Q3 2026 Highlights
In Q3 2026, Carvana sold 217,385 retail units (+39% YoY) for total revenue of $8.040 billion (+42% YoY), both all-time quarterly records. This growth was paired with industry-leading profitability, including:
•Net Income of $483 million and Net income margin of 6.0%
•Record Adjusted EBITDA of $800 million and Adjusted EBITDA margin of 10.0%
•Record GAAP Operating Income of $714 million and GAAP Operating margin of 8.9%

Outlook
Carvana's results through Q3 position the company well for a strong finish to 2026. Looking toward the fourth quarter, Carvana expects the following as long as the environment remains stable:
•A sequential increase in retail units sold, and
•Adjusted EBITDA1 in the higher end of our previously communicated range of $2.7 to $3.0 billion for the full year 2026.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement the consolidated financial measures, which are prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, we also refer to the following non-GAAP measures in this press release: Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income plus (minus) income tax provision (benefit), interest expense, net, other operating expense, net, other expense, net, depreciation and amortization expense in cost of sales and SG&A expenses, share-based compensation expense in cost of sales and SG&A expenses, and loss on debt extinguishment, minus revenue related to our Warrants. Adjusted EBITDA margin is Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of total revenues.
We believe that these metrics are useful measures to us and to our investors because they exclude certain financial, capital structure, and non-cash items that we do not believe directly reflect our core operations and may not be indicative of our


recurring operations, in part because they may vary widely across time and within our industry independent of the performance of our core operations. We believe that excluding these items enables us to more effectively evaluate our performance period-over-period and relative to our competitors.
