## Recoveries, underwriting and the Carvana join

- **Recovery / severity:** no directly comparable borrower-default severity percentage was established from these earnings decks. Capture any disclosed repossession recovery, gross charge-off/recovery data, vehicle-price assumptions and flow-to-loss commentary tomorrow. Q2 deck p10 reports **off-lease** average vehicle loss$82; that measures lease residual economics and is **not** recovery on repossessed delinquent loans. Do not fill the gap with it. Manheim September−0.6%YoY remains the existing dated macro input, not a substitute lender recovery series.
- **Credit mix [CONF `ally_q2_10q`, MD&A Consumer Automotive Financing Volume, printed pp85–86]:** nonprime FICO<620 was11% of Q2 originations, versus9% a year earlier; below540 was2%, versus1%. Thus Ally is not a pure prime lender and also does not represent the fixed Exeter/Bridgecrest deep-subprime pools. Keep loan versus lease and originations versus outstanding-book denominators explicit.
- **Carvana [CONF same Q2 filing, p86]:** receivables sourced from Carvana were12.1% of total consumer automotive receivables/loans atJune30, versus10.4% atDecember31. Purchases were16% of Q2 consumer automotive originations. These are distinct denominators. Management said performance was generally consistent with similarly rated indirect-dealer loans; no numeric Carvana-specific delinquency/NCO series was established here. Do not infer one from aggregate Ally results.
- **Current amended commitment [CONF CarvanaJuly29 10-Q Notes7/19; July27 Exhibit10.10 §§2.01–2.02]:** purchases increased to **$8B throughJuly2027**. The10-Q narrative ends the windowJuly26,2027; the exhibit initially namesJuly27,2027. Preserve both statements without reconciling them. Earlier$6B window wasOctober28,2025–October27,2026. The contract adds qualifying repurchased/remediated/resold balances to its amount definition; neither headline capacity nor an endpoint proves remaining availability. **Each Ally purchaser can cancel future purchase obligations immediately in its discretion without a Seller/Transferor breach/default, subject to applicable law.** The separate90-day notice provision is not a guaranteed cushion. No actual cancellation established. Whole-loan purchases are not warehouse lending.
