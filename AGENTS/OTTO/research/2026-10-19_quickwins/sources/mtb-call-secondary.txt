INDUSTRY GLOSSARY AOCI : Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income; a component of shareholders' equity that includes unrealized gains and losses on certain investments. CET1 Ratio : Common Equity Tier 1 ratio; a core measure of a bank's financial strength that compares its capital against its risk-weighted assets. Criticized Loans : Loans that management identifies as having potential weaknesses and a higher risk of default than standard loans. LCR : Liquidity Coverage Ratio; a requirement ensuring that financial institutions have enough high-quality liquid assets to survive a 30-day stress scenario. Non-accrual Loans : Loans for which the bank no longer expects to receive full payment of principal and interest, stopping the accrual of interest income. RMBS : Residential Mortgage-Backed Securities; debt obligations representing interests in a pool of residential mortgage loans. SBA 7(a) Loans : The Small Business Administration's primary program for providing financial assistance to small businesses in the United States.
