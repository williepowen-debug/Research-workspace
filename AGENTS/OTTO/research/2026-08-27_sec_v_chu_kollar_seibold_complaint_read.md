## 1. The bottom line for OTTO's ledger: **OTTO-33 does NOT move**

> underwriter…"* · *"…**Financial Institution A, Financial Institution B, and Financial Institution C**

**OTTO-33 requires a *corporate counterparty* to be *named*.** The three defendants are **individuals**,

**Kollar + Seibold — the two cooperators OTTO identified on 2026-08-03** from SDNY 25-cr-584/585.
**Zero new names, corporate or individual. OTTO-33 holds at 68%, unchanged.**

⚠ **This is the trap in the inbound signal, and it is worth naming.** The headline —
*"SEC charges three NAMED Tricolor executives"* — is true and reads like OTTO-33 firing. It is not:
**the NAMES are individuals OTTO already had, and the CORPORATIONS are deliberately anonymized.**
A signal can be entirely accurate and still be the opposite of a hit on your claim. *(Auto-memory

## 2. 🔴🔴 THE FINDING OTTO DID NOT HAVE — Chu sat on the board of a Tricolor-exposed public bank

> *"During the Relevant Period, Chu also served as a member of the **Board of Directors of publicly
> traded Origin Bancorp, Inc. (NYSE: OBK)** and its wholly owned subsidiary **Origin Bank**. Chu
> resigned from those board positions in **September 2025**, around the time Tricolor [collapsed]."*

**OTTO has carried OBK since 2026-05-22 as a Tricolor-*exposed* bank** ($16.2M executive mortgages +
$28.4M charge-off + $30.1M commitments = **$74.7M**, disclosed Oct 23 2025) and logged it as a
discovery miss. **What OTTO did NOT have is that Tricolor's CEO — now an SEC securities-fraud
defendant — held a seat on that bank's board while the scheme ran.**

**This re-classifies the OBK relationship from EXPOSURE to RELATED PARTY.** The **$16.2M of
"executive mortgages"** on OBK's own disclosure reads differently once the borrower's principal is a
sitting director. **Sizing and the governance read are REGINALD's** (bank monitoring is explicitly out
of OTTO's cut) — **OTTO stops at "the relationship is related-party, not arm's-length, and it is
primary-sourced."** Routed to REGINALD via WALTER.

⚠ **Scope discipline: this does NOT move OTTO-33.** OBK is *already on* OTTO's named list, so it cannot
satisfy a claim that requires a counterparty **not** on that list. **The finding is important and the
prediction is unmoved — those are different questions**, and collapsing them is how a claim gets graded
on a technicality it never asked about.

## 4. Double-pledging quantified at a counterparty, for the first time

> *"**Financial Institution A's August 2025 analysis** identified the following volume of double-pledging
> for loans pledged to TAST securitizations: 2025-2, 2025-1, 2024-3, 2024-2, 2024-1, 2023-1, 2022-1…"*
> — and *"the most recent securitization, **TAST 2025-2, included approximately $49 million in
> double-pledged loans**."* Also: *"Financial Institution A also identified the **3,225 loans
> double-pledged from the TAST 2022-1 securitization to the TAST 2025-2 securitization**"* — the same
> loans **Kollar instructed an employee on June 17 2025 to list as owned by both trusts.**

This is the **first primary, per-deal quantification of the double-pledge** OTTO has seen; prior figures
were aggregate ("29,000 loans"). **The detection path is also on the record:** Financial Institution E
spotted discrepancies, **Financial Institution A confirmed them**, and A's analysis *"uncovered
significant double-pledging between the warehouse lending facilities and the TAST ABS Offerings."*

## 5. ⚠ A date divergence to carry, not to resolve

The SEC pleads **"Between at least 2020 and September 2025."** The **DOJ superseding indictment** (Chu,
unsealed Jun 24) alleges a **continuing enterprise "from at least 2018."** **Different agencies, different
charges, different periods — do not splice them into one range.** OTTO's own 2026-07-25 retraction
already applies here: the *"2018-2021 vintages implicated"* gloss was wrong (only 2 of 11 Tricolor deals
sit in that window vs 9 in 2022-2025), and **the SEC's 2020 start is consistent with the 2022-centric
frame OTTO corrected to.**
