# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-2.1
## 2022 Vintage Subprime Auto ABS — Exposure Mapping

### Context

The 2022 vintage of subprime auto ABS is the worst-performing cohort in recent history:
- **CNL:** 22.42% at 31 months (vs 19.34% for 2021 at same age)
- **60+ DQ:** 6.80%
- **Assessment:** Peak vehicle prices + stimulus withdrawal = structural vulnerability

If CNL approaches 30%, subordinate tranches face downgrades and potential losses. This prompt seeks to identify who is most exposed.

### Research Questions

**Part A: Originator Exposure**

1. **Top 2022 vintage issuers by volume:**
   - Which originators/shelves issued the most 2022 subprime auto ABS?
   - Rank by total issuance volume
   - Examples to investigate: SDART (Santander), EART (Exeter), AMCAR (AmeriCredit/GM), CACC (Credit Acceptance), ALLY, DRIVE (Santander deep subprime)

2. **Deal-level performance vs expectations:**
   - For major 2022 deals, what was the original ECNL vs current trajectory?
   - Which specific deals are most behind projections?
   - Any deals already on negative watch or downgraded?

3. **Originator financial health:**
   - For public originators (ALLY, CACC, CPSS, SC), what's their Q4 2025 guidance on 2022 vintage?
   - Any provisions specifically tied to 2022 performance?
   - Stock price reaction to vintage news?

**Part B: Institutional Holders**

4. **ABS fund holdings:**
   - Which mutual funds, ETFs, or hedge funds have significant subprime auto ABS exposure?
   - Can we identify 2022 vintage concentration specifically?
   - Sources: Fund prospectuses, quarterly holdings reports

5. **Insurance company exposure:**
   - Insurance companies are major ABS buyers
   - Which P&C or life insurers hold subprime auto ABS?
   - Source: State insurance filings (Schedule D), NAIC data

6. **CLO exposure:**
   - Are 2022 vintage auto ABS used as collateral in CLOs?
   - Which CLO managers have concentration?
   - Secondary market pricing on 2022 CLO tranches?

7. **BDC and private credit:**
   - Any BDCs with direct auto ABS residual exposure?
   - Private credit funds holding subordinate tranches?

**Part C: Bank Exposure**

8. **Warehouse lender vintage concentration:**
   - Banks with warehouse lines to subprime originators — what vintage is the underlying collateral?
   - Any 10-K disclosures mentioning 2022 vintage specifically?

9. **Bank trading book holdings:**
   - Do major banks hold subprime auto ABS on their trading books?
   - Market-making inventory that could have 2022 exposure?

**Part D: Tradeable Implications**

10. **Short candidates:**
    - Which publicly traded companies have outsized 2022 vintage exposure?
    - Rank by: (a) concentration, (b) financial leverage, (c) stock liquidity

11. **ABS relative value:**
    - Are 2022 subordinate tranches (BBB, BB, B) mispriced relative to loss trajectory?
    - Spread vs comparable vintages?
    - Any distressed opportunities or shorts?

12. **CDS or options:**
    - Is there a way to express a view on subprime auto via CDS indices (ABX equivalent)?
    - Options on ALLY, CACC, CPSS?

### Deliverables

1. **Issuer ranking table** — Top 10 2022 vintage issuers, volume, deal count, current CNL trajectory
2. **Deal watch list** — Specific 2022 deals most at risk of downgrade or trigger breach
3. **Institutional holder map** — Funds, insurers, CLOs with identified exposure
4. **Short candidate screen** — Public equities ranked by 2022 vintage risk
5. **Trade ideas** — Specific actionable positions (long/short/relative value)
