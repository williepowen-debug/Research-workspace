# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.6
## Subprime Auto Origination Volume and Credit Tightening

### Context

The subprime auto fraud cases and warehouse lender reassessment are expected to tighten credit availability for subprime consumers. This prompt seeks to quantify the origination decline and credit tightening.

### Research Questions

1. **Subprime auto origination trends:**
   - Total subprime auto loan originations (2023, 2024, 2025 YTD)
   - YoY change in volume
   - Market share shift (subprime vs. prime vs. near-prime)
   - Average loan amount and term trends

2. **Credit tightening evidence:**
   - Fed Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey (SLOOS) — auto lending standards
   - Approval rates by credit tier
   - Lender-specific tightening announcements
   - Dealer floor plan availability

3. **Consumer impact:**
   - Subprime auto loan denial rates
   - Alternative sources (buy-here-pay-here, lease, etc.)
   - Used car prices and affordability
   - Consumer complaints (CFPB database)

4. **Geographic concentration:**
   - Which states/metros have highest subprime auto concentration?
   - Regional lender dependencies

5. **2026 outlook:**
   - Industry forecasts for subprime origination
   - Expected warehouse capacity
   - New entrant or exit activity

### Deliverables

1. **Origination volume table** — Annual data with YoY change, by credit tier
2. **Credit tightening indicators** — SLOOS data, approval rates, lender actions
3. **Consumer access assessment** — How hard is it to get a subprime auto loan now?
4. **Geographic heat map** — States/metros most exposed to subprime auto credit crunch
5. **2026 projection** — Expected origination decline and implications

### Sources to Consider

- Experian, Equifax, TransUnion auto credit reports
- Fed SLOOS (Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey)
- CFPB consumer complaint database
- Cox Automotive, JD Power auto lending reports
- Dealer association data
- State DMV registration data (if accessible)

### Output Format

Quantitative report with origination trends and credit availability metrics. Assess whether the "credit crunch" scenario is materializing and how severe it might become.
