# Research Prompt: Large Bankruptcy Surge Analysis — February 2026

**Goal:** Analyze the recent surge in large corporate bankruptcies and identify exposure to auto lending and consumer finance sectors.

---

## Background Context

Recent data indicates a significant spike in large corporate bankruptcies:
- **9 large bankruptcies (>$50M liabilities)** filed in the week ending approximately Feb 11, 2026
- **18 total large bankruptcies** in the preceding 3-week period
- This 3-week rolling average is reportedly the **highest since the COVID pandemic**

Historical context: Only the 2001 recession, the 2008-2009 Global Financial Crisis, and the 2020 pandemic saw comparable bankruptcy rates.

---

## Research Questions

### 1. Identify the Bankruptcies
- List all large corporate bankruptcies (>$50M liabilities) filed in February 2026
- For each, provide: Company name, industry, location, total liabilities, date filed
- What were the stated reasons for filing?

### 2. Industry Breakdown
- How many are in auto lending or auto-related industries?
- How many are in consumer finance (personal loans, credit cards, BNPL)?
- How many are in retail or consumer discretionary?
- How many are in real estate or construction?
- What is the industry concentration?

### 3. Auto/Consumer Finance Focus
- Are any subprime auto lenders in the list?
- Are any Buy Here Pay Here (BHPH) auto dealers included?
- Are any auto parts suppliers (similar to First Brands)?
- Are any consumer finance companies included?
- What is the total liabilities exposure in auto/consumer finance?

### 4. Geographic Analysis
- Is there concentration in specific states?
- Are Southern states (Texas, Florida, Georgia, etc.) overrepresented?
- Is there correlation with states that have high auto delinquency rates?

### 5. Bank and Lender Exposure
- Which banks provided credit facilities to these companies?
- Are any regional banks disproportionately exposed?
- What is the estimated loss exposure for major lenders?
- Are any Business Development Companies (BDCs) or private credit funds exposed?

### 6. Pattern Analysis
- Do any of these bankruptcies involve fraud allegations?
- Are there common factors (rising interest rates, declining consumer demand, etc.)?
- Is this a leading indicator of broader consumer credit stress?

---

## Requested Output Format

1. **Table**: List of all large Feb 2026 bankruptcies with key details
2. **Industry Breakdown**: Pie chart or table showing sector distribution
3. **Auto/Consumer Exposure**: Specific companies in these sectors with details
4. **Bank Exposure Map**: Which lenders are most exposed?
5. **Assessment**: Is this a normal economic cycle or sign of systemic stress?

- Industry publications (Auto Finance News, American Banker)
