# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.3
## Subprime Auto Fraud — January/February 2026 Developments

### Context

As of January 26, 2026, the known status was:
- **Tricolor**: Chapter 7; Daniel Chu and Daniel Goodgame charged; Jerome Kollar and Ameryn Seibold pled guilty and cooperating
- **First Brands**: Bankruptcy; trial scheduled June 2026
- **PrimaLend**: Chapter 11; restructuring in progress
- **Bank losses**: JPM $170M, Fifth Third $200M disclosed

This prompt seeks any developments since January 26, 2026.

### Research Questions

1. **Tricolor case updates:**
   - Any new court filings or motions?
   - Has cooperating witness testimony been filed?
   - New defendants charged?
   - Asset recovery status?
   - Creditor committee actions?

2. **First Brands case updates:**
   - Trial preparation status
   - Discovery disputes or evidence releases
   - Settlement discussions?
   - New allegations or defendants?

3. **PrimaLend restructuring:**
   - DIP financing status
   - Plan of reorganization progress
   - Creditor disputes
   - Going concern viability

4. **New fraud discoveries:**
   - Any new subprime lender failures announced?
   - DOJ or SEC announcements?
   - Warehouse lender actions against other borrowers?

5. **Bank disclosures:**
   - Any new bank losses announced?
   - Q4 2025 earnings mentions of auto warehouse exposure?
   - Provision increases related to auto?

6. **Regulatory developments:**
   - OCC, Fed, or CFPB statements on subprime auto?
   - State AG actions?
   - Industry guidance or examination focus changes?

### Deliverables

1. **Timeline of developments** — Date-ordered list of any new events since Jan 26
2. **Case status updates** — Current status of each known fraud case
3. **New entities identified** — Any new lenders, executives, or banks mentioned
4. **Implications assessment** — What do new developments mean for thesis?

### Sources to Consider

- PACER (Tricolor, First Brands, PrimaLend dockets)
- DOJ press releases
- Bank earnings calls and 8-K filings
- Auto Finance News, Auto Remarketing
- Bloomberg, Reuters legal coverage
- Restructuring publications (Reorg, Debtwire if accessible)

### Output Format

Chronological report of developments with source citations. Clearly flag anything that changes the fraud scope, bank exposure, or contagion risk assessment.
