# RP-OTT-2.4: Historical Precedent (2007-2008) & Tricolor Portfolio Forensics

## Objective
Examine historical precedent from the Bush-era immigration enforcement + financial crisis period, and conduct forensic analysis of Tricolor's portfolio for demographic/geographic patterns.

## Research Questions

### Part A: Historical Precedent (2007-2008)

1. **Immigration Enforcement + Financial Crisis Overlap**
   - Bush administration ramped up workplace enforcement 2006-2008
   - Coincided with housing crisis and recession
   - Did subprime auto perform worse in immigrant-heavy areas during this period?
   - Source: Academic papers, Fed research, historical ABS performance data

2. **Academic Research**
   - Any papers on immigration status and auto loan performance?
   - Research on "credit invisibles" during the financial crisis?
   - Studies on informal economy workers and consumer credit behavior?
   - Source: NBER, Fed working papers, Journal of Consumer Finance

3. **State-Level Enforcement Effects**
   - Arizona SB 1070 (2010) — did auto DQ spike in AZ post-passage?
   - Alabama HB 56 (2011) — similar test case
   - Any state-level natural experiments on enforcement → credit performance?
   - Source: State-level auto loan data, academic policy analysis

4. **Recovery Rate History**
   - Were recovery rates lower in immigrant-heavy MSAs during 2008-2010?
   - Any evidence of elevated skip rates during that period?
   - Source: Historical ABS data, Manheim historical reports

### Part B: Tricolor Portfolio Forensics

5. **Geographic Distribution**
   - Tricolor operated primarily in CA, TX, AZ
   - What's the geographic distribution of their loan book at bankruptcy?
   - Are there concentrations in specific MSAs/counties?
   - Source: Bankruptcy trustee reports, Vervent (servicer) data, court filings

6. **Recovery Rate by Geography**
   - Are Tricolor recovery rates worse in border-adjacent counties?
   - Does recovery rate correlate with immigrant population density?
   - Source: Trustee reports, servicer data

7. **Loss Timing**
   - When did Tricolor losses accelerate relative to immigration enforcement news?
   - Is there a pattern we can extrapolate to other lenders?
   - Source: Tricolor financial history, timeline analysis

8. **Customer Profile Data**
   - Any demographic data disclosed in bankruptcy?
   - Marketing materials that reveal target customer profile?
   - Dealer network locations (indicator of customer base)
   - Source: Bankruptcy docs, archived marketing, dealer agreements

9. **Fraud vs Credit Loss**
   - Tricolor collapsed partly due to fraud, partly due to credit losses
   - Can we separate the two? What % was pure credit deterioration?
   - Is credit loss pattern replicable to non-fraud lenders?
   - Source: DOJ filings, trustee analysis, forensic accounting reports

## Output Requested

**Historical Section:**
- Summary of academic/Fed research on immigration-credit nexus
- 2007-2008 precedent data on auto performance in immigrant areas
- State enforcement natural experiment results if available

**Tricolor Section:**
- Geographic distribution of Tricolor portfolio
- Recovery rate breakdown by geography
- Timeline of loss acceleration vs enforcement environment
- Any demographic data from bankruptcy proceedings
