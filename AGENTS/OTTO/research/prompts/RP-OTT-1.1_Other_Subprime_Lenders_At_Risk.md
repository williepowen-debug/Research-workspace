# Research Prompt: RP-OTT-1.1
## Other Subprime Auto Lenders at Risk

### Context

Three subprime auto lenders have been exposed with fraud or severe distress in 2025:
- **Tricolor Holdings** — Chapter 7; ~$2B debt; executives charged with double-pledging and data manipulation
- **First Brands** — Bankruptcy; double-pledging in supply chain/inventory finance; trial June 2026
- **PrimaLend** — Chapter 11; $286M debt; warehouse line stress

The pattern suggests systematic fraud may extend beyond these three companies. Warehouse lenders are now auditing all subprime auto exposure.

### Research Questions

1. **Who are the other major subprime auto lenders in the US?** Map the landscape:
   - Buy-here-pay-here (BHPH) specialists
   - Deep subprime originators (credit scores <500)
   - Captive finance arms of used car dealers
   - Private equity-backed platforms

2. **Which lenders have similar risk characteristics to Tricolor?**
   - Heavy reliance on warehouse financing
   - Multiple warehouse line relationships
   - Rapid growth in recent years
   - Opaque corporate structures or multiple SPVs
   - Private/non-reporting ownership

3. **Are there any signals of stress at other lenders?**
   - Recent layoffs or executive departures
   - Covenant breaches or facility amendments
   - Rating agency warnings or downgrades
   - Litigation or regulatory actions
   - Auditor changes or delayed filings

4. **What is the industry structure?**
   - Total subprime auto origination volume (2023-2025)
   - Market share of top 10 subprime originators
   - Concentration of warehouse lending relationships

### Deliverables

1. **Lender landscape table** — Name, ownership, estimated origination volume, warehouse lenders (if known), risk flags
2. **Risk watchlist** — 5-10 lenders with characteristics similar to Tricolor (ranked by concern)
3. **Industry structure summary** — Market size, concentration, key players
4. **Red flags identified** — Any specific lenders showing distress signals

### Sources to Consider

- Auto Finance News, Auto Remarketing
- Rating agency reports (Moody's, S&P, Fitch on subprime auto)
- Court filings and PACER (bankruptcy, litigation)
- State AG offices (consumer protection actions)
- Private equity portfolio company databases
- BHPH dealer associations

### Output Format

Structured report with tables. Focus on identifying the most likely "4th case" — the next lender to be exposed with similar fraud or distress.
