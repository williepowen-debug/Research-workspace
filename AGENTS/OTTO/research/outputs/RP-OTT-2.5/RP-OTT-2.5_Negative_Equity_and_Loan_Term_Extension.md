## 0. BOTTOM LINE

**Loan terms are stretching fast, negative equity is spreading — and the DEPTH of negative equity is flat. That combination is the finding, and it independently corroborates the Severity-Divergence Test's REFUTE.**

The term stretch has **not** converted into deeper negative equity, because **used-vehicle values absorbed it.** That absorption is the load-bearing condition, it is **dated**, and as of **mid-August 2026 it is ending**: Manheim went from **+1.3% YoY in July to FLAT in mid-August** (non-adjusted **−0.2%**).

⇒ **OTTO now has a named hinge with a live instrument.** Negative-equity depth is insulated by used values; if Manheim turns materially negative, the accumulated long-term cohort surfaces as a step-change in depth, and severity — the SDT's dead leg — should finally move.

## 1. LOAN TERMS — deteriorating, fast `[CONF Experian, State of the Automotive Finance Market Q1 2026, pub 2026-05-28]`

| Metric | Q1 2025 | **Q1 2026** | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| New loans **>72 months** | 30.83% | **35.55%** | **+4.72pp** |
| Used loans **>72 months** | 28.60% | **31.54%** | +2.94pp |
| New loans **>85 months** | 2.95% | **3.33%** | +0.38pp |
| Used loans **>85 months** | 1.32% | **1.40%** | +0.08pp |
| Avg new term | — | **69.48 months** | — |
| Avg used term | — | **67.73 months** | — |

**+4.72pp in one year on the >72-month bucket is a large move** — roughly one new-vehicle borrower in twenty migrated past six years within twelve months. **This is the mechanical generator of negative equity**: amortization slows while depreciation does not.

⚠️ **This is NOT the extension rate OTTO tracks in its 10-D panel.** Origination *term* (contracted at signing) ≠ loss-mitigation *extension* (granted later on a delinquent loan). Two different instruments, two different behaviours, **never splice them.**

## 2. NEGATIVE EQUITY — share rising, depth FLAT `[CONF Edmunds, via GlobeNewswire 2026-07-17]`

| Metric | Q2 2025 | Q1 2026 | **Q2 2026** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of new-vehicle trade-ins underwater | 26.6% | 30.9% | **29.6%** |
| **Average amount underwater** | $6,754 | $7,183 | **$6,884** |
| Avg monthly payment (negative-equity buyer) | — | — | **$944** (industry avg $777) |
| Avg lifetime interest paid | — | — | **$16,270** (avg buyer $9,811) |
| Avg trade-in age | 3.8 yrs | — | 4.0 yrs |

**Read the two rows against each other — that is where the finding is.**
- **SHARE: +3.0pp YoY** (26.6 → 29.6%). Real spread. Highest Q2 since 2020.
- **DEPTH: +$130 YoY, or +1.9% nominal** — **negative in real terms**, and **below Q1's $7,183.**

⚠️ **Context that kills the "record/epidemic" framing: Q2 2020 was 37.2%.** Today's 29.6% is elevated and **well below** the 2020 comparison.

**Edmunds' own causal read is a cohort effect, not an underwriting collapse:** *"Buyers who financed at 2022's peak prices are starting to come back to trade in"* — 2022's limited inventory, minimal incentives, at-or-above-MSRP transactions, now hitting the 4-year trade-in mark. **A vintage working through, not a deterioration in current origination quality.**

## 3. WHY THE CHAIN BREAKS — and it is the same answer the SDT gave

More borrowers are underwater (§2 share) because terms stretched (§1). But **how deep** they are underwater has not moved. The only variable that can hold depth flat while terms stretch is **collateral value**, and it did:

| Manheim UVVI (mix/mileage/seasonally adj.) | Reading |
|---|---|
| July 2026 | index **210**, **+1.3% YoY** |
| **Mid-August 2026** | index **207.4**, **FLAT vs Aug-2025**; non-adjusted **−0.2% YoY** |

**⇒ Independent corroboration of the Severity-Divergence Test.** The SDT returned **REFUTE** on its first reading (ΔSeverity **+0.06pp** vs ΔFrequency **+1.85pp**, Manheim-gated at +1.3%): delinquency rose while loss *severity* did not. **Negative-equity depth is a completely different instrument, from a different provider, measuring a different population — and it says the same thing.** Loss-per-default is not deteriorating.

**This materially strengthens the SDT's pre-set decision rule** (*"this REFUTE + one more ⇒ cut the Secondary thesis's load-bearing role, do NOT narrow again"*). The second reading now has a corroborator before it is even run.

## 4. THE FORWARD TEST — a named hinge, live instrument, already moving

**Negative-equity depth is insulated by used values, and the insulation is thinning in the data as of mid-August.**

## 6. 🔴 UNPLANNED FINDING — OTTO-10's instrument disagrees with its peer bureau, in direction

Experian's release carries a subprime-share series `[CONF Experian Q1 2026 pub 2026-05-28; Q4 2025 pub 2026-03-05]`:

| Series (Experian, share of **total** financing) | Prior year | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| Q4 | 14.54% (Q4-24) | **15.31%** (Q4-25) |
| Q1 | 14.40% (Q1-25) | **15.75%** (Q1-26) |
| *New vehicles only* | 5.74% (Q4-24) | **6.61%** (Q4-25) |
| *Used vehicles only* | 22.11% (Q4-24) | **22.47%** (Q4-25) |

**OTTO-10's instrument is the Equifax subprime UNIT share: 16.5% → 15.2% → 14.7%, decelerating — and OTTO cut the row 65% → 20% on that deceleration (2026-08-14).**

**⚠️ Experian points the OTHER WAY: rising, on both the Q4 and Q1 comparisons.**

**Do NOT score OTTO-10 off Experian** — different bureau, and **Experian's own release discloses neither the units-vs-dollars basis nor the credit-score cutoff.** That is precisely the perimeter error that nearly falsified this row off the NY Fed dollar share on 8/14.

**But two things now sit on the record:**
1. **OTTO-10's 65→20% cut was made on a single bureau's series whose nearest independent peer moves the opposite way.** That does not make the cut wrong; it makes it **less well-supported than the confident wording implies.**
2. **The row's perimeter ambiguity is worse than it admits.** OTTO-10 claims *"subprime origination share <13%."* On Experian's **new-only** basis that is **6.61% — already CONFIRMED.** On its **used-only** basis it is **22.47% — unreachable.** **A claim that resolves either way depending on an unstated new/used mix is not yet falsifiable.**

**⇒ The owed "refresh Equifax unit share before Sep 30, or resolve explicitly as UNOBSERVABLE" item now has a third option and a mandatory sub-step: state the new/used perimeter, or resolve UNOBSERVABLE on perimeter grounds.** Do not let it drift to Sep 30 and resolve on whichever number is nearest.
