## PART 1: Ally Financial — Carvana Exposure & Systemic Role

### 1.1 Carvana Forward Flow Mechanics

#### Structure
The Ally-Carvana arrangement is governed by a **Master Purchase and Sale Agreement (MPSA)**, originally established in December 2016. Under this arrangement, Carvana sells automotive finance receivables to Ally Bank and Ally Financial Inc. on a **"without recourse" basis** — meaning Ally owns the loans outright and assumes full credit risk upon purchase.

| Date | Commitment | Term |
|------|-----------|------|
| Jan 11, 2024 | $4.0B | Jan 11, 2024 – Jan 10, 2025 |
| Jan 3, 2025 | $4.0B (renewed) | Jan 3, 2025 – Jan 2, 2026 |
| Apr 29, 2025 | $4.0B (renewed) | Apr 30, 2025 – Apr 29, 2026 |
| Oct 2025 | **$6.0B (upsized)** | Through Oct 2027 |

Key structural details:
- **Credit risk**: Ally bears full credit risk post-purchase. "Without recourse" means Carvana has no obligation to repurchase defaulted loans.
- **Performance triggers**: Specific triggers are **partially redacted** in SEC filings. Agreement has been amended six times since inception.
- **Servicing**: Loans sold to Ally continue to be **serviced by Bridgecrest** (owned by Ernest Garcia II), creating unusual dynamic where Garcia-controlled entity services loans on Ally's balance sheet.

#### Volume and Concentration
- **$19B of $46B** Carvana cumulative originations (42%) purchased by Ally — single largest buyer
- **17% of Ally's used-auto originations** = significant concentration risk
- Q1 2025: Carvana sold $0.8B to Ally, with $3.2B unused capacity
- **Critical gap**: Ally does NOT identify Carvana loans separately in DQ or NCO reporting

#### Gotham/Hindenburg Contagion Risk
The Gotham City Research report (Jan 28, 2026) alleges Carvana overstated earnings by **$1B+** through:
1. DriveTime "fuels over 73% of Carvana's EBIT" through undisclosed subsidies
2. Bridgecrest charges below-market servicing fees (~0.117% annually)
3. Bridgecrest appears as originator on loans tied to Carvana vehicles

**Ally contagion assessment:**
- **Direct credit losses**: If loan quality overstated, Ally faces higher NCOs on 17% concentration
- **Forward flow termination**: If allegations prove material, Ally would likely pull or reduce commitment
- **Disclosure risk**: Forced Carvana-specific breakout could reveal adverse quality

### 2.3 Competitive Impact on Monolines

#### Funding Cost Advantage
- GM's $6.0B interest on $114.3B debt = ~5.25% blended cost
- Retail deposits could fund at 4.0-4.5% = **75-125bps advantage**
- "Deposit funding makes the captives' advantage **structural, not cyclical**"

#### Monoline Squeeze
1. **Funding disadvantage**: Monolines have no deposit base, rely on warehouse → ABS
2. **Post-Tricolor tightening**: Warehouse lenders more cautious, raises costs
3. **Exeter vulnerability**: Expanded into deeper subprime, S&P noted "much worse" deterioration
