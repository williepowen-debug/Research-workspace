# RP-OTT-2.2: Abandonment Patterns & Cross-Border Collateral Risk

## Research Output - February 4, 2026

*Source: file_51 - The Invisible Exit: Subprime Auto Collateral Risk and the Geopolitics of Abandonment*

---

## Executive Summary

The "Invisible Exit" hypothesis is confirmed: subprime auto is experiencing a structural shift from traditional default to asset abandonment. Recovery ratios have collapsed to 30.58% (from 41% benchmark), cross-border lien enforcement is effectively impossible, and 70% of early payment defaults show fraud indicators. The traditional roll-rate model is breaking.

---

## Key Findings

### Abandonment vs Default — Operational Taxonomy

| Feature | Traditional Default | Abandonment/"Skip" |
|---------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Borrower Intent | Reactive/Hardship | Proactive/Fraud-linked |
| Collateral Status | Stationary/Recoverable | Mobile/Hidden/Exported |
| Communication | Occasional | Non-existent |
| Recovery Expectation | 30-60% | 0-10% (Total Loss common) |
| Data Signal | Linear roll through DPD | Sudden cessation/Skip |

### Recovery Rate Collapse

| Metric | Benchmark (2018-21) | Q3 2025 |
|--------|---------------------|---------|

| Total Recoveries | N/A | 769,295 |

**2.2 million vehicles repossessed in 2025** — but recovery efficiency at multi-year lows

### Cross-Border Lien Enforcement — The Jurisdictional Abyss

**Key finding: Once a vehicle crosses into Mexico, it is effectively lost.**

| Factor | U.S. Jurisdiction | Mexico |
|--------|-------------------|--------|
| Enforcement Cost | Low (self-help repo) | High (litigation) |
| Timeframe | Days to weeks | 1-2 years |
| Service of Process | Private servers OK | Court clerks only |
| Seizure Mechanism | Direct repossession | *Embargo Precautorio* |
| Success Probability | High (if located) | Very Low |

**Legal barriers:**
- *Homologación* (judgment recognition) is prohibitively expensive
- Arbitration costs ~$5,875 for $25,000 case (before Mexican counsel fees)
- U.S. State Department VRU has very limited role

### Fraud-Skip Nexus

**70% of Early Payment Defaults (EPDs) show evidence of origination fraud**

| Fraud Signal | Lift in Skip Probability |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| Credit Washing | +11.4 percentage points |
| Synthetic Identity | +22.8 percentage points |
| Income Inflation | +14.2 percentage points |

**Credit washing up 162% YoY** — borrowers disputing legitimate tradelines under guise of identity theft

### ABS Implications

- Subprime annualized losses spiked to **10.13%** (Oct 2025)

## Key Thesis Confirmed

**"The Invisible Exit"**: Immigrants facing deportation risk don't default through normal channels — they leave. The loan goes from current to "skip" with no recovery. This breaks traditional roll-rate models and creates a model blind spot for ABS investors.
