## Executive Summary

Carvana (CVNA) faces credible but unverified fraud allegations centered on a web of related-party transactions with DriveTime, a private used-car empire controlled by Ernest Garcia II — a **convicted felon** in the 1990s Lincoln Savings & Loan scandal. If validated, this would be the **largest specimen** in OTTO's "Cockroach" taxonomy, dwarfing Tricolor ($2B) and First Brands ($9.3B) combined.

**Key Thesis:** DriveTime/Bridgecrest subsidizes Carvana's profitability through below-market servicing fees, enabling Carvana to book inflated gains on loan sales. If true, Carvana's $404M net income (2024) reverses to ~$450M loss.
