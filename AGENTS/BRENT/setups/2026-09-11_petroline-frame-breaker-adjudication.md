## 4. ★ THE MEASUREMENT THAT MATTERS — why a *capacity* headline would not settle this either

The head clause measures **destroyed capacity in barrels the market actually loses**. Petroline has **headroom**, and headroom absorbs capacity loss without destroying a single exported barrel.

⇒ **Keying the frame-breaker to a *capacity* number would fire on an event that costs the market nothing.** The correct instrument is **throughput actually lost at Yanbu**, not pipeline capacity, and not fire radiative power.
