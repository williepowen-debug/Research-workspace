# ADDENDUM #3 — 2026-07-30 ~4:00 PM ET · **THE DIESEL FALSIFIER I REGISTERED HERE IS EFFECTIVELY UNFALSIFIABLE. RE-SPECCING IT — AND THE DEFECT IS ONE I HAD ALREADY RATIFIED A FIX FOR ELSEWHERE.**

## The measurement

Registered spec (§C above): *"distillate builds **≥+2.0M** AND the diesel crack falls **−>$5**, **IN THE SAME WEEK**; either alone does NOT falsify."*

| | 155 weeks (2023-08 → 2026-07) |
|---|---|
| Leg A alone — distillate build ≥ +2.0M | 33 wks = **21.3%** |
| Leg B alone — diesel crack falls > $5 | 12 wks = **7.7%** |
| **BOTH, same week (the falsifier as written)** | **1 wk = 0.6%** |
| `corr(distillate build, crack change)` | **+0.095** — effectively independent |

**One fire in three years.** Each leg is individually common; the conjunction is a ~once-per-three-years event. **A falsifier that fires once every 155 weeks is not a falsifier — it is a licence to keep the thesis.**

## ★ The diagnosis, and it is embarrassing because I ratified the fix for this exact defect the day before

**This is the LESSONS #21(b) LATENCY DEFECT, in a spec I wrote myself.** I forced together:
- **distillate stocks** — a **weekly SURVEYED quantity**, published with a lag, that moves on a physical-inventory clock, and
- **the diesel crack** — a **live daily PRICE** that can move $5 in a session.

They are **near-independent at weekly frequency (corr +0.095)**, so requiring them inside one shared week multiplies two marginals into near-impossibility. **"An indicator cannot satisfy a test faster than it can physically move"** — I wrote that on 7/27, had Will ratify the off-ramp fix for it on 7/29, and this falsifier had the same defect sitting in it the whole time. **Fixing an instance is not fixing the class** (`[[finding_mechanize_the_cap_not_the_ritual]]`, `[[finding_compound_gate_jointly_unsatisfiable]]`).

## Re-spec — same fix shape as the ratified off-ramp: give the legs a shared but HONEST window

**NEW FALSIFIER (frozen, effective 2026-07-30):** *the diesel leg is falsified if, measured over any **rolling 4-week window**, **US distillate stocks build ≥ +4.0M** AND **the diesel crack falls > $5.00**.* Both still required — the "either alone does not falsify" principle is **retained deliberately**, because a build alone is seasonal and a crack drop alone is often mechanical (crude moving, as on 7/29).

**Calibration, shown so the choice is auditable rather than asserted:**

| Window / thresholds | Fires | Rate | ~1 per |
|---|---|---|---|
| *same week, ≥+2.0M & −$5 (AS REGISTERED)* | *1/155* | ***0.6%*** | *155 wks* |
| 4-week, ≥+2.0M & −$5 | 10/152 | 6.6% | 15 wks |
| **4-week, ≥+4.0M & −$5 ← ADOPTED** | **9/152** | **5.9%** | **17 wks** |
| 4-week, ≥+4.0M & −$10 | 3/152 | 2.0% | 51 wks |
| 4-week, ≥+6.0M & −$10 | 2/152 | 1.3% | 76 wks |

**Why ≥+4.0M rather than keeping +2.0M:** widening the window from 1 week to 4 mechanically loosens the build leg (four weeks of builds accumulate), so **holding +2.0M would have been a silent loosening dressed as a latency repair — the exact error LESSONS #21(b) warns about.** Doubling the build threshold restores the leg's original strictness; net rate moves 0.6% → 5.9%, which is a **falsifier that can actually fire roughly twice a year** rather than twice a decade.
