## BG-05 — Roll scope and guard

> **THE `60–90 DTE` BAND GOVERNS *NEW STRUCTURAL DEPLOYMENTS*. IT DOES NOT GOVERN THE ROLL OF AN EXISTING LEG.**
>
> **Why this is a SCOPE ruling and NOT an exception, recorded because the distinction is the whole point:** ruling ② above states the band exists so a shorter-dated vertical cannot make **leg (b)** easier to satisfy. **Leg (b) is an ENTRY-economics test. A roll of an existing leg is never leg-(b) gated.** ⇒ **read for its purpose, the band was never about rolls — so this states what was already true.** An *exception* would have implied the rule should bind and was being set aside; it should not, and it was not.
> **Precedent on this desk:** `RISK_RULES #15` was scoped the same way on 2026-07-30 — **60–90 structural vs 21–35 off-ramp.** Scoping-by-role is the established pattern here, not a novelty.
>
> ⛔⛔ **THE GUARD, AND IT IS BINDING — A SCOPE RULING IS BROADER THAN AN EXCEPTION AND NEEDS ONE.** **"ROLL" IS DEFINED NARROWLY: SAME UNDERLYING · SAME STRIKE · LATER EXPIRY. NOTHING ELSE.**
> **ANY change of STRIKE or STRUCTURE is a NEW DEPLOYMENT and the `60–90 DTE` band BINDS IN FULL.** ⚠️ **Without this, a genuine new deployment arrives wearing a roll's clothes and the only economic gate on this trade is gone by relabelling.**
>
> ⚠️ **WHY THIS WAS ESCALATED RATHER THAN SELF-FIXED, kept on the spec:** the obvious repair was to widen the band to admit `Dec-18`. **Widening is what makes the roll possible** — i.e. it relaxes a gate in the exact direction that authorises the proposer's own trade. **That is the direction-neutrality trap ruling ② names, and the reason this went to Will as a question instead of being "clarified" into place.**
> ⚠️ **`RISK_RULES` is TERRY's file — this scope ruling is flagged to TERRY for its own mirror, NOT edited here.**
