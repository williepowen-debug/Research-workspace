**★ THE ONE-LINE FINDING: the letter's sinking limb was written for a hull loss that TAKES BARRELS OFF THE WATER. Every instance the carve-out names is **supply being destroyed and the market losing it**. Here the **US destroyed Iran's ability to SELL** — a sanctions/financial-interdiction action, in CENTCOM's own words a *"multibillion-dollar network that finances the IRGC"* and *"an even higher economic cost."* That is an **enforcement escalation**, and enforcement is **reversible on de-escalation** — the same reversibility class as premium, and the same class as the CPC halt this desk already ruled **barrels-DEFERRED-not-destroyed**.
