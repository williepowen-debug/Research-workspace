## Structural Change

**Kharg Island offline changes Iran's incentive structure:**
- Iran's primary export revenue (Kharg = 90% of exports) is gone
- Iran now has limited economic upside to preserve and maximum deterrence value from escalation
- Each US strike on Iranian infrastructure → Iran targets highest-value GCC export nodes
- The cycle is NOT self-limiting: Iran cannot "lose more" economically than it already has

## Infrastructure Targeting Doctrine

Iran appears to be systematically targeting:
1. **Processing** (Shah Gas Plant — ultra-sour, no substitute)
2. **Export terminals** (Fujairah — last UAE bypass)
3. **Transportation nodes** (Kuwait airport)

**Pattern implication:** Saudi Petroline → Yanbu is now the only remaining major bypass route (~3.3M bpd). If Ras Tanura or Yanbu terminal is targeted next, total Gulf export capacity approaches zero.
