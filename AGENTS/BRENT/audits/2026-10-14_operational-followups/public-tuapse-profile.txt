<div class="text common-text">
    <p>RN-Tuapse Refinery in Krasnodar region is a part of Rosneft vertically integrated structure. This is the only Russian oil refinery located on the Black Sea coast, as well as one of the oldest - it was commissioned in 1929. In 1942 the equipment was dismantled and evacuated to the city of Krasnovodsk. Post-war reconstruction of the plant began in 1948, and the first unit was commissioned in 1949. In 2024 the refinery celebrated its 95-th anniversary.</p></div>
