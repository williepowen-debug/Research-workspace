## CORE THESIS

**The oil thesis is a race between the supply deficit closing and inventory buffers declining.** Prompt scarcity and route costs can support nearby oil while demand softens; neither establishes a durable rise in outright prices. The September 2 v5.8 revision moved the buffer risk further out: combined US crude draws slowed markedly from June and Cushing rebuilt. The older claims that Cushing is presently at its floor, draws remain at their June maximum, and the curve is in June contango are superseded.

The authoritative dated physical and price observations are in [STATUS](../STATUS.md) and [TRACKER](../demand_destruction/TRACKER.md). The v5.8 comparison is week ending June 12 versus August 28; it is not a September 8 survey. Current-looking quantities are not duplicated here. The September 8 named-contract reconstruction also supersedes the original September 2 curve capture; those vendor daily bars remain distinct from authenticated exchange settlements. [Owner source/basis record](../setups/2026-09-08_market-docket-owner-read.md).

Three mechanisms can coexist:

- **Inventory releases supply today's market.** An SPR exchange borrows against future return obligations. Its release rate, contractual delivery schedule, return schedule, applicable legal authority and physical operating limits are different objects. The old early-July cliff is withdrawn. The v5.8 mid-November calculation was an extrapolation to an assumed reference level, not an authenticated legal or physical deadline; it cannot time the thesis without the missing authority/schedule evidence. See the catch-up report's SPR review.
- **Diplomacy and route costs affect prices before measured barrels arrive.** A signed agreement or official declaration that Hormuz is open does not measure throughput. Conversely, few observed strait transits do not prove equivalent lost supply: bypass pipelines, cargo size, rerouting and incomplete tracking matter.
- **Demand can weaken while prompt supply stays tight.** Gasoline and jet product supplied are wholesale balance measures, with the early-shock stockpiling caveat in LESSONS #9. BRT-29 separately tests the pump-price premise, aviation mechanism and later gasoline threshold; one leg cannot stand in for another.

**Dated v5.8 forecast, not reissued:** September 2 assessment was roughly 70% for a front-month bid into mid-September, and roughly 25–30% for sustained prices above $100 through end-September. These were thesis assessments, not new BRT registrations; their horizons and probabilities are preserved, not rolled forward. Earlier June price probabilities have expired as forecasts.

**Existing v5.8 research falsifiers, unchanged:** `M1−M3 back under +$3.50`, **or** `Cushing building two more weeks on a halving draw pace`. Read the September 2 registration in [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) and its source record before grading. No new persistence count, comparison baseline or contract mapping is invented by this cleanup. ## TWO PHASES

**Phase 1 — prompt scarcity / route premium.** A constrained or costly route can sustain a premium without confirmed destruction of crude-production capacity. The original July re-squeeze remains historical evidence of that mechanism. LNG damage and refinery outages must be assessed in their own products; no “zero crude loss” shorthand may be upgraded from an absence of verified loss to a verified zero.

**Phase 2 — normalization or demand destruction.** Path A is operational supply relief; Path B is demand weakening. Price can anticipate either before the physical data confirm it. The June diplomatic repricing is history, not proof that today's operational reopening is complete. BRT-08/09/21's old trigger set is closed; do not revive it to grade this regime. BRT-29 owns the successor demand test.

The long-run Phase-2 short and the rapid off-ramp premium-unwind trade have different mechanisms and clocks. Their historical explanatory examples are in the archived thesis. Current binding structure, tenor, harvest, persistence and permission requirements live exclusively in [TRADE's decision paths](../TRADE.md) and the complete specs they name.
