## Oil-Specific

5. **Oil prices are 24/7.** STATUS.md prices go stale fast. Always `web_search` for live prices before updating.
6. **Crack spreads tell the real story.** Crude price alone is incomplete — refinery margins show where stress concentrates.
7. **Two-phase thesis is the core.** Every data point should be assessed against: "Are we still in Phase 1? Any Phase 2 signals?"
8. **Storage data has reporting lag.** EIA weekly is delayed. Gulf state storage is estimated (JPMorgan, Kpler). Note the lag.
9. **EIA 'product supplied' MISLEADS in weeks 1-4 of a shock.** Secondary stockpiling (wholesale hoarding) inflates apparent demand. True destruction is invisible until wholesale buyers step back. Do NOT call demand destruction early from EIA data. Use aviation cuts + ATA Truck Tonnage as leading indicators instead.
10. **OPEC+ paper quotas ≠ physical production.** In 2021-2022, the gap was 2.7-2.89 mbpd between announced quotas and actual output (Nigeria, Angola, Malaysia couldn't hit targets). Discount paper unwind numbers — only Saudi Arabia and UAE have genuine deployable spare capacity.
11. **Phase 2 price crash triggers at ANNOUNCEMENT, not delivery.** When Hormuz reopens, oil prices will collapse on Day 3-8 (meeting + satellite VLCC loading confirmation), not Week 6-8 (physical delivery). The market is forward-looking. Position SHORT before reopening is confirmed — waiting for barrels to arrive means missing 80% of the move.
12. **Demand destruction timeline in 2026 will be LONGER than historical analogs.** EVs have removed the most price-sensitive marginal drivers from gasoline consumption pool. Remaining demand is essential and inelastic. Historical 20-24 week timeline likely stretches to 28-32+ weeks. This supports Phase 1 duration and delays Phase 2 via demand channel.
13. **Oil shock macro damage is SEQUENTIAL, not simultaneous.** The full transmission chain takes 6-24 months: shock → consumer squeeze → manufacturing jobs → GDP contraction → demand destruction → price crash → E&P credit stress → regional bank stress. Don't expect all links to fire at once. Positioning must align with the stage — Phase 1 (long energy) transitions to Phase 2 (short energy + long consumer discretionary) well before the tail (credit/bank stress).
14. **1986 = the canonical glut/bank crisis analog.** When oil crashes, the danger isn't E&P corporate debt (which is managed) — it's GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION in regional banks. 1986: TX C&I loans 16%+ of assets, no interstate branching → catastrophic (425 TX bank failures, FDIC $15.3B). 2014: same energy exposure, interstate diversification → painful but no systemic failure. The key variable for Phase 2 is: which regional banks have concentrated energy loan books today?
15. **Don't buy outright puts at 100th IV percentile — use bear put spreads.** USO IV at 100th percentile means vol crush will destroy put premium even if oil falls. Buying outright puts when IV is 101% above HV = paying to lose. Bear put spreads neutralize Vega exposure: long put buys direction, short put offsets the vol premium. Optimal structure: 60-90 DTE, 10-15% OTM strikes, 3:1 risk-reward. Execute within 48 hours of Phase 2 trigger confirmation.
16. **Equity stocks LEAD physical commodity rates — exit on announcement, not delivery.** STNG will reprice at escort/ceasefire ANNOUNCEMENT, not when tankers physically transit. The equity market discounts the future; freight derivatives often diverge from spot rates as institutional traders price in resolution. Waiting for physical barrels to move = missing 80% of the re-rating. Same applies to OPEC+ price collapse: sell before reopening is confirmed.
17. **The OPEC+ "5-6M bpd spare" narrative is wrong — always pressure-test official figures.** Confirmed true spare: 4.35M bpd (not 5-6M). 90% is trapped behind Hormuz and physically inaccessible during the crisis. Iraq has zero spare. Russia has negative spare (stranded). Only Saudi (1.84M bpd) and UAE (0.67M bpd) have verified spare — both useless while Hormuz is closed. Never accept cartel self-reporting without cross-referencing IEA secondary sources.
18. **Disambiguate rhetorical from operational announcements before calling Phase 2.** LESSONS #11 says Phase 2 triggers on announcement not delivery — but the announcement must be OPERATIONAL (signed framework, blockade lift, coordinated sovereign action), not RHETORICAL (unilateral minister statement, especially one contradicted by concurrent state action). **Apr 17 2026 case:** Iran FM declared Hormuz "completely open" → Brent -12% intraday paper, but Trump kept US port blockade on Iran "in full force," Lloyd's showed transits still restricted, Dated Brent spread WIDENED to ~$43 (not narrowed), STNG UP +3.4%. Paper overshot; physical didn't confirm. **Verification gate:** require ≥1 of (a) Platts Dated Brent convergence toward futures, (b) Lloyd's vessel transit counts returning to 60-135/day baseline, (c) P&I insurers issuing resumption notices, (d) sovereign action on blockade/naval escort. Tanker equity (STNG) is the fastest sanity check — if tankers aren't repricing down on an "announcement day," the market isn't treating it as operational either.
