## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Oil hoarding is the invisible variable in the Phase 1→2 transition. Three layers of hoarding — sovereign SPR builds, commercial/trader stockpiling, and wholesale secondary inventory builds — collectively distort the demand picture and create a barrel overhang that amplifies the Phase 2 crash.

**Key findings:**
1. The $32 dated-futures spread is ~25-38% hoarding/panic premium, not pure scarcity
2. EIA "product supplied" is inflated by ~200-500K bpd of phantom demand from secondary stockpiling (weeks 1-6 of crisis)
3. Total hoarding across all layers in a crisis of this scale: **300-600M bbl above normal**
4. When hoarded barrels unwind, they add an effective **3-6M bpd** of supply — on top of resumed Gulf flows and OPEC+ spare capacity
5. Historical rule: the unwind retraces **50-75% of the crisis spike within 3 months** of credible resolution
6. China buys crashes, not spikes — they are likely REDUCING spot purchases and buying deferred futures at $109

**Implication for Phase 2:** The crash will be deeper and faster than supply math alone predicts. The hoarding overhang is the hidden amplifier.

## 4. HOW HOARDING DISTORTS EIA DEMAND DATA

### The Problem

EIA "product supplied" is a residual calculation:

```
Product Supplied = Refinery Production + Imports - Exports - Primary Inventory Change
```

EIA only tracks **primary inventories** (refineries, pipeline terminals, major bulk facilities — Form EIA-810/811/812/814). It does NOT track secondary inventories (wholesale distributors, jobbers, commercial tanks). When wholesalers pull product from primary storage to stock their own tanks, EIA counts it as "demand."

**Result:** Apparent demand stays robust or SPIKES during weeks 1-6 of a crisis, creating phantom demand of ~200-500K bpd. True end-user consumption is already declining.

### Historical Evidence

| Crisis | Phantom Demand Duration | When True Demand Became Visible |
|--------|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 1990 Gulf War | ~8-10 weeks | October 1990 (product supplied 7.6M→6.4M bpd) |
| 2011-12 Iran sanctions | ~6-8 weeks (compounded by EIA export methodology error) | Late 2011 after EIA correction |
| 2022 Russia-Ukraine | ~6-8 weeks | Late April/May 2022 |
| **2026 Hormuz (est.)** | **~4-6 weeks** | **Likely May 2026** |

2026 estimate is shorter than historical because physical scarcity limits the ABILITY to hoard — if barrels aren't available (North Sea: no offers), you can't stockpile even if you want to.

### Diagnostic Tools — Detecting Hoarding in EIA Data

| Diagnostic | What to Look For | Data Source |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Refinery inputs vs product supplied** | If inputs FLAT/DECLINING but product supplied RISING = secondary stockpiling | EIA Table 10 |
| **Adjustment factor** | Persistently large positive adjustment = product disappearing into unmeasured storage | EIA Table 1 balancing item |
| **Import surge pattern** | Gasoline/distillate imports spiking = distributors importing directly to fill own tanks | EIA Table 3 |
| **Days of supply vs product supplied** | Both high simultaneously = red flag. Genuine consumption draws down days of supply | EIA Tables 1, 9 |
| **Cushing draw vs total stocks** | Cushing drawing while total holds = crude redistributing to private strategic storage | EIA Tables 1, 4 |

### The Bypass — Leading Indicators That Skip the Distortion

Per existing BRENT research (LESSONS #9, KB-BRT-026, KB-BRT-027):

| Indicator | Lead Time Over EIA | Why It Works |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Airline ASM / capacity cuts | 4-8 weeks | Airlines cut immediately on fuel cost — no inventory buffer, razor margins |
| ATA Truck Tonnage Index | 2-4 weeks | Diesel-dependent, margin-compressed, no lag |
| Initial jobless claims | 1-2 weeks | Direct labor market signal, weekly frequency |
| GasBuddy real-time driving data | Real-time | Bypasses all inventory accounting — measures actual trips |

**Operational rule:** Do NOT use EIA gasoline "product supplied" as demand destruction evidence until week 6+ of the crisis. Use aviation, trucking, and claims data instead.

## 5. THE HOARDING UNWIND — PHASE 2 AMPLIFIER

This is the most operationally important section. When Phase 2 triggers, hoarded barrels hit the market simultaneously with resumed Gulf flows and OPEC+ spare capacity.

### Unwind Mechanics

| Layer | Unwind Speed | Trigger | Volume |
|-------|-------------|---------|--------|
| Wholesale/secondary | **2-4 weeks** (fastest) | Price decline — unsophisticated holders panic-sell | 30-60M bbl |
| Commercial traders | **2-6 weeks** | Resolution signal — they sell FIRST (best intelligence) | 50-100M bbl |
| Refiner excess procurement | **4-8 weeks** | Normalized procurement cycle resumes | 50-100M bbl |
| Floating storage | **2-4 months** | Backwardation collapse, storage economics flip | 100-200M bbl |
| Sovereign SPR refill | **Months** (builds, not draws) | China refills at $70-80 — this puts a FLOOR, not a ceiling | — |

### The Self-Reinforcing Crash

The unwind is a mirror image of the panic-buying feedback loop:

```
Resolution signal
  → Falling price expectation
    → Holders sell to avoid losses
      → Supply surge from destocking
        → Price falls faster
          → More holders sell
            → Overshoot below pre-crisis levels
```

### Effective Supply Addition During Unwind

If 300-600M bbl unwinds over 2-3 months:
- **Low estimate:** 300M bbl / 90 days = **3.3M bpd** of effective additional supply
- **High estimate:** 600M bbl / 75 days = **8.0M bpd** of effective additional supply
- This is ON TOP of: resumed Gulf flows (~8-9M bpd), OPEC+ spare capacity (~3.24M bpd deferred), and SPR releases

**The combined Phase 2 supply wave could exceed 15M bpd above current constrained levels.** The market cannot absorb this without a violent price correction.

### Historical Unwind Outcomes

| Crisis | Peak→Trough | Time to Retrace 50% | Time to Retrace 75% |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| 1990 Gulf War | $41→$20 | 1 day (Desert Storm) | 2 days |
| 2008 super-cycle | $147→$33 | ~3 months | ~5 months |
| 2020 COVID rebound | Storage unwind orderly — demand recovery absorbed it | N/A (unwind was bullish) |
| 2022 Russia-Ukraine | $139→$85 | ~3 months | ~6 months |

**Rule of thumb:** The price decline from peak retraces **50-75% of the crisis spike within 3 months** of a credible resolution signal.

**Exception:** 2020 — the unwind was orderly and NOT bearish because demand recovery simultaneously absorbed the destocking. This exception applies only when the crisis is demand-driven (COVID) not supply-driven. In a supply-driven crisis like 2026, the unwind adds to supply without offsetting demand recovery — it is unambiguously bearish.

### China's Floor

The one moderating factor: China will aggressively buy the crash. In 2015 they added ~300-400K bpd to SPR. In 2020 they added ~1.5-2.0M bpd. At $70-80 Brent, Chinese SPR filling puts a floor under the crash. This floor is significant (~1-2M bpd of demand) but does not prevent the initial overshoot.

- When resolution signal fires, commercial traders sell FIRST (1-2 days)
- Wholesale secondary inventory dumps within 2-4 weeks

- Total effective supply addition: 3-8M bpd from destocking alone

- The Phase 2 crash will be **deeper than supply math alone predicts** because the hoarding overhang (300-600M bbl) releases simultaneously with resumed Gulf flows
- Historical retracement: 50-75% of spike within 3 months

  - 50% retrace from $109 = **$92** (futures)
  - 75% retrace from $109 = **$83** (futures)
  - Overshoot scenario (unwind + OPEC+ unwind + demand destruction): **$65-75**
- China SPR refill at $70-80 provides a floor — the crash doesn't go to $50

- PHASE2_SHORT_PLAYBOOK.md models $85/$75 USO bear put spread
- Hoarding unwind analysis supports this target range — $75 USO (~$65-70 WTI) is achievable if all three forces converge (resumed flows + destocking + demand destruction)
