## 2026-04-30 — To: CARL
**Signal:** Distillate demand +4.8% YoY (4-wk avg) — trucking/goods economy still RUNNING despite Brent $110+
**Detail:** EIA WPSR for week ending Apr 24 (released Apr 29) shows distillate fuel product supplied averaging 4.0 mbpd, **+4.8% YoY** on a 4-week basis. Distillate inventories drew −4.5M bbl this week and now sit **11% below 5-yr average** (worsening from −9% prior). The +4.8% YoY is a hard counter-signal to a pure consumer-collapse / "stagflation already biting" thesis: goods transport is not just holding, it's growing. Combined with motor gasoline +1.2% YoY 4-wk and total products +4.6% YoY, **the demand-side picture is softening only at the gasoline edges, not collapsing.** Jet fuel −4.6% YoY 4-wk is the only product showing genuine destruction (consistent with 20+ airlines cutting capacity).
**Implication for CARL:** the consumer pain is real (gas $4.12 above $4 breakpoint, retail/consumer-discretionary stress) but the macro transmission to goods volumes hasn't fired yet. Either (a) the lag is longer than the front-loaded 12-16 week timeline, (b) hoarding/inventory rebuild is masking weakness in trucking demand, or (c) the goods economy is genuinely more resilient than consumer-discretionary. Worth refining your CARL transmission-chain narrative — Phase 1 is tightening physically (crude −6.2M, Cushing back drawing) but not yet pulling goods demand down.
