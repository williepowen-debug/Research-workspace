## Diplomatic/Military Developments (Aug 7–10, 2026)

### 1. Mecca Joint Defence Agreement signed (Aug 7)
Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan signed a trilateral mutual-defense pact in Mecca (MBS, Erdoğan, Sharif) — an armed attack on any one signatory is to be treated as an attack on all three. Builds on the Sept-2025 Saudi-Pakistan bilateral pact, now extended to include Turkey and (notably) Pakistan's nuclear status. Signed against the backdrop of the ongoing Iran war and renewed Saudi-Houthi fighting in Yemen. [CONF Al Jazeera 8/7; Wikipedia "Mecca Joint Defence Agreement"]

### 2. Second Houthi attack on Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery (Aug 9)
Houthis (Yemen, Iran-aligned) struck the Jazan refinery with a drone Sunday 8/9 — two days after the Mecca pact was signed, and explicitly framed by the Houthis as retaliation for Saudi drone incursions into Saada/Hajjah governorates (Yemen). Saudi energy ministry: fire broke out, was extinguished, no injuries. **This is the SECOND attack on this facility** — Jazan (400,000 bpd capacity) was already shut down since 7/27 after an earlier Houthi strike damaged the IGCC complex and tank farm. Facility status: still offline; this event does not change that (it was already down), but signals the Houthi campaign against Saudi oil infrastructure is continuing/escalating alongside the separate Iran-Hormuz theater. [CONF NBC News, Al Jazeera, NPR, Reuters via Investing.com — all 8/9]

### 3. Trump pivots to "low-keying" / economic pressure (Aug 9–10)
Trump told Axios (8/9) the US is "low-keying" negotiations with Iran — "only semi-negotiating," citing Iran's high inflation and difficulty paying its own troops, and crediting the US naval blockade (in place since April) with deepening the pressure. This is a shift in emphasis from the imminent-strike framing of early August (Trump called off a planned major strike 8/1, citing an imminent deal) toward a longer economic-attrition posture. [CONF CNN, NBC, Axios, Bloomberg, Al Jazeera — all 8/9-10]

### 4. Iran issues new preconditions for full Hormuz reopening (Aug 8)
Iran's Supreme National Security Council said the strait will not fully open until the US "corrects its behavior" — a list of demands (end US attacks, end the naval blockade, withdraw forces, lift sanctions, unfreeze assets, compensate war damage) that go well beyond the narrower Oman-brokered temporary shipping-route framework. Iran and Oman remain close on that narrower deal (joint statement described 8/7 as "in the final stages of review and drafting," per Baqaei) but a **full reopening is now explicitly conditioned on the broader demand list**, not just the transit-route mechanics. [CONF CNN, NBC News — 8/8]

### Net read
No new kinetic escalation in the Hormuz theater itself this weekend (the Jazan strike is the Yemen/Red Sea theater, not Hormuz — same distinction BRENT's own 8/7 STATUS banner drew for the 8/5 Al Mukha sinking). The oil bid is a **repricing of reopening-timeline risk**, not a supply-shock event: markets are reading Trump's economic-pressure pivot + Iran's expanded precondition list as pushing any *full* Hormuz normalization further out, even as the narrower temporary-route deal stays "close." Consistent with the still-live thesis frame — no Path A trigger conditions (signed ceasefire text, full reopening, P&I normalization, escort resumption) have fired.
