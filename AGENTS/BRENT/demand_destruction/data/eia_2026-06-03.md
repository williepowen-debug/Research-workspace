**Read:** the draw is migrating from delivery point to export coast. Crude is being pulled OUT for global delivery even as US imports ramp. **Mildly Brent-bullish vs WTI.**

**Watch:** WTI-Brent spread (currently ~-$2.20 / -$3.27 vs Fri). If PADD-3 export-pull is real, spread WIDENS NEGATIVE (Brent premium expands toward -$4 to -$6). The standing **+$5 US-decoupling threshold** in STATUS sits in the opposite direction — today's signal does NOT support that threshold; it supports Brent-premium-widening as the live channel.
