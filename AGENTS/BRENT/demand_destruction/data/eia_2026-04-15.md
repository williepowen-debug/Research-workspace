- Crisis Week ~6 = end of hoarding distortion window per HOARDING.md framework

### 2. Cushing Reversal — Watch Carefully
- Cushing went from building (+4M bbl/2wk) to drawing (−1.7M bbl this week)
- Prior build was SPR-release volume flowing via pipeline; that flow appears to be slowing
- At −1.7M/week: threshold approaches ~mid-May
- If ceasefire frays and supply re-tightens + SPR releases slow, Cushing draw pace could accelerate
- **Cushing below 20M bbl = WTI dislocation risk (threshold from BRENT domain/REFERENCE_TABLES.md)**

### 3. Crude Surprise Draw vs Consensus
- −0.913M bbl vs +0.2M bbl consensus = meaningful surprise
- Post-ceasefire, market expected builds to continue (SPR→commercial)
- The actual draw suggests either: (a) SPR release pipeline volumes slowing, or (b) refinery demand absorbing more crude (consistent with +0.9pp utilization)
- **Bullish crude price signal** despite ceasefire reducing geopolitical risk premium

### 4. Distillate Deficit Widening
- 5% below 5yr avg and drawing −3.4M bbl/week
