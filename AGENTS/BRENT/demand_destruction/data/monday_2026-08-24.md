The campaign reportedly centers on secondary sanctions against nations/entities that continue purchasing Iranian oil, process its finances, operate related banks, or support shipping/commercial channels — layered on top of the existing naval blockade. Bessent's stated objective: "sever every economic lifeline that sustains the tyrannical regime until Tehran stands alone." Washington's immediate demands: end Iran's nuclear program and fully reopen the Strait of Hormuz. [CONF CNN, Fox News, Euronews, NPR, WVIA — all Aug 23-24]

### 2. Iran threatens "seismic" retaliation; warns neighbors against joining sanctions (Aug 22)

Top security chief Mohsen Rezaei separately threatened to halt ALL oil flow through the Persian Gulf if any of Iran's neighbors join the US economic crackdown: "we will not allow a single drop of oil to pass." [CONF CNN Aug 22]

Read alongside item #2, this is Iran demonstrating continued selective-access leverage even as it threatens a total shutoff. [CONF Al Jazeera, The National — Aug 22]

Separately, the US DOE said oil traffic through Hormuz has averaged 8-9M bpd via "dark transits" — tankers running with transponders off to move crude out of the Persian Gulf.
