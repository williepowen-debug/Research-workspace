## REFINERY DYNAMICS — HOARDING UNWIND CHECK

**Hoarding window status: CLOSED (Crisis week 7-8)**

TRACKER prior note: "If inputs declining but product supplied rising, that's hoarding"

This week:
- Refinery inputs: **DECLINING** (-55K bpd crude runs)
- Gasoline stocks: **DECLINING** (-4.6M bbl)
- Gasoline product supplied: **PENDING**

Pattern interpretation: Declining runs + declining gasoline stocks = possible **hoarding unwind**. Distributors are selling from excess inventory (built in weeks 1-6) rather than ordering from refineries. This INFLATES near-term product supplied while stocks draw. As hoarding inventory exhausts, product supplied will decline to reflect true consumption.

If this interpretation is correct:
- The Apr 10 +7.4% YoY weekly was the PEAK of hoarding-inflated product supplied
- Apr 17 and beyond will start showing the real demand picture
- The stock draw (-4.6M gasoline) is the hoarding unwind in action

**Flag for CARL:** Gasoline hoarding unwind beginning. Real demand destruction signal expected to emerge May-June in EIA data. Consumer spending transmission will lag further.
