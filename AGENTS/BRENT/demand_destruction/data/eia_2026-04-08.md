### 2. Refinery Input / Product Supplied Divergence — Watch Carefully
- Refinery inputs declining (-219K bpd WoW) 
- Product supplied still +0.8% YoY
- This divergence is the hoarding signal: the product supplied is coming from inventory, not current production
- If this pattern continues for 2+ more weeks, secondary stockpiles are being drawn down, and real demand destruction will become visible when those stockpiles are exhausted

### 3. Cushing Build vs Prior Draws — SPR Fingerprint
- The dramatic reversal from -18.7M bbl (Mar 12-27) to +3.7M (Apr 3) likely reflects SPR releases finally reaching commercial storage
- This is consistent with the 6-8 week delay from SPR authorization to commercial delivery
- Does NOT indicate supply normalization — it's government inventory transferred to commercial ledger

### 4. Import Surge (+12.8% YoY) Partially Masking Supply Crisis
- Atlantic Basin crude (North Sea, West Africa, US domestic) being diverted to replace Gulf supply
- This is an unsustainable substitution: North Sea grades at record premiums (Statfjord +$17.60), volumes finite
- When Atlantic Basin substitution capacity is exhausted, supply crisis will re-intensify
