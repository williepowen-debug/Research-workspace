## Diplomatic/Military Developments (Aug 10–17, 2026)

### 1. Trump says he will declare the Strait of Hormuz US territory (Aug 14)
At a rally at a police academy in Garden City, New York, Trump said: "After we finish defeating Iran, which is being very badly defeated – pretty soon I'll be declaring the Hormuz Strait a territory of the United States." Unclear whether this represents an actual policy shift or rally rhetoric; Oman and Iran jointly administer the strait's territorial waters, and Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi rejected the claim, saying the waterway "cannot be seized with a tweet, an aircraft carrier, an executive order, or a campaign speech." [CONF SCMP, Al Jazeera, Jerusalem Post, The Hill, Asia Times — all Aug 14–15]

### 2. Cluster of THREE tanker-attack incidents in Hormuz (Aug 13–15)
Two ADNOC (UAE state oil co.) vessels were attacked transiting Hormuz on Thursday 8/13. A third incident followed Friday 8/15: UKMTO reported an "unknown projectile" struck the hull of a bulk carrier; crew reported safe, no damage assessment given at the time. UAE's Foreign Ministry accused Iran's IRGC of "piracy," calling the strait a "tool of economic coercion and blackmail" and the attacks a "direct threat" to the region. Iran claimed official responsibility for the strikes and warned ships against attempting to cross. [CONF Ship & Bunker, search sources Aug 15]

### 3. Third Houthi strike on Aramco's Jazan-area refinery complex (Aug 13)
Houthis fired two drones at Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery (Red Sea coast, 400,000 bpd capacity, already offline since 7/27) — the second attack on the facility in under a week (after 8/9) and the third targeting of Aramco facilities in the area since 7/25. Houthi source framed it as retaliation for Saudi airspace incursions into Yemen's Saada/Hajjah governorates. [CONF Bloomberg headline (403-blocked, not directly read), Business Standard, Yemen Monitor — all Aug 13–14]

### 4. Oil prices surged ~5-6% on the attack cluster (Aug 15–16)
The combination of items 2 and 3 (plus the standing Hormuz-reopening-uncertainty backdrop) pushed Brent above $88/bbl mid-week, a ~5-6% jump reported by multiple outlets. Diesel margins were also reported higher on the renewed Jazan disruption (product-side, not this desk's instrument). [CONF search sources Aug 15–16 — headline-level only, not independently re-derived from settle data this run]

### 5. Novorossiysk Sheskharis terminal suspends crude exports — NON-HORMUZ, flagged for completeness (Aug 12–14)
Russia's Sheskharis terminal at Novorossiysk (Black Sea, ~700,000 bpd of Russian crude export capacity) suspended crude loadings 8/14 following a large-scale Ukrainian drone attack on the port 8/12 and a renewed drone-threat warning 8/14; a tanker scheduled to load departed empty. This is a Russia/Ukraine-theater supply item, separate from the Hormuz/Iran thesis this desk owns, but material to global crude supply and flagged for completeness per the task's scope.
