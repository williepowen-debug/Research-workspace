## Diplomatic/Military Developments (Weekend May 2–4)

- **May 2 (Sat):** OPEC+ agreed in principle to modest output hike, per Bloomberg. UAE formally quit OPEC+ (attendance at May 3 meeting was first without UAE).

- **May 3 (Sun):** OPEC+ formally announced **188K bpd output increase** (symbolic; first meeting without UAE). Breakdown: Saudi Arabia +62K, Russia +62K, Iraq +26K, Kuwait +16K, Kazakhstan +10K, Algeria +6K, Oman +5K. Al Jazeera characterized it as "symbolic" given Hormuz closure limits actual delivery capacity. [CNBC, Al Jazeera]

- **May 3 (Sun):** Iran confirmed it **received US response** to its 14-point peace proposal (conveyed via Pakistan). Tehran is reviewing Washington's reply. Iran FM: "We are examining the US response carefully." [CNBC]

- **May 3 (Sun):** Trump: US is having **"very positive discussions"** with Iran. [CNN]

- **May 4 (today — MAJOR):** Trump launched **"Project Freedom"** — US military begins escorting commercial ships through the Strait of Hormuz. Scale: 100+ aircraft, 15,000 personnel. First convoys underway. [CNBC, Invezz, ABC7, Al Jazeera]
  - Iran immediately stated Project Freedom **"violates the ceasefire"** — top Iranian official Ebrahim Azizi warned US. [Al Jazeera]
  - Iranian state media claimed **2 missiles struck a US frigate** in Hormuz after it "ignored warnings." US military **denied** any vessel was struck. [Al Jazeera, US CENTCOM]
  - Ceasefire status: **EXTREMELY FRAGILE** — active kinetic incidents concurrent with diplomatic track.

**P&I insurance status:** NOT resumed as of this pull. Key Path A criterion unmet.

Project Freedom is the most significant US military action in Hormuz since the war began. It represents a unilateral decision to force open the strait rather than wait for a diplomatic framework. The dual track (Project Freedom + positive diplomatic discussions) creates a complex dynamic:
