## HOARDING DIAGNOSTIC

**No hoarding distortion. Inputs and demand aligned:**
- Refinery utilization: 91.7% (+1.6pp WoW) — refineries running MORE
- Refinery inputs: ~16.3M bpd est (from utilization × ~18M operable capacity)
- Gasoline production: 9.8M bpd (↑ per reporting) — production rising
- Gasoline PS 4-wk: 8.9M bpd — demand LOWER than production
- Gasoline stocks: −4.084M bbl — but production > demand, so who's buying?

**Possible explanations for stocks falling despite production > demand:**
1. Exports drawing inventories (post-Hormuz rerouting to Europe/Asia)
2. Regional distribution imbalances (PADD-level effects)
3. Blend component shifts (ethanol vs finished gasoline accounting)

This is worth monitoring. If gasoline is being exported in volume, reported product supplied (domestic proxy) would understate actual global demand but overstate domestic stock draws.
