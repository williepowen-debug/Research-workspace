## HEADLINE — 5 THINGS THAT MATTER

1. **BIG CRUDE DRAW: −6.2M bbl** to 459.5M (vs consensus −0.2M). Reverses last week's +1.925M import-surge build. Crude imports fell −329K bpd to 5.8M bpd — the surge was a one-week anomaly.

2. **CUSHING RESUMED DRAWING: −796K bbl.** From 30.6M → ~29.8M. The 20M-bbl operational-minimum trajectory is **back on** at ~−800K/wk pace (≈12 weeks to threshold). Prior week's +806K reversal was noise, not a trend break.

3. **REFINERY UTILIZATION 89.6% (-2.8pp from 92.4%).** Crude inputs +85K bpd (16.1 mbpd) but %util dropped — implies operable-capacity recalc post permanent closures (Wilmington, Benicia). Either way, **refineries running less while products draw hard** = tightening signal.

4. **GASOLINE DEMAND HOLDING — Path B Trigger #2 NOT firing.** 4-week avg gasoline product supplied: **9.0 mbpd, +1.2% YoY**. Distillate +4.8% YoY (trucking demand RISING). The post-Easter base effect did not produce a false-negative collapse.

5. **JET FUEL −4.6% YoY** (4-wk avg). The airline-cut narrative (Lufthansa 20K flights, 20+ carriers) is feeding through to data. Closest of any product to the −5% destruction threshold. Approaching but not triggered.

## CRUDE INVENTORY ANALYSIS — IMPORT-SURGE REVERSAL

Last week (Apr 17): +1.925M build driven by +1.21M bpd import surge (consensus had expected −1.2M draw).
This week (Apr 24): −6.2M draw with imports falling −329K bpd (back to 5.8 mbpd).

**Diagnosis:** the Apr 17 build was a **one-week artifact** — likely IEA-coordinated cargo timing or ceasefire-window arrivals — not a trend change. Underlying balance is tight: imports normalizing + refinery activity steady = inventory drawing again. This is consistent with Phase 1 thesis (squeeze persisting) and with Brent's recovery from $87.51 → $110.90 over the same period.

## HOARDING UNWIND — VERDICT

Last week's hypothesis: declining refinery runs + declining gasoline stocks = distributors selling from hoarded inventory rather than ordering from refineries. As hoarding exhausts, weekly product supplied normalizes to true consumption.

**This week supports it:**
- Gasoline stocks: −6.1M bbl (continuing draw)
- Refinery util: dropped to 89.6%
- 4-wk gasoline YoY: +1.2% (down from peak +7.4% weekly Apr 10)

**The hoarding bulge is deflating into genuine but mildly positive demand growth.** Real demand-destruction signal cannot emerge until hoarding is fully exhausted (likely 2-4 more weeks). Watch May 6 and May 13 prints.
