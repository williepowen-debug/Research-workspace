## Kernel
20261015-008 headlined "Russia lifts its diesel export ban", relaying Energy Intelligence (10/13), and its body said the ban "has been lifted". YURI's primary read (government.ru news/60156, read 10/12) shows that this over-states it. The government granted a PARTIAL permission, 500,000 t "at this stage", effective 10/10, under government-coordinated contracting. Government Resolution No 1252 (signed 9/29) keeps the producer export restriction to 31.10.2026 and the broader restriction to 31.01.2027. The signed act/annex for the partial permission is still unlocated (YURI; DEWEY's 10/13 final concurs). WALTER could not re-open government.ru this evening and relays YURI's read with attribution.

Direction: WEAKEN. Any downstream read built on "the ban is gone" over-states the Russian supply return. The volumes in play are a permission path plus announced intent, not a lifted regime. The dated test inside the window is No 1252's producer restriction expiring October31: the Government extends it, lets it lapse or replaces it. Today's related items are consistent with "partial permission": SIG-W-20261021-004 (Leruo/Dolunay departures) and the Kremlin October21 Putin line on a first 40,000 t "in two to three days" (announced intent, SIG-W-20261021-017).

BRENT ACTION: replace "lifted" with "partial permission" in any diesel-supply note that cites 008. OSPREY/DEWEY/HAWK/HENRY/RED/PROME INFO.
