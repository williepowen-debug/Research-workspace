# Hurricane Isaias: northern Gulf landfall late Fri 10/9–early Sat 10/10; 25% of US Gulf oil shut in; Florida Panhandle under warning

**What changed (dated):** Isaias became the season's first Atlantic hurricane late Wed 10/7 (Category 1, 75 mph; CBS/ABC relays of NHC). Landfall is forecast **late Fri 10/9 to early Sat 10/10 between Gulfport MS and Panama City FL**, with the track drifting **east toward Pensacola** in the latest runs; physics models pull west, AI models push east. Peak-intensity forecasts range from high-end Category 2 (~110 mph) to near Category 3 before weakening at landfall. Hurricane **warning** from Ocean Springs MS to the Bay–Gulf county line FL; surge 5–7 ft from Mobile Bay to Indian Pass FL. DeSantis declared a state of emergency for **25 north-Florida counties** on Tue 10/6 evening.

**Oil:** the offshore regulator (Marine Minerals Administration, formerly BSEE) reported **511,619 b/d of Gulf oil = 25.08%** and ~350 MMcf/d of gas = 16.37% shut in as of Wed 10/7; 8 of 371 manned platforms evacuated. Shell is shutting in Mars, Olympus, Ursa, Vito and Appomattox; Chevron four facilities; BP removing non-essential staff (Anadolu/Splash/Reuters relays). The Thursday 10/8 crude move (Brent Dec ~$104.70, +4.5% at ~12:00Z, WALTER fetch.py named contract) is attributed by wires to BOTH Hormuz tanker attacks and this storm — do not assign the whole move to either.
