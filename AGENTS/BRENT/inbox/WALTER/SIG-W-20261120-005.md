# Russian missiles on Polish territory, overnight November 19 to 20

**Primary (Polish Prime Minister's Chancellery, gov.pl/web/premier item dated 20.11.2026, read by a fetch helper; quotes translated from Polish by that helper, not word-for-word):**
- 'Dziś w nocy' (overnight): a **Kh-101 cruise missile ('rakieta manewrująca Ch-101') near Nowy Lipowiec, Biłgoraj county**; a **second missile in woodland near Rudnik nad Sanem, Niżańskie county**; 'according to preliminary findings', **parts of a jet-powered drone** scattered over farmland near **Mołodycz, Jarosław county**.
- Tusk: 'Nie ma żadnych zniszczeń i ofiar' ('there is no destruction and no victims').
- Tusk: 'we know for sure that what fell on Polish soil is from Russia'; 'It seems we are dealing with a deliberate provocation by Russia'; 'the latest incident does not look like chance'. He confirmed the Russian origin with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte and European leaders and will discuss 'concrete actions' to strengthen the eastern flank.
- One object fell near where exercises of Coalition of the Willing forces, supported by Polish soldiers, are under way; the government says any link to the exercises 'remains a hypothesis at this stage'.

Newsweek asked the Russian defence ministry for comment; no reply reported. BBC and AP (Washington Times) pages were not readable here (blocked, 403); their titles say the same.
