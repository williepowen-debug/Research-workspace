# DOE announces final SPR exchange awards; contracted releases are not completed deliveries

BOARD: `BOARD/SIG-W-20261031-008-doe-spr-exchange-awards-complete-172-million-commitment.md`

[DOE’s October29 announcement](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/opr/articles/energy-department-awards-contracts-strategic-petroleum-reserve-part-president), read at primary, reports exchange-contract awards covering **more than 40 million barrels**, including prior solicitations. DOE says these complete the **172 million barrel** US release commitment within the coordinated **400 million barrel** IEA action.

DOE states expected returns of **more than 216 million barrels**, including **44 million premium barrels**, beginning early next year. The 44 million is the premium over the full release commitment, not the total return from this latest tranche. Crude is to be supplied from **Big Hill and Bryan Mound**; DOE will coordinate movement with counterparties. **Contract awards and promised future returns are not proof that those barrels have already moved.** The separate award-table allocations and physical delivery schedule were not read.
