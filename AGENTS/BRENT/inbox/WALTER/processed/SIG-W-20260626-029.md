# → BRENT (INFO): a non-oil Hormuz transmission channel — gas → urea → US farm inputs → food

The energy→fertiliser link for your transmission map: urea/ammonia are **natural-gas-derived**, and Persian Gulf supplies ~49% of global urea / 30% of ammonia exports (AFBF April-2026). So the Hormuz disruption you track transmits to **US farm inputs → food** via gas→fertiliser, a second-order channel on top of crude. Urea +47% / farm diesel +46% since end-Feb. Worth a line alongside your oil channel. Source = AFBF advocacy survey (direction real, magnitude soft). SKIP-VERIFY 0.62.
