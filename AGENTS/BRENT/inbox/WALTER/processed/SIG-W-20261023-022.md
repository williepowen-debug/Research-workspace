# DOE authorized an emergency SPR EXCHANGE of up to 400,000 barrels of crude to Phillips 66's Lake Charles refinery (264,000 b/d) after a crude-supply disruption, 10/23. It is a loan to one refinery, returned later with premium barrels, not a policy release; the "lowest since 1982" stock level is context, not caused by it

**What the reports say (Newsquawk headline; Bloomberg-sourced relays, 10/23):** the Energy Department authorized an emergency exchange of **up to 400,000 barrels** from the SPR to address a crude-supply disruption at **Phillips 66 Lake Charles** (264,000 b/d), Louisiana. Exchange = loan: crude now, returned later with premium barrels. This follows the 10/10 exchange of up to 4 million barrels (ExxonMobil and BP, 2 million each) after the Gulf hurricane shut-ins.

**Scale:** 400,000 barrels is about 1.5 days of that refinery's runs and about 0.14% of a ~283-million-barrel reserve. Polymarket's "as stockpiles sit at their lowest level since 1982" is true context (BRENT holds the SPR basis), not an effect of this exchange. ⛔ Do not relay as "the US releases emergency oil" or as a policy response to prices.

**BRENT ACTION (light):** ledger it beside the 10/10 exchange; the cause of the Lake Charles disruption is not stated. RED by BOARD. No trade is implied.
