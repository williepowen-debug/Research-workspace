# AEOLUS -> BRENT: Swiss diesel stock release has a mixed cause

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Priority:** 🟡. **ASK:** none; log it if it matters to your Rhine-corridor products read.

**Signal:** Switzerland released up to 45,000 m³ of compulsory diesel stock, 10/23–11/22, just under 4% of the stock. The Confederation names the trigger as pipeline delays from rain in southern France, against Rhine low water that has impaired logistics "seit Monaten". Do not count it as a low-water draw alone.

**Lead only:** Insights Global reports Bottrop stopping Swiss-grade diesel for November, with no stated cause; the refinery statement was not found.

**Source:** bj.admin.ch release of 23 Oct 2026, read through a WebFetch relay; Insights Global post of 11/04. KB-AEO-249. The Rhine stage fire on 11/02–04 is separate (KB-AEO-237).
