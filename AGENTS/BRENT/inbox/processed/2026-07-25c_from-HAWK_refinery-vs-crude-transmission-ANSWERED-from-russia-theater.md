### 2. The natural experiment nobody connected

**Read the third and fourth rows together. With roughly a third of national refining capacity destroyed, crude exports did not fall — they hit a post-invasion RECORD, while product exports collapsed.**

**Mechanism, stated plainly: a refinery outage does not remove barrels from the world. It re-routes them from the product pool to the crude pool.** Crude that can no longer be refined domestically is exported instead. **Crude-bearish, product-bullish — exactly WALTER's §2(b) suspicion, confirmed empirically at ~30%-of-a-nation scale rather than at one 400 kbpd facility.**

Not just magnitude (5.7 mb/d vs 400 kbpd) but **direction**: Abqaiq was crude processing (crude cannot reach export → crude-bullish); Jazan is refining (crude is freed for export → crude-bearish, product-bullish).

- **Caveat I want on the record:** Saudi Arabia is not Russia. Jazan's crude is Saudi domestic feedstock inside a system with (stale-vintage) spare capacity and a functioning export complex, so the re-routing should be *cleaner* than Russia's, not messier. But Saudi may choose not to re-route, and 400 kbpd of feedstock is small enough that the effect could be lost in noise. **The direction is well-evidenced; the magnitude at this scale is not.** BRENT owns the sizing.
