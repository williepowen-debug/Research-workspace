# BRENT OUTBOX — Mar 24

## Overnight Supply Developments (Mar 23-24)

### 1. Primorsk Strike — Russia's Largest Baltic Oil Terminal
- **What:** Ukrainian drone swarm (35+ drones over Leningrad region, 249 total over Russia overnight) hit Primorsk oil terminal. Multiple fuel reservoirs set ablaze. Ukraine General Staff confirmed strike.
- **Capacity:** Primorsk exports >1M bpd crude (Urals flagship) + high-quality diesel. Exported 16.8M metric tons in 2025.
- **Status:** SHUT as of Mar 24. No restart timeline.
- **Ust-Luga:** Also suspended Sunday, RESTARTED Monday after drone alert lifted. Handles ~700K bpd. No confirmed damage.
- **Ufa refinery (Bashneft):** Struck simultaneously — fires reported. ~200K bpd capacity.
- **Context:** Novorossiysk (Black Sea, Russia's largest southern port) also hit earlier in March. **All three of Russia's major petroleum export outlets attacked in the same month.** This is unprecedented kinetic escalation against Russian energy infrastructure.

### 2. Valero Port Arthur Refinery Fire
- **What:** Explosion + fire at Valero's Port Arthur, TX refinery — Monday night (Mar 23). Likely caused by industrial heater failure.
- **Capacity:** 380,000 bpd — Valero's 2nd largest refinery, one of the biggest on the Gulf Coast.
- **Status:** Fire extinguished, shelter-in-place lifted. No injuries. BUT: damage assessment and restart timeline TBD. Explosion + fire at a unit this large typically = weeks offline minimum.
- **Product impact:** Port Arthur produces gasoline, diesel, jet fuel. Gulf Coast is ~50% of US refining capacity. Even temporary loss of 380K bpd tightens product markets when crude is already $107+.

This is no longer just a Gulf story. Russian export capacity is being systematically degraded across BOTH coasts (Baltic + Black Sea) while US domestic refining takes a hit. The supply picture has gotten materially worse since Mar 22.
