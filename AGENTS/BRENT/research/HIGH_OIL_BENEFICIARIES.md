## THESIS FRAMING

At $90 Brent / $80 WTI, every US basin operates at 30-100% above breakeven. This is not marginal profitability — it's a free cash flow explosion. The question isn't *if* they benefit, it's *who captures the most leverage per dollar invested*:

1. **E&P (upstream)** — maximum operating leverage. Revenue rises linearly with price; costs are largely fixed.
2. **Oilfield Services** — secondary beneficiary. More drilling → more revenue, but with lag.
3. **Midstream/Pipeline** — volume-driven, commodity-insulated. Steady cash flows, less upside.
4. **LNG Exporters** — Qatar curtailment creates US LNG pricing power. Indirect oil/gas linkage.
5. **Tankers** — direct beneficiary of rerouting. War risk = structural ton-mile increase.
