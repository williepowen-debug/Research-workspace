For the week ending November 06, 2026, U.S. refineries processed 17.0 million barrels per day (b/d), up 322,000 b/d from the previous week, at 95.2% capacity utilization. Gasoline output averaged 9.6 million b/d, and distillate production decreased to 5.1 million b/d. 

Crude oil imports increased 325,000 b/d to 6.7 million b/d, and the four-week average of 6.3 million b/d is 14.0% above the year-ago level. Gasoline imports averaged 202,000 b/d; distillate imports averaged 99,000 b/d. 

Commercial crude oil inventories (excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve) increased 4.7 million barrels to 414.4 million barrels, 4% below the five-year average. Gasoline inventories increased 1.8 million barrels, 1% above the five-year average. Distillate inventories increased 4.1 million barrels, 8% below the five-year average. Propane/propylene inventories increased 0.1 million barrels, 12% above the five-year average. Total commercial petroleum inventories increased by 11.4 million barrels for the week.

Over the past four weeks, total product supplied averaged 20.8 million b/d, up 0.9% year over year. The four-week average for gasoline product supplied decreased 1.0% year over year to 8.7 million b/d, while the four-week average for distillate product supplied increased 4.2% to 3.9 million b/d. The four-week average for jet fuel product supplied increased 1.8% year over year.
