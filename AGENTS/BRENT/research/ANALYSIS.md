## What Contradicts or Complicates the Two-Phase Thesis

### Minor Complications
1. **ADCOP being more intact than modeled** slightly reduces the urgency on UAE side. The worst-case scenario (ADCOP fully disabled) would make UAE curtailment contemporaneous with Kuwait (mid-March), which would be more explosive than our current Mar 31 model. The corrected 1.5 mbpd ADCOP gives UAE more time — potentially the market slightly overprices UAE curtailment urgency relative to Kuwait urgency in the 2-week window.

2. **50% Hormuz reopening extends Kuwait to Apr 3** — If any negotiated partial transit arrangement emerges (e.g., UN-brokered corridor), this shifts the Phase 1 peak timing. Our model accounts for this but the market may rally on partial news.

3. **US munitions constraint (BRT-19) creates a wildcard:** If the US decides to de-escalate to preserve interceptor stocks, this could trigger an unexpected diplomatic overture even without Iranian consent. Araghchi said "not seeking ceasefire WITH WASHINGTON" — but if US signals step-down and Iran declares unilateral "victory," there's a face-saving off-ramp scenario that doesn't require Iran to come to the table. LOW probability (~15%) but exists.

4. **Iran asymmetric viability is strong** — The ballistic threat degradation is real, but Iran doesn't need ballistics to maintain commercial deterrence. Shahed-136 drones + IRGCN fast boats + occasional cruise missile = sufficient to keep APs at 1.5% and institutional shipping sidelined. This means the "Iran runs out of missiles = ships resume" thesis that some analysts propose is **wrong**. The threat doesn't require expensive missiles.

### Two-Phase Thesis: INTACT
The core logic — Phase 1 squeeze to $100+ → Phase 2 demand destruction / OPEC+ flush — remains intact. New reports strengthen Phase 1 duration case and add new Phase 1 complexity (Ras Laffan, food, fertilizer). Phase 2 timing is unchanged (earliest Q2-Q3 visibility).
