Who We Are
ADNOC is a leading diversified energy group taking transformative steps to make today’s energy cleaner while investing in the clean energies of tomorrow. Our network of fully-integrated businesses operates across the energy value chain, helping us to responsibly meet the demands of an ever-changing energy market.
/-/media/adnoc-v2/adnoc-enhancement-v2/images/mega-navigation/our-story/1-overview.ashx?h=384&w=280&hash=16F177CEE747B4F5BAA9FC9413877E85
Our Strategy
Maximum Energy. Minimum Emissions. Under the leadership of His Excellency Dr. Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber, ADNOC Managing Director and Group CEO, we are on a journey of transformation, placing decarbonization and sustainability at the heart of our strategy. We are supplying the world with the energy that it needs, delivering tangible progress towards the energy transition and future-proofing our business. Our growth strategy is focused on maximizing our competitive advantage as a leading low-cost and lower-carbon oil and gas producer and is underpinned by our commitment to 100% HSE and sustainability.

ADNOC Drilling
We are the largest drilling company in the Middle East by rig fleet size, and publicly listed on ADX in October 2021. We are a crucial link in the upstream value chain, enabling ADNOC to unlock the UAE’s conventional and unconventional oil and gas resources reliably and efficiently.
/-/media/adnoc-v2/adnoc-enhancement-v2/images/mega-navigation/sustainability/sustainability10-beat-the-heat.ashx?h=384&w=280&hash=9638237287ED6E500B57DBB763D748E3
ADNOC Offshore
We are the largest offshore oil and gas producer in the UAE, accounting for about half of Abu Dhabi’s total oil production capacity, with operations across nine established fields, six artificial islands, three natural islands, eight offshore super complexes and over 400 offshore structures.
/-/media/adnoc-v2/adnoc-enhancement-v2/images/mega-navigation/our-business/7-offshore.ashx?h=384&w=280&hash=20A0221B38A870E5A4044D73A2231EF6
ADNOC Onshore
We are the legacy producer within ADNOC, with our historic operations dating back as far as 1939, and home of Murban grade crude. We manage 11 onshore fields and two world-class export terminals, with our concession area spanning 12,000 square meters.
/-/media/adnoc-v2/adnoc-enhancement-v2/images/mega-navigation/our-business/8-onshore.ashx?h=384&w=280&hash=68CC09F8D5F4AD7A4AD462A91DAAF527
ADNOC Sour Gas
We operate the Shah Gas Field and Plant, located 210km southwest of Abu Dhabi city, producing natural (sales) gas, ethane, NGLs, condensate and sulfur. As of 2021, we account for about 5% of the world's total sulfur production and we are the world’s largest producer of granulated sulfur from a single site.
/-/media/adnoc-v2/adnoc-enhancement-v2/images/mega-navigation/our-business/9-sourgas.ashx?h=384&w=280&hash=6F2761E2DD24C8F0C4492EA30CA39A16
Al Dhafra Petroleum
We focus on developing new oil and gas fields in an onshore concession block located in the southeast of Abu Dhabi, supporting ADNOC’s ambition to cost-efficiently unlock and maximize value from all of Abu Dhabi’s oil and gas resources.

Al Yasat Petroleum

RUWAIS REFINERY
Situated on the coast of the Arabian Gulf, some 245 kilometres west of Abu Dhabi City, our refinery in Ruwais is perfectly placed to serve our export clients. It refines over 922,000 bpsd and is one of the largest and most advanced refineries of its kind in the world.
The Ruwais Refinery was inaugurated in 1982 by the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan (may Allah, Almighty rest his soul in peace) and has grown significantly in the decades since to become the world’s fourth largest single-site refinery.  Products produced here include liquefied petroleum gas, unleaded gasoline, diesel, naphtha, jet fuel, group III base oils, MTO, propylene  and speciality products like carbon black and calcined coke.
The refinery possesses three  jetties with multiple berths to allow several large and small cargo ships to load simultaneously. Several long-term upgrade projects are underway, including expanding the range and volume of crude oil that we refine.
PIPELINE AND TERMINAL OPERATIONS
Our Pipeline and Terminal facility distributes our finished products across the emirate of Abu Dhabi. The complex is located in Musaffah, some 22 kilometres from the center of Abu Dhabi City. From here, trucks transport our fuels by road, while a 1,600-kilometer pipeline network supplies our customers in Al Dhafra, Shuweihat, Al Ain, Madinat Zayed, and the Abu Dhabi International Airport.
