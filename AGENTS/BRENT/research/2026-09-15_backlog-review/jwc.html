Joint War Committee Risk List - Listed Areas


The London Market's Joint War Committee publishes a list of areas of perceived enhanced risk in relation to hull war, strikes, terrorism and related perils. Ports, places and coasts which feature on the list will have been assessed by an independent consultant to exceed an enhanced risk benchmark established by them.
