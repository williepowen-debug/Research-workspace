About
​
Capitalizing on its unique position next to the oil
berths of the Port of Fujairah,
VTTI in Fujairah offers
a full range of energy storage and services for a
variety of customers.
VTTI is able to accommodate
any size or type of vessel whilst offering facilities for
products right across the barrel.
Value Proposition
​
Manages and operates a refinery (100,000
BPD) and operates crude processing units
(80,000 BPD). Providing large captive and
commercial storage demand.
​
Dedicated cargo lines to the Port of
Fujairah. Terminals in the Fujairah Oil
Industry Zone (FOIZ) are inter-connected
via the port’s matrix manifold system and
dedicated pipeline to some commercial
terminals.
