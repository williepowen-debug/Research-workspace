For the week ending October 30, 2026, U.S. refineries processed 16.7 million barrels per day (b/d), up 199,000 b/d from the previous week, at 94.5% capacity utilization. Gasoline output averaged 9.7 million b/d, and distillate production decreased to 5.2 million b/d. 

Crude oil imports decreased 1,000 b/d to 6.4 million b/d, although the four-week average of 6.1 million b/d is 9.5% above the year-ago level. Gasoline imports averaged 351,000 b/d; distillate imports averaged 117,000 b/d. 

Commercial crude oil inventories (excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve) decreased 0.9 million barrels to 409.8 million barrels, 4% below the five-year average. Gasoline inventories decreased 0.2 million barrels, 2% below the five-year average. Distillate inventories decreased 3.6 million barrels, 13% below the five-year average. Propane/propylene inventories decreased 1.6 million barrels, 10% above the five-year average. Total commercial petroleum inventories decreased by 8.2 million barrels for the week.

Over the past four weeks, total product supplied averaged 21.2 million b/d, up 4.2% year over year. The four-week average for gasoline product supplied decreased 0.4% year over year to 8.6 million b/d, while the four-week average for distillate product supplied increased 8.7% to 4.2 million b/d. The four-week average for jet fuel product supplied decreased 2.8% year over year.
