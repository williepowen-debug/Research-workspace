### 2. EIA Weekly Data Will Mislead for First 4-8 Weeks

**What the research says:** Secondary stockpiling by wholesale distributors and large commercial buyers artificially inflates "product supplied" during the initial weeks of any price shock. True underlying demand destruction is invisible in primary data for 4-8 weeks.

**For positioning:** Do NOT short crude on the basis of early EIA weekly gasoline demand data appearing weak. The data structure is noisy. Instead, watch:
- **Airline capacity cuts** (leading by 4-8 weeks — aviation is the best early warning)
- **ATA Truck Tonnage Index** deceleration
- **Diesel PPI spike** (middle distillates stressed first)

### 4. OPEC+ Paper Quotas ≠ Physical Production

**What the research says:** During the 2021-2022 unwind, OPEC+ was underproducing its stated quotas by 2.7 mbpd (April 2022) to 2.89 mbpd (July 2022). Countries like Nigeria, Angola, Malaysia couldn't hit targets due to years of capital underinvestment and reservoir damage from prolonged shut-ins.

**Only Saudi Arabia and UAE have genuine deployable spare capacity.** Everyone else adds headline but not barrels.

**For positioning:** The advertised 3.24 mbpd Phase 2 flush needs to be discounted. Real incremental supply from OPEC+ reopening is probably 1.5-2.0 mbpd in the first months. This means the Phase 2 price crash, while sharp (announcement-driven), may not be as sustained as a naive 3.24 mbpd addition would imply. Phase 2 short trade may be a shorter-duration position than originally modeled.

Additionally, the compensation mechanism (Iraq, Russia, Kazakhstan must make deeper cuts through June 2026) further reduces effective net new supply from the unwind.

### 5. Kuwait Precedent: Kinetic Infrastructure Destruction = Years, Not Months

**What the research says:** In 1991, when 750 of Kuwait's 943 wells were destroyed, the last fire wasn't capped until November 1991 (9 months post-war). Full production recovery to pre-war levels took until 1993-1994. Complete infrastructure rehabilitation took ~7 years.

**Contrast:** Saudi Arabia (intact infrastructure, political will) ramped 5.4 → 8.5 mbpd in 90 days = 1 mbpd/month.

**For positioning:** If the current conflict causes physical infrastructure damage (as opposed to merely curtailment from storage full), Phase 2 supply recovery timeline extends from months to years. This is the **tail risk scenario** that makes Phase 1 positions much more valuable as longer-duration holds. HAWK should be asked to assess infrastructure damage risk.
