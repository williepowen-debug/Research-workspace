PG Crude Flows Analytics by LVision

The loadings data (vessel count and crude oil volumes) is compiled by LVision based on Open Sources and analyzed for personal use. Due to the OSINT data limitations the accuracy is likely 80-85%, all viewers are suggested to do their own due diligence. For questions, corrections and suggestions please reach out to @LVision_Trading.
Unless identified via public sources, vessel identification is performed by @AtlasObserver using proprietary SHIP LEDGER database.
