For the week ending November 20, 2026, U.S. refineries processed 17.3 million barrels per day (b/d), up 428,000 b/d from the previous week, at 97.5% capacity utilization. Gasoline output averaged 9.7 million b/d, and distillate production decreased to 5.2 million b/d. 

Crude oil imports increased 673,000 b/d to 6.2 million b/d, and the four-week average of 6.2 million b/d is 5.8% above the year-ago level. Gasoline imports averaged 314,000 b/d; distillate imports averaged 103,000 b/d. 

Commercial crude oil inventories (excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve) increased 1.1 million barrels to 418.9 million barrels, 3% below the five-year average. Gasoline inventories increased 1.7 million barrels, 3% above the five-year average. Distillate inventories increased 0.7 million barrels, 6% below the five-year average. Propane/propylene inventories decreased 1.0 million barrels, 11% above the five-year average. Total commercial petroleum inventories decreased by 2.4 million barrels for the week.

Over the past four weeks, total product supplied averaged 20.8 million b/d, up 2.2% year over year. The four-week average for gasoline product supplied decreased 0.2% year over year to 8.8 million b/d, while the four-week average for distillate product supplied increased 3.9% to 3.9 million b/d. The four-week average for jet fuel product supplied increased 5.2% year over year.
