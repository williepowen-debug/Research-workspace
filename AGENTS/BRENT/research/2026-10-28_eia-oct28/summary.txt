For the week ending October 23, 2026, U.S. refineries processed 16.5 million barrels per day (b/d), down 123,000 b/d from the previous week, at 92.8% capacity utilization. Gasoline output averaged 9.2 million b/d, and distillate production decreased to 5.2 million b/d. 

Crude oil imports increased 616,000 b/d to 6.4 million b/d, and the four-week average of 6.3 million b/d is 9.3% above the year-ago level. Gasoline imports averaged 399,000 b/d; distillate imports averaged 157,000 b/d. 

Commercial crude oil inventories (excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve) decreased 1.9 million barrels to 410.6 million barrels, 4% below the five-year average. Gasoline inventories increased 3.9 million barrels, 2% below the five-year average. Distillate inventories decreased 1.5 million barrels, 10% below the five-year average. Propane/propylene inventories decreased 0.1 million barrels, 12% above the five-year average. Total commercial petroleum inventories decreased by 3.0 million barrels for the week.

Over the past four weeks, total product supplied averaged 20.8 million b/d, up 0.2% year over year. The four-week average for gasoline product supplied decreased 0.7% year over year to 8.6 million b/d, while the four-week average for distillate product supplied decreased 0.6% to 4.0 million b/d. The four-week average for jet fuel product supplied decreased 3.5% year over year.
