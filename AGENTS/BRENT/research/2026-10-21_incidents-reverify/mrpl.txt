•  Throughput declined 5.6% QoQ/YoY to 4.2mmt. 
• 

Implied GRM stood at USD14.8/bbl 

MRPL’s  Q2FY27  EBITDA  beat  estimates  at  INR32.8bn  (PLe:INR23.3bn;  BBGe: 
INR30.2bn), vs INR13.2bn in Q1FY27, driven by lower QoQ crude procurement costs 
and  elevated  refining  cracks.  Implied  GRM  stood  at  USD14.8/bbl,  vs  USD8.3/bbl  in 
Q1FY27  and  USD9.1/bbl  in  Q2FY26.  Adj.  PAT,  after  excluding  the  exceptional  loss 
arising  from  the  fire  incident,  improved  to  INR19.7bn  from  INR5.6bn  in  Q1FY27. 
Throughput  declined  5.6%  QoQ  and  YoY  to  4.2mmt,  primarily  due  to  maintenance 
shutdowns during the quarter. Following the strong 1HFY27 performance, we raise our 
FY27E GRM assumption to USD11.3/bbl vs USD7.8/bbl. However, we expect GRMs to 
normalize  to  USD7.4/7.5/bbl  in  FY28E/FY29E  as  geopolitical  disruptions  ease  and 
refining  cracks  revert  towards  normalized  levels.  We  maintain  “HOLD”  rating  while 
revising  our  TP  to  INR180  (earlier:  INR165),  based  on  6.0x  Sep’28E  EV/EBITDA.  We 
continue  to  assign  an  option  value  of  INR23/share  to  the  chemicals  project,  which 
remains several years away from commercialization. 

Throughput declined, but implied GRM improved: Throughput declined 5.6% QoQ and 

YoY to 4.2mmt, primarily due to an expected maintenance shutdown during the quarter. 

However, calculated implied GRM improved to USD14.8/bbl from USD8.3/bbl in Q1FY27 

and  USD9.1/bbl  in  Q2FY26,  driving  the  stronger  than  expected  EBITDA  performance. 

SAED during the period was an average of USD3.3/bbl on MS and USD32.6/bbl in HSD.  

EBITDA beat estimates: EBITDA increased to INR32.8bn in Q2FY27 from INR13.2bn in 

Q1FY27, supported by lower crude procurement costs and other expenses. MS cracks 

averaged USD31.4/bbl, broadly flat QoQ at USD31.6/bbl in Q1FY27 (net of SAED), but 

significantly higher than USD9.5/bbl in Q2FY26. HSD cracks rose to USD38.7/bbl from 

USD20.4/bbl  in  Q1FY27  (net  of  SAED)  and  USD18.8/bbl  in  Q2FY26.  Implied  crude 

procurement  costs  declined  to  USD96.3/bbl  from  USD116.1/bbl  in  Q1FY27,  although 

remaining elevated versus USD70.8/bbl in Q2FY26.. 

Half  yearly  EBITDA  and  PAT  improved  YoY:  Strong  refining  cracks  supported  robust 

1HFY27 performance, with EBITDA growing ~1.7x YoY to INR46.0bn and Adj.PAT rising 

to INR25.3bn from INR3.7bn in 1HFY26.
