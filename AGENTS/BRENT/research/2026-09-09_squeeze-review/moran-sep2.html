Port Update

Port Arthur, TX / Sabine / Neches

September 02, 2026

Notices

2350 Aug 31 -1100 Sep 02 . WILL BE REOPENING WITH OUTBOUND TRAFFIC @ 1100. WILL BE POSTING TIMES ON INBOUND TRAFFIC A LITTLE LATER ONCE WE GET STARTED MOVING
The Sabine Pilots are monitoring the condition continuously and will provide an update when the condition status changes.
—————————————-


The US Coast Guard MSU Port Arthur’s Vessel Traffic Service (VTS) held a Port Coordination Team (PCT) conference call this morning at 1130 hours to look at the situation on the Sabine/Neches Waterway and surrounding region post T/S Edouard. 



National Weather Service
T/S Edouardo, now a tropical depression, has moved well to the north of the area
Max wind gusts recorded was 91 mph, about 2 miles south of Port Arthur
A few rounds of showers expected today
Max wind gusts 20 mph today, mainly from the south
No significant weather impacts anticipated for the next 24 hours

Sabine Pilots
This storm packed a little more punch than expected
Visual surveys conducted this morning at first light with a pilot boat
Their helicopter ran the entire channel and visually confirmed the ATONs were in good position
No debris fields seen
Current queue: 8 outbound ships (already started moving this morning), 1 inner harbor shift, 20 inbound pending
Probably will start inbound ships about 1700 hours today
Offshore seas choppy and confused
Helicopter ready to go, pending lightning
High outbound currents anticipated so some future, temporary restrictions may be implemented by destination and airdraft

MSU Port Arthur
New MSIB 22-26 just released
No significant impacts to the port
Port Condition X-Ray rescinded
Port Condition Recovery in place
All terminals need to submit post storm assessment reports

Port / Terminal Restrictions

General Channel Restrictions/Info

* Max Safe Draft 40ft FW

* Air Draft above Valero Terminal – Less than 136ft for passing 3 fixed bridges

* 800ft wide channel from 29/30 Buoy to Texaco Island

* 400ft wide channel from Texas Island to Port of Beaumont

* “SB” Buoy is located at Lat. 29-25N Lon. 93-40W

* Navigational Charts NOAA 11341, 11342 & 11343 cover the entire waterway

* Sabine Pilotswww.sabinepilots.com

Sabine Pilot Restrictions

* Vessels 85,000 DWT or over, 875 FT LOA or over or 125FT beam or over will transit during daylight hours only above Texaco Island. Only one (1) of the limitations has tobe met to restrict a vessel.

* Daylight Restriction times are based on the daylight hours and are adjusted according to sun rise/sun set. Current times as follows, the terminals shown are the ones that handle Aframax size vessels which are typically daylight restricted.

* Vessels with the combined beam of one-half the width of the channel will not meet/pass day or night.

* Any vessel that meets or exceeds either or both 860FT LOA or 120FT beam will require two pilots.

 

Vessels that do not exceed the above size restrictions can transit day or night consistent with other port traffic movements. 

 

Vessels that are daylight restricted will generally have priority to move before non-daylight restricted vessels.
