# Does the RCL/NCLH cushion comparison survive? — October 19, 2026 after close

**CRUISE verdict: the dated financial-cushion ordering survives the identified transactions as an illustrative comparison. An actual September/October balance-sheet ordering remains unverified, and financing does not explain the equity gap on the available evidence.** Norwegian retained access, extended debt and increased nominal bank capacity while paying more. Royal Caribbean refinanced before the return window and committed to a material acquisition during it. Demand, capacity, brand mix and execution provide direct competing explanations. The October 14/16 judgment of **no demonstrated near-term funding crunch** survives, conditional on operating cash flow, usable facilities and covenant compliance.
