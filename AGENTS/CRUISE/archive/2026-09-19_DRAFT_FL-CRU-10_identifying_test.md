## 1 · WHY THE FIRST TEST FAILED, STATED PLAINLY

I wrote a test on an **aggregate level**: Carnival's Q4 constant-currency yield guide near ~0.5% confirms the channel, ≥~1.15% refutes it.

**It cannot identify anything, in both directions:**
- **False confirm:** a 0.65pp drag from *anything* — Europe/Med, Alaska, deployment mix, occupancy, onboard spend, FX, a dry-dock — produces exactly 0.5% with **zero Norwegian effect.**

**⇒ A single aggregate number cannot separate a mechanism from everything else moving inside it.** The replacement must test **attribution and location**, not level.

### 4b · WHAT COUNTS AS A QUALIFYING MANAGEMENT STATEMENT
All four bind:
