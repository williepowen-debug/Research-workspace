## ⚖️ VERDICT

**No near-term funding crunch. The 9/30 deal bought time and paid for it.** Liquidity was ~$1.5B at 6/30 against a $250M covenant floor; the refinancing frees ~$376M of revolver room and moves $525M of 2028 maturity to 2031. **The cost is a step-up in the price of money:** NCLH paid **8.750%** for 2031 money on 9/30/26 versus **5.875%** for 2031 money in September 2025. About half of that +2.9 points is higher Treasury yields; **about +1.4 points is NCLH-specific or high-yield credit spread.** **The pressure point is cost and the 2028–2030 refinancing wall, not liquidity in the next 12 months.**

## 2 · The revolver's springing maturities — the tripwires

The ~$2.5B revolver matures **early** if any of three conditions bites (ARCA definition, verbatim terms):

| Trigger | Date | Bites only if | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2027 exchangeables** (1.125% $192.0M + 2.5% $24.1M = **$216.2M**) | **Tue 2026-11-17** (91 days before their stated maturity) | not repaid/refinanced past 4/23/2030 **AND free liquidity < $800M** | **Low risk:** free liquidity was ~$1.5B at 6/30, ~$700M above the line. Cash settlement was irrevocably elected 5/29/26, so they're a ~$216M cash call in Feb 2027 |
| **Any NCLC senior note > $400M** maturing before the revolver | 91 days before that note's maturity | not refinanced past 4/23/2030 | The **$525M 6.125% 2028 notes** sat here (issued by subsidiary NCL Finance, NCLC-guaranteed — whether they counted is a legal reading not settled here). **The 9/30 deal redeems them.** **Next in line: NCLC's $600M 7.75% 2029 notes** ⇒ a tripwire in **late 2028** |
| Original maturity | 2030-01-22 | — | the **2030 wall** (§4) |

🔑 **The deal's real job was probably the tripwire, not the cash.** Retiring the 2028s removes a likely late-2027/early-2028 revolver acceleration; the press release says only "redeem," and motive is inference.

## 3 · What the 9/30 deal does, pro forma on 6/30

| | $M |
|---|---:|
| New 8.750% senior notes due 2031 (upsized from $750M) | +950.0 |
| Redeem 6.125% 2028 notes (face per 10-K) | −525.0 |
| Repay secured revolver | −376.3 |
| Prepay export-credit debt | −42.2 |
| **Net, before redemption premium and fees** | **+6.5** (≈ cash-neutral; "together with cash on hand") |
| **Revolver room restored** | **+376.3** |

**Annual interest effect ≈ +$30M:** new $950M × 8.750% = $83.1M; retired $525M × 6.125% = $32.2M + $376.3M revolver at ~5.5% (the 12/31/25 rate; current unknown) ≈ $20.8M + ECA ≤ ~$2M. ⇒ **The refinancing explains only ~$30M of the ~$155–175M step-up in 2027 net interest** (FY26 ~$705M → FY27 $860–880M). **Most of the rest is unreconciled** — plausibly newbuild export-credit debt and lower capitalized interest; not established.
⚠️ **Not stated anywhere read:** whether the new notes are secured, their issue price, and the 2028 notes' redemption price.

## 8 · What would change this read

1. **Closing 8-K (~10/15+):** secured vs unsecured, issue price. If **secured**, the pledged pool grows and the unsecured holders sit further back.
2. **Q3 10-Q (~early Nov):** 9/30 liquidity, revolver drawn, advance-ticket build. **A second quarter of slower build is the leading indicator.**
3. **2026-11-17:** free liquidity must be ≥ $800M or the 2027 exchangeables gone, or the revolver matures that day. **Expected to pass.**
4. **Rating actions** on the new notes or the issuer.
5. **Late-2028 tripwire** on the $600M 7.75% 2029s, then the **2030 wall** (~$3.6B incl. the revolver and ~$1.76B of exchangeables).

⇒ **For the NCLH WATCH (TRADE.md row 1):** the funding leg supports neither a forced-raise story nor a near-term liquidity story. **A bear case on NCLH rests on demand/execution and on the cost of capital**, not on running out of money in 2026–27.
