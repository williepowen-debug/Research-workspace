## Operator Profiles

### Carnival (CCL) — Most Vulnerable
- Stock: -25 to -30% since Feb 28. Seven consecutive down days through Mar 9.
- **Unhedged on fuel by corporate policy.** Single largest vulnerability in sector.
- $24B long-term debt (2.3x pre-pandemic levels).
- Fuel spike could wipe 40-50% of net income.
- Sensitivity: $145-156M net income hit per 10% fuel price move.

### Royal Caribbean (RCL) — Relative Strength
- Stock: -13 to -18% since Feb 28.
- Better hedged than CCL on fuel.
- 2/3 of 2026 booked at record rates as of Jan 29 (pre-conflict data).
- Morgan Stanley already cut yield assumption by 100bps.

### Norwegian (NCLH) — Most Fragile Balance Sheet
- Stock: -15 to -20% since Feb 28.
- ~$700M/year interest expense vs ~$600M guided net income. Interest coverage <1.0x.
- Already missed Q4 2025 estimates.
- Guided 2026 EPS $2.38 vs $2.60 consensus.
