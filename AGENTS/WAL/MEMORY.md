- **★ Pin frames to the EARLIEST plausible filing date, not the expected one.** STATUS carried "~Aug 7-10"; the 10-Q landed **7/31**. Even a session on Aug 6 — inside the stated window — would have found the filing already public and the frame already void. It returned a **hybrid label**, an **arithmetic inconsistency** pointing at a third bucket (Construction & land development), and the discovery that **no 10-Q publishes the metric at all**. The call may have meant something narrower — but the score moved on the paraphrase, not on the measure. **A convergence score should cite the instrument it can be re-tested against.**
