## The Fraud

**Perpetrators:** Andrew Stupin, Gerald Marcil, Deba Shyam (and associates)
**Vehicle:** Multiple "Cantor Group" investment funds (II, IV, V, etc.)
**Total known exposure:** $270M+ across WAL, ZION, BANC, EFSC, and others (ML-REG-039)

### Three Pillars of the Scheme (ML-REG-046)

1. **Title Forgery** — manufactured title insurance policies that omitted existing liens
2. **Collateral Substitution** — transferred properties between SPVs between audit cycles (quiet swap)
3. **Forceful Takeover** — physical seizure of properties using armed security (Hotel Laguna, May 2nd)

### Mechanism
1. Created multiple Cantor Group investment funds
2. Obtained secured loans from regional banks for distressed mortgage purchases
3. Banks promised **first-priority collateral interest**
4. Perpetrators secretly **subordinated the collateral** to other creditors (themselves)
5. Properties transferred or went into foreclosure
6. Collateral became "irretrievably lost" (ZION's language)
7. Fraud discovered only after WAL filed lawsuit (August 2025)

**Key detail:** Maintained appearance of "performing" loans by making regular interest payments — fraud invisible through standard monitoring.
