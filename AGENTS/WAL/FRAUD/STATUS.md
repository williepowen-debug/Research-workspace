**Read on the silence:** Vectors 3 and 4 were unquantified pre-print; they remain unquantified post-print. WAL did NOT volunteer disclosure on First Brands or Tricolor. Two interpretations: (a) WAL has no material exposure (consistent with "largely behind us" framing), or (b) any exposure sits inside the $87M ex-LAM provision and was not separately broken out. **Cannot rule out** ongoing latent exposure on these rails until 10-Q Table 16 lands (~May 4-10) or analyst Q&A on subsequent calls forces disclosure.

## Open V2 Threads

1. **Other LAM/Leucadia-era credits in the WAL book.** The disclosure was for "the loan to a fund of LAM" (singular). The Jefferies/Leucadia platform operates multiple funds; if WAL has additional facilities to other LAM funds or post-2013 Leucadia-era credits, those are latent. Mgmt's "no further commentary" lockdown means no near-term disclosure. **Test:** 10-Q Table 16 large-credit detail (~May 4-10) and any analyst Q&A on subsequent calls (May 12 Investor Day).
2. **Cantor residual recovery realism.** ~$70M residual on book; recovery depends on (a) UHNW springing guarantees actually being collectible, (b) mortgage fraud policy paying out, (c) senior-lien position holding. Mgmt: *"complex and potentially of long duration."* Downside scenario: full $70M loss = additional $70M provision over 2026-2027. Base case: 30-50% recovery = $35-50M residual loss.
3. **First Brands / Point Bonita silence.** Jefferies took $17M Q1 on this credit. WAL is on the Jefferies rail per LAM linkage. Any WAL exposure to Point Bonita SPV or related Jefferies-leveraged fund credits remains unquantified.
4. **Tricolor silence.** WAL never had named Tricolor exposure pre-print, but the NDFI/warehouse/BDC chain remained an inferred path. No update in print. May reside inside $9.2B mortgage warehouse book (counterparty exposure). Apollo Atlas SP linkage at peer non-bank servicers (PFSI 78% concentration) suggests transmission landing point ≠ WAL.
5. **RSM auditor adequacy.** WAL took the Cantor charge — directionally validating the ZION 83% benchmark thesis (WAL utilized 89% of its 30% reserve). The pre-print "RSM under-reserved" critique is partially vindicated. But LAM was on a different rail (previously-reserved per "remaining balance" language) and not directly an RSM-collateral-verification failure. **Demoted from primary to tertiary thesis.**
