## Earnings: good headline, less decisive underneath

The bank earned$277.2M and EPS2.45. Revenue increased18.5M; expense increased11.8M; operating profit before provisions therefore increased6.7M. Net credit provision fell0.4M, making pretax income rise7.1M. Tax expense fell1.3M, producing the8.4M net-income increase. Common income rose258.5→266.9M; diluted shares edged108.0→107.8M. Lower taxes and fewer shares help EPS, but the earnings increase is not wholly those effects.

Security-sale gains increased3.0→16.8M, while the separate net fair-value line declined8.3→0.5M. Combined, those lines added6.0M more than Q2. Subtracting them from PPNR gives401.8M versus401.1M. **This is a narrow analytical screen, not a complete measure of recurring earnings or issuer-adjusted EPS.** A derivative gain can offset a retained loss elsewhere, and some customer derivative income is now included in commercial banking fees.

The release reclassifies customer swaps/risk-participation/FX income into commercial banking fees without changing net income. Q2's net-FV line is now8.3M, versus12.8M used in the older October14 screen. The old annualized~8.98EPS screen is consequently not directly comparable. No revised target or FY2027 earnings forecast follows from this exercise. Current release EPS is GAAP2.45; the9M adjusted7.01 is a different period. The old vendor consensus remains stale and its accounting basis unmatched.

## Deposits, interest income and costs

End-period deposits are broadly flat,81.874→81.755B. Non-interest-bearing balances decline2.068B, offset largely by interest-bearing balances. Some non-interest-bearing deposits carry earnings credits paid through other income-statement lines, so their name does not mean they cost nothing. The company's optimization moves some of that business and changes its funding mix.

Deposit-cost expense falls179.2→165.4M, while interest expense on deposits rises370.5→382.5M. The three earnings-credit/referral lines together fall234.2→223.7M. These reductions overlap the expense-line savings and must not be added twice. Commercial banking fee income rises73.1→78.7M on the new presentation. REGINALD retains all-in funding economics and peer/severity judgments; these are issuer accounting observations, not a substitute funding study.

Average interest-earning assets fall91.661→90.876B, but the mix shifts from cash toward loans and the average earning-asset yield rises5.43→5.53%. That helps net interest income increase even as average funding costs rise. Borrowings at quarter end fall6.236→5.613B. These actuals do not support assuming simultaneous pressure on both the interest business and securities book from the same rate move: interest earnings improved while securities marks reduced equity.

Mortgage production falls11%QoQ to14.5B, rate-lock volume27% to13B, and gain-on-sale margin29→27bps. Parent mortgage income still needs its production/servicing/valuation/hedge bridge; the separate bank Call Report line cannot replace it.

## Credit: accept the improvement, keep the remaining risks specific

Gross charge-offs48.0M less recoveries2.2M gives45.8M net, down from55.0M. The published30bps ratio governs the frozen test; it is not the9M35bps adjusted number. Nine-month LAM/Cantor fraud adjustments belong to the historicalQ1 charges, not inferred newQ3 exclusions.

Office classified assets on slide13 are25% of1.091B, or272.75M, versus the frozen~316M Q2 anchor. Even adding back all25.7M of quarterly CRE-non-owner-occupied gross losses gives298.45M. The registered >500M prediction is provisionally invalidated pending its named10-Q cross-check. There is no basis to call this an Office-credit escalation. No new pass-to-classified Office credit was explicitly identified in the materials; the addressed-topic Stage1 DP2 remains provisional until the call.

The caution is that nonaccrual562→485M overstates the decline in the combined nonaccrual-plus-foreclosed stock:688→650M. Foreclosure is not a cash cure. The company states that properties have as-is valuation support and aggregate revenues exceeding expenses; that is positive management evidence, not a guarantee of book-value realization on sale. Special-mention loans, an earlier warning category, rise316→397M; classified loans excluding repossessed property fall1002→926M. The81M special-mention rise offsets the76M classified-loan decline, leaving total criticized loans up5M. This is not a loan-level migration bridge.

Reserves rise539.4→574.4M. **Provision reconciliation:** funded-loan provision71.2M plus unfunded-commitment provision9.6M equals80.8M; less a0.8M release for held-to-maturity securities equals the80.0M consolidated provision. The loan-reserve change ties exactly:80.8−45.8=35.0M. This is reserve building alongside lower realized losses, not a provision release flattering the headline credit result.
