### Method lessons from this sweep

1. **An EDGAR *form-type* scan is not an EDGAR *content* scan.** The chairman change reached EDGAR only as an **Item 5.07** (annual-meeting votes) 8-K — no Item 5.02. Scanning for governance form types would have missed it. Scan **items**, and read 8-K bodies.
2. **Counterparty-initiated litigation is invisible to issuer-centric monitoring.** Jefferies suing WAL does not appear in WAL's EDGAR feed, was not on the call, and would only surface in a 10-Q footnote weeks later. **Dockets and news are the only timely instruments for the other side's actions.** [[finding_asymmetric_rigor_counterparty_claims]]
3. **"Waiting for an integration" masqueraded as "the data isn't available."** See Tier 1 #1.
