## 7. Thesis note (two lines max)

Going into the resolution window, institutional 13F ownership **rose** Q1→Q2 (84.7% → 90.0% of shares outstanding). The increase is concentrated in one quant book (AQR +4.24M), with passive index creep behind it; the fundamental active holders who moved were mixed and leaned toward cutting (T. Rowe −19.5%, Wellington, Barrow Hanley, AllianceBernstein) against Invesco, Davis and Smead adding. This is 6/30 data, **before** the Q2 print (7/28) and before the $99M appraisal outcome.
