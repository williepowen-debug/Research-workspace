## Core Hypothesis

The $126.4M dispute is the visible tip of a larger issue involving receivables control, perfection failures, and legal ambiguity between SPV non-recourse lending and sponsor-level reliance. If true, the opportunity is identifying which institutions carry similarly structured exposures not yet forced into daylight.

## Three Investigation Tracks

### 1. Legal Paper Trail (Can't do — litigation discovery)
- Source-of-obligation matrix: who signed what, who guaranteed what
- Key question: did the Oct 2025 forbearance change recourse in substance?
- If WAL can show a direct commitment → JEF defense weakens
- If WAL can't → left arguing conduct/fraud/reliance (weaker)
- **What we CAN do:** Monitor court filings in NY state court (WAL v. JEF). Watch for motion to dismiss or answer.

### 2. Perfection & Collateral (Partially accessible)
- UCC filings lapsed Sept 2025 — was this simple error or systemic?
- Other lenders also claim first-priority interests in First Brands receivables
- Key question: documentation failure vs. fraud vs. both?
- If perfection failure → readthrough to other receivables programs at WAL
- **What we CAN do:** Check bankruptcy docket for examiner findings on overlapping claims, double pledges.

### 3. Cash Dominion & Collections (Bankruptcy docket)
- Who controlled lockboxes? Who could redirect funds?
- If First Brands retained practical control despite representations → investor plaintiffs strengthened + WAL may have been misled
- Court-appointed examiner investigating prepetition factoring
- ~$35M of contested receivables in dispute
- **What we CAN do:** Monitor Chapter 11 docket for examiner report, factoring disputes, lockbox findings.
