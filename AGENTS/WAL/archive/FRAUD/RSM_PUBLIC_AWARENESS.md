## Executive Summary

Extensive search across short seller reports, accounting trade press, Seeking Alpha, Twitter/X, and analyst coverage found **no public source that has identified WAL as "Issuer B"** in PCAOB inspection reports of RSM US LLP. ## Key Takeaway

**The Issuer B = WAL thesis is differentiated.** While the raw ingredients are public (RSM audits WAL, RSM has PCAOB deficiencies), **nobody has publicly connected these dots.** No short seller, no accounting journalist (including McKenna who covers this exact beat), no Seeking Alpha contributor, and no sell-side analyst has raised RSM audit quality as a risk factor for WAL.
