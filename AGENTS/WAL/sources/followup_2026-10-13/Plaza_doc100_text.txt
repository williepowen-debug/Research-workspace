                                      22   “[t]he appointment of a conservator or receiver can often change the character of litigation; the

                                      23   stay gives the FDIC a chance to analyze the pending matters and decide how best to proceed.”

                                      24   H.R. Rep. No. 54(1), 101st Cong., 1st Sess. 331 (1989), reprinted in 1989 U.S. Code Cong. &

                                      25   Admin. News 86, 127.
