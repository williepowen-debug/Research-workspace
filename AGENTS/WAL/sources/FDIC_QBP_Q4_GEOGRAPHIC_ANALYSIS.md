## OZK Read-Through
1. **Primary loan markets (FL/NY/GA) are the most stressed CRE districts.** NY nonfarm noncurrent 1.57% (+27bps above national). Atlanta 1.36% (at national). These are where OZK actually writes loans — not Dallas.
2. **Dallas extend-and-pretend: 1.69pt gap.** OZK's home district has the worst PDNA/NCO divergence in the country. Low CRE noncurrents in Dallas are being offset by deferred loss recognition — the 0.89% may be suppressed, not clean.
3. **Community bank nonfarm nonres noncurrent in Dallas: 0.86%** — modestly above community bank national avg (0.80%). Thin cushion given OZK's CRE concentration ratios.
4. **OZK = reservoir thesis.** Stress accumulating in the loan book, masked by reserve cuts and classification management. Maturity wall forces recognition Q1-Q3 2026.

## WAL Read-Through
1. **CRE noncurrent in San Francisco below national** (0.90% vs 1.30%). Surface reads bearish for the short.
2. **But SF has the highest NCO rate of any district: 1.13%.** Losses are being taken, not deferred. PDNA/NCO gap is tightest (0.54pts) — SF banks charging off aggressively.
3. **WAL's CRE problems, when they surface, hit the P&L faster than OZK's.** Different transmission speed.
4. **WAL = fast-transmission thesis.** Losses reach P&L directly. Cantor fraud (Oct 2025) fits this pattern — specific events forcing recognition.

## Position Implications
- **OZK and WAL are NOT symmetric short theses.** Different mechanisms, different timing.
- OZK: reservoir building → maturity wall forces recognition → Apr 16 earnings catalyst
- WAL: faster transmission → embedded fraud/distress episodes surface → Jefferies Wed, Q1 earnings ~Apr 21
- OZK puts (May/Aug) align with maturity wall timeline + IQHQ Aug maturity
- WAL puts (Jun/Sep) should catch both Jefferies contagion and Q1 earnings

## Key Analytical Insight: Pipeline Methodology Breaks for WAL
- SF 30-89 day is CLEANEST (0.26%) but NCO is HIGHEST (1.13%)
- This is NOT a delinquency-to-charge-off funnel. It's direct loss recognition.

- WAL losses appear episodically (Cantor fraud pattern), not gradually through the pipeline
- Makes the two positions more complementary as a paired trade
