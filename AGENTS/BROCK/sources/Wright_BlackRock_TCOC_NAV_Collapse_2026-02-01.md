# Wright: BlackRock TCOC NAV Collapse

*Source: Melody Wright "Read My Lips" (Feb 1, 2026)*

---

## The Event

**BlackRock TCP Capital (TCOC)** — one of BlackRock's business development companies:
- **NAV decline: 19%**
- **Loss: $140M**
- **Timing:** "Investments had been marked at full value as recently as November"

---

## Key Quote

> "BlackRock's disclosure underscores the risks investors face inside the **opaque private-credit world**. It can be difficult to know what investments are worth at any given time, given that they hardly ever trade and instead are valued by fund managers using a mix of internal analysis and third-party pricing services. The way private funds mark their holdings determines the fees they charge clients."

---

## Pattern Recognition

This mirrors **TCPC** (BlackRock TCP Capital Corp) Q4 2025:
- TCPC NAV: $8.71 → $7.05-7.09 (**-19%**)
- 6 companies = 67% of decline
- "Cliff risk" — quarterly valuations lag actual deterioration

**Also mirrors HRZN:**
- NAV: $9.06 → $7.12 (**-21%**)
- 5 investments at Grade 1 (64% write-down)
- Forced merger with Monroe Capital

---

## The "November to December" Warning

Wright's personal experience:
> "I remember how my life changed from November of 2006 to December of 2006. I have detailed here how my former company went from celebrating its best year ever to writing down millions and millions of dollars seemingly overnight."

**Implication:** BDC marks are lagging. What looks fine in November can collapse in December. We're seeing the same pattern now.

---

## Prior Warning Signs

> "There were signs at BlackRock in 2024 that all was not well (including one of its loans to Renova, a home repair company) but by moving things around and **firing their rating agency** they were able to convince any - and there are so few - handwringers that all was fine and dandy."

**Red flag:** Firing rating agency to avoid scrutiny = PSEC playbook.

---

## Shadow Banking Scale

> "To put it simply, the banks pulled back lending to Main Street after the GFC due to various regulatory requirements. Instead, they lent to or facilitated the lending to companies like BlackRock who then went off and did the risky business... some believe the **shadow banking sector is now 2-2.5x bigger than it was in 2008**."

---

## Implications for BROCK

1. **Third BDC NAV collapse** — TCPC (-19%), HRZN (-21%), now TCOC (-19%)
2. **Mark-to-model fiction** — "Full value in November" → -19% in December
3. **Cliff risk confirmed** — Quarterly marks lag reality by months
4. **Rating agency gaming** — Fire the skeptics, hide the problems
5. **Shadow banking 2-2.5x 2008** — Systemic scale much larger

**Pattern:** Every BDC collapse follows same script:
1. Marks look fine
2. Suddenly they don't
3. 15-20% NAV haircut overnight
4. "Opaque" private credit blamed

**Who's next?** Watch for: PSEC, FSK, GBDC, any tech-heavy BDC
