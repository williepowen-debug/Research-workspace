## 🔴 Data Verification

1. **PSEC PIK was 8.6%, not 35%.** Agent hallucinated a 4x inflated number. Always verify BDC financials against SEC 10-K/10-Q filings, not summaries.
2. **BDC earnings dates shift.** Check company IR pages, not cached data. OZK was wrong twice in other agents.
3. **NAV is self-reported.** BDCs mark their own books. Market price (discount to NAV) is the market's opinion of real value. Always track both.

## 🟡 Process Rules

1. **Don't conflate "gating" with "restricting."** Blue Owl restricted quarterly redemptions ≠ formal gating. Blackstone honoring 100% ≠ stress-free. Precision in language matters for cross-agent signals.
2. **Track the delta, not just the level.** BCRED going from 5% to 7.9% redemptions matters more than the absolute 7.9%. Rate of change is the signal.
3. **Mark-to-model ≠ mark-to-market.** Private credit NAVs are model-derived. When BDCs trade at 73% of NAV, the market is saying the model is wrong. Don't trust the model over the market.
4. **Insurance/reinsurance is a parallel domain.** Don't let Athene/ILS analysis crowd out core BDC monitoring. Track both but keep STATUS.md focused on credit first, insurance second.

## 🔴 Lessons from August 28, 2026 (PROME-orchestrated catch-up, two touches + QC)

28. **A correctly-caveated body does not protect a headline that claims past it — and my "limits stated" discipline cannot catch this, because the limits ARE stated.** Twice in one session, RAV QC found me over-claiming in a bold clause while the paragraph beneath it was accurate. (a) BCRED's distribution cut: I wrote *"sized exactly to the coverage gap"* and *"cut to the coverage line and not a basis point further."* The arithmetic I had published two sentences earlier — $2.16 post-cut against a $2.14 NII run-rate — is **99.1% covered, ~$0.02/share short.** Cut *toward* coverage, not *to* it. (b) First Brands: I headed the re-size *"ESTIMATE → MEASUREMENT"* while the body correctly said the $237M par was unverified with an unstated perimeter and that two holders are not the sector. **Two measured marks on an unverified par is a REVISED ESTIMATE; it inherits the par's defects however good the marks are.** ⚠️ **This is NOT my registered bias.** My known failure is too-easy-to-fire-bearish (RED, n=4), and I spent the same session guarding it — I even *withdrew* an over-concession. This is a different mechanism: **the caveat and the claim live in the same artifact, the caveat is correct, and the reader who reads only the bold clause gets the stronger claim.** `[[finding_summary_section_merges_what_the_body_separates]]`, in my own files, twice, and neither instance tripped any check I run. **Test to add: after writing a bold headline clause, read it ALONE, with the paragraph covered, and ask what a reader would carry away. ## 🔷 Lessons from September 3, 2026 (BCRED Q3 graded · the "0 of 9" denominator audit)
