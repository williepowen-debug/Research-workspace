## KEY SIGNALS FOR SHORT THESIS

1. **Q4 GAAP EPS collapse: $0.08** — market saw this Feb 5 (stock was already declining)
2. **Fee rate compressing** 1.02%→0.99% — growing AUM but earning less per dollar
3. **Net interest expense doubled** — balance sheet leverage costing more
4. **$42.9B credit AUM below hurdle** — underperforming capital, no performance fees
5. **PE Realized Income -65% Q4** — exits market frozen
6. **PE Secondaries negative Q4 return** — secondary market deteriorating
7. **ASIF raised $11.5B** then gated at 11.6% redemptions 7 weeks later
8. **Insurance/Other = $139B AUM** (22% of total) — much bigger than "capital-light" narrative suggests
9. **Perpetual capital $200B** — this IS the bull case, fees are sticky. Must respect this.
10. **85% perpetual/long-dated + 93% of fees** — hard to kill the fee machine quickly
