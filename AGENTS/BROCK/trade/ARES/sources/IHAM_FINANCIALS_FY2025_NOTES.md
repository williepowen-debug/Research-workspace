## Note 1: Organization
- IHAM = Delaware LP, wholly-owned portfolio company of ARCC
- Registered investment adviser with SEC
- **AUM: ~$14.6B as of Dec 31, 2025**
- **Manages 23 vehicles** (collectively "IHAM Vehicles")

- Admin agreement with Ares Operations LLC — IHAM reimburses ALL actual costs

## Note 2: Accounting Policies
- Consolidated IHAM Vehicles = investment companies under GAAP (FV accounting)
- PIK interest accrued and added to principal. Collected upon repayment.
- Non-accrual at 30 days past due (with management discretion for "sufficient collateral")
