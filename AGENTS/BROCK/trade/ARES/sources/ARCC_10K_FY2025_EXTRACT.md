## RED FLAGS FOR SHORT THESIS

1. **PIK at 16% of income and rising** — $487M of $3,052M is non-cash. Up from 15.5% prior year.
2. **NII declining** — $1,415M vs $1,436M despite 10% larger portfolio. Yield compression (11.2%→10.0%).
3. **Dividend coverage thinning** — 1.05x (FY2025) vs 1.20x (FY2024). Lowest since 2021. One bad quarter away from under-earning.
4. **Non-accrual rising** — 1.8% cost / 1.2% FV, up from 1.7% / 1.0%. Direction matters more than level.
5. **Net unrealized losses** — ($96M) vs +$188M prior year. Marks flipped negative.
6. **Software 23.8% concentration** — Largest sector. Per Zito (Apollo): "20-40¢ recovery" on distressed software.
7. **Healthcare 11.3%** — Second largest. 2021-22 vintage healthcare cracking per DBRS.
8. **Yield compression accelerating** — New commitments earning less, competing harder for deals.
9. **Debt growth outpacing equity** — Debt +16.5% vs equity +7.2%. Leverage creeping up.
10. **EPS $1.86 vs NII $2.02** — Gap = realized losses eating into returns.
11. **Market return collapsed** — 1.12% (FY2025) vs 19.80% (FY2024). Market already skeptical.
