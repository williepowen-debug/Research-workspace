## MANAGER-BY-MANAGER DETAIL

### 1. ARES MANAGEMENT (ARES) — **PRIMARY SHORT TARGET**

**Why ARES is uniquely vulnerable (not just riding the wave):**
- **Software 23.8%** = highest BDC concentration. At Zito 20-40¢ recovery: $1.4B impairment = ~10% NAV hit
- **PIK at 34% of NII** = highest phantom income dependency. If PIK reverses → dividend cover drops below 1.0x
- **GAAP already undercovered**: EPS $1.86 < dividend $1.92. One more bad quarter → dividend cut territory
- **IHAM/CLO hidden risk**: $14.6B AUM across 23 CLO vehicles inside ARCC. Unrealized losses growing ($70M→$82M). Not discussed by analysts
- **Net unrealized flipped negative** for first time (-$96M vs +$188M prior year)
- **ARESSI gated** at 11.6% requests. Emergency town hall Mar 11
- **Aspida (insurance)**: "capital-light" claim unverified. No statutory analysis done. Potential hidden tail risk
- **Asset coverage 189%** declining. At 150% regulatory minimum = BDC must stop borrowing
- **Cash $638M vs $16B debt** = 4% cash-to-debt ratio
- **72% floating rate** = if Fed cuts, NII drops mechanically

**Catalyst:** Q1 2026 earnings (late Apr/May). Non-accrual >2.2% or dividend cut = thesis acceleration.

### 2. APOLLO (APO) — **STRUCTURAL / INSURANCE SHORT**

**Unique vulnerabilities:**
- **Athene is the real trade**: $386B investments, $225.7B reinsurance receivables, 55:1 surplus ratio, Iowa negative surplus, Bermuda subsidiary declining 22%
- **Epstein class action** (Feldman v. APO, SDNY) — May 1 lead plaintiff deadline. $12B mkt cap erased
- **APODS gated** — 11.2% requests, $730M trapped
- **Zito admission**: "I literally think all the marks are wrong." 20-40¢ software recovery
- **MFS exposure** (UK £2B fraud) — cockroach risk
- **Launching UK pension product** (LTAF) during own fund gating — aggressive optics

**Unique strengths vs ARES:**
- No public BDC (harder to track portfolio deterioration in real-time)
- Insurance tail risk is KNOWN and documented (Eisman thesis) — may be partially priced
- Athene spread still positive (1.61%) though compressing

### 3. BLUE OWL (OWL) — **MOST ADVANCED STRESS CASE**

**Already in distress:**
- **OBDC II permanently frozen** — $1.6B, zero redemptions, forced $1.4B asset sales
- **-67% from peak** = worst performer
- **OBDC dividend already cut** ($0.45→$0.37)
- **Saba hostile tender at 65¢ of NAV** = market's real clearing price
- **TD Cowen + Citizens both cut price targets** (Mar 23)
- **Software ~55% in OBDC II** = extreme concentration
- **Portfolio overlap with ARCC** on worst names (Pluralsight, National Dentex, CivicPlus) = correlated marks

**Why OWL may be LESS attractive as short now:**
- Already -67% — much of the move is done
- El-Erian "August 2007" comparison already public = narrative priced
- Saba buying OWL stock (smart money thinks managers survive)

### 4. BLACKSTONE (BX) — **BIGGEST AUM, MOST DEFENSIVE**

**Gate situation:**
- BCRED ($82.5B): 7.9% requests ($6.5B actual) — largest $ amount
- BX injected $400M own capital + $150M exec money to maintain "open" status
- HLEND (BlackRock/BX joint): 19% software, gated at 9.3%/5% cap
- HPS ($26B): largest single gated fund by AUM

**Relative strengths:**
- Massive scale ($1.1T AUM) = franchise value protects equity
- Can inject own capital to stabilize (did so)
- Lower non-accrual rates on BXSL
- Real estate diversification (BREIT precedent — survived 2022 gate)
- First monthly BCRED loss is negative but BX has more cushion

**Relative weaknesses:**
- Sheer size = systemic. If BCRED spirals, it IS the market
- Evermore insurance subsidiary less researched than Athene

### 5. KKR — **ATHENE 2.0 IN EARLY INNINGS**

**Insurance risk (Global Atlantic):**
- $219B AUM, growing fast
- Funding agreements +71% YoY ($12.2B) — fastest growth rate in sector
- Same Athene playbook: FHLB, FABN, Bermuda cessions ($26-48B)
- Commonwealth (US sub) has NEGATIVE unassigned surplus — cannot pay dividends without regulator approval
- Surplus 7.9% (much better than Athene's ~1.8%)
- Currently Tier 2 — less advanced on vulnerability curve

**FSK (BDC):**
- **DOWNGRADED TO JUNK** by Moody's (Baa3→Ba1, Mar 23) — first BDC IG downgrade
- Non-accruals 5.5% — one of highest in sector
- Net loss $114M Q4 2025
- -30% YTD
- Software 16.4%
- Forces CLO/IG-mandated holders to sell FSK bonds = forced selling cascade

**KKR as short:**
- Less attractive near-term — no fund gate confirmed, insurance risk is early-stage
- FSK downgrade is meaningful but FSK trades independently
- Better surplus ratios than Athene

## BOTTOM LINE

**ARES is the best short in the peer group because its vulnerabilities are the LEAST visible to the market.**

- APO's problems (Athene, Epstein) are public and partially priced
- OWL's problems (OBDC II, -67%) are fully visible and largely played out
- BX has franchise protection and self-funding capability
- KKR's insurance risk is Tier 2 / early-stage

ARES has:
1. The **highest software concentration** (23.8%) in the largest BDC ($29.5B)
2. The **highest PIK dependency** (34% of NII = phantom) masking dividend vulnerability
3. A **hidden CLO vector** (IHAM, $14.6B) nobody discusses
4. An **unresearched insurance subsidiary** (Aspida) that could be another Athene
5. A **dividend that's already GAAP-undercovered** — one bad quarter from a cut
6. All of this in a stock that's "only" -48% vs OWL's -67% — meaning more downside to reprice

The market treats ARES as "the quality name" in private credit. The 10-K says otherwise.
