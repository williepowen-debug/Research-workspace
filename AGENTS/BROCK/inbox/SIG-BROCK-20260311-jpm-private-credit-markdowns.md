# JPMorgan Marks Down Private Credit Portfolios, Restricts Lending — March 11, 2026

**Source:** Bloomberg, Reuters, FT (all Mar 11)
**Priority:** 🔴🔴 CRITICAL — THESIS CONFIRMATION

## What Happened
- JPMorgan Chase is **restricting lending to private credit funds** after marking down the value of loans in their portfolios
- Markdowns apply specifically to **loans made to software companies** — AI disrupting business models
- Mechanism: JPM provides fund-level leverage to private credit funds. Funds use that leverage to buy middle-market loans. JPM's credit agreements allow re-marking collateral values during market dislocation.
- JPM went through its financing portfolio "name by name"
- Reuters separately notes: "investor withdrawals surge from private credit vehicles like BlackRock's fund"
- Reuters ran a companion piece: "Private credit alarm bells echo 2007 subprime warnings" (Mar 10)

## Why This Is Critical for BROCK

1. Portfolio company values drop (software/AI disruption)

JPM is the largest bank. If they're remarking, others will follow. This is the "banks pulling back" inflection point.
