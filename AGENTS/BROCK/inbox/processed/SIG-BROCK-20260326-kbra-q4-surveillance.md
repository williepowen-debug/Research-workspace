## The Kill Quote

**"KBRA attributes the uptick [in multilevel downgrades] to sponsor and lender support becoming exhausted in several cases. This suggests that when liquidity backstops and support are withdrawn, credit deterioration can accelerate quickly."**

## Why This Matters

1. **Sponsor lifelines exhausting** — the only thing preventing defaults is PE firms injecting capital. When they stop, it's a cliff not a slope. 2.9x QoQ multilevel downgrades = the cliff is starting.
2. **25% sub-1.0x ICR** — one quarter of borrowers can't cover interest. In a rising rate / no-Fed-cut environment (OECD 4.2%), these are zombies.
3. **Median masks tails** — "Stability at the Median, Stress at the Margins." The 19% declining sales / 22% declining EBITDA cohort is the one that defaults. Median looks fine because survivors keep growing.
4. **Near-term maturity concentration** — Consumer Retail + Media + Chemicals = demand-destruction sensitive sectors. Oil shock + consumer fatigue (JPM -30% retail spending) hits these first.
5. **Connects to Cliffwater/gating cascade** — when sponsors stop supporting borrowers, BDC NAVs drop, fund gates tighten, FOF redemptions fail, and the cascade we're tracking accelerates.
