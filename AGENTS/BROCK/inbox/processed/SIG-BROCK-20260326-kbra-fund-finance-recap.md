# SIGNAL: KBRA Fund Finance Europe Conference Recap (Mar 11)
**Date:** 2026-03-12 (published) | **Source:** KBRA, DealCatalyst Fund Finance Europe Conference | **Priority:** 🟠

---

## Key Takeaways

Conference tone: "cautiously optimistic." ~500 attendees. KBRA was patron sponsor.

**Relevant signals:**
1. **NAV lending expanding rapidly** — funds using NAV loans strategically to "manage holding periods, smooth distributions, support fundraising." Translation: borrowing against illiquid, potentially mispriced assets to manufacture liquidity. Leverage on top of gating.
2. **"Lenders not underwriting solely to sponsor marks"** — they acknowledge marks are suspect but lend anyway citing structural protections, stress testing, manager track record. Pre-crisis language.
3. **Single-asset/concentrated NAV structures most vulnerable** in risk-off — exactly the profile of funds now gating.
4. **Defaults "still viewed as relatively low"** — consensus. Our data: DBRS 4-5% true rate, 16% CCC-C. Conference is behind.
5. **Secondaries market growing** as liquidity substitute — creating secondary exit when primary redemptions are gated. Bandaid.
6. **"Regulatory complexity"** in Europe noted — connects to UBS CRE gating (36mo).

## Why This Matters

This conference was Mar 11 — **15 days before $4.6B trapped, Cliffwater FOF gating, UBS CRE gating, Apollo caps withdrawals on Fox Business.** The "cautiously optimistic" consensus is stale. The NAV lending expansion is the leverage layer that amplifies the cascade when marks finally adjust.

Fund finance = the plumbing underneath private credit. When funds gate, NAV lenders face collateral they can't value or liquidate. This is where BROCK's thesis meets LIQUID's domain.
