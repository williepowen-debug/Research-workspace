# Firmus withdrew its ASX IPO on 10/9 — the open "cut or pull" in SIG-W-20261008-024 resolves as PULLED; the public market refused AI data-centre equity at ~US$30B

Its board said the terms on which the offer could proceed "would not reflect the strength of the business", citing **recent market volatility and prevailing market conditions**, and that it will **pursue capital from the private markets** and consider other public and private options.
