## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

National Dentex Labs (NDX) is the largest network of fully-owned dental laboratories in North America, owned by **Cerberus Capital Management** since October 2020. The company's debt is held across at least **four BDC/fund vehicles** — all three Blue Owl entities (OBDC, OBDC II, OBDE) plus Silver Point Specialty Lending Fund. Critically, **ARCC does NOT appear to hold National Dentex** — this corrects our earlier assumption. The $162M→$78M markdown (-51%) with 100% PIK and April 2026 maturity makes this a pure Blue Owl contagion event, not an ARCC catalyst.

## 4. APRIL 2026 MATURITY MECHANICS

### Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Probability | What Happens | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| **Refinancing** | LOW (10-15%) | New lender takes out existing debt at par or near-par | Holders made whole. Unlikely given 100% PIK + 51% markdown = no willing lender. |
| **Extend & Pretend** | MODERATE (30-40%) | Cerberus negotiates maturity extension with existing lenders. PIK continues. | Markdown stabilizes or worsens. No resolution. Buys 6-12 months. |
| **Restructuring (out-of-court)** | MODERATE (30-40%) | Debt-for-equity swap. Lenders take haircut, get equity. Cerberus diluted or wiped. | Realized loss for BDC holders. Recovery 40-60¢ on cost? FV already at 48¢. |
| **Chapter 11** | LOW-MODERATE (15-20%) | Formal bankruptcy. Cerberus walks. Lenders fight over remaining value. | Recovery likely 20-40¢. FV may need further write-down from 48¢. |

### Key Factors:
1. **100% PIK = company cannot service ANY cash interest.** This is the worst-case credit scenario short of default.
2. **51% markdown already priced.** The $78M FV implies BDCs expect ~48¢ recovery. Further downside limited to 20-30¢ in full liquidation.
3. **Cerberus's incentive:** They bought in Oct 2020, spent on DSG acquisition in 2021, and multiple add-ons through 2022. Their equity is likely near-zero. They have no economic incentive to inject more capital unless the business has strategic value they want to preserve.
4. **Dental labs have tangible asset value** — equipment, customer relationships, real estate leases. This is NOT a software company with intangible-only collateral. Recovery should be higher than software LBOs (e.g., Pluralsight at near-zero).
