### Per Share Data

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2024 | FY2023 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **EPS (basic)** | **$1.86** | **$2.44** | **$2.75** |

### Key Income Statement Signals:
- **NII DECLINING** — $1,415M vs $1,436M. First YoY decline. On a per-share basis even worse due to dilution.
- **PIK income rising** — $184M interest PIK + $266M dividend PIK = **$450M total PIK** (14.7% of total income). Up from $427M (14.3%) in FY2024. PIK as % of income growing.
- **EPS collapsing** — $1.86 vs $2.44 vs $2.75. Down 24% YoY, down 32% from FY2023. Dilution + NII decline + realized losses.
- **Net realized losses** — $(20M) vs +$100M prior year. Swing of $120M. Exits going badly.
- **Net unrealized losses** — $(96M). Second consecutive year of unrealized losses. Marks declining.
- **Interest expense surging** — $793M (+10.9% YoY). Leverage cost rising.
- **Management fees $425M** — Ares Management extracting record fees even as returns decline.
