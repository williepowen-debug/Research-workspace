## NET ASSESSMENT

The Zito quote changes the character of this crisis. Previously, all the "marks are wrong" voices were external (Eisman, Gober, Gundlach, El-Erian, Dimon). Now an insider at the industry's most important firm has said it publicly. The defense ("not us, them") won't hold — Athene is Apollo's real exposure, not software.

Combined with four major firms not reporting Q1 tenders yet, the $10B confirmed outflows, and the reserves-in-software-bonds structural revelation, this is no longer a sentiment problem. The mechanics are broken.

FOMC tomorrow is the accelerant: 0 cuts = no refinancing relief for distressed borrowers = PIK keeps compounding = marks keep deteriorating.
