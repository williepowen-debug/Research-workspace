# Morningstar DBRS: Private Credit Default Momentum Increasingly Tied to Distressed Debt Exchanges
**Published:** March 19, 2026 | **Authors:** Candice Gao, CFA; Michael Dimler, CFA

---

## Key Findings

### Default Acceleration
- **16 of 17** private credit downgrades to D/SD in trailing 12 months were distressed exchanges (94%)
- Compare: only 4 of 9 defaults in 2024 were distressed exchanges (44%)
- **78% YoY increase** in default events in 2025
- Expect accelerated pace to **continue into 2026**

### Distressed Exchange Mechanism
- Distressed exchanges = borrower negotiates loan amendment (partial/full suspension of cash payments or principal forgiveness) to avoid imminent default
- Considered "distressed" when creditors are disadvantaged without adequate compensation
- Sequence: covenant waivers → amendments → PIK elections → distressed exchange → (payment default or foreclosure)
- Lenders often prefer distressed exchange over foreclosure (higher expected recovery)

### PIK Doom Loop
- Material increase in PIK and deferred interest among distressed borrowers
- PIK addresses near-term liquidity but **capitalizes interest → grows debt balances → weakens credit metrics → lowers recovery rates**
- Prolonged reliance rarely compensates lenders for additional risk
- Self-reinforcing: deferral makes borrower more leveraged, increasing probability of next default

### Vintage Analysis
- Defaults concentrated in **2019-2021 vintages**, average time-to-default **3.4 years**
- 2021-2022 vintages now the kill zone: **63% of CCC-to-C borrowers** clustered there
- 2023 vintage defaults **beginning** — expected to increase if conditions remain challenging
- 2024-2025 vintages already appearing in CCC bucket (some entered at CCC initially)

### Default Pipeline
- **16% of active ratings** are CCC-through-C (most vulnerable pool)
- Defaults disproportionately concentrated among borrowers that previously received external capital support (10% of actively rated)
- Additional **18% vulnerable** based on weak sales growth or margin trends
- If operating performance doesn't improve and capital support diminishes → default likely

## Relevance to Thesis
- Confirms private credit cascade is accelerating, not stabilizing
- Distressed exchange dominance means **losses are being hidden** — NAVs still marking these as performing while Morningstar calls them defaults
- PIK capitalization = the mechanism by which BDC dividend coverage erodes (cash interest deferred, replaced by paper)
- 2021-2022 vintage concentration aligns with peak ZIRP leverage + peak valuations
- 18% vulnerable + 16% already CCC = **34% of rated universe at risk**
- Directly feeds APO (Athene illiquid marks), ARCC, OWL (OBDC) positioning
