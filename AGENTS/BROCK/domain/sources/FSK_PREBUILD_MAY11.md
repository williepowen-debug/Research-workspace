## X. BRANCH READS — DECISION MATRIX

### BULL FSK
- NAV >$20.50
- Non-accruals down or flat below 5.5% cost
- Adjusted NII >$0.52 and cash quality improves (PIK <14%)
- Dividend $0.48 maintained with real cash coverage
- Leverage stable/down, no funding stress

**Implication:** Q4 was closer to a kitchen-sink reset. OBDC+FSK both say grind/stabilization, not cascade.

**Action read:**
- **APO puts:** reduce near-term conviction; Jun should be rolled/cut unless separate APO/Athene trigger emerges. Dec can remain as structural optionality.
- **ARES Jun $95P:** likely exit on next rally / do not wait for theta decay.
- **ARCC puts:** do not enter; wait for ARCC Q2.
- **BIZD:** sector crash thesis deferred.

### MIXED FSK
- NAV $20.20-$20.50
- Non-accruals 5.3%-6.0%
- Adjusted NII $0.48-$0.52, dividend covered but thin
- PIK 14%-17%, leverage stable

**Implication:** Confirms OBDC pattern: income/NAV grind, no forced mark break. Bears stay in, but the clock slows.

**Action read:**
- **APO puts:** hold/roll bias, no add.
- **ARES Jun $95P:** hold only through ARES print / next BDC filing if imminent; otherwise theta risk rising.
- **ARCC puts:** still deferred unless PIK/non-accrual details are worse than headline.
- **BIZD:** watch income/NAV grind, no immediate crash signal.

### BEAR FSK
- NAV $19.85-$20.20 or non-accruals 6.0%-7.0%
- Adjusted NII $0.45-$0.48 or total dividend uncovered
- PIK 17%-22%
- Leverage >1.25x or liquidity deterioration

**Implication:** OBDC was too clean; FSK shows the weak-BDC tail is still deteriorating. Public-BDC credit stress is real, but not yet disorderly.

**Action read:**
- **APO puts:** hold; roll Jun later rather than cut if APO tape weakens.
- **ARES Jun $95P:** hold through ARES/BDC wave; no exit on benign headline rally.
- **ARCC puts:** re-open ARCC Sep/Dec entry work; FSK confirms sector canary.
- **BIZD:** downside pressure likely; watch for sector discount widening.

### STRONG / MAX BEAR FSK
- NAV < $19.85, especially < $19.50
- Non-accruals >7.0% cost or FV non-accrual >4.5%
- PIK >22% or PIK dollars >$70M
- Base dividend uncovered or second cut signaled
- Funding/covenant/revolver stress disclosed

**Implication:** FSK validates Stage 3 public-BDC forced recognition. This is the first clean post-OBDC acceleration print.

**Action read:**
- **APO puts:** do not cut on manager-rally tape; roll Jun → Sep/Dec if needed.
- **ARES:** hold / consider add only if ARES has not yet reported and tape has not priced it.
- **ARCC:** enter Sep/Dec puts if pricing allows; FSK is the canary BROCK was waiting for.
- **BIZD:** sector short/put structure becomes live.
- **Cross-agent:** route to LIQUID (HY OAS likely lagging), REGINALD (bank-BDC revolvers/NDFI), and NEXUS.
