## 1. THE HEADLINE RESULT — RESOLVED PRE-PRINT. THERE IS NO CONTRADICTION.

I have carried "OTF PIK 13% combined" as **⚠️ SUSPECT** since 7/17, against the §36(b) complaint's "PIK ~$43M ≈ 25% of NII." **Both are true. They are the same dollars over two different denominators, and the print is not needed to establish it.**

| | Figure | Denominator | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mine** | PIK **13%** combined (7.6% PIK interest + 5.4% PIK dividends) | **TOTAL (gross) investment income** | OTF Q1'26 call transcript |
| **Complaint** | PIK **~$43M ≈ 25%** | **NET investment income** | Siegel & Kelly v. Blue Owl Technology Credit Advisors, SDNY, filed 6/18/26 |

**Primary (SEC XBRL, CIK 1747777, Q1-2026 10-Q):** gross investment income **$325.9M** · net investment income **$172.6M** ⇒ **NII/TII = 52.96%**.

- 13% × $325.9M = **$42.4M** → matches the complaint's "~$43M" ✅
- $43M ÷ $172.6M = **24.9%** → matches the complaint's "~25% of NII" ✅

⇒ **Both describe ~$42-43M of PIK. My SUSPECT flag is CLEARED. No reconciliation is owed at the print.**

## 2. THE ANALYTICAL POINT (this is the part worth carrying, and it cuts both ways)

**The complaint is not revealing concealed PIK — it is RE-BASING a disclosed number onto a smaller denominator, which roughly doubles the headline.** Same money, bigger number, chosen for impact.

- **Against the complaint:** its PIK-*magnitude* leg is weak. Nothing was hidden; management disclosed the same dollars on the conventional BDC basis.
- **NOT against the complaint:** this says **nothing** about the valuation-inflation allegation, which is a separate claim about *marks* and the Rule 2a-5 process. Do not let a cleared PIK-basis question read as a cleared complaint.
- **Against me:** I had my own correct figure flagged SUSPECT because a louder restatement of the identical fact arrived and I assumed one of us was wrong. The denominator branch was the whole gap.
