- P&I war risk cancellation (Mar 2) was shipping-focused. Declaring *banks* as military targets is categorically different — this is political violence/terrorism coverage territory.
- Lloyd's, Swiss Re, Munich Re will immediately reprice or exclude political violence coverage for GCC financial institutions. ILS cat models don't handle state-declared military targeting of financial infrastructure — underwriters will pull back.
- **Cascade:** Banks → correspondent banking risk premium → trade finance insurance → D&O liability on ME financial institution boards → property insurance on DIFC skyscrapers.
- **Amplification risk:** If DIFC-domiciled entities can't obtain political violence coverage at reasonable premiums, their operational risk profiles change. Global bank risk limits (internal VaR/credit limit systems) will automatically reduce exposure to counterparties in declared military target zones. This is a *mechanical* contagion — not discretionary.
- Insurance repricing also hits any Apollo/private credit portfolio company that has GCC-based insurance coverage — their renewal costs spike, squeezing already-negative FCF borrowers further.
