## §2. THE BASE RATE — FIVE- vs SIX-FUND OR-GATE

**Question as posed:** how much easier does a sixth member make an OR-gate fire?

`P(fire | k funds) = 1 − (1−p)^k`, where *p* = per-fund probability of a sub-100% satisfaction quarter.

**Measuring *p* on BRK-30's own population** (`workbook/PC_REDEMPTION_REGISTER.tsv`, 2026 fund-quarters):

| Fund | Sub-100% quarters / measured | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| BCRED | 2 / 3 (Q1 100% w/ cap flex; Q2 ~50%; Q3 ~50%) | issuer-stated |
| MS North Haven PIF | 2 / 2 (Q1 47.8%, Q2 41.6%) | issuer-stated |
| Monroe | 1 / 2 (Q1 in full, Q2 pro rata) | issuer-stated |
| ADS | 2 / 2 | **INFERRED** (cap-hit + missing footnote — my own 9/3 ruling: inference does not count) |
| CCLFX | not measurable | fulfilment series, not satisfaction (KB-BRK-238) |

- Strictly issuer-stated: **5 / 7 = p ≈ 0.71**
- Including the inferred ADS pair: **7 / 9 = p ≈ 0.78**

**Marginal effect of the sixth member:**

| p | P(fire \| 5) | P(fire \| 6) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.78** (observed, upper) | 0.99948 | 0.99989 | **+0.04 pp** |
| **0.71** (observed, lower) | 0.99795 | 0.99941 | **+0.14 pp** |
| 0.50 (stress) | 0.96875 | 0.98438 | +1.56 pp |
| 0.30 (deep stress-down) | 0.83193 | 0.88235 | +5.04 pp |
| 0.20 | 0.67232 | 0.73786 | +6.55 pp |

**At the observed base rate the sixth member moves the gate by 0.04–0.14 pp.** BRK-30's confidence was registered at **65%** — stated to the nearest 5 pp. **The proposed change is 35×–125× smaller than the instrument's own stated resolution.** It is below the noise floor of the thing it would modify.

⚠️ **Why the effect is so small, stated plainly because it is also a criticism of BRK-30:** the gate is **already saturated at five**. That is not a defence of the addition — it is the same **spec-vs-spirit** defect I registered against BRK-30 myself on 7/27 when I built BRK-32 (*"its letter fires on <100% satisfaction at any ONE of five funds — it would fire even in a substantially clearing market"*). A gate that is ~99.8% likely to fire is measuring the letter, not the thesis. **Adding a sixth member makes a weak instrument imperceptibly weaker; it does not make it wrong.**

**Why I reject it — one reason, and it is not the base rate:**

The reading is **correct on its premise and wrong on its remedy.** The count *was* wrong; I said so on 8/13 and I have not retracted it. But a mis-enumeration **discovered post-data, by the interested party, through a channel that preferentially surfaces confirming instances** cannot be repaired by *adding the discovered member* — because the set of members you would have found is not the set you did find. Repairing enumeration by appending whatever the sweep turned up **imports the sweep's selection bias directly into the population**, and it does so invisibly: the resulting six-fund list looks exactly like a correctly-enumerated six-fund list.

The admissible repairs are the two already executed on 8/13, and they remain the right ones:
- **record the coverage limit** on the frozen instrument (done — `BRK32_QUEUE_AMPLITUDE_SPEC_JUL27.md` dated population annotation, letter and baselines untouched); and
- **track the omitted member on a TRACKING surface** where no threshold depends on membership (the register — item ①, executed this session at primary).

**Neither repair moves a registered population. That is the whole distinction.**
