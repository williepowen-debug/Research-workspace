## HEADLINE: Software & Services = 23.8% of Total Portfolio

Arougheti claims "12% of direct lending book" in software. The 10-K says **23.8% of total portfolio at fair value.** This is the single largest industry concentration. On a $29.5B portfolio, that's approximately **$7.0 billion** in software exposure.

YoY: 24.2% (2024) → 23.8% (2025) — barely reduced.

## Leverage & Liquidity

- Total debt: $16.0B. Asset coverage: 189%.
- 150% minimum = $16B debt needs $24B assets. Current assets $29.5B.
- **A $5.5B asset decline (18.6%) would breach the 150% coverage ratio.**
- If software ($7B) takes a 20% haircut ($1.4B) AND other assets decline 5% ($1.1B), total loss = $2.5B → coverage drops to ~170%. Two more bad quarters → covenant territory.
- Cash $638M vs $16B debt = **4% cash-to-debt ratio**
- Unfunded commitments: $5.2B revolving + delayed draw. If borrowers draw these down in stress, cash evaporates.

- PIK phantom income ($207M uncollected) inflates NII — actual cash coverage is worse

## IHAM (Ivy Hill Asset Management) — Hidden Risk

- IHAM is a wholly-owned CLO manager with $14.6B AUM across 23 vehicles
- ARCC's investment in IHAM: $2.4B (8.3% of portfolio)
- IHAM has $82M in unrealized losses (up from $70M in 2024)
- IHAM consolidated entities have $167M in unrealized depreciation
- IHAM's own debt: $893M standalone + $8.6B in consolidated vehicles
- If CLO tranches deteriorate, IHAM's equity ($1.77B) is at risk → flows through to ARCC NAV

## KEY TAKEAWAY

**ARCC is more vulnerable than the market thinks.** The "12% software" claim is a lie of omission — it's 23.8% of total portfolio. Unrealized gains have flipped to losses. PIK is accelerating ($487M accrued, only $280M collected). Dividend is GAAP-undercovered. Leverage is rising toward covenant territory. And the company just issued $750M MORE debt in January 2026 (April 2031 Notes) while the portfolio was deteriorating.

**The trade:** ARCC puts. The same "marks are wrong" thesis that Zito just confirmed applies to ARCC's own portfolio — the world's largest BDC with 23.8% software exposure, accelerating PIK, and thinning coverage.
