# DBRS Morningstar — Private Credit Default Momentum (Mar 19, 2026)
**Source:** Morningstar DBRS Research #476605

## Key Data Points
- **94% of defaults are distressed exchanges** (16 of 17 D/SD downgrades, trailing 12mo through Feb 2026)
- **78% YoY increase** in default events in 2025
- **16% of active ratings are CCC-to-C** — the default pipeline
- **10% of rated borrowers received external capital support** — defaults concentrated here
- **18% additional vulnerable** on weak sales/margins → ~28% of rated universe in trouble
- **Average time to default: 3.4 years** from rating assignment
