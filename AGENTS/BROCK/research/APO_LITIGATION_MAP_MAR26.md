## CASE 1 — PRIMARY SECURITIES CLASS ACTION (Epstein Misrepresentation)

**Case Name:** *Solomon Feldman v. Apollo Global Management, Inc., et al.*
**Case No.:** 1:26-cv-01692
**Court:** U.S. District Court, Southern District of New York (SDNY)
**Date Filed:** March 2, 2026
**Basis:** Securities Exchange Act §§10(b) and 20(a), Rule 10b-5
**Class Period:** May 10, 2021 – February 21, 2026
**Lead Plaintiff Deadline:** **May 1, 2026** (Hagens Berman / Schall) | May 5, 2026 (ClaimsFiler — possible second complaint)

### Allegations
Apollo and its executives repeatedly stated the firm "never did any business with Jeffrey Epstein." DOJ file releases (Feb 2026) via FT and CNN revealed:
- CEO Marc Rowan communicated with Epstein on Apollo tax affairs in mid-2010s
- Epstein received internal Apollo financial records (including Athene Holding share offering docs forwarded from Black's family office)
- Senior decision-makers were in contact with Epstein despite public denials
- On Oct 30, 2020 earnings call, Black stated: "First and most important, Apollo never did any business with Epstein." — now alleged to be knowingly false

### Catalyst Events
- **Feb 1, 2026:** FT reports Rowan held wide-ranging tax discussions with Epstein
- **Feb 17, 2026:** Two teachers' unions (>$27.5B Apollo commitments) request SEC investigation into Apollo's "lack of candour" on Epstein ties
- **Feb 21, 2026:** Class period end date (corrective disclosure date)
- **Mar 2, 2026:** *Feldman* complaint filed in SDNY
- Market cap destruction: ~$12B+ lost from corrective disclosures

## THESIS IMPLICATIONS

1. **Single case = consolidation coming:** Under PSLRA, the court will appoint one lead plaintiff by May 1. The firm with the largest institutional loss gets priority. All four firms are competing. This is the normal pre-certification phase.

2. **Leon Black naming = media amplification:** Black is more famous than Rowan with general public. His formal defendant status guarantees continued headline risk even after the initial shock fades.

3. **No APODS private credit lawsuit yet** — but the gating (Mar 23, $730M trapped) creates investor grievance conditions. Watch for follow-on filing in Q2 2026 if APODS investors organize.

4. **APO stock:** Trading ~$110-113 range as of late Mar 26. Down from ~$140+ before Epstein disclosures. Class period market cap loss estimated at $12B+.

5. **May 1 deadline:** Key date — watch for institutional plaintiff announcements (pension funds, large asset managers) filing to lead. Large institutional lead plaintiff = better-funded litigation = higher settlement risk for APO.
