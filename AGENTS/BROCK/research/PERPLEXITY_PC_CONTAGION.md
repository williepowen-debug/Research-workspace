# Perplexity — Private Credit Contagion Mechanics
**Source:** Perplexity (standard), received Mar 25 2026

## KEY FINDINGS

### Perplexity's Unique Value: The Clean Stage Framework

Most useful output is the 6-stage contagion map — cleanest of the three sources:

| Stage | Mechanical Break | Most Exposed | Observable Signal |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1. Gate | Redemption queue exceeds liquidity sleeve | Semi-liquid PC funds, feeders | Pro-rata payouts, longer queues, platform cash stress |
| 2. Cash substitution | Investors raise liquidity elsewhere | Pensions, insurers, FoFs, wealth platforms | Redemptions from liquid credit funds, commitment freeze, secondary discounts |
| 3. Financing tighten | Lenders cut advance rates / resize facilities | NAV lines, warehouse borrowers, weaker managers | Higher spreads, lower haircuts, non-renewals |
| 4. Honest marks | External prices override manager marks | BDCs, marked portfolios, overlapping issuers | NAV losses, downgrades, rising non-accruals |
| 5. Structured spillover | CLO tests and tranche economics worsen | CLO equity, junior debt, weak loan issuers | CCC pressure, OC erosion, lower tranche pricing |
| 6. Bank impairment | Facilities marked down or not rolled | Bank fund-finance books, leveraged-credit lenders | Reserve builds, write-downs, reduced credit availability |

### 2007 Lag Estimate
- First fund gate/bailout → visible bank balance sheet impairment: **~one quarter**
- First fund failure → existential dealer failure: **8-9 months**
- Applied to Jan 2026 start: bank effects visible ~Q2 2026, franchise-threatening stress later IF marks keep falling

### Marks Resolution
- No automatic SEC/OCC/FDIC trigger forces industry-wide repricing
- Requires chain of corroboration: public BDC NAV declines → rating downgrades → secondary trades → tighter facility terms → realized losses/restructurings
- Lag from first honest mark to industry-wide repricing: "measured in months, not days"

### Allocator Behavioral Sequence (Consistent with Claude)
1. Conserve liquidity (immediate)
2. Slow/stop new commitments (weeks)
3. Use secondaries or liquid adjacent assets for cash (1-3 months)
4. Only stickiest liability-driven investors wait for gates to lift

### Where Perplexity Adds Nuance

**On CLO transmission:** "A BDC downgrade mainly raises that vehicle's own funding cost. It does not mechanically force CLOs to dump loans unless underlying corporate borrowers themselves migrate into buckets that breach CCC concentrations." Confirms Claude's correction of Gemini.

**On bank exposure comparability:** "The systemic vulnerability is similar in form: opaque collateral, correlated marks, short-dated financing against longer-dated assets. The key difference is that private credit is less mark-to-market day to day, so transmission can look slower at first and then accelerate once external marks appear."
