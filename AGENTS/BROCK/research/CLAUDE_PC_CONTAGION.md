# Claude Deep Search — Private Credit Contagion Mechanics
**Source:** Claude Deep Search, received Mar 25 2026

## KEY FINDINGS (unique vs Gemini)

### 1. Four Transmission Channels — Same Framework, Better Evidence

**Same four channels as Gemini but with superior sourcing:**
- Feeder fund cascade: Blackstone $400M injection into BCRED feeder confirmed
- Preemptive redemptions: Academic evidence (Boyson/Stahel/Stulz 2010, *Journal of Finance*) — probability of extreme poor performance jumps 2%→21% as concurrent fund stress rises. Apollo 11.2% redemptions, Ares 11.6% — investors pulling PREEMPTIVELY, not based on credit losses
- Counterparty tightening: BIS (2024) confirms procyclical behavior — lenders tighten across similar collateral types, not just the specific gated fund
- CLO mechanics: More nuanced — OC failure diverts cash but forced liquidation requires EOD (adjusted par <102.5% of AAA par) + majority AAA holder vote. HIGH BAR. BlackRock Baker CLO 2021-1 already failing OC tests since Apr 2024.

### 2. Key Corrections vs Gemini

**Gemini overstated several things:**
- **CLO forced selling:** Gemini implied automatic. Claude clarifies: OC failure = cash diversion, NOT forced selling. Actual liquidation requires EOD + AAA holder vote. Much higher bar. Managers sell CCC voluntarily to cure tests — "voluntary but compelled."
- **Bank exposure:** Gemini said $410-540B. Claude says $95B committed (Y-14Q data), $56B utilized. Broader measure $300-322B. **Fed estimates simultaneous drawdown = ~2bps CET1 impact.** Not existentially threatening.
- **Timeline:** Claude's is more conservative. Gemini had manager insolvency by Jul, credit crunch by Aug. Claude pushes bank margin calls to Sep-Oct, noting lower leverage (1-2x vs Bear's 10-35x) slows the spiral.
- **Norinchukin-style avalanche framing:** Claude explicitly says "NOT a 2007 replay" — structurally distinct, lower leverage, slower-moving.

**Critical nuance:** 2026 has THREE structural differences:
1. Fund leverage 1-2x (not 10-35x) → slower liquidation spiral
2. Retail BDC base ($222B, up from $34B in 2021) → PERSISTENT non-strategic redemption pressure (accelerates)
3. No collateral fraud analog — stress is AI disruption of software borrowers (genuine economic shift, not rating failure)

### 4. Allocator Behavior — Three Crisis Precedents

**2008-09:** Harvard ($36.9B) had 79% in illiquid + unfunded → lost 27.3%, forced to borrow $2.5B, sell $2B equities at distressed prices, halt $1.2B construction. Buyout fund secondaries hit 50.7% of NAV at trough. Forced sellers earned ~1% IRR vs 23.7% for buyers.

**2020 COVID:** Sophisticated investors ran PREEMPTIVELY based on threat of gates, not actual imposition. Called fund managers to learn gate thresholds, then redeemed just before. Structure predicted outflows better than portfolio quality.

**2022 UK LDI:** £25B in gilts + £10B in corporate bonds sold in 5 weeks. Pension funds with illiquid allocations couldn't rebalance — denominator effect made them MORE overweight illiquid as they sold liquid. Post-crisis: regulators explicitly warn about illiquid allocations.

**Behavioral escalation sequence (consistent across all 3):**
1. Preemptive redemption from ungated funds (IMMEDIATE — happening now)
2. Inflated redemption requests to account for proration (WEEKS)
3. Secondary market sales at widening discounts (1-3 MONTHS)
4. New commitment freeze (3-6 MONTHS)
5. Permanent allocation reductions (6-18 MONTHS)

Goldman projects $45-70B in total outflows from retail PC vehicles over next 2 years.

### 5. The Marks Problem — Central Vulnerability

**Five forces that compelled honest marks in 2007-08:**
1. Actual liquidation (Merrill's failed $850M auction — only $100M sold)
2. ABX index as transparent reference (collapsed 100→<20)
3. Rating agency mass downgrades ($2T in MBS, 80% of AAA CDOs → junk)
4. Auditor liability risk (Big Four pushed back)
5. Competitive pressure (UBS marked first Oct 1 → everyone had to follow. Meredith Whitney proved reputational cost of being last.)

**Lag: 3-6 months for initial adjustment, 18-24 months for full resolution.**

**Current markers already engaged:**
- BlackRock TCP Capital: 19% NAV plunge (admission prior marks were wrong)
- FSK: 5.5% non-accrual, 14.7% PIK income (vs 6.3% peer median)
- Listed BDC sector: median 21% discount to NAV = market says "we don't believe the marks"
- Jay Clayton (former SEC Chair, now SDNY US Attorney): "sketchy marks" drawing prosecutorial attention
- Blue Owl $1.4B sale at 99.7¢ — but truly distressed sales haven't occurred yet

**Key signal:** "The most important observable signal over the next 90 days is not additional gates — it is the emergence of market-clearing prices significantly below stated NAV."
