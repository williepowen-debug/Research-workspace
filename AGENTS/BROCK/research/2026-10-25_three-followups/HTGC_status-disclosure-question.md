# HTGC status disclosure — response to Will's hypothesis, October25

Will asked whether HTGC might purposefully obscure loan statuses. A narrow re-read of the existing June30 primary filing supplies an item to scrutinize, but does not establish intent. No further study, monitor, altered forecast or threshold is commissioned by this discussion.

The current SOI shows Annex Cloud debt principal10.683M, FV3.325M. Two tranches totaling7.358M principal are marked at zero; a3.325M tranche is marked at100% of principal. All three carry footnote8 (non-accrual/non-income producing). The par-valued tranche has December2025 stated maturity and footnote26, which explicitly describes remaining outstanding beyond maturity in an ongoing workout. The disclosed record therefore makes the trouble observable. The economic justification for full recovery of that tranche, including any distinct priority/collateral/other rights, was not established by our comparison.

Suzy principal22.745M/FV17.037M (~74.90%) carries no footnote8 in the reviewed row. A markdown, non-accrual classification and cash receipt are different measures. The mark alone cannot establish missed payments; absence of a non-accrual flag cannot establish strong borrower finances. The issuer policy says it may continue accrual during collection where collateral is sufficient to collect contractual amounts.

Our prior peer comparisons and clean-reported-metrics falsifier are tests of reported outcomes, not an independent audit of borrower cash generation or management intent. Peer agreement can share inputs/assumptions. Keep the registered Q3 falsifier exactly as written; an opacity/concealment hypothesis needs separate evidence, such as serial maturity/PIK concessions without adequate valuation response, contradictory borrower/other-creditor records, or inconsistent borrower identities/disclosures. A current data gap is not proof of deliberate concealment.
