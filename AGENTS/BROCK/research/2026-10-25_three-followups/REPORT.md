## 3. Named borrower draws and conditional funding

### HTGC: commitment pressure is possible, but the distressed-borrower link is not established

June 30 immediately available unfunded commitments total **$408.242M** in the named table (issuer rounds to $408.2M). These are available without achievement of specified milestones; they are not all future/contingent contractual promises. The named rows sum to the issuer total. Matching names to funded software positions identifies **25 borrowers with positive immediately available commitments totaling $191.686M**. All of those USD funded borrower aggregates mark at least 96.26% of principal. Examples: Neo4j $25.0M undrawn against $10.0M funded principal/$9.878M FV; Mango Technologies $23.45M against $45.25M/$44.49M; Intercom $22.5M against $22.554M/$23.785M. `HTGC_named_commitments.json` preserves the joins.

By contrast, the three HTGC software borrower aggregates containing below-90 funded positions are Annex Cloud (31.12% aggregate FV/principal), Simon Data (43.59%) and Suzy (74.90%). They do not appear with positive amounts in the immediately available commitment table. That establishes a missing link in this specific mechanism, not zero legal or contingent obligations to these borrowers. It also does not mean drawing near-par loans cannot consume cash.

June cash is **$48.176M**; SMBC available borrowing approximately **$77.7M**, net of outstanding LOCs, plus MUFG **$352.0M**, gives cash plus conditional borrowing **$477.876M**. Exclude the separate **$175M letter-of-credit facility** and all uncommitted accordions from cash funding. Under an artificial simultaneous 100% draw, and with full eligibility/availability of both borrowing facilities, remaining cash plus borrowing is about **$69.634M** before interest, distributions, maturities and other cash flows. A 50% draw needs $155.945M of bank funding after using cash. At full draw, a loss of approximately **16.2% of the quoted bank capacity** exhausts this limited buffer. This is a sensitivity of a June snapshot, not a predicted haircut or an identified covenant breach. Existing asset coverage alone does not establish that collateral and facility tests would permit every assumed borrowing.

Subsequent financing prevents calling that June arithmetic current. HTGC's October prospectus confirms $325M July 2031 notes and repayment of all $325M September 2026 notes on September 16. The October 8 closing adds $400M gross notes, about $392.6M estimated net; permitted uses include repayment of unsecured/secured debt, investments and general purposes. Net current cash/capacity cannot be reconstructed by adding the whole offering to June cash. Its $350M January 2027 notes are a separate upcoming obligation. No current funding crisis is established.

### GBDC: Pluralsight supplies a named contingent-funding link, but amounts remain unknown

June unfunded commitments are **$580.832M**, of which **$229.880M** are undrawn revolvers. The accessible aggregate XBRL and Note 8 do not provide a complete numerical per-borrower allocation. The SOI nonetheless names **two entirely unfunded Pluralsight one-stop positions**, with fair-value adjustments of **−$0.050M and −$0.124M**. Funded Pluralsight principal is $13.491M and FV $7.476M (55.41%); one $7.417M tranche is non-accrual and marks 23.00%, while two other tranches mark approximately 95%. This demonstrates why neither borrower averages nor zero principal on an undrawn line means no commitment. **The $174,000 valuation adjustment is not the amount available to draw.** Legal draw conditions and undrawn principal are UNKNOWN. No invented draw size is attached to this case.

GBDC has $13.0M cash equivalents plus about $7.0M foreign currencies (exact combined $20.006M). Restricted cash $50.657M is excluded as free cash. The JPM facility has $1,997.500M commitments less $271.559M drawn and no LOCs = **$1,725.941M nominal unused capacity**, subject to leverage and borrowing-base limits. The adviser facility is $300M less **$33.2M already drawn** = **$266.8M**, also conditional; do not count all $300M as unused. Uncommitted JPM accordion capacity is excluded.

Assuming all quoted JPM capacity is usable, all $580.832M borrower commitments draw, and no other flows, the remaining cash plus nominal bank capacity is **$1,165.115M excluding the adviser**. Also charging the June-known $600M August 24 note maturity leaves **$565.115M**, or $831.915M including unused adviser capacity. The June maturity is included as a stress assumption, not asserted as still unpaid in October. Subsequent 8-Ks inspected do not establish the full post-maturity cash reconciliation; a current balance-sheet squeeze cannot be certified. The October 6 issuer 8-K sets the next annual results for **November 17 after close**.

**Judgment:** the general draw mechanism is real and GBDC/Pluralsight is a named example of contingent credit exposure to an impaired borrower. Public data do not establish the amount or unconditional exercisability of that draw. HTGC's thin historical conditional buffer merits attention at its already scheduled Q3 read, but its named impaired-software/large-immediate-draw link is not demonstrated. Neither case supports a current forced-sale conclusion on this evidence.
