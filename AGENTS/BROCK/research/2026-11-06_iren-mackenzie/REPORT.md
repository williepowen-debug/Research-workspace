# IREN and Blue Owl: the Mackenzie loan at the primary

**Date:** 2026-11-06, on Will's word via PROME, 15:34 ET. $0, no paid data, no trade. Basis: KB-BRK-456, 457. Figures are 9/30/2026 unless dated. All derived figures are marked.

## Bottom line

This is a real exposure and not yet a credit event. Four Blue Owl funds hold $282.3M of a 9% loan to IREN's GPU subsidiary with $447.7M more to fund by 12/31. The Microsoft delay does not touch the loan's collateral. But my earlier line that the Microsoft link "runs only through the parent guarantee" was wrong on two counts: the agreement has a default clause naming the Microsoft contract, and IREN's CFO puts Mackenzie capacity in the same late-December to early-2027 window as Horizon.

The loan's real risk is not Microsoft. IREN itself describes this financing as supporting **non-investment-grade customers**. The lenders have no claim on those customer contracts, no financial maintenance covenant, and no lien on IREN's data centers. They have GPUs, 30-month amortization and an unsecured parent guarantee.

If draws lapse at 12/31, IREN loses funding and the funds lose no money. A loss needs a payment failure or a parent-level debt acceleration, and nothing read shows either. No row should change score. Convergence 58/70.

**Parent guarantee (10.3):** a guaranty of payment, not collection. It covers all Borrower obligations, including payments, prepayment premiums, accelerated amounts and enforcement costs. Limit: the fraudulent-transfer savings clause only.

## 3. If the delay reaches it

**Case A: draws lapse at 12/31. Maximum case, every undrawn dollar.**

| | IREN | The four funds |
|---|---|---|
| Effect | Loses up to $1,500M of committed money, 16.8% of LIQUID's $8.935B sources sum | $447.7M commitment ends. Position stays $282.3M and amortizes |
| Cash | The $6B still to fund rises to as much as $7.5B. It must carry the GPUs on cash or re-finance them | Up to $226.9M less to fund at OCIC, $110.4M at OTF, $98.1M at OBDC, $12.3M at OTIC |
| Income | No 9% interest paid on that money | Forgone interest about $57M over the loan life, peak about $40M a year, derived, before fees |
| Credit loss | Not a default | **None** |

"Fully funded" is expressly subject to "applicable availability periods" (release note 5). So this date is inside IREN's own caveat. No figure shows draws since 9/30, and the 10-Q's subsequent events are silent on them. A delivery slip of weeks would hit only GPUs not delivered by about 12/28. Whether that is any of the $1.5B is **unknown**.

For a redemption-limited fund like OCIC, a lapse frees cash. That is my reading.

**Case B: a covenant trips.** Only §26 can trip, and it resolves by prepayment. Facility-wide that is about $0.9B to $1.1B to reach 50% in early 2027, derived, plus premium. The funds' 29.8% share is about $280M to $325M, paid to them. It is IREN's cash problem and a return of capital to the funds. It becomes a default only if IREN cannot pay.

**Case C: real default, funds' share only, before any parent recovery.** Illustration, 6 months after funding, balance 81.7% of original. GPU recovery is my assumption, not a market value.

| Funds' claim | GPU recovery, % of original cost | Shortfall | % of four funds' $35.16B net assets |
|---|---|---|---|
| $230.7M (drawn stays $282.3M) | 50% | $74M | 0.21 |
| $230.7M | 30% | $137M | 0.39 |
| $596.4M (fully drawn $730.0M) | 50% | $191M | 0.54 |
| $596.4M | 30% | $353M | 1.00 |

The shortfall is then an unsecured claim on IREN, beside $8,061M of recourse debt principal including $6,746M of converts. IREN's $6B of data centers are unencumbered today, and it plans to borrow against them first (Horizons 1 to 4). That would put new secured debt ahead of this guarantee on those assets. Even the worst row leaves BRK-07's $500M unreached on this name alone.

**The Microsoft route:** GPU Financing 1.0, $1,980M funded at 9/30, is non-recourse at IE US Hardware 3, apart from limited parent guarantees. On my reading, its acceleration would stop Mackenzie draws but would not default the Mackenzie loan. Hardware 3 IS a Non-Recourse Subsidiary by name: Annex 4 lists IE US Hardware 3 LLC, its Holdings and FinCo, IE US Development Holdings 3 Inc. and Granite Holdings LLC (page image exhibit101-mfsa055.jpg). *(Corrected 2026-11-10, KB-BRK-474: this line said Annex 4 did not list it; the text layer prints the names before the heading, the page image shows them under it.)* Not a lawyer's reading.

## 7. What would change this

**Real credit story, any one:**
1. A missed payment on this loan, or an acceleration over $250M at IREN or a Significant Subsidiary.
2. Over $500M of draws lapsing with lenders declining to extend, and the $6B not closed.
3. A Mackenzie customer default or a prepayment clawback. Non-IG customers are about one-third of the $20.3B backlog, about $6.8B, derived.
4. A 12/31 mark under 95 of commitment at any of the four funds, or non-accrual.

**Kills it:** GPU 2.0 fully drawn by 12/31, over $4B ARR operating by 2/28/2027, Horizon data center financing closed, first amortization paid, and 12/31 marks at 98 or better of commitment.
