## V. REDEMPTION REQUEST % — REFLEXIVE LOOP SIGNAL

**Background:** OBDCII is a non-traded BDC with periodic tender offers (currently SUSPENDED as of recent disclosure).

| Redemption Scenario | Signal Level | OWL Impact |
|--------------------|-------------|------------|
| Tender still suspended, no update | Continued gating | Yellow — narrative building |
| Tender reinstated with <5% queue | Stress managed | Neutral — exit position |
| Tender reinstated with 5-10% queue | Demand significant but manageable | Orange |
| Tender reinstated with >10% queue OR suspended again | Reflexive loop starting | **Red — HOLD position aggressively** |
| Gate formally extended, distribution suspended | BCRED parallel event | **Maximum hold — potential 10-bagger on OWL move** |

**Reflexivity threshold:** >10% redemption queue = system is in feedback mode. At this level:
1. Management must sell assets to fund redemptions
2. Selling → marks decline
3. Declining marks → more investors want out
4. Repeat until gate or stabilization

**BCRED precedent:** Blackstone BREIT limited redemptions at ~5% of NAV/month. When they hit that cap consistently, the negative narrative accelerated. OBDCII has already suspended its tender — the question is whether Q4 data shows the pressure that caused the suspension.
