**The contractual distinction that matters:** §5.13 defines performing first-lien bank loans as requiring both performance and cash-pay status. Cash-pay status requires at least quarterly cash interest and at least two-thirds of total current-period interest, also satisfying a rate floor. Thus fund accounting accrual and facility “performing” collateral are different tests. The all-PIK disclosed A terms cannot automatically qualify for the higher cash-pay first-lien category. Contractual non-performing first-lien is defined by exclusion from the performing category; it therefore need not mean the fund has recorded non-accrual or a default. Unquoted advance-rate bands are 72.5/67.5/65.0% for performing first-lien and 40.0/37.5/35.0% for non-performing first-lien across the three debt/equity bands.
