New York, October 13, 2026 – Citigroup Inc. today reported net income for the third quarter 2026 of $5.2 billion, or$2.85 per diluted share, on revenues of $23.8 billion. This compares to net income of $3.8 billion, or $1.86 perdiluted share, on revenues of $22.1 billion for the third quarter 2025.
As previously disclosed(3), third quarter results in the prior-year period included a notable item consisting of agoodwill impairment of $726 million ($714 million after-tax), recorded in operating expenses, related to Citi’sagreement to sell a 25% equity stake in Grupo Financiero Banamex, S.A. de C.V. (Banamex).
Revenues increased 8% from the prior-year period, driven by growth in Services, Wealth, Markets and Banking,partially offset by a decline in All Other.
Net income was $5.2 billion, compared to $3.8 billion in the prior-year period on a reported basis, driven by higherrevenues and lower expenses, partially offset by a higher provision for credit losses. Excluding the notable item, netincome in the prior-year period was $4.5 billion(3).
Earnings per share of $2.85 increased from $1.86 per diluted share in the prior-year period on a reported basis,reflecting higher net income and a lower share count due to share repurchases. Excluding the notable item,earnings per share in the prior-year period was $2.24(3).
Percentage comparisons throughout this press release are calculated for the third quarter 2026 versus the thirdquarter 2025, unless otherwise specified.

Citigroup
Citigroup revenues of $23.8 billion in the third quarter 2026 increased 8%, driven by growth in Services, Wealth, Markets and Banking, partially offset by a declinein All Other. Net interest income increased 14%, driven by growth in Markets, Services, Wealth and U.S. Consumer Cards (USCC), partially offset by declines in AllOther and Banking. Non-interest revenue decreased 4%, driven by declines in Markets and USCC, largely offset by growth in All Other, Services, Banking andWealth.
Citigroup operating expenses of $14.0 billion were down 2% on a reported basis, driven by the absence of the notable item as well as lower professionalservices expenses, partially offset by higher transactional and product servicing expenses, deposit insurance costs, compensation and benefits as well as thecontribution to the Citi Foundation. Excluding the notable item in the prior-year period, expenses were up 3%(3).
Citigroup provision for credit losses was $2.6 billion, reflecting $2.2 billion of net credit losses and a net reserve build of $369 million, driven by changes in themacroeconomic outlook and portfolio growth. Net credit losses were unchanged from the prior-year period. The provision in the prior-year period was $2.5 billion,reflecting $2.2 billion of net credit losses and a net reserve build of $236 million, driven by portfolio growth and transfer risk, partially offset by changes in themacroeconomic outlook.
Citigroup net income was $5.2 billion in the third quarter 2026, compared to net income of $3.8 billion in the prior-year period on a reported basis, driven byhigher revenues and lower expenses, partially offset by a higher provision for credit losses. Excluding the notable item, net income in the prior-year period was$4.5 billion(3). Citigroup’s effective tax rate was approximately 26% in the third quarter 2026 compared to 29% in the third quarter 2025.

Services
