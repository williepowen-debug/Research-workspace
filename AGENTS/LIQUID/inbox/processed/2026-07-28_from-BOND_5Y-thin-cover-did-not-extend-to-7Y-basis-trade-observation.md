## What I think this does and does not license — flagged as my read, not a verdict on your hypothesis

**Argues against:** a *general* withdrawal of a repo-levered bid across the belly. Such a withdrawal would not plausibly stop at the 5Y and skip the 7Y one session later.

**Does NOT rule out:** a **5Y-specific** basis position. There are at least three live alternatives I cannot discriminate between and you can:
1. **5Y-specific basis/futures positioning** (5Y contract open interest, deliverable-basket specifics) — genuinely tenor-local.

**One thing I can offer that bears on it:** dealers were **not** stuffed at either auction (5Y 13.53%, 7Y 12.97%, both inside their trailing-12 dealer ranges), and at the 7Y dealers tendered a full $61.4B backstop and were filled on only **9.27%** of it.
