## Why you

WALTER's commission said explicitly: *"if filed and cross-defaulted, LIQUID's spread thesis gets a mechanism map."* **They are not cross-defaulted. Here is the map anyway, and the transmission channel is real — it just isn't the one the frame assumed.**

**The load-bearing negative:** I searched all four issuers (NVDA, ORCL, META, CRWV) for cross-default and cross-collateral language. **There is no filed contractual link between any two of them.** What exists is (a) *intra*-entity security at CoreWeave's SPV, and (b) *rating-triggered* obligations at ORCL. **The AI-credit web is economic and rating-mediated, not contractual** — which means a contagion model built on covenant cascades will not fire, and one built on rating migration will.

**Where the spread thesis does get a mechanism:**

1. **ORCL's rating-triggered collateral channel, disclosed by ORCL itself:** a downgrade *"could reduce our access to, or increase the cost of, commercial paper or other short-term financing, **affect the terms or availability of certain long-term commitments (including data center leases)**, limit eligibility to contract with certain customers, and **increase collateral, letter of credit or other credit support requirements under certain contractual arrangements.**"* ORCL is at **BBB-/A-3** (S&P, 2026-07-09) — **one notch above sub-IG** — with S&P-adjusted leverage expected **mid-4x in FY27 against a 4x trigger**. Its revolver margin is itself rating-linked (SOFR **+87.5 to +150bp depending on rating**). **This is the non-linear node in the chain.**

2. **The contractual covenant is nowhere near binding, which matters for timing.** ORCL's EBITDA/net-interest covenant sits at **7.83x vs a 3.0x floor** (EBITDA $29.9B / net interest $3.819B, FY26). Even fully seasoning the $43.0B FY26 issuance with zero coupon already recognized — a deliberate worst case — coverage only falls to ~4.82x. **Do not expect a covenant event; expect a rating event.**

3. **META is the largest unsecured borrower here and has *no* financial covenants** ($84.0B notes outstanding, stated explicitly), plus **~$41B of off-balance-sheet residual value guarantees** on data-center campuses (~$28B threshold + ~$13B on a second venture closing Q3 2026, exposure peaking from 2029). **Contingent obligations on data-center asset values, at ~11× NVIDIA's entire filed guarantee book.**

4. **Where the market is already pricing the risk explicitly: CoreWeave.** $24.9B total debt, DDTL stack at stated rates of **15% / 11% / 9% / 9% / 7%**, interest expense **+103% YoY**, with the $8.5B DDTL 4.0 held **non-recourse** at an SPV (CCAC VIII, MUFG agent) secured on the SPV's equity and substantially all its assets. **The lenders hold the asset risk there and have priced it at 7–15%.** Two customers = **65% of revenue.**
