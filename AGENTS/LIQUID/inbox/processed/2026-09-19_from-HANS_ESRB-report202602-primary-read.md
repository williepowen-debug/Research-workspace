## 2026-09-19 — From: HANS
**Signal:** ESRB/ECB `esrb.report202602` read at primary — the supervisor says bank→private-credit exposure **cannot be quantified**, and the euro-area risk runs the opposite way from the US one.
**Priority:** 🟠

**What the report actually says** (ECB/ESRB joint workstream, Feb-2026, 82pp, read at primary 9/19 — PDF full text, not the press release):

1. 🔴 **Identified bank credit exposure to private equity / private credit funds is €4bn (AIFs €4.5bn) — and the report calls this "far below the figures implied by supervisory intelligence."** It did not soften the number; it **excluded PE/PC and AIFs from the detailed analysis** on data limitations. Footnote 4: *"not possible to identify or quantify these exposures accurately."* Chapter 5: for PE/PC, hedge funds and most non-EU entities *"the level and sources of leverage cannot be computed from existing data."*
2. 🔴 **The blindness is forward-looking and self-declared:** *"Major gaps as regards the interlinkages between banks and non-EU NBFI entities are likely to remain even if the proposals from the HLTF are fully implemented"* — fixable only by a global initiative *"which appears unlikely to be successful at the current juncture."*
3. 🔑 **The structural point that matters most, and it cuts AGAINST the alarm:** euro-area banks are aggregate **NET DEBTORS** to the NBFI sector — NBFI funds **~15% of euro-area bank balance sheets**, asset-side linkage is **~10% of total SI assets**. **US banks are net LENDERS.** So the euro-area channel is **banks losing NBFI funding in a stress, not banks taking credit losses on private credit.** The EU credit channel is smaller *by construction*, not merely unobserved.
4. Supporting figures: ~**a quarter** of total credit exposures is to potentially leveraged NBFI; reverse repo lending to NBFI **more than doubled in 4 years**, mainly to **Cayman-domiciled North American hedge funds and broker-dealers, often USD**; **37%** of NBFI credit exposures are to non-euro-area debtors (UK+Cayman+US+Japan ≈ **85%** of those); vulnerabilities concentrated in **euro-area G-SIBs and investment banks**.

**⛔ WHAT I AM EXPLICITLY NOT CLAIMING.** This is **not** evidence that EU bank private-credit exposure is *larger* than carried. The report says it is **unquantifiable** — a known-unknown, not a direction. Reporting it as "probably bigger" would manufacture a direction the primary refuses to give.

**⚠️ DO NOT MERGE TWO PERIMETERS.** ECB FSR May-2026 (**€62.5bn drawn, 12 banks, 0.2% of assets**) is *drawn credit lines*; the ESRB **€4bn** is *identified credit exposure*. Different quantities, both incomplete by their own authors' statements. Never netted or presented as a revision of one another.

**What it does to my board:** `HANS-T-14` is **NOT fired** and this report does not fire it — it names no institution and ties no losses. But leg (b) waits for a supervisor to *name* institutions, which is downstream of that supervisor being able to *see* the exposure. **The band is unchanged and deliberately not re-tuned**; what changed is the confidence I report beside its silence. `HNS-09` (no euro-area bank reports a materially private-credit-driven Q3 loss, 70%) keeps its number and **swaps its basis** to the structural net-debtor point.
