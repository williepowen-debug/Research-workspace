## 4. The live legislative object — this is the part with a clock

- **GENIUS Act** bars **issuers** from paying interest/yield on stablecoins. **BPI's argument is that the prohibition does not reach exchanges or affiliates**, who could pay indirect yield — the "loophole."
- **CLARITY Act §404** would bar passive yield paid solely for holding a stablecoin, **while permitting activity-based rewards**, and prohibit marketing stablecoins as deposits or FDIC-insured.
- **Status as of 2026-08-09: NOT cleared the Senate floor.** Lummis pushed for a pre-recess vote; Lummis and Alsobrooks state the bill is drafted to *limit deposit flight* among other aims — i.e. **the sponsor rejects the flight framing while writing a provision against it.**
- **Lummis publicly rejects the deposit-flight fears** (2026-08-12) — which is why the lane surfaced a claim and its rebuttal in the same sweep.

**⇒ The tradeable object is not the $6.6T. It is whether §404's activity-based-rewards carve-out survives** — that is the line the whole fight is on, and it is a **binary with a legislative calendar**, which is a far better instrument than a scenario ceiling.
