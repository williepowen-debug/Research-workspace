## Kernel
KFF's October 30 analysis reports finalized 2027 benchmark-silver premiums averaging +13% nationally: +11% in HealthCare.gov states and +16% in state-platform states. This is new final-rate evidence relative to CARL/DOC's proposed +14–15% context. The earlier approximately +15% measure was a median proposed insurer increase; it is not a like-for-like series revised down to 13%.

The figure describes gross benchmark premiums, not every household's net bill. Subsidies cushion most enrollees; households above 400% of the federal poverty level lack premium credits and face full prices. This strengthens the specific health-cost/affordability pressure channel before November 1 enrollment, without establishing broad consumer spending collapse or a uniform 13% disposable-income shock.
