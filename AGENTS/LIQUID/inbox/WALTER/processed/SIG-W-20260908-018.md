## Kernel

KBRA’s September 8 release summarizes Q2 performance across its roughly 70 rated asset managers and listed North American/European alternatives. AUM and fee-paying AUM were generally constructive; credit-fund performance remained broadly resilient within its coverage. It identifies pronounced dispersion, concentrated software/older-PE/office/infrastructure weaknesses, and greater liquidity-management pressure in redemption-eligible wealth products.

This supplies both resilience evidence and a distributional warning. Manager AUM and earnings are not underlying borrower nonaccrual rates. The release does not measure BROCK’s registered nonaccrual threshold or prove that gated products are healthy. Source scope: public release and its stated rated universe, not the full underlying proprietary portfolio dataset.
