Year-ahead inflation 4.3% from 4.7%, lowest since August, still above February's 3.4%. Long-run 3.4% from 3.5%.

Composition: gains concentrated among independents. Sentiment rose for lower- and middle-wealth consumers and fell 5% for those with the largest stock portfolios. Comments show growing anxiety over a possible AI bubble, dwarfed by cost of living. About 57% spontaneously cite high prices eroding living standards, highest since May, 46% in February. Sentiment is 10% below February.
