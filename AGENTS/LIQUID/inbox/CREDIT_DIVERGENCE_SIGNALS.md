# Credit Divergence Signals (Feb 2026)
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research on equity-credit divergence after layoffs

## Key Data Points

1. **IG spreads touched 78bps in late 2025** — tightest in 20 years. Now widening.
2. **BBB widening while AAA stays tight** — credit market starting to discriminate. BBB is where fallen angel risk lives (one downgrade → forced HY selling).
3. **44% of Jan 2026 defaults were distressed exchanges** — companies restructuring debt quietly rather than formally defaulting. Headline default rate understates actual stress.
4. **Bank bond supply may drop 40% in 2026** — M&A + regulatory capital relief = technical support for bank spreads (could mask fundamental weakness).
5. **Industrial net leverage flat at 2.9x, but Basic Industries +0.5x** — sector dispersion increasing.
