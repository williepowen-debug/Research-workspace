## Result and decision relevance

**Confirmed:** Car-Mart disclosed liquidity/collateral covenant trouble, a lack of additional origination financing and constrained lending to its customers in its September9 10-Q. Its October21 filing then disclosed further temporary relief and a signed incremental loan agreement with stronger payment priority for the new money. That supplies an observable borrower→financing-provider response and an operating credit-supply channel. It does **not** establish bank credit losses, fund distress, a forced sale or system-wide contagion.

**Approaching:** relief currently runs through **Sunday November1**. The same contract also requires specified legal invoices to be paid in cash by **October28**, but the invoice total is not public. **November1 is a relief deadline, not the scheduled maturity of the roughly $305.6M previously outstanding term debt.** Scheduled maturity remains October30,2030, subject to acceleration and other contract terms. No new acceleration act is evidenced in this review.

**Unobservable here:** current legally eligible cash, actual receipt/net use of the incremental money, current borrowing-base deficiency, the source/cash cost of the ABS redemption and individual lenders’ allocations or bank funding. Therefore there is no defensible current cash-runway calculation, bank-loss amount or probability of failure. Those gaps are the boundary of this one case, not a reason to expand to another borrower.

**Decision improved:** use a disclosed contract deadline and named lender action to distinguish (a) recurring accommodation, (b) signed conditional financing, (c) cash actually funded, and (d) remedies executed. The case adds evidence beyond a spread or share-price move. It does not justify translating Car-Mart stress into a bank trade or treating November1 as automatic bankruptcy. BROCK retains L741/L794; the next checks already exist.

## What crossed a link, and what remains a warning

1. **Borrower→customer credit supply: observed historically.** S1 says additional origination funding/warehouse financing was unavailable at its September9 issuance date and constrained the company’s ability to originate receivables and serve demand. It reports reduced originations, tighter underwriting, lower inventory and prior FY2026 closures/staff reductions. The fiscal-year actions predate the October amendment; they cannot be represented as consequences of the new $5M agreement. Management’s account supports a named financing constraint, not isolation of credit deterioration from every operating cause.
2. **Borrower→existing creditors: observed contractual action.** S4 confirms senior new-money terms, controlled use of proceeds and borrowing-base prepayment accommodation; S3 confirms extension through November1. These show negotiated action by identified financing parties. We have no lender mark, realised loss, refused request or evidence a financing vehicle itself is distressed.
3. **Trust→noteholders/collateral: observed transaction, incomplete cash bridge.** S3 says all2024-2 notes were redeemed and receivables released. The source of call cash is missing. A completed redemption is counterevidence to an assertion that those noteholders went unpaid; no bank loss is inferred.
4. **Fund creditors→banks: unobserved.** The Agent and Trustee are named; no actual bank loan exposure to the borrower or lending funds is established. Fund names and pledge permissions do not close this link. That is the stopping point for the bank-contagion claim.

Contractual rights to accelerate, charge default interest or control accounts are **approaching-risk mechanisms**, not proof those rights were exercised. Defaults under prior terms, compliance with revised terms and unresolved going-concern doubt can coexist; the July31/September9 assertions do not grade October compliance. The public conformed agreement’s base minimum-liquidity clause must not override the later specifically extended relief.

## Containment, falsifiers and the precise stopping condition

Containment evidence already present: repeated accommodation; signed additional commitments; deferral of deficiency prepayment unless requested; completed repayment of one ABS trust’s noteholders; historical revised-covenant compliance. These weaken an automatic-imminent-failure interpretation without proving sustainable financing.

Evidence that would materially contain the next step: a completed recapitalization/refinancing with actual net proceeds and known uses; permanent waiver or cured covenant/deficiency with eligible cash reconciliation; confirmed drawable origination financing; independently disclosed funded repayment/current cash. Another extension reduces immediate deadline risk but does not by itself resolve the case.

Evidence of a further transmission step would be an actual deficiency demand/cash-dominion or acceleration notice, a missed payment or filed restructuring, a lender’s attributed write-down/cash need, or identified bank financing withdrawn/called against the lending vehicle. None is invented here. A funding line’s existence or expiry alone would not prove a cash shortfall.

**Missing fields:** completed current Liquidity Report and account eligibility; borrowing-base certificate/data tape and deficiency amount; current Approved Budget and invoices; incremental Funding Notice/flow of funds; ABS call price/cash-source/post-release lien bridge; lender Register/participants and any lender-to-bank funding agreement and current funded balance. These are specific private/unprovided fields, not substitutes for aggregate H.8 changes.

**Next already-scheduled sources:** BROCK L741 **October28** full issuer-feed check for other agreements dated≤October22; BROCK L794 **October30 after close and November2 AM**, around the Sunday November1 relief date, for a further extension, transaction, remedy or other filing. Their existing letters govern; silence records no public filing, not no extension/default. Those public sources may disclose a new action but are **not promised to reveal the private cash or ultimate bank-risk bridge**. No publication date for such private evidence is established. Stop at that limit; no further borrower/fund search is opened.
