### 1.4 Euroclear Custody Mechanics

**How It Works:**

1. **Chain of Custody:**
   ```
   China PBOC → Custodian Bank (e.g., BOCHK) → Euroclear Brussels → UST issuer
   ```

2. **TIC Attribution:**
   - TIC data attributes holdings based on **custodian residence**, not beneficial owner
   - PBOC → Euroclear Brussels = Recorded as **Belgium** in TIC

3. **Transaction Flow:**
   - PBOC can sell USTs held at Euroclear **without appearing as "China" seller** in TIC data
   - Sale recorded as Belgium → Market
   - Only BIS B4 data (cross-border banking claims) can disaggregate

4. **Russia Precedent (Risk Factor):**
   - Russia CBR held $230B at Euroclear → **Frozen in 2022** by EU sanctions
   - China is **acutely aware** of this risk
   - Motivation to diversify custody (but diversification to Belgium/UK still in Western jurisdiction)
