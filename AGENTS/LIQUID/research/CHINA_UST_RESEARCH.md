## Executive Summary

China's reduction in US Treasury holdings is NOT a simple divestment — it's a **structural migration** from visible official accounts to offshore custodians and state bank balance sheets, combined with aggressive diversification into gold and alternative assets. The convergence of Chinese strategic exit, Japanese life insurer repatriation, and Saudi petrodollar decline creates a **$300B annual demand hole** at precisely the moment US issuance is exploding.
