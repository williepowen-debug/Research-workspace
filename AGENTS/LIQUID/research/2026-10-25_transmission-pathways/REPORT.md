# Credit deterioration: transmission pathways and evidence coverage

LIQUID · October 25, 2026 · Will-directed bounded review: “all the ways in which the credit deterioration would or could transfer up the ladder.” This is a research map and coverage assessment, not a new gate, standing collector, probability, trade proposal, task assignment or changed routing rule. Existing owners retain their evidence and judgment. Market observations retain their original dates; no live tape was re-pulled for this review.

## Judgment

**We have identified many important channel families. We have not established complete, operational coverage of the links connecting them.** The reviewed materials support stronger coverage of traded credit prices and measured overnight funding than of the intermediate contracts, contingent cash demands and ultimate loss-bearing entities. This assessment concerns the listed reviewed surfaces, not a census of every fleet artifact.

“Up the ladder” has three distinct meanings: weaker-rated debt affecting stronger-rated debt; borrower losses reaching larger or more senior financial institutions; and financial constraint reaching otherwise healthier operating businesses. These need different tests. The system is a network: a stronger borrower need not default for its bonds to be sold, its lender to cut availability, or its supplier to fail. Conversely, weaker collateral need not impair a senior investor if subordination, cash, term funding or new capital absorbs the shock.

**Calm SOFR is evidence about measured overnight Treasury funding, not a necessary condition for other credit-transmission routes.** The prior LIQ-07 S2 verdict only says its specified reserve-channel loop was not observed inside its frozen window. It does not settle bank credit substitution, insurer cash calls, contract-level funding restrictions or a real-economy credit-supply contraction. No retrospective change to LIQ-07's letter or outcome is proposed.

## Perimeter and coverage vocabulary

Perimeter: deterioration in corporate/consumer/CRE credit moving through lenders, investors, market funding and the real economy, including relevant cross-border and public-sector feedback. Independent oil, policy-rate, growth, cyber or geopolitical shocks are competing causes or amplifiers unless a credit-origin link is evidenced.

The numbered rows below are mechanism families, not independent signals or an exhaustive count of possible contractual structures. Rows can share a borrower, fund or underlying loan. “Partial” means some relevant observations exist but the causal link or usable warning remains incomplete. “Unmeasured” means the relevant contract-level field was not established in this review. Neither means healthy. Ownership is existing domain responsibility from the network/desk surfaces; no new recipient priority or obligation is created here.

These routes operate on different clocks. Collateral, redemptions and short-term funding may matter before quarterly losses are reported; refinancing follows contractual deadlines; real-economy and capital effects may take longer. Those are mechanism-based expectations, not measured lead times for this episode. Credit strength, claim seniority, institution size and funding liquidity are separate axes.

## How to identify a pathway early enough to use

Trace forward from a named weak borrower/asset, then backward from the bank, fund, insurer or liquid market alleged to receive the stress. The two traces must meet at a real exposure or shared constraint. A graph arrow in AGENTS/_NETWORK.md specifies responsibility/routing; it does not prove a balance-sheet transmission edge.

For each candidate bridge, establish:

1. **Identity and exposure:** legal borrower, lender/holder, facility/security, funded amount versus unused commitment, seniority, recourse and source date. Distinguish group ownership from enforceable support; consolidate overlapping claims before summing.
2. **Compulsion and timing:** what event requires cash, absorbs capital or removes funding? Read actual borrowing-base, maturity, coverage, redemption, downgrade, guarantee or termination terms. Show whether a condition is near, met, waived or undisclosed. No generic threshold is imposed on a bespoke contract.
3. **Available absorbers:** cash at the correct entity, unencumbered eligible collateral, genuinely drawable lines, junior protection, new inflows, repayments or capital. Do not double-count a line as both the fund's cash buffer and an independent bank-free absorber.
4. **Earliest reachable evidence:** document/form, reporting date/lag, observation definition and who owns it. Examples: SEC 8-K/exhibits; 10-Q facility tables; trustee 10-D reports; bank RC-L/earnings/10-Q; insurer statutory disclosures; existing fund tender notices. Explicitly mark private certificates, PB terms or offshore quotes unavailable when they are.
5. **Transmission observation:** connect the event to a documented response at the next node—actual draw, cash diversion, sale, credit-line reduction, capital use or declined refinancing. A correlation, falling share price or another desk citing the same source is not that bridge. Attribution can remain unresolved despite a documented sequence.
6. **Falsifier / containment test:** record successful funding, restored collateral headroom, new equity, improving cash flows, no associated response in a sufficiently observed matched institution, or a better common-factor explanation. No disclosure is not a falsifier. Keep “link exists,” “pressure building,” “response observed” and “contained/unresolved” distinct in ordinary prose; these are proposed research annotations, not new machine-state tokens or registered gates.

Use separate units: exposure stock, actual cash requirement over a stated horizon, loss after protection, and funding availability. Do not aggregate them into a convergence score or use a calibrated-looking probability without a base rate. Where data permit, compare the same borrower across lenders and exposed institutions against a suitable control while accounting for rates/sector/growth. Document that such matching is often infeasible; no causal coefficient is inferred here.
