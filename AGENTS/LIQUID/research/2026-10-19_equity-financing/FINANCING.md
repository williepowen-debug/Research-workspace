## Credit deterioration versus absent fields

Higher coupons, higher interest bills and weaker equity returns are not interchangeable credit events. No failed financing or current covenant breach was established in these four checked cases. This is a bounded evidence statement, **not** a clean bill of health or a census of credit events. No fresh rating-action census, issuer bond/CDS time series, current lender certificates, Q3 liquidity balances or complete net-floating exposure was obtained. Market index spread broadening from the morning work cannot fill these issuer-specific fields. Stronger financing evidence would require those fields and dates preceding, rather than selected after, the equity weakness.
