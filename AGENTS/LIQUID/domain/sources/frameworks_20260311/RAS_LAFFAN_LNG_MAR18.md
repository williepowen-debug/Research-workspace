# Ras Laffan LNG Supply Disruption Analysis — Mar 18, 2026

## Facility Profile
Qatar is the world's largest LNG exporter. Ras Laffan Industrial City is the **primary** LNG complex — essentially all of Qatar's liquefaction capacity.

| Metric | Estimate | Basis |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Qatar LNG exports | ~77 MT/yr | ~2024 baseline |
| Global LNG trade | ~420 MT/yr | IEA/industry consensus |
| **Qatar share of global LNG** | **~18-22%** | Direct calculation |
| Ras Laffan as % of Qatar capacity | **~95%+** | Ras Laffan = primary complex |

Hosts QatarEnergy, ExxonMobil, Shell, TotalEnergies, ConocoPhillips joint ventures. Also hosts NGL, petrochemical, GTL plants.

Prior QatarEnergy force majeure had already disrupted. Mar 18 event is kinetic strike damage — not just shipping disruption. Repair timeline: weeks to months minimum.

- (a) domestic emergency capital needs

**QIA becomes a forced seller of liquid assets including USTs.** This is additive to the $50-90B/month anchor selling thesis. Qatar was NOT in the original 4-anchor model (Japan, China, Korea, Gulf broadly). This is a new forced seller.
