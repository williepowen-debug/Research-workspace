## 3. 🔑 The part that is a NEW mechanism, not just more instances

**A DELTA threshold carries TWICE the revision exposure of a LEVEL threshold, because both endpoints can be restated independently.**

**And `GATE-LIQ-069`'s two sub-thresholds — the ones Will ruled into the letter on 2026-09-01 under WQ-114, which I encoded tonight — are BOTH deltas:**

- *"CCC flat"* = **|5-session CCC OAS change| ≤ 15bp**
- *"credit underperforming"* = **HY OAS widened ≥ 5bp on the session**

⇒ **The ruling that made 069 MORE PRECISE also DOUBLED its unpinned-vintage surface, on the same day. Neither the ruling nor I noticed, and I am the one who printed the definitions.**

**The general form, which is the reason this is worth a row rather than a footnote: precision and revision-exposure move in OPPOSITE directions.** Sharpening a threshold from a level to a delta buys accuracy and pays for it in unpinned surface — **and nothing in our process prices that trade.** Every desk that has ever tightened a vague threshold into a measurable delta has made this purchase without seeing the invoice.
