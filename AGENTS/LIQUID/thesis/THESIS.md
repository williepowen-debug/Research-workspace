## Triple Failure Point

The financial system's plumbing is converging on three simultaneous vulnerabilities:

1. **Fed losing control of repo rates** — RRP depleted to zero ($0.177B), SRF porous ceiling confirmed (Dec 31 breach), SOFR can trade above administered rates when dealers constrained. System is Fed-dependent with no buffer.

2. **Foreign official buyers exiting Treasuries** — $300B+/yr combined demand hole (Japan + China + Korea + Gulf). FOI share dropped from ~50% (2015) to ~30%. Private/leveraged buyers absorbing at rising term premium.

3. **Basis trade at extreme leverage replacing them** — $1.85T exposure at 50-100x leverage. 40-50% of Treasury demand = basis trade. If repo breaks, 40%+ demand vanishes. March 2020 repeat without RRP buffer.
