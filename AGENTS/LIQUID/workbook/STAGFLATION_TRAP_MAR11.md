# Stagflation Trap — Structural Confirmation Analysis
**Filed:** 2026-03-11 | **From:** LIQUID Q3 signal processing

---

## Confirmation Event: Mar 10, 2026

**Oil -11% (intraday), 30Y +4bps**

This is the clearest possible confirmation of the stagflation trap. In a normal recession, oil falls → inflation fear drops → bond yields fall (price UP) → flight-to-safety works.

**In our regime:** Oil falls → Treasuries DON'T rally → because the bond-selling driver is NOT oil/inflation alone. It is **structural term premium repricing** (FOI exit + fiscal deficit + Warsh uncertainty).

Mechanism: Oil -11% removes ~2bps of inflation premium. But ongoing FOI selling adds ~6bps of term premium. Net: **bonds SELL on an oil crash**.

---

## The Trap Is Energy-Independent (Key Insight, Mar 10)

Even a historic 11% single-session oil crash could not break the trap. This means:

- The trap is **structural**, not cyclical
- The driver is the FOI/fiscal/term-premium story, NOT the oil price story
- Any "inflation relief from lower oil" thesis for Treasury rallies is FALSE in this regime

## 60/40 Breakdown

Both legs are now positively correlated (both falling simultaneously). Risk parity funds (Bridgewater, AQW) are being forced to delever BOTH sides simultaneously.

This is an **amplifier, not a stabilizer**. When the correlation assumption breaks, systematic deleveraging adds fuel to both equity and bond selloffs.

---

## Basis Trade Implications

The basis trade is long cash Treasury, short futures. When cash Treasury prices DON'T rise even when oil falls, the "fundamental value floor" under Treasury prices has moved lower.

Repo funding costs (SOFR 3.64%) remain elevated relative to Treasury yields → margin on basis trade is still squeezed even when the headline inflation driver deflates.
