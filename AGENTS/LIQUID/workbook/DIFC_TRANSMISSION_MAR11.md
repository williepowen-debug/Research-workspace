## Transmission Paths (4 mechanisms)

### Path 1: DIFC → Gulf Sovereign Dollar-Clearing → FOI Exit (HIGHEST STAKES)
DIFC operational impairment → disrupts dollar-clearing for Gulf sovereign flows → Saudi/UAE/Kuwait SWFs cannot roll UST positions → forced to hold cash rather than recycle into USTs → **accelerates FOI exit thesis**.

This is the most dangerous path because it converts a geopolitical event directly into the $300B/yr FOI demand hole thesis activation — at speed (hours vs months for structural exit).

### Path 2: DIFC → Bank CDS → HY Facility Terms → Spread Widening
DIFC strike → financial sector CDS widens (US/European banks with MENA exposure) → HY OAS excludes financials BUT credit committees tighten HY facility terms → new issuance pulled → **spread widening +5-15bps over 2-3 days**.

Not immediate but mechanically reliable. Estimated lag: 1-3 days post-event.

### Path 3: War Risk Insurance Cascade
DIFC hit = largest war-risk commercial insurance loss since 9/11. Reinsurers crater (Lloyds, Swiss Re, Munich Re all writing war risk for commercial financial infrastructure). Insurance stocks → collateral for margin calls → forced selling in other assets.

This is an **amplifier mechanism** — creates correlated selling pressure across asset classes not explained by direct exposure.

### Path 4: CLO Arranger Pause (Slow)
Any material escalation near financial centers causes arranger risk committees to pause new CLO pricing. Removes bid from leveraged loans → widens HY spreads indirectly.

Effect: 1-2 week delay in CLO supply/demand imbalance. Slow-moving but cumulative.
