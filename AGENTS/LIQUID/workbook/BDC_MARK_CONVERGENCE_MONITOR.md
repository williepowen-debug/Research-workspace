## Why this monitor exists (mechanism — unchanged)

TCW writing Red Lobster equity to 2¢ while keeping the 2029 loan at par (Apr 14, Bloomberg) is the canonical Stage-3 precursor. The chain: (1) equity written to zero quietly → (2) loan stays at par ("PIK'ing, no covenant breach") → (3) redemption/audit/secondary sale forces the loan mark → (4) reference pricing forces mark-downs across holders → (5) **public BDCs (daily NAV) hit first — prices/dividends/NAV move** → (6) the public repricing event.

**Watching for Step 5 — the public tell.** In X1 terms: the **publicly-marked wrapper** (BDC NAV + equity + BIZD + the APO/alt-manager basket) repricing DOWN *ahead of* the non-traded manager marks (BCRED/ADS/etc.) = "**wrapper leads managers down**." When that is confirmed **AND** HY OAS >280, the X1 conjunction fires. Q2 marks are the test of whether the marks are now **catching down to where public equity already priced them** (divergence closing = wrapper-led transmission real) or **holding** (divergence persists/resolves benign = wrapper-lead NOT confirmed).
