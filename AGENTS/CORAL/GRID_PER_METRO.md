## Convergence read (what the H1 print changes)

**1. Core epicenter = UNCHANGED but HARDENED.** The SW/Central Gulf Coast (Punta Gorda, Cape Coral, Lakeland) is where foreclosure + negative-equity + SF-correction + fire-sale + snowbird/migration loss ALL stack — the maximum-convergence cells. The H1 print **hardens** this: FL now holds the nation's **#1 (Punta Gorda 0.50%) and #2 (Lakeland 0.48%)** metros by foreclosure rate, and Cape Coral carries the #1 US negative-equity share. This is not new geography — it is the same metros getting a harder foreclosure confirmation.

**2. Foreclosure channel BROADENED (geography), but multi-channel convergence did NOT.** Jacksonville + Ocala are **new foreclosure entrants** (not in the May top-5). But they light **1–2 channels only** — Jacksonville has MF oversupply and a decent fiscal/labor base (not migration-central); Ocala is a near-total data gap. So the foreclosure channel spread NE/Central, but no NEW metro has begun stacking channels. Watch NE-FL for channel-stacking; do not yet treat Jax/Ocala as convergence metros.

**3. Tampa + Orlando ROTATED OUT of the FC top-5** (were named in May, absent H1). Both were never the multi-channel leaders (Tampa MF-driven; Orlando tourism-strong). Read as within-noise metro rotation, NOT a de-escalation of the core.

**4. Driver signature — the load-bearing nuance.** The metros lighting up on foreclosure (SW/Central Gulf Coast SF metros) are **negative-equity / carrying-cost driven** (2024-vintage underwater + insurance/assessment cost-stack), NOT the Miami-Dade/Broward condo-tower metros that CORAL's *condo-recap* thesis mechanism runs through. The foreclosure epicenter and the condo-assessment epicenter are **different geographies**. This matters for the bank-transmission read (below): the H1 print does NOT show foreclosure broadening INTO SE-FL condo collateral — the specific bridge condition the thesis pre-registered.

**Grid verdict: 🟠 holds. Epicenter hardened, foreclosure channel broadened NE, no new multi-channel metro. Convergence read does NOT upgrade.**
