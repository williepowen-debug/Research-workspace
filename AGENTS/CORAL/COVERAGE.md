## Cross-pillar convergence (the CORAL edge)

The point of owning all ten is seeing them **stack on the same geography**. Live examples (7/21 state):
- **SW-FL Gulf Coast (Punta Gorda/Cape Coral/North Port/Lakeland belt):** nation's #1/#2 foreclosure metros + >10% recent-vintage negative equity + **Parcl MSI 6.1-6.96 (🔴 supply-side leg FIRED 7/23, Will-ratified — cash capitulation-clearing marking collateral down)** + snowbird/Canadian demand loss — four channels on the same metros. This, not the statewide blend, is the stress epicenter.
- **SE FL Atlantic coast (Miami-Dade/Broward/PB/Keys):** deepest metro condo inventory + vintage price leg ($216-313/sf and falling) + assessment/master-policy cost stack + record-band sargassum amenity tax + the condo-tower bank collateral. The condo-recap mechanism runs here; note the foreclosure epicenter (SW-FL SF) and the condo-recap epicenter (SE-FL towers) are DIFFERENT geographies — the bank bridge requires the second, not the first.
- **The household cash drain** = special assessments (1) + master-policy insurance (5) + property tax (9, two-sided after Nov 3) stacking on the same owner → forced selling (2/3) → out-migration (7) → demand hole loops back. Property-tax relief, if passed, partially decompresses exactly this stack — framing-dependent passage is therefore a first-order thesis variable.
- **Tourism (8) + migration (7)** demand engine: bifurcated, not failed — Orlando/overseas strong, Canadian/SW-FL broken. The bifurcation maps onto the same SW-FL stress belt above.
