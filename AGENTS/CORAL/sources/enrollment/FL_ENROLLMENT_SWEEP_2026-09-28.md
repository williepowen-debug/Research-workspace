# Florida public-school enrollment sweep, 2025-26 and 2026-27

**Prepared for:** CORAL research desk
**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Method:** a sweep of news reports plus district and state primary documents. Full sub-reports are in `groupA.md` and `groupB.md` in the same scratchpad.

## Bottom line

- **2025-26: the decline was near-universal.**
  - The state's October membership count (FLDOE Survey 2, Oct-2025 vs Oct-2024, **charters included**) was 2,859,655 → 2,792,954, a drop of **-66,701 (-2.33%)**.
  - **61 of 67 county districts declined.** Only 6 rose, all small: Dixie, Sumter, Hendry, St. Johns, Charlotte and Walton.
  - **Of the 25 districts on the target list, 24 declined.** The exception is **St. Johns (+159, +0.3%)**.
- **2026-27: declines are widespread on the early counts. The official October count does not exist yet.**
  - On a same-count-point basis, **9 of 11 districts with such a figure declined**. The other 2 are **St. Johns (flat: +39 total, -49 in district-run schools)** and **Lee (up, "several hundred" ahead, no number published)**.
  - 3 more districts report declines on a mixed or unstated basis: Hillsborough, Pasco and Duval.
- **The state's own forecast (EDR Education Estimating Conference, 8/11/2026)** has 2026-27 district FTE at 2,722,531, down from 2,749,749 (**-27,218, -0.99%**).
  - That forecast has **47 of 67 districts declining and 20 growing**.
  - Where actual counts exist, **they are coming in weaker than that forecast.** Broward, Miami-Dade, Orange, Palm Beach and Osceola all missed their own projections to the downside, as did St. Johns (forecast +2.2%, came in flat).
- **The operator's recollection holds.** Declines hit every region: South Florida, Tampa Bay, Central Florida, Jacksonville, the Panhandle, Gainesville and Tallahassee. The only districts growing or flat are a handful of exurban counties: St. Johns, Lee, Manatee, Polk (FTE counts only), Marion (a new school, but it is running below its own projection), plus small rural counties.
