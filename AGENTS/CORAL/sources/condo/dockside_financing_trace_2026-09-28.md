## Summary (established vs unknown)

1. **Established (court filing plus press):** In Sept 2023 the association took an "over $18 million" private "trade credit" loan under a Promissory Note from **South Florida Real Estate, LLC**, an affiliate of its Ian contractor SFR Services. The loan is **not from a bank**. It was repaid through a 12-year special assessment ($650 to >$1,000/month per unit; 10.5% per press). Proceeds were to be held by the lender and paid to SFR on approved draws.
2. **Unknown for the 2023 loan:** exact face amount, amount actually drawn, collateral, and how it is treated in the bankruptcy. The association has contested the Note since 1/8/24. SF Real Estate is on the creditor matrix with no amount and is absent from the top-20 list. Schedules, claims and Doc 89 are unread.
3. **Unknown for the 2026 DIP:** lender, amount, rate, collateral, priority and funding. Only the short titles are free: motion Doc 238 (9/1/26) and approval Doc 247 (9/8/26).
4. **Connection between the two loans: UNKNOWN.** No free document links them. UCC shows **no** filing against the association in 2022–2026 (two lapsed filings, 1997 Eastern Savings Bank and 2011 LM Funding, both assessment pledges). Orange County Official Records were **not searchable** (reCAPTCHA).
5. **Correction:** the court's calendar shows the 9/29/26 hearing covers fee, stay-violation and repair motions. **It is not a plan confirmation.** No plan appears in the free record.
