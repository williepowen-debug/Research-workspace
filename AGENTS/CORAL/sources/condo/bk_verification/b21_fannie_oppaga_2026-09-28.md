## 2. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac: condo project ineligibility for bankruptcy or receivership

### 2a. Rule text (PRIMARY: selling-guide.fanniemae.com, read by curl; Guide "Published: September 02 2026")

**B4-2.1-03, Ineligible Projects (08/05/2026)**, § "Projects Terminating or Involved in Insolvency Proceedings", verbatim:
> "A project must not be the subject of an action that would cause the project to cease to exist, including termination, deconversion, or dissolution of the project's legal structure. In addition, a project must not be the subject of a voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation, or receivership proceeding, or any substantially similar action under state or federal law. This includes any project that has voted or is in the process of voting on any of the actions or proceedings described above."

The same topic's litigation row, verbatim:
> "Projects in which the HOA or co-op corporation is named as a party to pending litigation, or for which the project sponsor or developer is named as a party to pending litigation that relates to the safety, structural soundness, habitability, or functional use of the project."

The rule has minor-litigation exceptions (non-monetary disputes, insurer-defended cases, minor HOA-plaintiff suits, and so on). HOA-plaintiff construction-defect suits are "not considered a minor matter" unless the defects were already remediated.

How to read it:
- The subject is **"a project"**, not "the HOA as debtor." The text contains **no carve-out** for post-confirmation plans, a non-debtor HOA, or a receivership limited to litigation.
- A termination **vote in progress** already disqualifies the project.
- The only relief valve is a project-by-project exception through PERS (B4-2.2-05, 08/06/2025): "Exceptions … are considered on a project-by-project basis."

### 2b. Scope: which loans the rule reaches (PRIMARY)

| Path | Does the insolvency/termination rule apply? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Full Review with CPM (>10 attached units) | **Yes**: part of project eligibility | B4-2.1-01 (08/05/2026) lists the review methods; B4-2.1-03 |
| PERS (standard or streamlined) | **Yes** | B4-2.1-01; B4-2.2-04 |
| FHA-approved project (FHA and conventional loans) | **Yes**: "the project is not an ineligible project as defined in B4-2.1-03" | B4-2.2-03 (09/04/2018) |
| **Waiver of Project Review**: detached units; 2–4 unit projects; 5–10 unit projects outside a master association; PUDs; Fannie-to-Fannie limited cash-out refi at ≤80% LTV | **Yes, explicitly.** Verbatim: "the project is not terminating and is not involved in insolvency proceedings (described in B4-2.1-03…)". The CPM "Unavailable" bar also applies. The critical-repairs bar applies under a waiver **only** to Fannie-to-Fannie limited cash-out refis in projects with >10 attached units (or 5–10 units in a master association). | B4-2.1-02 (08/05/2026) |
| High LTV refinance (B5-7-01) | **No, and this is the only carve-out found.** Verbatim: "all project review requirements are waived with the exception that the lender must confirm the project is not a condo or co-op hotel or motel, houseboat project, timeshare, or segmented ownership project." | B5-7-01 (08/06/2025) |
| CPM status "Unavailable" | Ineligible on every path: "Loans secured by units in projects with a status of 'Unavailable' in Condo Project Manager (CPM) or on the DU Underwriting Findings report are ineligible for purchase" | B4-2.1-03 Note |

**Bottom line (PRIMARY):** a bankruptcy, receivership or termination vote makes the project ineligible for essentially **every** Fannie loan on its units, including waived-review loans. The one exception is High LTV refinances, which are a narrow refinance product. The rule is not limited to Full Review or PERS.

## 3. OPPAGA Report 26-04: *Milestone Inspection Reporting Data 2024 and 2025*, July 2026

**PRIMARY:** oppaga.fl.gov/Documents/Reports/26-04.pdf, 24 pp, PDF created 2026-07-09. Page numbers below are the printed page numbers.

- **It is not an audit.** It is a statutory data report under s. 553.899(13), F.S. OPPAGA analyzed data that the Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) collected, and interviewed some building officials (p.2).
- **Window:** milestone inspections completed in calendar 2024 and 2025, as received by DBPR **through 2026-03-31** (p.6).
- Data that arrived after 2026-04-01, from 3 jurisdictions (2024) and 20 (2025), is excluded (p.6 fn6).
