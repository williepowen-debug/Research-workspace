## 6. Five-line summary

1. **No bank creditor was found in the documents we reviewed.** This is **not** a finding of "no bank exposure". Schedules D and E/F for Green Terrace, Sunset Palm Villas, Dockside and OIRC, and Dockside's September 2026 DIP (post-petition) lender, are UNRESOLVED (PACER only). The only financial lender to an association found so far is **Boken Lending II, LLC**, a *private* mortgage lender at Green Terrace. Its lien was ruled junior to the City of West Palm Beach, and the City's lien exceeds the $13.2M-plus sale.
2. **Banks appear only in side roles.** U.S. Bank / Nationstar (Ocean Five) and U.S. Bank Trust (Dockside) hold **unit owners' mortgages**, and the Ocean Five motion states "The Debtor is not the borrower." First American Trust, FSB (OIRC) is a **timeshare trustee and co-owner**. First Citizens (Ocean Five) is only the **depository**. Small bank-affiliated finance companies (First Insurance Funding, Navitas, TIAA Commercial Finance) appear on Dockside's list with $0 or unknown amounts.
3. **Documented causes:** a unit-owner judgment ($583K) at Ocean Five; a default judgment, probably held by a decedent's estate, at Sunset Palm Villas; Hurricane Ian flood damage plus a $6.02M contractor lien dispute at Dockside; unpaid city water and code liens, assessment non-payment and mismanagement at Green Terrace; and a timeshare brand exit with an aging property and reserves short of need at OIRC.
4. **Claim errors:** Ocean Five had 16 units, not 13, and its judge suffix was LMI until June 2026. "South Florida Real Estate LLC" is not a Dockside lender. The Charleston "Dockside" press story is a different building.
5. **Status:** Ocean Five's plan is confirmed and consummated. The other four are active. Green Terrace (trustee sale approved 5/11/26) and OIRC ($8.1M sale approved 5/18/26) are sale / wind-down cases. Sunset Palm Villas' plan is not yet confirmed, and Dockside's amended plan is contested and it has new DIP financing.
