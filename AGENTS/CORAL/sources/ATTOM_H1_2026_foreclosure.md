## Divergences / nuances called out

1. **Window discipline** — the old CORAL/HOMER figure "FL 1 in 2,110" is **May MONTHLY**; this "1 in 373" is **H1 CUMULATIVE** (Jan–Jun). Consistent (~0.047%/mo × 6 ≈ 0.28% ≈ 1 in 356), NOT a step-change. Always tag the window.
2. **FL rate-rank rose #3 → #1 over the half.** HOMER's Q1 print had FL #3 by state rate (IN 1/739, SC 1/743, FL 1/750). By May-monthly and H1-cumulative FL is #1. FL is deteriorating *faster than the Q1 leaders* — a genuine relative signal, though the SC margin is thin.
3. **Press "Jacksonville" framing understates it.** Jax is the least-bad named FL metro; the story is the SW/Central Gulf Coast (Punta Gorda #1, Lakeland #2, Cape Coral). Press likely led with Jacksonville because it's the largest (1M+) FL metro.
4. **ATTOM's own framing = "gradual normalization,"** not crisis. Weight the +21% YoY as real but off suppressed lows.
