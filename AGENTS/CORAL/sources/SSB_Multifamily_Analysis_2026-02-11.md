**Exposure:**
- Total MF Exposure: ~$4.4B (~9% of loans)
- MF Term Loans (tracked): $1.16B

- South Carolina: ~20% (LOW/MODERATE RISK)
- Texas: ~14% (Austin = HIGH RISK, Dallas = MODERATE)
- Colorado: <5% (MODERATE RISK)

**"Support and Survive" Dynamic:**
- Borrowers subsidizing negative carry to protect 48% equity stakes
- NPLs near zero despite 9.36% substandard
- Dependent on sponsor liquidity continuing
