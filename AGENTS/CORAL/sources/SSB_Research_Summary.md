## Association Prime / HOA Exposure (CRITICAL)

**What we now know:**

1. **Association Prime deposits:** ~$2 billion
2. **IBTX had NO HOA vertical** — SSB is expanding this into TX/CO
3. **SSB lends to associations** for capital repairs (not just deposits)
4. **"Non-warrantable condo" financing** — SSB keeps GSE-rejected loans on balance sheet

**The disclosure gap:**
> "The silence on this specific vector suggests management categorizes this exposure within broader '1-4 Family' or 'Investor CRE' buckets"

> "SouthState is retaining the exact tail risk that the GSEs are avoiding"

**SB 4-D Risk:**
- No quantitative disclosure on "SB 4-D impacted loans"
- Assessments can range $20K-$100K+ per unit
- If owners can't pay → default on mortgages OR association insolvency
- Association loan collateral = right to collect assessments → deteriorates if owners default
