## The Florida Thesis

### Core Mechanism: "The Coral Bleaching"

Florida's aging condo stock is undergoing forced recapitalization. Post-Surfside legislation (SB 4-D, HB 913) eliminated reserve waivers and mandated structural inspections.

### Transmission Chain

```
SIRS Deadline (Dec 31, 2025)
    → Reserve Gap Discovery → Special Assessments ($30K-$110K/unit)
    → Owner Strategic Defaults → Association Revenue Collapse
    → Master Loan Default (VLY, regional banks) → Receivership
    → Bulk Sale at 40-60% Discount → Bank Loss Crystallization
```

### The Biscayne 21 Complication

FL Supreme Court (Oct 2025) upheld 100% owner consent for termination. Freezes voluntary exits — receivership becomes primary resolution path.
