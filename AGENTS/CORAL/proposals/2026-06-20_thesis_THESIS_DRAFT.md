## Current Thesis

Florida is not in a clean statewide crash. It is in a **geography-concentrated cost-stack and demand-normalization cycle** where condo/household stress is real, but bank losses have not yet crystallized.

**Working model:**

> Post-Surfside reserve law forces aging condo stock to recapitalize. Special assessments, commercial/condo insurance, HOA burden, and weak migration/tourism demand push owners toward forced selling, default, bankruptcy, or association delinquency. The bank leg only becomes tradeable when that household/association stress appears in Q2+ FL bank NPL/NCO/reserve data.

**Core distinction:** Do not equate “Florida real estate is stressed” with “Florida banks are breaking.” Q1 2026 says the first is true and the second is not yet true.

---

## Core Mechanism — “The Coral Bleaching”

```text
SIRS / reserve funding mandate live Jan 1 2026
  → reserve gaps surface
  → special assessments / association borrowing
  → owner forced selling, strategic default, or bankruptcy
  → association delinquency + cash-flow stress
  → master-loan / condo-association loan stress
  → receivership / termination / bulk-sale path
  → bank loss crystallization
```

**Status:** mechanism intact at the household/condo level; final bank-loss leg delayed.

**Legal bottleneck:** *Biscayne 21* keeps voluntary termination hard where declarations require unanimous consent, making receivership / judicial resolution the live endgame to monitor.

## Current Operating View

1. **Household/condo stress is confirmed.** Foreclosures, condo price declines, assessments, negative equity, and bankruptcy acceleration all point the same way.
2. **Bank transmission is not confirmed in Q1 2026.** Classified CRE looks mostly like rate-shock reclassification with current payments and collateral cushion, not loss content.
3. **Acute stress window moves to Q2/winter retest.** Q2 2026 bank earnings test whether upstream stress migrates into credit metrics; winter 2026-27 tests snowbird demand + assessment/default behavior.
4. **Geography is the edge.** The signal strengthens when multiple pillars stack on the same metros — especially SW-FL Gulf Coast and SE-FL vintage condo corridors.

## Confirm / Falsify Criteria

### Confirm / upgrade toward 🔴

- Synchronized Q2/Q3 bank deterioration across >1 FL-exposed bank:
  - NPL / NPA migration,
  - realized NCOs,
  - specific reserve builds,
  - classified CRE moving from “current/reclass” to nonaccrual/loss.
- USCB condo-association book begins to sour.
- Condo association bankruptcies / receiverships form a visible cluster beyond one-offs.
- Ch.7 filings in M.D./S.D. Fla exceed ~230/100k and keep accelerating.
- Foreclosure + negative-equity clusters broaden from SW-FL into SE-FL condo collateral.
- Hurricane landfall reverses insurance easing and shocks commercial/condo master-policy costs.

### Falsify / downgrade toward 🟡

- Q2/Q3 banks keep curing: low NCOs, stable/improving NPLs, no reserve build.
- Condo inventory tightens and price declines stabilize without forced-sale acceleration.
- Personal and commercial/condo insurance both ease materially.
- Bankruptcy acceleration fades on a per-capita basis.
- Property-tax relief passes and materially lowers carrying-cost pressure without fiscal backlash.
