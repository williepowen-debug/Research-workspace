## 2026-03-03 — Red bank-cascade / SSB short frame

**Prior frame:** CORAL carried a more acute 🔴 bank-cascade read centered on Florida condo stress transmitting quickly into FL-exposed regional banks, with SSB framed as a short candidate.

**Mechanism then:**
- Surfside reserve law → assessment shock.
- Owner default / association failure → bank collateral and association-loan losses.
- FL bank exposure, especially SSB/VLY/SBCF/BKU, expected to show loss content soon.

**Status now:** superseded by the 2026-06-19 thesis split.

## 2026-06-19 — Thesis split installed in operating view

**Move:** CORAL downgraded from 🔴 immediate bank cascade to 🟠 thesis split.

**New read:**
- Household/condo stress is confirming hard.
- Bank-loss transmission is not confirmed in Q1 2026.
- The durable question is no longer “is Florida stressed?” but “when, if ever, does household/association stress hit bank P&L?”

**Evidence:**
- FL #1 foreclosure ranking; Q1 REOs +108% YoY.
- Condo prices −6.1% YoY; 92% of FL condo markets declining.
- Reserve mandate live Jan 1 2026; special assessments now the forcing function.
- Q1 2026 SSB/SBCF/BKU/VLY credit stable, low NCOs, and no clear FL condo/CRE loss content.

## 2026-06-19 — Insurance channel reclassified from broad easing / broad hardening into split read

**Old risk:** insurance was easy to summarize incorrectly as either “the amplifier is easing” or “insurance remains a crisis.”

**New classification:** split channel.

| Layer | Read | Thesis effect |
|---|---|---|
| Personal lines / Citizens personal / reinsurance | Easing: Citizens depopulation, personal rate cuts, 6/1 reinsurance down 15-20% risk-adjusted. | Counterweight to acute-crash frame. |
| Commercial / condo-association master-policy layer | Still rising: Citizens Commercial Lines +10.4% capped vs +18.8% uncapped indication. | Still an assessment / HOA / association cash-flow amplifier. |

**Rule added:** never write “insurance easing” without specifying the layer.

## 2026-06-20 — Recent-vintage negative equity added as upstream collateral canary

**Move:** Added `VX-CORAL-NEG-EQ-01` and KB entry ML-CORAL-021.

**Why it matters:** Negative equity is the missing precondition between price declines and default behavior. Recent buyers underwater are less able or willing to fund special assessments or wait through a condo/SF downturn.

**Current read:**
- ~18-20% of 2024-vintage financed FL buyers underwater.
- 84%+ of underwater loans originated in the last ~3.5 years.
- Cape Coral / Punta Gorda / Fort Myers / Naples / North Port / Lakeland cluster overlaps with foreclosure and SF correction.

**Boundary:** upstream collateral stress, not bank P&L evidence yet.

## 2026-06-20 — Bankruptcy filings added as consumer canary, with per-capita caveat

**Move:** Added `VX-CORAL-BKCY-01` and KB entry ML-CORAL-022.

**Finding:** M.D. Fla #2 and S.D. Fla #6 by volume is true, but population-inflated. The better signal is acceleration and per-capita pressure.

**Current read:**
- FL roughly ~190 filings / 100k vs national ~168-173.
- +22.2% YoY acceleration, consumer-led.
- Business filings remain a small share of district volume.

**Tripwire:** Ch.7 per-capita in M.D./S.D. Fla >~230/100k moves bankruptcy from canary to broader consumer-stress channel.

## 2026-06-20 — SSB short retired / bank-loss transmission delayed

**Move:** SSB short thesis explicitly retired.

**Why:** Q1 2026 evidence broke the tradeable bank-short expression even though the underlying Florida household mechanism remains intact.

**Evidence:**
- SSB Q1 EPS beat, stable credit, NCO 9 bps, NPA 0.66%.
- Classified CRE framed as rate-shock reclassification, not loss content: ~56% weighted-average LTV and 98% current.
- Management watchlist pointed to lower-income consumer + SBA floating-rate, not FL condo/CRE.
- SSB $90P Jun 18 expired worthless.

**New timing:** bank-leg retest moves to Q2 2026 earnings (~late July) and winter 2026-27 seasonal/assessment window.
