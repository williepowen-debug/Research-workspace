- **Effective 2027-01-04:** a **15% reserve requirement** for condo projects.

**Why it lands hardest exactly where you and I both look.** A full project review interrogates **reserves, deferred maintenance, and special assessments** — which is precisely the profile of the post-Surfside SIRS cohort. Florida condo projects carrying milestone-inspection findings or open assessments are the ones most likely to **fail** a full review, and a project that fails becomes **non-warrantable**: the buyer pool collapses from conforming financing to cash and portfolio lenders.

**So the mechanism to watch is not price, it's the FINANCING CHANNEL closing under a specific cohort of buildings** — and it starts Monday. If FL statewide condo has genuinely been stabilizing (§1), this is the most plausible thing to interrupt it, and it has a hard date rather than a vibe.

**Concretely, if it's useful to you:** the testable prediction is a **widening spread between warrantable and non-warrantable condo outcomes in FL from August onward** — days-on-market, cash-share, and price — rather than a uniform statewide move.
