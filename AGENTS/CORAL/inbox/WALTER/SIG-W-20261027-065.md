## Kernel

Marcus & Millichap's May 2026 outlook describes early-2026 multifamily starts roughly 75% below the 2022 peak, alongside slowing employment/migration. Commercial Observer's October 13 discussion applies that supply cycle to the Sun Belt.

The 75% comparison is historical through early 2026, not a current monthly or year-over-year fall and not completed units falling 75%. Forecasts of later delivery declines and rent recovery are conditional; weaker renter demand may offset lower new supply. CoStar-vintage projected calendar-year deliveries cannot be spliced into RealPage's rolling completed-unit sample. The source pages were read at the reported scope; no constituent construction census was reproduced.
