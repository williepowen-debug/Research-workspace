## PILLAR 7 — Migration & Demographics (Census Vintage 2025, released Jan 2026)

**The growth engine is breaking down.**
- **Net domestic migration +22,517** (yr-end Jul 2025) — **−93% from the 2022 peak** (+310,892); FL fell from #1 to **#8**, passed by Alabama (+23,358) — a historic first. "Cooling, not reversing" (FL Realtors, May 2026).
- **Kept positive only by international migration: +178,674** (#1 in US) — but **−57% YoY** and Census projects a further **−75%** under enforcement. "9 of 10 new Floridians came from overseas" (WLRN Feb 2026).
- **Total pop 23,462,518** (Jul 2025), +196,680 (+0.84%, no longer a top-10 growth rate). **Natural change went NEGATIVE (−1,300)** — deaths exceed births; FL now 100% migration-dependent to grow.
- **Metro domestic net:** Tampa +10,544 (−70% YoY), Orlando **+779 (−95%)**, Miami **−67,418** (offset by intl → +123,471 total), Jacksonville positive, Cape Coral +1.88%. Pinellas County: largest population loss of any US county except LA (Axios, Apr 2026).
- **Gross out-migration ~510K** (post-GFC high). Destinations: GA (52,371), TX (52,219), NC (33,591), NY, PA. **Drivers = CORAL's own channels:** insurance (181% above natl), condo assessments, housing cost, RTO, climate.
- **Retiree story weakening:** FL still #1 net 60+ (+44,504, 2023) but SC may have taken the crown in 2025; "halfback" migration (FL→SC/NC/TN/GA) accelerating; **20-29 cohort reversed to net outflow** (affordability). Foreign-born share 25.6% (up from 23.1%).

*Sources: Census Vintage 2025 (census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026); WLRN Feb 10 2026; Newsweek; ResiClub; FL Realtors "cooling not reversing" May 2026; ClickOrlando May 21 2026; SmartAsset 2025; 247WallSt Jun 17 2026; FL EDR componentsofchange Dec 2025.*

## PILLAR 9 — State Fiscal & Property-Tax Policy

**🔴 Property-tax amendment ON THE NOV 3 2026 BALLOT (HJR 1F).**
- Passed special session **Jun 2 2026** (House 75-26, Senate 30-9). Needs **60%** to pass.
- Homestead exemption **$50K → $150K (2027) → $250K (2028)** → inflation-adjusted, **non-school levies only** (school portion, ~40% of a bill, still owed). New-resident phase-in ($50K first 5 yrs). Non-homestead assessment cap cut **10% → 5%**.
- Lays a **legislative pathway to full elimination.**
- **Local-gov't (non-school) revenue loss −$4.6B (2027) → −$8.4B (2028)**, no backfill specified → municipal-credit / local-services risk channel.
- *Two-sided for FL real estate:* tailwind for homestead owners (attacks the carrying-cost stack driving out-migration; could support values/slow the bleed) vs. municipal-finance risk + favors owner-occupants over investors.

**State budget cracking at the margin:**
- 2026 regular session **ended Mar 13 with NO budget passed** (extraordinary); resolved in special session. FY26-27 proposed $117.4B ($16.75B reserves); FY25-26 enacted $114.77B (−1.5%).
- Surplus FY26-27 ~$3.77B BUT **deficits FY27-28 (−$1.53B) + FY28-29 (−$6.57B) = −$8.1B combined** (Bond Buyer, Sep 2025). Amy Baker (EDR): "the revenue forecast will not support spending at its recent rate of growth."
- Sales tax (~75% of GR) revised modestly up but "softening at the margins"; flagged "deceleration in construction/real estate and slowing tourism." Corporate income tax down on tariffs. Cruise passengers −7.2% YoY.

**Condo legislation (resolves earlier NOT FOUND):** No new 2026 reserve bill. Operating under **HB 913 / SB 328 (July 2025)** — SIRS due 12/31/25, full reserve funding live **1/1/26**, but with **relief valves: funding via loans/lines of credit, a 2-year reserve pause for repair-priority associations, reserve threshold $10K→$25K, small-building exemption (≤3 stories/≤4 units).** These partially soften the forced-assessment cascade (counter to pure-bear read).

**Insurance reform working:** litigation −35%+ since 2021; 17 new carriers; **rate CUTS filed spring 2026** — Citizens −8.7% statewide (Miami-Dade −14%, Broward −14.1%, PB −11.9%), 73 private carriers −5-15%. HB 815 (eff 7/1/26) bars non-renewal solely on roof age; SB 1028 adds commercial clearinghouses for Citizens depop. **FL homeowners premium ~$7,136/yr ($300K dwelling) vs $2,543 national (+181%)** — direction now DOWN for the first time in years.

*Sources: Tax Foundation Jun 3 2026; CBS Miami Jun 2026; FL House HJR 1F; FL Realtors special-session May 29 2026 + 2026 Legislative Final Report; Bond Buyer Sep 18 2025; Florida Trend Jan 26 2026; Perez Mayoral (HB 913); Boggs Law Jan 2026; Greene & Assoc Apr 2026; Insurance.com Mar 20 2026.*

## PILLAR 4 + ECONOMY — Labor, Construction, CRE

**Labor softening, now above US:**
- **FL unemployment 4.8%** (Apr 2026), **+1.1pp YoY**, 7 straight monthly rises, now **0.5pp ABOVE the US** (4.3%) — reversal of FL's long outperformance. Metro (Mar, NSA): Miami 3.7%, Orlando 4.4%, Tampa/Naples 4.5%, Jax 4.7%, Lakeland 5.4%.
- **7 of 10 sectors negative YoY.** Only healthcare material (+36,400/+2.3%); L&H stalled (+800/+0.1%); construction −4,600 (−0.7%).

**Construction into an ICE labor squeeze:**
- Immigrants **37.9% of FL construction workforce** (4th-highest; roofing ~52%, drywall ~61%). FL is the **national epicenter of ICE enforcement** — 295 287(g) agreements (3× TX), Operation Tidal Wave (1,120 arrests May 2025), Tallahassee site raid (100+), Wildwood (33). Labor supply compression → cost pressure as volumes fall.
- **Permits −6.1% (2025), −31% vs 2021 peak.** Builders: Lennar −53%, D.R. Horton −65% from peak; buydowns/concessions. Tampa Bay counties bucking it (Pinellas +42%, Manatee +42%, Hillsborough +18.7%). Lennar (Miami HQ) top builder; "City Park" 7,800-home Miami-Dade project.

**CRE is condo/residential-specific, NOT commercial-wide (key nuance):**
- **Office:** Miami **12.5%** vacancy — *lowest among top-25 US metros* (down from 15.7%, driven by JPMorgan/Citadel/Amazon; $58.41/sf); Tampa 15.1%; US 17.6%. Miami office is *tight.*
- **Industrial:** Tampa 3.2% (very tight, +6.5% rent); Orlando 7.4% (recovering); Miami 7.7% (softening, −1.8M SF absorption).
- **Retail:** healthy ~4.1% statewide; Miami 3.3%.

**Consumer/housing stress:** FHFA FL HPI −2.3% YoY (vs +2.2% natl, Q3 2025); Case-Shiller Tampa −3.9% (13 straight declines), Miami −1.0%. FL foreclosure rate leads nation; HOA burden Miami-Dade median ~$617/mo (26.9% of median mortgage), +59% 2019-23; 2nd-home Miami mortgage originations −32.2% YoY.

*Sources: BLS LAUS Apr 2026; WUSF May 3 2026; FloridaJobs LMI; Shovels.ai Feb 25 2026; HBWeekly Apr 2026; FL Realtors office May 28 2026; GlobeNewswire industrial Feb 10 2026; Roofing Contractor (ICE); constructionowners.com; LongYield; Fortune Apr 11 2026.*

## PILLAR 2 — Single-Family Housing

- **Statewide SF median $420K** (Apr 2026, +1.8% YoY); closed sales +2.4%; **months supply 4.7** (vs condo 8.9); active listings **−13.7% YoY** (aggregate tightening); 8th straight month of YoY sales gains — but FL Realtors flags a possible **"inflection point"** (Apr-2026 mirrors Apr-2025 & Apr-2019, which then diverged).
- **Correction is Gulf-Coast-concentrated:** Cape Coral −4.8% (metro −9.6 to −10%, "worst in nation," Fortune); North Port −3.1 to −9.3%; Palm Bay −3.8 to −6.1%; Orlando −2.0%; Miami −0.4%; Jacksonville −0.1%; **Tampa SF +2.5%** (divergent).
- **Price cuts on ~43-44% of listings** statewide (Tampa/Sarasota ~49%, Miami-FL 36.8%, Orlando 25-26%). Forecast statewide SF −2 to −4% through YE2026; SW FL condo −8 to −15% peak-to-trough.

*Sources: FL Realtors Apr 2026 (PRNewswire May 15) + inflection-point May 21 2026; Fortune Apr 11 2026; Norada; Redfin (Cape Coral/North Port); HousingWire (price cuts).*

## PILLAR 8 — Tourism & Snowbird Economy (now in)

**Total still growing on records, but the mix is shifting and the snowbird channel is in structural decline.**
- **CY2025 total visitors 143.3M (+0.2%, record)**; out-of-state spend $134.9B (+3%); ~1.8M jobs. BUT **Q1 2026 −1.0% YoY (39.88M)** — lowest Q1 domestic since 2022.
- **Canadian snowbird pullback is structural:** CY2025 3.17M (revised up from 2.9M); **Q1 2026 1.05M, −12.1% YoY.** Airline capacity *deleted*: Air Transat 100% US exit, WestJet −32% transborder ASMs (MCO-Vancouver cut), Air Canada suspended Toronto/Vancouver–Tampa. **52% of Canadians considering selling US property** (~$60B Canadian-owned FL property); ~$2.1B lost spend per 10% Canadian decline. **Hardest-hit: SW FL Gulf Coast (Naples, Marco Island, Ft Lauderdale)** — same metros as the single-family correction.
- **Overseas is the bright spot/offset:** Q1 2026 **+8.5% (record quarter)**; FL #1 US state for international (9.3M, beat NY). Brazil #1, UK +17.2%, Ireland +14.5%, Spain/Argentina strong.
- **Orlando / Epic Universe = game-changer:** opened May 22 2025; ~10M year-1 visitors (~$2B impact); **Orange County TDT record $384.6M FY2025**, Jan 2026 $35M (+6%). MCO 57.8M pax (+1.2%). Disney WDW ~flat (FY25 −1%, FQ1'26 +1%); Universal legacy parks down YoY but Epic offsets.
- **Miami hotels:** event-driven ADR spikes (Apr +12.5%, #1 among top-25 US) **mask softer underlying demand** (RevPAR +0.8% 2025). Tampa-St Pete first ADR decline since 2020.
- **World Cup (Jun 11–Jul 19, Miami host, 7 matches, $920M-$1.3B projected):** national host-city hotel demand ~80% *below* forecast; Miami better-positioned (LatAm access) but a "rate event, not occupancy event."
- **L&H jobs +0.1% YoY (Apr 2026)** — stark deceleration from +1.8% (2024); confirms the labor-pillar stall.

## CROSS-PILLAR SYNTHESIS — the whole-Florida read (2026-06-19)

**The state is in a slow, broad demand-normalization, not an acute crash — and the channels reinforce each other on the same geography.**

1. **The demand engine is failing on two fronts at once:** domestic migration −93% (now #8) AND the Canadian snowbird channel in structural decline (−12% + capacity deleted), both concentrated in the **SW FL Gulf Coast** — which is exactly where single-family prices are falling fastest (Cape Coral −10%, North Port). International migration + overseas tourism are the only offsets, and the migration piece is itself crumbling under enforcement (−57%, −75% projected).
2. **The household cost-stack is the through-line:** insurance (181% above natl) + condo assessments ($25K-$400K/unit) + HOA fees (+59%) are simultaneously (a) driving out-migration, (b) the cited reason Floridians leave, and (c) about to be *partially offset* by the property-tax amendment if it passes Nov 3.
3. **Two genuine COUNTER-currents keep this 🟠 not 🔴:** (a) the **insurance channel is easing decisively** (Citizens 294K + rate cuts, reinsurance −15-20%, 17 new carriers, litigation −35%); (b) **CRE distress is condo/residential-specific, not commercial-wide** (Miami office is the tightest in the US; retail/industrial healthy) — and the **banks aren't breaking** (Q1 NCOs 9-14bps; new names AMTB/BAFN stressed but idiosyncratic, USCB clean).
4. **The bank-transmission leg still hasn't fired** — consistent with the 6/19 thesis-split. Watch USCB (direct condo-association lender) and AMTB (low ACL coverage) as the canaries; Q2 earnings (~late Jul) is the re-test.
5. **Biggest forward variable: the Nov 3 2026 property-tax vote** — a genuine two-sided catalyst (homeowner-carrying-cost tailwind vs. −$8.4B municipal-revenue / local-credit risk).
