## Synthesis

The "Coral Bleaching" mechanism is playing out **at the household/condo level** — reserve mandate live, assessments $25K-$400K/unit, condo prices −6%+ with 92% of markets down, FL #1 in foreclosures with REOs doubling. But the **bank-loss-crystallization leg is not in the Q1 2026 prints**: SSB/SBCF/BKU/VLY all show 9-14bps NCOs and stable/improving credit, with CRE classified explicitly framed as rate-shock reclass (56% LTV, 98% current), and *none* flagged FL condo/CRE as a watchlist. The **insurance channel is easing** (reinsurance −15-20%, Citizens depopulated to 294K + 8.8% rate cut). Net: durable mechanism, delayed transmission — acute bank stress pushed to ~winter 2026-27, which **converges with MARCO's independent FL read.** Re-test at Q2 2026 earnings (~late Jul) — does rate-shock classified CRE migrate to nonaccrual/charge-off, or cure?
