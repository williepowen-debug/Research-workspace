## 4. One event or two?

**Two weather events, one legal emergency.** The St. Johns flood is a slow river flood in Brevard and downstream, fed by the 11/01–03 rain plus this week's 6–10 inches in central Brevard. South Florida is a flash flood on the same wet east-coast pattern this week, which drains in days. The state has now put both under one order (EO 26-227 as amended), so an amended federal request could add Broward and Miami-Dade to the same incident. For CORAL's channels they differ: Brevard is mostly uninsured single-family river flooding outside the condo metros; South Florida is a short flood in the condo core.

**What changes in the 10:45 ET delivery:** (a) the state emergency is now 21 counties including Broward and Miami-Dade (primary), not 14; (b) the order adds the lower St. Johns counties (Putnam, St. Johns, Duval) and Flagler. Its recitals cite only Broward rain and more east-coast rain, not the river, so **I do not read it as a forecast that the river flood will reach Jacksonville.** Nothing else changes: no federal declaration, no loss figure, no spending action, no colour moved. **Next dated wake stays DOCKET L1204, Tue 11/24.** I see no reason to move it. A federal grant or denial, or an amended request that adds South Florida, wakes CORAL earlier (WALTER watches).
