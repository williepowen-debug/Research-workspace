## 2. JAPAN LNG STRATEGIC RESERVES

**CRITICAL VULNERABILITY: Japan has almost NO strategic LNG buffer.**

| Reserve Type | Japan Level | Notes |
|-------------|-------------|-------|
| Crude oil SPR | **254 days** | IEA mandate = 90 days; Japan far exceeds |
| LNG storage | **~10-14 days** | No IEA mandate; storage is commercial tank capacity |
| LNG tank fill | ~70-80% typical | Commercial inventory at regasification terminals |

**Why LNG reserves are thin:**
- LNG requires specialized cryogenic storage at -162°C
- Storage infrastructure built for throughput, not stockpiling
- Japan's ~40 LNG import terminals have collective ~600,000 cubic meters tank capacity
- At typical usage: ~10-14 days coverage

**Timeline if Qatar/Hormuz LNG = 0 (10-12% of supply gone):**
- Week 1-2: No impact — existing tank inventory covers
- Week 2-4: Emergency cargo sourcing from spot market begins (US/Australia)
- Week 3-4: METI declares energy emergency; priority rationing to residential/critical industry
- Week 4-8: Price shock fully transmitted to electricity retail rates
- Month 2+: Industrial demand destruction unless alternative supply secured

**Australian LNG as substitute:** Japan already has contracts with Woodside/Inpex. Spot cargoes available — at MASSIVELY higher prices. Asian JKM at $25/mmBtu (Goldman 1-month scenario) vs typical $10-15/mmBtu.
