  - **Series found: "Sources of Changes in Current Account Balances at the Bank of Japan and Market Operations"** (the DAILY series, `boj.or.jp/en/statistics/boj/fm/juq/` — distinct from the similarly-named MONTHLY "juqp" projections series, which is the wrong one, a monthly-average forecast not a daily fast-confirm).
  - **Cadence:** every business day, ~6:00 PM JST (~7:00 PM JST on month-end, except Dec).   - **URL pattern — highly automatable, predictable date-based construction, no index-page scrape needed for the fetch itself:**
    - Projections: `boj.or.jp/en/statistics/boj/fm/juq/d_release/jp/jp{YYYYMMDD}.xlsx`
    - Provisional: `boj.or.jp/en/statistics/boj/fm/juq/d_release/jx/jx{YYYYMMDD}.xlsx`
    - Final: `boj.or.jp/en/statistics/boj/fm/juq/d_release/jd/{YYYY}/jd{YYYYMMDD}.xlsx`
    - Format: XLSX, ~18-19KB each. (Would need `openpyxl`/`pandas` in `.venv` — not yet confirmed installed; SAM's other scripts use `pdfminer` for PDF sources, this would be a new format.)
  - **Why NOT built tonight (the real gap, not a source problem):** the source is clean and the URL pattern is trivial to script.
