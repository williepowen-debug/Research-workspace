### When to ADD (Tranche 2: +4 shares to 12 total)

**Hard triggers (act on any one — these are the ONLY add greenlights):**
- **BOJ hikes at June meeting** (base case; ~70% SAM / 74% market) — primary
- USD/JPY sustains below 155 for 3+ sessions WITH oil normalizing toward $90

**Soft confirmation (raises conviction; do NOT act alone):**
- BOJ hawkish-hold + dissent split (delivered Apr 28 — 3 dissents)
- Tokyo session re-rates JPY post-binary catalyst (delivered Apr 29-30)
- Vol convergence signal fires (2+ of 3 legs — see vol section)
- FXY call OI clustering at $60-62 (delivered — Apr 30 P/C 0.10x)
- CFTC JPY shorts exceed -100K (currently -93,742)

**Soft-signal convergence rule:** A scenario matrix in STATUS.md may pre-define "+2 at $X.XX" entries tied to specific binary outcomes (e.g., the Apr 28 BOJ matrix). Those entries fire ONLY if **the price is in the planned zone and at least one hard trigger is approaching** (e.g., June BOJ within 14 days, ESR disclosure window open). **Soft signals alone — no matter how many converge — do not greenlight an add.** They keep the position on alert; they do not authorize buying.

**No-chase rule:** If a matrix-defined zone is breached upward before the add fires, the add is **forfeited at that level**. Do NOT pay above the zone. Either:
- Place a limit at the planned zone for a pullback, OR
- Wait for the next zone in the matrix
This rule prevents conviction-bias adds at deteriorated R:R.

**DO NOT add when:**
- Oil is surging and yen is weakening (headwind phase)
- USD/JPY is drifting toward 160 (wrong direction)
- None of the hard triggers are met, regardless of conviction
- Price has run past the planned matrix zone (no chase)

**Hold through target if:**

**Stop loss:**

### Three Use Cases

**1. Conviction Check — "Are we early or wrong?"**
- If evidence mounts (Tankan strong, BOJ hawkish, auctions weak) but vol stays complacent → yellow flag. Smart money disagrees. Ask what they see that we don't.
- If vol starts moving WITH our evidence → confirmation. We're early and right.

**2. Timing — "Is the move imminent?"**
- Vol signals are LEADING indicators. They move before spot.
- Critical check window: April 14-18 (one week pre-BOJ April 28)
- If convergence is firing by then, the move is being front-run. Maximum confidence when trigger fires.

**3. Exit — "Is the move done?"**
- At target ($60-62): check if CVOL is at 18+ and risk reversals deeply negative = peak fear = take profit
- If vol is moderate at target = move may have more to go = hold
