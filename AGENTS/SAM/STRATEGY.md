- Thesis intact, all 3 channels loading

### When to EXIT

**Take profit:**
- FXY reaches $60-62 target AND vol convergence shows RED (CVOL 18+, deeply negative risk reversals, peak fear)

- FXY hits $61 but CVOL only at 13 and positioning moderate — move may have more to go

**Stop loss:**
- FXY below $55.05 / USD/JPY above 167
- Thesis break: no MOF response at 167 AND BOJ turns dovish
- Either alone is insufficient — both required to exit

### Three Use Cases

**1. Conviction Check — "Are we early or wrong?"**
- If evidence mounts (Tankan strong, BOJ hawkish, auctions weak) but vol stays complacent → yellow flag. Smart money disagrees. Ask what they see that we don't.
- If vol starts moving WITH our evidence → confirmation. We're early and right.

**2. Timing — "Is the move imminent?"**
- Vol signals are LEADING indicators. They move before spot.
- Critical check window: April 14-18 (one week pre-BOJ April 28)
- If convergence is firing by then, the move is being front-run. Maximum confidence when trigger fires.

**3. Exit — "Is the move done?"**
- At target ($60-62): check if CVOL is at 18+ and risk reversals deeply negative = peak fear = take profit
- If vol is moderate at target = move may have more to go = hold

### Key Check Dates

- **Apr 28-29:** Pre-BOJ May meeting. Second window.
