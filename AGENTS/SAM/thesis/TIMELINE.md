## THIS WEEK (Mar 31 – Apr 4)

### Mon Mar 31 — FY2025 END
- **Event:** Japan fiscal year closes. Repatriation window shuts.
- **Our view:** Bulk of FY-end selling already done (Feb ¥3.42T was the peak). Today is adjustment, not a wave. After today, the repatriation tailwind for JPY fades near-term.
- **Watch for:** Any outsized flows in final hours. Post-close, the market shifts focus to FY2026 investment plans (announced in April by Big 4 insurers).
- **MOF intervention:** Still on alert. USD/JPY at 159.65 after 160.1 breach Mar 27. Katayama warned at 159.5. Monday/early week = highest intervention probability window (FY-end settlement, thin liquidity). July 2024 precedent: $37B, 5-6% reversal in days.

### Tue Apr 1 — TANKAN SURVEY
- **Event:** BOJ's quarterly business sentiment survey. Manufacturing outlook expected ~15 (up from 12). Non-manufacturing ~28.
- **Our view:** Strong Tankan = April 23-24 hike becomes genuinely live (not just "possible"). The Summary of Opinions already showed the board debating SIZE of hike, not timing. A strong Tankan removes the last data excuse for delay.
- **Branch point:**
  - Strong (mfg outlook ≥15): April hike probability jumps significantly. Market reprices front-end JGBs.
  - Weak (mfg outlook <12): April off the table, May 1 remains base case. Yen weakens near-term.
- **Why it matters:** Tankan is the last major data point before the April 23-24 BOJ meeting.

### Early Apr — SK REFINER CUTS DEEPEN
- **Event:** Korean refinery run cuts (350K+ bpd confirmed) bite harder. GS Caltex, S-Oil offline. SK Energy/HD Hyundai Oilbank unconfirmed but accelerating.
- **Our view:** Asia-Pacific product shortage becomes visible in spot crack spreads this week. Alternative crude sourcing (US Gulf via Panama) arrives late April at earliest — the gap is structural and unbridgeable in April. Japan feels this through imported product prices.
- **Watch for:** Additional refiner announcements, crack spread data, any Japan refiner run cut signals (Japan is next domino if ME supply worsens).

## WEEK 4-5 (Apr 21 – May 2) — THE DECISION WINDOW

### Apr 23-24 — BOJ MEETING (LIVE FOR HIKE)
- **Event:** BOJ Monetary Policy Meeting. Rate decision + Ueda presser.
- **Our view:** April hike is LIVE but not base case. Base case remains May 1. The Summary of Opinions showed board readiness — Takata dissented for 1.00%, members discussed "rapid tightening" and "scale" of hike. If Tankan was strong, April becomes 40-50% probability.
- **Scenarios:**
  - **Hike to 0.75% (our ~35% prob):** Market shock — JGB 2Y spikes, USD/JPY drops 3-5 big figures in days. FXY +3-5%. Carry unwind fires. Mortgage transmission starts (floating rates reprice within months).
  - **Hike to 1.00% (our ~5% prob):** Nuclear scenario. Takata's dissent becomes majority. USD/JPY drops 5-8 big figures. Carry unwind accelerates violently. Household stress immediate.
  - **Hold + hawkish guidance (our ~55% prob):** "We will raise at the next meeting." Market prices May 1 at near-certainty. USD/JPY drifts lower. FXY grinds higher.
  - **Hold + neutral/dovish (our ~5% prob):** Board spooked by oil/geopolitics. Delays again. USD/JPY rallies. FXY gives back gains. May 1 still live but confidence drops.
- **Watch for:** Ueda language on mortgages/household consumption (new since floating mortgage discovery). Any Katayama/Aida commentary in the days after.

### May 1 — BOJ MEETING (BASE CASE HIKE)
- **Event:** BOJ Monetary Policy Meeting + Outlook Report.
- **Our view:** This is THE date. If they didn't hike in April, May 1 is when it happens. Shunto 5.26% gives cover. Ueda removed the growth precondition. Oil-driven CPI makes delay harder to justify. Board is ready.
