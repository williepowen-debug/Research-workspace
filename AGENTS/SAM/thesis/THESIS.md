## STRUCTURAL PILLARS — why long even if Jun 16 disappoints

Under v1.5.1, the thesis backbone is structural, not catalyst-dependent. June 16 is the dominant remaining *catalyst* for resolving the carry trade, but the yen-strengthening direction does not require it. If Jun 16 disappoints (BOJ holds), the position bleeds time and FXY-IV decay, but the structural setup below — none of which resolves at the meeting — remains intact and continues to pressure carry over weeks-to-months. This is the answer to "why am I long if the catalyst slips."

**Pillar 1 — Rate differential at multi-decade extreme.** Fed funds 3.50-3.75% vs BOJ 0.75% = ~280-300bp gap. The gap is the carry. Compression requires either Fed cuts (multi-month tail per § INDEPENDENT CATALYST, Jun 17 reads >97% no-change) or BOJ hikes (Jun 16). The gap is wide enough that even a 25bp BOJ move only compresses it by ~9% of the differential — meaningful as confirmation, not as full resolution. The structural pressure is the *level* of the gap, not the change at any single meeting.

**Pillar 2 — J-ICS lifer long-end abandonment (DOMESTIC; independent of BOJ).** Under J-ICS (live Apr 2025), super-long JGB moves reprice the entire balance sheet immediately (duration mismatch surfaces). Mid-size lifers (Fukoku, Asahi) pivoted 30/40Y → 10-15Y; Big 4 sidelined at the long end. **Critical inversion: lifer absence is the cause of the yield blowout, not the consequence — higher long-end yields don't draw insurers back under solvency repricing.** This pushes BOJ toward normalization OR a YCC-style cap (D2 scenario, 32-40%) regardless of the June meeting. JGB 30Y 3.817% (Jun 3) and a soft May 27 40Y auction (BTC 2.702) confirm the mechanism is live and self-perpetuating. The June meeting could deliver the catalyst; the mechanism continues to operate even if it doesn't.

**Pillar 3 — Hedge ratio at 14-year low.** Life insurer foreign-bond hedge ratio 44.4% (Mar 2025) — ~55% of foreign bonds (~$370-550B) unhedged, vol-weighted entry USD/JPY 135-145. At ~159, foreign books are in unrealized GAIN (Big 3 confirmed); below 145 they cross into mechanical-selling territory regardless of regulatory state. The hedge-ratio collapse is the slow-burn version of the Channel 1 forced-repatriation mechanism — it does not require ESR pressure to fire; it requires USDJPY <145, which a sustained Fed-side compression (multi-month) eventually delivers even without BOJ.

**Pillar 4 — Positioning fuel load near cycle peak.** CFTC JPY net short -114,667 (May 26) = 63.7% of Jul-2024 -180K cycle peak; 4 consecutive build weeks; +27K new shorts WoW; broke the -102K recent-cycle peak. Per the CH-004 METHOD, this places us in the **+5pp amplifier / residual-ON regime**. Positioning does not trigger an unwind — it amplifies the violence of one *when* a trigger fires. The fuel load growing INTO a hawkening market is the asymmetric setup; the precise catalyst that lights it (Jun 16, Fed-side, oil-shock risk-off, MOU re-escalation) matters less than the existence of any catalyst within the eligibility window. Sat Jun 6 CFTC print is the first scheduled re-check; cover below -108K drops the amplifier and the residual.

**What this means for position sizing.** The Jun-18 $58C ($40 premium) is the catalyst-conditional bet — pure BOJ binary, decays to zero if Jun 16 disappoints. ## CARRY-UNWIND PROBABILITY METHOD

The 7d/30d/60d buckets in `STATUS.md` § CARRY UNWIND PROBABILITY are **decomposed estimates** from ~10 hand-set anchors — value is **auditability** (every move traceable to a named driver), not point-precision. Live anchor states + current bucket values live in STATUS. Method added Jun 3 2026 to close RED CH-004 (prior buckets were judgment-calibrated conviction shipped to LIQUID/HENRY as if probability; methodology correction surfaced a ~33pp catalyst→unwind conflation overstating 30d/60d).

### Formula

```
P(unwind, T) = 1 − ∏ (1 − pᵢ(T))     over 5 trigger channels + residual term
where pᵢ(T) = P(catalyst i fires in T) × P(unwind | catalyst i fires) × CFTC_amplifier
Final = bottom-up union − overlap discount (judgment, joint-escalation tail)
```

### CFTC amplifier + residual gate (explicit)

Positioning is the amplifier on P(unwind \| trigger), not a trigger itself. The residual term is **fuel-load-contingent** — it comes OFF when positioning covers. NOT a permanent floor.

| CFTC state (% of cycle peak, -180K Jul-2024 reference) | Amplifier on baseline unwind\|fires | Residual term |
|---|---|---|
| < 30% of cycle peak | 0 (baseline applies) | **OFF** |
| 30-60% | +2-3pp | **OFF** |
| **> 60% (current: 63.7% / -114K)** | **+5pp** | **ON** |

Re-evaluated weekly on the Sat CFTC print. If positioning covers below ~-108K (60% line), the +5pp amplifier drops AND residual turns off — a future SAM should NOT treat 5pp as a permanent floor.

### Overlap discount — judgment, NOT derived

Independent-union formula over-counts where triggers correlate. The joint-escalation tail:
- **Oil/MOU shock (#5) often IS the risk-off trigger (#3)** — one Iran shock fires both
- **Oil/MOU pushes USDJPY through 160 → fires intervention (#2)** — chain correlation
- **BOJ hike + intervention combo explicitly cabled** (Reuters Jun 1) — #1 and #2 correlate at the meeting

**Discount:** −1pp (7d) / −5pp (30d) / **−7pp (60d, biggest)** — more time → more chance for chain-firing. Sized by judgment, not derived from a joint-distribution model.

## OIL-IN-YEN STRUCTURAL DYNAMIC

Oil shock creates a two-phase JPY dynamic:
- **Phase 1 (weeks 1-2):** Oil spike → trade deficit widens → JPY WEAKENS → carry survives
- **Phase 2 (weeks 2-8):** Recession risk compounds → safe haven yen WINS → carry unwind

**Current state (Jun 1 reframe):** Brent live level in STATUS — collapsed from ~$108 (May 21) to ~$92 (late May) on Iran/Hormuz MOU framework hardening, **then re-accelerated +4% Jun 1 on MOU break** (Tehran suspended document exchange + Hormuz block threat). **Phase 2 inception PAUSED; Phase 1 oil pressure rebuilding** (with v1.4 supply-destruction caveat — blockade severity can choke import volumes and invert the trade-deficit mechanism; May TB Jun 18 is the diagnostic). Forward watch is now walk-back (Trump-Khamenei reset) vs further escalation, not sign-vs-collapse.

**v1.4 revision — Phase 1 mechanism INVERTED by supply-destruction effect:** April trade balance (May 21 print) posted ¥+301.9B **SURPLUS** vs ¥-30-45B deficit consensus. **Crude oil imports -64% YoY** (steepest since 1980); ME crude -67.2% YoY (lowest since 1979); LNG from ME -76.1%. The blockade didn't INCREASE Japan's oil bill — it **collapsed import volumes** (physical supply choke). Japan couldn't buy ME barrels. **The Phase 1 mechanism ("oil → trade deficit widens → JPY weakens") inverts when blockade severity chokes physical flow — supply destruction shows up as smaller deficit, not larger.**

**Japan's own oil-reserve buffer (added 2026-06-15 — KB-SAM-187; distinct from the US SPR tracked in STATUS, which is BRENT's domain):** Japan held **~205 days of import cover at May 9 2026** (national/JOGMEC 121d + private/refiner-mandated 84d + joint Saudi/UAE-leased 1d), down from a **254-day pre-blockade baseline** (Dec 31 2025, ~470M bbl) — a ~49-day draw absorbing the import collapse, with **>50% of May supply rerouted to non-ME crude** (~1.4M bbl/d). Takaichi declared no further release needed (May 12, June crude secured). **Two implications:** (1) the blockade's economic bite on Japan is **slow-fuse** — a deep buffer that never approached scarcity — so the Phase-2 recession-risk yen-bid path was always going to lag the price signal; (2) Japan's live exposure is now **COST-side** (pricier alternative crude), not volume-scarcity — which sharpens the May-TB (Jun 17) diagnostic toward the petroleum-input-cost decomposition (branch c: surplus persists, ME volumes still depressed). *Caveat: May 9 is the latest hard figure; no post-deal June print yet.*

**Energy-complex transmission is SEVERED (added 2026-06-15 — KB-SAM-188/189/190; full picture in `research/outputs/JAPAN_ENERGY_COMPLEX.md`):** The blockade is an **oil shock, not a gas/grid shock** — crude is ~96% ME / ~93% via Hormuz, but LNG is only ~11% ME-exposed (Australia 39%), and the grid is buffered by restarting nuclear (15 reactors / 33 GW / ~10% of grid; Kashiwazaki-Kariwa Unit 6 restarted Feb 2026, METI accelerating restarts) + non-ME coal (~27%, Australia/Indonesia). **Critically, the shock's BOJ transmission is rerouted from monetary to fiscal:** May PPI +6.3% YoY (passthrough alive upstream) but April core CPI +1.4% *below all estimates* because the ¥3T subsidy package deliberately severs the PPI→CPI link.
