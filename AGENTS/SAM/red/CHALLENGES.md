## CH-010 — The 4.5% "forced-SELLER" reflexive trigger may have the sign backwards under economic-value ALM
**Target:** `JGB_SUPPLY_DEMAND_THESIS.md` LEG 3 + THESIS KEY THRESHOLD "JGB 30Y 4.5% (disorderly) — lifer impairment / forced-selling reflexive zone."
**Axis:** Logic / Evidence
**Severity:** 🔴 Critical (highest-upside-if-true; RED's own confidence: CONTESTED, not certain — flag for SAM's strongest rebuttal)
**Challenge:** The reflexive forced-SELLER at 4.5% is the load-bearing piece that makes this "more than yields drift up," and it rests on a *single trade-press analyst* (Insurance Business, May 2026). But J-ICS is an **economic-value / ALM** regime, and Japanese lifers are the textbook **duration-SHORT** institution (liability duration >> asset duration). Under economic-value accounting a rate rise marks *liabilities* down MORE than the shorter asset book → **net economic solvency RISES with yields.** That is exactly why the *consensus* pre-2025 read of J-ICS was that it would force lifers to **BUY super-long to close the duration gap** — the OPPOSITE of forced selling. SAM's stated mechanism ("rising yields mark down legacy holdings first, hurting ESR, before reinvestment helps") looks only at the asset leg and silently omits the liability leg. For SAM's sign to hold, the asset-side markdown must dominate via a specific **J-GAAP-locked-liability-vs-marked-asset accounting mismatch** that the package never establishes.
**Counter-evidence:**
- The legacy low-coupon super-long bonds lifers hold are their *duration hedge* against long-dated liabilities; marking them down hurts, but the liabilities they hedge mark down more.
- Counter-cyclical / matching-adjustment measures in economic-value regimes exist *precisely to prevent pro-cyclical forced selling* (the Solvency-II pro-cyclicality lesson) — design intent cuts against a 4.5% forced-sell cascade.
- The "buffer-conditional" framing is the *defensible* version (reported-ESR volatility under a J-GAAP/economic mismatch can constrain new risk-taking); the *forced-SELLING* strong form is the overreach.
**Resolution criteria:** CONFIRMED if ≥2 lifers are documented force-SELLING super-long as 30Y approaches/exceeds 4.5% *with an explicit impairment/accounting basis* (not a lone analyst assertion). DISMISSED if rising yields are shown net-positive for lifer economic solvency (duration-short), or if lifers BUY into a 4.5% spike to close the duration gap.
**Status:** OPEN — **needs SAM's strongest rebuttal** (establish the specific accounting basis on which asset markdown dominates the liability gain).
