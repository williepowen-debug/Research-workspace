## CH-004 — Carry Unwind Probabilities Are Not Independently Derived
**Target:** THESIS.md Channel 2 — "85% (7d) / 97% (30d) / 97% (60d)"
**Axis:** Logic
**Severity:** 🟡 Minor
**Challenge:** These probabilities are judgment-calibrated, not model-derived. The 7d and 30d/60d numbers are not independent — if the 7d event fires, the 30d/60d are near-certain by definition. If it doesn't fire in 7d, the 30d/60d should be lower than 97%. The way they're presented implies false precision and overstates conviction.
**Counter-evidence:** No systematic methodology documented. No backtesting. No calibration tracking. "85%" could mean "I feel pretty confident" — which is fine as a gut read but shouldn't be presented as a probability.
**Resolution criteria:** This is a methodology critique, not a factual one. Resolved if SAM documents the reasoning chain behind each probability estimate, or acknowledges these are conviction levels not calibrated probabilities.
**Status:** OPEN

## CH-005 — BOJ 0.75% Doesn't Kill Carry
**Target:** THESIS.md Channel 3 — rate differential compression → carry unwind
**Axis:** Logic
**Severity:** 🟠 Serious
**Challenge:** SAM's own thesis identifies 0.75% as the political ceiling. US rates are at 4.25-4.50%. Even at BOJ 0.75%, the rate differential is 350bp+. That's an enormous carry incentive. The Aug 2024 unwind happened on a SURPRISE hike from near-zero — the shock factor was the catalyst, not the rate level. A well-telegraphed May 1 hike to 0.75% is the most expected outcome in the market. Where's the surprise element?
**Counter-evidence:** Rate differential: 425bp (current) → 350bp (post-hike). Still positive. Carry trades are profitable until the FX move exceeds the yield pickup. At 350bp annual carry, USD/JPY needs to move ~3.5% against you in a year to break even. That's 155.
**Resolution criteria:** If BOJ hikes and USD/JPY drops to 155+ within 2 weeks (not just intraday), the carry math becomes unfavorable and the unwind case strengthens. If USD/JPY drops 2-3% then stabilizes above 155, carry survives.
**Status:** OPEN
