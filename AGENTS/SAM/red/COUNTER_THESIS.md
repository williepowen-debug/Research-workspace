# ▰▰▰ 2026-06-30 PASS — JGB DEMAND-VACUUM ▰▰▰

## ONE-LINER (counter)

The Japan-long-end "demand vacuum" is **real, consensus, and already in the price** — and the version SAM bolts on top (no clearing price at any yield · a self-reinforcing forced-SELLER at 4.5% · a one-way reflexive street that becomes a carry-unwind trigger) is the **widow-maker dressed in J-ICS clothing**. The vacuum has been *live for 13+ months* and has produced exactly the boring outcome: an **orderly grind** (30Y ~3.20% record May-2025 → ~3.76% Jun-2026, +56bp/13mo) with the **carry trade working at a 40-year yen low** — not a break, not a cascade, not an unwind. SAM's own framework squeezes the thesis from both sides: the orderly base case doesn't transmit to carry, and the disorderly tail that would is the one SAM's *own* BOJ leg says the BOJ truncates. Net: a sound *observation* over-extended into a strong-form *prediction*, with no action attached.

---

## THE NARRATIVE (why SAM is wrong on the long end)

**Start with full credit, because it's earned.** SAM's central analytical move — the supply/demand *reconciliation* ("lower net super-long supply YoY + higher yields = a DEMAND collapse dressed up as a supply problem") — is genuinely sharp and, as far as it goes, correct. The lifer pivot to 10-15Y is real (MOF auction internals). MOF *did* cut FY2026 gross super-long to ¥17.4T, a 17-year low (MOF FY2026 Issuance Plan, Dec 26 2025). The BOJ pace-not-level reaction function is well-evidenced (the 2025 precedent — BOJ let 30Y run +100bp to a record intraday ~3.20% uncapped — is the strongest single fact in the package). And framing SAM-32 as a *mechanism* call rather than a yield-threshold bet is the correct lesson learned from SAM-26's failure. RED is not contesting that long-end yields drift up over time. That is true, and it is consensus.

**The problem is everything SAM adds to make it "more than yields drift up."**

**1. The strong-form claims are the widow-maker.** "Japan's bond market has lost its buyer and no yield brings it back" is the single most-repeated, most-wrong macro thesis of the last twenty-plus years — the literal origin of the term *widow-maker*. Kyle Bass and a parade of others were carried out shorting JGBs on exactly this logic. SAM's sophisticated rejoinder is "this time the buyer-of-last-resort is structurally, regulatorily gone (J-ICS), and yields HAVE already repriced, so the naïve version is dead." Fair — the *yields-can-never-rise* version is dead. But SAM has simply moved the same overreach up the curve: the new strong-form is **"no clearing price exists near current levels"** and **"4.5% is a self-reinforcing forced-SELLER, not a buying floor."** Those are absolute, no-equilibrium claims. Markets clear. At 30Y 3.76% / 40Y 3.69% (MOF, Jun-26 pub) a domestic JPY liability-matcher is being offered **~1.7% real** against a 2% target — the most attractive super-long real yield in a generation. The marginal buyer at *some* price is not "nobody"; it is mega-banks as IRRBB room opens on a stable curve, GPIF rebalancing toward its 25% domestic-bond policy weight, retail via individual JGBs/Japan Post, and foreign real-money once the carry/hedge math turns. SAM's own buyer-map table lists six candidate buyers and waves them all off — but the existence of a *demand curve* (more buyers as yield rises) is the null hypothesis, and "the curve is vertical / there is no clearing yield" is the extraordinary claim that needs extraordinary evidence. It doesn't have it.

**2. The realized path already refutes the reflexive frame.** The demand vacuum is not a forecast — it has been the live state of the market since at least Apr-May 2025. So we have a 13-month natural experiment. Result: 30Y went from its ~3.20% record (May 2025) to ~3.76% (Jun 2026) — **+56bp over 13 months, an orderly grind**, with no disorderly break, no forced-selling cascade, no auction failure (30Y BTC still 2.94x Jun-10-2026; 40Y 2.54-2.76x — weak but covered), and — decisively — **the carry trade not unwinding but *thriving*, USD/JPY at a 40-year high of 162.40** (STATUS, Jun-30). If the "no buyer cushion + self-reinforcing seller above + one-way reflexive street" mechanism were the dominant force, 13 months of it being switched on should have produced more than a slow drift through the old records into an orderly grind. It produced the muddle-through. The boring outcome isn't a counterfactual RED has to argue for — **it is the realized 13-month tape.**

**3. The reflexive 4.5% forced-seller may have the sign backwards.** This is the load-bearing reflexivity — the thing that makes the thesis "more than drift" — and it rests on a *single trade-press analyst* (Insurance Business, May 2026) asserting that at 30Y ~4.5% "legacy books hit impairment thresholds → FORCED SELLING." But the J-ICS regime SAM invokes is an **economic-value / ALM** framework, and Japanese lifers are the textbook **duration-SHORT** institution (liability duration >> asset duration). Under economic-value accounting, a rate rise marks down long-dated *liabilities* MORE than the shorter asset book → **net economic solvency RISES** with yields. That is precisely why the consensus pre-2025 read of J-ICS was that it would force lifers to **BUY super-long to close the duration gap** — the *opposite* of SAM's forced-selling claim. For SAM's sign to be right, the asset-side markdown must dominate, which requires a specific J-GAAP-locked-liability-vs-marked-asset *accounting* mismatch that the package never establishes. The package's stated mechanism ("rising yields mark DOWN legacy holdings first, hurting ESR, before reinvestment helps") looks only at the asset leg and silently omits the liability leg. At best the 4.5% trigger is *contested*; at worst it is *inverted*, and counter-cyclical/matching-adjustment measures (the entire design intent of these regimes is to PREVENT pro-cyclical forced selling) cut against it further.

**4. The carry-tail route is self-defeating on SAM's own premises.** The only thing that makes this thesis *matter* to SAM's book is the new route: "30Y 4.5% disorderly → JGB stress → risk-off → carry unwind." But a disorderly forced-selling spike is, by definition, a *rapid* move — and SAM's own Leg 1 finding is that the BOJ's reaction function is **pace-not-level**, that it "nimbly intervenes to truncate a disorderly spike" with fixed-rate ops and increased purchases. So the carry-tail route needs the exact event (disorderly velocity) that SAM's own central-risk leg says the BOJ caps. The base case (orderly grind) is carry-*neutral* by SAM's own framework — 13 months of grind, zero unwind. The thesis is therefore pinched: **orderly = no transmission; disorderly = BOJ truncates.** The route survives only in a narrow timing sliver where disorder fires fast enough to spasm the yen before the BOJ caps JGBs (the Aug-2024 template, where yen damage doesn't un-happen once done) — a real but much rarer and narrower path than "30Y 4.5% is now a named entry-trigger" implies.

**5. It's consensus, and it's already in the price.** This is the textbook definition of a priced-in structural feature: a buyer strike so well-understood that **MOF has already publicly cut issuance to a 17-year low to match it** (SAM's own Leg 2). The Apr-May 2025 super-long spike was global front-page news; every sell-side desk has published the J-ICS / lifer-strike story; foreign outflow and the 10-15Y pivot are widely reported. MOF cutting supply to meet dead demand is the *system self-correcting toward equilibrium* — which argues against a one-way blowout, not for it. SAM provides no evidence the market UNDER-prices the vacuum; the thesis is "the vacuum exists" (known) not "the vacuum is worse than the curve already reflects" (unargued). SAM's own SIGNAL-ONLY verdict is nearly an admission: there's no edge to capture.

**6. And it changes no action.** SAM is FLAT. The only expression is the carry-convexity-tail, which is *separately* break-even-to-negative EV. So the JGB thesis adds a 4.5%-disorderly trigger to a watchlist for a position SAM isn't holding and isn't EV-positive to initiate — and the trigger is one the thesis itself says the BOJ won't fully let develop. That's analysis, not a trade. Worse, the 4.5% level has been re-installed as a headline KEY THRESHOLD — re-importing exactly the threshold-framing that burned SAM-26 (30Y ≥4.0% → FALSE), even as SAM-32 is carefully built to avoid it.

## WHAT THE WORLD LOOKS LIKE IF I'M RIGHT

- **30Y range-bound ~3.5-4.0% through 2026**, drifting up *slowly* (consistent with the +56bp/13mo realized pace), never sustaining a disorderly break. 40Y similar in the high-3s/low-4s.
- **Auctions stay weak-but-covered** (30Y BTC 2.5-3.0x, 40Y 2.3-2.8x) — tails widen, but no failure (BTC never <2.0x). The "vacuum" shows up as soft tails, not a clearing failure.
- **Clearing happens at higher yields**, not via the named lifer bid: a mega-bank/GPIF/retail/foreign mix absorbs at ~1.7% real, possibly with intermittent buffer-conditional lifer dip-buying that SAM pre-excludes as "not counting."
- **No carry unwind from the JGB channel.** USD/JPY weakness continues to be a *funding-currency / yield-gap* story (orderly), decoupled from JGB stress, exactly as the last 13 months.
- **BOJ muddles**: slows/pauses the taper (Apr-2027 floor), holds 25Y+ purchases, truncates any velocity spike. Fiscal-dominance worry stays a slow-burn, not a 2026 detonation.
- **SAM-32 technically resolves TRUE-but-trivial**: lifers don't return to *sustained* super-long buying — but that's because the buffer-conditional/intermittent base case (which SAM defines as not-counting) is exactly what plays out, so the "structural vacuum" framing earns no incremental edge over "lifers buy less, as everyone knows."

## WHAT WOULD PROVE ME WRONG (i.e., confirm SAM's JGB thesis)

1. **A disorderly 30Y/40Y session** (≥20-40bp intraday or a 2.0x BTC auction failure) that the BOJ does NOT promptly cap → demand vacuum is genuinely unstable, BOJ-let-run confirmed at velocity.
2. **A documented forced-SELLING wave** by ≥2 lifers as 30Y approaches/exceeds 4.5%, with an explicit accounting/impairment basis (not a single analyst's assertion) → 4.5% reflexive-seller sign confirmed.
3. **A JGB-stress → carry-unwind co-move**: a long-end disorder episode that *does* spasm USD/JPY lower (cross-pair yen-haven re-couple) before/despite BOJ JGB-capping → the carry-tail route is real, not self-defeating.
4. **GPIF disclosed as a NON-buyer / net seller of domestic super-long** at these yields → closes the package's biggest admitted gap in SAM's favor.
5. **A second-and-third consecutive month of lifer net super-long SELLING materially above the ¥201B May print** → "structural," not single-month noise.

## WHERE SAM IS RIGHT (acknowledged)

- The supply/demand **reconciliation** (lower net supply + higher yields = a demand problem) is a correct, non-obvious insight.
- **SAM-32 as a mechanism (not threshold) call** is the right application of the SAM-26 lesson.
- The **2025 let-run precedent** is strong, verifiable evidence for pace-not-level.
- Routing the work to **BOND (term-premium input)** is the correct home for its genuine value, and the **SIGNAL-ONLY / don't-double-count-Pillar-2-as-Channel-1** disciplines are honest and correct.
