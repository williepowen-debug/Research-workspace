## THE NARRATIVE

SAM v1.5 honestly demoted Channel 1 after RED's CH-002 thesis was confirmed by the Big 3 ESR window. That intellectual honesty is real and credit-worthy — most theses degrade by adding epicycles rather than admitting demotion. But the v1.5 reframe creates a new, harder problem: the thesis is now structurally single-path, and the single path it bets on (June BOJ hike) is the consensus trade.

**The case for SAM-21 ~57% / market 55-65% being WRONG-WAY-OVERCONFIDENT:**

The Apr 28 BOJ meeting produced a 3-board dissent split for 1.00% — but the BOARD STILL VOTED TO HOLD. That's a meaningful signal that the median voter is more dovish than the dissenters. The board has known about Takaichi's 0.75% political ceiling since at least October 2025 (Aida statement: "tolerate to 0.75%, pause until 2027"). The dissenters are signaling intent, not driving the vote. Why would the median voter who held in April hike in June, when:
- JGB 30Y has retraced from 4.000% → 3.866% (relieving the urgency to "stabilize the curve")
- April CPI core printed 1.4% (well below target, 3rd month below 2%)
- Brent collapsed -12% to $93.13 (CPI passthrough headwind resolving, removing inflation urgency)
- Q1 GDP came in +2.1% (no acute growth risk to use as cover for delay) — but the BOJ doesn't NEED cover, the median voter just doesn't want to hike
- Sato joins the board on the same date (Jun 16), a hawk-to-dove swap that telegraphs medium-term policy preference

The fiscal-dominance frame (eval Case 02, CH-008) goes further: BOJ isn't just dovish-by-preference, it's structurally frozen. JGB long-end blowout is doing the tightening already. Hiking on top compounds curve damage in the belly, pushes lifers from solvency stress to formal distress, detonates Takaichi politically, risks a disorderly 30Y auction. BOJ's actual move is a targeted long-end operation (rinban 25Y+ size increase, unscheduled op) — NOT a rate hike. The hike is now LESS likely, not more.

**Even if BOJ hikes:** The June 16 hike is the most-telegraphed BOJ event of the cycle. Polymarket 59.5%, ING desk base case maintained, Apr 28 dissent split widely covered, swap markets 55-65%. SAM-21 at ~57% sits INSIDE the market band — there is no edge above consensus. The position size that captured asymmetric multi-channel convergence under v1.4 is now a flat-edge consensus bet under v1.5. Aug 2024 violence required SURPRISE positioning unwind; this would be the most-priced hike of the cycle.

**The CFTC fuel load is misread.** SAM treats -93,905 (92% of cycle peak) as "fuel for unwind when catalyst fires." RED reads it differently: the speculator base is positioned NET SHORT JPY going INTO a fully-priced telegraphed event. When the event fires as expected, the positioning unwind happens (modest yen pop), then the same positioning re-establishes within sessions because the rate differential (still 300-325bp at 1.00% BOJ vs ~4.25% Fed) keeps the carry profitable. This is exactly the July 2024 / Apr 30 / May 6 intervention pattern that CH-003 confirmed: spike-and-reverse, net effect zero.

**Phase 2 (oil resolving) cuts BOTH ways.** SAM v1.5 treats Brent collapse as yen-bullish (safe-haven flow + rate differential mattering more than trade balance). But oil resolving ALSO removes the CPI passthrough pressure that BOJ would have cited as cover for the June hike. Same data point, opposite implication for the central catalyst.

**The boring single-path outcome:** BOJ holds at 0.75% on Jun 16 (vote 6-3 or 5-4, dissent split widens or narrows but board holds). USDJPY pops briefly on hawkish hold language (Ueda presses normalization rhetoric), then resumes drift toward 160 as carry math intact. FXY tests $56 area within 2 weeks.
