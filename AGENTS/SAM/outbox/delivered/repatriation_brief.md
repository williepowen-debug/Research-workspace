## THE 5 ACTIONABLE FINDINGS

### 1. FRONT-LOADED — THE SELLING ALREADY HAPPENED
**Finding:** FY-end repatriation is NOT concentrated in the final week. Japan's institutional pattern is Oct→Mar rolling selling with peak in February. February 2026 confirmed: ¥3.42T ($21.8B) in foreign bond net sales — largest since Oct 2024. Most of this FY's repatriation is done.

**Action:** Don't expect a dramatic March 29-31 spike in UST selling that triggers TLT move. The calendar flow is winding down, not loading up. The catalyst for the next TLT leg is BOJ hike (May 1) + fiscal supply, not FY-end mechanics.

---

### 2. TIC DATA IS 7 WEEKS BEHIND — DON'T RELY ON IT FOR TIMING
**Finding:** January 2026 TIC data (released March 18) shows Japan ADDED +$39.8B in USTs. This looks contradictory. Reality: TIC has 1.5 month lag. It captured January positioning BEFORE the February selling wave. February TIC releases April 15; March TIC releases May 18.

**Action:** For real-time flow intelligence, watch **MOF weekly securities data** (released weekly with ~1 week lag). Next release (~Apr 3) will show final March selling. TIC is for trend confirmation, not timing. Japan is NOT the buyer the TIC headline implies.

---

### 3. LIFE INSURERS ARE THE SELLERS — MECHANISM IS OUTRIGHT SALES
**Finding:** Life insurers (not GPIF, not banks) are driving the flow. They are doing outright UST/foreign bond sales, not FX forward adjustments. Reason: hedge costs (~4.35%) now exceed UST yield (~4%), so hedged returns are negative (-0.34%). Cheaper to just sell and repatriate. Fukoku Mutual confirmed: "selling low-yield foreign bonds and shifting into yen bonds since last April."

**Action:** This mechanism has DIRECT upward pressure on UST yields (not just FX). Each $10B in outright sales = ~1-3 bps on 10Y. The selling supports TLT put thesis structurally, but the monthly pace ($5-8B/month in UST-specific terms) is insufficient alone to move TLT dramatically. It's additive to fiscal supply, not a standalone trigger.

---

### 4. GPIF TRIGGER ≠ BOND SELLING (EQUITY REBALANCING ONLY)
**Finding:** GPIF's Nikkei <52K trigger causes foreign EQUITY selling (via futures), not foreign bond selling. GPIF rebalances its 25/25/25/25 allocation by selling the overweight asset class and buying the underweight. With domestic equities down, GPIF buys domestic equities (selling foreign equities to fund). Foreign bond allocation ~unchanged. A GPIF bond allocation POLICY CHANGE would be the earthquake — but that's not triggered by Nikkei.

**Action:** Don't conflate GPIF trigger with bond repatriation. The GPIF trigger is bearish Nikkei, mildly bullish JPY (selling foreign equities → converting to JPY). It's NOT adding to UST selling pressure in any meaningful way absent a policy change announcement.

---

### 5. OIL SHOCK IS SUPPRESSING THE FXY MOVE — ENTRY TIMING MATTERS
**Finding:** Japan repatriation (selling USD/buying JPY) and oil shock (buying USD/selling JPY for imports) are offsetting in FX. USD/JPY stuck 159-162 zone. The oil shock doesn't change the bond repatriation math (lifers still sell USTs for JGBs regardless), but it delays yen appreciation in spot markets. FXY entry before oil resolution = bad timing; you're fighting the oil flow.

**Action for FXY:** Wait for Iran resolution (Trump ultimatum expired ~Mar 25). Strike → oil spike → JPY hits 160+ → MOF intervenes → violent snapback. No strike / ceasefire → oil retreats → repatriation + rate compression dominate → USD/JPY 155-157 territory. FXY entry is better AFTER oil shock clarity or on green day with reduced oil premium. Current entry is fighting two contradictory flows.
