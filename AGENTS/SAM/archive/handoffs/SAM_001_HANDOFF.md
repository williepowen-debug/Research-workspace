## THE STRUCTURAL PROBLEM: "Bond Vigilante" Attack

### Why This Is Different from Normal Volatility

**Classic buyer's strike characteristics:**[281][283]

1. **Zero structural demand** — Typically Japanese domestic institutions (banks, insurance, pension funds) are passive buyers. This time, they're **selling or not participating**[281]

2. **Dealer inventory collapse** — Primary dealers have **minimal inventory** to support the market. They're not stepping in[281][283]

3. **Failed auction** — When the 20Y auction couldn't clear Monday, market interpreted as **"game over"** on yield support[277][281]

4. **Self-reinforcing downward spiral** — Each failed auction → higher yields → more pain for existing bondholders → more selling → higher yields[281]

**Comparison made by market commentators:** **2022 UK Gilt Crisis (Liz Truss fiscal shock)**[281][283]
