## S — SYNTHESIS

### Key Mental Model

**Core Thesis:**
US-Japan coordination opened an FX escape valve that reduces acute panic probability. The NY Fed rate check + Takaichi warning signals the US is willing to support yen intervention, transforming Japan's position from "alone with $130B reserves" to "backed by unlimited US firepower." This is a significant counter-signal to Scenario C (acute panic).

**What Matters Most:**
The FX valve is open but the fiscal valve is still sealed. Intervention treats the symptom (weak yen) but not the disease (fiscal doom loop). The Feb 5 30Y JGB auction is still the critical test — yen strength doesn't create JGB demand. If that auction fails, the crisis resumes through bonds, not FX.

**Biggest Uncertainty:**
Will rate check + verbal warning be backed by actual intervention? BMO: "Rate checks haven't necessarily meant intervention imminent." If this is a bluff and no action follows, credibility damage could make the eventual crisis worse.

**Where We Might Be Wrong:**
1. Mar-a-Lago Accord could be more real than TD Economics believes — Bessent's actions suggest intent
2. China could shift to cooperation if yuan appreciation pressure builds
3. The fiscal picture may improve if Takaichi moderates after narrow election win

### Key Findings This Session

1. **NY Fed rate check = US willing to intervene** — First US involvement signal since 2011. BMO: "implies intervention won't be unilateral."

2. **Takaichi escalated to PM-level warning** — Sunday TV debate: "all necessary measures against speculative movements."

3. **USD/JPY major move** — 154.71 from 158.87 high. Biggest single-day move since August. 5+ handles cushion to 160 trigger.

5. **China NOT coordinating** — PBOC managing yuan independently. Allowing gradual appreciation but slowing pace. Two-speed system emerging.

6. **1998 precedent matters** — Coordinated intervention worked (>10 day effect) vs short-lived unilateral attempts. Key: success required policy shift backup.
