## Competing Hypothesis Tracking

### CH-SAM-01: Soft Landing Scenario

**Hypothesis:** Japan achieves controlled normalization without crisis.

**Probability:** 12-17% (Scenario A/A+)

**Requirements:**
1. BOJ successfully raises rates to ~1.25% by end 2026
2. Yen stabilizes between 145-155
3. JGB yields settle at 2.5% (10Y), <4% (30Y)
4. No major institutional failure
5. Fiscal path credible (Takaichi moderates or narrow win)

**Evidence Supporting (Log in COUNTER_EVIDENCE_LOG.md):**
- Post-BOJ partial stabilization (Jan 23)
- Norinchukin past acute crisis
- SMFG/Japan Post Bank domestic bid
- LDP approval at 29.7% (narrow win likely)

**Evidence Against:**
- 40Y still at record 4.24%
- 20Y auction failed Jan 20
- Takaichi 70%+ personal approval (mandate if wins)
- ESR structural void permanent

### CH-SAM-02: Managed Devaluation Scenario

**Hypothesis:** Japan accepts yen weakness as preferred outcome, avoiding bond crisis.

**Probability:** 10-15% (subset of Scenario B)

**Requirements:**
1. BOJ pauses hikes despite yen weakness
2. Market accepts 165-175 USD/JPY as new normal
3. JGB yields stabilize (buyers return at higher yields)
4. No panic selling by institutions
5. US tolerates weak yen

**Evidence Supporting:**
- BOJ held Jan 23 despite 158+ yen
- No MOF intervention despite weakness
- Bessent focused on rate normalization, not yen level
- Global inflation easing reduces FX pass-through concern

**Evidence Against:**
- Takaichi likely to prioritize yen defense politically
- GPIF trigger at 160 (mechanical rebalancing)
- Life insurer hedging costs unsustainable at 165+
- US Treasury Monitoring List pressure
