## 1. THE EXACT MECHANISM: JGB LOSSES → UST SELLING

### A. The Balance Sheet Reality

**How life insurers work:**
```
ASSETS                          LIABILITIES
- JGBs (40-50% of portfolio)   - Life insurance policies (long-duration)
- Foreign bonds (20-25%)        - Annuities
- Equities (10-15%)             - Reserves
- Corporate bonds (10-15%)      - Capital
- Real estate (5-10%)
```

**Duration mismatch problem:**
- Liabilities: 20-30 year duration (life policies, annuities)
- Must match with long-duration assets (30Y/40Y JGBs)
- When JGB yields rise → massive price losses on long-duration bonds

**Math of the massacre:**
- 40Y JGB duration: ~22 years
- Yield rise: 1.5% (2024) → 4.24% (Jan 2026) = +274 basis points
- Price loss: ~274bp × 22 duration = **-60% price decline**
- On ¥100 trillion 40Y JGB holdings → ¥60 trillion paper loss

**But insurers don't mark-to-market like banks:**
- Hold bonds to maturity → no forced realization of losses
- Unrealized losses don't hit P&L immediately
- BUT they DO hit regulatory capital ratios

### B. The Regulatory Capital Squeeze

**Two regulatory systems:**

**OLD: Solvency Margin Ratio (SMR)**
- Minimum: 200%
- Formula: (Total capital + hidden reserves) / (Risk-weighted liabilities)
- Problem: Allowed insurers to ignore unrealized losses via "hidden reserves"

**NEW: Economic Solvency Ratio (ESR) — Effective April 2025**
- Minimum: **100%** (with Early Remedial Action at 120%)
- Formula: Economic value of assets / Economic value of liabilities
- **Key change:** Mark-to-market on BOTH assets and liabilities
- Unrealized losses NOW COUNT against capital

**The ESR shock:**
```
Under SMR (old):
- Unrealized JGB losses = ignored (hold-to-maturity)
- Solvency ratios looked fine

Under ESR (new):
- Unrealized JGB losses = SUBTRACTED from capital
- Solvency ratios deteriorate sharply
```

**Example: Nippon Life**
- ESR: 222% (as of March 2025)
- Unrealized bond losses: ¥3.6 trillion (up from ¥1.0tn in 2024)
- Total unrealized gains: ¥7.4 trillion (down from ¥12.0tn)
- **Net cushion eroding fast:** ¥7.4tn - ¥3.6tn = ¥3.8tn buffer remaining

**If JGB yields rise another 50bp:**
- Additional ¥2-3tn unrealized losses
- ESR falls to ~200% (danger zone)
- Regulatory pressure activates
