## REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

46. [ESR NEW REGIME] New Economic Solvency Ratio (ESR) effective April 2025. Minimum: 100%. Early Remedial Action threshold: 120% (FSA intervention). Better threshold: 150%. Warning zone: 180-200%. Source: DEEP_DIVE §1B.

47. [SMR OLD REGIME] Old Solvency Margin Ratio (SMR) minimum 200%. Allowed insurers to ignore unrealized losses via "hidden reserves." REPLACED by ESR April 2025. Source: DEEP_DIVE §1B.

48. [ESR vs SMR DROP] JFSA field test results: Life insurer SMR 933% → ESR 219% under new MTM methodology. Non-life: SMR 750% → ESR 200%. Source: RP-SAM-4 §3.

49. [DUAL REGIME PROBLEM] Two parallel regimes: J-ICS/ESR (regulatory — full MTM, YES) vs JICPA accounting (financial statements — held-to-maturity, NO). Insurers can hide from investors but NOT regulators. Source: RP-SAM-4 §3.

50. [JICPA PROPOSAL] JICPA proposed rule change: policy reserve-matching bonds reclassified as "held to maturity" → exempt from 50% impairment trigger → only credit losses recognized (JGBs ~0%). Exposure draft published Feb 17, 2026. Public comment ends Mar 17, 2026. Likelihood: HIGH. Source: RP-SAM-4 §2.

51. [JICPA DOES NOT FIX] JICPA approval does NOT fix: economic losses remain, J-ICS solvency still MTM, low-coupon legacy bonds persist, incentivizes further risk accumulation. Source: RP-SAM-4 §2.

52. [FSA EARLY REMEDIAL] FSA triggers Early Remedial Action at ESR <150%. Insurer required to submit capital improvement plan. Source: DEEP_DIVE §1B.

53. [FOREIGN BOND RISK WEIGHT] Foreign bonds have 2-3x risk weight vs JGBs under ESR. Selling $100B UST improves ESR more than selling ¥10T JGBs. Source: DEEP_DIVE §1B Trigger 2.
