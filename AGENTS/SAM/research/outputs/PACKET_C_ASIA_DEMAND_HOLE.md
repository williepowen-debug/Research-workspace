## 2. Transmission — JGB super-long stress → US duration / USD / risk

*(From the JGB→US transmission map, `research/outputs/JGB_SUPPLY_DEMAND_THESIS.md` §US-TRANSMISSION.)*

**Japan transmits to US duration DIFFUSELY via correlated global term premium — NOT via repatriation.** Repatriation (Channel 1) is RETIRED/dormant: Japan is still a **net UST buyer**, no direct foreign-SALES print. Two mechanisms actually carry:
1. **Term-premium correlation (price, not flow):** JGB 30Y and US 30Y co-move UP on a **shared driver** (global long-end term-premium repricing), not on a Japan→US flow. US 30Y ~4.96% is rising *with* JGB, onto softer US ground (ACM 10Y term premium turned positive first time since 2023). Japan doesn't sell USTs — it **prices** them higher by moving the global long-end anchor.
2. **Marginal-recycling subtraction (the new leg):** a **real ~4% JGB** competes with hedged UST for the same domestic-Japan pool. At ~4% JGB vs ~5% UST, the FX hedge cost roughly eats the pickup → the marginal Japanese yield-buyer stays **home**, reducing UST *recycling* (not selling the stock). A higher JGB floor is therefore mildly UST-**negative** at the margin.

**Net direction: both mechanisms push US term premium UP with no offsetting Japan flow.** The tail = a **disorderly** JGB break dragging the US long end via correlation alone (VIX 16 / MOVE ~68 today = tape calm; this is a tail, not the base case). USD: yen-side weakness (yen weakest major today) keeps the carry/funding channel a mild USD tailwind, not a driver.
