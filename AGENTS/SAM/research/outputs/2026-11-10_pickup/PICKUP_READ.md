## Carry read in plain words

Both central banks are now priced to hike in December — the Fed close to fully (HENRY's own calc), the BOJ at a 76 equivalent. Because the US move can be 50bp against the BOJ's 25bp, the rate gap that pays yen-funded carry is not being closed by these events; today it widened on the US leg, and the yen weakened accordingly. That is the opposite of August 2024. Speculators were net long yen going in (CFTC +102,321 at 11/3), so the short-yen pile that unwound then is absent.

What would change it: (1) USD/JPY near 160 with a fast move invites Ministry of Finance action — the yen-strength shock would then come from official selling of dollars, not from carry fuel; 160 is about 1.2 yen from today's high. (2) A BOJ December skip with the yen near 160 is the branch the 11/6 read named; the 76 equivalent makes it less likely, not excluded. (3) Offshore FX-swap funding remains unmeasured, so "no unwind" rests on spot, positioning and crosses, not on funding data.
