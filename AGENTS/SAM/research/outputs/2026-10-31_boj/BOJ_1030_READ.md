**Plain words:** the BOJ held but told markets inflation is now at its 2% goal and that it intends to keep raising rates — the hawkish version of a hold. The yen firmed only a little (about 0.2% on the day, under a yen of range), with no disorderly move. Speculators were already net long yen and got longer the week before, so the crowded short-yen position that blew up in August 2024 is still not there to unwind. The decision **does not change SAM's view: no observed Japan-led carry unwind.** The one way it cuts the other way: speculators are now heavily long yen (+101,591; the ledger's largest since it starts 2026-04-07 is +120,359 on 9/15), so a dovish surprise later (or a December skip) is the event that would move the yen fast — in the yen-WEAKENING direction, which is not the carry-unwind channel. Offshore yen FX-swap funding stays unmeasured.
