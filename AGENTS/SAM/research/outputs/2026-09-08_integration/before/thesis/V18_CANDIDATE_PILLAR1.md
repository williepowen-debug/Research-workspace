## 1. THE CLAIM BEING TESTED

*The original (pre-v1.6) yen thesis — the US-Japan rate differential compresses, and the yen strengthens on
that compression — was declared **directionally broken** at v1.6 (2026-06-22). The candidate asks whether the
condition that broke it has now reversed.*

**Why it broke, precisely:** on 2026-06-16 the BOJ hiked +25bp; on 2026-06-17 the Fed under new Chair Warsh
revised its dots **+40bp**. Japan tightened and the US out-tightened it. **The gap got wider on the exact
sequence the thesis said would narrow it.** That is a mechanism failure, not a timing miss.

**What is being asked now:** has that configuration stopped — and more importantly, has the gap actually *moved*?
