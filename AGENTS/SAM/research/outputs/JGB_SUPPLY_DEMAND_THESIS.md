## LEG 1 — BOJ BACKSTOP (the central risk) → CONDITIONAL LET-RUN

The one thing that could cap this thesis is the BOJ. It won't, for a gradual move.

- **Reaction function is pace-not-level.** BOJ policy language (Jun 2025 MPM, boj.or.jp): "In the case of a *rapid* rise in long-term interest rates, [BOJ] will make nimble responses by... increasing the amount of JGB purchases and conducting fixed-rate purchase operations" — available regardless of the monthly schedule. Uchida reiterated Jun 16 2026 ("nimbly increase bond buying if yields surge"). The trigger discriminator is explicitly *abnormal pace*, NOT a named yield level. **No BOJ official has named a 30Y/40Y ceiling.**
- **2025 precedent — let it run.** In Apr–May 2025, 30Y spiked **~+100bp to a record intraday ~3.20%** (40Y also all-time high ~3.0%). BOJ did NOT increase purchases or deploy fixed-rate ops. It instead (a) slowed the future taper (¥400B/qtr → ¥200B/qtr from Apr 2026) and (b) concentrated remaining cuts on ≤10Y, leaving **25Y+ purchases unchanged**. Structural accommodation, not yield-capping.
- **FY2027 taper-pause = passive support, not a cap.** Jun 16 2026: taper halts Apr 2027 at ~¥2T/mo ("market functioning and stability"). Arrests QT-vacuum risk; does NOT commit to a yield level. 25Y+ purchases held constant throughout QT.
- **What WOULD trigger action:** a *disorderly* spike (precedent language ≈ "abnormal move"; est. 20-40bp/session), an auction failure (BTC <2.0x), or JGB-market stress threatening financial stability (FSR Apr 2026 flags foreign hedge-fund JGB leverage as the channel to watch).

**Net:** the BOJ truncates the *violent* tail but won't lean on a *grind*. The central risk to the thesis is real but conditional on velocity — and the base case (gradual) survives it.

## LEG 2 — SUPPLY → NOT the current driver (forward amplifier only)

Counterintuitive but important: supply is *not* what's pushing yields up right now.

- **FY2026 gross super-long (20+30+40Y) was CUT to ¥17.4T — a 17-year low, −¥7.2T vs FY2025** (MOF FY2026 Issuance Plan, Dec 26 2025; Reuters/Bloomberg). MOF cut it *because* demand deteriorated and lifers requested less; funding shifted to shorter maturities (2Y, T-bills). Total FY2026 gross ¥180.7T; market issuance ¥168.5T (vs FY2025 ¥172.3T).
- **Net private super-long supply is therefore LOWER YoY** — est. ~¥13-14T FY2026 vs ~¥18-20T FY2025 (gross cut exceeds the BOJ's reduced absorption). BOJ purchases step down ~¥2.7T/mo (Q1 FY2026) → ~¥2.1T/mo (Q4), ~¥28.8T/yr all-maturities; super-long absorption est. ~¥3-4T/yr (BOJ doesn't disclose per-bucket — estimate).
- **The supply *surge* is a FORWARD (FY2027+) risk:** the FY2027 taper-pause floor + Takaichi fiscal expansion (¥3T supplementary budget May 25 2026; Reuters: "likely to issue fresh debt" despite the "no extra borrowing" claim — 10Y tagged **2.8%, a 29-yr high**, on fresh-debt reports ~May 18) + the reflationist board ("issue more JGBs"). An unconfirmed mid-year super-long add (the Jun 3 2026 revised plan) would aggravate it.

**Reconciliation (the key analytical point):** *lower* net supply + *higher* yields = a **DEMAND collapse dressed up as a supply problem.** Supply is the forward fuel; demand is the current cause.
