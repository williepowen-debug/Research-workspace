# SAM news sweep: Japan macro, Sat 2026-09-19 → Thu 2026-09-24 (~16:50 ET = ~05:50 JST Fri 09-25)

**Scope:** read-only catch-up covering the Silver Week closure (Japan shut Mon 9/21 through Wed 9/23, reopened Thu 9/24). **Author:** SAM news-sweep subagent, 2026-09-24. **Book:** FLAT, so this is monitoring only.
**Basis rules used here:** REPORTED = press or secondary only. CONFIRMED = a primary source (MOF, BOJ, Kantei, US Treasury) or an on-record quote carried by a wire. Yen amounts are never converted unless the source converted them, and in that case the source's own rate is shown. Headline JGB levels are **on-the-run / JBTS-quoted**, which is a different basis from the MOF constant-maturity curve (see §3).
**Timing caveat:** at the time of writing the **Fri 9/25 Tokyo session had not opened**, so "Sep-24/25 Tokyo session" here means the **9/24 session only**.

## What changed (≤10 bullets)

1. **No intervention is confirmed, and no rate check was reported after 9/18.** On 9/24 (~11:07 JST), FinMin Katayama said *"the principles since the previous joint intervention remain alive"* (Reuters). She gave no level and did not escalate beyond that. The next hard evidence is MOF's monthly intervention release for the period **Aug 27 to ~Sep 28, due ~Sep 30**. That release will not show whether anything happened on 9/24, because MOF publishes monthly totals, not daily ones.
2. **USD/JPY traded 157.80 in the Tokyo AM → 159.04 in NY on 9/24** (Fisco/Zaikei, 04:13 JST 9/25). That is the weakest yen since Sep 2. The Tokyo-morning dip on Katayama's remark was called "temporary" (Minkabu). The 200-day MA is ~158.43–158.47.
3. **The 10Y JGB touched 3.075% on 9/24, highest since Aug 1996** (Nikkei/NHK). It was 2.985% on 9/18 per JBTS. The OSE triggered its **dynamic circuit breaker on JGB futures** (Nikkei, 9/24). Levels at the close (Fisco): 2Y 1.886 / 5Y 2.366 / 10Y 3.070 / 20Y 3.909. **No super-long record:** the 30Y was ~4.13–4.16% against a 4.21% all-time high (Sep 2026), and the 40Y was ~4.20% against a 4.40% high (May 2026).
4. **UST "two-decade highs" = 9/23 (Bloomberg).** The 5Y breached 5% for the first time since 2007. Official Treasury par curve: **10Y 5.11% (9/23) → 5.18% (9/24); 30Y 5.40% → 5.47%**. On 9/24 the 30Y was the highest since 2004 (CNBC/Bloomberg), and the **20Y par reached 5.53%**. Drivers: hot US flash PMIs, a weak 5Y auction (9/23), then a weak 7Y auction and a **Treasury buyback that took less than its maximum** (9/24).
5. **The US–JP 10Y par spread widened.** UST 10Y 5.18% minus JGB 10Y ~3.07% is ~211bp on 9/24. The two sides are on different bases, so this is indicative only.
6. **Japan Sep flash PMI (S&P Global, 9/24):** manufacturing **54.1** (Aug 54.9; consensus 55.0), services **51.6** (52.5), composite **52.5** (53.5, slowest since May). The weak yen and Middle-East energy costs are named as drivers of input costs.
7. **BOJ: no speeches, no out-of-schedule operations, and no new guidance after the 9/18 press conference.** July MPM minutes are **confirmed for Mon Sep 28, 08:50 JST**. The Aug SPPI is **also Sep 28** (not 9/24–25). The Sep SoO and the Tankan are both Oct 1. The next MPMs are **Oct 29–30** and **Dec 17–18**.
8. **Takaichi–Trump summit, NY, 9/22 (13:05 local, ~35 min).** The official Kantei readout mentions **tariff implementation, not FX**. "Discussed conditions in international financial markets and exchange rates" appears **only in secondary press** (Seoul Economic Daily, 9/23). REPORTED.
9. **Nippon Life (Nikkei, 9/20):** targets **¥2T project-finance balance by FY2035**, i.e. doubling from **~¥1T as of end-March 2026**. **Not confirmed by Nippon Life**: Reuters could not verify it and the company could not be reached. No Nippon Life press release was found.
10. **UBS AM's Kevin Zhao (Bloomberg, 9/21):** would **sell yen on any further Japanese intervention**. Quoted: *"the fundamental policies driving yen weakness have not changed."* The exact Bloomberg text was not retrievable (paywall, HTTP 403); wording below is from secondary relays.
