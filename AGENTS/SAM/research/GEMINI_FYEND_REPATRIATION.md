### 2. Execution: Outright vs Derivative Roll-off
- **Outright sales** → direct upward pressure on UST yields. 30Y market depth down 30% — Japanese sales now cause 10-20bps intraday moves without macro catalyst
- **Hedge roll-off** → majority of holdings hedged via 3-month FX forwards. Hedging cost 450-500bps vs UST yield ~4.0-4.5% = **negative carry**. Repatriation = simply not renewing the hedge → must sell physical UST to settle expiring forward
- Banks favor outright sales (short-to-intermediate, 2-5Y). Insurers/pensions favor hedge roll-off.

### 4. TIC Data Lag

### 5. BOJ QT Circular Flow
- BOJ reducing JGB purchases ~¥400B/quarter, targeting ¥3T/month by Mar 2026
- Creates "supply vacuum" in JGBs → filled by repatriating insurers/pensions
- **Circular flow:** BOJ retreats from JGBs → yields rise → insurers find domestic attractive → sell USTs → buy JGBs → BOJ QT succeeds without domestic crisis
- BUT: this drains liquidity from UST market. Global term premium must rise in BOTH markets.

### 6. What Makes 2026 "Stressed"
Three compounding factors:
1. **J-ICS goes live Mar 31** — "regulatory cliff." Punitive for unhedged foreign assets. Hard deadline = forced selling. No equivalent in prior years.
2. **Yen at 159** — carry trade at breaking point. MOF intervention pressure. Pre-emptive repatriation to avoid gap move if BOJ hikes.
3. **Oil at $100+ / Hormuz** — Japan 90% ME oil dependent. Energy imports drain current account → corporates MUST convert foreign earnings/sell USD assets. This is liquidity necessity, not yield choice.

### 7. Norinchukin — Leading Indicator
- $700B+ portfolio, heavily foreign sovereign/credit
- >$20B unrealized losses on foreign bonds in recent years
- Forced to liquidate before Mar 31 to prevent capital erosion
- Their selling = leading indicator for broader banking sector

### 8. Conclusion — "Structural End of the Japan Bid"
- Not temporary seasonal. Permanent realignment driven by J-ICS + BOJ exit from ultra-loose + geopolitics
- "Japan bid" being permanently replaced by requirement for higher term premia
