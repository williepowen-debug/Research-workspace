## 1. What Is Round 2?
Rengo (Japan Trade Union Confederation) runs Shunto ("spring offensive") in sequential rounds:
- **Round 1** (~mid-March): Large firms (Toyota, Sony, Nippon Steel, etc.) — ~3,000-5,000 workers/union, pace-setters
- **Round 2** (~late March / Mar 27): Mid-size + smaller firms, SMEs, regional unions — broader base, less favorable bargaining power
- R2 covers ~1,500-2,500 firm settlements. Final tally typically published end of March / early April.
