## Kernel

Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority (PGSA) said on X on October 27 that "legal action will be taken against all vessels belonging to Sinokor Shipping Company" from **November 1**, citing the most transit violations over three months; enforcement has already been applied to "at least seven" Sinokor vessels (Tasnim 10/27, read at source). What "legal action" means is **not defined**; earlier PGSA warnings named passage restrictions, fines, detention and confiscation (Maritime Executive, Gulf News 10/27). This is a dated **declaration**, not an executed seizure.

Scale: Lloyd's List Intelligence (via Bloomberg/secondary, **not read at primary**) puts Sinokor at **47% of Hormuz VLCC transits**; Sinokor has acquired 70+ VLCCs this year (fleet ~150 tankers, secondary). Korea Shipowners' Association says the named vessels were sub-chartered, not Sinokor-owned. Do not merge "7 under enforcement" with Seoul Economic's "7 tankers hit" or the IRGC's "seven tankers" of 10/22.

FALCON ACTION: whether this changes Hormuz ladder/transit expectations from 11/1. BRENT ACTION: VLCC availability/freight read if Sinokor tonnage exits the route. Watch date: November 1.
