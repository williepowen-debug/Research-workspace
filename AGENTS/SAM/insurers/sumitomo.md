## FOREIGN BOND POSITIONING

**Updated post-FY2025:**
- **Direction confirmed: GROWING.** Foreign bonds +¥543B (+6.2%) and total foreign securities +¥1.11T (+9.3% to 35.5% of GA). This is the largest single-insurer foreign-book expansion among Big 3 — and it came in the year that v1.0-v1.4 thesis predicted forced repatriation.
- **AFS unrealized loss IMPROVED** (-¥161B → -¥20B, ¥141B closer to flat) — book direction is healing not deteriorating.
- **Symetra structural function clarified:** the pre-FY2025 "outsourced ¥2T to Symetra for efficient management" framing was a restructuring signal, but the FY2025 outcome shows the relationship is also a vehicle for US in-force growth via Dearborn — not a precursor to portfolio reduction.
- ¥20B AI investment over 3 years (Bloomberg, Mar 2026) — independent.

## SIGNALS TO WATCH (v1.5)

- **H2 FY2026 plan (Oct-Nov 2026)** — Sumitomo's FY2026 plan was PARTIAL (Mar 18 disclosed PC + operational only); the H2 disclosure typically includes formal asset allocation guidance. Watch for any signal on foreign-bond expansion pace.
- **Dearborn Life integration trajectory** — Symetra in-force +23.7% was Dearborn-driven; sustained US growth confirms the US-direction thesis. Any reversal would be a meaningful signal.
- **PC stress visibility** — Sumitomo's $10.7B PC stack is the largest among Big 4. If US private credit cascade deepens (gating, marks), Sumitomo is the first place stress would appear. Quarterly disclosures and Symetra basic-profit commentary are the watch points.
- **FY2026 ESR (May 2027)** — does the +19pt improvement hold, or does it reverse if equity-rally tailwind fades? Sub-200% via market stress (not capital action) would reactivate Channel 1.
- **Any explicit foreign-bond reduction language** (Fukoku-2023-style) would reactivate Channel 1 immediately.

## WHY IT MATTERS (v1.5)

The most powerful individual evidence point for the v1.5 Channel 1 demotion. Sumitomo was the Big 3 insurer with the lowest starting ESR (178%) and the largest PC exposure (4.3% of AUM) — exactly the profile the v1.0-v1.4 thesis predicted would FIRE Channel 1 stress. Instead, ESR ROSE +19pt during the JGB-blowout year, foreign book GREW +¥1.11T, US expansion ACCELERATED via Dearborn. No M&A artifact, no stress framing, no forced rebalance. The directionally-opposite outcome from the most-vulnerable Big 3 insurer is the load-bearing 3-of-3 datapoint for the v1.5 demotion. If Channel 1 ever reactivates, Sumitomo is also the most-likely first-mover — but not in 2026 absent a new shock.
