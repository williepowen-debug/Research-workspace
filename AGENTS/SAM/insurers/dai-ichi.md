## FY2025 ESR DISCLOSURE — RESOLVED MAY 13-15 (RESILIENT, LEAST-REPRESENTATIVE OF BIG 4)

**Headline:** ESR **~220%** (+~10pp YoY) — **above target band 170-200%, no stress threshold breach.** First Big 4 mutual / listed to print FY2025. Disclosed May 13-15 ahead of the Big 3 mutual window.

**Decomposition reading:** Domestic equity rally (~+¥1.5T eligible-capital uplift) offset +¥530B mass-lapse risk. Dai-ichi is the **most equity-heavy** Big 4 balance sheet, which is why it's the **least-representative of the Big 4 stress thesis** — the equity rally cushion that lifted Dai-ichi by ~+10pp does not protect Big 3 mutuals as much (Nippon foreign-book gain absorbed; Meiji JGB markdown absorbed; Sumitomo equity-rally-lifted but smaller domestic equity book).

**Apr 1 management split** flagged at FY2026 plan window — internal leadership pivot but no public foreign-bond reduction signal.

**v1.5 implication:** Dai-ichi 220% was the FIRST datapoint in the Big 4 FY2025 disclosure cycle and SAM correctly read it as "least-representative" rather than load-bearing for Channel 1. The Big 3 mutual window (May 26, 3-of-3) provided the actually load-bearing evidence. Dai-ichi is now confirmed-resilient — no acute Channel 1 signal from this insurer in 2026.

## SIGNALS TO WATCH (v1.5)

- **M&G / Challenger / Capula integration** — strategic-equity stakes' contribution to consolidated earnings
- **H2 FY2026 plan (Oct-Nov 2026)** — formal asset allocation guidance not yet given for FY2026; H2 is the next window
- **FY2026 ESR (May 2027)** — if equity rally fades, the +10pp tailwind reverses and Dai-ichi could drift back toward target-band midpoint. Below 200% via market stress (not equity reversal alone) would partially reactivate Channel 1.

## WHY IT MATTERS (v1.5)

Second largest by AUM. Canyon Partners ownership is the **direct bridge between Japanese insurer balance sheets and US private credit cascade** — not just bond exposure but subsidiary P&L. The 220% ESR resilience confirms that for the most equity-heavy Big 4 member, FY2025 stress was fully absorbed by equity gains — but this doesn't generalize to Big 3 mutuals. Treat Dai-ichi as a US-PC-stress amplifier indicator more than a Channel 1 transmission datapoint.
