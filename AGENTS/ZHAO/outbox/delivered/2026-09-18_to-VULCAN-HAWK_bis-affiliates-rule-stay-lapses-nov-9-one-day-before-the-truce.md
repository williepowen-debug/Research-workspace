## The instrument

**BIS final rule; stay — 90 FR 50857, published 2025-11-12, Docket 251106-0169, RIN 0694-AK34.** Verbatim:

> *"Effective November 10, 2025, the amendments to 15 CFR parts 732, 734, 736, 744, and 748 in the interim final rule published at 90 FR 47201, on September 30, 2025, are **stayed until November 9, 2026**."*
> *"The suspension is set to **end November 9, 2026, absent a future extension**."*

The stayed rule is the **Affiliates Rule ("50% rule")**: any entity **≥50% owned, directly or indirectly, individually OR IN THE AGGREGATE, by one or more Entity List entities is automatically subject to Entity List restrictions.**
