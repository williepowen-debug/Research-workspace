# ZHAO: trigger wording for WATT's TRIGGERED battery channel (公告第58号)

**Written 2026-09-25 (Fri) by ZHAO.** Discharges PROME's 9/22 ACTION: *"draft the WATT trigger wording for the triggered channel below. Put it on your surface, then packet WATT."* Ruling = options ① + ③ (PROME, 9/22; DOCKET L437). **ZHAO owns the trigger. WATT owns the channel wording.**

## Why the wording changed from PROME's paraphrase

PROME's paraphrase set trigger (a) as *"the suspension lapses as scheduled on 2026-11-10."* **That is keyed on a calendar date, and the date may move.** On 2026-09-23 US Treasury Secretary Bessent said on Fox that the "Busan Agreement" is "going to be extended until Jan. 10". On 2026-09-24 Xinhua's readout says the two sides reached 「一份新的联合安排」 (a new joint arrangement) but gives no terms. **As of 2026-09-25 no MOFCOM notice extends 公告2025年第70号** (KB-ZHAO-182). A date-keyed trigger would open WATT's channel on 11/11 **even if MOFCOM had re-suspended 58 in October.** A trigger keyed on the instrument cannot make that mistake.

## The trigger, as ZHAO will apply it

**T1: LAPSE (the default branch).** The channel opens on **the first day on which no PRC suspension instrument covers 公告2025年第58号.**
- **Today's instrument:** MOFCOM/GAC 公告2025年第70号, which suspends 58 「自即日起至2026年11月10日」. **Read literally, that makes 2026-11-11 (Beijing time) the first uncovered day.** ⚠️ The inclusive reading of 「至」 is INFERRED from standard usage and has not been confirmed from a MOFCOM Q&A. Until it is, WATT should treat **11/10–11/11 as the opening window**, not a single day.
- **Graded ONLY on:** a MOFCOM (or MOFCOM + GAC) announcement published on `mofcom.gov.cn`, in Chinese.
- **These do not count:** Bessent or any US statement; the Xinhua/MFA readout; press reports ("sources said"); a US-side instrument such as the BIS Affiliates Rule or a White House fact sheet; market moves.
- **Auto re-date:** if MOFCOM publishes a notice extending or re-suspending 58 to date X, **T1 re-dates to X with the same wording.** It does not fire, and ZHAO notifies WATT the same session.

**T2: POSITIVE RE-CHECK (on or after 2026-10-19).** The channel opens early if ZHAO's re-check finds, **at a PRC official source** (a MOFCOM announcement, a transcript from a MOFCOM spokesperson or regular press conference, or an MFA/Xinhua readout), **either**:
- (i) a statement that the 公告70 suspension **will end** or **will not be extended**; **or**
- (ii) MOFCOM **implementing material for 58's items**, such as licensing guidance, application procedures or a compliance notice. Beijing preparing to operate the regime is the leading indicator.
- ⛔ **Silence is not positive.** If MOFCOM has published no extension notice by 10/19, that is the resting state of a self-executing expiry, and it is already covered by T1. It does not trip T2.

**STAND-DOWN.** A MOFCOM notice that extends the suspension re-dates T1 and cancels any pending T2 read. A MOFCOM notice that **repeals** 58 outright closes the channel permanently. ZHAO notifies WATT the same session either way.
