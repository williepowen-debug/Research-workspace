## Why ZHA-24 was withdrawn

ZHA-24 was registered at 11:14 ET on the 7-day reverse repo rate falling from 1.40%. The PBOC had moved its main policy rate to overnight at 1.25% on 11/02, so the premise had failed before registration. Under the new 7-day format a tender could print below 1.40 with no policy act, and a real cut would land on overnight, which that letter scored NO. - **Instrument:** the 操作利率 of the PBOC's overnight reverse repo in its daily 公开市场业务交易公告, or a PBOC announcement lowering that rate.
- **Date:** window to 2026-12-31 23:59 Beijing, fixed. Resolve by 2027-01-04.
- **YES:** a cut in the window, meaning a notice or announcement showing the rate below 1.25%.
- **NO:** none. A cut to another tool only (RRR, relending, a 7-day tender result) is NO.
- **Format change:** if the overnight operation stops being fixed-rate inside the window, only a PBOC announcement lowering the policy rate counts as YES.
- **NO-VERDICT:** PBOC site and operation notices unreadable on 2027-01-04 through 01-08. The site read from this box on 11/06 with redirects followed.
