### 🔴 AMENDMENT 1 — made 2026-09-02, ~40 minutes after the letter was first written, BEFORE the event, on a HAWK packet that landed mid-session

**HAWK's finding (2026-09-02, from the 8/22 US §338 Canada post-mortem):** a dated branch can be **MIS-SHAPED rather than incomplete.** All three legs of that branch were enumerated and it still failed, because **one leg was the SELF-EXECUTING DEFAULT and the other two were OVERRIDES.** Testing the enumeration for *completeness* cannot catch that. The question that catches it is: **"which branch happens if NOBODY ACTS?"** `[[finding_no_action_ruling_does_not_disarm_an_automatic_mechanism]]`

**HAWK explicitly left the 11/10 instrument's structure BLANK — "YOURS TO ESTABLISH, I have NOT read the instrument" — and warned against importing a base rate rather than the question. I read the instrument. HAWK is right, and my letter above was mis-shaped in exactly the way described.**

**The operative language, White House presidential action, 2025-11 (fetched and quoted 2026-09-02):**

> *"Heading 9903.01.63 and subdivision (v)(xvii)(10) of U.S. note 2 to subchapter III of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States **shall continue to be suspended until 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time on November 10, 2026.**"*
> and: *"Should the PRC fail to implement its commitments under the Arrangement, I may modify this order."*

**⇒ THE SUSPENSION EXPIRES BY ITS OWN TERMS. It is a time-limited suspension, not a rate modification that persists until changed.** Nobody has to decide to re-impose the heightened reciprocal tariff on 2026-11-10 — **somebody has to decide NOT to.** No delegation to extend appears in the text; the President retains unilateral modification power.

**⇒ WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE LETTER — three things, and branch B is the one that moves:**

1. 🔴 **BRANCH B IS NOT NEUTRAL. It is the escalation-by-default branch.** I wrote B as *"base case, 11/10 stays live and becomes the binding date"* — which is true and **materially understates it.** Correct reading: **B means that, absent a further affirmative act between the summit and 12:01 a.m. on 11/10, the heightened rate returns automatically.** B is therefore 🟠-with-a-🔴-tail, not a quiet outcome.
2. ⚠️ **The rate that would apply after 11/10 is NOT SPECIFIED in the order.** It references heightened reciprocal tariffs resuming without stating the number. **I am not filling that in.** Current suspended-state rate is **10%**; the post-expiry rate is an **UNKNOWN**, and any figure quoted for it needs its own source. `[[finding_level_without_a_reference_has_two_failure_modes]]`
3. ✅ **HAWK's operational bar is ADOPTED verbatim as the grading test for branch A:** ***is there a published instrument with an effective date beyond 2026-11-10?*** Everything else is commentary. This is stricter than my original "official output" instrument and it replaces it for branch A. **The August precedent is the reason: Trump and Carney announced a deal, USTR described its coverage, and the §338 tariff went live three days later — because the announcement was never converted into an instrument.** Grade the instrument, never the atmospherics.

The §338 default was *tariff lives*; their 9/8 Canadian counter-tariff default is *no counter-tariff*, and they priced it 65% rather than 85% for exactly that reason. **The lesson is the question, not the prior.** I established the 11/10 default from the instrument itself, not from the August outcome.
