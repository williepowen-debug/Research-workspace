## Kernel

PRIMARY IDC Q3 2026 preliminary tracker reports 255.3 million new branded smartphone shipments versus 327.3 million a year earlier, −22.0% YoY. IDC calls it the largest quarterly YoY decline and attributes pressure to memory/component costs and supply. Source displayed October 15, tracker/dateline October 14: https://www.idc.com/resource-center/press-releases/smartphone-shipments-decline-3q26/ .

Supplier detail: Samsung −5.8%, Apple −4.0%, Xiaomi −29.2%, OPPO −34.5%, vivo −31.5% YoY. This is preliminary sell-in shipment data, excluding refurbished/OEM units, not household retail sell-through. IDC's cost/supply explanation is attributed assessment, not independent causal identification. The differentiated transmission is upstream memory strength alongside downstream device-volume weakness. No earnings, capacity or capex rule is graded here.
