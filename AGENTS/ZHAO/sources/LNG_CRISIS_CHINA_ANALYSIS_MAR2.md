## 1. CHINA'S LNG EXPOSURE TO QATAR/HORMUZ

### Import Mix (2024/2025 estimates)
- China total LNG imports: ~80-85 MT/year (world's largest importer)
- **Australia: ~35-40%** (largest supplier) — Pacific routes, NOT Hormuz-dependent
- **Qatar: ~15-18%** (~12-15 MT/year) — CNOOC, Sinopec long-term contracts — 100% Hormuz-dependent
- **Malaysia: ~12%** — Strait of Malacca, not Hormuz-dependent
- **Russia (Yamal + Arctic LNG): ~8-10%** — Arctic routes, NOT Hormuz-dependent
- **USA: ~5%** — Pacific LNG, not Hormuz-dependent
- **Other (PNG, Indonesia, Nigeria): ~10-15%**

**Key finding:** ~15-18% of China's LNG is Qatari/Hormuz-dependent. Additional Gulf LNG (UAE, Oman) adds ~3-5%.

**Total Hormuz-exposed LNG: ~18-22% of China's imports = ~15-18 MT/year**

### Replacement Capacity
- Australia has spare capacity on some projects — but spot cargoes now going to price war with Japan/Korea
- Russia Yamal LNG: contractual, limited spot availability
- US LNG: spot market available but at massively elevated JKM prices
- **Net: China can replace maybe 50-60% of lost Qatari volume, at 2-3x the cost**

### Strategic Reserves Buffer
- China's gas storage capacity: ~17-20 bcm (far smaller than EU proportionally)
- Currently estimated ~60-65% full (better positioned than Europe)
- Buffer: 3-4 weeks at current consumption rates if Qatari LNG fully cut

## 5. MAR 5 INSURANCE CLIFF — CHINA'S PLAYBOOK

### China's unique tools:
1. **PICC (People's Insurance Company of China)** — state insurer, can cover Chinese-flagged vessels regardless of P&I club withdrawal
2. **COSCO Shipping** — state-owned, self-insures, operates outside Western P&I system
3. **China-Iran bilateral** — pre-existing energy relationship; Iran needs China as buyer
4. **SPR drawdown** — burn through strategic petroleum reserve while Hormuz resolves
5. **Alternative routes** — Russia pipeline (ESPO), Central Asian gas pipeline, Myanmar oil

### COSCO Hormuz exposure:
- COSCO has operated through sanctioned waters before (Iran, during US sanctions 2019-2020)
- State guarantee = no P&I needed
- Likely to continue Hormuz transits using PICC coverage + state guarantee
- **China effectively self-insures its own tanker fleet**

### Bilateral deal with Iran:
- 25-year Iran-China cooperation agreement (2021) remains operative
- China already buys ~1M bbl/day Iranian oil at discount
- Iran needs China's market — mutual interest in keeping supply flowing
- IRGC likely to allow Chinese-flagged vessels "safe passage" (de facto carve-out)

### Assessment: China is LEAST impacted by the Mar 5 insurance cliff among major importers
