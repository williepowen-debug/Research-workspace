**Source:** Gemini Deep Research (Feb 13, 2026)

## Executive Summary

China is in "managed volatility" — NOT heading for Lehman-style collapse, but prolonged zombie financial activity. The risk is capital tied up servicing legacy debt rather than supporting growth.

**Key transmission path:** LGFV stress → Regional bank NPLs → Liquidity crunch → UST liquidation to meet dollar needs

## Shadow Banking Collapse

- SOE-backed (Aviation Industry Corp of China)
- **60B RMB failed**, 10,000+ investors
- Placed under custodianship April 2025
- Provided 15% loans to leveraged developers
- **Signal: SOE background no longer guarantees repayment**

### Zhejiang WMP Crisis (Late 2025)
- 20B+ RMB in WMPs failed to pay

## Land Sale Revenue Collapse

**The fiscal bedrock is crumbling.**

| Year | Land Revenue | YoY Change |
|------|-------------|------------|
| 2021 (Peak) | 8.70T RMB | — |
| 2022 | 6.70T RMB | -23.0% |
| 2023 | 5.82T RMB | -13.2% |
| 2024 | 4.89T RMB | -16.0% |
| **2025** | **4.15T RMB** | **-14.7%** |

**Revenue has halved since 2021.** LGFVs were "buyers of last resort" at land auctions. As revenue falls, local governments can't subsidize LGFVs or provide collateral for loan rollovers.

## Dollar Transmission Channel

### February 2026 Liquidity Crunch
- **$456B (3.2T RMB) shortfall**
- Drivers: Lunar New Year + LGFV bond issuance + corporate FX demand
- PBOC response: Record 1T RMB injection, rate cut to **1.4% (record low)**

### UST Retreat
- Feb 2026: Banks advised to reduce UST exposure
- Holdings: **$682.6B (Nov 2025)** — lowest since 2008
- Decade-long decline accelerating
- NOT just custodial arbitrage — actual stress liquidation during crunches

### CIPS Growth
- 2024: **$24T transaction volume** (+43% YoY)
- Alternative settlement infrastructure building

1. **Hidden Debt Swaps:** 2T RMB/year (2024-2026)

## Risk Assessment

### NOT a Lehman Scenario
- Central government has eliminated "sudden stop" risk
- Massive swap quotas + megabank recaps
- Controlled deleveraging, not collapse

### The REAL Risk: Zombie Economy
- Capital tied up servicing legacy LGFV debt
- No productive investment capacity
- Prolonged stagnation, not crisis

### Global Transmission
- UST liquidation during liquidity crunches = **recurring feature**
- PBOC managing "trilemma": domestic stability + trade surplus + yuan defense
- Dollar funding stress → Treasury sales → feeds back to LIQUID thesis
