## 1. LGFV Resolution: Historical Precedent Works

### 1990s NPL Crisis — Proof of Concept

| Metric | 1990s Crisis | 2024-2026 LGFV |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Scale | NPLs 25% of loans (20% of GDP) | 60T RMB LGFV debt |
| Mechanism | Centralized AMCs | Debt-swap program |
| Fiscal Instrument | 30-year special bonds | Refinancing + special bonds |
| Outcome | Successful recapitalization | In progress |

**Key point:** China has done this before. The institutional memory and capacity exists.

### Debt-Swap Program Progress

- **71% reduction** in LGFV count by Sep 2025
- **62% decrease** in operational financial debt vs Mar 2023
- NOT mass defaults — conversion from opaque "hidden" debt to transparent bonds
- Interest savings: **~1.1T RMB** (2024-2028) = liquidity injection to local govts

### The "Gap" Is Manageable

- Remaining gap after 10T RMB program: ~4.5T RMB
- Central government balance sheet **relatively clean** (vs leveraged local govts)
- Unified Debt Management Department (late 2025) enables systematic rollover
- This is a **long-term fiscal commitment**, not a crisis

- **Structural dependence on USD system to absorb savings**

## 8. Where the Bear Thesis Is Weakest

### 1. Overestimating "Lehman Moment" Potential

- Chinese debt is **largely internal** and **RMB-denominated**
- State can restructure without threat of external creditor "run"
- 71% LGFV reduction proves state is culling weak entities **before** contagion

### 2. Underestimating Export Resilience

- $1.2T surplus despite 20% drop in US exports
- "Front-loading" orders and diversification (ASEAN, EU)
- Resilient buffer prevents desperate reserve liquidation

### 3. The Demographic Miscalculation

- Yes, aging population is long-term headwind
- But **urbanization runway is significant** (66% → 70% by 2030)
- Millions moving to cities annually = sustained infrastructure/housing demand
- Prevents "total collapse" scenario

### 4. Institutional Resilience of HK Peg

- Bears underestimate "capital preservation first" principle
- **$409B reserves** with **110%+ backing ratio**
- One of most over-collateralized currencies in the world
- Successful speculative attack **highly improbable**
