## 4. UST Market Impact

### Yield Impact Estimates (Warnock model baseline)

| Liquidation | Est. 10Y Yield Impact | Systemic Consequence |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| $200B (methodical) | **+30-40 bps** | Manageable, flight-to-safety offset |
| $500B (accelerated) | **+60-100 bps** | Severe tightening, mortgages 10%+ |
| **$1T (forced)** | **+150-250 bps** | Market dysfunction, auction failures |

### The Yield Impact Equation

```
ΔY = (S / D) × σ
```
- S = Supply of sold Treasuries
- D = Market depth (shrinks as dealers pull back)
- σ = Volatility/panic multiplier (at historical highs in crisis)

**Non-linear:** In crisis, σ spikes while D collapses → impact far exceeds models.

### Fed Response: Emergency QE

Fed would be forced into emergency buying to prevent fiscal crisis ($34T debt, $1T+ annual interest).

**Policy Trilemma:**
1. **Price stability:** QE is inflationary during supply shock
2. **Market stability:** Must keep yields low
3. **Currency stability:** Massive buying weakens USD

**Likely action:** Expand FIMA to friendly central banks (Japan, UK, EU) to socialize absorption burden.
