## "Backdoor Intervention" — The Invisible Reserve Strategy

### Mechanism
- State-owned banks conduct currency interventions through **swap market**
- PBOC's own balance sheet remains unchanged
- "Buy spot / sell forward" maneuver

### Evidence (Dec 2025)
- Banking system purchased massive FX (hundreds of billions adjusted for forwards)
- Gap between cumulative FX settlement and PBOC balance sheet = intervention measure
- Gap widening since 2018

### Pattern
- When yuan strengthens → State banks buy dollars → Rein in yuan
- When yuan weakens → Opposite
- **Not visible in official reserve data**

### U.S. Treasury Response (Jan 2026)
> Treasury stated it would **no longer rely primarily on PBOC's formal reserves** to assess China's FX policies

This acknowledges the opacity.

## Capital Controls: "Facilitated Entry, Gated Exit"

### Entry Facilitation (SAFE Circular 28, Sep 15, 2025)

| Reform | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| FX Profit Reinvestment | FIEs can reinvest domestically without additional registration |
| Equity Investment | Any FIE can use registered capital for equity investments |
| Property Purchase | Nationwide FX settlement facilitation for foreign individuals |

### Exit Restrictions (Tightening)
- **Exit bans** for foreign executives increasing
- Personal fines under 2025 Cybersecurity Law
- SAT (late 2025): "Self-inspections" of overseas income (3 years)
- Targets undeclared offshore assets of Chinese citizens

**Result:** Capital can enter, but increasingly cannot leave.
