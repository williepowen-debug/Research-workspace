# RP-ZHAO-5: Developer Bonds and Property Sector Deleveraging

**Source:** Gemini Deep Research (Feb 13, 2026)
**Prompt:** Property sector restructuring landscape, offshore bond recovery, transmission to banking

---

## Executive Summary

The Chinese property sector has transitioned from **"chaotic contagion"** to **"structured resolution."** Four years of cascading defaults have produced a fragmented landscape: over-leveraged entities face terminal liquidation, while others pioneer "holistic restructurings" prioritizing equitization over cash payouts. The era of full par recovery for offshore bondholders is **over**.

| Treatment | Offshore | Onshore |

**Key insight:** Offshore bondholders face haircuts/equitization. Onshore creditors get soft terms. Country Garden focusing on delivering **~130,000 homes in 2025**.

## Sunac China — First "Full Equitization"

**First major developer to undergo TWO offshore restructurings.**

### First Restructuring (Nov 2023)
- Partial debt-to-equity swaps + maturity extensions
- **Failed** — persistent sales decline prevented meeting interest payments
- Debt-to-asset ratio: **>94%** by mid-2025

### Second Restructuring (Effective Dec 23, 2025)

- **$9.6B** offshore debt converted entirely to MCBs
- **Conversion prices:** HK$0.48 (Short-Term), HK$3.85 (Long-Term)
- **Automatic conversion:** All remaining bonds → equity on **June 30, 2026**
- Total debt reduced by **~RMB 60B**

**Key insight:** Full equitization = creditors now hold equity, betting on market recovery. Risk fully transferred.

## Key Takeaways for ZHAO

### Transmission to Banking
1. **Offshore bondholders** are absorbing principal losses via MCBs → equity conversion
2. **Onshore creditors** (including banks) get soft terms — maturity extensions, 1% interest
3. **White List** filtering mechanism protects banks from worst exposures
4. But: **State banks backstopping Vanke = implicit guarantee expansion**

### Timeline Implications
- **H2 2027:** Earliest stabilization for Tier 1/2 cities (Morgan Stanley, Goldman)
- **2028:** Possible maturity cliff from extended debt (kick-can effect)
- **Property drag:** Narrowing (1.5-2.0 ppt → 0.5-1.0 ppt) but persistent

### Recovery Reality
- Full par recovery is **over** for offshore bonds
- Risk transferred to equity holders betting on recovery
- Sector transitioning to "stability-and-service" utility model

## Integration with LGFV Thesis (RP-ZHAO-2)

The property crisis and LGFV crisis are **interlinked**:

1. **Land revenue collapse** (8.7T → 4.15T RMB) = LGFV funding source dried up
2. **LGFVs were "buyers of last resort"** at land auctions — now absent
3. **Regional banks exposed to both:** CRE developer loans + LGFV loans
4. **"Managed volatility"** strategy applies to both — extend, extend, extend

**Synthesis:** Property sector is being "zombified" alongside LGFVs. Capital trapped servicing legacy debt. Not Lehman-style collapse, but decade of slow deleveraging.

---

*Key insight: The "real" recovery is not a return to 2019 sales levels, but the emergence of a leaner, state-integrated real estate sector that functions as a utility for urban management rather than a vehicle for speculative wealth.*
