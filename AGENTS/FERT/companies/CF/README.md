## Earnings quality and cash ownership

The issuer's adjusted EBITDA **retains the $170 million litigation gain recognized in Q1**. The EBITDA reconciliation (table13) has no removal; the adjusted-net-earnings reconciliation (table14) separately removes it and its tax impact. These two adjustment definitions must not be conflated. Subtracting only that gain gives a diagnostic H1 figure of **$2,005 million**, up42.7% from issuer adjusted EBITDA of$1,405 million in H1 2025; this is **FERT arithmetic, not issuer adjusted EBITDA or a clean recurring-earnings estimate**. Other comparability items remain, including Q2 $50 million business-interruption insurance recovery. The litigation cash arrived in April; Q2 cash receipt is not another earnings gain.

H1 operating cash flow less capex is **$880 million** ($1,374 − $494 million), an arithmetic cash measure before noncontrolling flows and other issuer FCF adjustments. Do not call it CF's reported free cash flow. The issuer reports **$1,823 million trailing-twelve-month FCF** through June 30, a different period and definition. June 30 cash $2,480 million includes$341 million of Blue Point One JV cash; long-term debt $3,216 million is consolidated book debt. H1 capex $494 million includes$143 million at the JV. Consolidation is not the same as unrestricted parent-company cash or wholly owned spending; JV contributions/distributions are separate rows.

## Supply and the next decision

The May 6 release expected Yazoo City production to resume **late Q4 2026 at earliest**; August 5 moved the expected restart to **H1 2027**. This is a dated guidance change, not a newly announced October outage. Full-year gross ammonia guidance remained approximately 9.5 million short tons. Q2's98% available-ammonia-capacity utilization **excludes Yazoo**; it does not describe total nameplate utilization. The supply ledger preserves both guidance vintages without double-counting the site.

CF can benefit from higher realized nitrogen prices while losing available volume and spending more on projects. That is the relevant combination to test at Q3. No gas-to-ammonia theoretical margin, cross-product urea spread, consensus earnings surprise, live share valuation, or legacy $800 million-per-$50 sensitivity is inferred.
