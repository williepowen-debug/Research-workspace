## Validation and capability limits

The offline extractor checks source-label coverage, unique company keys, segment net-sales/gross-margin/sales-volume totals, and Q1+Q2=H1 for additive observations. All seven TSVs retain source/vintage and units. Product production cannot be totaled across gross ammonia and upgraded products. Unknown capacity is blank plus UNKNOWN; missing product-months are not zero-filled. Published forecasts carry a forecast qualifier, not an assertion of realized outcomes.

KB maintenance classified 16 overdue ACTIVE rows as 7 SUPERSEDED / 9 STALE, and all 10 blank-deadline rows as dated historical records, stale receipts or named future rechecks. It mapped 17 safe aliases into existing nitrogen/phosphate groups; 12 potash-group rows remain a vocabulary-owner issue. Historical evidence is preserved, not declared false for age. `kb_lifecycle_review.json` records dispositions. The owner-authored read declaration is ready for PROME's shared registry; no claim that registry ingestion is complete.

The repository virtual environment supplies pandas/BeautifulSoup needed for the extraction; system Python lacks those modules. Direct Census API returned HTTP200 **Missing Key HTML**, so it was rejected as data; the AMS API supplies a usable alternative. Requests403 on SEC10-Q/BLS PDF did not prevent browser primary reads. China primary access remains the research limitation. No claim of a continuously running feed is made.
