Parker Delivers Record Fiscal 2027 First Quarter Results, Raises Full Year Outlook
Record first quarter sales, segment operating margin and EPS; Completes Filtration Group acquisition
CLEVELAND, November 5, 2026 -- Parker Hannifin Corporation (NYSE: PH), the global leader in motion and control technologies, today reported results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 that included the following highlights (compared with the prior year period):
Fiscal 2027 First Quarter Highlights:
•Sales increased 18% to a record $6.0 billion; organic sales increased 11.5%; acquisitions added 7%
•Net income was $0.9 billion, an increase of 9%, or $1.1 billion adjusted, an increase of 23%
•EPS increased 10% to $6.92, adjusted EPS increased 23% to a record $8.90
•Segment operating margin was 24.4%, an increase of 20 bps, or 28.6% adjusted, an increase of 120 bps
•FY27 outlook increased for organic sales growth, adjusted segment operating margin, adjusted EPS and free cash flow
“Parker team members delivered an exceptional quarter of record performance and an impressive start to our fiscal year,” said Jenny Parmentier, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “We experienced increased demand across all market verticals, resulting in double-digit organic sales growth and record backlog levels. Strong market conditions, combined with execution of The Win Strategy™, delivered record adjusted segment operating margin of 28.6% and adjusted earnings per share growth of 23%. We are pleased to have completed the acquisition of Filtration Group Corporation, significantly expanding our filtration offering and aftermarket business and are excited to welcome these talented team members to Parker. With strong first quarter performance and increased order rates, we are raising our full year fiscal 2027 guidance for organic sales growth, adjusted segment operating margin, adjusted earnings per share and free cash flow.”

This news release contains non-GAAP financial measures. Reconciliations of adjusted numbers and certain non-GAAP financial measures are included in the financial tables of this press release.

Fiscal 2027 Outlook
The company has updated guidance for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, which now includes Filtration Group Corporation.
•Reported sales growth of 16.0% to 19.0%
•Organic sales growth of 7.5% to 10.5%; acquisitions of 9%, and currency of (0.5%)
•Segment operating margin of 23.6% to 24.0%, or 27.7% to 28.1% on an adjusted basis
•EPS of $29.65 to $30.45, or $36.45 to $37.25 on an adjusted basis

Note on Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release contains references to non-GAAP financial information including (a) adjusted net income; (b) adjusted earnings per share; (c) adjusted segment operating margin for Parker and by segment; (d) adjusted segment operating income for Parker and by segment; (e) free cash flow; and (f) organic sales growth. These measures are presented to allow investors and the company to meaningfully evaluate changes in net income, earnings per share, segment operating margins, cash flow and sales growth on a comparable basis from period to period. Although these measures are not measures of performance calculated in accordance with GAAP, we believe that they are useful to an investor in evaluating Parker's performance or expected performance for the periods presented. Additionally, adjusted net income, adjusted earnings per share and organic sales growth include an adjustment related to IEEPA tariff returns to customers, which returns we believe are discrete, non-operating items that are not indicative of our ongoing operations. Comparable descriptions of record adjusted results in this release refer only to the period from the first quarter of FY2011 to the periods presented in this release. This period coincides with recast historical financial results provided in association with our FY2014 change in segment reporting. A reconciliation of non-GAAP measures is included in the financial tables of this press release.
