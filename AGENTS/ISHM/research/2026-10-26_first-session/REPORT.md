## Judgment

**The US factory upturn is observable, but its durability as broad end-demand is unproved.** September ISM new orders exceeded raw-material inventories by 6.7 index points, and September real manufacturing output increased. Those observations argue against calling a national contraction. The forward warning is composition: October S&P manufacturing orders and output growth slowed while hiring, input stocks and delivery delays lifted its headline. Dallas and Philadelphia orders slowed sharply; New York provides a meaningful counterexample.
