Note: Survey responses were collected between October 2 and October 9.

Business activity rose sharply in New 
York State in October, according to 
firms responding to the Empire State 
Manufacturing Survey. The headline 
general business conditions index 
climbed eighteen points to 25.5, its 
highest level in nearly five years. New 
orders increased markedly and shipments 
grew. Unfilled orders rose, delivery times 
lengthened substantially, and supply 
availability worsened further. Inventories 
increased. Employment continued to 
rise modestly and the average workweek 
rose. The pace of input and selling price 
increases remained elevated. Firms 
continued to expect conditions to 
improve in the months ahead, though 
optimism moderated.

strongly in New York State, according to 
the October survey. The general business

Price Increases Remain Elevated
The index for number of employees held 
steady at 10.3, and the average workweek 
index came in at 9.1, suggesting ongoing 
modest growth in employment and 
an increase in hours worked. Prices 
continued to rise at an elevated pace, 
with the prices paid index dipping four 

points from its four-year high last month 
to 59.5, and the prices received index 
rising three points to 30.6.

Optimism Moderates
The index for future business conditions 
dropped eight points to 21.0, indicating 
that firms expect conditions to improve 
in the months ahead, though they are 
not as optimistic as in recent months. 
New orders and shipments are expected 
to increase, and employment is expected 
to grow. Supply availability is expected 
to worsen. Both future price indexes 
climbed further from already elevated 
levels, suggesting that expectations for price 
increases have become more widespread. 
Capital spending plans remained modest. ■
