"Improving productivity through structural transformation and a stronger pipeline of differentiated technologies reinforce our confidence in the full-year outlook and long-term growth and margin potential. I want to thank the 3M team for their focus on customers and disciplined execution, which continues to strengthen our performance and create shareholder value."

Strategic and operational highlights
The following are recently announced highlights:
3M entered into an expanded strategic commercial relationship with Amazon to deploy and scale 3M's optical connectivity and other Data Center related products.
3M issued a warrant to Amazon to purchase up to 3.2 million shares of 3M common stock based upon the achievement of $6 billion in cumulative sales of optical connectivity and other Data Center related products over the next seven years, and other event-based milestones.
3M is collaborating with Genius Electronic Optical Co., Ltd. (GSEO),
an optical technology company, to advance technology development and manufacturing for 3M's Expanded Beam Optics (EBO) solutions. In support of these efforts, 3M is investing to acquire ~4.7% of GSEO's common shares. The collaboration brings together 3M's EBO technology leadership, optical interconnect expertise, materials science, manufacturing scale and customer relationships with GSEO's precision optics manufacturing capabilities and production scale.
3M introduced Cooling Film technology for fleet and rail applications
, expanding its portfolio of thermal management solutions for transportation customers. Applied to vehicle roofs, the highly reflective performance film uses thermal management technology to reduce heat buildup and help keep interiors noticeably cooler.
3M showcased how its materials science expertise supports innovation in golf equipment through its collaboration with PING
. By pairing PING's expertise in club design and custom manufacturing with 3M's broad portfolio of materials science solutions, the two companies can collaborate on engineering solutions that turn complex technical challenges into repeatable, high-quality results on the course.
3M and BOE established a joint innovation laboratory to accelerate next-generation display technology development.
The joint research and development platform brings together BOE's display innovation and end-use application expertise with 3M's materials science capabilities to accelerate higher-performance solutions across LCD, OLED and MLED applications, including improvements in light efficiency, foldable displays and ultra-narrow bezel processes.
3M continued to expand its work with U.S. and international government customers, with third-quarter awards supporting defense, health care, and emergency response needs. Awards included PELTOR™ communication and hearing protection solutions for various U.S. military services and Scott SCBAs for the U.S. Navy, respirator and SCBA products for the National Institutes of Health, and respirators for international customers, reflecting 3M's growing work with government partners around the world.
