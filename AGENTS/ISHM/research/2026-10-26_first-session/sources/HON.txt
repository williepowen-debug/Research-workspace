CHARLOTTE, N.C., October 22, 2026 -- Honeywell Technologies (NASDAQ: HON) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
The company reported third-quarter sales of $5.0 billion, up 1% reported and 7% organic
1
, driven by broad-based strength across all three segments, pricing actions, and continued demand across key end markets. Backlog increased 7% year over year to nearly $19 billion, excluding Warehouse and Workflow Solutions (WWS) and Productivity Solutions and Services (PSS) divestitures in 3Q 2026.
Operating income increased 79% and segment profit
1
increased 23% driven by productivity, including elimination of stranded costs related to prior spin-offs and divestitures, pricing actions that more than offset inflation, and volume leverage. Operating margin of 14.8% increased 640 basis points and segment margin
1
of 21.3% expanded 370 basis points, led by Industrial Automation and Building Automation.
Reported EPS decreased 20% primarily due to a one-time gain from the Resideo termination agreement in prior year. Excluding the impact of this and other items, adjusted earnings per share
1
increased 34% year over year to $2.27 driven by organic sales growth, higher segment profit, and lower net interest expense.
Finally, operating cash flow of $607 million decreased 74% primarily due to the one-time settlement from the Resideo termination agreement in prior year, while free cash flow
1
of $688 million increased 15%, with free cash flow conversion
1
of  95%, driven by higher operating income and lower capital expenditures.
Management Commentary
“Honeywell Technologies delivered strong results across the business in our first quarter as a standalone company. Continued momentum across all three segments, together with our improving position in high-growth verticals, translated to 7% organic sales growth, above the three-year target set at our June Investor Day. This performance, coupled with continued progress reducing fixed cost, enabled adjusted operating income to increase at more than three times the rate of sales, while adjusted EPS rose 34%, well ahead of our expectations for the quarter. As a result of this strong performance, we are raising our full-year 2026 outlook, which gives me great confidence in our long-term strategy and our ability to deliver on our 2029 targets,” said Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Vimal Kapur.

Revised 2026 Guidance
Honeywell Technologies is raising its full-year outlook for organic revenue, adjusted EPS and free cash flow, and also now expects segment margin to be at the previous high end of guidance, following strong third quarter performance and continued momentum across the portfolio. The company now expects full-year sales of $20.0 billion to $20.1 billion with organic
1
sales growth of 4% to 5%, and 5% to 6% organic
1
growth in the fourth quarter; segment margin
1
of approximately 20.5%, at the upper end of its previous guidance range, representing segment margin
1
expansion of approximately 290 basis points; and adjusted earnings per share
1
of $8.40 to $8.45, up 30% to 31%, representing a $0.23 increase from the midpoint of its previous guidance range. Finally, operating cash flow and free cash flow are now expected to be ~$2.1 billion each.

About Honeywell Technologies
Honeywell Technologies is a global, pure-play automation company with a legacy of innovating to help solve the world's most mission-critical challenges, enhancing the quality of life for people and communities around the world.  We serve the building, industrial, and process sectors with a broad portfolio of services, solutions, and products, underpinned by our Honeywell Technologies Accelerator operating system and Honeywell Technologies Forge intelligence layer.  By combining the deep domain expertise of our more than 50,000 employees with decades of data from our global installed base, we are uniquely positioned to lead the industrial sector's transition from automation to autonomy. For more news and information on Honeywell Technologies, please visit Honeywell Technologies Newsroom.
