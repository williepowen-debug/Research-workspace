GE AEROSPACE ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Year-to-date orders, revenue and total engine deliveries up over 20%, raising full-year guidance
Third Quarter 2026:
Total orders of $19.0B, +49%;
Total revenue (GAAP) of $14.1B, +16%; adjusted revenue* $13.3B, +18%;
Profit (GAAP) of $2.8B, +12%; operating profit* $3.0B, +30%;
Profit Margin (GAAP) of 19.9%, (80) bps; operating profit margin* 22.5%, +220 bps;
Continuing EPS (GAAP) of $2.32, +14%; adjusted EPS* $2.25, +36%;
Cash from operating activities (GAAP) of $2.2B, (14)%; free cash flow* $2.0B, (14)%
CINCINNATI — Oct 20, 2026 — GE Aerospace (NYSE:GE) announced results today for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026.
GE Aerospace Chairman and CEO H. Lawrence Culp, Jr., said, “We delivered strong results this quarter as solid demand drove orders up 49%, with strength in commercial services up 23%, total revenue up 18% and EPS up 36%. We are raising our 2026 guidance across the board, and we now expect 2027 commercial services to grow low-double-digits supported by our over $170 billion commercial services backlog.”
Culp continued, “Thanks to the collaboration of the GE Aerospace team with our supplier partners we continue to build momentum. By leveraging FLIGHT DECK fundamentals, year-to-date commercial services revenue and total engine deliveries were both up over 20%, including another quarter of record internal shop visit output. All the while, we're continuing to invest in our customers' needs with our agreement to acquire CPP to expand capacity, and over
$3 billion in annual R&D to advance current and future technologies."
Recent highlights include:
Increased material input from priority suppliers double-digits year-over-year, contributing to strong year-to-date Commercial Engines & Services (CES) services revenue and total engine deliveries, including LEAP growing 26%.
Announced commitments for more than 1,800 engines at a historic
Farnborough International Airshow
, including the largest LEAP agreement ever for more than 1,000
LEAP-1A
engines with IndiGo and in October, signed an agreement with Turkish Airlines for more than 300
LEAP-1B
engines along with a long-term services agreement covering its entire LEAP-1B fleet of over 600 engines. In Defense, signed a contract worth up to $2.9B to provide ongoing support for the F414's powering the U.S. Navy's F/A-18 and EA-18G fleets.
Signed an agreement to acquire
Consolidated Precision Products (CPP)
to expand mission-critical castings capacity for the aerospace industry to meet demand across commercial engines, aftermarket and defense. This acquisition would also accelerate the introduction of new engine technologies to our current fleet and next-generation platforms.
Completed the
world's first high-altitude hybrid electric flight
above 30,000 feet in partnership with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), BETA Technologies, and Boeing, demonstrating the viability of more electric aircraft engine systems for greater efficiency, durability, and range.
Advanced key defense programs, including the
GEK800
(F143) engine with Kratos being designated as a second-source propulsion solution for the Joint Air-to-Surface Standoff Missile (JASSM) and continued progress on the
XA102
adaptive-cycle engine as the U.S. Air Force’s Next Generation Adaptive Propulsion (NGAP) program moves into hardware procurement and subcomponent testing.
GE Aerospace Full-Year 2026 Guidance:
Based on GE Aerospace's year-to-date performance and outlook for the fourth quarter, the company is raising total company guidance across the board and now expects
*Non-GAAP Financial Measure
(a – Adjusted Corporate & Other operating costs* represents the sum of Corporate & other profit (costs) and (Eliminations). In 2026, the company is expecting this to trend toward the lower end of our prior $(1.2)-$(1.3)B guidance range with ~$(500)M Corporate cost, which is ~flat y/y, and ~$(700)M Eliminations, which is up ~$(100)M y/y.
(b – FCF* conversion: FCF* / adjusted net income*
For the quarter, orders of $15.3 billion increased 58%, with services increasing 23% and equipment more than tripling. Revenue of $10.3 billion was up 21%. Services grew 17% with internal shop visit revenue up 12% from higher volume, including LEAP up over 40%, and heavier workscope; spare parts revenue grew over 20%.
Equipment revenue grew 38%, driven by engine deliveries up 8%, including LEAP up 5%, and GEnx growth. While we delivered GE9X engines in the quarter, the impact from these shipments will be recognized post midseal certification. Profit of $2.9 billion was up 21% from higher services volume and price. Margins contracted (10) basis points from mix and inflation.
In 2026, CES now expects revenue growth of low 20s, up from our prior guide of ~20%, driven by higher services revenue, which we now expect to grow at the higher end of our prior guide of low 20s, and equipment revenue growth of low 20s, up from ~20%. Operating profit is expected to be in the range of $10.5-$10.6 billion, up from our prior guide of $10.25-$10.35 billion, driven by higher services growth and reduced GE9X impact.
For the quarter, orders of $4.3 billion were up 18%. Revenue of $3.5 billion grew 7%. Defense & Systems revenue was up 5% driven by growth in both services and equipment, including engine deliveries up 4%. Propulsion & Additive Technologies revenue increased 10% with growth driven by Avio Aero and Unison. Profit of $475 million was up 8% and margins increased 20 basis points from increased volume and price, partially offset by mix and inflation.
In 2026, DPT continues to expect revenue growth of low double digits and operating profit of $1.6-$1.7 billion.

We also use Adjusted EPS* as a performance metric at the company level for our performance stock units granted in 2026.

In addition, we include dispositions of property, plant and equipment, including sale leaseback activity. We believe this measure will better allow management and investors to evaluate the capacity of our operations to generate free cash flow*. We also use FCF* as a performance metric at the company level for our annual executive incentive plan and performance stock units granted in 2026.

About GE Aerospace
GE Aerospace is a global aerospace propulsion, services, and systems leader with an installed base of approximately 50,000 commercial and 30,000 military aircraft engines. With a global team of approximately 57,000 employees building on more than a century of innovation and learning, GE Aerospace is committed to inventing the future of flight, lifting people up, and bringing them home safely.
