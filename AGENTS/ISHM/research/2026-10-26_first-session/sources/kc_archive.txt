2026 Manufacturing Surveys

Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Increased Steadily in October
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
Oct. 22, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity increased steadily, and expectations for future activity rose slightly from last month with their highest readings since July 2022. Growth in prices paid for raw materials continued to rise, while finished prices growth eased.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Continued to Increase in September
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
Sept. 24, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity continued to increase, and expectations for future activity remained steady. Both month-over-month price indexes increased slightly from last month, with the finished products price index reaching its highest reading since July 2022.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Increased Further in August
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
Aug. 27, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity increased steadily, while expectations for future activity remained expansionary. The month-over-month price indexes for finished products and raw materials ticked up, while year-over-year price growth fell slightly.
Tenth District Manufacturing Growth in Activity Remained Steady in July
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
July 23, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing growth in activity remained steady, while expectations for future activity rose slightly from last month. The month-over-month price indexes for finished products and raw materials fell slightly, but year-over-year price growth increased further.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Rose Moderately in June
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
June 25, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity rose moderately, with further growth expected in six months. Price indexes for finished products and raw materials continued to increase at a faster pace than last month and marked their highest levels since 2022.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Continued to Increase in May
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
May 21, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity continued to increase, while expectations for future activity grew slightly from last month. Raw materials prices remained elevated this month, while growth for finished products rose modestly.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Grew Further in April
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
April 23, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity grew further, while expectations for future activity rose slightly from last month. Raw materials prices increased sharply this month, while fewer firms reported increasing their finished product prices.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Increased Moderately in March
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
March 26, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity increased moderately, and expectations for future activity remained expansionary. Raw materials price growth cooled slightly but continued to outpace finished product price growth.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Rose Slightly in February
B
y Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
Feb. 26, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity rose slightly, and expectations for future activity ticked up. Although still positive, growth in indexes for the price of finished products and the price of raw materials eased slightly from last month.
Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Was Unchanged in January
By Cortney Cowley and Megan Williams
Jan. 22, 2026
Tenth District manufacturing activity was unchanged, and expectations for future activity cooled but remained expansionary. Prices paid for raw materials increased further this month, while finished product prices growth cooled.
