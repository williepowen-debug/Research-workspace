       Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Remained Flat in October               
                                                                                    
       Fifth District manufacturing activity remained                               
       flat in October, according to the most recent                                
       survey from the Federal Reserve Bank of                                      
       Richmond. The composite manufacturing                                        
       index remained at -2. Two of its three                                       
       component indexes decreased in October:                                      
       new orders to -8 from -6, and employment to                                  
       3 from 7. Meanwhile, the shipments index                                     
       rose to 0 from -5.                                                           
                                                                                    
       The future indexes for shipments and new                                     
       orders remained in positive territory while the                              
       expectations index for employment remained                                   
       at 8 in October.                                                             
       The local business conditions index                                          
       decreased from -6 in September to -8 in                                      
       October. The future local business conditions                                
       index also fell from 10 to 7.                                                
                                                                                    
       The average growth rate of prices paid was                                   
       flat from September to October, while the                                    
       average growth rate of prices received                                       
       increased. Firms expected growth in prices                                   
       paid and prices received to fall over the next                               
       12 months.

          Technical Notes:                                                          
           1 Each index equals the percentage of responding firms reporting increase minus the percentage reporting decrease. Results are
            based on responses from 120-130 firms. All firms surveyed are located within the Fifth Federal Reserve District, which includes
            the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia. Data, excluding price
            trends, are seasonally adjusted. Seasonal adjustment factors are recalculated every July and the entire series is revised to better
            reflect current economic trends.                                        
           2 Expectations refer to the time period six months out from the survey period.
           3 The manufacturing Index is a gauge of broad activity in the District's manufacturing sector. It is a composite index representing a
            weighted average of the shipments (33 percent), new orders (40 percent) and employment (27 percent) indexes.
           4 Current price changes are percentage changes over the last 12 months.  
            Expected price changes are expected changes over the next 12 months.
