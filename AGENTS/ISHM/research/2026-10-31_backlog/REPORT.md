2. **The durable-goods headline was aircraft.** Total orders $345.4B, +1.8%, but excluding transportation only +0.5%; nondefense aircraft orders +28.4%. Exactly the case the October 26 frame warned against reading as broad demand.
3. **Q3 real equipment investment boomed, led by computers.** BEA Q3 advance (October 29): real equipment +20.9% q/q SAAR (Q2 13.4%), information processing +37.4% (7.6%), computers and peripherals +66.7% (−6.7%), industrial equipment +24.7%, transportation equipment −7.9%. With it the October regional panel is complete: 1 of 5 districts with falling orders, as in September, but order readings fell sharply in Philadelphia (29.2 to 4.3), Kansas City (24 to 3) and Dallas (30.7 to 9.3). New dated vintage: `REGIONAL_BREADTH_2026-10_COMPLETE.tsv`.
5. **End users, not only dealers, are buying, and the hottest line is data-center power.** Caterpillar Q3 (October 29): sales $20.682B, +17%, volume +$2.207B against price +$663M; "strong order rates and a record backlog" (dollars not stated); dealer retail sales +22%, North American construction +27%; Power Generation $3.421B, +30%, "primarily in data center applications", dealer retail +75%. IATA September air cargo (October 29): +7.7% y/y globally, North American carriers +11.8%, with AI-related shipments driving the Pacific.
