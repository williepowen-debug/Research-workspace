## Regional mechanism detail

- **Atlanta:** factory activity flat; advanced manufacturing/data-center strength offsets weaker sectors. Wholesalers order more inventory partly to minimize transport expense; soft trucking coexists with strong intermodal. [Primary district report](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202610-atlanta.htm).
- **Richmond:** unchanged factory activity contains panels for data centers and filters for aerospace. Contacts report fuel-related margin pressure; some truckers defer new equipment or maintenance. Do not treat this October 14 qualitative report as the October 27 manufacturing diffusion survey. [Primary](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202610-richmond.htm).
- **Chicago:** machinery demand increased, but capital spending fell modestly and mostly replaced equipment. Manufacturing stocks somewhat low; large data-center buyers compete for electrical equipment. Selected manufacturing clients faced input-cost/supply stress, not a measured fleet-wide credit event. [Primary](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202610-chicago.htm).
- **Dallas:** demand spans durable and nondurable manufacturers, including energy-related machinery. This directly challenges an AI-only explanation. Reported project-permitting constraints remain contact evidence, not independent legal verification of any statewide order. [Primary](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202610-dallas.htm).
