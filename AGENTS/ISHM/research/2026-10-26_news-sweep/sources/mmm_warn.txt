Re: WARN Notice for 3M Fairmont 

To Whom It May Concern: 

3M Company has announced its intention to close its Fairmont, MN, facility, located at 710 North 
State  Street,  Fairmont,  MN  56031.  This  closure  will  result  in  approximately  62  permanent  job 
eliminations.  This  notice  is  being  provided  to  you  in  compliance  with  the  federal  WARN  Act 
(Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act). 

3M began providing employees with information regarding these developments in January 2026, 
and has provided regular updates since that time.  The entire facility will be closed on a permanent 
basis.  This facility closure will occur by December 31, 2026.   With very limited exceptions for 
senior plant management needed to complete the site closure, job eliminations are expected to 
occur between December 18-31, 2026.   

The site closure is anticipated to impact approximately 62 employees.  Affected employees do 
not have “bumping” rights. This information is based on the best information currently available to 
us but may change due to subsequent unforeseen events. 

Affected  employees  will  be  offered  the  opportunity  to  accept  severance  benefits  under  the 
applicable severance plan, subject to the terms and conditions of that plan.  

Some of the affected employees are represented by the United Steelworkers Union, Local 142B 
Unit 11.  The union president is Adam Werner and his address is 403 East 12th Street Fairmont, 
MN,  56031.    The  other  union  officials  at  the  site  are  Carrie  Kluver,  Martin  Ruiz,  Tyler  Meixell, 
Aaron Langager, and Justin Preuss.   

For further information, contact me at 507-887-0826.
