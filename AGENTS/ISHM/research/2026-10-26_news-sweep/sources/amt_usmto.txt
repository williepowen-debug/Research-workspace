McLean, Va. (October 12, 2026) — New orders of metalworking machinery, measured by the U.S. Manufacturing Technology Orders Report published by 
AMT – The Association For Manufacturing Technology
, totaled $732.9 million in August 2026. Just one month before 
IMTS – the International Manufacturing Technology Show
 opened its doors in Chicago, orders of manufacturing technology reached the third-highest monthly total recorded. Orders in August 2026 were 21.0% above 
July 2026 
and 36.3% above 
August 2025
. Thus far in 2026, orders totaled $4.76 billion, a 36.9% increase in orders over the first eight months of 2025.
The current rally in manufacturing technology orders now includes six consecutive months of orders exceeding half a billion dollars, the longest such streak recorded. Since the current expansion began in 
September 2024
, order values have grown nearly three times faster than the growth in units. The 
aerospace sector
, which tends to purchase high-value machinery, has accounted for a growing share of total orders since prior to the current rally. Across all industries, an ever-growing 
share of new machinery containing added automation
 has further intensified order values growing faster than the growth in unit sales.
If 
IMTS 2024
 is remembered as the beginning of the current expansion, 
IMTS 2026
 may be remembered as the point where 
contract machine shop
 orders began to lead the market. Historically, an uptick in orders from job shops would lead to a general recovery in demand for manufacturing technology. The current expansion has largely been driven by demand from OEMs, who generally have a greater appetite for automated machinery than job shops, thereby sustaining the 
disparity between value and unit growth
 in recent years. In August 2026, growth in orders from contract machine shops exceeded overall market growth. If the resurgent demand for manufacturing technology from job shops marks the beginning of a sustained trend, 
order activity for the remainder of the year could exceed expectations
.

The United States Manufacturing Technology Orders (USMTO) Report
 is based on the totals of actual data reported by companies participating in the USMTO program. This report, compiled by AMT – The Association For Manufacturing Technology, provides regional and national U.S. orders data of domestic and imported machine tools and related equipment. Analysis of manufacturing technology orders provides a reliable leading economic indicator as manufacturing industries invest in capital metalworking equipment to increase capacity and improve productivity.
