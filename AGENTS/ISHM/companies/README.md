# ISHM company files

The authorized membership is [PERIMETER.tsv](../PERIMETER.tsv); no list expansion is implied here. Each ticker file uses the same eight-cell checklist and points to its issuer source, release vintage and next scheduled read. The print ledger remains the figure/vintage home. Different fiscal periods are explicit; these reports are not one synchronized national sample.

- [CAT](CAT.md): broad equipment volume, power-generation applications, dealer/channel questions.
- [DE](DE.md): equipment demand and used inventories; farm income belongs to FERT/CARL.
- [PH](PH.md): industrial/aerospace orders; November's rolling-order-method change requires a rebased comparison.
- [EMR](EMR.md): automation orders; underlying-order exclusions and tariff-refund accounting matter.
- [GE](GE.md): aerospace, not GE Vernova.
- [HON](HON.md): remaining automation company; aerospace spin excluded.
- [MMM](MMM.md): industrial/electronics sales; prospective optical contracts are not present orders.
- [UNP](UNP.md): physical freight volumes versus price/fuel and mix.

Company files are maintained at their print-driven wakes, not daily. `NOT-STATED` means absent from the reviewed source, not no economic exposure. Prices, valuation and portfolio consequences remain with HENRY/TERRY and the relevant domain owners.
