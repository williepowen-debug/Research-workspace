## Primary Thesis: "The Convergence"

**Confirmed if:**
- FHLB advances spike >$650B (early warning)
- 2+ Tier 1/2 banks miss earnings same quarter
- BDC dividend cut triggers bank fund finance stress
- Office DQ >15% AND bank provisions spike

**Partially validated:**
- ✅ Office CMBS DQ at ATH — 12.34% (Jan 2026)
- ✅ SLOOS shows C&I tightening — Jan 2026 survey
- ✅ BDC PIK at elevated levels — PSEC 35%, FSK 27%

**Invalidated if:**
- FHLB stays <$550B through 2026
- Office DQ drops <10% sustained
- BDC dividends maintained, PIK declines
- Employment stays strong (claims <230K)

## Secondary Thesis: "Multi-Channel > Single-Channel"

**Confirmed if:**
- WAL/VLY/ZION underperform SBCF/BKU during stress
- Multi-channel bank has capital event while single-channel survives

**Invalidated if:**
- Single-channel pure plays (EGBN, SBCF) fail first
- Multi-channel diversification proves protective
