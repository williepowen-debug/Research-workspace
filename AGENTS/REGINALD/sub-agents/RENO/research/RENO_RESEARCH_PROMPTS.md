## RP-RENO-1: Canadian Tourism Impact

**Goal:** Quantify Nevada's exposure to Canadian tourism decline

**Prompt:**
```
Analyze the impact of Canadian tourism on Nevada's economy, specifically Las Vegas. Looking for:

1. VISITOR DATA
   - How many Canadian visitors come to Las Vegas annually?
   - What % of total Vegas visitors are Canadian?
   - What is the average spend per Canadian visitor?
   - Total economic impact ($ billions)?

2. CURRENT TRENDS
   - YoY change in Canadian visitors (2024 vs 2025)?
   - Air Canada / WestJet capacity to Las Vegas — any cuts announced?
   - CAD/USD exchange rate impact on travel decisions?
   - Any data on Canadian sentiment toward US travel (boycott surveys)?

3. TARIFF/TRADE WAR IMPACT
   - Historical precedent — did Canadian travel decline during 2018-2019 tariffs?
   - Current rhetoric — are Canadian politicians/media calling for travel boycotts?
   - Corporate travel policies — any Canadian companies restricting US travel?

4. GAMING REVENUE CORRELATION
   - Can you isolate international vs domestic gaming revenue?
   - Which casinos have highest Canadian exposure (marketing, partnerships)?

5. FORWARD INDICATORS
   - Forward booking data (if available)
   - Convention/trade show Canadian attendance
   - Airline forward capacity

Sources: Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority (LVCVA), Nevada Gaming Control Board, Statistics Canada, airline announcements.

Output: Quantified exposure ($ and %) with trend direction.
```

## RP-RENO-3: Lake Mead / Water Crisis

**Goal:** Understand water constraints and timeline for escalation

**Prompt:**
```
Analyze the Lake Mead water crisis and implications for Nevada development and municipal credit. Looking for:

1. CURRENT WATER LEVELS
   - Current Lake Mead elevation (feet)
   - Historical trend (5-year, 10-year)
   - Seasonal patterns
   - Current vs capacity (% full)

2. SHORTAGE TIERS
   - What are the federal shortage tier thresholds?
   - What tier is Nevada currently in?
   - What are the allocation cuts at each tier?
   - When is Tier 2 / Tier 3 projected to hit?

3. NEVADA ALLOCATION
   - What is Nevada's annual Colorado River allocation?
   - How much does Southern Nevada Water Authority (SNWA) use?
   - What % of Vegas water comes from Lake Mead vs groundwater vs recycling?
   - Conservation measures already in place

4. DEVELOPMENT IMPACT
   - Are there any development moratoriums due to water?
   - Water rights requirements for new development
   - Cost of water rights ($ per acre-foot)
   - Which planned developments are at risk?

5. MUNICIPAL CREDIT IMPLICATIONS
   - Have any Nevada municipalities been downgraded due to water?
   - S&P/Moody's/Fitch commentary on NV water risk
   - SNWA bond ratings and outlook
   - Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas credit ratings

6. LONG-TERM PROJECTIONS
   - Climate models for Colorado River basin
   - 2026 Compact renegotiation — what's at stake for Nevada?
   - Worst-case scenario timeline

Sources: Bureau of Reclamation, SNWA, rating agency reports, academic climate studies.

Output: Current status, escalation timeline, and municipal credit risk assessment.
```
