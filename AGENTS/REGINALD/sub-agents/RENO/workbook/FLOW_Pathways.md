## PATHWAY 1: TOURISM → GAMING → BANKS

### The Route
```
Canadian boycott (-24%)
    → Leisure visitor decline (-7.5% total)
    → Hotel occupancy drops (83.6% → 80.3%)
    → RevPAR declines (-8.8% to -10%)
    → Operator margin compression
    → Gaming borrower stress
    → WAL gaming vertical exposure
    → Bank credit losses
```

### Key Metrics

| Node | Metric | Current | Trigger Level |
|------|--------|---------|---------------|
| Tourism | Visitor YoY | -7.5% | -15% |
| Gaming | Strip revenue YoY | +0.03% | Negative |
| Operator | Caesars EBITDA | -19% | Covenant breach |
| Bank | WAL gaming NCOs | 0.24% | >0.50% |

### Mitigants (Why It's Slow)
1. **Whale concentration** — Baccarat +53% masks volume loss
2. **Convention calendar** — Record 1.23M attendees in 2026
3. **Luxury segment** — Venetian/Wynn hitting records
4. **Price discipline** — Hotels not discounting (yet)

### Timeline
- Tourism stress: NOW ✅
- Operator stress: NOW 🟠
- Bank transmission: H2 2026+ ⏳

```
    → Related services decline
    → Consumer income stress
    → Mortgage/auto delinquencies
    → Bank consumer loan losses
```

### Key Metrics

| Node | Metric | Current | Trigger Level |
|------|--------|---------|---------------|
| Construction | Jobs YoY | -9.3% | -15% |
| Employment | UR | 5.2% | >6.5% |
| Consumer | NV auto DQ | TBD | >7% |
| Housing | Foreclosure rate | 1:248 | <1:150 |
| Bank | Consumer NCOs | Low | Rising |

### Historical Parallel: 2008
- Construction led Nevada's 2008 collapse
- Jobs peaked 2006, employment peaked 2007
- UR went from 5.2% → 14.5% over 3 years
- Current decline mirrors RATE but not SCALE

### Mitigants
1. **Equity cushion** — Homeowners have 30%+ equity
2. **Rate lock-in** — Sub-4% mortgages = no strategic defaults
3. **Cash buyers** — 26% floor provides price support
4. **No subprime** — Different loan quality vs 2008

### Timeline
- Construction stress: NOW ✅
- Employment deterioration: UNDERWAY ⚠️
