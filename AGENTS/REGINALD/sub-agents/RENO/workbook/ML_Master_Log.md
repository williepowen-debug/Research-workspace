## Historical Reference

### 2008 Crisis Trajectory (for comparison)
- Dec 2007: UR 5.2% (recession begins)
- 2008: UR doubles as construction/gaming collapse
- Oct 2010: UR peaks at 14.5% (highest in nation)
- Initial claims peak: 36,414/month (Dec 2008)
- Construction job loss: -70,000 over 3 years

**Current vs 2008:**
- Construction decline rate: Similar (-9.3% vs similar early-2008)
- Construction decline scale: Much smaller (10.5K vs 70K)
- Banking system: Different (no liquidity crisis)
- Institutional response: More prepared (CLN hedging, reserves)
