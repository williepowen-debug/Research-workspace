## TX Exposure — The Reframe

Management pitches TX (via IBTX merger, Jan 2025) as "diversification." It's not.

**TX is Sunbelt correlation risk:**
- Austin MF oversupply (same dynamics as FL)
- Same rate sensitivity (refi stress)
- Same insurance pressure (TWIA rising)
- If LABOR breaks, TX MF fails alongside FL MF

**What SSB absorbed from IBTX (Jan 2025):**
- $17B+ in loans
- Primarily TX geography
- Still digesting integration

**The flanking risk:**
If transmission chain activates (LABOR → consumer stress → lease-ups fail), SSB gets hit on BOTH flanks (FL AND TX) simultaneously. The "diversification" becomes correlation.
