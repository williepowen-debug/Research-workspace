## Executive Summary

Management is in full victory lap mode on IBTX integration. Bullish tone throughout. **One credit question revealed MF stress already showing up in classified loans** — management dismissed it as "timing." They're doubling down on TX CRE growth at exactly the wrong time. Analysts didn't push back.

**Verdict:** Transcript CONFIRMS thesis. MF stress visible, management dismissive, TX correlation risk accelerating.

## 🚨 Critical Finding: Multifamily Stress in Classified Loans

**The Exchange:**
> Q (Barclays): "It looks like there was some increase in substandard loans this quarter. Just any color on what drove that?"
>
> A (Steve Young, Chief Strategy Officer): "The increase was due to a **handful of multifamily properties that are in lease-up**. The credit team is not concerned about those. In fact, they've got a weighted average loan-to-value of 52%. So really tons of equity. It's just a **timing issue in lease-up**."

**Why This Matters:**
1. MF lease-up stress is EXACTLY the risk we identified
2. "Timing issue" is classic pre-deterioration language
3. 52% LTV assumes current valuations hold — may not if rents don't stabilize
4. "Not concerned" = not building reserves
5. This is showing up BEFORE employment breaks — what happens after?

## TX/CO Growth: Correlation Risk Accelerating

**Pipeline Growth:**
- TX/CO pipeline: $800M → $1.2B (+50%)
- Q4 production: $888M (+15% vs Q3)
- Full year 2025 vs 2024: +10%

**Hiring:**
- 17 of 26 new commercial RMs in Q4 were TX/CO (65%)
- Dan Strodel (TX President) "very successful" in recruiting

**Strategy Quote:**
> "Historically, they've been a great CRE lender, and **we want them to continue to do exactly what they've been doing**... we don't want them to stop what they're so good at."

**Translation:** They're DOUBLING DOWN on TX CRE at the cycle peak.

**CEO on Growth Drivers:**
> "One of the things that we're seeing in the pipeline is some growth in **investor commercial real estate**, which really lagged last year. And we're seeing really nice pipeline builds in **Texas and Colorado**. If that momentum continues... that would be the tailwind."

They view growing CRE as upside. We view it as concentration risk.
