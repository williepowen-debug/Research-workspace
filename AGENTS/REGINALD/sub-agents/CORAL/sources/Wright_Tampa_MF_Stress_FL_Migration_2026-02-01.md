# Wright: Tampa MF Stress & FL Migration Collapse

*Source: Melody Wright "Read My Lips" (Feb 1, 2026)*

---

## Tampa Multifamily Stress

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| MF Vacancy Rate | **10.3%** | Well above national average |
| Units Delivered (past year) | **8,350** | Supply pressure |
| Units Absorbed | **6,300** | Demand lagging |
| Pipeline | **10,000 units** | More coming |
| Rent YoY | **-1.9%** | $1,800/month avg |
| New Starts | Multi-year low | Finally slowing |

> "Tampa's multifamily market softened through Q3 2025 as elevated vacancy and moderating demand coincided with continued supply pressure."

> "10,000 more deliveries on the way and vacancy already over 10%."

---

## Florida Migration Collapse

> "New Census estimates have just been released showing that net migration to Florida between 2024 and 2025 has slowed significantly. In fact, Florida in total showed **22,517 in net migration gains** during that time period."

**Historical context:**
- Peak migration: 300K-400K/year
- Current: 22,517/year
- Decline: **>90%**

**Implication:** The demand source that drove FL housing is exhausted.

## Tampa Housing Affordability

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Median Household Income | $74,475 |
| Median Home Price | ~$400K |
| Price-to-Income | **5.4x** |
| Sustainable Ratio | 3.0-3.5x |
| Implied Fair Value | **~$250K** |

> "Incomes in no way match sales prices in Tampa."

---

## Insurance Crisis

> "After Ian, Debby, Helene and Milton, Florida homeowners have seen their insurance premiums significantly rise and affordability erode."

> "Prior to 2024, Tampa's hurricane history only included two major hurricanes in 1921 and 1848. Many friends and colleagues I've known over the years told me that they chose this area specifically because of this history. That of course changed in 2024 which has reduced the luster of Tampa's sheen."

---

## Hidden Inventory

> "According to Property Radar, Tampa currently has **2,030 properties with deceased owners**. Those properties can take time to come to market but show up they will at a rate which will increase as older landlords look to divest."

**Also:**
- 6,038 new homes listed
- 4,452 existing homes listed
- Low owner occupancy = landlord class will need to exit

## Implications for CORAL

1. **MF vacancy 10.3%** — Above threshold, more supply coming
2. **Migration collapsed >90%** — Demand source exhausted
3. **Affordability broken** — 5.4x income ratio unsustainable
4. **Insurance crisis** — Post-hurricane premiums eroding affordability further
5. **Hidden inventory** — 2,030 deceased-owner properties + aging landlords
6. **Tech hub myth** — 0% tech job growth despite marketing

**Tampa joins SSB risk profile:**
- SSB has heavy Tampa/FL exposure
- MF vacancy + migration collapse + affordability = stress
- Watch for Tampa-specific NPL increases at SSB
