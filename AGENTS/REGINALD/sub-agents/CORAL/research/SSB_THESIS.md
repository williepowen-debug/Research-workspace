## The Bear Case (Our Thesis)

### 1. Multifamily Stress is REAL and ELEVATED

**Substandard Rate: 9.36%** — Highest of ANY CRE category at SSB

| Credit Grade | % of MF Portfolio | Implied DSCR |
|--------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Substandard | 9.36% | <1.0x |
| Special Mention | 1.80% | 1.0x-1.2x |
| Pass | 88.8% | >1.2x |

**The "Support and Survive" Dynamic:**
- 9.36% of MF loans are technically in default (DSCR <1.0x)
- BUT borrowers keep paying from outside sources
- WHY? They have 48% equity at stake (52% LTV)
- Walking away = wiping out massive capital
- So they subsidize negative carry, hoping rates drop

**The Vulnerability:**
- Sponsor liquidity is FINITE
- If "higher for longer" persists 12-18 more months, some sponsors exhaust reserves
- Then NPLs spike from 0.02% to ???
- The 9.36% substandard is a LEADING INDICATOR, currently suppressed by sponsor support

### 2. Geographic Concentration = Correlated Risk

| Region | % of MF Book | Risk Factor |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Georgia | ~38% | Atlanta supply glut (worst in nation) |
| Florida | ~24% | Insurance crisis (structural, permanent) |
| Texas | ~14% | Austin rent contraction, supply wave |
| South Carolina | ~20% | Stable (low risk) |

**Florida Insurance Crisis:**
- Even fully leased properties have DSCR <1.0x due to insurance cost explosion
- Unlike Austin supply glut (will burn off), FL insurance is STRUCTURAL
- 24% of portfolio facing non-cyclical expense-side destruction

### 3. Vintage Risk is Concentrated

**2021-2023 Originations = "Broken" Vintage**

Underwriting assumptions at origination:
- Base rates: ~0% (construction loans at 3-4%)
- Rent growth: 5-10% modeled
- Exit cap rates: 3.5-4.5%

Current reality:
- Base rates: 5.25-5.50% (floating rate reset to 8%+)
- Rent growth: Negative in supply-heavy markets
- Cap rates: 5.5-6.5%

**Result:** Loans with doubled debt service into stagnant/declining rents

### 4. Floating Rate Exposure = Fed Sensitivity

- **64% of CRE portfolio is floating rate**
- If Fed cuts: DSCRs snap back, substandard migrates to pass
- If Fed holds: "Support and Survive" gets tested to exhaustion

**This position is essentially a bet on "higher for longer"**

### 5. The "Quality Premium" Works Against Us Until It Doesn't

SSB's defensive profile (low beta, institutional ownership) means:
- First mover in any sector selloff will be weaker names
- SSB may be last to fall, but fall it will if thesis plays out
- We need a MACRO catalyst that overwhelms dip-buying
