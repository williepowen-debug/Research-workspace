## Risk Factors

### 1. Power Procurement
- Electricity access is the new bottleneck
- Multi-year deposits required from utilities
- "Behind-the-meter" generation adds risk

### 2. Construction Delays
- **57% of projects at least 3 months late** (2025)
- Liquid cooling complexity
- Tenant termination rights if delayed past drop-dead date

### 3. GPU vs. Shell Obsolescence
- Shell: 50-year asset
- GPUs: 1-3 year practical life
- Tenor mismatch = valuation risk for lenders

### 4. Circular Financing
- Nvidia investing in its own customers
- $770B in potentially "circular" commitments
- Echoes Lucent/Nortel vendor financing (2000)
