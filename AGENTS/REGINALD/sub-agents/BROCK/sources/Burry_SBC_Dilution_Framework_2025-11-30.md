# Burry: The Tragic Algebra of Stock-Based Compensation
**Source:** Michael Burry / Cassandra Unchained (Substack)
**Date:** November 30, 2025
**Relevance:** 🟢 HIGH — Framework for evaluating AI/tech valuations

---

## Core Thesis

Wall Street adds back SBC expenses in "adjusted" earnings, making tech earnings appear 30-50% better than reality. Even 1% annual dilution dramatically destroys present value.

## The Formula

Traditional DCF:
```
PV = CF / (d - g)
```

Modified for dilution:
```
PV = CF / (d - g + y)
```

Where y = dilution rate

**Key insight:** Higher growth rates don't solve dilution — they make it worse.

## Key Examples

### Nvidia — "Buyback to Nowhere"
- $91B spent on buybacks since 2018
- Share count: 24.3B → 24.3B (UNCHANGED)
- $205B net income, but true owners' earnings = $135B
- $91B transferred from shareholders to employees

### Tesla
- 3.6% annual dilution (no buybacks)
- Musk's $1T pay package ensures continuation
- Burry: "The Elon cult was all-in on electric cars until competition showed up, then autonomous driving until competition showed up, now robots – until competition shows up."

### Palantir
- 4.6% annual dilution despite buybacks
- "First billionaire:revenue ratio >1" — 5 billionaires, <$4B revenue
- No earnings after adjusting for SBC

### Amazon
- 1.3% annual dilution
- $220B net income since 2018
- Gave employees $233B in stock value
- "What shareholders gave employees has more value today than what Amazon earned"

---

## Implications for BROCK Thesis

1. **AI darlings are overvalued by 30-50%** due to SBC manipulation
2. **"Adjusted earnings" is fiction** — don't add back SBC
3. **Buybacks to nowhere** = cash burned without reducing dilution
4. **Any earnings disappointment gets amplified** when true costs recognized
