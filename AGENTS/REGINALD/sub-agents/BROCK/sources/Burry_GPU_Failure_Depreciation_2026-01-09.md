# Burry GPU Failure & Depreciation Analysis

*Extracted from "The Blessed Fraud Recurrence" (Jan 9, 2026)*

---

## Core Thesis

GPU failure rates and technological obsolescence make 5-6 year depreciation schedules untenable. This means:
- **Overstated earnings** at hyperscalers
- **Overvalued assets** on balance sheets
- **Failing private credit loan securitizations** (BDC exposure)

---

## GPU Failure Rate Data

### First-Year Failure Rate
> "Meta released a study dealing with a training run of Llama on a cluster of Nvidia Hopper H100 GPUs... faulty Nvidia GPUs and GPU memory made up almost half the failures. This amounts to about a **9% failure rate if extended over the first year**."

### Aging Curve
> "Oak Ridge Titan supercomputer study... found that after 2-3 years of operation, older GPUs had **12x worse mean time between failures** than fresh units."

### Projected 3-Year Failure Rate
> "A 9% first-year failure rate for Hopper could grow to a **35-45% 3-year failure rate**, which would seem incompatible with 5-6-year depreciation schedules."

### Arrhenius Problem
> "For every 10 degrees Celsius increase in circuit temperature, halve the lifetime."

---

## Blackwell Will Be Worse

- Power usage 43-71% higher than Hopper
- Higher thermal density (two dies in one package)
- Documented overheating issues
- Larger package = more thermal differentials = more failure points
- Requires liquid cooling

> "I would imagine Blackwell failure rates would be higher than 9% in the first year. Failures at 3 years could be greater than 50%."

---

## Technological Obsolescence

### Annual Chip Cycle
- Hopper → Blackwell → Rubin (2024-2026)
- Vera Rubin announced January 8, 2026

> "Inference 5x greater than Blackwell? In one year? A **6-year depreciation life fails the common sense test here**."

### Satya Nadella Confirmation
> "Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said as much when he did not want to get stuck with 4-5 years of depreciation on one generation."

---

## Power Grid Bottleneck

> "Access to energy, T&D, does not magically appear in the US: it takes years of planning which we have not done and the **current map of US electrical grid is not capable of supporting $3T in Capex spend**."

### SMR Not a Solution
> "I am of the opinion, after substantial work, that **SMRs are simply not economically feasible**."

> "A dozen SMRs by 2030 (likely optimistic) would imply on the order of less than 3.6 GW of generation capacity."

### China Comparison
- China has 2x US power generation capacity
- China responding quickly to AI power demands
- US falling behind due to politics/regulations

> "I see a big inventory problem in the AI buildout as a result of the current power generation setup."

## Why Short NVDA (Not Hyperscalers)

> "If I short Meta, I'm also shorting its social media/advertising dominance. If I short Alphabet, I'm shorting Google Search... The big ones are not pure shorts on AI."

> "Nvidia is simply the **purest play**... Nvidia will sell $400 billion of its chips this year and there are **less than $100 billion in application layer use cases**."

> "Nvidia also is the most loved, and least doubted. So shorting it is cheap, and its puts are cheaper than some of the other big shorts."
