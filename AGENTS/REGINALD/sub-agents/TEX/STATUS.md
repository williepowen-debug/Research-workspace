## Mission

Track Texas's converging stress channels and their transmission to regional bank balance sheets. Texas matters for three reasons:

1. **SSB exposure** — 19% of loan book post-IBTX merger (Austin, DFW, Houston)
2. **Austin MF crisis** — Worst multifamily oversupply in nation
3. **Border cities** — MARCO overlap (El Paso, McAllen, Laredo stress)

**Core hypothesis:** Texas is "correlation risk" for SSB, not diversification. Same Sunbelt dynamics as Florida — rate sensitivity, MF oversupply, insurance pressure. If the thesis works, Texas is a second front.
