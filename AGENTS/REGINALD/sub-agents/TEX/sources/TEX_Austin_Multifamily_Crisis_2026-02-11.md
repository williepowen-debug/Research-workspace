# Austin Multifamily Deep Dive: The 2024-2026 Oversupply Crisis

**Source:** Deep Gemini research  
**Date:** February 11, 2026  
**Research Prompt:** RP-TEX-1

---

## Executive Summary

Austin is the **primary national case study** for Sun Belt multifamily oversupply. After 2020-2022 boom, the market faces severe structural imbalance:
- **14.2% vacancy** (Q4 2025) vs 6.6% baseline (2019)
- **10 consecutive quarters** of rent decline
- **31,894 units delivered in 2024** (130% above 10-year average)
- **20-25% valuation decline** from peak

**Critical Finding:** This is a SUPPLY shock, not a DEMAND shock. Austin's economy remains strong. Recovery projected Q3-Q4 2026 as supply cliff hits.

|--------|----------|
| 2022 Peak | **3.5-3.8%** |
| 2025 | **5.6%** |

### Per-Unit Pricing

| Period | $/Unit |
|--------|--------|
| Early 2022 | ~$275,000 |
| 2025 | ~$220,000 |
| **Decline** | **-20-25%** |

### DSCR Compression — THE KEY FINDING

**Formula:** DSCR = NOI / Debt Service

**The Math:**
- NOI down ~20% (falling rents + rising OpEx)
- Rates reset from 3.5% → 6.5%
- Result: DSCR below 1.0x on many 2021-23 vintage loans

**Current observation:** Some loans at **1.02x** — "near-breakeven"

**"The property is barely generating enough cash to meet debt obligations."**

## Assessment for SSB/TEX Thesis

### Austin MF Channel: 🔴 RED — CONFIRMED STRESS

**This is the sharpest stress point in Texas.**

### Transmission to SSB

**Question to answer:** How much of SSB's 9.36% MF substandard is Austin vs Florida?

**What we know:**
- SSB CRE concentration: 272.1%
- SSB has significant Austin exposure post-IBTX
- IBTX was running 405% CRE pre-merger
- Austin MSA showed 20.4% growth in SSB production
- DSCR compression to ~1.02x matches SSB's "support and survive" MF description

### "Support and Survive" Dynamic — MATCHES SSB

**From SSB analysis:** 9.36% MF in substandard, but NPLs near zero

**From Austin data:** Borrowers subsidizing negative carry to protect equity stakes

**This is the same dynamic.** Austin properties likely contributing to SSB's substandard bucket.

### Recovery vs. Position Timing

**Risk:** Recovery projected Q3-Q4 2026 — overlaps with June 2026 puts

**Mitigation:**
1. Recovery requires "higher for longer" to end
2. Maturity wall creates forced selling even in recovery
3. Bank recognition of losses lags property market by 2-3 quarters
4. SSB Q1 earnings (late April) = key data point before June expiry
