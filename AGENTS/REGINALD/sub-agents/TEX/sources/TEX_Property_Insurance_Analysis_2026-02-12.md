## Executive Summary

**Texas Property Insurance: Structural Assessment of Risk, Resilience, and the Florida Comparison**

Texas operates as the **second-largest P&C jurisdiction** in the US (>11% of national homeowners premiums). The central question: Is Texas on trajectory to replicate Florida's systemic insurance crisis?

**Key Finding:** Texas faces severe property insurance stress, but is **NOT facing a Florida-like systemic collapse** due to:
1. Chapter 542A (2017) litigation reform — firewall against lawsuit epidemic
2. File-and-use regulatory regime — maintains private market participation
3. Rate adequacy achieved — 2024-2025 showing stabilization

**Primary risk is meteorological and fiscal, not legal.**

| Freeze | 1 | $500M–$1B | 0.1% |

**190 billion-dollar events** from 1980-2024 have forced structural repricing.

### Severe Convective Storms (SCS) — The Hail Crisis
- Texas leads nation in tornado activity (>100 annually)
- **878 hail events** in 2024 (1"+ stones) — most persistent driver
- Lightning claims: **$38,558 avg** (vs Florida $23,686)
- Hail is **statewide, year-round** — unlike concentrated coastal wind

### Tropical Cyclones — Coastal Tail Risk
- 16 events = 57% of total disaster costs ($200-300B)
- Houston-Galveston concentration dictates reinsurance pricing
- Hurricane Beryl (2024) led Progressive to restrict new business

### Winter Storms — The Resilience Gap
- Uri (Feb 2021): 80% of insured losses in Texas alone
- **$1.41 payout for every $1 collected** in 2021
- Burst pipes (water damage) covered under standard policies

### Flooding — The Protection Gap
- Kerr County: Only **2.5% of homeowners** had NFIP coverage
- Inland flooding decoupled from coastal surge
- Uninsured losses impair mortgage collateral value

## Insurers of Last Resort

### Texas Windstorm Insurance Association (TWIA)
- Serves **14 coastal counties + Harris County**
- **276,220 policies** (March 2025) — up from 2015-18 lows
- **$117.2B in insured value**
- >50% exposure in Galveston and Brazoria counties
- 2024 funding requirement: **$6.5B** (up from $4.5B)
- 62% from traditional reinsurance + cat bonds

### Texas FAIR Plan Association (TFPA)
- **Statewide** coverage (vs TWIA coastal only)
- Enrollment **doubled**: 73,000 (Sept 2023) → **120,000+** (Q1 2025)
- Projected **135,000** by year-end 2025
- "Market in crisis" — inland hail belt homeowners can't get admitted coverage

## Florida Comparison

### Burden Comparison
| State | Avg Premium (2025 Proj) | Ratio |
|-------|------------------------|-------|
| **Florida** | **$15,460** | 1.0x |
| Texas | $6,522 | 0.42x |

Florida remains **2.4x more expensive** than Texas.

### The Critical Difference: Litigation Environment

**Florida's crisis was fueled by:**
- "One-way attorney fees"
- Assignment of Benefits (AOB) abuse
- Florida = **76% of all property insurance lawsuits** despite 6.9% of claims (2021)

**Texas enacted reform earlier:**
- **Chapter 542A** (effective Sept 1, 2017)
- 60-day pre-suit notice required
- Insurers can assume agent liability (avoid local court bias)
- Restricts attorney fee recovery for inaccurate estimates

**This legislative buffer prevented the "lawsuit epidemic" that collapsed Florida.**

Florida enacted similar reforms in 2022-2023 (SB 2A) — but damage was done.

## Transmission Pathways to Banks

### 1. Mortgage Delinquency
- Premium increase of 1 standard deviation → **+0.6pp delinquency probability** (+16%)
- Disproportionately affects high-DTI borrowers
- "Cross-delinquency" effect: Homeowners sacrifice credit card payments for mortgage/insurance
- Texas mortgage delinquency: **+24 bps** in late 2025

### 2. Tax & Insurance (T&I) Burden
Harris County example:
- T&I portion of mortgage payment: **41.4% (2014) → 51.8% (2024)**
- 75% of high-risk zone housing units lack flood insurance (cost prohibitive)

### 3. CRE / Multifamily NOI Compression
- TX multifamily insurance: **$39/unit (2019) → $68/unit (2024)** (+75%)
- Austin rents: **-4.5% YoY** (Q4 2025) — can't pass costs to tenants
- **$1 insurance increase → $0.72 NOI reduction**
- **Latent distress exceeds reported delinquencies by 4x**

### Transmission Chain
```
Weather Event → Reinsurance Hike
    ↓
Carrier Rate Filing → Household Premium (+20%+)
    ↓
Liquidity Constraint → Credit Card Usage ↑
    ↓
Loan Delinquency (esp. high-DTI, FHA)
    ↓
NOI Compression → Asset Devaluation → Undercollateralized Loans
```

## Key Conclusions

### Texas is NOT Florida (Yet)
1. **Chapter 542A (2017)** = critical firewall against litigation collapse
2. **File-and-use** maintains private market participation
3. Rate adequacy largely achieved by 2024-2025

### Primary Risks
1. **Meteorological**: Relentless SCS + tropical tail risk
2. **Fiscal**: Population's ability to pay may be exceeded
3. **FAIR Plan doubling**: Warning sign that "availability" becoming luxury

### Bank Exposure
- **Latent distress** in MF portfolios = significant systemic risk
- Regional banks may be forced to recognize losses where insurance has permanently impaired asset value

### 2025-2027 Outlook
- **Near-term**: Rate stabilization if no major catastrophe
- **Long-term**: Bifurcated market emerging
  - Private market for low-risk, resilient properties
  - Massive state-backed residual market for rest

### Avoiding Florida Outcome Depends On:
1. Maintaining favorable litigation climate
2. Incentivizing resilient construction methods
