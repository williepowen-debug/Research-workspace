# RQ-CREED-005: Florida Condo Crisis Scale

**Date:** 2026-01-27 | **Status:** COMPLETE

---

## HEADLINE

FL condo crisis is a self-reinforcing negative cycle: **SB 4-D mandates → $50K-$400K assessments → owner distress → Fannie blacklist (1,438 FL buildings) → cash-only sales → price collapse (-9.9% YoY, worst since 2009) → foreclosures (FL #1 nationally, 8 consecutive months rising)**. 7 of 10 worst US condo price decline metros are in Florida.

---

## 1. SCALE

| Metric | Data | Confidence |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Total FL condo associations | ~28,000 | Medium |
| Total FL condo units | ~1.5M | Medium |
| Buildings on Fannie Mae "unavailable list" (FL) | **1,438** | High |
| South FL buildings blacklisted | **~700** (doubled in 2 years) | High |
| National Fannie unavailable list | 5,175 properties | High |
| SIRS completion rate | Partial uptake; many failed deadline | Low (no precise %) |

### Fannie Mae Blacklist Impact
- Fannie/Freddie back ~70% of residential loans nationally
- Blacklisted buildings = effectively **cash-only purchases** or expensive non-QM loans
- List is **not public** — owners discover status only when mortgage rejected
- South FL blacklist doubled in two years

- Buildings **under 30 years old**: +10% value increase

- Sharp bifurcation emerged 2023-2024 as regulations took effect

## 5. BANK/LENDER EXPOSURE

- 1,438 FL buildings Fannie-ineligible → conventional lending effectively blocked
- Underinsured buildings cannot get Fannie/Freddie approval
- FHA loans at >11% delinquency = acute vulnerability

## SELF-REINFORCING CYCLE

```
SB 4-D mandates → massive assessments ($50K-$400K)
    → owner delinquency → association shortfalls
    → deferred maintenance → Fannie blacklist
    → cash-only market → demand collapse
    → price decline (-9.9% to -32%) → negative equity
    → foreclosures (#1 nationally) → more supply
    → further price decline → more walkaways
    → insurance exits → higher premiums (+164%)
    → higher assessments → cycle continues
```

## CREED IMPLICATIONS

4. 7 of 10 worst US condo price decline metros in FL = geographic concentration risk
5. Foreclosure trajectory (8 consecutive months rising, +57% YoY) approaching 2008-era patterns in FL specifically
6. Insurance cost escalation (+164%) is a structural amplifier with no near-term relief
