# RP-CREED-11: Multifamily Demand Stress
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research  
**Date:** 2026-02-13  
**Category:** Multifamily / NOI Stress

---

## Executive Summary

Multifamily "demand stress" is a **cash-flow problem** driven by three interacting forces:
1. **Rent growth reversal** — flat/negative vs 2-3% underwriting
2. **Concession surge** — 15.6% usage, 10.5% depth (highest since post-GFC)
3. **Supply wave** — 590K deliveries vs 519K absorptions in 2025

**Key insight:** "Extend and pretend fails because tenants don't renew at underwritten rents."

## NOI Stress Model

### Underwriting Assumptions (Typical 2021-2022 Vintage)

- Rent growth: 2.5%/year
- Occupancy: 95%
- Expense growth: 3%/year
- DSCR: 1.25x (agency benchmark)

### Actual Conditions (2022-2026)

- Rent growth: **0% or negative**
- Occupancy: **92-95%** (Sun Belt worse)
- Expense growth: **5-7%** (insurance +27.7%!)
- Concessions: **1.6-4% effective haircut**

### NOI Gap vs Underwriting

| Scenario | NOI Gap |
|----------|---------|
| Flat rents + 5% expenses | **-21%** |
| -5% rents + 5% expenses | **-29%** |
| Flat rents + 1.6% concession + 5% expenses | **-29%** |
| Flat rents + 4% concession + 5% expenses | **-39%** |

### DSCR Compression

If underwritten DSCR = 1.25x and NOI prints 29% below:
- **Actual DSCR = 0.89x** → Covenant breach

## Stress-to-Lender-Action Flow

```
Supply wave + weak absorption
        ↓
Higher vacancy / slower lease-up
        ↓
Operators increase concessions
        ↓
Effective rent < Asking rent
        ↓
Revenue growth below underwriting
        ↓
NOI underperforms (expenses rising)
        ↓
DSCR and debt yield decline
        ↓
DSCR below trigger → Cash sweep / reserves
        ↓
Persistent breach → Modification / extension
        ↓
Severe distress → Special servicing / workout
```

## Status: ✅ COMPLETE

This research validates the "NOI collapse" thesis for multifamily. The mechanism is clear:
1. Supply > Demand → Vacancies rise
2. Operators compete with concessions → Effective rent drops
3. Expenses rise (insurance +27.7%) → Margins compress
4. NOI breaks vs underwriting → DSCR covenants breach
5. Sponsors capitulate → Special servicing / sales

**2021-2022 vintage MF deals are the stress test. Sun Belt is the epicenter.**
