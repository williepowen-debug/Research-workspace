## WHAT TO WATCH IN Q1 2026

### Primary Monitoring Vectors

#### 1. Florida Performance (CRITICAL)

**Context:** SIRS deadline passed Dec 31, 2025. Q1 is first full quarter post-compliance.

**Watch For:**
- **Association Loan Performance** — Any HOA/condo association defaults?
- **Blacklist Growth** — 1,438 → how many by March 31?
- **Non-Accrual FL CRE** — Specific disclosures on FL multifamily
- **DSCR Compression** — Insurance/assessment costs hitting cash flow
- **Special Assessment Impact** — Did any major assessments hit Q1?

**Key Question for Call:** "Can you quantify exposure to condo associations on the Fannie/Freddie blacklist?"

#### 2. Those Two CRE Loans

**Q4 Flag:** $44.4M in "two large well-secured CRE loans" hit 30-59 DPD.

**Q1 Resolution Paths:**
- ✅ **CURED** — Returned to current (bullish)
- 🟡 **60-89 DPD** — Migrated to next bucket (watch)
- 🔴 **NON-ACCRUAL** — Migrated to NPA (bearish, reserve build)

**Impact:** If these hit non-accrual, NPA ratio jumps to ~0.96% from 0.87%.

#### 3. Net Charge-Off Trend

**Q4 Pattern:** $22.6M NCOs (0.18% annualized) driven by CRE partial write-downs.

**Q1 Scenarios:**
- **GREEN (<$20M):** Q4 was cleanup, trend normalizing
- **YELLOW ($20-30M):** Continuing elevated but manageable
- **ORANGE ($30-45M):** Acceleration, reserve build needed
- **RED (>$45M):** Severe stress, Q4 2024 flashback ($98.3M)

**Threshold:** NCO ratio >0.25% annualized = status change consideration.

#### 4. Deposit Mix & Funding Cost

**Q4 Win:** Cost of deposits fell 24bp to 2.45% (indirect CD runoff + non-interest deposit growth).

**Q1 Risk:** 
- Did non-interest deposits hold at $12.2B?
- Did indirect deposits continue declining from $5.4B?
- FHLB borrowings at 4.10% — any increase?

**Bullish:** Continued deposit improvement  
**Bearish:** Reverse of Q4 trends, FHLB dependency rising

#### 5. BDC/Fund Finance Exposure

**Known Exposure:** $85M Saratoga facility, Citizens-style fund finance desk.

**Context:** PSEC at 35% PIK, BDC sector burning cash.

**Watch For:**
- Any BDC-related charge-offs
- Mentions of "specialty finance" or "fund banking"
- Reserve builds in C&I attributed to BDC
