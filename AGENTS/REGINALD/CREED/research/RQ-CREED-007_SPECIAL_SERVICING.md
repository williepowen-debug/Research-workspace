# RQ-CREED-007: Special Servicing Deep Dive

**Date:** 2026-01-27 | **Status:** COMPLETE

---

## HEADLINE

Overall CMBS special servicing rate has **surpassed the GFC peak** (10.86% vs ~10.67%). Office at **16.64-17.30%** is more than **2x the GFC office peak** (8.1%). Loss severity at **49.7-63%** far exceeds GFC-era ~33%. "Extend and pretend" is dominant ($39.3B cumulative mods) but sentiment shifting toward harder resolutions in 2026.

## 2. RESOLUTION TIMELINES

- **72%** of 2025 conduit maturities paid at/before maturity
- **4%** liquidated
- **24%** remained outstanding past maturity
- **Office specifically:** Only **44%** paid on time; **51%** still outstanding past maturity
- ~59% of all distressed CMBS loans are past maturity date (40.81% non-performing matured + 17.91% performing matured)
- Resolution timelines lengthening due to extend-and-pretend dominance
- Nov 2025: $2.32B transferred in vs $2.37B resolved — roughly balanced flow

- **Peak expected:** H1 2026, with flattening H2 2026
- **Overall rate expected:** 11-12% before declining (UNCONFIRMED exact peak)
- **Office could peak:** 18-20% (trend extrapolation, not published forecast)
- **New issuance growth** ($183B CMBS forecast for 2026) will help dilute overall rate
- **Maturity wall feeds inflow:** $525B maturing 2026, $587B in 2027 — $93B CMBS in 2026
- **Property type risk ranking:** Office >> Retail > Mixed-Use > MF >> Industrial

---

## CREED IMPLICATIONS

1. **Overall CMBS SS has surpassed GFC peak** — this is no longer a "pre-crisis" metric, it IS the crisis
2. **Loss severity far exceeds GFC** — 49.7-63% vs 33.3% means realized losses will be proportionally worse per dollar
3. **Office is 2x the GFC peak** and still rising — structural, permanent repricing
4. **Extend-and-pretend is the dominant strategy but shifting** — 2026 will see more foreclosures
5. **51% of office maturities still outstanding past maturity** = massive unresolved overhang
6. **Servicer capacity is likely strained** at $64.6B volume — could slow resolution pace
7. **Peak in H1 2026** aligns with CREED's "H2 2026 forcing window" thesis
