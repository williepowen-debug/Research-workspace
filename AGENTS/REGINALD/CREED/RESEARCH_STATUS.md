## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY NOTES

### Bank vs CMBS DQ Reconciliation (RQ-CREED-001)
The bifurcation between bank-reported and CMBS-reported DQ is the single most important metric for CREED. Session 001 revealed a potential series discrepancy: FRED DRCRELEXFACBS may show 1.56% (all banks) while FDIC QBP shows 4.18% (large banks >$250B). Reconciliation requires understanding:
1. Bank size cutoffs in each series
2. Property type scope (all CRE vs non-owner-occupied)
3. Delinquency definition (30+ vs 60+ vs 90+)
4. Whether modifications are excluded from both series equally
5. The true masking gap once apples-to-apples comparison is achieved

### Maturity Wall Analysis Framework (RQ-CREED-002)
Track maturity volume → refinancing rate → maturity default rate → loss severity. The key ratio is % failing to refinance. Historical norm: <5%. Current office estimate: ~25%. When this exceeds 20%, narrative shifts from "idiosyncratic" to "systemic."

### Refinancing Gap Framework (RQ-CREED-003)
Total maturity volume ($930B) minus total refinancing capacity (bank + CMBS + CLO + life co + debt fund + agency) = refinancing gap. If gap is >15%, forced recognition occurs at scale. Key variables: bank willingness to originate new CRE, agency MF lending limits, CLO market depth.
