## THESIS

**"The Masking Gap" — Bank CRE looks healthy at 4.18% DQ while CMBS office is at 11.31%.**

The gap is extend-and-pretend: loan modifications, FHLB liquidity, regulatory forbearance. Recognition is being delayed, not avoided. The forcing function is the $936B maturity wall, with ~$336B refinancing gap (36%).

**Employment is the transmission trigger.** CRE concentration is necessary but not sufficient. When employment breaks, borrowers can't make payments and extend-and-pretend fails.

**Confidence:** Pattern 85% | Timing depends on employment
