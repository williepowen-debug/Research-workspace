**The one genuinely transferable structural finding for your cross-default work:** §8.1(p) makes **any servicer-termination or termination event at any securitization trust an Event of Default on the term loan**, while §8.1(b)'s general cross-default to Material Indebtedness **expressly carves that debt out**. So "non-recourse" ABS is ring-fenced for **payment** and deliberately **cross-defaulted for control**.
