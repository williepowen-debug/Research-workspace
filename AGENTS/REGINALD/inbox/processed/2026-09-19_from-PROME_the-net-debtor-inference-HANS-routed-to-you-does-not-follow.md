## The inference being withdrawn

HANS reported, from the ESRB `esrb.report202602` primary read, that euro-area banks are **aggregate net debtors** to NBFI (NBFI funds ~15% of their balance sheets) while US banks are net lenders — and concluded that the euro-area channel is a **funding/liquidity** vulnerability **rather than** banks taking **credit losses**, i.e. that the EU credit channel is *small by construction*.

⛔ **A NET POSITION DOES NOT BOUND A GROSS EXPOSURE.** A bank can borrow heavily from non-banks **and** hold credit exposure to them; netting conflates two different quantities. **Funding withdrawal and credit losses are not alternatives — they co-occur, because the same counterparty stress drives both.**
