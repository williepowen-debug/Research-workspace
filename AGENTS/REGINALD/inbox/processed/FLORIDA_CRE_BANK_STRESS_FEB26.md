## BayFirst National Bank (St. Petersburg)
- Cut **51 jobs (17% of workforce)**
- Shuttered "Bolt" SBA loan platform entirely
- Reason: macro headwinds + unprofitability of small-dollar lending at current rates
- **Signal:** Small bank exiting lending lines = credit contraction in real time

## FPI Management (6th Largest US Property Manager)
- Eliminated 100+ positions across HQ and remote ops
- Tied to merger with Asset Living + pivot to AI-driven property management
- **Signal:** CRE operational layer is consolidating/cutting, not expanding

## Construction Cost Squeeze
- 50% tariffs on steel, aluminum, copper already inflating building material costs
- JP Morgan: tariffs projected to further squeeze multifamily development pipeline
- Immigration policy shifts projected to increase construction wages
- **Signal:** New supply gets more expensive → existing distressed properties can't be replaced cheaply → loss severity on CRE defaults stays high

## Divergence: Big Banks Expanding While Regionals Retrench
- BofA: $29M investment + office reopening in Sarasota
- LPL Financial: cut 300 (3% of workforce), targeting back office for AI replacement
- BayFirst: cutting 17% and exiting business lines
- **Signal:** Bulge bracket is cherry-picking Florida growth markets. Regionals are shrinking. This is the consolidation pattern that precedes regional bank failures.

## Florida WARN Filings (Feb 2026)
- Saks Global (Miramar): 74 layoffs, permanent closure of SE Service Center
- Naples Grande Beach Resort: confirmed upcoming layoffs
- Legoland Florida: 234 employees laid off

## Relevance to Thesis
Florida is a live test case for the CRE → bank → employment chain. BayFirst exiting SBA lending while foreclosures are at #2 nationally = credit is already tightening at the small bank level. The question is whether this stays contained to small players or transmits up to WAL/OZK-sized exposures.
