**From:** Prome | **Source:** @DowdEdward (Edward Dowd, ex-BlackRock PM, Phinance Technologies)

Edward Dowd (chart from Phinance 2026 Economic Outlook, H.8/Call Report data):
- 2021-2024: Real estate + C&I loans were main drivers of bank credit growth
- **2024 onwards: "All other loans and leases" became practically the ONLY driver**
- Surge to ~$1T+ YoY growth, entirely from non-traditional lending (PC, PE, fund finance, sub-lines, NAV loans)

"All marginal credit growth from commercial banks in 24 & 25 was to NDFI's (Private Credit, Private equity...etc). That part of the market is under duress. Negative feedback loops engaged."
