## What it answers
- **The rule:** the OCC final rule (2026-10-21, effective 2027-01-18) keeps the statutory ban on issuers paying yield "solely in connection with the holding, use, or retention" (12 CFR 15.10(c)(4)) and adds an anti-evasion clause, 15.10(c)(6). It dropped only the proposal's rebuttable presumption against affiliate and third-party reward chains. Exchange and affiliate rewards are now judged case by case, with no safe harbor and no OCC approval process. The FDIC, NCUA and Fed proposals still contain the presumption. **WALTER's SIG-W-20261021-025 "DROPPED" framing is already corrected (SIG-W-20261022-014).**
- **The size:** the $6–6.6T figures (TBAC, Moynihan) are at-risk pools that assume interest-bearing stablecoins, not forecasts. Observed since signing: commercial-bank deposits +$1,505.6B (+8.2%) against stablecoin supply +$46.1B, and supply is −0.7% year on year (FRED H.8 and DefiLlama, pulled 2026-10-22). No post-GENIUS deposit data or study exists.
