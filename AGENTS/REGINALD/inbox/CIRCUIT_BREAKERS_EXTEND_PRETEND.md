# Circuit Breakers & Extend-and-Pretend Analysis (2024-2026)
**Source:** Gemini Deep Research, Feb 28 2026

## THE FEEDBACK LOOP
Hot PPI → elevated yields → unrealized losses on bank HTM portfolios → credit contraction (SLOOS) → real economy stress → more defaults → loop repeats

## EXTEND AND PRETEND: QUANTIFIED

### Scale
- CRE loan modifications reached **$27.7B** as of June 2025 — **+66% YoY**
- Median extension length: **exactly 1 year**
- Result: $957B CRE maturity wall in 2025 (20% of all commercial mortgages)
- Breakdown: 35% hotel/motel, 24% office, 22% industrial, 14% multifamily

### Shadow Cost
- Extensions caused **4.8-5.3% drop in new CRE originations** since Q1 2022
- Zombie loans crowd out new lending — market can't clear to new price equilibrium
- Banks choose years of compressed NIM over crystallizing capital losses in one quarter

### The Clock Keeps Resetting
Banks are atomizing an acute solvency crisis into a chronic earnings drag. Each year they extend another year. The system bends but doesn't break — UNLESS a catalyst forces simultaneous recognition.

## PAIN THRESHOLDS (When Fed MUST Intervene)

| Threshold | Trigger Level | Precedent |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Capital insolvency** | Unrealized losses >100% of Tier 1 capital | SVB was at 104% |
| **Equity velocity** | KRX (regional bank index) -20% in 5 trading days | March 2023 |
| **Reserve floor** | Bank reserves below $2.8T | October 2025 SRF injection |
| **Uninsured deposit run** | Flight of institutional/corporate deposits | SVB lost $42B in 1 day |

Current: Unrealized losses fell to $306.1B in Q4 2025 (lowest since Q1 2022). Still substantial but not at 2023 crisis levels.

## SHADOW EASING (The Invisible Circuit Breaker)

### October 2025 SRF Injection
- **$29.4B overnight repo** — largest since dot-com era
- Triggered by reserves hitting $2.8T (down $102B in weeks)
- Fed injected liquidity while maintaining 4.22% rate and continuing QT
- **Translation:** Fed is doing stealth QE while publicly fighting inflation

### Standing Repo Facility = Permanent Backstop
- Guaranteed, stigma-free liquidity against Treasury collateral
- Caps short-term rates regardless of balance sheet constraints
- Allows Fed to patch plumbing without formal rate cuts
- This is why a 2023-style seizure hasn't repeated

## JANUARY 2026 SLOOS: "EASING" IS AN ILLUSION
- Headline: first easing signal since rate hike cycle began (-3.16% net easing on CRE)
- Reality: standards can't get any tighter without killing origination entirely
- Banks STILL expect credit quality deterioration for: small-firm C&I, residential RE, subprime auto, credit cards
- Construction/land development standards expected to TIGHTEN further

## WARSH REGIME CHANGE (May 2026)
- Kevin Warsh nominated to succeed Powell
- Background: Morgan Stanley, Druckenmiller family office
- Stance: "inflation is a choice," wants "strategic reset" at Fed
- **Implication:** Threshold for BTFP 2.0 or emergency cuts goes SIGNIFICANTLY HIGHER
- May tolerate localized bank failures to enforce market discipline
- Less likely to view regional bank equity drops as automatic trigger
- Banks that expect a bailout may not get one → forces preemptive capital raises

## THE BEAR CASE (Why the Loop Might NOT Complete)
1. **Extend-and-pretend works as temporal shock absorber** — atomizes crisis into chronic drag
2. **Private credit fills the gap** — NBFIs capturing market share from retreating banks (not subject to same mark-to-market rules)
3. **SRF decouples liquidity from monetary policy** — Fed can patch plumbing without cutting rates
4. **Warsh changes expectations** — if markets believe Fed Put is lower, banks self-police and raise capital preemptively

## WHAT WOULD BREAK THE LOOP (Our Catalyst Scenarios)
1. **Simultaneous recognition event** — regulatory action forcing mark-to-market across banks (unlikely under current regime)
2. **Uninsured deposit run at a mid-size bank** — would cascade like SVB
3. **Treasury market dislocation** — if SRF capacity is overwhelmed
4. **Private credit seizes** — if NBFIs stop filling the gap, the zombie loans have nowhere to go
5. **Warsh refuses to intervene** — a bank fails and the Fed lets it, creating panic

## APPLICATION FOR REGINALD
1. **The $957B maturity wall is being rolled, not resolved.** Each year adds another year. Track CRE modification data from St. Louis Fed quarterly.
2. **SLOOS "easing" is a ceiling, not a recovery.** Don't read it as bullish.
3. **Watch the $2.8T reserve floor** — if reserves approach this level again, SRF injection incoming
4. **Warsh confirmation is a regime change.** Our thesis timeline (April-June catalyst window) may PRECEDE the new regime, which helps us.
5. **The bear case is strong.** System can metabolize pain slowly through extend-and-pretend + private credit + SRF. Our thesis requires a CATALYST that forces recognition — grinding alone isn't enough.
