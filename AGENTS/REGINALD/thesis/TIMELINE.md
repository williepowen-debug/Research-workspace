## JUNE 2026

### Jun 18 — AOCI CAPITAL REWRITE COMMENT PERIOD CLOSES 🔴
- **Event:** Fed/FDIC/OCC mandatory AOCI phase-in for Cat III/IV banks. $49.5B aggregate hit across 21 banks.
- **Our view:** This is the slow-burning bomb. Comment period close → finalization likely H2 2026 / Q1 2027. Banks that used HTM accounting to hide 2022-23 rate shock losses will be forced to recognize them. This is a SEPARATE capital drain on top of credit losses — hitting the same banks from a different direction.
- **The compound:** A bank already bleeding capital from CRE charge-offs (Channel A) now also loses capital from AOCI recognition. Two simultaneous capital drains with different causes. The market isn't modeling this compound effect.
- **Watch for:** Industry comment letters — who pushes back hardest reveals who's most exposed. If OZK or WAL file aggressive opposition, that's confirmation.

### Q2-Q3 2026 — 2022 VINTAGE HARD MATURITIES PEAK
- **Event:** The forcing function. 2022 vintage CRE loans with 3-year terms + 2 one-year extensions exhaust all options in 2025-2027. The concentration peaks mid-2026.
- **Our view:** This is the calendar-driven inevitability. Banks can extend-and-pretend on individual loans, but they cannot extend the calendar. When a maturity hits and the borrower can't refi (because rates are still high, values are still down, and fraud inflated the original basis), the bank must recognize.
