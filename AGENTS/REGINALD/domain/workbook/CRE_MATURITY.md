## Refinancing Stress Factors

### The Math Problem

Loan originated 2021:
- Rate: 3.5%
- LTV: 65%
- DSCR: 1.35x

Same loan refinancing 2026:
- Rate: 7.0%+ 
- Property value: -25% (office worse)
- New LTV: 87%+ (blown)
- New DSCR: <1.0x (doesn't work)

**Options:**
1. Borrower injects equity (rare)
2. Bank extends and pretends (regulatory pressure)
3. Bank takes loss (capital hit)
4. Workout/modification (time-consuming)

### Extend & Pretend Limits

- 2023-2024: Regulators allowed extensions
- 2024 OCC/FDIC guidance: Tightened standards
- 2025-2026: Banks running out of runway
