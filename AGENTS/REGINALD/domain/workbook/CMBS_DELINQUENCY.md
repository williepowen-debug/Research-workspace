## Connection to Bank Stress

CMBS is the **leading indicator** for bank CRE books:

1. **CMBS is marked-to-market monthly** — stress shows immediately
2. **Bank loans are held at cost** — stress is hidden until default
3. **CMBS office at 12.34%** → Bank office books likely have similar underlying stress
4. **Lag is 6-12 months** — what's delinquent in CMBS today hits bank NCOs in 2-4 quarters

### The Math

- Banks hold $1.77T in CRE
- If bank office exposure is ~25% = $442B
- If bank office DQ matches CMBS (12.34%) = **$55B of stressed office loans**
- At 50% loss severity = **$27B of potential losses**

This doesn't break the system, but it hits capital ratios hard at regionals with concentrated exposure.
