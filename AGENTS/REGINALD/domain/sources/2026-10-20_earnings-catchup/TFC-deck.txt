Non-GAAP financial information

This  presentation  contains  financial  information  and  performance  measures  determined  by  methods  other  than  in  accordance  with  accounting  principles  generally  accepted  in  the  United  States  of  America  ("GAAP").  Truist’s  management  uses  these  non-GAAP 
measures in its analysis of Truist’s performance and the efficiency of its operations. Management believes these non-GAAP measures are useful to investors because they provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations, enhance comparability of results with 
prior periods, and demonstrate the effects of significant items in the current period. Truist believes a meaningful analysis of its financial performance requires an understanding of the factors underlying that performance. These disclosures should not be viewed as a 
substitute  for  financial  measures  determined  in  accordance  with  GAAP,  nor  are  they  necessarily  comparable  to  non-GAAP  performance  measures  that  may  be  presented  by  other  companies.  Below  is  a  listing  of  the  types  of  non-GAAP  measures  used  in  this 
presentation:

Adjusted net income available to common shareholders and adjusted diluted EPS - Adjusted net income available to common shareholders and adjusted diluted EPS are non-GAAP in that these measures exclude selected items, net of tax. Truist’s management uses 
these measures in its analysis of Truist’s performance. Truist’s management believes these measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhance comparability of results with prior periods, as well as demonstrate the effects of significant 
gains and charges. Diluted EPS impact for individual items may not foot to difference between GAAP diluted and adjusted diluted EPS due to rounding.

Taxable-equivalent measures - Taxable-equivalent revenue, taxable-equivalent interest income, taxable-equivalent net interest income, and taxable-equivalent net interest margin include a taxable-equivalent adjustment utilizing the federal income tax rate of 21% 
for certain tax-exempt instruments. Truist’s management uses these measures in its analysis of Truist’s performance. Truist’s management believes these measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhance comparability of results with 
prior periods.

Adjusted revenue - Adjusted revenue includes a taxable-equivalent adjustment utilizing the federal income tax rate of 21% for certain tax-exempt instruments. Adjusted revenue additionally excludes selected items. Truist’s management uses this measure in its 
analysis of Truist’s performance. Truist’s management believes this measure provides a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhances comparability of results with prior periods.

Adjusted  noninterest  income  and  expense  -  Adjusted  noninterest  income  and  expense  are  non-GAAP  measures  that  exclude  selected  items.  Truist’s  management  uses  these  measures  in  its  analysis  of  Truist’s  performance.  Truist’s  management  believes  these 
measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhance comparability of results with prior periods.

Pre-provision net revenue (PPNR) - PPNR is a non-GAAP measure that adjusts net income determined in accordance with GAAP to exclude the impact of the provision for credit losses and provision for income taxes and includes a taxable-equivalent adjustment 
utilizing the federal income tax rate of 21% for certain tax-exempt instruments. Adjusted PPNR is a non-GAAP measure that additionally excludes selected items. Truist’s management believes these measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations 
and enhance comparability of results with prior periods.

Tangible common equity and related measures - Tangible common equity, average tangible common equity, and related measures, including ROTCE, adjusted ROTCE, and TBVPS, are non-GAAP measures that exclude the impact of intangible assets, net of deferred 
taxes, and its related amortization. Adjusted ROTCE is a non-GAAP measure that additionally excludes other selected items. These measures are useful for evaluating the performance of a business consistently, whether acquired or developed internally. Truist’s 
management uses these measures to assess profitability, returns relative to balance sheet risk, and shareholder value.

Adjusted return on average assets - Adjusted ROA is a non-GAAP measure in that it excludes selected items. Truist’s management uses this measure in its analysis of Truist’s performance. Truist’s management believes this measure provides a greater understanding 
of ongoing operations and enhances comparability of results with prior periods, as well as demonstrates the effects of significant gains and charges. 

Net charge-off ratio, excluding RAC - The net charge-off ratio excluding Regional Acceptance Corporation (RAC) is a non-GAAP measure in that it excludes certain loans and the related charge-offs. Truist’s management uses this measure in its analysis of Truist’s 
performance. Truist’s management believes this measure provides a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhances comparability of results. 

Truist does not provide reconciliations for forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures because it is unable to provide a meaningful or accurate calculation or estimation of reconciling items and the information is not available without unreasonable effort. This is 
due to the difficulty of forecasting the occurrence and the financial impact of various items that have not yet occurred, are out of Truist’s control, or cannot be reasonably predicted. For the same reasons, Truist is unable to address the probable significance of the 
unavailable information.

Update on strategic review of loan portfolios

Portfolio actions sharpen strategic focus and enhance risk profile 

Completed RAC loan sale and AFS securities repositioning

Signed agreement to sell LightStream(1)

– Sold $5.5 billion RAC auto loan portfolio for $5.2 billion

– Sale created $990 million or 23 bps of CET1 capital(2) 

– Signed agreement to sell $7.7 billion of loans representing 

substantially all assets of LightStream, a national unsecured 
lending platform, to Carlyle Group Inc.

– Reduces NCOs by ~10 bps annually and NPLs by >10 bps

– Non-core, loan only business generating a below target ROTCE  

– Repaid $5.2 billion of borrowings  

– Transaction expected to:

– Sold $8.6 billion of book value AFS securities with a weighted 

average book yield of 2.67% and a duration of 5.8 years

– Recorded a pre-tax loss of $1.4 billion 

– Purchased $7.2 billion of securities with a weighted average 

yield of 5.60%(3) and a duration of 2.3 years

– Transactions modestly accretive to earnings, ROTCE, and 

tangible book value per share

– Generate $7.2 billion of net proceeds

– Create ~$850 million or 20 bps of CET1 capital(4)

– Reduce NCOs by ~7 bps annually 

– Closing anticipated in 4Q26, subject to satisfaction of 

customary closing conditions

– Plan to utilize proceeds to repay wholesale borrowings

– Will evaluate a potential AFS securities repositioning to offset 

capital created from sale 

– Broader strategic review ongoing

        after-tax RAC loan sale impacts

(4)  ~$850 million estimated capital increase driven by a $70 million after-tax loan loss reserve recapture and $820 million of capital created by a reduction in risk-weighted assets partially offset by 
        $40 million of after-tax impacts
