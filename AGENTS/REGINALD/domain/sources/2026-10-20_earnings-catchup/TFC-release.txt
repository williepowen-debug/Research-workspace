AFS securities repositioning

of unsecured consumer loans representing substantially all 
assets of LightStream, our unsecured lending platform, to 
Carlyle Group Inc. 

• Adjusted net income available to common shareholders(1) of 
$1.4 billion, resulting in adjusted diluted EPS(1) of $1.16 per 
share and adjusted ROTCE(1) of 14.6%, which excludes:
◦ $1.4 billion pre-tax, or $0.90 per share, loss on the sale of 

AFS securities

◦ $581 million pre-tax, or $0.36 per share, loan loss reserve 
recapture and $106 million pre-tax, or $0.06 per share, of 
impacts associated with the RAC loan sale

up two basis points

◦ Noninterest income was impacted by losses on the RAC

offset by record quarterly income for wealth management

costs, was stable

• End of period loans and leases HFI were $323.9 billion, down

◦ NCO ratio of 77 basis points, which includes 39 basis points

CEO Commentary

“Since  joining  Truist,  I’ve  become  even  more  confident  in  the  strength  of  our  franchise  and  the  significant  opportunities  ahead.  We 
delivered  a  solid  quarter  while  also  taking  decisive  actions  to  better  position  Truist  for  the  future.  These  actions  strengthen  our  risk, 
liquidity, and funding profile and create greater capacity to invest in our best businesses. We’re simplifying the company and focusing 
our resources, capital, and expenses on the businesses and capabilities that will help us become the most important financial partner to 
our clients. Looking ahead, we remain confident in our ability to deliver against our near- and long-term ROTCE targets and will work to 
build a more focused company positioned to deliver sustainable growth and stronger returns for shareholders.”

— Mike Lyons, Truist President & CEO

Taxable-equivalent net interest income was up $40 million, or 1.1%, compared to the second quarter of 2026, driven 
by  an  additional  day  and  fixed  asset  and  liability  repricing,  partially  offset  by  lower  loan  balances.  NIM  -  TE  was

Average earning assets decreased $1.0 billion, or 0.2%, primarily due to a decrease in average total loans 
of $2.3 billion, or 0.7%, partially offset by an increase in other earning assets of $1.2 billion, or 3.4%.
The  yield  on  the  average  total  loan  portfolio  was  5.71%,  up  three  basis  points. The  yield  on  the  average 
securities portfolio was 3.03%, up seven basis points.
Average deposits increased $5.8 billion, or 1.4%, average short-term borrowings decreased $10.3 billion, or 
36%, and average long-term debt increased $4.5 billion, or 11%.
The  average  cost  of  total  deposits  was  1.59%,  up  three  basis  points.  The  average  cost  of  short-term 
borrowings was 3.96%, down one basis point. The average cost of long-term debt was 4.73%, down four 
basis points.

Taxable-equivalent net interest income was up $27 million, or 0.7%, compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily 
driven by fixed asset and liability repricing, higher client deposit balances, and higher loan balances, partially offset 
by lower loan spreads and unfavorable deposit mix. NIM - TE was 3.00%, stable compared to the third quarter of 
2025.

About Truist

Truist  Financial  Corporation  is  a  purpose-driven  financial  services  company  committed  to  inspiring  and  building 
better lives and communities. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, Truist has leading market share in many 
of  the  high-growth  markets  in  the  U.S.  and  offers  a  wide  range  of  products  and  services  through  wholesale  and 
consumer  businesses,  including  consumer  and  small  business  banking,  commercial  and  corporate  banking, 
investment banking and capital markets, wealth management, payments, and specialized lending businesses. Truist 
is a top-10 commercial bank with total assets of $554 billion as of September 30, 2026. Truist Bank, Member FDIC. 
Equal Housing Lender. Learn more at Truist.com.

enhance comparability of results with prior periods, and demonstrate the effects of significant items in the current period. Truist 
believes a meaningful analysis of its financial performance requires an understanding of the factors underlying that performance. 
These  disclosures  should  not  be  viewed  as  a  substitute  for  financial  measures  determined  in  accordance  with  GAAP,  nor  are 
they necessarily comparable to non-GAAP performance measures that may be presented by other companies. Below is a listing 
of the types of non-GAAP measures used in this news release:

Adjusted  net  income  available  to  common  shareholders  and  adjusted  diluted  EPS  -  Adjusted  net  income  available  to 
common shareholders and adjusted diluted EPS are non-GAAP in that these measures exclude selected items, net of tax. 
Truist’s  management  uses  these  measures  in  its  analysis  of  Truist’s  performance.  Truist’s  management  believes  these 
measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhance comparability of results with prior periods, as

Taxable-Equivalent  Measures  -  Taxable-equivalent  revenue,  taxable-equivalent  interest  income,  taxable-equivalent  net 
interest  income,  and  taxable-equivalent  net  interest  margin  include  a  taxable-equivalent  adjustment  utilizing  the  federal 
income  tax  rate  of  21%  for  certain  tax-exempt  instruments.  Truist’s  management  uses  these  measures  in  its  analysis  of 
Truist’s performance. Truist’s management believes these measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations 
and enhance comparability of results with prior periods.
Adjusted revenue - Adjusted revenue includes a taxable-equivalent adjustment utilizing the federal income tax rate of 21% 
for certain tax-exempt instruments. Adjusted revenue additionally excludes selected items. Truist’s management uses this 
measure  in  its  analysis  of  Truist’s  performance.  Truist’s  management  believes  this  measure  provides  a  greater 
understanding of ongoing operations and enhances comparability of results with prior periods.
Adjusted  noninterest  income  and  expense  -  Adjusted  noninterest  income  and  expense  are  non-GAAP  measures  that 
exclude  selected  items.  Truist’s  management  uses  these  measures  in  its  analysis  of  Truist’s  performance.  Truist’s 
management believes these measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhance comparability of 
results with prior periods.
PPNR - PPNR is a non-GAAP measure that adjusts net income determined in accordance with GAAP to exclude the impact 
of the provision for credit losses and provision for income taxes and includes a taxable-equivalent adjustment utilizing the 
federal income tax rate of 21% for certain tax-exempt instruments. Adjusted PPNR is a non-GAAP measure that additionally 
excludes  selected  items.  Truist’s  management  believes  these  measures  provide  a  greater  understanding  of  ongoing 
operations and enhance comparability of results with prior periods.
Tangible Common Equity and Related Measures - Tangible common equity, average tangible common equity, and related 
measures, including ROTCE, adjusted ROTCE, and TBVPS, are non-GAAP measures that exclude the impact of intangible 
assets,  net  of  deferred  taxes,  and  its  related  amortization.  Adjusted  ROTCE  is  a  non-GAAP  measure  that  additionally 
excludes  other  selected  items.  These  measures  are  useful  for  evaluating  the  performance  of  a  business  consistently, 
whether acquired or developed internally. Truist’s management uses these measures to assess profitability, returns relative 
to balance sheet risk, and shareholder value.
