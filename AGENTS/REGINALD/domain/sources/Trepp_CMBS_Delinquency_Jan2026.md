## Key Takeaways for REGINALD

1. **Office stress is accelerating** — 12.34% is a new record, up 211 bps YoY
2. **Multifamily is the emerging problem** — +232 bps YoY, spreading beyond office
3. **Matured balloons (4.07%) show extend-and-pretend scale** — this is the shadow stress
4. **Net $1.6B new delinquencies in one month** — flow is still negative
5. **Banks hold 36% of CRE but CMBS shows the stress first** — leading indicator for bank books
