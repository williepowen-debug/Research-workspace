Earnings Call: Q3 2026

Oct 20, 2026

  • Slides

Summary

Q3 earnings and returns improved as credit quality strengthened, deposit costs fell and repurchases accelerated. FY26 deposit and loan growth guidance was lowered, while NII and fee-income growth outlooks were revised to 11%-12% and 18%-21%.
