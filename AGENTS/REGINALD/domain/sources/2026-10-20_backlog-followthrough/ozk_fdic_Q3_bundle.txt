Bank OZK Announces 
Third Quarter 2026 Earnings 

LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS:  Bank OZK (the “Bank”) (Nasdaq: OZK) today announced that net income 

available to common stockholders for the third quarter of 2026 was $164.3 million, a decrease of 9.0% from 

$180.5 million for the third quarter of 2025, but an increase of 0.6% from $163.3 million for the second 

quarter of 2026. For the first nine months of 2026, net income available to common stockholders was $487.0 

million, a 7.7% decrease from $527.4 million for the first nine months of 2025. Diluted earnings per common 

share (“EPS”) for the third quarter of 2026 were $1.50, a decrease of 5.7% from $1.59 for the third quarter of 

2025, but an increase of 0.7% from $1.49 for the second quarter of 2026. EPS for the first nine months of 2026 

were $4.43, a 4.5% decrease from $4.64 for the first nine months of 2025.

George Gleason, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “We are pleased to report our solid 

financial results for the quarter including net income available to common stockholders of $164.3 million; EPS 

of $1.50; an annualized return on average assets of 1.55%; a net interest margin of 4.24%; an efficiency ratio 

of 39.9%; and record levels of total assets, loans and deposits. We continued to make significant progress on 

numerous strategic initiatives.  We believe we are well-positioned for the future."

MANAGEMENT COMMENTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2026 

We are pleased to provide our third quarter 2026 Management Comments.   

During the quarter just ended, we delivered solid financial results including net income available to common 

stockholders of $164.3 million; diluted earnings per common share of $1.50; an annualized return on average 

assets of 1.55%; net interest income of $403.0 million; a net interest margin of 4.24%; and an efficiency ratio of 

39.9%.  We also achieved record total assets of $43.2 billion; record loans of $34.1 billion, with continued 

meaningful portfolio diversification; and record deposits of $34.8 billion. 

In recent years we have made significant investments and taken important steps to achieve our long-term strategic 

objectives.  Substantial progress is already evident, and we expect much more in 2027.  We remain focused on 

enhancing long-term shareholder value through: 

•  Diversifying our loan portfolio as recently reflected by significant growth from Corporate & Institutional 

Banking (“CIB”) and other lending teams offsetting lower Real Estate Specialties Group (“RESG”) 

outstanding balances.  We believe that continuing to grow our loan portfolio while materially reducing 

loan portfolio concentrations is essential to enhancing long-term shareholder value.  

• 

Improving our deposit mix by increasing the volume of non-interest bearing deposits and low-cost 

deposits, growing our valuable Sun Belt deposit franchise and emphasizing deposit growth through our 

expanding CIB and Community Banking relationships.  

•  Growing non-interest income by capitalizing on the numerous CIB revenue opportunities and expanding 

revenue through our trust and wealth, treasury management and mortgage lending services.  

• 

Increasing return on equity and capital efficiency through various actions including, among others, 

enhancing our investment securities portfolio mix, loan portfolio mix and utilization rate on loans.  All of 

these actions should provide more flexibility to efficiently deploy capital while maintaining appropriate 

capital levels.     

We look forward to sharing some of the details of our progress with these strategic objectives and many other 

accomplishments from the quarter just ended in the following pages.

Net interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $403.0 million, a decrease of 2.6% from the record third 

quarter of 2025, but an increase of 2.8% from the second quarter of 2026.  Net interest income for the first nine 

months of 2026 was $1.18 billion, a 0.4% decrease from $1.19 billion for the first nine months of 2025. 

As expected, the level of RESG loan repayments has remained elevated in recent quarters (i.e., $9.16 billion over 

the last four quarters).  At the same time, CIB and our other lending teams have generated strong growth.  These 

offsetting factors have restrained growth in average earning assets and net interest income.  Specifically, our 

average earning assets in the first nine months of 2026 were up only slightly to $37.9 billion from $37.1 billion for 

the full year of 2025 and were essentially unchanged compared to $37.9 billion for the fourth quarter of 2025.

Period-End Loans

Loan Yield

In the third quarter of 2026, our yield on loans was 7.21%, a decrease of 53 basis points (“bps”) from the third 

quarter of 2025, but an increase of 4 bps from the second quarter of 2026.  As reflected in Figure 2, the decrease in 

loan yields from 3Q 2024 through 2Q 2026 reflects the impact of the 100-bps reduction in the Fed funds rate 

during the last four months of 2024 and the 75-bps reduction during the last four months of 2025, among other 

factors.  The increase in loan yields in the quarter just ended reflects the initial impact of the recent 25-bps 

increase in the Fed funds rate, among other factors.  That Fed funds rate increase should result in a similar 

increase in loan yields in the fourth quarter of 2026.  Barring further changes in the Fed funds target rate, we 

expect loan yields to be relatively unchanged in 2027 (compared to full-year 2026).

Investment Securities 

Investment securities, all of which are categorized as available-for-sale, were $4.36 billion at September 30, 2026, 

increases of $0.12 billion from June 30, 2026 and $1.75 billion from December 31, 2025, as shown in Figure 3.  Our 

investment securities purchases this year, including purchases with previously uninvested cash, have resulted in 

investment portfolio growth and, along with the roll-off of lower yielding securities, contributed to our improved 

portfolio yield.  In the third quarter of 2026, our tax-equivalent yield (“TEY”) on investment securities was 4.88%, 

increases of 63 bps and 16 bps, respectively, from the third quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026. 

Our investment portfolio generates meaningful principal cash flow.  Through year end 2027 we expect principal 

repayments of approximately $100 million per quarter with an average TEY of approximately 3.50%.  The 

continued roll-off of these lower yielding securities, along with reinvestment at or near current rates, should 

contribute to further improvement in our portfolio yield.  For example, we expect our TEY on investment securities 

to increase further in the fourth quarter of 2026 to somewhere in the range of 5.20% to 5.30%.
