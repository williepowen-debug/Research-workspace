FDIC explicitly flags non-owner CRE and multifamily PDNA "well above pre-pandemic averages." Total PDNA 1.56%, up 7bps QoQ.

## Reserve Coverage Compression
- **Industry: 171.2%** — declining. Noncurrent ↑, allowance ↓
- **Community banks: 154.3%** — -2.9pts QoQ. WAL/OZK peer universe.
- Noncurrent loans: +$4.1B QoQ / +$4.6B YoY
- Allowance: -$2.1B QoQ / -$0.5B YoY
- Net charge-offs: 0.63% — +15bps above pre-pandemic avg
- **OZK's 41% reserve cut is the aggressive version of a system-wide trend.**
- FDIC cites pending loan portfolio sale as "one-time" — but doesn't explain noncurrent rise.

## Funding Stress
- FHLB advances: $419.4B (-13.7% YoY from $485.9B)
- Surface: less reliant on emergency funding. Bear read: preemptive deleveraging ahead of stress.
- Community banks: $96.5B in FHLB advances with PDNA rising.
- **Uninsured deposits: +7.0% YoY to $8.14T** — fastest-flight-risk category growing fastest.

## Key Stats
- Industry ROA: 1.20% (FY 2025)
- Industry NIM: 3.39% (Q4, highest since 2019)
- Community bank NIM: 3.77% (highest since 2018)
- Unrealized losses: $306.1B (down from peak but still elevated)
- DIF reserve ratio: 1.42%
- 4,336 FDIC-insured institutions (down 43 in Q4, all mergers)

## REGINALD Implications
1. Reserve coverage declining industry-wide validates OZK thesis — they're just more aggressive
2. Community bank coverage at 154.3% vs OZK cutting reserves = outlier behavior on an already-declining trend
3. Nondepository financial lending +24.5% = banks increasing private credit exposure at worst possible time
4. Problem bank list rising with zero failures = extend-and-pretend industry-wide
5. Uninsured deposits $8.14T = SVB-style run risk if stress event triggers flight at concentrated CRE lender
