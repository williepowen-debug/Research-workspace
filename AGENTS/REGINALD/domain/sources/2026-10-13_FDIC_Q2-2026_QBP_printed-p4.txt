Unrealized losses on securities totaled $326.7 billion, up $1.6 billion (0.5 
percent) from the prior quarter but down $68.6 billion (17.4 percent) 
from the year-ago quarter. Unrealized losses on securities were 5.5 
percent of amortized cost.2 Unrealized losses on available-for-sale 
securities were 2.9 percent of amortized cost. Unrealized losses on 
available-for-sale securities were $109.8 billion, down $780 million (0.7 
percent) from the prior quarter and down $33.9 billion (23.6 percent) 
from the year-ago quarter. Unrealized losses on held-to-maturity 
securities were 10.5 percent of amortized cost. Unrealized losses on 
held-to-maturity securities were $216.9 billion, up $2.4 billion (1.1 
percent) from the prior quarter but down $34.7 billion (13.8 percent) 
from the year-ago quarter. 

The banking industry reported total assets of $26.5 trillion in the second 
quarter, an increase of $316.8 billion (1.2 percent) from the prior quarter and 
$1.5 trillion (5.9 percent) from the year-ago quarter. The quarterly increase 
was driven by growth in total loans (up $243.5 billion or 1.8 percent), higher 
assets in trading accounts (up $43.0 billion or 3.2 percent), federal funds 
sold and reverse repos (up $20.1 billion or 2.5 percent), and securities (up 
$19.4 billion or 0.3 percent). On an annual basis, growth in total loans 
and leases (up $885.5 billion or 6.8 percent), assets in trading accounts 
(up $252.2 billion or 21.9 percent), and securities (up $138.0 billion or 2.4 
percent) drove the increase.
