# Trepp: Inside the $4.9T CRE Debt Universe
**Source:** Trepp Research | **Date:** February 2026 | **Data as of:** Q3 2025 (Sep 30)

---

## Summary

After two years of elevated rates and selective credit formation, the CRE debt market entered Q3 with a largely steady overall profile. Total outstanding CRE debt stands at **$4.9 trillion**.

Key finding: Institutional lenders (GSEs, securitized, insurance) continued to deploy capital while banks remained cautious but steady.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Banks are the backbone** — $1.77T (36.3%) but growing only +1.7% YoY (cautious)

2. **Institutional lenders filling the gap** — GSEs (+7.3%), Securitized (+7.3%), Insurance (+5.6%)

3. **Near-term maturity concentration:**
   - Banks: $488B maturing through 2026 (28% of book)
   - Securitized: $286B through 2026 (38% of total)

4. **Refinancing challenge:** Loans from low-rate periods face higher projected payments at maturity

5. **Long-dated tail:** $1.9T in 2030+ category, primarily GSE and insurance (structural, not near-term stress)

6. **Bank median tenor:** 3 years (per T-ALLR consortium) — continual refinancing pressure
