# FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile — Q4 2025

**Source:** https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2026/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-fourth-quarter-2025  
**Released:** February 24, 2026  
**Analyzed:** February 25, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

The FDIC Q4 2025 report shows **strong headline numbers** (profits up 10.2%, NIM highest since 2019) masking **persistent CRE stress concentrated in regional banks**. Large banks (>$250B) are healing; smaller banks carry higher CRE concentrations and more risk.

**Key finding for thesis:** FDIC explicitly states regional banks have **higher CRE concentrations** relative to assets and capital than large banks.

## CRE Stress — The Real Story

### Non-Owner-Occupied CRE PDNA (Past Due & Non-Accrual)

| Bank Size | PDNA Rate | vs Pre-Pandemic (0.58%) | Trend |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|-------|
| **Large Banks (>$250B)** | 4.06% | **7x elevated** | ↓ Declining (5th consecutive quarter) |
| **Smaller Banks** | Higher concentration | **More exposed** | FDIC flagged explicitly |

**FDIC Quote (Chart 11):**
> "The non-owner-occupied CRE PDNA rate for banks with assets greater than $250 billion declined for the fifth consecutive quarter to 4.06 percent, below the recent peak of 4.99 percent in third quarter 2024 but **well above the pre-pandemic average rate of 0.58 percent**. However, these large banks have **lower concentrations** of such loans in relation to total assets and capital **than smaller banks**, mitigating the overall risk."

**Translation:** 
- Big banks = improving, lower concentration
- Regional banks = higher concentration, MORE risk

### CRE Volume Still Growing
- Industry PDNA non-owner-occupied CRE loans: **+$269M (+1.2% QoQ)**
- Not shrinking — still accumulating

## Implications for Thesis

### What FDIC Says vs What It Means

| FDIC Says | What It Means |
|-----------|---------------|
| "Strong earnings" | NIM expansion, not credit improvement |
| "Asset quality generally favorable" | Except CRE, auto, cards — the stress sectors |
| "Large banks improving on CRE" | They're offloading/healing |
| "Smaller banks have higher concentrations" | **Regional banks carrying the bag** |
| "Problem banks in normal range" | 60 banks, rising, before any catalyst |

### For KRE / Regional Bank Thesis

1. **Big banks healing** → XLF may hold up
2. **Regional banks MORE exposed** → KRE vulnerable
3. **CRE PDNA 7x pre-pandemic** → Extend-and-pretend, not resolution
4. **Problem banks rising** → Early warning
5. **Reserves decreasing** → Less cushion for losses

### For Specific Names (WAL, OZK)

Banks with **hidden CRE** (Memo Item 3 exposure) face double risk:
- Headline CRE already elevated
- Hidden CRE in C&I adds to true exposure
- When marks hit, capital impact amplified

## FDIC Conclusion (Verbatim)

> "In conclusion, the banking industry had a strong quarter and a strong 2025. The industry saw robust loan growth, wider NIMs, and strong earnings growth during the year. Strong capital and liquidity levels continue to support lending and protect against potential losses. **However, the industry still faces weakness in certain loan portfolios and elevated unrealized losses. These issues will remain matters of ongoing supervisory attention by the FDIC.**"

Translation: We're watching. You should too.
