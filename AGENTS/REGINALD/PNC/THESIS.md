## Why We Track PNC

PNC ($560B assets) is a super-regional with three structural attributes that make it a useful pattern read for the convergence thesis:

1. **Diversified NDFI book ($73B, 20% of loans)** — largest and most granularly disclosed of Q1 2026 reporters. Validates "NDFI is thematic, not sectoral" — $31B sits outside Table 16 "Financial services" bucket.
2. **Disclosure sophistication** — deck Slide 11 gives 5-way NDFI split. Sets the investor-facing benchmark for how granular disclosure should look. Gap between 10-K (least granular) and deck (most granular) is widest at PNC — useful for measuring peer opacity.
3. **Scale cushion** — CET1 10.1% reported, but AOCI = −$3.8B. Unlike Cat IV regionals, PNC is Cat III and will reflect AOCI in regulatory capital under current reform direction. Provides a real-time read on how AOCI reform reshapes super-regional capital.

## What PNC Is Not

- Not a convergence concentration — diversified C&I, national franchise, institutional investor base.
- Not a CRE-concentrated regional (CRE ~6% of loans vs WAL 20%+, OZK 70%+).
- Not an insider/governance story.
- Not a short candidate in our book. If anything, a relative-safety name within the sector if convergence breaks.
