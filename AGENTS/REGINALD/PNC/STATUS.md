### 4C. The Hidden Convergence (Key Insight)

- PNC 10-K Table 16 "Financial services" C&I sub-line at 1Q26 = **$42.2B**
- PNC deck NDFI total at 3/31/26 = **$73B**
- **Delta = $31B of NDFI sitting OUTSIDE "Financial services" in other C&I sub-buckets**

That $31B is likely spread across:
- "Real estate related" ($14.9B YE2025 — likely includes REIT lending, multifamily warehouse)
- "Other industries" ($29.1B YE2025 — likely includes trade receivable securitizations for non-financial corporates)
- Possibly "Service providers" ($24.2B YE2025 — fund admin / structuring services)

**NDFI is thematic, not sectoral.** PNC is the clearest evidence yet that a bank's NDFI exposure can only be measured by a cross-bucket rollup. Reading Table 16 by itself would MISS 42% of PNC's NDFI exposure.

This directly supports v1.4 "C&I as Convergence Hiding Place" thesis: the convergence isn't eight channels hitting the same bank — it's eight channels hiding in multiple buckets that don't ladder up to a single disclosable number.

## Implications for WAL/OZK (Apr 21)

1. **NDFI transparency template now exists.** PNC's $73B 5-way split is the benchmark investors will likely use to read WAL/OZK disclosure. If WAL/OZK provide less granular detail, that itself is a signal.
2. **FHLB surge = 3/3 confirmed.** WAL/OZK supplements must be checked. OZK's FHLB trajectory already watched — expect another spike.
3. **Reserve direction matters.** PNC building, CFG releasing, MTB building. If WAL/OZK RELEASE reserves into rising CRE nonaccruals, that's the CFG pattern (most bearish) — "managing reported metrics rather than building cushion."
4. **Reclassification is a universal pattern.** Every bank re-segments Table 16 buckets. Watch WAL's MI3/C&I breakdown for any new carve-outs — especially around NDFI-adjacent buckets.
5. **Thematic > sectoral for NDFI.** A bank's disclosed "Financial services" or "Finance and Insurance" line UNDERSTATES NDFI by up to 40-50% (PNC evidence). Real NDFI is thematic and spread across multiple C&I sub-buckets.
6. **PNC's 20% NDFI / loans is the new high-water-mark** for disclosure. CFG's 13% is bounded by their deck carve-out; actual NDFI could be higher if measured PNC-style.

## Net View — Does This Change Anything?

**On PNC specifically:** PNC is not a position name and remains outside the convergence matrix. Q1 was mostly noise from FirstBank. The $73B NDFI book is material but ~90% investment-grade and well-diversified. PNC's CET1 of 10.1% (vs CFG 10.5% / 9.3% AOCI-adjusted) is THINNER than CFG under AOCI reform — a new data point, not a thesis shift.

**On the sector thesis:** Modestly strengthened.
- 3/3 reporters showing FHLB surge = systemic, not idiosyncratic
- NDFI thematic hiding confirmed across different bank sizes and disclosure styles
- Reclassification pattern universal (PNC split Retail/wholesale, CFG carved out Secured PC)
- Reserve-release divergence (CFG) from reserve-build (MTB, PNC) isolates CFG as the most aggressive "managed metrics" reporter

**Method takeaway:** When reading WAL/OZK, measure NDFI by thematic rollup (deck) not industry-sector line (10-K). The gap between those two measures is the signal — PNC gap = $31B / 42%.
