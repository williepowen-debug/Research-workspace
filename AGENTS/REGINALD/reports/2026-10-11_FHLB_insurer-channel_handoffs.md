## Two protections must stay separate

The assembled FHFA [AB 2013-09](https://www.fhfa.gov/advisory-bulletin/ab-2013-09) read says the insurer’s domiciliary-state rehabilitation/liquidation law controls and federal §10(e) priority for insurer collateral is judicially untested. Indianapolis’ FY25 filing warns that federal priority may not fully apply; its cited protections are state statutory protections. Delivery/perfection and collateral control matter. The Iowa seven-day stay example is jurisdiction-specific, not an automatic assumption for DLIC or every insurer. Generic federal-lien language at another district does not settle an insurer resolution.

**Inference, limited:** a protected collateral lender can recover while loss remains elsewhere in an insurer estate. Policyholder shortfall, guaranty-fund coverage/assessments, unsecured-creditor loss and any bank facility’s recovery need their own insurer, contract and jurisdiction evidence. The assembled files do not compute that waterfall, guaranty limits, loss magnitude or tax-credit pass-through; no automatic system cascade follows. We preserve the difference without assigning an invented loss distribution.
