## What loss makes the valuation unattractive?

There is no honest bank-specific dollar answer until the sustainable earnings/multiple assumption is stated. The following **conditional** reverse calculation makes that dependence visible; it does not infer investor beliefs from price/TBV.

`scenario value = assumed multiple × [June TBV − additional expense × (1 − tax rate) / ending shares]`.

For EGBN, using the earlier **October13 ~19:07 ET quote28.19**, June TBV37.73 and25% tax benefit:

| Assumed sustainable price/TBV after recognition | Additional pre-tax expense at which scenario value equals28.19 |
|---|---:|
| 0.75× | **5.8 million** |
| 1.00× | **387.8 million** |
| 1.25× | **617.0 million** |

The enormous difference is the finding. A200 expense leaves scenario TBV≈32.81; at0.75× it is≈24.61, while at1.00× it is≈32.81. The same credit loss supports opposite valuation comparisons when sustainable profitability implies a different multiple. Neither multiple is endorsed or described as consensus.

A simple steady-state relationship, under clean-surplus and constant growth assumptions, is `price/TBV ≈ (sustainable ROTE − growth)/(cost of equity − growth)`. Growth must be below cost of equity; observed current ROTE and management goals are not sustainable forecasts. For example, assuming10% cost of equity and2% growth, EGBN's mixed-date0.747× ratio corresponds to sustainable ROTE≈7.98%. That is **conditional inference**, not proof the market expects7.98% or a particular loss. Changing the required return changes the inference.

Therefore the next valuation evidence is: a dated estimate of sustainable earnings after cleanup, provisions already embedded in that estimate, a defensible required return/multiple, and current price. Cash exits can reduce required risk compensation while the loss shrinks book, allowing price to rise despite recognition. Persistent retained-risk costs can do the opposite. Current four-bank consensus remains a gap; the September28 WAL snapshot is historical. No new mispricing claim or TERRY commission is made.
