## 3. Where I differ

The notes are a simpler test: they see money only if recoveries exceed about $2.96bn (June deposits + AFD), and its high case is $2.98bn. **The 0–5 cents holds unless assets are far better than reported.** Base arithmetic reproduces.
2. **The 86 "post-failure" mark is suspect.** It is dated the morning the resolution was published, so treat it as stale. No real post-failure price exists here.
3. **Peña impeachment vote conflicts.** The report says Diputados approved it; the ABC live blog at 14:08 local showed a recess and no vote. Open. Congress had not accepted the resignation in anything I read at 13:06 ET; it may by tonight.
4. **Citi and BNY are not an exposure.** A correspondent normally holds the foreign bank's money, so the risk tends to run the other way (my inference). No amounts, no terminations found.
5. **The related-party figures are prosecutors' allegations through press.** Mowi, Hendy, Grupo M, Vinanzas and the $222M are not findings. The G1.62tn and 5.56% are secondary, and consistent with "below 50% of 12%".
6. **The contagion names are the thin part.** Sudameris, GNB, Continental and Zeta are mostly secondary, and no bank has published outflows. LIQUID's to grade.
