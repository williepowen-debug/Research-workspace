### §5 — Curley new-CEO reserve overlay → **★ a FOURTH pattern my frame did not enumerate**
The frame offered three cells: (build + improving credit) = reset · (build + deteriorating credit) = deterioration · (no build + deteriorating) = under-provisioning.

**What actually happened is none of them cleanly:** provision ROSE $8.1M, credit metrics IMPROVED (NPL −13.7%, NPA 1.31%→1.17%, SM −5.7%), **and coverage still THINNED** — because the improvement *is* the realized loss. The reserve is being consumed by charge-offs on assets leaving the balance sheet, not under-provisioned against assets staying on it.

**Nor is it a kitchen-sink.** Curley explicitly endorses the predecessor's work rather than resetting: *"it is clear that the organization has made significant progress in strengthening its balance sheet, reducing risk, and positioning the Company for the future"* — and his stated focus is *"listening to our clients, employees, and shareholders,"* i.e. no strategy reset announced. Newell frames the quarter as *"execution of the Bank's problem asset resolution strategy."*

> **The discriminator my frame was missing: coverage thinning has two causes with opposite meanings** — ACL not keeping pace with *incoming* problem credits (bear, under-provisioning) vs ACL consumed by *outgoing* realized losses (de-risking). Q1 looked like the first. Q2 is unambiguously the second. **§5 verdict: ACCELERATED DISPOSITION — neutral, mildly constructive.**
