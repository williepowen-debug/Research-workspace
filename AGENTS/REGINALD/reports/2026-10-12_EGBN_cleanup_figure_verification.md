## What the cleanup actually did

The largest correction is interpretation. Q2 deck s24 attributes the **17.637** reduction in nonaccrual HFI to **14.658 paydowns +24.982 charge-offs +14.035 transfers to HFS**, offset by36.038 inflows; return-to-accruing is zero on this bridge. Recognition and portfolio disposal are real; a transfer moves a loan outside the headline HFI metric before a sale. Held-for-sale treatment does not eliminate every remaining lender risk.

A second composition caution: the multifamily increase is not a clean count of newly troubled borrowers. Q1 deck construction-by-geography shows approximately56M Prince George's multifamily inside **construction**; Q2 removes it there and includes it in IPCRE multifamily. Its grade/maturity remain separate concerns. The July22 release also reports a35.4M loan paid off after June30. Thus the quarter-end MF level and +62% change deserve scrutiny, not an assumption that every added dollar represents a newly impaired loan. No new migration decomposition/model is performed here.

**Additional source-file wording corrected:** the September dossier said nonaccrual outflows exceeded inflows in all four recent quarters. That fails at Q1-2026: the issuer records nonaccrual increasing106.897→128.761. The dossier now distinguishes Q2 resolution from that prior-quarter exception. This does not negate the year-on-year reduction.

**Judgment after recheck:** Will's recollection of substantial EGBN cleanup is supported. Office reduction, realized charges and completed HFS sales are affirmative evidence. The residual MF/maturity question survives, with composition and already-recognized-loss qualifications. No proof that EGBN was best in the cohort, every remaining office asset is resolved, or the shares are mispriced. L694 candidate remains conditional and awaiting existing issuer evidence; no numerical re-mark.
