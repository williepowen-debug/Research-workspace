## 6. What the registered alarm would say

`REG-T-06` (registry/THRESHOLDS.tsv): `FHLB-ADVANCES > 700` for 3 consecutive quarterly prints ⇒ **`EARLY-CRISIS`**; exit `≤ 650` on 2 consecutive prints. **A Q3 print over $700B fires it (~late Oct / early Nov).**

**Bold = >$700B.**
- **Backtest of the letter:** fires at Q2-23, after Q4-22 / Q1-23 / Q2-23. It **never exits**, because every quarter from Q3-22 on has been above $650B (the low is $655.0B [9/30/22]; since then $676.7B [12/31/25]). On its own letter it would have read EARLY-CRISIS from Q2-23 to today, including the whole of 2024.
- **It did catch the 2023 episode,** but the 3-quarter sustain means it would have confirmed only with the June-2023 figure, published about 4–5 months after SVB failed (3/10/23). It cannot separate that episode from the 2024 plateau.
- **How the line got here:** the threshold was set while this row read ~$480B from the wrong publisher (Fed H.8) for 190 days (STATUS FHLB row). **The $700B line was never base-rated against the real series.**
