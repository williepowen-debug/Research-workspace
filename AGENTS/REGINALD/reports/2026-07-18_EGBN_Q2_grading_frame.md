## 5. ★ NEW-CEO RESERVE-PHILOSOPHY OVERLAY (the Curley discriminator — pre-registered)
Curley's **first call as CEO** (planned succession, NOT distress). New CEOs frequently **"kitchen-sink"** — front-load a reserve build / recognize problem credits early to reset the baseline. This can look identical to deterioration. **Pre-committed discriminator (do NOT conflate on the day):**
| Pattern | Read |
|---|---|
| Reserve build **+ management frames it** as "refreshing assumptions / new-leadership review / conservatism" **AND** underlying nonaccrual + criticized are **flat-to-down** | **NEW-CEO RESET — neutral-to-bull.** Do NOT over-read as credit deterioration. Coverage rebuild (§1) with stable/improving credit = clean slate. |
| Reserve build **+ rising nonaccrual + more office migration** (§2/§4) | **GENUINE DETERIORATION — bear.** The build is chasing real credit, not resetting a baseline. Do NOT dismiss as a kitchen-sink. |
| **No** reserve build **despite** rising nonaccrual + thinning coverage | **BEAR — under-provisioning** (the 114%→ thinning path continues; new CEO chose not to catch up). |
**Rule:** the overlay resolves by pairing the provision move with the nonaccrual/criticized direction — a build alone is ambiguous; a build against improving credit = reset, a build against deteriorating credit = deterioration.
