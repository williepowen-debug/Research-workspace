## 1. Coastal Financial (CCB) Q3 — release-only, PARTIAL

**Primary:** SEC 8-K accession `0001437958-26-000075`, Item 2.02, Exhibit 99.1, filed 2026-10-29: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437958/000143795826000075/erccb-20261029xexx991.htm. The 10-Q and call were not read.

### Bluevine and partner funding

- On-balance-sheet Bluevine deposits were **$452.4M [9/30]**, against approximately $447M [9/25] in the earlier event 8-K. The two observations are five days apart, not a Q2-to-Q3 change.
- The like-date denominator is total deposits of **$4,678.831M [9/30]**, so Bluevine was **9.67%** of deposits. This replaces the live mixed-date ~9% estimate; the dated 10/28 calculation remains unchanged as written.
- Coastal also swept **$1.85B** of Bluevine deposits off balance sheet. Total CCBX swept deposits rose to **$6.291B** from $4.261B at Q2. CCBX on-balance-sheet deposits fell $246.2M to $3.042B primarily because of greater sweep use, not a disclosed decline in underlying partner activity.
- Valley now indicates an expected **Q2 2027** close, when the Bluevine relationship is expected to terminate and associated deposits to transition out. This is the live planning guidance; the earlier early-1Q27 wording remains in dated records. The exit is conditional on the acquisition closing. Coastal expects existing sweep capacity and other funding sources to replace the on-balance-sheet funding without a material adverse liquidity effect.

**Grade:** funding concentration is better measured, not presently transmitting. No deposit flight or credit loss is established. No trade, gate or threshold under WQ-228.

### Partner credit and CARL consumer-leg reconciliation

- Total provision for credit losses was **$46.019M**, down from $92.157M in Q2. The unnamed partner's **$22.8M specific allowance was unchanged**; the credit-enhancement valuation allowance was **$45.6M**, versus $46.0M.
- The updated review substantially supported Q2's loss estimates, although actual charge-offs were modestly higher than projected. **$3.1M of additional provision was not offset by credit-enhancement income** because collection of that portion was not considered probable.
- Nonperforming assets fell $14.7M quarter over quarter and the consolidated annualized NCO rate fell to **4.43%** from 4.90%. These support stabilization, not resolution of the partner's indemnification collectability.
- CARL's Q2 category figures reconcile exactly to the issuer table. Q3 CCBX credit-card loans were **$552.078M**, versus $753.171M (−$201.093M / −26.7%); other consumer and other loans were **$1,091.049M**, versus $918.779M (+$172.270M / +18.7%).
- CCB sold **$5.99B** of loans in Q3, including **$4.69B** of additional credit-card receivables under forward-flow arrangements. The card-stock decline therefore must not be read as a stand-alone asset-quality or demand signal.

**Limits:** the partner remains unnamed; product-level partner allocation, filing-note recourse detail and current indemnification collectability beyond the release remain unobserved here. Release-only coverage is PARTIAL under the CARL feed definition.
