# Ueno Bank, Paraguay: failure, routes out, and what it means here (2026-11-05, 12:15 ET onward)

**Authority:** Will in this session, 12:15 ET. LIQUID owns funding and contagion (WALTER -20261105-013); this is a bank-side read, not a LIQUID grade. $0. No score, threshold, gate or trade change.

## 1. Bottom line

**A big bank by Paraguayan standards failed, and it has now taken down the country's president. I found no route that makes it a US bank event.** The identified US exposure is small: **$17.9M of face in 17 US fund series, 5.1% of the $350M bond, at most 1.22% of any one fund** (reports dated before the failure). What it adds to this desk is a lesson. Ueno reported clean credit and adequate capital until days before it was found below half the legal minimum.

⚠️ **Known-unknown:** 144A bonds are mostly held by investors who never disclose. About 95% of the bond is unidentified, and no bank loan or correspondent exposure can be seen. "No US route found" is not "no US exposure".

**Why it failed (as far as public):** lending to companies tied to the owners, then provisions on "loan documentation and IT services recorded as assets without sufficient supporting documentation" (UPI). **No figure exists for the hole.** A judge froze about $70M of owner assets (AT). Eight detention orders issued (AT); these are allegations, not findings. Peña denies wrongdoing and Ueno says he was never a shareholder of the bank; he was a Grupo Vázquez partner until March 2025 (AP).

**Depositors:** guarantee ~$38,000 per person, ~97% of depositors covered; 15 days, extendable 15. First payments had not been confirmed as paid in anything I read. **Public money:** $800–850M, including the state pension fund's $355–380M, about 9% of its investments (EFE; conflicting figures). The guarantee fund's size against the claims: not found.

## 4. What it means for this desk

- **No score, threshold or thesis change.** Nothing here touches a US regional.
- **The lesson is the useful part, and it is the same one Preferred taught this morning.** NPLs 0.4%, a BB rating, capital reported at 13.8% in October, and "below 50% of the minimum" within weeks. Reported credit metrics did not warn; related-party limits and asset documentation did. This fits Will's 10/20 point that reported figures do not prove loss completeness. One foreign bank is not evidence about US banks.
- **It is not a model for a US failure.** Weak supervision, owner-affiliated lending and a presidential connection are specific to this case.
- **Trades:** no candidate. Nothing on the book touches Paraguay, and sovereign or EM credit belongs to LIQUID and TERRY.

## 5. What to watch, all owner-held unless stated

1. **Withdrawals or stress at another Paraguayan bank.** This turns one failure into a banking crisis.
2. **Who governs and who pays.** Congress accepting the resignation; first guarantee payments; whether the fund covers claims.
3. **A rating action on the sovereign** (Fitch was BB+ positive in March).
4. **Bond recovery and any holder claim** against the offering.
5. **Fund marks:** the next N-PORTs show who held it at failure.
