## Plain-language summary

**The jump itself looks mostly like noise. The stress behind it is real, but it is the slow, known kind, and the next risk is the reset wall.**

I called this jump the important Flagstar finding this morning from two data points. With 12 quarters in front of me, that was too strong. $392M is not a new high: it sat at $392M a year and a quarter ago, was $677M at 3/31, and peaked at $893M in mid-2024. Q2's $233M was the unusually low quarter, so Q3 is a bounce back to a level this bank has lived at.

**What is actually new:**
- **Loans 90+ days late but still accruing:** across ALL loans, zero at every quarter-end through 12/31/25, then $32M, $51M, $107M. Small, but new. ⚠️ **Multifamily is only $50M of it ($38M at 6/30).** Most of Q3's rise is owner-occupied commercial real estate, $0 → $49M in one quarter, which looks like one or two loans (INFERRED). Banks may accrue only on loans well secured and in collection, so why these loans qualify is the question. UNKNOWN.
- **Modifications restarted:** $556M in H1 2026 vs $19M a year earlier, mostly rate cuts. Q2's fall in late loans came alongside $259M of multifamily modifications, so that low was probably flattered (INFERRED).
- **Special mention is growing:** multifamily $2,757M at 6/30, up $692M in H1; total criticized and classified rose another $670M in Q3. Management ties this to reviewing loans that reset or mature in the next 18 months.

**Why it is not a danger to the bank on these figures:** CET1 12.38% reaches the 10.5% bottom of its target only at about **$1.56B of pre-tax loss** (25% tax, no earnings or reserve offset). My severe case is about **$1.38B**, which takes CET1 to ~10.7%.

**Caveats that could change the view:**
- ⚠️ **Thin earnings.** Pre-provision earnings were only about $66M in Q3, so capital and reserves absorb losses, not income. A $1.38B loss is about five years of that run rate.
- ⚠️ **The scenarios are assumptions,** especially the value haircuts. Nothing here is a forecast.
- ⚠️ **The $1,571M with no allowance depends on appraisals.** On the rent-regulated nonaccrual book's 85% LTV, a value fall of about 7.6% after selling costs ends the cushion on average (INFERRED; the no-allowance loans may have lower LTVs; the figure is 6/30).
- ⚠️ **"Two borrowers" is management's word,** from a third-party transcript with no sizes. It could explain the jump, or it could be narrower than the release table, which shows late multifamily up 68% and CEO Otting saying "we have not seen an increase in past due loans" otherwise.
- ⚠️ **What moved is mostly UNKNOWN.** No source splits late loans by rent regulation or maturity.
- ⚠️ **It is still the worst large book.** At 1.51% late and 8.18% nonaccrual, FLG is the highest among lenders with at least $1.5B of multifamily on my list. The two earlier jumps in this window both became nonaccrual in the 2024 recognition wave, so "it has been here before" does not mean "safe".
- **The wall ahead:** ~$12B of sub-3.9% loans reset or mature from 10/1/26 through 2027 (call). The bank expects to keep 35–40%. That tests these numbers more than Q3's late bucket does.

**The 10-Q decides the rest.** Q3 10-Qs came 11/6/25 and 11/8/24; 2026 filings came 5/7 and 8/6. **Expect Thu 11/5 or Fri 11/6.** Not filed at 13:39 ET. No trade view here; TERRY has the evidence.
