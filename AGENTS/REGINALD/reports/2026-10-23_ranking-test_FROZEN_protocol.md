**B2** = B1 minus the same ratio at 3/31/26, in percentage points, computed at run time from the 3/31/26 Call Report with the same fields. This is pre-period data, not outcome data.

## Outcomes
- **Primary:** change in nonaccrual ÷ total loans from 6/30/26 to 9/30/26, in percentage points. Fields: total nonaccrual `RCON1403` ÷ total loans `RCON2122` (RCFD equivalents for FFIEC 031 filers, resolved by the same fallback chain as `scripts/mi3_cohort_screen.py`). The 6/30 value is re-pulled, not taken from B1, so both endpoints share one vintage.
- **Secondary:** Q3 net charge-off rate = (9/30 YTD − 6/30 YTD) of [total charge-offs `RIAD4635` − total recoveries `RIAD4605`] (Schedule RI-B) ÷ 9/30 total loans × 4, in %.

## Statistic
- Spearman rank correlation between each of S1, S2, B1 and B2 and each outcome. Ties take average ranks. Positive means a higher risk score went with more deterioration.
- Also report, for each ranking, the mean outcome of its top 3 versus the other 11. Ties at the third place are included in the top group, with its size stated.

## Reading rule (fixed in advance)
- With n = 14, correlation differences under 0.2 are reported as "no distinguishable difference".
- No significance claims.
- All results are shown whichever way they fall.
