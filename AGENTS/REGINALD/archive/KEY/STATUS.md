## Why KEY Matters

KEY is a lower-risk read-through name. CRE concentration is modest (~15%), credit is improving, capital is strong. The value is:

1. **Office NPL trajectory** — was 5.1% and doubling YoY (Q3 2024). Any continued deterioration reads through to our high-CRE targets.
2. **Blackstone fund finance partnership** — launched 2024, originating middle-market fund finance facilities. New vector connecting to our PC thesis.
3. **IB fees sensitivity** — $243M in Q4. Tariff-driven deal pipeline slowdown would signal broader capital markets stress.
4. **NIM trajectory** — targeting 3.0-3.05% by Q4 2026. On track = deposit cost pressure manageable sector-wide.
