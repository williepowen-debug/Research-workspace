## Executive Summary

The Florida property insurance market has transitioned from crisis to **fragile stabilization**. Citizens has successfully depopulated (1.42M → ~385-395K policies), shifting $214.5B in exposure to private carriers. The "doom loop" mechanism has evolved: the risk is no longer state-backed Citizens assessments, but **private insurer failure → FIGA activation → forced-place insurance at 3-5x rates**.

## The Condo Crisis: Where Risk Concentrates

### The Bifurcated Market

**Single-Family**: Stabilizing, premiums flattening, capital returning

**Condos**: Acute non-meteorological crisis
- 1,438 buildings on Fannie/Freddie blacklist
- 696 concentrated in Miami/Palm Beach corridor
- Master policy availability scarce
- Deductibles now 5-10% of building value

### Insurers Avoiding Blacklisted Buildings

Major carriers refusing coverage for buildings that:
1. Failed Phase One milestone inspection without repairs
2. Have special assessments exceeding threshold % of value
3. Are on Fannie Mae blacklist due to inadequate insurance
4. Have ACV-only policies or excessive deductibles

**Who's Still Writing Condo Master Policies:**
- Frontline
- Heritage
- Citizens (fallback) — requesting +10.4% increase for commercial lines

## The Cascade Scenario: Top-5 Insurer Failure

### Trigger Event
If Slide, Tower Hill, or similar top-5 carrier failed...

### Cascade Sequence

1. **Mortgage Violations** (Day 1-30)
   - Most mortgages require A-rated insurance
   - Insolvency triggers 30-day "default" clock
   - Thousands of unit owners simultaneously affected

2. **Coverage Gap** (Day 1-30)
   - FIGA takes over claims (with caps)
   - Policyholders must find new coverage
   - 30-day grace period before cancellation

3. **Forced-Place Insurance** (Day 30+)
   - If association can't find new master policy...
   - Lenders force-place coverage at **3-5x market rate**
   - Zero liability coverage for common areas
   - $3,000/year policy → $15,000/year forced-place

4. **Association Insolvency** (Day 60+)
   - 500% insurance cost increase
   - Plus existing SIRS reserve requirements
   - Pushes associations into receivership or bulk-sale liquidation

### Bank Portfolio Impact

- **Collateral Risk**: Blacklisted condo values drop 20-40%
- **Secondary Market**: Can't sell mortgages to Fannie/Freddie → held on balance sheet
- **Insurance-Driven Defaults**: Borrowers can afford mortgage but not 5x forced-place premium

## Implications for Thesis

### Bull Case (Why Crisis May Not Cascade)

1. **No insolvencies in 2025** — cycle may be ending
2. **Litigation reform working** — loss ratios improving
3. **Reinsurance softening** — capital costs declining
4. **OIR monitoring** — 14 companies under enhanced scrutiny
5. **2024 hurricanes absorbed** — no new wave of failures

### Bear Case (Why Risk Remains)

1. **14 companies still under enhanced monitoring** (~15% of market)
2. **Universal P&C at AM Best B** — weakest major player
3. **Condo master policy market extremely thin**
4. **One major storm could stress reformed market**
5. **FIGA caps insufficient for large association claims**
6. **Blacklist growing** — 1,438 buildings and counting

### The Evolved Doom Loop

**Old (2020-2023):**
Hurricane → Citizens losses → Statewide assessment → All policyholders hit

**New (2024+):**
Private insurer failure → FIGA activation (capped) → Coverage gap → Forced-place at 5x → Insurance-driven mortgage defaults → Bank losses
