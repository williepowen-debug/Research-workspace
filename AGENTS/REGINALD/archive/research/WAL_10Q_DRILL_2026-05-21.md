## 2. SUBSEQUENT EVENT — $99M Life-Science Office Walk-Away (B1 FIRED)

**The single most important disclosure in this 10-Q.**

> "In late April 2026, the Company received notification from a borrower regarding its **intention not to repay** a CRE non owner occupied loan with an outstanding balance of **$99 million**. Prior to this notification, the loan had been performing in accordance with its contractual terms and was internally graded as **pass**. The property securing this loan is a 6-story newly constructed, **LEED Silver-certified, Class-A life-science laboratory/office building** with a 7-story parking structure, located on a 6.0-acre site in a **gateway life-science market**. After receiving this notification, the loan was downgraded to substandard and placed on non-accrual status. Following its internal procedures, the Company has ordered an appraisal and will conduct a valuation analysis to determine any financial impact."

**Why this matters:**

1. **Same mechanic as IQHQ (OZK).** Sponsor strategic-default in the life-science office sector. Two life-science office walk-aways across our watchlist in 6 months = potential cluster signal.
2. **Pass → substandard in a single quarter.** Confirms rating-quality concern (RED M5 family). The loan was performing per contractual terms — this is sponsor election, not credit deterioration.
3. **B1 condition satisfied.** Per Investor Day prep framework: "Bucket B1 = at least one named new Office credit walking away." We have one, with size + property detail.
4. **Quantitative impact on REG-25:** Q1 ex-fraud NCO was 39bps (above 25-35bps guide). At 60% loss severity on the $99M = **~$60M Q2 charge-off** = ~10bps annualized incremental on $58.2B average loans. Q2 NCO trajectory crosses 40bps before any normal Q2 activity. **REG-25 confidence: 55% → 75%+.**
5. **Pulled forward.** V2.1's "Bear-slow 23%" case was Office migration across Q2-Q3-Q4. The first material credit migration is already disclosed at start of Q2.

**Sector identification:** "Gateway life-science market" = Boston / SF Bay Area / San Diego. The Class-A LEED Silver newly-constructed 6-story specification is consistent with the post-2020 life-science building boom. Many of these were leased to tenants like Moderna / Vir / Sana / single-tenant biotechs that have since downsized.

**Offset:** $60M LOI from third party in early May 2026 to purchase collateral on an **unrelated** substandard non-accrual loan at price approximating carrying value of $60M. Net positive but doesn't change Q2 NCO trajectory.
