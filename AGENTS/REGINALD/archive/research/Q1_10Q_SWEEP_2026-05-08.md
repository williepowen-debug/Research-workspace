## EGBN — initial scan

**Filing:** `https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050441/000105044126000066/egbn-20260331.htm`

### Strategic de-risk language CONFIRMED in primary filing

EGBN's 10-Q explicitly states:

1. **Portfolio reshape:** *"These shifts reflect our strategic focus on reshaping the portfolio toward relationship-driven commercial lending and asset classes aligned with our long-term risk-adjusted return objectives."*

2. **Office de-risk action:** *"office concentration, including actions to reduce exposure to short- and intermediate-term valuation risk in the office portfolio. These steps reflect our strategic focus on improving portfolio resilience and risk-adjusted returns."*

3. **High-risk acknowledgment:** *"the ACL as of March 31, 2026 remains adequate to absorb estimated losses inherent in the portfolio following the loss recognition on **high-risk loans concentrated in the commercial real estate office segment**."*

4. **HFS transfer mechanism:** *"The losses recognized in the first quarter of 2026 were primarily due to transfer of certain loans to HFS and the incorporation of new information about borrower performance."*

### Implications — V1 Hidden CRE thesis getting direct confirmation

This is the **strongest primary-filing language** we've seen on V1 Hidden CRE thesis:
- Bank ADMITS "high-risk loans concentrated in commercial real estate office segment"
- Bank IS RECOGNIZING losses on those loans (transferring to HFS = forced disposal)
- Bank IS SHIFTING the portfolio away from CRE concentration
- This is exactly what V1 thesis predicted: forced de-risking from MI3 23.7% baseline

### Watchlist score recalibration

EGBN was at score 12 (Tier 1 Max Stress). Q1 print acknowledgment of de-risk + actions taken is an open question:
- **Bear case for stay-at-12:** acknowledgment of "high-risk office concentration" AT the bank's own admission validates V1 thesis. Hidden CRE was real.
- **Bull case for downgrade to 8-9:** the de-risk is happening; the recognition is happening; if PPNR holds and CRE % drops below 547%, the structural risk is being walked down.

**Open question:** What % of office loans got transferred to HFS in Q1? What's the post-transfer office concentration? Need 10-Q drill on Note: Loans by category and Note: Loans Held for Sale to quantify the de-risk magnitude.

If office HFS transfers were >$200M (vs $342M C&I + $128M Construction baseline), EGBN is meaningfully derisking and the watchlist score should drop. If transfers were <$50M, the language is window-dressing.
