## THE QUESTION

WALTER's cohort scan (SIG-W-20260511-030) found 5 of 10 peers improving NPA YoY Q1'26: ZION −3bp / CFG −11bp / MTB −25bp / FITB −24bp / EGBN −48bp. Did they share EGBN's pattern (Hyp B → cohort fade intact, hidden in NCO line) or genuinely heal (Hyp A → cohort de-stressing, WAL bear is idiosyncratic)?

**What it changes:** whether the WAL bear is a "broad regional cohort stress" thesis or a WAL-idiosyncratic one — and whether the v2.2.1 Bear-medium 30→25 trim should hold (Hyp A) or partially revert toward 30 (Hyp B).

## METHOD (pre-registered BEFORE data — see Phase 0 plan)

**Primary discriminator — flow test:** is the NPA decline caused by charge-offs (cosmetic) or cures/paydowns/upgrades (genuine)? If NCO is *accelerating* while NPA falls, and provision ≤ NCO (reserve depletion), the problem credits are being written off, not cured.

**Corroboration — NCO bp-change YoY vs each bank's OWN trailing baseline** (not a flat cohort threshold: a 4× blowout off a 5bp base ≠ +15bp off a 40bp base).

| Class | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **GENUINE** | NPA ↓ AND NCO flat-or-↓ YoY; reserve build or stable coverage reinforces |
| **COSMETIC** | NPA ↓ AND NCO materially ↑ YoY AND provision ≤ NCO (charge-off-driven resolution) |
| **MIXED/CONFOUNDED** | signals conflict, data insufficient, OR NPA move driven by a non-credit artifact (denominator, single-name one-off) |

All figures below primary-sourced from SEC EDGAR; **decisive ratios re-verified by REGINALD's own curl** (not relayed from subagent). WALTER's 5 NPA deltas all confirmed against primary (Rule #3 satisfied).
