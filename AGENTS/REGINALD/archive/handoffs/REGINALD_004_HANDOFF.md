2. **Timing is the Core Challenge** — The bear thesis on regional banks is directionally correct but timing-dependent. Management can extend runway longer than expected.

3. **Employment is Master Variable** — Per RED SWEEP 001, employment break is the transmission mechanism. Until claims spike or NFP goes negative, banks can continue extend-and-pretend.

4. **Respect Earnings Power** — Original ratings underweighted management's ability to navigate exposure. Earnings beats are information; adjust accordingly.

5. **Surface vs Hidden Still Valid** — The divergence between surface indicators (prices GREEN) and hidden indicators (BDC NAV, PIK, mods ORANGE) persists. Recognition will eventually come, but timing uncertain.
