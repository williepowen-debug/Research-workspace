# RQ-CREED-011: AI Office Demand Sustainability

**Date:** 2026-01-27 | **Status:** COMPLETE

---

## HEADLINE

AI companies leased **2.5M SF in SF in 2025** alone, now occupying **7M SF** (~12% of SF occupied stock). AI = **35% of SF office demand** and **80% of newly leased space**. Every major AI company is unprofitable (OpenAI projects $115B cumulative burn through 2029). **More durable than dot-com** (absorbing oversupply, not creating it) but **less sustainable than bulls project.** Base case: durable 2-3 years with 20-30% contraction risk in 2027-2028.

## 2. LEASE TERMS & QUALITY

- **54% to Class A**, 46% to Class B
- SF trophy vacancy tightened to **15%** (vs citywide 33.5%)
- Large AI (OpenAI, Anthropic): long-term, full-building/multi-floor trophy
- Smaller AI startups: short-term, flexible, Class B
- **75% of remaining SF office pipeline is pre-leased**
- AI absorbing existing inventory, NOT driving new construction (key difference from dot-com)

## 4. DOT-COM COMPARISON

| Metric | Dot-Com Peak (~2000) | AI Boom (2025) |
|--------|---------------------|----------------|
| SF vacancy at start | ~10% → **<1%** | **~37%** → 33.5% |
| SF vacancy after bust | Rose to **16-20%** | N/A |
| Tech footprint growth | 11M SF added | 5M SF added; 16M projected by 2030 |
| VC funding in hub | <$103B combined | $103B to SF AI since 2020 |
| CBD rent collapse post-bust | **-51%** | N/A |
| New construction | Massive speculative | Falling 75% in 2026 |

### Key Structural Differences
1. Starting from **oversupply** (33.5%), not scarcity (<1%)
2. AI absorbing **existing space**, not fueling speculative construction
3. Larger, better-capitalized companies ($500B+ valuations)
4. Real revenue: OpenAI $12-13B, Anthropic $9B ARR (dot-com often zero)
5. Enterprise adoption (~80% of Anthropic revenue)
6. **BUT:** Still unprofitable; $115B projected cumulative burn (OpenAI)

## 6. SUSTAINABILITY ASSESSMENT

### Bull Case
- Revenue growth projections (OpenAI $200B by 2030; Anthropic $26B by 2026)
- Enterprise adoption accelerating from pilots to integration
- CBRE: 16M SF additional AI leasing in SF through 2030
- Supply pipeline at all-time lows (completions -75% in 2026)
- Backed by MSFT, Google, Amazon, Meta balance sheets

### Bear Case
- Every major AI company unprofitable; OpenAI $115B cumulative burn
- AI = ~50% of all VC funding — extreme concentration
- VCs warning of startup "weeding out" in 2026
- AI hiring showed sharp decline in H2 2025 after H1 surge
- 95% of enterprise AI projects show no ROI (MIT)
- $211B VC funding unsustainable if returns don't materialize in 2-3 years

### **Base Case: Durable 2-3 years, 20-30% contraction risk in 2027-2028**
- **2026-2027:** Large AI companies have sufficient capital. Demand secure.
- **2027-2028:** Inflection. Must demonstrate path to profitability.
- **Structural floor:** Google, Meta, MSFT, Amazon AI divisions (~30-40% of demand) durable regardless of VC cycles
- **Vulnerable:** VC-funded startups on short-term leases (20-30% of demand)
- **Mitigant:** Even 30% pullback leaves SF vacancy in low-to-mid 30s% (2023-2024 level, not a crash)

---

## CREED IMPLICATIONS

1. AI demand is the **single most important positive variable** for office vacancy trajectory
2. **7M SF in SF** = material. But SF is one market — AI doesn't solve Houston, Chicago, Detroit office vacancy
3. **35% of SF leasing** = high concentration risk. If AI disappoints, SF re-collapses
4. **Not dot-com** because absorbing oversupply, not creating construction. Even a bust doesn't crash below current levels.
5. Manhattan AI leasing growing = positive diversification signal beyond SF
6. **Counter-signal for CREED thesis:** AI demand is a genuine bullish factor for gateway city office — NOT for suburban or secondary market B/C
7. **Data center demand is the bigger story** ($1T potential investment) but separate CRE sector
8. **Watch signal:** AI hiring trends. H2 2025 showed cooling. If hiring declines, leasing follows.
