**Date:** 2026-01-27 | **Status:** COMPLETE (partial — some metros lack granular data)

## HEADLINE

National MF vacancy at **9.3%** (record high). Sunbelt bore the brunt of historic supply cycle (~400K units delivered 2025). Construction pipeline has collapsed (starts down 40%+ from 2023), setting stage for 2026-2027 recovery. Sunbelt vacancy declined ~10bps YoY — first reversal.

## KEY FINDINGS

### Oversupply Severity Ranking (worst to least)
1. **Austin** — 15.3% vacancy, national worst
2. **Atlanta** — 12.0%, but showing early recovery signals
3. **Phoenix** — est. 10-12%, still elevated pipeline
4. **Nashville** — est. 10-12%, similar to Phoenix
5. **San Antonio** — est. 10-13%
6. **Charlotte** — 8.2%, pipeline collapsing fast
7. **Raleigh** — 7.7%, strongest fundamentals

### Recovery Signals
- **Atlanta**: Net absorption outpaced deliveries Q2 2025 — first time since mid-2021
- **Raleigh**: Vacancy declining, absorption far exceeds historical averages, pipeline normalizing
- **Charlotte**: Pipeline dropping from 15K to 6.5K units = fastest supply correction
- National: Sunbelt vacancy declined ~10bps YoY = first reversal after years of increases

### DSCR Threat Confirmed
- $115B of CRE loans maturing by end of 2026 with DSCR below 1.20x (Trepp)
- 66% increase in CRE loan modifications (Fed Reserve Bank of St. Louis)
- Markets most at risk: **Austin, Phoenix, Atlanta, Nashville, Tampa**
- Insurance + property tax escalation compressing NOI even on stable-income properties

### 2021-2022 Vintage Distress
- Two-thirds of MF foreclosures involve 2021-2022 vintage loans
- Syndicator failures producing forced sales (GVA Real Estate Group — Austin, Nashville portfolios)
- Loss severity below 2% so far — lenders working with borrowers, not forcing mass liquidations

## CREED IMPLICATIONS

1. MF stress is real but NOT uniform — bifurcation within Sunbelt
2. Recovery timeline: 2026 for leaders (Raleigh, Charlotte), 2027+ for laggards (Austin, Phoenix, Nashville)
3. Pipeline collapse is the bullish signal — starts down 40%+ means supply relief is coming
4. The 60% H2 2026 MF maturity concentration hits hardest in markets still oversupplied
5. Insurance/tax cost escalation is a hidden NOI drag not captured in rent data alone
