## Key Findings

### 1. MASTER FINDING: CRE is Bifurcating, Not Collapsing Uniformly

The extend-and-pretend thesis is **partially correct but more nuanced** than the skeleton assumed. Two narratives are simultaneously true:

**Bear narrative (confirmed):** $930B maturity wall, MF DQ 6.64%, $5.5B double defaults, FL condo crisis ($100K-$400K assessments), office special servicing 17.16%.

**Bull narrative (new):** BREIT gates lifted, ODCE positive returns, CRE CLO issuance +374% YoY, transaction volume +17%, CMBS spreads at 77bps (from 200+), SF/NYC net absorption positive, office construction at 25-year lows.

**Synthesis:** Credit markets are reopening fast enough to refinance SOME of the maturity wall, but not all. The reckoning will be **selective** (Class B/C office, Sunbelt MF, FL condos) rather than systemic.

### 2. Maturity Wall Much Larger Than Estimated

$930B+ in 2026 (annual), not $300-500B/Q. Peaks at $1.26T in 2027. Exceeds $1T annually through 2030. The rate gap is 148bps (6.24% new vs 4.76% maturing). Q1 2025 distressed assets: $116B (+31% YoY). **This is the single most important vector in CREED.** Upgraded to RED.

### 3. Multifamily Stress Broadening

MF CMBS DQ surged to 6.64% (Dec), peaked at 7.12% (Oct, 10yr high). +280bps YoY. Two-thirds of MF foreclosures involve 2021-2022 vintage loans. CRE distress is no longer office-only — it's spreading to multifamily via Sunbelt overbuilding. Austin (8.6% of stock delivered), Charlotte (7.4%), Phoenix, Raleigh most affected. 60% of MF loans mature H2 2026.

### 4. Bank CRE DQ Correction

Large bank (>$250B) CRE DQ at **4.18%** (Q3 2025), not 1.56%. Series discrepancy: FRED DRCRELEXFACBS may use different bank-size definitions. 4.18% is 7x pre-pandemic avg (0.59%) but declining for 4 consecutive quarters from 4.99% peak. The CMBS-bank masking gap is still large (11.31% vs 4.18%) but much smaller than initially modeled. **Upgraded from GREEN to ORANGE.** Q4 data (FL-CREED-007) will be critical.

### 5. FL Condo Crisis Far Worse Than Modeled

Assessments at $100K-$400K per unit (was $10K). Cricket Club (N Miami): $134K. Mediterranean Village (Aventura): $400K. FL condo median prices -10.8% (H1 2025 vs H1 2023). SB 4-D SIRS deadline passed Dec 31, 2025. Super-lien risk to banks MUCH higher than initially assessed. **Upgraded to RED.**

### 6. Office Vacancy Improving — AI as Structural Demand

Net absorption positive for 5 consecutive quarters. SF down to 33.5% from 36% (AI = 25% of leases). NYC Manhattan availability down to 15% from 18%. Leasing at highest since 2019 in both cities. Trophy/Class A stabilizing while Class B/C diverges. AI is a structural demand factor, not cyclical.
