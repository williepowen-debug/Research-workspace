# Webster Anomaly — Resolved

**Flag:** Q1 2026 earnings call (Apr 15), CFO Daryl Bible said: "Fund banking and capital call lines is a business **we acquired from Webster.**"
**Problem:** M&T Bank has never acquired Webster Bank (NYSE: WBS).
**Resolution:** CFO misspoke. The fund banking business was built at **People's United Financial**, not Webster.

---

## The Actual History

1. **Nov 2018:** Michael Sinclair hired by People's United Bank to **build a fund banking practice from scratch.** Prior: co-founded PE team at East West Bank (7 years), started in fund finance at SVB (2009). Announced in Cadwalader's *Fund Finance Friday*, Issue #5 (Nov 30, 2018).

2. **2018–2022:** Sinclair built the fund banking book at People's United — subscription/capital call lines, fund-level financing, sponsor coverage to private capital funds. Active by Dec 2020 (appeared on *Fund Finance Friday*).

3. **Apr 2, 2022:** M&T completed $8.3B acquisition of People's United Financial. Sinclair and team transitioned to M&T.

4. **Post-merger:** Sinclair became **EVP & Head of Fund Banking at M&T Bank.** Per his May 2023 *Fund Finance Friday* profile: People's United was "the precursor to our current M&T business." Team is "heavily focused on subscription lines" — bilateral, agented, participations.

5. **Apr 15, 2026:** Bible said "acquired from Webster" on Q1 call. Factually wrong. No analyst challenged it.

---

## Why Bible Said "Webster"

**Most likely: simple geographic confusion.** People's United was headquartered in **Bridgeport, CT.** Webster Bank is headquartered in **Stamford, CT.** Both were long-time Connecticut competitors. The names are associated with the same geography in any M&T executive's mental map.

**Not a sub-acquisition:** United Financial Bancorp (acquired by People's United Nov 2019) did purchase 6 Webster Bank retail branches in Oct 2018 (~$120M deposits). These were deposit-only branches — no specialty lending or fund banking component.

**No personnel overlap:** Sinclair's career path (BankBoston → TSG Equity Partners → Raymond James → SVB → East West Bank → People's United → M&T) shows no Webster Bank connection at any point.

---

## Webster Bank's Own Fund Banking

Webster Bank has its **own, separate fund banking business** — operated "for over 20 years" per its website. Currently: Sponsor & Specialty Finance Group, 50+ bankers, 125+ sponsor relationships.

Webster Financial is being **acquired by Banco Santander for $12.3B** (announced Feb 2026). Webster's fund banking will go to Santander — not M&T, not ever M&T.

## Assessment

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Was Bible lying? | No — misspoke. Geographic confusion between two CT banks. |
| Does it matter? | **Yes, as a disclosure-quality signal.** A CFO misidentifying the origin of a multi-billion-dollar business on a recorded call shows either (a) he doesn't know the provenance of his own book, or (b) he doesn't distinguish between acquired business units at a granular level. Neither is reassuring for a portfolio where we want counterparty-level transparency. |
| Does it change the risk? | No. The fund banking book exists regardless of who built it. What matters is the LP/GP counterparty concentration — which remains undisclosed. |
| Should we escalate? | No. Flag resolved. File for reference. If fund banking exposure becomes material in a future quarter, this is useful context for questioning management credibility on NDFI provenance claims. |
