## Summary Judgment

MTB is NOT a traditional REGINALD short. By classical convergence metrics (CRE concentration, MI3 hidden CRE, NCO trajectory, capital adequacy), MTB is best-in-class among regionals. It has been actively de-risking CRE for 18 months and it shows.

**But the thesis is evolving.** Post-Apr 14 warehouse research, the transmission chain is: non-bank stress → warehouse opacity → counterparty concentration risk. On THAT axis, MTB scores high — and the 10-K revealed several things bigger than expected.

**Position candidacy: NOT YET.** Three findings below need to develop before conviction reaches actionable level.

## Three Findings That Changed the Picture

### 1. NDFI Is $24B in Commitments, Not $13B Outstanding

The Q1 call said $13.4B outstanding (10% of loans). The 10-K shows **$23.9B in total commitments** — $11.4B undrawn. If stressed counterparties draw facilities (classic bank-run mechanic applied to NDFI), MTB's balance sheet exposure nearly doubles without originating a single new loan.

The commitment/outstanding ratio (1.9x) is high and means MTB's NDFI risk is materially understated by the outstanding balance alone. Analysts focused on the 10% figure on the call. The commitment figure lives only in Table 11 of the 10-K.

### 2. Bayview Is a Multi-Vector Concentration, Not a Passive Equity Stake

The call said: 20% equity stake, $33M distribution, small-balance CRE. The 10-K reveals:

| Vector | Amount | Growth |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Lending commitments | $984M ($635M drawn) | +57% YoY |
| Sub-servicing UPB | $156.9B | +41% YoY |
| Sub-servicing revenue | $224M | +82% YoY |
| Deposits | $3.5B | +59% YoY |

MTB is DEEPENING this relationship (added $51.7B sub-servicing in Feb 2025, took lending from $404M to $635M drawn). A Bayview disruption hits MTB on four vectors simultaneously: deposits, lending, fee income, and equity. And Bayview Financial is a Blackstone-affiliated private specialty finance platform — it's inside the private credit ecosystem, not outside it.

### 3. Counterparty Opacity Is WORSE Than WAL

WAL at least filed Exhibit Index amendments that let us deanonymize some facility counterparties (Apr 14 research). MTB files **zero material lending contracts as exhibits.** No Master Repurchase Agreements. No warehouse facility amendments. The 10-K's NDFI disclosure is sub-category level only. $10.2B in "mortgage credit intermediaries" commitments with no visibility into who the counterparties are.

## What Would Make This a Position

### Upgrade Triggers (any one = reassess)

1. **MTB < $205** — 6% below current. Would indicate market pricing new risk, not just sector rotation.
2. **Bayview distribution cut or skip** — Q2 distribution doesn't materialize → fee income pressure.
3. **Bayview credit event** — any stress at Bayview Financial Holdings or its affiliates → multi-vector MTB hit.
4. **NDFI drawdown spike** — commitment/outstanding ratio narrows materially (outstanding approaching $15B+) → counterparties pulling lines.
5. **CET1 < 10.0%** — breaches stated target → buyback pause + potential capital raise narrative.
6. **Tricolor trustee judgment > $50M** — "not expected to be material" tested.
7. **Warehouse counterparty identified as stressed non-bank** — MTB confirmed lending to PFSI/LDI/COOP/RITM-tier names.
8. **Q2 provision continues building** (>$140M while NCO stable) — confirms forward-looking stress recognition.

### What Would Kill This

1. **NDFI commitment/outstanding ratio declines** — counterparties paying down, not drawing → stress not materializing.
2. **Bayview deposits + lending stable or declining** — relationship not deepening.
3. **CET1 stabilizes >10.3%** — buyback pace moderates, capital rebuilding.
4. **Q2 provision < $120M with NCO still low** — Q1 reserve build was one-time, not trend.
