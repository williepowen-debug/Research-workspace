2. **Auto ABS warehouse / NDFI exposure** to subprime originators like Tricolor. The "Consumer Warehouse" + "Consumer Finance Securitization Vehicles" = 31% of NDFI = ~$2.95B. FITB does NOT break out whether this includes subprime auto ABS exposure. 1Q26 catalyst: does FITB call out any specific auto ABS reserve build or name Tricolor on the call?
