## Funding — the biggest thesis-relevant finding

### FHLB advances crashed, not surged

| Bank | Q1-26 FHLB direction | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| MTB | +265% avg | Funding stress |
| CFG | 60x YoY ($42M → $2.5B) | Funding stress |
| PNC | +64.7% QoQ period-end (+$8.4B) | Funding stress |
| **FITB** | **-96% QoQ avg ($4,767M → $99M)** | **Deal-specific — $65B Comerica deposits + $2B Jan LT debt issuance** |

**FITB Q4 2025 FHLB avg was $4,767M (~2x YoY).** FITB was in the surge pattern until Comerica arrived. Brian Preston: "average wholesale funding declined 3% YoY even with Comerica balances included."

**Thesis implication:** FHLB surge is **not contradicted** — FITB's decline is deal-driven (paid down with Comerica deposit inflow). The MTB/CFG/PNC funding-stress pattern stands. **RF is the clean test** (no merger, no $65B deposit injection).

**Regulatory signal:** Brian explicitly lobbied for "credit in liquidity rules for FHLB borrowing capacity" — tells us regulators have been pushing back on FHLB-as-liquidity in horizontal exams ("absurdly high" outflow assumptions). Underlying funding stress is real enough that the CFO wants regulatory relief.

## NDFI disclosure

**Press release:** No NDFI table. Less provision-side transparency than CFG (Slide 24 deck) or PNC (Slide 11 deck, $73B rollup).

**Transcript (unprompted by Tim Spence):**
- NDFI = 7% of total loan portfolio (below industry avg)
- **Three categories:** (a) subscription/capital call lines, (b) corporate credit facilities to payment processors/insurance/brokerage, (c) secured residential mortgage lending
- **<1% combined private credit + BDC exposure** (deliberate decision, "not missed opportunity")
- **<$100M funded data center exposure**
- "Structural complexity" in PC/BDC avoided; "don't like things we don't understand"

**Tim Spence's competitive weaponization (direct quote):**
> "Less than 10% of our loan growth from PE/private capital. My quick read-through of others may be as high as 80% in a lot of other places."

This is a **new analyst hook** for WAL/OZK/ZION on Apr 21. FITB painted a target on peers' growth composition.

**For WAL V3 (warehouse):** Status mostly unchanged. No hook fired at FITB means WAL's warehouse composition likely also escapes direct interrogation. But Tim's growth-composition quote introduces an orthogonal pressure vector.

**For the FHLB indicator:** Don't downgrade on FITB. Wait for RF — if RF (no merger, no deposit infusion) also declined, the thesis narrows. If RF surged, it reconfirms.
