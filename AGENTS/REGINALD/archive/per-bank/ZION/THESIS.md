# ZION — Thesis
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-27

---

## Role in Our Research

ZION is the **benchmark**, not a trade. It answers: "What does honest accounting look like when you get hit by the Stupin fraud?"

## The Comparison Framework

ZION and WAL were hit by the same fraud ring (Stupin/Marcil/Cantor Group). The divergence in their response is the thesis:

1. **ZION charged off 83% immediately.** WAL reserved 30% and hasn't moved it in 6 months.
2. **ZION's auditor (EY)** is Big 4 with institutional resources. WAL's auditor (RSM) is mid-tier with PCAOB deficiencies in collateral verification.
3. **ZION led disclosure** (Oct 15). WAL followed (Oct 16). ZION was transparent about "irretrievable" collateral. WAL claims it "covers the obligation."

The question: Is WAL right that their collateral is better than ZION's, or are they under-provisioned with an auditor that can't (or won't) challenge them?
