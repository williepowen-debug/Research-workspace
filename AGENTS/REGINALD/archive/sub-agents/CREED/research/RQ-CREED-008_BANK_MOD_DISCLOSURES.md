## 4. ASU 2022-02 IMPACT

### Changes
- Eliminated TDR designation; replaced with "modifications to borrowers experiencing financial difficulty"
- No more concession testing
- **12-month reporting window** (vs previous "once a TDR, always a TDR")
- Modified loans can remain in collectively evaluated ACL pools

### Transparency Impact
- **More detail** in some areas: structured disclosure of mod types, financial effects, 12-month performance
- **Less visibility**: 12-month lookback means loans "age off" reporting. Eliminates clear TDR "flag"
- **Net effect: REDUCED TRANSPARENCY** per industry observers and NY Fed

### Regulatory Concern
- **NY Fed researchers:** Banks "'extended-and-pretended' their impaired CRE mortgages" — could lead to "credit misallocation and a build-up of financial fragility"
- **FDIC:** Revised Call Reports to incorporate new mod disclosures into bank scorecards. 12-month window revision effective **Dec 31, 2025**
- **CRITICAL: Upcoming disclosure cliff** — as mods age past 12 months, they drop off reporting. Could materially reduce visible mod volumes in 2026 filings even if credit stress persists
