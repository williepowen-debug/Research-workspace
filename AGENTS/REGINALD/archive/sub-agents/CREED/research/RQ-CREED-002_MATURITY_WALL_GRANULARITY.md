# RQ-CREED-002: Maturity Wall Granularity

**Date:** 2026-01-27 | **Status:** COMPLETE

---

## HEADLINE

- **2026:** $930-936B (MBA/S&P Global)
- **2027:** $1.26T (peak year; S&P Global)
- **Total outstanding CRE debt:** $4.8T
- **Annual maturities >$1T through 2030**

---

## 1. QUARTERLY DISTRIBUTION

**Quarterly data not publicly available.** However:
- **H2 2026 concentration CONFIRMED for multifamily**: 60% of 2021-22 vintage MF loans mature H2
- Bridge loan rate-cap expirations clustered Q3-Q4 2026 (40% of securitized CRE CLO loans)
- Overall wall likely somewhat back-loaded due to MF skew (~32% of total)

Estimated: H1 ~$375B / H2 ~$560B (60/40 split driven by MF concentration)

## KEY IMPLICATIONS FOR CREED

1. **VX-CREED-4.01 confirmed RED** — $930B+ is real and well-sourced
2. **Office is 14-20% of the wall** — concentrated distress but not dominant by volume
3. **MF is the biggest single piece** (~32%, $300B) and 60% matures H2 2026
4. **1,788 banks at 300%+ CRE/equity** — systemic banking risk is real but concentrated in regionals
5. **20-30% of the wall is extended loans** — extend-and-pretend is creating a compressed, higher-risk maturity profile
6. **44% of office loans in negative equity** — these cannot refinance without equity injection or loss recognition
