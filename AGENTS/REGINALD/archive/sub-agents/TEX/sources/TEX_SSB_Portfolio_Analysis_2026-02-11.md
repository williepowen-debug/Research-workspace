## THE KEY FINDING: Multifamily Substandard Analysis

### The 9.36% Question Answered

**Q:** Is the 9.36% MF substandard ratio Texas or Florida?

**A:** Management confirmed these are primarily **"lease-up" properties** — newly constructed Class A buildings not yet stabilized.

### Substandard Profile (Q4 2025)

| Metric | Value | Implication |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Payment Status | **99% Current** | Borrowers subsidizing |
| Average LTV | **52%** | ~48% equity cushion |
| Property Phase | Lease-up | Timing issue |
| Geographic Concentration | High-growth markets (TX + FL) | Supply-driven |

**Critical insight:** Low LTV = massive buffer. SSB protected against 40%+ value decline before principal loss.

### Why Classified as Substandard?

Technical DSCR test failure during lease-up:
- Properties not yet at stabilized occupancy
- High interest rates make DSCR <1.0x during ramp
- Doesn't mean borrower is in distress
- Borrowers have 48% equity at stake — strong incentive to support

### The "Support and Survive" Dynamic — CONFIRMED

Same pattern as SSB's own Q4 2025 call:
> "We've got a handful of multifamily assets that are in lease-up... they have a weighted average LTV of 52%... 99% are paying current."

**This matches Austin MF data:** DSCR ~1.02x but payments current.
