# Texas Banking Sector Strategic Review 2026

**Source:** Deep Gemini research  
**Date:** February 11, 2026  
**Research Prompt:** RP-TEX-6

---

## Executive Summary

Texas banks show divergent models: TCBI transforming to fee-based, CFR organic growth fortress, BOK diversified with exceptional credit. High CRE concentration remains the key risk for smaller players.

**Key Finding:** TCBI and CFR are NOT compelling short targets — too well-run. Community banks with 300%+ CRE concentration warrant monitoring.

## Alternative Short Targets?

### Criteria for Texas Bank Short

1. CRE concentration >300%
2. C&D concentration >100%
3. Lack of fee income diversification
4. Lower capital ratios
5. Texas geographic concentration

### Potential Candidates

| Bank | Why Consider | Why Not |
|------|--------------|---------|
| Simmons Bank | 361% CRE, 98% C&D | Multi-state diversification |
| Valley National | 434% CRE | Not Texas-focused |
| SBSI | 81.8% RE | Small, East Texas focus |

**Conclusion:** Major Texas banks (TCBI, CFR, BOKF) are well-run. CRE stress more likely to hit community banks with concentrated portfolios.

## Assessment for SSB Thesis

### Texas Bank Landscape: 🟢 DEFENSIVE

**Key findings:**
1. TCBI actively de-risking CRE — not a short target
2. CFR is fortress Texas bank — not a short target
3. BOKF has exceptional credit (0.26% NPAs) — not a short target
4. CMA merging with Fifth Third — not actionable

### SSB Position Implications

**SSB remains the preferred Texas-exposed target because:**
1. Higher CRE concentration than TCBI (which is reducing)
2. MF substandard elevated (vs TCBI minimal)
3. Correlation risk with FL (not present in pure-play TX banks)
4. Management "leaning in" to TX/CO (+15% QoQ) vs de-risking

**KRE captures SSB + all Texas banks** — remains best vehicle.
