# DFW Growth Assessment: Structural Integrity Analysis

**Source:** Deep Gemini research  
**Date:** February 11, 2026  
**Research Prompt:** RP-TEX-3

---

## Executive Summary

DFW's growth story remains **intact** for corporate investment but shows **stress cracks** in demographic and affordability foundations. The "Texas Miracle" is transitioning from exponential growth to mature market stabilization.

**Assessment:** 🟡 YELLOW — Growth continues but friction points emerging.

---

## Migration Deceleration

### The Cooling Trend

| Component | 2022 Peak | 2025 | Change |
|-----------|-----------|------|--------|
| Net Domestic Migration (TX) | **222,154** | **67,299** | **-69.7%** |
| Net International Migration (TX) | 319,569 (2024) | 167,475 | -47.6% |
| TX Population Growth Rate | ~1.6% | 1.2% | -25% |

**Key insight:** The "well" of potential migrants may be reaching exhaustion, OR Texas cost-benefit has become less compelling.

### DFW Specific

- Still #1 metro for movers in 2025 (U-Haul)
- But: Inbound +3% vs Outbound +1% — margin tightening
- Texas now "balanced" migration state (United Van Lines)

### Labor Market Implications

- International migration decline = tighter labor supply
- Construction, logistics, manufacturing rely on migration inflows
- Must now rely on productivity gains vs human capital volume

## Property Insurance Crisis: STRESS CRACK 🔴

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Avg Annual Premium (TX) | **$6,000** | 2x national avg ($3,200) |
| 2023 Increase | +21% | — |
| 2024 Increase | +19% | — |
| 2026-27 Projected | +16% | Continued escalation |
| 2024 Hail Events (TX) | 878 | 1"+ stones |

### "The 16% Homeowner Trap"

**New underwriting reality:**
- AI and satellite imagery for hyper-local risk assessment
- ZIP code modeling reclassifying "safe" neighborhoods
- Non-renewals based on aerial hazard detection (moss, branches)
- Percentage deductibles (1-5%) replacing flat deductibles
  - 2% deductible on $500K home = $10,000 out-of-pocket

**FAIR Plan enrollment doubled to 100K+** — insurer of last resort growing.

### Impact on Housing

- Hidden tax on DFW residency
- Discouraging potential buyers
- Forcing existing owners to choose between insurance and essentials

## "Reverse Migration" Signal

### Emerging Destinations

| Destination | Status | Draw |
|-------------|--------|------|
| Oklahoma | Net gain of ~2,000 from TX | Housing 30-70% cheaper |
| Arkansas (NWA) | #1 inbound state 2025 | Property tax 0.6% vs 1.5-2.5% |

### Motivations

1. **Housing arbitrage** — Gen Z/millennials seeking affordability
2. **Tax structure** — AR property tax ~0.6% despite income tax
3. **Quality of life** — Away from "endless traffic," extreme heat
4. **Education** — OK universities offering in-state tuition to Texans

**"Texodus" emerging** — DFW suffering from its own success.

## Assessment for Bank Thesis

### DFW Growth: 🟡 YELLOW

**INTACT:**
- Corporate relocations continue (AT&T, GEICO, life sciences)
- Industrial market strong
- Sublease overhang clearing

**STRESS CRACKS:**
- Migration deceleration (-70% domestic)
- Affordability erosion (income vs mortgage gap)
- Insurance crisis ($6K avg, +40% in 2 years)
- Property tax disadvantage vs competitors
- Reverse migration to OK/AR emerging

### Bank Implications

1. **Near-term:** Corporate relocations support high-end lending
2. **Medium-term:** Affordability/insurance strain middle-market
3. **Long-term:** Migration slowdown = labor supply constraint

**Not a near-term catalyst for SSB stress** — but structural headwinds building.
