# Houston Energy Landscape: Oil Price Sensitivity, Labor Dynamics & Bank Transmission

**Source:** Deep Gemini research  
**Date:** February 11, 2026  
**Research Prompt:** RP-TEX-2

---

## Executive Summary

Houston is navigating a "dual-energy" transition — traditional oil/gas complemented by rapidly growing renewables. Despite record 13.7M bpd US production, employment is measured due to automation and capital discipline. Current bank credit quality is exceptional, but a "hedging cliff" in 2027 poses the real stress test.

**Key Finding:** Houston's fate no longer solely tied to oil. Diversification has weakened (not eliminated) the price-employment correlation.

## Oil Price Triggers

### Price-Employment Correlation

Historical lag: **6-9 months** from price to employment change

**2026 Reality:** Correlation has become "subtle" rather than dominant:
- Houston diversified into healthcare, aerospace, professional services
- Despite WTI <$65, region added tens of thousands of jobs overall
- Energy sector shedding positions, but absorbed by other sectors

### Trigger Thresholds

| WTI Price | Corporate Action | Houston Labor Impact | Bank Credit |
|-----------|------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| **$75-95+** | Aggressive expansion | High hiring / wage growth | Robust |
| **$65-75** | Maintenance & consolidation | Stable / replacement | Neutral |
| **$55-65** | CAPEX reduction / efficiency | Selective layoffs (services) | Rising criticized |
| **$45-55** | Rig demobilization / M&A | Significant E&P layoffs | NPL spikes |
| **<$45** | Survival mode / distressed | Widespread slowdown | Downgrades / losses |

### The $50 "Shale Crush" Scenario

Per Kpler/EIA analysis:
- Would "cripple" US crude supply
- Output cut: **-700,000 bpd** by end of 2026
- Rig count: Falls to **360** (vs ~320 at $60)
- DUC wells: Rapidly exhausted
- Houston impact: "Dual shock" — direct E&P + secondary professional services

## 2015-2016 Comparison

### What Happened

- WTI: $100+ (mid-2014) → **$26.68** (Jan 2016)
- S&P downgraded BOK, Comerica, Frost (Feb 2016)
- Asset quality deteriorated significantly

### Bank Stress (2015-16)

| Bank | 2015-16 Stress |
|------|----------------|
| BOK | Energy = 19.4% of loans, 92% of equity; provisions jumped to $22.5M Q4 |
| Frost | NPAs tripled; provisions $8.2M → $28.5M; reserved ~1.5% of loans |
| Comerica | Criticized energy reached **40%** of portfolio |

### Why 2026 Is Different

1. **Lower concentration:** BOK down from 19.4% → 14%
2. **Higher capital:** Frost CET1 14.1% vs lower in 2016
3. **CECL accounting:** Proactive reserves during growth
4. **Client hedging:** 68% hedged vs less disciplined in 2015
5. **Diversification:** Houston less oil-dependent

## The 2027 Hedging Cliff ⚠️

### Current Protection (2026)

| Commodity | 2026 Hedge | Price |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| Crude Oil | ~40,000 bpd | **$68.70** swap |
| Natural Gas | 60% of production | **$4.06** swap |

### Exposure (2027)

| Commodity | 2027 Hedge | EIA Forecast |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Crude Oil | Significant decrease | **$53.00** |
| Natural Gas | **30%** (down from 60%) | $4.40 |

**The Cliff:** If EIA's $53 forecast holds, producers hedged at $68+ face massive cash flow reduction when derivatives roll off.

**"Lower for longer into 2027 will be the true test of regional banking sector's new-found resilience."**

## Assessment for TEX Thesis

### Houston Energy Channel: 🟢 GREEN (Currently)

**Not a near-term catalyst because:**
1. WTI at ~$75 provides substantial buffer
2. Bank credit quality exceptional (0-0.26% NPAs)
3. Client hedging protects through 2026
4. Renewables providing diversification
5. Different from 2015-16 (higher capital, lower concentration)

### Triggers to Upgrade to 🟡 YELLOW

- WTI sustained <$65
- OFS layoff announcements accelerate
- Q1-Q2 2026 bank earnings show criticized assets rising

### Triggers to Upgrade to 🟠 ORANGE

- WTI sustained <$55
- Major E&P bankruptcy or distressed sale
- Bank energy NCOs spike

### The Real Risk: 2027

If "higher for longer" persists AND oil stays <$55 through hedge rolloff, 2027 becomes stress test. **This is NOT a 2026 story unless oil crashes.**
