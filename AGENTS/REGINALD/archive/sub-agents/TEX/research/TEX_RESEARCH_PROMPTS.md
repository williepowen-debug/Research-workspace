## RP-TEX-1: Austin Multifamily Deep Dive

**Objective:** Comprehensive analysis of Austin's multifamily oversupply crisis and transmission to bank balance sheets.

**Research Questions:**
1. What is the current apartment vacancy rate in Austin MSA? How does it compare to 2019 baseline and national average?
2. What is the year-over-year rent growth (or contraction) for Austin apartments by class (A/B/C)?
3. How many new multifamily units were delivered in 2024-2025? What's the 2026 pipeline?
4. What is the absorption rate vs new supply? When does the market clear?
5. Which lenders have the largest Austin MF exposure? (SSB, TCBI, regional banks, CMBS)
6. What are current cap rates for Austin MF? How have they moved since 2022?
7. Are there visible distressed sales or loan modifications in Austin MF?
8. What is the DSCR profile of 2021-2023 vintage Austin MF loans?

**Output Format:** Metrics table, supply/demand chart, bank exposure matrix, timeline for stress resolution

## RP-TEX-2: Houston Energy Landscape

**Objective:** Assess Houston's oil & gas sensitivity and potential transmission to regional banks.

**Research Questions:**
1. What is the current employment level in Houston's energy sector (E&P, services, equipment)?
2. How does Houston energy employment correlate with WTI oil prices historically?
3. What oil price triggers significant E&P layoffs? (<$60? <$50?)
4. What is the current Houston office vacancy rate? How much is energy-sector driven?
5. Which regional banks have significant energy loan exposure? (BOK, Frost, Comerica)
6. What is the current energy loan quality at Texas-focused banks?
7. How did Houston banks perform during 2015-2016 oil crash?
8. Is there a hedging cliff coming (producer hedges rolling off)?

**Output Format:** Oil price sensitivity matrix, employment correlation, bank exposure table, 2015-16 comparison

## RP-TEX-3: DFW Growth Assessment

**Objective:** Evaluate whether DFW's growth story is intact or showing stress cracks.

**Research Questions:**
1. What is the current net migration into DFW? Is it accelerating or decelerating?
2. Which major corporations have relocated to DFW since 2020? Are relocations continuing?
3. What is the current DFW housing affordability index? How has it changed?
4. What are property insurance trends in DFW? (Hail, wind, rate increases)
5. What is the property tax burden in DFW vs other major metros?
6. Is DFW office market healthy or showing stress? Vacancy rates by submarket?
7. What is DFW's multifamily supply/demand balance?
8. Are there signs of "reverse migration" — people leaving DFW for lower-cost markets?

**Output Format:** Migration trends, corporate tracker, affordability comparison, insurance cost analysis

## RP-TEX-4: Texas State Fiscal Health

**Objective:** Verify Texas's fiscal position and identify any emerging stress.

**Research Questions:**
1. What are Texas's current credit ratings? (S&P, Moody's, Fitch)
2. What is the size of Texas's Economic Stabilization Fund ("rainy day fund")?
3. How does Texas's fiscal position compare to other large states?
4. What is the status of property tax reform? Budget impact?
5. What are the major unfunded liabilities? (TRS pension, OPEB)
6. How dependent is Texas on sales tax revenue? Cyclicality risk?
7. Are any Texas local governments showing fiscal stress?
8. What would trigger a Texas credit rating downgrade?

**Output Format:** Rating summary, reserve analysis, liability breakdown, comparison table

## RP-TEX-7: Texas Property Insurance

**Objective:** Assess whether Texas faces FL-like property insurance stress.

**Research Questions:**
1. What are the primary perils driving Texas insurance costs? (Hail, wind, tornado, flood)
2. How much have Texas homeowners insurance rates increased 2020-2025?
3. Are any major insurers exiting or reducing Texas exposure?
4. Is there a Texas equivalent to FL's Citizens Insurance (insurer of last resort)?
5. How does Texas insurance burden compare to Florida?
6. Are there specific metros with acute insurance stress?
7. How does insurance stress transmit to housing/bank balance sheets in Texas?
8. What is the historical loss ratio for Texas property insurance?

**Output Format:** Rate increase timeline, insurer market share, FL comparison, transmission pathway
