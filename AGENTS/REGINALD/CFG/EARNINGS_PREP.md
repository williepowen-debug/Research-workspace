## WHY THIS EARNINGS MATTERS

This is the first quarter where:
- Private credit stress is undeniable (PCDR 5.8%, 9+ funds gated, Goldman tightening)
- CFG's fund finance book was $12.5B and GROWING 40% into it
- C&I NCOs were already trending up 75% YoY (masked by CRE improvement)
- Q4 showed $2.3B C&I growth in a single quarter (largest ever, sponsor-driven)

Q1 2026 is the first real test. If stress is transmitting, the earliest it appears is HERE.

## 10 THINGS TO WATCH (in order of importance)

### 1. Capital Call Facility Balance
**Q4 2025:** $8,579M (6% of loans, +41% YoY)
**Watch for:** Did it grow again? If >$9B, acceleration continues. If flat or down, may signal reduced sponsor demand or CFG pulling back.
**Where to find:** 10-Q Table 14 equivalent (C&I by Industry Sector). May not be in press release — check the 10-Q filing.

### 2. Secured Private Credit Finance Balance
**Q4 2025:** $3,963M (3% of loans, +36% YoY)
**Watch for:** Growth or contraction. This is the HIGH-RISK tranche (NAV-based collateral). Any decline could signal collateral impairment forcing repayment. Any growth means CFG is still leaning in.
**Where to find:** Same Table 14 in 10-Q.

### 3. C&I NCO Rate
**Trend:** 14bp → 28bp → 35bp → 26bp → 28bp (Q4'24 through Q4'25)
**Full year 2025:** 29bps ($133M) vs 17bps ($76M) in 2024
**Watch for:** If Q1 2026 >35bps, the deterioration is accelerating. If it spikes >50bps, something broke in the C&I book — possibly fund finance.
**Where to find:** Press release credit tables + supplement.

### 4. C&I Criticized Balances
**Q4 2025:** $2.6B (stable YoY)
**Watch for:** Any increase signals emerging stress in C&I. A jump of $500M+ into the $3B+ range would be a warning. Criticized = Special Mention + Substandard + Nonaccrual.
**Where to find:** MD&A credit quality section.

### 5. Unfunded Commitments
**Q4 2025:** $105,880M (+$12.4B YoY)
**Watch for:** Still growing? If >$110B, the contingent exposure is expanding. If funds are drawing on facilities (stress draws), the funded balance rises AND unfunded declines — watch BOTH directions.
**Where to find:** 10-Q Note on Commitments.

### 6. Management Commentary on Private Credit / Sponsors
**Q4 language:** "Powerfully growing" (McCree), "Sponsor line utilization picking up" (Banerjee)
**Watch for:** Any change in tone. Key phrases: "monitoring closely," "well-managed exposure," "no material impact" = first acknowledgment of risk. If they DON'T mention fund finance at all = still in denial.
**Where to find:** Prepared remarks + Q&A.

### 7. Private Bank Loan Growth
**Q4 2025:** $7.2B (targeting $11-13B)
**Watch for:** Pace of growth. If PB loans >$8.5B, they're on track to double. 1/3 of growth is PE-based. Match PB growth to capital call growth to size the overlap.
**Where to find:** Prepared remarks (they always highlight PB).

### 8. Provision Expense Direction
**Q4 2025:** $155M quarterly ($608M full year, down from $687M)
**Watch for:** If provision rises despite CRE improving, it means C&I provisions are increasing. A provision build in C&I while CRE provisions release = the risk shift is materializing in reserves.
**Where to find:** Press release + supplement.

### 9. Analyst Questions
**Q4 2025:** ZERO questions on fund finance
**Watch for:** Does ANY analyst ask about private credit exposure, sponsor concentration, NBFI risk, or fund finance? If Goldman or Morgan Stanley asks, the sell-side is waking up. If still zero questions, the blind spot persists.
**Where to find:** Q&A section of transcript.

### 10. AOCI / Capital
**Q4 2025:** CET1 10.6%, AOCI unrealized -$1,603M
**Watch for:** Direction of AOCI losses (rates moved in Q1). Any commentary on the AOCI capital rewrite rule (comment period closes Jun 18).
**Where to find:** Capital ratios table + MD&A.

## SCENARIOS

### SCENARIO A: "Nothing to See" (50% probability)
- Fund finance grows to ~$13-14B, no NCOs disclosed
- C&I NCO rate stays 25-35bps range
- Management repeats "Private Bank zero losses" claim

- Multiple analyst questions on fund finance
- Stock: $45-50 (sharp repricing, 15-20% drop)
- **Thesis impact:** Thesis confirmed. Transmission active. Cascade begins.
