# REGINALD bank research standard

**Established:** 2026-10-13, Will's review feedback and follow-up on continuity for future REGINALD sessions.
**Scope:** investment-relevant bank-credit research within existing authorized coverage. This standard changes research documentation and follow-through, not approvals, ownership, frozen grading rules, prediction probabilities, thresholds, cadence or trade authority.

## The deliverable

A useful bank research judgment connects these layers. State an unsupported layer as UNKNOWN and identify the evidence needed; do not convert a sensitivity into a forecast.

| Layer | Required answer |
|---|---|
| Property | What supports recoverable collateral value, on which date and appraisal/sale basis? |
| Loan | What cash reaches the bank after costs/senior claims; what funded, replacement, property or commitment exposure remains? |
| Accounting | What was already charged off/provisioned; what new expense or reserve replenishment remains? |
| Earnings/book | How does additional expense affect common earnings and tangible equity, using explicit taxes, shares, earnings capacity and distributions? |
| Regulatory capital | What changes CET1 and matched risk-weighted assets for the same legal entity? An equity deduction alone is not a complete capital model. |
| Valuation | How does the outcome differ from dated expectations? What sustainable earnings/return and valuation assumptions connect it to price? |
| Catalyst | Which existing disclosure can change the conclusion, by when, and which result would refute it? |

## Before a relevant release

1. Recover existing studies and the responsible desk's frozen forecasts/frames. Reuse their evidence and preserve their ownership. Do not restart a completed investigation merely because a new session has no transcript memory.
2. Record REGINALD's working expectation **before the disclosure**, with date, horizon, observable outcome, basis, supporting evidence, contrary evidence and a falsifier. Distinguish an issuer's guidance, consensus, a peer's forecast and REGINALD's judgment. A known contract term has limited forecasting novelty.
3. Where a numerical loss forecast is defensible, show its derivation and range. Where it is not, identify the missing inputs and write a narrower testable expectation. Do not manufacture a point estimate or probability. New scored fleet predictions and revised owner priors follow their existing authorization/registration rules.
4. Attach material financial claims to primary URLs/accessions and page/table/row locators, with observation date, measurement basis and actual verification scope. A prior report is a pointer; another summary of the same source is not independent corroboration. A hash establishes artifact identity, not accuracy. Keep source access failures and incomplete verification visible.
5. Separate gross economic loss, prior recognition, new earnings expense, loan allowance, HFS/OREO accounting and cash recovery. Reconcile overlaps and changing cohorts. UNKNOWN is never zero; a category disappearance is never sufficient evidence of cash recovery.
6. Record a dated expectations benchmark when obtainable. If consensus is unavailable, use explicitly labeled issuer guidance or assumptions and retain the gap. A price/book discount is not a dollar estimate of market-implied loss. Sensitivities and conditional valuation examples must state what is held fixed.
7. State what changes the research conclusion in either direction. Keep that separate from an approved trading trigger. Recovery can refute a bearish mechanism without proving a long; a new loss can confirm stress without proving mispricing.

## At the existing release/filing read

Append the actual outcome beside the dated expectation; do not rewrite the expectation after seeing the result. Record CONFIRMED, REFUTED or UNKNOWN in plain analytical prose, the decisive source, incremental recognition versus expectation, retained risk and the implication for valuation. These labels do not replace registered state tokens or specialist grades.

Separate forecast error from missing disclosure. A revision needs its own date and new evidence. Grade specialist/fleet predictions only under their original rules. Compare current capacity and capital with the scenario rather than carrying historical buffers forward as current.

Persist the result and unresolved dependencies in the existing report/STATUS/handoff/ROADMAP workflow. The next session must see which expectation still needs an outcome and what would change the investment judgment. Use existing release obligations; do not silently create a standing monitor.

## Data and operational failures

Label failed retrieval, successful fresh retrieval, previously cached data and source unavailability separately. Follow the receiving runtime's approved network/Git mechanics; no permission bypass or repeated open-ended retry loop. Respect other desks' files and cache ownership. Shared ignore-policy changes remain with their authorized owner.
