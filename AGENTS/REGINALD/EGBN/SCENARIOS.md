## BEAR (45%) — DOGE Second Shoe + CRE Acceleration

**Trigger:** Q1 earnings show DOGE impact in government contractor book + DC office CRE deterioration. NCOs spike again. Deposits resume outflow.

| Metric | Expected |
|--------|----------|
| Q1 NCOs | >$80M (Q3 was $140.8M, Q4 normalized) |
| Provisions | Significant build (>$50M) |
| DOGE commentary | Vague, defensive, no quantification |
| DC office vacancy | Rising, no guidance on reabsorption |
| Deposits | Resume -2%+ QoQ |

**Price target:** $18-22. Approaches book value discount territory. M&A or capital raise becomes necessary.

**Key signals to watch:**
- Government contractor lending losses appearing
- DC office vacancy data (CBRE/JLL Q1 reports)
- Insider selling beyond CEO retirement
- Credit rating downgrade

| Metric | Expected |

| Provisions | Moderate build ($20-40M) |
| DOGE commentary | Acknowledgment + "monitoring" |
| Deposits | Flat to slightly positive |

**Price target:** $23-28. Trading range around current levels. Put expires near-worthless.

**Key signals:**
- Government contractor book performing (loans current)
- New CEO announcement with credible turnaround plan
- DC office leases holding (federal agencies not downsizing)

## BULL (20%) — Turnaround + M&A Floor

| Metric | Expected |

| Provisions | Release or minimal build |
| DOGE commentary | Proactive risk management |

**Key signals:**

- Dividend restored (even partial)

- New CEO from credible regional banking background
