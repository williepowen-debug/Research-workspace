## 3. Fraud / Cantor / Stupin Disclosure

| | ZION | WAL |
|---|---|---|
| **Names "Cantor" or "Stupin"?** | **No — zero mentions** | Mentions "fraud" in risk factors re NDFI |
| **How fraud is described** | "apparent irregularities and misrepresentations" by "two related commercial borrowers" | — |
| **Loan type** | "revolving lines of credit... to finance origination and purchase of commercial and residential mortgages" | — |
| **Charge-off amount** | **$50M** in Q3 2025 | $29.6M reserve on $98.5M exposure |
| **Reserve ratio** | Charged off (100% loss recognition on that portion) | 30% reserved, unchanged 6 months |
| **Recovery language** | "legal action has been initiated to pursue recovery of the outstanding amounts owed from the guarantors" | — |
| **NDFI ACL ratio** | 1.03% (up from 0.64%) | — |
| **NDFI criticized/classified** | 0.8% (down from 5.5%) | — |

**Critical detail — ZION's NDFI section disclosure (verbatim):**
> "Total NDFI net charge-offs primarily included a $50 million charge-off recorded in the third quarter of 2025 associated with revolving lines of credit extended to two related commercial borrowers to finance the origination and purchase of commercial and residential mortgages. This resulted from a review of the borrowers, guarantors, and associated collateral, which identified apparent irregularities and misrepresentations. As a result, legal action has been initiated to pursue recovery of the outstanding amounts owed from the guarantors of the credits."

**Risk factor disclosure (verbatim):**
> "we recently incurred significant credit losses in connection with revolving lines of credit extended to two related commercial borrowers to finance the origination and purchase of commercial and residential mortgages."

**Key observations:**
1. ZION never names Cantor or Stupin anywhere in the 10-K — uses euphemistic "two related commercial borrowers"
2. Uses "apparent irregularities and misrepresentations" rather than "fraud"
3. No update beyond the Q3 2025 $50M charge-off — no additional reserves or recoveries disclosed
4. Pursuing legal action against **guarantors** (not the borrowers directly), suggesting borrower recovery is unlikely
5. NDFI classified ratio dropped from 5.5% to 0.8% — the charge-off cleaned the ratio by removing the assets

## 4. Allowance for Credit Losses (ACL) — MD&A

| | ZION | WAL |
|---|---|---|
| **Total ACL** | $724M (1.19% of loans) | — |
| **ACL trend** | Down from $741M (1.25%) YoY | $29.6M Cantor reserve unchanged |

| **Key ACL drivers YoY** | +$58M from more adverse scenario weightings; -$78M from reduced CRE portfolio-specific risks; +$3M from loan growth | — |
| **Provision** | $72M (flat YoY) | — |
| **Net charge-offs** | $89M (0.15%) vs $60M (0.10%) prior year | — |
| **Cantor impact on ACL** | Not separately quantified in ACL discussion — absorbed into overall metrics | Reserve specifically identified |

**Notable:** ZION's ACL actually *decreased* YoY despite the $50M fraud charge-off, because reduced CRE risks (-$78M) more than offset both the fraud loss and more adverse scenario weightings (+$58M). The $50M was a charge-off (P&L hit), not an ongoing reserve — ZION took the loss and moved on.

## 5. Analytical Comparison

| Dimension | ZION | WAL |
|---|---|---|
| **Fraud recognition approach** | Charge-off (took the loss immediately) | Reserve (holding, not yet charged off) |
| **Loss severity** | $50M charged off = definitive loss | $29.6M reserved on $98.5M = 30% expected loss |
| **Disclosure specificity** | Euphemistic, no names, buried in NDFI subsection | Mentions fraud in risk factors |
| **Auditor treatment** | No emphasis, no fraud CAM, clean opinion | No emphasis, no fraud CAM, clean opinion |
| **Post-Q3 update** | None — no new information in 10-K beyond Q3 disclosure | Reserve unchanged 6 months |
| **Recovery posture** | Suing guarantors | — |

**Bottom line:** Both auditors (Big 4 EY and mid-tier RSM) treated the Cantor fraud as immaterial to the overall audit opinion. Neither elevated it to a CAM or added emphasis-of-matter language. ZION took a more aggressive loss recognition approach (immediate $50M charge-off) compared to WAL's holding pattern ($29.6M reserve on $98.5M, unchanged). ZION's refusal to name Cantor/Stupin anywhere in their 10-K is notable and suggests ongoing litigation sensitivity.
