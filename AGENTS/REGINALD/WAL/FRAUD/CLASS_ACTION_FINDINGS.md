# WAL Class Action / Investigation Findings

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-27
**Research Scope:** Rosen Law Firm, Shamis & Gentile, Portnoy Law — actions/investigations re: Western Alliance Bancorporation (NYSE: WAL)

---

## Summary

As of this research date, **no actual class action complaint has been filed** against WAL by these firms. Both Rosen Law Firm and Shamis & Gentile announced **investigations** (not lawsuits) following WAL's October 16, 2025 disclosure of the Cantor Group V fraud. Portnoy Law's website has no active WAL page (404). These are ambulance-chasing investigation announcements — standard securities plaintiff's bar activity after a stock drop — not filed complaints with specific factual allegations.

## Answers to Specific Questions

### (1) What specific factual allegations do the complaints make?

**There are no filed complaints.** The investigation announcements contain no factual allegations beyond what WAL itself disclosed:
- WAL sued Cantor Group V LLC in August 2025 alleging borrower fraud related to collateral loans
- Stock dropped ~11% on October 16, 2025 when this was disclosed
- Shamis & Gentile adds the theory that WAL's prior statements about "stable asset quality," "declining criticized assets," and "disciplined credit culture" were misleading given the undisclosed Cantor exposure

**No new facts, no witness statements, no internal documents, no whistleblower allegations.**

### (2) Do they mention auditor quality, RSM, or internal controls?

**No.** Neither Rosen nor Shamis & Gentile mentions:
- RSM (WAL's auditor)
- Auditor quality or audit failures
- Internal controls over financial reporting
- Any audit-related concerns

### (3) Do they allege material weakness in ICFR?

**No.** No mention of ICFR, material weakness, or internal control deficiencies in any announcement.

### (4) Any new facts about the Cantor/Stupin fraud not in WAL's own disclosures?

**No.** Both firms' announcements are entirely derivative of WAL's own October 16, 2025 disclosure. They add zero new factual information about:
- The nature/mechanics of the Cantor fraud
- Boris Stupin's involvement
- The scope of exposure
- How the fraud was perpetrated or discovered
- Any other borrowers involved

## Assessment

These are **boilerplate securities investigation announcements** — the plaintiff's bar equivalent of ambulance chasing after a significant stock drop. Key indicators:

1. **"N/A" for class period and court** (Rosen) — meaning no complaint has been drafted, let alone filed
2. **No PACER filings found** — no federal securities fraud complaint appears to have been filed
3. **Generic language** — standard "may have issued materially misleading business information" template
4. **No new factual content** — everything derives from WAL's own 8-K/earnings call disclosure

**Bottom line for the WAL/fraud thesis:** These investigation announcements add nothing to what WAL has already disclosed. They do not provide evidence of auditor failures, internal control weaknesses, or undisclosed fraud facts. If/when an actual complaint is filed, it would likely appear on PACER and Stanford Securities Class Action Clearinghouse — worth monitoring but nothing actionable exists yet.
