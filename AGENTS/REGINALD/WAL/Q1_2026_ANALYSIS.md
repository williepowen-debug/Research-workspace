## 1. THE TWO FRAUD CREDITS — V2 CONFIRMED

### 1a. LAM charge-off — $126.4M

**Press release verbatim:** *"The provision for credit losses during the first quarter 2026 was primarily driven by higher net charge-offs totaling $208.5 million, which included a charge-off of $126.4 million for the remaining balance of the LAM loan..."*

**What is LAM?** Leucadia Asset Management. Leucadia National Corp merged with Jefferies in 2013; LAM was Leucadia's alternative asset management platform and continued as a Jefferies subsidiary operating multi-strategy funds. **This credit is on the Jefferies/MFS contagion rail.**

**Key facts:**
- Charge-off is *"remaining balance"* — implies reserved portion previously built; this is the final write.
- $126.4M single-credit charge-off at a $59B loan book = 21bps of loans, one credit.
- C&I charge-offs totalled $181.4M for the quarter — LAM is ~70% of the C&I NCO line.

**Thesis mapping:**
- V2 (Jefferies/fraud) was documented in STATUS with Cantor focus. **LAM was not on REGINALD's radar** as a named credit — this is net new.
- Confirms V2 is broader than the single Cantor exposure: WAL had *two* separate fraud rails active, both now resolved in Q1.
- Pulls forward the question: **are there OTHER Jefferies/Leucadia-era credits in the WAL book?** Needs transcript and 10-Q Table 16.

### 1b. Cantor Group V charge-off — $26.1M

**Press release verbatim:** *"a $26.1 million charge-off from the specific reserve previously established on the Cantor Group V, LLC ('Cantor') loan."*

**STATUS baseline reconciliation:**
- STATUS had Cantor $98M, 30% reserved (~$29.4M reserve) vs ZION 83% reserved.
- $26.1M of the $29.4M reserve converted to a charge-off. **Reserve coverage was substantially complete — utilized ~89% of the specific reserve.**
- Remaining Cantor balance (~$72M original principal minus $26.1M realization) still on book. Protected by **$13M senior liens** (press release: *"Senior Liens: $13M in senior liens acquired to protect position on Cantor loan"*).

**Thesis mapping:**
- V2 Cantor leg is *partially* resolved. Residual $46-72M still exposed depending on recovery. Transcript needed for recovery posture.
- WAL now explicitly moved from "underreserved relative to peers" (ZION 83% vs WAL 30%) to "took the charge." ZION still hasn't; ZION's $42M CRE nonaccrual line may already capture this.
- Senior lien acquisition = aggressive recovery posture. Management intends to work the estate.

### 1c. Management language — "fraud-related"

**Vecchione verbatim:** *"Western Alliance achieved solid first quarter results featuring robust deposit growth, net interest margin expansion, and core earnings momentum, while taking decisive action to resolve two fraud-related credits, partially offset by gains from a series of well-executed security sales."*

**Why this matters:**
- *"Fraud-related"* — management PUBLICLY labeling. No euphemism. No "commercial disputes." No "isolated counterparty event." Direct.
- *"Decisive action to resolve"* — signaling closure, not open-ended uncertainty.
- *"Partially offset by gains from… security sales"* = $50.5M from securities (discussed §2). Coordinated absorption.

**Disclosure grade:** A. Clean labeling beats FITB/MTB/CFG/PNC on fraud transparency. Matches RF's capital-markets-stress transparency on the credit side.
