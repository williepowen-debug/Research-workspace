## HEADLINE — 7 MATERIAL FINDINGS

### 1. 🔴 **WAL CROSSING $100B THRESHOLD NOW**

> Idnani: "Our balance sheet expanded in total by $6.1 billion from year-end to **just shy of $99 billion in assets**."

**Net-new thesis lever.** WAL's balance sheet is $98.9B — one quarter's growth from crossing $100B. Crossing triggers Category III/IV regulatory status → mandatory AOCI phase-in, enhanced liquidity, LCR, full stress testing.

- **AOCI position already a drag** — Idnani: "slight decline in total equity resulted from … rate-driven change in our AOCI position"
- **Jun 18 AOCI comment period closes** — STATUS had this flagged, but the $100B cross elevates urgency. If WAL breaches while AOCI rules finalize, the $1-2B unrealized loss re-enters capital in real time.
- **Basel III +81bps is NET POSITIVE** (Vecchione confirmed to Haire). ZION got +93bps. Same offset mechanism.
- **Management "does not expect capital ratios to meaningfully change from these levels over the near term"** — i.e., they're going to hold 11% CET1 through the $100B cross, not build a buffer first.

### 2. 🟢 **V2 FRAMED AS "LARGELY BEHIND US" — thesis challenge**

> Vecchione: "**Importantly, these matters are now largely behind us.** By removing these lingering distractions, we can refocus attention on the trajectory of our underlying operating performance."

Management is explicitly signaling LAM + Cantor are **closed chapters**, not leading indicators of more fraud. This is a direct challenge to V2 "fast-transmission = next episode unknown until hits" framing. If WAL delivers Q2/Q3 clean, mgmt's framing is validated. If another named credit surfaces, they're caught in a "now largely behind us" gaffe — leverage either way.

**Also important:** LAM charge-off was **pre-announced March 6, 2026** (Vecchione opening). Not a print-day surprise. Market has had 6 weeks to digest. Explains muted -2% tape vs "should have been -10%" expectation.

### 3. 🟢 **CORE NCO 25-35 BPS RE-CONFIRMED — "at or slightly above the midpoint"**

> Vecchione guide: "We reaffirm our core net charge-off guidance of 25-35 basis points … we anticipate full-year results will be at or slightly above the midpoint of this range, **with charge-offs declining in the back half of the year**."

Q1 ex-fraud ran 0.39% (39bps) — above the top end. Mgmt says decline is coming. Back-half decline is a commitment, not a hope. Falsifiable by Q2 NCO print.

### 4. 🟠 **BUYBACKS ENDING — NOT A PROGRAMMATIC LEVER**

> Vecchione (to McGratty): "**We're not expecting to go deep back into the market to do stock buybacks. They're not in our models right now.** If there is a reason for the stock, if it gets disrupted in the market, then we'll come back and look to support it as we did in Q1."

Q1-to-Apr-7 buybacks totaled $120.4M @ avg $76.55. **That's the budget.** Any further buybacks are opportunistic, not planned. Means short-term technical support from buyback flow is *off* going into Q2. Only stress-day dip-buying is expected.

Additional color: Vecchione cites **investment-grade rating defense** (Corporate Trust / Business Escrow / **Digital Asset Group** / Juris Banking all require IG rating) as the anchor on keeping CET1 at 11%. Plans to "slightly over time, continue to migrate that number upward." → **WAL is a capital builder, not distributor.**

### 5. 🟠 **HFI GROWTH DELIBERATELY THROTTLED — not demand-driven**

> Vecchione: "We **deliberately grew the HFS portfolio** with lower risk-adjusted weighting so we could repurchase shares or remain at our target CET1 ratio of 11%. This strategy afforded us the opportunity to **delay loan growth into Q2** and reevaluate the credit, macroeconomic, and geopolitical environments."

- HFI growth only 3.2% LQA (8% YoY)
- Total loan growth $903M, split nearly 50/50 HFI/HFS
- **Vecchione re-commits to $6B target but flags "we were just a little bit too conservative here"**
- Tim Bruckner (new addition): "we pulled back a little bit or showed some hesitancy … in some of the asset-specific finance-oriented segments, predominantly the **commercial real estate related segment**"

**Thesis read:** WAL pulled back on CRE-adjacent finance specifically in Q1. Defensive posture despite "past peak stress" messaging. Growth substituting into HFS (mortgages warehoused) to stay CET1-neutral. **This is a management that's acting defensively while talking confident.**

### 6. 🟢 **DEPOSIT OPTIMIZATION = ACTIVE SHRINKING OF WAREHOUSE DEPOSITS**

> Vecchione: "We are trying to finesse the deposit growth and deposit pricing in this bank. **We are starting with warehouse lending where we have some of the higher cost deposits. We are going to work with our clients to see if we can move some of those higher priced deposits out of the bank.**"

- Q2 deposits: explicitly FLAT (management "finesse operation")
- Q3: seasonally high production
- Q4: less runoff because the push-out happens in Q2
- Target: LTD ratio back to mid-70s by year-end (currently 71.5%)
- **Mortgage warehouse deposits specifically being capped — Vecchione says held FLAT for the year**

Elian (JPM) flagged this as unusual: "I don't think I've heard that before, right, for a company that has grown as fast as you do." Confirms this is NEW behavior. Rate-cut removal is main driver (more than half the $115M deposit cost increase comes from pulling 2 rate cuts out of the forecast per Idnani).

### 7. 🟡 **NDFI / LENDER FINANCE RECONCILIATION (Gary Tenner / Janet Lee Q&A)**

Slide 24 "Business Credit Intermediaries" bucket ≈ 5% of loan book ≈ **~$3B**
Slide 20 Lender Finance = **$2.3B within that category**
- Top fund-level credit: **$60M commitment / $30M+ funded**
- ~40 major funds
- <$2M avg funded per underlying obligor across ~2,000 obligors / 50+ facilities
- **<5% tech/software exposure** (under $115M on $2.3B)
- **WAL serves as trustee on ~60% of the lender finance book** — Idnani: "helps us a lot in terms of oversight and monitoring the cash flows in and out"

**Critical gap:** Janet Lee asked for count of exposures >$100M. Vecchione: **"No, we're not going to share that."** Explicit refusal. Fund-level exposures above $100M exist but remain undisclosed.

**LAM reconciliation puzzle:** LAM charge was $126.4M — **2x the stated $60M top-end commitment.** Either (a) LAM was outside the "lender finance" category (possibly note finance, structured credit, or a different bucket), or (b) the $60M top-end is the CURRENT ongoing book post-LAM charge and LAM was dropped out. Worth probing in the deck detail.

## Q&A — CRITICAL EXCHANGES

### Clark / Piper on Cantor resolution

- $29.6M reserve validated by current as-is appraisals
- All appraisal values held; liens in front less than expected
- **Charged $26.5M (headline: $26.1M press release)** — reflective of strategies to collect equity behind buildings
- **Recoveries excluded from charge math:**
  - UHNW springing guarantees
  - Mortgage bond up to **$20M after $5M deductible** = $15M max additional
- Vecchione: "We don't see any other charge-offs or reserves coming from this point"
- "We're not going to talk about this every quarter"

### Haire / Autonomous on Basel III

- "All in, we expect it based on the rules that we're reading to **increase our CET1 by 81 basis points**."

### Braziler / UBS on past-peak credit vs special mention increase

- Bruckner: special mention is "transitional rating" — directed early-warning tool
- Vecchione: "Special mention increase in $75 million is really no big whoop"
- Framing: peer-comparison dodge ("3rd best of 22") rather than addressing the trend at WAL
- **ACL building 78 → low 80s is automatic from C&I mix shift, not voluntary conservatism**

### Spahr / Wells Fargo on reserves to 1%

- "It would take a deterioration in the economy, and we're not seeing that. The economy is strong."
- Dismissive. Half of portfolio "really has never had a loss"
- Idnani clarifier: ex-CLN resi $8B, the ACL is already 1.00%

### Tenner / Davidson + Lee / TD Cowen on Slide 20 detail

- Covered in Finding #7
- Lee: "are you able to share the number of exposures that are over $100 million"
- Vecchione: **"No, we're not going to share that."**

### Von Gizycki / Deutsche Bank on LAM residual $26M

- $50M securities + $50M expense savings = $100M mitigation
- **$26M of the $126M NOT mitigated**
- Vecchione: "you and many of your colleagues were suggesting that we shouldn't fully resolve the $126 million charge-off … to ensure that we have enough money available for product development, enhanced services, and also to ensure that our loan growth and deposit growth continues"
- "We only offered $100M against the $126M as a solve"
- **Mgmt explicitly chose to let $26M hit tangible book by analyst-consensus advice**

### Elian / JPM on deposit exit

- "I don't think I've heard that before, right, for a company that has grown as fast as you do"
- Vecchione candid: "Deposits came in from some of our higher priced customers"
