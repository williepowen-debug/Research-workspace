## 1. Management Outlook — Revised (Slide 17)

**The forward guide is THE key new disclosure in this deck.** Transcript referenced it but didn't detail.

| Metric | 2025 Actual | Original 2026 Outlook | **Revised 2026 Outlook** | Commentary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Loans HFI | $58.7B | Up $6.0B | **Up $6.0B** | Unchanged |
| Deposits | $77.2B | Up $8.0B | **Up $8.0B** | "Strong Q1 positions us for deposit optimization" |
| CET1 | 11.0% | ~11% | **~11%** | "Opportunistically repurchased $50M in Q1" |
| NII | $2.86B | Up 11-14% (assumed 2 rate cuts) | **Up 11-14% (assumes NO rate cuts)** | "Tracking to upper end. Variable-rate loans benefit from fewer rate cuts." |
| Non-interest Income | $678M | Up 2-4% | **Up 20-25%** (ex $51M gains: +13-17%) | "Strong Q1 from Juris Banking" |
| NIE Total | $2.11B | Up 2-7% | **Up 7-11%** | "Higher variable comp for production" |
| NIE ex Deposit Costs | $1,481M | $1,620-1,670M | **$1,600-1,650M** | Opex mitigation |
| Deposit Costs | $631M | $535-585M | **$650-700M** | "Fewer rate cuts + Q1 outperformance" |
| NCO | 24 bps | 25-35 bps | **25-35 bps (EXCLUDES LAM/Cantor)** | Management sticks to range |
| Effective Tax Rate | 18% | ~19% | **~19%** | Unchanged |

### Key interpretations

**The NCO guide is the one to watch.**
- Q1-26 adjusted NCO = **39bps** (ex LAM + Cantor). Above top of 25-35bps guide.
- For full-year 25-35bps to hold, Q2-Q4 must average 22-33bps — a step DOWN from Q1.
- Reconciliation: Q1 had $27.7M CRE-NOO charge-off. If that was one-time (single credit), ex-that the quarter runs cleaner.
- **Guide is tight. If Q2 delivers another elevated CRE-NOO quarter, guide breaks.** This is the Q2 earnings test.

**NII is the bull narrative.**
- Original outlook: +11-14% with 2 rate cuts built in.
- Revised: +11-14% with NO rate cuts.
- Implication: WAL benefits from the "higher for longer" environment — variable-rate loans keep earning, and ECR drag is bounded.
- If rates DO cut, NII outlook tightens. If they don't cut, WAL hits upper end.
- This is a **reversal of the cohort narrative** — most banks guide lower NII on "no cuts." WAL guides upper end.

**Juris Banking is a real earnings driver, not noise.**
- Raised non-interest income guide from +2-4% to +13-17% (ex security gains).
- Slide 17 commentary: "Strong Q1 performance from Juris Banking."
- Q1 non-interest income jumped to $252.6M vs $127.4M Q1-25 (+97% YoY). Juris + mortgage banking recovery together.

**Expense trajectory is the friction.**
- NIE total guide raised +2-7% → +7-11%.
- Higher variable comp (production) + Juris expense.
- Deposit costs guide RAISED $115-120M ($535-585M → $650-700M).
- Net impact: opex expansion offsets some of the NII and non-interest income upside.
