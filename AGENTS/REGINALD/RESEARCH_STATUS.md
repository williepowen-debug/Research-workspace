## TRANSMISSION MECHANISM (REVISED)

```
OLD UNDERSTANDING:
  Japan sells CLOs → Supply glut → Spreads widen → US bank marks decline

NEW UNDERSTANDING:
  Japan is BUYING CLOs (anchoring spreads)

  TRIGGER: US credit cycle deteriorates
    → Corporate defaults rise
    → Leveraged loan prices fall
    → CLO tranches mark down
    → Japan investment trust NAVs collapse
    → Japan banks redeem trusts (forced selling)
    → CLO spreads blow out (Japan bid disappears)
    → US regional banks (esp. RF) take AOCI hits
    → BDC draws on bank lines accelerate
    → Regional bank liquidity stress
```

The risk is a **correlated withdrawal** triggered by US credit deterioration, not a unilateral Japan selling decision.
