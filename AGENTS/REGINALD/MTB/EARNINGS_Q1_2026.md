## CRE — THE CORE READ-THROUGH

### Balances
- **Total CRE: $23.5B** (down 3% QoQ from ~$24.1B). Paydowns moderating but volume soft Jan-Feb.
- **March CRE originations: >$1B** — management confident CRE will grow from here.
- Bible: "We had over $1 billion in originations in March. We are off to a great start in the second quarter."
- Five distinct CRE lines: (1) regional portfolio, (2) originate-and-sell (RCC), (3) institutional CRE/REIT, (4) affordable housing, (5) warehouse.
- RCC had "record performance last year" — off-balance-sheet CRE growing via fee income.

### Credit Quality
- **Criticized loans: $6.6B** (down from $7.3B Dec, ~$7.8B prior). **$700M+ reduction.**
  - CRE criticized: -$400M
  - C&I criticized: -$300M+
- Bible on drivers: "broad-based improvement in operating performance, and some borrowers are paying off."
- **No office-specific disclosure.** Office NPL trend, at-risk %, sector breakdown — none provided and **not asked by any analyst.**

### Provision Signal 🔴
- **$140M provision vs ~$125M Q4 = +12% increase.**
- Meanwhile NCO fell from 54bps to 31bps and ACL held at 1.53%.
- **Interpretation:** Provision > NCO = management is NET BUILDING reserves. They expect future losses to exceed current charge-offs. This is the single most bearish data point in the print, and nobody asked about it on the Q&A.
- **Read-through:** If MTB (CRE/Tier 1 128%, actively de-risking, best-in-class NCO) is building reserves, what does that imply for OZK (358%, NOT de-risking, NCO 118bps, ACL 1.16% BELOW NCO rate)?

### CRE Cycle Tone
- Bible was cautious-optimistic, not "turning the corner" triumphalist.
- "I have been saying that for a couple of quarters, so you probably do not believe me anymore" — self-aware that the CRE growth call has been premature.
- Management framing: CRE balances still falling, but origination activity strong, revenue growing via fees even if balance sheet flat.
- **No mention of CRE maturity wall, extension risk, or loss severity trends.**

## CAPITAL & BUYBACKS

### CET1 Trajectory
- **10.33%** (-51bps QoQ). Drivers: $1.25B buybacks + RWA increase, partially offset by earnings.
- **Target: moving to bottom of range (10.0%).** Bible: "continued improvement in asset quality" justifies lower capital.
- Without buybacks: accretes ~25bps/quarter. So $1.25B in buybacks consumed ~76bps of capital generation.
- **Bible explicitly said they would pause buybacks if stress emerges:** "If we see signs of stress, we will stop buybacks and accrete capital."

### Regulatory Capital Proposal (ERBA)
- **~90bps CET1 benefit** under standardized approach from lower RWAs (LTV-driven — MTB's conservative lending helps).
- Additional 10-20bps under expanded risk-based approach.
- Bible: likely to adopt if advantage holds post-comment period. Not committed yet.
- **Rating agency reaction** cited as key gating factor — agencies may not give full credit for RWA reduction.

### AOCI
- **Only 4bps impact on CET1 fully phased-in.** MTB has minimal unrealized losses.
- Securities portfolio: **unrealized pre-tax GAIN of $9M.** Duration 3.8 years (well managed).
- **Read-through:** This is a STARK contrast to OZK/WAL, which face Cat III/IV AOCI recognition with likely much larger underwater positions. MTB's securities book is NOT a problem.

## NIM & FUNDING

### Margin
- **NIM 3.71%** (+2bps QoQ). Driven by: +8bps from fixed-rate repricing + deposit discipline + swap portfolio; -6bps from lower free funds (buyback impact + rate effect).
- **FY2026 guide: NII $7.2-7.35B, NIM high 3.60s.** Modest QoQ step-down implied — 3.71% now, "high 360s" full year = some NIM compression ahead.
- Loan yields: -14bps to 5.86% (repricing headwind on the asset side).
- Securities yield: +9bps to 4.26% (reinvestment tailwind).

### Deposits
- Average deposits: -$800M to $164.3B (decline is brokered, not core).
- NIB deposits: **+$400M to $44.6B** — growing. Good sign.
- IB deposit costs: **-21bps to 1.96%** — strong discipline.
- Deposit beta: 56% (cutting cycle); expected low-to-mid 50s continuing.
- Bible: "customer deposits outpacing loan growth by more than $1 billion."

### Borrowings 🟠
- **Analyst Siefers (Piper Sandler) flagged short-term borrowings "about as high as I can remember."**
- Bible deflected: "managing to short-term ratios" + "ICS business volatility" (institutional cash sweep — deposits come and go).
- **REGINALD read:** Elevated borrowings + deposit decline + aggressive buybacks = balance sheet is being managed at thinner margins. LCR 107% is above minimum but not excess.

## WEBSTER ANOMALY

Bible on Q1 call: "Fund banking and capital call lines is a business **we acquired from Webster.**"

**Problem:** M&T Bank has never acquired Webster Bank (WBS). They acquired:
- People's United Financial (closed Apr 2, 2022, $8.3B all-stock)
- Provident Bankshares, Wilmington Trust, Hudson City Bancorp, People's United — none named "Webster"

**Possible explanations:**
1. **Misspeak** — meant People's United. Most likely. But this is the CFO speaking on a recorded earnings call about a material business line.
2. **Person named Webster** — a team lead or executive whose name became shorthand internally. Bible may have used internal jargon on an external call.
3. **Sub-acquisition** — People's United may have acquired a fund banking team or book from Webster Financial before the MTB merger. Possible but unverifiable without People's United M&A history.
4. **Intentional misdirection** — lowest probability, but if the fund banking book has problems, misdirecting provenance muddies the trail.

**Resolution path:** Pull People's United 2020-2021 investor materials for any mention of fund banking capabilities or team acquisitions. Also check if Webster Financial (WBS) sold any business lines 2019-2022.

**REGINALD flag:** A CFO misidentifying the origin of a multi-billion-dollar business line on an earnings call is a disclosure-quality signal regardless of the reason. Flag to Will.
