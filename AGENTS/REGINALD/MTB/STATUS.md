## CRE De-Risking Story

MTB has been the poster child for proactive CRE management:
- CRE concentration: 183% → 148% → 128% of Tier 1 (over 18 months)
- Sold $661M residential builder/developer portfolio (Jun 2025)
- Deliberately refusing to match "aggressive pricing and structure" from competitors
- Criticized CRE declining ~$671M per quarter
- Management expects CRE portfolio to bottom in Q1 2026

**The contrast with our targets is stark:** MTB is shrinking CRE at ~$1B/quarter. OZK hasn't shrunk RESG meaningfully. WAL is relabeling CRE as C&I (MI3 growing 15.5% → 24.2%).
