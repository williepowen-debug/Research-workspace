## EXPOSURE MATRIX

### Legend
- 🔴 = HIGH exposure (confirmed, material)
- 🟠 = ELEVATED exposure (confirmed, moderate)
- 🟡 = WATCH (potential exposure, needs verification)
- ⬜ = LOW/NONE or UNKNOWN
- ❓ = Research gap — needs data

### Channel Key
1. **CRE** = Commercial Real Estate concentration
2. **NDFI** = Non-Depository Financial Institution / Auto warehouse exposure
3. **DC** = Federal employment / DC corridor exposure
4. **BDC** = Business Development Company credit lines
5. **CONS** = Consumer credit (auto loans, credit cards)
6. **FHLB** = Federal Home Loan Bank dependency
7. **GEO** = Geographic concentration (FL, TX border, distressed markets)
8. **CLO** = CLO holdings concentration

## SCORING METHODOLOGY

**Overlap Score Calculation:**
- Each 🔴 = 3 points
- Each 🟠 = 2 points
- Each 🟡 = 1 point
- Sum across 8 channels

**Tier Assignment:**
- **Tier 1 (Maximum Overlap):** Score ≥8 OR 3+ channels at 🔴/🟠
- **Tier 2 (Elevated):** Score 5-7 OR 2 channels at 🔴/🟠
- **Tier 3 (Monitoring):** Score 2-4
- **Green:** Score ≤1
