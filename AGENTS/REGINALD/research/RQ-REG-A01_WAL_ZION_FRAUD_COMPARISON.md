## Executive Summary

WAL and ZION were both victims of the **same fraud ring** (Stupin/Cantor Group) using the **same scheme** (collateral subordination). Yet their accounting responses differ dramatically:

| Bank | Exposure | Loss Recognition | % Recognized |
|------|----------|------------------|--------------|
| **ZION** | $60M | $50M charge-off | **83%** |
| **WAL** | $98.6M | $30M reserve | **30%** |

**Key Finding:** WAL appears under-provisioned by $20-50M. Either WAL has genuinely superior collateral, or losses will be recognized in future quarters.

---

## The Fraud Scheme

### Perpetrators
- **Andrew Stupin** — Real estate investor, fund manager
- **Gerald Marcil** — Fund manager, Cantor Group
- **Deba Shyam** — Associate

### Mechanism
1. Created multiple "Cantor Group" investment funds (II, IV, V, etc.)
2. Obtained secured loans from regional banks for distressed mortgage purchases
3. Banks were promised **first-priority collateral interest**
4. Perpetrators secretly **subordinated the collateral** to other creditors (themselves)
5. Underlying properties were transferred or went into foreclosure
6. Collateral became "irretrievably lost"
7. Banks discovered fraud only after Western Alliance filed lawsuit in August 2025

### Total Known Exposure
- **$270M+** across ZION, WAL, BANC, and others
- Same scheme, same perpetrators, multiple bank victims

## The Asymmetry Problem

### Same Fraud, Different Treatment

| Factor | ZION | WAL |
|--------|------|-----|
| Fraud ring | Stupin/Cantor | Stupin/Cantor |
| Scheme | Collateral subordination | Collateral subordination |
| Exposure | $60M | $98.6M |
| Loss recognized | $50M (83%) | $30M (30%) |

### Possible Explanations

**Scenario A: WAL Has Better Collateral (Benign)**
- WAL's Cantor Group V loan has different/superior collateral structure
- Personal guarantees from creditworthy guarantors
- CRE collateral genuinely in first position

**Scenario B: WAL Is Masking (Concerning)**
- Same fraud scheme means same collateral manipulation
- WAL's "belief" in collateral is same belief ZION had before discovery
- Future quarters will require additional recognition
- Implied future charge-off: **$50-68M** (to match ZION's 83% rate)
