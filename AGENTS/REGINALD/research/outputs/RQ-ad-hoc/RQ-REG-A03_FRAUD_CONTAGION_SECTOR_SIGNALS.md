## Early Warning Signals: Where's the Next Fraud?

### Signal 1: Repossessed Asset Spikes
WAL's repossessed assets went from $52M to $137M (+163% YoY). Sharp increases in repos signal collateral recovery on bad loans.

**Watch for:** Repossessed asset changes >50% YoY in bank filings

### Signal 2: Provision Trajectory Breaks
When provisions suddenly jump without clear explanation, fraud may be hidden inside "qualitative overlays."

**Watch for:** Provision increases that don't match disclosed NPL trends

### Signal 3: Lawsuit Filings
Stupin fraud came to light via WAL lawsuit (August 2025). ZION lawsuit followed.

**Watch for:** Bank litigation disclosures in 8-Ks

### Signal 4: "One-Time" Charge-Off Language
Banks often call fraud losses "one-time" or "idiosyncratic" to avoid sector contagion.

**Watch for:** Multiple banks using similar language = pattern, not idiosyncratic

### Signal 5: CEO Warning Signals
Dimon's "cockroach" comment is a tell. When major bank CEOs warn publicly, they've already found more.

**Watch for:** Defensive language in earnings calls about credit quality

## The "Idiosyncratic" Narrative Problem

### What Banks Are Saying
- BlackRock CFO: "Idiosyncratic pockets of stress... don't look like broad stresses"
- Goldman CFO: "Robust upfront due diligence"
- Citi CFO: "No exposure to any of those things"

### Why This Matters
This is the exact narrative pattern from pre-2008:
1. First failure called "idiosyncratic"
2. Second failure is "coincidence"
3. Third failure prompts "sector review"
4. Cascade follows when pattern recognized

The October 2025 cascade (First Brands, Tricolor, Stupin) already hit stage 3.
