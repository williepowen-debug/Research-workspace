## VLY's HOA/Condo Association Lending Business

### The Double-Edged Sword

VLY has a **dedicated community association lending division** that provides:
- Loans and lines of credit to HOAs/condo associations
- Treasury management solutions (CDARS, money market sweeps)
- Funding for milestone inspection repairs

**Collateral Structure:** Association loans secured by **assignment of assessment rights**, NOT physical property. Bank can step into association's shoes to collect dues if default occurs.

### Risk Factors

1. **Delinquency Threshold**: Bank requires <10% of units 30+ days delinquent; >15% = building likely "non-warrantable"
2. **Blacklist Exposure**: 1,440 FL associations now on Fannie/Freddie blacklist (~6% of total)
3. **Strategic Default Risk**: If 20%+ of unit owners default on assessments, association could default on master loan from VLY

### Underwriting Criteria
- Delinquency ratios (<5-10%)
- Reserve funding adequacy per SIRS
- Insurance viability (Citizens/private carrier)
- Litigation history

## Insurance Stress Transmission

### The Escrow Spiral

1. Annual escrow analysis reveals insurance premium spike
2. Creates "escrow shortage" or "deficiency"
3. Borrower monthly payment increases
4. If borrower can't pay → Lender-Placed Insurance (LPI) at 3-5x normal rates
5. LPI cost further compromises ability to pay → potential foreclosure

### DSCR Impact

Formula: DSCR = NOI / Annual Debt Service

When insurance costs rise 30-50%, NOI falls, compressing DSCR. VLY's FL multifamily already at 1.41x (tight) vs. 2.08x for office.

## De-Risking Strategy

### Brookfield Transaction (Dec 2024)

VLY sold **$1B ($925M net) in performing CRE loans** to Brookfield Asset Management at only **1% discount to par**.

**Strategic Impact:**
- Accelerated concentration reduction (400% → 333%)

## Stress Scenarios for VLY

### 1. Miami Condo Cascade
- 45% of FL exposure in Miami MSA
- If blacklisted associations surge → HOA loan defaults
- Collateral (assessment rights) worthless if owners abandon units

### 2. Insurance-Driven NOI Compression
- Multifamily DSCR already at 1.41x
- Another 20-30% insurance hike → DSCR falls below 1.0x
- Refinancing impossible at maturity

### 3. Maturity Wall (2026)
- Nationwide: $539B CRE maturing in 2026
- VLY borrowers who originated at low rates face "refinance shock"
- Particularly acute for Miami office/retail
