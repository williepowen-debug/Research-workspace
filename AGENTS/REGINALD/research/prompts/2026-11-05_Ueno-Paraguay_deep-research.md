You are a forensic bank and emerging-markets credit analyst. Research the failure of Ueno Bank in Paraguay. Your job is to answer three things: how the bank really failed, whether the crisis spreads beyond one bank, and whether any route reaches US banks, US credit funds or wider emerging-market credit. Be equally willing to conclude it stays local. A well-supported "this is contained" is a good result.

## What is already known (correct it if a primary source disagrees)

- **Size [12/31/25, Fitch]:** 8.7% of system deposits, 5.8% of loans, ~2.7M clients, the most of any Paraguayan bank; about 3M customers by 2026. Merged with Visión Banco in 2024. NPLs 0.4%. Fitch core capital 15.2%; 10.3% at June 2026. Rated BB.
- **Timeline:** Supervisors ordered related-company loans back under the legal limit, 20% of capital, on 6/5/26. Fitch kept BB with a negative outlook on 10/16. The banks association asked the central bank on ~10/18 to report on the solvency of entities that received facilities in merger processes. A $100M capital plan was announced 10/29, the same day a central bank director resigned. The central bank (BCP) began resolution on 11/2 after adjustments put the solvency index below 50% of the required minimum. Carlos Carvallo, BCP governor and former chair of Financiera Ueno, resigned at the president's request. Fitch reportedly cut Ueno to D and senior debt to C/RR6 (secondary; verify). President Santiago Peña announced his resignation by video on 11/5; Congress must still accept it, and VP Pedro Alliana is the expected successor.
- **Failure mechanism as reported:** lending to owner-linked companies, and provisions on "loan documentation and IT services recorded as assets without sufficient supporting documentation". A loan sale used to cure the limit breach is under investigation as possible prohibited indirect financing. No figure for the capital hole has been published.
- **Debt and owners:** $350M 6.70% senior notes due 2031 (CUSIP 903934AB3, 144A/Reg S, Luxembourg listing, BofA and JPMorgan bookrunners, closed 3/6/26). Reported ~$510M of international financing including Finance in Motion, OeEB, DW Markets; also Triodos and GAWA. OTP Bank invested $47M for 9.99% of parent U Holdings S.A.R.L. (Luxembourg). A judge froze about $70M of assets of U Paraguay, U Holdings, Grupo Vázquez and directors. Eight detention orders were reported, including CEO Federico Miguel Vázquez Villasanti and chairman Juan Manuel Gustale; these are allegations.
- **Public money:** $800–850M, including the state pension and health fund IPS at $355–380M, about 9% of its investments.
- **Depositors:** guarantee ~₲228.3M (~$38,000) per person, per institution, ~97% of depositors covered; 15 days, extendable once. Advance of up to ₲10M. A Senate bill for a larger advance was reported; final status unverified.
- **Funds:** BCP Resolution 0015 suspended redemptions at 13 funds from five managers (CADIEM, Puente, ueno, BNB Asset Management, Basa). Resolution 0016 values Ueno financial and subordinated bonds at zero, stopping interest accrual, from 11/2. This is a prescribed valuation, not proof of zero recovery. Reopening from 11/5 is conditional, fund by fund.
- **US registered funds [SEC N-PORT, reports dated 3/31–8/31/26]:** 17 series, $17.9M face, 5.1% of the issue; maximum 1.22% of a fund. Largest: Western Asset Emerging Markets Debt $6.6M.
- **Prices:** bond 99 [March], 91.6 [7/31], 94.9 [8/31], ~86–87 [late October]. Sovereign spread reportedly 119 → 138bp. Dollar reportedly 5,661–5,900 → 6,160–6,500 guaraní. All secondary; figures conflict.

## Questions, in priority order

**1. How big is the hole, and who pays?**
- Total assets, loans, deposits and equity at the last date available.
- Size of the capital shortfall, related-party book, and unsupported assets.
- What are the resolution steps under Law 2334/03? Is a bridge bank, deposit transfer or liquidation planned? Who buys the good assets?
- How large is the deposit guarantee fund against about 97% of depositors? Can it pay in cash, or does it need Treasury or BCP support? Has anyone actually been paid?
- What do uninsured depositors, IPS, public entities and bondholders each recover? What ranks ahead of the notes?

**2. Is any other Paraguayan bank in trouble?** (the most important question for contagion)
- Which other banks received "facilities in merger processes"? Has BCP answered the association's request? The October debate implied more than one.
- Deposit flows and withdrawals at other banks since 11/2; interbank market stress; BCP liquidity lines; the reported app outages at Banco Familiar, Continental and Atlas.
- Which other banks hold large public deposits or lent to Grupo Vázquez?
- Bank exposure to the 13 suspended funds and to Ueno paper.
- A reported 14-bank solvency request is single-source; confirm or kill it.

**3. How was this missed?**
- What did the 2026 offering memorandum disclose about related-party loans, intangibles, regulatory forbearance, and the 6/5 supervisory order timing?
- Who audited Ueno? Any qualification or change of auditor?
- What did Fitch and S&P see? Why BB on 10/16 with solvency below half the minimum two weeks later? How much of the failure came from a change in the rules applied, not in the assets?
- Was reported capital inflated by merger forbearance or goodwill from Visión Banco? What were the "IT services" assets?
- The bond fell to ~92 by July. Who knew what, and what moved it?
- What were the warnings in December 2025 Paraguayan press, including ABC Color's reporting on state deposits and on the worst-recovery loans at Ueno, BNF and Basa?

**4. Who holds the $332M of the bond we cannot see?**
- UCITS and European fund holdings, insurer filings, 13F-adjacent disclosures, Bloomberg holder data if public, and any bondholder group or counsel.
- Do any banks hold it on balance sheet? Any total-return swaps, repo or warehouse financing against it?
- Are BofA or JPMorgan left with inventory, or exposed to offering-disclosure claims? Which law governs?
- Post-failure price, trading volume, and any acceleration or missed coupon (the next coupon date).

**5. Is there any US bank link?**
- Who are Ueno's correspondent banks for dollars and trade finance? Did any US bank end the relationship earlier, and when?
- Credit-card network, processor or fintech ties in the US.
- Any OFAC or anti-money-laundering angle, given the 2023 US sanctions on Horacio Cartes and Hugo Velázquez. Do not imply a link without a document.
- Any US development-finance money (DFC, IDB Invest, IFC)?

**6. Sovereign and currency.**
- Verified guaraní and sovereign bond moves, with dated closes.
- BCP reserves, intervention and rate response. Does a large Treasury or IPS backstop change the debt path?
- Rating agency statements. Fitch had Paraguay BB+ positive in March; what do Moody's and S&P now say?
- Who holds Paraguay's sovereign bonds, and how much index weight is at stake? Is there any forced-selling risk from a downgrade or an investment-grade loss?

**7. Politics.**
- Status of the resignation, the succession and the impeachment. What can Alliana do? What does Horacio Cartes's position mean?
- Does the new government change the rescue? Protests, public order, any capital controls or deposit-freeze extension?
- Separate confirmed, reported and rumoured. Flag premature "resigned" headlines by time.

**8. Does it travel in the region?**
- Bolivia, Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay: bank links, depositors, and Itaipú-related flows.
- Any effect on Latin American digital banks or on investor appetite for debut EM bank issuers? New-issue concessions, pulled deals, spreads on peer names.
- Compare to Banco Master in Brazil (2025) and other recent related-party failures: what spread, what did not, how recovery ended up.

**9. Is there anything here for US banks?**
- Is there a structural lesson, or is this local? Compare reported NPLs, capital and ratings before failure with what regulators later found.
- Which public warning signs came first: related-party limits, deposit growth (54% in 2025), intangibles, public-deposit concentration, or governance?
- Would any of these be observable at US regionals? Say so only if the mechanism truly transfers.
