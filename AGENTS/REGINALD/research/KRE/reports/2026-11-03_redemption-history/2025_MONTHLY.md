## 2025 versus 2026 year to date

| Period | NAV change | Shares outstanding change | Total net assets change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full-year 2025 | +7.41% | −29.45% | $5.103B → $3.867B (−24.22%) |
| 2026 through 11/02 | +13.66% | −10.39% | $3.867B → $3.939B (+1.84%) |

The price/share-count divergence is therefore not new in 2026. In 2025, KRE's per-share NAV rose while the number of ETF shares contracted much more sharply than in 2026. Share-count contraction alone is not a reliable directional price or bank-credit signal.
