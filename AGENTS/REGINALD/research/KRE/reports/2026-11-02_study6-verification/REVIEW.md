## Limits that remain

1. **Window sensitivity is material.** The five-bank share ranges from 37% to 98% across the report's nearby start/end choices. The 73% figure belongs to the stated 8/3–10/30 window and is mostly a late-October result.
2. **This is accounting, not cause or duration.** It identifies holdings' contributions, not why their prices changed, whether weakness is temporary, or what returns come next.
3. **It does not explain co-movement.** KRE's correlation with the ex-five portfolio is reported at 0.824 and remains the lowest comparable window since 2015. The historical proxy uses fixed current constituents/weights and carries survivorship bias.
4. **Daily KBWB NAV is unavailable.** The fee/premium-discount/lending split of the 0.044 tracking residual is unresolved. Monthly N-PORT NAV checks support the reconstruction but do not close that decomposition.
5. **Other Study 6 claims remain outside this verification:** the traditional-bank basket, long HY regressions, company-level fundamental tests and any AI-specific credit explanation.
