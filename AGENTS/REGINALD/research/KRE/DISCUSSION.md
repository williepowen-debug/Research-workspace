## Working synthesis, not an owner thesis update

Observed: technology weakness, broad regional-bank rebound, weaker-than-usual KRE co-movement with larger banks, and uneven bank fundamentals. Rate relief and rotation are plausible contributors; their causal shares are unresolved. Technology stocks can fall even with strong industry demand if margins, investment costs or valuations disappoint.

Broad buying does not settle individual WAL/OZK/FLG credit questions. Conversely, individual troubled loans do not establish generalized banking distress without a demonstrated transmission mechanism. Preserve distinctions between actual credit resolution, reclassification, extensions and transfers, and measure economic cost.

Useful conditional tests discussed, not standing monitoring instructions:

- KRE holding gains alongside improved earnings expectations and comparable credit measures would strengthen the recovery interpretation.
- Holding relative strength through renewed yield pressure would suggest support beyond rate relief alone.
- Technology weakness spreading to banks and equal-weight equities, accompanied by wider credit spreads, would strengthen the broader-stress interpretation.
- Rising prices alongside worsening credit and delayed resolutions would warrant investigation of the expectations gap, not automatically supply a timing signal.
