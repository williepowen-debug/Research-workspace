# KRE price patterns through October 9 2026

**Written October 12, 2026.** Historical price windows end October 9. Inputs are the already saved October 12 Yahoo requests and October 9 State Street holdings. This is synthesis of existing research and newly calculated period comparisons; no new market-price or credit observation.

The clearest pattern is a substantial recent decline in KRE accompanied by more uniform weakness among the ten heaviest current holdings. The credit cause and whole-fund return contributions remain unresolved. Earlier relative winners, EGBN cleanup and the positive October 12 intraday snapshot are material counterevidence to treating every regional bank as deteriorating alike.

## Bank weakness relative to other equities

The [morning comparison](../../../reports/2026-10-12_morning-regional-bank-tape.md) shows July 10–October 9 KRE −8.01%, KBE −8.17%, SPY +3.13%, IWM −5.76% and XLF −1.76%. Banks weakened while the broad large-cap benchmark rose. That establishes sector-relative price weakness. Small-cap exposure and other factors can contribute; this comparison does not isolate loan losses, interest rates or earnings revisions as the cause.

## Outliers and the attribution gap

First Hawaiian's −18.06% three-month move stands out, followed by Atlantic Union at −10.13%. First Hawaiian's larger decline occurred before September 11; its later four-week decline was −4.14%, within the range of this group. United Community had the largest later-window decline among the ten, at −5.41%. The reason for any particular bank's move remains unverified in this pass.

Eight of the ten held up better than KRE over the full window. Their October 9 weights total only 11.34%, so a ranking of current holdings does not explain the whole fund's loss. Historical beginning weights, holdings changes and rebalances are needed to attribute fund returns. Applying October 9 weights to July–October returns would not establish actual historical contributions.

## Counterevidence and limits

EGBN rose 5.02% over the same three-month window in the morning dataset. Its [verified cleanup](../../../reports/2026-10-12_EGBN_cleanup_figure_verification.md) supplies a plausible improving-trajectory explanation, still INFERRED as a price driver; underlying credit vintage remains June 30. AMTB and SSB also rose in the maintained cohort. These exceptions weaken a blanket bearish bank claim.

The saved October 12 13:33 snapshot had all ten selected holdings up and KRE +1.06%. It was a broad rebound at that observation, not a verified reversal of the historical decline. Declared bank-feed delays are 15–20 minutes and Nicolet's last trade was 13:12 EDT. The older volume/positioning ledgers remain historical or FROZEN; they do not authenticate current short covering or institutional distribution.

The next analytical gap is whole-basket historical return contribution and associated current earnings or funding evidence. It is a gap in the existing conclusion, not a new assignment, recurring monitor or trade proposal. This note changes no credit score, target, threshold or position.
