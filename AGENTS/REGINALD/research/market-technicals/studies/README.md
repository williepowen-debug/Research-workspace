# Regional bank technical studies

Save new regional-bank and KRE technical analysis here as dated files. Link the input data from each study and keep measurements separate from interpretations.

[October 12 KRE price-pattern synthesis](2026-10-12_KRE_price_patterns.md) examines the saved price windows through October 9. Its [period comparison CSV](2026-10-12_KRE_period_comparison.csv) separates July 10–September 11 from September 11–October 9.

The existing [April 9 volume study](../../../trade/market-microstructure/RP-REG-5.1_KRE_Volume_Analysis.md) covers moving average volume, relative volume, up-day versus down-day volume and positioning. Its figures are historical; the original report retains its established path. Current saved price comparisons are in the [snapshot index](../SNAPSHOTS.tsv).

Each study should identify its observation date and timezone, tickers, bar interval, source-data path, dividend or split treatment, and calculation parameters. Moving averages need their window and type; RSI needs its lookback and smoothing method; relative volume needs the comparison window and treatment of partial trading days. Support and resistance levels need the observed price window and the analyst's basis for choosing them.
