## What reported cleanup actually means

Rechecked Q2 deck slide24: nonaccrual loans held for investment reconcile as

`128.761 +36.038 new inflows −14.658 paydowns −24.982 charge-offs −14.035 transfers to held for sale =111.124`.

Thus14.658 of53.675 gross outflows was labeled paydowns;39.017, or72.7%, was charge-offs or HFS transfers. **This percentage concerns gross outflows, not the17.637 net decline.** The paydown line is still issuer-reported and does not identify funding source, individual properties, net recovery against original par or offsetting replacement lending. Charge-offs recognize losses; HFS transfers retain an asset pending sale. Neither is equivalent to full cash repayment. Real cleanup occurred, but the headline decline cannot independently validate full economic recovery.

The separate35.431 DC apartment payoff footnote on Q2 slide25 is genuine issuer-reported contrary evidence. It is **not** the Prince George's loan. The bank's completed HFS sales and capital buffer also remain counterevidence to an indiscriminately bearish interpretation.

1. Same-loan identifiers, new balance/maturity/grade/accrual and explicit outcome. A disappearance without explanation remains UNKNOWN.
2. Borrower/third-party principal cash, funded interest, interest reserves, new bank advances or capitalized interest, concessions and any replacement claim. A smaller balance is not automatically fresh cash.
3. Prior versus new charge-off, sale/write-down, allocated allowance and new provision attributable to that exposure. Do not charge provisions and allowance-funded charge-offs twice.
4. Address/borrower or recorded instrument enabling independent property, court or transaction verification. A release of lien alone does not establish par recovery or identify who financed the repayment.

**Strengthens concern:** continued inadequate cash flow with additional bank-funded carry, a matched discounted sale or attributable new loss. **Weakens concern:** independently supported cash repayment near carrying, or credible new outside equity/refinancing that reduces bank exposure and supports debt service. **Leaves unresolved:** issuer-only grade upgrade, extension, unexplained table disappearance or aggregate unnamed provisions. A prudent restructuring can improve recovery; no presumption that extension is misconduct.
