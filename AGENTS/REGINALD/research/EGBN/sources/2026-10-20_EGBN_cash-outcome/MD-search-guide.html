Deeds and Mortgages

Deeds & Mortgages

Go to: Maryland Land Records Online

At the county level of government, land records have always made up the largest series of records. Land record books record deeds and mortgages, but you also can find bills of sale, manumissions, and certificates of freedom prior to Emancipation.

These land and property records, formerly held at the county courthouses, are now held at the Maryland State Archives for preservation. These are no longer circulated as preservation measures, and now are available online from home through MdLandRec.net.

Please check out our FAQs and Tips for Hard to Find Records for helplful tips for your land record research. Still hitting a brick wall? Send us an email at msa.helpdesk@maryland.gov, and we will be happy to help.

Introduction to Version 5.0.0

The Maryland State Archives is excited to share that an updated version of MDLandRec was launched today, November 17th. Please note that all users must create a new login. The Maryland Land Records website provides free online access to Maryland’s land records from modern land records back to the earliest surviving land record for each county. You can access the website through the link on our home page, or by going straight to landrec.msa.maryland.gov.

Notable changes include multi-factor authentication: a security code will be sent to the email associated with the account upon logging in. Additional new features include "Indices" on the top of the page ("Active Indices" used to be found on the left hand side of the webpage, which now are renamed "Historical Indices."). Additionally, all text fields now contain the option to fine-tune a search by selecting from a list of search modifiers. For example, there are modifiers for partial information such as "Begins With", "Ends With", or "Contains" that can be applied to aid in the search process.

The URL is landrec.msa.maryland.gov, though the previous URL of "mdlandrec.net" will redirect users to the current version.

If you have questions or concerns, you can email our Help Desk at msa.helpdesk@maryland.govand we will return your email as quickly as possible.

Certified Copies

If you need a certified copy of a land record for legal purposes, then please visit our Order Website, and use the “Deed/Land Record Records” form. Otherwise, if you just need images and do not need legally certified copies, then no need to place an order: you can find what you need online from home!
