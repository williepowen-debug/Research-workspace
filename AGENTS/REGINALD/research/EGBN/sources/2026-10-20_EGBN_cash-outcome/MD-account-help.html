Creating an account



In order to comply with the Maryland State Information Technology Security Policy and Standards, login accounts are needed for remote access to Maryland Land Records. A user name and password is not necessary to access Maryland Land Records from the public workstations at the Maryland County Circuit Courts or the Maryland State Archives.

We require Maryland Land Records accounts to include contact information to better communicate with users regarding the questions, suggestions, and corrections that they report. We also use patron information to generate aggregate statistics regarding site usage that help us further improve the site.

If you have not received a response within 30 minutes of your application, check your spam or junk folders to see if they contain an email from us. If you still cannot find a response, please contact the Archives' Help Desk.

Maryland Land Records allows one account per email address.
