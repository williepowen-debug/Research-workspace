RF is tracked for **three specific reasons** — the earnings watch list is structured around them.

### 1. Consumer DQ Leading Indicator (PRIMARY)

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Total consumer 30-89 DPD | 0.88% | 0.81% | 0.89% | Rising = early stage trouble |

**Questions:**
- Did total consumer NCO break 0.75%? Direction over 3 consecutive quarters?
- Is credit card NCO diverging upward from peer benchmarks (DFS ~5.6%, COF ~5.8%, regional peer CFG ~3-4%)?
- Does RF disclose any consumer recessionary commentary? (Prior calls have been "consumer remains resilient" — any shift to "bifurcated" is a signal)
- Any Ginnie/FHA buyback noise (like CFG Slide 27 FHA/VA/USDA $179M up from $141M)?

**CARL channel question:** If RF consumer NCO accelerates Q1, the CARL consumer-transmission thesis gains first-quarter evidence. If flat/improving, CARL channel stays weak at regional-bank surface level (even if subprime auto/ABS deteriorates).

## 4-TEST SECTOR SCORECARD (pre-filled with questions)

### TEST 1 — Beat-Fade Tape
- **Setup:** Consensus $0.60 EPS, $1.92B revenue. Q4 beat; momentum with 3 straight quarters ≥$0.58.
- **Watch:** Day-1 close vs pre-close ($27.92). Prior 3 reporters: MTB -1.55%, CFG -0.63%, PNC +0.39%.
- **Cohort pattern:** 2 of 3 faded on EPS beats. RF at similar setup. Would expect -0.5% to -1.5% if pattern holds.
- **Break read:** If RF rallies >+1% day-1, either (a) consensus was too bearish, or (b) RF's clean-narrative (improving credit, rising NIM, buybacks) breaks the cohort fade.

### TEST 2 — FHLB Trajectory
- **Setup:** RF is the OUTLIER pre-Q1. FHLB advances $1.75B YE2025 vs $2.50B YE2024 = -30% YoY.
- **Supplement check:** Short-term borrowings line (Q4 = $750M). If jumps above $2B, structural flip.
- **Deeper check:** Total borrowed funds line ($4.88B YE2025). A jump above $6B with short-term <$2B means senior/sub debt issuance, not FHLB stress.
- **Questions:** Any FHLB-specific commentary in earnings release prepared remarks? RF's rate for FHLB is 3.9% (vs 1.85% interest-bearing deposit cost) — no economic incentive to draw absent liquidity stress.

### TEST 3 — CRE Extend-and-Pretend
- **Setup:** RF's pre-Q1 baseline is the OPPOSITE of CFG. IRE NPL down 71% YoY; balance flat. No extend-and-pretend signal visible.
- **Watch:** Any QoQ reversal in IRE NPL. Any mention of modifications (vs charge-offs). Any FL/multi-family / condo-specific commentary.
- **Deeper:** Q1 supplement page 22-24 for IRE NPL by property type if disclosed (RF doesn't consistently break out property-level NPL).
- **Question:** "What's left in portfolios of interest?" — If management says "largely resolved" and criticized is down another $300M+, thesis pattern stays inverted. If "new identification in FL" or similar, pattern flips.

### TEST 4 — NDFI Disclosure / Reclassification
- **Setup:** RF is the most OPAQUE of the 4 Q1 reporters. Table 11 "Financial services" = $8.5B loans + $9.8B unfunded = $18.3B total exposure. No sub-categories. No deck slide equivalent to PNC Slide 11 or CFG Slide 24.
- **Watch:**
  - Does Q1 deck introduce an NDFI slide? If yes → joining disclosure cycle. If no → RF is the deck-disclosure laggard.
  - Table 11 "Financial services" balance in supplement (if disclosed): if >$9.5B = acceleration without explanation.
  - Any new language on "private credit," "fund finance," "capital call lines," "NBFI," or "sponsor lending"?
- **Reconciliation math to bookmark:** Using PNC's rule (42% of true NDFI sits OUTSIDE "Financial services" bucket), RF's implied true NDFI = **$12-15B (~13-16% of loans)**. A tentative estimate; only confirmable if RF provides a thematic rollup.

## SCENARIOS

- EPS $0.60-0.63 in line with consensus
- Consumer NCO 0.68-0.72% (flat)
- Credit card NCO 4.00-4.15% (flat)

### SCENARIO B: "Consumer Inflection" (40% probability)
- EPS in line or slight miss
- **Consumer NCO 0.75-0.80% (+5-10bps QoQ)**
- Credit card NCO 4.20-4.50% (accelerating)
- Other consumer NCO >3.00%
- Residential 90+ DPD >0.60%
- Management softens "consumer resilient" to "consumer bifurcated" or "watchful"
- IRE still declining (doesn't match CFG pattern)
- Stock: -1% to -2.5% day-1
- **Thesis impact:** FIRST Q1 consumer confirmation for CARL channel. Sector read-through: regional consumer credit turning. WAL/OZK don't have consumer books large enough to corroborate, but BROCK (BDC consumer sub-exposure) and CARL take the signal.

### SCENARIO C: "Southeast Cracks" (15% probability)
- EPS miss (<$0.58)
- IRE NPL reverses: +$30-80M QoQ, likely FL-driven
- Management mentions FL condo, multi-family stress, or specific Southeast deterioration
- Criticized loans flat or rising
- Consumer NCO also up (combined stress)
- Stock: -3% to -5% day-1
- **Thesis impact:** CORAL's FL condo/HOA thesis confirmed via large Southeast bank. Sector read-through: regional Southeast CRE pattern spreading. Big signal for OZK (FL construction exposure) and potentially WAL (FL commercial).

### SCENARIO D: "Disclosure Catch-Up" (5% probability)
- RF introduces NDFI slide in Q1 deck with sub-category breakdown
- Implies $12-18B in NDFI bucket
- Would be biggest pattern confirmation of Q1 cycle — RF joining CFG/PNC in voluntarily breaking out
- Stock impact depends on numbers (likely mild negative if big, neutral if in line)
- **Thesis impact:** Confirms sector-wide disclosure pressure. Template builds. WAL/OZK investor-pressure ratchets.
