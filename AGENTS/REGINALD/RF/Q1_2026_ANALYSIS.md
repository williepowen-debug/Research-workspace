## 1. NDFI — THE FORCED DISCLOSURE (biggest update)

**Cassidy's question (RBC):** "You are not really big NDFI lenders... as a regional bank you are down at the bottom of kind of the group... why have you not maybe pursued it as aggressively as some of your peers?"

**Turner (CEO):** "I think we just generally are more cautious, Gerard... We have some businesses where we have specialized capabilities... This would be an area where we are getting our feet wet, learning a little more about it. Today, we have relationships with just in excess of **25 funds**, and those funds are fairly broadly distributed in terms of the businesses and the sectors that they are lending into. **Total exposure, I think, is just above $3 billion to those funds within private credit, about $1.8 billion in outstandings.**"

**Chadda (CFO, prepared remarks):** "An area that has not been a meaningful growth driver over the past year is NDFR-related lending. These loans reflect long-standing client relationships with predominantly investment grade credits with **nearly half of balances associated with our long-standing REIT business**. Private credit exposure remains limited, **less than 2% of total loans**, largely investment grade, well-enhanced. **Existing client paydowns exceeded draws during the quarter.**"

## 3. CAPITAL MARKETS STRESS SIGNAL — CONCRETE CONFIRMATION

**Turner:** "Utilization rates are up about 200 basis points across both the corporate banking customer base and our middle market customers."

**Chadda:** "The line draws that we saw were **predominantly late in the quarter when there is uncertainty in the capital markets. That is really where we saw most of that come in.** I would not call it defensive in nature. I would just say given where the capital markets were, as we saw uncertainty in the market, customers drew on bank lines."

**Turner (follow-up):** "About half of the increase in line utilization was attributable to our larger corporate customers, the other half to our middle market customers."

**Tighter asset spreads location (Chadda):** "The tighter spreads are primarily in **larger C&I where we saw line utilization late in the quarter**."

### Thesis implications:
- **HENRY/LIQUID transmission concrete:** corporate clients drawing bank lines when bond market uncertainty closed capital markets = **paper→plumbing transmission** on the liability side. RF got the volume; spreads compressed (IG pricing).
- **$2.3B point-to-point loan growth** — half from line utilization, half from new loans, ~2/3 IG, near-IG for majority of remainder. Loan book getting HIGHER quality AND bigger — classic late-cycle regional bank setup before NDFI/corporate credit event.
- **Anaylst-question leading signal:** MS analyst asked about "defensive line draws" — FIRST cohort reporter where analyst framed it this way. Expect this question at WAL/OZK/ZION Apr 21.
- **Hormuz resolution implication:** If straits stay open, capital markets reopen, line draws reverse → RF NII guide may be challenged on volume alone (offsetting fixed-asset turnover tailwind).

## 7. SECURITIES REPOSITIONING — $40M LOSS POST-Q1

**Chadda:** "**Subsequent to quarter end**, higher interest rates created an opportunity to sell approximately **$900 million of shorter-duration securities that no longer support our balance sheet management objectives at a $40 million loss**, repositioning those into longer-duration product types. The transaction is also well aligned with our overall capital deployment priorities, carrying a short, approximately **two-year payback period** and enhancing overall security yields."

### Thesis implications:
- **Rate spike post-Q1 confirmed** in RF behavior — 10Y yield jumped from low-4.2s through 4.4+ in early April, triggering opportunistic repositioning.
- **Q2 headline dilution:** $40M loss is a non-GAAP exclusion but GAAP headline drag.
- **Signals active balance sheet management** — RF is repositioning INTO longer duration = betting on eventual rate decline. Contrarian to stagflation thesis, but Chadda's caveat: "at current rate levels, we would expect balance sheet repricing to support margin expansion over multiple years."
- **Read-through for cohort:** Other regionals may follow. Watch WAL/OZK/ZION for similar subsequent-events language Apr 21.
