## Key Analytical Points

### CRO Sale + Reserve Cut = Maximum Signal
Majumdar sold discretionarily (no 10b5-1) in the **same quarter** the bank:
- Cut ACL reserves by $56.6M
- Saw noncurrent loans double
- Had 75% of noncurrent concentrated in other nonfarm nonresidential CRE

The person responsible for assessing the bank's credit risk chose to reduce personal exposure while the bank was telling the market risk was declining. That's the gap.

### Gleason Framing Correction (per Audit C4)
Original thesis presented Gleason's non-selling as bearish ("captive, not confident"). This is weak — a 10% holder has legitimate liquidity constraints regardless of outlook. The real signal is the **other C-suite**: CFO staged $944K out, CRO sold discretionarily, and nobody bought. Gleason's non-selling is neutral, not bearish.

### What Would Weaken the Thesis
- **Any insider purchase** at current levels (~$42-44) = material counter-signal
- **10b5-1 plan disclosure** for Majumdar's Feb sale (if retroactively revealed) = downgrades from discretionary to planned
- **Gleason open-market purchase** = strongest possible counter-signal
