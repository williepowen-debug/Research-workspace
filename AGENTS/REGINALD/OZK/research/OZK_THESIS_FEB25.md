## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bank OZK is the **most concentrated CRE lender** in our screen with the **worst hidden CRE ratio** (37.6% Memo3/C&I — worse than Metropolitan Capital which failed). The bank is actively unwinding its Real Estate Specialties Group (RESG) from 70% → 50% of assets after a series of high-profile loan losses, but faces $915M+ in distressed life sciences exposure with 35% vacancy in its core San Diego market.

**Key Thesis:** OZK's "conservative underwriting" narrative is cracking. The bank just sold its first major distressed loan (signaling strategy shift), has taken title to multiple properties, and has several hundred million in loans where sponsors have stopped supporting. With earnings Feb 27, any negative surprise could accelerate the selloff.
