## 1. What We Can Confirm (with Primary/Independent Sources)

### The Loan: $915M Committed, $555M Funded, August 2026 Maturity
- **Bisnow (June 11, 2024):** "The $915M loan matures in August 2026, and as of May 29, $555M has been funded, with the other $360M mostly designated for tenant improvements and leasing commissions."
  - Source: https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/life-sciences/radd-aimed-to-change-san-diegos-life-science-market-the-target-remains-elusive-124629
- **Bisnow (Oct 18, 2024):** Confirms $915M loan, originated 2022, and adds: "IQHQ has kept current with payments on its loan, which was given a **two-year extension**, and contributed an additional $87M to finance the project."
  - Source: https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/life-sciences/how-will-bank-ozks-life-science-bets-play-as-supply-imbalance-grows-126346
- **Bank OZK IR presentation (undated, linked from EDGAR):** "Our largest loan is on a San Diego life science project that also includes retail and traditional office"
  - Source: https://ir.ozk.com/static-files/d6a51e6b-58c9-4542-a44a-111ce32e45a3
- **Bisnow (Oct 23, 2024):** OZK CEO George Gleason acknowledged the loan and imposed a **$500M cap on new loans** going forward, directly referencing the RaDD exposure.
  - Source: https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/commercial-real-estate/bank-ozk-taking-a-hit-from-rate-cut-payoff-wave-per-q3-earnings-126401
- **Commercial Observer (May 30, 2024):** Citigroup analyst Benjamin Gerlinger called out "$915 million" tied to IQHQ's RaDD as basis for downgrade from Buy to Sell.
  - Source: https://commercialobserver.com/2024/05/bank-ozk-life-sciences-downgrade/
- **Seeking Alpha (May 30, 2024):** Confirms the two loans (RaDD + Atlanta) constitute "3.8% of OZK's non-purchased loans."
  - Source: https://seekingalpha.com/article/4696479-bank-ozk-falls-by-over-10-percent-due-to-bad-loan-fears-what-to-do-now

**VERDICT:** The $915M committed / $555M funded / Aug 2026 maturity figures are **NOT Temple 8 originals** — they are widely reported by Bisnow, Commercial Observer, Seeking Alpha, CRE Daily, and referenced by OZK's own IR materials. Temple 8 aggregated these; they did not fabricate them.

### Two-Year Extension (Critical New Detail)
- Per Bisnow (Oct 2024), the loan received a **two-year extension**. If original maturity was Aug 2026, the extended maturity is likely **August 2028**. This is a major detail Temple 8 may have missed or omitted.
- IQHQ also contributed an additional **$87M** of its own equity to the project.

### OZK's Broader Life Sciences Exposure
- **$3.2B total life sciences loan book** (10% of RESG portfolio) — OZK Q3 2024 earnings
- Other troubled SD life science loans: **$265M to Pacific Center** (Sterling Bay, Sorrento Mesa) — OZK sold this loan to Strategic Value Partners in Jan 2026 at a likely discount (Bisnow, Jan 5, 2026)
  - Source: https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/capital-markets/bank-ozk-offloads-265m-life-sciences-construction-loan-132553
- $203M to Bioterra (Longfellow) in Sorrento Mesa — no tenants as of late 2024
- $106.9M to Chapter Building (Seattle) — no tenants

- **Bluerock is largest shareholder** with >$700M exposure. Made two PIK loans to IQHQ: $160M at 13.5% (maturing Dec 2027) and $86M at 14% (maturing Aug 2028). IQHQ is NOT paying cash interest on these — accruing ~$8-9M/quarter
  - Source: https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/life-sciences/iqhq-facing-downgrades-confronts-challenges-leasing-market-133748 (March 2026)

## 2. What Contradicts Temple 8

### Loan Maturity May No Longer Be Aug 2026
- **The two-year extension** (reported by Bisnow, Oct 2024) likely pushes maturity to **August 2028**, not August 2026. If Temple 8's thesis hinges on an imminent Aug 2026 maturity cliff, this is a critical gap. However, it's unclear whether the extension was from the original maturity or had conditions.
- Temple 8 published ~March 2026 — if they omitted the extension, this weakens the "imminent maturity" argument significantly.

### IQHQ Is Not Completely Without Resources
- $900M raised in 2024, additional $87M equity injected into RaDD specifically
- The PIK loans from Bluerock (while expensive at 13.5-14%) give runway
- IQHQ executive "strongly disputed the idea that the company is in distress" (Bisnow, March 2026)

### OZK CEO Confidence (for what it's worth)
- George Gleason (Q2 2024 earnings call): "If it takes two years to lease or three years to lease or five years to lease, those guys will get to the finish line"
- IQHQ has kept current on loan payments

## 4. Market Context

### San Diego Life Sciences Vacancy — Catastrophic
- **CoStar (Feb 2025):** SD life sciences vacancy at **18%**, highest in 15+ years, up 4pp YoY
- **Savills Q1 2025:** Vacancy surged to **25.0%**, up from 14.3% at start of 2024
- **Savills Q2 2025:** Vacancy hit **28.7%**, nearly double YoY from 15.3%
- **Commercial Observer (Jul 2025):** "Now we're approaching **20% and climbing**… probably going to take a few years to work out"
- **San Diego Union-Tribune (Dec 2025):** Record-high vacancies for life science space
- **Bisnow (Mar 2026):** Lab leasing nationwide at ~30% vacancy in prime markets (SD, Boston)
- **Cap rates:** Lab properties went from 4.4% trough (2022) to **6.6%** (2025) per PwC — massive value destruction

### Downtown SD Specifically Is Worse
- Downtown is NOT a traditional biotech submarket (Torrey Pines, UTC, Sorrento Mesa are preferred)
- Sorrento Mesa alone had 273K SF negative absorption, 36% availability, 4-year recovery timeline (JLL)
- Downtown faces an even steeper challenge — it must prove the concept of urban lab space in a market drowning in suburban supply

### Demand Trends
- 2021 peak: 3.5M SF of tenant demand in SD (Colliers)
- 2024-2025: Demand collapsed. Some positive signs emerging late 2025 with VC investment picking up
- Nationwide: "one-third of the nation's lab space available" (Bisnow)

## 5. Risk Assessment

### Is the August 2026 Maturity Actually Dangerous?

**If the two-year extension holds → maturity is ~August 2028**
- This gives IQHQ ~2.5 more years from today
- The market may recover partially by then, but vacancy is currently at 25-30% and expected to take "years" to absorb
- IQHQ has $900M in fresh capital + PIK loans providing runway
- But: PIK interest is accruing at ~$35M/year, eating into equity cushion

**If the extension did NOT happen or has conditions:**
- August 2026 maturity is only ~5 months away
- With ~3% occupancy (50K of 1.7M SF), refinancing on market terms is essentially impossible
- OZK would face extend-and-pretend, loan sale at discount, or foreclosure

### Key Risk Factors
1. **Occupancy is catastrophic:** ~3% leased (50K/1.7M SF) after 5 years of development
2. **Downtown SD is unproven for lab:** No evidence this submarket can attract major pharma/biotech
3. **Collateral value erosion:** Cap rate expansion from 4.4% to 6.6% implies ~33% value decline even for occupied properties. For a mostly-vacant building, value could be far below funded debt.
4. **IQHQ financial health declining:** Investor markdowns (T. Rowe at $4.92, Altegris at $2.26), PIK loans accruing, Bluerock (largest investor) itself in distress with 40% first-day trading loss
5. **OZK concentration risk is real:** $915M is their single largest loan. $3.2B total life sciences exposure. They've already sold the $265M Pacific Center loan at a loss.
6. **The $500M loan cap is an admission:** OZK management acknowledges this was too large

### Bull Case (for what it's worth)
- Two-year extension buys time
- IQHQ has $900M+ in fresh capital
- Trophy-quality waterfront asset with no comparable competition
- If even 2-3 anchor tenants sign, narrative shifts quickly
- Life sciences VC picking up could drive demand

### Bear Case
- 5 years in, only 50K SF leased out of 1.7M SF
- Market vacancy at 25-30% and rising
- IQHQ's own investors marking down positions 4-23%
- PIK loans at 13.5-14% eroding equity cushion
- Even with extension, eventual maturity will require either refi (hard at this occupancy) or sale (at significant loss)
- OZK may ultimately need to reserve significantly against this loan

### Bottom Line
Temple 8's core thesis — that the RaDD loan represents massive concentration risk for OZK — is **well-supported by independent sources**. The specific figures ($915M, $555M funded, Aug 2026) are widely reported by Bisnow, Commercial Observer, and OZK's own materials. The key nuance Temple 8 may miss is the **two-year extension**, which pushes the crisis out but doesn't resolve it. The underlying asset fundamentals are dire.
