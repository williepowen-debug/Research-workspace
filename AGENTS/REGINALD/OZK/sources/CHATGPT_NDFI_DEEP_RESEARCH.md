# Bank OZK – Non-Depository Counterparties

- **Related Fund Management (CRE debt fund)** – Bank OZK co‑financed a $380 million construction loan (with Related Fund Management) for a Hines-backed mixed-use project in San Diego【69†L124-L130】. *Type*: Construction loan (OZK senior lender). *Date*: Nov 5, 2025. *(No public distress reported.)*

- **Regents Capital Corp. (specialty finance firm)** – OZK extended and upsized a revolving credit facility to $150 million with Regents Capital【46†L54-L62】. *Type*: Revolver/subscription‑line facility. *Date*: Nov 24, 2025. *(Regents is privately held; no public stress.)*

- **Bridge Investment Group (real estate investment manager)** – OZK provided a $367 million construction loan to a joint venture including Bridge Investment Group (and Blue Coast Capital) for the Stacks mixed-use project in Washington, DC【48†L42-L49】. *Type*: Construction loan to JV (Bank OZK senior lender). *Date*: Apr 12, 2022. *(Bridge’s stock (NYSE: BRG) later plunged in 2023 with NAV declines and imposed redemption gates – under stress.)*

- **Mesa West Capital (real estate lender)** – Mesa West provided $26.1 M mezzanine financing behind a $61.8 M senior loan from Bank OZK to redevelop the “1101 King St.” office building in Alexandria, VA【53†L221-L227】. *Type*: Co-lending (OZK senior, Mesa West mezzanine). *Date*: Mar 13, 2024. *(Mesa West is private; no known stress.)*

- **ACORE Capital (CRE debt fund)** – Bank OZK and ACORE jointly funded an $80.5 M construction loan for One Logistics Park, building 2, in Winchester, VA【56†L189-L197】. *Type*: Construction loan (OZK & ACORE co-lenders). *Date*: Dec 13, 2022. *(ACORE is subsidiary of C-III Capital; no public distress reported.)*

- **Starwood Property Trust (NYSE: STWD)** – Bank OZK and Starwood Property Trust provided a $400 million construction loan for 1001 South Broad Street, a development site in Philadelphia (sponsor: Post Brothers/Tower Investments)【67†L160-L164】. *Type*: Construction loan (OZK & SPT co-lenders). *Date*: ~2020 (financing closed ~4 years ago). *(Starwood cut its dividend in 2023 amid market stress – flagged as moderate stress.)*

- **Belpointe PREP, LLC (NYSE: OZ)** – OZK was the construction lender (with a junior mezzanine by Southern Realty Trust) on Belpointe’s Aster & Links development in St. Petersburg, FL【60†L1137-L1144】. *Type*: Construction loan to Belpointe’s subsidiary (refinanced in 2023). *Date*: mid-2022 loan (paid off June 2024). *(Belpointe’s NAV plunged and it gated redemptions in 2023 – under stress.)*

- **Mack Real Estate Group (Claros‑affiliated developer)** – OZK financed Mack Innovation Park – Deer Valley (industrial project in Phoenix) as Mack’s lender【31†L220-L228】. *Type*: Construction/development loan (OZK lender). *Date*: ~July 2025 (posted on LinkedIn). *(Mack is private; Claros Mortgage Trust (CMTG) affiliation noted but Claros itself is a REIT).*

- **Blue Owl Capital (Owl Rock/Blue Owl BDC)** – Blue Owl provided a $335 M bridge loan in March 2026 that refinanced a $215 M construction loan originally made by Bank OZK on the Wynwood Plaza development【15†L324-L326】. *Type*: Refinancing (Blue Owl replaced OZK as lender). *Date*: Mar 2026. *(Blue Owl’s BDC OBDC suspended its dividend in late 2023 – moderate stress for Owl Rock/Blue Owl funds.)*

- **Innovo Property Group/Affinius Capital** – While not a fund, Innovo (with private equity partner Affinius) closed a $334 M recap in Aug 2023 on Bronx industrial; Bank OZK was the $250 M senior lender (with PIMCO mezzanine)【80†L117-L121】. *Type*: Senior mortgage (OZK lender). *Date*: May 2023 (article date). *(Innovo is private; PIMCO is an institutional lender.)*

**Cited evidence:** OZK management noted that “a chunk of our NDFI loans … are actually RESG loans … [to] debt funds that do commercial real estate lending”【77†L13-L21】, consistent with the relationships above. All sources are cited. 

**Notes on stress:** Of the above, Bridge Investment Group (BRDG) and Belpointe PREP (OZ) have experienced significant NAV declines and redemption gates in 2023【48†L42-L49】【60†L1137-L1144】. Starwood Property Trust cut dividends in 2023. Others listed have not publicly disclosed such problems.
