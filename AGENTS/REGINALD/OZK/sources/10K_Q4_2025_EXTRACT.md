- Tier 1 Leverage = 13.60% = Tier 1 / Avg Total Assets

- RWA = Tier 1 / 12.50% = **~$44,400M**
- **Total RBC = 14.80% × $44,400M ≈ $6,571M**

*KBRA's 197% construction likely uses a broader construction definition or a different period's data (Q3 2025 had $8,490M construction / $4,306M Tier 1 at that point?).*

**Temple 8's 455%:** Cannot be precisely replicated from primary data. Closest match: Total RE loans ($21,828M) / TCE ($5,130M) = 425%, or possibly using a Q3 2025 snapshot with higher CRE balances. The 455% figure appears to be an approximation or uses a slightly different numerator/denominator than what we can verify.

**The 900% figure:** Moody's concept of including unfunded commitments. $18B unfunded + $19.9B on-balance CRE = $37.9B / ~$5.5B Tier 1 ≈ 689%. To reach ~900%, they may be including total loans + unfunded ($32.3B + $18.0B = $50.3B) against Tier 1, or using RESG total commitment ($29B) + other CRE separately. **Cannot precisely source 900% from available data.**

## Updates Needed

After reviewing EVIDENCE.md and THESIS.md, the following corrections/updates are recommended:

### EVIDENCE.md
1. **Capital Ratios section says "Dec 2024, from 10-K"** — Should note Bank OZK has no 10-K (files with FDIC). Update to Dec 2025 preliminary ratios: CET1 11.70% (was 11.34%), Tier 1 12.50% (was 12.15%), Total RBC 14.80%.
2. **CRE/Tier 1 "415%"** — At Dec 2024, CRE/Tier 1 was approximately 415% using different loan balances. At Dec 2025, it's approximately **358%** (CRE declined, capital grew). This is an improvement, not deterioration.
3. **Unfunded commitments "$19.08B"** — Update to $18.0B (Dec 2025). Declined by $1.08B.
4. **NPAs $200.9M / 0.53%** — Update to **$402M / 0.99%** (nearly doubled YoY).
5. **Construction/Land Dev "$9.52B"** — Update to **$7.78B** (significant decline from payoffs and curtailments).
6. **"Construction = 67% of losses but only 0.89% reserve rate. Reserves CUT 41% ($235M → $139M)"** — Cannot verify from primary Q4 filings. Need Call Report data.
7. **IQHQ section:** Flag that this is from external sources only, not OZK primary filings.

### THESIS.md
1. **"ACL ratio: 1.16%"** — INCORRECT. The ACL ratio is **1.26%** (of loans + unfunded). Temple 8 appears to have made an error or used a non-standard denominator.
2. **"Inversion: -2bps"** — INCORRECT as stated. ACL ratio (1.26%) > Q4 annualized NCO rate (1.18%) = +8bps buffer, not -2bps inversion. The "inversion" narrative is based on a calculation error. **However**, the coverage ratio concern IS valid: ACL/FY NCOs = $632M/$160M = 3.95x, and ACL/annualized Q4 NCOs = $632M/$393M = 1.6x. This is low vs peers (4.5-5.1x) but NOT below 1.0x as Temple 8 claims.
3. **Coverage "below 1.0x"** — This is only true if you annualize Q4's spike ($98.3M × 4 = $393M vs $632M ACL = 1.6x). Temple 8's "<1.0x" framing is wrong. Even using the Q4 annualized rate, coverage is 1.6x, not <1.0x.
4. **CRE 455%** — Cannot replicate. Recommend using verified numbers: CRE/Tier 1 ≈ 358%, CRE/TCE ≈ 387%, Total RE/TCE ≈ 425%.
5. **"With unfunded: CRE concentration ~900% of Tier 1"** — Cannot verify from available data. Best approximation gives ~682%.
6. **CRE/Total RBC = 302%** — Worth noting this is *barely* above the 300% regulatory threshold. This is the ratio regulators actually use.
7. **RESG shrinking "via defaults/sales, not strategy"** — Partially true but incomplete. RESG declined $0.97B in 2025. Management explicitly guided for continued RESG repayments and portfolio runoff while growing CIB. The decline is a mix of deliberate strategy (selling, not originating as aggressively) AND defaults. $130.5M in RESG NCOs was only 0.7% of the portfolio — the decline was mostly payoffs/repayments.
8. **IQHQ as thesis cornerstone:** This is based entirely on non-OZK sources. Add caveat that OZK does not disclose individual borrower names and this cannot be verified through primary filings.

### Key Takeaway for Thesis
The core concern (elevated and accelerating CRE losses, reserve adequacy declining) is **VALID** based on primary data. But several of Temple 8's specific numbers are wrong or misleading:
- The "inversion" is not real at 1.26% ACL vs 1.18% NCO
- Coverage is 1.6x (low but not <1.0x)
- 455% CRE concentration is unverifiable
- The IQHQ whale is the biggest thesis risk and cannot be confirmed from OZK filings

The **strongest** primary-sourced bears signals are:
1. NPLs more than doubled in Q4 ($150M → $341M)
2. Q4 NCOs were $98.3M vs provision of only $50.6M (reserve consumed)
3. Two office loans appraised at 98.9% and 111.5% LTV (one underwater)
4. Management guidance: NCOs "remain elevated for 2026"
5. RESG NCO rate (0.68%) was 3.6x its 23-year average (0.19%)
