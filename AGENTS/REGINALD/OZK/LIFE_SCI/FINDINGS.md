## Bottom Line

IQHQ's RaDD campus is a **$1.9B development that is 3.3% leased**, delivered into a market with **>90% downtown vacancy** and **negative net absorption for two consecutive years**. The sponsor is paying **13.5-16.5% for rescue capital**, investors have marked positions down up to **94%**, and the original life sciences demand thesis has been undermined by a structural shift toward AI-native labs that need less space. Bank OZK extended the $915M loan to ~Aug 2028, buying time but not solving the fundamental mismatch between supply and demand.

This is OZK's single largest loan and the most likely source of a major future impairment.

---

## Finding 1: 3.3% Leased — Sole Tenant Got a Sweetheart Deal
**KB-137** | **Confidence: B1**

Of 1.5M SF lab/office, only JCVI signed (~50K SF). JCVI's founder publicly acknowledged "taking a fairly sizable risk." Brokers confirm a below-market deal to secure the marquee name. Retail is ~50% leased (Equinox, Rivian, Javier's) but represents only 12% of campus.

Pipeline: 5-6 non-life-sciences tenants (professional services, financial) negotiating — none signed. IQHQ building spec suites 10-40K SF, pivoting from original 50K floor plates. **The pivot away from life sciences tenants is an implicit admission the original thesis is dead.**

## Finding 2: Maturity Extended to ~Aug 2028
**KB-138** | **Confidence: A2**

Original maturity Aug 2026. Extended ~2 years after IQHQ contributed $87M to reserves. OZK committed $915M total, $555M funded, ~$360M in "good news funding" (released only when leases are signed).

**Implication for our trade:** Aug 2026 puts won't catch a forced RaDD loss event. But the Apr 16 earnings call WILL require disclosure of the extension and the 3.3% leasing rate. Every analyst will ask. The narrative damage is the catalyst, not a charge-off.

Note: Gemini still references Aug 2026 maturity — appears to have missed the extension. Claude's sourcing is more specific and credible on this point.

## Finding 3: IQHQ in Slow-Motion Collapse
**KB-139** | **Confidence: B1**

Capital distress cascade:
- **$900M rescue raise** (Dec 2024) — PJT Partners (restructuring advisor) facilitated
- **IIP $270M at 16.5% coupon** ($100M revolver + $170M preferred, senior to all common equity)
- **Bluerock PIK loans:** $160M @ 13.5% + $86M @ 14% — interest NOT paid, accruing ~$8-9M/qtr
- **Bluerock** (largest shareholder, $700M+ exposure) NYSE listing lost ~40% on first trading day
- **6 funds marked down $707M:** Aimco $50M→$3M (94%), Altegris at $2.26/unit, T. Rowe at $4.92
- **Co-founder Alan Gold departed** Dec 2024
- **Fenway Center** ($1B, Boston) — vertical construction paused Nov 2025
- **Brighton** (Boston) — sold at 2/3 of 2022 purchase price
- **109 Brookline** (285K SF) — completed 2024, entirely vacant
- **J.F. White sued** for $27M owed on Fenway Center

Positive offset: CZI signed 225K SF at Elco Yards (Redwood City), Lila Sciences signed 244K SF at Alewife Park (Cambridge). But these are at OTHER properties — they do nothing for RaDD's 1.45M SF vacancy.

## Finding 4: Downtown San Diego Is Dead for Lab Space
**KB-140** | **Confidence: A2**

- Downtown SD effective life sciences vacancy **>90%** (RaDD + Campus at Horton)
- Citywide SD vacancy: 28.6-31.2% (from 4% in 2021)
- Net absorption: negative 546K SF (2024), negative 497K SF (Q1-Q3 2025)
- Asking rents fallen **14 consecutive quarters** to $5.37-5.95/SF/mo NNN
- Effective rents 20-25% below asking after concessions
- Recovery estimates: **3-5 years** (JLL) to **9 years** (Mike Krenn) to reach 70-80% occupancy
- **Zero new construction underway** — supply shutoff will eventually help but existing overhang takes years

CBRE's Matt Carlson: "So far, the San Diego life sciences market has not said 'we need to be downtown, on the waterfront, near to the airport.'" Large pharma (Novartis, Pfizer) chose established suburban clusters, not downtown.

## Finding 5: Structural Demand Shrinkage (AI Effect)
**KB-141** | **Confidence: B1**

JLL (Jan 2026): AI-native biotechs use **25% less space/employee** and **15-20% less wet lab**. Average SD lease size shrunk to ~12K SF. RaDD's massive floor plates designed for a demand profile that no longer exists. Even in a recovery, filling 1.7M SF requires significantly more companies than originally modeled.

SD VC funding: $2.5B FY2025 (-34.4% from 2024) — early-stage startup demand collapsed.

Campus at Horton (Stockdale Capital, ~1M SF, zero tenants, potential foreclosure in late 2025) injects another million SF into the same dead submarket. Combined 2.4M+ SF injected into a formerly non-existent downtown lab market.
