# LIFE_SCI — Life Sciences Asset Type Risk

**Created:** 2026-03-25 | **Last updated:** 2026-04-23 (Q1 26 refresh; IQHQ maturity correction Aug 2028 → Aug 2026)

OZK's life sciences exposure is the asset type most likely to generate near-term losses. Unlike FL condos (well-structured, performing) or office (recognized and being worked out), life sci sits in the danger zone: massive projects delivered into historic vacancy, sponsors under severe financial stress, and a structural demand shift (AI-native labs need less space) that makes recovery timelines measured in years, not quarters.

**Q1 2026 (Apr 22 earnings) added three new life-sci-adjacent problem credits ($269M combined)** without IQHQ itself yet touching the problem book. The concentration is deteriorating broadly, not just at the RaDD pressure point.

---

## Scope

This folder covers life sciences exposure **across all geographies**:
- **San Diego:** IQHQ RaDD ($915M — single largest loan, **Aug 2026 maturity**), Pacific Center ($265M — sold to SVP Jan 2026), Aperture Del Mar ($475M — performing), Bioterra ($202M — vacant)
- **Boston:** 🆕 **Boston Life Sci $169M** (Q1 substandard — matured Dec 18 2025; ✅ sponsor CONFIRMED Apr 23 = **10 Prospect St / USQ D2.1 / Magellan + RAS + Cypress + Affinius JV**), Fenway Center ($1B — paused, separate lender JPM $165M, not OZK), 109 Brookline (285K SF — vacant), Sullivan Courthouse ($72.4M charged off)
- **Seattle:** 🆕 **Seattle U District Life Sci $50M** (Q1 substandard, signed LOI for recap; combined $127M with adjacent Office)
- **Chicago:** 🆕 **Chicago Life Sci $50M** (Q1 foreclosed asset, 68% of May '25 appraisal after failed short sale) + Lincoln Yards $9M charged off (earlier)

The common thread: all are construction/development loans on lab space delivered into the worst life sciences downturn in decades.
