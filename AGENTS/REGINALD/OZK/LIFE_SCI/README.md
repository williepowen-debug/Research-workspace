# LIFE_SCI — Life Sciences Asset Type Risk

**Created:** 2026-03-25

OZK's ~$3.2B life sciences exposure is the asset type most likely to generate near-term losses. Unlike FL condos (well-structured, performing) or office (recognized and being worked out), life sci sits in the danger zone: massive projects delivered into historic vacancy, sponsors under severe financial stress, and a structural demand shift (AI-native labs need less space) that makes recovery timelines measured in years, not quarters.

---

## Scope

This folder covers life sciences exposure **across all geographies**:
- **San Diego:** IQHQ RaDD ($915M — single largest loan), Pacific Center ($265M — sold to SVP Jan 2026), other SD life sci
- **Boston:** Fenway Center ($1B — paused), 109 Brookline (285K SF — vacant), Sullivan Courthouse ($72.4M charged off)
- **Chicago:** Lincoln Yards ($9M charged off, modified 6x before foreclosure)

The common thread: all are construction/development loans on lab space delivered into the worst life sciences downturn in decades.
