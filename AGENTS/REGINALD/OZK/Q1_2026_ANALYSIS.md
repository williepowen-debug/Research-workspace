## TL;DR

1. **Slow-grind thesis quarter, not a fire quarter.** Small EPS miss ($1.44 vs $1.46 cons), no capital stress, no funding stress. But the leading-indicator stress signals ARE moving the bear's way:
   - **Past due loans DOUBLED QoQ: $207M → $465M (0.64% → 1.41% of loans).**
   - **Classified + criticized assets +23% QoQ: $984M → $1,215M.**
   - **Three new RESG substandard credits** identified (2 Seattle, 1 Boston Life Science).
   - **Two new RESG foreclosed assets** (Chicago Life Sci + Santa Monica Office — only 15% leased).
   - **NCO $45.3M — up 138% YoY** from $19M Q1 25. In-line with ~50bps FY guide.
2. **But Gleason's tone is confident.** "Late stages of this CRE cycle", "green shoots" in office leasing, multifamily/industrial/condo "healthy." Expected sponsor support on IQHQ through August 2026 maturity. Dismissed IQHQ litigation as "inner family squabble." Resolution activity on 4/5 problem credits.
3. **Sub notes repricing IS a real 2026 headwind — NEW catalyst.** $350M of 2.75% sub notes reprice Oct 1 2026 to **SOFR + 209bps (~6.4%+)** and lose 20% Tier 2 treatment for 12 months. **~$12.8M extra annual interest expense** (0.03 EPS per quarter drag). Management: no plans to redeem.
4. **$350M in new "Other borrowings" QoQ — likely FHLB advances.** Funded the $1.44B investment securities purchase (40% muni housing bonds tax-equiv 6%, 60% agency MBS 4.60%). **This is offensive carry trade, not defensive funding stress.** Cross-signal: OZK now joins MTB/CFG/PNC on FHLB surge but for opposite reason.
5. **Fund Finance — OZK is RETREATING as other regionals press in.** Jake Munn (CIB President): *"we've had to pull back a little bit in our capital call subscription facilities just due to increased pressure there, specifically from non-bank lenders and then insurance companies."* **Disconfirming for the NDFI expansion narrative at OZK specifically** — they're ceding price to non-bank/insurance competition.
6. **Buyback pocket accretive:** $59.5M in Q1 at $45.51 avg vs $47.15 TBV. Another $9.1M April 1-20 at $47.43. **~$29.5M remaining** of $200M authorization (expires July 1 2026). Board expected to approve new program.
7. **Near-zero equity positions on 3 of 4 non-accrual credits:** Boston Office 95% LTV, Seattle Pioneer Square 100% LTV, Wauwatosa Hotel 103% LTV. **OZK is the borrower now at these assets** — no equity cushion left.

## 3. THE SUB NOTES OCT 1 2026 REPRICE — NEW CATALYST

**Fact pattern:**
- $350M aggregate principal, 2.75% fixed-to-floating subordinated notes due 2031
- Currently paying **2.75% fixed** ($9.625M/yr)
- **Oct 1, 2026: resets to SOFR + 209bps (~6.4% at current SOFR ~4.3%)** — ~$22.4M/yr
- **Tier 2 capital treatment reduced by 20% for 12 months** starting Oct 1
- Quarterly redemption right at par
- **Management stated: currently no plans to redeem or issue replacement**

**Delta:**
- Interest expense delta: **+$12.8M/yr** (~$3.2M/qtr)
- EPS drag: ~$3.2M × (1-22.8% tax) / 109.4M shares = **~$0.023/qtr EPS drag = ~$0.09 annual**
- ~1.6% of current EPS run-rate

**Why this is a catalyst:** Management's "no plans to redeem" is a choice. If they redeemed and issued new sub at current market rates, it would be ~7-7.5% for 10yr sub — WORSE than the 209bps spread. So they're taking the SOFR floating repricing as the least-bad option. This works only if they expect **SOFR to stay >5% through 2027** OR rates to fall meaningfully.

**Cross-agent read:** OZK's liability structure has a new 2026 NIM headwind that isn't in most models. CALENDAR should add Oct 1 2026 repricing catalyst.
