## Macro Warning Signs [KB-OZK-120]

### UBS Global Real Estate Bubble Index (2025)
- **Miami ranked #1 globally** for housing bubble risk
- Score: **1.73** (exceeds 1.5 "high risk" threshold, exceeds 2006 peak)
- "Over the past 15 years, Miami has posted the strongest inflation-adjusted housing appreciation among all cities in the study"
- "Historically, worsening affordability and widening gaps between prices and rents have served as forerunners of housing crises"

### Migration & Demand
- Net domestic migration to FL collapsed **93%**: 310,892 (2022) → 22,517 (2025)
- The wealth migration story (Bezos, Thiel, Zuckerberg, Brin) is real but represents ultra-thin demand layer
- 65.3% of Miami luxury transactions are cash — insulates from mortgage rates but narrows buyer pool

### Supply & Inventory
- FL statewide condo supply: **13.2 months** (elevated)
- High-end inventory: **+40% YoY** (Financial Times)
- Tampa multifamily vacancy: **8.9%**
- Sarasota condo vacancy: **16.5%** (25-year high per CoStar)
- Miami condo days to contract: **71 days** (up from pandemic lows)

### Insurance & Regulatory
- FL property insurance premiums surging — largest cost increase for condo owners
- SB 4-D (post-Surfside, 2022): Structural Integrity Reserve Studies mandatory
- As of **Jan 1, 2026**: structural reserve funding mandatory, cannot be waived by owner vote
- Special assessments of **$50,000-$200,000 per unit** common in older high-rises
- FL requires **25% down** for condos that don't meet reserve requirements (vs 10% nationally)
- Construction defect lawsuits proliferating (Commercial Observer, Mar 2026)

### Early Signal: SB 4-D May Help New Construction
- Mandatory reserves and special assessments primarily hit **older buildings** (25-30+ years)
- New construction exempt from milestone inspections for 25-30 years
- New builds have modern code compliance, lower insurance, no deferred maintenance
- Older condo market becoming less competitive → potential demand shift TO new construction
- **If true, this STRENGTHENS the paradox for current OZK loans**

## What We Don't Know (Investigation Gaps)

1. **Pre-sale conversion rates** — "90% pre-sold" ≠ "90% will close." What is the historical gap? What deposit structures are in place? Under what conditions do buyers walk?

2. **2008 analog** — Miami luxury pre-sales evaporated in 2008. What was the cancellation rate? At what point in the market cycle did walkaways accelerate? How does 2026 compare?

3. **Foreign buyer dependency** — What % of Miami luxury pre-sales are international (LatAm, European)? Are capital controls, sanctions, or currency moves affecting ability to close?

4. **Specific project health** — Only 3-4 of 14 projects have confirmed pre-sale data. What about the other 10? Is Waldorf Astoria Miami on schedule? Any cost overruns?

5. **Tampa/SW Florida vs Miami** — $1.11B Tampa + $499M Cape Coral + $478M Sarasota = $2.09B in SW Florida markets that are clearly weaker (Sarasota 25-yr high vacancy). These are NOT the luxury pre-sold Miami condos. Are they also well-protected?

6. **Water Street Tampa ($664M)** — Vinik/Gates development. Is it completed? Stabilized? What occupancy? This is a massive single credit outside the Miami condo bubble.

7. **New origination in weakening market** — Four Seasons Coconut Grove ($324M) originated **January 2026**. West Palm Beach condo ($475M) originated **2025**. These were underwritten AFTER the macro started deteriorating. Are the pre-sales as strong?
