## The Question

OZK claims: 14 Miami condo loans, $2.47B, WA LTC 45.9%, LTV 39.1%, 11/14 presales cover full repayment. Zero FL defaults. [KB-OZK-110]

We're testing:
- Are pre-sales legally binding enough to protect the lender?
- What's the historical walkaway rate in Miami luxury when markets turn?
- Does the SB 4-D regulatory crisis help or hurt new construction?
- Is the ultra-luxury demand layer thick enough to absorb $7.45B in new supply?
- Does "90% pre-sold" actually mean "90% will close"?

## Key Context
- UBS Global Real Estate Bubble Index: Miami #1, score 1.73 (exceeds 2006 peak)
- Net domestic migration to FL: -93% (310K → 23K)
- FL condo supply: 13.2 months
- High-end inventory: +40% YoY
- 65.3% of Miami luxury sales are cash transactions
- SB 4-D mandatory structural reserves effective Jan 1, 2026
