## Key Findings

**The Paradox:** FL is largest ($7.45B, 23%) but safest (39% LTV, 90%+ pre-sold, zero defaults). CA is second ($5.37B, 16%) but most stressed (SD life sci collapse, LA office nonaccruals).

**Distressed clusters ($2.9B across 5 metros):**
- San Diego: ~$1.4B — 30%+ vacancy, caps 4.4%→6.6%, RaDD as-market value $320-490M vs $915M loan
- Boston: ~$700M+ — $72.4M charged off, Sullivan Courthouse likely OREO in 2026
- Chicago: ~$400M+ (but mostly resolved — One Chicago $475M repaid, Lincoln Yards sold)
- Seattle: ~$235M+ — The Jack LTV 111% (only underwater credit), Chapter Bldg appears empty
- LA: ~$140M+ — two nonaccruals, land in OREO

**Bull corrections found:**
- 50 W 66th ($800M) fully repaid July 2024
- One Chicago Square ($475M) repaid via CMBS 2022
- These prove OZK's model works for luxury residential/mixed-use — thesis is specific to life sci + office

**The $2B pipeline:** Management admitted $2B+ of loans entering final year "completely or substantially unleased, many life science." This is the next wave.

**Supervisory mismatch:** 84% of RESG outside home Dallas district. Dallas examiners evaluate SD life sci and NYC luxury condos with less market-specific expertise.
