## Pattern Analysis

### The CRO Gap (Feb 2026)
CRO Majumdar sold discretionarily on **2026-02-24** — the same quarter the bank:
- Had just reported ACL cut of $56.6M while noncurrent doubled (Q4 2025)
- Was entering Q1 2026 with "elevated payoff velocity" and ~$1.8B estimated maturities
- Seized Sterling Bay Lincoln Yards building (Mar 20)
- Had 75% of noncurrent concentrated in the exact CRE category thesis targets

The person responsible for assessing credit risk reduced personal exposure without automatic plan cover during the most stress-concentrated quarter in the bank's history.

### The CFO Window (Oct 2024 – Jan 2025)
CFO Hicks staged $944K out across two tranches *before* the Q3-Q4 2025 deterioration became visible in public filings. By the time ACL collapsed and noncurrent doubled, Hicks had already reduced 22% of holdings.

### The Absence (12 months)
Zero insider buying during a drawdown to 52-week lows. Corporate buybacks occurred (2.25M shares, Q4 2025) but no officer or director put personal money in. The corporate treasury was buying while personal accounts were selling or silent.
