# OZK Q4 2025 Management Comments — Extract
**Source:** OZK 4Q25 Management Comments.pdf
**Period:** Q4 2025 (quarter ending 12/31/2025)

## Verdict
Q4 2025 was a material problem-credit escalation quarter, not a maintenance quarter. Four RESG loans charged off in Q4 (annualized NCO 1.18%), two credits migrated from special mention → substandard non-accrual, and substandard non-accrual jumped from $150M to $341M QoQ — a 127% increase in one quarter. Critically for trajectory purposes, Office and Life Science drove the newly surfaced substandard credits, and one $72.4M charge-off on the Boston office credit was the largest single-quarter recognition event visible in the 5-quarter table. Q4 25 is the inflection quarter that set up the elevated problem-share heading into Q1 26.

## Top Management Commentary Themes
- "A small number of sponsors have eventually become unable or unwilling to support their projects resulting in charge-offs and nonperforming loans that do not meet our historical standards."
- Record RESG repayments: $3.00B in Q4 25 (second consecutive quarterly record); full year 2025 record $7.24B. Attributed partly to "surge in liquidity available for debt financing."
- San Diego life science loan sold in Q4 — $0.10B funded, $0.27B total commitment — "Such occasional transactions do not reflect any change in our strategy."
- "Uncertainties remain, particularly related to some office and life science properties in some markets."
- 2026 guidance: net charge-offs "remaining elevated" with "an improving trend in 2027." NCO ratio expected to again outperform industry.
- RESG total commitments now $28.8B (down from $34.5B peak Mar 2024); RESG share of loans expected to go below 50% during 2026 as repayments continue.
- ACL held flat at 1.26% for full year 2025: "We believe our ACL is appropriate as we continue through what we expect to be the late stages of this CRE cycle."
- Buybacks done below tangible book ($44.45 vs. $46.48 TBV) framed as accretive; management signaling confidence on capital adequacy even with elevated NCOs.
