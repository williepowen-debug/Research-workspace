## Verdict
Q3 25 was a credit-active quarter — two RESG credits migrated to substandard non-accrual with partial charge-offs (Chicago Life Sciences $5.1M NCO; Baltimore Land $20.9M NCO), and one credit moved from special mention to substandard accrual (Seattle Office). Problem-category mix (Office + Life Sciences + Land + Hotel) as share of RESG total commitment was approximately 27.1% — flat to slightly up from Q2 25, making Q3 25 a continuation quarter rather than an inflection point. The big driver of Q3 credit activity was charge-offs, not new classification entries.

## Top Management Commentary Themes

- **Record earnings, record NII:** "Net income available to common stockholders was a record $180.5 million... Net interest income was a record $413.9 million." NII record is second consecutive quarterly record.
- **RESG repayment surge deliberate:** "$2.44 billion in RESG loan repayments" — record single-quarter repayment. "Too many lenders chasing too few deals" restraining originations ($0.70B in Q3 25 vs. $1.51B in Q2 25).
- **RESG below 60% of portfolio — explicitly flagged as a transition:** "RESG's percentage of loans declined to 57.7%... we expect it will go below 50% during 2026." Management framing the RESG rundown as intentional diversification ("growth baton handoff to CIB").
- **Credit: contained migration, but notable charge-offs:** "Modest net reduction in total classified and criticized assets." Despite total C&C falling $92M, Substandard Non-Accrual rose $91M and Substandard Accrual rose $178M — the reduction was driven by Special Mention falling $280M (partially into substandard, partially resolved) and the Chicago OREO sale.
- **ACL scenario weighting is bearish:** "Combined weighting assigned to the Moody's S4 (Alternative Adverse Downside) and S6 (Stagflation) scenarios exceeded the weighting assigned to the Moody's Baseline scenario."
- **Sponsor support narrative maintained:** $1.2B of total additional equity contributions (reserves + paydowns) across 541 modifications/extensions since the Fed tightening cycle began.
- **Full-year 2025 NCO guidance reiterated:** Will exceed 2024's 20bp ratio but remain below industry average. Appraisals: 98% of RESG loans appraised on/after Jan 2023.
