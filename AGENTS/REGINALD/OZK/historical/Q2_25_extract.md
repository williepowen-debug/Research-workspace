**Source:** OZK 2Q25 Management Comments.pdf
**Period:** Q2 2025 (quarter ending 6/30/2025)

## Verdict
Q2 25 was a record-earnings, strong-growth quarter with benign headline credit — NCO ratio 0.10% annualized, nonperforming loans fell to $59M (0.18% of loans), and only 3 RESG substandard credits all current and accruing. Problem-category share of RESG total commitment was approximately 28.9% (Office 13.4% + Life Sciences 10.5% + Land 3.9% + Hotel 1.1%), a modest level that represents a mid-range data point for the trajectory analysis — neither a spike nor a trough.

## Top Management Commentary Themes
- "This was our best ever quarter of organic loan growth" — $1.90B QoQ RESG + CIB-led; CIB contributed 47%, RESG 32% of growth
- RESG percentage of loans declining (70% peak → 62% Q1 25 → 60% Q2 25); "moderately decreasing trend in RESG total commitments is likely to continue for several more quarters"
- Elevated repayments expected: $0.47B in first 15 days of July 2025 alone; "significantly higher" H2 25 repayments guided
- Full-year 2025 loan growth guidance raised to 11–13% (from prior guidance); but growth increasingly non-RESG
- ACL weighted to adverse/stagflation scenarios; "constantly shifting mix of risk factors" — tariffs, immigration, fiscal policy cited explicitly
- Appraisal update (40 loans, $4.33B): 1 LTV decrease >10% (condo), 10 LTV increases >10%; notable: Land loan LTV 50.7% → 87.7% (+37pts), Multifamily 45.7% → 82.1% (+36.5pts)
- Sponsor support cited: $1.0B total additional equity contributions ($739M reserve deposits + $303M paydowns) across 500 modifications/extensions over last 12 quarters
