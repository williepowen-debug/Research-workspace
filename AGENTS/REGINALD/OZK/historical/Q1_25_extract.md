## Verdict
Q1 25 was a credit-event quarter, not maintenance. The Chicago land loan ($83.95M) transferred to foreclosed assets — the largest single REFO addition in the visible history — while the Boston office credit moved to non-accrual with a $6.8M charge-off. Substandard credits fell from $258M to $227M and special mention from $654M to $455M, but that net improvement masks the composition shift: the Chicago REFO transfer cleaned a prior substandard off the balance sheet into OREO, inflating the foreclosed-assets line by $82M QoQ to $151M total. Problem-category share of RESG total commitment (Office + Life Sciences + Land + Hotel) was approximately 29.3% at Q1 25 — the highest data point visible in the 5-quarter series and higher than Q2 25's 28.9%, suggesting the trajectory was not monotonically rising into Q1 26; Q1 25 was already elevated.

## Top Management Commentary Themes
- "Our annualized net charge-off ratio was 0.25% for the quarter just ended, well below the recent industry average" — but guided that full-year 2025 NCO ratio "may increase compared to 2024's 20 bps ratio"
- Chicago land credit transferred to OREO this quarter; management "commenced efforts to patiently market the property to a new sponsor" — signals no near-term resolution
- LA Land: contract reinstated with $2.5M fees + $1.5M non-refundable deposit; "should result in a small gain on sale" if closes by Sep 30, 2025
- RESG as % of total loans declined to 62% (from ~70% historical high); diversification strategy driving non-RESG growth in CIB, Indirect RV & Marine, Community Banking
- RESG total commitments declining from $34.5B peak (3/31/24) → $32.7B; "elevated repayments" expected to continue in rest of 2025 and 2026; origination guidance withdrawn ("heightened macroeconomic uncertainty")
- Appraisal update: 54 loans ($5.70B), 15 LTVs increased >10% vs 39 little changed; notable deteriorations in Multifamily and Mixed Use (several +20%+ LTV moves); Life Science LTV up 16.9pts on $475M commitment
- ACL scenario: weighted to adverse/stagflation above baseline; cited tariffs, immigration changes, possible recession, "higher for longer" rates as key risk factors
- Buyback triggered by "recent market turbulence" (April 3–15 window at ~$38.34), management signaling opportunistic intent at that level
