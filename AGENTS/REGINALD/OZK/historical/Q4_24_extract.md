## Verdict
Q4 24 is the historical baseline: record earnings, benign headline credit, but two embedded structural signals. Substandard count fell 5→4 (one LA office resolved in full), but the surviving Chicago Land credit took a $16.9M charge-off during the quarter — active loss recognition inside an "improvement" headline. More important for trajectory analysis: problem-category share (Office + Life Sciences + Land + Hotel per Q2 25 schema) at Q4 24 was approximately **30.9%** of total RESG commitment — materially HIGHER than later quarters. This means the trend from Q4 24 through Q1 26 is U-shaped (30.9% → 28.9% Q2 25 → ~27.1% Q4 25 → 29.6% Q1 26), NOT monotonic acceleration. The Q1 26 spike is a partial retracement toward the Q4 24 baseline, not a new structural break. Critical context for the trajectory stitch.
