## Research Question

Map America First Legal's funding, litigation strategy, personnel, and connections to current Trump administration officials. AFL is a potential "policy factory" that shaped immigration policy outside government before Miller's return.

## Background

Stephen Miller founded AFL after leaving Trump 1.0. AFL has been extremely active in litigation against Biden policies and FOIA requests. Now Miller is back in government as Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy. This creates several potential patterns:
- **Write the Playbook**: AFL legal positions → Miller implements as policy
- **Revolving Door**: AFL staff → government appointments
- **Dark Money Pipeline**: AFL donors → policy access via Miller

## Specific Research Angles

### 1. Organizational Structure
- Legal structure (501c3, 501c4, or both?)
- Board of directors / advisory board members
- Key staff attorneys and their backgrounds
- Any affiliated entities or PACs

### 2. Funding & Financials
- 990 filings: Total revenue by year
- Major donors (disclosed or reported)
- Connections to donor networks: Leonard Leo, DonorsTrust, Donors Capital Fund
- Koch network connections?
- Any dark money conduit relationships?

### 3. Litigation Portfolio
- Major cases filed (especially immigration-related)
- FOIA requests and what they targeted
- Win/loss record
- Cases that align with current Trump 2.0 policies

### 4. Personnel to Government Pipeline
- Which AFL staff have joined Trump 2.0 administration?
- Which current officials have AFL connections?
- Any pending appointees from AFL?

### 5. Project 2025 Connections
- AFL's role in Project 2025 (confirmed on advisory board)
- Which P2025 chapters did AFL influence?
- Personnel database overlap

### 6. Policy Alignment Analysis
- Compare AFL's stated policy positions to Trump 2.0 executive orders
- Immigration EOs that mirror AFL litigation/advocacy
- Did AFL pre-write any EO language?

Structure findings as:
1. **Organizational Profile** - Structure, leadership, staff
2. **Financial Summary** - Revenue, donors, dark money connections
3. **Litigation Summary** - Key cases, targets, outcomes
4. **Government Pipeline** - AFL → Trump 2.0 personnel flow
5. **Policy Influence Map** - AFL positions that became policy
6. **Network Connections** - Links to other network nodes (Leo, Heritage, P2025, etc.)

Include dollar amounts, dates, case names, and source citations.
