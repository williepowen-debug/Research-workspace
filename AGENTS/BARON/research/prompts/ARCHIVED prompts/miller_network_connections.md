## Research Question

## Background

Miller is one of the most powerful figures in Trump 2.0 as Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy. He controls domestic policy agenda, particularly immigration. Understanding his network reveals:
- Who has his ear (influence mapping)
- Who benefits from his decisions (policy-profit connections)
- Who he's placed in government (personnel capture)
