## Background

On January 7, 2026, Trump announced via Truth Social that he would ban "large institutional investors from buying more single-family homes" and would "provide more details at the World Economic Forum in Davos later this month."

**World Economic Forum 2026: January 20-24, 2026**

The critical open question: Does the ban apply ONLY to "scatter-site purchases" (buying existing homes on the market) OR does it also apply to "build-to-rent" (BTR) development (building communities specifically for rent)?

Our thesis: The ban is designed to appear populist while actually preserving PHM-INVH revenue via BTR exemption. If BTR is exempted, PHM (Pulte family company), INVH (PHM's BTR partner), and AMH (pure BTR model) all benefit.

## Key Language to Watch For

**Bullish for thesis (BTR exemption):**
- "New construction" exemption
- "Purpose-built rental" carve-out
- "Developer partnerships" preserved
- "Building supply" encouraged
- "Only existing home purchases" restricted
- Any reference to PHM-INVH type partnerships being OK

**Bearish for thesis (no exemption):**
- "All institutional investment" banned
- "No distinction" between buying and building
- "All single-family rental investment" restricted
- Silence on BTR (implies included in ban)
