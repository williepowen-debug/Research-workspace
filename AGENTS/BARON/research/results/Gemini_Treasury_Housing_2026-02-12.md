## 1. Single-Family Housing Executive Order

### Critical Deadlines
| Date | Requirement | Status |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Feb 19, 2026** | Treasury defines "large institutional investor" and "single-family home" | PENDING |
| **Mar 21, 2026** | Agency guidance + "first-look" anti-circumvention protections | PENDING |

### Threshold Debate
- **100-home threshold**: Used in Sen. Merkley's "Humans over Private Equity for Homeownership Act"
- **1,000-home threshold**: Would only capture mega-REITs (INVH, AMH, Blackstone)
- National share: Investors >100 homes = ~1-5% of housing stock
- **Regional concentration**: Sun Belt markets (Atlanta, Phoenix, Jacksonville) hit 3.1% institutional at 2022 peak

### BTR Carve-Out Exploitation (CRITICAL)
The EO explicitly exempts build-to-rent properties "planned, permitted, financed, and constructed as rental communities."

**AMH Case Study:**
- Virtually halted MLS acquisitions after 2022 rate hikes
- **95.7%** of Q1-Q3 2025 acquisitions via internal homebuilding unit
- Now the **37th-largest homebuilder** in the US
- Completely insulated from EO restrictions

**Implication:** EO doesn't eliminate institutional presence—it redirects capital to monopolize NEW housing development. Individual buyers protected from competing for existing homes, but will increasingly rent newly-built homes from the same corporate landlords.

### Evasion Tactics
- Disaggregating portfolios into hundreds of LLCs
- Using opaque trusts to circumvent thresholds
- Efficacy depends on Treasury's beneficial ownership aggregation rules
