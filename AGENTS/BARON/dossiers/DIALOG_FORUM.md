## Overview

**Dialog** is a secretive, invitation-only society founded in 2006 by **Peter Thiel** (N-006) and **Auren Hoffman** (N-180). Often dubbed a *"tech-era Bilderberg"* or *"shadow World Economic Forum,"* Dialog convenes annual off-the-record retreats for ~100 global elites across business, government, and academia.

**Key Features:**
- No public website or published member list
- Phones surrendered; strict confidentiality
- Spans left and right politically (unlike Executive Branch Club)
- Annual retreats in exclusive locations (Venice, Middle East, etc.)
- **Building permanent DC campus** (announced Aug 2025)
