## Overview

The Executive Branch is an ultra-exclusive members-only club in Georgetown, D.C., co-founded in 2025 by **Donald Trump Jr.** (N-018), **Omeed Malik** (N-015), **Chris Buskirk** (N-016), and the **Witkoff brothers**. With a **$500,000 initiation fee** plus annual dues, it serves as a *de facto* access point to Trump's inner circle.

**Key Features:**

- Invite-only, vetted for Trump loyalty
- Media banned; no recordings allowed
- "Swamp marketplace" - wealthy insiders meet Cabinet officials
