## THE PATTERN: "Regulatory Leverage Play"

Sook represents a more sophisticated version of the media-regulatory capture loop than Sinclair:

1. Build largest TV empire through aggressive M&A
2. Expand into national media (The Hill, NewsNation, The CW)
3. Position as "industry statesman" (NAB Chairman)
4. Announce mega-deal that requires rule change (Tegna $6.2B)
5. Demonstrate "cooperation" with FCC (preempt Kimmel)
6. Wait for friendly regulator to grant waiver

**Key difference from Sinclair:** No shell companies, less overtly partisan content, but equally dependent on Carr's rule changes.

## FOUNDING STORY & CAREER

### Pre-Nexstar
- Brief TV news anchor at CBS affiliate, Clarksburg, WV
- Cox Broadcasting (5 years): Pittsburgh sales, Telerep
- President/CEO of Seaway Communication (Bangor, ME; Wausau, WI)
- Principal at Superior Communication Group (sold to Sinclair 1995)

### Building Nexstar
- **1996:** Founded Nexstar; acquired WYOU Scranton, PA
- **2003:** Took Nexstar public after acquiring Quorum Broadcasting
- **2003-2025:** Aggressive M&A; built to largest US TV owner
- **Key moment:** Retransmission consent battle with Cox Cable - won ~$2B/year in new industry revenue
