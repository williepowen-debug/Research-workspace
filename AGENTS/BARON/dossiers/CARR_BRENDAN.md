## TRUMP vs CARR CONFLICT (Nov 2025)

**The Rift:** Trump publicly opposed Carr's ownership cap agenda on Nov 24, 2025 via Truth Social.

**Trump's position:** "If this would also allow the Radical Left Networks to 'enlarge,' I would not be happy... NO EXPANSION OF THE FAKE NEWS NETWORKS. If anything, make them SMALLER!"

**The Ruddy Factor:**
- Chris Ruddy (Newsmax CEO) is longtime Trump friend
- Ruddy argued cap lift would "silence Republicans" by letting big networks crush smaller conservative players
- If cap had been lifted 10 years ago, "Donald Trump would have never won the presidency" - Ruddy
- Trump's Truth Social post linked directly to Newsmax coverage of Ruddy's opposition
- Ruddy declared Trump "strongly standing with us"

**Conservative Coalition Against Cap Lift:**
- Newsmax (Ruddy)
- OAN
- CPAC
All aligned AGAINST Sinclair/Nexstar

**Legal Challenge (Dec 2025):**
- ATVA filed Dec 5: FCC **lacks legal authority** to eliminate cap
- Congress set 39% limit in 2004 and removed FCC's discretion
- If upheld, Carr cannot act even if Trump reverses

**Market Impact:**
- SBGI, NXST, TGNA stocks cratered after Trump post
- Rally unlikely until conflict resolved

**Scenarios:**
1. **Carr defies Trump** - Proceeds anyway; risks public rebuke
2. **Trump reverses** - Sinclair/Nexstar lobby changes his mind
3. **Legal obstacle** - Courts block FCC per ATVA argument
4. **Selective enforcement** - Approve friendlies only (legally dubious)

**Key Question:** Does Carr push forward or wait? Ripley still confident on H1 2026.
