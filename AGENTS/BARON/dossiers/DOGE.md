## Overview

The Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), established January 20, 2025 via E.O. 14158, is not an advisory body but a **parallel control mechanism for federal capture**. By repurposing the statutory authority of the United States Digital Service (USDS), DOGE has embedded politically-appointed teams within 30+ federal agencies with "full and prompt access" to all unclassified records and systems.

**Legal Basis:**
- E.O. 14158: Established DOGE, mandated agency teams, granted systems access
- E.O. 14222: Created centralized payment systems with DOGE oversight (fiscal choke point)
- 5 U.S.C. § 3161: Temporary organization authority (expires July 4, 2026)
