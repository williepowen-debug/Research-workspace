| L-08 | 2026-07-16 | **ASYMMETRIC RIGOR — the failure that actually happened.** Adjudicating WALTER's filter, VULCAN was rigorous where it grepped (270 kill-log rows → 6/6 kills correct; that verdict survived) and sloppy where it *inferred* — it told WALTER (a) `cluster_secondary` was "unused" (actually **199 tags live**; inferred from the primary-cluster count without grepping the field) and (b) obsolescence was "folded into" the financing signals (actually **0 hits** on SIG-626-008/031/627-017). **Both claims were asserted while holding ZERO coverage of the thing being claimed, and both were wrong.** The tell: *demanding receipts from a counterparty while shipping a hypothesis as an argument in the same document.* **Rule: apply the evidentiary standard you're imposing on the other agent to your own claims in the same breath — and when you've just disclosed "I have no coverage of X," that is a bar on asserting things about X, not a licence.** Related: `feedback_evidence_standalone`, `finding_same_datum_two_evidentiary_standards`. **CODA — it recurred INSIDE the retraction, one message later: DEFERENCE IS NOT VERIFICATION.** Retracting the `cluster_secondary` claim, VULCAN published 199, then deferred to WALTER's 192 reasoning *"you counted signals, I counted grep hits — yours is better."* **That premise was itself unchecked** — WALTER's 192 was tag *instances*, mislabeled; the real signals count is **190** (192 instances; 2 multi-tagged). **Neither published number was the signals count, and VULCAN's 199 doesn't reproduce even under its own method (198) — an arithmetic slip over a `head`-truncated list, never a defensible measure.** Accepting a counterparty's number without checking its **unit and provenance** is the same failure as asserting from priors, just outsourced — and conceding a point does not license sloppiness about *why* you're conceding. A shared metric reconciles to **one figure with its unit** (`[[finding_number_carries_threshold_unit_source]]`, `[[finding_asymmetric_records_need_reconciliation]]`), never to two numbers with a courtesy between them. | WALTER axis check (read-only-spawned instance) |
