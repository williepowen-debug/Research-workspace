| L-01 | 2026-07-10 | VULCAN's edge is the *mechanism*, not the *expression*: VIOLET already prices concentration-unwind vol, HENRY already values the FCF. VULCAN loses its reason to exist if it drifts into either — it must supply the fundamental driver (capex trajectory, memory cycle, compute demand, supply chokepoint) that those agents consume. Stay upstream. | build session |
| L-02 | 2026-07-10 | Systemic-lens, not sector-desk: the temptation is to become a semiconductor-earnings tracker (NVDA beat/miss). Resist — the mandate is the AI buildout as a systemic *vector* (concentration/memory/power/chokepoint). A single stock's earnings reaction is a confounded thermometer, not the mechanism. | build session |
| L-03 | 2026-07-10 | Born with no instrument (unlike WATT): the channels are earnings/policy-cadence, not per-boot. Don't force a scraper on day 1 — if a `semi_watch.py` gets built later, wire its cadence durably (PAT-041), not into SCRATCH. | build session |
| L-04 | 2026-07-12 | S2 and S1 are not actually independent roots the way the build-session matrix assumed: the memory shortage (S2) is a direct, quantified cost-push input into the hyperscalers' capex-guide raises (S1) — MSFT and META both name component/memory pricing as a guide-raise driver, and MSFT quantifies it (~$25B of $190B). Before treating channels as independent for a composite-stress count, check whether one channel's price action is literally inside another channel's headline number. | 2026-07-12 session |
| L-05 | 2026-07-12 | Real-money numbers move fast and diverge by source (AMZN Q1 capex reported as both $43.2B "cash capex" and $44.2B "property+equipment" depending on outlet) — carry both figures with their exact framing rather than picking one, and note the reconciliation in KB.tsv. Aggregator-cited concentration metrics (Mag-7 weight) are vaguer on vintage than earnings-release numbers — flag the sourcing gap explicitly rather than presenting both with equal confidence. | 2026-07-12 session |
