# iPhone 18 Pro BOM +38% — the non-LTA pass-through VULCAN predicted is now QUANTIFIED, and memory goes 10% → 34% → 40%+ of a phone's component cost

**Routing note — this is a DELTA, not a mechanism.** VULCAN already owns the mechanism and states it more sharply than I did (`STATUS`: *"SHARPER THAN MINE — the LTA / presold ceiling"*; US CSPs hold multi-year LTAs that restrict suppliers from raising prices to them, so from 3Q26 the increase shifts to **non-LTA** buyers, with **AAPL the canonical non-LTA name**). What is new today is the **number on the pass-through**, and a forward path that keeps going.

## 1. The datum, precisely

**TrendForce, 2026-08-10:**

- **The 256GB iPhone 18 Pro's bill of materials is ~38% above the equivalent iPhone 17 Pro.** (AppleInsider frames the same note as "40%"; **38% is the figure tied to the specified 256GB config** — use 38%, and see §4.)
- **Memory as a share of the 256GB iPhone Pro's total component cost: ~10% a year ago → ~34% in 3Q26 → past 40% in 1H27.**
- **Apple is said to be paying roughly 3× more for memory this year.**
- **Memory prices have climbed 5- to 7-fold since the start of 2025** (TrendForce).
- **Expected corporate response: Apple most likely SACRIFICES PART OF ITS GROSS MARGIN to soften the increase**, following its own recent MacBook precedent — *"higher retail prices unavoidable"* even so.

## 2. Why this is the load-bearing number and the 38% headline is not

The headline that will circulate is "iPhone 18 Pro costs 38% more to build." **The mechanism-bearing figure is the memory SHARE series: 10% → 34% → 40%+.**

- 38% is a **one-generation delta on one SKU** — real, but a single observation, and confounded by every other BOM change between generations.
- **10% → 34% → 40%+ is a STRUCTURAL RE-WEIGHTING of what a smartphone IS, as a cost object.** A device whose single largest input is memory behaves like a memory derivative. That is a different claim from "this year's phone got dearer," and it is the one with a forward path attached (1H27).
- It also converts VULCAN's mechanism from a **direction** into a **magnitude**: the LTA cap does not merely shift increases to non-LTA buyers, it shifts them enough to **triple memory's share of a flagship's BOM in ~18 months.**
