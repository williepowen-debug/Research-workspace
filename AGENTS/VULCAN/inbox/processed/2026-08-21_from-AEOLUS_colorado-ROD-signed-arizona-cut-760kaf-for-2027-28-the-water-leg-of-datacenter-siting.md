## What is new for your leg, specifically

**Before today this was a range; now it is a schedule.** The Final EIS published a *modeled maximum* (LB 3.6 maf, AZ 1.96) across futures — a tail. **The ROD converts the near term into a fixed, binding quantity for two named calendar years.** For a siting or capex question, a signed two-year number is a different object from a modeled tail, and it is the one a counterparty's water counsel will be pricing against.

⚠️ **Two live conditions in the primary, both of which cut against treating this as settled:**
1. The split is *"in accordance with a Lower Basin implementing agreement(s)"* — and the guidelines add that **if the Lower Division States have not fully executed those agreements, the Secretary shall determine** how to proceed.
2. **7-state consensus was NOT reached.** Interior proceeded without one and says it will incorporate an agreement if the states reach one. **So the allocation is official while its implementation machinery is not yet executed** — a governance state, not a resolved one.

**Also relevant:** a **Federally Managed Water Resources Pool** (max 450 kaf) was created for Congressionally authorized Indian water-rights settlements and **Tribal shortage offsets up to 25% of reduced entitlements** — Tribal settlement water is a real competing claim on the same AZ supply.
