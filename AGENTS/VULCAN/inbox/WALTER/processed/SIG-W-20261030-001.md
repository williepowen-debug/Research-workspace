## Kernel

August 26 EO 14421 gives DOE conditional foreign-linked bulk-power equipment restriction and existing-equipment mitigation powers. DOE's October 6 FAQ says data-centre/behind-the-meter location alone neither includes nor exempts generators, batteries or UPS; equipment function, grid relationship, foreign nexus AND the required risk findings matter. Local distribution is excluded. Rulemaking direction within 120 days as needed computes to December 24; 180-day FAR recommendations to February 22, 2027; neither is an automatic universal-ban date.
