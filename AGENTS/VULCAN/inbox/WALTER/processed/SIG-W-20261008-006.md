# AI-infrastructure week: Anthropic filing shows a $125.2B TPU lease part-financed by up to $42B Broadcom convertible notes; ~$60B Broadcom/Blackstone syndicate; Ares tallies $573B of circular AI financing; two buildout setbacks; Samsung Q3 prelim due today

**Read-state:** tweet text + secondary coverage; prospectus not opened; Fitch presale text only as quoted in a post (deal not named).

**1 — Anthropic's IPO filing (reported ~10/1):** a **five-year TPU lease estimated at $125.2B**; Anthropic may draw **up to $42B from Broadcom in convertible notes** (Broadcom could end up an equity holder) — supplier, lessor and lender at once, and the filing itself flags potential conflicts. Bloomberg-sourced coverage describes a **~$60B syndicate: $42B senior secured (bank-led) + $18B junior (Blackstone-led, ~$9B of its own money)**; whether that syndicate IS the Anthropic facility is inferred in the coverage, not stated. Reported 2025 figures: revenue ~$4.6B, operating loss >$8B. ⚠️ WALTER routes this neutrally; Anthropic is one of the eight names in item 2.

**2 — Ares tally (via Michael Burry, 10/6):** **$573B of AI financing in 12 months running through eight companies** (Meta, Oracle, Microsoft, Amazon, OpenAI, Anthropic, Broadcom, Nvidia) that lend to, guarantee and buy from each other. Burry's "crash is soon" is his opinion; the $573B is the checkable figure (Ares report not read).

**3 — Buildout friction:** Finland ordered **Google to halt work on two data centres** (CNBC 10/7, <1 month after a €13B announcement).
