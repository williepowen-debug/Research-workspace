# Micron splits the presidency: Bhatia → President & COO, DeBoer → President & CTPO — and CBO Sumit Sadana steps back to Senior Advisor

**One line:** MU 8-K (filed 8/26, event 8/24): **Manish Bhatia (54), EVP Global Operations → President & Chief Operating Officer**; **Dr. Scott DeBoer (60), EVP CTPO → President & Chief Technology and Products Officer**, both effective immediately; **Sumit Sadana transitions from EVP & Chief Business Officer to Senior Advisor to the CEO.** No CEO change (Mehrotra unnamed in the filing = unchanged). No compensation changes "at this time." Press release attached as Ex-99.1.

## Read, held to what the filing supports

- **Two co-Presidents is succession architecture.** Bhatia (operations, fab construction, supply chain — at MU since 2017) and DeBoer (technology — at MU since 1995) are now the two named seconds. The filing states no arrangements/understandings and no related-party items.
- **The Sadana move is the asymmetric piece.** The Chief Business Officer — the public face of MU's commercial/AI strategy — moving to "Senior Advisor to the CEO" is a step OUT of the operating line. The filing gives no reason and announces no departure; a later separation 8-K is the thing to watch for.
- **Timing context (yours, not the filing's):** this lands mid-supercycle — same-day trade press has Korean DRAM spot up ~12.5× in 3 years and memory projected toward a majority share of cloud-provider capex. An operations-heavy President (fab buildout) fits the capex-supercycle read; that is inference, flagged as such.

**ASK — VULCAN (action):** hold as a governance/KB row on MU; no threshold or band implied. Watch-fors: a Sadana separation 8-K, and any strategy inflection attributable to the new structure at the next earnings call (FQ4 report ~late Sep).
