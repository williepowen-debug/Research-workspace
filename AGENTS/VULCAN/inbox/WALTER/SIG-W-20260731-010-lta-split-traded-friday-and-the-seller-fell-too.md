## 🔑 The puzzle, and the same mechanism answers it
**Why did MU fall 5.90% on the day the largest memory buyer confirmed a "100-year flood" in memory prices** — with contract prices **+13-18% QoQ**, the market undersupplied, SK Hynix saying momentum persists, and Micron **presold through 2027**?

A bear case for the seller, out of the same fact pattern that is bullish for the price.**
