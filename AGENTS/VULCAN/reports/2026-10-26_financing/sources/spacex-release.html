SPACEX SECURES $40 BILLION IN THE LARGEST GPU FINANCING EVER

October 26, 2026
  • Download (opens in new window)

SpaceX has reached a binding commitment to enter into an approximately $40 billion master finance lease for GPUs and related equipment with an entity managed by Apollo Global Management, Inc. (Apollo). This lease will deliver the next generation of NVIDIA Vera Rubin chips for SpaceX’s terrestrial compute buildout and is an important step in our plan to build a Super Intelligence future. It represents the largest committed GPU financing, underscoring the strength of SpaceX’s infrastructure capabilities.

CFO Commentary 

“SpaceX aims to deploy the largest compute infrastructure in the world. As demand for compute continues to accelerate, we’re seeing less than one year payback periods on our SI infrastructure investments. This lease diversifies our funding sources and addresses a notable portion of the funding required to deliver on our growth plans as we aim to get additional compute online at record speeds to power Grok for enterprises and consumers, as well as serve existing and future cloud services customers.”

Transaction Details

The financing has been committed to by a number of financing parties led by Apollo and Goldman Sachs, who served as sole structuring agent. Funds and accounts managed by BlackRock, Goldman Sachs Asset Management, and capital accounts advised by KKR have also made anchor commitments in the transaction.

Entry into the lease remains subject to certain specified closing conditions and is expected to close prior to year-end 2026, with lease schedules expected to commence upon delivery of GPUs and related equipment in the first half of 2027.

Additional information regarding the terms of the lease will be available in SpaceX’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, also available on the Company’s investor relations website here.
