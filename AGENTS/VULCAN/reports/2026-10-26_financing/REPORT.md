## 1. Meta — external equity is now concrete, but not evidence of insolvency

PRIMARY: [issuer October26 release](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Announces-Launch-of-Offering-of-Depositary-Shares-Representing-Series-A-Mandatory-Convertible-Preferred-Stock-and-Offering-of-Class-A-Common-Stock-in-Connection-with-Forward-Sale-Agreements/default.aspx), [common preliminary supplement, accession 0001193125-26-431040](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526431040/d94886d424b5.htm), [preferred preliminary supplement, accession 0001193125-26-431043](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526431043/d133631d424b5.htm). Own full original retrievals succeeded; scoped reads include cover, offering terms, settlement and funding-plan sections.

Material delta from prior Class C authorization: actual marketed financing instruments now exist. The common forward delays receipt until settlement; the preferred introduces equity conversion/dividend terms, not a simple $12.5B cash debt maturity. Expected mandatory conversion is August15 2029; preliminary dividend rate and final conversion economics remain unspecified. Completion of either concurrent offering is independent of the other. Jan19 2027 is the charter effective date in the common supplement, not cash receipt.

NVDA, AMD and Broadcom have indicated aggregate interest up to $5B, severally and nonbinding: allocation may be more, less or none. This makes supplier/customer financial correlation relevant to S1/S5; it does not establish a quid-pro-quo chip purchase, supplier guarantee, money already paid or a new contractual circular-funding loop.

Counterevidence: issuer repeats capex guide $135–145B for2026, not a cut, and reports $97B operating cash flow for the first nine months and about $82B cash/equivalents/marketable securities at Q3 end. These are different periods and measures; no funding-gap subtraction of nine-month OCF from full-year capex is valid. Issuer says equity complements operating cash, IG debt and partnerships. If priced and completed, equity can reduce reliance on debt and absorb losses; it also dilutes holders. The existing META cluster grade remains RAISE/REWARDED after hours on Oct21; this later financing announcement does not regrade that earnings reaction.

## 2. SpaceX — strongest commitment, with rate and risk allocation still missing

PRIMARY: [October26 release](https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/SPACEX-SECURES-40-BILLION-IN-THE-LARGEST-GPU-FINANCING-EVER-2026-EnDqGhTAB3/default.aspx), [8-K Item8.01, accession0001104659-26-118614](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000110465926118614/tm2628247d1_8k.htm). Own original retrieval and Item8.01 read; no underlying master lease was reviewed.

The lessor would purchase GPU pods and lease them to company-selected datacenters. Separate tranche schedules run approximately5–5.25years; rent quarterly in advance generally includes 1/20 of financed tranche cost, fixed-rate interest on outstanding balance, and ongoing expenses. The fixed rate is to be set before entry into the lease. The ~$40B is expected aggregate principal payments across schedules, not interest-inclusive total cost or issuer cash received. No spread-to-talk flex can be graded without matched pricing.

Apollo leads financing parties; Goldman is structuring agent; funds/accounts managed by BlackRock, GSAM and KKR-advised capital accounts have anchor commitments. These roles do not identify final tranche holdings, named-bank retained losses, insurer allocations, sponsor recourse or residual guarantees. BROCK owns that structure read.

Counterevidence to a blanket funding-shut thesis: multiple institutions are willing to commit at large scale; this supports planned Vera Rubin acquisition and future buildout. It is stronger than an expression of interest. It is weaker than a closed/priced transaction and accepted energized hardware. The CFO's less-than-one-year payback claim is management assertion, not a verified return. Nothing here supplies chip count, site MW, firm grid rights, commissioning date or utilization; no dollars-to-GW conversion.
