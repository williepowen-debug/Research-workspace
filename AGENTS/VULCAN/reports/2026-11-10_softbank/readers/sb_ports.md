## Bottom line
Every outside record dated after September 30 that I found shows the campus and its power supply **moving forward, with no resizing or cancellation**:
- **AEP's Q3 10-Q (filed 10/28):** the August transmission agreements are in effect.
- **Ohio Power Siting Board (OPSB):** a local public hearing for the first gas plant is set for **December 10, 2026**.
- **The gas developer:** held information sessions for a second gas plant (up to 5 GW) on **November 5 and 12**.
- **FERC:** comments on the PJM study agreement closed on **November 3**. AEP and Ohio's Federal Energy Advocate support it; consumer and environmental groups want stronger cost protections. No FERC order was found.
- **SB Energy:** no EDGAR filing after the September 21 S-1/A. No withdrawal was filed.

SoftBank's "completely unrelated" claim is **narrower than it sounds**:
- SoftBank's own release says the equipment was bought for US projects **"including the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus"**.
- In May, SB Energy said turbines had been reserved for the **Ohio 9.2 GW gas plant**.
- The only SoftBank gas developer of that scale in any public record is the **non-subsidiary SoftBank affiliate that is building the PORTS-Pike gas plants** (SB Energy S-1).
- **INFERENCE:** the claim most plausibly means the loss does not reflect a smaller PORTS-Pike scope. It does not establish that none of the written-off equipment was originally intended for PORTS-Pike. No outside record identifies the written-off equipment or its intended site.
