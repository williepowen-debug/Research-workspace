# SoftBank SB7 power-equipment write-off: who supplied it?

Reader for VULCAN · read-only · started 2026-11-10 17:24 EST (from `date`) · $0 sources · EDGAR fetched with the edgar_watch.py `UA` (contact from the local .env, not reproduced here).

## Bottom line

**No source found names the supplier whose contract SB7 wrote off or modified.** That covers SoftBank, every supplier filing checked, Musk, and the press.

- **The only supplier publicly tied to SoftBank's Ohio gas plant is GE Vernova.** The basis is an SB Energy statement in May 2026, known only through secondary reports, that it had reserved turbines from "multiple suppliers, including GE Vernova." That ties GE Vernova to the Ohio project, not to the written-off advances. The other suppliers in that "multiple" remain unnamed.
- **GE Vernova's own Q3 disclosures show no cancellation.** Its slot-reservation bridge reconciles exactly with zero leakage: 63 + 8 − 13 = 58 GW. GE Vernova told Reuters on 11/10 that the SoftBank changes had "no negative or positive impact" on it.
- **Musk named no supplier, and the Ohio project may be shrinking or may not.** Accounts of the CFO's 11/10 remarks conflict on whether Ohio is being downsized.

## Inferences worth carrying (labelled; none is a finding)

1. **Timing mismatch.** SoftBank booked the charge in the July–September quarter, the same quarter GE Vernova reported. GE Vernova's 9/30 slot-reservation bridge shows zero unexplained loss, and its Q3 Other income shows no settlement gain. The SB7 counterparty is therefore one of the following: (a) **not GE Vernova**; (b) GE Vernova but booked after 9/30, consistent with SoftBank saying settlement cash flows "from Q3 onward"; (c) under 1 GW and lost in rounding; or (d) advances held by an EPC or intermediary, not the original equipment maker. **These cannot be distinguished from public data today.**
2. **What was written off is not necessarily turbines.** SoftBank says "power-generation-related equipment" and "manufacturing capacity" (製造能力確保). That could include transformers, switchgear, reciprocating engines or fuel cells. Musk said "turbines", without evidence.
3. **Earliest supplier-side tell.** GE Vernova's Q4 release (~late January 2027) would show it: a slot-reservation bridge with a residual, or a customer-settlement or forfeiture line in Other income.
