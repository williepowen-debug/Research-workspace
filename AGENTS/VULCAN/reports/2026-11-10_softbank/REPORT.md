## Answer first

SoftBank's $3.7B charge (¥597.2B) is a **write-off and renegotiation of power-equipment orders, not a default and not a cancelled campus**. The buyer was Silver Bands 7 (SB7), a SoftBank and Vision Fund 2 vehicle separate from SB Energy. It wrote off $2.9B of non-refundable advances for equipment "no longer in the revised procurement plans" and took $0.9B for contract changes and settlements. No supplier is named anywhere. Every outside record dated after September 30 shows the Ohio campus and its transmission going ahead. SoftBank's own split puts the written-off $2.9B in the bucket "other than" the "PORTS-Pike power generation project and others" ($6.6B kept), and its accounting deck says the loss is "completely unrelated to PORTS-Pike". Outside records agree there is no cut to the campus, but none can confirm which projects the dropped equipment was for, and the only SoftBank power-equipment reservations ever reported were for the Ohio gas plants. The CFO also put Ohio's equipment buying under "a thorough reassessment" (Bloomberg via TIKR). For this desk: **one filed observation for the armed S1 early-warning indicator (equipment-order cancellations), no channel letter met, 15/25 unchanged.**

## (e) The Intel prepaid forward, only where it touches the AI chain

A wholly owned SoftBank financing subsidiary raised **$7.35B** in August 2026 on prepaid forward collars over all 87.0M Intel shares, non-recourse to SBG (financial report p.6/4, p.14/12, via SAM). The ¥321.9B loss is the collar's liability rising as Intel rose to 9/30; in SoftBank's leverage ratio it nets against the shares, no separate effect (SAM). **Its only touch on the AI chain is as funding, and that touch is filed:** "The proceeds were used to fund part of the repayment of borrowings under the bridge facility agreement" (financial report PDF p.38), and the bridge was "the full $30.0 billion drawn under a $40.0 billion bridge facility agreement entered into in March 2026, primarily to fund follow-on investments in OpenAI", prepaid by 9/15 (PDF p.6). Chain: Intel collar → bridge repaid → the bridge had funded OpenAI. Other repayment sources: an $11.87B term loan and $5.0B more Arm margin loan (sb-docs, PRIMARY). So part of SoftBank's OpenAI money now rests on Intel's share price inside a collar band. Intel as a chipmaker is not on any VULCAN channel and nothing here moves S4. Counterparty, cap, floor and settlement dates: not disclosed.
