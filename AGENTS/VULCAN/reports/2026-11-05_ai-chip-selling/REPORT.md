# VULCAN owner read: the November 5 AI and chip selling

**Written:** 2026-11-05, morning, Eastern. **Basis:** every market figure is a vendor intraday quote against the November 4 close unless a close is named; a close supersedes all of them. Filing figures are SEC primary. Rows: KB-VULCAN-346 to 365.

**Bottom line.** Nothing that would prove the AI spending cycle has turned is established today. No big buyer cut spending, and no VULCAN letter fired. Composite stays 15/25. The cause of the selling is not established. What is established is the shape, and two harder facts underneath it: borrowing has become much more expensive for the same issuers, and backlog additions slowed in the latest quarter. Both predate today.

**The shape is the finding.** The sellers are hitting the companies that receive AI money far harder than the ones that spend it. On November 4 Microsoft rose 6.68%, Amazon 6.12% and Apple 5.31% while SMH fell 2.75% (closes). Cause of that payer rally not read.

**Most of it predates the vote.** At the November 3 close, before any result, SMH was already −18.2% from its October 5 peak and QQQ −9.8% from its October 6 peak. Today's intraday levels are about −26.9% and −13.0%. So roughly two thirds of the SMH fall and three quarters of the QQQ fall came first [KB-347]. An election story can explain only the last two sessions.

## 5. Owner judgment (working model, not truth)

The market is repricing who gets paid and at what cost of money. Big buyers hold because they can slow down; suppliers and developers fall because any slowdown lands on them first. The tape alone cannot separate that from a positioning unwind, and the two can coexist. What would confirm it: a guide-down, a pulled developer deal, or falling contract memory prices. What would break it: CoreWeave's print and Nvidia's guide showing demand and financing intact.
