*Compiled 2026-09-25 ~09:15 ET. Web sources only. No repo files edited. Prices below are dated SECONDARY quotes, not live. Root rule #4: re-pull before citing.*

## Top 5 for the semis/memory thesis
1. **The 9/14 selloff was a sentiment shock from AI-lab CEOs, not a supply or demand print. The desk's framing ("OpenAI shelved its IPO") is only half right.** Amodei's Saturday essay (slow capability gains), which Altman endorsed, was the main trigger. Altman's IPO delay (Fortune/Axios, 9/12) mattered most for SoftBank. KOSPI closed −3.26%, SK Hynix −6.35%, Kioxia −6.37%, MU −6%. All of it was retraced by 9/23.
2. **The US-China truce was extended from Nov-10 to Jan-10-2027 at the 9/24 Trump–Xi summit** (Bessent, per CNBC/NPR/US News, 9/24). There was no chip or export-control easing. **Not yet confirmed at a primary:** whether the BIS Affiliates Rule stay and MOFCOM's suspensions are formally carried to 1/10.
3. **Contract prices: still rising but decelerating.** TrendForce says it "lifts its 4Q26 contract price outlook" (DRAM bulletin, 9/23). The Korean sell-side DRAM ASP path runs **+16.5% QoQ in Q3 to +5.4% QoQ in Q4** (Seoul Economic Daily, 9/25). That is a cut in the rate of increase, not a price cut.
4. **Spot is flat and demand is thin.** DDR4 8Gb spot was $45.32 (9/9) and $46.107 (9/24), about +1.7%. TrendForce says "DDR5 inquiries … slowed, some chip prices beginning to soften" (9/16) and "no actual demand orders" (9/23). DDR4 16Gb spot ($83.78) is trading above DDR5 16Gb ($57.67).
5. **MU into 9/30:** the guide is $50.0B ±$1.0B and ~86% GAAP and non-GAAP GM (8-K, 6/24). That is +1.4pp over FQ3's GAAP 84.6%. Consensus revenue is **above** guide at $50.4–51.3B. No public GM consensus above 86% was found. MU ran from about $916 (9/14) to about $1,080–1,090 (9/22–23), roughly +18%.
