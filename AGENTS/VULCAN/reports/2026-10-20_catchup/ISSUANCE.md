## Does it establish the rates mechanism?

The positive result is supply of long original maturities. [Dallas Fed's February 10 primary analysis](https://www.dallasfed.org/research/economics/2026/0210-searls-aifinancing) distinguishes direct duration supply, swap-mediated supply and credit-spread effects; its forecast is not an estimate from this inventory. Issuance and Treasury yields share inflation, fiscal, monetary and risk-premium drivers. A yield move beside an offering cannot identify the offering's causal effect.

The dataset has no event-control design, interest-rate hedges, modified duration or net-duration measure.
