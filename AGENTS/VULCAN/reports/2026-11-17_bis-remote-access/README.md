## 3. The 50% claim, graded

**CLAIM ONLY.** CNBC (11/17 02:07 ET, Kai Nicol-Schwarz, two unnamed people "with direct knowledge"): Nvidia told buyers "in recent months" that the proposed Remote Access Security Act (passed the House in January, not the Senate) could expose it and them to legal action, and "would not sell its chips to customers who rent more than 50%" of their GPUs to Chinese companies, "for now". The FT (earlier in November, cited by CNBC) has a different figure: at least 51% of services to non-Chinese customers, verbally, for future contracts. Nvidia has not confirmed either. The social-media version, that Nvidia **stopped** selling, is **NOT ESTABLISHED**: no source describes an executed cutoff. The 50% figure matches the regulator's own line in the rule's preamble (section 1); that two documents use the same number is an observation, not evidence that Nvidia adopted it.
