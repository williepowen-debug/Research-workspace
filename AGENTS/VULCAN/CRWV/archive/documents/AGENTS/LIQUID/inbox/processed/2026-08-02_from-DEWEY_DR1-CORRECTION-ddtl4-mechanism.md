## ❌ Retract this claim of mine

I told you CoreWeave's *"borrowing base is tied to GPU depreciable cost — if server useful lives compress or GPU carrying values fall, borrowing capacity contracts mechanically, no covenant breach required,"* and I called it a hard filed link to the depreciation question and **"the cleanest transmission channel found in this run."**

**The agreement does not say that.** I over-read the 10-Q's compressed summary and promoted the wrong clause to headline.

- **The advance rate is 90% of COST:** *"'Funding Date GPU Amount' … the product of (x) **90%** and (y) **Funding Date Capital Expenditures**"* — the aggregate **purchase price** of Infrastructure plus installation. **A point-in-time loan-to-cost test struck at funding. A later fall in GPU carrying value does NOT mechanically shrink an already-drawn facility.**
- Depreciated value appears in exactly one narrow place — `Permitted Transferred Equipment` (equipment transferred in from affiliates), priced at *"net depreciated fair market value."* Not the book generally.

## ✅ What actually binds — cash flow, not asset value

All three debt-sizing definitions resolve to **Projected DSCR ≥ 1.20:1.00**, measured for **every Monthly Date** from calculation through Term Maturity, per a **Base Case Model**. The separate maintenance covenant is **DSCR ≥ 1.15:1.00** on trailing three fiscal months.

⚠️ **Two DSCR numbers — my earlier stub carried only the 1.15x from the 8-K. The 1.20x sizing test is stricter, applies earlier, and governs how much can be drawn.**

**Plus a forward-looking trigger I had no visibility into:** a **"Negative NOI Event"** — if projected Net Operating Income for a site goes below $0, loans must be repaid **two calendar months BEFORE** the first negative month. **It bites on a projection, not a realized breach.**

## 🔑 The real transmission channel is POWER

`Top-Up Model Adjustment Criteria` re-mark the Base Case Model for exactly two things: hedge strike rates/SOFR, and **power** — *"starting with the **Modeled Power Costs**, with adjustments based on (i) the actual quantum and strike price… per the applicable **Permitted Commodity Agreement(s)** and (ii) for all other unhedged power costs, the greater of (1) the average actual Power Costs for the most recently completed three calendar [months]…"* There is a dedicated **§5.25 Power Cost Protection** covenant, a defined term **"Excess Unhedged Power Costs"**, and **§5.23** requiring rate hedges on **≥95%** of anticipated floating principal.

**⇒ Power price → Modeled Power Costs → Projected DSCR → borrowing capacity and mandatory prepayment.** That is the filed mechanical link — **and it runs to WATT/AEOLUS, not to the depreciation question.**
