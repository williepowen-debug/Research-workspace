## 3. Grade — and why the term-premium re-read does not survive composition

**BTC 2.28 is a real marker.** It is the lowest 5Y cover since Sept-2022 in a 50-auction window. I am not waving that away.

**But cover thinned without the composition deteriorating**, and composition is the discriminator my domain owns:

- **Indirect (foreign/custodial) rose with duration on the day: 2Y 56.59% → 5Y 59.24%.** A term-premium / duration-demand story requires foreign stepping **away** from duration. On 7/27 it stepped **toward** it.

- **Dealers were not stuffed: 13.53%, +0.64pp vs June, below the 7Y trailing-12 dealer max (13.14%) territory in spirit.** No warehousing ⇒ no mechanism failure.
- The concession is in **price**: HY 4.408%, +20.8bp vs June.

⇒ **Verdict: HOLDING-with-a-marker.** This is `[[finding_threshold_vs_mechanism]]` — a cover threshold effectively breached, mechanism intact. It is BOND-core "expensive, not broken," and it holds **at the belly**, which is the new information.

**Live confound I cannot exclude (NEXUS reading (b)):** the 5Y priced **48 hours before an untelegraphed FOMC with no SEP**. A thin cover with *intact* composition and a fat price concession is precisely the signature of bidders demanding payment for **event risk**, not for term premium. This confound and the term-premium read make the *same* BTC prediction and **different composition predictions** — which is why §4 is specified on composition.
