## Answer

The bond market took a payroll beat above every forecast in the range as a reason to believe the next hike, and little more. The 2Y rose 5bp and the 30Y 1bp. I carry HENRY's odds and take no separate number: 76% from futures, HENRY 0.70, with the caveat that the same method read 72% for October on 9/24 and the Fed held.

What BOND adds: **hikes coming out of the 2027 path have not lowered real long rates.** Whichever start date since the October FOMC I use, the priced path fell 11 to 30bp and the real 10Y moved −4 to +3bp. Borrowers have had no relief in real terms. So a December hike or hold matters to the long end less for the 25bp than for what it says about the Fed's resolve.
