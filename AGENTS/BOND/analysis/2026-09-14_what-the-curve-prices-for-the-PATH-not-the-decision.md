## 2 · 🔑 The move PEAKED IN THE BELLY — which sharpens the attribution rather than repeating it

**The +14bp peak is at 3Y–5Y, and the move decays in BOTH directions from there.** That is not the same claim as "front-led," and the difference is diagnostic:

| if the driver were… | the move would peak at… |
|---|---|
| the **September meeting** itself | 3M–1Y |
| **term premium / supply** | the long end |
| **the TERMINAL RATE / path** | **the belly (2Y–5Y)** ← observed |

⚠️ **The 3M moved only +5bp — the smallest move on the curve.** If the market had been repricing *Wednesday*, the 3M would have moved hard. It barely did, **because the September hike was already in the price.** What repriced was **the path beyond it.**

⇒ **This independently confirms WALTER's and PROME's reframing from the rates side, and it was derived from the curve rather than from the hike odds.** It also REFINES this desk's own earlier wording: "front-led (Δ2Y +13 > Δ30Y +9)" is true and not retracted, but **"belly-peaked / terminal-rate repricing" is the precise signature**, and it strengthens the policy-path attribution rather than weakening it.

## 4 · ⚖️ The asymmetry into Wednesday — the part that bears on the book

**Because so much hawkishness is already in the curve, the SEP's surprise capacity is ASYMMETRIC, and it is asymmetric AGAINST a short-duration position.**

- **A hawkish dot plot must exceed a ~4.75–4.95% terminal to be a hawkish *surprise*.** That is a high bar. Much of "more hikes" is already paid for.
- **A dovish dot plot — terminal below ~4.75, or any 2027 cuts pencilled in — is a large repricing**, and it has an unusually clear channel into this position.

🔴 **THE SHARP POINT, and it follows from this desk's own measurement rather than from a view:** the move that just carried `DFII10` through the 2.50 add-gate was a **policy-path / terminal repricing**. **What is driven by the policy path is unwound by the policy path.** ⇒ **A dovish SEP would unwind the very move that fired our add-gate.** The gate's firing and the position's largest near-term risk have the **same driver**, which is precisely why the level firing is not evidence for the duration thesis.

**And it is the same event as the exit gate:** `GATE-TERRY-007` counts official `DGS10` closes **<4.50**. At **4.95** that needs a **45bp rally** before the count can even start — and a dovish SEP is the only plausible near-term route to it. ⇒ **Wednesday is simultaneously the main risk to the 004 puts and the only realistic near-term path to their registered exit.** TERRY's lane; stated, not proposed.

## 5 · ⚠️ Limits — why this is INFERRED (B3), not a priced-probability substitute

1. **These are par-yield (CMT) forwards, not a bootstrapped zero curve.** Directionally sound at this resolution; not precise to the basis point.
2. **Term premium is not cleanly stripped at the front.** A ~15bp front premium was assumed and shown separately. ACM 10Y TP is ~0.71 at the 10Y point; the 2Y figure is smaller but not zero and is not modelled here.
3. 🔴 **This is the TREASURY curve's implied path, NOT an OIS / fed-funds-futures strip.** They are different instruments and can disagree. **The proper instrument for a priced policy path is the fed funds futures strip, and this desk still does not hold one** — `KB-BND-282`, `re-test: 2026-09-16`. Treat this as an independent **cross-check**, never as the priced probability itself, and never average it with a prediction-market number.
