# CCC refinancing wall 2027–28 — bounded attempt (Will "go on the refinancing wall", 9/28 ~15:2x ET)

**Written:** 2026-09-28 15:32 ET by BOND · **Scope (as approved):** US HY/CCC maturities 2027–28 by industry, FREE published sources only, stop at the free sources, no inference past them · **Raw web pass (Opus subagent, ~40 searches/fetches, source tags per item):** `domain/sources/2026-09-28_ccc-refi-wall_web-pass_raw.md` · **KB:** `KB-BND-349`.

## Bottom line
**The mechanism is real and it is narrow.** A primary source (PIMCO, data as of 8/26/2026) says CCC bonds due 2027–28 would see coupons roughly **double** if refinanced at index yields. The same source says the step-up is **negligible for the HY index as a whole and small for BB**. CCC yields have risen another **+157bp** since that measurement. In dollars, the pressure sits in **2028–29 more than 2027**, and much of the low-rated 2028 debt is **floating-rate leveraged loans**. Those loans are already paying today's rates every quarter, so for them the maturity question is market access, not a coupon reset. **No free source gives a CCC-only dollar wall or a CCC industry split: both are GAPs.**

## 4 · Is the wall shrinking?
- **Yes, for the market as a whole.** S&P's spec-grade peak year moved **2028 (Jan 2026) → 2029 (Apr) → 2031 (Jul)**, as refinancing pushed maturities out.
- **But the weakest issuers are the minority of the extensions.** ~75% of 2025 loan amend-and-extends were for issuers rated **B- or better** (83% in 2024; LCD, snippet). The residual wall is therefore concentrating in the weakest names.
- **Distressed exchanges:** 65% of defaults in 2025 → 44% in H1 2026, while the default rate excluding exchanges rose to 2.8% (Moody's via a secondary source; scope unstated).
- 2026 YTD amend-and-extend volume: GAP. A named 2026 debt-swap wave tied to 2027–28 maturities: GAP.

## What this shows / does not show
