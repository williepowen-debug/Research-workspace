## ① T7 — FOMC-minutes resolver: **AMBIGUOUS** (on the frozen letter), with the population ruling recorded first

### The population ruling (made on the letter, BEFORE grading)

The frozen text reads *"≥4 **participants** beyond the 3 named dissenters explicitly discuss a near-term hike as appropriate/contingent-likely."* The flagged defect: **members** (12 voters) ≠ **participants** (19 attendees), and the minutes use both registers.

**RULING: the letter's own counting noun is "participants," so the counting population is the 19-participant set; the 3 dissenters are members and therefore also participants, and they subtract out of any participant count that includes them.** The FOMC's drafting convention supports this: the participant-discussion sections describe all 19, dissenting members included — there is no separate register in which "several participants favored an increase" could exclude the three members who then voted to hike. So CONFIRM's leg 1 requires **≥7 participants favoring a near-term hike** (3 dissenters + ≥4 beyond).

**The ruling is not outcome-determinative, which is what protects it from the pick-the-flattering-population charge:** under the alternative reading (count only non-dissenting people, i.e. "several" read as excluding the dissenters), "several" ≈ 3–6 in the Fed's counting lexicon still fails to *establish* ≥4 — it merely fails less decisively. Both readings fail leg 1; and CONFIRM independently dies on leg 3 below. Recorded per the tasking; no population was chosen after seeing a grade.

### The evidence (Fed primary, pulled this session — verbatim)

- **The count sentence:** *"Several participants favored an increase of 25 basis points in the target range at this meeting."* A subset: *"A few of the participants who favored raising the target range … judged that doing so would likely help forestall the need for a steeper and potentially more costly sequence of tightening moves at a later stage."*
- **The vote:** 9–3. *"Voting against this action: Beth M. Hammack, Neel Kashkari, and Lorie K. Logan, who preferred to raise the target range for the federal funds rate by 1/4 percentage point at this meeting."* **The minutes characterize the dissents in no way beyond the vote line** — neither "close call" nor "risk-management."
- **Staff inflation outlook:** *"Total inflation was expected to decline over the second half of the year …"*; *"Inflation was expected to step down next year, as the effects of tariffs and the Middle East conflict wane, and to be about 2 percent in 2028."* — the staff outlook **softens**; nothing hardens.

**The premise conflict, adjudicated:**

1. **On mechanics, this is not YCC and the letter matters:** capped size (≥$4bn/op), dated window (9/9–11/4), **no yield target, no unlimited commitment** — YCC's defining feature is an elastic quantity pledged at a price; this is a fixed-ish quantity with no price. Scale: even ~8–10 ops ≈ $32–40bn against a single $16B auction *per tenor per month* and a $31.45T stock — a flow nudge, not a cap.
2. **Treasury's "demand is strong" premise is mostly consistent with the record this desk owns:** 12 straight benign auction resolutions through 8/13, indirect at/above median at all three refunding tenors at the highest 30Y auction yield since 2001. **But today's 20Y erodes it at the margin** — below-median indirect (−2.0pp trailing-12, −5.8pp vs new-issue median), dealers above median, at the highest new-issue yield in the held series. "Consistent strong sponsorship" was written the morning of the softest composition print of the run.
3. **El-Erian's "demand is weak" premise is NOT supported by the primary auction record** — no composition failure at any tenor in 13 straight, and rates vol (^MOVE 75.63 [8/17]) *falling* into the highs. A YCC reading requires demand distress the instruments do not show.
4. **What the Liquidity-Support letter does NOT explain is the timing, and I will not pretend it does:** the *doubling* was announced (a) the morning after the 30Y's 19-year-high close, (b) hours before Treasury's own $16B 20Y, (c) targeted exactly at the 10–30y sector making the highs. A liquidity op's size should be set by liquidity metrics (off-the-run spreads, depth); nothing in the release cites one. **Reactive-to-yield-levels is not the same as targeting-a-yield-level, but it is the road there.**

**⇒ ADOPTED PREMISE: genuine liquidity-support operation on the letter, with yield-reactive timing — "YCC" is wrong today as a mechanism claim, and El-Erian's demand-weak premise fails against the auction record. The monotonic curve move (2Y −0.9 → 20Y −8.3bp, no inversion) is consistent with both premises (WALTER `-030` is right) and adjudicates nothing.**

**What flips me to YCC-lite (any one, registered now):**
- **F1 — Ratchet without a liquidity trigger:** a further size/scope increase or schedule acceleration following a fresh long-end high, with off-the-run/liquidity metrics quiet. (The release already promises "more information about future buyback sizes" at the 11/4 QRA — that is the dated checkpoint.)
- **F2 — Purchase composition:** operation results showing buybacks concentrating in on-the-run / recently-auctioned issues rather than deep off-the-runs. Liquidity support buys illiquid paper; yield management buys what sets the marginal price. (Results are published per-op on TreasuryDirect — scoreable from 9/9.)
- **F3 — The issuance leg joins:** long-coupon auction sizes CUT at the 11/4 QRA while buybacks run — buyback-plus-shorter-issuance is the maturity-structure operation the "fused" story claims today without evidence.
**What re-confirms liquidity-support:** op results with high offer-to-cover, purchases spread across deep off-the-run CUSIPs, no further escalation, and the 20Y/30Y auction composition normalizing once the official bid exists.

**Book implication (channel read only — TERRY owns the trade):** the op runs 9/9–11/4; **004 (TLT Sep-30 77P ×25) expires 9/30, so 21 days of its remaining life carry an official incremental long-end bid regardless of which premise holds.** Under my adopted premise that bid is a modest flow, already partly priced today (TLT +1.50%, 30Y −8bp); under the YCC-flip branch it is a structural impairment of the short-duration channel through 11/4.
