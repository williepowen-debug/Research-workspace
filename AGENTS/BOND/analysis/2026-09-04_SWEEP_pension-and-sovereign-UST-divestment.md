**Headline dollar figure (NBIM/wires):** cutting **~$80B** from ~**$215B** of USTs held at end-June.

### 1.1 🔴 The finding — and it is the OPPOSITE of the headlines

**Derived denominator** *(shown so it is checkable, not asserted)*: $215B ÷ 34.1% ⇒ **bond index ≈ $630B**. That reconciles independently with GPFG's strategic **28% fixed income** on a ~$2.0–2.3T fund. Applying the weight changes to $630B:

| Leg | Δ weight | **≈ $ effect [EST, derived]** |
|---|---:|---:|
| **US Treasuries OUT** | −12.2pp | **≈ −$77B** ✅ reconciles with the published "nearly $80B" |
| **Non-government IN** | +20pp | ≈ **+$126B** |
| **— of which MBS** (~13% of the new index, NBIM primary) | — | **≈ +$82B** |
| JGBs IN | +2.8pp | ≈ +$18B |
| Euro-area govt OUT | −2.7pp | ≈ −$17B |

> ### ⇒ **THE ~$80B LEAVING TREASURIES IS ALMOST EXACTLY OFFSET BY ~$82B GOING INTO US MBS. THE DOLLAR DOES NOT LEAVE — NBIM SAYS THE USD SHARE STAYS "JUST OVER 50%."**
> **This is a Treasury→spread-product rotation INSIDE the dollar bloc, not de-dollarization.** Every headline in the sweep frames it as flight from the US. **The primary says the fund is buying MORE US paper, just not the government's.** ⚠️ The $ figures above are **[EST]**, derived from one published anchor ($215B / 34.1%); the **weights** are [CONF NBIM primary]. **If NBIM publishes the sleeve size, re-derive — do not carry these as measured.**

### 1.2 What it does NOT do — the timeline, which the coverage buries

**This is a PROPOSAL, and it cannot touch the September refunding.** Sequence per the primary: Ministry of Finance must take a position → an **expert group reports by 25 January 2027** → *then* NBIM supplies mandate language and an implementation plan → **"adjustment … made gradually out of consideration for market impact and transaction costs"** (one-time transition cost ≈ NOK 750M).
⇒ **ZERO effect on 9/8–9/10. No BOND vector moves. No trigger fires. The frozen `I'` bars and the kill spec are untouched.** A benchmark proposal with a 2027 decision point is not an auction-demand event.

### 1.3 Why it is still the most important item in the sweep
Unlike every discretionary sale below, an index-weight change is **mechanical and persistent** — it does not depend on anyone's continuing conviction. And **≈$82B of phased MBS demand lands squarely in BOND's own MBS / housing-finance lane** (6/27 coverage extension). **That leg is BOND's to own, not to route.**

### 2.1 Read across the field
1. **Sum the discretionary pension sales and they are still not size.** Alecta + AkademikerPension + Lærernes + ABP ≈ **$21B**, spread across ~18 months, against a **$125B single quarterly refunding**. **Only Norway is size — and Norway is not leaving the dollar.**
2. **At least three of these are explicitly NOT anti-US-credit trades:** AkademikerPension moved to **USD short-duration**; Lærernes moved on a **hedged yield pickup** and says expected return was unchanged; ABP/Dutch is **rebalancing** per IPE. **A "foreign investors are dumping Treasuries" narrative is not what these funds say about themselves.**
3. **The genuine common factor is HEDGED RELATIVE VALUE and DURATION, not credit fear.** Japanese lifers name hedge cost. Lærernes names post-hedge Bund yield. Norway names **liquidity sufficiency and market-representativeness**, plus MBS/corporate pickup. **That is a story about the price of duration — which is precisely this desk's standing thesis, "expensive, not broken."**
