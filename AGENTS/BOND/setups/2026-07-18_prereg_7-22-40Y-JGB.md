## 4. ★ Channel-6 reconciliation with the 7/18 relabel — LEVEL-tell vs MOVE-driver (written down)

**The question the relabel opened:** now that BOND has established the US arm MOVE (7/6→7/13) was **real POLICY-PATH-led, NOT a term-premium expansion**, does a weak 40Y JGB still matter to the arm?

**Answer — a 40Y JGB tail is a 30Y-LEVEL tell, NOT an arm MOVE-driver. Decouple them:**

1. Channel-6 transmits to the US **30Y term-premium / tail leg.** Per the 7/18 decomposition, term premium is the **SMALL part of the recent arm move** (~0-7%; the 30Y *lagged* the belly — bear-flattener) but a **real part of the 30Y LEVEL** (ACM 10Y TP +0.73%, structurally elevated).
2. Therefore a weak 40Y JGB → pushes the US **30Y LEVEL** higher and bear-steepens the very long end (30Y-10Y). It does **NOT** move the arm's driver, which lives in the **2Y-DFII10 real policy path** (the front/belly, where the JGB super-long has no direct read-through).
3. **Discipline (avoids double-counting):**
   - Do **NOT** read a 40Y JGB tail as *arm confirmation* — that would count one-root term premium twice (JGB / 30Y-level / thin-bid are one term-premium root, not independent votes — my standing circularity rule).
   - Do **NOT** read a firm 40Y JGB as *arm falsification* — the arm doesn't depend on Japanese super-long demand; it depends on the Fed's real policy path.
   - **The 40Y JGB is near-orthogonal to the arm.** Grade it into the **30Y-level watch + channel-6**, NOT the arm scoreboard.

**Net:** a 40Y JGB tail escalates the *long-end / duration* vector (VX-BND-05, the LEVEL) and the global-long-end panel (channel-6), while the *arm* (VX-BND-14 real-policy-path decomposition) is graded off the Fed path (see `analysis/2026-07-18_fed-path-map_fomc-7-28.md`), not the JGB. Two separate ledgers — the same rigor that corrected the term-premium label applies here: **separate the LEVEL (term premium, where the JGB lives) from the MOVE/arm (policy path, where the arm lives).**
