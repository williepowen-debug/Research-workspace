- **PD 11.13% = at-median (54th).** Dealer absorption is normal — no balance-sheet stress, no end-of-cycle backstop spike.

### Interpreting direct surge alongside indirect cooling

The bidder-mix story is the most interesting layer of this print. Two competing explanations:

**Hypothesis A — Domestic real-money rotation INTO TIPS at 2.17% real.** Direct bidders tend to be domestic institutions (insurance companies, pension funds, certain hedge funds bypassing PD intermediation) and SOMA add-ons. At real yields near cycle highs (3rd highest 10Y-TIPS real of the 24mo window), domestic real-money sees 2.17% real + ~2.43% breakeven = ~4.60% nominal-equivalent forward returns with explicit inflation protection. This is real-money rotating INTO the security at attractive real-yield levels. **Consistent with the BTC 2.52 strong-demand signal — direct + indirect together accepted $16.83B of the $19B competitive, an 88.6% non-dealer take.**

**Hypothesis B — Indirect (foreign + SOMA) cooled selectively on TIPS.** Indirect 61.36% is 15th percentile within the cohort. Foreign demand for TIPS specifically may be softening even as nominal-Treasury foreign demand stays robust (5/20 20Y had indirect 67.7% of-competitive). Possible explanations: foreign reserve managers prefer nominal duration over inflation-linked at this point in the cycle, or breakeven-trade unwind by foreign holders.

**Honest verdict — both can be true simultaneously, but the direct surge dominates.** The fact that direct + indirect together rose to 88.87% of competitive (vs cohort mean ~88.02% direct+indirect; today is at-cohort-typical for non-dealer take TOTAL) means the overall *non-dealer demand* is at cohort-typical — what changed is the *composition*. Domestic real-money substituted for some indirect demand at this auction. This is informationally **bullish on domestic TIPS appetite**, neutral on foreign-bid story (indirect cooler but not a hole; dealer not forced to absorb).

**What this is NOT:** this is NOT a foreign-canary story for nominal Treasuries. The 5/20 20Y nominal showed strong foreign demand (indirect 67.7% of-competitive). Treating today's TIPS indirect 61.36% as a foreign-demand signal for nominal would be a misreading — these are different bidder pools with different mandates.
