# 20Y Auction Demand Assessment Framework
*Reusable template — first applied Mar 19, 2026*

## Demand Sources Assessment

### Gulf Sovereign Funds
- QIA (Qatar): potential emergency seller if domestic fiscal pressure
- Saudi SAMA: below fiscal breakeven even at elevated prices if war spending surges
- UAE ADIA: similarly stressed during Gulf conflict
- Gulf recycling collapses when: (a) fiscal pressure = sell liquid assets, (b) energy infrastructure uncertainty = capital preservation over duration risk

### Japan Life Insurers
- BOJ hold = JGB yields still rising
- USD/JPY FX hedge cost elevated
- Japanese life insurers — natural 20Y buyers — reducing UST exposure due to FX hedge drag
- No catalyst to return at $108+ oil + hawkish Fed

### Domestic Buyers
- Retail investor fatigue (-30% YoY UST purchases)
- Pension/ALM buyers favor 30Y (liability matching), not 20Y belly
- Less ALM-attractive than ultra-long end

### Primary Dealers
- Stuck with inventory from prior weak auctions
- Dealer balance sheets constrained
- Forced to absorb if indirect bid weak

### Fed
- **No coupon backstop at the long end.** QT **ENDED Dec 1 2025** (FOMC Oct-29-2025 decision; NY Fed 251210a; PROME-verified). Post-QT, the Fed **IS buying — but T-BILLS** (Reserve Management Purchases + reinvesting MBS principal into bills), **NOT coupons.** So RMP purchases do **not** absorb 20Y/30Y supply: the coupon/long-end demand read is **unaffected by Fed buying** — absorption at a coupon reopen is entirely private/foreign/dealer, no Fed bid at the back end.
