## Q3 Coupon shock
- **PIMCO, Karoui, "Credit Market Lens" (2026-08-31), [primary], text read:** "current face-value weighted coupons for [CCC] bonds maturing in 2027 and 2028 **could double** from current levels, if these issuers refinanced ... at today's index yields." Basis: Bloomberg US HY index plus ICE-BAML rating yields, **as of 2026-08-26**. At index level, YTW is "only marginally higher" than the average coupon. BB issuers face a *smaller* step-up than IG, because most HY issuers have already refinanced since the July 2020 low in the Treasury 5Y. Median net interest coverage is about 3x HY and 6x IG (6/30/2026).
- **The actual CCC coupon and yield figures are in PIMCO's Fig. 4, which is an image only. Not extracted: GAP.** The desk has the ICE CCC OAS at 1,128bp (9/25); I did not pair it with a coupon.
- S&P (July 2025, older vintage) [primary]: US **BB** bonds due 2026 face a step-up of about **+238bp**. BBB median coupons are 3.4-4.0%. It gives no CCC figure.

## Q4 Is the wall shrinking?
- **Yes, through the refinancing channel; the peak keeps moving out.** S&P's global spec-grade peak year moved from **2028 (Jan 2026) to 2029 (Apr 2026) to 2031 (Jul 2026)**. "Steady refinancing activity reduced speculative-grade maturities through 2029." [snippet of primary]
- S&P (July 2025): in H1 2025, US spec-grade 2026 maturities were cut 25%. Global spec-grade 2027 maturities were cut 12.5% and 2028 only 4.6%, so the reductions are concentrated in the nearest years. [primary]
- LCD, 2025 loan amend-and-extend (A&E): pro rata extended $37B (2026), $38B (2027), $14B (2028). Institutional extended $54B (2028) and $13B (2027). **About 75% of 2025 amendments were for issuers rated B- or higher (83% in 2024)**, so the riskiest credits are the smaller share. Refinancing was over 70% of HY issuance for the 2nd straight year. [snippet]
- **2026 year-to-date A&E dollar volume: not found in free sources. GAP.**

## Q5 LME / distressed-exchange wave (2026)
- Distressed exchanges were **65% of 2025 defaults and 44% in H1 2026**, while the default rate excluding exchanges rose to 2.8%. Source: privatemarketsinsights.com citing Moody's mid-year 2026 outlook. **Scope (US/global, loans/bonds) is unstated** [secondary]. ## WHAT THIS SHOWS / DOES NOT SHOW
- SHOWS: a published primary source (PIMCO, data as of 8/26/2026) supports the desk's mechanism for **CCC bonds due 2027-28** ("coupons could double"). The same source says the effect is **negligible at the HY index level and small for BB**.
- SHOWS: in dollars, the pressure sits in **2028-29 more than 2027**. HY bonds due 2027 are small ($68.5B, LSEG), while B-and-lower debt due 2028 is about $250-269B, and much of that is **leveraged loans (floating rate)**. Loans feel rates through the reference rate, not through a coupon reset at refinancing.
- SHOWS: the sectors named for low-rated maturities are **healthcare, high tech, and media & entertainment** (July 2026, wording only), with telecom and media & ent in the older CCC data.
- DOES NOT SHOW: a CCC-only dollar wall, or a CCC sector split, for 2027-28. Both are GAPs.
- DOES NOT SHOW: the actual CCC coupon vs yield numbers (they are in an image), or 2026 A&E volumes.
