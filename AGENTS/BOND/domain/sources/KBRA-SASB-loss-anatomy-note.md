# KBRA: Anatomy of Loss in Single-Borrower CMBS (Mar 18, 2026)
**Source:** KBRA research (paywalled — preview only)

## Key Findings (from preview)
- 861 SASB loans analyzed (1993-2024). 8 resolved with meaningful losses (3.9%-63.8%).
- **75% of defaulted SASB loans had minimal/no loss** — but when losses occur, they're severe. Binary outcome.
- **LTV and DSC did NOT predict losses** — "moderate LTVs and strong coverage metrics" still incurred principal erosion
- **Actual loss drivers:** tenant concentration, floating rate IO structures, mezzanine layering, macro disruption
- Property type didn't drive severity — macro timing did (GFC→lodging/retail, pandemic→office)
- Post-pandemic inflation + higher rates accelerated valuation declines

## Relevance
- Challenges "LTV looks fine" complacency on new CRE CLO issuance (BSPRT 2026-FL13: 67.6% LTV, floating rate, SOFR cap 4%)
- Current environment has all loss drivers firing: floating rates high, tenant stress, no amortization cushion
- Sequential sector stress pattern: lodging → retail → office → now multifamily?
- Feeds REGINALD (OZK/WAL CRE exposure) and BOND (CMBS market health)
