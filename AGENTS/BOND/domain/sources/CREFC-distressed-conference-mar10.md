**Source:** KBRA/KCP recap, 300+ CRE professionals

## Key Numbers
- **$3 trillion CRE debt maturing next 3 years** — 2021-22 vintage, originated at low rates
- **Property values ~20% below prior peaks** — still underwater
- Private credit originating at 65-75% LTC, 10-12% returns, 30-40% structural subordination

## Sector Reads
- **NYC Office:** trophy = fine, older = vacant/stuck. Conversions gradually reducing inventory.

- **Hospitality:** stress rising mid-tier. Borrowing costs + labor + renovation capex
- **Specialized (data centers, life science):** more complex underwriting needed

- NYC office SASB: $17B in 2025 = 19% of SASB market, 10% of total CMBS issuance. Concentrated.
