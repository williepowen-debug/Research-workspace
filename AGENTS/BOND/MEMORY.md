- **⚠️ AN ADJECTIVE OR AGGREGATION ATTACHED TO A COMPUTED NUMBER IS ITSELF AN UNCOMPUTED CLAIM — this desk's signature error, n=6, three nested layers all found 2026-08-18→08-21.** **My level citations stay clean; the errors sit in the words and the method around them** — which is why it reads as instability from outside: these are the most quotable sentences in a write-up.
  **L1 · the superlative hides FOUR free parameters (series · basis · window · n):** *"29-day run"* = counting convention · *"cycle high 5.28"* = a `^TYX` intraday standing in for a `DGS30` close, dated to a Sunday · *"DFII10 2.43 series high"* = window · *"carry trade largely unwound"* = a ~$15B CFTC futures proxy reported as the multi-trillion structural stock. **All CARRIED figures.**
  **L2 · declaring the parameters is not enough — they attach to the COMPUTATION, not the data.** I declared series/basis/window/n for `DGS30`, then published run-lengths computed **per-year on `>5.00`** beside day-counts on **`≥5.00` whole-series** — two conventions in one table. Per-year silently truncates any run crossing a year boundary: my 458 was **721**, 79 was **92**, 42 was **44**. ⇒ **name the AGGREGATION METHOD in the sentence** ("maximal run, ≥5.00, session closes, whole-series scan").
  **L3 · a clause about a computed SET is a second statistic wearing the first one's parameters.** *"CCC 1035 — 16 prior obs ≥1035, **every one April-2025**"*: count, max, window and the fresh-high call were all correct and survive; **the clause about WHERE the 16 sit was false — 12 Apr-2025, 2 Oct-2023, 1 on 2023-11-01, 1 on 2024-08-05. Twelve of sixteen.**
  **⇒ RULE: compute at write time, never carry an adjective forward, and treat "all of them X" / "clustered in Y" / "mostly Z" as a computation — run it or delete it.** ⚠️ **Two corrections here ran in my thesis's FAVOUR** (the true post-2007 comparison was 11 sessions, so "longest since 2007" *undersold* it) — **a method error is not reliably self-serving, so you cannot audit by checking whether a number flatters you.** Fleet: `[[finding_verified_figures_do_not_verify_the_shape_claim]]`, `[[finding_output_shape_implies_more_than_the_measurement]]`.
