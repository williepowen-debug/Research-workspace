- **⚠️ AN ADJECTIVE OR AGGREGATION ATTACHED TO A COMPUTED NUMBER IS ITSELF AN UNCOMPUTED CLAIM — this desk's signature error, n=6, three nested layers all found 2026-08-18→08-21.** **My level citations stay clean; the errors sit in the words and the method around them** — which is why it reads as instability from outside: these are the most quotable sentences in a write-up.
  **L1 · the superlative hides FOUR free parameters (series · basis · window · n):** *"29-day run"* = counting convention · *"cycle high 5.28"* = a `^TYX` intraday standing in for a `DGS30` close, dated to a Sunday · *"DFII10 2.43 series high"* = window · *"carry trade largely unwound"* = a ~$15B CFTC futures proxy reported as the multi-trillion structural stock. **All CARRIED figures.**
  **L2 · declaring the parameters is not enough — they attach to the COMPUTATION, not the data.** I declared series/basis/window/n for `DGS30`, then published run-lengths computed **per-year on `>5.00`** beside day-counts on **`≥5.00` whole-series** — two conventions in one table. Per-year silently truncates any run crossing a year boundary: my 458 was **721**, 79 was **92**, 42 was **44**. ⇒ **name the AGGREGATION METHOD in the sentence** ("maximal run, ≥5.00, session closes, whole-series scan").
  **L3 · a clause about a computed SET is a second statistic wearing the first one's parameters.** *"CCC 1035 — 16 prior obs ≥1035, **every one April-2025**"*: count, max, window and the fresh-high call were all correct and survive; **the clause about WHERE the 16 sit was false — 12 Apr-2025, 2 Oct-2023, 1 on 2023-11-01, 1 on 2024-08-05. ## Process
- **Audit surfaces by AGE, not by what looks wrong** — BOND instances: HY OAS sat 26 days at a *plausible* 275; "FOMC hold ~90% priced" survived because a hold is always plausible; three passes of section-edits still left THESIS asserting both the retired and the current label. **Editing a file is not the same as the file being current.** Rule + why: `[[finding_plausible_stale_value_evades_review]]`.
- **A percentile threshold is ONE rule with a DIFFERENT effective strictness in every stratum — measure the bite per stratum before adopting it, and never base-rate it pooled (2026-08-27, found by computing the adoption's own counterfactual).** MATRIX_V2 §3c replaced a trailing-12 **min**-based indirect bar with a **15th-percentile** bar, the point being a looser, better-calibrated trigger. Measured at adoption, the new bar sits above the old min by **+4.84pp at the 2Y, +0.82pp at the 7Y and +0.24pp at the 5Y** — at the 5Y it is barely a change at all, because that tenor's trailing-12 indirect distribution is bunched hard against its own bottom. **A percentile is a statement about a distribution's SHAPE, so a single percentile rule applied across strata with different shapes is several different rules wearing one name.** ⇒ **The Will-ruled base-rating must report hit-rate and separation PER TENOR; a pooled figure would average three rules and describe none of them.** Two transferable halves: **(a)** the uneven bite was invisible in the spec and appeared only on computing the numbers — *adopting a recalibration without measuring its bite per stratum is adopting an unknown*; **(b)** it surfaced because the adoption's **counterfactual** was computed rather than asserted (*"would this have fired on the prints I missed?"*) — and that same check caught a figure I had already written into the draft from memory. **Compute the counterfactual; it audits the rule and the author at once.** `KB-BND-173`, `KB-BND-174`.

- **A conjunctive test that fails by a rounding-scale margin is evidence the CONJUNCTION is brittle — not evidence the thing it tests turned (2026-08-27, `BND-19`).** The row required indirect ≥ its own trailing-12 median at **all three** new-issue tenors. It resolved **FALSE** on a single leg by **0.24pp** (5Y 61.51 vs 61.75), with the 2Y clearing by **+8.36pp**. **Scored FALSE as written — a registered conjunction is not re-specified after the fact, and the direction of the miss does not license a re-read.** But the REPORTING must carry the margin, because *"the stronger form of the thesis failed"* and *"demand was normal-or-better at two of three tenors and a quarter-point short at the third"* are the same verdict and radically different evidence. ⚠️ **The asymmetry is the trap: a conjunction's FALSE is cheap to produce (any one leg, any margin) while its TRUE requires every leg — so a conjunction's failures are systematically less informative than its successes, and a book scored only on the binary will read as more decisive than the evidence.** Log the spec observation for the base-rating; never fix the spec on the session it costs you. Related: `[[finding_broken_conjunction_leaves_its_other_legs_unrecorded]]` (record every leg — done here: the 7Y leg is graded even though the row was already FALSE).

- **When your evidence for a peer's error is a RELAY, write the correction as a CONDITIONAL — the mechanical form, adopted 2026-08-27 (PROME's suggestion, after it saved me from a false correction).** A doorbell told me a peer's board carried a "leg satisfied" marker that would have been wrong for the prediction it appeared beside. I wrote *"**IF** your board carries a 'leg satisfied' marker on 8/21, it should come off"* rather than *"your board is wrong."* **It didn't.** The annotation was TRUE and correctly scoped on their artifact to a different desk's frozen leg; **their MESSAGE had transplanted it into my prediction's path sentence.** The defect was in the relay, and the artifact was clean.
  **⇒ A relay's defect reads as the source's defect** — a message is not the surface it describes, and the transplant is invisible because both halves are individually true. **The fleet holds the OUTWARD half already** (`[[finding_asymmetric_rigor_counterparty_claims]]` — name your check's REFERENT before publishing a correction of another desk's figure). **This is the operational form of it: when the only thing you have read is a message ABOUT an artifact, the conditional is not politeness, it is the accurate epistemic state**, and it costs nothing — a peer whose surface IS wrong acts on it identically. **Reserve the flat assertion for when you have opened the artifact.**
  ⚠️ **Recorded as luck-adjacent, not as a good call:** I hedged out of habit, not because I had reasoned about relay-vs-artifact. The habit is what made it survive; **the reasoning is what makes it repeatable, which is why it is written down.** Same session, the mirror case: MIDAS caught a fused figure of mine **because they read the ARTIFACT and tried to reproduce the number rather than reading my summary of it.** Both directions of the same rule in one day.

- **The countermeasure to "the flagged cell gets fixed and its siblings don't" is to grep the FIGURE, not re-read the SURFACE (MIDAS, 2026-08-27, adopted).** This desk's dominant failure mode — five stale surfaces found in one audit, every one the same shape — is that **attention follows the gate**: a fix lands where the error was *demonstrated*, and the demonstration is always a single cell. MIDAS hit it twice the same day from the other side (the row about to grade absorbed a whole sweep while two siblings carrying the identical defect, five weeks out, went unswept). **Their operational fix, which is concrete where my "fix by pattern" was only an instruction: grep the TICKER or the FIGURE across every registered surface — do not re-read the one you were told about.** ⚠️ **And note what this defeats that no checker does:** a drift check compares latest-on-surface to latest-at-source, so it **passes on a correct endpoint and never looks sideways**; `assertion_check` has four shapes and none is *"this cell is older than the one above it."* **Sibling-staleness is invisible to every instrument this desk owns, so the grep is not belt-and-braces — it is the only coverage.** Pairs with `[[finding_summary_section_merges_what_the_body_separates]]` (fix the abstract FIRST, then walk outward).
