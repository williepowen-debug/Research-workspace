Photo: Reuters Riyadh : Yemen’s Houthi movement on Monday, October 26, claimed it had targeted King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh with a ballistic missile, a day after the facility resumed flight operations following a two-week suspension. In a post on X, Houthi military spokesperson Yahya Saree also claimed the group had targeted crude oil pumping stations in eastern and western Saudi Arabia using several drones. He described both operations as successful.
