Saudi Arabia says talks in Tehran aim to prevent escalation

  • Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Türkiye hold talks with Iran in Tehran, aiming to prevent escalation
Published October 26, 2026 Updated October 26, 2026 05:26pm
Save
Saved
1 min
Comments
By Reuters

Saudi Arabia, which, along with Pakistan and Türkiye, was set to hold talks with Iranian officials in Tehran on Monday, said it was important to open channels for negotiations.

The Saudi foreign ministry said in a statement that the talks in Tehran were aimed at preventing escalation and paving the way to agreement.
