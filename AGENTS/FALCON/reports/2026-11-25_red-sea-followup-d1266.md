## 1. For Will, in plain words

**The Houthis carried out their threat within a day, and one hit landed.** On Wednesday the Saudi-led coalition's own spokesman said a Houthi projectile struck Jazan Port, the southern Saudi Red Sea port nearest Yemen, causing minor damage and no civilian casualties. Later that night the coalition said it shot down a Houthi drone near Jeddah Islamic Port, with no damage. The Houthis say they hit both ports and call it a "port for port" policy: if Hodeidah is bombed again, they will answer at once. Separately, the coalition shot down four drones over Ras Tanura on the Gulf coast; the Houthis claim they set the refinery on fire, and nobody official has confirmed that.

The damage the coalition describes is minor. Yanbu, the main outlet for Saudi oil that avoids Hormuz, was not attacked.
