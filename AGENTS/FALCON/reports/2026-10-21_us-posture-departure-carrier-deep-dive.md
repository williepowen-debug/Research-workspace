1. **The Saudi departure is mostly a Houthi story.** It is Saudi-only, the reason is stated, the posts were already thin, and an American was killed at Riyadh airport on 10/10.
2. **The base drawdown plus the carrier timing is the strike-relevant signal.** It points to a decision window **after 11/3**, lining up with the Roosevelt's arrival in mid-to-late November.
3. **Base rates cut both ways.** June 2025: the Baghdad ordered departure (6/11) came two days before Israel struck Iran (6/13) and 11 days before the US strike (6/22) [desk knowledge, not re-sourced]. 2/28/2026: the war opened with **no** Gulf departures.
4. **The tell to watch:** ordered departure extended to the base-host states (Bahrain, Qatar, UAE, Kuwait), a withdrawn 11/3 pledge, and bomber or tanker surges.
5. **What would move a mark:** a resumed US campaign sits inside D 85. The step that moves a mark is **Iranian retaliation onto Gulf-ally crude production** (rung D 85→92).
