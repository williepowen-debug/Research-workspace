## 1. Bottom line

A Houthi attack damaged a tower in Riyadh's financial district early Thursday, and Saudi Arabia's civil defence has now said so on the record (via the state news agency SPA, relayed by Reuters): material damage to several floors of the tower and to nearby buildings, no injuries. The tower is the Public Investment Fund's headquarters on three unnamed sources (Reuters), not in the state's words. Whether the missile hit the building or debris from an interception did is not established. It is an office tower, so it does not touch my production rung, and **no mark moves: B 1 / C 14 / D 85 HOLD.** It is the second damaging hit on a Riyadh business landmark in 11 days and fits the pattern since 11/8: Houthi fire reaches Riyadh and the Saudi east-west oil route, and hits symbols and routes, not oil fields or processing plants.
