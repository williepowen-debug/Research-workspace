## ① The 7/12 headline finding — CORRECTED, and the correction is the lesson

**7/12 said:** *"production spared for the ENTIRE war" is wrong for Feb–Apr; the sparing thesis is cycle-scoped (post-MOU only), not war-scoped.* That re-scoping was right and stands.

**What 7/12 got wrong was the SUBJECT of the sparing.** It framed the restraint as a property of *the war*. It is a property of **a dyad**. The US-Iran pair sustained it to the end — **13 consecutive strike nights with zero production hits, then a full campaign pause on 7/24**. What broke on 7/25 was not that dyad's discipline. It was the arrival of a **third belligerent pair (Saudi-Houthi, four-year truce broken)** that had never been party to the restraint.

> **A restraint pattern is a property of the belligerents who practise it, not of the theater they fight in. A ledger scoped to the dyad you are watching will miss the belligerent you are not.**

This is the direct cause of **FAL-01's failure** (registered 70%, resolved FAILED 7/25 on Jazan) and the reason FAL-03 now enumerates **four belligerent axes** plus a fifth-actor re-registration clause.

## Materiality bar (what earns a row)

Log if **any** of: (a) named major facility, (b) capacity/throughput impact, (c) escalation marker (record wave / new geography / first-of-kind). **Skip** daily small-depot noise unless it marks a pattern shift. The ledger is a **material subset**, NOT an exhaustive count of every drone.

## ⚠️ Metric discipline (read before quoting a number)

1. **"% capacity offline" is an as-of, sourced aggregate — NEVER the sum of the `Capacity` column.** Summing nameplates double-counts repaired facilities and `reduced`-not-`offline` plants. Cite *currently-offline, as-of date, source*. **The Abqaiq "7 mb/d" claim is exactly this error made by a third party.**
2. **`Status` is point-in-time; `ReturnToService` closes the loop.** 25 of 30 rows have `ReturnToService=unk` — currently-offline is **uncomputable from the ledger alone**; use the sourced aggregates in ④.
3. **Row-count is not attack-count.** The ledger is a material subset.
4. **🆕 A `hit` is not a loss.** The Status column's operational/non-operational split (⓪) is the load-bearing distinction in this ledger — treat `hit` and `fire` as *damage without demonstrated supply consequence* until an operational status or a sourced aggregate says otherwise.
