## Materiality bar (what earns a row — template, copied from HAWK)

Log if **any** of: (a) named major facility, (b) capacity/throughput impact, (c) escalation marker (record wave / new geography / first-of-kind). **Skip** daily small-depot noise unless it marks a pattern shift. The ledger is a *material subset*, NOT an exhaustive count of every drone.

## ⚠️ Metric discipline (template, copied from HAWK — read before quoting a number)

1. **"% capacity offline" is an as-of, sourced aggregate — NEVER the sum of the `Capacity` column.** Summing nameplates of every facility ever hit double-counts (a) facilities since repaired and (b) `reduced`-not-`offline` plants. Always cite *currently-offline, as-of date, source*.
2. **`Status` is point-in-time; `ReturnToService` closes the loop.** "Currently offline" = rows where Status∈{offline,halted} AND ReturnToService is empty/future. All 4 inherited rows have `ReturnToService=unk` — currently-offline is presently uncomputable from the ledger alone.
3. **The ledger is a material subset.** Row-count is not attack-count; a thin ledger (4 rows) is not evidence of a quiet theater — it is evidence that the pre-split ledger was never built out for Gulf-Iran (HAWK's attention went to the Russia campaign, per the split's own root-cause finding — see `AGENTS/HAWK/design/2026-07-12_war-agent-split-spec.md` §1).
