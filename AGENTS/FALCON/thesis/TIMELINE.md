## DE-ESCALATION PHASE — April 1-20

### Apr 1 — Trump Ceasefire Talk + Oil Crash ✅ RESOLVED
- **Event:** Trump claimed Iran asked for ceasefire, said war could end in "2-3 weeks." Witkoff presented 15-point peace plan.
- **Outcome:** Brent cratered from ~$115 to ~$102 (-11%). $13 of war premium unwound.
- **⚠️ FRAGILE:** Iran REJECTED the 15-point plan, denied direct talks.
- **Impact:** First de-escalation signal. Ceasefire fade protocol activated (0/3 prior "breakthroughs" were real).

### Apr 2 — Trump Speech Reversal ✅ RESOLVED
- **Event:** Trump primetime speech: NO exit plan, NO Hormuz reopening, "2-3 more weeks" of strikes.
- **Outcome:** Brent surged $102→$109 (+7%). $7 of prior day's $13 unwind reversed.
- **Impact:** De-escalation probability dropped to ~25-30%. Phase 1 oil dynamics reasserting.

### Apr 6 — Strike Pause Expiry ✅ RESOLVED — NO KINETIC ESCALATION
- **Event:** Trump's deadline for energy plant strikes came and went.
- **Outcome:** No US kinetic action. No Iranian preemptive strike. Diplomatic channels remained open.
- **Impact:** Bear fork (escalation) avoided. Path to ceasefire opened.

### Apr 7 — 30Y JGB Auction / Ceasefire Deadline ✅ RESOLVED
- **Event:** Trump 8PM deadline for Hormuz reopening.
- **Outcome:** 2-week ceasefire announced 90 min before deadline. Iran reopened Hormuz (coordinated passage).
- **Impact:** Brent $110 → $91 (-16.6%). First genuine diplomatic breakthrough (Tier 1-2).

### Apr 8-9 — Ceasefire Fracturing <48HRS ✅ RESOLVED
- **Event:** Israel launched "Operation Eternal Darkness" — 50 fighter jets, 160 munitions across Lebanon (254 killed).
- **Outcome:** Iran accused US of violating ceasefire. Hormuz re-closed (White House disputes). Only 3 ships left Gulf on Apr 8 (normal: 135/day).
- **Impact:** Ceasefire priced as de-escalation. Reality: fragile pause with irreconcilable Lebanon carve-out.

### Apr 11-12 — Islamabad Talks → Ceasefire Declared ✅ RESOLVED
- **Event:** 21-hour marathon US-Iran talks in Islamabad (Vance, Witkoff, Kushner vs. Ghalibaf, Araghchi).
- **Outcome:** **NO DEAL on comprehensive terms.** But Israel-Iran ceasefire framework announced through Omani mediation.
- **Impact:** Brent collapsed $116 → $64.50 over following days. Scenario D reduced from 92% → 82%.

### Apr 13-20 — Post-Ceasefire Reality ✅ ONGOING
- **Event:** Ceasefire holding (Day 8 as of Apr 20). Brent at $64.50. Houthi stand-down achieved.
- **Current State:**
  - No mine clearance visible
  - No insurance reinstatement
  - No infrastructure repair timelines
  - US-Iran talks framework established but no concrete agreements
- **Impact:** Tactical pause, not strategic resolution. D remains elevated at 82%.
