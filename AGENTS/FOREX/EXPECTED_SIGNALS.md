## PRE-DOCUMENTED SIGNAL TYPES

These signals are expected based on domain analysis. When observed, log to ML.tsv and assess for escalation.

### SIGNAL: Carry Trade Unwind Initiation
**Trigger:** USD/JPY drops >3% in single week with rising vol
**Interpretation:** Leveraged positions being unwound
**Action:** URGENT to SAM, LIQUID
**Historical Precedent:** August 2024 unwind

### SIGNAL: Dollar Funding Stress
**Trigger:** Cross-currency basis widens >50bps
**Interpretation:** Offshore dollar shortage
**Action:** URGENT to LIQUID
**Historical Precedent:** March 2020, Sept 2019

### SIGNAL: EM Reserve Depletion
**Trigger:** Major EM CB reserve drawdown >5% monthly
**Interpretation:** Defending currency, unsustainable
**Action:** ELEVATED to MARCO
**Historical Precedent:** Turkey 2021, Argentina multiple

### SIGNAL: BOJ Intervention
**Trigger:** Confirmed BOJ FX intervention
**Interpretation:** Policy limit reached
**Action:** URGENT to SAM
**Historical Precedent:** Sept 2022, Oct 2022

### SIGNAL: Competitive Devaluation
**Trigger:** Multiple EM currencies devalue >5% against USD in same week
**Interpretation:** Contagion / race to bottom
**Action:** ELEVATED to BARON, MARCO
**Historical Precedent:** 2015 China deval cascade

### SIGNAL: CVIX Spike
**Trigger:** CVIX >15 (from <10 base)
**Interpretation:** FX volatility regime change
**Action:** ELEVATED to ALL
**Historical Precedent:** Brexit, COVID

### SIGNAL: DXY Breakout
**Trigger:** DXY breaks above 110 or below 100
**Interpretation:** Dollar regime shift
**Action:** ELEVATED to LIQUID, BARON, CARL
**Historical Precedent:** 2022 dollar surge
