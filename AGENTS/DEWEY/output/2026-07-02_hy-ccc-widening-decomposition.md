**ICE rule text (workflow-sourced, primary — resolves the timing question definitively):**
- **The rule:** Under the current ICE Data Indices Bond Index Methodology (Apr 30 2025 vintage, in force for June 2026): *"Securities in legal default are removed at the next month-end rebalancing following the default event, provided the event occurs on or before three business days before the last business day of the month (rebalancing lock-out date). Otherwise the security is removed at the following month-end rebalancing,"* and *"No changes are made to constituent holdings other than on month end rebalancing dates."* FRED's own BAMLH0A0HYM2 notes independently confirm the intra-month lock: *"Issues that no longer meet the criteria during the course of the month remain in the Index until the next month-end rebalancing."* [PRIMARY: ICE Bond Index Methodologies, https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/data/Bond_Index_Methodologies.pdf; FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2 series notes]
- **Applied to DISH:** June 2026 lock-out = 3 business days before the last business day. Last business day = Tue 6/30; three business days prior = **Thu 6/25**. DISH's Ch11 filing on **6/30 falls AFTER the 6/25 lock-out** → removal defers to the **7/31 rebalance**. **The 7/1-data print (CCC 9.68, published 7/2 08:57 CT) is therefore NOT ex-DISH — DISH paper is still in the index.** Verified against precedent: Russia-2022 names exited at month-end (not intra-month); COVID-2020's only deviation was a *postponement* (later, never earlier). [PRIMARY: ICE methodology + FRED/ALFRED metadata, workflow 3-0 verified]
- **Default trigger = individual bond legal terms, NOT an agency "D".** ICE: *"A rating of 'D' by a major rating agency is not a consideration for default status."* The 6/1 coupon skips were cured 6/18 (above), so they never became an Event of Default; the **sole legal EoD is the 6/30 bankruptcy filing** (immediate EoD w/ automatic acceleration). This forecloses the alternative that a pre-lock-out default could have pulled DISH out at the 6/30 rebalance. A competing "grace-period default lands ~7/1" theory was refuted 0-3 (coupons cured 6/18). [PRIMARY: ICE methodology; EchoStar 8-Ks 6/1 & 6/18]

## Counter-Evidence

- **Against "genuine widening, not composition":** DISH *does* still sit in the index and *will* mechanically move the CCC print at the 7/31 rebalance — so a naive reader comparing the July-average CCC to the August print could misread the mechanical DISH removal as "healing." The composition caveat is real; it's just displaced to July-end, not June. Flag for LIQUID: **re-pull after 7/31 and expect a mechanical CCC tightening that is NOT credit improvement.**
- **Against "AI/software is the driver":** Nuveen calls the June widening "modest," cites a heavy primary (new-issue) calendar as a competing technical, and notes IG total return stayed positive — i.e. part of the move is supply/technical, not pure AI-credit stress. The dispersion regime (Man Group) also predates June, so June may be a continuation of a months-long theme rather than a fresh catalyst.
- **Against a composition-driven aggregate-OAS blowout:** AllianceBernstein frames tech IG repricing as healthy, not stress; the big-5 hyperscalers are only ~3.5% of IG debt (low leverage), so the "record 10% tech weight" overstates the *distress-relevant* exposure. PGIM finds senior CLO tranches stable — the stress is in equity/mezz, which are not what index-level OAS is most sensitive to.
- **Against the "record dispersion 8.06" framing:** as computed above, the arithmetic gap is not a window-record; only the ratio is. And the record ratio is partly a *BB-too-tight* story (BB at 163bp) as much as a *CCC-blowing-out* story — decompose before treating it as pure tail stress.
- **Where counter-evidence is thin:** the leg (a) index-mechanics conclusion has essentially no credible counter — the ICE rule text, the lock-out arithmetic, precedent (Russia-2022, COVID-2020), and the FRED tape all point the same way, and the two competing theories (grace-period default ~7/1; near-par bonds exiting) were both refuted 0-3.
