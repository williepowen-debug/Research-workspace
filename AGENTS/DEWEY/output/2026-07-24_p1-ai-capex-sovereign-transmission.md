**Date:** 2026-07-24 | **Mode:** Thesis | **Confidence:** High (federal-$0 + debt-fragility legs = SEC/FRED-verified) / Medium (macro-reversal sizing; two China gaps 403-walled) | **For:** Will — China-strategic-exhaustion thesis, prompt P1 | **Routing:** Will (live) | **Companion to:** P0 analog audit (same day)

## Key Finding

**The "bankrupt the USA (sovereign)" chain is structurally weak — it FAILS at the transmission step, exactly where P0 predicted.** Private US AI capex (~$400-500B/yr) reaches the federal balance sheet through **~$0 of committed federal dollars**, so there is no arms-race-style fiscal drain. The real — and only — transmission is **indirect and market-mediated**: AI capex is now ~⅓ of US GDP growth and the AI complex is ~35% of the S&P, so an AI-trade *reversal* hits growth + equity wealth → federal revenue → the already-$1.8T deficit. **That makes the tradeable a private AI-concentration/credit bust that tips a fragile fisc via recession — NOT a Treasury drain, and NOT "China outspends the US into bankruptcy."** The thesis has to be re-specified around *that* mechanism to survive.

## Evidence

### Channel 1 — Government backstop: ~$0 today (the decisive negative)
The only channel that would put AI spend *on the federal balance sheet* (USSR-style) is essentially empty:
- **Stargate ($500B, Jan 2025) = ZERO federal dollars** — all private (OpenAI + SoftBank + Oracle + MGX); govt role = permitting/EOs only. And even the *private* headline is largely un-raised (Bloomberg Aug 2025: "no funds raised"; SoftBank's first $10B borrowed) [NEWS, verify: CONFIRMED as announced-only].
- **CHIPS Act $52.7B** — appropriated but to semiconductor **fabrication** (Intel/TSMC/Samsung/Micron fabs), **not AI-compute**; ~$33B "allocated," far less obligated. **$0 AI-compute-specific** [PRIMARY].
- **Intel equity stake $8.87B** (9.9%, Aug 2025) — a *foundry*, not a hyperscaler; an equity buy (up/downside), not a guarantee.
- **DOE grid/nuclear loans** ($26.5B Southern Co Feb 2026; $1.6B Michigan; AEP Texas) — **repayable, to IG regulated utilities, ratepayer-backed, not AI-earmarked.** The strongest *future* channel if the AI-power thesis materializes, but not sovereign risk today.
- **VERDICT: the government is NOT structurally on the hook; the USSR financing-transmission channel does not exist.** ⚠️ **Not airtight, though:** no explicit no-bailout statute exists, the hyperscalers are systemically large, and the Intel stake proves this administration *will* take direct positions — so "not currently on the hook" ≠ "structurally immune." "Sovereign AI" framing is rising. *(Gaps: DoD CDAO frontier-model contracts ~$200M-ceiling and GSA OneGov $1 deals — demand/procurement, not financing — 403-walled this run.)*

### Channel 3 — The indirect macro channel (the REAL transmission)
- **AI capex ≈ ⅓ of 2025 real GDP growth:** computers+software capex contributed **~0.7pp of ~2.0% growth (~37%)** — and **>100% of growth in the two weak quarters (Q1 & Q4 2025)**; a reversal that merely stops *growing* subtracts ~0.5-0.9pp before any multiplier/wealth effect [PRIMARY: BEA]. *(The viral "-1.1% ex-AI GDP" stat is NOT reproducible — this is the defensible version; consistent with DEWEY's 6/27 refutation.)*
- **Index concentration: Mag7 ~32.6% of S&P 500 cap; +Broadcom ~35.4%; NVDA alone ~7.6% — the largest single-stock weight ever** (on a $67.6T index) [INSTITUTIONAL, verify: CONFIRMED].
- **Federal revenue IS wealth-sensitive:** non-withheld individual income tax (where capital gains land) swung **−$347B / −28.2% in one year (2022→2023)** after the 2022 market drop, while withheld wage tax rose +4.3% — cleanly isolating the equity→revenue channel [PRIMARY: BEA NIPA].
- **So the actual chain is:** AI-trade reversal → −0.5-0.9pp growth + equity-wealth destruction → federal revenue drop (−$300B+ order, per the 2022 analog) → the **already-$1.8T deficit / 122.6% debt/GDP / $970B interest** widens. A market/recession→fiscal transmission — real, but ordinary, and *not* uniquely China-driven.

## Counter-Evidence (weighted equally)
- **The indirect channel cuts against the thesis as much as for it:** it's just "a big bubble bursting hurts a levered fisc" — true of *any* bubble (dot-com, housing), not evidence of a *China* strategy or a *uniquely AI* sovereign risk.
- **The government-backstop negative could flip:** if a bust hits systemically-large hyperscalers, ad hoc intervention (à la the Intel stake) or a "sovereign AI" pivot could *create* the federal channel that doesn't exist today. The thesis's best surviving form lives here.
- **Scout leg (P3 preview) points AWAY from the US as "the USSR":** neither power cleanly fits the profile, but the **US is the LEAST USSR-like** (private self-funded capex, reserve-currency issuer, thin sovereign transmission). **China carries more of the domestic-structural-strain half** (LGFV ~$8T/47% GDP; youth unemployment 16.5%; property crisis) *and* its AI spend is more state-coupled (state banks/LGFVs/national funds) — so if anyone is fiscally exposed *through* AI spend, the mechanism is stronger on the China side. ⚠️ But the "China gets AI cheap" (DeepSeek $5.6M) claim is **OVERSTATED** — that's a notional marginal training cost, not total cost; P2 must handle it carefully.
