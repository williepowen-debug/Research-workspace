# MARCO-DR-1: the Canadian–Florida spending base, rebuilt from primaries
**Date:** 2026-10-28 (research 22:14–22:4x ET) | **Mode:** Thesis | **Commission:** MARCO-DR-1 (MARCO 2026-07-31; DOCKET L466, needed by 2026-11-16). **Run as a from-scratch base on Will's word (22:19 ET), after MARCO's 10/25 withdrawal of the $600M–$1.2B figure (WALTER SIG-W-20261029-004).** This report does not revive that figure; MARCO adopts or declines what follows. | **Confidence:** High on the counts, the property-roll measurement and the absence findings. **No defensible current dollar figure exists in public sources.**

## Key finding
1. **No public source gives a defensible current figure for Canadian spending in Florida.**
   - Visit Florida counts **3,174,000 Canadian person-trips in 2025** (revised 2026-05-26; −6.8% vs 2024, −22.4% vs 2019). It publishes no Canadian spending figure, and it **excludes snowbirds who own homes**.
   - The last primary dollar figure is Statistics Canada's **C$5.625B for 2014**: 4.015M overnight person-visits averaging **22.7 nights**, so long seasonal stays and short trips are blended.
   - StatCan has published no state-level data since then.
2. **Where the old base came from.**
   - "3.4M" matches Visit Florida's **2024** trip count, which leaves out owner-snowbirds.
   - **No source exists for "$1,800 per visitor".**
   - The nearest circulating total is an unsourced "US$6.5B", attributed to different headcounts by different outlets.
3. **The owner population the visitor counts miss, measured for the first time here.**
   - Florida's 2026 final property rolls (values at January 1, 2026) show **48,107 homes in eight counties with a Canadian owner mailing address, with a just value of $21.32B**. That is about **3.4% of non-homestead residential parcels**.
   - Broward alone has 16,643 of them ($5.80B). They are mostly condos, and about two-thirds are Quebec addresses.
   - This is **ownership, not spending**, and mailing address is not citizenship.
4. **One methoded Florida loss estimate exists. It is a forecast, not a measurement.** Tourism Economics put lost Canadian visitor spending at **−$23 per Florida resident, 2025 vs 2024**: about **−$0.5B** at ~23.4M residents (DEWEY arithmetic). Every other Florida dollar figure in circulation has no method.
5. **County concentration cannot be stated in dollars.** It can be stated in owned homes (table 3) and, for Lee only, in a survey count of visitors: 165,160 Canadian visitors in 2025, −15.9%.

## 2. Spend per visitor: no current figure, and the blend is the finding
- **No published current per-visitor spend for Canadians in Florida** turned up anywhere searched: Visit Florida, the county development councils, StatCan, Rockport, news.
- **The last primary per-visit figure blends stay types:** C$1,401 per overnight person-visit, C$61.8 per night, 22.7 nights (2014, DEWEY arithmetic on StatCan). A 22.7-night average is only possible if multi-month stays sit in the same pool as week-long trips.
- **US-wide, the split is roughly tenfold** [PRIMARY StatCan 24-10-0070, US, CAD, quarterly, pulled 10/28]:
  - **Same-day:** C$125–206 per visit (2025 to Q1 2026).
  - **Overnight:** C$1,098–1,425 per visit.
  - So any single "per Canadian visitor" average depends on its stay-type mix more than on spending.
- **Visit Florida excludes seasonal owners, while Sarasota's DSG survey includes owners staying in their own condos.** These are opposite conventions in two official surfaces.
- **No source splits Canadians three ways** (overnight / same-day / seasonal).

## 4. Canadian-owned property: other sources (secondary)
- **No appraiser or state publication counts Canadian-owned parcels or their value.** Table 3a is, as far as this run found, the first such count.
- **Florida Realtors 2025** (survey; Aug 2024–Jul 2025):
  - **Buyers:** Canadians were 18% of international buyers (2024 survey: 17%), US$1.916B of volume (+52%).
  - **Sellers:** Canadians were **33% of international sellers** (2024 survey: 29%).
  - **Metro buyer shares:** Naples 52%, Cape Coral–Fort Myers 41%, North Port–Sarasota 38%, Tampa 32%, Miami–Fort Lauderdale–West Palm Beach 8%.
- **NAR 2025** (Apr 2024–Mar 2025): Canada was 31% of Florida's foreign buyers, and 48% of Canadian buyers bought in Florida.
- **The two surveys' Canadian shares (18% vs 31%) conflict** on windows and on unknown-origin handling (14% of buyers had no country in the Florida Realtors survey). Do not splice them into one series.
- **Royal LePage / Leger, Aug 2025:** 54% of Canadians who own US property plan to sell within a year. It is an online panel, the number of owners in the sample is undisclosed, and there is no margin of error. A stated intention is not a sale.
- **WPTV:** 321 Palm Beach homes with Canadian mailing addresses sold "this year" (≈2025; date unverified).
- **Canadian Snowbird Association:** "~500,000 Canadians winter in Florida" (2020) is an estimate with no method.

## Counter-evidence: was Canadian spending substituted rather than lost?
- **2025: substitution in visitor counts, yes.**
  - Florida set a visitor record: 143.3M, +0.2% (domestic 130.9M, +0.2%; overseas 9.3M, +4.0%), while Canadians fell 6.8% [PRIMARY Visit Florida FAQ].
  - The +0.2% domestic gain on 130.9M (~+0.26M) exceeds the ~−0.23M Canadian decline.
  - **Counts, not dollars.** This does not test substitution of long-stay spending.
- **H1 2026: no substitution.** Total visitors −1.4%, domestic **−1.3%**, Canadians −13.9% [NEWS FOX 35 2026-08-24, preliminary]. The rest of the market softened as well.
- **Tourist-tax receipts in the Canadian-heavy counties rose:**
  - Palm Beach FY2026 +9.76%, with peak-season months Dec–Mar up 7–12%.
  - Broward's Visit Lauderdale share +9.3% through June.
  - Lee +22.2%, on a hurricane-depressed base.
  - **But tourist tax cannot see the owner population.** Owner-occupied seasonal stays, and rentals over six months, are not taxable (F.S. 125.0104, read by leg B and not rechecked here). The 48,107 owned homes in table 3a are exactly the spending these instruments miss. Receipts are also nominal.
- **The pullback is uneven by segment.**
  - Kissimmee card data shows −24.6%.
  - Winter 2026-27 RV-park bookings are anecdotal and range from −70% (Bonifay) to +25% (a new park). Applebrook's CEO calls 2026-27 "the trough for the Canadian guest" [NEWS Woodall's 2026-10-16].
  - Canadians were **33% of Florida's international sellers vs 18% of buyers** (Florida Realtors 2025). That points to net Canadian disinvestment from owned property, with no count of how much.
- **Net:** the evidence refutes a *visible statewide visitor-count* hole in 2025. It neither confirms nor refutes a *dollar* hole, because no instrument measures Canadian dollars in Florida, and the owner segment is invisible to the count and tax data the fleet watches.

## Source quality assessment
**Strong:**
- StatCan and the Florida Department of Revenue property rolls (primary, pulled directly, hashed).
- Visit Florida FAQ counts (primary, checked 10/28).
- Lee and Sarasota DSG reports (primary surveys).

**Medium:**
- Visit Florida quarterly figures. They are heavily revised; give the vintage every time.
- Florida Realtors and NAR. These are surveys of transactions, not owners.
- Tourism Economics. A model forecast, with the Florida value unconfirmed by DEWEY.

**Weak:** every circulating Florida dollar total ($6.5B, $4.4B, $90M) has no method.

**Property-roll limits:**
- A mailing address in Canada is not citizenship. Canadians using a Florida address, an LLC or a trust are missed, so **48,107 is a floor on Canadian-held parcels**.
- US citizens resident in Canada are included.
- A parcel is not an owner.
- Only the primary owner's address is carried.
- Confidential (s. 119.071) records are excluded.
- Encoding differs by county: Sarasota stores the province code, the other seven store "CANADA". Both were captured and checked.
- `OWN_STATE_DOM`, which the 2026 user guide defines as "FC" for owners domiciled abroad, was "FL" or blank in Broward. It is not usable.
- Only eight counties were read, not the state.
