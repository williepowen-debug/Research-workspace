# PORTS-Pike power rights and payment bridge
**Date:** 2026-10-14 | **Mode:** Thesis | **Confidence:** High for the signed study agreement and published tariff; low for project-specific utility obligations and final cost allocation.

## Key finding

Five parties signed a **study agreement** on September 25, 2026, and PJM filed it on October 13 as ER27-126-000. SE DC DevCo must pay PJM's actual study costs, starting with a $500,000 deposit. The agreement expressly grants **no specific transmission rights, construction selection, or cost allocation**. Ohio Power's data-center tariff sets potential minimum-payment, collateral and cancellation rules for retail service, but the project-specific electric-service and buildout agreements were not available in this pull; their signatories, guarantees, posted collateral, contract capacity, start date and dollar exposure are **UNKNOWN**. **Population bridge.** PJM's 800 MW Mercury initial phase plus 9,570 MW Artemis expansion equals 10,370 MW of **requested grid load** through 2032. VULCAN's prior [KB-VULCAN-126](../../VULCAN/workbook/KB.tsv) records NVIDIA's nine data centers, approximately **4.25 GW committed plus 3.8 GW optional IT capacity**, first expected NVIDIA fiscal 2029. The roughly 8.0 GW IT population is inside the broader site but is not the same electrical quantity, commitment status or timetable as 10.37 GW requested grid load. SB Energy's September 21 S-1/A describes 8.0 GW IT as planned, about 10 GW gross load, and says utility service agreements for **9.2 GW** remain subject to final regulatory approvals; that statement was available only through indexed SEC text, not a full-document pull. None of these sources establishes that all nine phases have firm service rights. ## Counter-evidence and missing documents

The state letter, agreement recitals, AEP plan and proposed 10.17 GW generation are evidence of an intent to fund infrastructure and add supply. They argue against simply assigning every upgrade cost to other ratepayers or assuming no build path. They **do not** establish realized capacity, a final tariff cost allocation, or a parent guarantee. Conversely, Ohio Power's DCT does impose minimum payments on qualifying executed new-load contracts; the absence of the PORTS-Pike ESA prevents applying its formulas to this named project. PJM's study expressly checks full requested load under stressed seasonal conditions and forced outages, countering a gross-generation-minus-load spare-capacity calculation. [PRIMARY: [PJM Attachment A recitals, Art. 8, Schedules A–C](https://www.pjm.com/pjmfiles/directory/etariff/FercDockets/9925/20261013-er27-126-000.pdf); [Schedule DCT](https://www.aepohio.com/lib/docs/ratesandtariffs/Ohio/October_2026_Ohio_Power_Tariff_Book.pdf)]
