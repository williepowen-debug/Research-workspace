# FL coastal STR-host net-yield overlay: is there a distinct assessment-driven forced-sale channel?
**Date:** 2026-07-24 | **Mode:** Thesis | **Confidence:** High (pro-forma mechanics) / Medium (over-index) / Low (a *distinct* STR-host channel — contested)
**For:** CORAL (FL condo/insurance owner) — complement to your live vintage-condo + insurance-split work. **Companion to** the STR metrics catalog (same-day).

## Key Finding
**The carrying-cost squeeze on leveraged FL coastal condo owners is real and large — but it is a *leverage + vintage-condo* story, not a cleanly distinct "STR-host" forced-sale channel.** A leveraged (70% LTV) FL coastal STR condo is **cash-flow negative (~−$22k/yr) *before any special assessment*** — mortgage P&I + HOA dues alone are ~99% of gross STR revenue. So the binding killer is **debt service + HOA**, with the SIRS assessment and the STR-revenue glut as **accelerants** on an already-underwater position. The overlay verdict is **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED**: the foundational shock holds, but the *novel* claim — a distinct, additive STR-specific channel from hosts over-indexed to vintage condos — is **contested, not established** (STR-permissive product skews newer/compliant; vintage associations disproportionately *ban* STR). The genuinely additive DEWEY piece is the **pro-forma** below and one clean discriminator.

## 1. The pro-forma (the contribution) — carrying cost, not revenue, is the killer
Representative leveraged FL Gulf/coastal STR **condo**, $450k unit, 70% LTV @ 7%/30yr, gross STR revenue $37k/yr (Run-1 PCB/Kissimmee level):

| Line | Annual |
|---|---:|
| Gross STR revenue | +$37,000 |
| Airbnb host-only platform fee (~15.5%) | −$5,735 |
| Full-service management (~22.5%) | −$8,325 |
| Cleaning/turnover (net) | −$1,500 |
| **Mortgage P&I** ($315k @7%/30yr) | **−$25,148** |
| Property tax (non-homestead ~1.05%) | −$4,750 |
| **HOA/condo dues** (~$950/mo, bundles master policy) | **−$11,400** |
| Unit HO-6 insurance | −$1,800 |
| **NET (before any assessment)** | **≈ −$21,700** |

- **Mortgage P&I + HOA = $36,548 ≈ 99% of gross revenue** before tax/insurance/mgmt/fees. **The entire deficit is debt service** — remove the mortgage (all-cash) and the base flips to **+$3,490/yr**.
- **Assessment as accelerant, not trigger:** for the leveraged host there is no positive→negative "assessment threshold" — they're already negative; the assessment just deepens it. The threshold only exists for **all-cash** owners, whose +$3,490 cushion is wiped by any assessment amortized above ~$3,500/yr. Typical vintage-tower assessment **$30–75k/unit** (Run-1's $134k–$400k are the tail) → **$6–15k/yr over 5yr**, exceeding even the all-cash cushion **2–4×**. *(Amortization tenor is the swing variable and owner can't control it — FL associations often demand 1–3yr, compressing the annual hit.)*
- **Where your rising-insurance layer shows up:** PCB-type dues **bundle the master policy**, so the condo-association premium increases you track (the *rising* layer, vs the easing personal line) transmit into **HOA-dues inflation**, i.e. the −$11,400 line, not the −$1,800 unit HO-6. *(I used your canonical insurance split; I did NOT ship the $8,458 figure — flagged separately.)*

## 2. Over-index to the assessment cohort — directionally yes, but contested
- **STR listings ARE condo-heavy in beach markets:** Panama City Beach **71.6%** condo, Miami Beach **88.3%**, Destin 67.8% (vs single-family-dominant national STR baseline) [INSTITUTIONAL: AirROI 2026 market reports]. Exception: **Fort Myers Beach is now house-dominant (51%)** post-Ian rebuild — its old-condo exposure *fell*.
- **FL coastal condo stock is disproportionately old:** ~1.1M of ~1.5M FL condo units are 30+ yr; ~⅔ of South-FL coastal condo stock predates 1990; ~58% of aging units in 8 coastal counties; DBPR ~11,500+ buildings under milestone [NEWS: FL outlets citing DBPR].
- **⚠️ The contradiction (why this isn't a clean STR channel):** STR-*permissive* product tilts **new/purpose-built** (≈half the new SoFla pipeline is STR-friendly), and **vintage associations disproportionately restrict or ban STR.** So the assessment-exposed cohort and the STR-host cohort may be **partly disjoint** — the over-index is real at the "listings are in condos" level but **weaker and unmeasured at the host level** (no dataset joins STR listings to the specific buildings that took assessments).

## 3. Distress evidence — one clean discriminator, but STR-host attribution is inferential

## Counter-Evidence
- The verifier **contradicted** the distinct-STR-channel claim (§2 disjointness); the honest read is that this is ~85% the **general leveraged-vintage-condo distress you already track**, with the STR-host pro-forma as the incremental cut.
- The "distress" is heavily **leverage-conditional** — an all-cash or low-LTV host in the same building is fine until a large assessment; the negative pro-forma is a 70%-LTV artifact, and STR-host leverage/DSCR-loan penetration is unmeasured (private data).
- STR *operating* metrics are holding (revenue −3%, occupancy 57%), so this is **not** an "Airbnbust" story — it's a balance-sheet/carrying-cost story that would exist without STR.
