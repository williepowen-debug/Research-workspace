## Key finding

The FDIC's live Nano Banc failed-bank page still lists **only the Bid Summary** under “Transaction Documentation” as of this check; it does not link a Purchase and Assumption (P&A) agreement. This is a dated observation of that page, **not proof that the executed agreement does not exist**.
