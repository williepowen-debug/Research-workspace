# Japan super-long JGB demand — forced-seller SIGN + marginal-buyer map
**Date:** 2026-07-10 | **Mode:** Thesis | **Confidence:** Medium-High (mechanism/structure) / Medium (sign adjudication — two bases coexist)
**Flag:** REQ-DEWEY-20260702-004 (Batch-2 #08) | **Feeds:** SAM (carry-tail 4.5% trigger SIGN keep/kill; SAM-33 conf), LIQUID (repatriation channel), BOND/HENRY (info)

## Key Finding
Under Japan's new **economic-value solvency regime (J-ICS/ESR, effective 31 Mar 2026)**, a further 30Y rise toward 4.5% is **net demand-POSITIVE for the major lifers, not a clean self-reinforcing forced-seller trigger** — the regime marks BOTH assets and liabilities to economic value, and because lifers run a **negative duration gap** (liabilities longer than assets), higher long-end yields *close* the gap and *improve* ESR, rewarding MORE long-duration buying. **BUT the forced-seller mechanism is not dead — it survives on the coexisting J-GAAP statutory-impairment basis**: a named market strategist pegs **>4.5% 30Y as the impairment-loss / further-selling threshold** on legacy low-coupon holdings, and lifers did net-sell ¥201bn of super-longs in May-2026. **Net for SAM: KEEP the 4.5% trigger, but RE-CHARACTERIZE it** — it is a *statutory-impairment disorderly TAIL on legacy holdings*, firing AGAINST an economic-value regime that otherwise makes higher yields a buy signal — not the base-case mechanism. The sign is **structurally ALM-buyer with a real J-GAAP forced-seller tail at >4.5%.**

## Evidence

### (1) Accounting SIGN — J-ICS/ESR vs J-GAAP  ← THE CORE QUESTION
**Dominant regime = economic-value ESR → ALM-buyer.** The FY2025-effective J-ICS/ESR standard (promulgated Jul-2025, effective **31 Mar 2026**, first reporting FY-ended Mar-2026, replacing the legacy Solvency Margin Ratio) marks **both assets AND liabilities** to economic value and charges interest-rate-risk capital on the change in **NET asset value** (the ALM duration gap) [PRIMARY: FSA econ-value solvency council + 20220630_solvency.pdf; IMF FSAP CR24/110]. IMF verbatim: the ESR regime *"is less sensitive to interest rate changes due to liability values responding to the changes in interest rates along with assets… Under SMR, only the asset values respond"* [PRIMARY: IMF CR24/110]. Aviva: *"a move in the 30 to 40-year point can now reprice the entire balance sheet… Duration mismatch is no longer a low-key regulatory issue; it is immediate, visible and capital sensitive"* [INSTITUTIONAL: Aviva Investors, Feb-2026]. *(Fan-out: mechanism cluster unanimous 3-0 across 8 claims; spine primary.)*

**The negative duration gap — the load-bearing assumption — is structurally CONFIRMED (DEWEY primary check).** Japanese lifers historically hold liability duration > asset duration; when rates rise, liabilities fall more than assets → net worth *rises*, hedging BOJ hikes [INSTITUTIONAL, multi-source: TradingKey 2026, TwentyFour AM, SSGA, BIS WP519, BOJ FSR Oct-2025]. Confirmation the rate move does NOT crater solvency: **Nippon Life's ESR fell only ~2pp to 222%** (regulatory min 100%) despite material investment-value swings [INSTITUTIONAL: TwentyFour AM]. *(This DEWEY pull repairs the fan-out's CAVEAT-1 hole: the fan-out REFUTED the one secondary citation [Milliman] that asserted the negative gap [vote 1-2], but the direction is structural bedrock, independently corroborated — a citation failure, not a fact failure. Still not pinned to a single primary per-insurer ESR-sensitivity disclosure — see counter-evidence.)*

**BUT the forced-seller mechanism survives on the J-GAAP statutory basis — the two bases COEXIST.** ESR does not abolish statutory/J-GAAP accounting; legacy available-for-sale JGB markdowns can still bind dividend capacity and trigger impairment recognition in parallel. A named strategist (via Insurance Business Asia / JSDA reporting): **"*If the 30-year JGB yield were to exceed 4.5% from its current ~3.9%, life insurers would face a significant risk of impairment losses, making it highly likely that they would proceed with further bond sales*"** [NEWS-attributed, single strategist]. This is the *impairment-cascade* channel operating on legacy low-coupon holdings — the disorderly tail, distinct from the economic-value base case.

**Maturing-demand nuance:** lifer JGB buying has fallen from a post-2020 peak ~¥700bn/mo to ~¥100bn recently — the duration-gap-closing program is *largely complete*, so the incremental duration bid is fading even as the structural negative gap persists [INSTITUTIONAL: TwentyFour/market commentary]. This *supports SAM's demand-REDUCTION* (fewer marginal buyers) while *cutting against* a clean forced-seller (higher yields still net-positive on ESR).

**Adjudication:** structurally **ALM-buyer / net-demand-positive** under the binding solvency regime; a **real J-GAAP statutory-impairment forced-seller TAIL at >4.5%** on legacy holdings. The 4.5% trigger is a *disorderly-path* mechanism, not the base path — consistent with SAM's orderly-grind-base / disorderly-tail structure. **KEEP, re-characterized.**

### (2) Flow verification + cohort  *(DEWEY primary pull)*
**7/7 30Y auction (Issue #91) — CONFIRMED real end-investor bid.** Submitted ¥2,070.0bn / accepted ¥454.9bn → **BTC 4.55x**; avg price 100.09 = **3.993%**, low 100.05 = 3.996% → **tail 0.04 yen ≈ 0.3bp**; coupon 4.0% [PRIMARY: MOF eresul20260707]. Clears SAM's "real bid" bar (BTC ≥2.9x AND tail ≤3bp) decisively — the ~4.0% floor is FLOWING. Curve (MOF CSV, 7/9): 10Y 2.866 / 20Y 3.795 / **30Y 4.013** / **40Y 3.962** — **30s40s INVERTED** (40Y 5bp below 30Y), a tell of concentrated ALM demand at the longest tenor [PRIMARY: MOF jgbcme.csv].

**Cohort flow — leg-4 RESOLVED: one-month print, not a multi-month run.** JSDA super-long (>10Y) net flow: **April-2026 net BUYING ¥327.2bn** (FY-start, budget availability) → **May-2026 net SELLING ¥201.2bn**, which the source explicitly frames as *"partially offset[ting]"* April's buying [NEWS: Insurance Business Asia, citing JSDA]. **Two-month net = +¥126bn BUYING** — May was a partial giveback, NOT a sustained cohort exit. **Gaps:** no per-insurer breakdown (is Meiji Yasuda alone or cohort? — unresolved), no curve-repositioning (sell-long/buy-belly) detail; the source's "net exit" framing is in tension with the larger April buy — the two-month net is the honest read. *(Cross-border context, LIQUID-relevant, DEWEY primary: MOF weekly foreign-LT-debt — wk 6/25–7/1 −¥889.9bn sale, wk 7/2–7/8 +¥697.1bn buy [PRIMARY: MOF week.csv] — this is the outbound/repatriation series, NOT the domestic-JGB cohort.)*

### (3) GPIF / marginal buyer + Katayama jawbone  *(DEWEY primary pull; fan-out concurs)*
**GPIF is NOT the marginal buyer — jawbone, not a portfolio change.** 5th Medium-Term policy portfolio (effective **Apr-1-2025**): **25% domestic bonds** in an equal 25/25/25/25 split, **UNCHANGED** from the 4th period; only change is *narrower* deviation bands [PRIMARY: GPIF "Adoption of New Policy Portfolio (details 2)"]. FinMin **Katayama** (~7/10) said the government is *"seeking to encourage"* GPIF (¥293.4tn) to boost **domestic-asset** investment — reporting describes seeking *measures to encourage*, NOT a mandated allocation change; yen +~0.6% to 161.4, 10Y −10bp to 2.775% on the remark [NEWS: Reuters/Bloomberg/Yahoo, 7/9-10; Japan Times paywalled — no verbatim quote]. *(Two fan-out claims asserting a 35%→25% cut were REFUTED [0-3 / 1-2] — the correct read is 25% unchanged.)* Note: an equal-weight rebalancer this size is a passive *stabilizer* (buys JGBs as they cheapen to hold 25%), but that is not a fresh incremental bid.

### (4) BOJ reaction function  *(DEWEY primary pull — PRIMARY-confirmed; fan-out concurs)*
**Velocity-triggered, not level-triggered — "let a gradual move run, cap the disorderly spike."** BOJ 6/16 JGB-purchase plan: *"In the case of a **rapid rise** in long-term interest rates, the Bank will make nimble responses by … increasing the amount of JGB purchases and conducting fixed-rate purchase operations … regardless of the monthly schedule"* [PRIMARY: BOJ k260616b.pdf]. Same meeting (7-1): **halt the taper** — monthly ¥2tn held from Apr-2027 *"from the perspective of maintaining downward pressure on long-term interest rates through the stock effect"*; rate raised to **1.0%** with guidance to keep hiking as CPI nears 2%; Tamura dissent (let rates form freely); Ueda hospitalized, Uchida chaired [PRIMARY: BOJ opi260616.pdf / k260616a.pdf]. **6/24 Summary of Opinions:** super-long real rates *"at a level comparable to those overseas"* (i.e. not disorderly); a dissent warns rate-capping perceived as fiscal financing would undermine credibility. The record contains **NO reference to unscheduled/emergency ops and none to the Apr-May 2025 episode** (fan-out: these two absence-inferences are 2-1, appropriately hedged as inference-from-silence). **Apr-May 2025 precedent:** BOJ did **NOT** run unscheduled ops during the super-long spike (30Y ~3.1% record); it let the move run and ran a bond-market-participants survey (~5/20), while MOF weighed cutting super-long issuance [NEWS/INSTITUTIONAL: Bloomberg/Wolf Street 5/20/2025 — BOJ ops-archive not primary-verified]. **Net:** a *gradual* grind through 4.5% would NOT trigger a BOJ cap; only a *rapid/disorderly* spike would — confirming SAM-33's structure (BOJ truncates the violent tail, not the grind). The taper-halt is mildly *supply-supportive* of the long end (marginal against a disorderly break).

## Counter-Evidence
- **The forced-seller thesis is NOT refuted, only re-based.** A named strategist explicitly pegs **>4.5% 30Y = impairment-loss / further-selling** on legacy holdings, and May saw real ¥201bn selling. If the J-GAAP statutory-impairment channel binds harder than the economic-value ESR incentive for a given insurer (esp. mid-caps with weaker buffers), that lifer could be a forced seller even as majors are ALM buyers → a **bifurcated cohort** near 4.5% (Fitch flags smaller non-covered insurers — Fukoku/Asahi — that *"may not have adjusted duration"* as facing capital pressure).
- **The negative-duration-gap sign is NOT pinned to a primary per-insurer disclosure.** Direction is structural bedrock + multi-source corroborated + the fan-out's own refutation was of a *secondary citation*, not the fact — but no single lifer's FY2025 ESR interest-rate-sensitivity table was read. This is the #1 residual (hardenable via a Nippon/Meiji/Dai-ichi/Sumitomo ESR-report pull if a trade needs it).
- **Two accounting bases coexist** — the clean "ALM-buyer" read holds on solvency; it does NOT prove J-GAAP asset markdowns *never* bind dividend/statutory capacity (fan-out flagged the "never" tail as a 2-1 overstatement).
- **40Y sensitivity dampened** — liabilities extrapolate to a ~3.8% Ultimate Forward Rate beyond the 30Y last-observed term, so the two-sided asset/liability offset is cleanest AT 30Y, weaker in the 40Y sector (SAM-35 40Y test 7/22 is the live check).
- **What would disprove the ALM-buyer base case:** a primary lifer ESR disclosure showing a *positive* or near-zero duration gap; a multi-month (Mar-Jun) sustained net-sell run in JSDA data (contradicted here — April buying dominated); or a BOJ statement introducing a level (not velocity) trigger.
