## Vulnerability and Mitigation
The September 14 CPC site briefing describes Yongan operating around **13 million tonnes/year against 12 million planned**, with roughly two ships every three days. That is throughput pressure on a working terminal, not tank fill. It also demonstrates that replacement supply can physically arrive. One observed arrival neither proves adequate national stocks nor permits counting all procurement promises as delivered. [NEWS/on-site CPC briefing: S4]

MOEA's September 15 and 22 statements describe diversified sourcing, shipping contingency arrangements and higher stocks. These are useful official mitigation evidence with an observation date. The October 1 government budget explanation says CPC was directed to procure emergency supply and maintain high tank levels, without reporting a measured LNG quantity. [PRIMARY: S1, [S2](https://www.moea.gov.tw/Mns/Populace/news/News.aspx?kind=1&menu_id=40&news_id=123979), [S9](https://investtaiwan.nat.gov.tw/newsPage99139cht?lang=cht&search=99139)]

## Counter-Evidence
No verified current shortage or enforced fuel-related curtailment was found, and the September delivery plus official procurement record contradict treating shipping disruption alone as demonstrated fuel exhaustion. Conversely, “supply arranged through December” cannot establish a physical buffer against a new delivery interruption. The central quantity remains unknown.

**No inventory-days calculation is made.** Required inputs are same-population usable LNG mass or gas-equivalent stock at a specified time, plus actual comparable consumption per day and conversion basis. Coal requires the equivalent stock/use pairing and plant-level usability. A historical annual demand estimate, ship size or operating electricity reserve percentage cannot supply the missing denominator.

## Source Quality Assessment
Primary official statements and the legislative table are clear about their dates and prospective status. The September cargo and May inventory-days figure are named-source CNA reporting; neither is relabelled as an independently obtained primary inventory dataset. The latest numerical value located in this bounded 2026 search is historical, not asserted to be the latest value that exists anywhere.
