## Evidence

### Institutional legs (the reopening test)

**I-1 · JWC / JWLA-033 — NOT DE-LISTED.** Direct fetch of the primary Lloyd's Market Association JWC page confirms **JWLA-033 (Iran, 3 Mar 2026)** — covering the entire Persian/Arabian Gulf + Gulf of Oman incl. the Strait — is the current listed-area document with **no de-listing or amendment since the 6/17 ceasefire** (no JWLA-034/035 exists). The footprint was *widened* (adding Bahrain/Djibouti/Kuwait/Oman/Qatar/US-base states), not narrowed. Trade press (Insurance Business Mag, 6/25): *"Underwriters will require the formal removal of the JWC's listed-area designation… before rates retreat meaningfully"* — i.e. de-listing is the reopening precondition, and it hasn't happened. *Nuance: the Gulf-wide expansion is a 3-Mar structural event (Gulf listed since JWLA-024, 2019) — record as still-listed/static, not a fresh July action.* [PRIMARY: LMA JWC page + JWLA-033_Iran.pdf; INSTITUTIONAL: Insurance Business Mag 2026-06-25]

**I-2 · P&I war-risk Additional Premium — NOT NORMALIZED (the BRT-07/17 start-gun).** Hormuz war-risk AP **halved from ~5% to ~2% of hull within 6 days of the 6/17 ceasefire** — but even that left it ~20× the ~0.1-0.25% pre-conflict baseline. By 6/28 (post-re-escalation) it ran **3-8% of hull = $3-8M per large-tanker transit, ~12-32× baseline**, and the 6/27 re-escalation ended any near-term reversal. Corroborated across Insurance Business Mag, Lloyd's List (2.5% most transits, up to 10%, $10-14M for a $138M VLCC), S&P Global, Howden Re, WEF. **BRT-07/17 P&I start-gun: NOT fired.** *Soft-number caveat: exact 3-8% band is named-trade-press estimate; one early-July summary hints at slight easing (~2%/~8× baseline) for lower-nexus vessels — an easing at the margin, not a resumption.* [INSTITUTIONAL: Insurance Business Mag 2026-06-25/28; Lloyd's List]

**I-3 · OFAC / PGSA trap — STILL OPERATIVE / BLOCKING.** OFAC **FAQ 1249** (28 Apr 2026, expanded 29 May 2026): US persons are not authorized to make safe-passage payments to — or accept guarantees from — the Government of Iran or IRGC for Strait transit. A vessel paying such a toll **cannot be covered by any IG P&I club or war-risk underwriter**, and this held as of 7/1-4. The PGSA entity was SDN-designated 27 May 2026. **This leg blocks commercial cover structurally, independent of ceasefire status** — so even a JWC de-listing would not fully "reopen" cover while FAQ 1249 stands. [PRIMARY: OFAC FAQ 1249; corroborated Swedish Club, West P&I, Steamship Mutual, Baker McKenzie 2026-05-29]

**I-4 · Liner routings — NO RESUMPTION (Medium confidence).** Maersk's official Hormuz advisory ("Middle East Operational Update 37," last updated **6/26**) **predates the 6/27-28 re-escalation and carries no resumption/return-to-Gulf language**; the big-four remained on Cape-of-Good-Hope routing, posture stated as returning "only when security teams deem it 100% safe." **Caution:** two adjacent claims were **refuted 0-3** — (a) that Maersk/Hapag/CMA confirmed *successful* Gulf transits, and (b) that Maersk diverted specific ME11/MECL services — so carrier-specific routing detail is soft, and post-re-escalation posture is *inferred*, not directly confirmed. Net: no verified resumption; leg fails. [PRIMARY: Maersk advisory (chronology tops at Update 37); trade-press/trackers]

## Counter-Evidence

- **The strongest "it's reopening" case is the tape + throughput, and it is exactly the base-rate trap.** Brent at a fresh low and reportedly-high transits *feel* like reopening — but 1987-88 shows oil flows through a harassment campaign with flat price and no de-listing. The decoupling corroborates BRENT's structural read, not an institutional reopening. Grading the institutional legs on price would be the error the prompt explicitly warns against.
- **Against "fully stalled/reversed":** the P&I premium *did* briefly halve post-6/17 (real institutional easing before the re-escalation undid it), and one early-July note hints at marginal easing for lower-nexus vessels — so the institutional side is not frozen at maximum stress; it is elevated-and-re-elevated, which is a *stall/partial-reverse*, not a hard re-closure. And "substantial" JMIC / "dozens" of mines are mid-tier, not worst-case — the physical reversal is real but not maximal.
- **Against the base-rate read:** 1987-88 is not a clean analogue — no modern JWC/OFAC insurance architecture existed then, and today's *institutional* closure (JWC + FAQ 1249) can choke *insured/compliant* flow even when physical transit continues. So the institutional legs can stay closed and bite (financing/cover) even in a "flows-anyway" physical world — which is precisely why the scorecard grades institutions separately from throughput.
- **Where counter-evidence is genuinely thin:** the core verdict (JWC not de-listed, FAQ 1249 operative, no liner resumption, demining not begun) rests on primaries with no credible contradicting source — the reopening simply has not happened on paper.
