## Counter-Evidence and Comparison Limits
Insurance remains conditionally obtainable in the public contractual descriptions; an AP-area designation alone does not mean cover is wholly unavailable. The wide discount and affiliation differences also defeat a single “Red Sea rate.” [PRIMARY: S2–S3; NEWS: S6, S8]

No cross-theater ratio is constructed. The source dates, vessel populations and periods differ. No quote is represented as bound/paid. Freight dollars/day, additional cargo premium and dollars/barrel cannot be compared directly with hull AP percentages. Older figures in the owner ledgers remain historical, not silently refreshed or overwritten.

**A date trap was rejected:** Eastern Newsline labels a Yanbu/Bangladesh Shipping Corporation story October 18, but the same named quotation appears in the [original Daily Star article dated September 15](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/red-sea-tensions-raise-insurance-costs-4273801). The >1% ship-value quotation therefore does not establish October repricing. Its hull quote is also distinct from the article's cargo/reinsurance surcharges.
