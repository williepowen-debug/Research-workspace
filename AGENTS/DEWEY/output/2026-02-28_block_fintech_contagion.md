## 2. HISTORICAL PRECEDENT: FINTECH CONTAGION WAVES

### The 2022-2023 Wave — Best Historical Template

**Trigger sequence:**
1. **May 2022**: Klarna announced 10% cut (first major BNPL/fintech to move)
2. **Within weeks**: Affirm announced ~20% cut. Coinbase cut 18%. Robinhood cut 9%.
3. **By end of 2022-2023**: 100+ fintechs had cut staff

**Pattern**: Macro trigger (rate hikes, funding dry-up) drove simultaneous decisions, not true sequential contagion. The "copycat" lag was **days to weeks**, not months — because the underlying pressure (rising cost of capital, valuation compression) hit everyone at the same time.

### Key Distinction from 2026

The 2022-2023 wave was **macro-forced** (funding disappeared, multiples collapsed). The current Block announcement is **AI-efficiency-driven from a position of strength** (strong gross profit, accelerating margins, beat-and-raise quarter). This is categorically different.

**True contagion risk = normalization/permission-giving**: Dorsey explicitly said "your company is next." He's signaling that 40% cuts are now socially acceptable for profitable tech companies pursuing AI leverage. This is a **permission-giving event**, not a distress signal.

**Lag time estimate**: If contagion occurs, expect announcements within **30-90 days**, concentrated around next earnings cycle (May 2026). Companies that were already considering restructuring will now have political cover.

**Most vulnerable peers**: Mid-size fintechs with high support/ops headcount, low AI investment, and board pressure on margins. PayPal is already in restructuring mode. Affirm's ops/CS functions are the most exposed.

## 4. BLOCK REVENUE TRAJECTORY — THE "SALESFORCE PATTERN"?

### Revenue History

| Year | Revenue | YoY Growth |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 2021 | $17.66B | +86% |
| 2022 | $17.53B | -0.7% |
| 2023 | $21.92B | +25% |
| 2024 | $24.12B | +10% |
| 2025 | $24.19B | **+0.3%** ← near-flat |

**Revenue has essentially stalled.** The Bitcoin trading business is volatile and dominates topline; excluding it, underlying fintech revenue growth is also decelerating.

### Gross Profit (The Real Metric)

| Period | Gross Profit | Growth |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| FY 2025 (full year) | $10.36B | +17% YoY |
| Q4 2025 | $2.87B | **+24% YoY** (accelerating) |
| 2026 Guidance | $12.2B | ~+18% |

### Margin Trajectory

| Metric | 2025 | 2026 Guidance |
|--------|------|--------------|
| Adj Operating Income | $2.08B (20% margin) | $3.2B (26% margin) |
| Adj EBITDA | $3.47B (33% margin) | — |
| YoY margin expansion | +2 pts | +6 pts (expected) |

### Verdict: **YES, This Is the Salesforce Pattern**

Classic setup:
- **Topline revenue**: nearly flat (+0.3%) — decelerating from 86% → 25% → 10% → 0.3%
- **Gross profit**: +17-24% — growing faster as mix shifts
- **Operating margins**: expanding aggressively (+2 pts in 2025, +6 pts guided for 2026)
- **EPS guidance**: $3.66 vs $3.22 consensus — meaningful beat

The layoffs **accelerate** this dynamic by removing ~40% of fixed cost base. If AI absorbs the work, margins could expand dramatically beyond guidance. If AI doesn't deliver, you get a service quality crisis with a hollowed-out workforce.

**The Salesforce comparison is apt but with a caveat**: Salesforce's revenue was still growing (just slower) when they cut. Block's total revenue is essentially flat. That makes the gross profit story even more important — they're redefining themselves as a gross profit / fee-based business, not a transaction-volume business.

## SYNTHESIS: CONTAGION THESIS ASSESSMENT

### What's Real
1. **Block's move normalizes massive AI-driven cuts** — Dorsey explicitly invited peers to follow. This is the biggest contagion vector.
2. **PayPal is already restructuring** — most likely to announce a Block-scale cut next, potentially at next earnings
3. **Gemini's C-suite purge** signals crypto-adjacent fintech stress (separate from AI efficiency — this is distress)
4. **Remitly (110 jobs)** is noise-level, not a signal

### What's Not Real (Yet)
- No confirmed cuts at Affirm, Robinhood, Klarna, Nubank, Stripe
- Stripe is in aggressive growth mode ($159B valuation, exploring PayPal acquisition)
- Affirm's BNPL model is labor-light; exposure is lower

### Key Vectors to Watch
1. **PayPal earnings** (next cycle, ~May 2026) — management under pressure, McKinsey reportedly involved in restructuring
2. **Affirm** — if lending losses rise (Block's 108% increase is a warning), Affirm may face dual pressure: credit risk + board pressure to cut
3. **Mid-market fintech** — companies with 500-2,000 employees that over-hired in 2021-2022 now have Dorsey's letter as board justification

### Lag Time Estimate
- **High-conviction copycat**: 30-60 days (PayPal most likely)
- **Board-approved restructurings**: 60-90 days (May earnings cycle)
- **Sector-wide normalization**: 6-12 months

### Investment Implication
Block's own move reads as **bullish on XYZ** (margins, guidance, analyst upgrades) but **bearish on fintech labor/services** and potentially **neutral-to-bearish on sector peers** in the short term as they face pressure to prove similar efficiency. Stocks of peers who announce similar cuts will likely pop (market is rewarding the behavior). The risk is companies that **don't cut** — they may get re-rated as bloated.
