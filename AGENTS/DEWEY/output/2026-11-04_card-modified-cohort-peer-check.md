# Is the Capital One modified-card move shared? Peer check at JPMorgan, Bank of America and American Express

**Date:** 2026-11-04 | **Mode:** Thesis | **Commission:** Will's go in the DEWEY window, 2026-11-04 20:59 ET; follow-on to `output/2026-11-04_carl-dr1-cof-q3-fdm-three-issuer-final.md`. **Confidence:** High on disclosed amounts; Medium on comparability (issuers define these tables differently); cause of the Capital One move still UNKNOWN. Citi NOT READ.

## Key finding

**On the five other issuers read, Capital One's jump is not shared.** Its domestic modified-card cohort went from 17.2% to 25.2% delinquent between June 30 and September 30, 2026. The other five stayed within about one percentage point of their June share. So this evidence points to a Capital One-specific move, not a class-wide break in modified borrowers. **A separate, smaller point:** JPMorgan's modified group has grown 74.1% in a year with its delinquent share flat, and its filing does not say why. Citi has not filed, so "five of five" is not "all large issuers". [PRIMARY: 10-Q credit notes, listed under Reproduction.]

### JPMorgan's growth

The group rose from $1,642M to $2,859M in a year (+74.1%). Modifications rose 40.5% and defaults rose from $56M to $127M. Delinquent share fell slightly (12.4% to 11.6%), and the book rate is 1.97%. **The Apple Card purchase does not explain this:** it is a forward commitment with closing expected about 24 months after the January 2026 announcement, so those balances are not on the book. The filing states no reason. Whether this is deliberate outreach or rising need is UNKNOWN. [PRIMARY: JPMorgan Q3 10-Q, consumer credit note and MD&A.]

## Counter-evidence and limits

- **Against "Capital One only":** Citi is unread. Synchrony's share did rise 0.8 points, and flows rose at every issuer but one on the quarter (Bread rose 1.1% and fell on the year), so a mild common rise in need is possible.
- **Against "real borrower stress at Capital One":** one quarter, cause unknown, and an internal reporting or program change would produce the same picture. The filing states none, which is not proof there was none.
- **Comparability:** Bank of America pools card with other consumer loans. JPMorgan's first bucket includes under-30-days past due. Issuers differ on whether balances are period-end or at modification, and on which exits leave the table. These issuers are not pooled here.
- **Not a measure of hidden delinquency.** The class-wide causal test stays NOT COMPUTABLE and is CARL's.
