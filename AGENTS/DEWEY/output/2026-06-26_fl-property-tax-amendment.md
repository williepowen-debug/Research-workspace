## LEAD ANSWER

**What's on the ballot:** **Amendment 3 / HJR 1F** ("Save Our Homes from Excessive Property Taxes") — a **legislatively-referred** constitutional amendment (companion implementing bill SB 4F) that **already passed both chambers June 2, 2026** (House 75-26, Senate 30-9) in a DeSantis-called June 1-3 special session and was **filed with the Secretary of State June 16, 2026**. It is **on the ballot, not pending in the legislature, and not a citizen initiative.** It is a **multi-pronged bundle, not a single homestead change.**

**P(pass):** Requires a **60% voter supermajority** (FL Const. Art. XI §5). The most recent poll surfaced — **Sachs Media, 850 FL voters, June 22-24 2026 — puts support at 64% (±3.8pp MOE)**: above the 60% bar but **within the margin of error of failing** (64−3.8 = 60.2). Combined with a legislative supermajority, DeSantis backing, a popular homeowner tax cut, and no organized statewide opposition surfaced → **odds are elevated but genuinely tight** — a "likely-but-not-safe" pass that a normal polling drift or the pending ballot-language lawsuit could push under 60%.

**Net directional read on the FL thesis (medium confidence):** genuinely two-sided, but **the housing tailwind is structurally MUTED** and **the larger identifiable tail is muni/county/special-district fiscal stress** in non-school-dependent governments — *not* a residential demand surge. Closer to **neutral-to-modestly-two-sided** than a clean FL-housing bull catalyst.

## FINDINGS (verified)

### 1. EXACT MEASURE — Amendment 3 / HJR 1F, certified, NOT pending *(high, 3-0)*
HJR 1F "Save Our Homes from Excessive Property Taxes," companion SB 4F. Passed House 75-26 and Senate 30-9 on June 2 2026; "Signed by Officers and filed with Secretary of State" June 16 2026. HJR = legislatively-referred by definition; SoS filing places it on the Nov 3 ballot. "Amendment 3" designation already used by Ballotpedia/CBS (formal SoS number finalized later). Effective Jan 1 2027 if ratified.
- **Primary:** flhouse.gov BillId=84452; flsenate.gov 2026F/1F. **Corrob:** Ballotpedia, Tax Foundation, CBS Miami, GrayRobinson, Florida Policy Institute, Barnes Walker.

### 2. STRUCTURE — four prongs, not a single homestead change *(high, 3-0)*
(a) **Non-school homestead exemption** $50K → **$150K (2027)** → **$250K (2028)**, inflation-indexed from 2029; **school-district levies CARVED OUT** (existing $25K school exemption retained). (b) **Non-homestead assessment-growth cap cut 10% → 5%** effective Jan 1 2027 — applies to **rentals, vacation homes, apartments, commercial/industrial/office** (a constitutional provision, Art VII §4, so it must ride in the amendment). (c) **New residents** establishing FL residency after Dec 31 2026 limited to a **$50K exemption for their first 5 years** — explicitly "designed to give existing Floridians the benefit without fueling a fresh migration wave" (Florida Phoenix). (d) **Local-government spending restrictions** + a legislative pathway toward fuller elimination.
- **Key:** CORAL's ~$250K assumption is correctly the **2028 step, not the 2027 day-one $150K level**. DeSantis: a $250K exemption fully eliminates non-school property tax on **~60% of FL homes**.
- **Primary + 6 corroborating:** flhouse.gov, Tax Foundation, FL Senate press release, CBS, JMCO, Barnes Walker, GrayRobinson.

### 3. NOT A FULL REPEAL — the extreme scenario is off the table for Nov 2026 *(high, 3-0)*
The aggressive full phase-out of ALL non-school homestead property taxes was **HJR 203** (House 80-30, Feb 19 2026, sponsor Rep. Monique Miller R-Palm Bay) — it **DIED in the Senate** (~Mar 13 2026) and is listed "Not on the ballot." This rules out the most-bearish-for-muni / most-bullish-for-housing scenario. *(Verifier REFUTED 1-2 the spin that this signals "multiple live proposals" — HJR 203 is dead; treat Amendment 3 as the single certified item.)* Caveat: Amendment 3 still zeroes non-school tax for ~60% of homesteaders + cuts the non-homestead cap + directs a full-elimination schedule, so the gap vs full repeal is **magnitude (60% vs 100%), not category.**
- Ballotpedia; Florida Phoenix.

### 4. BALLOT COMPOSITION — 3 statewide measures, 2 touch property tax *(high, 3-0)*
As of June 26 2026, exactly three certified: (1) **Amendment 3** (homestead/assessment/spending — the major one); (2) an **agricultural/agritourism tangible-personal-property exemption** (also property-tax); (3) a **Budget Stabilization Fund cap 10%→25%** (HJR 5019). A citizen Initiative-Process amendment may appear but is NOT certified.
- Ballotpedia (verbatim "As of June 26, 2026, three statewide ballot measures were certified").

### 5. FISCAL HIT — large, NON-school, credit-negative *(high, 3-0)*
Non-school local-government revenue loss: **$4.6B FY2027-28** ($150K year) → **~$8.4B FY2028-29** ($250K year), per FL legislative staff / Office of Economic & Demographic Research. The separate **Revenue Estimating Conference consensus runs HIGHER** — ~$5B yr1 → ~$8.8B yr2 → ~$9.7B → ~$10.75B → **~$12B recurring by FY2031-32** (inflation indexing), and that **$12B EXCLUDES the cost of the full-elimination pathway.**
- **CORAL's ~$8.4B is VERIFIED as the FY2028-29 number** (not year one, not terminal).
- **Scope note (load-bearing for CORAL):** ALL loss is **non-school** local revenue — **school districts are shielded** by the carve-out.
- **GrayRobinson (Jun 5):** "without this fund, many counties will likely become insolvent under the proposal" — the **revenue-protection trust fund was STRIPPED** from the final bill, making the insolvency risk genuinely live.
- **S&P Global Ratings (Jun 12):** FL local-government bond issuers face "greater credit pressure"; revenue/spending changes "material."
- Florida Policy Institute (relaying primary staff analysis), Tax Foundation, Barnes Walker, CBS Miami, GrayRobinson, Insurance Journal/S&P, Bond Buyer.

### 6. PASSAGE MECHANICS — 60% bar; poll at 64% within MOE *(facts high 3-0; odds = inference)*
60% supermajority required. Legislature cleared it House 75-26 (62.5%) / Senate 30-9 (75%) — ~98% R / ~11% D, **3 Senate Democrats crossed** (Bernard, Rouson, Sharief), 2 Republicans defected (Boyles, Maney). **DeSantis backs it** (while criticizing the trimmed version for excluding school levies + dropping his local-government grant fund).
- **⚠️ ROUTER CORRECTION (see Process Note):** the synthesis summary states "NO voter polling surfaced / odds cannot be priced from polls." **That is wrong** — the search layer surfaced and fetched a **Sachs Media poll (850 voters, June 22-24 2026, 64% support, ±3.8pp)** that was dropped before the verified-claim set. **64% is above the 60% bar but within MOE of failing.** P(pass) is therefore **polling-supported but tight**, not unpriced.
- Note: "veto-proof margin" is a misnomer — a FL constitutional joint resolution isn't subject to gubernatorial veto at all; and legislative margin only weakly predicts clearing the *voter* 60% bar.

### 7. TWO-SIDED TRANSMISSION *(medium — synthesis layer)*
- **HOUSING — bullish channel STRUCTURALLY MUTED.** Lower carrying cost for existing owner-occupants (exemption to $250K on non-school levies), BUT: (i) the **5-year residency gate is explicitly designed to suppress an in-migration demand surge**; (ii) **school levies** (typically the largest bill component) and **FL's dominant insurance costs** are untouched; (iii) relief **phases in over 2027-28**. Tax Foundation's caution: savings tend to get **capitalized into prices** rather than improving affordability.
- **MUNI/FISCAL — bearish.** $4.6B→$8.4B (→~$12B by FY32) non-school local revenue loss + stripped revenue-protection fund + spending restrictions + an explicit county-insolvency flag + S&P credit-pressure warning. **School districts shielded.**
- **BANKS — mixed.** The 10%→5% non-homestead cap is a **distinct CRE/multifamily/rental carrying-cost easing** (modest support for FL commercial/rental cashflow & values), set against credit pressure on bank-held **FL muni paper** and **smaller property-tax escrow balances**.

**Net:** closer to neutral-to-modestly-two-sided than a clean housing tailwind; the larger identifiable tail = **muni/county/special-district fiscal stress in non-school governments.**

## COUNTER-EVIDENCE (the single datapoint that moves each side)

- **→ muni time-bomb:** a specific FL **county/special-district downgrade or disclosed budget gap** tied to the amendment, OR confirmation the legislature will **NOT backfill** the lost revenue (the insolvency flag is conditional on no backfill).
