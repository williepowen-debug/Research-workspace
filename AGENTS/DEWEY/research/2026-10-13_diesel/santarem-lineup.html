Belem Port – Belém – Para – Brazil

Belem is a city located at Para State, at the board of Guajara bay, a main public port of cargo and passenger movement. The port is managed by “CDP – Companhia Docas do Pará”.

Location

Latitude: 01° 26’ 45.6 S

Longitude: 48 ° 29’ 52.2 W

 

Information & Restrictions

Maximum Arrival draft: 7.00 meters FW (depending on the height of the tide of the day)

Maximum Sailing draft: 7.30 meters.(depending on the height of the tide at of the day)

Maximum air draft: N/A.

Maximum LOA: 180.00 meters.

Maximum beam: N/A.

 

Warehouse 05, 06 and 07 

Operation: Loading and discharge general cargo

Warehouse 08

Not available

Warehouse 09 and 10

Operation: For local boats and passengers movements

Warehouse 11 to 12 (Ocrim) 

Operation:  Loading and discharge general cargo/ Discharge wheat in bulk.

Method of discharge: One suction pipe running over trails, but vessel must shift (using crew and ropes) along the wharf in order to the unloader to reach all holds.

Discharging rate: 1.000 – 1.200 MT/day agw wp

Not discharging under rain.

Port Information / Facilities

Water Density: 0.996 FW.

Working times: 24 hours daily SHINC.

Towage assistance: Compulsory for vessel with loa smaller or equal to 160 m and no bow thruster available/operant .

Bunker: Available at berth and anchorage.

Shore Crane / Grabs: N/A.

Fresh water supply: Available, by hydrant.

Garbage and Sludge removal: Available.

Weather seasons: Rainy season usual from December up to April.

Berthing/Unberthing Maneuvers: During day and night time. The berthing maneuvers must be by starboard side only.

Local Time: GMT – (minus) 03 hours.

Pilotage: Pilotage from Mosqueiro Pilot Station (Lat 01° 06’00 S – Long 048° 29’50 W) to Belem port is compulsory.

Access channel: Quiriri channel, the self-pilotage navigation till Mosqueiro is allowed with max draft 13.50 mts FW. For 13.50 mts FW draft the pilots are compulsory. The navigation is safe day and night time.

Additional Information:  Favorable for crew change, spare parts receiving. The nearest airport is Belem city airport.

PORT OF OUTEIRO – Belem – Para – Brazil 

The port of Outeiro is located at Caratateua Island (Outeiro) in the right bank of the Guajara Bay at 38km from Belem  port. The terminal is managed by CDP – “Companhia Docas do Pará”. 

Location 

Latitude: 01° 16’ 40.8” S 

Longitude: 48 ° 28’ 39.0” W 

Information & Restrictions Maximum Arrival/Sailing draft: 10.60 meters.

Berth 101 

Maximum LOA: 200.00 meters. 

Minimum depth alongside: 11.80 meters. Maximum air draft: N/A. 

Maximum beam: N/A. 

Maximum DWT: 40,000 mt. 

Operations: General cargo. 

Berth 102 

Maximum LOA: 160.00 meters. 

Minimum depth alongside: 6,80 meters. Maximum air draft: N/A. 

Maximum beam: N/A. 

Maximum DWT: 20,000 mt. 

Operations: General cargo. 

Port Information / Facilities 

Water Density: 0.997 FW. 

Working times: 24 hours daily SHINC. 

Towage assistance: Not Available. 

Bunker: Available at berth and anchorage. 

Port Crane / Grabs: N/A. 

Warehouse: 07 warehouses available with 2,257m² each. Storage areas: 04 areas with 10,200m² each and 02 areas  with 9,800 m² each. 

Fresh water supply: Available by hydrant. 

Garbage and Sludge removal: Available. 

Weather seasons: Rainy season usual from December up to April. 

Berthing / Unberthing Maneuvers: During day and night  time. The berthing maneuvers must to be by starboard side  only. 

Local Time: GMT – (minus) 03hours. 

Pilotage: Pilotage from Mosqueiro Pilot Station (Lat 01° 06’00  S – Long 048° 29’50 W) to Outeiro is compulsory. Access channel: Quiriri channel, the self-pilotage navigation  till Mosqueiro is allowed with max draft 12.20 mts FW. For  13.00 mts FW draft the pilots are compulsory. The navigation  is safe day and night time. 

Additional Information: Favorable for crew change, spare  parts receiving. The nearest airport is Belem city airport.
