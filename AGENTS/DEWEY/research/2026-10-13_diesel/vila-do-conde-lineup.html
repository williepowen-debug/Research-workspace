consists of 08 berths, easy accessed through Pará River and road.

Pilotage

Designated positions to drop anchor at Mosqueiro Pilot Station (Chapeu Virado Lighthouse):

LAT 01 05,0 S  / LONG 048 28,5 W

LAT 01 06,5 S  / LONG 048 28.5 W

LAT 00 24,5 S  / LONG 047 46,0 W

LAT 01 06.5 S  / LONG 048 30.5 W

LAT 01 05.0 S  / LONG 048 30.5 W

The Pilot Stations are located at Espadarte and Mosqueiro. From Espadarte to Mosqueiro there are 02 access channels, Quiriri and Taipú.

At Quiriri Channel, Pilotage is not compulsory for vessels with draft up to 12.20 meters FW carrying nondangerous cargo. So self-Pilotage via Quiriri channel up to Mosqueiro P/S (inner P/S) are allowed up to 12.20 meters FW. However, for draft over 12,20 up to 13,80 Meters, Pilot must be hired since Espadarte (outer P/S) and additional costs involved. Teste for vessels with draft over 13,50 up to 13,80 Meters.

At Taipú Channel, the employment of pilot is always compulsory. Max allowed draft 11,50 Meters FW. Highlight that at this channel the Self-Pilotage is not allowed.

At Espadarte Pilot Station (Outer P/S) (it takes about 9 / 10 hours to reach Vila Do Conde anchorage, considering vessel’s speed 15 knots, under normal conditions) or at Mosqueiro Pilot Station (it takes about 3 / 4 hours to reach Vila Do Conde Anchorage, considering vessel’s speed 15 knots, under normal conditions).

–  (Outer PS) Espadarte PS [LAT 00°22’0”S /LONG 047°49’0”W (NORTH POINT) or LAT 00°24’5”S /LONG 047°46’0”W (EAST POINT)] – Pilotage compulsory to vessels carrying dangerous cargoes  as caustic soda, gasoline, gasoil, liquid gas, etc. For general cargoes vessels, this pilotage can be used as an alternative channel to reach Mosqueiro Pilot Station. The navigation from Espadarte P/S as far as Mosqueiro P/S should be done only with pilot on board, through Taipu channel. Self pilotage (without pilot) is not allowed through Taipu Channel. The port captaincy is able to follow up the navigation by AIS system and fines shall be applied to Master / owners in case of non compliance of above. As per Brazilian regulation, After the vessel takes pilot at Espadarte P/S, a compulsory change of pilots is carried out at Mosqueiro P/S to continue navigation to Vila do Conde. This move is mandatory. Steaming time from Espadarte P/S up to Mosqueiro P/S of about 6 hours + 2 hours to Vila do Conde.

In Case of No Free Berth – Vila Do Conde anchorage is the position where vessels drop anchor and get in port LineUp where first arrived at Vila Do Conde is the first vessel to come alongside. Vila Do Conde Anchorage where NOR  is usually tendered by ship and accepted by terminal, of course depending on contract and Charter Party involved.

Tugboats : The use of tugboats is mandatory (Compulsory).

Launch Boat: For berthing Maneuver, the use of launch boat is mandatory (Compulsory)

Line Man: The use is mandatory and compulsory for berthing and berthing maneuver

Water and Fuel supply (Bunkering) :

Fresh Water Supply and fuel oil or lubricants are allowed during port stay.  Bunkers are supplied by barge (Via Transpetro Co.,), Brokers Petrobras, but it should not interfere on port operations. Also, there is a option to suply bunker at VDC Acnhorage area too. However, in these two situation it is compulsory to use protection buoys retenction arround the vessel.

Deballasting Operation:

Deballasting is allowed inside the port as long as vessel complies with Brazilian Harbor Master rules in force for ballast exchange.

Vessels must conduct 01 exchange, as per following procedures:

  • BW exchange must be conducted 200 nm offshore and 200 meters depth and or in the mind of ocean;
  • It has to be reported to the Harbor Master 24 hours before arrival at 200 nm and or in the mind of ocean as
  • above mentioned.
  • If vessel is proceeding from another Brazilian port, exchange can be conducted on a highest distance and depth from the coast, being not necessary to deviate the original route.
  • Vessels are allowed to keep seawater inside the ballast tanks, as long as it remains on board, as deballasting in the river is forbidden. It also has to be informed in the Ballast Water Form.
  • All the forms must be presented to the agent as soon as the exchange is completed, once vessels are subjected
