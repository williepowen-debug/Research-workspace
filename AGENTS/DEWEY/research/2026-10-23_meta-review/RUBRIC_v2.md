# Rubric v2 — code each report from its FULL text (not just the opening summary)

For each report answer three things.

A. HYPOTHESIS axis — was the commission's working hypothesis supported? First write the hypothesis in one line, as the report states it (or as its question implies).
   SUPPORTED · REFUTED · PARTIAL (one material part each way) · UNRESOLVED (deciding fact not available) · NO_HYPOTHESIS (catalog / document pull / structure map / design)

B. RISK axis — compared with what the commission assumed going in, does the report's conclusion imply the risk the network is watching is
   HIGHER · LOWER · MIXED · UNCHANGED · NA
   (Note: refuting a reassuring hypothesis means risk HIGHER; supporting a reassuring hypothesis means risk LOWER.)

C. v1 outcome, for comparability with an earlier coding: CONFIRMED (concern held/worsened) · DEFLATED (concern smaller/narrower/slower/refuted) · MIXED · NULL · DESCRIPTIVE

D. HEADLINE FIDELITY — does the report's opening Key Finding / bottom-line section fairly represent the body? FAITHFUL · OMITS_MATERIAL_CAVEAT · OVERSTATES · UNDERSTATES. If not FAITHFUL, quote the body caveat (≤30 words).

E. Confidence in your coding: high/low, and a ≤25-word reason.
