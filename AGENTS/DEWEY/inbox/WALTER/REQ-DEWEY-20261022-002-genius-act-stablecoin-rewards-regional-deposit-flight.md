# DEEP-RESEARCH REQUEST — GENIUS Act stablecoin rewards and regional-bank deposit flight

**Will's view (10/22):** the GENIUS Act "will ALSO effect banks and their deposits". **The joint hypothesis to test with REQ-DEWEY-20261022-001:** regional banks' advantage is cheap, sticky deposits. Yield-paying stablecoins give money a new place to go, and AI agents make moving it effortless. Together they could raise regional deposit costs or speed outflows, which is a funding channel separate from credit and from cyber.

**Decision question:** Under the final GENIUS Act rules, can stablecoins (through issuers, exchanges or affiliates) pay holders something that competes with bank deposit rates? How large is the plausible deposit migration, which banks are most exposed (small and regional vs large; high vs low noninterest-bearing share), and what early data would show it happening?

## Scope (in bounds)

1. **The rule itself, primary first.** What the OCC final rule (10/21/2026) and the statute (signed July 2025) say on rewards, interest and yield: the issuer ban vs exchange and affiliate "rewards"; reserve-asset requirements (T-bills, deposits at banks); which banks can issue or custody; and the effective date. Note what other agencies (Fed, FDIC, Treasury, state regimes) still have to finalize. Quote the exact rule text for the rewards provision.
2. **Size estimates, all sides.** Published estimates of deposit outflow to stablecoins (Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee, bank trade groups such as the ABA and BPI, the Fed, rating agencies, sell-side), each with its assumptions. Keep the bank-lobby high-end and the crypto-industry low-end figures separate; never average them.
3. **Who is exposed.** Deposit mix by tier (noninterest-bearing share, retail vs commercial, uninsured share) for the fleet's names (WAL, ZION, OZK, FLG, HBAN, VLY, SSB, EGBN) and KRE's largest holdings, from call reports or 10-Qs. Do small banks lose more because their depositors are rate-sensitive, or less because their customers are older and less crypto-active? Find evidence either way.
4. **Reserve recycling.** If stablecoin reserves sit as deposits at large custodian banks or in T-bills, deposits may move from regionals to big banks or out of the banking system. Map where reserves of the largest dollar stablecoins are actually held.
5. **The AI-agent link.** Any evidence or credible commentary on AI agents or "agentic payments" routing idle cash to the highest yield, including stablecoins. Name who is building it.
6. **Early-warning data.** Which public series would show migration first: stablecoin market cap, weekly H.8 deposit data by bank size, deposit-cost prints, money-fund flows? Give the release cadence for each.
7. **Counter-evidence, required.** Analyses finding limited deposit impact (e.g. stablecoins substituting for cash or offshore dollars rather than US deposits), and the 2023 lesson that deposit flight came from uninsured commercial deposits, not retail.

Stock picks or position sizing (TERRY), grading REGINALD's thesis, crypto price calls.
