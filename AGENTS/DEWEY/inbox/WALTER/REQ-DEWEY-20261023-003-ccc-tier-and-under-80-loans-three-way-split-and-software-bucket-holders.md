# DEEP-RESEARCH REQUEST — What is inside the stress? The CCC tier and the under-80 loan bucket, split three ways, and who holds the software bucket

**Why now:** REQ-002 found the HY widening mostly broad, with the AI-builder slice (CoreWeave, SoftBank, compute SPVs; ~3% of HYG) at ~10–15% of October. Its one open question can flip that: CCC widened +161bp in October (1,179 → 1,340, widest since July 2020) while BB moved +16 and B +30, and the CCC tier's composition is not public. Separately, JPM via Bloomberg (10/6) puts tech at 39% ($54.4B) of leveraged loans priced below 80, driven by software and AI-disruption fear, not capex borrowers. If "AI victims" (software with PE debt) dominate the distressed tier, AI broadly defined is a much larger share of the stress than REQ-002's builder-only estimate.

**Decision question:** of the CCC tier's October widening and of the under-80 loan bucket, how much is (i) AI builders, (ii) software / AI-disruption names, (iii) everything else, and which listed vehicles carry the most of bucket (ii)?

**What changes with the answer:** LIQUID/REGINALD read a REG-T-04 fire as a cycle signal or a sector one; BROCK learns whether the private-credit channel is where the stress sits; VULCAN separates builder fragility from disruption; TERRY (if Will commissions WQ-509) chooses between a broad HY expression and a BDC expression.

## Scope

1. **Three-way cut, two pools.** (a) ICE CCC & lower: best public evidence of composition (ICE/Fidelity sector tables at the tier level if published; HYG/JNK holdings filtered to CCC-rated lines, with weights and October yield change by bucket, using LIQUID's saved holdings files where they exist). (b) Leveraged loans below 80: the JPM 39% figure at its closest primary, plus any LSTA/PitchBook/Morningstar breakdown. Buckets: AI builders · software / AI-disruption · everything else. State the classification rule; carry REQ-002's caveat that ICE "Technology" ≠ AI.
2. **Who holds bucket (ii).** For listed BDCs (BIZD constituents: ARCC, FSK, OBDC, BXSL, MAIN and peers): software share of portfolio at fair value, from each BDC's latest 10-Q/10-K or investor presentation, with the as-of date and their own industry labels. For HYG and JNK: software-classified weight and its CCC subset, as-of date stated.
3. **Default and distress context.** Latest ICE/agency default rate and distressed ratio by sector if published; October issuance split if reachable.
4. **Counter-evidence, required.** Evidence that the CCC move is broad within the tier (e.g. telecom/cable, which led September) rather than software/AI.
