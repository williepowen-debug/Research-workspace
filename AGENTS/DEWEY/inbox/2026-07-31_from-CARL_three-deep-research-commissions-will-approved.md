## CARL-DR-2 — WHERE DID THE CHARGED-OFF BORROWERS GO · consumers: CARL action; RED/REGINALD info

**Decision question:** Is the exited/charged-off consumer cohort of 2024-26 **measurably deteriorating outside the credit system**, or being **reabsorbed** into it?

**Why now:** CARL's masking framework claims issuer books (SYF/COF/ALLY) print clean because the worst borrowers already exited — but nobody observes the exited population, which makes the claim quasi-unfalsifiable. The Q2 cluster resolved 0-of-4 against the framework; this is the direct test that doesn't route through issuer survivor pools at all.

**Scope:**
- **Debt-buyer market as the price signal:** Encore (ECPG) + PRA Group 10-Qs — charged-off paper supply volumes, purchase-price multiples, management supply commentary. Rising supply at falling prices = deterioration; tight supply at firm prices = the exit wave already cleared.
- **Collections performance:** liquidation/recovery-rate trends on recent vintages.
- **Credit-invisible / unscorable population:** FICO + CFPB research series — is the excluded population growing?
- **Reabsorption channel:** subprime origination approval rates and volumes (bureau publications, auto: Dealertrack/Experian AFS), secured-card and subprime-rebuild product growth, deep-subprime auto share.
- **Terminal outcomes:** Chapter 7 trajectory (Epiq), and any measurable growth in the informal/alternative-finance layer (pawn, title — public-company KPIs: FCFS, EZPW).

**Pre-registered two-way read (report whichever the data supports):** (a) rising charged-off supply + weakening recovery + growing credit-invisibles + rising Ch-7 = **exit-and-deteriorate → masking confirmed independent of issuer data**; (b) stable pricing + recovering subprime approval + reabsorption = **churn-and-recover → CONTAINMENT evidence, route the report to RED as counter-thesis primary**.
