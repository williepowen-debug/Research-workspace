## Scoring Guide

**Signal Quality:**
- **Very High:** Almost everything relevant, low noise
- **High:** Good signal, some filtering needed
- **Medium:** Worth checking, significant filtering needed
- **Low:** Occasional gems, mostly noise

**Check Frequency:**
- **On release:** Monitor for updates, check when new
- **2x/week:** Monday + Thursday
- **Weekly:** Monday scan
- **Monthly:** First Monday of month
