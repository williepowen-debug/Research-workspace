# EARNINGS Expected Signals

## PRE-DOCUMENTED SIGNAL TYPES

These signals are expected based on domain analysis. When observed, log to ML.tsv and assess for escalation.

---

### SIGNAL: Earnings Season Deterioration
**Trigger:** S&P 500 beat rate falls below 65%
**Interpretation:** Estimates too high, fundamental weakness
**Action:** ELEVATED to HENRY
**Historical Precedent:** Q1 2020, Q3 2022

### SIGNAL: Guidance Capitulation
**Trigger:** >35% of companies cut/withdraw guidance in single quarter
**Interpretation:** Visibility collapse, recession risk
**Action:** URGENT to ALL
**Historical Precedent:** Q1 2020 COVID

### SIGNAL: Mag 7 Miss
**Trigger:** 3+ of Mag 7 miss on EPS or revenue
**Interpretation:** Leadership failing, index risk
**Action:** URGENT to HENRY, CARL
**Historical Precedent:** Q4 2022 tech reckoning

### SIGNAL: Consumer Sector Warning
**Trigger:** WMT, TGT, or major consumer discretionary guides down
**Interpretation:** Consumer spending inflection
**Action:** URGENT to CARL
**Historical Precedent:** May 2022 retail warnings

### SIGNAL: Bank Provision Spike
**Trigger:** Major bank provision increases >30% QoQ
**Interpretation:** Credit deterioration expected
**Action:** URGENT to REGINALD
**Historical Precedent:** Q1 2020, pre-GFC

### SIGNAL: Margin Compression Wave
**Trigger:** >40% of S&P report declining margins YoY
**Interpretation:** Pricing power loss, cost pressure
**Action:** ELEVATED to CARL, HENRY
**Historical Precedent:** 2022 inflation squeeze

### SIGNAL: Revision Breadth Collapse
**Trigger:** Earnings revision ratio falls below 0.7 (downs > ups)
**Interpretation:** Fundamental deterioration broadening
**Action:** ELEVATED to HENRY
**Historical Precedent:** Pre-recession pattern

### SIGNAL: Capex Guidance Cuts
**Trigger:** Multiple large caps cut capex guidance
**Interpretation:** Demand outlook weakening
**Action:** ELEVATED to ALL
**Historical Precedent:** 2019 industrial slowdown
