### Bear: "Fed can't save everyone"

**Rebuttal:**  
"Fed's batting average when systemic risk emerges: 3 for 3 (2008, 2020, 2023). The question isn't 'Can they?' but 'When will they?' And the answer is: **before catastrophic decline.** BTFP 2.0 announcement in April/May kills June puts."
