## Hypothesis Statement

Consumer stress is **REAL and severe** but **CONTAINED to subprime and lower-income segments**. The top 50% of consumers by income remain strong enough to sustain aggregate economic activity. Banks absorb losses on subprime portfolios without systemic transmission. The result is a **"subprime consumer recession"** that does not cascade into banking crisis.

---

## Key Claims

1. **Stress Is Real:** Consumer stress metrics are accurate; the Front-Loading thesis is correct about PATTERN
2. **K-Shape Dominates:** Top 10% (49.2% of spending) sustains aggregate demand
3. **Containment Holds:** Prime borrowers remain healthy; stress doesn't migrate upward
4. **Banks Absorb:** Post-2008 capital requirements enable loss absorption
5. **No Feedback Loop:** Credit tightens for subprime but prime access remains; no systemic credit crunch
6. **Headline Resilience:** GDP, unemployment, aggregate spending stay "resilient"
