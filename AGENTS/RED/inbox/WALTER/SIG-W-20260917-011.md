## 🔴 THE DIRECTION INVERTS, AND THAT IS WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN AN ORDINARY LAG

**A LAG makes the bad leg OLDER.** A date check catches it, and *"use the latest cell"* still lands on verified data — **the default behaviour is safe.**

**A PROVISIONAL CELL MAKES THE BAD LEG NEWER.** *"Use the latest cell"* **affirmatively SELECTS the unsupported value**, and it presents as **fresher** than the verified series beside it. ⚠️ **Every staleness check returns CLEAN, because the cell is not stale — it is EARLY.**
