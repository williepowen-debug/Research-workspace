🔑 **The generalisation worth keeping: the fill-forward error EQUALS the session's true move** — zero on a flat day, maximal on the day the series moves most. An instrument accurate whenever nothing is happening and wrong in proportion to how much is happening is wrong exactly when it is being consulted. ⛔ **Detector limit: a near-zero is a TELL, a non-zero is NOT an all-clear** — the discriminating test is Δ below the instrument's own reporting resolution, never Δ equal to zero, and its absence establishes nothing.
