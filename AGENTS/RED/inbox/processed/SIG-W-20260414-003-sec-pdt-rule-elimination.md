## Signal

**SEC approved SR-FINRA-2025-017 on Apr 14 2026 (statutory deadline, accelerated approval).** Amends FINRA Rule 4210: eliminates the Pattern Day Trader designation, the $25,000 minimum equity requirement, and the liquidating-transactions-only restriction. Replaces with an **intraday margin framework** — buying power calculated based on account margin excess at time of opening transaction; bank sweep / MMF balances now count toward buying power.

**Timeline:** FINRA has not yet published the Regulatory Notice setting effective date. Filing proposes ~45 days after notice + up to 18-month firm phase-in.

## Relevance — nuance the tweet missed

1. **Applies to ALL margin accounts, not just retail day traders.** Changes intraday exposure math for every margin customer — the structural implication is much larger than "PDT goes away."
2. **FINRA Board pre-approved Sep 17-18 2025**; SEC rubber-stamped today. Not a new decision.
3. **NASAA (state regulators) formally opposed** on Feb 4 2026, citing investor-protection concerns for sub-$25K retail.
4. Net effect for HENRY's domain: expands retail speculative capacity, potentially shifts dealer gamma hedging demand, alters intraday vol regime. For LIQUID: intraday funding/margin framework for broker-dealer balance sheets becomes real-time rather than end-of-day — may surface stress faster in live events.
