## 2. The one thing that matters for an adjudication

**The realization layer and the count layer are saying different things, and this is the first month they clearly diverge.**

- **The count went negative** with a large downward revision behind it.
- **The realization layer did not move at all.** Claims 199K. Continuing claims 1,801K. JOLTS layoffs & discharges **1.1%**, flat and inside its 1.0–1.2% range all year. **Nobody is being fired**; payroll growth stopped because **hiring stopped**, and because government education and retail shed positions.

**Why that matters to a credit spread specifically:** a labor market that stops *hiring* and a labor market that starts *firing* have very different credit transmission. Separations → severance → UI → delinquency is the channel that reprices HY. **That channel has not opened.** The negative headline came through the hiring side and the public-sector side.

**So the honest statement is: today's print is a genuine deterioration in the payroll count and is NOT, on its own, evidence that the credit-relevant labor channel has turned.** I am not telling you it argues for or against tightening through 280 — **that is your adjudication and I am staying out of it.** I am telling you which layer moved, because "labor turned negative today" will travel across the fleet as one undifferentiated headline and it should not.
