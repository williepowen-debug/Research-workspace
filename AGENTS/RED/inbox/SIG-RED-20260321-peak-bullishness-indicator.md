# Signal: Peak Analyst Bullishness — Contrarian Indicator
**Source:** Albert Edwards (@albertedwards99) citing FactSet/Daily Chartbook, Mar 20, 2026
**Priority:** MEDIUM

## Key Facts
- 58.2% of S&P 500 analyst ratings are "BUY" — highest since at least 2010 (FactSet data, 12,733 ratings)
- S&P has just fallen below its 200 DMA
- Edwards: "if the S&P now go on to see a pronounced bear market, we will look back at charts like these as an indication of peak bullishness"

## Relevance
- Classic contrarian setup: maximum consensus optimism at a technical breakdown
- Supports thesis that market is pricing in "soft landing" while structural risks (credit, energy, rates) accelerate
- Useful for RED team calibration: sellside still overwhelmingly bullish = repricing has further to run
