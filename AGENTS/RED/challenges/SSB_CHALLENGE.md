- Sponsors have **nearly half their capital** at stake

**Firewall #3: 99% Payment Current**
- Despite 9.36% rated substandard, **NPLs are 0.02%**
- Sponsors ARE paying, even when DSCR <1.0x
- "Support and Survive" is WORKING so far

**What This Means:**
The 9.36% substandard figure is scary in isolation, but the actual *loss* to SSB requires:
1. Sponsor liquidity exhaustion (not yet visible)
2. Property value collapse >48% (historically rare for MF)
3. Migration from substandard → NPL → REO → charge-off (multi-quarter lag)

**Counter-Strength:** **STRONG**  
The path from 9.36% substandard to actual P&L damage is longer and more attenuated than the thesis implies. June 2026 may capture rising NPLs, but not realized losses.
