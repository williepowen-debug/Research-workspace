# Proposed confirmation policy — for Will after the batch review

**Status:** proposal only. Every answer still requires matching chat confirmation in the live app and this candidate. Will approved implementation of points 1–3 and one review; point 4 was reserved for his separate decision. This is not a new ruling or an OPEN queue entry. PROME owns registering/presenting that decision.

**Recommendation:** consider a narrow app-only class after the replacement passes review. Do not use a count of clean decisions or a week elapsed as automatic authority. The choice accepts a local-computer trust limit; it does not establish stronger identity.

| Choice | What an explicit app Send means | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Keep confirmation for every answer | A delivered answer remains a proposal until Will confirms the exact answer in PROME's chat. | Existing trust rule and duplicate step remain. |
| Permit explicitly eligible routine answers | For a question explicitly marked eligible under the rule below, final Send is Will's authoritative answer. Other questions still require matching chat confirmation. | Removes duplicate confirmation for the eligible class; Will accepts the possibility of same-account software fabricating such an answer. |

Proposed eligibility is based on consequence, never just HANDS/DECISION type or a button's label. Eligible examples: acknowledging that a document was read, a display preference, or reporting an administrative task done **when that report neither releases another action nor changes evidence requirements or authority**. PROME must make the exact eligible question and its limited consequence visible before the final Send. An answer adding conditions beyond that scope falls back to chat confirmation. Unknown or ambiguous eligibility falls back to confirmation.

Always retain confirmation for money, positions/trades, purchases/accounts/credentials, external sends, gate/threshold/risk changes, permissions, standing rules, agent/desk authority and any task whose completion unlocks one of those. A process change can alter authority; a task report can release a gate. Neither label establishes low stakes. Acknowledging receipt never proves that the reported task is actually complete.

The final app review must show the exact question, chosen response and conditions, and whether Send is authoritative or still requires chat. Save-only stays a draft. Changed terms, stale or conflicting answers, missing packet and uncertain delivery cannot be integrated automatically. PROME must preserve the exact submitted words and source terms in its canonical ruling. Existing restrictions on execution survive; an authoritative answer is not a blanket permission to spend, trade or launch work outside its explicit scope.

Local-account limit: the app's loopback/session controls do not prove Will authored the answer. Agents and other software under the same OS account can read/write relevant state. This policy is Will's explicit acceptance of that limit for a narrow class, not a claim that forged answers are impossible. The matching human message in chat is stronger evidence than an agent-written file claiming confirmation occurred.

If Will chooses the narrow class, implementation and validation of the visible eligibility/final-submit behavior still need a concrete scoped change. This candidate contains no hidden switch, automatic graduation, altered receipt authority or classification algorithm for that choice. The reviewer should assess whether the proposed boundary is understandable and identify missing consequences; the reviewer cannot make Will's trust ruling.
