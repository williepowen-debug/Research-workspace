The source's $0.25 and $0.30 forecasts were not independently verified here; the companion file labels the Goldman source snippet-only.

**Smallest correction:** retain the qualitative risk, unit conversions and dated observed proxies. Remove the asserted threshold timings or recast them as explicit sensitivities with named product-futures, crude, geography, horizon and pass-through assumptions. Do not grade a gate from them.
