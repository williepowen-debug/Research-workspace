## MR19 — BRENT retail scenario does not directly determine a futures-crack threshold date

**Material, draft-stage; owner correction recommended before consumer use.** Inspected the live, initially untracked `AGENTS/BRENT/research/2026-09-28_us-diesel-export-ban-risk.md`, written 16:57 EDT, especially §3, and its companion source-research file §Goldman scenario. BRENT labels the study Will-directed, no grade/trade. This is work in progress, not a claim that a defective final deliverable was published.

The draft converts an attributed US retail diesel decline of $0.25/gal/week to $10.50/bbl/week, then says the November futures crack's $1.23 F1 cushion disappears eight times over and HEN-46 breaks within a week. It similarly converts retail gasoline increases into a forecast date for a gasoline-crack threshold. The ×42 unit conversion is valid; the cross-instrument inference is not established. A retail price includes crude, refining, distribution/retail and taxes, while a crack subtracts crude from wholesale/futures product prices. Geography, contract horizon and crude movement also matter. The draft itself anticipates weaker WTI, which changes the subtraction.

[EIA's diesel-price explanation](https://www.eia.gov/TODAYINENERGY/detail.php?id=68164) explicitly separates those components and defines its crack on wholesale diesel minus crude. **CATO inference:** absent a stated pass-through and crude-price assumption, a retail scenario alone cannot establish either futures-crack move or breach date. Counterexample: the same retail decline could accompany a matching crude decline, leaving the product-minus-crude spread unchanged. The source's $0.25 and $0.30 forecasts were not independently verified here; the companion file labels the Goldman source snippet-only.

**Smallest correction:** retain the qualitative risk, unit conversions and dated observed proxies. Remove the asserted threshold timings or recast them as explicit sensitivities with named product-futures, crude, geography, horizon and pass-through assumptions. Do not grade a gate from them. A single weekly export drop also does not, by itself, prove voluntary restrictions caused it. **Closure:** the consumer-facing note separates the source's retail scenario from conditional crack calculations, without a fresh study, probability, threshold or trade change.
