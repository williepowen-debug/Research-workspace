## F1 — HIGH, model captured as draft and subsequently committed: endpoint arithmetic is not an 18-month liquidity clearance

**Source:** funding snapshot lines 11–53, 93, 103–118 and 140–145. The base-case arithmetic reproduces: approximately $2,892M future operating cash flow, $695M additional revolver use, $605M unused capacity. The approximately −7.84% annual cash-flow sensitivity also reproduces. These are useful scenario results, but the draft overstates what they establish.

- **Timing and minimum liquidity are absent.** It spends the opening cash and tests remaining capacity at December 2027, without scheduling the interim cash trough, financing draws, or required liquidity reserve. Positive ending headroom can coexist with an earlier shortfall. The filing expressly mentions minimum-liquidity covenants and possible processor collateral requirements; current compliance does not demonstrate future availability. This also makes the new TRADE claim that *any* draw before 2027 means the base case is early unsupported: the model contains no draw schedule. See the company's [June 2026 10-Q, Liquidity and Funding Sources](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001104659-26-089657/nclh-20260630x10q.htm).
- **The stated sensitivity assurance is false.** The draft says a ±$200M error in its assumed 2027 non-newbuild capex changes no verdict. At −5% annual cash-flow growth, its $170M cushion becomes approximately **−$30M** with $200M extra capex. Conversely, reducing capex by $200M changes its −10% cash-flow case from short to covered. Cash flow is therefore not the only material uncertain input; principal timing and capex are expressly assumptions in its own table.
- **Do not substitute one unverified conclusion for another.** Failure to reconstruct an unidentified analyst's $1.3B gap does not refute that analyst's horizon, reserve or stress assumptions. Retire the unsupported claim of an inevitable equity raise, but call this an illustrative funding scenario rather than proof that the window closes. Reconcile incremental interest and repayment terms on new financing without double-counting interest already inside operating cash flow. A forecast debt numerator is not established future debt.
- **One source-absence claim is demonstrably wrong:** EBITDA is in this 10-Q. Its printed page 35 provides Q2/H1 EBITDA of $668.049M/$1,202.411M and adjusted EBITDA of $665.515M/$1,198.412M. This does **not** supply a 2027 denominator or justify an invented leverage ratio; it corrects the reason for not calculating one. [Company filing](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001104659-26-089657/nclh-20260630x10q.htm).

**Bounded remedy / acceptance:** keep the scenario table, add a dated cash schedule with explicit minimum-liquidity and financing-availability assumptions, and cross-check principal/interest against the current maturity disclosures. Show at least the base case and the −5% cash-flow/+ $200M capex case. If covenant thresholds or draw dates cannot be verified, mark the sufficiency conclusion unresolved.
