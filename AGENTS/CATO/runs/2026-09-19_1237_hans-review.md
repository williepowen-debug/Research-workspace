### H1 — High: an observed maximum cannot settle the storage forecast early

**Source:** `workbook/PREDICTIONS.tsv:8`, HNS-07 September 19 rule (d); repeated in `SESSION_LOG.md:32` onward. The rule says to resolve MISS early when 80% is unreachable at the season's maximum observed daily pace applied to the remaining days. The registered resolver remains AGSI fill on gas day November 1.

The best pace observed so far is a sample maximum, not a proven maximum possible future injection rate. Counterexample: fill **79%**, four days left, season-to-date best **0.20pp/day**. The proposed calculation gives **79.8%** and resolves MISS. A new **0.25pp/day** pace reaches **80%**; nothing in the rule establishes that this is physically impossible. This is a constructed counterexample, not an observed current forecast path.

**Fix:** treat this as evidence for a probability reassessment, keep the prediction OPEN, and grade on the registered gas day. Any early impossibility rule needs a justified physical upper bound on the same metric, including its capacity denominator. Preserve the original resolver and append an explicit correction to rule (d); do not quietly replace its outcome definition. Define precedence if multiple re-mark conditions fire together. This review assigns no replacement probability.

**Acceptance:** the counterexample cannot produce a terminal MISS. Checkpoint evidence and outcome evidence are recorded separately. The forecast remains resolvable from the original November 1 observation.
