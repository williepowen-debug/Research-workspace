## RC1 — Medium: NEXUS confuses no sustained run with a quiet market

**Source:** `AGENTS/NEXUS/research/2026-09-24_t12_successor_DFII10_letter.md` at `aea73389d` (also in `7a22921c6`), lines 63, 82, 86 and 99. The frozen letter grades C when neither a five-consecutive-cell UP nor DOWN run completes in 15 published cells. But the construction checklist describes C as staying inside ±10bp throughout, and §3 says two Cs mean yields held inside ±10bp for roughly 30 sessions and the price-of-money channel went quiet.

**Independent counterexample:** anchor 2.70%; alternate 3.00% and 2.40% for 15 cells. No five-cell run completes: C, despite every observation being outside the band. Re-anchor at the last cell, 3.00%; alternate 3.30% and 2.70% for another 15 cells. Again C, with every observation outside its new band. These are 60bp daily swings, not a quiet channel. Control cases of five consecutive high/low cells produce UP/DOWN; a flat window produces C. Evidence JSON preserves all cells and outputs.

The same paragraph gives a double-C chance of roughly 31% unconditional / 13% in-regime. These reproduce squaring the marginal C rates (0.562² and 0.36²), but the letter supplies no independence assumption or joint-window estimate. Adjacent, re-anchored windows need not be independent; the second anchor also need not remain in the ≥2.50 regime. Those numbers are not established double-window base rates.

**Consequence:** an earned no-verdict could be turned into unsupported evidence against NEXUS's price-of-money thesis. This does not show that the UP/DOWN grading arithmetic or published single-window historical rates are wrong. The historical grid and source vintages were not independently reproduced here; scratch-only preservation limits reproducibility.

Describe C as failure to sustain either directional run, correct the checklist and repeated-C interpretation, and remove or explicitly label the squared rates as an independence illustration rather than a measured frequency. If NEXUS wants a claim about actual stability, it needs a separate measurement; changing C into an all-cells-inside-band rule would change the instrument and require the existing re-registration discipline.
