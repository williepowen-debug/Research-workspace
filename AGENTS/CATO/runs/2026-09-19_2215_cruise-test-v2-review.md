## F1 — High: mixed outcomes have no prediction grade, and the disclosure remains coupled to the channel assessment

Section 9b maps section 4 row 4 to CONFIRMED, rows 3/5 to FAILED, and row 1 to NO-VERDICT. **It never maps row 2 (mixed).**

More importantly, row 2 fires whenever competitive drag and firm/improving Caribbean pricing coexist. Consider this hypothetical, fully scoped statement: **“For Q4 2026, competitor promotions are holding back our Caribbean pricing relative to our other regions, although Caribbean pricing is still improving.”** Management has made the exact disclosure CRU-09 predicts, including concentration, yet the first-match table puts it in mixed and section 9b supplies no grade. Simply assigning NO-VERDICT to mixed would still conflate an observed disclosure with uncertainty about the economic effect.

**Smallest repair: give CRU-09 its own exhaustive resolver.** Missing source coverage at the exact cutoff yields NO-VERDICT. Otherwise, a management statement meeting all frozen scope conditions yields CONFIRMED even if pricing is improving; complete reviewed coverage with no qualifying statement yields FAILED. Explicit retractions/corrections should be handled by a declared rule. True ambiguity about whether a statement meets the wording is different from economic offsetting strength and should have a declared no-verdict disposition. The separate FL-CRU-10 assessment must not determine this forecast's grade.

## F2 — High: the channel can still be falsely contradicted

Section 9a says pressure attributed to another named operator, with no Norwegian link, is CONTRADICTED on the Norwegian link. That does not follow: another operator and Norwegian can both contribute. An attribution to one without mention of the other does not establish exclusion. Use unresolved absent an explicit relevant exclusion or other evidence that distinguishes the alternatives.

Section 4 row 3 also still calls firm/improving Caribbean pricing plus no competitive-drag language CONTRADICTED. Actual competitive drag can be offset by stronger demand or other support without being mentioned. This observation can fail the disclosure forecast, but does not refute the causal channel. An explicit denial for the same region, mechanism and horizon would be contrary attribution evidence; a positive price trend plus silence is not equivalent.

Keep multiple periods separate. Under the present row 2, a Q3 improvement statement can combine with a FY27 drag statement to create a “mixed” outcome despite no temporal conflict. A named period is useful only if the evidence being compared refers to the same one.
