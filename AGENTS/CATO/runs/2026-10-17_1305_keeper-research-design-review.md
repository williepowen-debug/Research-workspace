## Research interpretation

- **B1: avoid the filing/search dichotomy.** Whittaker et al.'s 2011 email study covered 345 users and over 85,000 refinding actions. Elaborate preparatory folders did not improve retrieval success; both search and threading helped. Email conversation threads are not proof for AI-generated idea threads. Recommendation: retain raw captures, make them searchable regardless of filing, and allow multiple connections without requiring Will to maintain a taxonomy. [Original study, IBM](https://research.ibm.com/publications/am-i-wasting-my-time-organizing-email-a-study-of-email-refinding).
- **B2: total capture is adjacent evidence.** Sellen and Whittaker criticize assumptions behind lifelogging and argue for designing around specific memory uses. Deliberately saving selected thoughts differs from passive recording of a life; do not transfer negative findings wholesale. [Original article](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/p70-sellen.pdf).
- **E1: match the evaluation to the desired outcome.** Generative Agents evaluates believable simulated behavior, not fidelity to a real person's memories. LongMemEval's extraction, multi-session and temporal reasoning, knowledge updates and abstention are closer to Keeper's retrieval needs, but still do not establish personal usefulness or reliable real-world actions. Borrow failure cases, not architecture prestige or leaderboard guarantees. [Park et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442); [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813).
- **E2/E3: separate source evidence from the model's interpretation.** Sycophancy has empirical support in tested assistants, but the cited study does not prove Keeper will flatter or that labels alone prevent it. Keep the capacity to infer connections; require evidence and an easy way to reject them. A corrected inference must cease to influence future summaries. [Anthropic research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models).
- **C2: control and uncertainty matter more than a magic question count.** Horvitz discusses uncertain user goals, interruption costs, direct invocation/termination and repair of poor guesses. This supports asking how to defer, dismiss and correct assistance; it does not validate 06:00 or three questions. [Original paper](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf).
- **A2/C3: an idea is not necessarily a goal.** Masicampo/Baumeister's original experiments concerned specific plans for activated unfulfilled goals. They do not establish that an assistant filling a `next` field provides the same benefit. An if–then plan also differs from a generic suggested step. Keep `next: none` legitimate; separate model suggestion from Will's commitment. Replication and ADHD-specific transfer remain unreviewed. [Original study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21688924/).

Do not require a clinical justification for Will's preferred tone or predictable format. Conversely, preferences do not establish treatment efficacy. D1–D3 remain unassessed, not classified as having no good trials. Numerical defaults (3/10 seconds, 06:00, 14 days, three questions) are hypotheses to test. Capture latency should distinguish finishing/sending a thought, durable receipt, transcription and filing; saving before model processing is already a sensible design separation in the plan.

## Suggested research deliverable and trial

For each decision: current assumption; strongest relevant evidence and counterevidence; population/task studied; what transfers and what does not; proposed design consequence; smallest local check. A result of "evidence does not choose this default" is useful. Avoid selecting studies to substantiate the already-written prompt.

Before a personal trial, use a few fictional examples: ambiguous voice transcription, one capture relating to two threads, an explicit correction to a preference, a wrong folder, a question with no supporting memory, ignored resurfacing, killed thread and an unavailable relay. These are proposed checks, not executed tests.

During a trial, retain concrete examples of successful retrieval, worthwhile connections and chosen next actions; note missed captures, incorrect recollections and effort spent correcting/reading. Use a lightweight retrospective rather than requiring a daily research diary. Compare against ordinary capture/search as a practical baseline where feasible; a two-week personal trial can support continue/change/stop, not prove clinical benefit or causal cognitive improvement. Set acceptable burden and useful-result expectations with Will before starting; no arbitrary numeric success threshold imposed here.
