# Feb 2021 Meme Stock Volatility

## Summary
Retail-driven gamma squeeze in GME, AMC caused elevated VIX without credit stress. Demonstrates equity-specific volatility disconnected from credit markets.

## Key Data
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| VIX Average | 24.13 |
| VIX Range | 19.97 - 37.21 |
| HY OAS Average | 3.65 |
| HY OAS Range | 3.41 - 3.93 |
| VIX/Credit Divergence | High |

## Pattern Analysis
- **Credit lead?** No — credit spreads remained stable
- **Driver:** Retail gamma squeeze, short covering
- **VIX-Credit Correlation:** Decoupled
- **Regime:** Low vol with equity-specific dislocations

## Key Events
- Jan 27: GME squeeze peak, VIX spikes to 37
- Credit markets unaffected — HY OAS <4 throughout
- Demonstrates VIX can reflect equity microstructure, not macro stress

## Implications for VIOLET
- Elevated VIX alone is not a systemic signal
- Must check credit spreads to distinguish idiosyncratic vs systemic vol
- Term structure and VVIX help identify the driver
