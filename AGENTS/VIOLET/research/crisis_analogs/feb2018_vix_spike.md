# Feb 2018 VIX Spike (Volmageddon)

## Summary
A sudden volatility spike caused by the unwind of short-volatility ETPs (XIV, SVXY), not credit stress. VIX doubled in a day. Credit spreads barely moved.

## Key Data
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| VIX Pre-Spike (20d avg) | 14.86 |
| VIX Peak | 33.46 |
| Peak Date | Feb 8, 2018 |
| Spike Magnitude | +125% |
| HY OAS Pre-Spike | 3.36 |
| HY OAS Peak | 3.82 |
| Credit Spread Change | +46 bps |

## Pattern Analysis
- **Credit lead?** No — credit spreads were stable
- **Driver:** Technical unwind of inverse VIX ETFs
- **Lesson:** Not all VIX spikes are credit-driven
- **Regime:** Low vol → spike → quick mean reversion

## Implications for VIOLET
- VIX can spike without credit stress (technical factors)
- Check VVIX and term structure to distinguish technical vs fundamental spikes
- Feb 2018 was a "vol of vol" event, not a credit event
