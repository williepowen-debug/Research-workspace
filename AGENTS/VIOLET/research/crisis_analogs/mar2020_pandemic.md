# Mar 2020 Pandemic Crash

## Summary
COVID-19 pandemic caused simultaneous credit and equity volatility spike. VIX hit 82.69 — highest since 2008. Credit spreads widened dramatically.

## Key Data
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| VIX Pre-Spike (20d avg) | 38.85 |
| VIX Peak | 82.69 |
| Peak Date | Mar 16, 2020 |
| Spike Magnitude | +113% |
| HY OAS Pre-Spike | 5.23 |
| HY OAS Peak | 10.87 |
| Credit Spread Change | +564 bps |

## Pattern Analysis
- **Credit lead?** No — VIX spiked first (Feb 27), credit followed
- **Driver:** Pandemic shock — simultaneous risk-off
- **Lead-lag:** VIX led HY OAS by ~21 days
- **Regime:** Rising vol → crash → sustained high vol

## Timeline
- Feb 19: S&P 500 peak
- Feb 27: VIX first >30
- Mar 9: Oil price crash accelerates selling
- Mar 12: VIX >70
- Mar 16: VIX peak at 82.69
- Mar 19: HY OAS peak

## Implications for VIOLET
- In crash regimes, VIX leads credit (not the other way around)
- The "credit leads vol" thesis may be regime-dependent
- High VIX + rising credit = systemic stress confirmed
