## What I am measuring

**Primary:** VIX at open+10min and open+35min, vs **both** the 9/3 settle (14.32) and the pre-open anchor (14.16).
**Secondary:** VVIX (does vol-of-vol confirm?), `^SKEW` (does the tail bid extend or fade?), and — the one that matters most for my domain — **whether the front end firms relative to the tail**, i.e. whether any of this shows up in VIX9D/VIX and the M1:M2 contango.

## Outcomes, declared in advance

- **A — HAWKISH REPRICING:** VIX up meaningfully off the settle (**≥ +1.0 pt, i.e. ≥15.3**) with VVIX confirming. ⇒ the front end has finally joined the tail; the 9/3 divergence starts closing from the cheap side. **This is the outcome that would most change my read**, because the whole standing tension is that equity vol will not participate.
- **B — DIVERGENCE PERSISTS (my prior, stated as such):** VIX flat-to-lower or up trivially (**< +0.5 pt**) while SKEW holds ≥150. ⇒ the market takes a 3× jobs beat that keeps a hike live and **still** will not bid the front end. That *strengthens* the cheap-tail configuration rather than resolving it.
- **C — RELIEF / RISK-ON:** VIX down through **~13.9** with SKEW fading below 150. ⇒ the tail bid was not about the macro path; FT-10's count resets and the 9/3 print reads as a one-session anomaly.
- **D — NULL:** moves inside noise (**|ΔVIX| < 0.3** and no SKEW change). ⇒ **report as a null.** A null is a result; it is not licence to narrate one of A/B/C.

## Constraints I am binding myself to

1. ⛔ **No causal attribution to NFP from a single session.** CPI is 7 days out and the FOMC 8 — the tape carries at least three live drivers. I can report **what vol did**; I cannot report **why** from one print. `[[finding_confounds_align_with_the_prior_you_brought]]`
2. ⛔ **`^SKEW` and FT-10 are graded at CBOE, on the 9/4 bar, which does not exist until after the close.** Any intraday SKEW number is the **provisional mirror**. **The FT-10 count cannot move today** — it stays **1 of 4** on this surface no matter what the mirror prints. This is the exact kill-on-sight WALTER flagged, and today is the day I am most likely to trip it myself.
3. ⛔ **Outcome B is my prior.** I am naming it so a B reading is held to the same evidence bar as an A reading, not waved through as confirmation.
