# Volume Dynamics Around FOMC Announcements

**Source:** BIS Working Papers No 1079 (2023). "Volume Dynamics Around FOMC Announcements."

**URL:** https://www.bis.org/publ/work1079.pdf

---

## Key Findings

- **FOMC Day Effect on VIX:**
  - VIX consistently **decreases** on FOMC meeting days, regardless of meeting content
  - This is the "FOMC put" or "Fed relief" effect
  - Uncertainty resolution dominates directional implications

- **Pre-FOMC Pattern:**
  - VIX often rises in days leading up to FOMC (uncertainty building)
  - Abnormal turnover increases before announcements
  - 24 hours before FOMC shows elevated VIX levels

- **Post-FOMC Pattern:**
  - VIX decline typically immediate (within hours of announcement)
  - Effect persists for 1-2 days
  - Exception: when Fed surprises significantly (rare)

- **Volume-VIX Relationship:**
  - Abnormal turnover contemporaneous with VIX changes
  - Volume spikes precede VIX changes around announcements
  - Weekday fixed effects important (Monday vs Friday FOMC different)
