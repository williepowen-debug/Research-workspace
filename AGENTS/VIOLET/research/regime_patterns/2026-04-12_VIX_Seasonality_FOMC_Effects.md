# VIX Seasonality and FOMC Effects

**Sources:**
- Nikkinen, J. & Sahlström, P. (2004). "Impact of the Federal Open Market Committee's Meetings on Stock Market Uncertainty."
- Krieger, K.; Mauck, N.; Vazquez, F. (2015). FOMC day effects on VIX.
- Equity Clock VIX Seasonal Data

---

## Key Findings

- **FOMC Day Effect:**
  - VIX consistently **decreases** on FOMC meeting days
  - Effect occurs regardless of meeting outcome (hawkish/dovish)
  - Driven by uncertainty resolution, not directional policy
  - Magnitude: typically -0.5 to -2.0 VIX points

- **Pre-FOMC Pattern:**
  - VIX tends to rise 1-3 days before FOMC (uncertainty building)
  - Abnormal options volume before announcements
  - VIX futures curve steepens (near-term premium)

- **Monthly Seasonality:**
  - **January:** Elevated VIX (earnings season, new year uncertainty)
  - **February-March:** Moderate, tax-related flows
  - **April-May:** Lower VIX (earnings clarity, spring rally)
  - **June-August:** Mixed, summer doldoms vs event risk
  - **September-October:** Historically highest VIX months
  - **November-December:** Declining VIX into year-end (Santa Claus effect)

- **Day-of-Week Effects:**
  - Monday: Slightly elevated VIX (weekend risk)
  - Friday: Lower VIX (weekend theta decay)

## Relevance to VIOLET's Thesis

**MEDIUM:** Calendar effects provide context for VIX interpretation.

1. **Baseline Adjustment:** VIX readings must be adjusted for calendar position
2. **Event Timing:** FOMC weeks have different dynamics
3. **Seasonal Patterns:** September-October weakness is historical regularity

## Actionable Implications

| Time Period | Expected VIX Behavior | Interpretation |
|-------------|----------------------|----------------|
| FOMC week | Elevated pre, lower post | Normalize readings |
| Sep-Oct | Seasonally higher | Don't overread seasonal rise |
| Apr-May | Seasonally lower | Low VIX may be normal |
| Year-end | Declining | Window dressing effect |

- **VIOLET Adjustment:**
  - Compare VIX to seasonal average, not just absolute level
  - FOMC week VIX > 25 = more concerning than non-FOMC week
  - September VIX spike = partly seasonal, partly genuine
