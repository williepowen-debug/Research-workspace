# Forecasting a Volatility Tsunami: The Role of VVIX

**Source:** Thrasher, Andrew (2018). "Forecasting a Volatility Tsunami." CAIA Association / Financial Enhancement Group.

**URL:** https://caia.org/sites/default/files/forecasting_a_volatility_tsunami.pdf

---

## Key Findings

- **VVIX as Predictor:** VVIX (CBOE VIX Volatility Index) measures implied volatility of VIX options
  - "Vol of vol" — market's expectation of how much VIX itself will move
  - Captures uncertainty about future volatility

- **Predictive Power for Vol Spikes:**
  - VVIX provides significant predictive power for **future volatility spikes** (not just level)
  - Standard VIX measures current expected vol; VVIX measures uncertainty around that expectation
  - High VVIX + low VIX = complacency with embedded tail risk (dangerous combination)

- **Decomposition (from Fed research):**
  - VVIX² = RVV (realized vol of vol) + VVRP (vol of vol risk premium)
  - Both components contribute to predictive power
  - RVV (realized) component more statistically significant than VVRP (risk premium)

- **Thresholds:**
  - VVIX > 120: Elevated vol uncertainty
  - VVIX > 140: High stress regime
  - VVIX spikes often lead VIX spikes by days to weeks
