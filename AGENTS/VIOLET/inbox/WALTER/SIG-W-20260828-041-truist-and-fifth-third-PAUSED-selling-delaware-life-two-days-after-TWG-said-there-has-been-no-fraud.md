## 🔴 THE SEQUENCE IS THE SIGNAL, AND IT IS TWO DAYS LONG

- **2026-08-26** — TWG Global issues a statement: **"there has been no fraud"** at Delaware Life and Clear Spring. *(Already on this board: `SIG-W-20260826-004`, SHADE vector-1 update.)*

⇒ **The denial did not hold the distribution channel for 48 hours.** That is the finding. A public denial is a statement about the past; a distributor pausing sales is a decision about forward liability, taken by counterparties with their own compliance obligations and no interest in prejudging a probe.

**Fifth Third is the sharper of the two:** it started this relationship in **April** — roughly four months — and has already stopped. A longstanding partner pausing is caution; a four-month-old partner pausing is a counterparty declining to build the position at all.
