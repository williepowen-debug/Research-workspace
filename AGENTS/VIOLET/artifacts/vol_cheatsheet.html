02

The one idea that ties it together

The surface — one thermometer

All four gauges are built from the same S&P options. When they move together, that's one opinion moving — like four thermometers in one room.

VIX VVIX SKEW TERM

So four gauges agreeing is not four confirmations. It's one.

Independent rooms — real confirmation

A signal is real when a separate market agrees with the surface. These are the rooms that matter.

CREDITjunk-bond stress — real money
MOVEthe bond market's own VIX
POSITIONINGwho's actually betting which way
OIL-VOLenergy-shock transmission

Surface and these rooms pointing the same way = a signal to respect. Surface alone = usually noise that fades.

Two tools, opposite ends

Cheap / calm floorBreak confirmed
Fires early — at the floor

Cheap-tail alert

When insurance is cheapest, before an event

"Is protection cheap enough to buy ahead of a catalyst?" Catches the quiet, cheap window so we don't miss it. Stops us paying full price later.

Fires late — on confirmation

Confirmation gate

Only once the independent rooms agree

"Is the break actually real?" Waits on purpose — an independent room must confirm — so we don't chase a fake-out. Stops us buying into noise.

The alert and confirmation-gate concepts are different. The former July hedge packet and GATE-VIO-RV1 are retired; neither is an active trade rule. Last recorded VIOLET book is flat in the September 10 broker mirror. New construction belongs with TERRY and requires Will’s approval.

Read gauges together, never alone. A high SKEW can be fear or just hedges rotating into cheap far-out options — which mechanically pushes VIX down. Pair it with other reads before judging. Use the publication, not an assumed clock. SKEW grades require the dated Cboe archive bar; no guaranteed publication hour is established. Credit and positioning carry their own observation dates. Coincident, not predictive. VIX reacts to what already happened. The edge is in the divergences and the independent rooms, not the headline number.
