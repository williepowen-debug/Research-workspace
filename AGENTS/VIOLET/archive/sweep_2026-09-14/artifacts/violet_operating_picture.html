The three kinds of instrument — they are not interchangeable
GateAction trigger

Has a registered consequence written down before it fires. When it trips, a specific pre-agreed thing happens — usually routing a trade look to TERRY for your approval. Never self-executes.

CanaryContext only

An early-warning read that informs but never triggers action — oil-vol and yen-carry are canaries. If one fires, it goes to the agent who owns that substance, not to a trade.

AlertYour decision

Surfaces a choice and hands it to you with a menu of options. The cheap-tail window is the only one. It deliberately does not decide anything.

Pre-regLocked branch map

Written before the data arrives, graded after. Its whole purpose is to stop the agent rationalising whatever happened into whatever it already believed.
