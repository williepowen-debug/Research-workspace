The File & ServeXpress Difference
File & ServeXpress focuses on providing users with case, document and party management solutions that simplify the litigation workflow of any case.
By offering electronic filing to courts, process service, secure document exchange among judges and attorneys, alert and notification tools, and a document repository, File & ServeXpress offers a complete litigation solution throughout the life of a case.

Built-In Security You Can Trust
At FSX, protecting sensitive public data is built into everything we do. Our SOC 2 certification reflects our commitment to secure, compliant, and reliable systems. We also offer configurable document and case-level security settings—giving firms more control over how information is shared and stored. Security isn’t just a feature—it’s part of the foundation.
