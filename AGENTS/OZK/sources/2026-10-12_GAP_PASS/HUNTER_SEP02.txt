Hunter Advisors Brokers Sale of Renaissance Milwaukee West Hotel
ATLANTA (September 2, 2026) — Hunter Advisors (Hunter) announced the sale of the
Renaissance Milwaukee West Hotel
, a 196-room hotel located in Wauwatosa, Wisconsin. Spencer Davidson, Senior Vice President at Hunter, brokered the transaction, with Chicago-based Witness Investment acquiring the property.
“This transaction reflects continued investor confidence in recently built, brand-affiliated hotels in strong secondary markets, and the Renaissance Milwaukee West offers a rare combination of modern construction, prime location, and long-term upside,” said
Davidson
.
Built in 2020 and located at 2300 North Mayfair Road, the 12-story, AAA Four Diamond hotel sits adjacent to Mayfair Mall, Wisconsin’s largest mixed-use shopping destination, and offers institutional-quality accommodations designed to serve corporate, medical, leisure and event travelers. The property features approximately 9,864 square feet of flexible meeting and event space, the ELDR+RIME Restaurant, the Silhouette Lobby Lounge, and a 12th-floor fitness center.
“
It’s always a pleasure working with Hunter, and we’re glad to have gotten another deal done together,” said
Gurtej Medi, Managing Partner of Witness Investment
.
“
This was a rare opportunity to acquire a modern, well-located Marriott asset with significant growth ahead of it, and we couldn’t be more excited to add it to our portfolio.”
The hotel sits within one of Wauwatosa’s primary retail, medical, and corporate corridors, approximately 10 miles west of Downtown Milwaukee. Nearby Mayfair Mall spans 1.2 million square feet and attracts approximately 4 million annual visitors, complemented by the dining, retail, and residential offerings of the Mayfair Collection. The area is poised for further growth, with more than $500 million in planned development adjacent to the hotel, including a new Scheels flagship store and more than 400 Class A residential units at Mayfair Mall. The hotel also benefits from proximity to the Milwaukee Regional Medical Center, one of Wisconsin’s largest medical consortiums.
