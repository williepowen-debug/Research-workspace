RETR status update
Content_Area1
February 24, 2026
To: Statutory Assessors, Assessors
The Wisconsin Department of Revenue (DOR), State and Local Finance Division is providing you with the below real estate transfer return (RETR) update.
RETR Status Update
The new RETR filing system is available in
My Tax Account (MTA)
, under Real Estate Transfer Return (RETR) in the lower right corner.
Provide Assessment Data (PAD) and 2025 RETRs
PAD system and 2025 RETRs prior to January 9, 2026 –
continue to be accessible from the
Assessor
page by clicking
System login
under "Provide Assessment Data" in the "Online Services" box
Sales conveyed in 2025 and recorded after January 8, 2026 –
will be sent to assessors via email; they cannot be loaded into the PAD system
RETR Sales Public Search
The Wisconsin property sales information search is available in MTA. To access the public search, follow these steps:
Go to
My Tax Account
Scroll down to the Real Estate Transfer Return section (lower right corner)
Select "Search Wisconsin Property Data"
Important note
2026 RETR data –
can only be accessed through the Public Search portal in MTA. The portal does not include email addresses and phone numbers for grantors and grantees
Schema file
The Office of Technical and Assessment Services (OTAS) will have a schema file ready soon
Reach out to your software vendor and let them know to contact
OTAS
to access this schema file
When your software vendor updates the schema file, you will be able to continue e-transfer of RETR information
