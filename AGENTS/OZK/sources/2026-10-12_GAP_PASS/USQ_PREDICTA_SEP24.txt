Predicta Biosciences Expands to 
10,500-Square-Foot Space at USQ 

Growing Molecular Insights Company for Blood Cancers Selects                         

10 Prospect Street in Somerville’s Union Square For Its Next Stage of Growth 

SOMERVILLE,  Mass.  –  Sept.  24,  2026  –  USQ,  the  20-acre  transit-oriented  development  in 
Somerville’s Union Square, today announced that Predicta Biosciences has signed a lease for 
10,500  square  feet  at  10  Prospect  Street,  becoming  the  building’s  first  lab  tenant.  The 
company  will  move  into  its  new  space  in  October 2026, marking a significant expansion of its 
team and operations. 

Founded  by  oncologists  and  scientists  from  Dana-Farber  Cancer  Institute  and  the  Broad 
Institute,  Predicta  is  developing an integrated molecular diagnostics platform for hematological 
malignancies. Its technology sequences whole, intact circulating tumor cells from a single blood 
draw, generating genomic and immune insights that can help guide therapy selection and track 
disease evolution over time. 

“Predicta  is  expanding  rapidly,  and  the  move  to  10 Prospect marks our next stage of growth,” 
said Brian McKernan, CEO of Predicta Biosciences. “The space allows us to grow our team 
and  scale  our  operations  as  we  advance  a  diagnostic  platform  that  provides  clinicians  with 
earlier, more precise insights into blood cancers.” 

Predicta  is  relocating  from  Cambridge  into  one  of  10  Prospect's  move-in-ready  lab  suites, 
designed so growing companies can scale without a lengthy build-out. 

Located  approximately  a  mile  from  Kendall  Square,  10  Prospect  places  Predicta  within  a 
Somerville  innovation  community  that  includes  Greentown  Labs,  the  largest  climatetech 
incubator  in  North  America,  and  a  growing  cluster  of  life  sciences,  robotics  and  hardware 
startups.  Union  Square  Station  on  the  MBTA  Green  Line  provides  convenient  access  to  the 
region’s research hospitals, universities and talent base. 

“Predicta is exactly the kind of tenant we built 10 Prospect for: a company founded here in the 
Boston region, growing quickly and ready for its own lab,” said Greg Karczewski, President of 
USQ. “10 Prospect gives them specialized infrastructure and a transit stop at the front door, and 
Union  Square  gives  them  a  neighborhood  full  of  researchers,  founders  and  builders,  with

About Predicta Biosciences 
Predicta Biosciences is unlocking therapy selection with ultra-sensitive molecular diagnostics for 
hematological  malignancies.  Founded  by  world-leading  oncologists  and  scientists  at 
Dana-Farber and the Broad Institute, Predicta is built on a distinctive technology that sequences 
whole tumor cells from a single blood draw. 

Through  the  integrated  analysis  of  ultra-sensitive  rare-cell  sequencing  and  proprietary 
bioinformatics,  Predicta  delivers  genomic  insights  including  Measurable  Residual  Disease 
(MRD),  single-cell  immune  profiling,  T-cell  receptor  profiling  and  clonal  evolution  tracking  that 
reveals how resistant populations are emerging before they become clinically apparent. Predicta 
is  delivering  unequivocal  diagnostic  insights  that  transform  therapy  decisions  and  patient 
outcomes. For more information, visit www.predictabiosciences.com. 

About USQ 

USQ is creating a new center for innovation, life sciences and technology in the heart of 
Somerville, bringing together ambitious companies, entrepreneurs, researchers and talent in a 
vibrant, transit-connected community. Anchored by the MBTA's Union Square Station, the 
20-acre, 3.8 million square foot project will ultimately include 2.4 million square feet of lab and 
innovation space alongside housing, a hotel, retail and dynamic public spaces. Phase 1 is 
complete and open, including 10 Prospect, a state-of-the-art life sciences building with 60,000 
square feet of move-in-ready lab suites. USQ is a place where people and ideas converge to 
shape what's next.
