Item 7.01 

Regulation FD Disclosure 

In response to inquiries, Bank OZK (the “Company”) is providing information on the long-term 

extension of its largest credit, a $915 million loan commitment secured by a project in San Diego, 
California. On October 9, 2026, the Company and the borrower entered into an agreement extending the 
maturity date of the loan to December 2029. In connection with the extension, the sponsor and its capital 
partners contributed an additional $100 million of capital. That contribution, together with $21.3 million 
previously held by the Company in a reserve account, has been applied to reduce the outstanding 
principal balance of the loan. The $121.3 million principal reduction may be readvanced during the 
extended term to fund approved interest and other costs in accordance with a revised budget.  

The sponsor and capital partners have continued to demonstrate their commitment to the project. 

Prior to the recent $100 million capital contribution made in connection with the extension, the sponsor 
and its capital partners previously made additional capital contributions of $87.1 million in June 2024, 
$8.7 million in September 2024, $9.3 million in November 2024 and $82.0 million in January 2025. In 
aggregate the sponsor and its capital partners have invested approximately $1.23 billion in this project. 
The long-term extension executed on October 9, 2026 followed a 45-day short-term extension during 
which the sponsor and its capital partners negotiated an agreement to govern the terms of future capital 
contributions, if needed, further indicating their commitment to this project. 

San Diego Project Additional Details as of October 9, 2026 

•  Total loan commitment – $915 million 
•  Funded balance as of October 9, 2026, following paydown – $579.1 million 
•  Unfunded balance as of October 9, 2026, following paydown – $335.9 million, which is 

designated for “good news funding” (tenant improvements and leasing commissions), interest and 
other costs. In addition, the Company maintains a reserve account for good news funding of $28.6 
million, which was previously provided by the sponsor and its capital partners.   

•  Updated maturity date – December 2029 
•  Loan-to-cost ratio – 44.8% assuming loan is fully funded 
•  Loan-to-value ratio – 68.4% assuming loan is fully funded, based on “as-stabilized” value from 

appraisal dated September 2026
