**4. The "Well-Secured" Loophole**
- Banks can keep 90+ day delinquent loans OFF non-accrual if "well secured and in process of collection"
- Requires collateral appraisal covering full principal + interest + costs
- CRE appraisals lag reality by 6-12 months — stale appraisal says $50M, market says $35M, bank claims "well secured"
- OZK specifically: construction reserves CUT 41% while losses accelerated. Consistent with using stale appraisals + loophole to suppress NPL recognition.
- **This is the mechanism by which stress stays hidden until it can't.** When appraisals finally catch up or auditors force re-evaluation, the dam breaks → 8-K forced disclosure.

**5. TDR/Modification Surge = Extend-and-Pretend Confirmation**
- Under CECL (ASC 326), modifications to borrowers "experiencing financial difficulty" must be disclosed
- Check OZK/WAL modification disclosures in next 10-Q
- Pattern: rising TDRs + flat NPLs = extend-and-pretend actively masking stress
- When mods exhaust (borrower can't perform even under restructured terms), assets cascade from TDR → non-accrual → charge-off in rapid succession

**6. Auditor Mechanics Driving 2-4 Week Lead Time**
- Under US GAAP, material impairments must be recognized when "known and estimable"
- If major borrower defaults late in quarter, bank can't wait for scheduled earnings
- Internal process: credit committee risk ratings → provision severity assessment → 8-K draft → file
- This 2-4 week lag is structural — it's the time between internal recognition and public disclosure
