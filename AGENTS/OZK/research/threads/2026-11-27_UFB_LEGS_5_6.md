## Leg 5: does the UFB parent paper loss or the brokered outcome change refinancing for OZK's sub notes or preferred?

**OZK's instruments (desk record):** $350M sub notes, reset 10/1/26 to 3M Term SOFR + 209bp, 6.14% for Q4-26, Tier 2 credit falling 20% a year, callable at par each quarterly date; the next call date is 2027-01-01 and the holder-notice window runs 11/2 → 12/22 (DOCKET line 588). Management (Q2-26): "no plans to replace them." **OZKAP** 4.625% Series A non-cumulative perpetual preferred: the 10/1 dividend release lists a period ending 11/15/26 with the dividend payable 11/16 (web search summary of that release). No redemption notice was found. The first call date is still **unverified at the prospectus**.

**Structural difference (VERIFIED from the FDIC claim priority REGINALD read and the desk record):** UFB's lost paper was **holding-company** notes and preferred, behind every bank creditor. Fidelity Federal Bancorp sat outside the receivership, was privately held, and was under a Fed written agreement with distributions stopped. OZK has **no holding company** (dissolved 2017). Its sub notes are bank-level, listed, rated BBB+ by KBRA (Negative outlook, 10/29), and backed by CET1 11.45% [Q3-26]. This was a loss on the most junior paper of a troubled private group, not on a rated regional bank's Tier 2.

**Brokered deposits:** UFB's Cede & Co. deposits (about $0.9B) were excluded from First Citizens' assumption. Under precedent, the FDIC pays the insured portions to holders through the broker (WALTER, not confirmed for this case). OZK uses brokered deposits: **$3.02B, 8.7% of deposits at 9/30/26** (from $2.51B, 7.5% at 12/31/25; 10-Q). If brokered CDs reprice for all issuers, OZK's funding cost moves with them. A failure in which insured brokered holders are paid does not, by itself, argue for that.

**Forward view (unscored, horizon to 12/22, the end of the notice window):**
- **Expect (~75%):** no measurable repricing of rated regional bank sub debt or preferred from UFB through 12/22. Any effect stays in unrated holdco paper of small banks under orders or with concentrated CRE: wider asks, a few marks down at the specialist funds. Why: depositors were made whole, the buyer is large, the loss sits in a related-party book, and the market had two weeks of public warning.
- **Alternative (~20-25%):** small-bank holdco sub debt and preferred visibly reprice (a pulled deal, coupons 50bp+ above the 2025 comps, peer marks down 10+ points). That would still reach OZK only if it chose to replace the notes.
- **OZK's call decision:** UFB does not move it in either direction in any way I can measure. The call trades ≈$12.0M a year of pre-tax cost against the notes' ~$280M of Tier 2, a capital choice for a bank with CET1 11.45%, not a question of market access. Management has said it has no plans to replace the notes. A call notice is still a 🟠 send to REGINALD and PROME under the desk's table, and nothing here changes that trigger.
- **Would change my mind:** a rated regional bank pulling or widening a Tier 2 deal in December; OZK sub-notes TRACE prints well below par; KBRA or other agencies citing UFB in a small-bank capital note.
