## What completes the outcome leg

For a named transaction collect: **old dated loan/carrying amount; third-party/sponsor cash reaching the bank; charge-off/reserve/markdown and its period; loan or OREO removed; new/retained funded loan and unfunded commitment; fees/capitalized amounts; risk rating/accrual and subsequent cash performance**. Reconcile on a consistent principal/carrying basis. Loan principal, property proceeds and interest/fees cannot be silently interchanged.

A principal bridge may be expressed as opening funded principal + actual new draws/capitalized items − principal cash − charge-offs − transfers out = closing principal, **only when every term uses the same credit, interval and treatment**. A loan transferred into OREO needs a separate asset bridge; a new financing can leave economic exposure despite loan payoff. Contractual redraw permission is not a draw already made. Missing items prevent certification; do not balance an equation with an invented residual labeled cash.

**Symmetric test:** sourced cash payoff at carrying, adequately funded new equity and stable current performance weaken the stress case. Attributable losses, fresh weak classifications or a documented shortfall strengthen it. New retained financing alone is not proof of a failed cure; undisclosed information is not bearish evidence. A reduction in nonaccrual or special mention may be cash cure, refinancing, title transfer or reclassification; named flows decide.
