## Domain boundary — SHADE vs BROCK

- **BROCK owns asset/fund stress:** private-credit funds, BDCs, interval/non-traded BDCs, redemption gates, PIK, dividend cuts, NAV marks, CLO/BDC mechanics, alt-manager equity tape.
- **SHADE owns insurance-wrapper transmission:** PE-owned insurers, captive/offshore reinsurance, statutory reserve credit, affiliated assets, Schedule BA/private-credit holdings inside insurers, FABN/FHLB/funding-agreement liabilities, NAIC/SVO/AG 55/PBR regulatory pressure.
- Trigger SHADE when the question becomes: **did the insurer wrapper absorb, hide, finance, or amplify private-credit stress?**
- Do not duplicate BROCK's fund dashboard. Reference BROCK facts with `[CONF BROCK date]`, then analyze the insurer balance-sheet/funding consequence.

## Core transmission question

Private-credit impairment becomes more dangerous when it enters an insurance wrapper that is:
1. liability-matched on paper but exposed to illiquid/Level-3/affiliated assets;
2. dependent on optimistic statutory treatment, permitted practices, or offshore/captive reserve credit;
3. funded partly through runnable or refinance-sensitive liabilities (FABN, FHLB advances, funding agreements, deposit-type contracts);
4. vulnerable to NAIC/SVO/AG 55/rating-agency changes that turn opacity into forced capital pressure.

## Source-quality caveats

- Statutory insurance filings and primary regulatory documents beat media summaries. Use annual statements / NAIC / state insurance filings / SEC filings when trade-relevant.
- Old SHADE `STATUS.md` contains March position rows and market prices. Treat them as historical until refreshed.
- Bank NDFI exposure numbers should be routed through REGINALD/BROCK/LIQUID owner docs before SHADE uses them as system-wide facts.

## SHADE forensic-discipline lessons

- **FHLB "borrowing capacity" ≠ the drawn book.** An FHLB figure on an insurer's IR liquidity slide is often *undrawn available capacity*, not advances outstanding. (6/21: baseline carried "$2.4B FHLB capacity"; the FI deck showed **$28B advances out / $38B pledged collateral** — the $2.4B was the undrawn line.) Always pull advances-outstanding + pledged collateral from the FI deck / statutory, and net encumbered assets out of the "highly liquid" cushion. Concrete instance of auto-memory `finding_number_carries_threshold_unit_source`.
- **A funding-spread threshold needs a peer-relative sub-row.** An absolute spread can sit in the green band while the issuer is the *widest* of its cohort. (6/21: Athene 5Y FABN T+123 = green absolutely, but +43–48bp vs IG peers = the load-bearing kill-path-1 canary.) The peer-relative penalty, not the absolute level, is the signal. Single-issuer-vs-peers variant of auto-memory `finding_blended_index_masks_bifurcation`.
- **Surveillance ≠ a rating action.** Authoritative quantification (Moody's/FSB/FSOC/Treasury/Proskauer) can rise in chorus while zero downgrades/negative-outlooks land on watchlist PE-insurers. Keep "rising attention" on the surveillance side of the green/yellow line until a rating-agency *action* or enforcement *escalation* actually fires. Catalyst ≠ consequence (`finding_catalyst_vs_consequence_conflation`).
- **APO price is not a SHADE stress gauge.** Athene is ~60% of Apollo equity value but the wrapper risk is a balance-sheet/funding/ratings mechanism that can crack while the equity holds. The March APO threshold band is a stale tape level; confirm the canonical APO mark with BROCK rather than carrying a fixed band as "green = no stress."
- **The Athene FABN maturity wall is private-by-construction — kill-path-1's quantum is 144A-invisible.** (6/22) Athene Global Funding is **not an SEC filer**; FABN is **100% 144A/Reg S**, so per-tranche principal exists only in private Pricing Supplements and **no public ladder can exist by design.** ⇒ **When a number cannot be sourced, establish WHY before hunting harder — "unsourceable" and "not yet found" need different work.** The recovery route was **holder-side**: NPORT-P fund filings reconstruct a floor the issuer never publishes. *(Sized figures live in `REFERENCE.md` §2.)*

- **The related-party disclosure line is a self-graded exam — treat it as self-reported, not measured.** (7/27) SSAP No. 25 identifies related-party investments where return is *"predominantly contingent on the performance of related parties"* — and **the filer defines the threshold.** Delaware Life defined "predominantly contingent" as **>50%**, and **$16.4B of affiliate-linked private credit sat in the general-account BOND line, outside the related-party disclosure, until a federal grand jury forced a re-look** (restatement: FY2024 **$2.26B → $11.56B = 5.1×**; FY2025 total **$17.24B = 37.6% of GA assets**). It was not hidden offshore and not in a captive — **classification alone did it.** **Consequence to SHADE's own instruments: Key Ratio #1 (Affiliated Reinsurance) and #3 (Capital Leakage) are built on this self-reported line and inherit its error bar.** Athene's related-party figures get re-labelled **self-reported/unverified-by-construction**, the same treatment as the 144A FABN wall — *not* a claim they're wrong. Kin to [[finding_private_by_construction_unverifiable]] but distinct: the FABN wall is invisible because it's **144A**; this is invisible because the **filer sets the test**.
- **Statutory-basis audits ALWAYS carry an "Adverse Opinion on U.S. GAAP" — it is boilerplate, never a finding.** (7/27) SAP ≠ GAAP, so every statutory-basis audit pairs an **unmodified statutory opinion** with an **adverse GAAP opinion**, and routinely calls the variances "material and pervasive." Reading it as a red flag would have manufactured a scandal out of a formatting convention. **The real audit signals are: whether the STATUTORY opinion is modified (Delaware Life's was NOT), and whether an Emphasis of Matter was added (it was).** Concrete instance of auto-memory `finding_read_the_artifacts_own_header_first`.
- **Distinguish a DISCLOSURE correction from a CREDIT event — the loudest number may carry no loss.** (7/27) Delaware Life's 5.1× restatement moved **no** write-down and **did not restate surplus**. "Impaired vs mis-labelled" stayed open. A 12× headline multiple with a grand jury attached reads like a solvency event and is, so far, an **accounting-classification** event. Ask "what was written down?" before letting a restatement into any default/mark/loss series. Kin to `finding_catalyst_vs_consequence_conflation`.
- **N-VPFS is a real but ISSUER-SPECIFIC window into statutory financials — check per issuer, never assume.** (7/27) Delaware Life bundles the **depositor insurer's full statutory financial statements** (with the related-party notes) into its separate-account **Form N-VPFS** filings, making a normally-off-EDGAR statutory note free-text-searchable. **Athene does NOT** — Athene Variable Annuity Account A and ALAC Separate Account 1 (both FY2025, filed 2026-04-30) carry **separate-account statements only**. So: worth checking for any insurer with variable-product separate accounts (Global Atlantic/Commonwealth, Corebridge/American General, Brighthouse, F&G all file N-VPFS) — **but the Athene Schedule-BA wall STANDS.** ⚠️ **And an EDGAR full-text screen for a statutory-note phrase is a FALSE ZERO as a cohort test** — it measures *which insurers bundle statutory financials onto EDGAR*, not which insurers use the practice; most statutory financials aren't on EDGAR at all (`finding_discovery_tool_wrong_slice_false_zero`).

- **⚠️ PRE-REGISTER AGAINST THE INSTRUMENT'S PUBLICATION CADENCE, NOT THE EVENT DATE.** A frozen card can be right about everything except *when the artifact appears*, and then it grades NO-VERDICT-BY-CONSTRUCTION. (8/4) Leg 1 was registered on Athene's earnings date; Athene publishes the FABN spread/peer table **only** in a standalone quarterly **Fixed Income Investor Presentation** on its own Item-7.01 cadence — **14-for-14, never on an earnings date.** ⚠️ **And the trap inside the trap: the absence-is-a-datum band FIRED, and its consequence text was unsupportable** — absence on an earnings date is the norm, so routing it as perimeter opacity would have manufactured a signal. ⇒ **Before registering a test, verify the instrument (a) EXISTS, (b) will SPEAK inside the window, and (c) CAN answer the question asked.** *(Worked example → `archive/STATUS_PRE-ROTATION_2026-08-28.md` §0g.)*
- **A deal's registered resolution instrument may already have spoken in an EARLIER filing — check the prior period before declaring a test unresolved.** (8/4) Athene's **Q1-26** 10-Q already said *"**we** entered into a definitive agreement… closed on April 24, 2026"* in its own name with no designation/portion language — a second primary, found only because the earlier filing was re-read. **A weak prior, never evidence.** *(Deal-specific state lives in `REFERENCE.md` §2 and the §2.8 thread, not here.)*

- **🔑 ALLOCATION DISCRETION is SHADE's unifying mechanism — the categories are set by the entities being measured.** (7/27, from two independent findings the same day) The filer sets the related-party test (**SSAP 25**, self-defined ">50%" — Delaware Life); the **buyer** sets the landing entity (**§2.8**, above); and the **measurement systems** set the perimeters (Moody's $807B illiquid vs Chicago Fed $849B life-insurer PC vs MBA $775B CM/MF — **three unreconciled perimeters that must never be summed**; the first two likely measure substantially the same balance sheets). **This is a stronger claim than "the data is incomplete."** It is why no "combined insurer sink" total can be stated, and it should be SHADE's through-line rather than any headline size. Corollary for framing: **the sink currently absorbs at PAR (99.7%), not at a discount — so the exposure is invisible FUTURE deterioration, not hidden PRESENT impairment.** Detail: `research/COMBINED_INSURER_SINK_WELD2_2026-07-27.md`.
- **Insurer-flow series are seasonal — never baseline a falsifier on the latest single quarter.** (7/27) MBA life-insurer CM/MF net change: **H1-2025 +$4.4B across two quarters** vs **H2-2025 +$23.6B** (5.4×); Q3-25 **+$12.1B**, Q4-25 **+$11.5B**, Q1-26 **+$3.3B**. A falsifier written as *"materially above the +$3.3B Q1 run-rate"* is anchored to the **seasonal trough** and resolves TRUE on a perfectly normal +$11B print. Stack four quarters before setting any insurer-flow threshold. Transferable kernel promoted to auto-memory: [[finding_seasonal_trough_baseline_resolves_true_on_normal]].

- **When testing an anomaly, hunt for SAME-PERIOD, SIZE-MATCHED controls inside the same filing before reaching for outside data.** (7/27) The DLIC accrued-interest anomaly (9.77% accrued-to-book on a 2025 CSLAC purchase) looked like it needed CUSIP-level data to test. It did not — **the same filing disclosed five other affiliate securities transactions** (CSLAC 2023 **0.88%** / 2024 **1.03%**, Barbco 2024 **0.43%**, GLIC 2025 purchase **1.86%**, and a **nearly size-matched same-year GLIC sale at 0.95%**). Those five controls killed the rate-environment, accounting-change and year-end-timing explanations in one pass, and the *mechanism* (deferred-interest trust notes) was named elsewhere in the same document. **Read the whole note for siblings of the transaction before declaring an anomaly unresolvable.**

- **When a threshold is wrong, the defect is almost always the BASELINE, not the level — and the fix usually inherits the same error inverted.** (7/27, caught in both directions inside one session) CREED baselined `PRED-006` on **one prior quarter** (+$3.3B) — a seasonal trough, so a normal +$11B print would have resolved it TRUE carrying zero information. I proposed **~+$20B** instead, anchored to **Q3/Q4 (+$11–12B)** — but those are **H2** quarters and **Q2 is an H1 quarter**, so my bar would have resolved FALSE **even if the entire $9B landed.** A false negative reading as *"the thesis is refuted"* is worse than the false positive it replaced. **Rule: anchor to a DISTRIBUTION and to the MATCHING SEASON, and state the baseline's percentile. Then check the counterfactual explicitly — "what does this print if the event does NOT happen, and what if it fully does?" Both bars failed that one-line test.** Also: **a threshold that derives its delta by subtracting a previously-recorded stock is fragile** — MBA rounds to the nearest $B and revises priors, so measure the change **as printed**. Transferable kernel: [[finding_seasonal_trough_baseline_resolves_true_on_normal]].

- **A self-reported metric can be independently rebuilt from holder filings — test that before declaring a refresh impossible.** (7/27, re-confirmed 8/28) I told Will an independent FABN refresh could not be done before the print; **122 NPORT-P filings later it could, and it bracketed the deck.** The control that makes it valid: **only funds holding Athene AND ≥1 peer in the SAME filing**, so pricing-vendor differences cancel. **8/28 rerun: Athene T+112.0 vs the deck's T+110, from unrelated data.** ⇒ **Holder-side filings are a standing route to any issuer-reported security metric.**
- **Report every estimator when they disagree, and carry the strictest.** (7/27) The same FABN dataset gave **+55.4bp** pooled, **+58.8bp** per-CUSIP, and **+40.2bp** within-fund paired. Quoting the pooled number would have been the most dramatic and the least defensible. **The within-fund paired design is strictest, so it is what SHADE carries — along with its dispersion (11/15 positive, IQR −1 to +52), because 4 funds showing zero-or-negative is part of the finding.** Corollary that saved a bad citation: **always check unique-instrument counts before quoting a bucket** — the 6–11y "penalty" rested on **4 holdings of ONE CUSIP.** One bond is not a tenor bucket.

Confidence must not travel by adjacency.** *(Banked fleet-wide as `finding_asymmetric_rigor_counterparty_claims`; local detail rotated 2026-08-28.)*

## 2026-08-28 — lessons from the Delaware Life distribution pause

- 🔑 **AN ESCALATION LADDER BUILT ONLY FROM OFFICIAL ACTIONS IS BLIND TO ITS OWN FIRST OBSERVABLE.** All six markers of my 7/27 Delaware Life ladder were acts of a **regulator, a rating agency, or the issuer**. When two banks paused distribution on 8/28, **the ladder had no rung for it — and a counterparty decision is structurally the FIRST thing that happens**, because it needs no finding, no filing, no charge and no rating committee, only a compliance officer acting on the public record everyone already has. ⇒ **When registering an escalation ladder, ask who each marker's ACTOR is. If every actor is an official body or the subject itself, the ladder cannot see the fastest-moving class of evidence.** (`finding_hypothesis_needs_an_instrument_for_its_defining_mechanism`, applied to a ladder rather than a metric.) 🚩 **PROMOTION CANDIDATE — flagged to PROME, not appended to auto-memory** (this file's standing practice at the byte cap): it generalizes past insurers to any "what happens next" list assembled from formal actions, which inherits the formal actors' latency.
- ⚠️ **A NEW RUNG IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY A PRECURSOR TO AN OLD ONE.** The tempting read — *"a distribution pause precedes a downgrade"* — was offered to me and I declined it. **The carriers report that the PROBE cast a shadow over ratings; nobody published a link from the PAUSE to a rating action.** Registering it as a precursor would have fused two true facts with a premise of my own and quietly converted an observation into a forecast. ⇒ **Add the rung as INDEPENDENT unless the ordering itself is sourced.** (`finding_fused_true_facts_false_premise` — caught before it shipped this time.)
- 🔑 **WHEN A VECTOR IS ALREADY AT ITS SCALE MAXIMUM, A REAL EVENT PRODUCES A BASIS CHANGE, NOT A LEVEL CHANGE — and saying so is the honest output.** Vector #1 sat at 5-of-5 and the pause could not move it; the pressure is to find *some* number to move so the event looks like it registered. **What changed was the CLASS of evidence — the vector's first third-party BEHAVIOURAL confirmation after a month on a disclosure-and-investigation record alone.** ⇒ **Record the class change explicitly and leave the score alone.** A composite that only moves on levels will silently under-record exactly the evidence that is hardest to get.
- ⚠️ **"THE CHANNEL NARROWED" AND "FLOWS MOVED" ARE DIFFERENT CLAIMS, and only one had a source.** No carrier published a redemption, surrender, sales or AUM figure; *"regulatory margin call"* and *"flows going the wrong way"* were the **relayer's**, stripped by WALTER at intake. ⇒ **When an event is reported as a DECISION, the quantity it implies is usually unpublished — name the missing figure and where it would be read, rather than letting the decision stand in for it.** Here that named a **dated** instrument (Q3 statutory annuity considerations, ~11/15) and exposed that **it sits behind an access gate I already owed work on** — which is how a news event promoted a 9/30 obligation to first place.
- ⚠️ **n=2 IS NOT A CHANNEL.** Two named distributors of an unknown total is not "distribution is closing," and **the paused SHARE — the one number that would make it actionable — does not exist publicly.** ⇒ **State the denominator problem in the same breath as the finding, and pre-register the refutation** (here: probes close with no charges AND a distributor resumes ⇒ the rung reverts and is recorded as a **false positive**, not quietly dropped).
- ✅ **A CORRECTION ON ANOTHER DESK'S INSTRUMENT CAN AUDIT YOUR OWN — take the read-across, and be willing to find you already passed.** WALTER/CREED's `-021-CORRECTION` was that a **SHARE plateaued while the DOLLARS more than doubled** on a denominator that moved 2.3×. **My canary's S2 is that exact shape** (unsecured share over a funding stock that grew +$8.7B in six months) — and **the spec already required S1 and S2 as a placed PAIR, never a composite scalar**, so the answer was a design confirmation, not a fix. ⇒ **Run the read-across anyway: "we already handle that" is worth nothing until checked.** Standing rule: **never quote S2 without S1.**
- ⚠️ **A SESSION DELTA INSIDE A STATE FILE IS THE ACCRETION MECHANISM.** STATUS reached **125,359 B = 231% of the harness read cap** *while* a "retire the oldest §0 delta each closeout" rule was in force and being obeyed — each new delta simply arrived bigger than the one retired. ⇒ **The rule that holds: the session delta lives in `research/`; the state file carries only the verdict and the live rails.** ⇒ **And a compression pass must MEASURE, not estimate — three of my passes each *felt* large and delivered 93–239 B**, because the compressed prose was as long as what it replaced. **Measure per section, cut the biggest block structurally, re-measure.** 🚩 **PROMOTION CANDIDATE — flagged to PROME** (fleet-wide: 16/37 desks were over the cap).
- ⚠️ **A POINTER SATISFIES A PRESENCE AUDIT WHILE STARVING THE READER** — rotating §2 out whole would have left every downstream cite aimed at an archive file. **Fix: restate the load-bearing NUMBERS in full (source, date, handling rule) and point only the NARRATIVE at the cold copy.** (`finding_required_field_satisfied_by_a_pointer_passes_every_presence_audit`.)
- ✅ **VERIFY A VERBATIM ROTATION WITH A CHECKSUM — the first attempt will probably be wrong.** My banner claimed `tail -n +8` reproduced the original; it was **6 lines, not 7**, so the check silently dropped the original's first line (**STATUS short by exactly 15 B = `# SHADE STATUS\n`**). Caught only because I computed the crc32 instead of trusting the claim. ⇒ **State the integrity check as a runnable command AND run it before committing.** **A rotation banner that lies about its own offset is worse than none: it certifies a corrupted archive.** 🚩 **PROMOTION CANDIDATE — flagged to PROME.**

## 2026-08-28 (touch 2) — the enumeration lessons

- 🔑 **THE SESSION'S UNIFYING DEFECT: I ENUMERATED THREE INSTRUMENTS AND TESTED NONE OF THEM.** In one day: ① an **escalation ladder** whose six markers were all official actions, so it could not receive the counterparty event that actually happened; ② a **"the Q3 flow figure is GATED" registration**, made after listing three gated routes and never asking whether an ungated one existed — the issuer publishes the statement free, and it was on its website while I wrote that it was unreachable; ③ a **trigger cited across five surfaces for 15 days with no locus in my own charter.** **All three were LISTS I had written and then treated as facts about the world.** ⇒ **A list you authored is a claim about your own search, not about what exists.** Before recording that a thing is absent, gated, or exhaustively covered, **name the test that would show otherwise and run it.** 🚩 **PROMOTION CANDIDATE — flagged to PROME.**

LOOK AT THE ISSUER FIRST.** `[[finding_unfetched_is_not_unavailable]]` — **second instance in 15 days**, the first being `fetch.py --history`.

- ⚠️ **REPORT THE PAIR WHENEVER A RATIO AND ITS NUMERATOR CAN DISAGREE — and expect them to.** Delaware Life's affiliate-contingent book went **UP 2.75% in dollars** and **DOWN 2.85pp as a share of the general account**, because the denominator grew 11.6%. **Both are true and they support opposite headlines about whether a regulator-mandated remediation plan is working.** ⇒ **When the answer decides whether someone is complying, the metric's own definition is the finding — say which one the counterparty is measured on, or say that it is not public.** *(Adopted from WALTER/CREED's `-021-CORRECTION` the same day, then found on my own primary vector within hours — which is the argument for taking read-acrosses seriously even when you think your instrument already handles it.)*

The scary half is the searchable half.**
