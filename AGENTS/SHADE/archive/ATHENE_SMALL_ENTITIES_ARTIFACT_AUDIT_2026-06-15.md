## Verdict

- **AANY is the clearest small statutory entity risk read:** large Schedule D book with fair-value deficit, rapidly rising Schedule BA from YE to Q1, meaningful ceded/funds-withheld reinsurance to unauthorized reinsurers, Apollo-affiliated asset management, but no visible FHLB/repo/borrowed/capital-note funding stack.
- **ALRE is dominated by reinsurance/funds-held mechanics, not Schedule D/BA disclosure:** Bermuda return gives high-level asset/liability lines; extracted text does not support a US-style invested-asset audit.
- **ALIRT is useful for AARe proportional allocation/story, not for exact statutory schedule audit:** top-line and ACRA reconciliation are usable; detailed asset-category figures are a blocker due to broken table extraction.
