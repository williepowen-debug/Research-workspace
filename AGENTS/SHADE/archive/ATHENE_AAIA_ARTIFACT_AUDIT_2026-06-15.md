# AAIA statutory artifact audit — 2025 annual + Q1 2026 selected extracts

Sources used: `tmp_aaia_extract/AAIA_2025_Statement.pdf.selected.txt` and `tmp_aaia_extract/AAIA_Q1_2026_Statement.pdf.selected.txt`. Did **not** run full extraction on 9,612-page annual PDF.

## Key figures / findings

- **Bonds / Schedule D scale:** 2025 annual reports Bonds (Schedule D) **$158.852B net admitted** vs $130.962B prior year (`2025:17`). Q1 2026 reports bonds **$162.540B net admitted** vs $158.852B at YE2025 (`Q1:17`).
- **Schedule BA / other invested assets:** 2025 annual reports Other invested assets (Schedule BA) **$17.387B net admitted** on $17.462B gross, with $74.7MM nonadmitted (`2025:36`). Q1 2026 rises to **$17.935B net admitted** on $18.012B gross, with $77.1MM nonadmitted (`Q1:36`).
- **PBBD / Schedule D→BA migration:** 2025 adoption moved securities from Schedule D Part 1 to Schedule BA: pre-adoption BACV **$1.6485B general account + $229.0MM separate accounts**; post-adoption lower-of-cost/FV amount **$872.1MM general account + $127.7MM separate accounts**; surplus impact not material (`2025:755-766`). This matters because some “bond-like” exposure is now BA.
- **AAA / Apollo Aligned Alternatives permitted practice:** Iowa permitted AAIA to account for **Apollo Aligned Alternatives Aggregator, L.P. (AAA)** via look-through as if AAIA owned underlying investments directly (`2025:604-608`). Impact: carrying value reduced **$212.3MM** at 1/1/25; capital/surplus reduced **$8.1MM after reinsurance** initially; YE2025 cumulative surplus reduced **$263.0MM before reinsurance**, but **no surplus impact after reinsurance** and net income increased **$8.1MM after reinsurance** (`2025:609-614`, `2025:785-787`).
- **Deposit-type contracts / funding agreements:** 2025 liability for deposit-type contracts **$64.260B**, including **$40.788B Modco reserve** (`2025:96`). Exhibit 7 reconciles to **$64.260B net after reinsurance** (`2025:378-383`) and separately shows FHLB funding agreements reported as GICs / deposit-type contracts only **$568.8MM** (`2025:384-390`). Q1 2026 deposit-type contracts rise to **$65.313B**, including **$40.449B Modco reserve** (`Q1:98`).
- **FHLB / borrowed money:** Balance sheet borrowed money is blank/zero in both 2025 and Q1 2026 (`2025:135`, `Q1:136`). Note says AAIA is FHLB Des Moines member, can borrow, but had **no outstanding borrowings** at YE2025/YE2024; FHLB funding agreements produced **$22.9769B liability** at YE2025 and are accounted for under SSAP 52 deposit-type contracts, not borrowed money (`2025:3416-3427`). This creates an apparent distinction: operational borrowings = none; FHLB investment-spread funding agreements = large.
- **Repurchase liabilities / secured funding:** 2025 write-in liability **Repurchase Agreement Liability $5.046B** vs $5.626B prior year (`2025:168`); Q1 2026 falls to **$3.154B** (`Q1:169`). Policy note: AAIA lends bonds to unaffiliated financial institutions and receives cash collateral; securities may be sold/re-pledged (`2025:1725-1736`). Restricted asset table shows securities subject to repos **$6.429B** (`2025:2339`, `2025:2537`), plus separate-account repo footnote says separate account sold bonds FV **$110.0MM** and received **$90.2MM cash** at YE2025 (`2025:4742-4746`).
- **Reinsurance / funds withheld / MODCO:** Asset lines show funds held by/deposited with reinsured companies **$2.766B** and other reinsurance receivables **$225.1MM net** at YE2025 (`2025:51-54`), rising to **$2.982B** and **$672.4MM net** at Q1 (`Q1:51-54`). Liability lines show funds held under unauthorized/certified reinsurers **$1.151B** and funds held under coinsurance **$65.544B** at YE2025 (`2025:139-144`), rising to **$1.141B** and **$67.867B** at Q1 (`Q1:140-145`). Restricted assets include **assets held under modco reinsurance agreements $176.261B** and **funds withheld reinsurance agreements $66.319B** (`2025:2352-2353`, `2025:2550-2551`).
- **Ceded reinsurance to affiliates:** Note 10 says AAIA cedes quota share of annuity/funding agreement business to parent **Athene Annuity Re Ltd. (AARe), Bermuda**, cedes **100% of Closed Block liabilities to Athene Re IV**, and cedes structured annuity benefits to **Structured Annuity Reinsurance Company**; it points to Schedule S/Y (`2025:3344-3347`). Note 23 says AAIA has modified coinsurance agreements with AARe covering in-force/future funding agreements and annuity business; 2025 amendments consolidated treaties; disclosed modified coinsurance reserves **$31.9MM** and **$51.2MM** for two treaties (`2025:4110-4117`). Annual reserve analysis shows reinsurance ceded **$70.147B** against annuity reserves (`2025:4310-4313`) and **$217.1MM** against deposit-type contracts (`2025:4448-4451`).
- **Parent/subsidiary/affiliate balances:** 2025 assets include affiliate receivables **$4.553MM** (`2025:63`) and affiliate payables **$142.863MM** (`2025:141`). Note 10D confirms **$4.6MM receivable** and **$142.9MM payable**, settled within 90 days under intercompany expense sharing (`2025:3359-3362`). Q1 2026 affiliate receivables increase to **$17.851MM** (`Q1:63`) and payables to **$177.558MM** (`Q1:142`).
- **Apollo affiliate / org references:** AAIA has investment management agreement with affiliate **Apollo Insurance Solutions Group, LP**, paying **30 bps per annum** on managed assets; expense **$894.3MM in 2025** vs $689.7MM in 2024 (`2025:3363-3366`). Q1 Schedule Y org chart explicitly includes Apollo Global Management / Apollo Operating Group / Apollo Funds and states Apollo Funds are entities managed by Apollo Operating Group (`Q1:606-612`), with long Apollo fund list in Part 1A.
- **Schedule BA non-registered private fund FE certification:** Q1 general interrogatory says AAIA answered **No** to assigning FE to Schedule BA non-registered private funds complying with criteria (`Q1:312-322`). I did not find the same annual interrogatory in the selected annual extract.
- **Mortgage foreclosure data:** Annual mortgage note includes “Mortgage loans derecognized as a result of foreclosure” but the selected extract omits values (`2025:1025-1031`). Another table shows **mortgages in process of foreclosure $19.839MM / $21.987MM / $55.059MM** across displayed columns (`2025:4042-4046`); column headers are truncated in selected text, so treat as directional only without PDF spot-check.
- **Schedule S / ceded reinsurance:** Selected extract includes Note 10/23 summaries and Schedule S references but not full Schedule S ceded rows. Ceded reinsurance report includes “No” answers to several interrogatories and estimated aggregate surplus reduction from termination of all reinsurance agreements **$0** (`2025:4197-4207`). Full Schedule S counterparty-level cessions are **not visible** in the selected artifact.

## Verdict: supports vs complicates Apollo/Athene defense

- **Supports defense:** No balance-sheet borrowed money; FHLB operational borrowings disclosed as none; affiliate receivables/payables are small relative to balance sheet and contractually settled within 90 days; FE certification answer is “No”; no affiliated captive reinsurer disclosures in Note 23 (`2025:4164-4170`).
- **Complicates defense:** Defense cannot simply say “plain vanilla insurer balance sheet.” AAIA has huge related-party/reinsurance architecture: $64-65B deposit-type liabilities, $40B+ Modco reserve inside deposit contracts, $176B Modco-restricted assets, $66B funds-withheld assets, $65-68B funds held under coinsurance liabilities, $22.98B FHLB funding agreement liability accounted as deposit-type contracts, $3-5B repo liability, and a permitted-practice look-through for Apollo Aligned Alternatives managed by an affiliate. The structure looks highly engineered even where stat treatment is disclosed and compliant.
