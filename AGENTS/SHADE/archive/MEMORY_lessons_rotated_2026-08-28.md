# SHADE — rotated lessons (thesis/deal-specific)

- **Statutory-disclosure visibility tracks PRODUCT MIX, not materiality — and state examination is not the forcing function SHADE assumed.** (7/27) Two lessons from the same event. **(a)** Delaware Life's related-party restatement is public **only because it sells variable products**, which forced its statutory financials into an SEC N-VPFS filing. **Clear Spring Life — same parent, same subpoena, same restatement — writes fixed/FIA only, has no separate accounts, no EDGAR registration, and is therefore invisible.** Before concluding an insurer "didn't disclose," check whether it has any SEC-filing obligation at all. **(b)** The Delaware DOI's **multi-state statutory examination of Clear Spring (as of 12/31/2023, adopted 6/23/2025) reported "no significant findings" and "no recommendations"**, and **AM Best revised the outlook to POSITIVE on 10/10/2025** — then a **Feb-2026 federal grand jury subpoena** produced a 5.1× related-party restatement. **Neither the examiner nor the rating agency found it.** This directly weakens kill-path #2 (AG 55 forced disclosure), which assumes supervision eventually forces disclosure: the realistic discovery channels here were **prosecutorial or product-mix-accidental**. Do not treat a clean exam or an affirmed/upgraded rating as evidence an affiliated-asset book is sound.

- **A fully-disclosed affiliated transfer can still be silent on WHERE the assets landed — check the assignment clause, not just the price and the vote.** (7/27) ARI→Athene is disclosed on every axis SHADE scores (8-K, special committee with BofA Securities + Fried Frank, stockholder vote 4/21/26, cleared at **99.7% of commitments**) — and the Purchase Agreement's **§2.8 "Designation of Buyer Affiliates"** lets the buyer designate *"one or more of its **Affiliates, Managed Accounts or Portfolio Companies**"* to acquire *"**all or any portion** of the Assets"* **by private written notice 10 business days before closing**, with **Athene Co-Invest Reinsurance Affiliate Holding Ltd. / Holding 2 Ltd. named as Buyer subsidiaries.** **The split was never disclosed and no public filing carries it.** So the benchmark is **four-of-five** (mechanism/size/disclosure/price ✅, landing entity ❌). **Practical rule: for any insurer asset acquisition, read the assignment/designation clause of the purchase agreement — it determines statutory entity, regulatory regime, RBC treatment, and whether the exposure is countable in ANY US series at all.** ⚠️ Routine structuring — **not** a conduct allegation. ⚠️ Primary: **Athene Holding Ltd. is a DELAWARE corporation**; the offshore question lives at the **ACRA** layer, not the parent — do not let "Athene = Bermuda" propagate.

- **A "past due" test cannot detect contractually DEFERRED interest — and the reassuring number will look like it can.** (7/27) Delaware Life non-admits accrued investment income *"over 90 days past due or where collection is uncertain"*, and reported only **$225 (2025) / $46 (2024)** against a **$45.9B** general account and **$2.45B** of NII. That reads as near-total collectibility. **But the company also holds "trust notes that allow for deferred interest" and recorded $159,580 of deferred interest in 2025 — and deferral is the contract, so such interest is NEVER past due and can never trip the non-admission test, however doubtful the ultimate collection.** **Rule: before citing a delinquency/non-admission statistic as evidence a book performs, ask what fraction of the book is contractually incapable of becoming delinquent.** Kin to the SSAP-25 self-graded test above: both are cases where the *test's own definition* excludes the exposure you care about.

- **Retire your own flags out loud when they fail.** (7/27) The accrued-interest anomaly was **my** 7/27 flag, and it did not survive its own test — it has a disclosed contractual explanation and PIK moved the *opposite* way (−55.9%). Retired explicitly in STATUS, SCRATCH and `board_log.tsv` rather than quietly dropped. **What replaced it was better than the original claim** (the undisclosed deferred-interest denominator; 96% of the $16.37B structurally undescribed) — which is the usual pattern, and the reason retiring cleanly costs nothing.

- **A binary bar over a partitionable quantity cannot distinguish "partial" from "none."** (7/27) `PRED-006` fires at ≥+$10.0B on a $9B transfer — but **§2.8 permits designating *"all or any portion"*** to different entities, so a ~$4–5B partial landing prints ~+$7–9B, resolves FALSE, and looks **identical to full exclusion.** **Whenever a threshold tests an event that can happen fractionally, say what fraction the bar actually detects, and phrase the negative branch as "wholly or largely not X" rather than "not X."**

- **⚠️ Read the methodology note before using a published series as a perimeter.** (7/27) I used MBA's **$775B life-insurer line** as *"insurer CRE exposure"* in a document about measurement integrity. **MBA attributes holdings to whoever holds the NOTE** — so insurer-held **CMBS sits in the CMBS/CDO/ABS bucket (~$637B), not the insurer bucket.** $775B is a **FLOOR on insurer CRE exposure, not a measure of it**, and the two buckets split by **the legal form of the instrument, not by holder type** — an insurer buying a CMBS bond prints as CMBS. ⚠️ **I had extracted that exact passage myself and did not act on it: a read-it-and-missed-it, not a data gap.** **Rule: when a series is load-bearing for a perimeter claim, read its attribution rule and ask "could my subject appear in a different bucket?"** Kin to `finding_read_the_artifacts_own_header_first`.

- **n=1 on the null-hypothesis case supports nothing.** (7/27) I wrote *"the sink is absorbing at PAR, not at a discount"* off ARI→Athene's **99.7%**. But that is **one transaction**, on **performing** paper — and **performing paper clearing at par is the null hypothesis, not a finding.** The alarming version would need *impaired* paper clearing at par. Worse, the datum's owner had logged 99.7% as a **counter**-datum against "CRE marks are fictional," so my use **inverted the role he gave it**. **Rule: before a datum carries a general claim, ask (a) what the null outcome would look like, and (b) what the originating agent recorded it AS.** The mechanism survived once decoupled from price entirely.

## Second rotation pass, 2026-08-28 closeout (touch 2) — five 8/13 lessons whose live form now lives elsewhere

- ⚠️ **"Could not verify at primary" must distinguish MY QUERY MISSED IT from THE ISSUER DIDN'T DISCLOSE IT.** The 8/4 proxy flagged $34.5B FABN / $28.2B FHLB / $2.0B FABN issuance as unverifiable after searching the Q1 10-Q. **All three were in that filing** — two in Note 11, one **MD&A-only**. Nine days of a false "undisclosed" flag. **Fix: search MD&A separately from the notes, and grep the bare program name (`FABN`) rather than a phrase.** (`finding_verification_zero_is_ambiguous`.)

- ⚠️ **A price-only canary is confounded by supply withdrawal.** Athene's FABN peer penalty **narrowed 11.7bp** in the same six months FABN issuance fell to ~$1.2B/qtr and the program **shrank**. A secondary spread is a price struck on a float; withdrawing supply mechanically supports it. **"Credit improved" and "the issuer stopped issuing" are indistinguishable on this instrument.** Same class as the Q9b identification defect — no number of quarters fixes it; only a different instrument does.

- ✅ **A well-constructed calendar binary names a date the instrument is guaranteed to speak on.** Egan-Jones 8/12 was the **§15E(a)(2)(B)(iii) statutory deadline** — the SEC ruled on the deadline day. Contrast the 8/4 leg-1 defect, which named a date the FI deck was never going to appear on. **Prefer statutory/contractual deadlines over "earnings season" windows.**

- ⚠️ **Denial of a NEW application ≠ revocation of an EXISTING registration.** SEC `34-106092` denied Egan-Jones in classes (iv) ABS and (v) government/muni; **EJR retains (i)/(ii)/(iii) — including INSURANCE COMPANIES — held since 2007.** The A-CAP/777 private-credit ratings sit in the retained classes. **The kill-path-3 ladder did not advance**, and the Commission **expressly declined to reach** the integrity question (fn.23). Adjacent-fact drift guard: the **Josh Wander / 777 indictment is 777's principal, not Egan-Jones.**

- ❌ **I recorded a "tooling gap" that did not exist, and PROME caught it within the hour.** I ran `fetch.py history <TICKER>`, got an error, grepped the script's subcommand dispatch, saw only `price`/`prices`/`fred`, and wrote *"no history/closes subcommand — a standing hole"* into **five** surfaces plus a PROME ask. **The flag was in the tool's own usage line: `fetch.py price <TICKER> --history N`.** `finding_unfetched_is_not_unavailable`. **Rule adopted: before recording ANY tool or source as LACKING a capability, read its usage/help output — a failed invocation is evidence about my syntax, not about the tool.** A false *unavailable* claim is worse than silence: it propagates into other agents' plans and manufactures backlog work that isn't needed. **Same class as the 8/4 "could not verify at primary" flag** — that was also *my query missed it*, not *the issuer didn't disclose it*. **Twice in one session, both times a failed lookup written up as an absence in the world.**


- 🔑 **A band derived as a PERCENTAGE of an unverified quantity must be registered as the RATIO, not as a LEVEL.** The M-11 leg-2 LANDED floor was frozen as "$7.0B ≈ 78% of $9B." The deal turned out to be **$8.7B** (ATH Q2 10-Q, first time priced). 78% × $8.7B = $6.786B, and Δ = $6.897B = **79.3%** — so the **letter** graded PARTIAL while the floor's own **stated intent** graded LANDED, on a $103M margin. A level silently hard-codes an unverified input; a ratio resolves its denominator at grade time. **Grade the letter anyway** — but report the divergence at every surface, or "PARTIAL" propagates as "the assets didn't show up" when 79% of the book was sitting in the registered line. *(Promotion candidate for auto-memory — flagged to PROME rather than appended, since `MEMORY.md` is near its byte cap.)*

- ⚠️ **Peer-set composition is chosen by the entity being measured.** Athene added **LNC** (its widest peer, T+89) to the Q2 comparison, cutting the reported penalty by **3.0bp**. Immaterial to the band and no accusation is warranted — but **always compute like-for-like against the prior edition's peer set and quote both.** Fifth instance of the **allocation-discretion** through-line.

- ⚠️ **A dated claim's CITATION rots even when its WORDING is right.** I cited my own trigger as "registered, verbatim" from a SCRATCH line that sits inside a preserved historical block whose adjacent text was stale by 10bp and a full sign-leg state. The wording was correct; the locus was a restatement. **And the deeper defect it exposed: the trigger has no canonical home in my domain spec at all** — `CLAUDE.md` carries a *different* HY OAS system. ⇒ **Before citing your own registration, check the instrument HAS one. A threshold that lives only in deltas and handoffs is not registered — it is remembered.**
