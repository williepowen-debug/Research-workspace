## 3. ⚠️ WHAT WOULD **NOT** MOVE SHADE — written down now so it cannot be re-argued Wednesday

- **A NAV decline of ~1–2% QoQ.** That is **in line with Q1's −1.76%**, not an acceleration. Continuation ≠ deterioration. *(This is the most likely outcome and the easiest to over-read.)*
- **Realized losses from exiting known-bad positions.** That is cleanup, not re-marking. Only **unrealized** marks speak to carrying-value staleness.
- **A dividend cut.** Painful, headline-grabbing, and a **BROCK** signal — but it is a *distribution-policy* decision, not an asset-value measurement. It does not by itself change any insurer's Schedule-BA carry.
- **The stock falling.** ARCC already trades at **0.964× NAV**; equity moves are sentiment, and per MEMORY the same logic that bars APO as a SHADE stress gauge bars ARCC.
- **Any single-name blowup in the portfolio.** SHADE needs the *pool*, not an idiosyncratic credit.
- **Commentary about "spread compression" or "competitive markets."** That is a return story, not a marks story.

## 4. ⚠️ THE BETA TRAP — the one way this print could produce a false arm

**The wrapper-decoupling trigger is a two-part AND: HY OAS >280 SUSTAINED (5+ sessions) *AND* the wrapper basket (ARCC/FSK/OBDC/BIZD) leading managers (APO/ARES) DOWN.**

**HY OAS is 279 — one basis point under the level leg.** A bad ARCC print could easily push HY through 280 on a general risk-off.

**That would NOT arm the trigger, and I am recording why before it happens:**
- A single-session cross is **not** "sustained 5+ sessions."
- A broad risk-off moves wrappers **and** managers together — the sign leg requires wrappers to lead **DOWN specifically**, which is the signature of *collateral re-marking* rather than *multiple compression*.
- Pre-registered from 7/9 and unchanged: **a beta-driven re-approach to 280 does not satisfy the sign leg.**

**The statutory dig stays holstered unless both legs are met on their own terms.**
