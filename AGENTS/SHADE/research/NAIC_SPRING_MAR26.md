## KEY OUTCOMES

### 1. PBR Reinvestment Guardrails ADOPTED ✅ (Material)
**What:** NAIC Life Actuarial Task Force adopted Amendment Proposal Form 2025-16
**Details:**
- New reinvestment assumptions for principle-based reserves (PBR)
- Baseline: BBB-rated bond yields + 0.5% illiquidity premium
- Applies uniformly across life insurance and annuity products
- **Specifically includes PRT (Pension Risk Transfer) segment** — regardless of insurer's investment strategy
- Additional draft guardrails for PRT reviewed but not yet finalized

**Why it matters for SHADE:**
- Athene is a dominant PRT player (pension risk transfer)
- Athene's competitive advantage = higher-yielding alternative assets in reserves (vs. the BBB benchmark)
- If reserves must be calculated against BBB + 0.5% illiquidity premium, Athene's investment spread advantage is structurally constrained
- Offshore reinsurance arrangements cited specifically as "heightened regulatory scrutiny" area
- This tightens the screws on exactly the structures AG 55 is designed to expose

**Opposition noted:** American Academy of Actuaries raised concerns about applying BBB baseline across all strategies. One regulator questioned whether lower-rated benchmarks appropriately reflect statutory conservatism. Rule faced pushback but was **adopted over objections.**

### 5. NAIC Transparency Controversy (New Risk Factor)
**What happened:**
- Open meetings policy formally challenged at Spring Meeting
- David Snyder (APCIA): NAIC becoming "quasi-legislative" entity with key decisions made behind closed doors
- Consumer advocates (Peter Gould, Blood Cancer United) called for livestreaming + 1-year recording retention
- Michigan's Anita G. Fox defended closed sessions for regulatory complexity
- NAIC undergoing formal open meetings policy review

**Why it matters:**
- Regulatory decisions affecting Athene/Apollo may be happening in closed sessions
- PRT-specific guardrails "under review" — final version not yet public
- AG 55 implementation details may be less transparent than expected
- **Risk to thesis:** Regulatory capture possible in opaque settings; if PE industry lobbies effectively in closed sessions, enforcement teeth may be pulled
