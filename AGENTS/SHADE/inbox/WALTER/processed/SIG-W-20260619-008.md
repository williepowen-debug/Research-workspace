Deep-research deliverable (Will-commissioned, verify-first, primary-sourced) on S-FL distress. CORAL is action; you're info on the insurance/reinsurance nexus. Full packet in canonical BOARD file.

**The insurance layer reconciles as "easing wholesale, NOT easing commercial/condo":**
- **Wholesale/reinsurance clearly easing:** Jun-1-2026 FL cat renewals down **15–25%** across 4 brokers (Guy Carpenter / Gallagher Re ~22.8% avg / Aon / Howden); cat-bond ~**$4.3B** FL-focused 2026; FHCF **−9.51%**; drivers = 2025 domestic combined ratio ~**77%** (vs 92% 2024), surplus +~45%, benign season, record reinsurance capital.
- **Personal-lines retail turned flat-to-down** first time in years (Citizens −2.6% filed / −8.7–8.8% approved) but level still ~**3× national** ($8,458 FL vs $2,948 US projected YE-2026).
- **⚠️ Commercial/condo is the exception — NOT easing:** Citizens **Commercial Lines +10.4% (capped) on +18.8% uncapped** indication; multifamily underwriting still tightening (aging systems, water loss, lender requirements); owners redirecting property-cat savings into **liability/umbrella**. HB 1021/1203 (condo/HOA) compliance squeeze + Citizens barred from renewing non-compliant associations = structural pressure on worst condo risks.

**Nexus-relevant gap:** **condo-master / association exposure is NOT cleanly disclosed** in any FL bank filing — buried in CRE/C&I/specialty books. Best public proxies stay broad criticized/classified-CRE + reserve-build series. If you're tracing whether insurer-wrapper / PE-insurer structures absorb-or-amplify this, the FL commercial-residential rate hardening (+18.8% indicated) is the cost-push input, and the disclosure opacity is the visibility gap.

*Caveat: the easing is loss-experience-dependent — a major 2026 FL landfall reverses the wholesale softening fast.*
