## (c) What FLG will NOT infer from silence

1. **No modification/FDM table in the release ≠ modifications improved.** The tables are a 10-Q item; wait for T-02 / T-13.
2. **No mention of the rent freeze ≠ the freeze is not biting.** The pre-registered fuse is Q2-2028 (T-11); a quiet Q3/Q4 is the expected path.
3. **No buyback figure in the release ≠ nothing was repurchased.** Check the share count and the 10-Q equity statement.
4. **No disclosure of buyer financing ≠ none occurred.** "UNDISCLOSED" is a limit, not a signal (WQ-312 rule).
5. **No new exhibit ≠ no event.** A separate 8-K can follow; check EDGAR, not only the release.
6. **No roll-forward in the release ⇒ no flow claim.** Payoff, cure and formation shares come only from the 10-Q.
7. **"Par payoffs" ≠ exits without loss.** Prior charge-offs on those loans are not disclosed (KB-061/066).
8. **Silence on the litigation ≠ no ruling.** T-12 is read at the docket/press separately (full City production due ~10/24; merits "before year-end").
9. **Friday's price move is not thesis evidence.** Price is not a thesis input; REGINALD owns VX-REG-6.03 (already RED since 10/7).
10. **A management "CRE concentration" figure is not the SR 07-1 ratio** unless numerator and denominator are stated; the desk's ratio comes from the Call Report.
11. **A release figure does not grade FLG-02 or FLG-03.** Their instruments are the Q3 10-Q; the release is a preview recorded as such.
