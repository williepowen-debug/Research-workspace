Flagstar Bank, N.A. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
NEW YORK, October 23, 2026 – Flagstar Bank, N.A. (the "Bank") (NYSE: FLG), today reported third quarter 2026 net income of $12 million compared to net income of $34 million for second quarter 2026 and compared to a net loss of $36 million for third quarter 2025. Third quarter 2026 net income attributable to common stockholders was $3 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, compared to net income attributable to common stockholders of $26 million, or $0.06 per diluted share in second quarter 2026 and compared to a net loss attributable to common stockholders of $45 million, or $0.11 per diluted share in third quarter 2025.
CEO COMMENTARY 
Commenting on the Bank's third quarter 2026 performance, Executive Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Joseph M. Otting stated, "Our third quarter results reflect the fact that the Bank's core underlying fundamentals continued to improve despite the emergence of several headwinds, including a higher interest rate environment, deposit competition, persistent inflation, as well as the continuing impact of rent regulation policies on our New York City multi-family portfolio, which pressured our credit costs. We believe we are on track toward achieving our long-term strategic objectives, however given the higher for longer interest rate environment, the full realization of these objectives has shifted to 2028 from 2027. 
"Notwithstanding these headwinds, during the third quarter, we made progress in multiple areas. Our net interest income increased 5% compared to the previous quarter, net interest margin expanded by 4 basis points, and adjusted pre-provision net revenue improved 6%, reflecting continued momentum in our operating performance.
"We also recorded another quarter of solid loan growth driven by broad-based growth across the C&I platform, along with deposit growth within that business. We continue to diversify the balance sheet through a strategic reduction in the multi-family and commercial real estate portfolios, offset by originations of new CRE loans across our footprint. The growth in C&I and the reduction in the multi-family and CRE portfolios are diversifying our loan portfolio. Additionally, the repricing and ongoing reduction of our multi-family portfolios are also positives as we work to improve our loan mix and net interest income. 
"We believe these trends demonstrate ongoing progress in strengthening our core franchise and improving the quality and composition of our balance sheet. We remain focused on executing our long-term strategic plan of transforming Flagstar into a high performing regional bank. While the current interest rate environment may affect the pace at which we achieve certain objectives, our commitment to the transformation remains unchanged. We will continue to prioritize disciplined risk management, sustainable earnings growth, balance sheet strength, and the consistent execution of our strategic plan to position the Bank for long-term success."

EARNINGS SUMMARY FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026
Net Interest Income, Net Interest Margin, and Average Balance Sheet
Net Interest Income
Third quarter 2026 net interest income totaled $460 million compared to $440 million, up $20 million or 5% compared to second quarter 2026 and rose $35 million or 8% compared to third quarter 2025.
Linked-Quarter Comparison
•Average interest-earnings assets increased $1.2 billion or 1.4% to $84.3 billion due to higher average loan balances and higher average securities, partially offset by lower average cash balances.
•Average interest-bearing liabilities increased $0.9 billion or 1.4% to $66.4 billion, as average borrowed funds increased 2% and average interest-bearing deposits rose 1%.
•The net interest margin increased 4 basis points to 2.17% driven by a 6 basis point increase in the average yield partially offset by a 2 basis point increase in the average cost of funds. 
Year-Over-Year Comparison
•Average interest-earning assets decreased 5% to $84.3 billion driven by lower average loans and lower average cash balances partially offset by higher average securities balances.
•Average interest-bearing liabilities decreased 6% or $4.0 billion to $66.4 billion as both average deposits and average borrowings balances declined due to strategic actions taken by the Bank to reduce its cost of funds.
•Average deposits declined 2% to $55.9 billion as the Bank significantly reduced brokered deposits throughout 2025.
•Average borrowings declined 21% or $2.7 billion to $10.5 billion as the Bank continued to pay down wholesale borrowings.
•The net interest margin increased 26 basis points driven by a 51 basis point decline in the average cost of funds as the average cost of borrowings decreased 33 basis points and the average cost of deposits declined 51 basis points. This was partially offset by a 17 basis points decrease in the average yield as the average yield on securities declined 42 basis points, the average yield on cash balances declined 69 basis points and the average loan yield declined 12 basis points.

RECONCILIATIONS OF CERTAIN GAAP AND NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
In addition to GAAP measures, management considers various non-GAAP measures when evaluating the performance of the business. 
We believe that non-interest income, operating expenses, pre-provision net (loss) revenue (which includes both non-interest income and non-interest expense), net income (loss), net income (loss) attributed to common stockholders, diluted earnings (loss) per share, the net interest margin, and our efficiency ratio as adjusted for items that we believe are not indicative of core operating results, such as but not limited to merger and restructuring expenses, litigation settlement expenses related to cases prior to the acquisition of Flagstar Bank, NA, fair value adjustments on non-core equity investments, as well as adjustments for severance and impairment charges and other exit costs resulting from strategic shifts in our operations provide valuable insights to investors by highlighting our underlying performance. These non-GAAP metrics also facilitate meaningful comparisons to other financial institutions, as they are widely used and frequently referenced by investors and analysts. 
We believe average tangible common stockholders’ equity, tangible common stockholders’ equity, average tangible assets and tangible book value per share are important measures for evaluating the performance of the business without the impact of our intangible assets. These non-GAAP metrics also provide investors with important indications regarding our ability to grow the business, our ability to pay dividends as well as engage in capital strategies in addition to facilitating meaningful comparisons to other financial institutions, as they are widely used and frequently referenced by investors and analysts.
These non-GAAP measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for comparable measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Moreover, the way we calculate these non-GAAP measures may differ from that of other companies reporting non-GAAP measures with similar names.
