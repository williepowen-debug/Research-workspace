## Executive Summary

Analysis of three DC-corridor regional banks reveals a **spectrum of federal employment/DOGE exposure risk**, with Burke & Herbert Bank (BHRB) at highest risk due to its explicit government contractor strategy, TowneBank (TOWN) at moderate-high risk due to Hampton Roads military dependence, and M&T Bank (MTB) at lowest risk due to proactive de-risking actions.

## 1. TowneBank (TOWN) — Hampton Roads/Norfolk, VA

### Federal/Defense Exposure Profile
- **Geographic Concentration:** #1 deposit market share in Hampton Roads (~30%), home to Naval Station Norfolk (world's largest naval base), 80,000+ active-duty military, 19 military installations
- **Defense Contractor Presence:** All top 10 U.S. defense contractors have operations in Hampton Roads
- **Federal Employment:** ~16.1% of metro employment is federal/military

### CRE Exposure Analysis

| Metric | Value | Risk Assessment |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Total CRE Exposure | **$7.88B** (59% of total $13.4B loans) | 🔴 HIGH |
| CRE Non-Owner Occupied | $3.81B (28.5% of loans) | Office-heavy |
| Construction & Development | $1.24B (9.3%) | Elevated |
| Total Assets | $19.69B | Regional powerhouse |

### Defensive Actions Taken
- ❌ **No significant portfolio sales** identified (unlike AUB's Blackstone deal)
- ✅ Acquisition-driven growth (Old Point, Village Bank) — diversifying geographically into Richmond, Carolinas
- ✅ Strong credit metrics: NPAs 0.05%, charge-offs 0.06% annualized
- ⚠️ Announced Dogwood State Bank acquisition adds SC/TN exposure

### Earnings Call Intelligence (Q4 2025)
- **No explicit DOGE or federal risk mentions** in public statements
- Management tone: Optimistic about "transformation" and expansion
- Focus: Integration of 2025 acquisitions, not de-risking

### Stock Performance Since Jan 2025
- Jan 2025: ~$32.50 → Jan 2026: ~$35.00-$36.00
- **YTD +8-10%** — Market has NOT priced federal risk
- All-time high: $37.35 (Aug 2025)

### Risk Rating: 🟠 **ORANGE**

**Rationale:** 
- Extreme CRE concentration (59%) in federal/military-dependent metros
- No proactive de-risking like AUB/MTB
- Hampton Roads more insulated than DC (military vs. civilian federal)
- Strong current credit metrics provide buffer
- Risk is 12-24 month lagged if defense contractors face procurement freezes

## 2. Burke & Herbert Bank (BHRB) — Alexandria, VA

### Federal/Defense Exposure Profile
- **Geographic Core:** Alexandria, VA — pure DC suburbs, Loudoun County, Fredericksburg, Richmond
- **Explicit Strategy (May 2025):** Hired Oliver James to *"build business in the Government Contracting Industry"* — actively increasing exposure
- **Federal Employment Context:** Northern Virginia lost ~16,000 professional services jobs in 2025 (contractor sector)

### CRE Exposure Analysis

| Metric | Value | Risk Assessment |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Total CRE Exposure | **$2.77B** (~50% of $5.4B total loans) | 🟠 HIGH |
| Total Loans | $5.4B | Smaller bank |
| Total Deposits | $6.4B | |
| Total Assets | $7.8B | |

### Office CRE Market Context (Northern Virginia Q1-Q4 2025)
- **Vacancy Rate:** 23.2% overall, **27.2% Class A** (Ballston 47.4% vacant!)
- **DOGE Impact:** 29 lease terminations in DMV, 1.75M SF, $64.6M annual rent savings
- **Net Absorption:** Negative 286,510 SF in Q1 2025 alone

### Defensive Actions Taken
- ❌ **ACTIVELY INCREASING government contractor exposure** (opposite of de-risking)
- ✅ Recently completed merger with Summit Financial Group (May 2024)
- ✅ Announced LINKBANCORP merger (Dec 2025) — $11B combined assets, diversifies into DE, KY, PA, WV
- ✅ Exited $201.5M "non-strategic loans" in Q4 2025

### Earnings Call Intelligence (Q4 2025)
- **No DOGE or federal risk acknowledgment** in public statements
- CEO Boyle: "Foundation as we enter 2026 is solid"
- Focus on "granular, diverse, relationship-based loan portfolio"
- Strong capital: CET1 13.2%, well above minimums

### Stock Performance Since Jan 2025
- Trading at ~$56-60 range (Nasdaq-listed mid-2025)
- IPO/listing activity masks true performance assessment
- Revenue up 144% YoY (merger-driven)

### Risk Rating: 🔴 **RED**

**Rationale:**
- **Actively building government contractor lending** at precisely the wrong time
- Pure DC suburbs geography = maximum DOGE exposure
- Northern Virginia office market in distress (27%+ vacancy)
- LINKBANCORP merger adds complexity but diversifies eventually
- Small size = less ability to absorb losses
- This is closer to EGBN model than AUB model

## 3. M&T Bank (MTB) — DC/Baltimore Regional

### Federal/Defense Exposure Profile
- **Geographic Footprint:** Buffalo HQ, but significant Mid-Atlantic/DC/Baltimore presence
- **Federal Employment:** ~15% of DC federal workforce in region, but MTB diversified across Northeast
- **Less Direct Exposure:** Broader geographic diversification reduces single-market risk

### CRE Exposure Analysis

| Metric | Value | Risk Assessment |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Total CRE Exposure | **$24.1B** (declining from $27.9B in Q4 2024) | 🟡 MODERATE |
| CRE as % of Equity | 124% (down from 183%) | Well below 160% internal limit |
| Total Loans | $137.6B | Large regional |
| Criticized CRE Loans | Down $429M in Q4 2025 | Active de-risking |

### Defensive Actions Taken
- ✅ **PROACTIVE DE-RISKING** — Reduced CRE concentration from 183% to 124% of equity
- ✅ Sold out-of-footprint loan portfolio ($15M gain)
- ✅ Reduced criticized loans 27% YoY
- ✅ Nonaccruals at 90 bps — lowest since 2007
- ✅ CRE portfolio down 9% from $27.9B to $24.1B (Q4'24 to Q2'25)

### Earnings Call Intelligence (Q4 2025)
- **No explicit DOGE mentions**, but CFO Bible discussed:
  - "Cautious about possible revisions and slowdown"
  - "Attuned to risk of slowdown in coming quarters due to weakening labor market"
  - Expects CRE growth to "turn positive point-to-point by Q2 2026" — indicating de-risking phase complete
- Record $2.85B net income, $17 EPS for 2025

### Stock Performance Since Jan 2025
- Jan 2025: ~$175 → Jan 2026: ~$210
- **YTD +20%** — Market rewarding de-risking strategy
- All-time high: $214.07 (Nov 2024)
- Analyst target: $250 (TD Cowen, Jan 2026)

### Risk Rating: 🟢 **GREEN**

**Rationale:**
- **AUB Model Exemplar** — proactive CRE de-risking, portfolio sales, reserve builds
- Geographic diversification across Northeast reduces DC/Baltimore concentration
- Strong capital (CET1 10.84%, targets 10.25-10.5%)
- Record profitability provides cushion
- Management explicitly aware of macro risks
- Nonaccruals at 17-year lows
