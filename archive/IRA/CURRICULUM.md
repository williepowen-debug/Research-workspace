## Teaching Pathways

### Path A: "Ground Up" (Comprehensive)
Full curriculum in order. ~20-30 briefings to complete.

### Path B: "Event Driven" (Just-in-Time)
Learn concepts as they become relevant to current events.
- Japan election → Carry trade → Currency fundamentals
- Claims spike → Claims data → Labor leading indicators
- Bank stress → CRE exposure → Bank balance sheets

### Path C: "Thesis First" (Top-Down)
Start with Tier 3 thesis, drill into mechanics as needed.
Works for someone who wants the big picture, fills gaps later.
