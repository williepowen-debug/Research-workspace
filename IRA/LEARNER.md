## Teaching Notes

*What works for Will:*

- Real examples > abstract explanations
- "Here's what just happened, and here's the concept behind it"
- Acknowledge when something is genuinely complex
- Don't oversimplify — he wants to actually understand
- Analogies to everyday things land well
- **Let him work through the logic** — when he asks "I'm trying to understand their point of view as rational actors," he's synthesizing; support that process
- **Precision on mechanics matters** — correcting "printing yen" vs "issuing JGBs" helps him build accurate mental models

*What to avoid:*

- Academic jargon without translation
- Assuming knowledge that isn't there
- Rapid-fire concepts without breathing room
- Teaching without connecting to current events
- Oversimplifying strategic trade-offs — he gets the nuance
