## MARKET MECHANISMS

- **Private-credit → insurance → bank cascade.** Gates and cash substitution create funding pressure; funding pressure forces honest marks; hidden leverage (Level 3 subs, first-loss notes, parent support) and PE-insurer wrappers with fast-fuse wholesale liabilities (FHLB advances, funding-agreement-backed notes, repo-like) amplify losses; bank exposure to non-depository financial institutions carries them into the banking system. Reconcile agent stage labels before acting; verify subsidiary economics before trading a parent narrative. Named firms and current stages → `AGENTS/BROCK/research/PC_CONTAGION_MECHANICS.md`, `AGENTS/BROCK/STATUS.md`, `AGENTS/SHADE/`, `AGENTS/REGINALD/domain/NDFI_HIDDEN_CRE_HYPOTHESIS.md`.
- **Dispersion is not breadth.** A tail-quality ratio (CCC/HY, CCC/BB) at a record while the index sits at a low reads as quality dispersion, never broad widening; it is an ownership-split instrument and never standalone systemic proof. Current levels and split → `HEARTBEAT.md` channel 3 (Credit); owners `AGENTS/REGINALD/` (CCC/HY) and `AGENTS/BROCK/` (CCC/BB + gap).
- **Physical clocks and lagged credit damage.** Financial prices can adjust before physical constraints resolve: a falling crude print is not physical-system repair while infrastructure, chokepoints or insurance stay unresolved. Hard supply depletions create nonlinear deadlines the tape ignores until late, and the resulting GDP and credit drag arrives with a lag — credit can peak before equity. Correct macro still needs execution rails. → `FORGE/research/SEVEN_DEPLETION_CLOCKS.md` and `FORGE/research/DEMAND_DESTRUCTION_FRAMEWORK.md` (March-2026 vintage — the mechanism, not the clocks; their own "ACTIVE" headers are history), `FORGE/research/iran-war/`, `AGENTS/BRENT/STATUS.md`, `AGENTS/HAWK/STATUS.md`. Active clocks live in current owner files, never in old clock lists.
- **Housing is a parallel stress path.** Housing / consumer stress can crack before employment, so the sequence is not cleanly labor-first (CARL's Path C — the owner still labels it provisional). → `AGENTS/CARL/STATUS.md`, `AGENTS/HOMER/`.
- **Labor data can look healthier than the system.** Administrative and data timing can suppress reported claims (LABOR's shadow adjustment), and headline payroll beats can mask deterioration when concentrated in healthcare, strike returns, government, or revisions. Inspect timing and composition before updating a labor thesis. → `AGENTS/LABOR/STATUS.md`.
- **Permanent data gaps.** When an official series is cancelled (USDA Ag Labor Survey, DOL NAWS), the blind spot is permanent — use indirect signals rather than treating absence of evidence as evidence. → `AGENTS/MARCO/STATUS.md`.
- **Sovereign-buyer regime shifts.** Structural changes in reserve and institutional demand for Treasuries — geopolitical stigma turning diversification into political cover; Japan's multi-year buyer-regime shift in insurer hedged returns, BOJ policy and carry — are regimes, not single-meeting or FY-end events. Track TIC / FX / BOJ / Gulf flows as one demand-hole complex. → `AGENTS/ZHAO/` (stigma thread in its sources, not the live STATUS), `AGENTS/SAM/STATUS.md`, `AGENTS/SAM/research/JAPAN_FYEND_REPATRIATION.md`, `AGENTS/LIQUID/`.
- **Policy liquidity masks stress.** Surface calm can be intervention working, not system health: treat Fed / Treasury bill absorption, reserves and repo plumbing as regime modifiers before reading a calm tape as clean risk appetite. → `FORGE/research/FED_TBILL_REPO_ANALYSIS.md` (March-2026 thread — mechanism; current plumbing at `AGENTS/LIQUID/`).
- **Dealer-capacity nonlinearity.** Treasury clearing can turn nonlinear under dealer balance-sheet constraints; SLR / eSLR policy may be decisive. → `FORGE/timing/research/DEALER_CAPACITY_RESPONSE_1B_PERPLEXITY.md` (frozen corpus — history; current numbers at BOND / LIQUID).
- **Fast-transmission vs reservoir banks.** In a fast-transmission bank, losses bypass the delinquency pipeline into P&L and management guidance can lag already-visible loss velocity; in a reservoir bank, losses accumulate behind structures and reserves and surface late. The two need different expiry logic and different gating (idiosyncratic / print-driven, not cohort assumptions). Examples at their owners → `AGENTS/WAL/`, `AGENTS/OZK/`, `AGENTS/REGINALD/STATUS.md`.

## RESEARCH OPERATING PRINCIPLES

- **Primary evidence over agent confidence.** Tone does not imply coverage; model agreement is not a source. Weight methodology and primary-source citations over confidence, triangulate across independent sources when the claim is load-bearing, and flag blind spots and stale dependencies proactively.
- **Independent cold review tests coverage, not confidence.** A cold-boot reader with no fleet context, reading one domain or one artifact whole, catches staleness, orphan files and evidence gaps the daily operator cannot see. → `.claude/agents/coldreader.md`, `PROME/tools/spine_audit.workflow.js`.
- **Execution rails complete the thesis.** A thesis without pre-registered ladders, branches or decision triggers is still a hypothesis; the canonical failure is a correct call with no roll rule. → `PROME/GATES.tsv`, `PROME/DOCKET.tsv`, `AGENTS/TERRY/RISK_RULES.md`, BROCK LESSONS #16.
- **Content freshness, not timestamp freshness.** A fresh header can certify a stale body. Audit dated references inside entries, not just the stamp; a freshness check cannot catch a fresh lie. → `scripts/ledger_staleness.py`, root `CLAUDE.md` § Data Hygiene.
- **Explicit change history for material thesis revisions.** Thesis and framework files must not drift silently — a material edit needs a changelog entry or audit trail first, and the prior text stays reachable (`git show <sha>:<path>`).
- **Reconcile before acting on a shared figure.** When two desks carry the same metric, reconcile to one figure at the owner rather than siloing; a corrected figure owes its consumers a packet. → `scripts/consumer_check.py`, root `CLAUDE.md` § Roster (scoped overlaps).
